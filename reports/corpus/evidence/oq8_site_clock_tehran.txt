OQ-8 — Site wall clock is Asia/Tehran local.
================================================================
Site: https://radio.iranseda.ir. Probed 2026-11-05 ~17:12–17:26 UTC.

[1] Time-slot URL with a Gregorian d= and a ti=:
        https://radio.iranseda.ir/timeArchivePart/?VALID=TRUE&ch=14&ti=12:30&d=11/5/2026
    -> 200, 40,892 bytes
       title: "آرشیو رادیو   - پنجشنبه 14 آبان 1405 - ساعت 12:30"

    d=11/5/2026 is Gregorian 5 Nov 2026. The page title renders it as
    Jalali "پنجشنبه 14 آبان 1405" (Thursday 14 Aban 1405) and echoes the
    exact requested time "ساعت 12:30". The body carries the episodes that
    aired in that 30-minute slot (e.g. e=154148408, the 12:30 session).

[2] Control time-slot 13:30 on the same day:
        https://radio.iranseda.ir/timeArchivePart/?VALID=TRUE&ch=14&ti=13:30&d=11/5/2026
    -> 200, 40,937 bytes
       title: "آرشیو رادیو   - پنجشنبه 14 آبان 1405 - ساعت 13:30"

    (An earlier 12:30 probe that used a Jalali d= returned the 15-byte
    empty page, consistent with OQ-1.)

Conclusion: the site converts the Gregorian d= to a Jalali day and
renders the exact requested slot time. The title is the canonical
time-slot signature:
    "آرشیو رادیو   - {weekday} {d} {month} {year} - ساعت {HH:MM}"
used by the validator's strict check.
