On the eve of the return of the Free Men, the program began by honoring their sacrifices and emphasizing their role in preserving national security and faith.
Subsequently, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi discussed the religious virtue of avoiding fame and not seeking public recognition in the "Parsaiyan" segment.
Citing narrations from Imam Ali and the Prophet, he stressed that God loves the humble believer and that one should not seek to be famous.
Stories of the humility of Ashgh-Abbas Qomi and Ayatollah Bahjat were shared to illustrate their consistent refusal of titles and public attention.
In the "Book Narrative" section, a story about two pilgrims in Mecca was told, highlighting the deep spiritual connection with the Ahl al-Bayt.
Additionally, the legal ruling on prayer for travelers staying more than ten days in a city was explained based on Ayatollah Khamenei's opinion.
The program concluded with advice to dedicate time for reflection on religious teachings and invited listeners to the "Barkaran-e Noor" program.
