The radio program begins by arguing that hypocrites did not join Imam Ali's faction after the Prophet's death, citing narrations that they were identified by their hatred of him and later supported the opposing side in the Battle of Jamal. The discussion then shifts to the conflict with the Banu Qurayza, explaining that their betrayal during the siege of Medina led to their siege and the execution of their warriors based on the judgment of Sa'd ibn Mu'adh. Furthermore, Imam Hasan's peace treaty with Muawiya is described as a "heroic flexibility," as it prevented the martyrdom or capture of the Imam and his followers due to the betrayal of his own commanders. The program concludes with the recitation of a prayer for rain from the Sahifa Sajjadiya to alleviate drought, while inviting listeners to submit their questions to the experts via text message to number 304.
