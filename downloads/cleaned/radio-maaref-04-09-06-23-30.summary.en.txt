In the radio program "Mr. Judge," a criminal case was examined where a man's illegal parking in front of a neighbor's door delayed the transport of a sick father to the hospital, resulting in his death. Forensic medicine confirmed that the patient would have survived if transported just five minutes earlier, directly linking the delay to the blocked exit. Hojjat-ol-Islam Rezaei, head of a High Court branch, identified the parker as the cause of involuntary manslaughter under Articles 506 and 618 of the Islamic Penal Code. Consequently, the defendant was sentenced to one year of imprisonment and ordered to pay blood money, as his negligence directly led to the fatality. This ruling emphasizes that any action violating regulations that causes harm or death carries significant criminal and financial liability. The program concluded with the recitation of Ziyarat Al-Yasin and the Dua of Ahad, highlighting the importance of awaiting the Mahdi's appearance. It also stressed the need for Muslims to awaken and follow the practical, political, and defensive legacy of Prophet Muhammad.
