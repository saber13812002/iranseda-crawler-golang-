During a parliamentary session, Ghalibaf emphasized the Supreme Leader's call for unity and expressed strong support for the government to resolve public issues. The head of the Center for Mosque Affairs announced upcoming events honoring war martyrs and the sixth national student prayer conference in Semnan. A national campaign titled "For the Love of the Prophet" was launched to facilitate the release of prisoners convicted of non-intentional crimes. In the housing sector, land was allocated to over 2,700 families eligible for the population youth plan in Khorasan Razavi, alongside the inauguration of new housing units. The National Space Organization reported the imminent launch of the Zafar, Paya, and second Kousar satellites, along with the start of the first phase of the Chabahar space base. These developments highlight the private sector's entry into the space industry and Iran's rising global ranking.
