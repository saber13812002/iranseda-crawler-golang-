In this radio program, Professor Amir-Mohsen Erfan analyzed the cultural and social characteristics of the Arbaeen pilgrimage. He emphasized the purity and selflessness of relationships during the event, contrasting them with the calculated nature of everyday social interactions. Erfan described Arbaeen as a voluntary and conscious spiritual experience that generates genuine believer behavior and action. He criticized the "Christ-like" or sacrificial view of Imam Hussein's martyrdom, warning that mourning should not be seen merely as atonement for sins but as a path to behavioral change and salvation from ignorance. The key features of this culture were identified as competitive, pure, responsible, and loving social relations. Ultimately, self-sacrifice and altruism were presented as the core of the Husseini culture and a model for religious life in the era of occultation. The final goal is to extend this brotherhood and conscious service throughout the year and into the daily lives of the awaiters.
