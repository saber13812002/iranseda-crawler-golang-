In this radio program, analysts emphasized the necessity of nuclear negotiations between Iran and the United States to lift sanctions, while expressing caution regarding the counterpart's sincerity due to the previous withdrawal from the JCPOA.
The Supreme Leader supported negotiations within the framework of national interests, noting that Iran does not seek nuclear weapons based on religious principles, and adopted a realistic approach that is neither overly optimistic nor pessimistic.
In the second segment, Dr. Sajjad Mirkhaili explained the strategic importance of Yemen due to its control over the Bab el-Mandeb strait and its impact on global trade and Israel's logistical costs.
He stressed that Iran's support for Yemeni resistance is rooted in shared goals and faith, rather than a proxy relationship or a transactional dynamic based on hegemony.
The program also commemorated weekly political anniversaries, including the establishment of the IRGC, the start of the Cultural Revolution, and the martyrdom of Colonel Gharani, the first Chairman of the Joint Staff.
It continued with a book review of "The Impact of the Islamic Revolution on International Relations," highlighting the revolution's role in transforming Western theories in the international arena.
Finally, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi cited narrations from the Infallibles to describe humility as one of the greatest acts of worship and a blessing accessible to all humans.
These discussions demonstrated that Iran's foreign policy is grounded in religious principles and the defense of the people's rights, maintaining its stance against injustice while engaging with the world.
