The program began by emphasizing the importance of time management and utilizing one's life effectively. It then transitioned into a dramatic retelling from the book "Let the Bad Blow," focusing on the tensions preceding the Battle of Karbala. The narrative highlighted Har b. Yazid Rihabi's efforts to prevent war with Imam Hussein (PBUH) and his subsequent despair after failing to dissuade Umar Sa'd. Witnessing the siege of the Euphrates and the army's disregard for the Imam's requests, Har felt trapped and conflicted. After hearing Sulaiman's words and Abdullah's distressing dream, Har ultimately decided to abandon Yazid's army and return to Kufa. The program concluded with a jurisprudential answer regarding the validity of prayer while moving in vehicles. It also mentioned a specialized seminar for Quran reciters and the radio station's media activities during the Arbaeen pilgrimage.
