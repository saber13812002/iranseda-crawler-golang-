The radio program "Narrative of Love," dedicated to the wives and mothers of martyrs, recounts the life story of Zahra Saeedin, the wife of Martyr Ahmad Ghanbari. Zahra grew up in a deeply religious family and strengthened her faith by memorizing the Holy Quran. She married her mother's neighbor, Ahmad Ghanbari, a pilot of ultra-light aircraft, and they welcomed their son, Hossein. Ahmad Ghanbari achieved the rank of chief pilot due to his precision and played a crucial role in missions to identify terrorist nests. Zahra shares her constant anxieties regarding her husband's dangerous profession and the final moments of their shared life. Ultimately, Ahmad Ghanbari was martyred in a flight accident on February 15, 2002, and Zahra accepted this tragedy with patience and resilience. The program concludes by teasing the next episode, which will detail Zahra's life after her husband's martyrdom, and invites listeners to provide feedback via phone.
