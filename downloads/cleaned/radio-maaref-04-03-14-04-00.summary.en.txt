The radio program began by discussing the blood money (diya) owed for an unmarried woman, noting it is payable to her father or herself. Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi emphasized that the Prayer of Verses must be performed immediately after an earthquake and repeated for each distinct occurrence. Ayatollah Javadi Amoli advised prostrating on the smooth, unmarked part of the prayer mat to avoid impurity or distraction. Regarding a forehead hitting the mat twice in one prostration, Ayatollah Sistani clarified that it counts as a single valid prostration if the intention remains unchanged. The distinction between kaffarah for intentional neglect and fidya for excused absence was explained, noting both are distributed to the poor. Finally, Ayatollah Sadrani stated that administering medication to an infant requires consensus among medical experts; if there is disagreement, it should be avoided.
