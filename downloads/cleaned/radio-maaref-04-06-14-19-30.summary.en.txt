The radio program begins by introducing the production team and providing ways to support the oppressed people of Gaza.
It then presents the story "Bright Future," which depicts part of Mohammad Ali Rajai's revolutionary struggles before the Islamic Revolution.
In the narrative, Rajai, a teacher at Kemaleh High School, travels to Qazvin to distribute Ayatollah Khomeini's announcements.
During his journey, he encounters suspicious men and is eventually arrested by the SAVAK intelligence agency.
Despite harsh interrogations and solitary confinement, Rajai remains calm and refuses to confess or reveal any information.
After his release, he continues his activities alongside his colleagues despite increased security pressure and a transfer to Pahlavi High School.
The story concludes by emphasizing hope for a bright future and paying tribute to the souls of martyrs Rajai and Bahonar.
