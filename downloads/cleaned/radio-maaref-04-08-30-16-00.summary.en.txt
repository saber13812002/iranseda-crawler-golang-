In the radio program "Historical Perseman," Dr. Mostafa Sadeghi Kashani addressed listeners' questions regarding Islamic historical events. He began by noting the potential discrepancies between historical films and authentic sources, then answered a query about why Abdullah ibn Muti did not aid Mukhtar al-Thaqafi. The expert explained that Ibn Muti remained loyal to Abdullah ibn Zubayr, Mukhtar's enemy, and was physically present in Mecca when Musayyab attacked Kufa, making assistance impossible. In the second segment, he refuted the notion that youth hinders spiritual attainment, citing the high status of Fatima Zahra (SA) and highlighting the ten-year age difference between Shia and Sunni traditions regarding her birth. The program also detailed Fatima Zahra's (SA) various acts of defense for Imam Ali (AS) after the Prophet's passing, including her public speeches, claim to Fadak, and boycott of the Caliph. Furthermore, it was clarified that the sword Zulfiqar was a war booty from the Battle of Badr that reached Ali (AS) before the Battle of Uhud. Finally, the structure of the three-pronged arrow that struck Imam Hussein (AS) was discussed, presenting two different hypotheses about its physical design.
