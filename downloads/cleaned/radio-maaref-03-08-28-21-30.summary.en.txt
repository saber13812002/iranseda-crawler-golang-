In this program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verses 162 to 164 of Surah An-Nisa, highlighting two exceptional groups among the People of the Book: the "firmly rooted in knowledge" and the "true believers." He defines being firmly rooted in knowledge as the manifestation of action, arguing that true knowledge is impermanent without practical application. Unlike other People of the Book, these groups do not differentiate between prophets or heavenly books, accepting the Quran and Prophet Muhammad with the same criteria they used for the Torah and the Gospel. The commentary also addresses the grammatical case of "those who establish prayer," attributing its accusative form to the high status of prayer and zakat as fundamental pillars of faith. Fakhr Razi refutes the claim of a scribal error in this verse, attributing such views to a lack of understanding of literary rules. Ultimately, the interpretation emphasizes that the criterion for accepting new revelation is the same logic and miraculous evidence previously used to accept earlier prophets.
