The Supreme Leader addressed the nation's sports and science medalists, describing their achievements as a decisive rebuttal to the enemy's soft war and attempts to instill despair in the Iranian people. He emphasized that Iranian youth possess the talent and perseverance to reach global peaks, serving as a symbol of the country's hope and dynamism. The Leader urged officials to encourage these young talents to aim for even higher achievements rather than resting on their current successes. In the second part of the speech, he characterized the US President's visit to occupied Palestine as an effort to boost the morale of the Zionist regime following its recent defeats. The Leader identified the United States as the primary accomplice in the atrocities in Gaza and the main driver of terrorism in the region. He dismissed US sanctions and interference in Iran's internal affairs as coercive impositions, asserting that the Iranian nation would never accept such dictation. Finally, highlighting the ingenuity of Iranian youth in developing advanced missiles, he underscored their crucial role in safeguarding national security and driving the country's progress.
