In this broadcast, Ayatollah Al-Hashem recounts a memorable anecdote about the close relationship between poet Shahriyar and Ayatollah Malekooti, the Friday prayer leader of Tabriz. Malekooti demonstrated his affection for the poet by gifting him a ring and requesting a recitation of his Turkish poems. The program also highlights the deep devotion of Ayatollah Al-Hashem's mother to Shahriyar's poetry, noting that she had memorized his entire divan. Al-Hashem further details the four official appointments he received from the Supreme Leader in various military and religious organizations. The segment concludes with the recitation of the Ziyarat of Imam Mahdi and the performance of expectation-themed poetry. Finally, a historical speech by Imam Khomeini is played, emphasizing the necessity of preserving the Islamic Republic and warning against those who seek to weaken it. The speech asserts the defeat of foreign powers in Iran and promises that they will not return while the current leadership remains.
