The head of the Unity Week central committee announced that over 12,000 cultural and religious programs will be held nationwide, focusing on strengthening unity and supporting Palestine.
This year's slogan is "In the light of Ahmad, the unity of the nation and the ummah," accompanied by the production of more than seventy artistic works.
News also reported the publication of thirty-two forewords by the Supreme Leader for various books, particularly those concerning the Holy Defense.
The "Islamic Digital Humanities" conference is being held at the Computer Research Center, having received two hundred submitted papers.
In the Parsaiyan program, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the importance of respecting animal rights and avoiding harm, citing historical narratives from the Ahl al-Bayt.
He shared stories of Imam Ali's kindness to a cat and Sayyad Shafati's charity to a dog, illustrating that compassion toward animals can lead to spiritual and material blessings.
In the rulings section, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidpour explained that consuming prohibited substances for medical treatment is only permissible in cases of genuine necessity when no halal alternative exists.
Additionally, fatwas clarified the prayer and fasting rules for frequent inter-city travelers and confirmed that feeding cats is a recommended act in Islam.
