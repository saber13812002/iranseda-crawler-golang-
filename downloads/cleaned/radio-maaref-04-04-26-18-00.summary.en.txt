In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Falahati identifies stinginess as a dangerous vice and a sign of practical shirk, based on Quranic verses. He argues that neglecting parents, orphans, neighbors, and the needy is essentially a disregard for practical monotheism, which God dislikes. Citing a narration from Imam Ali, the speaker emphasizes that preventing others from giving charity, even when the wealth belongs to God, is a major sin. Stingy individuals are compared to the ego-driven soul that encourages others toward miserliness and hides God's blessings. In the second segment, Hujjat al-Islam Ashnavid interprets a wisdom from Saadi's Gulistan, highlighting the importance of generosity for a good reputation. He explains that those deprived of a blessing appreciate it more than those who possess it, and that understanding others' pain requires personal empathy and experience. The final conclusion underscores the necessity of charity and generosity as manifestations of monotheism and a path to earning love and good character.
