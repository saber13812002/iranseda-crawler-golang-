The program begins with an exegesis of verse 17 of Surah Al-Imran, emphasizing the prohibition of breaking oaths and making false promises.
The commentator explains that trading God's covenant for material gain is a major sin rooted in worldly attachment.
It is stressed that trustworthiness and keeping promises are universal principles, and in the Hereafter, the punishment for oath-breakers is God's indifference to them.
In the rulings section, it is clarified that a few days' delay in salary payment does not incur Khums unless a full lunar year has passed.
The conditions for returning usurped rights (Radd-e-Mazalim) when the owner is unknown are explained as paying the poor on their behalf.
Regarding the Qunut prayer, reciting the Khasta Salawat is permissible, but the Nad-e-Ali prayer is not allowed due to calling upon someone other than God.
The program concludes with Quranic recitation, mystical poetry, and the morning prayer of need.
