In the "Persman Enghelab" program on Radio Ma'aref, Dr. Najaf Lakssai analyzed the region's security and political developments. He emphasized the risk of Syria's disintegration by Israel and the release of terrorists by armed groups, citing these actions as the primary causes of chaos and sectarian conflict in the country. Lakssai described the Islamic Republic's policy as consistently standing with the oppressed and opposing occupation, highlighting Iran's diplomatic efforts to preserve Syria's national unity. In response to questions regarding nuclear threats, he argued that Iran's nuclear technology is indigenous and irreversible, and that any attack would trigger a decisive and serious response from the Islamic Republic. He also characterized the concept of "ideological behavior" as a media tactic used by enemies to justify pressure and impose submission on Iran. Finally, Lakssai defined hijab as an inseparable part of Islamic jurisprudence and religious identity, defending it against the West's secularization agenda.
