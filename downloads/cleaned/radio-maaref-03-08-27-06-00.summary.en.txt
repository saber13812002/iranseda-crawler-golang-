The program begins with a public service announcement urging listeners to conserve gas and avoid smoking near windows to prevent harming neighbors, particularly vulnerable relatives.
The main segment, "Barakeh Noor," features Ayatollah Javadi Amoli's exegesis of verses 159 to 161 of Surah An-Nisa, focusing on the ascension of Jesus (PBUH).
The commentator cites authentic narrations from both Shia and Sunni traditions to affirm that Jesus is alive and ascended bodily to heaven, a view supported by a report from Imam Hasan (AS) on the night of the Prophet's passing.
The discussion then addresses the Quranic verse regarding the People of the Book believing before death, interpreting it as a reference to Jesus's return in the end times when all will believe in him.
The latter part of the lecture analyzes verses concerning the oppression and consumption of unlawful wealth by the Jews, attributing these sins to the corruption of their scholars and the distortion of their scriptures.
Finally, the commentator contrasts the Jewish clergy, who fell into materialism and forgery, with Christian clergy, who are described as closer to Islam due to their humility and lack of arrogance.
