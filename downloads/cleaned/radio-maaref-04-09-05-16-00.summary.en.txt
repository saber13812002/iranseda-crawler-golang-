In the radio program "Persaman I'tiqadi," Professor Mohammad Sharoudi addressed various theological questions from listeners. He emphasized that acquiring knowledge is essential for knowing God and maintaining humility before Him, while asserting that abandoning prayer is a grave sin that God does not forgive. Regarding the selection of names, he dismissed the use of Abjad calculations and superstition as baseless and psychologically harmful, instead recommending choosing names from the Quran. The professor clarified that sacrificing one's life is considered martyrdom only when done in the path of God and under the command of the Imam or his representative. He interpreted Prophet Joseph's actions in the Quran not as slander, but as part of a divine test and a necessary measure to prevent greater evil. Furthermore, he explained that Prophet Moses was sent to save the Israelites from oppression and misguidance, as prophets are tasked with guiding their people. Finally, he defined the "Greatest Name" (Ism-e-A'zam) as a combination of spiritual rank and specific recitation, and refuted the claim that the phrase "La ilaha illa Allah" is exclusive to the month of Rajab.
