In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Rostamnejad explores the multifaceted impact of human actions in this world and the hereafter, based on Quranic verses. He explains that every action, whether good or evil, affects the actor's character before it even reaches others. Furthermore, today's deeds not only determine one's ultimate fate but also influence future generations and the surrounding nature, as a lack of piety can lead to cosmic corruption and reduced sustenance. Regarding the afterlife, the concept of the "incarnation of deeds" is introduced, suggesting that actions will manifest as tangible, independent entities on the Day of Judgment. The Quran illustrates the horror of this scene by stating that sinners wish for a temporal distance of centuries between themselves and their evil deeds. Finally, the speaker emphasizes that forgetting the afterlife is the greatest obstacle to human moral growth, and that acknowledging this reality is the key to spiritual development and salvation in the modern world.
