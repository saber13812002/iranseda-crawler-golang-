In this radio program, the discussion begins by emphasizing the detestability of the world in the eyes of God, citing narrations and Nahj al-Balagha sermons that urge using worldly matters solely as a means to draw closer to Him. Dr. Saeed Abbas Hosseini Bagherabadi then highlights Imam Ali's (PBUH) steadfastness in defending the religion, referencing Sermons 37 and 39 of Nahj al-Balagha where the Imam describes his solitary struggle against injustice despite the people's indifference. The speaker compares the Imam to a mountain that storms cannot move, illustrating his resilience and the pain of being abandoned by his companions while fighting for truth. A historical anecdote from the Battle of Siffin is shared, where Muawiya's army retreated after witnessing Ali's (PBUH) unyielding courage and bravery in single combat. The program also marks the occasion of Eid al-Mubahala and the revelation of the Verse of Wilayah, praying for the removal of oppression from the world. Finally, the broadcast concludes with prayers for the Imam of the Age and an introduction to upcoming programs on the Ma'aref radio network.
