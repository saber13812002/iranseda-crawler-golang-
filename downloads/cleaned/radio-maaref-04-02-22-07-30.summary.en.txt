In this radio program, a listener first shares a personal experience highlighting the social harms caused by excessive and insincere hospitality, known as "ta'arof." The speaker emphasizes, citing religious narrations, that such gestures should be genuine and must not impose undue burdens or difficulties on the other party. Following this segment, the main discussion shifts to the topic of anger from the perspective of Islamic ethics and the teachings of the Infallibles. The program's expert identifies anger as an "illegitimate child," a "bad companion," and the "key to evil," based on hadiths from Imam Ali and Imam Sadiq (PBUH). It is explained that anger is an internal fire rooted in the ego-driven soul, which can become humanity's primary enemy if left unchecked. The program concludes by advising listeners to recognize the true nature of anger and avoid associating with angry individuals to prevent the social and personal damages caused by this moral vice.
