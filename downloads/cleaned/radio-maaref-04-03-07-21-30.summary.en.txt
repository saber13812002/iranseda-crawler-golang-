This program explores the concept of self-knowledge and its role in faith through the interpretation of verse 105 of Surah Al-Ma'idah. Ayatollah Javadi Amoli emphasizes that knowing oneself is the primary prerequisite for knowing God, noting that faith exists on various levels. He explains that most believers suffer from a hidden form of shirk due to incomplete knowledge and reliance on intermediaries. To overcome this, one must move beyond conceptual knowledge and attain intuitive, direct perception of the truth. The central argument is that God is closer to humans than anything else, including their own selves, with self-centeredness being the only barrier. By recognizing the true nature of the self and shedding mental veils, individuals can achieve divine proximity and complete faith.
