The program begins by emphasizing the role of national unity and resistance as the key to victory in the Sacred Defense.
The guest, Mr. Ghalami, shares his memories of captivity and the humiliating behavior of Ba'athist soldiers toward Iranian prisoners.
He explains that prayer and connection with God provided comfort and transformed suffering into hope for the freed prisoners.
The second part of the program analyzes Iran's political and security situation, criticizing the policies of the United States and the Zionist regime.
The speaker refers to the US withdrawal from the JCPOA and the assassination of nuclear scientists as illegal and coercive interventions.
He argues that the Iranian people will not surrender to bullying and threats, remaining steadfast in their rights.
Finally, the program concludes with a tribute to Martyr Tehrani Moghaddam and a prayer for the listeners.
