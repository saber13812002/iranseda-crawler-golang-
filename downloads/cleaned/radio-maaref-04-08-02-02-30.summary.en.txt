In this radio program, Professor Sadeghi first examined the figure of Roshayd Hujri, a companion of Imam Ali, attributing his knowledge of the unseen to spiritual insights received from the Imam. He explained that Roshayd's tragic martyrdom at the hands of Ziyad ibn Abi Sufyan demonstrated his firm commitment to Shia Islam and his high spiritual status. Responding to listener questions, the professor clarified that the Kharijites initiated the Battle of Nahrawan and that Imam Ali personally broke the gate of the fortress of Khaybar, citing reliable historical sources. Regarding the rejuvenation of Zuleikha and her marriage to Prophet Yusuf, while some narrations exist, they are subject to caution due to weaker chains of transmission. The claim that prophethood was cut off from Yusuf's lineage due to a minor sin was questioned based on Quranic evidence and the general conduct of prophets. The professor also recounted the rescue of a group of besieged companions during the Battle of Siffin, highlighting the strategic role of Abd al-Aziz ibn Haris Jafari. Finally, he identified Habib al-Najjar, also known as the Believer of the Family of Yasin, as one of the three righteous men mentioned in the Quran, detailing his story of faith and martyrdom.
