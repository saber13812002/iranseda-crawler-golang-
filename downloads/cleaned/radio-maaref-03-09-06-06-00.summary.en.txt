In this program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verses 170 and 171 of Surah An-Nisa to refute five levels of denying monotheism. He argues that no created being can be a partner to God in essence, creation, or lordship, as a slave can never be a partner to the Master. The concept of "Miskin Rasul Allah" from Nahj al-Balagha is explained to mean that ignoring a needy person is ignoring God's messenger, and thanking the intermediary is thanking God. The text then addresses the People of the Book, warning against exaggeration (ghuluw) in religion and attributing sonship to God. Jesus (PBUH) is defined as the "Word of God" and "Spirit from God," rather than the Son of God, to counter extreme Christian beliefs. Finally, the belief in the Trinity is identified as disbelief, emphasizing that God is absolutely One and has no partners in His essence or attributes.
