This radio program examines the socio-political climate following the Prophet's departure, highlighting the conspiracies of political groups who sought to prevent the Prophet's family from assuming the caliphate by inciting chaos and civil war. Hazrat Zahra (SA) intervened at the household to prevent bloodshed, relying on Islamic modesty and moral presence rather than physical confrontation. Imam Ali (AS) demonstrated that true courage lies in restraining one's sword and maintaining piety, thereby protecting the foundations of Islam from being undermined by internal strife. The discussion also references the Prophet's just ruling in a dispute over an infant, illustrating his commitment to preserving life over personal claims. The latter part of the broadcast strongly criticizes opponents of the Velayat-e Faqih and the Assembly of Experts, labeling them as covert agents of Islam's enemies who are more harmful than open adversaries. The speaker asserts that the Velayat-e Faqih is rooted in the Prophet's authority and that the awakened nation will not allow these individuals to derail the revolutionary path. The program concludes with the Ziyarat of Al-Yasin and prayers for the reappearance of Imam Mahdi (AJ).
