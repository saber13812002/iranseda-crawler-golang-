On the anniversary of the martyrdom of Imam Javad (AS), mourning ceremonies were held at the shrine of Imam Reza (AS) and the mausoleum of Musa ibn Ja'far (AS). The Supreme Leader emphasized the need for joint efforts between Iran and Pakistan to stop Israeli crimes in Gaza and strengthen Islamic unity during a meeting with the Pakistani Prime Minister. The head of the Hajj organization reported that 81% of Iranian pilgrims have entered Saudi Arabia, with the days of Tashriq approaching soon. The 39th National Quran and Obedience Student Festival was held in Ardabil, where 120 participants were selected as winners across 32 disciplines. The Minister of Science announced that PhD graduates subject to military service can fulfill their obligation by completing post-doctoral programs, following the Supreme Leader's approval. Construction of 1,151 standard classrooms has begun under the Foundation for the Oppressed to eliminate unsafe school structures. The religious section detailed the conditions for the Tawaf of Nisa and its prayer as the final obligatory act of Umrah al-Tamattu. Finally, a jurisprudential question regarding the permissibility of spending money given by a husband without specific conditions was addressed.
