The radio program began by emphasizing the significance of Friday prayers and related narrations from the Prophet of Islam. In the Q&A segment, Professor Yousefian addressed a narration from Sheikh Tusi regarding "twelve Mahdis" following the twelve Imams. He refuted this claim by highlighting internal contradictions within the text and the unknown status of its narrators, reaffirming the consensus of twelve Imams among both Shia and Sunni Muslims. The broadcast also announced the 20th specialized conference for Quran reciters, focusing on innovation in recitation. Furthermore, the program called for solidarity with the people of Gaza and encouraged participation in the Friday prayer in Tehran led by Ayatollah Khatami. These segments collectively presented a blend of theological, cultural, and social discussions.
