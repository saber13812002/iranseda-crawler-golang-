In this radio program, Professor Mohammadi emphasized the necessity of adhering to religious laws, labeling pre-marital illicit relationships as sins that destroy social order. He described marriage as the primary path to human perfection, warning that avoiding it leaves individuals spiritually incomplete and distant from their ultimate purpose. Responding to a listener's query, he clarified that while the title "Sayyid" carries specific jurisprudential implications for the descendants of Hashim, spiritual greatness and high ranks are accessible to all humans regardless of lineage. The professor rejected the notion of "blocked fate," arguing that marriage delays are often caused by slow courtship processes that allow for satanic interference. He recommended a practical approach involving a rapid selection of suitable partners to prevent repeated failures and advised using specific prayers from the Mafatih al-Jinan to facilitate marriage. In the final segment, the program highlighted that neglecting the poor and denying them basic rights is a primary driver of crime, economic inequality, and the erosion of social trust.
