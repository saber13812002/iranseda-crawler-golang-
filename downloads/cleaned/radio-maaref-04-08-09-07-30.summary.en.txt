The radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat" began with religious prayers and an introduction to the annual slogan of "investment for production."
The main segment featured an interview with Professor Mohammad Hadi Falah regarding the ethics of supervision and evaluation.
He emphasized that negative judgment and inaccurate assessments can profoundly impact the destinies of individuals and families.
Professor Falah stressed the necessity of professional ethics in selection processes to prevent harm to others.
He called for a practical code of conduct based on religious principles, going beyond simple moral advice.
According to him, any biased, arbitrary, or unfair evaluation constitutes a serious ethical violation.
The program concluded by discussing the essential indicators required for professional and ethical supervision.
The show aimed to promote moral virtues and reduce vices in the social lives of its audience.
