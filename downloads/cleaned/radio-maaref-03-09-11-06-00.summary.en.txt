The radio program "Barkarane Noor" begins with a tafsir of verses 174 to 176 of Surah Nisa by Ayatollah Javadi Amoli. The discussion explains the concept of "Burhan" as a clear proof and definitive argument applicable to both the Prophet's existence and the Holy Quran. The interpretation highlights that the Prophet is the primary entity, while the Quran is a clear light revealed alongside him. From an ontological perspective, the Prophet's light is the first creation of God, and the Quran is the speech revealed for guidance after that. Consequently, the rank of Wilayah and Prophethood is ontologically superior to the Kaaba and even the Quran as a divine effect. This superiority is clearly articulated in narrations such as "Istaghliqin" and the concept of seeking refuge in the Kaaba against the authority of Wilayah. Ultimately, the Quran is considered a divine proof due to its illuminating nature and dominance over darkness, requiring faith and adherence.
