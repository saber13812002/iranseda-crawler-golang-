Unity Week and the Prophet's birthday were celebrated with mass participation, particularly by students, in Indian-administered Kashmir. Participants viewed these events as a symbol of Islamic unity and a means to thwart enemy plots, emphasizing the importance of avoiding division. It was noted that Imam Khomeini established Unity Week by bridging the gap between the Prophet's birthday and Imam Jafar Sadiq's birth to foster Muslim cohesion. The second segment featured the radio special "Jan-e Jahan," which began with congratulations for the Prophet's birthday and Imam Sadiq's birth. The host invited listeners to share the Prophet's key messages for 21st-century humanity via text or phone. Dr. Rostam Najat, a researcher and guest, joined the studio to begin the main discussion with greetings and well-wishes.
