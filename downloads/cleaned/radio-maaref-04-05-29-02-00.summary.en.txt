The program begins with a romantic narrative of Seyed Ali and Rakhshan Sadat in the shrine of Imam Hussein (AS), where pilgrimage is portrayed as a source of peace and a commitment to staying in Karbala. Subsequently, Hojjat-ol-Islam Vahidi emphasized the virtues of pilgrimage based on the narrations of the Infallibles, highlighting its role in spiritual purification and gaining worldly and otherworldly blessings. He cited the practices of Allameh Amini and Ayatollah Behjat to demonstrate that great scholars considered pilgrimage an inseparable part of their daily lives and a means to resolve their problems. The second part of the program featured the memories of the mother of Martyr Seyed Mohammad Hosseini, who described her emotional journey to Syria and her visit to the shrine of Lady Zainab (SA) after her son's martyrdom. She shared a dream of seeing her son reciting elegies and her act of offering his blood-stained belongings to the shrine, revealing her patience and steadfastness in the face of grief. Referring to Lady Zainab (SA) as her model of patience, she asked the listeners to pray for the peace of her son's soul.
