The seventh-day mourning ceremonies for the slain Islamic Revolution leader were held nationwide, marked by large gatherings and anti-American and anti-Israeli slogans. Senior clerics, including Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi and Nouri Hamedani, urged the Assembly of Experts to expedite the selection of a new leader to maintain national cohesion. The President and military officials justified attacks on US bases in the region as a legitimate right of self-defense, warning that any location used to launch aggression against Iran would be targeted. Hezbollah in Lebanon announced missile strikes on Israeli military bases, continuing its retaliatory operations. Field reports describe widespread destruction, numerous casualties, and Israeli citizens fleeing to shelters or abroad. International media have criticized the Israeli military for heavily censoring war news and concealing the true extent of the damage. In Moscow, Russian officials visited the Iranian embassy to offer condolences and reaffirm their support for Iran. Meanwhile, the Iranian people demonstrated their solidarity with the defenders by gathering in mosques and providing material and moral support.
