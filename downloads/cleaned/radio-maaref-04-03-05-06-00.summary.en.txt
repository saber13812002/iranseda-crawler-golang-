In this program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets the verse "Take care of your own souls" from Surah Al-Ma'idah, emphasizing the importance of self-purification and personal accountability for one's actions. He explains that on the Day of Judgment, a person acts as both the scribe and the book of their deeds, as their actions are inscribed in their inner soul and will be revealed. A central point of the discussion is the distinction between "cosmic knowledge" and "self-knowledge"; while both lead to God, self-knowledge is considered more beneficial because it is accompanied by practical moral cultivation. The interpretation of the verse "And We have inspired it to know its wickedness and its righteousness" indicates that humans are created with an innate understanding of good and evil, with a natural inclination toward the good. It is also noted that God, through His grace, not only forgives the sins of those who repent but may transform them into good deeds or erase them from memory to ensure no shame remains in Paradise. Finally, it is emphasized that knowing the Divine Essence cannot be achieved through mental concepts alone; rather, it requires an inner presence and spiritual journey to serve the Truth.
