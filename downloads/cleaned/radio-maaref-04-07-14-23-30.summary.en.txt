Ayatollah Al-Hashem, the Friday Prayer Leader of Tabriz, discusses his honor of winning the Oxy documentary award in Tehran and the praise he received. He refutes an online accusation that he received congratulations due to a family connection with the previous leader, proving through security officials that the accuser was merely a distant relative. Al-Hashem highlights his close and friendly cooperation with provincial security officials to resolve public issues, even mentioning interactions with former ministers. He explains his decision to abstain from the elections of the Central Council of the Society of Participating Clerics due to his affiliation with the armed forces and respect for the Supreme Leader's stance. The Ayatollah emphasizes that he declined ministerial positions after the presidential election because the strategic importance of East Azerbaijan province was deemed higher than a ministry role. He also notes the role of Ayatollah Abbavi in leading prayers for war martyrs following the martyrdom of Ayatollah Madani. The program concludes with the recitation of Ziyarat Al-Yasin and a tribute to Imam Mahdi, while affirming support for the government's anti-espionage policies in the eastern regions.
