In a discussion with experienced referees, the importance of psychological readiness and motivation for managing errors and maintaining match integrity was emphasized.
The use of international instructors in pre-season courses was suggested to enhance the quality of training and increase the credibility of Iranian referees.
Respecting the human dignity of referees and creating a supportive environment were identified as prerequisites for expecting better on-field performance.
In the news section, a rule change regarding drop balls in Iran's Premier League was announced, allowing the referee to award the ball to either team.
The Asian Football Confederation selected Iranian referees and observers to officiate and monitor the AFC Women's Champions League.
Additionally, a retrospective on the life of Joe Crawford, the legendary and controversial NBA referee, was presented following his retirement after 39 years.
The program concluded with wishes for a productive season for referees and Iranian football.
