In this radio program, the speaker emphasizes that the primary goal of marriage is building a family and companionship rather than accumulating wealth, urging young couples to avoid excessive material expectations such as expensive houses, cars, and high dowries. By referencing historical examples and the experiences of previous generations, the importance of conscious partner selection through consulting elders and understanding cultural and family backgrounds is highlighted. Effective crisis management, maintaining affection, and viewing temporary quarrels as opportunities for reconciliation are presented as key factors for a successful marriage. The second part of the program shifts to a jurisprudential discussion on the nature of justice in testimony and leading prayers. It explains the view of the scholar Mohaghegh al-Jawaher, who argues that "apparent goodness" is sufficient for ordinary witnesses, rather than requiring a deep-seated spiritual character.
