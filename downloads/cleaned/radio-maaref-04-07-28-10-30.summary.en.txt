In the first segment, a traditional medicine expert emphasized the importance of consuming fresh, warm milk during autumn while advising against cold dairy products like yogurt due to their excessive cold nature.
He also taught the audience how to make fresh homemade cheese and recommended pairing it with warming remedies such as honey and walnuts to neutralize its cold effects.
Subsequently, a Quranic scholar stressed the necessity of avoiding waste in the consumption of divine blessings like electricity and gas, citing the Quranic verse "do not be extravagant."
He argued that since the entire world belongs to God, overconsumption is a form of ingratitude and resembles the behavior of devils, urging social responsibility in energy usage.
The program concluded with a political commentary highlighting the need for unity among Islamic nations to counter American hegemony and for leaders to manage power wisely.
Together, these segments portray a vision of a healthy life based on traditional medicine, responsible resource consumption, and political awareness against external threats.
