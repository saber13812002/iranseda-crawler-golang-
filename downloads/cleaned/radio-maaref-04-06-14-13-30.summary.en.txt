The primary goal of the Islamic Revolution is the revival of the Ummah, a return to Islam grounded in both reason and revelation, and the support of the oppressed, which must not be confused with the enemy's diversionary tactics. To advance this path, one must prevent the conflict between justice and tyranny from being distorted into sectarian or ethnic wars, while firmly upholding the unity of the Ummah as a pillar of divine obedience. A divine lifestyle requires sanctifying all aspects of life, from politics and economics to leisure, to distance society from materialism and moral corruption. The main obstacles to this path include ignorance, intellectual deviations, moral decay, and economic, scientific, and military dependencies on foreign powers. Consequently, a multidimensional jihad in the fields of science, ethics, media, and defense is essential to remove these barriers and build a righteous society. This process of Islamic civilization-building demands continuous effort, precise planning, and sacrifice to ultimately move toward establishing a global government based on monotheism and justice.
