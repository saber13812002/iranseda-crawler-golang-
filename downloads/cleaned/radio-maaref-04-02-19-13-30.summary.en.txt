This program explores the political struggles and life of Imam Reza (PBUH) during the reign of Harun al-Rashid. It begins by acknowledging the Imam's spiritual miracles and occult knowledge before detailing the oppressive and complex security apparatus of the Abbasid Caliphate. Imam Reza (PBUH) employed intelligent intelligence tactics and the precise concept of "Taqiyya" as covert resistance to outmaneuver his enemies and mislead their surveillance. Despite intense pressure, he expanded his influence by converting enemy spies into loyal companions through his exemplary character and moral leadership. The Imam's humility, such as eating with slaves and emphasizing piety as the sole criterion for superiority, serves as a profound model of social justice and humanity. In his recommendations, he defined a true Shia community as rational, honest, and committed to trust, avoiding unnecessary disputes. He stressed the importance of balancing religious devotion with worldly responsibilities, warning against neglecting economic and social order in the name of spirituality. Ultimately, the program highlights the enduring impact of Imam Reza (PBUH) in spreading the school of Ahl al-Bayt during his relatively short period of Imamate.
