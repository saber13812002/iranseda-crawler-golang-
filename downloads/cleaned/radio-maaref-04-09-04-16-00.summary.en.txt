In the "Persaman" program on Radio Ma'aref, Professor Mohammad Sharifi addressed various theological questions from listeners. He argued against a materialist perspective, asserting that Paradise and Hell were created before the Day of Judgment and are visible to the spiritual eye. Regarding prayers for changing one's fate, he emphasized that supplication without righteous action is ineffective, recommending specific dhikr like Istighfar and Salawat. The professor also explained the Imams' role in guiding humanity through providing the path and sending believers to their destinations, citing the rapid growth of Shia Islam as evidence of this guidance's success. Finally, while acknowledging the existence of sorcery, he noted that most alleged cases are due to ignorance or psychological factors rather than actual magic.
