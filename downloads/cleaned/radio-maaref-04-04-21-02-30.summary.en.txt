In this radio program, the concept of a "resilient society" is emphasized, highlighting the need for comprehensive preparedness against modern hybrid and cognitive wars.
The guest, Professor Vahidpour, explains based on Islamic jurisprudence that national defense is a "kafai obligation" primarily borne by experts, scientists, and officials.
He notes that the martyrdom of nuclear and military scientists has left a void that must be filled by a new generation of capable individuals to ensure national security.
The second part of the program addresses listeners' jurisprudential questions regarding prayer details, such as breaking prayer due to forgetting the salam and congregational prayer for those with respiratory issues.
The Islamic government is obligated to provide equipment and train specialized forces, while citizens must participate in this process to the best of their abilities.
The program concludes with the recitation of the "Habibi" prayer and a recommendation to maintain mindfulness and adherence to religious laws in daily life.
