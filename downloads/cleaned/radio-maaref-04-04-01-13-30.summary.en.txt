In this program, Dr. Larijani congratulated the recent martyrs and praised the courage of national media while emphasizing Iran's imminent victory against enemy aggression. He identified the root of the conflict as the West's attempt to impose dependency and prevent Iran's independence, urging the use of post-crisis opportunities to secure regional stability. Larijani proposed suspending operational cooperation with the IAEA and the NPT until the agency's director is prosecuted for his betrayal. He declared the nuclear file closed, insisting that Iran will not negotiate regarding its internal policies or nuclear energy capabilities. The political analyst warned that a US military intervention would be prohibitively expensive and would ultimately force this power out of the Middle East permanently. Finally, the discussion highlighted the necessity of elevating religious propagation to the primary rank, identifying it as the essential tool for achieving divine goals and human spiritual elevation.
