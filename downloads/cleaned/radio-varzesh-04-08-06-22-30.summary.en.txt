In a crucial Round of 16 match of the Saudi King's Cup, Al-Ittihad defeated Al-Nassr 2-1 to advance to the next stage of the competition. Karim Benzema and Hossam Awar scored the goals for Al-Ittihad, while Angelo netted the only goal for Al-Nassr in the first half. The second half was marred by a serious incident when Al-Ittihad's Ahmed Joulaidan was sent off for a dangerous collision with Al-Nassr's Aymen Yahya. The impact caused a severe injury to Yahya, who was removed from the pitch by ambulance, leading to a brief suspension of the match. Despite Cristiano Ronaldo's efforts and Al-Nassr's attempts to equalize, Al-Ittihad managed to hold their lead while playing with ten men. In other matches, Al-Hilal and Al-Qadsiah also secured victories to progress in the cup. This win keeps Al-Ittihad alive in their pursuit of the title in one of Saudi Arabia's most prestigious tournaments.
