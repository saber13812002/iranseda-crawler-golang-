This radio program discussed various Islamic rulings, emphasizing the prohibition of disclosing secrets and the ethical obligations of medical professionals.
Experts clarified that doctors must inform a prospective spouse about conditions like infertility, while public disclosure remains strictly forbidden.
Concealing such information is considered fraud or "tadlis," a major sin that can lead to the collapse of a marriage.
The Q&A section addressed inheritance distribution according to Ayatollah Khamenei's fatwa and the requirement to redirect charity to genuine needy individuals.
It was also ruled that reciting the names of martyrs in night prayers without their fathers' names is permissible, even in Persian.
Guidance was provided on how to repay a childhood debt for a borrowed item without causing embarrassment to the recipient.
Finally, rulings on praying in clothes with animal images and the necessity of separate Aqiqah for each child were presented, concluding with a reminder for Friday prayers.
