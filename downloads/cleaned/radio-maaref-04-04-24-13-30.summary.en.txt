In the radio program "Mashq-e Rah," Hujjat al-Islam Seyyed Ahmad Faqih emphasized the importance of seeking intercession through the Ahl al-Bayt and understanding their spiritual status in daily life. Addressing listener questions, he suggested practical solutions for reducing workplace and family tensions, such as avoiding the perception of tasks as burdens, eliminating excuses, and performing duties with pure intention and affection. The expert also highlighted the necessity of interpreting others' actions positively and avoiding misunderstandings born from pessimism or premature judgment, which he identified as pathways for Satan's influence. In the final segment, he attributed indifference to others' problems to selfishness, citing Quranic verses to stress the need for empathy and mutual understanding among believers. He concluded by referencing Imam Hussein's movement for establishing justice, describing silence in the face of tyranny as a grave sin that effectively endorses corruption.
