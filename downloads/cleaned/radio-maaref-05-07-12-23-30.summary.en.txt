This dramatic retelling from the book "In the Ambush of the Red Rose" chronicles the life of Martyr Ali Siyad Shirazi and the battle to liberate Sanandaj. Colonel Siyad Shirazi coordinated with the 3rd Brigade of the 16th Armored Division and the Hamedan Basij to launch an operation for the strategic Selvat-Abad pass. During the fierce battle, the Hamedan Basij commander was martyred, but the fighters' bravery led to the enemy's defeat and the liberation of the heights. Following the victory, the military column entered Sanandaj to a warm welcome from local residents, who helped restore security by forming defense bases. The discovery of a mass grave containing sixty revolutionary supporters highlighted the atrocities committed by the counter-revolution during their rule. With the installation of civil and military officials, urban life returned to normal, solidifying Sanandaj as a key resistance hub in Kurdistan. After reporting the victory in Tehran, Colonel Siyad Shirazi returned to the region with expanded authority to continue liberation operations toward Baneh and Sardasht.
