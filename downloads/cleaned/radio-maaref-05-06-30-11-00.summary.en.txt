The President, at the first annual performance evaluation festival for executive agencies, identified improving people's livelihood as the government's top priority and emphasized the need for inter-branch coordination to achieve this goal swiftly. He also urged agencies to implement energy-saving plans for the upcoming winter to prevent production disruptions. In the diplomatic section, it was stressed that the Strait of Hormuz will remain closed until Iran's conditions are met, and the country's stance against unilateral impositions is non-negotiable. The Martyrs Foundation announced extensive programs, including pilgrimages to martyrs' shrines and artistic productions, coinciding with the anniversary of the Sacred Defense. In education, 91 percent of the new school year's textbooks have been distributed, with content revisions reflecting recent war experiences. The broadcast included a report on the martyrdom of Dr. Afshar Khosravi, a prominent aerospace expert killed in a missile strike along with his family. Fiqh experts answered religious questions regarding wudu, night prayers during travel, and the ruling on selling pork and alcohol to non-Muslims. The program concluded with an invitation to prayer and a note on the sunrise time in Qom.
