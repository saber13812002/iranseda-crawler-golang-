In this radio program, Professor Panahian argues that the power of the Imam is crucial for the rational acceptance of his message, suggesting that supporting Imam Mahdi through the grand Arbaeen pilgrimage awakens minds and elevates the intellect of the audience. He identifies Arbaeen as the primary factor for security and stability in the Islamic community, considering it more fundamental than the Sacred Defense because it eradicates the roots of tyranny and prevents future wars. The discussion also highlights the philosophy behind reciting salawat loudly to maintain divine dignity, whereas other dhikr should be performed quietly and privately. In the second segment, scholar Vahidpour addresses the Sharia rulings for pilgrims facing crowded conditions during Arbaeen, explaining the principle of presumption of purity. He emphasizes that unless there is absolute certainty of impurity, everything is considered pure, advising pilgrims to avoid obsessive-compulsive behaviors. Adhering to this principle makes the journey easier and more enjoyable for the majority of ordinary pilgrims, preventing unnecessary hardships.
