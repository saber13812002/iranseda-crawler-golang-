In this radio program, Professor Mohammadi emphasized the constant presence of angels in human lives and their role in protecting believers. He explained that even angels cease to exist with the second blast of the trumpet, affirming that only God is eternal. Responding to a teenager's question about the temptation to remove her hijab, the professor advised that modesty is a virtue that pleases the Imam of the Time and helps resist satanic whispers. He also clarified that denying God out of anger or sarcasm does not constitute true apostasy, as it stems from a lack of understanding of divine laws rather than genuine disbelief. The discussion further distinguished between the singular "religion" and temporary "shari'as," presenting Islam as a comprehensive, universal, and eternal system that corrected the extremes of previous laws. Finally, the program addressed the soul's connection to the body after death, noting that reciting prayers for the deceased brings them joy, while repentance remains the only way to erase major sins.
