In this radio program, legal and jurisprudential experts discussed the challenges of excessive and unconventional dowries, emphasizing the need for public awareness. A major misconception is the belief that a husband is exempt from payment if he lacks financial means under the "andl-estita'ah" condition; however, Islamic jurisprudence and law require the husband to make every effort to acquire the ability to pay in both "andl-matalabah" and "andl-estita'ah" cases. The notion that a husband does not need to work if the dowry is conditional on his ability is entirely false, as scholars mandate that he must strive to accumulate the funds. On the other hand, men often accept heavy dowries due to a fear of losing their emotional partner and a lack of communication skills. Conversely, women may seek high dowries due to a sense of legal insecurity, which should be addressed through legislative reforms and the establishment of legal security. Therefore, the ultimate solution lies in a combination of precise legal education for couples, lifestyle improvements, and the removal of legal concerns to prevent social and legal issues in marital relationships.
