In this radio program, Professor Javad Mahkami emphasized the vital role of insight and certainty in boosting the morale of fighters, drawing on Nahj al-Balagha. He highlighted Imam Ali's (PBUH) conduct at the Battle of Siffin, demonstrating that recognizing one's own righteousness and the enemy's falsehood sustains steadfastness against threats and doubts. The discussion also addressed the Imam's warnings about eight traits that hinder the acceptance of prayers, such as failing to practice the Sharia and lack of gratitude. In another segment, Imam Hussein's (PBUH) journey from Medina to Mecca was interpreted as the "birth of his personality" and the beginning of his righteous uprising. This movement was driven not by fear, but by unwavering persistence in the path of truth and the pursuit of divine encounter. The distinction between "sabr" (patience and self-restraint) and "istiqamah" (active steadfastness in the right path) was also clearly explained. Finally, the necessity of raising the current generation with a Husaini upbringing to foster a spirit of jihad and insight in society was discussed.
