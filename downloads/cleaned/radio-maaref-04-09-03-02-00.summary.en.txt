This program recounts the harrowing experiences of captivity and torture in the Mosul and Hesarat camps, emphasizing the critical importance of relying on God and maintaining prayer and fasting under extreme duress. The narrator shares memories of severe violence by guards, including the martyrdom of three companions, and notes that the prisoners' steadfast faith impressed even their captors. The second part of the broadcast focuses on interpreting narrations from the Book of Kafi regarding the principle of Taqiyya, which Imam Sadiq (AS) described as essential for preserving one's life. The narrations stress that Taqiyya is obligatory during times of peace and non-war, serving as a means of spiritual elevation for believers. Conversely, abandoning Taqiyya and risking one's life without the Imam's command is considered a sign of religious negligence. The episode concludes with the recitation of the fourteenth supplication from the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya, seeking protection from oppression.
