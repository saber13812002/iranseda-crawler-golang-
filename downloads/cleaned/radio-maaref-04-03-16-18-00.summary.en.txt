In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Mohammad Hadi Fallah discusses the virtue of humility and its profound effects. He presents humility as a path to instilling good morals and eliminating vices, starting with self-awareness and knowledge of God. One of the key benefits of humility is preventing arrogance and fostering a culture of mutual respect within society. Humility also cultivates love, promotes physical and mental health, and adorns individuals with dignity and grandeur. The speaker emphasizes that humility of the heart for God's sake frees a person from fatigue in worship and brings order to their affairs. This virtue completes material and spiritual blessings and increases one's sustenance. Ultimately, humility multiplies virtues and spreads them throughout the community, whereas arrogance leads to the proliferation of vices.
