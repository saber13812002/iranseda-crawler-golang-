In this radio program, following a segment on listeners' wishes, the main discussion focuses on "slander" as a linguistic and moral vice. The guest explains that slander is the sum of lying and backbiting, defined as falsely attributing a flaw to someone who does not possess it. This act, driven by the ego and contrary to divine tradition, functions as a character assassination that destroys social psychological security. Slander is not only an injustice to others but also a self-inflicted harm, reflecting the perpetrator's spiritual poverty and lack of monotheistic knowledge. Described as a sudden ambush on a person's reputation, it uses false words to target individuals and weaken the foundations of social order. The program concludes by emphasizing that abandoning moral vices and embracing divine virtues is the only path to resolving these social crises and achieving inner peace.
