This radio program examines the criminal case of Abbas Momeni against Yaser, who is accused of theft, bank fraud, and identity document misuse. Judge Razaghi highlighted the victim's negligence in leaving his bag on the car and the bank employees' failure to verify identity, emphasizing the need for caution with personal documents and bank accounts. The role of Masoud Talebi as an accomplice in forging the birth certificate and altering documents was also discussed, citing Quranic verses to warn against aiding in illegal activities. The segment concluded with the recitation of Ziyarat Al-Yasin and songs of anticipation for the birth of Imam Mahdi (AJ). Subsequently, a speech by Imam Khomeini was broadcast, stressing the importance of national unity and the spiritual power of the people against enemies. The Imam noted that adversaries fear the unity of the Iranian people and called for maintaining cohesion to defend the honor of Islam and the country. These remarks aimed to strengthen the spirit of resistance and hope for the awaited savior.
