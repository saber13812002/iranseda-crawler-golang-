In this radio program, the claim that Imam Zaman (AJ) lives in a basement is rejected, emphasizing instead his presence among the people. Professor Mahmoud Abbazari discussed the role of the Imam's deputies during the Minor Occultation, particularly Muhammad ibn Shazan, in managing religious funds and khums. Citing narrations, he highlighted the Imam's divine knowledge of his followers' financial details and the sending of divine blessings to those who pay khums. The primary philosophy of khums is presented as ensuring the financial independence of Shia Muslims from tyrannical rulers, preventing the religion from becoming dependent on state apparatuses. The characteristics of the Imam's companions were also described, including humility towards believers, patience against enemies, and striving in the path of God. Finally, the necessity for expectants to maintain a strong bond with the Marja-e-Taqleed as the representative of the Imam during the Major Occultation was stressed. Supporting the Imam through community awareness and establishing Mahdavi educational centers was also proposed as a key duty.
