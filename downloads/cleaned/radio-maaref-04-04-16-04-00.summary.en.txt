In this radio program, Ayatollah Sobhani's ruling was discussed regarding the obligation of fasting for those with chronic illnesses, stating they do not need to make up missed days but must pay expiation. It was also clarified that a follower of Ayatollah Sistani who has made a vow cannot change the location of its fulfillment and must perform it at the originally intended place. In response to another inquiry, Hojjat al-Islam Jawaheri, citing Ayatollah Shubri Zanjan's fatwa, stated that a charity collector cannot deduct their own travel expenses from the funds. This deduction is only permissible if the donors explicitly or implicitly consented to it during the collection process. Finally, listeners were encouraged to submit higher-quality questions, and the program's contact phone and SMS numbers were announced.
