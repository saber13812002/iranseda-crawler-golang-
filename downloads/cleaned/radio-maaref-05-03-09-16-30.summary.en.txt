In this radio program, the book "Ayat al-Wilayah" by Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi was introduced as a key source for proving the guardianship of Imam Ali.
The main focus was on verse 67 of Surah Al-Ma'idah (the event of Ghadir), which is accepted by both Shia and Sunni scholars based on unanimous reports from 110 companions and 360 scholars.
It was emphasized that the term "Rasul" in this verse refers to the duty of conveying the message, with God promising to protect the Prophet from the fear of the religion ending after his departure.
The program then analyzed verse 55 of Surah Al-Ma'idah, where Imam Ali gave charity while in prostration, prompting the Prophet to ask God for a successor.
Scholars clarified that "Wali" in these verses means guardian or ruler, not merely a friend, a fact reinforced by the public pledge of allegiance at Ghadir.
The story of Allameh Amini's efforts to prove Shia roots using Sunni sources, and the acceptance of these proofs by scholars like Sheikh Shaltout, was also highlighted.
The discussion concluded that the guardianship of Ali is an established truth supported by Quranic and unanimous narrations, which Sunnis acknowledge but interpret differently.
