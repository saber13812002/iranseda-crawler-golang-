The radio program "Parsaiyan" began by introducing its segments, followed by a discussion on the virtue of generosity led by Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi. Citing Quranic verses and narrations, the speaker highlighted generosity as a key to beautiful character and the love of others, using the Prophet, the Ahl al-Bayt, Imam Khomeini, and Sheikh Ansari as practical examples. The program then aired a narrative from the book "Okyanos Mashreq," following a man named Aman who is searching for the water of life. During his journey, Aman travels with Hajj Karim and his daughter, Rahleh, and after Hajj Karim is injured, Aman rushes to find a doctor. He encounters a man who claims to have seen Imam Reza (AS) and explains that the true source of life is the pilgrimage to the Imam's shrine, rather than physical searches. In the final segment, the Supreme Leader's ruling was shared regarding administrative purchasing, stating that any discounts obtained must belong to the organization, not the individual. The program concluded with Quranic recitation and a narration from the Prophet (PBUH) emphasizing the duty to help fellow believers solve their problems.
