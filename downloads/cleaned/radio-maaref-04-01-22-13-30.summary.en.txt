This radio program argues that the essential difference between humans and animals lies not in the body, but in the spirit and the human capacity to choose to become truly human. The speaker posits that biologically, humans are no different from animals and are naturally "actual animals," yet humans are the only beings capable of becoming "actual humans" through will and reason. True humanity requires a threefold elevation in existential beliefs, ethics, and lifestyle, which cannot be sustained or completed without relying on divine and religious foundations. A sharp critique is offered against superficial human rights slogans and moral hypocrisy in modern societies, where individuals without commitment to God cannot experience genuine, selfless sacrifice. The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) outlines the practical model of humanity through hadiths such as "put yourself in another's place" and "helping before being asked." This authentic humanity involves deep empathy, avoiding self-centeredness, and even respecting the rights of animals and plants. Ultimately, a true believer is one who gains God's pleasure by bringing joy to others and alleviating their suffering, viewing the world not as an end in itself, but as a means for spiritual growth.
