The radio program "Let's Be Kind" focused on the situation of visually impaired individuals in East Azerbaijan Province. According to the provincial Welfare Organization, approximately 30,000 people in the region suffer from visual impairments, with over 13,000 currently receiving services. Key challenges highlighted include the lack of participation of blind individuals in decision-making processes and the private sector's limited trust in their professional capabilities. Although financial support is provided to 85% of identified individuals and student scholarships are available, full implementation of the law protecting the rights of persons with disabilities remains a pending goal. The broadcast also noted the success of the Esteghlal football team in the Asian Champions League and celebrated the poet's day. Finally, the host praised the public's patriotic presence in the streets for disrupting the enemy's calculations.
