The radio program "Radio Ma'aref" began by broadcasting news, including the inauguration of the new head of the Hajj and Pilgrimage Organization and the start of the Mirza Naeini International Conference. The main segment featured the recollections of Mohammad Hossein Keshani, a former prisoner of war from the Iran-Iraq War. He described the harsh conditions in Iraqi camps, such as widespread disease and severe restrictions on access to water and food. Keshani recounted how the POWs maintained unity against Iraqi and Meiteh plots and taught each other the Quran using needles in difficult circumstances. He emphasized the importance of relying on God and performing prayers during moments of danger, noting that this devotion even earned the respect of enemy guards. The program concluded with the recitation of patriotic poetry and an introduction to upcoming evening segments, including a review of Hafez's poetry and pilgrimage memories.
