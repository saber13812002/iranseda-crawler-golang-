In the radio program "Ganj-e Sadat," Professor Mohammad Hadi Falah Bastani described stinginess as a dangerous disease that distances individuals from God's mercy and the love of Imam Ali (AS). He emphasized that Imam Ali (AS) cursed the stingy, a curse that prompts angels to pray for the destruction of their wealth. Other consequences of stinginess include poverty in this world and being judged as a wealthy person in the afterlife. Furthermore, it was noted that the fire of Hell will address the stingy on Judgment Day, swallowing them and dragging them into hell. In the second part of the program, the Quranic School of Hazrat Mohammad Amin in Mahshahr Karkheh Jonoubi was introduced, highlighting their initiative to hold home-based Quran sessions with family participation to mitigate the impacts of the pandemic. These sessions, focusing on Quranic recitation, reflection, and concepts, have been widely welcomed by parents and students. This approach has significantly contributed to expanding the culture of Quranic engagement within families.
