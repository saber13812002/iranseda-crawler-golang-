In this radio program, the analyst confirms the direct role of the United States and its intelligence agencies in the 1953 coup against Dr. Mossadegh. The speaker condemns the West's double standards, which label resistance groups as terrorists while supplying heavy weapons to the Israeli regime. Furthermore, the implementation of the Zangezur corridor by Azerbaijan is interpreted as an economic and security threat to Iran, potentially activating Pan-Turkism. In the final segment, classmates and family members shared emotional memories of Mohammadta Qadiri, a student who was martyred in the Kerman martyrs' garden explosion. These narratives highlight the 11-year-old boy's kindness, compassion for his mother, and academic interests. The program concluded by inviting listeners to send text messages and express solidarity with the martyrs' families.
