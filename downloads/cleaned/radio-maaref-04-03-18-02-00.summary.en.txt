The program begins by promoting the virtue of humility, citing narrations and historical examples from great scholars and Martyr Raisi to emphasize the necessity of modesty before God and people. The second part examines the political background of promoting ancientism in Iran, identifying it as a tool for cultural imperialism and weakening Islamic identity. Dr. Rasoul Rezaei argues that Orientalists have attempted to create a rift between the Iranian nation and the Islamic world by distorting history and constructing false perceptions of the Achaemenid era. He refutes the claims of Achaemenid Zoroastrianism based on historical evidence, noting that this civilization was largely rooted in Greek culture. A critical analysis of the irrational and superstitious laws in the Vendidad of the Avesta, such as the treatment of women and severe punishments, is presented to demonstrate their incompatibility with human reason and nature. Finally, the superiority of Islamic civilization in terms of knowledge, ethics, and justice over pre-Islamic periods is emphasized, and it is requested that ancientism be accepted only within the framework of reason and benevolence.
