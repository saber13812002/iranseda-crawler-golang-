The program began by outlining the hardships and precise details of the Umrah rituals, emphasizing the spiritual transformation this journey offers. In the fatwa section, Ayatollah Khamenei's view on the inappropriateness of handshakes in mosques was discussed. The ruling on Khums regarding retirement bonuses and investment profits for a son was explained according to Imam Khomeini's fatwa. It was clarified that converting small change into larger bills for charity is permissible if the total amount is preserved and proper authorization is obtained. Ayatollah Sistani's ruling stated that obligatory ghusl before the Fajr adhan is not required outside of Ramadan. The marriage of a Muslim woman to a non-Muslim was declared invalid, and rules for prostration on clothing or metal were detailed. A moving story about Ayatollah Hairy Shirazi, who gave his entire savings to a bathhouse attendant and later received a tenfold charity from a merchant, illustrated God's promise to those who give charity. Finally, the program concluded with a historical analysis of the early Islamic period, focusing on the companions' protests against Caliph Uthman's governance.
