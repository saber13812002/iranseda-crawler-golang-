In a Radio Sport broadcast, the guest emphasized the vital role of morning exercise in preventing diseases like diabetes and criticized the lack of national will to promote mass sports. He highlighted the need for competent managers in sports federations and noted that consistent physical activity can make a person's biological age younger than their chronological age. The program also celebrated Nurses' Day by praising nurses for their dual role in healing patients' bodies and soothing their spirits. The radio drama "Salam" then aired, featuring a wheelchair tennis player named Niaz who regains sensation in her legs after falling down a staircase. Although a doctor deems this rare event a potential sign of full recovery, Niaz fears losing her eligibility for the Asian Games and asks the federation to replace her with her rival, Mina. The segment concluded with a comedic scene depicting a dispute over parking in a garage that ended in a mutual reconciliation.
