In the first segment, a psychologist highlighted the role of social media in fostering envy, emphasizing the need to manage these feelings and practice gratitude for one's own possessions.
He advised focusing on personal growth and thankfulness rather than comparing one's inner life with the curated appearances of others to avoid bitterness.
A subsequent religious query was addressed, stating that watching Hollywood movies is permissible unless they contain scenes that lead individuals toward sin.
The program then honored the memory of Abu Talib, praising his unwavering support for the Prophet and his crucial role in protecting the early Islamic community.
In the educational section, Dr. Haidari explained that addiction to video games and social media stems from the "dopamine trap" and the brain's reward structure.
She noted that platform designers exploit this mechanism to keep users engaged in cycles of short-term pleasure and repetition.
The recommended strategy for parents is to provide real-life emotional rewards to activate the child's brain healthily and reduce dependence on digital platforms.
Finally, regarding post-divorce custody, the importance of maintaining a strong emotional bond between the child and the mother was stressed, urging parents to avoid transferring personal conflicts to their children.
