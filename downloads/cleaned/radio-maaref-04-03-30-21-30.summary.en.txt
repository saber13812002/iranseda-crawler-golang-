This radio program discusses the theological and historical narratives regarding the descent of Jesus (PBUH) and the status of the Jews during the reappearance of Imam Mahdi (AJ).
The central argument refutes the claim that Jews will hold military power or authority during that era, as no credible narrations support such a scenario.
A detailed analysis of sources reveals that the specific narration where Jews claim to be Jesus's allies is rarely found in Shia sources and appears in Sunni texts with weak or Isra'iliyyat chains.
Major Sunni sources like Bukhari's Tarikh al-Kabir and Sahih Muslim contain the narration of Jesus's descent but lack the specific details about the Jews' claims and Jesus's response.
The speakers emphasize that narrations depict Jesus (PBUH) as a follower of Imam Mahdi (AJ), rather than an independent power engaging in direct conflict with the Jews.
The interaction with the Jews in these texts is primarily moral, involving the refutation of their false claims, rather than a direct physical battle.
The final conclusion is that popular interpretations suggesting bloody wars with the Jews at the time of reappearance lack solid evidence in authentic and primary narrations.
