In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Mirbagheri discussed Imamology, emphasizing the central role of the Imam in fostering unity and brotherhood among believers. Citing the Quranic verse "Hold firmly to the Rope of Allah," he explained that clinging to the Imam protects individuals from sin, error, and the plots of enemies. The speaker argued that a community gathered around the Imam attains heavenly qualities, transforming enmity into love and salvation from hell. Conversely, distancing oneself from the Imam leads to selfishness, satanic influence, and the deterioration of social relations into hostility. Drawing on narrations, he highlighted that love for the Imam is the prerequisite for true obedience and spiritual connection. The sermon also stressed the importance of self-sacrifice and generosity, illustrating this with a story involving Prophet Musa and his followers. The program concluded with prayers for the health of the Imam of the Age and the Supreme Leader, urging the creation of a society based on love and service to the Ahl al-Bayt.
