In this radio program, Professor Mehdi Moshkibaf analyzed Imam Khomeini's strategic thoughts regarding the United States. The first objective was to dismantle foreign dominance structures and remove American forces from Iran to ensure political independence. The second axis involved moving beyond the bipolar East-West system to establish a model of an independent, non-aligned Islamic state. The third goal was to mobilize the world's oppressed against global imperialism, relying on Quranic verses. This approach transformed the world's bipolar structure into three poles: oppressors, the oppressed, and the resistance. This paradigm shift directly opposed the demands of the global hegemonic system, particularly the United States. The program also briefly discussed proper eating habits and the medicinal properties of rose water.
