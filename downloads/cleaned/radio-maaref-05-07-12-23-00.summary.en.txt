In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Radtmand interprets the Du'a Makarim al-Akhlaq, defining blessing (barakah) as a concept that may be small in volume but yields extensive effects in life. He identifies three main factors for societal blessing: piety, the presence of a just and pious ruler, and the observance of justice in governance. For individual longevity and blessing, he highlights the importance of performing tasks with precision, maintaining family ties, charity, paying religious dues, kindness to parents, and good conduct with family and neighbors. Visiting Imam Hussein (PBUH), desiring to perform Hajj or Umrah, and maintaining ritual purity are also presented as ways to increase life's blessing and duration. Regarding household blessings, the speaker recommends greeting upon entering the home, having daughters in the family, hosting guests, and naming children after prophets. Finally, he warns that betrayal, lying, swearing, and neglecting prayer are significant factors that strip blessing from a person's life.
