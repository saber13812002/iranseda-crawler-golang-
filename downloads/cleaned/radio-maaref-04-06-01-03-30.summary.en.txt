The radio program begins by discussing Islamic inheritance laws and the ruling on winning prizes in SMS competitions.
According to the religious authority, participating in free SMS contests is permissible, but winning prizes in paid competitions is considered haram.
The discussion then shifts to the spiritual concept of "self-awareness" and the value of time, citing a narration from Imam Sadiq.
The Imam emphasized that each day is a new opportunity for reward, and time will testify for or against a person on Judgment Day.
Individuals are urged to fill fleeting moments with righteous deeds to ensure they benefit from time in the afterlife.
The program also announced the morning prayer and sunrise times in Qom, followed by recitations of dawn prayers.
It continued with a recitation and translation of verses from Surah Ghafir and Surah Fussilat by Master Bas Aman Jome.
These verses emphasize monotheism, the fate of disbelievers, and the futility of faith when faced with divine punishment.
