The radio program began with recitations of Ashura poetry and then transitioned to interviews with pilgrims traveling to Karbala near the shrine of Hazrat Masoumeh.
A pilgrim from Qazvin described Arbaeen as an essential spiritual journey, emphasizing the profound sense of Imam Zaman's presence during the difficult moments of the trek.
The core of the discussion featured a theological analysis by Hujjat al-Islam Falah, who highlighted that guarding one's tongue is a primary indicator of practical monotheism.
He cited narrations stating that true faith and a straight heart are unattainable without a straight tongue, identifying bad speech as a sign of weakness in theological knowledge.
The speaker explained that preserving the tongue is itself a major act of worship, and failing to do so renders other religious deeds invalid and ineffective.
The conversation distinguished between the common view of speech and the Sharia perspective, which treats every word as a significant action with lasting spiritual consequences.
The program concluded with an exhortation to flee from the ego's influence on speech toward divine law, ending with prayers for the early return of the Imam.
