The radio program began by explaining the jurisprudential ruling regarding deviation from the Qibla during prayer, noting that it does not invalidate the prayer if unintentional and corrected calmly. It then addressed a listener's question about the items subject to Zakat, listing wheat, barley, dates, raisins, gold, silver, camels, cows, and sheep based on Imam Khomeini's legal code. Specific conditions were highlighted for these items, such as livestock feeding on wild grass and agricultural products reaching the minimum threshold. The show provided contact numbers and a text message system for viewers to receive further religious answers. The hosts also expressed appreciation for the resilience and resistance of the Iranian people in the streets. Prayer times for the city of Qom were announced for Fajr and sunrise. The broadcast concluded with the recitation of morning supplications and the playing of religious music.
