In this radio program, Professor Pishvayi first discussed the divine upbringing of Prophet Muhammad prior to his prophethood, noting that he was under the care of angels and engaged in worship and contemplation. The discussion then shifted to the famous poem by Da'bal Khaza'i, identifying "Bakhmarah" as the location of the martyrdom of Ibrahim ibn Abdullah, a descendant of Imam Hassan, near Kufa. The professor addressed objections regarding the presence of idols in the Kaaba, clarifying that the structure itself was never an idol temple. Quranic evidence indicates that major idols such as Lat, Uzza, and Manat were located in specific places outside the Kaaba, and Muslims in Medina faced the Holy Temple for prayer. It was emphasized that the idols inside the Kaaba were removed and destroyed by order of the Prophet with the assistance of Imam Ali after the conquest of Mecca. Finally, the use of cinematic films as reliable historical sources was rejected in favor of authentic historical records.
