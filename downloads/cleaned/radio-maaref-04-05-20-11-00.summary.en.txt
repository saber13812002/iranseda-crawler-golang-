This radio program reported on the services provided to Arbaeen pilgrims in various routes, including Tamarchin and the Path of Scholars. Specialized hospitals of the IRGC in Khorramshahr and Mehran borders are also offering round-the-clock medical services to Arab pilgrims. Furthermore, the Supreme Leader approved the extension of the term for the board members of the Imam Khomeini Relief Committee. The head of the Martyrs Foundation stated that the bodies of approximately twenty veterans from the Sacred Defense remain unidentified. The tragic news of the martyrdom of seven journalists in Israeli attacks on Gaza was also discussed. In the educational segment, the program introduced the angels Kiraman Katibin and their role in recording human deeds. The broadcast concluded with the performance of religious songs and special prayers for Arbaeen.
