The program began by emphasizing the importance of avoiding depression and offering general prayers for the youth. In the "Jor'eh" segment, Professor Masoudi interpreted hadiths from the book of Kafi that highlight the immense reward for assisting distressed and exhausted believers. According to these narrations, God writes seventy-two mercies for the helper, which include resolving worldly livelihood issues and removing the trials of the Day of Judgment in the afterlife. In the "Manshoor Amir" segment, Professor Mohdasi analyzed the 38th letter of Nahj al-Balagha. In this letter, Imam Ali (AS) praises the people of Egypt for their divine anger against oppression and describes the era of tyrannical rule where enjoining good and forbidding evil were impossible. Finally, in the "Zamzam Ahkam" segment, two religious questions were addressed. The first answer stated that according to the specified marja, one-fifth of the profit from gold trading is subject to Khums, as is the principal capital if it has not been paid annually. The second answer clarified that if a veil obstructs the forehead during prostration, the prayer is invalid and must be corrected.
