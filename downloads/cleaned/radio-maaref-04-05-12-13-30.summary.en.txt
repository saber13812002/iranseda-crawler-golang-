He characterizes the Zionist regime as lacking the genuine attributes of a "nation-state," describing it as a structure dependent on external powers and devoid of historical continuity. Conversely, Dr. Hassan Khani stresses the distinction between "public opinion" and "the people," warning that reducing the conflict to a purely military or state-level confrontation obscures its fundamental civilizational nature. He argues that recent crises have redefined core concepts such as independence and identity, which should not be reduced to mere internal political disputes. Finally, the conversation shifts to critiquing "Jahiliyyah" (ignorance) as a persistent phenomenon opposing guided reason and prophetic wisdom, identifying it as the root cause of colonialism and the humiliation of nations.
