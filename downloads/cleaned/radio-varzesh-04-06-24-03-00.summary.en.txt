In this interview, Mohsen Gharaji reflects on his career with the Iranian national football team and the futsal World Cup in Hong Kong. He recalls the painful defeats in the final stages and highlights the positive impact of Iranian expatriate fans on the team's morale. Gharaji also expresses regret over his ankle injury and sudden removal from the national team camp for the Beijing games. He discusses the tactical risks taken by coaches during that era and his individual awards, such as top scorer and most technical player in indoor competitions. A significant part of the conversation covers his time at Pas Tehran and winning the Asian Championship with the club. Gharaji details the administrative and contractual issues he faced while returning to Esteghlal and later transferring to Sepahan. Finally, he announces his decision to retire from professional play and enter coaching, expressing disappointment that he did not reach his full potential with the national team.
