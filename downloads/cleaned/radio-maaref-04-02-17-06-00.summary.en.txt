In this program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verses 94 and 95 of Surah Al-Ma'idah, emphasizing the distinction between "interpretation" and "application." He explains that the prohibition of hunting during Ihram and the ruling that the hunted animal becomes carrion are not derived from the literal text but require narrations for clarification. The qualifier "intentional" in the verse, due to its context of revelation, does not cover accidental killing unless other narrations mandate expiation for negligence. The expiation for hunting involves feeding the poor or fasting, serving as a deterrent rather than a means of forgiveness. The prohibition of hunting applies to both Umrah and Hajj, though the location for paying expiation varies based on the type of Ihram. Finally, hunting in the sea is declared permissible for those in Ihram, while even guiding or pointing to prey is considered forbidden during this state.
