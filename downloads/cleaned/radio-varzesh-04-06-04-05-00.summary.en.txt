Veteran wrestling referee Mohammad Abrahim Imami shares his experiences officiating at World Championships and the Olympics in this interview. He discusses the financial challenges faced by referees and the disparity in compensation between domestic and international events. Imami expresses gratitude for his smooth collaboration with former federation president Hajj Mohammad Reza Talaghani. He also highlights his artistic career, including acting roles in films and series such as "Parviz Khan" and "Setayesh," and mentions his passion for football and the Saipa team. Finally, he announces the publication of a book on the history of Pahlavani wrestling and his current activities in the international Zourkhaneh federation. The program concludes with a brief historical overview of sporting events that occurred on August 26.
