In this radio program, family counselor Mr. Youssef Zadeh addressed two key questions from listeners. Regarding the first query, he emphasized the importance of setting boundaries in social media and advised couples to reach a mutual agreement through empathetic and transparent dialogue rather than imposing one-sided rules. He noted that concerns should be raised based on principles of justice and mutual respect to prevent harm to the relationship. In response to the second question about a talkative spouse, the counselor suggested that this trait might stem from personality differences or sociability and should not be labeled as a flaw. He recommended understanding the root causes of the behavior and establishing clear private boundaries instead of criticizing, leading to a calm and stable understanding. The core message in both cases is replacing imposition with constructive dialogue and reaching shared agreements. This approach helps spouses feel valued and keeps their relationships free from unnecessary tension. Finally, the program concluded with thanks to the guest and contact information for listeners.
