This program features Ayatollah Javadi Amoli's interpretation of the first verse of Surah Al-An'am, analyzing a detailed narrative from the book "Ihtijaj." The text recounts how the Prophet (PBUH) engaged in debate with five distinct groups: Jews, Christians, materialists, dualists, and Arab polytheists. Using the method of "Jadal Ahsan" and rational argumentation, the Prophet refuted the materialists' claim of the universe's eternal existence by rejecting sensory perception as the sole criterion for knowledge. He also dismantled the dualists' theory of two origins (light and darkness) by pointing to the universe's inherent order and the coexistence of contradictory attributes. The discussion further highlights the role of religious scholars as guardians of the intellectual frontier against the doubts and whispers of Satan. Ultimately, the verse is presented as a definitive Quranic response to these groups, affirming the unity of the Creator and the creation of the heavens and earth.
