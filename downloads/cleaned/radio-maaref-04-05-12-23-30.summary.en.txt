This radio program discussed practical strategies for managing digital addiction and reducing stress. The host emphasized using screen time controls and enabling "Do Not Disturb" mode to minimize disruptive notifications. Diaphragmatic breathing and mindfulness were introduced as non-pharmacological methods for achieving mental calm. In the second segment, the host addressed anxiety caused by Israeli attacks, advising against self-medication and recommending professional psychiatric help if needed. The importance of accepting emotions and limiting news consumption to ten minutes daily was highlighted. Additionally, tips for optimizing water and electricity usage to protect the environment and reduce costs were provided. The program concluded with music and a recitation of Imam Khomeini's words.
