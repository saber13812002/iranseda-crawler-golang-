In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Morteza Bahrami initially emphasized the virtue of humility and modesty in the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH), contrasting it with arrogance and self-centeredness. He cited narrations showing that the Prophet treated guests and companions with utmost respect, yet never allowed others to stand up in his honor. The discussion then shifted to the vice of cursing and foul language, which is described in the Quran and hadith as a Satanic and ignorant act. Hojjat al-Islam Fala Ba used the metaphor of a "barking dog" to explain that responding to insults lowers a human from a divine status to an animalistic one. He argued that cursing others is essentially cursing oneself and even one's parents, as it reflects the speaker's own spiritual state. The speaker identified foul language as a weapon wielded by the ignorant and lowly, which harms the speaker more than the target. Finally, it was stressed that cursing destroys the four fundamental relationships a human has with God, themselves, their fellow humans, and the universe, urging listeners to avoid this collective vice.
