In this radio program, the wife of Dr. Masoud Ali-Mohammadi, a martyr scientist, shares memories of their shared life and his character. She highlights his meticulous nature, kindness, and the positive impact he had on students and families, including a student who adopted religious practices due to his guidance. She recounts a sweet memory of him buying flowers to show affection and his expressed fears before his final trip to the nuclear facility. Following his martyrdom, Mrs. Karami found the strength to become independent and successfully manage her family's life. The latter part of the program analyzes the role of Persian poetry as a powerful cultural medium. It emphasizes that poetry must possess high artistic and linguistic quality to have a lasting impact on minds, similar to the works of Hafez.
