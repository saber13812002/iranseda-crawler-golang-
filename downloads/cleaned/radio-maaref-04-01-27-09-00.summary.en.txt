Furthermore, the program explains that God's merciful gaze in Paradise is the greatest blessing, with its intensity varying based on the individual's spiritual rank and capacity. Finally, the show addresses the modern trend of Feng Shui, noting that while it has ancient origins, it has become a billion-dollar industry.
