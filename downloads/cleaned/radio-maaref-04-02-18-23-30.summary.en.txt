In this radio program, relying on the narrations of Imam Reza (AS), the importance of reciting "Ya Ra'uf wa Ya Rahim" during times of distress and divine mercy is emphasized.
The key point is that the Eighth Imam, as a "Ghouth" or responder to cries, deeply cares about the suffering of believers, even asking a pilgrim to return home early to comfort a distressed child.
Visiting Imam Reza (AS) in Khorasan is equated with visiting God on His Throne and is considered to have a reward far exceeding that of visiting other infallibles.
According to narrations, anyone who visits this Imam is as if they have visited God on His Throne and will surpass the prophets and Imams on the Day of Judgment.
It is emphasized that the quality of intention in visiting is more important than repetition, and a visit with a pure intention is considered a direct form of worship before God.
Sending abundant salutations (Salawat) on Muhammad and his family is introduced as an effective and immediate way to purify sins and remove afflictions.
Finally, the martyrs of the revolution, including Ayatollah Dastgheib and Ayatollah Hashemi Rafsanjani, were honored, and the recitation of verses from Surah Al-Ahzab was dedicated to their souls.
