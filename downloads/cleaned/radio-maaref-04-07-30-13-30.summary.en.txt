In this episode of the "Narrative of Love" program, Nafiseh Kermani, the wife of Martyr Colonel Ali Aghazadeh, shares memories from their 23 years of marriage and his service in Syria. She describes the spiritual peace she found during pilgrimages with her husband and the inevitable separations caused by his military missions abroad. Nafiseh recounts the moment she learned of her husband's martyrdom during the Israeli terrorist attack in Syria, triggered by a call from her daughter and news alerts on social media. She details the guests' hesitant behavior and her realization of the tragedy after losing contact with her husband. The discussion highlights the resilience and self-sacrifice of martyrs' wives and mothers during these difficult times. The program concludes by emphasizing the need for public participation in production and investment to address economic challenges. Additionally, a message from the Supreme Leader is shared, urging the "Khast" to maintain the society's direction and avoid hesitation in the face of enemy plots.
