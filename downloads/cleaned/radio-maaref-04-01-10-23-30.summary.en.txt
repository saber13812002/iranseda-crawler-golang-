In this radio program, a family counselor addresses the concerns of an 18-year-old boy facing his parents' impending divorce, who is exhibiting signs of depression and aggression. The counselor emphasizes that parents must reassure their child that the parent-child bond remains intact, while avoiding blaming each other for the relationship's failure. It is recommended that parents listen actively and patiently to help the child release his anger and grief, rather than labeling one party as the villain. Given the worrying symptoms such as lack of motivation and hopelessness, immediate consultation with a psychologist is deemed essential to prevent the depression from becoming chronic. If the teenager resists professional help, leveraging the grandmother's influence to motivate him to see a counselor is suggested. Finally, the importance of creating a sense of security during life crises and maintaining the parents' own mental health to effectively support their children is highlighted.
