The radio program "Narrative of Love" highlights female martyrs, focusing on the life of Shahid Fahimeh Seiri. Born in Tehran in 1960, she graduated in mathematics and physics before moving to Qom to study at the women's seminary. In 1978, she studied at Maktab-e Tawhid in Qom alongside Dr. Fatahi-Zadeh, balancing her education with participation in revolutionary demonstrations. Fahimeh was highly respected by her peers for her intelligence, strict time management, and exemplary moral character. Beyond her academic excellence, she served as a trusted confidant and advisor to her classmates and even her own family. Following the Islamic Revolution, she was deployed to the western city of Baneh in 1980 to combat insurgent groups. During her journey, she was attacked in the Divandarreh region and was martyred.
