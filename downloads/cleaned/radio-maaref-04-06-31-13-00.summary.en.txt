Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei congratulated the Iranian national wrestling teams for their simultaneous world championships, marking a historic first in the country's sports history. Following this, Professor Panahian discussed the theme that "life without struggle is meaningless," arguing that human nature and history are fundamentally built on competition and conflict. He emphasized that jihad in the Quran and the conduct of the Prophet and Imams is not merely a choice but a basic principle of existence, with religion serving only to organize these natural struggles. Panahian asserted that teaching the principle of struggle and resilience is a prerequisite for religious education, and neglecting it constitutes a betrayal of future generations. He presented the culture of the Sacred Defense and the spirituality of its fighters as a model for family and school upbringing, viewing it as a sign of rationality and human instinct. Finally, he called for preserving this combative culture in society to prevent division and enemy dominance, thereby paving the way for the appearance of the Mahdi.
