A blind student's mother shared her concerns on a radio program about her son's abandonment of religious duties and reduced family contact after moving to Tehran for university. She sought guidance from the host, asking whether this change resulted from their parenting mistakes or the influence of bad friends. The host acknowledged the profound impact of the university environment on an individual's behavior and criticized the educational system's lack of readiness to manage diverse cultural and belief backgrounds. He advised the family to clearly communicate their expectations to the student to prevent misunderstandings and emotional distance. Additionally, he suggested leveraging the support of the student's older brothers to maintain a closer connection and avoiding dormitory living if possible. Finally, he emphasized the importance of regularly reminding the student of his past positive traits to strengthen the emotional bond and alleviate the family's distress.
