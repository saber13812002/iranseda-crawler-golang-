The program began with a discussion on national unity and strength during the Arbaeen pilgrimage, featuring guest instructor Mandegari.
The core of the conversation focused on the importance of tongue discipline and the severe consequences of verbal sins, described as the key to both good and evil.
Instructor Fala Baestant cited religious narrations to emphasize that the tongue is a divine asset requiring more protection than any other body part.
He explained that a single wrong word can cause suffering that exceeds the combined punishments for all other bodily sins.
True spiritual growth involves taming the ego-driven tongue to transform it into a voice of wisdom and the heart, which is essential for a true believer.
The session concluded with collective prayers for the pilgrims and the Imam of the Age, highlighting the spiritual significance of the journey.
The discussion ended by reiterating that most human misfortunes stem from the tongue, and guarding it is considered a form of charity and piety.
