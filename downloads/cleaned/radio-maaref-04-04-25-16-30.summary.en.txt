The radio program begins by emphasizing the establishment of an Islamic government as a religious and rational obligation during the occultation of the Imam.
It highlights the consensus among jurists that the authority of the Supreme Leader is absolute and stresses the crucial role of the people in supporting this leadership.
Contemporary people are considered superior to those of the Prophet's era due to their deeper understanding of the Leader's status and their active cooperation.
In the Q&A section, it is explained that the grief of the deceased in the Barzakh heaven stems from their need for prayers and charity to resolve any deficiencies.
The program warns against contact with disbelieving Jinn, which is dangerous and can lead to the loss of faith, and asserts that prayer is irreplaceable even for those who perform good deeds.
The divine trust mentioned in the Quran is interpreted as a unique form of authority (Wilayah) that transcends mere free will and is exclusive to humans.
Finally, it is clarified that previous human generations lacked the current intellectual capacity, and the present generation represents the final stage of human evolution before the Day of Judgment.
