The radio program begins with a jurisprudential discussion on the necessity of self-evident principles to maintain public welfare and prevent people from turning away from Islam.
It emphasizes that Islam is easy and simple, prioritizing social order and peace even if some absolute truths are compromised.
The conversation then shifts to the 1404 national slogan of "Investment for Production," highlighting the need for government and public cooperation to resolve economic issues.
A subsequent educational segment stresses the importance of discipline, order, and seriousness in both work and education for achieving success.
The speaker argues that life should not be taken lightly and that continuous effort and clear goals are essential for personal growth.
Through Quranic interpretation, the contrast between the joyful faces of the righteous and the humiliated faces of sinners on Judgment Day is explained.
A philosophical and physical argument is presented regarding the impossibility of reversing time, illustrating that existence is a continuous, non-reversible movement.
Finally, listeners are encouraged to understand the truth of divinity and prepare for their meeting with God by taking their lives seriously.
