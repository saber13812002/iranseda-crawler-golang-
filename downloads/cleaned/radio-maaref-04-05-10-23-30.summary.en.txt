This program addresses the theological objection raised by Ayatollah Dehlavi regarding the Prophet's (PBUH) prayer for the Ahl al-Bayt, arguing that it was not a futile request but rather a manifestation of God's eternal will. The speaker refutes the claim that this constitutes "achieving the inevitable" by citing narrations indicating the Prophet's supplication preceded the revelation, thereby aligning with divine predestination. Furthermore, the interpretation by Zayd ibn Arqam that "Ahl al-Bayt" refers to the Prophet's relatives is rejected on two grounds: the context of his statement pertains to the Saqifah incident, and including personal opinion in the text of Sahih Muslim is methodologically flawed. The discussion concludes with a strong emphasis on the necessity for Muslims to awaken and preserve their distinct cultural identity. The speaker warns that Western and Eastern cultural dominance has led to a loss of self-awareness among Muslim youth. Consequently, Islamic scholars and writers are urged to revive indigenous culture and resist the infiltration of foreign ideologies.
