Ahead of the anniversary of Fatima Zahra's martyrdom, the mobile shrines in Najaf and Karbala were prepared with black flags for mourning ceremonies. In the UK, the non-profit organization "Hussein Keyst" set up food distribution stations for the needy, coinciding with Black History Month. The Grand Egyptian Museum was inaugurated with international delegations, while Egyptian religious authorities emphasized the religious duty of preserving historical heritage. Shocking reports emerged from El Fasher, Sudan, detailing massacres of civilians by paramilitaries, leading to protests by the Sudanese diaspora in France who blamed the UAE for the conflict. In Gaza, despite the ceasefire agreement, military attacks continue and aid entry is severely restricted, with only a fraction of the agreed-upon trucks entering the strip. In Italy, a proposed ban on face coverings in public places has faced strong backlash from human rights and Islamic groups. In Iran, religious and security bodies stressed the importance of high voter turnout and combating the illegal buying and selling of election cards ahead of the parliamentary elections. Finally, a specialized session discussed the economic and strategic potential of cooperation between Iran, China, and Russia within the BRICS and SCO frameworks, focusing on de-dollarization and the use of cryptocurrencies to bypass sanctions.
