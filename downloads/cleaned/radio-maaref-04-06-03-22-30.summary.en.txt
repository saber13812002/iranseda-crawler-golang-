In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Panahian emphasizes the vital importance of curiosity as the primary driver of learning and mental growth, drawing on psychological insights. He warns that social media and the internet exploit this natural trait, leading to the degradation of analytical thinking, memory, and deep cognitive abilities. Panahian argues that religion and the Holy Quran serve as the most effective tools for properly guiding this innate potential away from meaningless digital distractions. He highlights the crucial role of parents in responding to children's questions with affection to transform their curiosity into stable, creative knowledge. The discussion also touched upon the beginning of the month of Rabi al-Awwal and the night of Laylat al-Mabit, honoring Imam Ali's sacrifice during the Prophet's migration. The program concluded with a call for prayers for the health of Imam Mahdi and an introduction to upcoming segments on child-rearing with expert guests.
