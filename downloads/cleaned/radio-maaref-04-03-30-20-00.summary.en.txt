The broadcast began by covering widespread post-Jumu'ah prayers marches in Tehran and other cities, where citizens voiced strong anti-American and anti-Israeli sentiments in support of Iran. International analysts, including journalists and U.S. senators, condemned the recent Israeli strikes as the start of a war and a blatant violation of international law, while criticizing the United Nations for its silence. Experts from Turkey and France highlighted Iran's missile capabilities, viewing them as evidence of technical parity with the West and a sign of Iran's growing regional influence. Reports indicated that the Israeli regime attempted to suppress news coverage by detaining journalists and banning networks like Al Jazeera to prevent the dissemination of missile damage footage. The second segment of the program focused on the jurisprudential and legal analysis of the Israeli military attack on the IRIB building. The guests described this action as a clear aggression against the people's right to information and a violation of international laws of war. They emphasized the need to educate the public about the legal frameworks governing these conflicts, noting the gradual progress in the implementation of human rights and laws of war globally.
