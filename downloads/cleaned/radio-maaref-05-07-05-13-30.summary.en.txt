The radio program began with an interview with the official of the Hazrat Abdol-Azim shrine, where condolences were offered for the passing of his mother and appreciation was expressed for the team's services. The legal segment then addressed a complaint from a man who accused a funeral service provider of fraud. The complainant claimed he paid a significant sum for specific services, such as an Italian marble tombstone and a live video stream, which were not delivered as promised. The judge determined that the dispute was civil in nature rather than criminal, noting that failure to fulfill a contract does not constitute fraud without initial deception. It was clarified that while the service provider is liable for damages if unable to perform, it does not meet the legal definition of a criminal offense. Finally, a cleric emphasized the importance of honoring contracts based on Quranic and civil law principles. He also noted that excessive and unnecessary funeral rituals do not benefit the deceased in the afterlife.
