This radio program examined the critical role of Imam Hasan Askari (AS) in protecting the birth of Imam Mahdi (AS) during the severe Abbasid oppression.
The birth of Imam Mahdi (AS) in 255 AH and the start of his Imamate in 260 AH were secured through the Imam's precise security measures and taqiyya, despite intense enemy surveillance.
The primary characteristic of Imam Mahdi's global government will be filling the earth with justice and equity after it has been filled with oppression and tyranny.
Historical evidence indicates that deviant sects did not survive the Minor Occultation due to the security and unity established by Imam Hasan Askari (AS) within the Imami community.
The Imam prepared the Shia community for the era of Occultation by establishing the role of jurists and scholars as intermediaries for religious guidance and social interaction.
The program also featured a summary of the book "Let Us Help Him," which outlines practical ways for believers to support the Imam during his absence.
Praying for the Imam's reappearance and weeping for the martyrdom of Imam Hussein (AS) were highlighted as the most effective means to hasten the Day of Judgment and gain the Imam's pleasure.
