This radio program discussed various Islamic rulings, focusing on the prohibition of disclosing secrets and its specific exceptions.
Professor Baidpour explained that while revealing a defect or illness is generally forbidden, it becomes obligatory to inform the relevant party if silence would harm the family unit or lead to deception.
He emphasized that doctors must disclose conditions like infertility to the prospective spouse to prevent fraud, known as Tadlis, which is strictly prohibited in Islam.
The program also addressed the fatwa of Ayatollah Khamenei regarding inheritance, confirming that a son's share is twice that of a daughter, regardless of whether the deceased is the father or mother.
Additionally, it was stated that using charity items without need is impermissible, and such items must be donated to those in need.
Other rulings included the permissibility of mentioning martyrs by name in night prayers without their fathers' names and the method of repaying debts without exposing the debtor's shame.
Finally, the validity of praying in clothes with animal images and the requirement for a separate Aqiqah sacrifice for each child were confirmed.
