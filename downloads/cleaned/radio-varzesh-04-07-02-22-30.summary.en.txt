In the round of 16 of the English FA Cup, Manchester City hosted Huddersfield Town and established their dominance with a controlled performance. Phil Foden scored the opening goal in the 14th minute, assisted by a precise sequence involving Nunes and Musiala. Pep Guardiola's team maintained full possession and created numerous scoring opportunities through rapid passing inside the opponent's penalty area. Huddersfield, a League One side, adopted a defensive strategy and only posed a threat during set pieces such as throw-ins and free kicks. The match was briefly interrupted due to an injury to Huddersfield defender Redmond, but play resumed after medical staff attended to him. At the end of the first half, Manchester City led 1-0 and continued to press for additional goals.
