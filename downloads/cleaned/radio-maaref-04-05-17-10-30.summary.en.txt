In this radio program, the fundamental difference between the "signs of reappearance" and the "conditions of reappearance" was explained.
The guest emphasized that signs merely indicate the occurrence of reappearance, whereas the conditions are the actual causes that bring it about.
Therefore, the primary duty of awaiters is to recognize and fulfill the conditions of reappearance, rather than solely focusing on tracking signs.
Excessive attention to signs can lead to neglecting the duties of awaiters and falling into the trap of false claimants.
The second guest highlighted the necessity of complete submission in thought, heart, and action to the Imam as the key indicator of Mahdi acceptance.
He introduced the two stations of "patience" and "contentment" as outcomes of submission, explaining their difference in enduring hardships and perceiving divine mercy.
In the final section, the concept of "pre- and post-action management" for awaiters was discussed, involving enhancing insight before action and self-accountability after it.
The ultimate goal of this process is to gain the Imam's satisfaction and continuously refine behavior in line with true expectation.
