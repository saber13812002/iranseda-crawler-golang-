In this radio program, traditional medicine experts advise against consuming kiwi in autumn due to its cold and dry nature and the potential health risks associated with its skin.
The discussion emphasizes the moderate consumption of natural sweet foods like grape molasses and dates, while warning against excessive intake of industrial sweets made with flour and oil, which can cause organ congestion.
The second part of the program focuses on social justice and the prohibition of consuming unlawful wealth, grounded in Quranic verses.
Hojjat al-Islam Rasoul Hafez cites verse 188 of Surah Al-Baqarah to condemn bribery of judges and experts as a form of consuming wealth through falsehood.
Referencing Nahj al-Balagha, he warns that renaming illicit gains as gifts or bonuses does not change their sinful nature.
The program concludes with excerpts from Imam Khomeini's speeches, highlighting the vital role of youth in preserving Islam and Iran.
These discussions are set against a backdrop of autumn-themed music, aiming to guide listeners toward a healthy and ethical lifestyle.
