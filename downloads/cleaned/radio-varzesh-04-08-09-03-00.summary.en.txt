Major Sadeq Shirzad, the founder of mass sports in Iran, emphasized the vital role of daily walking in preventing diseases and reducing heavy healthcare costs. Citing World Health Organization guidelines, he argued that regular exercise not only improves physical health but also enhances quality of life by boosting mental well-being and sleep. Shirzad shared a personal anecdote about a severe accident, demonstrating that athletes can recover their health through continued physical activity even after sustaining injuries. He also highlighted structural challenges, such as the lack of adequate sports spaces in parks, which are often converted into non-sporting recreational areas. In his professional career, Shirzad recalled his collaborations with various federation presidents and the success of track and field teams in the Asian Games. He proposed that professional sports development relies on attracting private investors and establishing partnerships with commercial brands to support athletes. Finally, Shirzad, drawing on his extensive experience as an international referee and coach, stressed the necessity of professionalizing sports management and utilizing the country's economic potential to elevate competitive standards.
