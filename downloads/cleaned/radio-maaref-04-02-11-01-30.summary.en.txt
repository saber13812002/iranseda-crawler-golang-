The program begins by interpreting the concept of "sacred doubt" as a spiritual pain that serves as a catalyst for awakening and the pursuit of truth. The discussion then shifts to governance principles based on the hadith of "the soldiers of intellect and ignorance," emphasizing that intellect must dominate ignorance to prevent arrogance and disobedience. Within this framework, "good" is identified as the minister of intellect, while "evil" is the minister of ignorance, determining the trajectory of societal policy and legislation. A virtuous society is defined as one that possesses both knowledge of felicity and the action to achieve it, unlike an ignorant society which lacks this understanding. In the second part, the program critiques media narratives that link internal problems to negotiations with the United States. The expert clarifies that negotiations aim to curb hegemony and lift oppressive sanctions, rather than legitimizing the system of domination. He asserts that relying on domestic capabilities and self-sufficiency is the only sustainable solution to reduce dependency and effectively counter sanctions.
