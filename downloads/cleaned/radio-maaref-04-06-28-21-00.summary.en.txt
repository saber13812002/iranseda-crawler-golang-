The radio program began by emphasizing the concept of "high ambition," illustrating through stories of Ayatollah Khoei and his teachers how scholars persisted in their scientific duties despite poverty and personal grief. In the "Ocean of the East" book segment, the story of a man named Amran seeking the water of life was narrated, where he met Hajj Karim the cobbler and a stranger who spoke of Imam Reza's miracles. Hajj Karim logically argued to Amran that humans cannot witness everything personally and must therefore rely on the authentic narrations and sayings of the truthful. The "Did You Know?" section clarified the religious ruling that reciting Bismillah is obligatory at the beginning of all Quranic surahs in prayer, except for Surah Tawbah. The broadcast concluded with a call to free debtors during the Prophet's birth month and an invitation to submit documents related to the Sacred Defense to the relevant foundation. Finally, listeners were encouraged to participate in content creation and direction through the "Ma'aref Yararan" program or by calling the hotline.
