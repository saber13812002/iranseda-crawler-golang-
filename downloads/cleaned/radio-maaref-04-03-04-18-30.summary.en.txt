The program begins by interpreting the Quranic verse regarding making room for newcomers in a gathering.
While some scholars limit the divine reward of "expansion" to Paradise, the Nahj al-Balagha interpretation suggests it applies to worldly life as well.
According to this view, God grants expansion in dimensions such as the soul, lifespan, sustenance, and overall life in return for this kindness.
Respecting elders and the elderly in public spaces, such as buses, serves as a practical example of this Quranic social etiquette.
Honoring the elderly is considered a form of divine reverence and a way to uphold human values.
This behavior is not only a moral duty but also a path to gaining divine pleasure and increasing blessings in one's life.
Ultimately, creating space for others to enter is a sign of humility and the observance of social rights in Islam.
