Dr. Khosrow Ebrahimi Nia emphasized the necessity of combining knowledge with action and achieving managerial positions through merit and personal effort. He viewed nepotism and unwarranted support as a betrayal of society and an obstacle to individual and national growth. By sharing the story of Nader Ayazi, a disabled rower, he demonstrated how proper support and thinking can restore hope to a defeated individual. With Ayazi's inclusion in the national team, he succeeded in winning continental medals and lifting the suspension of the rowing federation. This experience proved that big-picture thinking and recognizing human potential can lead to remarkable success even in difficult conditions. Ebrahimi Nia also stressed the importance of continuous learning, reading, and avoiding jealousy and empty negative criticism. He believes that true cultural development relies on practicing ethical values and raising children with intellectual and practical independence. Finally, he insisted on the necessity of making the best use of life's time and serving the community sincerely under the Islamic system.
