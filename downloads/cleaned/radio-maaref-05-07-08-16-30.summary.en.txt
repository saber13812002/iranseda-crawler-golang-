In this radio program, Professor Mohammadi addressed various theological and jurisprudential questions. He emphasized the importance of honoring the deceased through charitable deeds dedicated to them, noting that such actions can help save them from the punishment of the Barzakh. The discussion also covered the etymology of the word "Jahannam" and the presence of Persian words in the Quran. Furthermore, he explained the high spiritual value of serving the legally incapacitated, stating that such service can sometimes yield greater reward than other acts of worship. Citing religious narrations, he described listening to anti-religious media as worshipping Satan and enduring verbal abuse from enemies as a form of major jihad with significant rewards. In response to a question about hearing a deceased person's voice, he confirmed that spirits in the Barzakh have the power to speak and send messages. He also clarified that misfortunes are not necessarily caused by the victim's own sins but may result from the negligence or sins of others. Finally, the conditions for following a prayer leader and the differing impacts of burial locations were discussed.
