The radio program "Tales of Freedom" begins with the memoirs of Sayyid Mahmoud Pourmousavi, a former POW captured during the 1983 Khyber operation. He recounts the harsh conditions of the front lines, the encirclement of forces, and the transfer of wounded soldiers to Urmia and later Baghdad prison. Pourmousavi describes how prisoners formed religious, cultural, and medical councils to manage their affairs and save the lives of the sick during their captivity. The program also features a review of the best-selling book "Hello Ibrahim," which documents the life and martyrdom of a war hero. The broadcast concludes with condolences for the passing of Ayatollah Seyyed Mousa Sadr, a prominent Shia cleric. He is celebrated as a jurist who strengthened the link between jurisprudence and hadith, leaving behind significant works in source criticism. His memory is honored by the Supreme Leader, the seminary, and the people of Iran.
