In this radio program, Professor Mahmoudi discussed the third duty of waiting, which is the love and affection for Imam Mahdi (aj). Citing Quranic verses and narrations, he emphasized that having affection for the Ahl al-Bayt, particularly the Imam of the Time, is obligatory for every believer. He noted that God loves those who love the Mahdi, thereby elevating the spiritual status of the lover. Through stories of the Prophet's (pbuh) and Imam Baqir's (as) devoted followers, the professor illustrated how this love can purify sins and raise a person's worth. It was highlighted that love for the Imam must surpass love for family and wealth to avoid being categorized as a sinner. Listeners expressed their longing for the reappearance through messages and poetry sent to the show. A book titled "Practical Ways to Remember Imam Mahdi" was recommended to help audiences cultivate this spiritual connection. The ultimate goal was to encourage a dynamic, love-driven life that accelerates the arrival of the Savior.
