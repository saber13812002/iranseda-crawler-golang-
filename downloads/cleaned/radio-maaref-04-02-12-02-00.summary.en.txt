The program first examined the marriage of Sayyida Zainab to Muawiya ibn al-Zubayr, noting that some sources describe it as forced while others deny it entirely. It then addressed the prohibition of narrating hadiths during the early caliphates, identifying the primary motive as preventing the spread of political hadiths favorable to Ali (AS). The reason behind the title "Ja'far al-Kadhdhab" for Imam Sajjad's (AS) brother was also explained as stemming from his baseless claim to succession. Furthermore, the role of Salman al-Farisi in the conquest of Iran was discussed, highlighting his encouragement of the Muslim army to cross the Tigris and fight the Sassanids. Finally, the program concluded by emphasizing the high status of daughters in Islam and the loving conduct of the Prophet (PBUH) and Shia scholars towards their children.
