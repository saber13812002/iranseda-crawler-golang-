In this radio program, the host first references the historical marriage of Fatima and Ali to emphasize the necessity of mutual acquaintance and full consent in marriage.
By recounting the story of their modest and symbolic dowry, the instructor defines mahr not as a financial transaction but as a right to education and a sign of affection.
He criticizes the common and expensive tradition of dowry, stating it has no roots in Islam, and advocates for simplifying wedding ceremonies.
Subsequently, a narrative segment from the series "The Book Left Behind by the Son" is aired, where Azar, a woman whose husband Salār has left her, encounters him at her uncle's coffeehouse.
Salār, seeking to atone for his mistakes and regain Azar's trust, expresses remorse and asks for another chance to rebuild their relationship.
Azar, deeply upset by Salār's past behavior and her son Sohrab's threats, reacts to this return with hesitation and anger.
Salār's sudden appearance intensifies family tensions and Azar's anxieties, especially on the eve of the Muharram ceremonies.
The program concludes with a prayer for successful and affectionate marriages and an announcement of upcoming Radio Ma'aref programs.
