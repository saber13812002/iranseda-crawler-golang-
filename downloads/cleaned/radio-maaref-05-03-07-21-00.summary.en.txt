In this radio program, experts characterized the Supreme Leader's message not merely as a political statement, but as a "governance idea" and a third civilizational model following Marxism and liberalism. They argued that this message, by presenting a doctrine of resistance and a monotheistic horizon, neutralizes the West's plans to isolate and erase Iran, turning it into an intolerable model for Western civilization. Media analysis indicates that the global coverage of this message has transcended transient news, becoming a "meta-text" discussed in international dialogue forums. Outlets like Fox News and former US diplomats have highlighted the message's civilizational and eschatological aspects, interpreting them as an existential and deeply rooted threat to their interests. A key point in these analyses is the strategic use of language and metaphors, such as "Allahu Akbar" contrasted with the "Great Satan," which defines Iran's identity and strategy against its adversaries. Ultimately, the experts believe that Iran, relying on both soft and hard power, is becoming a pole that competes with major world powers in cultural, economic, and security domains.
