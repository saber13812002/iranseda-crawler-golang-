The program began with a narrative about the hardships of a winter journey on a freight train, highlighting the severe cold and darkness.
The main segment focused on interpreting a hadith from Imam Sajjad in the book of Kafi, which emphasizes the superiority of being an enemy to the world after knowing God.
Scholars like Allameh Majlisi clarified that this enmity does not mean abandoning work but rather avoiding moral vices such as arrogance, envy, and greed.
Referencing Nahj al-Balagha, arrogance was identified as Iblis's first sin, which led to his expulsion from heaven.
The broadcast included a detailed prayer for the removal of oppression and a request for divine justice.
The final section stressed the importance of water conservation through changing daily habits and adopting efficient agricultural irrigation methods.
Finally, the program highlighted the plight of the people of Gaza and encouraged listeners to donate via the provided numbers.
The show concluded with a musical performance by Salarr Aghili accompanied by a poem from Hafez.
