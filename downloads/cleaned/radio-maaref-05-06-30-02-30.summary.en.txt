The "Zamzam Ahkam" program on Radio Ma'aref addressed various religious questions from listeners. According to Ayatollah Falahzadeh, women during menstruation may recite the Quran, but it is forbidden for them to recite prostration verses. Ayatollah Jawahiri clarified that failing to specify a mahr (dowry) does not invalidate a permanent marriage contract, unlike temporary marriages which become void in such cases. Regarding wudu, Ayatollah Moeini stated that wiping the head and feet must be done with the moisture remaining on the hands, and re-wetting the hands is not permitted. Ayatollah Al-Najat explained that the night prayer remains recommended during travel and is not waived, though caution is advised against performing the Isha optional prayer while traveling. Ayatollah Falahzadeh ruled that selling alcohol and pork to non-Muslims is strictly forbidden. In another response, Ayatollah Mohammadi Sheikh Shabani noted that debt does not prevent the obligation of Khums, although debts can be settled before calculating the tax. The program concluded with Ayatollah Mazarrahi discussing the sixth stage of spiritual development, "Tajalli," emphasizing the joy derived from worship and a deeper understanding of prayer.
