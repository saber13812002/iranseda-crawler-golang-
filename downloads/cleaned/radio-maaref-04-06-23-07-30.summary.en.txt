The radio program emphasized the spiritual and material benefits of waking up early and performing night prayers, citing hadiths that link these acts to increased sustenance and divine blessings.
Key strategies for overcoming negative habits such as oversleeping, overeating, and excessive talking were discussed to promote a healthy and blessed lifestyle.
The second part of the show focused on methods to avoid slander and backbiting, highlighting the importance of staying away from negative social circles and controlling one's speech.
Supreme Leader Khamenei described slander as a form of shirk that darkens society, stressing that avoiding it is a strict religious and ethical obligation for believers.
The Leader also warned that slandering the system is more severe than slandering individuals and cautioned against repeating foreign media claims under the guise of transparency.
Finally, the program advised listeners to rely on God and distance themselves from malice to remain protected from the psychological harm of baseless accusations.
