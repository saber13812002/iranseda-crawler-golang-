In this radio program, specific hadiths related to historical events were critically examined using reliable historical sources such as Masudi's history. The main discussion focused on narrations concerning the Qirqisiya and a detailed analysis of their chains of transmission, particularly regarding the narrator Muhammad ibn Sinan. Evidence from scholars like Sheikh Tusi was presented to demonstrate that the criticisms against certain narrators were based on fabricated reports or later exploitation rather than genuine weakness. It was emphasized that the fall of the Abbasid Caliphate was not solely due to internal disputes but resulted from mismanagement, corruption, and external invasions. A narration from Ghaybat al-Nu'mani referencing Abbasid conflicts was interpreted as one of the signs of the Mahdi's appearance, though it does not explicitly indicate a return of Abbasid rule. The textual analysis concluded that many of the discussed narrations are reliable from a chain-of-transmission perspective and should not be dismissed easily.
