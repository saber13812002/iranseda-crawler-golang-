In this radio program, Professor Jafari first details the four Battles of Fijar during the pre-Islamic era, noting that they were named so because they occurred in sacred months and involved grave sins. He provides specific examples of tribal conflicts, including an incident in the Ukaz market that highlights the practice of women wearing veils in that period. The discussion focuses on the fourth battle, the Fijar of Burayd, which lasted four years and involved the Banu Kinanah and Banu Amir tribes. A central point is the refutation of the claim that the Prophet (PBUH) participated in this war, as his infallibility predates his twenty-second year, making involvement in a war violating sacred months theologically unacceptable. Historical evidence from al-Ya'qubi's history is cited, where Abu Talib forbade the Banu Hashim from joining the battle, citing it as an act of oppression and severing family ties. The second part of the discussion addresses the birth of Imam Ali (AS) inside the Kaaba, a claim supported by the claim of tawatur (mass transmission) by Hakim al-Nishaburi, a prominent Sunni scholar. Other Sunni scholars, such as Ibn al-Jawzi and Sheikh Ali Qari, have also corroborated this narrative, indicating its widespread acceptance in traditional sources. The program concludes with the recitation of Imam Sadiq's prayer for rain and announcements regarding special radio programs for the Prophet's birthday and Unity Week.
