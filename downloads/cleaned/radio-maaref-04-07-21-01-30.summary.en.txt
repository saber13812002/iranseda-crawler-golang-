In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Saeidi emphasizes the importance of perceiving God as the greatest power, based on Nahj al-Balagha. He argues that when God is truly magnified in one's heart, fear of enemies and worldly calculations disappear, leading to genuine peace. The speaker cites a story of Imam Khomeini during his arrest to illustrate how reliance on God prevents spiritual trembling even in the face of death. The discussion also contrasts the divine perspective with worldly power, referencing the story of Moses and Pharaoh. The second part of the program features testimonies from veterans of the Iran-Iraq war who described their courage and faith during combat and captivity. These veterans expressed that they had no fear of death and considered martyrdom an honor despite the harsh conditions and enemy interrogations. The program concludes with a narration about the reward of martyrdom in the path of Imam Mahdi (AJ), reinforcing hope for his reappearance.
