The radio broadcast began with the farewell ceremonies for martyred IRGC commanders, including Sardar Rashid Ameli and Major General Nasir Baghban.
The Supreme Leader emphasized that the enemy failed in the recent war and that the martyrs' blood is essential for strengthening the Islamic system.
The Minister of Economy announced the establishment of a compensation fund for war damages and support for industries and financial markets.
Basij volunteer groups have started clearing rubble and rebuilding damaged areas in coordination with the Housing Foundation.
The Islamic Propagation Office dispatched twelve thousand preachers nationwide to promote the culture of martyrdom during the month of Muharram.
In the religious rulings segment, a scholar clarified that mourning rituals are permissible as long as they adhere to Islamic laws, allowing for diverse expressions.
Responses to religious queries addressed the obligation of Takbir in prayer and confirmed that mutual financial help between parents and children is not considered usury.
The program concluded with the recitation of hymns and prayers dedicated to Imam Hussein.
