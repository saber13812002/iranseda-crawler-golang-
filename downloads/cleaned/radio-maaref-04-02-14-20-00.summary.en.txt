The Supreme Leader of Iran emphasized that the unity of the Islamic Ummah is its highest benefit, describing the Hajj as a combination of political and spiritual dimensions essential for managing humanity. He offered condolences for the victims of the Bandar Abbas terrorist attack, urging families to remain patient while promising that material damages would be swiftly compensated. The radio program then marked the sixth of Dhu al-Qi'dah as the commemoration of the birth of Imam Ahmad ibn Musa, known as Shah Cheragh in Shiraz. Guest speaker Seyyed Hossein Zadeh, a preacher at the Fatima Masumeh Shrine, discussed the spiritual significance of naming children after the Ahl al-Bayt. He highlighted religious narrations suggesting that names like Ali, Fatima, and Ahmad bring specific blessings and peace to families. The episode concluded with an invitation for listeners to send messages and recite prayers for the Prophet and his family.
