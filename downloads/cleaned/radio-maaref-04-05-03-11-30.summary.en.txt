The radio program "Zamzam Ahkam" began by addressing religious questions from followers of various clerical authorities. Regarding a Quran page stained with blood, it was explained that the page must be washed with running water and buried in clean soil if it becomes unusable. The program clarified that depositing unjustly acquired money into charity funds is a precautionary measure, whereas direct payment to the rightful owner or with the permission of the religious authority is preferred. It was also stated that forgetting the testimony (Shahada) during prayer requires performing two prostrations of forgetfulness after the final salam, followed by reciting the testimony and salam again. A 19-year-old listener was reassured that adding an extra word to the prayer salam does not invalidate past prayers, as it is not a core pillar of the ritual. Additionally, the program emphasized that withdrawing money from a parent's account without their legal permission is impermissible. The segment concluded with contact information for submitting further questions. The final part of the transcript featured anecdotes about the noble character of Ayatollah Saduqi and highlighted the importance of Friday prayers and solidarity marches in support of Palestine.
