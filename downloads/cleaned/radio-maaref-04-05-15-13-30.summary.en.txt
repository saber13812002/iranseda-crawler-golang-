The broadcast began with a prayer for the oppressed people of Palestine and a condemnation of Zionist aggressors. In the "Narrative of Love" segment, Zahra Nasiri, the widow of martyr Javad Akbari, shared her grief and the societal stigma faced by martyrs' families. She described her children's anxiety over their father's absence and her own sense of orphanhood while caring for them. Nasiri emphasized that martyrs remain spiritually present, often appearing in dreams to comfort their loved ones. She viewed her husband's martyrdom as a conscious and beautiful choice. The program concluded with a critique of US policies and the "jahiliyya" front, accusing them of destroying regional infrastructure and waging war against the Islamic Republic and the Muslim Ummah.
