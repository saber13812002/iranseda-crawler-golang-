In the "Zamzam Ahkam" program, jurisprudential answers were provided to followers' religious questions. Hojjat al-Islam Mahdavi clarified that uncertainty regarding bodily impurity from urine does not impose a specific obligation, as the person is presumed pure unless certainty of impurity is established. The rules for joining congregational prayer at different stages and the conditions for valid following of the imam were also detailed. Hojjat al-Islam Jawahiri explained that if a follower says the opening takbir but fails to reach the imam's bowing position, they must continue their prayer individually. The free purchase and sale of Umrah Hajj tickets were declared permissible and obligatory for those with financial means. In the "Sarmashk" segment, Professor Javad Mohaddesi emphasized the importance of recognizing the true value of knowledge and avoiding its misuse by deceivers, citing Nahj al-Balagha. He highlighted the Prophet's Ghadir Khumm sermon as the primary proof of Imam Ali's authority and stressed the social responsibility of knowledgeable scholars to stand against oppression and corruption.
