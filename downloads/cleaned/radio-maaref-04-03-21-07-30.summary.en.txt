In this radio program, the host first congratulates the birth of Imam Hadi before discussing the role of humor in Islamic ethics. Based on religious narrations, humor is encouraged as a sign of good character, but excessive joking that leads to offense or loss of dignity is strictly condemned. The discussion then shifts to the vice of arrogance, which is identified as the greatest sin and the "garment" of Satan. The guest expert cites Quranic verses and hadiths to explain that arrogance is the primary cause of human downfall and opens the gates of hell. He emphasizes that arrogance stems from Satan's whispers and distances individuals from humility and true servitude to God. Conversely, humility is presented as a border checkpoint and a weapon for resisting Satan. The session concludes by stressing the necessity of abandoning pride to achieve moral perfection and spiritual elevation.
