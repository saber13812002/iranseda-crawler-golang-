The radio program "Khaneh-ye Doost" begins with a spiritual and mystical atmosphere, inviting listeners to recite religious rulings and prayers. Various segments emphasize the importance of adhering to the rulings of the Supreme Leaders and following Sharia law. The program also announces student admissions for the 1404-1405 academic year at women's seminaries, offering both in-person and non-in-person modes across different levels. It highlights the preservation of the Persian language by discouraging the use of foreign terms like "alarm" in favor of native words such as "ajir" or "hashdar." Through the recitation of mystical poetry and religious supplications, the show creates an intimate and spiritual environment for its audience. The program aims to deepen the connection between listeners and religious concepts by exploring various dimensions of spiritual life. Finally, it mentions upcoming broadcasts on Radio Ma'aref, including the "Basta" program and Islamic world news, to keep listeners informed about the schedule.
