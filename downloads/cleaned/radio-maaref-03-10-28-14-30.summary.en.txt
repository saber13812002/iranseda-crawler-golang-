In the radio program "Ma'aref," expert Mehdi Yousefian addressed Mahdavi questions, explaining that the Imam of the Age possesses non-human knowledge akin to divine awareness, making the "record of deeds" a symbol of his constant oversight of believers. He clarified that the weekly presentation of these records on Tuesdays and Fridays is not a literal need for paper but a reminder that no action is hidden from the Imam's view. The discussion then shifted to the moral landscape after the Imam's reappearance, noting that while non-punishable sins are mitigated by repentance in a pious society, punishable crimes are swiftly addressed by a justice-based system. The presence of the Imam is essential for managing society according to religious values, thereby minimizing the environment conducive to sin. Subsequently, the "Rahbord" program featured Dr. Mehdi Moshkibaf analyzing recent political shifts in Syria. The analyst highlighted that ignoring intelligence warnings about the enemy's rapid actions was a critical lesson from the crisis. He also referenced the 2006 strategic pact and Syria's fair support for Iran during the Iran-Iraq war. Finally, Iran's presence in Syria was examined as both a negative factor for countering threats and a positive factor for securing shared interests.
