This radio program highlights the exalted status of Imam Hussein (PBUH) as the most beloved figure on earth and in the heavens, citing prophetic traditions. Relying on the words of Imam Reza (PBUH), the speaker emphasizes that visiting the shrines of the Infallibles is essential for fulfilling one's spiritual debt and securing intercession on the Day of Judgment. A primary benefit of visiting Imam Hussein (PBUH), especially for those who walk, is the complete forgiveness of sins and the erasure of past deeds. Furthermore, visitors are promised salvation from the fire of Hell and inclusion in the party of God and His Messenger. The program also lists worldly blessings such as extended lifespan, increased sustenance, and the removal of calamities for those who make the pilgrimage. The speaker notes that God granted four special attributes to Imam Hussein's shrine in recognition of his martyrdom. One of these attributes is that the time spent traveling to and from Karbala is not deducted from the pilgrim's actual lifespan.
