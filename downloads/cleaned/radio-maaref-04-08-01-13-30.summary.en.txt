In the radio program "Narrative of Love," Nafiseh Kermani, the wife of Martyr Colonel Ali Agazadeh, recounted the moments of her husband's martyrdom during a terrorist attack on IRGC advisors in Syria. She described her unusual calm on that day and her daughter Fatemeh's anxiety at university, where she sought solace at a martyr's shrine by invoking Lady Zainab (SA). Following the martyrdom, transporting the body from Damascus to Latakia posed significant challenges due to Israeli bombings of the airport. Nafiseh described the road from Damascus to Latakia as a painful path filled with shared memories of her late husband. She expressed her belief that martyrdom was worthy of her husband, a conviction that brought peace to her and their children. The program concluded with a message from the Supreme Leader, emphasizing the remarkable growth of the private sector under sanctions and the necessity of government oversight over these enterprises.
