This radio program discusses hadiths regarding the occultation of Imam Mahdi and the figure of Fadl ibn Shadhan. The speaker analyzes the critiques by Sheikh Tusi and Allameh Khoyi, demonstrating that the critical narrations against Fadl ibn Shadhan are weak or suspicious in their chains of transmission. Conversely, Sheikh Tusi cited valid chains in his book Al-Mushaykha that establish the reliability of Fadl ibn Shadhan. After addressing these chain-of-transmission issues, the focus shifts to the implications of the primary narrations explaining the reason for the prolonged occultation. Imam Sadiq (AS) stated in a narration that the wisdom behind the Imam's occultation is the same as that of the previous Imams and prophets. This wisdom includes testing the people and removing doubts from the hearts of the faithful. It is also noted that, similar to the story of Khidr and Moses, the full understanding of the occultation's wisdom may become clear in the future or at the time of reappearance. The overall conclusion is that the occultation is a divine matter with deep roots in the tradition of the Infallibles, rather than a new or unexplained phenomenon.
