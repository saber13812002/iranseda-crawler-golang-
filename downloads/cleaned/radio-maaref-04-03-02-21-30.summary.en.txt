In this program, Ayatollah Najm al-Din Tusi examines the narrations regarding Imam Zaman's (AJ) confrontation with the Jews during the era of the Mahdi. Citing authentic traditions, he argues that the inheritance of the prophets, including the Tablets of Moses and the Prophet's sword, remains in the possession of the Ahl al-Bayt rather than being hidden in Antarctica. Imam Sadiq (AS) explicitly stated that these divine assets and knowledge are "with us" and under the control of the Infallibles. Consequently, claims that these physical items exist elsewhere are inconsistent with the traditions of the Ahl al-Bayt. The Imam's approach to opponents involves debate, taxation, exile, and ultimately war to establish divine proof. The discussion also references a narration from Imam Baqir (AS) indicating the return (Raj'ah) of the Prophet and Imam Ali (AS) alongside Imam Zaman (AJ). At that time, angels will descend to earth, and the Prophet's banner will be handed over to Imam Zaman (AJ) to establish justice with divine assistance.
