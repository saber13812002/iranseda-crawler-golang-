The radio program "Parsaiyan" emphasizes the Islamic obligation to respect animal rights and avoid causing them harm, citing examples from the lives of Ayatollah Behjat and Shahid Motahhari. It also recounts the story of Sayyid Mohammad Baqir Shafie, whose kindness to a starving dog led to divine blessings and financial prosperity. While advocating for compassion, the program notes that managing stray animals is necessary to maintain public order and safety. The broadcast then transitions to a dramatic retelling of the book "Ocean of the East," featuring the characters Aman and Hajj Karim. In this narrative, Hajj Karim uses the parable of Iblis and his father to explain that Aman's skepticism regarding the miracles of Imam Reza stems from a lack of true spiritual knowledge. By drinking from the Imam's eternal waterskin, Hajj Karim demonstrates that miracles transcend human logic and are fundamentally different from magic, which is merely an illusion.
