During the 36th anniversary of Imam Khomeini's passing, Iran's Supreme Leader emphasized that uranium enrichment is the core of the country's nuclear industry and an inalienable right that the US and Israel cannot strip away. He highlighted the Islamic Revolution's role in fostering a multipolar world and urged Muslim governments to avoid any normalization or support for the Zionist regime. The broadcast also covered memorial events in China and Pakistan, as well as the ongoing transfer of Iranian pilgrims to the holy sites for Hajj. Internationally, the UN Security Council is set to vote on a draft resolution for a ceasefire and humanitarian aid in Gaza, which the US has reportedly delayed. The program further discussed the historical significance of London's Central Mosque in spreading Islamic culture in Europe. Finally, it detailed the establishment of the Expediency Discernment Council by Imam Khomeini to resolve legislative conflicts between the Parliament and the Guardian Council. This mechanism was presented as a crucial tool for maintaining systemic stability and navigating complex political and economic challenges.
