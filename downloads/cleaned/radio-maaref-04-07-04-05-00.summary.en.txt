This radio program explores the nature of "intellect" as a divine attribute and internal force that distinguishes the human soul from other animals.
It emphasizes that intellect alone is insufficient for correct guidance and must be controlled by the chain of Sharia and religion to achieve perfection.
The discussion highlights that without religious guidance, intellect may lead to significant deviations, whereas following divine laws leads to the station of guardianship and God's love.
Subsequently, detailed philosophical arguments from Mulla Sadra regarding the difference between "mental existence" and "external existence" were presented.
It was explained that concepts like disbelief or heat, when merely imagined in the mind, do not possess their external, physical effects.
A precise analysis was conducted on the distinction between "abstract concepts" that have external origins and purely rational concepts that lack independent external existence.
The final conclusion asserts that correctly understanding this divine force and placing it on the path of Sharia is the key to achieving complete humanity and the purpose of creation.
