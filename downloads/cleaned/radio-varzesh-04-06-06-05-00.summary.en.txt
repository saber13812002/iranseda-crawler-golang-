Abbas Aghabaghi, a prominent Iranian basketball coach, highlights his pivotal role in moving Iran from the Asian to the African zone, which significantly increased the team's visibility and secured international quotas. He reflects on national team successes, including the 2003 Asian Youth Championship and the fourth-place finish at the 2024 Paris Paralympics. Aghabaghi also discusses his international executive roles in the World and Asian federations and his efforts to develop basketball in Malaysia, Thailand, and Afghanistan. He emphasizes that working with youth has been his primary model, leading to the discovery of talents like Behnam Yakhchali and Sina Vahidi. His coaching mission in Afghanistan, conducted under difficult conditions with Red Cross support, helped establish the country's national teams. Finally, Aghabaghi notes that his continuous educational work at the Dookhanian club and Pishgaman basketball school has been his main focus since his injury, dedicated to training the next generation of players.
