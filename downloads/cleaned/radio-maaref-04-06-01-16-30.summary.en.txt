The first segment of the program emphasized the importance of the rule of "entering and exiting with truthfulness" in religious and worldly actions.
The key educational point is to abandon hypocrisy and insincerity in all interactions and partnerships.
Sincerity in performing tasks, from product manufacturing to social relations, leads to divine blessings and public satisfaction.
The second segment began with the topic of "border guarding" and the high status of border guards in Islamic teachings.
Protecting the physical and cultural borders of the country is considered a very noble and rewarding act of worship.
Imam Sajjad (AS) sent numerous prayers for the protection of border guards and national security in the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya.
Border guarding is not limited to preserving the land but includes safeguarding the identity, culture, and moral health of society.
People have a duty to support border forces and national security through prayers and moral and material assistance.
