This program compares the world to two competitive arenas: servitude to God and slavery to Satan, each with its own specific judge and coach. Based on the Holy Quran, the speaker identifies four main groups: the cursed (losers), the deprived, the beloved, and the medal winners. The cursed group includes disbelievers, hypocrites, liars, Sabbath-breakers, oath-breakers, and intentional murderers who have excluded themselves from God's mercy. It is emphasized that disbelief and lying deprive a person of God's service and mercy, even if they perform good deeds on the surface. The audience is also urged to avoid spreading rumors, accusing believing women without evidence, and transgressing divine limits. Finally, the divine intention behind actions and revolutions is highlighted as more important than the actions themselves, with a prayer for success in this existential competition.
