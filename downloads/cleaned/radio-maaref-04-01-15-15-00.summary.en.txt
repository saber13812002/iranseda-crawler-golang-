This radio program examined the Supreme Leader's security strategies, focusing on the deterrence doctrine and the preservation of the Islamic Republic.
It was emphasized that the leadership insisted on defense industry self-sufficiency and technological advancement in missiles and drones from the early stages of the Iran-Iraq War.
Despite severe sanctions and a global blockade, Iran transitioned from relying on foreign arms purchases to producing and exporting advanced weaponry.
Recent military operations demonstrated that Iran's missile strike capability can successfully penetrate multiple layers of enemy air defense systems.
These achievements have forced adversaries to move from denial to acknowledgment, and subsequently to efforts aimed at containing this power.
The country's security strategy has not only guaranteed national security but also contributed to the economy by creating employment and generating foreign exchange revenue.
Ultimately, this deterrent power has established Iran as a significant regional and global force, compelling enemies to reassess their strategic approaches.
