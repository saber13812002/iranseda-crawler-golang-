In this program, Hojjat al-Islam Seyyed Mohammad Hossein Raji introduced cultural and propagandistic jihad as a crucial dimension of Lady Zahra's (SA) struggle. He highlighted her role in teaching religious laws and training capable students, noting that the Ahl al-Bayt (AS) consistently continued their jihad through education and the upbringing of future generations. The speaker cited the story of how Martyr Kamirab's short speech transformed the life of Brigadier General Qasem Soleimani, emphasizing that even a brief address can alter a person's destiny. He stressed the importance of mosques as the primary base for human development, urging administrators to support youth participation with patience and kindness rather than driving them away with inappropriate behavior. Good manners and humanism were presented as essential tools and a necessary lifestyle for success in cultural jihad. Citing narrations, the speaker identified faith in God and benefiting others as the two main pillars of religiosity. Finally, he called upon all Muslims to inspire others towards religion and the revolution through their conduct, warning against underestimating the power of propagation.
