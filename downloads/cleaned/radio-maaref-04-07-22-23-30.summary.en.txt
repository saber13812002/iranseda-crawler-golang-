Ayatollah Ali Doost recounts his initial failed attempt to study in Qom and his subsequent move to Shiraz at the suggestion of local scholars. He spent the years 1975 to 1976 in Shiraz, studying at the Martyr Dastgheib school where he benefited from the spiritual guidance and special affection of the martyr himself. Feeling that Shiraz was insufficient for his academic goals, he migrated to Qom in 1976 to continue his studies at the Golpayegani school. Despite his short prior experience, his strong grasp of foundational texts earned him the attention of prominent teachers in the advanced levels. He later studied under distinguished scholars such as Ayatollah Khatam Yazdi and Makarem Shirazi during his Kharij studies. For approximately twelve years, he focused on jurisprudence and principles under the guidance of Ayatollah Vahid Khorasani. These memories are part of the radio program "Hajar," which concludes with the recitation of Ziyarat Al-Yasin and religious poetry on the night of Jamkaran.
