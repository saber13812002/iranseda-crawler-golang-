This radio program outlines spiritual strategies for balancing worldly life with the afterlife based on Islamic teachings.
The first two strategies are filling the heart with the remembrance of God and practicing asceticism in the material world.
The third strategy is a strong desire for the afterlife, illustrated by Imam Sadiq's (AS) prayers to transfer worldly losses instead of spiritual ones.
A major focus is placed on "sabr" (patience) against sin, specifically regarding the control of sexual and egoistic desires.
The interpretation of verse 25 of Surah An-Nisa highlights the virtue of self-restraint for those who fear falling into sin.
Through the story of Pouria-ye Vali, it is emphasized that the strongest person is one who masters their own ego.
The conclusion asserts that overcoming self-interest and humbling oneself before one's desires opens the heavenly gates for the individual.
