In this radio program, the importance of "basirat" (insight) as an innate and acquirable quality for discerning truth during crises was emphasized over mere academic knowledge or social status.
The guest warned, citing historical examples, that a lack of insight can lead even scholars and officials astray during critical moments.
In the Q&A section, the necessity of good ethics within the family and avoiding severe complaints about spouses to protect children's mental health was highlighted.
The concept of sustenance was redefined, explaining that material wealth is merely a treasury, while true sustenance includes both spiritual and material benefits from life.
Finally, the topic of citizenship rights was explored with a focus on child-rearing, stressing the need for parents to understand children's developmental stages and model correct behavior.
The program concluded with advice on making a spiritual investment in raising a faithful generation and an invitation for listeners to share their feedback.
