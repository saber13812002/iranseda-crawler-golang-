In this radio program, Professor Tarashoun addressed the parenting challenges of the new generation and the pivotal role of mothers as role models. He emphasized that a mother must be perceived by her child as popular, respected, and strong to be an effective influence. A key recommendation is managing the child's peer environment and avoiding negative comparisons or behaviors at home. To prepare an older child for a new sibling, storytelling and strengthening the father's role in the newborn's first weeks are suggested. The main goal is to reduce jealousy and create a sense of security by addressing the child's emotional and environmental needs. Finally, the program highlighted the status of Imam Mahdi (AJ) in the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya and the importance of praying for him on the Day of Arafah.
