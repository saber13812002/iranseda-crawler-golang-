Former Persepolis and national team player Ardalan Ashtiani recounts his playing career in this interview. He discusses the turbulent departures from Persepolis following Denizli's arrival and his subsequent transfers to teams like Homa and Damash Dorud. Ashtiani details his severe ACL injuries, which led to his early retirement after three ruptures and two surgeries. He also criticizes the financial and structural challenges in Iranian football, expressing concern about the difficulty for young coaches to secure sponsors. Now an AFC football instructor, Ashtiani emphasizes the importance of education and ethics in the sport. He names Ibrahim Ashtiani as the best player in Iranian football history and dreams of one day coaching Persepolis against Esteghlal.
