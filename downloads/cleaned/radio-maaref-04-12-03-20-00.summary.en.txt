The seasonal radio station "Niyayesh" has launched for Ramadan, aiming to promote spiritual culture and deepen religious knowledge through 24-hour broadcasting. The station, available on 95.5 MHz, features programs such as Sahifa recitations, Quranic readings, and dawn supplications. News segments reported on an Iftar gathering in New York's Times Square, where Muslims distributed copies of the Quran to counter negative media portrayals. The International Al-Mustafa Translation and Publishing Center highlighted its role in global Islamic education by publishing approximately 7,000 titles in over 50 languages. Historical reports detailed the construction of the Safa Mosque in Algeria as a symbol of resistance against French colonialism. Additionally, Cambodia's strategy to develop halal tourism and the unveiling of a rare 19th-century Quran manuscript in Albania were covered. The segment concluded with updates on Malaysia's Quran endowment project, which is nearing its goal of distributing one million copies worldwide.
