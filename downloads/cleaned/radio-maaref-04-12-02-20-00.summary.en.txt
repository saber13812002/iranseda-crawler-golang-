On the eve of the 39th Golezaran Festival, Iran's Supreme Leader allocated 50 billion rials for the release of needy prisoners. Concurrently, the administration of the mobile shrine of Imam Ali in Najaf launched extensive Ramadan programs, including daily iftar distribution and care for orphans. The Dar al-Quran center at the Holy Shrine of Imam Hussein also announced over 250 Quran recitations at the Karbala shrine. Muslims in Russia welcomed Ramadan with a special iftar ceremony attended by diplomatic delegations from Islamic countries. Sheikh Ravil Gainutdin, the Mufti of Russia, emphasized the need for the Islamic Ummah to stand in solidarity with Palestine and Jerusalem during this holy month. He highlighted Russia's close political and cultural ties with Islamic nations as a factor supporting the Palestinian cause. Economically, experts noted that Ramadan is more than a religious occasion, serving as a significant season that influences consumer purchasing trends in Arab countries.
