Hojjat al-Islam Mirbagheri explains that the Imams' assumption of human form was the essential condition for the events of Karbala and a major divine test. He argues that Imam Hussein's acceptance of humanity allowed for confrontation with enemies and the separation of truth from falsehood, which would have been impossible otherwise. The tragedy is presented not merely as a battle, but as a spiritual process guiding humanity through suffering and patience in the face of divine trials. The speaker emphasizes that personal hardships, when embraced with love for Imam Hussein, can elevate individuals to high spiritual stations and secure the Imam's intercession. By describing the emotional farewell between the Imam and his companions, he highlights the necessity of readiness for sacrifice and steadfastness. Ultimately, walking the path of Imam Hussein's suffering leads to the spiritual rank of "true steps" and eternal companionship with the martyrs.
