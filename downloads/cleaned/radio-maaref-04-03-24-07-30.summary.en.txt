In this radio program, the importance of Quds Day and honoring martyrs was emphasized, highlighting the necessity of absolute obedience to leadership and avoiding division to maintain national unity and defeat enemies.
The core of the discussion focused on the destructive effects of arrogance based on Quranic verses, including denying truth, being deprived of divine attention, abandoning worship, and falling into oppression and sin.
Arrogance was identified as the greatest barrier (hijab) in the path to human perfection and worldly and otherworldly happiness, causing individuals to be deprived of God's guidance and love.
To remove this obstacle, the importance of shedding arrogance and embracing humility in religious conduct and personal development was stated to reopen the paths to divine guidance.
Finally, the program concluded by congratulating Quds Day, honoring nuclear and military martyrs, and emphasizing Iran's scientific achievements and resilience against foreign conspiracies.
