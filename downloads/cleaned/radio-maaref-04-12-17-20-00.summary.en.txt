In this radio program, a narration from the Prophet Muhammad is cited to introduce Ali (AS) as the most courageous and valiant individual in difficult missions.
The story recounts three celestial storms that occurred when Ali returned with water, during which angels greeted him.
The audience expresses their love and loyalty to the Ahl al-Bayt by reciting a panegyric dedicated to Ali (AS).
Subsequently, the tragedies of Fatima (SA) and Ali (AS) are mentioned, along with a call for retribution against their enemies and Jewish plots.
The program concludes with prayers for the reappearance of Imam Mahdi (AJ) to establish justice and defeat oppression.
Referencing the martyrdom of the leader and children, the listeners view the Imam's appearance as the only path to ultimate victory.
