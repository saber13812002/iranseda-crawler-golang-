The guest discusses his experience training in Kangang and returning to Iran after achieving success. He explains that his primary motivation for writing the first Persian Taekwondo book in 1971 was to create a reliable source to prevent educational errors in the sport. The book was published with a print run of three thousand copies at a price of 4,300 Rials due to the use of glossy paper. Initially, sales were challenging, with copies being returned to bookstores, but it eventually succeeded through direct sales and the help of a friend. The guest shares anecdotes about public interaction and the book's eventual reception. The segment concludes with a review of historical sporting events on this day, including Argentina's 1936 Olympic polo victory and Brazil's 1968 win over Argentina.
