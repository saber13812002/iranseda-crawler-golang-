The radio program begins by criticizing blind imitation of Western culture and emphasizes the importance of raising children according to Islamic values. It then addresses theological questions, clarifying that believers do visit each other in the Barzakh, and that the phrase "meet at the Day of Judgment" does not negate these interim encounters. The hosts also debunked astrological superstitions like the "moon in opposition," explaining that such days are not absolute causes of misfortune and can be mitigated by giving charity. Furthermore, it was stated that Imam Mahdi does not know the exact time of the Day of Judgment, and the Prophet's ontological priority transcends specific time and space. The segment concludes with historical accounts of the early stages of the Iran-Iraq war, highlighting the role of Martyr Gheyoor Aslali in training Basij forces in guerrilla tactics to defend Khuzestan.
