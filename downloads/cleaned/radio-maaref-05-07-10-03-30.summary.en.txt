The program began with Quranic recitation and morning prayers before narrating the story of a young man who proposed building a shop in the mosque courtyard. By covering half the construction costs, he established a sustainable income source that funded the mosque's cultural activities and met the neighborhood's needs. Following this narrative, the broadcast featured a hadith from Imam Jafar Sadiq regarding God's closeness to His servants and the recitation of the Mutawwileen supplication. The segment concluded with the announcement of prayer times in Qom and an introduction to a new Quranic recitation and translation section.
