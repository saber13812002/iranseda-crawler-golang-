This radio program begins by honoring the memory of Martyr Nader Mahdavi, who played a pivotal role in countering American forces in the Persian Gulf by establishing the Zolfaghar naval group in 1984. The main focus then shifts to the life story of Fatemeh Hajimirza, the mother of two brothers, Majid and Hossein Daljoon, who both became martyrs during the Iran-Iraq War. She recounts their revolutionary activities against the Pahlavi regime during their youth and the unwavering support of their parents for their path of sacrifice. The narrative details the tragic loss of Majid in Operation Khorramshahr and Hossein in the Halabja region in 1988. A poignant highlight of the story is Hossein's preemptive letter to his fiancée, in which he informed her that he would leave for the front lines on their wedding night. The mother shares touching memories of her sons' dedication, including an instance where Hossein wore her wet clothes in his final moments before martyrdom. The program concludes with prayers for the martyrs and an emphasis on the high status of martyrdom in Islamic culture.
