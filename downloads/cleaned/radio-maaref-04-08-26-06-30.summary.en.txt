The program begins with a Quranic interpretation regarding the nature of the soul, emphasizing that sleep and death are temporary returns of the spirit to God. It then marks the autumn morning of Aban 26 by commemorating the liberation of Susangard 45 years ago, highlighting the joint efforts of the army, IRGC, and civilians. Personal anecdotes about the bravery of soldiers and the people's role in defending the homeland are shared. The broadcast also recalls the deaths of Mirza Mohammad Hossein Naini, a prominent jurist, and Ibrahim Pardawood, the founder of Iranology, on this historical date. The opening of the Suez Canal is mentioned as a significant global event that occurred on this day. The program concludes with patriotic and religious poetry, inviting listeners to share their war memories via text message.
