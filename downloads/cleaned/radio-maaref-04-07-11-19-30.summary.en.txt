The program began with the recitation of the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya and a prayer for rain, followed by a discussion on the consequences of foul language and cursing. Hujjat al-Islam Fallah, citing religious narrations, identified the deprivation of answered prayers and the loss of companionship with the Ahl al-Bayt as major penalties for such behavior. He illustrated through stories involving Imam Sadiq (AS) and Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) that even minor insults can lead to exclusion from the presence of the Infallibles and regret on the Day of Judgment. A key point emphasized is that judging others by one's own religious standards while failing to adhere to them oneself is a grave sin. Cursing not only causes the speaker's own words to return to them but also hinders the attainment of spiritual ranks and divine love. Therefore, guarding one's tongue and avoiding false accusations is essential for maintaining a connection with God and the Ahl al-Bayt.
