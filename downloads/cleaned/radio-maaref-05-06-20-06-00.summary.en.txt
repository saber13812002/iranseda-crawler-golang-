This radio segment examines the theological issue of Imamate and the interpretation of the Quranic verse "O Messenger, proclaim that which has been revealed to you."
Sunni commentators, such as the author of Al-Manar, argue that this verse was revealed in Mecca during the early stages of prophethood to announce the principle of Tawhid.
They suggest its placement in Surah Al-Ma'idah serves as a reminder of the Prophet's initial struggles and divine protection.
However, numerous Shia narrations, which are considered a consensus, attribute this verse to the event of Ghadir Khumm and the announcement of Ali's Imamate.
According to jurisprudential rules, the multiple Shia narrations take precedence over the single, non-consensus report from Ibn Abbas, rendering the latter invalid.
The defense of the Meccan interpretation using a poetic analogy from Abu al-Najm is rejected due to a logical mismatch between a single obligation and two distinct concepts.
Ultimately, the Ghadir interpretation is deemed superior, as it is both narratively stronger and logically more coherent regarding the specific duty of announcing the Imamate.
