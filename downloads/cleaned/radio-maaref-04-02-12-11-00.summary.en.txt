The program began by honoring the memory of Martyr Morteza Motahari, highlighting his role in addressing doubts and preventing revolutionary deviations after the Islamic Revolution. The second segment introduced the first "Khadem Razavi" media content production event, focusing on Imam Reza and the resistance front, where participants created graphic and animation works. News of the registration opening for the 48th National Quran Competition by the Endowments Organization was also shared. The touching story of the mother of martyrs Davoud and Hamid Abdi was recounted, detailing her years of waiting for her son's remains in the desert until they were finally identified. The memory of Sardar Martyr Abdolhossein Baransi, a construction worker and commander of the 18th Jawad al-Amir Brigade, was also commemorated by the Workers' Basij. In the final segment, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the blessings of daughters and the affectionate behavior of clerics like Ayatollah Hujjat and Talagani towards their children. This section concluded by stressing the importance of daughters' education and the crucial role of mothers in raising the next generation.
