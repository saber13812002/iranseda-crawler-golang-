In this radio program, experts emphasized the importance of managing teenagers' pocket money by setting a logical budget to foster financial independence and prevent misuse. They also addressed parental concerns about children's lack of motivation for prayer, advising against excessive strictness and instead presenting prayer as a calming divine duty rather than a tool for worldly success. Regarding stuttering and misbehavior in children, the experts noted that emotional reactions and parental sensitivity can reinforce these behaviors, suggesting smart indifference or positive activity substitution as better coping strategies. The discussion also highlighted the necessity of adult presence in social environments and neighborhoods to indirectly monitor and reduce risky behaviors among adolescents. Finally, it was recommended that if behavioral or speech issues persist, consulting a professional counselor is essential before pursuing any other therapeutic interventions.
