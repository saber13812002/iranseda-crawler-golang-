This radio program from the Ma'aref network, featuring Mr. Hashanghi, discusses the religious rules governing marriage and contracts.
The first segment explained the identity verification process, specifically the requirement for couples and their parents to present their national IDs.
The show then reviewed special events scheduled for the martyrdom anniversary of Imam Reza in Mashhad.
A key highlight was the farewell ceremony for Sunni pilgrims from Qazvin province who are traveling to Karbala.
This event is expected to be attended by approximately 800 of these pilgrims currently staying in Mashhad.
Additional cultural and religious programs were also announced for Sunni visitors and the general public during this period.
The broadcast concluded with a religious song expressing longing for Imam Reza.
