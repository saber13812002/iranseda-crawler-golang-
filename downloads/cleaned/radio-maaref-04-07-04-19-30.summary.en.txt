The program began with a historical anecdote about a wrestling match between the arrogant young wrestler Mirza Mohammad Ali Azkooi and the elder, humble wrestler Mirza Abdolhossein Ali Shahi. The elder defeated the youth, illustrating the consequences of arrogance and the value of humility in the face of experience. The main segment, featuring Hujjat al-Islam Falahati, explored the Islamic jurisprudential and ethical stance on cursing and verbal abuse. Citing Quranic verses and Hadiths from the Ahl al-Bayt, the speaker emphasized that Islam strictly prohibits cursing anyone, including opponents and even Satan, as cursing Satan merely invites his retaliation and validates God's own curse. Imam Sadiq (AS) stated that insulting God's allies is equivalent to insulting God Himself, while insulting the Prophets is a grave sin. Imam Ali (AS) advised believers to respond to abuse with silence, patience, and forbearance, thereby earning God's pleasure and frustrating the enemy. The conclusion was that a true believer must avoid verbal abuse and resist Satan's provocations through silence and dignified restraint.
