In the first segment, Colonel Heshami from the Qom Traffic Police noted that women have fewer accidents than men due to their better adherence to traffic laws. He clarified that speed cameras have a two to three percent error rate, allowing citizens to appeal incorrect fines through administrative units. The police also emphasized the strict prohibition of motorcycles on highways and their ongoing efforts to curb motorcycle violations within the city. In the second segment, Professor Yafar discussed the moral and spiritual consequences of arrogance in the "Ganj-e Sa'adat" program. Citing religious narrations, he identified arrogance as a cause of intoxication, the waste of life, and the hardening of the heart. He warned that arrogance prevents individuals from accepting advice, diminishes their thinking capacity, and corrupts their actions. Finally, he expressed concern that if a person's current state is worse than their past, their ultimate afterlife is also at risk.
