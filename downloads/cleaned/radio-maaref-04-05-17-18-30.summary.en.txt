The program begins with two short stories emphasizing gratitude for simple blessings and the avoidance of injustice, even in minor transactions. In the second story, a caliph refuses to overpay for salt to prevent establishing a precedent of price gouging and societal oppression. Following this literary segment, the broadcast shifts to religious and social topics, announcing a specialized seminar for Quran reciters focused on creating an Iranian recitation style. Practical tips for saving water in toilet cisterns are also shared with the audience. The main focus of the program is covering the Arbaeen pilgrimage, featuring live reports from the Tamechin border and other crossing points. These reports detail the atmosphere among pilgrims, the efforts of volunteer hosts, and health recommendations for the walking journey. Finally, listeners are encouraged to participate in charity campaigns via text message and stay connected through the provided contact numbers.
