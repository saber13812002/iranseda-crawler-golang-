The program first addressed the rules of breastfeeding, clarifying that no legal effect of kinship arises if there is no certainty about the sufficient amount of milk consumed.
Regarding gifts purchased with haram (illicit) income, it was explained that while the transaction itself is valid and the recipient owns the item, directly using the illicit cash is prohibited.
The ruling on prayer for a couple visiting their original hometown was discussed; if they intend to stay or return, their prayers remain full, but their daughter residing in another city is considered a traveler there and prays in a shortened form.
If the family travels from their hometown to a village beyond the legal travel distance, all members' prayers become shortened during that journey.
The ruling on eating fruit from a neighbor's tree branch extending into one's yard was also covered, stating it is impermissible without the owner's confirmed consent.
This differs from trees in public paths, where limited picking is allowed to prevent waste.
The broadcast concluded by providing phone and SMS numbers for listeners to submit their religious questions.
