In this radio program, Dr. Javad Jafari refutes the claim that the Imam of the Time (AJ) resides in the Samarra cellar, asserting instead that he lives naturally among the people based on authentic narrations. Professor Mahmoud Abbasi examines the documents of the specific deputies, highlighting the Imam's supernatural knowledge of the financial contributions made by the Shia community. He emphasizes that the management of Khums through these deputies was crucial for maintaining the financial independence of the Shia from oppressive rulers. The Imam's awareness of the source of these funds allows him to bless the contributors with divine prayers, serving as a reward for their obedience to religious laws. In the final segment, Professor Kafil discusses the Quranic characteristics of the Imam's supporters, stressing the necessity of avoiding enemies of God and strengthening ties with the Wali al-Faqih. He identifies the Marja as the general deputy during the major occultation, essential for political and social guidance. The program concludes by suggesting practical ways to support the Imam, such as establishing educational centers with a Mahdavi approach to nurture the next generation of expectants.
