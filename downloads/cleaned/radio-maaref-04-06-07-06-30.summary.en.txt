This radio program explores the philosophical and theological relationship between infallibility (Ismat) and free will. The speaker argues that infallibility does not contradict free will but rather reinforces it. By analyzing voluntary actions, the discussion highlights that such acts are rooted in knowledge and will, with sins stemming from weaknesses in either. Consequently, an infallible person possesses a stronger and more complete free will due to superior knowledge and willpower. The program then shifts focus to the Quranic concept of tawassul, or seeking intercession through the Prophet and Imams. Citing a holy hadith, the speaker emphasizes that God fulfills the needs of believers through the virtue of the Prophet and Ali. The segment concludes with the narrative of Salman al-Farisi's martyrdom as a symbol of steadfast faith. This story illustrates how Salman, relying on divine prayer and patience, survived the persecution of Jews and hypocrites.
