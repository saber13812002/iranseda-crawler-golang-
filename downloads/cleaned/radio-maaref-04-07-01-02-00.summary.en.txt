The first segment of the program emphasized the importance of avoiding waste and conserving God's blessings, citing a narrative from Imam Ali ibn Musa al-Rida.
The late Imam Khomeini was highlighted as a model of extreme frugality, demonstrating strict caution in the consumption of electricity, water, and food.
In the second segment, expert Baqer Sabahi analyzed Imam Khomeini's philosophical thoughts regarding the School of Illumination and the works of Sheikh Suhrawardi.
While accepting the concept of God's "actual knowledge," the Imam critiqued certain aspects of Suhrawardi's theories and elevated them through the concept of "sacred grace."
A key point was the Imam's assertion that the "primacy of light" in Illumination philosophy is equivalent to the "primacy of existence," defending Suhrawardi against historical accusations.
Unlike the Peripatetic philosophers, the Imam used Illuminationist foundations to critique the theory of transmigration and examine the soul-body relationship more precisely.
These discussions reveal that Imam Khomeini not only utilized the foundations of Illumination philosophy but also charted a new path in Islamic wisdom by critiquing and completing them.
