In the radio program "Narrative of Love," Razi Sadat, the wife of martyr Mohammad Hossein Mirdousti, shared memories of their shared life. The couple, who were cousins, overcame a ten-year age gap and Razi's initial resistance due to Mohammad Hossein's persistent devotion. Their marriage was marked by economic hardships and frequent separations as the martyr served in various duties. Razi recounted the painful final moments before her husband's martyrdom in Syria, highlighting the emotional toll of their last phone calls. She also described her ongoing spiritual connection with her late husband, feeling his constant presence in her life. This narrative underscores the martyr's deep commitment to his faith and his wife's resilience in honoring his memory.
