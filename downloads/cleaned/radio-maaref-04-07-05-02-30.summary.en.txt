In this segment of the Quranic Q&A program, Professor Rostam Nejad addresses listener questions regarding the nature of Jinn and humans. He explains that although Jinn and humans are created from different elements, they are addressed together in the Quran due to their shared possession of intellect, will, and moral responsibility. Despite these similarities, humans are considered superior to Jinn because of their greater spiritual evolution and the existence of specific human prophets, while Jinn follow the laws revealed to humans. The discussion then shifts to Arabic grammar, clarifying the semantic difference between transitive and intransitive meanings in verb forms like "Falaha." The program subsequently explores the concept of life in non-human objects within the Quran, asserting that the universe possesses a form of consciousness and praise inherent to its nature. The professor cites verses about the earth and body parts testifying on Judgment Day to emphasize the innate awareness of non-human entities. Finally, he distinguishes between creative divine commands, such as "Be, and it is," and actual addresses to conscious entities, like the mountains accompanying Prophet David.
