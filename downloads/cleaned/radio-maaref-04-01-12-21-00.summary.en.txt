The radio program "Tales of Freedom" began with Nowruz greetings and safety tips for travelers.
The first segment featured clerics discussing the spiritual importance of remembering the Imam of the Age.
The main focus was Mehdi Nazari's account of his captivity during the 1982 Ramadan Operation in Basra.
Nazari described the initial breakthrough, the forced retreat, and his capture by Iraqi forces.
He recounted dangerous moments under tank fire, a near-fatal gunshot, and harsh treatment by enemy soldiers.
The book "Nordin, Son of Iran" was also introduced as a sincere narrative of the Sacred Defense.
The episode concluded with an announcement for the next part of his story and the daily prayer times.
