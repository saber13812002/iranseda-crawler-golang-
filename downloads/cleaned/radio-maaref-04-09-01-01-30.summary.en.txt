The radio program begins by outlining five characteristics of the Fatimid family based on the Quran, including keeping promises, fear of divine punishment, self-sacrifice in feeding others, sincerity in actions, and reverence for God.
The speaker emphasizes the importance of secret charity and avoiding hypocrisy in worship, citing stories from Imam Reza and the Prophet.
The second part of the program features the war memories of Sardar Nik-Sokhan from Operation Badr, describing the critical situation on the front lines and the martyrdom of commanders.
A key highlight is the unusual martyrdom of Hadiyeh Ziba, a student and Pasdaran member, who, despite being severely wounded and losing consciousness, calmly instructed her rescuers with profound peace.
This narrative illustrates the deep faith and spiritual presence of the martyrs, even in their final moments, which was deeply moving for those present at the battlefield.
The program concludes with prayers for Lady Zahra and a call to accelerate divine retribution against enemies, urging listeners to remember the values of faith and self-sacrifice.
