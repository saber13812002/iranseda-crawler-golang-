In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Fallah identifies stinginess as a dangerous disease with severe consequences based on religious narrations. The primary consequence is being cut off from God's "soulful mercy," which, unlike universal existence mercy, is earned through human choice and action. Imam Sadiq (AS) stated that anyone who fails to give charity, even from the smallest amount of wealth, is cursed and distant from divine mercy. Stinginess also leads to alienation from the Prophet (PBUH) and a lack of love from Imam Ali (AS), as a miser cannot have lasting friends. A key point is that the expectation from the religious leader is to give charity from any income, no matter how small, rather than only the wealthy giving. Ultimately, a stingy person distances themselves from the true essence of Islam, the Quran, and divine authority, resulting in a fundamental flaw in their faith.
