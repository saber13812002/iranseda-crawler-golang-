In this radio program, Professor Maleki emphasizes the importance of avoiding arrogance and corruption in this world while practicing piety to achieve a good afterlife, citing verse 83 of Surah Ash-Shura. He references a narration of the Prophet (PBUH) in a cemetery to highlight that piety is the only valuable provision for the hereafter. The discussion then shifts to the Treaty of Hudaybiyyah, which is characterized not as a defeat but as a test of faith and a crucial precursor to the great victory of the Conquest of Mecca. The professor explains that the Prophet (PBUH) did not act based on hidden knowledge, making these apparent events opportunities to test the believers' faith. Additionally, interpreting verses from Surah Luqman, he underscores the importance of respecting parents and adhering to monotheism as paths to wisdom and salvation. Finally, he stresses the necessity of Muslim unity and identifies the knowledge of God as the ultimate goal of all prophets and the key to resolving human issues.
