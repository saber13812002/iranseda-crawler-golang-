The program begins with an exegesis of the verse "Fala Allahi tawakkaltu" from Surah Yunus, defining tawakkul as entrusting affairs to God.
Citing Hājjem al-Shirāzi and modern tafsirs, the speaker clarifies that tawakkul does not mean abandoning action but rather utilizing natural causes alongside faith in God.
It is explained that God and natural causes operate longitudinally, not laterally, as God has embedded causality within nature.
The story of Prophet Moses is used to illustrate that true reliance involves trusting both God and His ordained means of treatment.
The discussion highlights that trust in God strengthens, rather than contradicts, individual and national self-confidence by providing a sense of divine support.
The broadcast then transitions to the "Zamzam Ahkam" segment, addressing legal questions regarding income for benevolent loan funds and rental agreements.
Fatwas from various maraji' are presented concerning the mahram status of children in remarriage, a woman's right to manage her own finances, and the obligation of Hajj using mahr.
Finally, the ruling on disposing of Quranic verses in flowing water and the limits of children's obedience to their parents in personal matters are discussed.
