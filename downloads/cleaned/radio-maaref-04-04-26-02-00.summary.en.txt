In the first segment, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the virtue of contentment and simplicity, citing the Prophet's (PBUH) and Ayatollah Tabrizi's lifestyles. He warned that excessive attachment to material luxury can distract individuals from their spiritual path. Subsequently, Zainab Sadat Hosseini, the wife of martyr Seyed Ahmad Hosseini, shared memories of his final days. She described his deep spiritual concern about proving his loyalty during the night of Tashu'a and his fear of causing distress to Lady Zainab (SA). Zainab recounted how she helped him write his will and bought new clothes for his journey to Syria. During their farewell, the martyr asked her to seek his intercession in the afterlife should he be martyred. The young wife spoke of her Zainab-like patience and the gradual realization of the magnitude of her husband's sacrifice.
