Lebanese citizens gathered at the site of Sayed Hassan Nasrallah's martyrdom to mark the first anniversary, reaffirming their commitment to the path of resistance. The Iranian Foreign Ministry condemned the assassination as a terrorist crime and a blatant violation of international law, demanding justice for the perpetrators. The Head of the Judiciary visited the Islamic Revolution and Sacred Defense Museum, emphasizing national unity and the importance of preserving the historical truth of Iran's defense. A virtual encyclopedia of the Sacred Defense and a memorial for pre-revolutionary political prisoners were also unveiled. Culturally, the book "Our Lady" received the Supreme Leader's appreciation, and 15,000 stationery packs were distributed to students in underprivileged areas of Mashhad. Reports highlighted the significant growth and export potential of Iran's domestic stationery industry. The program concluded with a segment answering various religious questions regarding laundry purification, Arbaeen vows, hair covering for children, and marriage contracts.
