The program begins by highlighting the Prophet Muhammad's (PBUH) humility and modesty within his home, where he behaved like an ordinary human being, performed his own chores, and never acted arrogantly toward his family or servants.
Subsequently, Hujjat al-Islam Falah Bastant discusses the vice of cursing and swearing as a linguistic harm, defining it as an extremely ugly act rooted in disbelief, polytheism, and hypocrisy.
Citing Quranic verses and narrations, he describes swearing as following Satan's footsteps and a form of transgression that indicates ignorance and lowliness.
Imam Ali (AS) also identifies swearing as a sign of partnership with Satan and a disregard for honor and sanctity.
Finally, the speaker emphasizes that any form of verbal abuse or insult constitutes a departure from divine authority and servitude to Satan, urging listeners to avoid these linguistic vices.
