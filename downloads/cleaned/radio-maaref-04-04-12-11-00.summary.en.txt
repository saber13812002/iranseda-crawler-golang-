The commemoration of the sixth night of Muharram was held at Imam Khomeini's Hosseiniyeh with significant attendance from the public and political leaders. Hojjat-ol-Islam Eslani described Imam Hussein's jihad as a symbol of the war of hope against despair and the will of the people. The Speaker of the Parliament emphasized that the Zionist regime has no lasting power against Iran without direct American support. General Mosavi also stressed the resilience of the armed forces and the direct command of the Supreme Leader during crises. Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi, in a message, characterized Western human rights as a tool for advancing illicit interests and hegemony. The memorial for the soldiers shot down in the 1988 Iranian airliner incident was observed by condemning America's unconditional support for Israel. In another segment, a religious expert advised recognizing psychological barriers to prayer as Satan's whispers and overcoming them with strong will.
