The radio program "Imam Reza" begins by inviting listeners to send text messages expressing their devotion to Imam Reza (PBUH).
The broadcast creates a spiritual atmosphere through the playback of religious songs and recitations.
The main discussion highlights the fundamental difference between Islam and other worldly laws regarding human development.
Unlike restrictive laws, Islam starts preparing individuals for healthy family life even before marriage.
The host uses an agricultural metaphor to describe this process, from preparing the soil to harvesting the crop.
The ultimate goal of Islam is to build a person who remains consistent in character, whether in private or public settings.
In contrast, the text argues that certain misconceptions of freedom lead humans toward savagery and harming others.
Islam aims to eliminate malicious behaviors to foster individual ethics and create a dynamic, virtuous society.
