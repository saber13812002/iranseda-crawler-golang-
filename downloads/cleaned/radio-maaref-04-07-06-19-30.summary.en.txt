The program began with the recitation of a rain prayer from the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya before transitioning to a discussion on the prohibition of cursing.
The instructor cited narrations to classify cursing nature, time, or even enemies as a major sin and a form of self-harm.
It was emphasized that Shia Muslims must follow Imam Ali's command to avoid cursing, even during the Battle of Siffin.
Cursing natural elements like wind or mountains is considered an insult to God's creation, with negative consequences returning to the speaker.
The primary remedy for this sin is explicit repentance and a firm commitment to never repeat the behavior.
Imam Sadiq (AS) instructed Sam'ah to seek forgiveness from God after cursing his worker and to refrain from such actions in the future.
Finally, listening to these narrations in family gatherings was deemed essential for improving the language and conduct of believers.
