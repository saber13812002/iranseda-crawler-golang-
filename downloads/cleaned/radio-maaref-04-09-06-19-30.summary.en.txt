In this radio program, Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallah discusses verbal vices, focusing specifically on flattery and sycophancy. Citing the Holy Quran, he emphasizes that God only praises devoted servants who are sincere in their repentance. According to narrations from Imam Ali (AS), praising someone beyond their merit is flattery, while withholding praise due to envy is a moral failing. The best praise is that which comes from God and the Ahl al-Bayt, whereas the worst is the admiration of the wicked and Satan. The professor warns that if enemies or corrupt individuals praise us, we must question our path and be vigilant against deviation. Flattery and the misuse of titles act as significant barriers to true servitude and the strengthening of moral virtues. Finally, he urges listeners to resist undue flattery and find contentment solely in the pleasure of God and the Ahl al-Bayt.
