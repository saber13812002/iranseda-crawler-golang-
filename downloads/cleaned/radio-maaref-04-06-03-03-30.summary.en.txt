The program clarified that while Zakat al-Fitr cannot be paid to Sayyids, other obligatory alms such as returning usurped rights are permissible. According to Ayatollah Javadi Amoli's fatwa, holding a baby with impure clothing during prayer only invalidates the prayer if it causes excessive movement, not merely due to the impurity. In the spiritual segment, Hujjat al-Islam Razani emphasized the importance of patience in obedience and abstention from sin, citing a narration from Imam Sadiq (AS). He explained that enduring the brief hardships of this world leads to unlimited spiritual perfection in the hereafter. This perspective encourages a lifestyle based on mindfulness and existential responsibility. The broadcast concluded with the recitation of verses from Surah Fatir and Surah Shura, reminding listeners of divine unity and the Day of Judgment.
