Dr. Mohammad Zademehr, a former footballer and dentist, shares his memories of playing for Persepolis and the national youth team in this interview. He recalls key moments such as crucial goals and winning the Asian Championship with the youth team under coach Mehdi Majarani. Zademehr emphasizes that the main difference between Iranian and European football lies in culture and lifestyle, rather than just physical training. He also discusses his choice of dentistry after retiring from football and how this profession has impacted his life. The sports doctor mentions creating the first night guard for Iranian athletes and his efforts to register it under the country's name. Finally, he talks about his continued involvement in football with veteran players and enjoying nature and family during his post-football retirement.
