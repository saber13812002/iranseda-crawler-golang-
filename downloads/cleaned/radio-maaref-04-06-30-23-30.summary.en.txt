In this radio program, the expert discusses the pressure families exert regarding marriage and offers strategies to manage it. He emphasizes that understanding parents' concerns and viewing the issue from their perspective is the first step to reducing tension. Self-awareness and emotional management are essential for addressing internal stress stemming from fears of marriage or failure. For external interactions, it is recommended to engage in empathetic dialogue with close relatives while firmly and politely maintaining privacy boundaries with distant acquaintances. In the final segment, the importance of internal change and avoiding the dangerous phrase "I am who I am" for personal growth and relationship improvement was highlighted. Change and relationship improvement must originate from the individual, not from a spouse or others. This approach helps prevent the repetition of mistakes and psychological harm. Finally, listeners were invited to recite a specific prayer and listen to religious songs.
