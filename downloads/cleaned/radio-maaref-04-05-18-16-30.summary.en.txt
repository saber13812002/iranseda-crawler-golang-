This radio program covered diverse topics, starting with the Quranic concept of "Dabt al-Arzi" as a sign distinguishing truth from falsehood during the return of the Imams. The discussion then shifted to the approaching Arbaeen, emphasizing the need to emulate the martyrs through ethical behavior and prayer rather than mere emotional attachment. The core segment focused on "independence and the rejection of humiliation," analyzed from religious, political, and social perspectives. A guest scholar highlighted that true dignity is found only in devotion to God and refusing to submit to oppressors, citing the legacy of the Ahl al-Bayt. A field report defined national independence as achieving economic, political, and social self-sufficiency while preventing foreign interference. The program concluded by asserting that individual and national independence are fundamental to preserving identity and fostering progress in an Islamic society.
