This radio program examined the jurisprudential and legal differences between "Moharebeh" and "Baghy."
The punishments for Moharebeh and the conditions for collective and organized rebellion were explained.
The discussion addressed whether two individuals can be considered guilty of Baghy.
It was argued that due to the principle of interpreting in favor of the accused, a rebellion by two people does not qualify as Baghy.
The conditions for applying the death penalty to members of a group engaging in armed rebellion were analyzed.
Legal distinctions between the Islamic Penal Code and previous laws regarding these crimes were clarified.
The final segment featured the recitation and translation of verses from Surah Dukhan.
Additionally, tips on saving energy and water were provided.
