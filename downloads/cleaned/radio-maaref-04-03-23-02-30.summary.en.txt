In a narrative from his youth, Latif and his brother successfully trapped a fox that had been attacking the village's poultry. After confining the animal in a wooden cage, Seyyed Aghil, the mosque's caretaker, urged Latif to release it based on the religious wisdom of always leaving an escape route for living creatures. Latif realized that the fox needed to warn its kin and opened the cage, allowing it to flee toward the hills. This personal experience served as the inspiration for the core message of the radio program "Sobh-e Niaz," which emphasizes mercy and ethical conduct toward all living beings. The broadcast subsequently concluded with the recitation of verses from Surah Al-Isra and morning supplications.
