The radio program "Tales of Freedom" emphasizes the importance of artistically documenting the experiences of Iranian POWs to honor their resilience against Ba'athist torture. Amir Eskari, a POW from Shalamcheh, recounts the details of his capture, harsh interrogations, and the martyrdom of two comrades during their transfer to Baghdad. Another narrative describes a frontline medic's self-sacrifice in treating wounded soldiers using his own clothing, amidst the martyrdom of a brigade commander. Hojjat-ol-Islam Nadem describes the freedmen as the freest souls of their era, whose faith remained unbroken despite the isolation and darkness of solitary confinement. The program also addresses the critical issue of public livelihood, advocating for the stabilization of basic goods prices using existing electronic card infrastructure. Officials highlight the readiness of retail chains to meet public needs and the necessity of managing import risks to prevent price fluctuations. The broadcast concludes by inviting listeners to share their stories and support the oppressed people of Gaza.
