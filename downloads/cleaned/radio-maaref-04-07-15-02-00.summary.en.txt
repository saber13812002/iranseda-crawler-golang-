The radio program "Hekayat-e Azadegan" began with the recollections of Major General Mohammad Hossein Ansari from his time as a prisoner of war. Ansari described the self-sacrificing behavior of fellow prisoners who refused to witness each other's torture and shared their limited food. He also detailed the severe torture inflicted by the Iraqi Security Organization and its devastating physical and psychological consequences, including madness and paralysis. The second segment featured the narrative of Hojjat al-Islam Nader Ravi, who emphasized how captivity became an opportunity for spiritual and intellectual growth. He highlighted the transformation of illiterate prisoners into Quran memorizers and educated individuals as a model for investing in life. Subsequently, hadiths from the book of Kafi were recited, focusing on the piety and contentment of the faithful with worldly matters. These narrations stressed that believers take little from the world but offer extensive help to others, even reminding them of forgotten requests. The program concluded with a prayer for rain and an expression of solidarity with the people of Gaza.
