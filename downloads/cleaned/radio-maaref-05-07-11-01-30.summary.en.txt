Hojjat al-Islam Madani Bojestani emphasizes the fundamental importance of the family as the core of social issues in this radio program, criticizing society's neglect of the root causes of family crises. Citing global and domestic statistics, he identifies the decline in family formation, the rising age of marriage, and youth reluctance to have children as the first major crisis. The speaker attributes these trends to a combination of economic and cultural factors, with a particular focus on the cultural shift from viewing marriage as a social and religious duty to a personal preference. He argues that modern media has reduced marriage to an individual choice, a perception that must be corrected by reviving religious values through media and preaching. Madani Bojestani also highlights the religious and social obligation of acting as a matchmaker, citing narrations that promise great rewards for facilitating marriages and divine wrath for those who cause family discord. Ultimately, he calls for practical action by media, religious platforms, and families to restore the sacred status of marriage and address the resulting societal crises.
