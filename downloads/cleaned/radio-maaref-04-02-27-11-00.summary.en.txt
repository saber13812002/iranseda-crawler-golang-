This news segment covered the meeting between teachers and the Supreme Leader, alongside the President's emphasis on military independence and condemnation of global silence regarding the Gaza massacre. The historic trip of Ayatollah Javadi Amoli to Iraq and his significant meetings with Ayatollah Sistani and other scholars in Najaf were reported as steps to strengthen the unity of the Qom and Najf seminaries. Other news included the launch of a new "Religious Studies and Cyberspace" field in the seminaries and specialized sessions held at the Tehran Book Fair. The publication of a book about the Supreme Leader's father, titled "The Story of the Master," and the international conference on the thoughts of Allameh Bahabadi Ghezdi were also highlighted. In the religious section, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the importance of keeping hearts open to divine guidance, citing stories of how words can transform individuals. Finally, a discussion with Hojjat al-Islam Tootbavi addressed the rulings of Hajj, specifically regarding the stoning of the pillars and the permissibility of using umbrellas in Mina to protect against the heat.
