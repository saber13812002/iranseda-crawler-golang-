In this radio program, the Ghadir Khutbah was used to emphasize the divine nature of Imam Ali's (PBUH) authority, distinguishing it from a mere human appointment.
A key point highlighted was the continuation of the Imamate through Ali's (PBUH) descendants until the Day of Judgment, signifying the institution's eternity.
The guest introduced Ghadir as the foundation of knowledge and religion, as well as a source of light and spiritual peace, while reciting profound poetry.
The discussion focused on the importance of visiting the holy shrines in Najaf and Karbala, particularly the spiritual experience of the between-the-shrines route on Friday nights.
The guests expressed their deep longing for pilgrimage, describing how the presence in these sacred places brings healing and spiritual refreshment to the soul.
The program concluded with recitations of visitation prayers and salutations, urging listeners to honor this significant day through participation in rituals and good deeds.
Additionally, special service offers with discounts were mentioned to celebrate the occasion, alongside calls for community solidarity and neighborly kindness.
