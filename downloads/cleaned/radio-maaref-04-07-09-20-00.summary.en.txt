The radio program began by reporting on the celebration of Imam Hassan Askari's birthday and the "Mercy to the World" conference in Pakistan, which emphasized following the Prophet's path and condemned the Zionist regime. International news covered the appointment of Thailand's first Muslim female culture minister, rising Islamophobia in Germany and France, and Spain's ban on advertising Israeli goods. Political updates included the UK government's internal defeat in a vote affirming the UN's genocide finding regarding Gaza and the approach of the "Flotilla of the Sumud" to break the blockade. In the analytical segment, Mohsen Mehdiyan interpreted the Supreme Leader's remarks, arguing that negotiation is merely a tool and that continuing the current path without fundamental changes is illogical given the West's history of betrayal. He stressed that the leadership guides the society through educational reasoning and deep understanding of national necessities, rather than acting under external pressure.
