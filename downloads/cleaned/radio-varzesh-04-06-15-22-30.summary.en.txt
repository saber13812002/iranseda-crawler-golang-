Ashoor Golmohammadi, a former basketball player and coach, shared his memories of playing for the Homa and Islamic Azad University teams during this interview. He highlighted the success of the Azad University team, including their championship in the international Dehfor tournament and the domestic league, crediting Dr. Goudami for the development of university sports. Golmohammadi also discussed his coaching career at clubs like Paykan and Shimidour, as well as his international experience in Iraq. He expressed gratitude for the moral and financial support from colleagues and his second wife following the passing of his first wife, which helped him return to professional success. He continues to coach Paykan's corporate and labor teams, emphasizing the importance of strong team spirit and human relationships in sports. Golmohammadi described the golden generation of Iranian players as unique and expressed concern about the lack of players at the level of Hamed Haddadi in the future. He concluded by stressing the importance of supporting sports legends and continuing cultural and media activities in the field.
