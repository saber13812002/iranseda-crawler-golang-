In this program, Hossein Shariatmadari argues that the primary objective of the military attack was to change Iran's regime, citing statements by Trump and Netanyahu. He points to the enemy's failure to achieve this goal and the global backlash against the Zionist regime. Shariatmadari characterizes negotiations with the US under current conditions as a psychological operation designed to impose the enemy's demands. He asserts that the ceiling for negotiation has been pre-determined by Washington, requiring capitulation on enrichment and missile programs. Therefore, accepting the enemy's terms in the name of preserving victory is a sign of weakness and a deviation from the revolutionary path. He emphasizes that national cohesion and being armed are the essential keys to maintaining Iran's victory and authority.
