This radio program began by honoring the martyrs of the Sacred Defense and then featured recollections from commanders and fighters regarding the early days of the war. Ayatollah Khamenei discussed the enemy's attempts to capture Abadan and the brave resistance of fighters in Abadan Island during Operation Karbala 5. He also shared a memory of Martyr Sadegh Abdolazadeh, a young merchant who went to the front with calmness and trust in God. Colonel Hafezi, an air force pilot, recounted the first days of the war and the crash of an aircraft at Hamedan base, where the pilot was martyred while his wife was still at the base. The final segment included reports on the sacrifices of workers in Tehran's fruit and vegetable markets who were injured during drone attacks. These narratives emphasized the national will, self-confidence, and the unranked sacrifices that occurred during the imposed war.
