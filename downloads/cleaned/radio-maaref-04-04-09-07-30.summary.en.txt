The radio program began with religious music and condolences for the month of Muharram, setting a spiritual tone. First, Hujjat al-Islam Babanajad interpreted the slogan "Death to America" based on Imam Khomeini's teachings as a manifestation of Tawhid rather than mere political opposition. He argued that aligning with non-divine powers is a betrayal of God-centricity, positioning the slogan as a continuation of the prophets' path. In the second segment, Hujjat al-Islam Falahati re-examined the concept of "Infaq" (charity) from a Quranic and mystical perspective. He explained that the perfection of lower existential levels, from minerals to animals, lies in serving humanity. Finally, he concluded that the highest form of Infaq is "self-sacrifice," where humans must expend their lower existential traits, such as animal instincts, in the path of God's servitude and obedience to the Ahl al-Bayt to achieve human perfection.
