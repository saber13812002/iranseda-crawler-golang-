In this radio program, Dr. Mohammad Javad Fallah explored the spiritual and ethical dimensions of the Arbaeen pilgrimage. He explained that participating in this journey increases the likelihood of prayers being answered due to the creation of inner humility and the remembrance of God. From an individual ethical perspective, emulating Imam Hussein (PBUH) strengthens self-esteem, courage, and mental openness among pilgrims. This broad-mindedness leads to forgiving others' mistakes and reducing social tensions within the massive crowds present on the route. In the realm of social ethics, virtues such as trustworthiness, cooperation, empathy, and respecting boundaries are prominently observed. This moral and spiritual transformation highlights the civilizational dimensions of the Ashura uprising in the lives of Muslims. Subsequently, recitations and translations of verses from Surah Zumar and Surah Ghafir were broadcast.
