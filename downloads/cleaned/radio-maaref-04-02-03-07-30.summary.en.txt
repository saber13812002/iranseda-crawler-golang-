In the first segment of the program, Engineer Naimi emphasized the importance of preserving family privacy in architectural design, referencing the book "Modesty and Architecture." He argued that modern glass architecture violates Islamic privacy norms, unlike traditional designs that integrated religious values into daily life. Naimi illustrated how historical homes were structured to maintain modesty, such as placing kitchens away from public view. Subsequently, Hojjat al-Islam Fallah introduced a new topic on "Anger," categorizing it into divine wrath and ego-driven anger. He identified anger stemming from the ego and Satan as a vice and a veil that obstructs the heart from divine mercy. Citing Quranic verses, Fallah explained that unwarranted anger distances individuals from God's grace. Finally, he stressed the necessity of understanding the causes of anger and replacing it with patience and compassion in both personal and social life.
