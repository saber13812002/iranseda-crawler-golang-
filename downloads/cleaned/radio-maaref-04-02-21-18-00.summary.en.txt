In the radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat," Professor Hujjat al-Islam Fallah discussed the moral vice of anger. Relying on Quranic verses, he explained divine wrath through worldly and otherworldly punishments, emphasizing that these warnings are real reflections of human actions and intentions rather than mere symbols. Citing hadiths, the professor classified anger as a soldier of ignorance and mercy as a soldier of compassion, stressing the necessity of self-discipline to control this vice. He warned that a society where anger and aggression replace logical dialogue is dangerous and requires social and psychological intervention. The main challenge lies in an individual's ability to be their own opponent, managing internal impulses with patience and reflection instead of reacting with emotion. The segment concluded by highlighting the importance of "enjoining good and forbidding evil," noting the courage required to stand against wrongdoing even in the face of potential danger.
