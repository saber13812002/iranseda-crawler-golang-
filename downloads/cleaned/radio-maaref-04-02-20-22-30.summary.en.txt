The program begins by highlighting Sheikh Abdolkarim Haeri's deep concern for Iran's political and ideological stability, which led him to refuse returning to the country. He emphasized the importance of supporting domestic products to prevent local unemployment, a principle he strictly followed in his personal life and family. His extensive social contributions, such as building hospitals and libraries, along with his moral asceticism, including caring for his blind wife, demonstrated his profound commitment to the people. The story of his support for Dr. Qarib in choosing medicine over seminary studies illustrates his realistic and forward-looking perspective on societal needs. In the second segment, Hujjat al-Islam Najafi Zanjan discusses the structured supplications of Imam Ali (PBUH). He outlines four essential steps for complete worship: knowing the Creator, feeling a sense of need, loving the Creator, and finally engaging in prayer. The program concludes with musical pieces and an invitation for listeners to share their favorite supplications via text message.
