In the "Fiqh Payva" program, experts Hamidreza Jafari and Qasem Babaei discussed the philosophy of political jurisprudence.
They defined political jurisprudence as a second-order perspective on the relationship between humans and sovereignty.
The methodological and teleological differences between political jurisprudence and other legal branches were analyzed.
The practical challenges of dowry payment and the impact of interpretive methods on fatwas were examined.
Views by Ayatollah Sistani and Makarem Shirazi regarding dowry and consequentialism in legal rulings were presented.
Subsequently, a dramatic translation of verses from Surah An-Nisa and Al-Ma'idah was broadcast.
These verses emphasized monotheism, avoiding extremism in religion, and the rules of inheritance and Hajj sanctity.
The program concluded with a call for production investment and the playing of a musical piece.
