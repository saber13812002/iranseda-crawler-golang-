The Supreme Leader congratulated the national Greco-Roman wrestling team on their world championship victory, praising the athletes and coaches for bringing joy to the nation and honor to the country. This achievement marks the first time that Iran's free-style and Greco-Roman wrestling teams have simultaneously won world titles in the same competition cycle.
