In this radio program, the Arbaeen walk is introduced as the supreme act of worship and a primary source of peace and faith reinforcement. The speaker emphasizes that love for the Ahlulbayt, particularly Imam Hussein (PBUH), creates psychological security and opens the human mind. Although participation in Arbaeen is driven by joy and love, the fear of being deprived of this great blessing on Judgment Day must also be considered. Imam Sadiq (PBUH) stressed the necessity of visiting Karbala annually for the poor and twice a year for the wealthy. For those unable to attend physically, sincere intention and financial or logistical support for pilgrims are recommended. The "Samte Khoda" program facilitates participation in sending youth and supporting camps through specific SMS codes. Finally, mind management is suggested through mental alignment with Imam Zaman (AJ) and reciting specific supplications like the Dua of Kumayl.
