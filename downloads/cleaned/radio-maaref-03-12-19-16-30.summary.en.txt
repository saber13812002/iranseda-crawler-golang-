In the radio program "Persoman Family," two family challenges were discussed with practical solutions provided. For the first case, the counselor advised addressing financial misunderstandings and trust issues with the son-in-law through transparent dialogue, sincere apologies, and using technology to maintain emotional bonds despite distance. In the second case, a woman worried about her husband's lack of trust after a past mistake was guided to first forgive herself and then address the issue through open communication. The counselor emphasized that this lack of trust likely stems from the woman's own guilt and can be resolved through clarity and letting go. Finally, Professor Reza Yousefi, referencing the Quran, encouraged spouses to foster a close relationship through free and honest conversation. He explained that being "each other's garment" means expressing feelings and thoughts without secrecy or holding words in. This approach helps fill emotional gaps and creates a more dynamic and peaceful home environment.
