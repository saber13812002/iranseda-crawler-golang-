The program begins by criticizing laziness and lethargy, citing hadiths and the lives of great scholars to emphasize the necessity of diligence for achieving worldly and otherworldly goals.
It then transitions to the weekly story, which follows Major Ranjbari, the new commander of the Veliabad base, as he arrives after a long journey from Baneh.
Witnessing the people's deprivation and recalling the injustices of the previous regime, he decides to prioritize friendship with the locals over a purely military approach to ensure lasting security.
His young adjutant, who fully trusts him, inquires about his plans, and the Major emphasizes the need to clear the roads of mines planted by the Christian Revolution.
While watching war footage on television and reminiscing about his childhood, the Major contemplates practical solutions to reduce casualties and improve the region's situation.
The narrative highlights the commander's shift in perspective from violence to diplomacy and service to the people.
Ultimately, the story underscores that effort and determination are the keys to success in both military and social spheres.
