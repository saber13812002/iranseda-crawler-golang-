Following a missile attack by the Zionist regime on Iran that resulted in the martyrdom of several commanders and scientists, the IRGC launched Operation "Sacred Promise 3" targeting Israeli military positions. The Supreme Leader condemned the enemy's action as a major mistake and emphasized the armed forces' firm resolve to counter any aggression. Military officials reported the effective impact of dozens of ballistic missiles on strategic targets and the degradation of the enemy's air defense systems. Supreme clerics also condemned the crime and stressed the necessity of maintaining national unity and avoiding division during these sensitive times. Coinciding with the festival of Ghadir Khum, national ceremonies were held in Tehran and other cities under the slogan "Iran is the Zulfikar of Ali." The public demonstrated widespread support for the armed forces and expressed satisfaction with the retaliatory operation's success.
