In this program, Professor Fatemi emphasizes the correct understanding of the Ahl al-Bayt and God's saints, citing the Ja'fariyya Ziyara to explain that their knowledge of the unseen is granted solely by God. He criticizes common people who complain about unfulfilled worldly requests after visiting shrines, distinguishing between seeking material and spiritual intercession. The professor stresses that spiritual purity and refraining from anger and backbiting are prerequisites for receiving divine mercy and the intercession of the Imams. The second part of the program features the memories of Sardar Zolqadr from the Karbala 8 operation, describing intense resistance against Iraqi tanks and martyrdom scenes reminiscent of Karbala. A particularly moving anecdote involves finding a martyr's notebook where he had recorded his date of martyrdom twenty days before it actually occurred. These narratives highlight the soldiers' firm faith in martyrdom and the spiritual presence of the Imams on the battlefield.
