In the radio program "Persaman Enghelab," Dr. Lakzai addressed key political and security challenges facing Iran. Responding to questions about the lack of prosecution for corrupt officials, he highlighted the judiciary's weakness in media communication, noting that while many cases are investigated, the final verdicts are often not properly publicized. The discussion then shifted to America's influence in international organizations and the global financial system. Lakzai analyzed the decline of US power and its rising debt, predicting that the dollar will gradually be removed from global trade cycles. Finally, the issue of internet filtering was framed as part of "cyberspace governance." By comparing the virtual space to geographical borders, he emphasized the necessity of controlling this domain to prevent enemy infiltration and ensure citizen security, while criticizing the current filtering methods for their ineffectiveness.
