In this radio program, several jurisprudential questions were answered based on the fatwas of Islamic scholars. Hujjat al-Islam Mahmudi, citing Ayatollah Sistani, stated that lying about having a job to purchase a card reader is haram and impermissible. Additionally, Hujjat al-Islam Alian, referencing Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi, confirmed that a wife's refusal to share a bed with her husband due to night prayers is jurisprudentially acceptable. Regarding the sale of loan quotas, Hujjat al-Islam Jawaheri explained that if the bank does not sign a new contract with the buyer, the sale is not Sharia-compliant. Finally, Hujjat al-Islam Badini, citing Ayatollah Javadi Amoli, ruled that a single ghusl performed with the intention of covering both janabah and hayd is sufficient. This ghusl also replaces wudu, so no separate ablution is required. Listeners were encouraged to send their questions via the provided phone numbers or the Radio Ma'aref website.
