This radio program discussed the psychological impact of renting on family stability and the resulting irritability in spouses. The expert explained that a wife's anger often stems from a deep sense of insecurity and the inability to provide a safe nest for her children, rather than just financial stress. Recommended solutions include relaxation techniques, empathy from husbands instead of self-defense, and creating a realistic plan for homeownership. Allowing small personalizations in the rented space was also suggested to enhance the sense of belonging. The second part of the show addressed children's distractibility and lack of focus. Key causes identified include excessive digital media consumption, insufficient sleep, anxiety from frequent parental commands, and perfectionism. The expert emphasized the importance of a quiet study environment, a protein-rich breakfast, and praising the child's effort rather than their intelligence. Finally, consulting a child psychologist was advised if these strategies do not yield improvement.
