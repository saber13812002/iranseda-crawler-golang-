This radio program begins by introducing the Iran Sound website and emphasizing the divine call to a straight path.
The guest, Professor Faghihi, a prominent researcher and student of Imam Khomeini, discusses the concept of monotheistic ethics.
He describes this ethics as a process that refines human behavior by establishing a deep connection with God.
The ultimate goal of this spiritual journey is to reach a state where one feels and perceives God with their entire being.
Professor Faghihi highlights the crucial role of prayer and supplication in facilitating this spiritual connection and moral growth.
Listeners are invited to submit their questions regarding monotheistic ethics via the provided phone and SMS numbers.
The core argument is that knowing God through ethics is the fundamental basis for correcting both the inner self and outward actions.
The discussion focuses on the importance of linking monotheistic beliefs with ethical practices in daily life.
