The program begins with a tafsir of the opening verses of Surah Al-An'am by Ayatollah Javadi Amoli, emphasizing the concept of "Shi'a" as adopting the color of its environment. In contrast, the Divine Essence is present everywhere with a strong universality but does not take on the color of any entity, remaining free from attributes that imply possibility. The commentary addresses the verse "He knows your secrets and what you reveal," urging humans to reflect on the soul's sky and the body's earth regarding their eschatological responsibilities. Referring to Surah Al-Baqarah, the speaker highlights God's absolute knowledge of hidden and manifest matters and the necessity of final accountability. The role of Surah Al-An'am as a surah of argumentation is noted, where the repeated command "Say" encourages the audience to engage in rational and scriptural reasoning. Arguments for proving prophethood through the necessity of resurrection and a guide are presented, distinguishing between ordinary signs and miracles in establishing monotheism and prophethy. Finally, citing Surah Saba, the speaker warns that previous powerful civilizations were destroyed, urging people to avoid arrogance and denial of divine laws.
