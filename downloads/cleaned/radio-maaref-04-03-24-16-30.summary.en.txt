In this radio program, the host congratulated listeners on the occasion of Eid al-Ghadir while addressing Quranic questions.
The first discussion focused on Surah Al-Falaq and An-Nas, explaining that the seeking of refuge in An-Nas involves three names of God.
It was emphasized that the danger of "whispering Satan" is more severe than physical threats due to its hidden and internal nature.
Reciting these surahs with a deep sense of spiritual refuge is recommended to overcome such internal temptations and psychological issues.
The second question concerned verses 41 and 42 of Surah Az-Zukhruf regarding God's promise of retribution against the disbelievers.
Citing verse 46 of Surah Yunus, the scholar explained that while some retribution has occurred in this world, the ultimate judgment is reserved for the Day of Judgment.
It was highlighted that Imam Ali (AS) will execute the final retribution against oppressors on the Day of Judgment as the ultimate authority.
The program concluded by affirming that tyranny and oppressors will never achieve lasting success and are destined for ultimate defeat.
