The Israeli regime's attack on a journalist shelter in Gaza resulted in the martyrdom of seven media activists, including Al Jazeera's Anas al-Sharif. This atrocity, which sparked global outrage, occurred after more than 235 journalists had already been killed in Gaza. At a UN Security Council meeting, details of Israel's plan to fully occupy and clear Gaza by the end of this year were revealed. The US vetoed any practical action by the Council, while Russia and China strongly opposed the implementation of this plan. Australia and New Zealand announced that they recognize Palestine as an independent state. Meanwhile, the Global Sumud Flotilla is moving to break the Gaza blockade and deliver humanitarian aid. Ali Larijani, Iran's National Security Council Secretary, signed a security memorandum during his visit to Iraq. Finally, Scottish police arrested a neo-Nazi teenager who intended to set fire to a mosque.
