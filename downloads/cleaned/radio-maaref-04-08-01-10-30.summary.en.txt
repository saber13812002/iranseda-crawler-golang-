The program began by introducing the "Nafas" festival and emphasizing the importance of kindness within families. The "Shafak" segment explored Islamic traditional medicine, highlighting the necessity of managing sleep habits during autumn to maintain health and counteract the season's cold and dryness. Subsequently, Hujjat al-Islam Rasoul Hafez discussed the Quranic imperative to avoid negative assumptions and unfair judgments about others. He provided practical examples and recommended strategies such as optimism and fact-checking to combat suspicion. The latter part of the broadcast addressed the 2025 national slogan of "Investment for Production," stressing the need for public participation in the economy. It also emphasized national unity and the defense of Islam against external threats. The program concluded with a call to preserve peace, strengthen social bonds, and rely on spiritual values.
