In this radio program, the analyst highlights the opportunity for strengthening national unity and increasing public resentment towards the US, following the parliament's approval of a law to exit nuclear treaties like the MPT. He argues that the US blocks Iran's access to peaceful nuclear energy under the pretext of military and economic concerns to prevent Iran's self-reliance. The primary strategy to deter future attacks is identified as demonstrating power on military, national, and political levels, where public unity and the firm stance of officials play a crucial role. Since Iran has already demonstrated its willingness to negotiate, the focus should now shift to projecting a message of authority and strength to the world. The program concludes with greetings for the start of Muharram and an announcement of the "Muharram Radio" schedule. News segments report on widespread destruction in occupied territories and the Turkish president's criticism of Israeli policies. Additionally, there are updates on the disbursement of cash subsidies to lower-income households and the normalization of urban services.
