In this episode of the radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat," Hujjat al-Islam Falab examines the reasons for God's anger towards His servants based on Quranic verses. He explains the descending trajectory of the ego-driven soul, detailing how humans progress from denying God's existence to rejecting divine authority. The core of the discussion is "worshipping Taghut," defined as venerating anything other than God, including the ego and Satan, which leads to severe punishment and greater deviation from the right path. The sixth cause of divine wrath is identified as abandoning one's post in the "front of truth," symbolizing the unjustified retreat from religious duties or moral battles. The speaker emphasizes that a true believer must remain steadfast in the front of truth, constantly defending against evil and the ego in both external and internal struggles. Key virtues such as honesty, sincerity, justice, and prayer are highlighted as essential fortresses for believers against satanic temptations. The program concludes with a prayer for peace, reinforcing the importance of maintaining faith and moral integrity in all aspects of life.
