The radio program begins with religious prayers, emphasizing the deep connection between faith and security.
The host highlights the characteristics of Shia identity, focusing on the red past of Karbala and the green future of the Mahdi's appearance.
The narrative section recounts the story from the book "Oxianus Mashreq," where a man named Aman is lost in the desert.
An old cobbler named Pinduz hosts him and engages in a dialogue that strengthens Aman's belief in the presence of Imam Reza.
Pinduz uses logical and mystical arguments to explain the concepts of guardianship and infallibility to Aman.
He clarifies that geographical distances are irrelevant to an Imam who is the master of the earth and time.
At the end, Pinduz encourages Aman to establish a spiritual connection with the Imam through prayer and greetings.
The program concludes with collective prayers for peace and an introduction to a specialized session for Quran reciters.
