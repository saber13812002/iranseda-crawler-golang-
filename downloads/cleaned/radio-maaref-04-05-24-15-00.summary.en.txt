In this radio program, Professor Rasakhi describes the "Axis of Resistance" as a decentralized, belief-based structure with geographical diversity, designed to achieve military self-sufficiency through local technology transfer.
The core strategy involves teaching resistance groups how to manufacture low-cost, high-impact weapons like drones and precision missiles rather than simply supplying them with arms.
This approach ensures regional balance, as other fronts like Yemen and Lebanon can maintain deterrence even if one front is compromised.
The most significant feature of this axis is its cultural and educational dimension, which fosters a culture of martyrdom to replace fallen commanders with new, motivated generations.
Professor Rasakhi emphasizes that Iran's defensive power has risen from zero to the top rank in the region due to the Supreme Leader's strategic focus on indigenous capabilities.
In the second segment, Dr. Sarbasheh Hosseini discusses osteoporosis, identifying regular exercise and adequate calcium intake from dairy or plant-based sources as primary preventive measures.
She advises consuming dairy products as separate meals to avoid digestive issues and stresses the importance of medical supervision for any pharmaceutical treatments.
Finally, brief answers were provided to listeners' questions regarding conditions like psoriasis and general principles of healthy nutrition based on traditional medicine.
