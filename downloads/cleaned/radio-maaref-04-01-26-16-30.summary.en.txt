In this radio program, Professor Mohammadi addresses listeners' theological and educational inquiries. He explains that the question "what comes next" stems from a misunderstanding of the ultimate destination, where neither anxiety nor fatigue exists. The professor emphasizes that the pleasures of the afterlife transcend material senses and include boundless spiritual benefits. Regarding religious upbringing, he advises parents to first thoroughly understand the foundations of monotheism themselves before teaching their children using simple examples and a question-and-answer approach. He recommends resources such as Shahid Motahhari's "Introduction to Worldview" and Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi's books for further study. The question of how God came into existence is answered by using the analogy of salt's saltiness to prove that God's existence is self-subsistent and requires no external cause. Finally, the program concludes by emphasizing the importance of maintaining family ties and honoring parents, even after their passing.
