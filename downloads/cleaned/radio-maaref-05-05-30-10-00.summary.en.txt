In this radio program, while offering condolences for the martyrdom of Imam Hasan Askari (AS), his efforts to protect and introduce Imam Zaman (AJ) were discussed. These actions are categorized into protective measures (concealment) and evidentiary acts (introduction to Shias). To conceal the Imam's existence, tactics such as changing the mother's name, announcing the death of another child, and hiding the birth were employed to deceive the government. Conversely, to prove the Imam's existence, he occasionally showed his son to specific companions and performed the Aqiqah ritual. After the Imam's martyrdom, the four deputies continued these security measures to prevent physical assassination. During the occultation, Imam Zaman (AJ) still plays cosmological and legislative roles, secretly resolving fitnas and freeing captives. Today, the primary threat is not physical assassination but the ideological assassination of the concept of Mahdism by enemies and even some uninformed Shias. The duty of the awaiters is to preserve this belief and counter the media and cultural plots of enemies aimed at undermining the concept of Mahdism.
