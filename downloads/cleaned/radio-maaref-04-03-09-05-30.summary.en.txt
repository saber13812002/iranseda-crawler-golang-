The text then addresses Sheikh Tusi's statement in "Shifa" that "the differentiae of substances are not substances," meaning they do not have a genus and are not included in the category of substance as a genus.
If differentiae were considered substances, it would lead to an infinite regress of genera and differentiae; therefore, they are simple concepts that lack a genus.
However, the concept of "substance" can apply to differentiae as an accidental concept, similar to how "accident" applies to accidental categories, without implying that they have a genus.
It is argued that the non-inclusion of differentiae in the category of substance does not necessarily imply their inclusion in the categories of accidents.
Sheikh Tusi also emphasized in other works that certain simple concepts, such as "point" and "unity," do not fall under any of the ten categories.
