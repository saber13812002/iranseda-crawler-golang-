The guest discussed the golden era of Iranian football in 1996, highlighting the positive shift back to domestic coaching after a series of defeats. He praised the smart integration of young and experienced players by the national team coach, which led to a third-place finish in the Asian Cup along with several individual awards. These achievements boosted the team's confidence and facilitated the transfer of players to European leagues. The narrative detailed key matches against Iraq, Thailand, and South Korea, noting the painful loss to Saudi Arabia due to poor refereeing and psychological pressure. He also recounted the bitter elimination from the 2002 World Cup under coach Branko Ivankovic, criticizing last-minute hotel changes and inappropriate pre-match nutrition during the Bahrain game. The guest emphasized that such issues are baseless excuses for failure and that focus should remain on technical and managerial quality rather than political maneuvering.
