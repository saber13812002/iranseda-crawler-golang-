In this program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verses 40 to 43 of Surah Al-A'raf, emphasizing the distinction between arrogant disbelievers who face eternal punishment and those whose punishment may be temporary. He clarifies that entry into Paradise requires not only good deeds but also sincere faith, whereas Hell encompasses both physical and spiritual torments, with spiritual pain manifesting as resentment in this world. For believers, God removes all grudges from their hearts, allowing them to sit face-to-face in Paradise without any gossip or inner distress. This interpretation highlights that Paradise includes not only visible pleasures but also profound spiritual and internal joys that can be experienced even in this life. Ultimately, the divine men pray to God to keep their hearts free from any resentment towards their fellow believers.
