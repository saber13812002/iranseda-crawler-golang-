This radio program narrates the life of martyr Ali Siyad Shirazi based on the book "In the Ambush of the Red Rose." After studying in the United States with excellent grades, Ali returned to Iran and initially served at the Shahabad Gharb base. He became disillusioned with the upper ranks of the military due to their disrespectful treatment of him. General Owisi transferred him to the Isfahan Artillery Center to receive modern training. In Isfahan, Ali balanced his military duties with teaching English and correcting the English translation of the Quran. Despite strict surveillance by counter-intelligence, his religious devotion earned him the trust of General Yusefi. Ali served as Yusefi's operational adjutant in Kurdistan, a role that significantly enhanced his military and spiritual growth. This period of collaboration solidified his respected position among the commanders.
