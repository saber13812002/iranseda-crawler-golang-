This radio program, "Narrative of Love," features the story of Zainab Sadat Hosseini, the wife of the martyr defender of the shrine, Sayed Ahmad Hosseini, from Afghanistan. Zainab recounts the agonizing days of waiting for news about her husband's disappearance and the unanswered phone calls that heightened her anxiety. She describes the traumatic moment when officials informed her of his martyrdom and the shock of seeing his body, which had been decapitated. The narrative highlights the mixed reactions from the community, including a fellow martyr's mother who urged Zainab to stop crying after seeing the extent of her son's injuries. A pivotal moment in Zainab's healing journey was her visit to the shrine of Lady Zainab (SA), where she found solace by empathizing with the historical figure's suffering. The program concludes by discussing key Islamic revolutionary principles such as "We Can," "Resistance," and "Strengthening Defense Power" as pillars of national independence. This interview serves as a testament to the patience and resilience of martyr families in defending Islamic values.
