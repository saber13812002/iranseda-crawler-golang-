The radio program "Zamzam Ahkam" began by addressing various jurisprudential questions, including the payment of Khums by a spouse, the obligations of followers after their Marja's death, and the rules regarding ownerless land. It also clarified the necessity of returning hospital supplies taken without permission and the sufficiency of specific salaams in prayer according to Ayatollah Shabiri Zanjani. In the second segment, Professor Javad Mohdasi discussed the distinction between truth and falsehood from a rhetorical perspective in the "Sarmashk" program. He emphasized the importance of insight and deep religious knowledge to counter media deception, citing the sermons of Imam Ali. Finally, the "Andisheh Aftab" program featured Professor Sabeti discussing the interpretation of Surah Tawhid based on the works of Ayatollah Javadi Amoli. This section highlighted the high status of the Surah and the need for a true understanding of its content, promising to continue the discussion from the first verse in the next episode.
