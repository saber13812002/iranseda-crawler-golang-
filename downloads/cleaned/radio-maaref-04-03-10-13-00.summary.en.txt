In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Rashed Yazdi discusses the characteristics of an Alawi and Fatimi life and the criteria for a successful marriage. He emphasizes that marriage is not merely a physical union but requires religious, cultural, and familial compatibility between the partners. Mutual understanding and respect for each other's nature, including gentleness and avoiding insults, are essential for family peace. The program also highlights the continuous expression of affection, trust, and mutual forgiveness as the main pillars of a shared life. The behavior of parents and their interaction with each other serves as a crucial educational model for the next generation. Finally, listeners are encouraged to follow the model of the Prophet and the Ahl al-Bayt by adhering to principles of fairness and respect.
