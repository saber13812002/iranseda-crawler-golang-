The program begins by analyzing Ayatollah Misbah Yazdi's philosophical arguments regarding the nature of mental existence and its relationship to substance and quality.
The speaker argues that knowledge is essentially a mental quality, yet it shares the characteristics of substance due to its unity with external objects.
To resolve this apparent contradiction, the text uses the analogy of a person who is inherently a substance but also possesses accidental attributes like being a writer.
This analysis demonstrates that mental forms can hold both essential and accidental properties without violating the principle of preserving essences.
The broadcast then transitions to the recitation of the Al-Yasin ziyarat and the announcement of prayer times for Mashhad, Shiraz, and Qom.
Finally, a political speech is aired that condemns enemies' attempts to demoralize the youth by denying the revolution's achievements.
The speaker emphasizes that the fundamental principles of the revolution, namely independence, freedom, and the Islamic Republic, have been fully realized.
