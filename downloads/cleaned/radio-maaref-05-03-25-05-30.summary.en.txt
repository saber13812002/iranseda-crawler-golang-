This radio program, based on the insights of Dr. Hosseini Bagherabadi and Nahj al-Balagha, emphasizes the importance of simple living and avoiding materialism. Imam Ali (AS) is presented as the quintessential model of asceticism, who maintained a humble lifestyle despite his political authority, even patching his own armor and shoes. A central theme is the distinction between superficial and genuine asceticism, arguing that true piety is determined by inner intention and love rather than mere external actions. The Prophet (PBUH) and Imam Ali (AS) demonstrated that one can be active in worldly affairs without harboring a deep emotional attachment to them. The program clarifies that loving the world and the Hereafter are incompatible, but utilizing the world as a means to gain God's pleasure is permissible and necessary. Consequently, Muslims are urged to ensure that their claims of asceticism do not contradict their luxurious behaviors. Finally, the program concludes with a prayer for God to purify hearts from worldly attachments and guide the followers of the Ahl al-Bayt on the path of truth.
