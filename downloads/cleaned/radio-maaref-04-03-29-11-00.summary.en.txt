The IRGC announced the successful execution of fourteen waves of powerful attacks on Israeli military targets, while the Chief of Staff of the Armed Forces emphasized that strikes will continue against any enemy objective without limitation. Following these exchanges, Iraq's heavy water facility was attacked, though timely air defense measures minimized the damage to the site. Concurrently, the Islamic Propaganda Coordination Council announced nationwide "Anger and Victory" rallies, and Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi condemned threats against the Supreme Leader, stressing the critical importance of the Wilayat system in Shia belief. The Martyrs Foundation stated that the names of martyrs and wounded have been received to establish official files for them. Additionally, the Endowments Organization announced that holy shrines are open 24 hours a day to accommodate the public, while the IRIB attributed network disruptions to cyberattacks by the enemy. In the live program "Man Gadirim," Hujjat al-Islam Nazari Monfared explained Iran's defensive stance and its legitimate right to nuclear energy, asserting the inevitable victory of the "Truth against Falsehood" front. He highlighted the resilience of the Iranian people against sanctions and threats, reaffirming their unwavering support for the Supreme Leader and their commitment to national sovereignty.
