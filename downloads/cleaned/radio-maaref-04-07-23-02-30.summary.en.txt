In the radio program "Persiman Ateghadi," hosted by Professor Mohammad Sharoudi, key theological and jurisprudential issues were discussed. The professor emphasized the importance of acquiring "beneficial knowledge" while warning against pursuing useless information, referred to as "ilm la-in fahm." Addressing questions about the Imams' knowledge of the unseen, he explained that although they possess this capability, they generally act based on apparent evidence to preserve human free will and avoid fatalism. The concept of "Bada'" was explained as a mechanism where destinies can change through repentance and righteous actions, meaning even prophetic predictions can be altered by human behavior. The program also provided advice on overcoming reluctance in night prayers and clarified that selling a deceased person's house does not harm their soul unless used for sinful purposes. Regarding evil eye and sorcery, the professor noted that these are spiritual factors that can influence fate but can be repelled through specific prayers and amulets. Finally, the virtue of visiting graves and the Barzakh realm, specifically the Wadi al-Salam as a gathering place for believers' souls, was discussed, highlighting that the good deeds of the living benefit the deceased.
