The program begins by promoting the principle of "budgeting" based on Imam Baqir's (AS) narration, which advises maintaining a balance between extravagance and stinginess in daily life.
The practical conduct of Imam Khomeini (RA) in Najaf is then presented as a model of this principle, where he strictly controlled personal expenses, from avoiding expensive cheese to reducing vegetable consumption and removing fat from food to prevent waste.
The second part of the program continues the dramatic narration of the book "Ouyanos Mashreq," in which the character "Aman" travels with "Haj Karim," a wounded Shia elder, in search of the water of life.
In this narrative, Ahmad highlights Haj Karim's deep devotion to the Imam of the Time (AJ), while Aman reflects on his own doubts and the impact of witnessing such profound faith.
Aman ultimately decides to travel to Khorasan with Haj Karim to meet the Imam of the Time (AJ) and find the truth, thereby resolving his spiritual uncertainties.
In the final section, the program addresses listeners' religious questions regarding the permissibility of backbiting when it is necessary to defend one's rights.
Supportive messages for the people of Gaza and calls for financial assistance via provided numbers were also broadcast.
Finally, listeners were encouraged to send their feedback about the "Asr-e Javani" contest through the specified SMS and phone systems.
