The program began with the recitation of a prayer for rain and a request for divine mercy before transitioning to a discussion on the ethics of cursing and foul language. Professor Fallah emphasized, citing religious narrations, that insulting a fellow believer results in exiting the sphere of divine guardianship and leads to the rejection of one's good deeds. He warned that cursing is considered a moral vice and is unacceptable, even when disguised as a joke or used within close friendships. The severe consequences of such behavior include multiplied punishment in this world and the hereafter, as well as permanent exclusion from entering Paradise. The speaker also noted that cursing women or specific individuals carries even harsher divine penalties. Finally, the program concluded by advocating for eloquent speech and mutual respect as the proper alternatives to verbal abuse.
