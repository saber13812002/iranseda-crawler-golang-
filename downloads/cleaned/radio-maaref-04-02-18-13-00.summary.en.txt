In this radio program, the speaker discusses the three essential traits for perfect faith based on a hadith of Imam Reza (AS). The first trait, "concealing secrets," is described as a divine sunnah that prevents the disclosure of personal matters to new acquaintances to avoid future enmity. The second trait, "tolerance and interaction with people," is presented as the prophetic sunnah, emphasizing active social engagement and inviting others to religion. The speaker criticizes the isolation of Muslims and urges them to emulate the Prophet (PBUH) in attracting the youth and future generations. Additionally, the story of a slave who visited the shrine at night highlights the importance of keeping secrets and trusting God over human suspicion. Finally, the speaker concludes by encouraging the audience to participate in social and religious activities, ending the program with blessings and the introduction of colleagues.
