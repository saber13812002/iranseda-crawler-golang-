This radio program provided jurisprudential answers to listener questions based on the rulings of religious authorities. Regarding Khums, it is necessary to either pay it or obtain permission from the Marja's office to use the funds. Other recommended prayers can serve as substitutes for the Janaba prayer. While women and children are permitted to slaughter animals, having a male slaughterer is preferred. Letting hair fall on the face during Wudu does not invalidate the ritual ablution. During a Ramadan trip, prayer and fasting rules remain abbreviated for the first ten days before becoming normal. Proxy performance of Friday prayer for the deceased is not valid, though dedicating its reward is permissible. Wearing black is inherently disliked, but it is acceptable in the month of Safar to express mourning. Finally, the program's contact numbers for phone and SMS inquiries were announced.
