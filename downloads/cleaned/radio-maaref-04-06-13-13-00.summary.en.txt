This program cites Imam Javad's hadith to identify reliance on God as the fundamental capital behind the Islamic Revolution's victories. The speaker illustrates the transformative power of monotheism by contrasting the pre-Islamic fear of the people of Mecca with their subsequent strength under the Prophet. The courageous act of the Prophet's envoy before the Roman Emperor is highlighted as a symbol of Muslim fearlessness against global powers. Similarly, the Iranian people's resilience during the bloody events of Khordad 1963 and their willingness to sacrifice are attributed to this divine faith. The speaker asserts that independence and liberation from the clutches of the arrogant are the primary goals achieved through these sacrifices. Conversely, the severe threats posed by the United States and its allies, including economic sanctions and support for terrorist groups, stem from their fear of the revolution's expanding influence. The speaker warns that Iran represents a serious danger to the imperialist agenda of the West, which seeks to suppress its ideological impact. Finally, the program concludes with a call for vigilance against foreign plots and an optimistic outlook for the revolution's ultimate success.
