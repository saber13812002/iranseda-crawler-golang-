The program begins by critiquing the pre-Islamic belief that dignity is derived from wealth, power, and tribal status, arguing instead that true honor comes solely from God and following the Prophet and his family.
Historical examples, such as the Prophet's dignified return to Mecca and Imam Hussein's martyrdom, illustrate that divine honor is far more enduring than transient worldly power.
It is emphasized that the world is merely a temporary stopover, and relying on material wealth or fame is futile as these things inevitably vanish.
The second part of the broadcast continues the narrative from the book "Oxianus Mashreq," where the character Imanan, seeking the water of life, encounters Hajj Karim and an old man in the desert.
The old man expresses his deep devotion to Imam Reza by showing a ring he purchased for the Imam, urging Imanan to prioritize the path to Khorasan over his northern journey.
While Imanan remains fixated on finding a mythical water source in the north, the old man warns him about the dangers of physical and mental misguidance along the way.
The segment concludes with the old man reflecting on his unique destiny and the program ends with a call to submit documents related to the Sacred Defense and the playing of music.
