In this radio program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verse 105 of Surah Al-Ma'idah, emphasizing the concept of self-vigilance. He argues that the path to God lies within the human soul, where the traveler, the path, and the destination ultimately become one. The phrase "watch over yourselves" is explained as a call to maintain constant awareness of one's inner thoughts and actions to prevent being enslaved by Satan. Using metaphors of internal growth in plants and minerals, he illustrates that spiritual development is an organic, inward process. Furthermore, it is explained that every good or evil deed primarily affects the doer themselves. Consequently, deceiving others is essentially deceiving oneself, and one's ultimate fate is the direct result of their vigilance or negligence regarding their inner self.
