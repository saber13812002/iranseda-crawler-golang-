In the Quranic Questions program, Professor Rostam Najat explained the subtle difference between the words "ala" and "ni'ma" in the Quran. He clarified that "ala" carries a broader and deeper meaning rooted in divine mercy and power, encompassing blessings that existed before the creation of humans, such as Paradise and Hell. In contrast, "ni'ma" has a more general usage and does not necessarily imply the dimensions of the Creator's mercy and power. Interestingly, the existence of Hell is also considered one of the "ala" due to God's mercy, as the fear of it can guide people toward Paradise. In the second part, the professor addressed a grammatical question regarding the verse "La yaflih al-zalimun," explaining that the Arabic verb form "af'ala" can sometimes mean "becoming" and takes a subject rather than an object. Therefore, the verse means "the wrongdoers will not succeed," and the verb is not transitive. The program concluded with the "Keys to Happiness" segment, emphasizing acceptance of fate and avoiding excessive strictness in life. The speakers advised that humans should find the right paths in life by relying on God and accepting their limitations.
