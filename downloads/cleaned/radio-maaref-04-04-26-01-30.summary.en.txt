The program begins by analyzing the jurisprudential distinction between opposing individual and social evil, citing a prophetic hadith and the Quranic verse "Alaykum anfusikum." It argues that while self-purification is primary, rising against widespread tyranny and deviation from the Prophet's Sunnah is a religious obligation. The broadcast then transitions to a dramatic retelling from the book "Neshan-e Hassan," depicting the caravan of Imam Hussein (PBUH) traveling to Kufa. The narrative focuses on Nafila, the mother of Qasim, who visits the grave of Amna to seek peace and expresses her deep anxiety regarding the potential betrayal by the people of Kufa. A poignant scene is also described where Hassan ibn Hassan prays wearing his grandfather's cloak, accompanied by his aunt Zaynab (SA), symbolizing spiritual resilience amidst the hardships of the journey.
