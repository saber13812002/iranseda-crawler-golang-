In the "Zamzam Ahkam" program, Hojjat al-Islam Mahmoudi emphasized the obligation of praying on time based on Ayatollah Sistani's fatwa and advised followers on the conditions for changing their marja, such as prioritizing the most learned scholar. He also clarified that travelers who missed prayers due to valid excuses must perform them as shortened prayers upon returning. Addressing a part-time truck driver, Hojjat al-Islam Najat cited Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi's ruling that prayers remain shortened unless the individual travels for work at least ten days a month. Hojjat al-Islam Jahvari, referencing Ayatollah Shabir Zanjani, permitted praying on a train provided there is sufficient time and the direction of the Qibla is observed. Additionally, Hojjat al-Islam Badini explained the correct method of wudu with a bandage (jabira) according to Ayatollah Javadi Amoli, stating that wiping the wet hand over the bandage suffices if direct water contact is harmful. The broadcast concluded with the "Khaneh-ye Doost" program, featuring a sermon by Hojjat al-Islam Taheri on the significance of prayer as the believer's ascension and the primary path to spiritual proximity to God.
