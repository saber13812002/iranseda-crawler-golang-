The program begins with prayers for the early appearance of Imam Zaman and introduces the 20th specialized session for Quran reciters focusing on innovation in recitation. The main discussion with Hujjat al-Islam Mohammad Hali centers on the destructive effects of backbiting based on Quranic verses and Ahl al-Bayt narrations. Hali emphasizes that backbiting involves not only the speaker but also the listener and anyone who fails to defend the victim, all of whom face severe consequences in the afterlife. The consequences of backbiting include humiliation in this world and the hereafter, being burdened with the sinner's guilt, and the destruction of one's faith and spiritual health. The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) warned that those who criticize a believer for a sin will eventually commit that same sin themselves, highlighting the importance of defending believers' honor. Finally, a story about a lion and a wolf illustrates that backbiting is a disease that destroys inner peace and prevents individuals from achieving high spiritual ranks.
