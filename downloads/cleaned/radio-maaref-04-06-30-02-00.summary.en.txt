In the Parsaiyan program, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the necessity of asceticism and detachment from worldly desires based on religious narrations. He illustrated this concept by recounting the simple and frugal lifestyle of Imam Khomeini, clarifying that asceticism means avoiding attachment and extravagance rather than absolute deprivation. The second part of the discussion involved an exegesis of verses from Surah Yunus, which identifies lying as an obstacle to success, except in specific legal contexts such as warfare or mediation. The core message asserts that worldly possessions are transient, and a person's true value lies in their eternal reward in the afterlife. The program also highlighted the Day of Judgment as a powerful deterrent against sin. Finally, the story of Prophet Noah was presented as a model of patience and steadfastness in the face of public denial.
