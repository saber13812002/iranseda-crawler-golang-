In this radio program, Dr. Mehdi Abutalebi emphasized the principle of rule of law, criticizing the imposition of internet restrictions through parallel, non-legal bodies rather than the Supreme National Security Council. He cited the Administrative Justice Court's ruling on the illegality of recent actions, urging authorities to enforce this decision decisively to avoid public confusion and maintain trust. Regarding the issue of hijab, the expert criticized a binary approach, advocating for a moderate stance that avoids extreme confrontations in public gatherings while utilizing soft power to guide the public. He also rejected the notion that an agreement with the United States would eliminate the shadow of war, arguing that the inherent conflict between hegemony and independence persists and that only military and diplomatic deterrence can ensure security. Finally, he defined the boundary between public demands and distrust of officials as adhering to the Supreme Leader's guidelines while not immediately validating foreign media rumors, thereby preserving national unity and exerting positive pressure on negotiations.
