The program begins by interpreting the verse "He revealed it with His knowledge," refuting the Ash'ari view that God's knowledge is an attribute separate from His essence.
It then analyzes a verse from Surah An-Nisa, describing a group of Israelites who rejected the truth and led others astray from the path of God.
The term "sadd" is interpreted as turning away, highlighting four actions of this group: being misguided, considering their disbelief as truth, misleading others, and gaining status through these acts.
The divine judgment for these opponents is described as eternal punishment in Hell, with the text emphasizing the permanence of their torment based on Quranic evidence.
The discussion shifts to the universal address "O mankind," which underscores the global nature of the Prophet's mission and the Quran for all of humanity.
The Prophet is presented as the bearer of truth from the Lord, where faith is defined as a benefit for humans, while disbelief is a loss for themselves.
This disbelief does not harm God, the absolute owner of the universe, but rather deprives individuals of spiritual and worldly benefits.
Ultimately, accepting the divine message is the only path to salvation, whereas rejecting it leads to eternal perdition.
