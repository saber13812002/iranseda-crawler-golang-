The radio program "I am Fatima" analyzes the Khutbah of Fadak to explore Fatima Zahra's (SA) teachings on the Prophet's mission and the hardships of pre-Islamic Arabia. The discussion highlights her profound historical knowledge and the importance of learning from the fates of past nations, particularly the Children of Israel. It also recounts the narrative of Imam Ali's (AS) marriage proposal and Fatima's implicit consent through her silence. A central theme is Fatima's assertion that the world is fraught with calamities and that absolute peace is impossible for believers in this life. The Prophet (PBUH) reinforced this by stating that worldly comfort is unattainable, urging believers to find sweetness in hardships through faith. The program further contrasts the unique and unprecedented bond between Iran's Supreme Leader and the people with other global revolutions. This deep connection, rooted in the relationship of a follower and a religious authority, underscores the leader's special status in the hearts of the Iranian people.
