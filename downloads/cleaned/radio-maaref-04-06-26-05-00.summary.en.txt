The radio program begins by emphasizing the pivotal role of Imam Ali (PBUH) in preserving Islam and his spiritual blessings.
The speaker highlights historical deviations and the incorrect comparison of Imam Ali with Muawiyah, stressing the need for accurate knowledge of his status.
Citing Nahj al-Balagha, the speaker identifies the roots of fitna as following personal desires and baseless opinions, urging listeners to remain vigilant.
The second part of the program features a fiqh lesson by Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi focusing on the Tawaf of Women.
The instructor emphasizes the obligation of this Tawaf for women, children, and specific individuals based on Quranic verses and narrations.
Conflicting views among jurists, such as Shaykh al-Thani regarding the ruling for married women in Ihram, are discussed.
Ultimately, multiple narrations are used to prove that the Tawaf of Women is the primary solution to lift the restrictions of Ihram.
This analysis demonstrates that the Tawaf of Women is mandatory for all mentioned groups and has no alternative substitute.
