The radio program began by celebrating the anniversary of Lady Fatima Masumeh's arrival in Qom.
In the first segment, Hujjat al-Islam Vahidi recounted a story about Ayatollah Shahbadi, who considered the detailed recitation of Lady Zahra's ziyarat forbidden for the general public due to its profound emotional impact.
He also mentioned Ayatollah Khoei's answered prayer for martyrdom and closeness to the Ahl al-Bayt.
The second part featured a literary narrative from the book "Ubaynus Mashriq," discussing the concepts of Imamate and divine love.
In the third segment, a religious ruling was provided regarding a deferred sale where the seller failed to deliver the goods on time.
According to the Supreme Leader's fatwa, the buyer cannot claim damages unless specified, but may seek compensation for currency devaluation or cancel the transaction.
The program concluded by emphasizing the importance of investment in production to resolve economic issues in 1404.
It ended with thanks to the listeners and an introduction of the production team.
