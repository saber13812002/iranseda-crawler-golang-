In this radio program, Professor Vahidpour initially distinguished between defending national territory and defending the Islamic system, characterizing recent events as a legitimate defense against the aggression of infidels and Zionists rather than a war. He argued that despite the safety of political leaders, a society under threat is obligated to defend itself, citing the surprise attack on military commanders and the global recognition of this right. In the second section, Hujjat al-Islam Fannalazadeh clarified that the Friday prayer has no make-up (qada); if it is invalidated, one must perform the Zuhr prayer with the intention of compensation. Responses to other religious inquiries indicated that calculating Khums based on either the solar or lunar year is permissible, and fasting for patients at risk of harm is conditional upon medical advice or the payment of fidya. The program also addressed Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi's ruling on women leading congregational prayers for women and Ayatollah Javadi Amoli's view on the impurity of imported leather from non-Muslims. Finally, the show concluded by reminding listeners of contact channels and emphasizing the importance of specifying their marja in their questions.
