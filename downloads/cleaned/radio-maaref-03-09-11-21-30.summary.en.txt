This segment of the program begins with an exegesis of the final verses of Surah An-Nisa, emphasizing the Quran's universal address to all humanity. The commentator analyzes verse 174 to elucidate the relationship between "burhan" (proof) and "noor mubeen" (clear light), arguing that both terms primarily refer to the Holy Quran. Burhan is defined as a clear and definitive argument that dispels the darkness of ignorance, while noor mubeen signifies the manifest illumination of the Quran. Although the Prophet's existence is also considered a divine proof, the primary focus of this specific verse is the Quran's role as a clarifying guide. The commentator cites other verses to demonstrate that the Quran was revealed alongside the Prophet, positioning the Prophet as the principal entity and the Quran as his accompanying companion. Furthermore, the text highlights the Prophet's higher existential and hierarchical status, describing the Quran as a speech derived from the Prophet's moving spirit. Ultimately, the inseparable unity of the Prophet and the Quran in guiding humanity toward the straight path and receiving divine mercy is affirmed.
