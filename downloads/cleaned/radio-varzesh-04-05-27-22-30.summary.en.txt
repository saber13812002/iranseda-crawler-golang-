Dr. Seyed Hadi Hosseini recounts the successful organization of the World Wrestling Championships in Sari, highlighting how the event was managed with a minimal budget and left no financial debt. He emphasizes that the key to this success was fostering a sense of ownership among all staff members, from managers to janitors, and leveraging the support of the public and media. By applying management principles such as precise task division and strict financial oversight, he demonstrated how transparency could ensure the full and effective use of allocated funds. Hosseini also highlights the critical role of inter-departmental cooperation and the strategic use of media challenges to secure additional financial support for sports projects. Regarding infrastructure development, he details the construction and renovation of dozens of sports halls in rural areas through public participation and collaboration with various government officials. These efforts resulted in a sevenfold increase in the per capita sports space in Mazandaran compared to the previous seventy years. Ultimately, he attributes the province's sports achievements to the synergy between the people, the media, and transparent, participatory management.
