The program begins with a reflection on the concept of "fuel and ash," using the metaphor of drought and pistachio trees turned into charcoal to illustrate the corruption of the human heart through persistent sin. It then analyzes the story of Prophet Noah in the Quran, categorizing the related verses into six groups covering his spiritual status, accusations, and the punishment of his people. The main segment addresses various jurisprudential questions from listeners, including the rulings on night prayer, the conditions for the permissibility of bank interest, and the calculation of Khums on loans. The discussion also covers the rules for congregational prayer in recommended prayers and the requirements for Wudu after different types of Ghusl. Finally, the program offers advice for managing OCD during ablution and water conservation, concluding with prayers for the Imam's reappearance and an introduction to other Radio Ma'aref programs.
