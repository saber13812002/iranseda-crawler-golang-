The Supreme Leader emphasized accelerating Iran's military and scientific progress and maintaining national unity in a message marking the 40th day of the martyred nuclear scientists. Radio Muharram concluded its one-month run after broadcasting over 720 hours of programs, while Radio Arbaeen launched to accompany pilgrims. The Central Bank initiated the sale of special currency for Arbaeen pilgrims through selected banks at the official rate. The Fatima Masumeh Shrine's mobq is providing specific accommodation and health services for female pilgrims. Filming for the historical series "Salman Farsi," which depicts three historical eras, is currently underway. The religious segment discussed the Prophet's miracles and their authentic sources, such as the Quran and Nahj al-Balagha. It also highlighted the historical steadfastness of religious scholars against British colonialism in Iraq and in defending the homeland. Finally, the jurisprudential ruling on using musical instruments in mourning gatherings was examined, distinguishing it from singing.
