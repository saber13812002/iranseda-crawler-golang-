In this radio program, religious rulings were provided by various Maraji' in response to listeners' questions. Ayatollah Shabizadeh clarified that the Sharia distance is approximately 48 kilometers, and passing through one's residence during this journey invalidates the condition. Sheikh Shabani emphasized the necessity of repentance and paying blood money to the father in cases of miscarriage. Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi stressed the obligation to perform the Prayer of Signs immediately after an earthquake, noting that delaying it constitutes a sin. Regarding prostration on decorated tiles, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli confirmed the validity of the prayer but recommended using the plain side for better spiritual focus. Ayatollah Sistani ruled that hitting the forehead twice during a single prostration counts as one prostration and does not invalidate the prayer. The distinction between Kaffarah and Fidyah was also explained based on the presence or absence of valid excuses. Finally, administering iron drops to an infant was deemed permissible only if medical experts unanimously agree on its necessity.
