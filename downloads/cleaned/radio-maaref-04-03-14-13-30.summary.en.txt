The program began with prayers for resolving economic crises and the appearance of the Imam, transitioning into the "Love Story" segment. Fatemeh Rezaei, the wife of martyr pilot Bahman Masaeibi, shared memories of their courtship and marriage. She highlighted her husband's simple yet profound character, noting their modest engagement rings and mutual support in their academic pursuits. Their life together involved enduring the hardships of a military spouse, including frequent separations and the dangers of his missions. Rezaei described her intense anxiety during her pregnancies while her husband was deployed to Syria. She recounted the final, heartbreaking phone calls before his martyrdom and the profound impact this event had on her life.
