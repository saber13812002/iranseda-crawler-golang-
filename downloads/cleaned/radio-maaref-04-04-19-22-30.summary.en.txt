In this radio program, the discussion began by criticizing Western and American policies regarding Gaza, emphasizing the necessity of adhering to religious and ethical principles.
The core of the debate focused on the three essential connections with the martyrs: moral, principled, and methodological, which are presented as prerequisites for true freedom and success.
It was explained that appearing dominant through un-Islamic or unethical methods is actually a form of defeat, while true victory lies in piety and avoiding sin.
Visiting Imam Hussein (PBUH) is described not merely as a ritual, but as a path to spiritual proximity to God and developing a healthy fear of sin in daily life.
For those unable to travel physically, the program suggested that visiting Karbala with the "eye of the heart" and "foot of the soul" is a valid way to establish a spiritual connection.
The session concluded with the recitation of elegies and prayers, urging listeners to connect their hearts to Karbala through the remembrance of the Holy Family during these Friday nights.
