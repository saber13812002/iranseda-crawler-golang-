In the "Persaman" religious program, discussions covered faith, the parent-child relationship in the afterlife, and the importance of presence of mind in prayer. It was explained that in Paradise, individuals are ranked by their faith and piety, and parents and children recognize each other even if their spiritual levels differ. The necessity of reading the Quran despite errors was emphasized, as repetition leads to gradual correction. The concept of "Khawf and Raja" was described as a balance between fear of divine punishment and hope for mercy, illustrated with poetry. The historical reason for Jewish opposition to the Prophet was analyzed as stemming from their exclusive claim to descent from Jacob and rejection of Ishaq's lineage. Practical advice was given to enhance spiritual focus in prayers by avoiding routine and maintaining intention. The "Khyaban-e Farhang" program focused on the rights of travelers, highlighting the virtue of hospitality and the dual impact of tourism on local communities.
