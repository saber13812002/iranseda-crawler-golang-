The program first addresses the Islamic ruling on facial hair, stating that trimming the mustache and growing the beard to the jawline is obligatory according to the majority of Maraji'.
Imam Khomeini also declared shaving the beard haram, specifying that the permissible length extends only to the jaw area.
The discussion then shifts to a question regarding how to perform a missed prayer (Qada) for someone who was partially in their home and partially traveling during the prayer time.
According to the response by Hujjat al-Islam Jawaheri, citing Ayatollah Shabiri Zanjani, the ruling depends on the person's location at the end of the prayer time.
If the individual was in their home when the time ended, they must perform the prayer in full; if they were traveling, they must perform it in a shortened form.
Therefore, being in a state of travel at the beginning of the time is not the determining factor for shortening the Qada prayer.
This general rule applies to any prayer where the person's location changed during the allotted time.
The crucial point is to determine the prayer format based on the location at the moment the prayer time expires.
