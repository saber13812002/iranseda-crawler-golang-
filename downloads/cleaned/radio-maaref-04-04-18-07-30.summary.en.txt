In this radio program, the guest highlights the role of the Guardianship of the Jurist and love for the homeland in fostering national unity amidst Iran's ethnic and linguistic diversity. He cites the late Qasem Soleimani to argue that defending the Islamic Republic is synonymous with defending Islam, identifying this convergence as a key aspect of Iranian identity. The discussion then shifts to the concept of "infak" (charity), emphasizing that true ownership of wealth belongs to God, while humans are merely nominal owners. The guest stresses that charitable giving, especially during Muharram, promotes social peace and strengthens the brotherhood within the Islamic community. He warns that such acts must be performed with pure intentions and within the framework of Sharia to prevent them from becoming tools for sin or illicit gain. Ultimately, the highest form of charity is defined as dedicating oneself to the Prophet and his family through strict adherence to religious duties. This approach not only aids individual spiritual growth but also frees society from material anxieties, guiding it toward divine truths.
