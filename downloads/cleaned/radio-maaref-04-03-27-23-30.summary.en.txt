In the "Persama Ateqadi" program, Professor Mohammadi addressed the theological questions of students and listeners. He emphasized that the Quran remains unaltered due to its miraculous nature, strong historical evidence, and abundant narrations. Conversely, he argued that other sacred texts like the Torah and Gospel are corrupted due to internal contradictions and the lack of miraculous attributes. The professor also used logic to demonstrate that any alteration of the Quran would undermine God's wisdom and mercy. In the second segment, he explained that visiting martyrs' graves and performing charitable acts benefit them as Barzakh blessings. The broadcast concluded with security warnings about suspicious activities and foreign calls. The program ended with gratitude to the audience and an invitation to share their feedback.
