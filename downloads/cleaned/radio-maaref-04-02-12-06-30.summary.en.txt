The program begins by citing authentic narrations from Musnad Ahmad and Mustadrak Hakim to affirm the infallibility of the Ahl al-Bayt as the primary interpreters of the Verse of Purification.
It highlights that the Prophet (PBUH) explicitly defined the Ahl al-Bayt through his specific prayer and the act of covering them with his cloak, thereby excluding others from this status.
The discussion then shifts to the interpretation of "Ar-Rahman Ar-Rahim" based on Imam Askari's Tafsir and narrations from Imam Ali (AS).
A distinct difference is drawn between "Rahman" and "Rahim"; the former encompasses general sustenance for all creation, while the latter is exclusive to believers and Shias.
"Rahim" is defined as the alleviation of religious duties, specific worldly and otherworldly blessings, and the distinction of believers from their enemies on the Day of Judgment.
The linguistic and conceptual link between "Rahm" (womb) and "Rahman" is explored, emphasizing that honoring the Ahl al-Bayt is the source of divine mercy.
Finally, a narration is presented stating that divine mercy has one hundred branches, with ninety-nine distributed in this world and the final branch reserved for the intercession of the Ahl al-Bayt on the Day of Judgment.
