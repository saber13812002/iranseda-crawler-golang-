In the Islamic Solidarity Games, Ali Torki Araghi defeated Majid Hashemabadi in the third round to win the gold medal in the 75-80 kg category.
With this victory, the Iranian team secured four silver medals in Muay Thai during the final night, placing them fourth in the overall medal table.
The reporter congratulated the champions and emphasized the need for more colorful medals in the coming days to improve the team's ranking.
The second part of the program focused on analyzing the crucial World Cup 2026 qualifier between Poland and the Netherlands at the National Stadium in Warsaw.
Experts highlighted the contrasting styles of the two teams, with the Netherlands relying on technique and possession while Poland focuses on physical power and long balls.
Lewandowski was identified as Poland's key star, and his presence, despite potential injury, boosts the host team's motivation to earn points.
The outcome of this decisive match could secure the Netherlands' direct qualification or extend the playoff race to the final week.
It was emphasized that both teams are entering the field with different strategies, making the match highly potential for tactical and technical appeal.
