Ali Shouri, a football veteran and manager of the Esteghlal Veterans' Association, recounts his playing career with Bank Melli and Esteghlal in this interview. He explains how he formed the "Estal" veterans team in 2008 and organized quadrangular tournaments at the IRIB artificial turf field, featuring teams like Artists and Media. Shouri expresses gratitude for the financial and moral support provided by Esteghlal FC for these events and the team's international trips. He highlights the financial struggles of some veterans and emphasizes the need for attention from club and federation officials. Shouri also discusses his efforts to create a social space for veterans to gather and his personal support for those battling addiction. Finally, he mentions the formation of a seven-member committee to manage the veterans' association and outlines future plans for their welfare.
