This radio program explores the educational lessons from the Quran and hadith, focusing on the selection of Talut as a commander for the Israelites.
The key point is that God chose a poor and unknown individual over famous and wealthy figures to test the people and teach them a lesson.
This event teaches us that in management and governance, we should not rely solely on fame or appearance but must seek talent in every layer of society.
The concept of the "Ark" and "Sakina" was also interpreted as symbols of existential peace and divine power, which flow into the lives of believers through obedience to the Divine Authority.
The second part of the program emphasized the concept of "Istiqna," or independence from people, citing narrations from the book of Kafi as the key to self-esteem and inner peace.
Imam Sajjad (AS) stated that all good lies in cutting off greed from others' hands and placing complete trust in God.
This mindset allows individuals to be free from social considerations and perform religious duties, such as enjoining good and forbidding evil, with courage.
Finally, the deep connection between brotherhood among believers and closeness to Imam Zaman (AJ) was highlighted as the path to peace and divine support during the period of occultation.
