This radio program, dedicated to the birth of Imam Ali (PBUH), emphasized the vital importance of emotional and social bonds with relatives.
Citing Nahj al-Balagha, it was noted that even the wealthiest individuals require family for empathy, consolation, and emotional support.
The host warned that severing ties with kin, however minor it may seem, leads to losing their extensive support and assistance.
In the quiz segment, a family from Isfahan emerged as the winner by correctly answering religious questions.
A guest from Bandar Abbas highlighted the local culture where caring for the elderly at home is preferred over nursing homes to maintain family unity.
Finally, referencing Nahj al-Balagha, the program advised responding to others' indifference with kindness and forgiveness.
This ethical approach was presented as a model for maintaining healthy relationships and fostering peace within the Islamic community.
