In the "Persaman" program on Radio Ma'aref, Professor Mohammadi Sharoudi addressed various theological questions from listeners. He refuted the notion of boredom in Paradise, emphasizing that it is a realm of constant evolution and fresh delights without monotony. For teaching abstract concepts to children, he recommended using simple analogies and consulting reliable, specialized sources. The pause between the two sermons in Friday prayers was explained as a way to distinguish them and insert a moment of remembrance. Regarding prostration on a prayer mat, he advised avoiding unusual shapes and using flat surfaces to prevent misinterpretation by opponents. Addressing the problem of evil, he clarified that differences stem from varying capacities, while evil is either non-existent or inseparable from good. He noted that removing any element from the creation's ecosystem would disrupt the entire system, and that hardships serve hidden wisdoms.
