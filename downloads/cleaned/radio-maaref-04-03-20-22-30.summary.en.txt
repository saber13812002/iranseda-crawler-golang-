The most crucial key to avoiding the pitfalls of this world is maintaining a correct perspective aligned with the Wilayat of Imam Ali.
Superficial popularity and worldly power hold no value without a genuine defense of justice and truth.
Life is like a minefield where one can only navigate safely by strictly following the guidance of divine authority.
All sins and injustices occurring globally stem from a flawed perspective and a distance from this spiritual leadership.
The race of life requires three conditions: aligning one's vision, actions, and outcomes with divine standards.
During Muharram and the 15th of Sha'ban, participating in Qadir and aiding the needy serves as a practical test of faith.
Seeking knowledge and acquiring spiritual awareness grants a form of life that distinguishes believers from the spiritually dead.
Ultimately, only by directing one's gaze toward God and His authority can one transcend worldly corruption and attain paradise.
