The program begins with a narrative of Imam Mahdi's birth, where Imam Ali (AS) keeps the child hidden from public view and orders a sacrifice to be distributed among the Banu Hashim. In the Q&A segment, the expert addresses public doubts about the Imam's supernatural powers in war, clarifying that while divine aid exists, the battle of reappearance follows natural logic and enemy submission rather than action-movie tropes. He also distinguishes between "unwarranted haste" and "accelerating the reappearance," explaining that prayers for a faster return mean hastening the necessary conditions rather than ignoring prerequisites. The broadcast concludes by introducing the book of memories of Azat Shahi, a revolutionary fighter.
