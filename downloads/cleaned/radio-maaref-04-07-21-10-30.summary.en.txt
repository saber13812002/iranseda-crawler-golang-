The program began with a health segment where Dr. Pourhosseini explained that acne is a sign of a healthy body expelling toxins, and suppressing it can damage internal organs. She emphasized the importance of a healthy diet, regular exercise, adequate sleep, and using rose water to manage skin issues. Subsequently, Hujjat al-Islam Rasoul discussed the ethical principle of not depriving people of their rights, citing verse 183 of Surah Shura. He used the story of a worker and a shopkeeper to illustrate that distancing oneself from God and fearing for one's livelihood leads to dishonesty and exploitation. Conversely, faith in God and reliance on Him guide individuals toward honesty and justice in their daily transactions and interactions. The speaker noted that this ethical principle extends beyond financial dealings to include all social interactions and defending the honor of others. Finally, he emphasized that the deep religious beliefs of the Iranian people protect the society from conspiracies and internal divisions.
