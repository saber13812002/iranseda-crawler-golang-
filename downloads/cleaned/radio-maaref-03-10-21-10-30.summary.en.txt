This radio program discusses the relationship between sincere hope for the future and acting in accordance with that hope.
A key point raised is that hope for a bright future should activate one's will, encouraging the experience of moral beauty in daily life and social transformation.
The program's expert, Hojjat al-Islam Jafari, answers a question about why the Imam of the Time is in occultation while previous Imams were not, despite people's injustice.
He explains that the absence of past Imams was necessary to preserve the religion and prevent its distortion in the lack of a present authority.
However, during the twelfth Imam's era, the existence of sufficient sources like hadiths and books ensures that his occultation does not lead to the destruction of the faith.
Listeners also participated in a Q&A session, stating that the duties of awaiters include maintaining visits and obeying the Imam's legitimate deputy.
The program concluded with Mahdavi prayers and expressions of hope for the appearance of Imam Mahdi.
