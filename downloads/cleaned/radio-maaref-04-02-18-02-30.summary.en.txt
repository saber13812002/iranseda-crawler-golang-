In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasizes the importance of night worship and the midnight prayer as a sign of a believer's dignity, citing narrations from the Infallibles. He also defines the honor of a faithful person as respecting others and avoiding causing them any harm. To illustrate the special status of the midnight prayer, stories are shared about the Supreme Leader's strict adherence to this ritual even during severe illness and difficult travels. The program also highlights the humble night prayers and spiritual weeping of Ayatollah Maleki Tabrizi and Sheikh Abbas Qummi. The main objective is to encourage listeners to stay awake during the night and fill those hours with prayer and supplication. The speaker urges the audience to avoid wasting their nights in sleep and to seize the spiritual benefits of the midnight and Fajr prayers. The segment concludes with the recitation of the "Ayuka fi al-Layl" supplication, asking God for guidance and closeness.
