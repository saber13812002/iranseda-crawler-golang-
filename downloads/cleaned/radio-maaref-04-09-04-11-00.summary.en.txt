The evening ceremony commemorating the martyrdom of Hazrat Zahra (SA) was held at Imam Khomeini's shrine with the presence of the Supreme Leader. Hujjat al-Islam Ali emphasized Hazrat Zahra's (SA) crucial role in preserving the front of truth and preventing deviation from the Wilayah. The Chief of Staff of the Armed Forces also expressed gratitude for the public's majestic attendance at the ceremony for unidentified war martyrs. The second part of the program featured religious rulings provided by various Maraji' in response to listeners' questions. Specific fatwas were issued regarding the funeral prayer, the recitation of the deceased's name, and correcting mistakes in prayer recitations. Additionally, rulings were clarified on performing wudu with eye kohl and the consumption of meat from non-preferred sacrificial animals. The program concluded with guidance on the permissibility of unintentionally hearing prohibited music and the parental duty to guide children away from harmful social media content.
