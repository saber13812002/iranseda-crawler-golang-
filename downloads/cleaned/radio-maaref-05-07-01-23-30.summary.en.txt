Ayatollah Khamenei responded to the US President's remarks by characterizing them as arrogance and bullying, asserting that such threats only strengthen the unity and resistance of the Iranian people. He emphasized that while Iran is open to dialogue, it will never accept a language of force. The program then transitioned to a dramatic retelling from the book "In the Ambush of the Red Flower," which chronicles the life of General Ali Siyazi Shirazi. The narrative depicts Ali's entry into the military academy and his confrontation with a battalion commander who discriminated against women wearing hijab. The company commander supported Ali by expressing pride in the hijab, thereby earning the young officer's trust. Later, a new major with Baha'i leanings attempted to prevent students from fasting during Ramadan by cutting off their pre-dawn meals. Ali insisted on observing the religious duty, and the other students joined him in solidarity. Witnessing their firm resolve, the major eventually reversed his order and allowed the students to fast.
