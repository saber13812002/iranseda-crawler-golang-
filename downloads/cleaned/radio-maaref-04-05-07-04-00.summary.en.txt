The radio program begins by highlighting the spiritual significance of seeking intercession through Imam Abbas, citing a personal account of miraculous healing. Subsequently, in the "Whispers of Rulings" segment, an expert outlines the jurisprudential conditions for holding Ta'ziyeh and Shabih-khani ceremonies. He emphasizes the necessity of avoiding falsehood, disrespecting the Ahl al-Bayt, and relying on authentic historical sources, based on fatwas from major clerics. The broadcast then addresses various fiqh questions, including the correct method of wiping the head during Wudu and its invalidators. It also clarifies the ruling on shaking hands with non-mahram individuals and the obligation to remain silent and attentive during Friday sermon speeches. Finally, the "Thought of the Sun" segment concludes by exploring Imam Khomeini's philosophical views on the essence of Tashahhud in prayer and its connection to divine monotheism.
