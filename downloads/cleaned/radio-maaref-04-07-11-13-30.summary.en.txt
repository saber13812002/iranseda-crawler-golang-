This radio program narrates the Hajj journey of a family led by Musa and Dawud, during which their mother, the servant of the Prophet's daughter, becomes separated from the caravan. After a fruitless search in the desert, the two young men encounter an elderly, exhausted woman who identifies herself as Fatima's servant. Upon hearing the names Muhammad and Dawud from the caravan leader, it is revealed that this woman is their lost mother, who had fallen behind due to physical weakness. After her reunion with the family, she recites Quranic verses to describe the grief and loneliness experienced after the Prophet's passing, highlighting Fatima Zahra's exalted status as part of the Prophet's body. The program concludes with a jurisprudential discussion on the necessity of addressing new economic and governmental challenges through Islamic law. It emphasizes the need for open jurisprudential forums and the production of scholarly works to guide intellectuals and the general public. The broadcast ends with an introduction to upcoming radio programs and a call to remain steadfast against enemy propaganda.
