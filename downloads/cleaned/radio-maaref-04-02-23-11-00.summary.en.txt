The Minister of Culture and Islamic Guidance proposed allocating paper subsidies to the government to reduce book printing costs. The National Post Company improved service quality, reducing the average delivery time for book shipments from 3.5 days to 2.5 days. The first specialized Tehran Literary Festival was held at the book fair, featuring publishers from 22 countries to enhance international cultural and economic interactions. The fair's spokesperson reported 173 billion tomans in book sales across physical and virtual sections by the fifth day. In the health sector, the Deputy Minister of Health announced a 7% decrease in births in 2024 compared to the previous year and the formation of a central committee to address population youth. The final stage of the national Quran recitation competition will be held in Sanandaj with participants from 110 countries. The commemoration ceremony for Martyr Khoodam will take place on May 16 at Imam Hossein Square, featuring a speech by General Salami. Additionally, various religious programs and the commemoration of martyrs buried in the Jamaran shrine are scheduled for the holy mosque.
