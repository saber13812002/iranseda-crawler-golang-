In this radio program, Dr. Mohammadi and Dr. Sadeghi discussed the legal and jurisprudential aspects of the crime of "ifsad fil-ard" (corruption on earth).
Article 286 of the Islamic Penal Code defines an "ifsad fil-ard" as one who commits widespread crimes such as disrupting public order or creating insecurity, punishable by death.
A key point in the discussion is the requirement of "widespread" impact and "intent to cause severe disruption," the determination of which is left to the judge based on social norms.
Unlike "moharebeh" (waging war against God) in Islamic jurisprudence, which requires ifsad fil-ard as a condition, the two crimes are defined independently in the Penal Code with subtle differences.
Due to the ambiguity regarding the extent of the crime, the advisory opinion of the Judiciary's Legal Department emphasizes that the limits must be determined by the judge according to the specific circumstances of each case.
The program concluded with the recitation and translation of verses 9 to 39 of Surah Tawbah, which emphasize the consequences of disbelief and the rewards for faith and righteous deeds.
This session clarified the legal boundaries of ifsad fil-ard and set the stage for a more detailed examination of its specific instances and differences from moharebeh in future meetings.
