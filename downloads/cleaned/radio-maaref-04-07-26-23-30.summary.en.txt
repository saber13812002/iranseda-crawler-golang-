Ayatollah Alidoust traces the origin of his surname to his father's deep affection for Imam Ali (AS) during the era of surname assignment. He recalls the simplicity of his family life and his father's financial support for his education and marriage during his youth. Emphasizing that he did not engage in typical teenage activities, he explains that he voluntarily dedicated his time to reading and studying. His memories of the revolution highlight the challenges of continuing secret Fiqh classes in back alleys due to martial law and the risk of arrest. He also contrasts the pre-revolutionary social environment, noting the prevalence of vice on roads and in public transport, with the post-revolutionary atmosphere. The program concludes with the recitation of Ziyarat Al-Yasin and religious songs focusing on the anticipation of Imam Mahdi's appearance. The final segment features quotes from Imam Khomeini regarding the power of the people and the revolution's emergence from nothing. This broadcast from Radio Saresari combines personal anecdotes, historical analysis, and religious content.
