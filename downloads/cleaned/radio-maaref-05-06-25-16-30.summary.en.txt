In this radio program, Ustad Mohammadpour emphasizes that a person's true worth lies in piety, faith, and knowledge rather than wealth or appearance, noting that mocking prophets and believers is an inevitable historical pattern. He explains that God does not rush to punish wrongdoers because the world lacks the capacity for complete divine justice, which is instead fully realized in the afterlife. The session addressed various theological questions, including the futility of repentance at the moment of death for disbelievers and the permissibility of marriage for Imam Mahdi after his reappearance. The speaker also clarified that Shia believers will be resurrected clothed, distinguishing this from the naked state of disbelievers. Furthermore, the concept of the "immortal soul" was defined as being independent of time and space, unlike material beings. The program concluded by interpreting the phrase "my father and mother" in supplications as a profound expression of love and devotion to the Ahl al-Bayt.
