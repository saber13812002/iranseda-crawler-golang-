The program began by announcing a special SMS system for Shiraz and reading heartfelt messages from listeners.
The first segment discussed life priorities, emphasizing the importance of family, earning a halal income, and above all, serving God and remembering the Imam of the Age.
Subsequently, Professor Falahati analyzed the vice of "shamat" (unjust criticism), identifying ignorance, hypocrisy, envy, and arrogance as its root causes.
He stressed that criticism is only virtuous when accompanied by etiquette such as gentleness, awareness, and adherence to religious boundaries.
Citing Quranic verses, the professor warned against repeating criticism and highlighted the necessity of supporting those who strive to reform others.
Finally, listeners were encouraged to abandon bad habits and recognize their own flaws to achieve an ethical life.
The program concluded with music and announcements for upcoming broadcasts.
