The head of the Hajj and Pilgrimage Organization reported that over 200,000 Iranian pilgrims performed Umrah recently, with approximately 50,000 women expected to join this year's Hajj.
For the first time in years, women have been appointed as assistants in large Hajj caravans on a trial basis to address the specific needs of female pilgrims.
The program also highlighted the significance of Imamzadehs and holy shrines as cultural hubs and sources of spiritual peace for the public.
The director of holy shrines noted that over 8,000 Imamzadehs in the country are managed by nearly 70,000 volunteers and religious groups under the organization's supervision.
The Karameh Foundation of the Astan Quds Razavi announced its initiatives to facilitate pilgrimages for needy families and its success in freeing over a thousand non-violent prisoners.
Commemorative ceremonies were held for the late scholar Sheikh Shoushtari and the 36th anniversary of the Sayed and Shahed operation in Ilam province.
In a spiritual segment, Hujjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized that true peace of mind stems from faith and remembrance of God, citing anecdotes about Imam Khomeini's composure.
The broadcast concluded with a fiqh expert explaining the recommended practices for Ihram and Umrah, while inviting listeners to participate in the spiritual activities of the Karameh Decade.
