The radio program "Zamzam Ahkam" began by discussing the prohibition of black market transactions and the rules regarding relatives.
In response to a question from followers of Ayatollah Sistani, Hujjat al-Islam Mahdavi explained that making up missed obligatory prayers during travel is permissible.
He clarified that if the missed prayer was originally performed during travel, it should be made up in its shortened form, whereas prayers missed while resident must be completed fully.
The program then shifted to a Quranic interpretation emphasizing the value of prayer and the specific attributes of true worshippers.
Subsequently, the "House of Friends" segment introduced the Imamzadeh Seyyed Mohammad shrine in Isfahan and featured interviews with local visitors.
The visitors described the spiritual atmosphere of the site as a source of peace and a refuge for overcoming loneliness and personal difficulties.
Listeners were reminded to always specify their religious authority when submitting questions to the program via phone or SMS.
The broadcast concluded with prayers for the audience's well-being and a reminder of the radio station's contact information.
