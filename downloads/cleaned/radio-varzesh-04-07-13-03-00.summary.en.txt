Morteza Founounizadeh shared his memories from his playing days at Pura and Persepolis, highlighting his collaboration with legendary coaches like Aliakbar Tafari and Hamid Derakshan. He recalled the peak of Iranian football in the early 1990s, including his participation in the Asian Cup and the 1994 World Cup qualifiers against teams such as Japan and Brazil. The former player also recounted personal anecdotes about financial support from veteran coaches that helped him buy his first home during his youth. Founounizadeh explained his motivation for participating in charity matches for veteran players, expressing joy at seeing prominent figures of Iranian football gather for these events. He expressed pride in working under knowledgeable Iranian coaches like Firooz Karimi and explained why he declined offers to join Persepolis' technical staff out of respect for the club's senior figures. Towards the end of the show, he discussed his youth career at Esteghlal and his transfer to Persepolis, reflecting on his memorable goals in the Tehran Derby.
