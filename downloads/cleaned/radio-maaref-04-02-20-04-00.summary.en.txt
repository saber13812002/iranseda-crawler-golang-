The first segment of the program explained Hajj rulings, noting that hurah (brisk walking) is recommended for men and does not require restarting if forgotten.
After sa'i, the fifth obligatory act of Umrah al-Tamattu' is taqsir, which involves trimming hair or nails to exit the state of ihram.
In response to legal questions, it was stated that dedicating the reward of voluntary prayers at home is permissible, and consent is required to use another's property.
For individuals with incontinence, using a containment bag and maintaining purity is advised for performing prayers.
The second segment emphasized the duty of enjoining good and forbidding evil, highlighting Ayatollah Baqir's bravery against government conspiracies as a model.
An example of this duty's impact was the story of a military officer who changed his behavior regarding beard shaving after a reminder from the Ayatollah.
Finally, from a Quranic perspective, the third theory regarding the primacy of society, which emphasizes a natural and spiritual social composition, was identified as the correct view.
