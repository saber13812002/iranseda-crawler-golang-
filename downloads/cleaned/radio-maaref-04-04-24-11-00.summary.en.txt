In the religious Q&A section, Hujjat al-Islam Siyahi emphasized the necessity of security inspections during sensitive times, deeming it obligatory when there is a potential threat.
While acknowledging the importance of privacy, he stated that public security takes precedence over personal privacy in cases of security threats.
Spreading rumors on social media was identified as one of the enemy's tactics to create public anxiety and disrupt social cohesion.
Religiously, reposting unverified news that causes fear or instability is considered haram and a serious crime.
People are obligated to maintain social calm by avoiding rumor-mongering and relying on official sources for information.
