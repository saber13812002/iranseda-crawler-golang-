In this radio program, the concept of "souvenirs" is explored from both spiritual and material perspectives within Islamic Iran.
The guests initially emphasized that the most valuable spiritual gifts, such as the Holy Quran, narrations, and the concept of Wilayat, are the finest offerings for Iranians.
The cultural and culinary diversity of Iran, from Kerman to other regions, was praised as a reflection of the country's authentic culture and hospitality.
Ayatollah Khamenei also sent a voice message suggesting that the most symbolic souvenir Iran can offer the world is a statue of martyr Hassan Tehrani Moghaddam accompanied by the Quranic verse "Say: My protectors and your protectors are Allah and His Messenger."
This message was presented as a spiritual and political gift to the world, highlighting Iran's power and dignity against global arrogance.
The final segment of the program, featuring Professor Mohammad Hadi Falah, focused on the consequences of stinginess according to the Quran and Hadith.
Professor Falah, citing verses from Surah Tawbah and Al-Imran, described stinginess in financial charity and even in love for God and the Ahl al-Bayt as a disease that leads to painful punishments in the afterlife, such as being branded with melted coins.
The overall conclusion of the discussion is a call for charity, kindness, and sacrificing personal interests for the sake of God and elevating the Iranian-Islamic culture globally.
