Ashur Golmohammadi, a veteran basketball player and coach, shared his sports memories in a radio interview. He recounted his playing career with teams like Homa and Azad University, highlighting their multiple championships in the Iranian Super League. Golmohammadi emphasized the crucial support from Azad University and the pivotal role of Hamed Haddadi in the golden era of Iranian basketball. After retiring from playing, he transitioned into coaching, working with clubs such as Pikan and Azad University. He also discussed the personal tragedy of losing his first wife and how his second wife helped him return to his professional life. He identified the youth team's Asian championship as the best moment in Iranian basketball history. Finally, he stressed the importance of staying in Iran to serve the sport and contribute to the nation's development.
