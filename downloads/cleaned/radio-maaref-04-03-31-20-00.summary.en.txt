This radio program discussed Iran's missile attacks on Israel and the resulting regional and global reactions. The Arab League and Turkey condemned Israel's aggression, while Iran's foreign minister emphasized the legitimacy of Iran's self-defense during a meeting in Istanbul. International media reported on Iran's robust air defense capabilities and the failure of Israeli systems to intercept ballistic missiles. Inside Israel, severe concerns were raised regarding the lack of shelters for citizens and soldiers, alongside heavy media censorship by officials. The desecration of Islamic holy sites, including the burning of the Quran by settlers in the West Bank, was also strongly condemned. The second part of the broadcast featured a documentary on the life and education of Ayatollah Mazarati, highlighting his deep influence from Imam Khomeini's teachings on guardianship and ethics.
