The radio broadcast began by emphasizing the paramount importance of prayer in Islamic texts and thanking those who promote it. The news segment announced the official agreement between Hamas and Israel to end the war in Gaza, which includes prisoner exchanges and the entry of humanitarian aid. It also reported on the simultaneous national Quran and ijtihad competition for students, where winners were selected across various religious and ethical categories. The head of the Public Libraries Organization stated that over 190,000 new books were distributed to public libraries nationwide to update their resources. The representative of the Supreme Leader at the Housing Foundation announced plans to construct 50,000 housing units for the underprivileged over the next three years and to reinforce rural homes. The fiqh section addressed several religious questions, including fasting for the sick, the method of wiping the head during wudu, the ghusl of the deceased, following an unknown imam, and the rules for paying zakat al-fitr. Finally, the program informed listeners that Friday prayers in Tehran would be held at the University of Tehran, followed by a nationwide march titled "Basharat Nasr" to mark the second anniversary of the Al-Aqsa Flood operation.
