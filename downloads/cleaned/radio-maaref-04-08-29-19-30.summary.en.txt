The radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat," featuring Hujjat al-Islam Fallah, examines the vice of mockery from the perspective of the Quran and Hadith. The scholar explains that mockery permeates all human relationships with God, oneself, and the environment, rooted in ignorance and a lack of reasoning. He emphasizes that mocking prayer and sacred matters is effectively mocking the entire Sharia and creation, which distances the individual from divine mercy. According to Quranic verses, ridiculing the call to monotheism, divine signs, and prophets is a characteristic of polytheists and hypocrites. God commands believers not only to refrain from mocking sacred matters themselves but also to avoid sitting with those who do so. Silence in the face of mocking divine signs places a person in the company of disbelievers and hypocrites on the Day of Judgment. Therefore, maintaining the sanctity of religious values and avoiding any jokes about faith is an essential duty for drawing closer to God.
