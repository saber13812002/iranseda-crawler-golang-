In this radio program, Professor Mohammadi analyzed the legal and Quranic concept of "ifsad fil-ard" (spreading corruption on earth), distinguishing it from similar crimes like moharebeh and bri. He explained that "ifsad" refers to creating widespread disorder in society and does not necessarily require national-scale disruption; even disturbing public order within a single city can qualify under this title. According to Article 286 of the Islamic Penal Code, this crime is established when actions cause severe disruption to public order, create insecurity, inflict significant damage to property or life, or spread corruption and immorality on a large scale. The professor emphasized that determining the extent and severity of such disruptions is a customary matter that must be assessed by a judge based on the specific circumstances of each case. The program also featured a short story about a hospital security guard saving a child and messages about performing small acts of service for the country. The broadcast concluded with a recitation and dramatic translation of verses from the Surahs of Hashr and Mumtahanah, which emphasized piety, avoiding friendship with enemies of the faith, and following the example of Abraham.
