In this radio program, the guests explored the concept of waiting for the Mahdi and the unique characteristics of the Shia belief in the Imam of the Time. They emphasized that, unlike other religions where the savior is vague or his arrival marks the end of the world, the Shia perspective identifies a specific figure whose emergence will initiate a long era of justice and happiness on Earth. The guest stressed the importance of deeply knowing the Imam and following his ethical principles to achieve spiritual growth and inner peace. He explained that life's hardships are divine tools designed to reveal personal weaknesses, awaken hidden talents, and break unhealthy dependencies. The ultimate goal of these trials is to cultivate resilient individuals who are prepared to cooperate with the Imam in establishing a just society. Finally, the program concluded that waiting is not a passive state but an active, continuous movement toward justice and the satisfaction of the Imam.
