In the radio program "Perso-Man Quran," Hujjat al-Islam Maleki addressed listeners' questions regarding Quranic verses and narrations.
The first inquiry concerned the angels' knowledge of future human corruption, which the expert explained through the existence of Jinn and human free will.
A key point was that God creates perfect individuals to establish a foundation for justice and divine mercy.
The second section examined Prophet Joseph's forgiveness of his brothers and the Prophet's (PBUH) pardon of his enemies during the Conquest of Mecca.
It was emphasized that the Prophet (PBUH) never initiated war and treated even his adversaries with kindness and benevolence.
The final question addressed Prophet Jacob's greater affection for Joseph, which the expert described as an involuntary response to the son's inherent virtues.
The discussion concluded that the conduct of the Prophets is rooted in mercy, forgiveness, and responding to evil with good.
