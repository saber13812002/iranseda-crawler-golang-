In this radio program, Dr. Ali Mohammadi and Dr. Ghulam Reza Pibandi discussed the principles governing Islamic criminal policy, with a specific focus on the principle of security. They emphasized that security, alongside justice and welfare, is a fundamental necessity for any society and holds a special place in Islamic narrations. Quranic exegetes have primarily interpreted the security mentioned in Surah Quraysh as physical safety, highlighting the importance of preserving human life in Sharia. According to religious teachings, ensuring security for all members of society is considered a primary duty of the Islamic government. Imam Ali (AS) also described creating security and peace for God's servants as the main objective of his assumption of power. Finally, public participation in obeying laws and cooperating with security forces was identified as the key to achieving sustainable peace and social progress.
