In this radio program, Saeed Hosseini introduces his colleagues and discusses the interpretation of verses 87 and 88 of Surah Al-Ma'idah by Ayatollah Javadi Amoli. The commentator references the story of the Companions of the Monastery, noting that Islam forbids monasticism and creating distance from God's creation. Ayatollah Javadi emphasizes that true asceticism does not mean abandoning the world, but rather avoiding sin and maintaining a reasonable, moderate lifestyle. Citing the verse "Do not make forbidden the good things that Allah has made lawful," he identifies the prohibition of lawful, pure items as a form of transgression and innovation. Additionally, transgression in worship and supplication, such as shouting in prayer or ignoring the rights of others, is condemned. The Prophet (PBUH) discouraged extremes in asceticism by modeling a simple and balanced life. The final conclusion is that piety is a comprehensive concept that must be practiced alongside worship and knowledge by adhering to moderation in social and family life.
