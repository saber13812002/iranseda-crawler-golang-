The first segment of the program discusses the moral and spiritual characteristics of Hazrat Zainab (SA), including her courage, wisdom, innate knowledge, loyalty to the Imam, and eloquence.
It is emphasized that despite the tragedies of Karbala, she remained a mourner but not depressed, demonstrating strong crisis management during the transfer of the captives to Sham.
In the second segment, the memories of Hamid Reza Jafari, a freed prisoner who was injured in the Muharram operation and captured by the Ba'ath regime, are recounted.
Mr. Jafari describes the hardships of interrogation, his temporary loss of vision, and his transfer to various prisons in Iraq.
He reports on the hunger strike and the breaking of cell doors by the prisoners to protest the lack of water and the pressure from the enemy.
During this protest, Iraqi forces intervened with violence, resulting in the martyrdom and injury of several freed prisoners.
This program highlights the steadfastness and resistance of the freed prisoners against the injustice and oppression during their captivity.
