The core message of this radio segment emphasizes that the Arbaeen pilgrimage is a unique opportunity for internal purification and acquiring spiritual capacity in the presence of Imam Hussein (AS).
The speaker criticizes the distraction of taking photos and videos, reminding listeners that the primary goal is heartfelt presence and spiritual understanding.
Citing religious narratives, he explains that the Imam's divine gaze can transform even the most insignificant beings into allies, making humans worthy of serving the Imam of the Time (AJ).
Arbaeen is further described as a school for education, purification from arrogance and hypocrisy, and a chance to serve other pilgrims.
The speaker stresses that this pilgrimage is an invitation to truth and awakening from ignorance for all humanity, not just Shia Muslims.
Finally, pilgrims are urged to seek the Imam's hand with humility and supplication to reach the station of true service and companionship.
