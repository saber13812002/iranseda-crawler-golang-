In the radio program "Shifa" on Radio Ma'aref, a traditional Islamic medicine expert discussed the root causes of fear and strategies to overcome it.
According to traditional medicine, fear may stem from heart weakness, a bilious temperament characterized by repetitive thoughts, a cold heart temperament, or heart dryness.
It was emphasized that identifying and addressing the root cause is more critical than merely treating the visible symptoms of fear.
In response to a listener's question about using deodorant to mask body odor, the expert strongly advised against it, citing risks of clogged pores and lymphatic issues.
Instead of using blocking agents, the recommendation was to address the underlying cause of body odor through lifestyle changes and internal detoxification.
The program concluded with excerpts from the late Dr. Bahonar, emphasizing the necessity of national unity based on Islamic principles and loyalty to the revolution.
These remarks stressed that true unity is only achievable through faith and service to Islam, rather than through group interests or political power.
Finally, listeners were encouraged to consult qualified medical professionals for proper treatment and to avoid ineffective self-medication.
