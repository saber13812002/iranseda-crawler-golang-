This radio program begins by addressing specific fiqh rulings related to Hajj, confirming that transferring sacrificial meat to the pilgrim's home country for the needy is permissible. It clarifies that performing the Jamarat throwing and sacrifice on the night of the 11th of Dhu al-Hijjah is valid, subject to certain precautions, and that shaving hair or cutting nails ends the state of Ihram, legalizing most prohibitions except for perfume and marital relations. The discussion then shifts to travel and fasting, noting that according to Ayatollah Khamenei, traveling less than 20.5 kilometers does not constitute a journey, thus keeping prayers and fasts intact. Regarding Khums, it is stated that paying an estimated amount without consulting the Marja is insufficient if there is a possibility that the actual Khums is higher. Other rulings include the permissibility of charging fees for notarizing documents, the validity of congregational prayer even if one participant's prayer is invalid, and the permissibility of fasting when in doubt about Janabah without visible signs. The program emphasizes the critical duty of religious propagation, citing historical examples of scholars who prioritized public preaching over academic teaching. Finally, the show introduces a series on Imam Khomeini's thoughts, focusing on the spiritual essence of prayer and the necessity of full presence of heart in worship.
