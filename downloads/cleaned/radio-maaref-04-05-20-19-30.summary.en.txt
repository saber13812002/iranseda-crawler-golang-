The program began with opening prayers and an announcement for the 20th specialized session for Quran reciters, focusing on the requirements of innovation in recitation, with a submission deadline of late September.
The main segment featured an interview with Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallah regarding the critical importance of guarding one's tongue and its role in maintaining physical, spiritual, and religious health.
Citing various narrations, Professor Fallah described the tongue as the key to both good and evil, emphasizing that true piety and faith cannot be achieved without controlling one's speech.
He referenced the hadith that "human health lies in the preservation of the tongue," warning that reckless speech can lead to war, bloodshed, and spiritual destruction.
The concept of "straightforwardness" was interpreted as the shortest and most direct path to reaching truth and God.
Professor Fallah advised Muslims to tame their inner demons and avoid sinning with their hands and tongues, similar to how Prophet Abraham dealt with his inner shaytan.
The program concluded by encouraging listeners to support the "Iran Empathetic" campaign for the people of Palestine using the provided bank account number.
