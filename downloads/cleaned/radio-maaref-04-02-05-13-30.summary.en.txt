The text interprets Imam Khomeini's views on the necessity of resisting global hegemony, particularly that of the United States. The Imam emphasizes that until the balance of power shifts in favor of Muslims, Western powers will ignore their rights and interests under various pretexts, such as protecting their own benefits. Consequently, the only viable solution is to strengthen scientific, military, and economic capabilities to reach the level of major world powers, thereby preventing the imposition of foreign will. In this regard, compromising with oppressors and expecting sanctions to be lifted by enemies are considered futile and ineffective paths. The Imam distinguishes between struggle and mere pursuit of luxury, asserting that officials must defend the revolution's principles and the rights of the oppressed rather than indulging in opulence. Furthermore, it is warned that internal and external currents aiming to hollow out the revolution's content must be identified and countered. Ultimately, the war against hegemony is a global ideological conflict without geographical boundaries, requiring a worldwide mobilization of Muslims to dismantle this unjust system.
