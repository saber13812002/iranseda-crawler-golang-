In this lecture, Ayatollah Jafari emphasizes that the Quran is the primary and ultimate source of human knowledge, examining the four fundamental relationships between humans and themselves, God, the world, and their peers. He argues that the most valuable final truths regarding these connections stem directly from divine revelation and pure human nature, surpassing all philosophical theories and literary works. By referencing the story of Sheikh Talaqani, he illustrates the role of spiritual insight and divine love in deeply understanding Quranic verses and transcending material history. The lecture further categorizes the driving forces of history into unconscious elements, such as personalities, and conscious ones, defined by human will and reliance on God. Jafari warns that modern industrialization and greed have alienated humans from themselves and God, leading to existential emptiness. Citing Quranic verses, he divides worldly life into "noble life" based on divine reliance and unstable life, while stressing both the innate and acquired dignity of humans. He concludes that the only path to truth and peace is following the Quran and monotheism, ending with a prayer for the guidance of young students and Muslims.
