In this Radio Ma'aref program, Seyyed Saeed Hosseini, relying on Ayatollah Javadi Amoli's tafsir, emphasizes the necessity of understanding the metaphysical realm and the limitations of purely material knowledge. By referencing Quranic verses, he argues that many advanced societies, despite their scientific progress, remain at a low level in understanding monotheistic concepts and divine decree, often resembling idolatry. The main discussion revolves around the concept of God's "Absolute Oneness" (Wahdat Qahira), which is neither numerical nor categorical but rather infinite and devoid of any partner or component. Drawing from Nahj al-Balagha and narrations, it is explained that God is free from all limitations, numbers, and divisibility, making the assumption of a second deity impossible. This profound understanding of monotheism, detailed in the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya and the words of Imam Ali (PBUH), eradicates the roots of polytheism and ignorance. Prophets, by presenting pure teachings and guiding humanity toward constructive work, liberate people from being confined to material sciences. Finally, the distinction between the numerical unity applicable to created beings and the absolute, unique unity of God is presented as the key to correctly understanding existence.
