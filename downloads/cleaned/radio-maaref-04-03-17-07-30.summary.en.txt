The radio program began by discussing the role of artificial intelligence in modern daily life and its associated challenges. The guest, Hojjat al-Islam Mohammadian Shahroudi, then explored the concept of happiness in religion and strategies for coping with sorrow. He emphasized that while one should not deny sadness, it is crucial not to be enslaved by it, suggesting a shift in mindset is necessary. Key strategies for maintaining happiness include distancing oneself from toxic individuals and focusing on small, achievable goals during difficult times. The use of psychological counseling when needed and expressing gratitude for God's blessings were also highlighted as essential keys to peace. Performing good deeds and helping others were recommended as sources of deep, spiritual joy. The program concluded by discussing the virtue of humility and its positive impact on society, ending with a recitation of Saadi's poetry and religious prayers.
