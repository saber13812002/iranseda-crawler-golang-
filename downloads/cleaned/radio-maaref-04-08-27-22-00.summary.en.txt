The news segment reported on the unveiling of the Supreme Leader's annotations on three books about the Sacred Defense and a meeting between Hajj officials and the UAE minister to enhance services. The UAE ambassador emphasized the strategic necessity of Islamic unity, while the secretary of the Population Youth Council warned of Iran's entry into a demographic crisis. Additionally, there was news of a 61% increase in charitable donations for the "Build a School" campaign and the introduction of a "Patience Bell" in schools. The program then introduced the 33rd Book Week, highlighting books as silent friends that liberate individuals from negative thoughts. In the "Story of Freedom" segment, veteran Mehdi Ghalafshani shared his six-year experience of captivity in Iraq and the resistance against enemy brainwashing. He recounted how the prisoners destroyed a television to prevent moral deviation and went on a one-week strike against the broadcast of vulgar songs during prayer times. Ghalafshani stressed that strict adherence to prayer and the Quran was the primary factor in preserving the identity and resilience of the freed prisoners amidst torture and harsh prison conditions.
