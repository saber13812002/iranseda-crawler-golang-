The radio program begins with a narration of Qasim ibn Muhammad's bravery and faith in the face of the Kufa army, highlighting his call to join Imam Hussein. Subsequently, the guest, Hujjat al-Islam Vahidi, emphasizes the Islamic virtue of avoiding fame, citing the life of Ayatollah Behjat as a prime example of humility. Anecdotes are shared about Behjat's refusal to accept titles, including an instance where he personally cut the word "Ayatollah" from a photograph. The second part of the show features Zahra Nasiri, the wife of martyr Javad Akbari, who recounts the agonizing three months of uncertainty after her husband's deployment to Syria. She describes the emotional toll of not knowing his whereabouts and shares a touching letter he wrote during his military training. Although Akbari promised not to return to the battlefield after his first deployment, his religious commitment led him to go again. The narrative illustrates the profound conflict between the hardships of family life and the ultimate sacrifice made in defense of the holy shrine.
