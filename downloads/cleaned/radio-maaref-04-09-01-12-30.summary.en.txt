In this Radio Ma'aref program, Dr. Naser Rafiei examines five moral characteristics of Hazrat Zahra and the Fatimi family based on the Quranic Surah Al-Insan. The first trait is keeping vows, illustrated by the family's three-day fast for the healing of Imam Hassan and Hussein, followed by distributing their breaking-fast bread to the needy. The second trait is the fear of the Day of Judgment, described as a powerful brake against sin and a source of inner peace. The third characteristic is altruism and feeding others, specifically giving away one's preferred food to the poor, orphans, and captives while remaining hungry oneself. The fourth trait is sincerity in good deeds, emphasizing the importance of concealing charity to avoid hypocrisy and ensuring the intention is purely for God. The fifth trait is the fear of the day when faces are gloomy, reflecting the dread of divine punishment and the hardships of the afterlife. The speaker highlights that both Shia and Sunni traditions recognize these virtues, noting that emulating them is the key to achieving God's pleasure and eternal peace.
