This program begins with a tafsir of verses 63 to 66 of Surah Al-An'am based on the views of Ayatollah Javadi Amoli, emphasizing the commitment of the grateful to God during times of hardship. The commentator explains that divine punishment encompasses various natural, artificial, and spiritual forms from above and below, which may manifest as internal enmity and discord among people. The discussion then addresses the theological debate of determinism and free will, noting that God grants humans the opportunity for repentance and punishment only occurs after persistent sin and exceeding the limits of divine mercy. The role of Satan as a "trained dog" operating within God's system to create fitna and enmity among humans is also elucidated. Furthermore, the necessity of deep understanding (tafaqquh) of the Quran through contemplation and diverse scientific and mystical methods is highlighted. Finally, citing hadiths, the Quran is presented as a source for uncovering the sciences of the past and future, urging the audience to delve into its meanings to attain wisdom and knowledge.
