The program begins with a Quranic interpretation emphasizing reliance on God in major decisions and the futility of worldly anxieties. The speaker argues, based on personal experience, that secular and false movements rely solely on media spectacle and fail to take root, whereas truth endures. The second part of the broadcast is dedicated to answering listeners' religious questions, covering rulings on making up missed prayers during travel, the permissibility of boiling date pits, and donating Hajj tickets to welfare organizations. The rulings on using donkey milk and the conditions for performing wudu in endowed mosques are also discussed. These answers are provided according to the fatwas of various Maraji, including Ayatollah Khamenei, Sistani, and Makarem Shirazi. Finally, the contact numbers and SMS codes for Radio Ma'aref are announced for further inquiries.
