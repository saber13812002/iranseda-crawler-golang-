Ayatollah Javadi Amoli argues in his tafsir of Surah Al-A'raf that Satan's respite does not last until the Day of Judgment, as he will also die in the Barzakh. However, Satan can still influence ordinary people within the realm of imagination and through dreams, though he is powerless against sincere and rational individuals who have reached intellectual detachment. He seeks to destroy communities by fostering internal hatred and conflict, which is considered the most severe divine punishment because it erodes both faith and national identity. The text clarifies that Satan's claim of being misled by God refers to "punitive misguidance," where God withdrew His grace after Satan's arrogance. Satan swears by God's might to attack humans from all four directions—front, back, right, and left—to divert them from the straight path. The solution to this deception is seeking refuge in divine monotheism and reciting God's names, which allows God to protect the believer and repel Satan. Finally, the text explains that Satan's "sitting" symbolizes his treacherous ambush, whereas human "standing" must represent firm resistance and moral integrity against these temptations.
