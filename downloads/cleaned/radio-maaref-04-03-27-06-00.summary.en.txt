Ayatollah Javadi Amoli categorizes the miracles of Prophet Jesus (PBUH) into scientific and effective types, emphasizing that all such acts, including creating birds from clay and reviving the dead, occur solely by divine permission. Through a detailed linguistic analysis of verses in the Surahs of Al-Ma'idah and Al-Imran, he demonstrates that the phrase "by the permission of God" is repeatedly used to ensure these powers are attributed to God rather than the Prophet himself. His interpretation further suggests that Christianity, like other heavenly religions, possessed a defensive and combative aspect, as the disciples were divinely inspired to renew their pledge of loyalty to support Jesus against Jewish conspiracies. The commentary highlights that prophetic miracles encompass not only foretelling the unseen but also healing congenital diseases and resurrecting the dead, all serving as manifestations of God's grace. Ultimately, Ayatollah Javadi argues, citing the Surah of As-Saff, that Muslims must emulate the disciples of Jesus by actively defending the faith during critical times rather than merely enduring oppression.
