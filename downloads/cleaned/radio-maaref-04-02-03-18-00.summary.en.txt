The radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat," featuring Hujjat al-Islam Mohammad Hadi Fallah, introduced a new ethical discussion centered on anger and wrath.
The guest categorized anger into two types: sacred anger in the path of God and profane anger rooted in the ego and desires.
He noted that the Quran frequently mentions wrath, primarily describing those who have angered God through their deviant beliefs and immoral actions.
Fallah warned that in modern society, anger and aggression have become a primary behavioral trait, often unjustifiably masked by religious or national pretenses.
Citing Quranic verses, he identified anger as a significant spiritual obstacle that must be actively managed and controlled.
The second segment analyzed a hadith from Imam Sadiq (AS) in the book of Kafi, emphasizing that deeper knowledge of God naturally leads to greater reverence and fear of Him.
The final conclusion stressed that a true believer must focus their life's calculations solely on God's pleasure, as seeking social status or public approval is incompatible with a heart that truly fears God.
