This radio program provided jurisprudential answers to several listener questions. According to Ayatollah Sobhani's fatwa, a husband may pay the Zakat al-Fitr of his wife and children to his mother, but he is prohibited from paying it to his own parents or children. Reciting the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya in the Qunut of the Eid prayer is also considered permissible. Regarding participation in mourning ceremonies, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli stated that performing Wudu is recommended but not obligatory, and omitting it does not constitute a sin. For extending the duration of Ruku and Sujud in prayer, reciting the specific supplications "Subhan Rabbi al-Azim" and "Subhan Rabbi al-A'la" is sufficient. The program concluded by sharing contact numbers for submitting further questions. Subsequently, segments of religious rituals and morning prayers were broadcast. This show is part of Radio Ma'aref, which aims to provide religious education and address theological doubts.
