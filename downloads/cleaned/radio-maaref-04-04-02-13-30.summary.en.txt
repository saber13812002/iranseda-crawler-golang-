In this radio program, host Saeed Hosseini discusses recent regional developments with Dr. Zahroond, a member of the Parliament's National Security Commission. Zahroond asserts that the United States has been at war from the beginning, characterizing recent strikes as tactical moves to reduce Iran's nuclear capabilities rather than a full-scale war. He identifies the enemy's ultimate goal as the collapse and fragmentation of Iran, following a Libya-style model, which he believes has been thwarted by the leadership's wise policies. According to him, Iran must maintain its strategic will and national interests, refusing to let hegemonic powers dictate its fate while delivering powerful responses. The discussion also highlights recent intelligence and security failures, emphasizing the need to reassess social engineering and internal discourse management. Zahroond warns that the enemy relies on crisis creation for survival, urging Iran to reduce its vulnerabilities through full readiness. The segment concludes by stressing the importance of international condemnation of aggression and the need for sustained security in the region. Finally, listeners are encouraged to stand firm in their respective positions by adhering to the Supreme Leader's directives.
