In this radio program, the birth of Hazrat Abdol-Azim al-Hasani is celebrated while highlighting the pivotal role of women and youth in the end times. The speaker argues that while a satanic culture based on individualism targets these groups, Islam transforms them into the strongest pillars of resistance through unity and love. The discussion outlines three governance paths, identifying the family as the primary model for the ultimate divine society through its foundation of affection. Women play a crucial role in this structure by fostering emotional cohesion within the home, acting as the mortar that binds the family unit together. This hidden, non-public role is considered more significant than public visibility, as it cultivates deeper devotion and earlier spiritual maturity in girls. Ultimately, maintaining this familial harmony and avoiding fragmentation is presented as the key to defeating the enemy's plots and realizing the promised Wilayat-based civilization.
