The first part of the broadcast recounts memories of Imam Khomeini and the Battle of Karbala 4, emphasizing that the primary goal was obedience to God and achieving paradise rather than mere military victory. The Imam believed that in the path of truth, both victory and martyrdom are successes, and one should not assign value to worldly power. This revolutionary generation, despite harsh conditions and a constant awareness of death, possessed a unique peace and fought solely for God. The second part discusses the interpretation of verse 159 of Surah An-Nisa regarding the faith of the People of the Book. Some commentators argue that this faith occurs during the reappearance of Imam Mahdi, while others suggest it happens at the moment of death but is futile because the obligation has ceased. The discussion also references narrations stating that humans see the Prophet and the Infallibles at the moment of death, with scholars debating whether this vision is physical or intellectual.
