This news segment reported on the condolence messages from Iran's Supreme Leader and other clerics following the death of Ayatollah Sistani's wife, alongside the closing ceremony of the seminaries' podcast festival which honored 16 selected works. The first major legal action by Iran was also announced, involving over 2,000 lawsuits filed against the Zionist regime and the United States to compensate for the damages of the imposed war. A special program was held at the National Museum of the Islamic Revolution to honor women who were active in the defense of the country during the war. The first anniversary of Sayyid Hassan Nasrallah's martyrdom is scheduled to be commemorated nationwide under the slogan "We are the people of Ahl al-Bayt." In the second part, clerics answered listeners' religious questions regarding the rulings on mobile phone top-ups, the sale of a deceased person's property, and the conditions for tayammum when water is unavailable. They also clarified the priority of performing the missed Fajr prayer before the Zuhr and Asr prayers. Finally, guidance was provided on the proper method of performing the prostration in the Ashura ziyarat.
