The Supreme Leader's message to Iranian pilgrims emphasized the necessity of defending Palestine and condemned the crimes of the Zionist regime and the United States in Gaza.
Iranian pilgrims were transported to the Plain of Arafat by bus, where the rituals of prayer and standing are currently underway.
Simultaneously, the Arfa prayer ceremonies are being held in mosques and shrines across the country, including the Imam Reza Shrine and the Hazrat Masoumeh Shrine.
At the Shalamcheh border, approximately 30,000 pilgrims cross daily to join the spiritual ceremonies in Karbala.
The commemoration of the 1963 uprising on Khordad 15 was held at the Hazrat Masoumeh Shrine with public participation and speeches.
Citizens can participate in purchasing sacrificial animals for the needy by dialing the short code 3333123333.
The radio program "Man Gadiram" focused on the concept of Wilayah and the martyrs of the Khordad 15 uprising, inviting listeners to call in and participate in a contest.
The main themes of the discussion linked the standing at Arafat with supporting the oppressed in Palestine and recalling the Islamic Revolution's roots in the 1963 uprising.
