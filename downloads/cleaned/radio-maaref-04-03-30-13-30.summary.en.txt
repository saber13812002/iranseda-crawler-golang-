This radio program examines the historical conflicts in the Islamic world, attributing them to economic disparities, Arab-centric racism, and cultural innovations. Imam Ali (AS) challenged the monopoly of power and social discrimination by distributing public funds equitably and rejecting ethnic prejudice. He fought against religious distortions and returned to the Quran and Sunnah to defend the authenticity of Islam while advocating for the rights of non-Arab converts. Imam Baqir (AS) defined the authority of Ali as synonymous with establishing unity and justice, which are essential for the spiritual revival of believers. The Prophet (PBUH) predicted that Islam might become an inverted cloak where the positions of good and evil are swapped. The discussion criticizes social, moral, and political deviations in the Islamic community, highlighting the rise of inequality and hypocrisy. Finally, Nahj al-Balagha is presented as a shared resource for all Muslims, revealing the unparalleled virtues of Imam Ali (AS) that remain evident even to his adversaries and allies.
