The UN Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs reported that Zionist settlers attacked Palestinians in the West Bank 264 times last month, resulting in 30 deaths and the destruction of thousands of olive trees. In Gaza, military strikes by the Zionist regime on southern and eastern areas have led to the destruction of homes and civilian casualties. Zionist military officials have warned that their army faces an unprecedented strategic risk due to manpower shortages and requires 12,000 new soldiers. In Lebanon, drone attacks by the Zionist regime on vehicles in the south continue, while the Lebanese President emphasized the need for full implementation of UN Security Council resolutions. Iran's Ministry of Foreign Affairs released its annual report on human rights violations in the US and UK, holding both countries responsible for aggression and the killing of innocent people. In Iraq, the first phase of parliamentary elections concluded with high participation from security forces, paving the way for general elections. Zaghoul Ragab Adnajar, a prominent Egyptian scholar and pioneer in explaining the scientific miracles of the Quran, passed away at the age of 92. Additionally, the 13th National Orphans Week campaign launched in Iran, and the 22nd International Conference of Husayni Servants is being held in Mashhad.
