Ali Shouri, a former football player, shared his career highlights in a radio interview, recalling his time at Esteghlal and Bank Melli. He explained that he joined Bank Melli to secure a marriage loan and eventually left professional football due to financial constraints and starting a family. Now serving as the manager of the Esteghlal Veterans' Association, Shouri discussed forming a veteran team in the 1980s and organizing friendly matches against teams like Artists and Persepolis. He expressed gratitude for the financial support from the Esteghlal club, which helps fund the association and create a social space for former players. Shouri emphasized that his primary goal is to ensure the physical and mental well-being of these veterans while addressing their financial difficulties. He also announced efforts to establish a dedicated office for the veterans and to regularly host friendly matches with fan participation. Finally, Shouri stressed the importance of recognizing veterans as valuable assets to Iranian football and the need for continuous institutional support.
