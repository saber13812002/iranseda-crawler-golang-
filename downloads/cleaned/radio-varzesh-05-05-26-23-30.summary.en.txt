The discussion highlights the challenges facing wrestling in Tehran and the decline in major cities due to social issues and a lack of well-paid coaches. Former national team head coach Mohsen Kabeh recounted his experiences with various coaching roles and the political changes within the federation. He expressed his dissatisfaction with being appointed as a technical director instead of head coach in 2008 and his subsequent parting ways with Rostam Khadem due to differing philosophies. Kabeh considered the national team's performance at the Paris Olympics to be improvable, noting the high potential of wrestlers like Amirali Azizi. He identified Hassan Yazdani as Iran's best wrestler and a moral role model. The conversation also touched on the emergence of exceptional talents like Rezaem Omoozati, who rose from the junior ranks to world champion. Finally, Kabeh emphasized the importance of champions serving the community and expressed hope for the future improvement of the country's sports landscape.
