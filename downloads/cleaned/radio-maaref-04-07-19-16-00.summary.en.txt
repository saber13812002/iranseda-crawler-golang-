In the radio program "Porsoman-e Quran," Hujjat al-Islam Maleki addressed listeners' questions regarding Quranic concepts. He defined the "middle nation" as the moderate Islamic community that avoids the extremes of materialism or otherworldliness. The scholar explained that the Quran's writing and memorization were entirely under the supervision of revelation and the Prophet's approval. He presented two views on the arrangement of surahs, either during the Prophet's lifetime or by Zayd ibn Thabit's committee. Maleki emphasized that the Quran guides the pious, and interpretive differences stem from personal bias or a lack of knowledge in Arabic and hermeneutics. He interpreted God's "gratitude" as bestowing abundant rewards for human beings' minor deeds. Finally, he highlighted the subtleties of other divine attributes like "The Accepter of Repentance" and "The Giver of Security," stressing the need for proper foundational knowledge to understand the verses correctly.
