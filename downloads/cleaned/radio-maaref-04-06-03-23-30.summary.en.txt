In this radio program, a parenting expert addresses two common concerns raised by parents. Regarding a seven-month-old infant who cries to sleep in their parents' arms, the expert advises strengthening verbal and visual communication to reduce exclusive reliance on physical contact. Using age-appropriate toys, such as balls or cloth dolls, is also recommended as a comforting alternative. The core strategy involves balancing the four communication channels: visual, auditory, verbal, and tactile. For the second question about a two-year-old child who has developed a habit of playing with their father's mobile phone, the expert emphasizes that young children are primarily imitators. Therefore, parents must model appropriate behavior by avoiding phone use in the child's presence, especially during face-to-face interactions. Establishing "no-phone zones" in the home and refraining from answering calls while talking to the child are key solutions to correcting this habit.
