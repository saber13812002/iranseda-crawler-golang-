The first segment of the program discussed internal challenges and moral dualities as opportunities for personal growth, emphasizing the need to reinterpret grand theories in academic and media spaces. In the "Love Story" section, the wife of martyr Dr. Ali Mohammadi recounted their 1982 marriage and shared religious values. She described his academic journey at Sharif University, where he chose to stay in Iran rather than accept a scholarship in the United States to pursue physics. Dr. Mohammadi, who became the first PhD graduate in physics in Iran, was known for his intense focus and for mentoring colleagues at the University of Tehran. The wife shared memories of their Hajj pilgrimage, which had a profound spiritual impact on him, leading to increased devotion after 2004. The program concluded with a call for individuals to perform small but meaningful acts to contribute to national peace and social well-being.
