The program begins with a critical analysis of Reza Pahlavi, detailing his obscure origins and aggressive behavior during his youth in the Cossack brigade. Reza is portrayed as a tool for suppressing the Constitutional Revolution and besieging Tabriz, playing a key role in the killings of Star Khan, Bagher Khan, and Sheikh Muhammad Khabir with the support of Russians and British. The speaker emphasizes the historical distortion by various political and Western currents, noting that the revolution's leaders, such as Star Khan, entered the jihad with religious motivations and the fatwas of clerics. The second part of the program presents a dramatic retelling from the book "Neshan-e Hassan," which explores Imam Hassan Askari's presence at the event of Karbala. In this narrative, Imam Hussein (PBUH) speaks about the corruption of the world and the hypocrisy of people at the Zuha camp, while his companions, including Qasim ibn Muhammad, pledge their loyalty. The story concludes with the sighting of the enemy caravan and the preparation for battle, filled with fear and hope for victory in the path of truth.
