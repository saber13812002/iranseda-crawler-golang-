In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Fala examined the Quranic reasons for divine anger, identifying deviation from the straight path, denial of monotheism, and having bad thoughts about God as primary causes. Citing a holy hadith, he emphasized that humans must accept divine laws to allow God to determine their well-being, noting that suspicion of God is a sign of hypocrisy and polytheism. He also highlighted that baseless arguments against revelation and the Ahl al-Bayt, along with envy toward their spiritual and political status, are roots of disbelief leading to severe punishment. In the second segment, Professor Tabatabai explained the jurisprudential rulings of Hajj, stating that its obligation requires financial, physical, and logistical capability. He clarified that if a capable person delays Hajj, the duty becomes permanent, and their heirs must send a proxy to perform it after their death. The program concluded with prayers for the listeners and a tribute to Imam Reza (AS).
