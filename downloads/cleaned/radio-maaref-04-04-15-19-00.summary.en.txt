The program began by emphasizing the need for moderation in Hussaini mourning rituals, warning against extremes such as self-harm or unconventional behaviors that diminish the dignity of these gatherings.
In the fatwa section, it was ruled that consulting a doctor of the opposite gender is permissible when no same-gender option is available and the treatment is medically necessary.
Additionally, washing the face with the left hand during wudu and receiving bank interest based on Islamic contracts like Murabaha were declared valid and halal by the consulted clerics.
Hojjat al-Islam Mollanouri then discussed the reasons for youth disengagement from mosques, highlighting the importance of providing adequate facilities and adopting democratic management styles.
He identified disorder in programming, repetitive content, and a lack of attention to the specific needs of groups like women and teenagers as key factors reducing participation in religious activities.
Finally, the spiritual significance of prayer was underscored, citing the Prophet's description of it as a source of continuous spiritual pleasure and a direct connection with God.
The broadcast concluded by inviting listeners to submit their religious questions and to continue following the content of Radio Ma'aref.
