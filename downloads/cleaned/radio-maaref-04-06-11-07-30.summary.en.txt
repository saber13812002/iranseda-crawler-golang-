In this radio program, the speaker emphasizes the importance of remembering God and awaiting the Mahdi, identifying mockery of believers, distraction by blessings, and Satan's whispers as the main causes of forgetting God. The discussion then shifts to interpreting hadiths regarding backbitting, which is described as the sustenance of the ego and Satan. According to these narrations, those who backbite and listen to it will be resurrected in the form of animals like pigs, effectively feeding on the honor of others. Imam Ali (AS) characterized backbitting as the act of the weak and incapable, whereas speaking well of others is a sign of strength and self-mastery. It is highlighted that backbitting is worse than adultery because while adultery can be forgiven through repentance, backbitting violates the rights of others and remains unforgiven unless the victim personally forgives the offender. Ultimately, backbitting is portrayed as a destructive act that severs one's relationship with God and society, holding no value in the divine system and being considered one of the lowest moral failings.
