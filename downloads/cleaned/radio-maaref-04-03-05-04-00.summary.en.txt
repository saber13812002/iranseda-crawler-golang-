In the first segment, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli ruled that audio and digital media containing the Quran are pure, provided the physical medium itself is clean and sacred names are not written directly on it with a marker. Other legal rulings clarified that specific Quranic surahs must be recited in pairs during prayer, and a follower who recites "Bismillah" in the first rakaat should perform the prostration of caution. The program also stated that soap residue on washed clothes does not render them impure, and excessive worry about purity is discouraged. Financially, receiving a fixed return on an investment without sharing the business risk was deemed usury and prohibited. Finally, the second segment utilized Nahj al-Balagha to describe Satan as humanity's open enemy, identifying self-admiration, envy, and arrogance as the primary pathways for his influence and the main tools of his deception.
