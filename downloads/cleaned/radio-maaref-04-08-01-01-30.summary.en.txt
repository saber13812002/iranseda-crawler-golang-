This text combines a speech by Martyr Bahشتی on the principles of Imamate with the testimony of Nafiseh Kermani, wife of Martyr Ali Aghazadeh. Bahشتی emphasizes that an Imam must lead from the front while remaining within the community, allowing the people to see and emulate him. He stresses the necessity of establishing moral, social, and economic justice in the Islamic Republic as the legacy of the prophets. The second part recounts Kermani's memories of her husband's martyrdom in Syria, highlighting her spiritual peace and the support of friends during that difficult time. She also describes the challenging process of transporting her husband's body via Latakia due to the bombing of Damascus airport.
