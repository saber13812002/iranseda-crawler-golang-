Ali Hamoudi, a former Iranian footballer, recounted his experiences playing for Esteghlal Tehran and Sepahan in a radio interview. He admitted that he received no salary during his two years at Esteghlal due to the club's financial issues and lack of sponsors. Hamoudi described the financial hardships he and his family faced, including taking out a loan to travel to his hometown. He also clarified that he received a military exemption card due to a congenital medical condition, which led to a period of service with Tractor Tabriz. The former player criticized the corruption and prevalence of match-fixing in Iran's second division, noting that players there were often approached with illicit offers. Hamoudi emphasized that he always had direct offers from clubs at the peak of his career and did not rely on agents. He concluded by expressing pride in his national team appearances alongside other prominent stars of that era.
