The spiritual connection with the Imam is described as the "rope of God," a divine mercy that elevates human relationships from mere acquaintance to deep brotherhood and self-sacrifice. In this perspective, an individual's wealth and life are no longer their own but are dedicated to the Imam and the service of the Ahl al-Bayt. A narration from Imam Sadiq (AS) illustrates that true Shi'a worthiness involves being ready to sacrifice material and spiritual assets without expectation of reward or even the other party's knowledge. When Muslims gather around the Imam, envy and greed disappear, transforming their living environment into a terrestrial paradise. Conversely, distancing oneself from the Imam allows Satan to infiltrate, leading to a loss of spiritual purity and the corruption of pure relationships into hostility. The principle of Wilayat is thus based on mutual love and obedience born from affection, creating a society where all resources are shared for the benefit of one another and the Imam of the Time (AJ).
