In this radio program, host Seyed Teyd Hosseini interviews Fatemeh Zahra Harb, a social activist from Gaza, about the resilience of the Palestinian people. Ms. Harb shares the martyrdom of her brother and father, a university professor and poet, and discusses their view that liberating the holy land is a Muslim right. She describes the harsh living conditions in Gaza, including the lack of medicine, hunger, and the psychological pressure on families, especially after the loss of loved ones. She also highlights the daily violations by Zionists in the West Bank, such as evictions and the imprisonment of children. Despite widespread destruction, the people of Gaza remain hopeful and are willing to sacrifice their blood to liberate Palestine. In the second part of the show, the discussion shifts to making the import of essential goods competitive in Iran to control inflation and reduce the burden on people's livelihoods. The proposal to use electronic cards to stabilize the prices of ten essential items and to secure gas supplies early for winter is also examined.
