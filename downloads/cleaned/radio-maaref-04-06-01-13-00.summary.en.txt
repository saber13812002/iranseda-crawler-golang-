In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Rafiei discusses the social and ethical conduct of Imam Hasan (AS), emphasizing the importance of addressing people's needs. Citing narrations, he highlights the martyrdom of Imam Hasan (AS) and the mourning of angels and all creatures for him. Examples of the Imam's practical actions, such as helping debtors, bargaining to buy more for the poor, and giving gifts to wealthy visitors, are shared to illustrate his nobility and generosity. Stories of his effective condolence messages and resolving people's issues during I'tikaf are also mentioned, showing the priority of alleviating public suffering over personal worship. The speaker urges officials and managers to follow the Imam's example by setting up service desks in mosques to address citizens' problems. Finally, the definition of politics from Imam Hasan's (AS) perspective is presented as respecting the rights of God, the living, and the dead. The discussion concludes with a reference to the Imam's martyrdom by poison at the age of forty-eight, leaving behind young children.
