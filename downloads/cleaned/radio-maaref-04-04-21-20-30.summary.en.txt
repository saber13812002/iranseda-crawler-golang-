The radio program begins by examining the resistance of clerics against Reza Shah Pahlavi's policies of forced unveiling and religious suppression.
Researchers highlight the exile of Ayatollahs Qomi and Ardabili and the bloody clashes at Goharshad Mosque in Mashhad as key examples of this defiance.
The discussion then shifts to the memoirs of Sayed Ali Ajaghi, a POW captured during the Khorramshahr operation.
Ajaghi recounts the arduous journey across the Hoveyzeh marsh and the martyrdom of his comrades in the initial moments of the battle.
He describes the harsh conditions of captivity, severe torture, and the enemy's attempts to strip prisoners of their faith, drawing parallels to the oppression of the Ahl al-Bayt.
The veteran also explains how Iranian prisoners used wit to answer interrogation questions, preventing the disclosure of military intelligence while preserving their lives.
Finally, the program emphasizes the steadfastness and freedom of these warriors in the face of the enemy's physical and psychological pressures.
