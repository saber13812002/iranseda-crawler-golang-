Sadegh Shirzad, a pioneer of track and field in Iran, discusses his pivotal role in establishing the country's mass sports and walking systems. He highlights how inviting the World Federation's walking committee chair and training graduates helped spread this sport nationwide. Shirzad criticizes the lack of proper infrastructure, noting that parks and bike lanes are often overcrowded or converted into parking lots. He shares memories of participating in the Asian Games and collaborating with federation presidents, particularly Qavam-oddin Shahkar. The guest emphasizes the need for direct partnerships between athletes and industrial companies to secure funding and professionalize the sport. He cites successful examples of private investors building stadiums to advocate for this model in Iran. Finally, Shirzad briefly mentions his family background and expresses wishes for health and continued support for the sports community.
