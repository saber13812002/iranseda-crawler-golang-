In this radio program, Professor Mohammad Hadi Falah discusses the vice of stinginess and the virtue of generosity. Citing Quranic verses, he emphasizes that all material and spiritual blessings are divine favors, and no one has an inherent right to them. Stingy individuals mistakenly believe that hoarding wealth benefits them, but the Quran states that such wealth is impermanent and will become a heavy burden for them on Judgment Day. Conversely, those who practice generosity gain eternal rewards and become beloved to God by sacrificing temporary material goods. The Quran in Surah Muhammad warns that God will replace those who obstruct the path of generosity with those who actively practice it. Therefore, humans should convert their fleeting worldly assets into lasting capital for the afterlife through charitable acts. The ultimate goal is to cultivate a soul adorned with the virtue of giving and to avoid spiritual diseases like stinginess.
