The program begins by interpreting the first sermon of Nahj al-Balagha, emphasizing the Prophet Muhammad's (PBUH) role in perfecting the concept of prophethood.
Imam Ali (AS) highlights three fundamental characteristics of the Prophet: his foreknowledge, the fame of his signs, and the nobility of his birth.
Regarding foreknowledge, Quranic verses are cited stating that God had taken a covenant from previous prophets to believe in Islam.
The fame of his signs indicates that the Prophet's name and attributes were recorded in the Torah and the Gospel, making him recognizable to Jews and Christians.
Despite this recognition, some people of the Book concealed the truth and refused to accept his message.
The nobility of his birth is interpreted in two ways: the greatness of his parents and the miraculous events that occurred at his birth.
These miracles included the breaking of idols, the extinguishing of the fire temple, and the drying of a lake, symbolizing the end of tyranny and polytheism.
The program concludes with prayers for the Ahl al-Bayt and a tribute to the martyrs of the Sacred Defense.
