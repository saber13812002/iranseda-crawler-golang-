The radio program focuses on theocentric ethics and the conduct of scholars, emphasizing the Quranic imperative of charity and sending rewards to the hereafter.
Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi illustrates practical models of simple living and supporting the poor through stories of Akhund Khorasani and Hajj Anjafi.
In the second segment, "theocentric ethics" is defined as divine education, with prayer and sending blessings upon the Prophet and his family presented as the best tools for spiritual connection.
Responses to listener questions provided practical solutions for overcoming lack of patience through focusing on monotheism and establishing life order.
To gain high ambition, relying on God's infinite power rather than personal limitations is recommended, citing the stories of Moses and Joseph.
The program aims to create fundamental changes in a person's inner and outer self through knowing God and following the path of the Infallibles.
Listeners are encouraged to traverse the path of divine education by sending blessings and practicing prayer to benefit from material and spiritual blessings.
Finally, it is emphasized that the only way to escape inner turmoil and find peace is to focus entirely on God and follow religious commandments.
