This radio program examines the sharp rise in cybercrimes and frauds associated with bank ATMs.
The primary offenses in this domain include theft, breach of trust, and fraud, which disproportionately target lower-income demographics.
From a jurisprudential and legal perspective, theft is categorized into three types: Had (fixed punishment), Ta'zir (discretionary), and cyber-theft.
Investigations in these cases are complex and time-consuming due to criminals' use of third-party bank accounts and sophisticated money transfer methods.
A key condition for Had theft is that the stolen amount must reach the Sharia threshold, equivalent to four and a half gold dinars.
If multiple small thefts are considered a single act by custom within the same time and place, and the total reaches the threshold, Had punishment applies.
Cyber-fraud is defined in Article 741 of the Islamic Penal Code, encompassing scams via fake SMS messages and fraudulent websites.
Preventing these crimes and raising public awareness are considered more critical than post-crime legal proceedings.
