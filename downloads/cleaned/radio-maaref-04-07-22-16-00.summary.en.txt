In this radio program, Professor Sharoudi addressed various theological and religious questions from the audience. He began by emphasizing the value of knowledge and intellect as the most beautiful attributes of human beings. Regarding visiting the graves of parents, he recommended reciting specific Quranic verses and surahs, such as Al-Mulk and Ya-Sin, along with charity and supplication for forgiveness. He also advised moderation in meat consumption, warning against both excess and extreme avoidance, while highlighting the virtue of helping those in need. The professor refuted the concept of fatalism, affirming that humans possess free will in their worldly decisions. He further explained that the second corruption of the Israelites likely refers to their current widespread oppression and injustice. Finally, he distinguished between true love and affection, which are virtuous, and base, instinctual love, clarifying that the word "love" itself is not cursed, but its misuse is condemned.
