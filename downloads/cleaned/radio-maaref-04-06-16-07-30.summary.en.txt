In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Fozooli emphasizes the concept of Tawakkul, advising young people to overcome anxiety and indecision by combining practical effort with heartfelt trust in God. Citing Quranic verses and narrations from the Infallibles, he clarifies that true reliance on God does not mean passivity but rather pairing action with inner peace. The second part of the show addresses the vice of backbiting, highlighting its severe consequences, such as the rejection of worship for forty nights. Several solutions are proposed to combat this vice, including gaining awareness, avoiding gatherings where backbiting occurs, covering the faults of others, and seeking forgiveness. The importance of patience and remembering God during moments of weakness is also stressed to protect individuals from spiritual harm. The program concludes with congratulations on the birth of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH), the announcement of a contest winner, and a final prayer for the listeners.
