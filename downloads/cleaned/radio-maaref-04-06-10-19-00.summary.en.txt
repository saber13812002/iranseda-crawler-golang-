The radio program began with religious prayers and congratulations for the start of Imam Zaman's imamate, followed by responses to listeners' jurisprudential questions. The host strongly advised against displaying photos of deceased individuals at mourning ceremonies, particularly if they are unveiled or if it might lead to disrespect. Regarding doubts about the impurity of automatic washing machines, the speaker recommended avoiding obsessive behavior and assuming the clothes are clean. It was explained that frequent travelers who are away from home for more than 16 days a month on a recurring basis are not considered travelers and must perform full prayers and fasts. The program also confirmed that paying blood money on behalf of a deceased person is legally valid and accepted. The broadcast concluded with prayers for the health and early appearance of the Imam, along with solidarity for pilgrims visiting holy shrines.
