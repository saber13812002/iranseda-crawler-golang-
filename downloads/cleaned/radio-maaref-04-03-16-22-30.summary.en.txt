The program begins by emphasizing the role of every Iranian in taking small, wise steps toward the country's prosperity.
The main slogan for this year is introduced as investment in production through the cooperation of the government and the people to resolve economic issues.
In the cultural segment, the use of Persian words instead of foreign equivalents, such as using "kar-gozar" for "agent," is recommended.
Reports from the "Yek Asr-e Javani" contest are shared, highlighting participants who won points with correct answers.
A portion of the broadcast is dedicated to congratulating the Decade of Imamate and Wilayah, accompanied by religious music.
Finally, a deep interpretation of the Quranic verse "Guide us to the straight path" is presented, citing narrations and Allameh Tabatabaei's commentary.
Commentators believe that the most prominent and spiritual embodiment of this path is the existence of Imam Ali (AS) and the Infallible Imams.
This interpretation suggests that the path of truth is a collection of moral virtues that are fully manifested in the Infallibles.
