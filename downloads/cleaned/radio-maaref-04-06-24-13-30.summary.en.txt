In this radio program, Professor Faqihian discussed the concept of "asking God" within the framework of monotheistic ethics. He emphasized that humans should make requests to God with a sense of need rather than entitlement, highlighting that God's failure to fulfill threats of punishment is a sign of His mercy. Citing the Quran and narrations, the professor explained that God commands believers to ask Him, a practice that deepens spiritual knowledge and improves behavior. In the Q&A section, the issue of despair among sinners was addressed, with a strong recommendation to maintain hope in divine forgiveness based on the Quranic verse "Do not despair of the mercy of God." The professor noted that God is happier with the repentance of a lost servant than a person finding a treasure in the desert. Finally, the importance of maintaining hope, effort, and avoiding despair in both spiritual and worldly paths was stressed to foster a culture of work and motivation in society.
