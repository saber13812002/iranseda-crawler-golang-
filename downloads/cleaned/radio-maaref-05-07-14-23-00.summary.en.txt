This radio program discusses the vital role of "hope creation" in maintaining peace and cohesion within Quranic families. By referencing the story of Prophet Jacob and Joseph, the speaker demonstrates that hope in God and the future can save a family from collapse, even during severe crises and economic hardships. Despair is identified as the greatest major sin and the most dangerous social disease, capable of leading to generational destruction and societal breakdown. Parents and family members are advised to emulate prophets and religious leaders by avoiding negative atmospheres and bad omens, while strengthening a positive spirit through trust in God and continuous effort. It is emphasized that social media and media can serve as tools for the enemy's despair, making it essential to change one's environment and associate with successful, striving individuals. Finally, hope for relief and ultimate victory is presented as the driving force of life and the key to solving economic and social problems.
