The program began by emphasizing the values of resistance and solidarity in defending the oppressed, followed by an analysis of the National Security Council Secretary's diplomatic trip to Iraq and Lebanon. International media assessed this visit as a significant step toward de-escalating regional tensions and strengthening security diplomacy between Iran and Iraq. The report also covered Yemen's missile attacks on occupied territories and its continued commitment to supporting Palestine. Other news segments highlighted the condemnation of Islamophobic incidents in France and Australia, as well as a report on discrimination against Muslims in the German job market. The broadcast further discussed the Central Mosque of London's role in cultural and religious interactions with non-Muslim citizens, noting the high number of conversions to Islam there. Finally, the program aired a documentary contrasting the inhumane treatment of Iranian POWs by Iraqi forces with the humane and Islamic conduct of Iranian soldiers toward Iraqi prisoners of war.
