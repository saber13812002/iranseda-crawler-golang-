The program begins by highlighting the virtues and humanitarian conduct of Imam Sajjad (AS), who exemplified dignity by forgiving sinners and sheltering the Umayyads.
It recounts his secret acts of charity and his profound emotional impact on the public, even in the presence of Umayyad rulers.
The second part features a narrative adaptation from the book "Neshan-e Hassan," reconstructing the lives of Imam Hassan's (AS) children during the journey to Karbala.
In this story, Nafila, the wife of Imam Hassan, cares for her son Qasim, who has fallen ill with a fever due to the grief of Musaylim ibn Aqil's martyrdom and the hardships of travel.
The presence of Hazrat Zainab (SA) in the tent and the transfer of Imam Hassan's (AS) turban to Qasim symbolize the transmission of spiritual and emotional heritage across generations.
This narrative illustrates how the tragedies of Karbala deeply affected the Ahl al-Bayt's spirit and strengthened their emotional bonds.
The program concludes by emphasizing the importance of visiting Imam Reza (AS) as a means of divine mercy.
Ultimately, the blend of history and literature in this broadcast provides a vivid image of the patience and steadfastness of the Ahl al-Bayt during the most difficult moments in Islamic history.
