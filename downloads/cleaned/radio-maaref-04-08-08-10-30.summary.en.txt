The radio broadcast began with a song for the youth, followed by the "Shifa" segment where Dr. Pour Hosseini discussed Islamic traditional medicine. He emphasized that sleep is primarily for the night, while short daytime naps are recommended only at midday and after a proper interval from meals. The doctor also highlighted the importance of managing sleep schedules during autumn to maintain overall health. Subsequently, the "A Drop from the Sea" segment, hosted by Ruhollah Rasool, focused on the Quranic obligation of kindness towards elderly parents. By interpreting verse 8 of Surah Ankabut, the host stressed that respecting and caring for aging parents is a divine and moral duty. The program then featured a historical speech on the nature of "Qiyam Lillah," or uprising for God, contrasting it with worldly revolts. This section highlighted the divine power behind the Islamic Revolution's victory against foreign powers. The broadcast concluded with music and a mention of Aerospace and Space Day.
