In this radio program, the guest emphasizes the vital importance of guarding one's tongue and controlling speech to achieve spiritual perfection and maintain physical and mental health. He explains that true piety and faith cannot be realized without mastering speech and avoiding verbal sins, as the tongue is the primary key to human good and evil. The program also offers practical advice for pilgrims, including reciting dhikr during the walk, assisting at service tents, picking up litter, giving charity, and avoiding waste. Furthermore, it highlights the necessity of changing one's behavior and ethics on the pilgrimage path to draw closer to the Ahl al-Bayt and receive spiritual blessings. Finally, participants are encouraged to perform their pilgrimage on behalf of others by reciting specific prayers and sending blessings upon the Prophet and his family.
