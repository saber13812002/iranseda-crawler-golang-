In the radio program "Mashq-e Rah," Hojjat al-Islam Seyyed Ahmad Faqih discussed the importance of prayer and monotheistic ethics. He emphasized that despairing of God's mercy is a trap set by Satan, and faith in His power to forgive is essential for genuine repentance. In response to listener questions, the scholar described eloquent speech as a divine gift meant for conveying truth and defending the oppressed, while condemning unnecessary quick-wittedness. Regarding the control of backbiting and strengthening willpower, he recommended constant self-monitoring and practical exercises like praying on time. The discussion later shifted to the Iran-Iraq war, characterizing it as an international conspiracy against the newly established Islamic Republic. Despite initial military weaknesses, the nation relied on the faith of its people and the leadership of Imam Khomeini to resist. The explicit use of chemical weapons by Saddam Hussein, supported by Western powers, was highlighted as a key aspect of this conflict.
