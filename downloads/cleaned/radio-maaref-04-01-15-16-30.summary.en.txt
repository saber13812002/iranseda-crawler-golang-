The radio program begins by discussing the historical incident of Jabir ibn Nadr's objection to the appointment of Ali ibn Abi Talib at Ghadir Khumm and the subsequent divine punishment, citing tafsirs of Surah Al-Ma'arij and Allameh Amini's works. It then addresses a listener's question regarding the pledges of allegiance at Ghadir, confirming that all attendees pledged to Imam Ali voluntarily and without any coercion. The discussion shifts to a historical correction concerning a martyr of Karbala often mistakenly referred to as "Wahba," clarifying that the individual was actually Abdullah ibn Amir Kalbi. It is explained that this error likely stems from the name of his wife, Umm Wahba, who accompanied him to Karbala as a volunteer. The program further clarifies that Abdullah ibn Amir Kalbi was not the first martyr of Karbala, a distinction that belongs to Muslim ibn Awsaja. Finally, the broadcast transitions to a segment on social ethics, emphasizing the Quranic and prophetic injunctions to treat neighbors with kindness and respect.
