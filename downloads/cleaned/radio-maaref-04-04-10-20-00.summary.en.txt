The news segment from Radio Maarif reported on the display of Iranian and Palestinian flags during Muharram processions in Europe, alongside a letter from Western university students to Iran's leader expressing solidarity with the resistance. It also covered the sharp diplomatic tensions between France and Iran, with French officials criticized for failing to condemn Israel's attacks on Iran. In Turkey, four individuals, including the cartoonist and editors of the magazine Leman, were arrested and the publication banned for publishing a blasphemous caricature of the Prophet. The Iraqi court sentenced two former Saddam Hussein regime officers to death for their role in the 1980 assassination of Ayatollah Sadr and his daughter. Sweden's largest Islamic center, Helsingborg, was inaugurated with a capacity of 3,500 people, funded entirely by community donations. The Iraqi government is finalizing its bid to designate Karbala as the Islamic World Tourism Capital for the current year. In India, a wave of forced deportations of Bangladeshi Muslims from the border was reported, which human rights activists condemned as a violation of human rights. Finally, the people of Afghanistan expressed their support for Iran's recent military victory against Israel, emphasizing the unity of Muslims in the face of common threats.
