The first segment of the program defines a true believer, based on the teachings of the Ahl al-Bayt, as one who desires for others what they desire for themselves and dislikes for them what they dislike for themselves. This attitude must become an unconscious habit, replacing the tree of vice in the heart with the tree of virtue. Citing the book of Kafi, the broadcast emphasized that fulfilling the needs of believers, especially the poor, is a superior act to freeing a thousand slaves or equipping a thousand horses for jihad. Listeners were encouraged to use their social standing and resources to mediate conflicts and support the community. In the second segment, various jurisprudential questions were addressed, including the ruling that debts owed to unreachable individuals should be deposited with an honest person or transferred with inflation adjustments. Gold and silver purchased for personal use are not subject to Khums, whereas those bought for investment are. A wife may annul her marriage if her husband is found to have an incurable sexual dysfunction after a one-year grace period for treatment. Clothing soiled by the urine of a breastfeeding infant is purified by a single washing. In the Prayer of the Sun and Moon, uncertainty regarding the number of prostrations is resolved by assuming the lesser number, while a prayer is invalid if the deviation from the Qibla exceeds ninety degrees.
