The radio program Zamzam Ahkam addressed the Islamic ruling on fasting for pregnant and breastfeeding women, stating that if it causes harm, they must break their fast and pay a specific expiation. It was clarified that delaying the makeup of these fasts incurs an additional penalty for the delay. Regarding sports competitions, Ayatollah Khomeini's fatwa was cited, explaining that such events are not considered gambling unless financial betting is involved among the participants. Additionally, Ayatollah Shahrudi Zanjan's opinion was shared, confirming that reciting the Quran while walking is permissible and retains its spiritual reward. The program concluded by providing contact numbers for listeners to submit further religious questions to Radio Ma'aref. The broadcast then transitioned to the morning segment, Sobh-e Niaz, which began with Quranic recitation and prayer. This final section marked the date in Muharram and announced the sunrise time in Qom, creating a spiritual atmosphere for the morning listeners.
