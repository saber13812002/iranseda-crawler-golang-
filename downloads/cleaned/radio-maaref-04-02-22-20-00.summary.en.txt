In this radio program, a narration from Imam Jafar Sadiq (AS) is cited to emphasize that earning a halal livelihood is a fundamental part of worshipping God.
Key ethical principles in business are highlighted, including honesty, disclosing product defects, avoiding exaggeration in advertising, and refraining from illegal commissions.
It is stressed that true life blessings lie in spiritual and family quality rather than merely in material quantity or outward appearances.
The second part of the program details the history of Ayatollah Golpayegani Hospital in Qom, which originated from the Ayatollah's concern about the lack of hijab compliance in state hospitals.
The bloody attack on the Feyh school in 1963 and the subsequent ban on treating injured students in other centers accelerated the construction of this facility.
The center initially started as a small clinic for students and the underprivileged, gradually expanding into a fully equipped hospital with the help of donors.
During the bloody uprising of June 16, 1963, the hospital served as a safe haven for treating injured protesters who were otherwise at risk of arrest by Pahlavi regime agents.
The hospital was fully completed in 1967 and played a crucial role in supporting activists and vulnerable members of society.
