In this radio program, Professor Mohammadi Sharodi addresses listeners' questions regarding their connection with Imam Zaman (AJ). He explains that while believers' deeds are formally presented to the Imam on Tuesdays and Thursdays, his collective status allows him to observe their actions and greetings at any moment without temporal limitations. The program also distinguishes between the angels accompanying humans in this world and in the Barzakh, noting that worldly angels strike the back at death, whereas Barzakh angels strike the face, with both serving as witnesses on the Day of Judgment. The second part of the discussion emphasizes the necessity of combining knowledge with piety, stating that neither is sufficient on its own. Finally, educators are urged to focus on moral upbringing alongside academic instruction, as the pursuit of knowledge continues until the moment of death.
