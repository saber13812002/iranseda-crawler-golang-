The radio program "Narrative of Love," dedicated to the mothers and wives of martyrs, features the story of Raghieh Hosseini, the mother of the Syrian martyr Seyed Mohammad Hosseini from Afghanistan. She recounts her childhood in Iran, influenced by the atmosphere of the Iran-Iraq War, and describes her simple marriage inspired by the life of Lady Zahra (SA). Although interested in bodybuilding, her son refused to use chemical supplements and ultimately volunteered to defend the shrine of Lady Zainab (SA) in Syria. The mother shares her emotional reaction to his registration and his specific instructions to remain calm and wear white on the night of his departure. The segment concludes with a discussion on the pivotal role of revolutionary students in the early years of the Islamic Revolution, including their participation in the Jihad-e-Sazandegi and defense against armed groups. This narrative highlights how deep devotion to the Ahl al-Bayt (AS) shaped the family's life and motivated their significant sacrifices.
