The speaker stresses the necessity of reconstructing and promoting this grand theory in academic and public spaces to remove the dust of practical errors from it. The importance of an effective media presence both domestically and internationally is also noted to convey this exalted discourse. Following this, a narrative from the book "Eastern Ocean" was broadcast, depicting Hajj Karim and his companions' encounter with bandits in the desert. This story segment focuses on Hajj Karim's courage and etiquette in the face of threats, as well as his deep connection to the guardianship of Imam Reza (PBUH).
