The radio program began by advising listeners to avoid using undocumented titles for the Imam and to strictly follow the names found in authentic narrations. The main discussion focused on "divine trials" as a permanent and inevitable part of human life, serving as a tool for spiritual growth. Experts explained that hardships help individuals discover hidden talents, build resilience, and cultivate patience, which are essential qualities for true awaiters of the Imam. The importance of maintaining a broad chest and managing crises effectively was highlighted as a key step toward spiritual perfection and the Imam's reappearance. Additionally, the program emphasized the power of kindness and friendship in guiding others toward the Ahl al-Bayt and preparing the ground for the Imam's return. Finally, a "true awaiter" was defined as an individual who remains steadfast on Islamic principles and resists various political, social, and moral challenges.
