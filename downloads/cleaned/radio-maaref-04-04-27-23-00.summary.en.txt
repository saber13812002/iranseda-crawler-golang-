The radio program emphasized the necessity of steadfastness in Imami faith and the anticipation of the Mahdi, identifying it as the most virtuous act.
The primary condition for the dominance of Islam over all religions was stated to be monotheism and adherence to Islamic teachings against enemy conspiracies.
General Mousavi, commander of the IRGC Aerospace Force, praised the force's heroic performance in the recent conflict and affirmed their full readiness to defend Iran's territorial integrity.
The second segment analyzed a narration from Al-Musannaf of Abd al-Razzaq regarding the Dajjal's siege of Muslims in the mountains of Sham and their rescue by Imam Isa (AS).
According to this narration, Imam Isa offered Muslims three options to confront the Dajjal, and they chose the third option: gaining dominance with their own weapons.
During the battle, the Jews lost the ability to wield their weapons due to trembling and fear upon seeing Imam Isa, and were subsequently killed by the Muslims.
Finally, the Dajjal melted like lead upon seeing Imam Isa and was then killed by him.
