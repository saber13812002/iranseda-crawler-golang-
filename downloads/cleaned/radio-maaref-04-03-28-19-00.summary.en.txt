In this radio program, the fatwas of Ayatollah Sistani were presented in response to various fiqh questions. First, the ruling on reciting the Quran for individuals in a state of impurity or while moving was discussed, which is permissible provided there is no direct contact between the body and the Quranic text and prostration verses are avoided. It was also clarified that reciting the adhan and iqamah while walking is valid, and reciting only the iqamah is sufficient for prayer. Additionally, a woman's use of her husband's haram income is permitted only to the extent of necessity if there is a threat to her life. The issue of nail polish was also addressed; if it prevents water from reaching the skin, it must be removed for wudu and ghusl, and otherwise, it must be covered from non-mahram men. Finally, a transaction involving money with the obligation to repay at the gold rate on the repayment day was declared invalid and impermissible due to the presence of riba and deferred payment.
