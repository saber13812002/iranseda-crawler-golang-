This radio program examines how social media conditionally shapes public perception, using the famous cat and book experiment as a metaphor.
Guests explained that excessive media consumption and imitation of Western lifestyles lead to "consensual domination" and the weakening of cultural and social structures.
Video games and Western series were cited as tools used to promote anti-ethical values and alter the lifestyle of the younger generation.
Techniques such as "message massage" and word engineering in international media were described as methods to distort narratives and confuse public opinion.
The discussion also highlighted technical and algorithmic challenges that silence critical voices against the overwhelming volume of promotional content.
Ultimately, the necessity of "cyber governance" and training specialized personnel to create local platforms and counter cultural infiltration was emphasized.
The program concluded that without a deep understanding of soft war techniques and the establishment of an independent media identity, the psychological security of society is at risk.
