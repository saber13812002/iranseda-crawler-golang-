The National Security Council established the National Defense Council under the presidency of the President to enhance the capabilities of the armed forces. As the Arbaeen pilgrimage approaches, the Minister of Interior inaugurated water supply projects and a new mosque at the Mehran border, which hosted over 111,000 pilgrims yesterday. To facilitate services, 160 elite clerics proficient in Arabic and English have been deployed to border crossings and Karbala, while emergency contact numbers for the Iranian consulate in Iraq were announced. In a jurisprudential segment, Hujjat al-Islam Vahidpour clarified that legally, adults do not require parental permission to participate in the Arbaeen walk, although obtaining their consent is ethically recommended. The program also highlighted the suffering of children in Gaza, emphasizing the need for unity and solidarity among Muslims during this significant period.
