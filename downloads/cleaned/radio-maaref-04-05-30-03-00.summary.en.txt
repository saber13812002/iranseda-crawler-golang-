In the radio program "Zamzam Ahkam," various religious rulings were discussed based on the fatwas of different Maraji. According to Ayatollah Khamenei, a wife cannot participate in significant events like processions or funeral rites without her husband's permission, unless she has general permission to go out. Regarding makeup, Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi stated that light makeup on the face and hands is permissible for women in the presence of non-mahram men, provided it does not lead to corruption. Selling smuggled goods or items with excessive profit is considered haram if it violates the law, according to Ayatollah Sistani. For prayer, followers of Ayatollah Khamenei who are ill and cannot stand should pray standing as much as possible and sit for the remainder. While in a state of major ritual impurity, reciting Ziyarat Ashura and the Quran is allowed, but touching Quranic verses or blessed names is prohibited. In terms of marital obedience, if a husband fails to provide maintenance, the wife is still obligated to comply unless there is another valid religious excuse. The program concluded with the recitation of the Ziyarat of Imam Hussein (PBUH) and a recommendation for self-accountability.
