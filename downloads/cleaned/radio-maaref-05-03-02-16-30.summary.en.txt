In this radio program, the concept of "divine signs" (Shia'ir) is explained as symbols of God, such as Hajj and sacrifice. The commentator emphasizes that honoring these signs is a deliberate, heart-based act rooted in faith and piety, which is not accepted by God without sincere intention, even if the outward action appears correct. Respect for religious and spiritual figures is also considered part of honoring divine signs, as they dedicated their lives to reviving the faith. The response to common questions about Hajj clarifies that the pilgrimage itself is a divine sign, and God's command is to revere it, not merely to spend money on it. The program references the Quranic verse stating that honoring God's signs is a mark of the piety of the heart. Finally, the broadcast concludes with Quranic recitation and references to Surahs Hajj and Balad, highlighting the spiritual atmosphere and their connection to Mecca and the pilgrimage.
