The fifth international Qadir campaign has been launched to preserve Qadir culture, focusing on themes such as its role in educating the generation of the awaited savior. In the news segment, Iran's Hajj and Pilgrimage Organization received the Arab Saudi "Lub Baitum" award for the first time, recognizing its excellent service delivery. The report also highlighted the establishment of over a thousand Arbaeen camps in Iran and Iraq, along with the renovation of ten thousand schools in underprivileged areas by the Student Basij. Filming for the TV series "Salman Farsi" is currently underway in the "Ancient Iran" season at the Iran Film City. In the interview segment, Hujjat al-Islam Saeidi Aria described Qadir as a pivotal moment for accelerating social reform and continuing the Prophet's guidance path under Imam Ali (PBUH). The program concluded by announcing special SMS and phone contests for children and adults, inviting listeners to submit literary works in praise of Imam Ali (PBUH).
