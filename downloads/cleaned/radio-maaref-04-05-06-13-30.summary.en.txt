In the radio program "Mashq-e Rah," Professor Seyed Ahmad Faqih emphasized the importance of sincere spiritual focus and patience in prayer, citing the story of a blind man who sought the Prophet's intercession. He explained that enduring hardship can sometimes be more beneficial than immediate relief, and that prayers should be accompanied by reliance on God and recitation of blessings upon the Prophet. Addressing listener questions, the professor advised that motivating teenagers requires clearly explaining the benefits of education while also considering their individual talents and rebuilding their confidence after past failures. Regarding children's perceived neglect of their parents, he suggested that parents should reflect on their own behavior and shift their emotional dependence from their children to God. The program concluded by stressing the necessity of maintaining national and religious unity against enemy plots, urging that political and sectarian differences should not hinder the collective defense of the system and the country.
