The radio program "Labbaik Ya Hussein" on the Ma'aref network began with condolences for the Arbaeen commemoration and prayers for the listeners.
The main segment featured an interview between Saeed Hosseini and Dr. Fallah, focusing on the practical lessons derived from the Arbaeen pilgrimage.
A key point discussed was the distinction between "Jazm" (theoretical knowledge) and "Azam" (action and determination), which is clearly demonstrated in the Arbaeen march.
Dr. Fallah emphasized that Imam Hussein's companions and today's pilgrims are people of action rather than mere slogans.
Referencing the 12-day war, he noted that the Iranian people have translated their knowledge into action by supporting the armed forces.
The program concluded with the recitation of patriotic and religious poetry by poet Hamed Ahouri and an invitation for listeners to send their names for proxy pilgrimage.
