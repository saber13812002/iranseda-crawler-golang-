The guest, a former football player, recounts his career with teams such as Esteghlal, Persepolis, and Ekbatan. Despite attractive financial offers from other clubs, he remained loyal to one team for nearly fifteen years due to his bond with teammates. His participation in the youth, junior, and national hope teams stands as the pinnacle of his sporting achievements. Ekbatan holds a special place in his memory as a character-driven team supported by private individuals. He expresses gratitude to the managers and private sponsors who supported the team during the difficult years of Iranian football. The program concludes by honoring the memory of martyr Masoud Tavazo, a volleyball player and Basij member.
