This radio program emphasizes the importance of educating the younger generation to correctly identify cultural and social trends. The speaker explains that many individuals, despite having motivation, misdirect their energy due to a lack of awareness regarding the current era. The significance of religious and cultural symbols as tools for shifting societal direction and countering enemy influence is detailed. The historical context of Imam Khomeini's return on February 1, 1979, and the crucial role of the army in supporting the revolution are also highlighted. The events of February 8 and 10 are presented as pivotal moments in the revolution's victory and the defeat of internal and external plots. Additionally, the program discusses the necessity of maintaining spiritual purity and adhering to religious rulings in daily matters, such as handling papers with sacred names. The broadcast concludes by inviting listeners to submit their feedback and continue supporting Radio Ma'arif.
