The program addressed several jurisprudential questions, starting with the conditions for breastfeeding to establish a permanent prohibition. It was clarified that if there is no certainty that the infant consumed the required amount of milk, no legal consequences apply. Regarding gifts from individuals with potentially illicit income, using such gifts is permissible unless there is definite knowledge that the money itself is haram. The ruling on prayer for a family visiting their hometown was also discussed; while the parents pray fully if they do not intend to settle permanently, their adult daughter is considered a traveler and prays partially if she lacks the intention to reside there. Finally, it was stated that one cannot consume fruit from a neighbor's tree that falls into their yard without the owner's explicit consent or certainty of their approval.
