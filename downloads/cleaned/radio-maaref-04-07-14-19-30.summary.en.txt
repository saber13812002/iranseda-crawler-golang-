In this radio program, Professor Hujjat al-Islam Fallah emphasized the importance of heeding the divine call and recognizing God's signs amidst worldly appearances. He refuted the claim that certain narrations permit cursing, asserting that the Infallibles (AS) strictly avoided foul language even when defending their own rights. Citing Ayatollah Javadi Amoli, the professor noted that one should avoid using harsh words even towards nature, as such language ultimately rebounds upon the speaker. Practical solutions for curing this moral defect include transforming ignorance into awareness, correcting misunderstandings of religious and national values, and controlling negative thoughts before they manifest in behavior. The program strongly condemned justifying cursing by blaming God or others, as well as the hypocrisy of being abusive in private while appearing polite in public. The ultimate goal is to encourage self-improvement and the avoidance of verbal harms to foster a healthy and ethical society.
