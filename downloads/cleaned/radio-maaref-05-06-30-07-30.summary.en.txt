The program began with the host expressing his long-standing political collaboration with a judge and his continued loyalty to their shared revolutionary ideals, while also thanking listeners from East Azerbaijan and other regions. Various listener messages were read, featuring gratitude towards family members, appreciation for border guards and security forces, and well-wishes for the pleasant climate of Azerbaijan. Following a weather forecast for the upcoming days, the show transitioned to a religious segment featuring Professor Mohammad Hadi Falahatpour. The professor discussed the Quranic concepts of "adornment" and "pride," explaining that worldly glitter is the primary factor leading humans astray. He cited numerous Quranic verses to illustrate that wealth, children, status, and even religious appearances can serve as deceptive adornments that distract individuals from their true path. He emphasized that every person is susceptible to these deceptions based on their personal desires, and that controlling one's inner inclinations is essential for resistance. Finally, the professor stressed that the true measure of cultural work should be the moral and intellectual development of people, rather than merely focusing on luxurious architectural projects.
