The eighth national Quran competition, themed "Quran, the Book of Unity," has begun in Sanandaj and will continue until the fifth of Aban. The Deputy of the Hajj and Pilgrimage Organization announced the launch of a new registration system for the 1405 Hajj, urging applicants to update their data and deposit 200 million tomans. The second international conference on Artificial Intelligence and Islamic Sciences, featuring over 150 researchers from 25 countries, is scheduled for next year. The first national festival for Iranian women's hijab design will be held in Tehran from the first to the fifth of Aban. The Representative of the Supreme Leader at the Housing Foundation announced plans to build 50,000 housing units for deprived families over the next three years. A comparative conference on ethics in Islam and Christianity was held, introducing 18 shared books on various ethical topics. The "Zamzam Ahkam" program addressed religious queries from followers of different marjas regarding khums, prayer, and transactions. Finally, the radio schedule for the subsequent hours of the day on the Ma'aref network was announced.
