This program interprets verses from Surah Al-Baqarah, recounting the trial of the Israelites under Talut and their battle with Goliath.
Although God appointed Talut as commander, the people initially rejected him due to his poverty and the fact that kingship had previously been in their own lineage.
The divine sign confirming Talut's leadership was the return of Moses' Ark, which brought spiritual calm to the army.
The primary test involved abstaining from drinking water at a river, a challenge that only a small, faithful group successfully passed.
This loyal group, relying on faith and prayer, defeated Goliath's massive army, with David ultimately slaying the giant.
The second part of the program discusses Surah Al-Imran and Prophet Jesus's reaction to the disbelief of the Israelites.
Jesus called upon his devoted followers, the Hawariyyun, to pledge their allegiance to God's path rather than just his person.
The overall conclusion is that divine leaders must rigorously test the loyalty of their followers during critical times to distinguish the faithful from the disloyal.
