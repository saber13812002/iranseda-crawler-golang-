In the radio program "Faith Questions," Professor Mohammad Sharoudi emphasized the necessity of seeking knowledge as the best guide to navigate the darkness of life and the afterlife. He advised listeners to utilize idle moments, such as traffic or waiting lines, for study and educational listening, rejecting the excuse of lack of time. In response to a theological query, he explained the concept of "renewal of creation" as God's moment-by-moment sustenance of the universe, asserting that existence is entirely dependent on divine will. He clarified that physical illnesses are exclusive to the earthly life, as humans will possess healthy, eternal bodies in the intermediate and final realms. The Prophet's vision of Paradise and Hell during the Ascension was interpreted as a simultaneous perception of all time, rather than merely witnessing future events. The professor also addressed a narration about sewing with dhikr, stressing that purifying the heart from enmity is a prerequisite for accepting spiritual blessings. Finally, Adam's expulsion from Paradise was explained as a divine necessity for humans to undergo trials and achieve ultimate perfection.
