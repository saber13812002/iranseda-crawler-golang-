The program began by commemorating the anniversary of the martyrdom of Imam Reza (PBUH), highlighting the presence of millions of pilgrims in Mashhad and mourning ceremonies across Iran. News segments covered the passing of a martyr's father and the release of a book titled "Meeting with the Sun," which contains personal accounts of interactions with Imam Reza (PBUH). The broadcast also addressed the situation in Gaza, urging listeners to support the oppressed people through a provided donation account. The main segment featured an interview with Hujjat al-Islam Vahidpour regarding the jurisprudential rulings on mourning. He clarified that there are no specific religious mandates for mourning in the month of Safar or thereafter, noting that such practices are largely based on historical traditions and narrations related to Muharram. The discussion also touched upon the asceticism and simplicity of the Imams and contemporary maraji', such as Imam Ali (PBUH) and recent clerics. The program concluded by emphasizing the importance of detaching from worldly attachments and maintaining a spiritual connection with the Ahl al-Bayt (PBUH).
