In this radio program, jurisprudential answers to various listener questions were provided by religious authorities. Hujjat al-Islam Mohammadi Shakhsvani, citing Ayatollah Khomeini's fatwa, emphasized that avoiding sins such as cheating and misappropriation of others' property is a religious duty, urging believers to enjoin good and forbid evil. Regarding digital currency trading, Ayatollah Sistani did not issue a ruling due to the ambiguity of these assets' usage, recommending consultation with other scholars. Hujjat al-Islam Edian Najat clarified that "qorokeshi," interpreted as family savings boxes, is religiously permissible. The program also explained that disrupting the order of prayer components invalidates the prayer, except in specific cases. On the topic of Khums, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli ruled that valuable gifts exceeding annual needs are subject to Khums. The second segment, titled "Sarmashk," stressed the importance of acting upon knowledge and wisdom to prevent learning from being futile. Finally, the discussion continued on methods to achieve spiritual presence in prayer and the levels of purity from the perspective of Imam Khomeini.
