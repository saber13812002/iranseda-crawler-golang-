Ali Hamoudi, a former national team player and veteran of major clubs, recounts his bitter financial experiences during his time at Esteghlal Tehran. He admits that despite playing for the club for two years, he received no salary due to financial issues and the club's priority in paying senior players. Hamoudi describes the hardships of that period, including lacking money for a train ticket to visit his family on New Year's Eve and having to borrow from friends to make ends meet. He also discusses the complex process of obtaining his military discharge card and the issues with serving in the military team at Tractor, which caused him to miss an entire season. Despite these challenges, Hamoudi emphasizes that he always valued his profession and maintained a consistent performance in the Premier League through discipline and adequate rest. He concludes by acknowledging the physical comparisons made to singer Siroos Khosravi and reflecting on his career as a well-mannered and hardworking player.
