In this radio program, human life is described as neither random nor entirely under our control, but rather a blend of divine will and human choice. True success is not defined by material wealth, but by understanding the roles that God assigns us in our life's journey. Citing a narration from Imam Sadiq (AS), the speaker emphasizes that accepting divine decree does not negate free will, but rather brings inner peace. Managing one's mind through sincere intention and a loving perspective of God is the key to receiving both spiritual and material assistance from Him. The more complete and pure one's intention is in their relationship with God, the stronger the divine response will be. Beginning every action with "Bismillah" and trusting in God's mercy lays the foundation for a blessed life. Ultimately, the program's main message is that by relying on God and correcting one's intentions, one can achieve the best outcomes within divine destiny.
