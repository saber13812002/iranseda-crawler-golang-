During this radio broadcast covering the Arbaeen pilgrimage, the importance of visiting with respect for one's parents and maintaining the spiritual quality of the journey was emphasized.
The core of the discussion focused on the necessity of training and guarding the tongue as the key to resolving individual and social problems within the Shia community.
Imam Ali (AS) and Imam Sadiq (AS) stated in various narrations that human salvation and peace depend on controlling speech and avoiding verbal sins.
Four main strategies were introduced to achieve this goal, including distancing oneself from corrupt environments, strengthening thought, linguistic piety, and rationality.
The tongue, at the level of the ego-driven soul, must be sentenced to long-term imprisonment to prevent it from harming others and oneself.
Guarding the tongue not only conceals sins and earns a good name before God but also promotes psychological and social peace.
The program concluded by dedicating the Arbaeen pilgrimage to the martyrs and the Infallibles, while expressing gratitude to the listeners.
