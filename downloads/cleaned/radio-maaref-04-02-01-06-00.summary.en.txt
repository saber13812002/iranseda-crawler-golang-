In the Ma'aref radio program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verses from Surah Al-Ma'idah and At-Tawbah to emphasize the necessity of independent research in religion and to warn against blind imitation. He cautions that following personal desires or imitating misguides leads one away from God's straight path. The exegesis reveals that the baseless claims of the People of the Book in early Islam were influenced by the disbelief and polytheism of ancient pagans. The Quran labels such statements as "words of the mouth," indicating they lack roots in reason or innate nature. These groups are cursed due to their continuous sins, failure to forbid evil, and allegiance to the enemies of religion. The concept of "mutanahin" implies being receptive to prohibition, yet these individuals persisted in sin because they rejected such admonitions. Ultimately, Imam Ali ibn al-Hussein (AS) also cited these verses to stress the importance of avoiding allegiance to disbelievers and steering clear of misguides.
