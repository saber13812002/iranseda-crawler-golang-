The radio program begins by emphasizing the importance of patience as the head of faith and a means of salvation from sins and misfortunes.
A narration from Imam Sadiq (AS) is cited, highlighting Imam Ali's (AS) advice to a grieving man to seek refuge in piety and patience.
The discussion then shifts to the vice of lying, which is described as contrary to both the natural order and religious law.
Based on Imam Ali's (AS) conduct, honesty and the refusal to conceal the truth are presented as essential conditions for true allegiance to his authority.
Key signs of liars are identified as contradiction, forgetfulness, and an unnecessary insistence on being perceived as truthful.
Using the story of Prophet Yusuf (AS) and the quick exposure of political lies, it is demonstrated that lies have a short lifespan and are often exposed by the liar's own incompetence.
Finally, relying on Quranic verses, it is concluded that liars do not attain guidance or salvation, and one must avoid seeking help from dishonest individuals.
