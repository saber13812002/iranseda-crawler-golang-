The radio program began by discussing the recommended practices for prayer in the Grand Mosque, highlighting the spiritual benefits of praying in quieter areas like opposite the Naf-Dan. Subsequently, various jurisprudential questions were addressed, including the ruling on praying in impure clothes, which requires repetition if forgotten but remains valid if due to ignorance. The fatwa of Ayatollah Javadi Amoli was cited, stating that thick steam does not invalidate fasting unless it turns into water and enters the throat. The program also explained the nuances of paying expiations and khums, noting that unused fabric purchased a year ago requires khums at its current market value. It was clarified that if a child is disciplined beyond the permissible limit, blood money (diyah) becomes obligatory. The ruling on performing missed prayers and fasts for deceased parents was also discussed, specifying the responsibilities of the eldest son. The broadcast concluded with a historical account of the shrines in Baku, Azerbaijan, and a report on religious ceremonies held at the Amir Fatemiyeh shrine in Lahijan.
