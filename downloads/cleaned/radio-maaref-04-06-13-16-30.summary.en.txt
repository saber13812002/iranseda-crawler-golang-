In the radio program "Persiman Enghelab," the book "Islamic Iran Against Zionism" by Dr. Seyed Jalaloddin Madani was introduced, detailing the historical struggle of the Iranian nation against the Zionist regime. The author highlights Ayatollah Kashani's historical support for Palestine and his three main objectives: cutting off British control over Iran's oil, supporting Palestine, and achieving Muslim unity. In the second segment, Dr. Mehdi Abutalebi addressed frequent public inquiries regarding the extent of the Islamic Republic's aid to Gaza. He emphasized that Iran has supported Palestine in spiritual, material, and military dimensions since the revolution, noting that the Palestinian cause might have been forgotten without this backing. The expert also distinguished Iran's intervention model from Yemen's, stressing the need for Iran to act more intelligently as the axis of the Resistance Front. He criticized the inaction of neighboring countries like Jordan and Egypt, urging Islamic leaders to wake up from their complacency. Finally, listener messages about the lack of medical facilities in rural areas and agricultural challenges were read to draw the attention of officials to these concerns.
