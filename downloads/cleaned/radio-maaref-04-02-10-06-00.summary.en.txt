This text analyzes Quranic verses regarding the prohibition of alcohol and refutes the theory of gradual legislation in this context. The speaker argues that true progression lies in the chronological order of Meccan and Medinan revelations rather than the apparent order of the Quran's chapters. It is asserted that alcohol was absolutely forbidden from the time of the Surah Al-A'raf, and its perceived benefits are merely material illusions for the ignorant. The text distinguishes between gambling tools (azlam) and Islamic istikhara, emphasizing that the latter is fundamentally different from pre-Islamic divination. Finally, the passage outlines methods for resolving human uncertainty, including rational thought, spiritual inspiration, and consulting with knowledgeable experts.
