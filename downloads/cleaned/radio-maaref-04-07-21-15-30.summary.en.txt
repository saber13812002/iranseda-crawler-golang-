This radio program discussed the principles of Islamic criminal policy regarding crimes against physical integrity. A key focus was the rule that a crime cannot be punished without explicit legal prohibition, emphasizing that ignorance of the law may be a valid defense under specific conditions. The principle of personal liability for punishment was also explained, citing the Quranic verse that no soul bears the burden of another. This principle, established in Islamic jurisprudence 1,400 years ago, predates its adoption in Western legal systems during the 18th century. Commentators highlighted the necessity of exact retribution for the perpetrator, prohibiting harm to their relatives or tribe. Subsequently, verses from the Quran's Surah Al-Baqarah were recited and translated, covering rulings on marriage with non-believers, menstruation, spousal rights, and divorce. These verses underscore the importance of faith in marital relationships, adherence to religious boundaries, and justice in mutual rights. The program concluded with an invitation to listen to future broadcasts on Radio Ma'aref.
