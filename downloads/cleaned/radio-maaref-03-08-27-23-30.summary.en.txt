The radio program began by offering strategies to manage family crises involving teenagers, suggesting temporary stays with grandparents and empathetic, non-judgmental communication to reduce tension. The discussion then shifted to social anxiety, emphasizing that while fear is natural, negative self-talk exacerbates it. Practical advice for overcoming this anxiety included consistent practice, deep diaphragmatic breathing, speaking slowly, and not worrying excessively about others' judgments. Next, the program addressed the financial and emotional challenges of a man who became blind due to an accident and is facing marital conflict. Recommendations included seeking professional career and financial counseling, exercising patience during the adaptation process, and reducing financial pressure from the spouse to maintain relationship harmony. Finally, the concept of a "divine family" was explained as a relationship based on trust, empathy, and mutual care during both hardships and ease.
