In this program, Hojjat al-Islam Morteza Bahrami Khoshnoudi emphasized the importance of hard work for earning a halal livelihood as a form of worship and jihad, based on the Prophetic tradition. He explained that the Prophet (PBUH) valued physical and mental health alongside spirituality, considering activities like travel, healthy recreation, and hygiene as sources of well-being. The role of bright clothing colors and personal grooming in fostering spiritual vitality and divine pleasure was also highlighted. In the second part, Professor Mohammad Hadi Fala discussed the severe consequences of backbiting, describing it as a major sin that leads to divine distance and the destruction of good deeds. Citing various narrations, he described the punishment in the Hereafter as the backbiter clawing their own face, noting that backbiting can erase all of a person's good deeds. Finally, the primary solution for this sin was identified as sincere repentance and seeking forgiveness from the victim.
