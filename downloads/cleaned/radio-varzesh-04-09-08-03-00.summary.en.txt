The most significant transformation in Iranian football refereeing history occurred in the 1980s when Mostafa Shahrbazi organized large-scale training classes that emphasized education and language skills. This initiative created a strong foundation for the league and produced referees who eventually reached the World Cup. The speaker, a former referee and student of Hamid Khoshkhan and Shahrbazi, recounts memories of media pressure, conflicts with committee presidents, and even bribery attempts. After leaving refereeing due to political-administrative disputes, he shifted his focus to researching goal-line technology and authoring specialized books on match management. In a phone interview, Shahrbazi emphasized that the success of Iranian referees stemmed from combining football knowledge with university education and foreign language proficiency. This approach allowed Iranian referees to align with international standards and maintain their credibility on the global stage.
