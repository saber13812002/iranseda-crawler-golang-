The radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat" began by commemorating the martyrdom anniversary of Lady Masoumeh (SA) before discussing the moral consequences of foul language and cursing. Hujjat al-Islam Fallah emphasized, citing various narrations, that cursing leads to the deprivation of answered prayers and the love of God and the Ahl al-Bayt. He illustrated how even a minor insult can permanently sever one's spiritual companionship with the Infallibles, as demonstrated in stories involving Imam Sadiq (AS). Additionally, a narration was highlighted where the Prophet (PBUH) warned that attributing sins to others makes one liable to retribution and punishment on the Day of Judgment. The core message is that foul language isolates an individual from divine guardianship and blocks the path to spiritual connection with God. Therefore, guarding one's tongue and avoiding slander are essential conditions for maintaining a bond with the Ahl al-Bayt and ensuring that prayers are accepted.
