This radio program begins by highlighting the extensive efforts to distribute the English translation of "Tafsir Noor al-Quran" to prisoners in American jails and globally, which led to numerous conversions to Islam. Ayatollah Faqih Imani, recognized as a prominent figure in global Islamic propagation, dedicated over 35 years to this 20-volume work, which is now also available online. The second part of the show features Ali Azghar Meshayekhi, a veteran of the Khorramshahr operation, sharing his experiences during his captivity in Iraq. Meshayekhi describes how he and other prisoner-students organized secret educational classes and even hand-copied missing textbooks to prevent academic regression. He also recounts the challenges of receiving and decoding letters written in onion water from their families under the strict surveillance of the camps. The program concludes with a brief introduction to the memoirs of another veteran, Abdolkarim Mazandarani, who participated in various military operations.
