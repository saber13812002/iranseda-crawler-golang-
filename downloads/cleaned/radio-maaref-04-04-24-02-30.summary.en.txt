In the "Zamzam Ahkam" program, Hujjat al-Islam Vahid Bor discussed the Islamic ruling on surveillance and inspection during wartime. Citing the Quran, he explained that while spying is generally prohibited, it becomes permissible and even obligatory when there is a significant and probable threat to public security. Bor emphasized that such inspections must be based on a high probability of danger and must respect individual privacy to prevent enemy exploitation. The broadcast also addressed the spread of rumors on social media, identifying it as a destructive tool used by enemies to create public panic and fear. From a religious perspective, sharing unverified news that causes public anxiety is considered haram and carries severe legal and moral responsibilities. The Q&A section covered various rulings, including proxy stoning of the Jamarat, foot coverage in prayer, early prayer timing, and the recitation volume for women. Additionally, inheritance rules for waqf properties and the khums on specific incomes like mahr and alimony were clarified based on the fatwas of major clerics. The program concluded with Quranic recitation and the morning supplication of "Sobh-e Niaz."
