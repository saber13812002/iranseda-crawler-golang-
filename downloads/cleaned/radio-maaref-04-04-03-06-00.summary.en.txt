This segment of the program features Ayatollah Javadi Amoli's interpretation of verses 116 to 118 of Surah Al-Ma'idah. Prophet Jesus (PBUH) refutes the accusation of promoting polytheism by using logical arguments to assert that he never called people to falsehood. He argues that if he had made such a claim, God would have known it; since God is unaware of this false accusation, it is proven to be untrue. The commentary clarifies that Jesus only spoke by God's command to invite people to monotheism, explicitly denying any claim to divinity. The term "Tawaffa" in the verse is interpreted not as definitive death, but rather as a state of unconsciousness or sleep. A key theological point is that God is the "Knower of the Unseen," meaning what is hidden from others is fully revealed to Him. Finally, the text distinguishes the meaning of "Shahid" in this specific context as God being a witness to human actions, rather than being the object of witnessing.
