The radio program begins by narrating two short stories about the lives of martyrs Ali Reza Nahidi and Yadollah Akbari, highlighting their sacrifices during the Iran-Iraq war. The first story illustrates Ali Reza's commitment to saving public funds by patching his torn pants instead of buying new ones. The second story, titled "Forgiveness," depicts Hajj Yadollah's intense sensitivity to backbiting and his efforts to protect the reputation of others at a Basij base. Following these narratives, the show plays a song honoring the martyrs and commemorates those who died in terrorism and health defense. The final segment features a live report from Medina, timed just before the start of the Umrah rituals for Hajj pilgrims. The reporter describes visiting the Baqi cemetery and the Green Dome, emphasizing the unity of pilgrims in their white garments. He also addresses the challenges faced by women and the elderly during visits, as well as the new regulations prohibiting the export of Zamzam water. The program concludes by inviting listeners to follow the "House of Friendship" show and wishing them a safe and blessed pilgrimage.
