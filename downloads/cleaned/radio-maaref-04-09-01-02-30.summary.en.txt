In the radio program "Porsaman," Professor Maleki addressed various Quranic questions from listeners. He explained that God's oaths to celestial bodies are not out of necessity but to emphasize the importance of the message and highlight divine signs. The term "day" in the six-day creation narrative refers to a stage of development rather than a 24-hour period. Regarding the Flood of Noah, the professor noted that due to the limited population at the time, Noah was the prophet for all humanity on Earth. He distinguished between "interpretation by opinion" and legitimate scholarly differences, stressing the need for adherence to jurisprudential and narrational principles in exegesis. The professor also highlighted the longitudinal evolution of Shia commentaries and recommended the "Nemooneh" translation for general audiences.
