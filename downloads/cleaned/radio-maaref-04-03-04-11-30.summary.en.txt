This radio program addressed several jurisprudential questions and provided authoritative answers. The discussion began with the ownership of leftover fruits at event halls, which depends on the contract or explicit permission from the host. Regarding the religious tax of Khums, it was emphasized that business assets like shops are subject to this tax, and consulting one's Marja is essential for accurate calculation. The ruling on a follower who joined the congregation in the third rak'ah without reciting the Surah was clarified; according to Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi, the prayer remains valid and does not need to be repeated. The issue of shared entry and exit for men and women in public places was also discussed, noting that it is permissible as long as it does not lead to improper mixing or gaze. Finally, contact numbers for submitting further religious inquiries were announced, and the program concluded with a recitation from the Holy Quran.
