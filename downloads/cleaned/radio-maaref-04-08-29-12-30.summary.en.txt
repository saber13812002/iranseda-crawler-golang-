In this radio program, a lecture by Allameh Hasan Zadeh Amoli on the exalted status of the Ahl al-Bayt, particularly Imam Hassan (AS), was broadcast. Citing narrations, the speaker asserted that Imamate is a divine appointment rather than a human choice, emphasizing that authority is designated by God. Through the story of Imam Musa ibn Ja'far and his father Imam Sadiq (AS), the speaker illustrated that Imams are among the "Lahbulah" and serve as the guardians of the religion and the Quran. The silence and peace treaty of Imam Hassan (AS) were highlighted as the primary factors in preserving Islam and preventing the division of Muslims. Furthermore, Imam Hassan (AS) was described as the "Vault of Knowledge" and the repository of innate science and cosmic truths. The lecture concluded by stressing the necessity of focusing on these spiritual realities and avoiding negligence in both worldly and afterlife pursuits.
