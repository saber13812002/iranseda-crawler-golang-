Ayatollah Mahmoud Abdollahi shares his memories of preaching trips undertaken before the Islamic Revolution in Iran. He describes the hardships of traveling in small groups to rural areas and cities like Tehran and Ilam during the early 1980s. The speaker recounts the clandestine activities conducted in Ilam and Shush during Ramadan, where they frequently changed locations to evade security forces. He also mentions his interactions with revolutionary figures such as Ayatollah Shah-Abadi and Ayatollah Tabasi during this high-risk period. Towards the end of the broadcast, a prayer for the reappearance of the Imam of the Age is recited. This is followed by an excerpt from Imam Khomeini’s speeches regarding the Iranian uprising and the fear of superpowers. These anecdotes highlight the crucial role of preachers in mobilizing the public and sustaining resistance before the revolution.
