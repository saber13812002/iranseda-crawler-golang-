The radio program "Parsaiyan" began by promoting the virtue of silence and speech management, citing narrations from the Prophet and Imam Sadiq (AS) regarding the link between quietness and wisdom.
The late Ayatollah Behjat was introduced as a prime example of this virtue, who considered silence a substitute for night prayer and referenced the story of Adam (AS) when questioned about his quiet demeanor.
The second part of the program presented a dramatic retelling of the book "Seyyed Man Hosseini," depicting the spiritual journey of Mohammad Taghi and Raza to Karbala and Bayn al-Haramayn.
In this narrative, the pilgrims' challenges, such as a dead phone battery and crowded shrines, are accompanied by internal reflections and poetry, defining "becoming a Karbalai" as an internal transformation.
The author, observing the diversity of pilgrims and the heroes of Karbala, seeks a personal role model and concludes the pilgrimage by purchasing prayer beads and a seal.
In the final segment, a jurisprudential question about a religious vow (Sadaqa Shar'iyya) for abandoning a sin was answered, emphasizing the necessity of fulfilling the vow and the impossibility of canceling it.
The program concluded by inviting listeners to follow the official Radio Ma'aref channel on the Eitaa messaging app and to tune in to future episodes.
