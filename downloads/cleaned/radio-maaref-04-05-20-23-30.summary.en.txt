This special radio program for the Arbaeen pilgrimage began with prayers for the reappearance of the Imam and the pilgrims' "Labbaik" chants on the route from Najaf to Karbala.
Pilgrims dedicated their steps to unknown martyrs and prominent figures such as General Qasem Soleimani and President Ebrahim Raisi.
The broadcast emphasized Islamic unity and the gathering of hearts from around the world centered on the love for Imam Hussein (PBUH).
The primary lesson of Arbaeen was identified as "steadfastness," rooted in the Quran and the legacy of Hazrat Zahra and Hazrat Zainab (SA).
A contrast was drawn between the terrifying image of Islam in Western media and the true, compassionate Islam exemplified by Imam Hussein.
Despite the hardships of the journey and the heat, pilgrims spoke of their patience and gratitude for the spiritual blessings and kindness received.
Finally, listeners were encouraged to participate in the "Nazar Salawat" campaign and support pilgrims by sending text messages to the provided numbers.
