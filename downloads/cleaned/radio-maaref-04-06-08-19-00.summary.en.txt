The radio program "Zamzam Ahkam" began with Quranic recitation and praise before discussing the jurisprudential rules regarding medical visits. Hujjat al-Islam Heidbour explained that the general rule is to see a same-gender doctor, but in cases of necessity, visiting an opposite-gender doctor is permissible if minimal necessity is observed and full coverage is maintained. He emphasized that in procedures like sonography, if a female doctor is unavailable, a male doctor must limit his actions to the absolute minimum, avoiding direct touch or looking at the patient's body. The program then addressed listener questions, clarifying that using lip balm does not invalidate fasting unless swallowed, and that divorce is valid with a proxy and two just witnesses even without the woman's physical presence. It was also stated that obstacles under toenails must be removed for wudu, and missed prayers must be performed in the same form they were missed. Finally, the program discussed the permissibility of selling long-term checks at a discount to the owner or others, concluding with contact information for listeners.
