In this program, Professor Mohammadi analyzes the issue of hijab as a multi-dimensional challenge encompassing political, security, social, and ethical aspects, rather than a simple jurisprudential ruling. By comparing the current situation with the era of the Prophet (PBUH) and Western societies, he emphasizes that the lack of hijab today is a media and security issue with devastating consequences, such as the destruction of families and the promotion of animalistic gazes in society. In the short answer section, the importance of waiting for the Imam and reciting the covenant during the period of occultation, as well as the protective role of the "Hifz of Imam Javad" against natural and supernatural events, were highlighted. The claim that humanity descends from Cain was refuted, explaining that the majority of humans are from the lineage of Seth and Prophet Noah (AS). In the theological discussion, referencing Avicenna, it was explained that the world was created with a single divine will, and God created beings with their inherent perfection, rather than assembling them piece by piece. Finally, the difference between the "ontological" and "epistemological" levels in Islam was clarified; Islam is inherently complete, but its practical implementation requires fundamental reforms by the Imam of the Time (AJ).
