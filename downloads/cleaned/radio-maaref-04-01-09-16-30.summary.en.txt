In the "Porsaman Quran" program, Professor Maleki addressed a question regarding the Prophet's dream and the Treaty of Hudaybiyyah, explaining that the treaty was not a defeat but a necessary precursor to the conquest of Mecca in the eighth year of Hijra. He emphasized that this event served as a test of faith for the companions, and the return from Mecca laid the groundwork for a greater victory and the fulfillment of God's promise. In the second segment, the discussion focused on the verse from Surah Luqman and the relationship between wisdom and upbringing, highlighting respect for parents as a path to monotheism and distinguishing divine wisdom from mere formal education. Professor Maleki also stressed the importance of effective and gentle speech in raising children and maintaining loving relationships with parents even in cases of ideological differences. At the end of the program, the host extended greetings for Nowruz and Ramadan, emphasizing the necessity of prayer for the unity of Muslims and the defeat of oppressors. Additionally, the Supreme Leader's remarks on the efficacy of prayer and the virtues of Imam Ali (PBUH), as narrated by himself and Imam Baqir (PBUH), were broadcast. These segments focused on the importance of spirituality, prayer, and learning from the conduct of the Infallibles in daily life and social struggles.
