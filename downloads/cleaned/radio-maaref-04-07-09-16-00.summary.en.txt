In this radio program, after reciting verses from Surah Al-Baqarah, Hujjat al-Islam Mohammadi-Sharoudi addressed various theological and jurisprudential questions from listeners. He affirmed that performing good deeds on behalf of the Infallibles and sharing their rewards with one's parents is entirely valid and meritorious. Regarding the topic of wishing for death, he explained that while desiring death for oneself is permissible if accompanied by spiritual readiness, cursing others or wishing death upon them is incorrect and may reflect back on the person making the curse. The expert also emphasized the importance of avoiding futile arguments and debates, advising that discussions should aim for truth rather than mere victory. In response to a historical query, he highlighted that Imam Hassan's (PBUH) presence in the Prophet's Mosque in Medina served as a barrier against Muawiyah's political infiltration and helped preserve the Islamic identity of the city. He recommended the book "Iyanushi" for further reading on this historical matter. The program concluded with closing prayers and information on how to submit new questions.
