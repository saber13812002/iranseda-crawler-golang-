The radio program began by explaining Ayatollah Shabiri Zanjani's ruling on calculating khums for grapes and their syrup, which is based on the principle of "realized benefit" and the passage of a financial year. It then covered Ayatollah Javadi Amoli's fatwa regarding prostration where the forehead touches a forbidden surface; if the person regains control and sits down, the prostration is considered valid without the need for repetition. The second part of the show focused on interpreting a narration from Imam Sadiq (PBUH) that emphasizes the transient nature of the world and the importance of mindful presence in the current moment. This narration advises against being distracted by the past or the future, urging believers to be patient in obedience and in abandoning sin during the present time. The program concluded with the recitation of verses from Surah Fatir and an invitation to perform the morning prayer.
