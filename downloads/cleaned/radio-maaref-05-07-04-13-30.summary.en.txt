The program began with a sermon by Hojjat-ol-Islam Shariaty and a prayer of gratitude, followed by an introduction to the ceremonies at the shrine of Imam Reza in Shiraz, coinciding with the start of autumn and the academic year. The main segment featured a court case where an Iranian couple was prosecuted for fraud against an Afghan citizen, who alleged they threatened him with a knife after he paid them for illegal border crossing. The judge, noting inconsistencies in the testimonies and the potential for other related cases, placed all three individuals under detention and ordered the return of the stolen property. Subsequently, Judge Mohsen Akbari provided an educational segment emphasizing the need for legal management of foreign nationals, the importance of treating them with kindness, and the equal application of the law to all individuals. He also highlighted the severity of organized fraud, warning of heavy prison sentences and fines for such crimes. The broadcast concluded with an inspirational speech on the spiritual authority of soldiers and the role of faith in national strength.
