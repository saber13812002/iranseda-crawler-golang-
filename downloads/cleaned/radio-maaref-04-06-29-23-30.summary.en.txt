In this program, Professor Maleki interprets Quranic verses to emphasize the unity of God as both Creator and Sustainer, arguing that these roles cannot be separated into distinct entities. He attributes the denial of this truth to stubbornness and a lack of deep reflection, noting that polytheists historically attributed governance to angels despite acknowledging God as the Creator. A key point is that the Quran guides only fair-minded individuals, while those who knowingly reject the truth are turned away from the right path. The discussion concludes by honoring the Sacred Defense week, stating that Iran's current dignity and life are indebted to its veterans and martyrs. The speaker urges Islamic nations to awaken and emulate the Prophet's and Ahl al-Bayt's example in both material and spiritual jihad. Listeners are encouraged to study early Islamic history and strive to Islamize their societies to counter enemy propaganda. The program ends with the recitation of Ziyarat Al-Yasin and the prayer for the Imam's reappearance, expressing hope for the Mahdi's arrival.
