This radio program explores the interpretation of verses 162 to 165 of Surah An-Nisa, drawing on the insights of Ayatollah Javadi Amoli. The central theme is the concept of "Rasikhun fi al-Ilm" and the inherent human limitation in fully comprehending the infinite essence of God. Citing Nahj al-Balagha, the commentator explains that complete knowledge of the Divine Essence is impossible, and acknowledging this limitation is itself a sign of deep scholarly insight. The distinction between "believers" and "those firm in knowledge" is clarified, emphasizing that true believers accept the final Prophet and the Quran. The discussion also highlights the mutual respect required between the public and scholars, warning against both scholarly arrogance and public disrespect. Finally, referencing Surah Al-Baqarah, the program underscores the high status of combining knowledge with action and the importance of prioritizing scholars in communal prayers and social affairs.
