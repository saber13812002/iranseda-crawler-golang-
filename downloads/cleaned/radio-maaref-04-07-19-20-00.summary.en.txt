Radio Ma'aref began by reporting on the Gaza ceasefire agreement, under which Israel will release 2,000 Palestinian prisoners in exchange for 20 Israeli hostages. Despite the truce, reports highlighted significant casualties and infrastructure destruction in Gaza, while resistance movements emphasized their commitment to continued resistance and rejected any foreign trusteeship. Israeli media analyses acknowledged Israel's defeat in the war and noted the resurgence of the Palestinian issue on the global agenda. Other news segments covered ongoing Israeli attacks in southern Lebanon and a French court's decision to cancel the eviction of an Islamic school. The broadcast also announced the introduction of Quranic education in Australian schools and the upcoming Arbaeen pilgrimage narrative conference in Karbala. The program concluded with a call for public support for Gaza and an introduction to the tourism potential of Fars province. Finally, a discussion with Hojjat al-Islam Faghi began, focusing on the central role of prayer and monotheistic ethics in transforming the human existential structure.
