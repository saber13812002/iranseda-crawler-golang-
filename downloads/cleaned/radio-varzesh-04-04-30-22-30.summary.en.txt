Mohammad Reza Mehranpour, a veteran Iranian footballer, discussed his significant early mistake of leaving Esteghlal due to a conflict with management during a radio interview. He expressed regret over this decision made in his youth, noting that his current experience would have led to a different choice. Mehranpour addressed his absence from the national football team, attributing it to his own lack of effort compared to superior players at the same position. He highlighted his five-year collaboration with Ali Daei as a major honor in his coaching career, having worked with him at Saipa and Naft Tehran. Despite Esteghlal fans' expectations, Mehranpour stated he would not accept a Perspolis offer out of respect for the emotional rivalry between the two clubs' supporters. He revealed that he is still playing official football in Tehran's club league at the age of 54, holding the record for the oldest active player in that category. Finally, Mehranpour emphasized the need for Esteghlal to hire a manager with a bold vision who can deliver attractive, attacking football to satisfy the fans in the upcoming season.
