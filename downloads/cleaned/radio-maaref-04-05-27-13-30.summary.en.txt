In this radio program, Professor Seyed Ahmad Faqih discusses "Tawhidic ethics" and the practice of spiritual paths, emphasizing that deep knowledge of God is essential for moral reform. He explains that intimacy with God requires a firm belief in His constant surveillance of both the external and internal aspects of a person, which must be cultivated through continuous practice to become a felt reality. In the Q&A section, the professor advises that even when one is innocent, one should kindly clarify misunderstandings to prevent others from being hurt or forming negative assumptions. He stresses the importance of forgiveness, genuine repentance, and weeping to purify the soul and remove barriers between believers. The discussion then shifts to the Quran's role as the "spring of hearts" and a comprehensive source of knowledge for both individual and social needs. The professor highlights that the Quran addresses societal and political structures, countering the view that it is solely for personal spiritual connection. Finally, he recommends engaging with the Quran through reflection and referring to the Ahl al-Bayt as the primary interpreters of its profound teachings.
