In this radio segment, Dr. Rafiee utilizes the teachings of Imam Reza (AS) to refute the claim that the Prophet (PBUH) did not appoint a successor, citing Quranic verses on the perfection of religion. He emphasizes that Imamate is a continuous creedal principle extending until the Day of Judgment, characterized by the Imam's compassion, protection, and support for the oppressed. The speaker rejects negative portrayals of the Mahdi, highlighting his role as a merciful figure who will bring justice to all, including non-Muslims. Historical anecdotes, such as Imam Reza's respectful handling of a Shia's coffin in Tus, are shared to illustrate the deep emotional and spiritual bond between the Imam and his followers. The segment concludes with a recitation of a prayer reportedly heard from Imam Mahdi (AJ) by Sayyid Ibn Tawus in Samarra, seeking healing for the sick and the return of the displaced. The broadcast ends with a supplication for the hastening of the Mahdi's reappearance and support for the oppressed nation.
