In this radio program, Dr. Pourhosseini first discussed the significance of therapeutic massage in traditional Iranian medicine for the elderly. He highlighted massage as an effective tool for eliminating waste products, improving blood circulation, and alleviating muscle weakness during old age. Subsequently, in the "Nashani" segment, Professor Mohammadi Shahroudi emphasized the importance of acting on religious values in worldly life by distinguishing between the "world as a school" and the "corrupt world." Later, Hujjat al-Islam Rasoul, citing a verse from Surah Al-Hijr, identified despair of God's mercy as a major sin and a sign of misguidance. He presented life's hardships as opportunities for spiritual growth and training, urging listeners against despair in the face of difficulties. Finally, the program addressed the necessity of unity between seminary scholars and university academics to address national needs and counter enemy-driven division.
