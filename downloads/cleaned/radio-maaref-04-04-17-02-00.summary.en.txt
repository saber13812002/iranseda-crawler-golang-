The broadcast continues the narrative from the book "Neshan-e Hasan," where the character Nafail, reflecting on martyrdom and separation from the Prophet's city, finds solace in Imam Hasan's patience and falls asleep after seeking forgiveness.
The program then transitions to the "Parsaiyan" segment on Radio Ma'aref, where Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasizes the critical importance of sincerity (Ikhlas) in worship and daily actions.
Citing Quranic verses and hadiths, he explains that true sincerity allows wisdom to flow from the heart to the tongue and fundamentally transforms a person's spiritual state.
To illustrate this concept, stories are shared about Ayatollah Ahmadi Mianjeh and Allameh Jafari, who demonstrated unwavering commitment to pure intentions for God, rejecting worldly fame and credit for their works.
The show also introduces the "Mahram Radio" station, which is broadcasting Ashura-themed poetry and music 24/7 during the month of Muharram.
The broadcast concludes with the recitation of dawn supplications and morning prayers, inviting listeners to immerse themselves in the spiritual atmosphere of the early morning hours.
