In this discussion, Dr. Tabossi emphasized the importance of returning to the fundamental principles of physical education to identify athletic and scientific talents among millions of students. He noted that during the 1990s and early 2000s, student sports focused on talent identification and mass participation, which laid the foundation for Iran's subsequent Olympic medal successes. Although the current focus has shifted toward competitive championships, he argued that maintaining the cultural and motivational aspects of student competitions is essential for fostering sportsmanship and social cohesion. Dr. Tabossi also highlighted the critical role of the Mass Sports Federation in improving public health and developing scientific guidelines for government ministries. Regarding skiing, he analyzed the financial paradox between local talents and urban amateurs, as well as the seasonal and structural limitations facing the sport. Finally, he advised that sports should be institutionalized as a lifelong habit to ensure healthy aging and physical well-being in later years.
