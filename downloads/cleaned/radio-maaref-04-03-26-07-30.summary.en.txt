In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Mohammad Hadi Fallah discusses the vice of arrogance and its devastating consequences. Citing narrations from Imam Ali (AS), he describes arrogance as the devil's greatest trap, which leads humans from spiritual light into darkness. A primary consequence of arrogance is the impairment of practical intellect, causing the individual to fall from the status of humanity. The speaker explains that arrogance does not elevate a person but rather diminishes them through ingratitude and the misuse of their existential potential. Furthermore, arrogance acts as a sin that fuels other vices such as envy and greed, leading to a deeper descent into transgression. The discussion also touches upon the exalted status of martyrs, who remain alive with the prophets, contrasting their humility with the self-centeredness of the arrogant. Finally, the importance of sincerity in reciting "La ilaha illa Allah" and avoiding sin is emphasized as the path to salvation from these moral defects.
