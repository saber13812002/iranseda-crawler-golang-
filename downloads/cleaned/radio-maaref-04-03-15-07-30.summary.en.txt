The radio program begins by highlighting the 15 Khordad uprising as a crucial precursor to the Islamic Revolution, emphasizing the people's resistance against tyranny. It notes the spiritual significance of this date coinciding with the Day of Arafah, linking moral integrity to the revolution's success. The segment then profiles Martyr Teyeb Haj Razzazi, whose deep devotion to Imam Hussein (AS) and defiance of the Pahlavi regime led to his martyrdom. Following this, Hujjat al-Islam Falah discusses the virtue of humility in the "Ganj-e Sa'adat" program. He defines humility as the result of knowledge regarding God's greatness and a proper understanding of one's own status. A sound and pure heart is identified as the essential prerequisite for achieving humility and accessing divine presence. The discussion concludes by stating that on the Day of Judgment, only a pure heart holds value, while attachment to the world hinders spiritual progress.
