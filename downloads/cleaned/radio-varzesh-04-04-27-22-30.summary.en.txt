Masoumeh Rezazadeh, a former national futsal player and current coach, recounts her transition from futsal to football. She explains that technical and hardware challenges, such as different footwear and field conditions, kept her primary focus on futsal. After retiring from playing in 2012, she pursued coaching and successfully obtained her AFC Level 3 license. Rezazadeh has coached various clubs, including Persepolis, Saipa, and Sepahan, achieving titles such as the Division One league championship. She attributes the dominance of futsal over women's football in Iran to the intense global competition in football and infrastructure limitations for women. She also cites managerial issues at Sepahan as the main reason for leaving her coaching position there. Her ultimate goal is to coach the women's national futsal team and eventually experience coaching in the men's top league.
