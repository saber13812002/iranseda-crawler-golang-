The radio program "Andisheha" explored the concept of divine laws, emphasizing their immutable nature and dominance over historical and social events. Experts categorized these laws into two main groups: the destruction of oppressors and the support of believers, citing Quranic verses as evidence. A specialized discussion followed regarding the role of these divine laws in management and social planning processes. Dr. Alavi argued that while divine laws are not measurable technical components of a plan, they serve as the governing framework and guiding spirit of any strategy. He stressed that faith in divine support does not replace precise planning but is a necessary and sufficient condition for the successful execution of such plans. Ultimately, divine support is contingent upon genuine faith, patience in the face of hardship, and practical action by believers. This divine aid manifests not only through visible victories but also through psychological peace and spiritual confidence when facing challenges.
