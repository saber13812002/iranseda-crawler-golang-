In the "Persoman Ateghadi" program, Professor Mohammadi addressed various listener questions regarding Islamic beliefs. He emphasized the necessity of acquiring knowledge as the best guide for life, noting that learning can occur during idle moments or while working through media. The professor refuted materialist views by explaining that God is continuously creating the universe moment by moment, and that the existence of all beings depends on their constant connection to Him. He clarified that physical illnesses are limited to the material world, as humans will possess healthy, spiritual bodies in the Barzakh and the Hereafter. Regarding a hadith from Imam Sadiq (AS), he stressed that in theological discourse, certainty and rational proof are required to accept a principle, rather than relying solely on the chain of narration. The professor also highlighted the spiritual impact of God's remembrance on matter, citing the inherent consciousness present in all creations. Finally, he explained that Adam and Eve were sent from a Barzakh realm to Earth to undergo worldly trials, which are essential for reaching their ultimate destination in Paradise.
