In this radio program, Professor Jafari analyzes the context of Imam Hussein's uprising and the pivotal role of Kufa as a base for the Shia community. He explains that despite receiving thousands of letters and signatures from the people of Kufa pledging their support, Yazid attempted to silence the Imam by sending assassins to Mecca. Imam Hussein (PBUH) left Mecca to preserve its sanctity and avoid shedding his blood there, choosing instead to be martyred in Karbala to deliver his message of truth more effectively. The professor emphasizes that allegiance to Yazid was impossible for the Imam, as pledging loyalty to falsehood, even as a precaution, was impermissible when Islam was being deviated. Had there been no invitation from Kufa, the Imam would likely have moved to a safer region like Yemen to continue his activities. The program concludes by stressing the importance of preserving the traditions of Muharram and Safar to keep the memory of Ashura alive and to defend Islam against its enemies.
