The radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat," featuring Hujjat al-Islam Fallah, explored the spiritual discipline of the tongue through the lens of monotheistic ethics. Fallah described the tongue of the nafs as a fierce beast that, if left untamed, becomes a source of disbelief, polytheism, and hypocrisy. He emphasized that most human sins and errors stem from speech, which will be the primary subject of divine judgment on the Day of Resurrection. The consequences of an untrained tongue include the loss of divine blessings and public humiliation before God and the Infallibles. Furthermore, the lecture highlighted that the wound inflicted by the tongue is far more severe and difficult to heal than physical injuries from swords or spears. Ultimately, the danger of the nafs-driven tongue lies in its capacity to destroy the honor and credibility of others, as well as the speaker themselves.
