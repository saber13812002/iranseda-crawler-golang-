The radio program "Shifa" explores ways to improve lifestyle and health by drawing on religious sources and Islamic traditional medicine. The show begins by reciting healing prayers and emphasizing divine greatness to create a spiritual atmosphere for the listeners. The main segment features historical narratives about Hatim al-Ta'i, a symbol of extreme generosity among the Arabs during the pre-Islamic era. Nordin Abd al-Rahman Jami recounts a story from his book Bahar al-Asrar about Hatim's encounter with an orphan named Qalam. In this tale, Qalam slaughtered all his sheep to cook a meal for his guest, demonstrating his unwavering dedication to hospitality. Hatim, who possessed significantly more wealth, admitted that Qalam was more generous because he gave everything he had, whereas Hatim only offered a portion of his surplus. This story serves as a model for unconditional giving and prioritizing others' needs over personal interests. The program concludes by highlighting how these ethical and spiritual values contribute to enhancing both mental and physical well-being.
