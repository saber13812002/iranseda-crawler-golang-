The radio program begins with a discussion on traffic management during the school reopening, featuring the head of Tehran's traffic police. The conversation then shifts to the spiritual distinction between knowledge and true insight, emphasizing that insight is the root of sincere love and obedience. Quranic verses are cited to warn that human devils use material and verbal allure to deceive people. Historical figures like Pharaoh and Qarun are presented as examples of those who used worldly splendor to mislead others. The speaker explains that many individuals are asleep in spiritual negligence, unaware of God's presence and the importance of a meaningful connection. It is concluded that any rebellious being can act as a devil, urging listeners to seek refuge in God from their own inner flaws.
