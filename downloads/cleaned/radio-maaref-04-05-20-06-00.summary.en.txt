This program analyzes the fundamental differences between the etiquette of prophets and that of atheists through Quranic interpretation. It highlights that prophets recognize only God as their Lord, attribute all perfection and blessings to divine grace, and rely on God for their continuity, whereas atheists replace revelation with personal desires and view their achievements as self-made. The prayer of Prophet Shu'ayb is presented as a model of reliance on God and a request for divine justice between him and his people. The supplications of David and Solomon are discussed, emphasizing their gratitude for God's favors and their request for righteous deeds that please the Divine. The narrative of Prophet Jonah illustrates that sincere repentance and recitation of specific invocations are the key to salvation and the resumption of one's mission. God saved Jonah through the grace of guardianship, enabling him to guide his people to faith and complete his prophetic duty. The program concludes by stressing that true spiritual etiquette involves gratitude for blessings and seeking actions that lead to divine satisfaction, distinguishing prophets from others.
