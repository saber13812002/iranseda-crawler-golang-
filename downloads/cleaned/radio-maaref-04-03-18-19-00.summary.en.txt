The radio program began by emphasizing the religious obligation to treat parents kindly and to perform their missed prayers. Subsequently, several jurisprudential questions were addressed, including the prohibition of combining intentions for two voluntary prayers and the invalidity of financial commitments made under coercion. The discussion also covered the rules for delaying the Zuhr prayer to attend Jumu'ah and the correct recitation of the opening Takbir. A significant portion of the broadcast focused on Imam Reza's (PBUH) advice to avoid backbiting and damaging the honor of others. Later, the host interviewed a devotee at the Sayyid Mohammad shrine in Khomeinishahr, who discussed the importance of the Istighfar prayer and the spiritual significance of the site. The program concluded with musical interludes and reminders about the virtues of congregational prayer and praying at the earliest time.
