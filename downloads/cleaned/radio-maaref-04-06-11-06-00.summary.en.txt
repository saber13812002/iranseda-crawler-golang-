In this program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli, while interpreting Surah Al-Ma'idah, emphasizes the central role of reason alongside revelation as sources of religion. He argues that reason is an independent authority in principles of faith, jurisprudence, and ethics, serving as a valid legal proof where textual sources are silent. Concepts such as "ilm al-ijmali," the rule of "ishtighal," and the obligation of prerequisites are cited as examples of reason's independent rulings that underpin jurisprudential decisions. Furthermore, in commercial transactions, reason plays a decisive role in matters like mutual rescission and options, reflecting the divine will. The speaker asserts that reason and revelation both reveal the same divine source and should not be viewed as opposing forces. Finally, referencing the Prophet's prayer at Masjid Khayf, he stresses the importance of relying on God's sustenance rather than worldly expectations.
