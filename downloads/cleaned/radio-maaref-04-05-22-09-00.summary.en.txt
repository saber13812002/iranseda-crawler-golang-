In this radio program, Professor Mohammadi addresses listeners' questions regarding Quranic interpretation. He emphasizes that the Quran and the Ahl al-Bayt are inseparable sources of guidance, and that following both simultaneously is the only path to human salvation. The professor clarifies that the world is neither poison with a bit of sugar nor a drink with a fly, but rather a farm for acquiring eternal rewards. He stresses that worldly assets like wealth and status are valuable only when utilized in the path of God and for serving others. In the second segment, the documentary "Front Line" features the testimony of Soheila Farjanfar, a young nurse who served in Ahvaz during the early days of the Iran-Iraq War. She recounts the initial terror, the shaking of buildings, and the sight of her city being destroyed by Iraqi forces. Farjanfar shares her experience of giving birth to her child amidst the war and the relentless efforts of the medical team at Taleqani Hospital to save the wounded despite shortages and bombings. This narrative highlights the courage and sacrifice of the medical staff and the people in defending their homeland.
