In this radio program, Professor Mohammad Sharoudi emphasized the importance of self-knowledge and sincerity in worship during sacred times. He addressed listener questions regarding the wisdom behind performing optional prayers before obligatory ones to maintain spiritual continuity. The professor also discussed the current existence of Paradise and Hell, noting that the righteous can perceive these realms through spiritual insight. He affirmed the legitimacy of seeking intercession through the Ahl al-Bayt, reassuring listeners that invoking their names is a valid form of devotion. Furthermore, he clarified that sorcery is not the sole cause of life's problems and can be neutralized through prayer, charity, and reliance on God. The professor warned against consulting sorcerers, stating that such practices are sinful and often result in further harm. Finally, he advised listeners to resolve issues through rational means, such as counseling and piety, rather than relying on dubious supernatural methods.
