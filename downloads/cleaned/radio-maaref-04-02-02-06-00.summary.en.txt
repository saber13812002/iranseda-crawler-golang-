In this program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verses from Surah Al-Ma'idah, emphasizing the necessity of avoiding the pursuit of personal desires and blind imitation of previous generations. He explains that the curses pronounced by prophets like David and Jesus upon the Children of Israel were actually by God's permission, signifying their distance from divine mercy. A primary reason for this curse was the failure of community leaders to forbid evil, which allowed sin and corruption to spread throughout the society. The commentator argues that if these groups were truly believers, they would never have adopted disbelievers as their allies, as indicated by the verse stating that many of them take disbelievers as friends. Consequently, the Quran preserves the rights of the believing minority by specifying that "many of them are corrupt," rather than generalizing the corruption to all People of the Book. Additionally, the verse regarding the intense enmity of certain groups is presented as a principle for international relations, highlighting the strong hostility of Jews toward Muslims and the relative closeness of Christians due to the presence of non-arrogant, wise clergy among them.
