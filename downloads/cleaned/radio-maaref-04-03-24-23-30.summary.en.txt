In response to questions regarding divine promises and retribution against oppressors, verse 46 of Surah Yunus was cited to explain that some of this retribution is fulfilled in this world.
The Prophet's (PBUH) victory over the Meccan polytheists and the historical defeats of the Umayyad and Abbasid dynasties are cited as examples of these promises being realized.
However, due to the limited capacity of the earthly realm, the complete and final punishment of all oppressors does not occur here, with the majority reserved for the Day of Judgment.
The discussion then shifted to the status of Imam Ali (AS) as the "Caesar of Justice" and the individual capable of establishing complete divine justice.
It was emphasized that Imam Ali's (AS) authority is not merely a political position but the foundation for establishing God's justice in society.
Keeping the ideology of Imam Ali (AS) alive means following his method of justice, rather than merely engaging in ceremonial mourning.
Finally, it was stressed that the justice-oriented methods of Imam Ali (AS) must be implemented by nations and leaders in all eras.
