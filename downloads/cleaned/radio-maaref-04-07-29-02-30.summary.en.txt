In the radio program "Persman Ateghadi" on Radio Ma'aref, Professor Mohammadi addressed various religious and social questions from listeners. He emphasized that human superiority is based on knowledge and intellect rather than race or tribal affiliation. Responding to a question about Khums, he proved its obligation and its applicability beyond war spoils by citing Quranic verses and the Prophet's practice. He also refuted the claim that the Prophet lacked knowledge of the unseen, demonstrating that he possessed such knowledge through divine instruction. The necessity of Hijab as a religious and legal principle was also discussed. Professor Mohammadi described the majority of Hijab violations as organized efforts aimed at subverting the system, while attributing the rest to personal desires. He stressed the duty of all people and officials to oppose evil and called for cultural and security measures to combat this phenomenon.
