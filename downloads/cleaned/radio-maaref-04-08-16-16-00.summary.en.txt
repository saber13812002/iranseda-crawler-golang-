In the radio program "Persaman Tarikhi," Dr. Mohammad Reza Jabbari addressed listeners' questions regarding Islamic historical events. Regarding the martyrdom of Imam Hussein's (PBUH) companions, the professor noted that the enemy army's celebrations were primarily driven by the "Uthmani" faction and those influenced by Ibn Ziyad's propaganda. Concerning the Ahl al-Bayt's caravan entering Kufa, he clarified that contrary to some claims, the people were mostly moved to tears upon seeing Zaynab's (SA) wounds and the captives' condition. The second part of the discussion introduced "Shahid al-Awwal," "Shahid al-Thani," and "Shahid al-Salith," prominent scholars who were martyred by contemporary governments for defending Shia Islam and their scholarly works. Finally, the professor discussed the Battle of Qadisiyah, identifying it as the key to opening the door for the conquest of Iran. He emphasized that this victory over the Sassanids was achieved through the strategic guidance and command of Imam Ali (PBUH). Additionally, he explained that these conquests were part of the expansion of Islam, although some extreme behaviors by commanders have been subject to criticism.
