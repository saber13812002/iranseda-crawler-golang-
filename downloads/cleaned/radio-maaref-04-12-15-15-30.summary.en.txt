In this radio broadcast, analysts highlight the strategic dilemma facing the United States in the region, where staying incurs heavy losses while leaving would damage its prestige. Iranian military commanders stated that the primary focus of missile and drone attacks is on American bases, noting that the enemy's air defense systems are being worn down. The spokesperson for the Khatam al-Anbiya Headquarters reported the successful downing of enemy jets and drones, as well as the retreat of the USS Abraham Lincoln carrier. International media have noted the unprecedented and widespread presence of Iranian people in public squares as a sign of national unity and resistance against the war. Reports also indicate that Iraqi citizens are chanting in support of Iran and against American influence. This popular presence and the continuation of military operations send a clear message to adversaries regarding the nation's resilience and readiness for full-scale confrontation.
