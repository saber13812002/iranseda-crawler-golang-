In the radio program "Rahbord," Professor Bahmani explained the Supreme Leader's new strategy following the enemy's tactical failure to overthrow the system. Citing game theory, he described this approach as an unpredictable and surprising game that has shifted the balance of power in Iran's favor. Key features of this strategy include limiting the battlefield for the enemy while expanding it for Iran, which has induced fear and passivity among American decision-makers. By referencing Iran's capabilities in various fronts, including the Strait of Hormuz, the Leader declared that he would deprive the enemy of peaceful sleep and even a peaceful death. This strategic shift has caused many global leaders and experts, who previously spoke against Iran, to remain silent and wait. The result of this new game is the return of the upper hand to Iran in both the operational field and domestic and international public opinion. This situation, achieved through the sacrifices of martyrs, has forced the enemy into hasty and cautious decision-making. Ultimately, this strategic victory demonstrates Iran's deterrent power and the changed nature of future confrontations with the enemy.
