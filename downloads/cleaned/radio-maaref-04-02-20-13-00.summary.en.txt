In this radio program, the speaker highlights the virtues of visiting Imam Reza (PBUH), noting that it brings spiritual joy, forgiveness of sins, and rewards equivalent to seventy Hajj pilgrimages. The discussion also covers the Quranic verses and poetry inscribed on the Imam's new tombstone, including a poem by the Imam himself. The core of the talk focuses on five characteristics of God's chosen servants, as described in a narration from Imam Reza (PBUH). The first trait is serving people selflessly and happily, a duty that extends even to helping animals. The second trait involves immediate and sincere repentance without making excuses for one's sins. The third characteristic is gratitude for God's blessings and maintaining a positive perspective despite life's challenges. The fourth trait is patience in the face of worldly hardships, while the fifth is forgiveness and restraint during moments of anger. The speaker concludes by recommending the book "Sources of Wisdom" and urging listeners to embody these traits to benefit from the Imam's intercession.
