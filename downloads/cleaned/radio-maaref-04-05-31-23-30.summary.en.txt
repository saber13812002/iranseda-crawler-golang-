The radio program begins by examining historical sources related to the event of Karbala. It highlights Abu Mikhnaf's reports, which were transmitted through Hisham al-Kalbi to al-Tabari's history and Sheikh Mufid's Irsad. Contemporary scholar Mohammad Hadi Yusofi Gharbi extracted and published these reports in his book "Maqtal Abi Mikhnaf." The discussion also notes that Sunni historians such as Masudi, Khwarizmi, and Ibn Asim included these accounts in their works. Subsequently, the "Maqtal al-Hasan" software was introduced, which contains over one hundred reliable historical sources. The second part of the discussion focuses on the Quranic interpretation of war in Islam. It is explained that war serves as a tool to eliminate fitna and establish global peace, rather than merely seeking military victory. Finally, the Prophet's battles are described as manifestations of divine mercy for all humanity.
