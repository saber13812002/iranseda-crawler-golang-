The program begins by expressing solidarity with the oppressed people of Gaza and providing ways to offer assistance. The speaker then highlights the month of Rabi al-Awal, lamenting the lack of grandeur in celebrating the Prophet's birth compared to Christian traditions. He urges for better planning and the spread of joy to honor the birth of the Seal of the Prophets. Two major advantages of Islam over Christianity are discussed: the unaltered nature of the Quran versus the contradictions in the Gospels. Additionally, the preservation of detailed accounts of the Prophet's life in Islamic narrations is noted. The main discourse, based on Nahj al-Balagha, describes three key characteristics of the Prophet: being foretold, famous, and having a noble birth. The concept of a noble birth is explained through the greatness of his parents and miraculous events like the sinking of Lake Homa. The program concludes with prayers for the reappearance of the Imam and blessings upon the Prophet and his family.
