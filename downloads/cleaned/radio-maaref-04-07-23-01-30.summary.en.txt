Hojjat al-Islam Rashed Yazdi emphasized that true peace of mind is achieved only through the remembrance of God and maintaining moderation in life. He identified dwelling on the past and excessive anxiety about the future as the primary causes of life's bitterness and the loss of tranquility. The core message urges individuals to accept their destiny and rely on God to escape mental obsessions and live fully in the present moment. The second part of the program featured the memories of Fatemeh Hajimirmza, the mother of two martyrs, who discussed the hardships of their sacrifice and her maternal longing. She explained that her peace stems from her sons' choice of martyrdom and the spiritual presence they maintain in her life. She also expressed her hope for the intercession of Hazrat Zahra (SA) for herself and her family.
