The radio program "Narrative of Love" featured Alham Haidari, the wife of Martyr Sardar Hassan Tehrani Moghaddam, recounting his life and memories. Mrs. Haidari described her husband's revolutionary activities during his youth, including a memorable anecdote about the occupation of the air force base in 1979. She highlighted his significant role and military creativity in the early stages of the Iran-Iraq War alongside other commanders. The conversation also covered their courtship and marriage in 1983, emphasizing the martyr's unique personality and strict personal discipline. The program then shifted to a critical analysis of global powers' double standards regarding human rights. The speaker condemned the Zionist regime's crimes against Palestinians and the assassination of Iranian scientists. She criticized the West's indifference to the suffering in Gaza and called for the eradication of this terrorist group.
