The radio program began by discussing the ethical conduct of Imam Ali (PBUH) and his defense of his loyal servant, Qanbar, against the arrogance of the elite. It then recounted Qanbar's martyrdom at the hands of Hujjaj ibn Yusuf for his steadfast Shia faith. The second segment addressed a listener's question regarding the historical shift of the Qibla from Jerusalem to the Kaaba in the second year of Hijra. The expert explained that this change served as a divine test to distinguish between pure obedience and religious practice driven by personal desires. Subsequently, the reporter detailed the annual commemoration of the US military defeat in the Dasht-e Lut desert. This event, where a sandstorm thwarted the American operation, was highlighted as a manifestation of divine intervention supporting the Islamic Revolution. The program concluded by honoring the memory of martyr Mohammad Montazer Qaem and announcing the construction of a museum to display the wreckage of the enemy's military equipment.
