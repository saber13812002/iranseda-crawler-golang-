This program features a tafsir of verses 38 to 41 of Surah Al-A'raf by Ayatollah Javadi Amoli, examining the Quranic perspective on the relationship between leaders of disbelief and their followers in the Hereafter. The commentator refutes the notion that the address "O mankind" is exclusive to the Prophet Muhammad, asserting that it applies to all prophets and their respective communities. He distinguishes the punishment of "misled leaders" from "followers," noting that leaders face double punishment for the twin, related sins of their own misguidance and misleading others, whereas followers are typically punished only for their own misguidance unless they actively reinforce the leader's falsehood. Citing verses from Surah An-Nahl and Al-Ankabut, the text clarifies that leaders do not bear the weight of their followers' sins, but rather carry the burden of their own sins and the sin of misleading others. The discussion also touches on the literary usage of the word "ukht" meaning "like" in the Quran, highlighting the parallel fates of past and future disbelievers. Finally, the program revisits the confrontation between the oppressed and the arrogant in Surah As-Sajdah, where the oppressed accuse their leaders of nightly and daily conspiracies that led them to disbelief.
