This radio program discussed legal mechanisms to ensure the financial and psychological security of spouses during old age.
Experts emphasized that individuals can secure their spouse's future through wills, usufruct rights, or reconciliation without requiring the consent of heirs.
It was noted that Islamic jurisprudence has recommended contractual mechanisms since early centuries to prevent future issues and support relatives.
One practical solution is a proprietary will of a residential property to the spouse, which prevents children from seizing it after death.
Another option is establishing a lifetime usufruct right, allowing the spouse to live in the property until death, even if ownership transfers to heirs.
These tools not only prevent the deprivation of the elderly but also demonstrate appreciation and responsibility within marital relationships.
The active involvement of cultural and legal authorities in educating the public about these legal tools was deemed essential to protect the rights of the elderly.
