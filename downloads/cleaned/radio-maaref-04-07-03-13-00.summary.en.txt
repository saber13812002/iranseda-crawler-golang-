The program begins by honoring the Holy Defense Week and commemorating General Soleimani, emphasizing the Supreme Leader's pivotal role in promoting the culture of jihad. The speaker argues that the sanctity of the Holy Defense was rooted not just in its objective, but in the practical implementation of pure religious ethics on the battlefield. This period is presented as a unique model for moral and social education, where rank and ego were absent, and commanders operated with spiritual maturity and humility. In the second part, the necessity of unity against the enemy is highlighted, warning that political double-voicing regarding the adversary constitutes a major betrayal that dulls societal sensitivity. The importance of public awareness and readiness to preserve the Islamic Republic is stressed, noting that this readiness requires elevating the community's consciousness and aspirations. The speaker calls upon officials to emulate paternal behavior and protect religious principles against corrupting trends. Finally, the preservation of the system is described as the embodiment of Islam and a valuable legacy from the martyrs, requiring collective effort to ensure its survival and elevation.
