The first segment presents a Quranic interpretation where "do not be among the ignorant" refers to the heavy burden of knowledge rather than general ignorance.
It emphasizes that those who have voluntarily deprived themselves of guidance are like the dead, making any coercion or invitation ineffective.
The program then transitions to a morning greeting, encouraging listeners to start the week with positive energy and physical activity.
September 25 is highlighted as Physical Education Day, urging the audience to begin their exercise routines.
The history of Physical Education Week and its importance for both physical and mental health are explained to the listeners.
Additionally, the opening of the National Quran Museum and the Farshchian Museum on this day in previous years is recalled.
The historical section reviews the deaths of Thomas Edison and Charles Babbage, two prominent figures in science and technology.
The broadcast concludes by reading listener messages and wishing them success in becoming champions in various sports.
