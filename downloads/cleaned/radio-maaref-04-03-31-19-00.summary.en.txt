The radio program "Zamzam Ahkam" began by addressing various religious questions from listeners. It was clarified that oil on the head does not invalidate wudu as long as it does not prevent water from reaching the skin. The rulings for Adhan and Iqamah, based on Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi's fatwa, emphasized facing the Qibla and maintaining wudu. Regarding moving within the prayer row, Ayatollah Shabiri Zanjani's fatwa stated it is permissible only if it does not interfere with obligatory recitations or cause deviation from the Qibla. The program also explained that the excess of government subsidies, after deducting annual living expenses, is subject to Khums. In the second segment, Hojjatol-Islam Tehrani highlighted the importance of prayer as a pillar of Islam and its necessary connection to the Wilayah of the Ahl al-Bayt. He described prayer as the believer's ascension and warned against negligence in this duty. The episode concluded with a tribute to the life and martyrdom of Shahid Rasoul Akrami during the Battle of Khorramshahr.
