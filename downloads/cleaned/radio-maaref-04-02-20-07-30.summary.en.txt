The "Ganj-e Sa'adat" program, featuring Professor Hadi Fala and Hajhe Falsafeh, examines the signs of divine punishment as described in the Quran. Natural disasters such as floods, earthquakes, diseases, and pest infestations are presented as consequences of denying monotheism and committing sins by past nations. Economic corruption, fraud in transactions, and financial injustice are identified as the primary causes of the punishment inflicted on the people of Shu'ayb and Lot through hail and fiery stones. A crucial point discussed is that these punishments are not limited to the past but can recur for anyone who commits similar sins, tailored to their specific circumstances. However, the community of the Prophet Muhammad is protected from such apparent, physical punishments due to divine mercy. Instead, this community faces "spiritual transmigration," where internal moral shifts can transform a person's character from angelic to bestial. Therefore, maintaining practical monotheism and avoiding ethical sins are essential for preserving spiritual health and avoiding internal divine retribution.
