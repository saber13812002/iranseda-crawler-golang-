In the "Zamzame Ahkam" program, Professor Tootbahi explained the rulings of Umrah al-Tamattu, detailing the Sa'i between Safa and Marwah as the fourth obligatory act. He emphasized performing seven rounds and noted that Haroula, a brisk walk, is recommended for men in a specific section, advising against backtracking if forgotten. The fifth act, Taqsir, involves trimming hair or nails, which releases the pilgrim from Ihram and makes its prohibitions permissible. In the Q&A segment, scholars provided guidance on seeking permission to cross private property and the prayer obligations for patients with urinary incontinence using protective devices. They also discussed purifying mosque carpets from children's urine and defined the uses of voluntary charity as alleviating the financial hardships of believers. The broadcast transitioned to the "Khaneh-ye Doost" program, inviting listeners to pray the Maghrib prayer in mosques. Finally, a scholar discussed the spiritual benefits of visiting Imamzadehs and seeking refuge in the Wilayat of the Ahl al-Bayt for protection from sin.
