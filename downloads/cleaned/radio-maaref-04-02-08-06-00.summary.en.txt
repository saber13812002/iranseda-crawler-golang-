In this program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets Quranic verses to distinguish between verbal and heartfelt faith and oaths. He emphasizes that swearing by anything other than God, such as the Quran or the Kaaba, has no legal or religious effect; only an oath by God's essence is valid. The expiation for a false oath or breaking a promise involves feeding, clothing, or freeing a slave, or fasting if these are impossible. Crucially, merely making an oath without breaking it incurs no penalty, and paying expiation before the oath is broken is ineffective. In the second part, using the verse of Mubahala, the status of Imam Ali (AS) as the soul and life of the Prophet (PBUH) is established. The argument is that the word "our sons" in the verse prevents interpreting "our souls" as merely "our men," indicating Ali's equivalence with the Prophet in receiving revelation. This interpretation demonstrates that Ali (AS), like the Prophet, perceives the light of revelation, although the station of prophethood is exclusive to the Prophet.
