In this radio program, Professor Malekootian analyzed the geopolitical position of Iran and the Middle East region. He emphasized the strategic power derived from Iran's access to the Indian Ocean and key straits such as Hormuz and Bab el-Mandeb. The speaker also distinguished between peripheral and central locations, defining "strategic position" as a decisive factor in international developments. Subsequently, the book "The Islamic Revolution and Its Roots," based on Imam Khomeini's testament, was introduced, detailing the religious, political, and economic roots of the revolution. The author identifies economic dependence on foreign powers and the Shah's anti-religious policies as primary catalysts for the uprising. The final segment discussed the role of the resistance movement in shifting the regional balance of power and reducing reliance on Western forces. Finally, the program highlighted the Islamic Revolution's significant impact on global destiny and its expected future expansion.
