In this radio program, the historical and spiritual significance of the Arbaeen day was discussed, focusing on the return of the Ahl al-Bayt to Medina and the visitation of Imam Hussein's (PBUH) grave by Jabir al-Ansari.
Dr. Vahid Najafzadeh, citing narrations from Imam Sadiq (PBUH) and Imam Sajjad (PBUH), described the Arbaeen visitation as a major sign of true believers and explained that Imam Hussein's (PBUH) martyrdom was intended to save humanity from ignorance and guide them toward a blessed, faithful life.
The program transitioned to a recitation of verses from Surah Al-Isra, followed by a discussion on the virtue of humility in the conduct of the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH).
Humility was highlighted as a core trait of the Prophet, evident in his simple lifestyle, such as riding modest mounts, and his choice of servitude over worldly status despite his prophethood.
The late Ayatollah Behjat was presented as a prime example of humility, who, despite his high jurisprudential and spiritual ranks, avoided any appearance of arrogance or special status in his dress and demeanor.
A story was shared by Ayatollah Rahmani Hamedani, who recounted how he initially mistook Ayatollah Behjat for a servant because of his plain clothing and humble appearance while praying.
This anecdote illustrates the profound humility of Ayatollah Behjat, who remained unrecognizable to many due to his simplicity, even to his own students.
Ultimately, humility was portrayed as a key factor for spiritual growth and elevating one's status in the eyes of God and society.
