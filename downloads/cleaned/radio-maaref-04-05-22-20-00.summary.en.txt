Millions of pilgrims from around the world participated in the Arbaeen walk from Najaf to Karbala, renewing their pledge to Imam Hussein's ideals and expressing solidarity with the people of Gaza despite the intense heat. Iraqi authorities deployed over four thousand Basij forces to ensure the security of the massive gathering in Karbala. Concurrently, the Iranian President criticized the Israeli Prime Minister for his interventionist remarks, accusing him of ignoring the humanitarian crisis in Gaza and mismanaging the water crisis in the occupied territories. The United Nations reported the deaths of dozens of Palestinian children due to starvation and malnutrition in Gaza, condemning the ongoing siege and attacks on aid centers. In a separate incident, a hijab-wearing Muslim woman was assaulted and threatened with Islamophobic slurs on a bus in Ottawa, Canada, which police are investigating as a hate crime. The Dominican Library in Cairo was highlighted as a significant repository of early Islamic scientific manuscripts, open to researchers every week. Finally, the second anniversary of Islam's arrival in South Korea was commemorated in Seoul, while discussions explored the impact of religious elegies on national and revolutionary identity in Iran.
