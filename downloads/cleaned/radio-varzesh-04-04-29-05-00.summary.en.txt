Ali Shouri, a football veteran, discusses his playing career at Esteghlal and Bank Mellat, explaining that he retired at age 27 due to financial hardships and a lack of stable employment. After getting married and completing coaching courses, he formed the veterans' team in 2008 to preserve memories and foster camaraderie among former players. Shouri recalls organizing four-way tournaments and international trips, noting the unofficial financial support received from former club officials when his team won. Currently serving on the seven-member veterans' committee under the Esteghlal club, he emphasizes the critical need to address the livelihood and health of these individuals. He announces efforts to establish a daily center for veterans to socialize and engage in recreational activities, expressing gratitude for the club's moral and material support. Shouri believes that participation in this group is valuable for all former players, regardless of their tenure at the club. His ultimate goal remains the well-being and happiness of his family and friends, highlighting the importance of community support for veterans facing health or financial challenges.
