The radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat," featuring Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallah, emphasizes that managing anger is essential for maintaining a healthy relationship with God.
Citing religious narrations, the professor explains that anger not only disrupts external relationships but also destroys one's internal spiritual security.
He argues that mastering one's temper is a fundamental condition for effective divine connection, as a lack of control turns a person into a tool for Satan.
A key point is the necessity of emulating divine attributes, which can only be achieved by replacing anger with mercy and kindness.
Repentance from anger must also be performed with a spirit of compassion to align with God's merciful nature.
These teachings extend beyond the individual to families, groups, and society at large, aiming to prevent collective anger and social insecurity.
Ultimately, the program's core message is that controlling anger and cultivating kindness prevents a fall from divine favor to divine displeasure.
