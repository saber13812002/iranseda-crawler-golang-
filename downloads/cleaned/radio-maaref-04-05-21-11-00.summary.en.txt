As the Arbaeen pilgrimage concludes, over 1.8 million pilgrims have returned to Iran, with authorities urging travelers to avoid congestion at the Mهران border by using alternative crossings. The Executive Deputy of the President inspected the Khorramshahr border to ensure smooth and secure transit for the returning faithful. In a religious segment, a cleric clarified that while reading the Quran translation is rewarding, it does not carry the specific legal status of reciting the original Arabic text. Another scholar emphasized the jurisprudential obligation to respect others' rights and maintain order in queues at shrines and service points. Special programs, including the Tawassul prayer and a march for those who missed the main pilgrimage, were announced for the Jamaran Mosque. The broadcast concluded with a call for public financial support for the people of Gaza.
