This lecture interprets the tasbih of living creatures as a cosmic act rooted in the inherent perfection of creation.
Here, tasbih signifies the purification of God from any deficiency, ignorance, or weakness in His creation.
The Quran emphasizes that there is no flaw in the creation of the Merciful, meaning every being possesses its specific perfection.
Detailed examples, such as ant colonies and the defensive tactics of a partridge against a snake, illustrate this divine order.
These intricate systems demonstrate that every creature is created at the highest level of its potential without any structural defect.
Scholars argue that the knowledge of this tasbih belongs to God, rather than implying a conscious monotheistic awareness in animals.
Thus, cosmic tasbih represents an implicit acknowledgment of the Creator's flawlessness, rather than a deliberate, conscious act.
