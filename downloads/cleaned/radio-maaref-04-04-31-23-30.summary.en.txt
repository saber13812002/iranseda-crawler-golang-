In this radio program, Professor Mohammad Shahroudi explained that the principle of Ijtihad in the Shia school allows jurists to issue immediate rulings on modern issues like new technologies and medical matters without requiring a complete re-evaluation of the system. He emphasized that since religious principles are based on human reason and nature, they remain stable and unchanged even with the emergence of technologies such as artificial intelligence. In response to a listener's question, the recitation of "La ilaha illa Allah" during prostration was identified as the best form of dhikr for drawing closer to God, as this is the state of closest proximity. The speaker also advised against excessive repetition of specific dhikrs to avoid potential negative spiritual influences, recommending moderation and caution. To distinguish between divine and satanic spiritual experiences, one must verify them against the Quran, reason, and the rulings of a qualified religious authority. The program concluded with condolences for the month of Muharram and the recitation of the Ziyarat of Al-Yasin in honor of Imam Hussein.
