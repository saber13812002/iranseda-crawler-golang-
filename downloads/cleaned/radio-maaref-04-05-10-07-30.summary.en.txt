In this radio segment, Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallah explores the existential and conceptual dimensions of "language" in humans, drawing on Quranic verses and narrations. He explains the three main existential levels of human existence—intellect, imagination, and matter—based on wisdom, noting that each level possesses its own specific tools and language. A key point in the discussion is the distinction between "inner language," which belongs to higher spiritual levels, and "outer language," which includes speech, senses, and body language and requires cultivation. The professor emphasizes that verbal language acts as a permanent, tireless medium for transmitting intellectual and emotional messages, distinguishing humans from other creatures. He also highlights the role of silence in controlling anger and passion, viewing it as a sign of inner peace and inner language. The upcoming discussion structure will cover general aspects of language, its status in the Quran and narrations, and finally, its harms for prevention and treatment. The ultimate goal of this series is to raise awareness among listeners to master their outer language and elevate it from the level of the commanding soul to moral virtues and the heart.
