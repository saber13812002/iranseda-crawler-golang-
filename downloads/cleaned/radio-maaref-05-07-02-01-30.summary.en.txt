This radio program highlights the remarkable resistance of Dezful's people during the Sacred Defense, positioning the city as a lasting model of standing against military aggression and global arrogance. Dezful's resilience not only thwarted Saddam's aims to cut off Khuzestan from the center but also sustained the spirit of self-reliance and perseverance among the Iranian nation despite severe aerial and missile attacks. The speaker emphasizes that today, we must rely on these lessons of resistance to counter the enemy's cultural and economic plots, particularly their efforts to dismantle the family unit and weaken religious values. A warning is issued that the enemy seeks to destroy national and religious pride by promoting Western lifestyles and creating generational gaps through the destruction of the family. The latter part of the discussion addresses the economic challenges of marriage, noting that excessive material demands from families are a major obstacle for young people. It is advised that marriage criteria should be based on character and faith rather than heavy material expectations, which could drive youth toward corruption and addiction. Ultimately, Iran's independence and territorial integrity are the fruits of martyrs' blood and resistance, and we must remain vigilant against the enemy's combined media and cultural warfare to preserve this legacy.
