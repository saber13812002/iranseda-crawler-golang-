The radio program began by discussing the concept of civilization as the fruit of culture, emphasizing that the only path to true civilization is following the Imam of the Age and the Supreme Leader during the period of occultation.
The main focus of the episode was an analysis of the annual slogan, "Resistance Economy in the Shadow of National Unity and National Security," featuring economic expert Dr. Mehdi Khatibi.
The expert explained that the global economy operates like a zero-sum game, where nations compete intensely for a larger share of limited global resources.
He categorized Iran as a developing country that, despite past growth, faces significant challenges in closing the development gap with the West due to a late start in industrialization.
It was highlighted that the Islamic Revolution's leaders have consistently prioritized economic slogans for years to address the urgent need to overcome economic hurdles and reduce dependency.
A report from Khuzestan province showcased a local fabric weaving workshop, illustrating how youth entrepreneurship and support for female household heads contribute to domestic production.
The program concluded by stressing the importance of a production leap and public participation in the economy to achieve the annual slogan and the Iranian-Islamic model of progress.
