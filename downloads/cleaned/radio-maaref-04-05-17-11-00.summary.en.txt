The Minister of Culture and Islamic Guidance honored journalists on Journalists' Day for their role in preserving truth and social capital. The IRGC Commander congratulated the appointments of Larijani, Ahmadian, and Shamkhani to national councils, affirming the Corps' readiness to cooperate with them. The Red Crescent reported that 70,000 rescuers and 8,200 medical personnel are deployed in Iraq to assist Arbaeen pilgrims. Traffic statistics indicate that the Shalamcheh border was the busiest with over 722,000 crossings, while the Mehran border served 1.3 million pilgrims, remaining the top choice for visitors. The Fatima Masumeh Shrine in Qom established seven tents to provide accommodation and medical services to pilgrims from 32 nationalities. A ceremony commemorating martyrs of the "Authority" event emphasized the enemy's miscalculation during the war and the strength of national unity. The religious segment highlighted the virtue of charity, citing stories of generosity from scholars like Ayatollah Dinparvar and Akhund Khorasani. Finally, it was stressed that Iranian pilgrims must strictly adhere to the laws of the host country as a religious and ethical duty to maintain their national reputation.
