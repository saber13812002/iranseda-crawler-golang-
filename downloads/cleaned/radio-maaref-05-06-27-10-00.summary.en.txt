In this radio program, waiting for the Imam of the Time (AJ) is presented as the primary source of hope and direction for life.
The most important condition for being a true waiter is abandoning sins, performing obligatory duties, and having good morals to align with the Imam.
Despair is condemned as a major sin and a main obstacle to individual and social efforts, making the strengthening of hope essential.
Love for the Imam keeps the spirit of waiting alive, ensuring that a person remains energetic and lively even in the harshest conditions.
One practical way to support the Imam is creating educational and cultural environments with a Mahdavi approach to introduce future generations to him.
Shifting the perspective from "what do we expect from God" to "what does God expect from us" is the key to activating Mahdavi behaviors in daily life.
Finally, even if the reappearance does not occur during a person's lifetime, perseverance on the path of waiting and following religious commands guarantees a high status in the afterlife.
