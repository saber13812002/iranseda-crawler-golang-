The program recounts the historical attempt by Mansur al-Dawaniqi to deceive the descendants of Hasan and Husayn into signing documents under false pretenses. Mansur tasked Muhammad ibn Ash'as with finding a trustworthy agent, leading to the selection of Ibn Mahajar, who was instructed to pose as a devoted Shia to gain their trust. However, Imam Sadiq (AS) exposed Ibn Mahajar's true identity in the Prophet's Mosque, demonstrating his divine knowledge and stopping the plot. This narrative serves to illustrate the concept of the Imam as a "Muhaddith," one who possesses knowledge of the unseen. The discussion then expands to the Quranic and Hadith perspectives on "Makr" (stratagem) and deception, emphasizing that such schemes ultimately rebound on the perpetrator. Historical examples, such as the fate of Abd al-Malik, are cited to warn that tyranny and treachery inevitably lead to the downfall of the oppressor. Finally, the host encourages listeners to participate in upcoming mourning rituals and support pilgrim initiatives through financial and spiritual contributions.
