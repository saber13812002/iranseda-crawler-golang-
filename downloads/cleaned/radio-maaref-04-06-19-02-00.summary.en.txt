The program begins by emphasizing the prohibition of extravagance, citing Quranic verses and hadiths that define waste as a major sin depriving individuals of sufficiency.
Practical examples of frugality from Ayatollah Behjat and Imam Khomeini, such as optimal water usage and paper conservation, are presented as moral models.
The second part of the program analyzes the political and economic reforms implemented by Imam Ali (PBUH) during his four-year caliphate.
Politically, the dismissal of most previous administrators due to incompetence is highlighted, with only Hazay Fadh bin Imran retained as a competent official.
Economic reforms involved reclaiming misappropriated public funds and distributing them equitably among the needy without any ethnic or racial discrimination.
Imam Ali (PBUH) insisted on not accumulating public wealth, immediately distributing assets to the poor to prevent waste and mismanagement.
Finally, the strict instructions given by Imam Ali (PBUH) to his officials regarding the conservation of paper and ink are presented as a model for public resource management.
