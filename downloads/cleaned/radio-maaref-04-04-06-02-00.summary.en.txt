The radio program begins with a historical narrative about Marwan's attempt to secure allegiance from Imam Hussein (AS) and his subsequent resistance. Following this, Hujjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasizes the importance of good morals and mutual respect, citing Quranic verses and traditions. He illustrates these values through anecdotes from the life of Ayatollah Golpayegani, highlighting his profound etiquette and kindness towards believers. The second segment of the program features the story of "The New Word," in which Abu Dharr al-Qiffari travels to Mecca to investigate the prophetic claims of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH). In Mecca, Abu Dharr is sheltered by a young Muslim who promises to guide him to the Prophet (PBUH) while maintaining secrecy. This narrative underscores the prevailing fear in Mecca and the loyalty and courage of the early companions of Islam.
