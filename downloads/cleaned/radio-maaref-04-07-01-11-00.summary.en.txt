The 1404-1405 academic year officially began with the President's visit to a school, while the Minister of Education announced the opening of 2,400 new schools. The first day of Mehr was designated as "Flag Day," and the commemorative week for Hazrat Abdol-Azim al-Hasani started in Rey under the slogan "Ambassador of Guidance." The Hajj and Pilgrimage Organization launched the registration for volunteer doctors for the 1405 Hajj and extended the deadline for the Youth Population Award. The Director of Social Development at the Ministry of Sports refuted claims of a rising divorce rate, describing the trend as linear and decreasing. Over 90 percent of inmates in the Governmental Regulations Organization were released following the Supreme Leader's pardon. Special programs were announced for visitors to the Holy Jamkaran Mosque in the coming days. The Sacred Defense Week began with the slogan "Mehori," and today is recognized as the Day of Youth in Science and Research.
