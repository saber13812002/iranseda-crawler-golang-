In this radio program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli provides an exegesis of verses 141 to 143 of Surah An-Nisa. He explains that while hypocrites appear to deceive God, the Prophet, and believers, they are ultimately only deceiving themselves. The concepts of "deception" and "stratagem" are interpreted to mean that these individuals are unknowingly moving toward the fire of Hell through their sins. Citing a specific narration, the speaker describes a stone falling from the edge of Hell for seventy years, symbolizing the spiritual descent of a seventy-year-old hypocrite who has reached the bottom of the abyss. It is emphasized that prayer is extremely difficult for hypocrites due to their lack of genuine faith and their practice of showing off, rendering their inner remembrance of God ineffective. Finally, the concept of "wavering" is defined as a state of confusion and helplessness where hypocrites fail to make meaningful progress toward either God or disbelief.
