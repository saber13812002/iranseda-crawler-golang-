The program began with a Quranic analysis, interpreting terms like "Wajh" and "Qama" as symbols of clarity and the concealment of truth.
A commentator then addressed the American officials' error in assessing Iran's reaction to military threats, noting that such threats strengthen Iranian faith rather than intimidate them.
The second part of the show focused on answering religious questions from followers of various Maraji', including the ruling on fasting for the sick based on medical or parental advice.
The permissibility of consuming food prepared by People of the Book and the approach of obsessive-compulsive individuals to purity laws were also examined.
Regarding divorce, it was emphasized that living separately without the formal pronouncement of divorce in the presence of witnesses does not constitute a valid divorce.
A question about paying Khums to a needy child was answered, stating it is permissible only with the advisor's permission and if it does not overlap with the father's mandatory maintenance.
Finally, rulings on praying behind a non-mahram woman with the required distance and the validity of Ghusl using a shower were discussed and clarified.
