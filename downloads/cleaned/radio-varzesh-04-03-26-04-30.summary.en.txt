Ahmad Sanjari, a former Iranian footballer and current AFC instructor, discussed his playing career with Radio Varzesh, highlighting his time at clubs like Rah Ahan, Darai, and Homa as well as his tenure with the national team in the 1950s and 1960s. He shared vivid memories of the Olympic qualifiers in Singapore and the Asian Cup, noting the significant challenges posed by the revolution and the Iran-Iraq war. After retiring from playing, Sanjari transitioned into coaching, eventually becoming a certified instructor for the Asian Football Confederation after completing rigorous training courses. He emphasized that becoming a successful coach requires more than just a certificate; it demands passion, extensive experience, and meticulous planning. Sanjari also noted the structural changes in modern football, such as the increased number of matches compared to his era. He concluded by mentioning his family's professional backgrounds and stressing the importance of coaches serving as behavioral role models for their players.
