The flag replacement ceremony for the moving dome in Qom was held to commemorate the anniversary of the passing of Hazrat Fatima Masumeh (SA). The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps issued a statement describing Operation "Sacred Promise 2" as a firm response to Zionist aggression and a testament to the country's defensive readiness. The Speaker of the Parliament emphasized the dynamic nature of Islamic jurisprudence and its necessity in addressing contemporary societal needs. The "Mujahid Preachers" conference was held, highlighting statistics on clerical martyrs and students during the Sacred Defense. The National Population Council Secretary announced a monthly subsidy of two million tomans for mothers starting in 2026 to support families. The program included jurisprudential answers regarding traveler prayers, khums on medical expenses, and mosque etiquette. The broadcast concluded with a schedule for upcoming radio programs and musical performances.
