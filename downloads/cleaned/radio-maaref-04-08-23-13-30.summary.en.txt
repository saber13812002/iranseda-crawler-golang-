The program begins by recounting personal memories of Hamid Bakri, highlighting the grief of his family and their ongoing search for his remains in the south.
It details Bakri's military career, from organizing the Basij in Urmia to commanding forces in major operations like Fath al-Mubin and Muslim ibn Aqil.
During Operation Khorramshahr, Bakri led a vanguard group to infiltrate the Majnoon Island, securing the bridge before being martyred on February 25, 1983.
His body was buried at the site, and his family continues to search for his grave despite previous unsuccessful attempts.
The latter part of the broadcast refutes claims of public apathy toward religion, citing the massive turnout for Qasem Soleimani's funeral as evidence of deepening faith.
The narrator points to religious anthems and large-scale gatherings as signs of the people's enduring loyalty to the revolution and the Imam's teachings.
Annual marches on February 11 and Day of Quds are presented as symbols of the nation's resilience and spiritual commitment.
The episode concludes with a statement from Radio Ma'arif, emphasizing the divine virtues and the enduring spirit of the Iranian people.
