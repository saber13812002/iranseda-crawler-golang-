Majid Halvavi, former captain of Pas FC, recounts his memories of playing for the club and the national team. He discusses initial offers from Esteghlal and Persepolis and explains why he ultimately chose Pas due to its unique educational and financial support system. Halvavi recalls playing alongside multiple generations of Iranian football stars and winning championships in the Jam-e Takhth-e Jamshid and the Asian Games. He emphasizes that Pas FC was not just a sports team but a school for teaching ethics, humanity, and sportsmanship. Citing an anecdote about respectful behavior towards opponents, he prioritizes moral values over material victory. Later, Dr. Bijan Zaraf, another football veteran, expresses pride in having played with Halvavi and praises his positive impact on the team's morale after his return from injury. Both athletes express regret over Pas FC's absence from the Iranian football scene, viewing it as the loss of a significant cultural and ethical legacy.
