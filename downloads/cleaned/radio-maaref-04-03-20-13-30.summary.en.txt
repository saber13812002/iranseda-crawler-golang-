In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Seyyed Ahmad Faqih discusses the importance of spiritual intimacy with God and the role of the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) as an intermediary for the acceptance of prayers. The expert emphasizes that direct communication with the Divine is difficult, and reciting salawat while focusing on the Ahl al-Bayt serves as a crucial bridge between the servant and God. Citing the story of Prophet Jonah (AS), he recommends repeating the phrase "La ilaha illa anta subhanaka inni kuntu min al-zalimin" as an effective remedy for resolving life's difficulties. Additionally, performing the first-time prayer with full presence of heart, reciting the Quran, and serving parents sincerely are identified as key factors in alleviating problems. In response to a listener's question about abusive parents, the expert advises against judging them and suggests praying for their forgiveness and treating them kindly to improve their behavior. He believes that expressing gratitude to God and parents, along with fulfilling filial duties, is the key to peace and blessings in life.
