This radio program emphasizes the necessity of prayer and the remembrance of God for achieving mental peace and combating depression in the modern world. Citing Quranic verses and narrations, the speaker asserts that God is the responder of prayers, making supplication vital not only for material needs but also for preserving blessings and ensuring a good afterlife. The discussion distinguishes between viewing prayer as a mere tool for fulfilling desires and seeing it as a direct, spiritual connection with the Divine. In the second part, the program highlights the critical role of poetry as a powerful medium in contemporary media wars. It is argued that Persian poetry must possess high artistic quality in both diction and content to exert a global influence that transcends linguistic barriers.
