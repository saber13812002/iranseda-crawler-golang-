The program began with an energetic morning greeting, encouraging listeners to start the day with movement and effort. The health segment reported on the expansion of health insurance coverage for the elderly and a significant one-million-unit increase in blood donations compared to the previous year. It also highlighted the importance of preventive measures in oral health to reduce treatment costs. In the discussion section, Hujjat al-Islam Fallah addressed the moral responsibility humans have towards animals and the need for harmonious coexistence with nature. He refuted claims that profanity is permitted in religious narrations, emphasizing that the Ahl al-Bayt strictly advised against bad language and disrespect towards all living beings. Fallah offered solutions for overcoming the habit of profanity, including converting ignorance into awareness, correcting misunderstandings of religious values, and controlling negative thoughts. He also stressed the importance of not justifying profanity by blaming God and avoiding hypocrisy in verbal and ethical behavior.
