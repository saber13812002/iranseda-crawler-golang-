In this radio program, listeners' theological questions were addressed, including the merit of reciting the Quran for the Ahl al-Bayt and the distinction between the Barzakh and the final Paradise. It was explained that both humans and Satan are materially empty and derive their power solely from God, yet humans can master Satan through will. Regarding intercession and writing petitions, it was emphasized that prayers are effective only when necessary conditions are met, without any absolute guarantee of results. The main discussion focused on the Gaza conflict, asserting that God does not merely observe but punishes oppressors and the silent, with full justice reserved for the afterlife since the world lacks the capacity for complete retribution. The philosophy of creation was described as a test where humans use their free will to navigate hardships toward their ultimate destination. Finally, the program recounted memories from the Iran-Iraq war, highlighting the medical staff's initial lack of knowledge and equipment when facing chemical and biological weapons.
