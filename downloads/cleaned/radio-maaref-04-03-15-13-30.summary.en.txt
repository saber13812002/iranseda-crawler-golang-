In this radio program, Fatemeh Rezaei, the wife of martyr Behman Mosaibi, shared memories of their loving and harmonious marriage. She highlighted her husband's moral character, including his patience, orderliness, and martyr-like conduct in daily life, noting how his positive influence improved her self-confidence and habits. Rezaei recounted the moment her husband volunteered for a second deployment to Syria despite having three young children, and how she granted her permission with love and trust in God. She described writing an emotional letter to him, expressing her readiness to sacrifice everything for their faith and family. The second part of the program analyzed the historical significance of the 15 Khordad uprising in 1963, which demonstrated the deep bond between the Iranian people and the clergy. The analyst emphasized that the success of any movement depends on public support, and this connection was a key factor in the revolution's victory. The program concluded with greetings for the anniversary of the 15 Khordad uprising and a tribute to the martyrs.
