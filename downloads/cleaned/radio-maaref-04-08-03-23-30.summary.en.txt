Ayatollah Abdollahi describes the collaborative process behind the compilation of the Tafsir-e-Namooh, highlighting a division of labor where various writers contributed insights from specific sources under the supervision of Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi. He recalls that this work emerged from a pre-revolutionary tafsir discussion circle led by Ayatollah Moshkini and Ayatollah Khazali, many of whose members, including Ayatollah Beheshti and Ayatollah Shuja'i, later became martyrs. Abdollahi also details his teaching career in the seminary, covering both general and specialized levels of Quranic sciences, where he discussed thematic topics such as prophecy and revelation in the Quran. Some of these classroom discussions were eventually published as the book "Revelation in the Quran" by the Islamic Propagation Office. The broadcast then transitioned to a spiritual segment featuring the Ziyarat of Al-Yasin and religious music, creating a contemplative atmosphere. Following this, the third part of the radio program aired speeches by Imam Khomeini, emphasizing the necessity of implementing Islam in all state institutions and the supreme value of martyrdom. The Imam defined true victory as triumph in the spiritual battle between faith and ego, asserting that martyrdom in this struggle represents an absolute victory. The segment concluded by aiming to inspire listeners with the ideological and spiritual legacy of the founder of the Islamic Republic.
