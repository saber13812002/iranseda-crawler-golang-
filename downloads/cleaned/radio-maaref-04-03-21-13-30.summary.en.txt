In the "Narrative of Love" program, Fatemeh Rezaei, the wife of martyr pilot Bahman Masaei, recounts the final moments of their shared life and her acceptance of his martyrdom. She describes the hardships of pregnancy and raising children while deeply attached to her husband, driven by her love for Imam Hussein and Zainab. Bahman, a pilot in a special forces unit, was frequently deployed to the Saravan border, and Fatemeh encouraged him to fulfill his duties despite her longing. On his last mission, he called her the night before his flight, but there was no news from him the following morning. Fatemeh learned of his martyrdom after seeing a message about a jetroplane crash in their family group and receiving a call from her brother-in-law. This painful memory highlights the sacrifices made by martyrs and the patience and resilience of their families in safeguarding the country's borders.
