In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Jabbari answers historical and religious questions from listeners. He traces the roots of Jewish presence in Palestine to the return of the Israelites from Egypt and their disobedience to the prophets. The claim of "from the Nile to the Euphrates" based on the Torah is rejected due to its absence in the Quran and the specific meaning of "descendants of Abraham" as the righteous. The story of Imam Ali's proposal to Fatima and the reason for the delayed marriage due to her young age is explained. The expert also notes that while a distinction between formal and colloquial Arabic existed in early Islam, it was not identical to the Quranic language. The association of the flute with Prophet David is attributed to his beautiful voice and spirituality rather than musical instruments. The historical boundaries of Canaan are identified as including Palestine, Lebanon, and Syria. Finally, Abu al-As ibn al-Rabi is introduced as the Prophet's son-in-law and Khadija's nephew, with evidence provided that he had no relation to Amr ibn al-As.
