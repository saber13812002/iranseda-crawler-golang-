In this radio program, political analyst Javad Larijani described recent Israeli attacks on Iran as signs of the regime's weakness and desperation, comparing them to throwing a stone into the Pacific Ocean. He emphasized the resilience and greatness of the Iranian people, arguing that the martyrdom of scientists and leaders would not weaken the nation and predicting the eventual removal of the Israeli regime from the Islamic world. In another segment, Dr. Mostafa Rasakhi analyzed the Supreme Leader's management strategies during the 1999 and 2009 unrests. Rasakhi highlighted that the nature of these crises was a soft war, and the Leader chose a strategy of patience, explanation, and giving time for reflection rather than using hard power. He also discussed the role of the "Lightseekers" (Rahayan-e Noor) as a soft power tool for educating the youth and fostering social cohesion. Finally, the weather forecast warned of strong winds and reduced air quality in the western and southwestern regions of the country for the next few days.
