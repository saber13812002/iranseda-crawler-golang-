In this program, Professor Laksai emphasized that Iran-US negotiations must be strictly limited to peaceful nuclear issues, while analyzing the recent meeting between Netanyahu and Trump. He argued that the encounter served more as a rebuke to Netanyahu, as Trump distanced himself from Israeli security demands regarding missiles and resistance, instead stressing the importance of direct talks with Iran. Laksai also highlighted the economic motivations behind Trump's planned regional trip, which aims to secure massive investments from Saudi Arabia to offset budget deficits and mitigate the negative impact of trade tariffs on the US stock market. In the second segment, the professor addressed the annual slogan of "Investment for Production," identifying historical dependence on the global hegemony system as the root cause of Iran's economic weaknesses. While acknowledging Iran's successes in security and science, he called for a people-centric economy by rigorously implementing Article 44 policies and providing strong support from the executive branch to domestic producers. Ultimately, it was stressed that for economic prosperity, the necessary infrastructure must be established to allow citizens to actively enter the production sector, making the economy as dynamic and people-driven as Iran's culture and politics.
