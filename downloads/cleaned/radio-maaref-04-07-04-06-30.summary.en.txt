This segment of the program continues the theological discussion on the infallibility of the Ahl al-Bayt and the Quranic evidence supporting it. The speaker argues, citing authentic narrations and interpretations, that the verse of Yusuf and Imam Ali's words in Nahj al-Balagha do not negate infallibility but rather highlight the human need for divine mercy to avoid error. By analyzing the tafsir of Ali ibn Ibrahim and narrations from Imams Sadiq and Baqir, it is established that the characteristics of the Prophet and his companions are mentioned in previous heavenly books like the Torah and the Gospel. Furthermore, a narration is presented where Jews demanded specific miracles, such as a carpet and animals speaking, to which the Prophet responded that divine miracles and previous scriptures were sufficient. The narration indicates that these miracles occurred, yet the Jews rejected them due to envy and stubbornness. Finally, it is emphasized that the infallibility of the Ahl al-Bayt is proven through their infallible speech and Quranic implications, effectively addressing the raised objections.
