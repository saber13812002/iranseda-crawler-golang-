This radio program discussed Islamic ethics in social and political interactions, emphasizing the need for strictness with foreign enemies and mercy among believers.
The legal exception allowing backbiting of judges to warn against unjust rulings was analyzed and clarified as a specific case, not a general license for criticizing officials.
It was stressed that preserving the dignity of the system and its officials is a religious obligation, and destroying their reputation without public sin is forbidden.
However, constructive criticism aimed at improving the system remains distinct from malicious backbiting and is still permissible.
The segment also addressed the global propaganda attacks against Iran, attributing them to the fear of Muslim unity and cohesion.
Finally, the importance of governments and Muslims awakening to strengthen brotherhood and counter the enemies' attempts to sow division was highlighted.
