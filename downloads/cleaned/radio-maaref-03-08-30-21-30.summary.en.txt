This radio segment analyzes Quranic verses from Surah An-Nisa regarding the faith of the steadfast in knowledge.
The commentator argues that the term "those who establish prayer" is grammatically the second predicate, not an apposition to the first.
This order, which places action before faith, is unusual but intended for believers to emphasize that faith requires righteous deeds.
The discussion highlights that prayer and zakat are essential complements to belief for true spiritual success.
It also distinguishes between the shared revelation of all prophets and the specific revelation of the great prophets.
Rational and scriptural proofs confirm that divine messengers were sent to every nation, including remote regions.
No community is punished without first being presented with clear evidence and a messenger.
This principle ensures divine justice in the final judgment for all humanity.
