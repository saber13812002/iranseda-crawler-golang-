In this radio program, the host emphasized the authority of Imam Ali (PBUH) as the central theme of Ghadir Khumm, citing a narration from Saduq's Amali.
The host detailed the narration's chain of transmission, which traces back to the Prophet, angels, and ultimately God, to highlight its authenticity and importance.
The concept of "it'am" (feeding the poor) was presented as a condition for salvation from hellfire, with a strong call to action for listeners during the Eid of Ghadir.
Listeners were invited to participate in this charitable act by sending a text message to the number 340552 to contribute to the feeding initiative.
The host clarified that even a small amount of food is valuable if offered with sincere intention and love for the Ahl al-Bayt.
Towards the end, the host congratulated the audience on the Eid and thanked the special guest, Dr. Hosseinzadeh, for encouraging the public to perform good deeds.
The program concluded with the recitation of Ziyarat Al-Yasin and an invitation for listeners to stay tuned to Radio Ma'aref until 6 AM for the live broadcast.
