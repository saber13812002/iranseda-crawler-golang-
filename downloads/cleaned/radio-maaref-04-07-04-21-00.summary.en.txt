In the Parsaiyan program, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the necessity of respect and humility towards teachers, citing Imam Sajjad's narrations and examples from great scholars like Allameh Tabatabaei and Imam Khomeini. He recounted a story of Ayatollah Moshkini who, despite his higher academic status, tied the shoes of his former teacher out of deep respect. The second part of the show featured the final episode of the radio drama "Ocean of the East," based on a novel about a man named Aryan seeking the water of life. Guided by the envoy of Imam Reza (AS), Aryan reaches Nishapur and, upon hearing the news of the Imam's martyrdom, realizes that the true water of life is the Imam's guardianship and the declaration of faith. The program concluded with a reference to the Supreme Leader's fatwa regarding selling property to pay off debts, followed by poetry and an announcement for the next episode on Mahdism.
