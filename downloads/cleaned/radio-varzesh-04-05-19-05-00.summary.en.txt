Former national basketball player Farzam Khalki recounts his early selection for the senior national team at age 17 and his participation in the Asian Youth Games in Manila and Beijing. After completing his military service in Urmia, he passed the university entrance exam with a rank of 104 and studied electrical engineering. Khalki won the gold medal at the 1990 Asian Games in Beijing and later chose to stay in Iran to care for his mother rather than pursue a scholarship in the United States. He subsequently built a successful career at IBM Iran, eventually becoming the CEO of the Central Bank's data processing company. In recent years, he served as the head of the national handball teams before retiring to lead the Sadaq Holding Company, where he launched the "Bale" application. Khalki defines success not as reaching a specific goal, but as the continuous effort and the maintenance of multiple objectives.
