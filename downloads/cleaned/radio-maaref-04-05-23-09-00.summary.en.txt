In this radio program, the expert argues that Iran is resilient against separatist plots and external pressures due to its strong, ideology-based governance and a composite Iranian-Islamic identity, unlike the fragmented situations in Lebanon, Syria, and Libya. He emphasizes that the deep national cohesion and the emotional bond between the people and the leadership prevent the country from suffering the same fate as its neighbors. The discussion also addressed the limited influence of anti-revolutionary and monarchist groups, noting that their ideological diversity, internal conflicts, and lack of public support render them incapable of posing a serious threat to the system. The expert highlighted that the Iranian people have demonstrated awareness and resilience in the face of internal tensions and external provocations. Consequently, despite the enemy's attempts to exploit various power factors, the nation's unified will and the government's hard and soft power ensure that Iran will not face a collapse.
