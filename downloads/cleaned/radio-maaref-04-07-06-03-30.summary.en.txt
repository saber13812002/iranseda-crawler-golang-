The tafsir section analyzed the concept of "walad" (child) and refuted the Quraysh's claim of divine offspring, emphasizing God's absolute independence. The term "Subhan" was interpreted as rapid movement in worship, marking the beginning of the spiritual journey. Politically, the US breach of the JCPOA and the subsequent increase in sanctions were condemned, rendering negotiations with them ineffective due to a lack of trust and ongoing threats. In the fiqh section, Ayatollah Khamenei's ruling confirmed that washing machines purify clothes without requiring additional washing of upper parts. Changing a verbal vow after it is fulfilled is impermissible and requires expiation, whereas a mental vow can be altered. For making vinegar from grapes, two-thirds must be evaporated to remove impurity, and consuming it before this stage is forbidden. The expiation for forgotten fasts from the previous year must be calculated at the current favorable price. A ten-year-old discerning girl should cover her hair in front of her ten-year-old cousin out of precaution. A marriage contract requires the recitation of the specific formula, not just mutual consent. Finally, water in a pool connected to a spring remains pure upon contact with impurity unless its color, smell, or taste changes.
