In this radio program, Dr. Mohammad Reza Jafari addressed several historical and religious inquiries. He dismissed the book "Aisha After the Prophet" by Kurt Frischler as a novel lacking historical credibility, recommending "Aisha's Role in Islamic History" instead for accurate information. The scholar also clarified that Zahhak and his thousand-year reign are mythical legends symbolizing the Damavand volcano rather than historical facts. He validated the story of the wounded soldiers' self-sacrifice during the Battle of Yarmouk as a testament to the Prophet's teachings on altruism. Regarding the Prophet's life before revelation, he outlined key events from his birth to his marriage with Khadija and recommended historical sources like Mehdi Pishvay's work. Jafari refuted the claim of a brotherly relationship between Harith ibn Ziyad and Kumayl ibn Ziyad, identifying the killer of Muslim's children as a different individual. Finally, he explained that the Imams' participation in Friday prayers was not primarily due to fear-based taqiyya, but to maintain social cohesion and community ties, noting that they attended while maintaining their own intention for prayer.
