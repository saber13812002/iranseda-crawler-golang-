In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the virtue of maintaining family ties, citing narrations and the experiences of prominent scholars. He illustrated through stories involving Imam Kazim and Ayatollah Khomeini that severing family bonds leads to misfortune and shortened life, whereas maintaining connections and forgiving wrongdoers brings blessings and spiritual elevation. The program highlighted the practice of Ayatollah Koohestani, who regularly visited neighbors after Nowruz, and shared anecdotes about how mending family relationships resolved personal and administrative crises. The second part of the show featured a narrative from the book "Galaxy of Nothingness," where Sheikh Muhammad Bahari guided Seyyed Ali Qazi to meet Seyyed Ahmad Karbala'i, a disciple of Mulla Hossein Qoli Hamadani. This encounter facilitated the introduction of Seyyed Jamal al-Din to Seyyed Ahmad, marking the beginning of a new phase in his spiritual training. Finally, the program addressed listeners' legal questions regarding business transactions, providing answers based on the Supreme Leader's rulings.
