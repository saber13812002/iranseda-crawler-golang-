In this radio program, Ayatollah Mazaheri emphasizes the necessity of planting virtues in the heart and uprooting vices, using the Quranic metaphor of the "Tree of Virtue." He states that the purpose of human creation is to achieve perfection and become God's property, a goal realized through piety and prayer. Prayer is highlighted as the most superior act of worship and a crucial source of patience, being easy for the pious but burdensome for sinners. Imam Khomeini's example of prioritizing prayer over important conversations underscores the importance of praying on time and in congregation, even under difficult circumstances. The second part of the program addresses a jurisprudential discussion regarding the rules of waqf (endowment) and the sale of endowed properties. It examines the distinction between properties that generate income and those that are merely land, ruling to maintain the current status if increasing income is not feasible. The segment concludes with Quranic recitation and an introduction to upcoming programs on the radio.
