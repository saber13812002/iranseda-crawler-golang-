The speech emphasizes the importance of role modeling in Islam and the Quran's role in presenting prophets as successful human examples.
Among these figures, Lady Zaynab (SA) is highlighted as a prominent model for both guardianship and nursing throughout her life.
From childhood to captivity, she demonstrated these roles by caring for Fatima (SA), Imam Hasan (AS), and the orphans during the events of Ashura.
During Ashura, she showed the pinnacle of nursing by protecting children from fire and providing emotional support and food to the captives.
Beyond nursing, Zaynab (SA) acted as a guardian of the martyrs' legacy by delivering powerful speeches before Yazid and Ibn Ziyad.
Without her cultural and political guardianship, the message of Ashura would have been forgotten by future generations.
Her name, meaning "the adornment of her father," was truly reflected in her actions as the adornment of the family of Ali (AS).
The speech concludes with prayers for the connection of the revolution to the global Mahdavi revolution and the destruction of Islam's enemies.
