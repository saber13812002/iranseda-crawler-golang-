Hojjat al-Islam Dr. Naser Rafiei discussed the Quranic etiquette of charity in the "Barbal Sakhn" program, interpreting verses 270 and 271 of Surah Al-Baqarah. He emphasized that charity must be given with sincere intention, avoiding ostentation, harassment, or any sense of entitlement toward the recipient. The speaker highlighted the importance of maintaining moderation in giving, ensuring the wealth is halal, and donating items that are personally valued rather than unwanted goods. Furthermore, he stressed the need for prioritizing genuine community needs, such as housing and education, over creating dependency, while prioritizing relatives and close kin. Rafiei also noted that the Prophet and Imams taught that charity is a trust from God that returns to the giver, often in better form. Finally, he concluded that secret charity carries greater spiritual merit than public giving, urging listeners to cultivate a strong belief in divine reward and the value of serving society.
