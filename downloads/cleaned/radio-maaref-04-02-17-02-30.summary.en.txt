The radio program "Parsaiyan," featuring Hojjat-ol-Islam Vahidi, explored the virtues and significance of the night prayer.
The night prayer was introduced as a worship whose reward is not explicitly defined in the Quran, indicating a special prize reserved for its practitioners.
Quotes from prominent religious figures such as Ayatollah Hakim, Ayatollah Pahlavani, and Allameh Tabatabaei were shared, emphasizing the importance of consistency and not abandoning the prayer.
Ayatollah Tabatabaei, referencing his personal experience, identified the night prayer as the key to achieving deep religious understanding and perseverance in the pursuit of knowledge.
Martyr Ayatollah Motahhari also spoke about the profound spiritual pleasures and intimate connection with God found in the night prayer, ranking it above any worldly pleasure.
The program concluded with long, mystical supplications seeking forgiveness, protection from punishment, and closeness to the Divine.
The main conclusion of the discussion was that the night prayer is not merely a communal obligation but a path to spiritual peace, enhanced religious insight, and closeness to God.
