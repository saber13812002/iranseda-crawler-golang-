This radio program discusses whether God must break His divine laws to ensure the victory of Shia Muslims. The respondent argues that God does not break His traditions; rather, victories like the Islamic Revolution and the Iran-Iraq War resulted from people's efforts, divine leadership, and pure intentions within the framework of established divine laws. Citing Quranic verses, he explains that angels assist Muslims only when they are on the path of truth and utilize all their available resources. The discussion also covers the story of the angels Harut and Marut, who were sent to teach the Israelites how to counter magic to prevent them from falling into disbelief. Subsequently, a documentary about the Operation Towhid (Tariq al-Quds) is presented, highlighting how Islamic fighters liberated Bastam despite the enemy's military superiority. This victory demonstrated that divine aid occurs when popular and military forces act in complete coordination with a martyr-seeking spirit. Finally, the bravery of commanders and soldiers against the enemy's numerical advantage is presented as a symbol of spiritual power and collective will in defending the truth.
