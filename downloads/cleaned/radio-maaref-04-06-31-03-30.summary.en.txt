The radio program began by analyzing Quranic verses that emphasize the unity of religion and the mutual acceptance among prophets, arguing that distinguishing between them contradicts total submission to God. It also announced the 20th specialized conference for Quran reciters, focusing on the requirements for innovation in recitation, and invited researchers to submit their papers. The main segment addressed jurisprudential rulings regarding medical centers, stressing the necessity of respecting women's privacy and hijab in internal and surgical wards. According to the rulings of religious authorities, male entry into these sections is strictly prohibited unless there is a compelling necessity where no female doctor is available, and even then, the presence of male staff must be minimized. Responses to listeners' questions included the ruling that aid stipends are not subject to Khums and that sequential ghusl is valid if specific details are observed. Regarding prayer, it was advised that missed prayers should be performed gradually according to physical ability, and voluntary prayers for the deceased are not permissible under normal circumstances. The program also discussed the prohibition of shaking hands with non-mahram individuals and the priority of settling debts and mahr from a deceased person's estate before distributing the inheritance.
