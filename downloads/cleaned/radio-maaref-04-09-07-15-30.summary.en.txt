In this radio program, experts emphasized the necessity of strengthening the judiciary to combat economic corruption and ensure social security, drawing on the Supreme Leader's thoughts. They referenced past uprisings and victims like Arman Alivardi and Ruhollah Ajamian to advocate for justice and the protection of the people's rights. The discussion focused on the hypocrisy of the global hegemony regarding human rights, noting that the West has lost moral credibility by supporting terrorist groups and violating rights in Palestine and Gaza. In contrast, Iran defends legitimate freedoms and the people's rights based on its religious beliefs and authentic values. The speakers also highlighted the human rights abuses committed by ISIS and the West's indifference to these atrocities. The program concluded with a call for public participation in rallies and support for the theocratic people's governance. Finally, a recitation and translation of verses from Surah Al-An'am were broadcast, emphasizing monotheism and warning against denial.
