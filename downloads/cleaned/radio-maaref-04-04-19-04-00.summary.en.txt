In this radio program, jurisprudential answers to listeners' questions were provided based on the rulings of religious authorities. According to Ayatollah Sadrani, calculating Khums based on either the solar or lunar calendar is permissible, with the solar calendar being more suitable for farmers. Regarding fasting, it was emphasized that if there is a health risk, individuals can consult a doctor to fast intermittently or pay fidya. Jurisprudentially, women can lead prayers for other women if they meet the necessary conditions, but men cannot follow a female imam. In the discussion on impurities, imported leather from non-Muslim countries is considered impure if it comes from an animal not slaughtered according to Islamic law, thereby invalidating the prayer. Paying Khums is a prerequisite for using unused assets such as veils. The final segment of the program focused on interpreting the characteristics of the "servants of Allah," emphasizing moderate charity, alleviating others' economic problems, and avoiding both extravagance and stinginess.
