In the radio program "Persman Tarikhi," Dr. Mohammad Reza Jafari addressed several historical and religious inquiries. He explained that al-Kulayni did not extensively narrate from the four deputies because their primary role was administrative and managerial rather than scholarly. However, he cited a specific narration in the book al-Kafi to prove that al-Kulayni did reference the deputies and their interactions with the Imam. Regarding the death of Zubayr ibn al-Awwam after the Battle of Jamal, the expert noted that he was killed by Amr ibn Jarmouz for failing to fully repent and rejoin the side of Amir al-Mu'minin. The program also clarified that while Malik Ashtar was present among the protesters against Caliph Uthman, there is no direct historical evidence proving his personal involvement in Uthman's assassination. On the topic of the conquest of Iran, Jafari stated that while some cities were conquered through treaties, atrocities were committed by incompetent commanders, particularly during Uthman's caliphate. He rejected the claim that Hasan and Husayn participated in these conquests, citing weak sources and the incompatibility of such actions with their sacred status. Finally, the discussion addressed the burial sites of Adam and Eve, suggesting that a narration about Noah moving Adam's body to Najaf helps reconcile conflicting reports regarding their final resting places.
