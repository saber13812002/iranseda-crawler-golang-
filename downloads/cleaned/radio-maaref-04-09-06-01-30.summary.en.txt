The program begins by discussing the philosophy of Mahdism, suggesting that the delay in the appearance of the Imam is due to humanity's lack of moral and social maturity.
It is argued that history follows a spiral path where current chaos serves as a precursor to a higher level of order.
The speaker emphasizes that waiting for the Imam does not exempt believers from their religious duties; instead, self-improvement and preaching are essential.
The second part of the show introduces Martyr Fahimeh Siari, a mathematics and physics graduate who studied at the women's seminary in Qom.
Her comrade, Dr. Fattahi-Zadeh, recounts their acquaintance at the Maktab-e Tawhid school during the turbulent revolutionary period.
Martyr Siari is described as an intelligent, studious, and morally upright individual who served as a role model in both academic and emotional contexts.
She was martyred in 1981 while traveling to the western regions of Iran, specifically in the Divandarreh area.
This narrative highlights the significant role played by female martyrs in the revolutionary struggles through their dedication and knowledge.
