The program begins by narrating excerpts from the book "In the Ambush of the Red Rose" about the life of Ali-Siyad Shirazi. In one scene, Ali corrects a mistake made by Major General Yusefi during a bayonet training session, which catches the general's attention. After witnessing Ali's flawless execution, the general orders all units to learn from him and announces a competition. Ali validates his methods by referencing military manuals and continues to train his troops diligently. As border tensions escalate, the Tabriz Corps is dissolved, and Ali's unit is transferred to the Kermanshah Armored Corps. Ali is assigned a critical mission to identify the exact location of Iraqi shelling near the border. A major refuses to provide troops for the mission, forcing Ali to proceed with a small group of loyal artillerymen. The episode concludes with the recitation of a prayer for the Hidden Imam and the Supreme Leader's remarks on honoring the fighters and protecting civilians.
