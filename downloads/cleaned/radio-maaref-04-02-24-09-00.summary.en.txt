In this radio program, the misconception that the grief of the bereaved causes pain to the deceased in the Barzakh was addressed, clarifying that angels protect the inhabitants of Paradise from witnessing misfortunes. It was emphasized that moderate crying does not diminish the reward of the deceased's good deeds but is a sign of love, although excessive sorrow is discouraged. The program also answered various theological and jurisprudential questions, noting that in the Barzakh, devils inflict punishment rather than deception, and that complete repentance and respect for the Quran are essential to remove calamities from a family. A key point discussed was the Prophet's vision of the future during the Mi'raj, illustrating that he transcended temporal and spatial limitations to see the destinies of future generations. Finally, charity and the recitation of "Masha'Allah" were recommended to ward off the evil eye, and the Supreme Leader's ruling regarding the lack of a mandatory expiation for bleeding during Ihram, with a recommended precautionary sacrifice, was also presented.
