In this program, West Asia expert Farhad Pashavand emphasized the strategic transformation of the Houthis from a domestic actor to a regional and global player following October 7. He highlighted Yemen's use of advanced technologies, such as cluster and hypersonic missiles, which have significantly challenged the Zionist regime's air defense capabilities. Pashavand attributed Yemen's resilience against intelligence operations and assassinations to its decentralized, network-based structure and the difficult terrain that hinders Israeli infiltration. He also noted the steadfast resistance of the Yemeni people against economic and military pressures, which has further complicated the enemy's efforts. The expert argued that the multi-front conflicts, including those in Gaza and Lebanon, have placed the Zionist regime in a costly and attritional situation, preventing it from achieving its broader objectives. Finally, Pashavand predicted that under intense internal and international pressure, Netanyahu will be forced to accept a ceasefire and end the war to preserve his political survival.
