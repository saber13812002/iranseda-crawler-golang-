The radio program "Zamzame Ahkam" began by addressing the Sharia rulings related to the Arbaeen pilgrimage.
Hojjat al-Islam Vahidpour emphasized the necessity of observing personal and social hygiene to prevent diseases among the massive crowds of pilgrims.
He also prohibited the wasteful consumption of food and beverages, labeling it as a religious and ethical sin.
It was clarified that bottled and sanitary water is exclusively for drinking, making it religiously impermissible to use it for wudu or cooling down.
In response to listener inquiries, reciting the Arbaeen ziyara from outside the shrine or on nearby days was deemed to carry spiritual merit.
The salary earned from a job secured through past exam cheating is considered halal if the individual currently meets the job requirements and compensates for any injustice.
The rulings on performing wudu with seawater and the conditions for gold zakat were also explained by various religious authorities.
Finally, varying product prices for different customers was declared permissible provided that no deception or fraud is involved in the transaction.
