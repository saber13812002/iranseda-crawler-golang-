In the fiqh section, Hujjat al-Islam Mohammadi advised seeking family mediation before filing a lawsuit against a father, noting that a father's curse has no effect while respecting him yields spiritual reward. Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi permitted individuals with missed obligatory prayers to perform recommended prayers, provided they do not neglect making up the missed ones. It was also ruled that reciting the Quran without wudu is permissible if the body does not directly touch the text and prostration verses are avoided. Ayatollah Sistani declared that borrowing money with the obligation to repay it in gold at the current rate constitutes usury and is therefore prohibited. Ayatollah Shabiri Zanjani described dedicating the rewards of charity to the deceased as highly effective and blessed. Ayatollah Javadi Amoli stated that nail polish acts as a barrier to wudu and ghusl, requiring tayammum if it remains, and must be covered from non-mahrams. In the historical segment, Professor Jafari discussed the burial of Uthman ibn Affan, noting that he was interred in a separate area near Baqi due to being considered an infidel by some at the time.
