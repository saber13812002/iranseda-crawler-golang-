The radio program began with a hymn praising the Prophet of Islam before transitioning to a special segment titled "The Story of Freedom." In this section, Kazem Zarandi, a former prisoner of war, shared his memories of eight years of captivity. He recounted his decision to join the front lines as a teenager and his participation in the Fath al-Mubeen and Ramadan operations. Zarandi described the critical moments of the Ramadan operation, where he was wounded and captured by Iraqi forces in a minefield under heavy fire. He also detailed the harsh conditions of his captivity, including being trapped in an artillery vehicle and the behavior of enemy soldiers toward Iranian prisoners. The program then introduced a book by Abdolkarim Mazandarani, another former POW who was wounded and captured during the Abolfajr operation. Mazandarani engaged in extensive cultural and propaganda activities during his captivity in the Tareeq and Baqubah camps. The broadcast concluded with messages to listeners regarding the management of energy and water consumption.
