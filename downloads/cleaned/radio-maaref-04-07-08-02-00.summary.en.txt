In the radio program "Hekayat-e Azadegi," Farzamars Chehregani recounts the bitter memories of his captivity. He identifies the day of Imam Khomeini's death in 1989 as the most difficult period, marked by intense camp pressure and strict prohibitions. On that day, some prisoners attempted to escape, leading to three days of severe physical punishment and execution threats from the Iraqis. Chehregani also describes the brutality of a notorious guard known as "Check," who subjected prisoners to harsh beatings in cold water. The second segment, titled "Jor'eh," analyzes a hadith from the book of Kafi where Imam Sajjad describes detesting the world as the best deed after knowing God. This detestation refers to opposing moral vices such as arrogance, greed, and envy, which are rooted in the sins of Iblis, Adam, and Cain and Abel. The speaker emphasizes that abandoning these vices is the key to escaping the love of the world and achieving moral virtues.
