In the radio program "Persaman Quran," expert Ajaghai Maleki addressed various Quranic questions from listeners. He clarified the relationship between human free will and divine will, explaining that misfortunes are attributed to human actions in terms of choice, but to God's permission in terms of the power to act. Regarding the verse "Arise for the night" in Surah Al-Muzzammil, he presented two interpretations: the Prophet's fever due to the weight of revelation, or his nightly seclusion for spiritual preparation to warn people. The linguistic difference between "dhunub" (sins) and "sayyi'at" (evil deeds) was also examined, with the former referring to heavy sins and the latter to ugly acts or the consequences of sins. In response to a question about visually impaired individuals, it was emphasized that auditory recitation and deep reflection are suitable alternatives to visual reading, and physical touching of the Quran is not mandatory. Finally, the distinction between the physical hardships of dying and the believer's spiritual longing for meeting God was explained. The discussion concluded by stressing the importance of deep reflection in the Quran's verses and adhering to the teachings of the Quran and the Ahl al-Bayt as its primary interpreters.
