This radio program examines the practical life and political philosophy of Imam Hassan (AS) in serving the community. Narratives highlight the Imam's efforts to buy more goods for the poor through bargaining and his insistence on giving gifts to all visitors, regardless of their wealth, as a model of generosity. Stories of the Imam resolving people's issues, such as consoling a governor and addressing a creditor during his i'tikaf, demonstrate his prioritization of relieving public suffering over personal worship. The core message defines true political leadership as respecting the rights of God, the living, and the dead, emphasizing that officials must not neglect people's religious duties. The segment concludes with a reference to Imam Hassan's martyrdom at age forty-eight and the legacy of his descendants. The final part of the text shifts to unrelated literary and Sufi anecdotes about Akhoun Mola Hossein Qoli and his students.
