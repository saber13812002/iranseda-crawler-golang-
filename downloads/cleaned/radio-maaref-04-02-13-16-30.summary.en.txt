In this program, Professor Maleki interprets verse 41 of Surah Az-Zumar to clarify that the Prophet's role is limited to conveying and teaching the message, not forcing guidance. Citing the verse "You are not a guardian over them," he explains that individuals are responsible for their own choices and cannot be compelled to believe. Nevertheless, Muslims are obligated to enjoin good and forbid evil, striving earnestly to prevent others from sinning without coercion. The discussion then expands to the role of an Islamic government in establishing laws based on Quranic values to maintain social order. Following this, the segment "Keys to Happiness" focuses on the importance of appreciating one's current blessings. Psychologists and guests emphasize that contentment and effective challenge management are essential for mental well-being. They also highlight the necessity of distinguishing between immediate pleasures and long-term rewards, suggesting that sacrificing momentary gratification is crucial for achieving lasting success.
