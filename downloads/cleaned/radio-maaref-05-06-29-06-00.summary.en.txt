This program interprets verses from Surah Al-Mu'minun, emphasizing that entry into Paradise requires both faith and righteous deeds, though God only obligates individuals according to their specific capacities. In Paradise, all resentment and sorrow are purged from the hearts of the believers, placing them in a state of complete peace. The discussion also addresses the fate of disbelievers, suggesting that if their disbelief is merely an acquired trait, they may return to their original fitrah after purification and enter Paradise. In the Hereafter, the inhabitants of Hell see the truth but cannot accept it because faith is a voluntary act that requires the specific conditions of worldly accountability. The people of Paradise express gratitude to God, acknowledging that the prophets guided them to truths that humans could never have discovered on their own. The concept of "inheritance" in Paradise signifies that believers directly attain their status through a spiritual connection with God, rather than through material exchange. Finally, the absolute freedom of the inhabitants of Paradise to shape their environment demonstrates that they are the creators of their own will, as whatever they desire immediately manifests.
