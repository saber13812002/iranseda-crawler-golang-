The program begins with a romantic song that emphasizes mutual connection and hope for a bright future. The host then recounts the story of Salman al-Farisi to illustrate the difference between accounting and punishment regarding lawful and unlawful wealth. By referencing listener donations, such as the gift of five packs of cigarettes, the host highlights the value of any support given according to one's ability. Using the story of Prophet Ibrahim as a metaphor, the host explains that even small acts of charity with pure intentions yield great rewards through divine mercy. Finally, listeners are invited to support needy families by sending money to a specified number. The broadcast concludes with the recitation of the Ziyarat of Al-Yasin and other religious supplications.
