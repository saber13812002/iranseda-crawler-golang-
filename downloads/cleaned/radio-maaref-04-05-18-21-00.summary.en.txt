This radio program honored the memory of freed prisoners and martyrs while discussing the religious and health benefits of eating moderately.
The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) and the Imams (AS) emphasized avoiding overeating, as it leads to physical illness and spiritual distraction from worship.
The simple lifestyles and frugality of scholars like Akhund Khorasani, Imam Khomeini, and Ayatollah Javadi Amoli were highlighted as practical examples of this teaching.
The second segment featured a dramatic narration from the book "Said Man Hosseini," depicting a teacher's struggle to obtain a travel permit for Karbala.
Despite significant bureaucratic and financial obstacles, the protagonist, Mohammad Taghi, eventually secured his exit permit with the help of friends and persistent follow-up.
In the Q&A section, it was clarified that a shroud purchased years ago is not subject to Khums tax.
The program concluded with advice to perform small, positive actions for societal peace and prayers for the safety of Arbaeen pilgrims.
