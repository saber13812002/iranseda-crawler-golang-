In the "World of Islam" news segment on Ma'aref Radio, the head of communications at the Hajj and Pilgrimage Organization reported an increase in applicants for Tabataba trips following the Arbaeen ceremony. He noted that the new round of permits began on September 17, and that currency fluctuations have not affected travel costs. The estimated cost for the Friday night visit to Imam Hussein's shrine, for Iranian pilgrims without specific residency, ranges from 3.7 to 4.7 million Tomans. Meanwhile, Ayatollah Ebrahimi, the head of the country's seminaries, traveled to Malaysia to participate in the second International Conference of Religious Leaders. The conference, focused on the role of religious leaders in resolving conflicts, is being held in Kuala Lumpur with the invitation of the Malaysian Prime Minister and the cooperation of the OIC. Ayatollah Ebrahimi stated that the primary goal of attending this event is to find solutions for the severe suffering of the people of Gaza and the Islamic Ummah. He emphasized the necessity of collaboration among religious scholars and academic figures to address the critical conditions in the Islamic world.
