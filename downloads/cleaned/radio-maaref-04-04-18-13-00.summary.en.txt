The program begins with a narrative of Abdullah ibn Abbas warmly hosting Imam Hussein (AS) and his companions after their journey from Medina. It then analyzes a Quranic concept stating that a sign of divine favor for a community is the increase of jurists and the decrease of the ignorant. Conversely, a society where the words of the ignorant are validated while jurists are ignored is considered to be under divine affliction. The speaker highlights the historical lack of loyalty during Ashura and Imam Sajjad's (AS) silence, emphasizing that indifference to the oppressed is itself a form of enmity. The danger of misinterpretations by the ignorant, which led to phenomena like ISIS, is also discussed. Finally, the necessity of referring to a jurist and avoiding satanic doubts that weaken faith and create hesitation are emphasized.
