This radio program focuses on the profound connection between the Supreme Leader and the defenders during the Iran-Iraq War. Sardar Aliakbar Khalghi recounts how Imam Khomeini's personal blessings significantly boosted the morale of soldiers during the Operation of Holy Quds. Sardar Mohsen Karimi highlights the motivational messages sent by the Imam during the Badr and Karbala 5 operations, which inspired troops to stand firm against the enemy. Another anecdote describes the Imam's warm and personal greeting to a wounded veteran, demonstrating his special affection for the fighters. The program also mentions Sardar Babai, who declined his Hajj pilgrimage because he could not bear to leave the front lines. Sardar Mohammad Bagheri recalls the Imam's strategic advice to young commanders, urging them to trust in God and overcome their fears. The segment concludes by emphasizing the critical need for national unity and public support for government officials in the face of external threats. These memories collectively illustrate that loyalty to the leadership and a spirit of sacrifice were key factors in the defenders' resilience.
