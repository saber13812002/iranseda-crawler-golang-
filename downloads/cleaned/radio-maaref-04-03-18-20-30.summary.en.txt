In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Mohammadi Eshtehadi discusses his efforts to simplify and rewrite the Tafsir al-Namuna under the guidance of Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi to make it accessible to the general public. He also highlights the services of Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi's information center, which provides online answers to religious and social questions in Persian, English, Arabic, and Urdu. Another segment covers the history of the "Maktab-e Eslam" magazine, launched in 1958, and the pivotal role of Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi in supporting it to guide the younger generation. The discussion then shifts to memories of the Iranian captives' return to Iran in 1985, emphasizing the moral and medical support provided to the disabled veterans. Despite harsh prison conditions, the Iranian prisoners learned the alphabet by writing on wet mud and persisted in studying the Quran and Arabic language. These efforts demonstrate the captives' strong will to maintain their religious and cultural identity against enemy pressure. The program concludes by emphasizing the importance of education and spiritual resilience during critical times.
