Hojjat al-Islam Rafiei discusses the distinctions between self-knowledge, self-discovery, and self-building based on a sermon by Imam Ali. He defines self-knowledge as recognizing human dignity and the unique spiritual nature that distinguishes humans from other creatures. Self-discovery involves assessing one's current moral and spiritual status to determine their position on the spectrum of human souls. Self-building is the subsequent process of correcting and elevating oneself based on these insights. The speaker identifies five major obstacles to righteousness: complacency with ignorance, stinginess with virtues, greed for the world, hypocrisy, and arrogance. Through historical anecdotes, he highlights that even those with poor reputations may possess hidden virtues. He concludes by emphasizing the importance of reflection and learning from others' experiences to achieve spiritual and moral progress.
