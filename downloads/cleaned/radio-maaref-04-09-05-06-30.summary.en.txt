The radio program begins with a Quranic interpretation, emphasizing the importance of deep reflection and understanding the inner meanings of the verses.
The primary occasion for the day, the fifth of Azar, is the anniversary of the formation of the Basij by Imam Khomeini and the day of Basij Construction.
The show highlights the decisive role of the Basij during the Sacred Defense, describing it as the world's largest cultural and social network and affirming that all Iranians share a Basiji identity.
It also commemorates the 1978 uprising in Gorgan, where citizens protested the desecration of Imam Reza's shrine, resulting in 14 martyrs and over 200 injuries under the Pahlavi regime.
The host invites listeners to seek peace and relief from anxiety by praying for the intercession of Imam Reza (AS) and reciting specific supplications.
Listeners are encouraged to send messages sharing their personal experiences with the Basij and their definition of being a Basiji.
The broadcast concludes with religious music and patriotic slogans supporting the Basij and the people of Iran.
