In the radio program "Persman Ateghadi" featuring Professor Mohammadi, various theological questions from listeners were addressed. The discussion began with the reproduction of humanity after Adam, noting that Islamic sources offer multiple theories, such as marriage with heavenly spouses or close relatives in the first generation. The relationship between God's will and human destiny was then examined, emphasizing that while all events are under God's will in a cosmic sense, sins and misfortunes are attributed to human choice in a legislative sense. The positive impact of sending salawat and good deeds for the deceased was affirmed, although setting conditions for the forgiveness of others' rights was deemed illogical. The timing of salawat and the status of Ziyarat Ashura as a sacred hadith were also explained. Finally, practical advice for achieving presence of heart in prayer was provided, rejecting eye-closing as a primary method and instead recommending focusing on the meanings of the prayer and visualizing God's presence.
