This radio program discussed the jurisprudential ruling regarding doubt in the number of prayer units. It was explained that in individual prayer, uncertainty about the number of units invalidates the prayer. However, in congregational prayer, the follower must ignore their own doubt and strictly follow the imam's actions. The follower's prayer remains valid upon the imam's greeting, without requiring the prostration of forgetfulness. The program then announced contact numbers and SMS services for listeners to submit feedback. Following the recitation of religious poetry and prayers, the segment for public inquiries began. One listener complained about the poor audio quality and excessive heat in the broadcast. The program concluded with religious slogans and background music.
