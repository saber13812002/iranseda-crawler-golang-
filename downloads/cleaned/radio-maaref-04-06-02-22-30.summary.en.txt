In this program, Hojjat al-Islam Rafiei emphasized the importance of having good opinion of God and the danger of having a bad opinion, citing narrations from Imam Reza (AS). He outlined five positive outcomes of this virtue, including the acceptance of prayers, provision of sustenance, forgiveness of sins, success in worldly affairs, and victory over hardships. The program then highlighted the virtue of reciting fifty verses of the Quran daily to avoid spiritual stagnation, followed by prayers for the Imam's intercession. Subsequently, the broadcast marked the beginning of the month of Rabi al-Awwal by commemorating Laylat al-Mabit and Imam Ali's (AS) self-sacrifice during the Prophet's migration. Later, Hojjat al-Islam Yousifzadeh discussed family dynamics, stressing the need to understand gender roles and family differences without judgment. He explained that deeply understanding parental behavioral patterns is key to managing expectations and reducing tension in marital relationships.
