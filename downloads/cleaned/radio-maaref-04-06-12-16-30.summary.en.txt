In the first segment, Professor Mohammadi addressed theological questions from listeners, refuting the notion that God is indifferent to the suffering in Gaza. He emphasized that God acts with patience and will deliver complete justice in the afterlife, as the world lacks the capacity for full retribution. The professor also explained that the purpose of creation is a test, highlighting human free will in choosing between good and evil. The second segment focused on the concept of "consultation" from the book Mafatih al-Hayat. Hujjat al-Islam Barmeslimin, citing the Quran and the Prophet's example, described consultation as a sign of intelligence and a way to avoid errors by leveraging others' experiences. He noted that even the Prophet consulted with the community on major decisions, a practice that guides society toward prosperity.
