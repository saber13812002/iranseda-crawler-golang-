This radio program explores the concept of mental management against the psychological operations of both jinn and human enemies. Citing Quranic verses, the speaker emphasizes that adversaries use accusations and temptations to undermine our beliefs at both individual and societal levels. One of the most dangerous tactics is labeling God's allies and religious leaders with negative stereotypes, which sometimes persists even after their martyrdom. The Prophet (PBUH) responded to such accusations not with personal defense, but by emphasizing obedience to God and love for the Ahl al-Bayt. The most severe consequence of these psychological operations is creating doubt in the truth of God's representative, ultimately leading to being "cast out of God's sight." Through the story of Prophet Jesus (PBUH) and his companions, the speaker illustrates that hidden doubt in the heart can invalidate worship. Therefore, maintaining vigilance against these tricks and keeping the heart pure are essential for staying on the path of truth and avoiding misguidance.
