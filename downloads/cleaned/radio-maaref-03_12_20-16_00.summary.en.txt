In this radio program, Dr. Fatemeh Hedayari emphasized the importance of instilling a culture of accountability in families to properly raise adolescents. She explained that parents must model responsibility so that children learn to answer for their mistakes rather than hiding them. Addressing listener questions, the expert described adolescent mood swings as natural hormonal changes, stressing the need to accept feelings while controlling behaviors. To reduce blind imitation of peers, she suggested building self-confidence and modeling simplicity inspired by successful public figures. Regarding digital spaces, the necessity of establishing fair, unified rules for mobile phone usage across the entire family was highlighted. Finally, it was warned that excessive parental control undermines a child's independence and decision-making skills in adulthood.
