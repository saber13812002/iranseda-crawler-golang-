In the radio program "Narrative of Love," Nafiseh Kermani, the wife of Martyr Colonel Ali Azaradeh, shared memories of their life together and the hardships following his martyrdom in a terrorist attack in Damascus. She spoke of her husband's deep love for martyrdom and his compassionate behavior during her pregnancy and illnesses, while also describing the profound grief and longing she experienced after his loss. Nafiseh recounted the process of transferring her husband's body from Syria to Iran and her complex emotions during the funeral procession and burial in Qom's Martyrs' Garden. She emphasized that patience and reliance on God, coupled with remembering her husband's goodness, helped her cope with this immense tragedy. She also highlighted her husband's dedication to moral self-improvement and following spiritual teachings throughout their life. Finally, she expressed her wish to see her husband in paradise and encouraged her daughters to follow their father's ethical example. The program concluded with an analysis of the US President's visit to occupied Palestine and Iran's missile capabilities.
