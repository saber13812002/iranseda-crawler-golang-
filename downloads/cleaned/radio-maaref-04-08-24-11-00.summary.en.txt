The "Hadith of Loneliness" mourning festival began in Qom to honor the memory of Lady Zahra (SA) with artistic programs. Simultaneously, the 33rd Book Week of the Islamic Republic of Iran kicked off nationwide under the slogan "Let's Read for Iran." Registration for the Rajabiyat I'tikaf at the Moving Shrine of Imam Reza (AS) also started on the 24th of Aban. People paid tribute to Sardar Hassan Tehrani Moghaddam, the father of Iran's missile program, on the anniversary of his martyrdom. The mother of three martyrs was buried in Najafabad alongside her sons. The International Conference on Islamic Humanities is scheduled to be held in Qom in Dey. In the religious rulings segment, various answers were provided regarding night prayers, prohibited animal parts, and issues related to ritual impurity.
