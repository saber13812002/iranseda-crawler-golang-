In this radio program, guests discussed the primary duties of Mahdi's awaiters based on Quranic verses and religious traditions.
The central theme was the necessity of constantly remembering the Imam as the most crucial duty for maintaining faith and finding inner peace.
The speakers explained that this remembrance invites the Imam's special guardianship and serves as a strong shield against satanic temptations.
They also emphasized that correct knowledge of the Imam and abandoning sins are prerequisites for approaching the time of reappearance.
According to religious narrations, when a believer remembers the Imam, the Imam reciprocates by remembering and caring for that individual.
Listeners were encouraged to live more Quranic lives and integrate the remembrance of the Imam into their daily routines to prepare for his return.
The program concluded with prayers for the Imam's health and the hastening of his glorious appearance.
