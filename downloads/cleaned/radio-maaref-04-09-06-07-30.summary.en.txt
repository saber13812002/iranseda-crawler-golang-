The radio program begins by clarifying that the Imam of the Time is not hidden, but rather human sins and bad deeds act as barriers to recognizing him. The discussion then shifts to physical health, emphasizing that smoking and using hookah are primary causes of heart, lung, and vascular diseases, urging listeners to quit at any age. The main segment addresses the moral harm of flattery and excessive praise, citing Quranic verses and hadiths to define such behavior as a sin. The instructor warns that the worst form of praise comes from wicked people, while the best praise is reserved for God and the Ahl al-Bayt. Listeners are advised to avoid begging for compliments from others and to refrain from self-praise or the misuse of titles, as these actions hinder spiritual growth and true servitude to God. The program concludes by calling on Muslims to stand against flattery in the camp of truth and the Ahl al-Bayt. Finally, it encourages the audience to heed the historical calls for help from Lady Fatimah (SA) and Imam Hussein (AS) to support justice and righteousness.
