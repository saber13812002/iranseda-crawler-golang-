In this segment, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verses from the Surahs of Al-Ma'idah and Al-An'am, emphasizing the critical distinction between the system of creation and legislation. He argues that while God grants humans free will in the system of creation, He clearly defines what is permissible and forbidden through revelation in the system of legislation. Relying on pre-Islamic traditions and blind imitation of ancestors is deemed baseless and stems from confusing inherent freedom with divine commandments. The speaker stresses that reason is not opposed to religion but rather stands alongside revelation as a fundamental pillar of faith and social governance. Imitating the ignorant, or even a knowledgeable person imitating the ignorant, is strictly prohibited, and individuals must use their intellect to seek the truth. Finally, he accuses those who deny revelation and prophethood of slander, insisting on the necessity of accepting human prophets as the intermediaries of divine guidance.
