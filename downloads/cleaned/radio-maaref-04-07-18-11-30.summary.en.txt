In this radio program, religious poetry and prayers were broadcast, calling on people to attend Friday prayers and the "Basharat Nasr" march to support Palestine on the second anniversary of the Al-Aqsa Flood operation.
The main discussion addressed Mahdavi questions, emphasizing the human innate need for a healthy society led by an Imam who implements divine law.
Regarding the economic status in the Mahdi's government, narrations were cited indicating that poverty will vanish and a spirit of contentment and self-sufficiency will prevail among the people.
It was also explained that while writing letters to the Mahdi is approved, its primary benefit is providing psychological peace to the writer rather than informing the Imam.
The program concluded with the Adhan and the start of Friday sermon in Tehran at the University of Tehran, delivered by Hojjat al-Islam Yeganeh.
The event aims to honor the resilience of the Palestinian people and the victory of resistance, expecting widespread public participation.
Finally, the importance of attending Friday prayers and adhering to religious commands to achieve justice and freedom was emphasized.
