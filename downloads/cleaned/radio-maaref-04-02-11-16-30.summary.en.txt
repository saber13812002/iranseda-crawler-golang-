In the first segment, a political analyst emphasized that the Zionist regime's crimes in Gaza have severely undermined the moral and political credibility of Western leaders and some supportive Islamic governments. He argued that social media has awakened global public opinion by exposing these injustices, thereby increasing expectations for concrete action from rulers. The program also commemorated the 18th of Ordibehesht as the anniversary of the Al-Bait al-Maqdis operation and the liberation of Hawizeh during the Iran-Iraq war. Additionally, the 21st and 22nd of Ordibehesht were noted as the anniversaries of the continuation of Mirza Kuchak Khan's movement and the separation of Bahrain from Iran, respectively. In the second segment, Professor Lakzai discussed nuclear negotiations, stating that Iran's internal strength is the key determinant of the outcome and that the mistakes of the JCPOA can be avoided through a step-by-step approach with strict oversight. He stressed the necessity of strengthening economic and defense self-sufficiency to render sanctions ineffective, while clarifying that current talks are strictly limited to nuclear issues and that red lines regarding missiles and resistance remain intact. Finally, the program concluded with a discussion on the high status of teachers in Islamic tradition and the importance of mutual respect between educators and students.
