In response to the first question, Hojjat al-Islam Mohammadi explained that according to Imam Khomeini's fatwa, selling one's only agricultural land is exempt from Khums if paying it would prevent the individual from covering essential living expenses. This exemption applies strictly when the land sale is the person's sole source of income and they have no other assets or earnings. Consequently, even if Khums was not paid in previous years, it is not mandatory in this specific scenario. Regarding the second question, Hojjat al-Islam Jawahiri clarified that there is no specific order required for making up missed prayers, with one notable exception. If both the Zuhr and Asr prayers of the same day are missed, the Zuhr prayer must be performed before the Asr prayer. However, there is no such strict sequence between other prayers, such as Fajr and Zuhr, allowing individuals to make up missed prayers in any preferred order.
