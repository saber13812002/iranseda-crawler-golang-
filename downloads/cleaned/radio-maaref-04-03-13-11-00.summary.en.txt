On the anniversary of the martyrdom of Imam Muhammad Baqir (AS), Mashhad and Qom are observing mourning rituals and religious lectures. Hujjat al-Islam Mousavi highlighted Imam Baqir's role in establishing the Shia scientific school and his ability to introduce pure Islam during a period of ideological conflict. Simultaneously, the Assembly of Experts and the Qom Seminary issued messages emphasizing the importance of holding grand ceremonies for the anniversary of Imam Khomeini's passing and the 15 Khordad uprising. The head of the Hajj and Pilgrimage Organization reported a reduction in delays and the average age of Iranian pilgrims this year, noting the presence of teenagers on this spiritual journey. Transportation plans for the Arbaeen period, including ticket pre-sales and the use of pilgrimage passports, have also been announced. The Eid al-Adha prayer will be held at the University of Tehran under the leadership of Hujjat al-Islam Abutrabee, with no Friday prayer scheduled for that day. A running aid caravan from Golestan province entered Qom heading to Imam Khomeini's shrine, while over a thousand assassination martyrs have been identified in Sistan and Baluchestan.
