The program began with the recitation of the Salawat prayer and an expression of deep love for Imam Ali Akbar (PBUH).
It highlighted that he was the most similar to the Prophet (PBUH) in character and appearance.
The main discussion focused on the virtue of charity, specifically giving away one's most beloved possessions.
The host cited narrations about the generous habits of Imam Hussein (PBUH) and Imam Sadiq (PBUH) in sharing sweets and food.
It was emphasized that God rewards all forms of charity, whether from wealth, status, or life, according to their value.
The segment included the recitation of poetic verses praising the beauty and noble conduct of Imam Ali Akbar.
The program concluded by encouraging listeners to practice sincere charity in the path of God's obedience.
This discussion was part of the "Ganj-e Sa'adat" show, broadcasted on the eve of Muharram 1447.
