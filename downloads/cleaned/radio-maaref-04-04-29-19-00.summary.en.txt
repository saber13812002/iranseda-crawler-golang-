In the "Zamzam Ahkam" program, Hojjat al-Islam Mahmoudi stated that, according to Ayatollah Sistani's fatwa, elderly women are exempt from making up missed fasts and paying expiation. Hojjat al-Islam Alian Nijat clarified that proxy Hajj for a deceased mother requires the proxy to meet general conditions like maturity and sanity, but does not require the proxy to be of the same gender or follow the same marja. Additionally, Hojjat al-Islam Mohammadi Sharbani explained that while the Isha supererogatory prayer is waived during travel according to Imam Khomeini, it may still be performed with the intention of seeking reward. The program featured a sermon by Hojjat al-Islam Seyed Reza Taheri emphasizing the importance of prayer, noting that its beautiful form in the afterlife depends on its proper performance in this life. He warned that neglecting prayer results in a bad form in the hereafter and stressed that adhering to prayer's rights is essential for receiving the intercession of the Ahl al-Bayt. The broadcast also mourned the martyrdom of Dr. Mohammad Mehdi Tehranchi, a prominent physicist and president of the Islamic Azad University, who was killed in a recent Israeli attack in Tehran. Dr. Tehranchi, who was placed on the US sanctions list in 2020, was martyred on June 13, 2025. The program concluded with religious supplications and a reminder of the contact channels for submitting religious questions.
