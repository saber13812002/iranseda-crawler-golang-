In this radio program, experts discussed several key domestic and international issues. First, the claim that Imam Khomeini promised free water and electricity was refuted, clarifying that this promise belonged to the provisional government, while the Islamic Republic has since provided extensive subsidies to the underprivileged. The water crisis was then addressed as a global challenge driven by climate change, emphasizing the need for government management of waste and public consumption adjustments without media exaggeration. The program also highlighted the Islamic Republic's material, spiritual, and military support for Palestine and Gaza, urging Islamic leaders to wake up and provide more practical aid to the suffering people. Finally, the trend of feng shui and superstitious beliefs in home decoration was examined, with psychologists warning that relying on such methods can lead to frustration and the wastage of financial and temporal resources.
