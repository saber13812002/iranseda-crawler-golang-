Mr. Polfarzaneh, a bowling veteran and former manager of the Enghelab Club, emphasized the critical need for investment in the academic and educational sectors of the sport. He highlighted a significant decline in players' average scores from world-class levels in 2014 to below 200 points today, attributing this regression to a lack of proper coaching and referee training. He announced his intention to invest in establishing a specialized center for coaching, talent identification, and player development to professionalize the sport. Polfarzaneh stressed the importance of making bowling a mass-participation activity to improve family health and increase public engagement from under 10% to 30%. He noted that bowling has high potential for international competition and requires federal support and a focus on ethical and family-oriented values. Finally, he argued that separating the bowling federation from billiards is essential for the independent growth of both disciplines and called for greater support from the sports community for investors and veterans.
