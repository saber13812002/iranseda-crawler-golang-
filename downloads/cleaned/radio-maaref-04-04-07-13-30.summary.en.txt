The final segment of the broadcast is dedicated to the Judiciary Week, referencing the 1989 assassination of 72 prominent figures and intellectuals in the Parliament bombing. The speaker asserts that this enemy act, rather than weakening the system, has strengthened the Islamic Republic's stability and the people's faith. The program concludes by praising the crucial role of the Judiciary in upholding justice and achieving the system's objectives.
