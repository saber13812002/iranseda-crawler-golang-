Sheikh Mufid agrees to teach fiqh to two children, Sayyid Murtadha and Sayyid Razi, as requested by their mother. A man urgently consults him regarding the burial of his deceased pregnant wife, to which Sheikh Mufid initially rules against surgical intervention. However, after the Imam's correction, the order is changed to extract the living fetus, successfully saving the baby's life. The man later brings the healthy infant to Sheikh Mufid to fulfill his late wife's wish for the child to be raised in knowledge. Following a letter from the Imam affirming his support, Sheikh Mufid resumes his teaching sessions after a brief period of self-imposed withdrawal. In the second segment, a Mahdavi expert emphasizes that inviting people to the Imam's cause requires practical action and consulting scholars for guidance during the occultation. The program also recounts the memoirs of Azat Shahi, highlighting his steadfast refusal to express regret to his court-appointed lawyer despite the threat of execution.
