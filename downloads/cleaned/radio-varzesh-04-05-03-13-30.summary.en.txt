Former footballer Ali Hamoudi recounts his bitter financial experiences during his time at Esteghlal Tehran. He admits that he received no payment for two years due to the club's financial issues and lack of sponsors, even borrowing money from friends to travel home. Hamoudi highlights the unfair treatment of young players compared to stars and the psychological pressure he faced during that period. He also discusses being accused of forging his military discharge certificate, which forced him to serve in the military team of Tractor Tabriz. Later, he mentions the significant reduction of his contract at Sepahan and his move to Paykan to maintain his playing level. Finally, Hamoudi criticizes the corruption and widespread match-fixing in Iran's second division, revealing that players there were openly offered bribes to manipulate games.
