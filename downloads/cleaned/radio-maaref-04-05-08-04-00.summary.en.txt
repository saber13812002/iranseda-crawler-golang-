The radio program began by highlighting Imam Khomeini's profound reliance on God and his calm demeanor during the Iran-Iraq war and early revolutionary crises. The discussion then shifted to the religious rulings regarding Ta'ziyeh and Shabih-khani, emphasizing the necessity of selecting pious performers and avoiding any form of hypocrisy or disrespect toward religious figures. Various jurisprudential questions were addressed, covering topics such as financial rights related to Mahr, prayer conditions, and the permissibility of performing missed prayers for the deceased. The broadcast concluded with a detailed analysis of Imam Ali's first sermon after his appointment as Caliph, focusing on the reactions of his opponents to his leadership.
