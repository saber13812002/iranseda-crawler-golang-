The head of the Arbaeen headquarters reported that 80 percent of pilgrims have returned to Iran, recommending the Shalamcheh and Khasrou borders over Mehran for a smoother exit. Iraq's Interior Ministry spokesperson stated that over four million foreign pilgrims from 40 countries participated in the Arbaeen ceremony without any reported security incidents. The commander of the Iranian police urged returning pilgrims to rest adequately and praised the national media for its comprehensive coverage of the event. In the second segment, Hujjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the necessity of humility for religious scholars towards both their teachers and students. He cited a narration from Imam Sadiq (AS) warning that arrogance and irritability can negate the spiritual value of one's knowledge. To illustrate this virtue, anecdotes about the humble behavior of Ayatollah Borujerdi and Akhund Khorasani towards their students were shared. The segment concluded by asserting that such humility is a fundamental trait derived from the conduct of prophets and the Infallibles.
