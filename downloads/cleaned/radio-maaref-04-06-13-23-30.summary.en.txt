The radio program "Hamneshin" began by celebrating the Prophet's birthday and the start of Unity Week.
Guest poet Seyed Mohammad Baba Miri recited verses honoring Lady Khadija and the Prophet's ascension.
A blind listener shared his dedication to visiting shrines and mosques, praying for Iran's health and security.
The show included the recitation of the Ziyarat Al-Yasin and prayers for the Ahl al-Bayt.
A concluding audio message highlighted the divine transformation and spiritual power of the people in revolutionary victories.
This message emphasized God's role in fostering a spirit of martyrdom and cooperation among the youth.
The program ended with blessings for all Muslims and the hope of visiting Medina.
