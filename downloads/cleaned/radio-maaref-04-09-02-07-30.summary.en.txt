The program begins by emphasizing the importance of special kindness towards daughters and raising them based on the model of Hazrat Zahra. The discussion then shifts to the topic of mocking others and its roots, identifying ignorance and lack of religious knowledge as the primary causes. Factors such as the illusion of material, positional, or professional advantages lead people to treat others with disrespect. Quranic stories and narrations are used to describe mockery as a sign of ignorance and distance from God. The program also highlights the dangers of mocking those who are financially or socially weaker. Finally, listeners are encouraged to build a more peaceful society through prayer and kindness.
