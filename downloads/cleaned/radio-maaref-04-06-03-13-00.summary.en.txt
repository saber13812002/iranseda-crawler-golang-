This radio program interprets the concept of Ziyarat (pilgrimage) as a spiritual migration and journey toward the Imam and God.
The main premise highlights the immense virtues of visiting the Infallibles in Shia narrations, often ranking it above the Hajj.
According to cited traditions, visiting Imam Reza (AS) can yield the reward of seventy thousand accepted Hajjs and the freeing of seventy slaves.
Hajj is described as an escape from Satan and worldly attachments, while Ziyarat serves as a similar path to achieving divine proximity.
Imam Reza (AS) is metaphorically described as the sun and a rain cloud that casts the shade of mercy and revives the dry hearts of believers.
To benefit from these rewards, sincere intention, spiritual purity, and full attention are essential before embarking on the journey to the Imam.
Even a distant greeting to Imam Reza (AS) with a pure heart grants the believer the complete reward of Hajj and Umrah.
Ultimately, Ziyarat is presented as a means to revive dead hearts and renew the pledge of allegiance to Imam Mahdi (AJ) during the era of occultation.
