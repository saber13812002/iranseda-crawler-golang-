The Radio Ma'aref program began by honoring the thinkers who bridged the Quran and the Islamic Revolution. The main segment featured personal anecdotes from the wife and mother of Martyr Mostafa Ahmadi Roshan. His family highlighted his strict adherence to prayer, rituals, and ethical principles such as honesty and fair wages. He was described as a humorous, kind, and deeply committed individual in both his religious and family life. The broadcast included remarks from the Supreme Leader praising the martyr's scientific and spiritual stature. In the final section, the Leader emphasized the critical importance of basic sciences and scientific diplomacy. He warned against security infiltration through academic exchanges and urged officials to support researchers to prevent their frustration.
