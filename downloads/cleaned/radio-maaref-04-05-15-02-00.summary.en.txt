The first segment of the program features a narrative dialogue between Amir Sadra and Hussein ibn Ali, emphasizing the importance of governing hearts rather than using force and maintaining self-respect. Subsequently, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi highlights the necessity of preserving human dignity and avoiding the expression of need to others, citing the lives of scholars like Sheikh Zahed and Ayatollah Ashi Najafi. In the following section, Zahra Nasiri, the wife of martyr Javad Akbari, recounts the painful moments of her husband's martyrdom and the grief of her children's orphanhood. She expresses frustration over the insensitive judgments made by people regarding martyrs and their families, detailing the hardships of daily life without her spouse and her concerns for her children's future. Nasiri also recalls her husband's spiritual dreams and his deep love for his family before his death. The program concludes with a call for greater empathy towards the families of martyrs and a plea to avoid causing further emotional harm through careless words.
