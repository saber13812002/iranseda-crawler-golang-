This radio program explains the concept of "istidraj" from Nahj al-Balagha, defining it as God granting more blessings to sinners as a form of divine delay. The speaker, referencing Imam Sadiq and Zaynab al-Kubra, emphasizes that all blessings must be attributed to God to avoid arrogance. Istidraj is described as the most severe trial, where divine aid is cut off, leading the individual deeper into sin. Key characteristics of those affected include pride in superficial appearances and being deceived by excessive praise from others. To prevent this, one must control their speech and avoid attributing personal success to their own efforts. The lecture highlights the humility of Sheikh Jafar al-Kabir, who remained modest despite his high scholarly status. The program concludes with prayers for the Mahdi and expressions of solidarity with the people of Gaza.
