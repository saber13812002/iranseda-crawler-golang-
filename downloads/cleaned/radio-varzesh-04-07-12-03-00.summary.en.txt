In this interview, karate veteran Mohsen Ashouri highlighted the pivotal role of Ali Nazarian in elevating Iran's international standing and securing the world championship in 2003. He attributed the subsequent decline in success to the frequent instability in the federation's leadership, which eroded trust among other international bodies. Ashouri criticized the current federation structure, emphasizing the need for an independent national team organization and technical committees composed of up-to-date experts to foster development. He described past conflicts with certain administrators as stemming from their lack of belief in the potential of Iranian karate. In the final segment, Ashouri spoke about his deep commitment to karate and its positive impact on instilling discipline and respect in his personal and social life. He also mentioned his enduring interest in football and boxing while expressing his readiness to return to management if invited. His ultimate goal remains the full realization of Iranian karate's potential and achieving global honors for the country.
