Freestyle wrestler Rahimeh Alibadi, a Munich Olympics medalist, shared his sporting memories in a radio interview. He highlighted the severe hardships and lack of facilities that older athletes faced while striving for medals. Alibadi urged sports officials to pay more attention to the financial and social needs of these aging champions. He also praised the recent positive transformation in Iranian Greco-Roman wrestling, particularly the historic gold medals won in London and Paris. The veteran athlete emphasized the crucial role of dedicated coaches like Mohammad Bana in nurturing talents such as Omid Norouzi and Qasem Rezaei. Finally, he stressed the importance of mass sports for public health and the need to support young athletes to ensure a bright future for Iranian sports.
