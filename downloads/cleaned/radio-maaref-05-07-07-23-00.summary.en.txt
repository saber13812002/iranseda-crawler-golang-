In this radio program, the importance of establishing and strengthening the family as the foundation of salvation and the front of truth was discussed based on Quranic verses and narrations.
The most crucial characteristic of a Quranic family was identified as the "art of tolerance, forgiveness, and forbearance" when dealing with differences in taste and domestic tensions.
According to Surah An-Nisa, believers must maintain composure and practice forgiveness even during moments of aversion or annoyance with their spouse or relatives to receive divine blessings.
Practicing tolerance is not a sign of weakness but rather a source of authority and dignity within the family and extended kin, making the individual respected and trusted by others.
This gracious behavior and overlooking minor mistakes prevent simple disagreements from escalating into major conflicts that destroy household peace.
These behavioral skills not only help reform the wrongdoer but also attract divine love and mercy while resolving long-standing grudges.
Finally, it was emphasized that strengthening hope and faith within the home serves as the best shield against the enemy's plots to destroy families.
