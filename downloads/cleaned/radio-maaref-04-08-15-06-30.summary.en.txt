The program's jurisprudential segment analyzed the Shia requirement for legal deductions to rely on definitive reason or explicit text, contrasting this with Sunni methods of analogy and utility.
A critique of Mohaghegh Astarabadi was presented, noting that while he rejects the rule of repelling harm as rational, his legal foundations ultimately circle back to preserving order and thus that very rule.
This contradiction suggests that without accepting reason or text, legal sources reduce to an unofficial fifth source.
The second part of the show began with morning invocations and expressions of love for the Ahl al-Bayt, emphasizing the role of devotion in happiness and ethics.
The historical section reviewed events of Aban 15, including Imam Khomeini's opposition to the State and Provincial Councils bill and the birth of Mirza Shirazi.
The most significant historical event highlighted was Mirza Shirazi's historic fatwa banning tobacco against British colonialism.
Finally, listeners sent prayers for the Imam's reappearance, health, and good outcomes, concluding the broadcast with a prayer for divine mercy.
