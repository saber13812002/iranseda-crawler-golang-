In this radio program, the importance of proper nutrition during autumn was highlighted, citing Islamic traditional medicine recommendations to maintain health and boost the immune system.
Experts emphasized the moderate consumption of warm meats like lamb and young beef while avoiding cold and dry foods.
Subsequently, Hujjat al-Islam Rasoul Hafiz critiquized the culture of "the sin is on me" by referencing verses from the Quran.
Relying on verses from Surah Al-Ankabut and Al-Isra, he asserted that no one can bear the burden of another's sin and that promises to cover sins are lies.
In the final section, the role of the people in the success of the Islamic Revolution and their empowerment from zero was discussed.
It was emphasized that general awakening and public unity, even without weapons, can defeat powerful regimes.
This perspective was presented as a model for other nations to awaken and defend their national interests.
Finally, listeners were encouraged to take steps towards justice and freedom by relying on the Quran and faith.
