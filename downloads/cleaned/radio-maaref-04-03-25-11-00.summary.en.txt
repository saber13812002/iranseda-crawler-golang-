The IRGC launched a massive wave of drones and missiles in Operation "Sacred Promise 3," targeting Israeli military and energy infrastructure. Israeli media reported dozens of casualties and noted that many missiles evaded air defense systems due to their high range and power. Analysts suggest these strikes are pressuring Israel to avoid prolonging the conflict, causing significant economic and psychological strain. Concurrently, millions of Iranians gathered at the Ghadir celebrations to express support for the armed forces and honor the martyrdom of General Hajizadeh. It was revealed that Hajizadeh had been appointed as a servant of Imam Reza's shrine shortly before his death. The Hajj and Pilgrimage Organization announced a combined air and land plan to ensure the safe return of Iranian pilgrims from Saudi Arabia. Finally, the Tehran Governor ordered government employees to work remotely until the end of the month to manage the current situation.
