In this radio broadcast, following the announcement of the legal midnight time, speeches by Imam Khomeini regarding the unique nature of the Islamic Republic were aired. The Imam emphasized that the combination of the army, the IRGC, the clergy, and the people constitutes a divine and unprecedented phenomenon in human history, which the West has failed to comprehend due to its lack of understanding. He highlighted the fundamental difference between the former army, which was under the control of foreign advisors and characterized by rigid hierarchies, and the current forces rooted in faith and solidarity with the populace. The Imam also underscored the critical role of the imposed war in training military personnel through practical combat experience and fostering a spirit of independence. He distinguished Islamic peace from the political and opportunistic peace sought by enemies, particularly Saddam Hussein. Finally, the Imam urged military students to rely on faith and intellectual independence to prepare themselves as the vanguard of Islam and Iran, aiming to bring greater victories to the nation.
