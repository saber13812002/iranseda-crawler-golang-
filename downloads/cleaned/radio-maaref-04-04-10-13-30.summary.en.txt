The radio program titled "Shahadat Nameh" aired on the fifth day of Muharram, beginning with recitations and remembrance of Imam Hussein (PBUH). The main segment featured a martyrdom narrative focusing on the heroic deaths of two loyal companions, Habib ibn Mazahir and Har ibn Riyahi. The recitation detailed Habib's fierce battle against the enemy and his eventual martyrdom at the hands of Husayn ibn Numayl and a Tameemi warrior. Following Habib's death, Har ibn Riyahi was also martyred while defending the Imam, who honored his freedom and sacrifice with the phrase "Inna lillahi wa inna ilayhi raji'un." The narrative also recounted the martyrdom of Sa'id ibn Abdullah Hanafi during prayer and his curse upon the Umayyad tribe. The program concluded by inviting listeners to join the spiritual atmosphere of Muharram and tune in to Radio Muharram on the 95.5 MHz frequency.
