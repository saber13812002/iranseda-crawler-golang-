In this radio program, Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallah examines the devastating consequences of arrogance based on Quranic verses. He identifies arrogance as the greatest barrier to knowing God and receiving divine guidance, leading to a denial of truth and a lack of monotheism. Other outcomes of arrogance include abandoning worship, committing injustice, and being deprived of God's love and mercy. Conversely, humility and modesty are presented as the keys to achieving justice, guidance, and worldly and otherworldly happiness. The second part of the program, featuring Hojjat al-Islam Mohammad Bagherian, discusses the virtue of leniency, which is highly valued by God. He emphasizes that one should be gentle and fair in transactions and judgments, while remaining firm against enemies. Ultimately, abandoning arrogance and cultivating moral virtues such as kindness and justice are presented as the primary solutions for attaining divine pleasure and spiritual peace.
