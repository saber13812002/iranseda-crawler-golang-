This radio program emphasized the critical importance of safeguarding the public treasury, or Bayt al-Mal, in Islam.
Imam Ali (AS) clarified that the wealth of the public treasury belongs to the people, not the officials responsible for it.
He explicitly stated that unjust distribution of public funds by authorities constitutes oppression and betrayal.
Imam Sajjad (AS) further stressed the necessity of earning wealth through lawful means and using it appropriately.
The story of Ayatollah Fazel Ardakani illustrated the extreme care required, as he avoided sitting on the ground to protect his robe.
He treated his clothing, purchased with funds from the Imam's share, as if it were public property that must be preserved.
Martyr Mohammad Taghi Aminian also requested in his will to repay all his educational expenses to the public treasury.
He feared that his studies had imposed an undue burden on the community and wished to remain free of any debt to God or people.
