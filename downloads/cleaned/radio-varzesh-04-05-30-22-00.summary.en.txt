Engineer Masoud Pourfarzaneh, a bowling pioneer and manager of the Enghelab Bowling Club, discussed the construction of Iran's largest bowling facility with 24 lanes and international standards in 2002. He highlighted the sport's ancient history and Iran's successful hosting of the 1976 World Championship, where Kambiz Kiani achieved a historic fourth-place finish. Pourfarzaneh emphasized that bowling is an inclusive, family-oriented sport that benefits physical health across all age groups. He credited the Ministry of Sports for crucial support, including land allocation and reducing the customs tariff on bowling equipment from 65% to 10% to facilitate investment. To attract a wider audience, the club organized events featuring sports legends and artists, even proposing a global twins bowling tournament. Furthermore, he noted the localization of professional ball drilling services in 2005 as a significant step in elevating the competitive level of Iranian players.
