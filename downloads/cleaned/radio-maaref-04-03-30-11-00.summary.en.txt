The radio program "Man Gadirim" began by congratulating the audience on the Eid of Mubahala and other related occasions.
The guest, Seyyed Ibrahim Hosseini Araaki, interpreted verse 61 of Surah Al-Imran.
He defined Mubahala as a mutual prayer between the Prophet and the Christians of Najran for divine punishment upon the wrongdoer.
The Prophet brought Ali, Fatima, Hasan, and Husayn, referring to them as his soul and life.
The Christians of Najran refused to accept Mubahala and Islam after seeing these specific companions with the Prophet.
This event is considered one of the most significant Quranic proofs of the virtues of the Ahl al-Bayt.
The program concluded with the announcement of a text message contest and the playing of religious songs.
