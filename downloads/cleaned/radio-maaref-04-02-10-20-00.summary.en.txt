Despite border difficulties, Pakistani pilgrims express their devotion to Imam Reza by holding religious gatherings in Pakistan. Concurrently, the ninth Arabic Book Fair has begun in Syria under a slogan emphasizing unity and support for Palestine and Lebanon. The sixth global conference on Imam Reza is also being held at the holy shrine with speakers from 21 countries. The main theme of this conference is utilizing the teachings of the Ahl al-Bayt to address deep human needs. The first International Conference on Human Rights in the Middle East was also held in Tehran and Qom with guests from 22 countries. This conference aimed to critique political approaches and double standards in the field of human rights. Out of the received papers, 75 were accepted for presentation at the conference. Finally, the Human Rights Garden in Isfahan will be inaugurated with the presence of representatives from various countries.
