The radio program began with religious invocations and news regarding the 33rd National Prayer Conference, which focuses on Imam Khomeini's views on prayer. The main segment covered the criminal trial of contemporary poet Khosrow Naseri against singer Sefidro and his wife for literary theft. Court experts confirmed over 90% similarity between Naseri's poems and the songs performed by the defendants. The judge convicted the defendants of literary theft under the Copyright Law, sentencing them to six months in prison. Judge Razaghi justified this ruling by citing Islamic principles of intellectual property and moral ownership. He further explained that theft in Islam extends beyond material goods to include moral theft, such as withholding zakat or dowry. The program concluded with recollections of the Battle of Khorramshahr, highlighting the role of faith in the defenders' victory.
