In this radio program, religious rulings were provided by various clerical authorities in response to listeners' questions. The first section addressed the proper etiquette for writing sacred names and the rules for reciting specific Quranic surahs in obligatory prayers, noting that certain surahs must be recited in pairs. The ruling on the imam's silence during congregational prayer and the purity of a cloth with soap residue were also discussed. Financially, the distinction between legitimate profit-sharing (Mudarabah) and usury in the context of house rentals using deposit money was clarified. Additionally, the necessity of performing Wudu after Friday Ghusl before praying was explained. The second part focused on the intercessory status of Lady Fatima Masumeh (SA) and her role in the salvation of Shia believers on the Day of Judgment. Finally, the program concluded by defining moral virtues as the true measure of human excellence in Islam, followed by a Quranic recitation.
