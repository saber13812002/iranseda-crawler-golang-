This lecture emphasizes the necessity of self-knowledge as a prerequisite for knowing God, relying on Quranic verses and narrations from Imam Kazim.
According to the texts, there is no material barrier between humans and God; the only obstacle is self-centeredness and negligence of the soul.
Humans are obligated to know God to the extent of their intellectual capacity, as the divine essence transcends complete human comprehension.
The universe acts as a clear mirror that reflects truth only when purified from sin and focused on the Divine.
The hadith "Whoever knows himself knows his Lord" is not a logical impossibility but a call for self-purification and genuine self-awareness.
Ignorance of one's own self is considered the greatest ignorance, requiring daily vigilance to prevent spiritual loss.
Ultimately, resolving theological and practical issues depends on overcoming internal ignorance and understanding one's correct position before God.
