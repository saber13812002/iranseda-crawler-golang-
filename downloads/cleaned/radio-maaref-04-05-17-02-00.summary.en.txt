The radio program "Parsaiyan" began by introducing its various segments before discussing the virtue of charity in the path of God. Citing narrations from Hazrat Zahra and the Prophet, the program emphasized that God replaces and increases whatever is spent in His way. To illustrate this concept, stories of generosity from the lives of Ayatollah Dinparvar and Akhund Khorasani were shared, highlighting their charity even during times of financial hardship. In the "Story of the Week" segment, the tale "Gratitude" was broadcast, teaching that one truly appreciates blessings only after experiencing loss. The second story, "In Search of Salt," used the metaphor of buying salt to explain that the root of injustice lies in demanding more than one's right. The program concluded with a message encouraging listeners to practice gratitude and avoid injustice in their daily transactions.
