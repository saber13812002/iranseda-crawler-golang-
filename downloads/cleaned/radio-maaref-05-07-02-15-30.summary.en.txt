In this radio program, Dr. Mehdi Moshkibaf presented the strategy of managing differences as the core of national unity, emphasizing the need to avoid both extreme optimism and pessimism in analyzing the war. He defined the specific strategy for the unity process as "unity in diversity," which implies achieving sustainable unity without eliminating religious, ideological, or political differences. To operationalize this concept, he proposed three distinct spheres: fundamental non-negotiable issues like territorial integrity, negotiable matters such as economic and social policies, and areas of pluralism like lifestyle and political preferences. The main strategic error is conflating these three levels, and their precise separation is the key to achieving sustainable unity and teaching society how to manage differences. Finally, the fourth strategy, derived from the previous three, was identified as transforming social capital into institutional capital. This process involves reviving historical memory, directing continuous public participation, and managing differences, ultimately creating a strong institution to insure the future of the Islamic Revolution.
