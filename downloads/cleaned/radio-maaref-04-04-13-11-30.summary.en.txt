In this radio program, the necessity of distributing a deceased person's estate according to Islamic law rather than personal discretion was emphasized. The show then addressed various fiqh questions, including the ruling on prayer for a traveler who owns a house in their former city and intends to return, which is considered a full prayer. It was explained that a person who doubts three times in one prayer or in three consecutive prayers is considered a "mushkil" and should ignore their doubts. Regarding the khums tax, deposit money is classified as a debt and is therefore not subject to khums. Ethically, leaving gatherings where alcohol is consumed and severing ties with Muslim alcoholics were deemed obligatory, although advising them is also recommended. For temporary marriage with non-Shia Muslims, the primary conditions are the valid formation of the contract and ensuring the rights of both parties are protected. Finally, the importance of all obligated individuals knowing the specific rulings applicable to them, such as those concerning istihadah, was highlighted to prevent the invalidation of their worship.
