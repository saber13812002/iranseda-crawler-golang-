This news segment begins by marking the martyrdom anniversary of Imam Sajjad (AS) and reports on the large gatherings of mourners at the shrines of Imam Reza and Imam Masoum. It highlights the readiness of the Shalamcheh and Chazabeh borders to host Arbaeen pilgrims, with hundreds of service tents established to provide support. The head of the seminary system emphasized the importance of utilizing artificial intelligence in Islamic sciences, while the army's operations deputy confirmed the replacement of air defense systems damaged during the recent twelve-day defense. The Minister of Education announced initiatives to improve public school quality and train teachers in modern pedagogical methods. In the religious rulings segment, a cleric stressed the necessity of maintaining scientific and respectful discourse in mourning gatherings, avoiding insults to other sects. Finally, a fatwa confirmed that it is permissible to include shipping costs in the product price for online sales, provided the customer is informed and agrees to the total amount.
