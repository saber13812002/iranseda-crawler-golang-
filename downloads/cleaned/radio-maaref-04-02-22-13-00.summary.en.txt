In the continuation of the "Green Blue" narrative, Seyyed Nassr reflects on the taste of a simple meal provided by a young girl, realizing that survival is paramount. The program then transitions to a theological discussion distinguishing between "grounded hope" and "baseless wishful thinking." True hope requires faith, righteous deeds, and good morals, whereas empty desires are compared to mirages in the desert, being hollow and fruitless. The Prophet (PBUH) likened the human heart to a pasture for wild animals such as envy, greed, and arrogance, warning that these traits expel one from spiritual paradise. To achieve genuine hope in God's mercy, one must avoid excessive talk and heart sins, relying instead on reason and the Quran to pursue perfection. Ayatollah Asse Jamal al-Din Golpayegani is presented as an exemplar of this grounded hope, having migrated and struggled for God with complete certainty in divine reward. Ultimately, Quranic texts confirm that only those who believe, migrate, and strive in the path of God are worthy of hoping for His vast mercy.
