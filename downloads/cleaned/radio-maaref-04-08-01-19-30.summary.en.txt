The radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat" began with the recitation of a prayer for rain before transitioning to a discussion on the moral vice of judgment. Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallah defined judgment as a dangerous verbal disease where individuals violate others' privacy based on hearsay without permission. He explained that this behavior is unethical and stems from an attempt to hide one's own flaws while attacking the reputation of others. A central point of the discussion was judging God, as religious narrations state that whatever a person assumes about God will manifest in their life. Consequently, if a servant views God as generous and benevolent, they will experience goodness, whereas negative judgments lead to corresponding hardships. The speaker emphasized, citing Quranic verses and narrations from Imam Reza (AS), that God has created human will such that one's perception of Him directly shapes their reality. In conclusion, listeners were urged to be cautious of their opinions and analyses, encouraging them to cultivate a positive perception of God to build a life filled with satisfaction and blessings.
