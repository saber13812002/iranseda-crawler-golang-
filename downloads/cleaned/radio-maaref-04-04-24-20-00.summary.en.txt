The Mar'ef radio program reported on regional developments, highlighting the extensive destruction in the West Bank and the blockade of Gaza by the Israeli regime. Statements from the UN and Qatar indicated that peace negotiations remain in their early stages, while criticizing the regime's refusal to allow humanitarian aid into Gaza. Meanwhile, the Mauritanian government denied rumors of normalizing relations with Israel and reaffirmed its support for Palestinian rights. A significant portion of the broadcast featured the dramatic story of a Palestinian mother whose husband and several children are held in Israeli prisons. In diplomatic news, the foreign ministers of Iran and Pakistan emphasized solidarity and the importance of diplomacy to de-escalate regional tensions. Other reports covered the ban on loudspeaker azan in India and the growth of the global halal industry, noting a significant rise in non-Muslim Germans consuming halal food. The program concluded with an explanation of electricity consumption models based on climate and season, followed by memories of telecommunications operations during the Iran-Iraq war.
