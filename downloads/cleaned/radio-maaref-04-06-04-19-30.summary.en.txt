The radio program began by addressing the Islamic tax of Khums, noting that according to Imam Khomeini, it becomes obligatory if money is held for over a year, though permission from a Marja's office allows for purchasing necessary assets like a car before payment. Other legal questions were answered, including the requirement for full hijab in the presence of non-mahram men and the ruling that a husband kissing his wife during a revocable divorce constitutes a return to the marriage. The majority of the broadcast was dedicated to explaining twenty-three punishments for lying, which include having a blackened face on Judgment Day, reduced sustenance, and the loss of divine guidance. Furthermore, lying leads to a loss of public trust, the departure of angels, and divine anger, while also resulting in humiliation and exclusion from night prayers. It was emphasized that lying prevents the acceptance of good deeds and causes disgrace in this world and punishment in the hereafter. Finally, the speakers stressed that lying is strictly forbidden even with the intention of making others happy, as it violates the rights of truthfulness.
