In this program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verses from Surah An'am to explore the relationship between divine power and mercy in establishing the necessity of the afterlife. He argues that while God's power merely demonstrates the possibility of resurrection, His attribute of mercy makes the return to God a rational and emotional necessity, freeing humans from a sense of emptiness. The commentator distinguishes between "talisib" (deception) and "punitive talisib" (bearing the consequences of wrong choices), explaining that God establishes justice by letting humans face the results of their actions rather than misleading them. The discussion also addresses the descent of angels, noting that even if disbelievers saw them, their inherent rejection would prevent them from believing, thus God would not grant them further opportunity. Furthermore, the concept of divine authority (Wilayah) is presented as the core of worship, encompassing three approaches: longing for perfection, hope for blessings, and fear of punishment. He emphasizes that the Quran combines knowledge and action to guide humans toward a deeper understanding of God as the Creator, Provider, and Avenger. The ultimate conclusion is that a correct understanding of God's attributes removes doubts about the afterlife and directs humans toward spiritual perfection and hope in divine mercy.
