The program began by introducing the book "Neshan-e Hasan" before shifting focus to Imam Sajjad's (PBUH) spiritual role in rebuilding society after the tragedy of Karbala. The speaker emphasized that the Imam countered the prevailing materialism and spiritual decline by promoting the culture of prayer, supplication, and piety. He highlighted the Imam's long-term mourning for Imam Hussein and his educational efforts, such as freeing slaves and teaching them moral and spiritual values. The discussion also covered the Imam's Friday sermons, which urged the public to abandon worldly attachments and prepare for the afterlife. The core message is that keeping the spirit of Ashura alive requires a return to spirituality, ethics, and a deep connection with God. The segment concluded with prayers for the reappearance of Imam Mahdi (AJ) and a call for the audience to maintain their spiritual integrity against material challenges.
