This radio program, titled "Tales of Freedom," explores the narratives of captivity and resistance during the Iran-Iraq War. The first segment features the wife of Martyr Ayatollah Shah-Abadi, who recounts the harsh conditions of imprisonment and the brutal treatment by Pahlavi regime officials. Qasem Mohibi, a former prisoner of war, then describes the rescue operation in the Panjwin area and his subsequent capture during an enemy airstrike. He shares the painful memory of fellow prisoners dying in the Mosul camp due to depression and the severe hardships of captivity. The program also introduces the book "In the Dust of the Tomb" by Gholamali Babaei, which chronicles the life of Ahmad Mousavian through new interviews and photographs. This work offers a fresh perspective on one of the prominent commanders of the Holy Defense. The program concludes by emphasizing the importance of self-discovery, hope, and the younger generation's appreciation for the gift of freedom.
