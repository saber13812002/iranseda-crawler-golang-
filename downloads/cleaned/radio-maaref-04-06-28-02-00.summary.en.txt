This radio program explores the concept of "high ambition" as the key to overcoming life's hardships and poverty. By citing narrations from Imam Ali and real-life stories of scholars like Ayatollah Mirjawadi and Sheikh Ali Yazdi, it demonstrates that they endured severe hunger and difficulties to pursue their scientific goals. The resilience of Ayatollah Khomeini's teacher, Ayatollah Khoei, in the face of his son's death and his insatiable thirst for knowledge, is highlighted as an example of absolute dedication to duty. The second part of the program examines the evidence for the occultation of prophets, recounting the story of Prophet Idris's occultation. In this narrative, a tyrannical king resorted to false testimony and the execution of innocents to seize land, which provoked divine wrath and forced Prophet Idris to flee the city. God promised Prophet Idris that He would take revenge on the oppressor by stopping the rain and destroying the city. This story shares deep similarities with the occultation of Imam Mahdi (AJ) and illustrates divine justice against tyranny.
