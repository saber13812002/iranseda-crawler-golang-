In the first segment, Professor Mohammad Hadi Falaki outlined practical strategies for curing arrogance, including abandoning arrogant behavior in walking and speech, avoiding association with arrogant individuals, and cultivating humility by focusing on correcting one's internal flaws. He emphasized that arrogance is itself a calamity and a cause of divine punishment, urging listeners to seek refuge in God from it. In the second segment, Seyyed Mostafa Khoshcheshm analyzed Iran's diplomatic situation, asserting that recent interactions with the West are not negotiations for a deal but part of a hybrid war involving cognitive, cyber, and political pressures. He characterized the IAEA report as a tool serving Israeli interests and argued that Iran is advancing in the diplomatic battle by maintaining a firm stance on its rights. Khoshcheshm warned that military action is only likely if the adversary's assessment of Iran's potential response to attacks on nuclear facilities changes.
