In the radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat," Hujjat al-Islam Falaki discusses the virtue of "infak" (charity) based on the fourth day of Muharram, emphasizing that the highest form of charity is dedicating oneself to God and the Imam. He argues that while lower existential levels like minerals, plants, and animals exist to serve humanity, humans must ultimately dedicate themselves to divine servitude and obedience to the Imam. In the second segment, Dr. Ali Lali-Jani claims that recent negotiations were merely a pretext for a coordinated military operation by the US and Israel. Citing statements from American and Israeli officials, he asserts that Mossad sent threatening messages to Iranian officials to incite internal chaos and regime change. Lali-Jani believes the enemy's strategy relied on killing military leaders to create a power vacuum and trigger a popular uprising against the system. However, he concludes that this miscalculation failed to achieve its intended goal of destabilizing Iran.
