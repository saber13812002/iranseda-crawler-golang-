In this radio program, the concepts of "Ebr-Mother" and "Ebr-Father" were explained as capable parenting models for the modern era. Professor Tofiqi emphasized the importance of a mother's physical health, noting that proper nutrition and avoiding physical exhaustion directly impact her temperament and the quality of child-rearing. The father's role in supporting his wife's physical and mental well-being to establish a strong foundation for upbringing was also discussed. In the Q&A section, strategies to reduce children's separation anxiety were offered, such as explaining delays through storytelling and reinforcing the child's sense of security. To decrease a child's excessive dependence on the mother, it was recommended that the father take a more active role in daily interactions and allow the child to play with peers. Finally, regarding encouraging teenagers to pray, the influence of healthy peers was deemed more significant than that of parents, and creating a space for religious dialogue within the family was suggested.
