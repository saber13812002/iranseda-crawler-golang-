The radio program began by emphasizing that God values the quality of actions over their quantity and that the human heart should be dedicated solely to Him.
A listener's question about forgiving an oppressor was addressed by recommending the recitation of Istighfar while still asserting one's rights.
The concept of the "seven earths" in the Quran was interpreted as referring to the earth's layers or its seven landmasses.
The transformation of the earth and sky on Judgment Day was explained as a change in nature, resulting in a flat earth for the gathering of souls.
The discussion highlighted the difference between perception in the afterlife and the world, cautioning against applying worldly rules to the hereafter.
The show continued with congratulations for the birth anniversaries of Prophet Muhammad and Imam Jafar Sadiq, accompanied by religious songs.
Finally, listeners were invited to send loving messages to participate in a special gift drawing for this occasion.
