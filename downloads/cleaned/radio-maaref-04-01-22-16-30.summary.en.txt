The first segment discusses the permissibility of holding court in mosques, citing the practice of Imam Ali in Kufa to distinguish between worship and administrative areas.
It then explores the migration of Ja'far ibn Abi Talib to Abyssinia, highlighting his role in spreading Islam and establishing the presence of Shia Islam in Africa.
Current statistics indicate that Shias remain a very small minority in Africa, emphasizing the urgent need for more extensive da'wah and the involvement of young scholars.
The second part analyzes a hadith from Imam Sajjad (AS) regarding his anxiety about the fitna of Ibn Zubayr and the divine response emphasizing reliance on God.
This narrative underscores that trust in God and confidence in His promises are the solutions to worldly and eschatological concerns.
The program concludes by introducing various Radio Ma'aref segments, including those dedicated to the visually impaired, and provides contact information for audience feedback.
