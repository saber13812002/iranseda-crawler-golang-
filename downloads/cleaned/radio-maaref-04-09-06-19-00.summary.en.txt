This radio program highlights the critical and exceptional role of religious scholars in preserving truth and avoiding courtly behavior to prevent harm to Islam. Citing the sermons of Hazrat Zahra (SA) and her prophecies, the discussion emphasizes the necessity of righteous leadership and the danger of deviating from the principles of faith. Imam Ali (AS) is presented as the ultimate model of integrity and management, who refused to compromise his dignity or allow corruption in power, even under extreme pressure. The concept of Taqiyyah is explained as a shield for believers and a worship more beloved to God than many other acts, focusing on preserving life and preventing the blame of the Imams. Referring to the book of Kafi, the speaker stresses the importance of observing Taqiyyah in critical situations to avoid endangering followers in various parts of the world. Finally, true freedom is defined as servitude to God and abandoning forbidden pleasures, concluding with prayers for the release of captives and hope for liberation.
