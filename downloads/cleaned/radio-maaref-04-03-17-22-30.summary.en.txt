In this radio program, Seyyed Hossein Hosseini Qoumi analyzed ten ethical and political traits of Imam Ali (AS) based on Nahj al-Balagha. He emphasized justice, military bravery, asceticism, and the mutual rights between rulers and citizens by citing various sermons. The speaker also referenced a narration from Ahmad Hanbal stating that Imam Ali's jihad in the Battle of the Trench is superior to the worship of jinn and humans until the Day of Judgment. The program highlighted the book "Alavi Governance" by the speaker and provided instructions for listeners to obtain it. Listeners were encouraged to participate in feeding the poor on Quds Day and to perform other charitable acts. Finally, the program transitioned to a Quranic Q&A session where Ustadh Maleki discussed the interpretation of Surah Al-Qalam.
