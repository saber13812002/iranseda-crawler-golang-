This radio program, featuring a lecture by Hojjat al-Islam Heydari Kashani, explores the role of Hazrat Zainab (SA) in natural jihad and enlightenment. The speaker emphasizes that Hazrat Zainab (SA) serves as the model for Husaini character, family, and society building, using her insight-filled sermons to provoke thought even among her enemies. He identifies the Arbaeen pilgrimage as a foundational movement for creating a monotheistic and Mahdavi society where arrogance is discarded. The lecture highlights recent spiritual awakenings, such as Western students supporting Palestine, as signs of the imminent appearance of Imam Mahdi (AJ). The speaker warns that the enemy now seeks to conquer minds, and the only defense is enlightenment jihad and emulating Zainab's conduct. Finally, through poetry and supplications, the importance of maintaining Husaini love and awaiting the Savior is reinforced.
