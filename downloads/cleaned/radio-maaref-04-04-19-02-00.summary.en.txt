This radio program highlights the Islamic principle that true leadership and nobility are defined by serving others and assisting the needy, citing narrations from Imam Sadiq, the Prophet, and Imam Ali.
It emphasizes that withholding charity from one's blessings distances a person from God's mercy, while a community leader must be a dedicated servant to its people.
The narrative illustrates this concept through the life of Ayatollah Seyyed Mehdi Rohani, known as the "Father of the Poor," who kept his home open to the needy at all hours, even after heart surgery.
Despite his age and health issues, he firmly refused to limit his availability or cut off phone lines, believing that people's hardships do not follow a specific schedule.
The story recounts how he relied on God's providence to help the poor, often finding unexpected financial support to meet their needs and deriving greater joy from their happiness than his own.
His unwavering commitment to serving believers without condition exemplifies the spiritual concept of "nobility" through selfless service.
The program concludes by encouraging listeners to emulate this lifestyle of attentive care and assistance to others.
Finally, Radio Moharram is introduced as a companion for mourners during the month of Muharram, broadcasting eulogies and religious speeches.
