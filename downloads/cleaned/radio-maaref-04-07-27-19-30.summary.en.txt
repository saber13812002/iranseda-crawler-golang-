The radio program begins by explaining the direct correlation between one's sustenance and religious obligations, citing Imam Reza and Allameh Majlisi to show that greater wealth entails greater social and financial responsibilities. Following the recitation of a rain prayer from the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya, the discussion shifts to the social harms of excessive criticism and fault-finding, referred to as "ta'ir." The guest emphasizes that nitpicking and blaming others, often driven by envy or a desire to show off, undermines collective self-esteem and disrupts social cooperation. He warns, based on hadiths from the Prophet and Imam Sadiq, that anyone who harps on another person's repented sins will inevitably commit the same sin themselves unless they seek forgiveness. The program concludes by stressing the importance of respecting believers and avoiding baseless accusations to maintain both individual and social well-being.
