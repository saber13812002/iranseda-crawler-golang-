In this interview with Ali Reza Haghjati, a prominent master of the Zourkhaneh, the importance of preserving the authenticity of Zourkhaneh music was emphasized. Haghjati explained that the audience seeks the specific historical atmosphere of this art, and any deviation from traditional principles diminishes its appeal. He detailed the administrative structure of the Zourkhaneh Federation and the role of the Masters' Association in organizing and training masters across the country. The guest also highlighted financial challenges in education and the need for greater support for students. By sharing memories of his role models, such as the late Moradi, he stressed the necessity of having goals and mentors in an artistic path. Finally, Haghjati mentioned that his sons are continuing their activities in the field of Zourkhaneh and expressed his deep love for this art and the people of Yazd.
