In this radio program, Professor Jabbari first clarified that the exact date of Lady Masoumeh's (SA) passing is not specified in reliable early sources like the History of Qom, nor is there definitive evidence of her martyrdom, although Abbasid pressures may have contributed to her death. He emphasized that using the term "anniversary" is historically more accurate than assigning specific dates for her birth and death. The second part of the program introduced books analyzing the Prophet's (PBUH) practical conduct in personal, social, and managerial aspects. The expert also addressed questions regarding the absence of the Prophet's (PBUH) handwritten documents, the location of the first Friday prayer in Medina, and the Mihna of the Quran during the Abbasid caliphate of al-Ma'mun.
