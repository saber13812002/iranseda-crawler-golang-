In this radio program, experts emphasized that foreign sanctions have served as a catalyst for Iran's self-sufficiency and significant advancements in its military and nuclear industries.
The strategic management by the Supreme Leader and regional presence have been pivotal in converting threats into opportunities, enhancing the country's defensive and missile capabilities.
Iran has transformed from a nation entirely dependent on foreign equipment into an exporter of advanced military technologies, such as drones, which hold distinct economic and technical competitive advantages.
In the second segment, Hujjat al-Islam Qomi discussed civic rights and the importance of avoiding moral harassment in both daily life and the digital space.
Citing religious narrations, he identified harming others as a grave sin that leads to divine distance, noting that even religious devotion should not come at the cost of family well-being.
Experts advised citizens to exercise caution when sharing information on social media and to respect neighbors' rights to prevent social and moral disturbances.
Ultimately, the discussion highlighted that fostering a culture of reduced social harm requires media cooperation and public awareness regarding civic duties and rights.
