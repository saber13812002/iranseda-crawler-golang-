The radio program "I am Fatima" emphasizes the practical love for the Ahl al-Bayt based on the Verse of Affection in Surah Ash-Shura. Hojjat al-Islam Hosseini Bagherabadi explains that affection extends beyond mere emotional love and must be reflected in the believer's actions and speech. He cites narrations stating that love for Fatima Zahra (SA) is the key to salvation and intercession in Paradise on the Day of Judgment. The program also discusses Fatima's lifestyle as a model for spousal obedience and domestic harmony within Islamic boundaries. Martyr Motahhari highlights the wisdom behind the Prophet's behavior in demonstrating Fatima's unique status compared to other prophets. The show critiques the early Muslims' failure to honor Fatima, noting the secrecy surrounding her burial due to the injustices she suffered. Finally, the second segment concludes with a mystical interpretation of "Allahu Akbar" and the concept of divine unity by Martyr Motahhari.
