This program features a tafsir of verses 15 to 18 of Surah Al-An'am by Ayatollah Javadi Amoli, emphasizing the oneness of God's essence and actions. The commentator outlines three primary proofs for monotheism: the contingency of existence, the removal of harm, and the fulfillment of human desires. The Prophet (PBUH) is described as the perfect synthesis of spiritual intuition, rational argument, and Quranic revelation, with the understanding that Quranic addresses to him are universally applicable to all humans. The concept of "removing punishment" is interpreted as a sign of divine mercy, indicating that any avoidance of sin or suffering stems from God's will and wisdom. God's attribute of "Al-Qahhar" (The Subduer) signifies the inescapable nature of His divine light and absolute power, rather than coercion or injustice. The term "above" in these verses is interpreted as a superiority of rank and knowledge rather than physical location, as God is transcendent from bodily attributes. Finally, the combination of the attributes "One" and "Subduer" in the Quran establishes the concept of "Ahadiyyah," representing absolute uniqueness and the absence of any counterpart to God.
