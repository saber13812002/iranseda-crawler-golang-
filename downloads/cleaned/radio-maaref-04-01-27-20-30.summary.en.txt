The radio program begins with a critical analysis of Feng Shui, which religious experts describe as superstitious, illogical, and contradictory to Islamic teachings.
Individuals who consult Feng Shui practitioners often incur significant financial and time costs but ultimately feel disappointed and demotivated due to a lack of results.
Hojjat al-Islam Mazaheri Saif emphasized that life success is based on reason, prudence, and human will, labeling belief in environmental energies as a form of modern idolatry.
The second part of the program featured the memoirs of Iranian POWs regarding their escape attempts from Iraqi camps, which were filled with tension and the risk of death.
The prisoners attempted to evade capture with intelligence and courage but were eventually identified and arrested by Iraqi police.
This bitter experience demonstrated that escaping captivity could lead to re-arrest and even harsher conditions.
Ultimately, the program's main message highlighted the importance of hope, perseverance, and returning to religious values to find peace and success in life.
