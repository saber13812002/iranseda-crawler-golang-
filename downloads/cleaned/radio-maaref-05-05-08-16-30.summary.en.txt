In this radio program, the expert emphasized the government's need to support hijab clothing manufacturers and establish social work teams to promote hijab respectfully. He also called for a strict governmental crackdown on organized movements advocating for the removal of hijab, viewing them as security and cultural threats. The role of successful hijab-wearing women as role models and the importance of respectful family dialogue in resolving doubts and ensuring women's psychological security were highlighted. The discussion then shifted to the necessity of retribution for the assassination of the Supreme Leader, justified by both reason and Islamic law. The expert stressed the importance of supporting judicial punishments against criminals and taking firm action against celebrities who defend perpetrators. He argued that supporting crime leads to societal instability and despair, requiring legal and financial restrictions. Finally, it was emphasized that social media must not be left unregulated, and officials must manage this space to prevent social harm.
