In this program, Dr. Laksayi outlined the Islamic-Iranian Development Model, identifying economic growth, cultural excellence, and political stability as the three pillars of civilization building. He emphasized that while Iran possesses rich natural resources, human capital, and a robust health network, the primary challenge lies in weak governance and the lack of integrated management. Dr. Laksayi stressed the need to reform economic and scientific governance to prevent brain drain and optimize the use of national assets. The second part of the program addressed the issue of illegal abortion facilitated by the internet. Medical and psychological experts detailed the severe physical and mental health risks associated with the self-administration of abortion drugs. They highlighted the urgent need for public awareness and strict control over the illegal sale of these medications in non-medical centers and online platforms to protect maternal health.
