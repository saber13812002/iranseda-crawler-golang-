This radio discussion analyzes the relationship between infallibility and free will from a philosophical and mystical perspective. The speaker argues that infallibility does not conflict with free will but rather reinforces it. The root of every voluntary action lies in knowledge and will, while sins stem from weakness in either of these factors. An infallible person is immune to such weaknesses due to possessing complete knowledge and a strong will. Therefore, infallibility does not mean the removal of free will but represents its peak and consolidation. Human power becomes a sign of complete spiritual and human freedom when accompanied by knowledge and will. Ultimately, the speaker emphasizes that infallibility is a sign of the strength of free will, not its negation.
