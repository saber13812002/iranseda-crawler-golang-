The radio program "Husainiyeh in the Heart" focused on Islamic world news and Muharram mourning ceremonies. Reports indicate that Israel's military casualties and financial losses in the twelve-day war with Iran were significantly higher than officially acknowledged. Western analysts assert that US strikes failed to destroy Iran's nuclear program and that Washington's isolation policy has backfired. Israeli media reported the failure of military operations in Gaza and rising domestic protests against Netanyahu, which escalated into attacks on security bases. Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi issued a fatwa declaring Trump and Netanyahu enemies of God and deserving of death. Meanwhile, Muharram mourning rituals were held worldwide, including in New York and Kashmir, with widespread public support for the resistance axis. The broadcast concluded by introducing the local "Sasang" mourning style in Behbahan as a significant cultural heritage of the month.
