The radio program "Parsaiyan" began with the recitation of the Dua-e-Tawassul and the performance of heroic poetry.
Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the religious duty of caring for fellow believers and assisting those in need.
He recounted a story about Ayatollah Tabrizi, highlighting his special attention to the homeless and his secret acts of charity.
In the literary segment, a historical narrative about Fatima bint Musa and her anxieties before Imam Hussein's journey was presented.
Hasan and Amir discussed the opposition of Muhammad Hanafiya to the trip to Kufa and the necessity of supporting the Imam.
In the Q&A section, the Supreme Leader's fatwa regarding the obligation to follow a previous religious opinion on Ashura was explained.
The program concluded with the recitation of Ashura poetry and tributes to the martyrs.
