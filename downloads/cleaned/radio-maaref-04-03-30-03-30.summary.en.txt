The program began with the recitation of the Dua al-Faraj and mourning poetry before shifting to a political analysis.
The speaker highlighted the Iranian people's resilience and rationality in the face of enemy threats.
He emphasized the importance of media accurately conveying the truth to prevent enemy distortion.
In the jurisprudential segment, Hujjat al-Islam Vahidpour discussed the rulings on forensic medical examinations.
He stated that examination by a non-mahram is permissible when necessary for legal purposes, such as determining blood money.
The program then addressed the ruling of Ayatollah Khamenei regarding surrogacy.
According to this fatwa, surrogacy is permissible provided that all religious boundaries, such as avoiding physical contact, are strictly observed.
It was clarified that the child belongs to the genetic parents, and the surrogate mother does not become a mahram to the child.
