This radio program explored the nature of the human divine spirit, highlighting its infinite capacity to ascend to angelic levels or descend below the animal state.
The hosts addressed listener questions, refuting superstitious beliefs that physical signs like a darkened face or restlessness are definitive indicators of impending death.
Regarding religious rulings, it was clarified that being a "bearer of the Quran" implies internalizing its teachings rather than mere memorization, and the obligation to perform Hajj requires funding it through any necessary means, including selling personal assets.
A significant portion of the broadcast featured a historical documentary on the establishment of Ayatollah Golpayegani's hospital, which originated from the government's violent attack on the Fizeh school in 1906.
The hospital was created to provide a safe haven for students and the vulnerable, who distrusted government medical centers due to the hostile behavior of Pahlavi regime officials toward the injured.
With a forward-looking vision and the active support of his son, Ayatollah Golpayegani expanded this initial clinic into a fully equipped hospital serving the community.
During the bloody uprising of 1963, the hospital played a critical role in secretly treating and sheltering revolutionary casualties from government persecution.
Ultimately, this initiative demonstrated the comprehensive service orientation of religious leaders, addressing societal needs and anticipating future requirements alongside their scholarly duties.
