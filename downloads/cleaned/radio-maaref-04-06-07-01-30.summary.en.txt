In this radio program, citing Imam Ali's sermons, it is emphasized that post-revolutionary deviations stem not from theoretical ignorance, but from deliberate betrayal and neglect of practical values. Relying on the interpretation of "vomiting" (rejecting what was consumed), the speaker warns that if society forgets the values of sacrifice and jihad, no lectures or classes can save it. The principle of "people's judgment over rulers" is also highlighted, noting that while people have the right to condemn unjust rulers, they must not remain indifferent to justice and its costs. In the second part, the difference between "secular worldliness" and "spirituality as an escape from responsibility" is explained, identifying both as ideological errors in the contemporary era. The speaker stresses that in moments of crisis, the sincere and the hypocritical are clearly distinguished, whereas in times of peace, hypocrisy remains hidden. Finally, the characteristics of the "Noble Government" are described by referencing the management of the Jihad Construction Organization and the necessity of distinguishing between principles and trivialities in policymaking.
