In this program, Ayatollah Saffaei Bushehr emphasizes the importance of "Zikr" (remembrance of God) as a means of spiritual elevation and protection from sin, based on Quranic teachings. He explains that devout individuals, by immersing themselves in divine remembrance, become the "remembered" themselves and are thus shielded from Satan's influence. The speaker distinguishes between verbal and heart-centered remembrance, stressing that the latter governs one's behavior and ethics. It is noted that God treats His beloved with gentleness and mercy, making this spiritual connection the most rewarding experience in life. Using the metaphor of the nightingale and the rose, the speaker illustrates that love for God must be accompanied by respect and etiquette. Finally, Muslims are encouraged to attain nearness to God through consistent remembrance and prayer, thereby benefiting from the blessings of this profound spiritual bond.
