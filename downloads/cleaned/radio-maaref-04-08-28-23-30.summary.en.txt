In this radio program, the host emphasized the significance of Fatimiyyah and the importance of gratitude for health, urging listeners to financially support a mother with three sick children by sending text messages to a specified number. The host shared the family's story, highlighting the children's specific illnesses, and stressed the importance of helping the needy and orphans in the name of Imam Reza and Lady Zahra. It was noted that many listeners had already expressed their willingness to provide monthly assistance by sending religious phrases like "Ya Ali" or "Ya Imam Reza." The host then recited the Ziyarat Al-Yasin, emphasizing the necessity of declaring faith and allegiance to the Ahl al-Bayt during critical life moments and against the deceptions of the end of life. The latter part of the program analyzed the Islamic Revolution's victory, identifying faith in God and the unity of various social classes as the primary forces that overcame major powers. The host warned that enemies are attempting to break this Islamic unity by sowing discord and promoting anti-brotherhood propaganda. Finally, it was concluded that maintaining brotherhood and cohesion based on the Quran is the key to resisting foreign and internal conspiracies.
