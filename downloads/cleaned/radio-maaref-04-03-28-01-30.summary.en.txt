This radio program explores the attributes of just governance based on Nahj al-Balagha and the teachings of Imam Ali (AS). The Imam emphasizes the execution of justice and adherence to divine commands, citing Quranic verses to support his stance. Through the anecdote of his torn shoe, Imam Ali (AS) demonstrates that his primary goal is to uphold God's laws and eliminate oppression rather than seek personal power. He firmly rejects threats and suggestions to distribute public wealth unequally among the elite to secure his political position. The Imam labels the misappropriation of public funds as squandering and equates it with devilish behavior. He also highlights his strict sensitivity in returning stolen property to its rightful owners and his fear of divine accountability on the Day of Judgment. The episode concludes with the recitation of the Hadith of Ghadir and a preview of the next installment of the novel "Jamandeh."
