In this radio program, the expert argues that negotiations with the US are merely a display for the world, given America's inherent imperialist nature, while Iran maintains its operational readiness. He asserts that direct talks with an aggressor state without strong military leverage are a weakness, emphasizing that Iran's true progress relies on political, economic, cultural, and security independence rather than dependence on foreign powers. In response to comparisons with countries like Japan and South Korea, the speaker notes that these nations are subservient to US policy and have lost their sovereignty, whereas Iran resists external pressure through indigenous technology and innovation. The discussion also touches on Pakistan's role as a mediator and the hidden support provided by European nations to Israel. Economically, raising gasoline prices is criticized as an incorrect solution to budget deficits, with a strong emphasis on combating tax evasion and monitoring currency exports. Finally, the expert calls for strict judicial action against infiltrators and ceasefire advocates who could cause significant harm to the country during wartime.
