Ali Roozbehani, former captain of the Armed Forces national team, recounts his experiences at the Seoul Olympics and their runner-up finish. He expresses his frustration over not being selected for the main national football team despite his outstanding performance in military clubs. Roozbehani discusses his coaching tenure with the Ground Forces and their unsuccessful attempts to reach the top league. He also criticizes the structural weaknesses of military football and the lack of strong teams in the main leagues. In the final section, he highlights his collaboration with the Tehran Football Federation to develop grassroots football and school programs. Roozbehani believes that despite the available talent, military players do not receive adequate support for national team selection. The conversation blends sports memories, structural critiques, and future plans for grassroots football development.
