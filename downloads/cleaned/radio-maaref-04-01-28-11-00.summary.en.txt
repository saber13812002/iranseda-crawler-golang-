The radio program "Qom" covered several significant events, starting with preparations for the 100th anniversary of the Qom Seminary's inspection department and the commemoration of Ayatollah Haeiri Yazdi. The broadcast also featured a memorial for the Iranian Army's role in the Sacred Defense, including veteran testimonies and a reference to over 48,000 martyrs. Additionally, the first advisory council for women at the Hajj and Pilgrimage Organization was established to improve conditions for the growing number of female pilgrims. The First Vice President emphasized the importance of introducing the moral character and service of Martyr Raisi to the younger generation during a commemorative meeting. The Prayer Establishment's secretaries met to discuss utilizing innovations and artificial intelligence in their 2025 planning. The fourth "Best Answer" festival was announced to support activists in the field of answering religious doubts through research and artistic categories. Finally, the first international conference on saviorship in monotheistic religions began in Zahedan, hosted by the Sistan and Baluchestan province.
