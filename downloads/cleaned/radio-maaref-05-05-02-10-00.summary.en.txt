The radio program begins by distinguishing between the minor and major occultations of the Imam of the Age, highlighting the role of specific deputies in maintaining contact during the earlier period. As the Ashura season approaches, listeners are encouraged to send "Ya Mehdi" messages and pray for the Imam's early appearance and the opportunity to visit Karbala. The core discussion focuses on enemy awareness, refuting naive perspectives that advocate for friendship with adversaries and warning against the risks of deception during the occultation. The guest emphasizes that true dignity and salvation are achieved only through steadfast adherence to monotheism and resistance against the tyranny of enemies, as supported by Quranic verses and historical precedents. The importance of including God in strategic calculations and relying on divine promises in the face of military and political threats is also stressed. Additionally, excerpts from the book "Let Us Help Him" are read, outlining methods for reviving the Shia school of thought and supporting the Imam. The program concludes by urging believers to strengthen their faith, believe in the afterlife, and maintain hope for the promised appearance, while avoiding naivety and engaging in the jihad of clarification.
