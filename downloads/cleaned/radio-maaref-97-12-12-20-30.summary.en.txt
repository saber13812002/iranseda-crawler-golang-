The radio program "Pick of Guidance" explores the theological dialogue between Imam Sadiq (AS) and an Indian physician, focusing on the concepts of monotheism and divine knowledge. The Imam argues that human cognitive limitations and the ancient nature of the cosmos prove that astronomical laws cannot be derived solely from human experience. Instead, he asserts that such profound knowledge is a gift from the Creator, who alone understands the intricate movements of the stars. The program also redefines prayer as a spiritual act of confession and intimate communication with God, rather than merely a means to request material needs. Additionally, it highlights the scientific and practical legacy of Allameh Bala'i to emphasize the importance of knowledge in shaping a bright future. Ultimately, the broadcast aims to strengthen faith and deepen the audience's understanding of the divine and the created world.
