This radio program emphasizes the spiritual and intellectual status of Imam Hussein (PBUH), exploring his virtues through the Quran and narrations.
The most significant virtue of Imam Hussein is described as being the "Depository of Revelation," meaning all spiritual ranks mentioned in the Quran are realized in his heart.
Citing narrations, the speaker highlights the birth of Imam Hussein as accompanied by divine blessings and the Prophet's hand on his head as a sign of perfect intellect and special divine grace.
The discussion also references the passionate love of Shia followers for Imam Hussein, explaining the deep impact of Karbala on their identity and sense of belonging.
The program introduces a comprehensive and powerful prayer taught by the Prophet (PBUH) to his wives, focusing on seeking spiritual ranks and seeking refuge in God.
Finally, by reciting Ashura poetry and inviting listeners to participate in the Arbaeen pilgrimage, the emotional bond between the audience and the shrine of Karbala is strengthened.
The main goal of the program is to foster a sense of companionship and coordination among Shias for the Arbaeen pilgrimage and to express their love for Imam Hussein (PBUH).
