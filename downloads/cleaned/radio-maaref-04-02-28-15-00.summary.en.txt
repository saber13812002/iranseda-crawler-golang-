In this radio program, experts emphasized the vital role of education and health in achieving the annual slogan of "investment for production." They argued that investing in workforce training and skill enhancement is the key to increasing productivity and preventing technological unemployment. Improving the business environment by simplifying administrative processes and clarifying laws was identified as a crucial factor for attracting public capital toward production. Furthermore, establishing legal security for intellectual property and regulating the relationship between innovators and investors was deemed essential for commercializing inventions. The experts stressed the government's responsibility to support non-profit sectors like public education and healthcare to prevent social harm and protect investors from losses. Finally, utilizing global experiences and creating transparent contractual models to support startups and domestic innovations was presented as a key strategy for sustainable economic growth.
