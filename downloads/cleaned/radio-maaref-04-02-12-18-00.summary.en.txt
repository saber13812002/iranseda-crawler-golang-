In the first segment, Professor Fallah analyzed the reasons for divine anger based on Quranic verses, emphasizing that terrorizing believers, whether by sword or through verbal abuse like backbiting, constitutes murder. He warned that harming the honor of believers, who are considered the spiritual lineage of the Ahl al-Bayt, leads to severe consequences such as God's wrath and hellfire, citing Surah An-Nisa and the Ziyara of Imam Abbas. In the second part, Professor Tabbatabai explained the rules of Ihram for Umrah al-Tamattu, highlighting the mandatory wearing of two unstitched garments and the recitation of Talbiyah. He also discussed the prohibitions of Ihram and the conditions under which some of them become permissible after the Sa'i between Safa and Marwa. The final section addressed religious queries, including the permissibility of spousal separation due to work with consent, the thresholds for sheep zakat, and the prohibition of using a mother-in-law's property without her permission.
