Shaklin visits Master Fakhr with anxiety to report the sudden disappearance of Farid al-Din. She confesses that her father, Davud, has seen their love letters and deliberately left them on the table to reveal his knowledge. Upon hearing this, Master Fakhr is shocked and falls to the ground, his concern for Farid al-Din's fate intensifying. Shaklin begs the master for help to save Farid al-Din, as she is aware of her father's hidden intentions. The story highlights how family obstacles and fear of the father have created a crisis trapping the two lovers. The narrative ends on a cliffhanger, promising the continuation of the story in the next episode.
