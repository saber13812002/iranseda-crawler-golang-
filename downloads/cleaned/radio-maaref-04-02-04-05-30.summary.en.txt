The radio program began by commemorating the martyrdom of Imam Jafar Sadiq (PBUH) with recitations and condolences for the listeners.
The main segment featured a detailed Quranic exegesis by Ayatollah Saffei Bushehri regarding the various classes of angels.
He categorized angels into two primary groups: those in the unseen realm and those in the visible world, highlighting their role in conveying revelation.
The speaker elaborated on the specific duties of angels in managing natural phenomena such as rain, thunder, lightning, and wind distribution.
The presence of angels in the afterlife, including Munkar, Nakir, and the interrogating angel, was also discussed in detail.
The roles of angels in Paradise and Hell, such as Ridwan, Sadan, Malik, and the treasurers, were further explained.
The speaker emphasized the absolute obedience of angels to God's commands and their continuous worship in the Bait al-Ma'mur.
The program concluded with blessings upon all angels and prayers for Hazrat Fatima Zahra (SA) and the Holy Family.
