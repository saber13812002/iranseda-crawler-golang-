In the radio program "Persaman Enghelab," Dr. Mehdi Abutalibi analyzed the factors behind the increased voter turnout in recent Iraqi elections. He emphasized that the fall of the Saddam regime, lessons learned from the 2019 protests, and Iran's victory in the 12-day war boosted the spirit of resistance and confidence among the Iraqi people. The election results, marked by a significant victory for Iran-aligned factions, indicate a strategic defeat for the United States in the region. In the second part, the analyst addressed the Reformist faction's criticism of President Pezeshkian, arguing that their call to "move past Pezeshkian" is an attempt to escape economic responsibilities and return to an opposition role. He also attributed the push for negotiations with the US to self-defeatism and a lack of trust in domestic power and divine support. Finally, regarding the hijab debate, he advocated for avoiding a black-and-white view of laws and urged the gradual implementation of regulations in official and university spaces, supported by grassroots cultural activities.
