In this radio broadcast, following the recitation of the rain prayer and condolences for the Fatimiyyah, Professor Mohammadi emphasized the importance of "pure worship" and knowing God as the best action during these days. He clarified that the order of optional and obligatory prayers is based on religious obligation rather than a specific logical deduction. The program also addressed the current existence of Paradise and Hell, explaining that spiritually aware individuals can perceive these realms, as supported by Quranic verses and traditions. A blind mother's concern about invoking the Ahl al-Bayt instead of God directly was dismissed as unfounded, citing the Quranic principle of seeking intercession through God's chosen ones. Finally, while acknowledging that sorcery is a real major sin in Islam, the professor cautioned against attributing all personal problems to magic, noting that in many cases, the root cause lies in the individual's own decisions and actions.
