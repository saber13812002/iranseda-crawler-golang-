The program begins by highlighting the fundamental difference between humans and animals, emphasizing that true human dignity is achieved through divine obedience and cultivating moral virtues like mercy and forgiveness.
It then recounts the story of Harun al-Rashid and Imam Musa al-Kazim (AS) to illustrate that genuine peace stems from devotion and trust in God, rather than wealth or power.
The discussion subsequently shifts to legal and judicial foundations, asserting that Islamic rulings are rooted in the teachings of the prophets rather than mere juristic interpretations.
The responsibilities of both the public and the government regarding the payment of debts from zakat funds, along with the proper administration of courts, are also examined.
Finally, the Quranic concept of "weighing" is interpreted as the scale of justice rather than a physical balance, supported by references to Surah Maryam and narrations from Imam Sadiq (AS).
This interpretation is further reinforced by discussions on divine justice and the importance of spiritual connection with God.
The overall conclusion underscores the necessity of linking good ethics with social justice and relying on authentic religious sources to resolve human issues.
