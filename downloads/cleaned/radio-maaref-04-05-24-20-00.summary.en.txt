Iraq's High Security Committee announced the successful execution of security measures for the Arbaeen pilgrimage, noting that the massive gathering occurred without any significant security breaches. Hezbollah Secretary-General Hassan Nasrallah, speaking in Karbala, described the resistance as the continuation of Imam Hussein's path and vowed to confront Israeli and American projects. The Arbaeen commemoration was also held in Pakistan and India, where large-scale processions symbolized religious solidarity and the transmission of Ashura's culture to future generations. Concurrently, while the Israeli Prime Minister touted the regime's water management capabilities, reports highlighted a severe water crisis and unprecedented drought in the occupied territories. An international scientific conference titled "Arbaeen and the Scientific Empowerment of Muslims" was held at Imam Hussein's shrine to address challenges in the Islamic world through knowledge exchange. In Sweden, a violent incident near a mosque was reported, heightening security concerns in Europe. These reports, alongside Arbaeen condolences, emphasized the unity of the resistance front and the people's enduring will against oppression.
