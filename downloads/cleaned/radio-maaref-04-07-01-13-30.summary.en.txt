In this program, Professor Faghihi emphasized the importance of prayer as a spiritual and educational tool, recommending that prayers be universal rather than personal to foster altruism and divine knowledge. In response to a question about finding joy and avoiding depression, he advised following God's path, maintaining family ties, and avoiding sin, noting that disobedience leads to spiritual death while worship brings true happiness. For a religious woman struggling to find a suitable spouse, he suggested avoiding arrogance about her virtues and increasing her reliance on God through supplication to Lady Zahra (SA), Imam Ali (AS), and reciting Surah Yasin. He also stressed the necessity of social etiquette, tolerance, and compromise in marital relationships to ensure a peaceful shared life. Furthermore, he urged intellectuals and university students to engage in "scientific jihad" by creating a new scientific surge and establishing knowledge-based companies to overcome national lag. He believes that scientific and technological superiority is not only a national pride but also a strong deterrent against enemy threats and a factor for regional stability.
