The Iranian men's junior volleyball team advanced to the semifinals of the World Championship by defeating the host team, China, 3-1 in the quarterfinals.
Coach Momeni Moghaddam secured the victory through smart tactical changes, effectively utilizing players like Momeni and Bavandnia to maintain Iran's dominance.
Iran's fast players executed cohesive defensive and offensive systems, forcing errors from the Chinese team and preventing them from regaining control of the game.
This marks Iran's seventh win in the tournament, highlighting the team's strong standing in the global junior rankings.
In a post-match interview, Momeni Moghaddam expressed satisfaction with his players' performance and the team's readiness for the upcoming semifinal against the USA.
He noted that while the American team has had an impressive run this year, Iran's international experience provides a significant advantage in the match.
The coach also emphasized the importance of developing young players with the potential to join the senior national team, citing the continuous talent production process within clubs and the federation.
