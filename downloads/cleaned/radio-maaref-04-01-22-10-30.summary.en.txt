In the radio program "Until Friday of the Appearance," experts in Mahdism discussed various aspects of waiting for the Imam and his appearance. Dr. Jafari, citing the narration of Abu Hamza Sammani, emphasized that no specific time for the appearance is mentioned in the narrations of the Ahl al-Bayt, and that past time-based promises were unfulfilled due to the mistakes of the Shia. He also identified ignorance, impatience, and distance from scholars as key factors contributing to the harm of setting a definite time for the appearance. In another segment, Hojjat al-Islam Kafil interpreted Quranic verses to stress the necessity of self-purification, patience, and piety as prerequisites for the appearance and ultimate salvation. He noted that a strong connection to Wilayah and vigilance over beliefs protect individuals from evil and lead to salvation. In the book introduction section, Hojjat al-Islam Mahmoudi introduced "In His Air," a book exploring ways to attain the love and affection of the Imam of the Time. He highlighted the obstacles and signs of loving the Imam, emphasizing that a heart alive with love is essential for preparing the ground for the appearance. Finally, the host concluded by clarifying that rejecting a fixed time does not imply passivity, urging waiters to remain vigilant and actively prepare for the appearance with insight and continuous effort.
