The program begins with a teaser from the novel "Neshan-e Hasan," featuring characters discussing a significant and worrying event. The main segment follows with poetry recitations and remarks by Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi, who emphasizes the critical importance of safeguarding public funds and avoiding administrative waste. Citing the teachings of Imam Ali (AS), he advises officials to be concise in their correspondence and strictly refrain from misusing communal resources. The discussion then highlights the extreme sensitivity of Imam Khomeini (RA) regarding the proper use of religious taxes and public money. Imam Khomeini even refrained from pilgrimage trips to Samarra and Kazimayn to ensure that no incidental costs were deducted from public funds for his companions. He only traveled to Karbala when free accommodation was available and personally covered his own expenses. This strict approach serves as a model for officials, demonstrating the necessity of integrity and the avoidance of any misuse of public rights.
