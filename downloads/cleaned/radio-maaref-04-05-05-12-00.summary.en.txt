In this radio program, Hojjat-ol-Islam Jaban offers strategies to revitalize congregational prayer in schools. He emphasizes selecting an attractive and approachable imam who understands the psychology of modern teenagers to bridge the psychological gap with students. Key attributes include beautiful recitation, avoiding lengthy prayers, maintaining punctuality, and paying attention to personal grooming. The speaker also suggests involving students by inviting them to share short, meaningful insights to boost their participation. Following this, a short story by Master Akbar Bana is broadcast, illustrating that adhering to prayer is more valuable than material gain. The narrative highlights that maintaining a spiritual connection with God outweighs the pursuit of a few coins. The program concludes by reinforcing the importance of full presence in prayer as a means to cleanse sins and draw closer to the Divine.
