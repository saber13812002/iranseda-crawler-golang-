In the radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat," Hojjat al-Islam Fallah discussed the vice of arrogance and its underlying causes. He described arrogance as the root of many evils and a major trap set by Satan, stemming primarily from ignorance and lack of awareness. This ignorance includes a failure to recognize the origin and destination of life, ignoring God's perspective, and disregarding the severe consequences of pride. Imam Ali (AS) characterized arrogance as a deadly poison that hinders self-improvement and makes humans resemble Satan. Identifying these factors is essential for treating this moral disease and avoiding it with full awareness. In the second segment, Hojjat al-Islam Irandoost interpreted a wisdom from Saadi's "Golestan," emphasizing the importance of action over words. He explained that a wise person is like a box whose scent of attar is perceptible, whereas a fool is like a gourd that makes noise but contains nothing. He also warned that unwarranted praise can inflate the ego of the praised and mislead the listener. Therefore, a person's true worth is determined by their actions and behavior, not by baseless compliments from others.
