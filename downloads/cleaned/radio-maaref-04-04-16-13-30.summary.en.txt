In this program, Professor Seyed Ahmad Faqeehi emphasized the importance of the sacred prayer of Salawat and its educational impacts. He explained that reciting Salawat fosters submission to the Prophet's commands and directs the soul's attention toward spiritual matters. The discussion also addressed the distinction between God's rights and human rights, identifying backbiting and injustice as major sins that disrupt the social bond among believers. Although God does not easily forgive human rights, His power to compensate and forgive on the Day of Judgment is limitless. In the final section, the necessity of recognizing the fronts of truth and falsehood in the contemporary era was highlighted. The ultimate goal of life is servitude to God and following the Quran, rather than pursuing status or wealth.
