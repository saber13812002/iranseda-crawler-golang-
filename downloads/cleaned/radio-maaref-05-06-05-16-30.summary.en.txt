The unusual closure of the Parliament was also addressed, with a call for its public sessions to resume to ensure economic oversight and accountability to the people's demands. Economically, cash subsidy distribution plans were criticized as ineffective and harmful, as they could trigger inflation and create parallel markets for items like gasoline and dollars.
