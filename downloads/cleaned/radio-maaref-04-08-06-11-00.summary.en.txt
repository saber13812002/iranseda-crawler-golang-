The head of the Hajj and Pilgrimage Organization announced that 40,000 people have pre-registered for the 1405 Hajj, with registration for deposit holders continuing until November 5. Additionally, 47,000 pilgrims have been dispatched for Umrah since early September, with the average trip cost estimated at 63 million Tomans. A ceremony was held to unveil the Special Shahid Salami Award and the "Hafiz Sho" initiative, aiming to promote Quranic culture and train memorizers. The 48th National Quran Competition, themed "The Quran is the Book of Unity," concluded in Kurdistan province, where top performers in various categories were recognized. The body of Ahmad Sidi Nijat, father of two martyrs, was buried in Mashhad, while the month of Aban was highlighted as a reminder of 72 years of US hostility against Iran. The program's jurisprudential segment addressed questions regarding the Khums on educational stipends, the validity of prayers with different surahs, and prayer rules during travel.
