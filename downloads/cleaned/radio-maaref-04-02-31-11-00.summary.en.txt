The radio program "Ma'aref" began by commemorating the martyrdom of Imam Reza (PBUH) and hosting the 8th International Congress on Quranic Thoughts, focusing on the role of women and family in reducing social harms. The news segment marked the first anniversary of the martyrdom of President Raisi and other service martyrs, with heads of state emphasizing their moral character and dedication to public service. Reports also highlighted the issuance of official land titles for thousands of shrines and the progress of cadastral surveys in holy sites. The head of the IRGC's Ideological Organization stressed the importance of enhancing Quranic literacy and establishing Quran schools within the force. In a separate ceremony, the "E'lam Tavater" award was presented to 20 distinguished scientists, with the President underscoring the critical role of elites in national progress. The broadcast concluded with Supreme Leader Khamenei's remarks, describing the late President Raisi as the perfect embodiment of the Quranic verse "they do not seek arrogance," and a model of sincerity, humility, and selfless service to the people.
