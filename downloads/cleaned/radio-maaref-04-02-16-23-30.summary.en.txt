The core message of this program is an invitation to "test God" through absolute trust, obedience, and sincerity. By comparing this trust to a child's reliance on their parents, the speaker emphasizes that God is even more merciful and capable than any earthly guardian. He cites Quranic narratives, such as the stories of Moses' mother and Abraham, to illustrate that the most challenging trials occur when one believes in divine promises despite fear. In the second part, sincerity in both words and actions is presented as the key to divine blessing, urging officials and citizens to be transparent about their mistakes. The speaker also highlights the importance of patience and resistance against enemies, as well as a steadfast commitment to justice. Finally, referencing Abraham's defiance of idolatry, the text concludes that standing up for justice and fighting falsehood is the most worthy path for believers, even if it involves hardship.
