In the first segment of the broadcast, the speaker harshly divides critics of the Islamic Republic into "devils" and "deceived individuals," accusing them of being influenced by the US and the former Soviet Union to undermine the government. He emphasizes the unprecedented services of the military and Basij, arguing that the current situation is superior to the Shah's era and that any criticism stems from ignorance or enmity. The speaker threatens to expose the identities of opponents, asserting the irreversibility of the Islamic system and the ultimate defeat of foreign enemies. The second part of the program is dedicated to answering religious questions from followers of various Maraji, covering topics such as puberty, missed prayers, travel rulings, Khums, and Wudu. The responses are based on the fatwas of scholars like Ayatollah Khamenei, Sistani, and Javadi Amoli, stressing the importance of adhering to precise jurisprudential rules in various life situations. Finally, listeners are encouraged to connect via phone and social media, with a specific request to send private questions through digital channels.
