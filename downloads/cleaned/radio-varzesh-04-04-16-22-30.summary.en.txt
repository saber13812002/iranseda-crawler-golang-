The discussion emphasized the need for financial support and equipment renewal to modernize zourkhaneh facilities and move them away from their outdated state.
The guest stressed the importance of nationwide attention to traditional wrestling, including remote areas like Sistan and Baluchestan, to ensure equal development.
He criticized the existence of two parallel international federations and past conflicts that caused stagnation, urging for unity and the resolution of internal disputes before entering the global arena.
The guest argued that internal cultural and sporting issues must be resolved domestically before promoting the sport internationally without flaws or controversy.
The conversation also covered the modernization of the "Morshed" training system, highlighting how it differs from traditional apprenticeship methods.
Drawing on personal experience, the guest described the spiritual and psychological impact of the zourkhaneh, noting its role in purifying the soul and alleviating life's problems.
He referred to the zourkhaneh as the "second mosque," emphasizing that entry requires ritual purity, respect, and spiritual awareness.
Finally, he expressed gratitude for the Ministry of Sports' support and expressed hope for a brighter future in fully reviving this cultural heritage.
