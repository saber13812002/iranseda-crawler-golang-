The news segment reported the arrest of Mossad spies in several Iranian cities and the Minister of Roads' inspection of Tehran's southern passenger terminal. The Road and Transportation Organization confirmed its readiness to transport returning pilgrims from Iraq, while the Minister of Justice assured the public of the availability of essential goods in Tehran markets. In Tehran's Palestine Square, citizens held a spontaneous rally chanting "Death to America" in response to President Trump's threats against Iran. In the religious segment, guest expert Saeidi Aria discussed how to convey the concepts of Wilayat and the Eid of Qadir to the younger generation. He emphasized using age-appropriate language to explain the Imam's role and the necessity of following his guidance. The discussion also highlighted the importance of religious rituals and creating a spiritual atmosphere at home to connect children's hearts to Wilayat. Finally, the program marked the anniversary of the Prophet's assassination and honored martyr Mism Tanamar as a supporter of the path of Wilayat.
