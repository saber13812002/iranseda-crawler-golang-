The program began with practical tips for conserving water and energy in the kitchen. Subsequently, Professor Mohammadi addressed listener questions on the "Persaman" radio show. He emphasized the qualitative difference between Paradise and the world, explaining that fatigue stems from monotony, whereas Paradise offers continuous spiritual evolution and fresh joys. To explain theological concepts to children, he suggested using simple analogies, such as comparing God's self-existence to the inherent saltiness of salt. Listeners were also advised to consult specialized religious channels for accurate answers to complex doctrinal questions. The discussion then covered the shape of prayer mats, recommending against unusual designs to prevent potential misuse by critics. Finally, the host addressed the problem of evil, arguing that perceived evils are either non-existent, relative, or integral parts of a divine system necessary for human perfection.
