The radio program begins by criticizing Western policies, arguing that their material progress is built upon colonialism, genocide, and human rights violations. It then details the history of Israel's establishment as a military occupation supported by Britain and the United States following the collapse of the Ottoman Empire. The speaker emphasizes that the duty of the "front of truth" is to educate and spiritually support the oppressed, rather than fighting wars on their behalf. The second part of the program focuses on the rights of people with disabilities and the sick, stressing the importance of preserving their human dignity and treating them with respect. Referring to the Prophet's (PBUH) visit to a sick Jewish man, the Islamic ethics of caring for the ill, even enemies, are highlighted. Finally, listeners share their views via phone calls, emphasizing empathy and natural, respectful behavior towards individuals with disabilities in society.
