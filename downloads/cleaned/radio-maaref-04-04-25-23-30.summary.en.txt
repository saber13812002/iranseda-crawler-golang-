During the radio program "Baran-e Mehrbani," the story of a single mother with three disabled children needing financial support for rent and living expenses was shared.
The host urged listeners to send text messages to specific numbers to help this family in the name of Imam Reza (PBUH).
The guest, Mr. Asad Al-Hassan, recited religious and Ashura-themed poetry to create a spiritual and emotional atmosphere for the audience.
Following the religious segments and the recitation of the Al-Yasin ziyarat, a speech by the Supreme Leader was broadcast.
In this speech, the necessity of paying special attention to the condition of hospitals and treating patients with respect was emphasized.
The Supreme Leader called for self-sufficiency in the fields of health and welfare, urging the nation to avoid dependence on foreign countries.
He also highlighted the moral responsibility of medical staff in dealing with the families of martyrs and suffering patients.
