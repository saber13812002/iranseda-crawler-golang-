In the radio program "Zamzam Ahkam," several religious questions were answered based on the rulings of different maraji'. Hujjat al-Islam Fallahzadeh, citing Ayatollah Khamenei, explained that requesting inheritance while the father is alive is meaningless, as inheritance only occurs after death. He added that a father can gift or bequeath assets during his lifetime, but the mother has no right to prevent such a gift. Hujjat al-Islam Mohammadi Sheikh Shabani, referencing Ayatollah Khomeini, stated that funds collected for general mosque expenses must be used for those purposes, unless the donors specifically intended them for an individual. Hujjat al-Islam Aliannejad, citing Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi, deemed organ donation from a living person permissible with consent and no life-threatening risk, and from a deceased person permissible if necessary to save a Muslim's life. Hujjat al-Islam Zin al-Abidini, based on Ayatollah Javadi Amoli, ruled that selling installment goods is permissible if legal, the buyer is fully informed, and they accept the installment conditions. Hujjat al-Islam Momeni, referencing Ayatollah Sistani, stated that a recreational trip reaching the sharia distance is considered a journey requiring shortened prayers, except for permanent travelers. Finally, Hujjat al-Islam Jawaheri, citing Ayatollah Shabiri Zanjani, declared a doctor excused and blameless if they amputate a limb to save a patient's life while adhering to medical standards.
