In this episode of the radio drama "Notebook of Memories," starring Simin Banai and other artists, the story continues. Saba, a journalist who was dismissed after publishing a sensitive story about sports drugs, discovers the diary of Arash, her late mother's neighbor. Reading the diary inspires Saba to investigate old wrestlers, specifically the martyr Mustafa Mirshaki, who was Arash's coach. Saba's mother, Maryam, is deeply worried about her daughter's reckless behavior and the anonymous threats Saba has been receiving. Arash's diary entries from 1365 recount his deployment to the front lines and his deep passion for wrestling. While Saba struggles with her past fears, Maryam refrains from interfering in her daughter's affairs but remains anxious about the potential consequences of Saba's investigation.
