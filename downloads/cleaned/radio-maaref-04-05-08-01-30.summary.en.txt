This radio program explores the upbringing of children through the educational model of Imam Hussein (PBUH), emphasizing the central role of love and a peaceful family environment. By noting that Imam Hussein named his sons Ali and daughters Fatima, the speaker highlights the importance of maintaining a spiritual connection with the Prophet's family. Key factors for proper upbringing, such as virtuous heredity, a clean family atmosphere, and monitoring social and virtual spaces, are identified as fundamental pillars. Historical anecdotes, including the Imam's deep affection for his daughters and the martyrdom of Ali Akbar at Karbala, serve as practical examples of this divine and courageous education. The program also references Lady Zainab's (SA) passionate sermon in Kufa and its impact on their young daughter Raghia, demonstrating how Husseini upbringing fosters insight and resilience in the harshest conditions. Finally, the show introduces the book "Let Asf Gallop," offering a literary perspective on the events in Kufa and the role of figures like Har Rihai during that historical period.
