In this radio program, topics regarding divine justice, martyrdom, and the concept of the human soul were discussed. The expert explained that God, knowing the fate of individuals, grants martyrs the reward of the good deeds they would have performed had they survived, with pure intention serving as a substitute for action. It was emphasized that minor sins, such as drinking alcohol, do not erase the reward of martyrdom, whereas shirk and doctrinal deviation do nullify these blessings. Regarding the concept of return (Raj'at), it was noted that returning believers come back to complete their spiritual perfection and aid divine promises, with the likelihood of them deviating again being extremely low. The phrase "spirit from God" was interpreted as a titular relationship indicating a potential divine capacity that allows humans to ascend to angelic status or descend to animalistic levels. In response to another question, the belief in physical signs like a black face indicating impending death was refuted, asserting that death is solely subject to God's will and material causes. Finally, it was clarified that the "Ijadas" mentioned in narrations are manifestations of Jinn capable of shape-shifting, rather than extinct physical creatures.
