In this radio program, the martyrdom of the previous leader is mourned, and the election of Ayatollah Seyed Mojtaba Khamenei as the third leader of the Islamic Republic is celebrated.
The program emphasizes the complete readiness of the Iranian people and armed forces to defend the country against its enemies.
Guest Ayatollah Mohammad Saber Jafari cites Quranic verses to argue for a firm stance against adversaries, specifically blaming the United States for the massacre of students in Minab.
He draws a parallel between the era of Imam Ali's martyrdom and the current situation, highlighting the unprecedented support of the Iranian people for their leadership as a sign of spiritual growth and preparation for the Imam's reappearance.
It is reported that the 30th wave of the "Sincere Promise 4" operation has begun with missile strikes on occupied territories.
The public is seen in the streets chanting religious and military slogans, demanding the destruction of Israel and the United States, which is viewed as a historic achievement.
The guest describes martyrdom as a stage of spiritual evolution and considers the leaders' sacrifice as an inspiration for the nation.
Finally, the program expresses confidence in an imminent victory and urges the people to remain calm, hopeful, and steadfast in their support for the Islamic system.
