In the radio program "Zamzam Ahkam," representatives of the Maraji' answered listeners' religious inquiries. The first segment clarified the rulings regarding the standing at Arafat and the necessity of maintaining proper decorum during Hajj. Responding to a question about dining etiquette, it was emphasized that stopping before full satiety is recommended, and efforts should be made to prevent food waste. The panel also addressed issues of trust regarding a gifted necklace, the rules of Mahram status, and the validity of prayers in a secondary residence. Furthermore, the financial rights of a wife in cases of divorce and mental disability were discussed. The "Parsaiyan" segment highlighted the importance of physical and spiritual cleanliness based on the conduct of the Infallibles, while cautioning against obsessive behavior in purity laws. Finally, the "Andisheh Eftehab" program featured a deep analysis of Imam Khomeini's thoughts on the essence and spiritual dimensions of prayer.
