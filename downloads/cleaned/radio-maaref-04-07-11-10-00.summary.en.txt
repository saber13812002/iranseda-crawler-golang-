The radio program "Ta Jome Zohour" examined the exemplary role of Hazrat Fatima Masumeh (SA) in expectant living and loyalty to the Imamate on the anniversary of her passing. Dr. Erfan Deh highlighted "insight" (basirat) as a key trait of her conduct, encompassing the recognition of truth versus falsehood, historical timing, and the quality of supporting the Imam of the Time (AJ). He emphasized that expectants should strive for "acquired infallibility" by avoiding sin to prevent breaking the heart of the Imam. Professor Kafil stressed the importance of the Ziyarat of Karima Ahl al-Bayt and its prayers for the relief of the Imam, urging for conscious worship and behavioral emulation. Practical methods for supporting the Imam, such as establishing educational centers with a Mahdavi approach to train future generations, were also discussed. Listeners' messages reinforced the necessity of behavioral adherence to the conduct of the Infallibles during the period of occultation. The program concluded by calling for practical emulation of Hazrat Fatima Masumeh's lifestyle to gain the satisfaction of the Imam of the Time.
