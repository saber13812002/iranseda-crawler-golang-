In this radio program, experts emphasized the importance of strengthening emotional connections and reducing blame in marital relationships. They advised couples to create a positive cycle by expressing gratitude for their spouse's small efforts and focusing on positive traits. In response to an elderly woman's question about spousal violence, the recommendation was to first achieve inner peace through emotional catharsis and psychological counseling, viewing forgiveness as releasing internal anger rather than accepting the other's behavior. For a young man experiencing obsessive thoughts after a temporary marriage, referral to a psychologist for diagnosis and potentially a psychiatrist for medication was suggested. Finally, the program concluded by highlighting the importance of spirituality and serving others, inviting listeners to participate in charity initiatives and promoting love within the community.
