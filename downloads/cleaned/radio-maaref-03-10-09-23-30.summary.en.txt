In this radio program, the inherent differences between men and women were presented as a natural reality that must be understood to achieve mutual empathy.
The guest emphasized that mutual understanding requires observing each other without judgment, focusing on strengths and weaknesses with an unbiased perspective.
In response to listener questions, it was analyzed that some apparent conflicts, such as disputes over minor details of hijab, often stem from hidden power struggles or the instrumental use of religion to exert influence.
The distinction between transactional relationships with strangers and emotional bonds with family members was also clarified, advising that interactions with loved ones should be based on internal values rather than mere reactions to their behavior.
Regarding a conflict between a wife and her sister, it was advised that cutting off ties with important family members is a mistake, and family bonds must be maintained with dignity and respect even in difficult circumstances.
Finally, active listening, study, and impartial observation were proposed as the three main solutions for reducing tension and increasing peace in married life.
