In this interview, Seyed Ali Akbar Daryabari highlights the pivotal role of local community groups in shaping the Islamic Revolution and the unwavering popular support for it. He recounts memories from his youth, including his participation in the tribute ceremony for Jahan Pahlavan Khajeh and his gratitude toward his late wife for raising their children and shaping his character. Daryabari criticizes the fundamental shift in sports culture from a focus on heroism and non-materialism in the past to the current era of financial contracts and conditional agreements. His son, Amir Houshang Daryabari, speaks about his father's esteemed status among Tehran's sports veterans and the presence of prominent athletes at the 40th-day ceremony for his martyred brother. He also details his father's social responsibilities, such as serving as the vice president of the local association and sports secretary of the youth organization, noting that veterans frequently sought his counsel to resolve neighborhood issues. Finally, the host expresses appreciation for Daryabari's decades of service to sports development and the Chaharstar Dasht neighborhood, concluding the program with an announcement to continue the conversation in a future episode.
