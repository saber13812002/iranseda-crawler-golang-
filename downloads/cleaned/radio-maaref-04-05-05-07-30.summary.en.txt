The radio program began by highlighting the success of a knowledge-based company in locally producing pharmaceutical raw materials for treating severe wounds, a significant step toward self-sufficiency amidst international sanctions. It also reported on recent birth statistics, emphasizing the Supreme Leader's call for families to contribute to rejuvenating the population. A substantial portion of the show featured a theological discussion on the vice of stinginess, led by a religious scholar who explained that it leads to inner turmoil, loss of honor, and severance of family ties. The scholar cited narrations from the Ahl al-Bayt to argue that stinginess is a form of spiritual disconnection, whereas spending in the path of God and helping the needy are essential duties. The discussion concluded that stinginess distances individuals from divine mercy and is considered a trait of the accursed. Finally, the program stressed the importance of maintaining revolutionary enthusiasm and awareness, particularly among the youth, as a key to overcoming soft war. Listeners were encouraged to support the nation by conserving resources and practicing generosity, thereby strengthening social bonds and upholding revolutionary values.
