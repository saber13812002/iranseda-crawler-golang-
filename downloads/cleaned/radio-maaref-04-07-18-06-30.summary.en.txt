The program begins by citing the hadith of Anas ibn Malik to emphasize the authenticity of Quranic narratives through multiple narrators. It then interprets the verse regarding "those nearest in kinship," explaining that it was applied to Imam Ali and Imam Hussein after the Prophet's passing. The concept of "khatm" (sealing) in the Quran is defined as a spiritual blockage that prevents individuals from perceiving divine truths. The speaker argues that this condition results from a lack of reflection on creation and the repetition of sins, which ultimately destroys the power of insight. Consequently, those who fail to learn from God's signs cannot comprehend metaphysical realities, regardless of external advice or wisdom. The broadcast concludes with an update on the radio schedule and a prayer for the reappearance of Imam Mahdi.
