In this radio program, religious rulings were provided in response to listeners' questions regarding prayer and fasting. Hujjat al-Islam Mohammadi Sheikhabani stated that performing the last rakat of night prayer while sitting is permissible due to back pain, though doubling the voluntary prayer is recommended for full reward. Hujjat al-Islam Jawahiri clarified that while lying reduces the reward of fasting, it does not invalidate the fast itself. Hujjat al-Islam Alian Natij explained the rules for wudu and tayammum for individuals with bandages, noting that full wudu is required if the wound can be safely exposed, otherwise tayammum is also necessary. Hujjat al-Islam Mahmoudi added that precautionary prayer must be performed in the same manner as obligatory prayer. Hujjat al-Islam Jabari confirmed that covering the private parts is sufficient for men praying in a bathroom. Finally, Hujjat al-Islam Mohammadi Sheikhabani emphasized the importance of repeatedly reminding one's spouse to pay khums and perform prayer, highlighting that prayer is the criterion for the acceptance of all other deeds.
