In the radio program "Historical Questions," Dr. Mohammad Reza Jafari analyzed two key historical and religious topics. First, he examined the role of Imam Ali in early Islamic battles, arguing that his bravery prevented the Prophet from engaging in mass slaughter, thereby avoiding deep-seated tribal enmity against Islam after the Prophet's departure. Second, the claim that figures like Sahib al-Rumi and Dhiya al-Kalbi influenced the Prophet's teachings was refuted; it was explained that the angel Gabriel appeared in the form of Dhiya al-Kalbi to remain hidden from the public, a fact known only to Imam Ali. Finally, relying on narrations from the book of Kafi, the necessity of reaching the state of "contentment" with divine decree was emphasized. This means that a believer must be grateful for blessings and patient during hardships to be counted among the sincere (Siddiqeen).
