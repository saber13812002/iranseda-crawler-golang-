The Ma'aref radio program commenced with the commemoration of the Sacred Defense Week, emphasizing the slogan "We are the Conquerors of Qal'eh" and the nationwide holding of memorials. The IRGC issued a statement warning that any new miscalculation by the enemy would be met with a firm and instructive response. Coinciding with the start of the academic year, the "Blossom Festival" was held for first-grade students, accompanied by educational advice to help parents manage their children's anxiety. In the cultural sector, the production of the TV series "Musa Kalim Allah" officially began, utilizing artificial intelligence for a global market release. The first conference on Digital Islamic and Humanities Sciences was held in Qom, highlighting the role of technology in religious research. The fifth exhibition of Waqf achievements concluded, aiming to connect charitable investors with knowledge-based companies. In the ethical segment, Hojjat-ol-Islam Vahidi discussed the necessity of asceticism and detachment from worldly possessions for spiritual growth, citing the life of Imam Khomeini. The program concluded with practical tips for water conservation and a legal ruling regarding a doctor's liability for extracting a tooth without patient consent.
