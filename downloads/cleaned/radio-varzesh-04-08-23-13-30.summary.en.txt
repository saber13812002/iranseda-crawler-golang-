In this discussion, Aliakbar Kashian emphasized the necessity of enhancing the scientific and ethical standards of sports coaches, highlighting recent specialized training courses held in Mashhad. He pointed out significant gaps in coaches' education regarding crisis management and psychological issues, advocating for these topics to be integrated into official curricula. The core of the conversation focused on a new initiative to develop sports within mosques, a project being pursued in collaboration with the Mosques' Council and the Ministry of Culture. The goal is to register sports clubs in 82,000 mosques across the country to boost public participation and attract youth to the mosque environment. Citing the successful experience of churches in the United States, Kashian stressed the high potential of this plan to promote sports justice and reduce deprivation in border and peripheral areas. He announced the support of the Minister of Sports and Youth for this initiative and called for simplifying the legal registration processes for these clubs. By focusing on mass sports, elderly health, and women's participation, this plan aims to activate local resources and strengthen the social identity of mosques.
