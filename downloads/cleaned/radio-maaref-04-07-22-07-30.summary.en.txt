In the radio program "Let's Be Kind," the guest emphasized the importance of having a guiding phrase to manage emotions and maintain patience, citing Quranic verses and narrations. He highlighted the virtue of kindness by sharing the story of a street sweeper who returned a valuable wallet to its owner. The main discussion focused on the psychological and social harms of "criticism and blame," which were identified as a moral vice. According to narrations from Imam Ali (AS), repeating criticism causes more pain than physical beating and increases stubbornness while reducing the effectiveness of moral correction. The guest also warned that unjust criticism can lead to severe consequences for the critic, advising caution when dealing with brave or patient individuals. Finally, it was recommended to maintain boundaries when criticizing others to avoid destroying the bridge of friendship.
