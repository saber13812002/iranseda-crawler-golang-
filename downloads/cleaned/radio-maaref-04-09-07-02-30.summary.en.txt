In this radio program, Professor Sadeghi answered historical questions from listeners. He explained that the Prophet's aunt, Baraka, was a freed slave who married Ubayd al-Khuzraji and later Zayd ibn al-Haritha, eventually passing away in Medina. Regarding the Prophet's era in Mecca, it was clarified that there were no written treaties, only oral agreements like the Pledge of Aqaba. The details of the Prophet's migration to Medina were discussed, highlighting Ali's role in sending Fatima and other family members to join him. On the topic of Nowruz, it was noted that the Ahl al-Bayt did not oppose healthy Iranian customs, with Imam Ali emphasizing the preservation of such traditions. Finally, the nature of early Islamic wars was debated, with some viewing them as expansionist and others as defensive or based on peaceful invitation, similar to the mission to Abyssinia.
