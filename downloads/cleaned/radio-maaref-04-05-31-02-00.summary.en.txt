The program begins with a call to support the people of Gaza before presenting two religious stories. In the first story, Sayyid Qutb faces a moral test from a woman during his voyage to America, which he passes by adhering to his religious principles. He leads Friday prayers on the ship's deck, delivering a sermon that deeply impacts the passengers, including a Christian woman. The second story features a letter from Ali Qazvani, an Iranian student in New York, to his teacher, Sayyid Mohsen Amin. Ali describes his steadfastness in performing his evening prayer during university exams and the respect shown by his examiners for his religious duties. This letter fills the teacher with pride, knowing his students remain committed to their faith even in exile. The broadcast concludes with a musical piece and an invitation to stay with Radio Ma'aref during the final days of the month of Safar.
