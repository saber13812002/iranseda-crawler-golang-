The radio program began by commemorating the martyrdom of Ayatollah Sadr and his daughter in Iraq, as well as the artist Seyed Morteza Avini. Although sanctions have driven self-sufficiency in the defense sector, the speaker emphasized the need for strategic planning and greater public participation in other economic fields like the automotive industry. The discussion underscored the importance of implementing Article 44 policies and supporting the resistance economy through legislative and executive action. Finally, the program concluded by stressing the vital role of youth in society, advocating for mentorship, respectful dialogue, and the effective utilization of their innate potential for national progress.
