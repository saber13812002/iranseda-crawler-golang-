In this radio program, experts emphasized the importance of explicitly expressing love within families, drawing lessons from Imam Hussein (PBUH).
Professor Yousafzadeh introduced five conflict resolution styles, recommending the negotiation and dialogue model as the best approach for maintaining marital mental health.
He also stressed the necessity of establishing clear boundaries between the new couple and their extended families to prevent harmful interference.
In response to a query about a long-term communication breakdown, the use of mediation and maintaining hope for relationship reconstruction were suggested.
A field report from Bojnord indicated that many couples do not seek professional counseling to resolve their disputes.
Finally, it was advised that the Muharram period offers an excellent opportunity to raise Husseini children and nurture a spirit of love and compassion at home.
