This radio program discusses theological issues and hadiths related to the consensus of the Muslim community.
Hojjat al-Islam Baranjkar addresses the concept of divine justice and critiques various views on infallibility and the role of the Ahl al-Bayt.
He argues that the hadith "My community will not agree upon error" is fundamentally flawed as a proof for consensus.
An analysis of the hadith's chains of transmission in Sunni sources reveals that the narrators are either unknown or deemed weak.
The lecture examines specific transmission paths found in the works of Ahmad ibn Hanbal, Ibn Majah, Abu Dawud, and Tirmidhi.
It is concluded that none of these chains are reliable due to issues such as broken chains and weak narrators.
Therefore, relying on this hadith to establish the consensus of the community is not acceptable from a jurisprudential perspective.
