In this radio program, the concepts of "Ubr Mother" and "Ubr Father" were defined as capable parenting models for the modern era. The guest emphasized the critical importance of a mother's physical health, explaining that proper nutrition and rest directly influence her temperament and the quality of her child-rearing. He criticized traditional behaviors where mothers neglected their own well-being, urging them to raise their awareness to better manage contemporary educational challenges. In response to listener questions, practical solutions were offered to reduce children's separation anxiety, such as explaining delays through storytelling, building a sense of security, and increasing the father's active role in childcare. To decrease a child's excessive dependence on the mother, it was recommended that fathers spend more one-on-one time with their children and avoid expressing excessive worry. Finally, regarding teenagers who neglect prayer, the expert noted that praying peers and healthy environments are often more influential than parents, while suggesting that fostering a culture of religious dialogue within the family can be beneficial.
