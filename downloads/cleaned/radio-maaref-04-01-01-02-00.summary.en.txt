The program begins by analyzing the challenges faced by Imam Ali's justice-oriented government, where the resistance of authoritarian classes and the people's lack of obedience to fair commands led to military and social failures. Imam Ali (PBUH) emphasized that leadership is meaningless without obedience, attributing the defeats to the people's inaction rather than fate. The discussion then shifts to the exegesis of a verse in Surah Al-Isra, which foretells the dual corruption of the Children of Israel in the Holy Land and the divine retribution against them. Exegetes identify the causes of this corruption as the killing of prophets, immense tyranny, and arrogance against God, which resulted in the dominance of groups like Nebuchadnezzar over them. Following this punishment, God restored the government and power to the Children of Israel, significantly increasing their numbers and wealth. This restoration of power was conditional upon correcting their behavior and practicing benevolence towards themselves and others to prevent a recurrence of corruption and destruction. Ultimately, the core message is that justice and obedience to leadership are the keys to survival and victory, while neglecting them leads to catastrophe.
