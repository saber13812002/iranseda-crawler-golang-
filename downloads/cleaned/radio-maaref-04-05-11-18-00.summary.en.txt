Hojjat al-Islam Mohammad Hadi Fallah discusses the significance of silence and inner stillness in the radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat." He argues that mental and verbal clutter prevents individuals from perceiving the voice of God and nature, urging listeners to control their senses to create a spiritual void. The speaker explains "sensory language" and "body language" as non-verbal communication tools that convey messages through the five senses and physical movements. Citing religious teachings, he emphasizes that during Ramadan, fasting must extend beyond food to include restraining the eyes, ears, and tongue from sin and distraction. The latter part of the program analyzes verses from Surah Al-Imran, comparing the creation of Jesus (PBUH) to that of Adam (PBUH). This comparison is used to refute claims of Jesus's divinity, affirming his status as a prophet created by God's will. The discussion concludes by highlighting that divine will is the sole cause of creation, just as human will can generate mental constructs.
