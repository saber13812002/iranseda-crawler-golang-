In this radio program, Professor Shakerin examines Darwin's theory of evolution and its initial conflicts with Mendelian genetics. He explains how the Modern Evolutionary Synthesis resolved these contradictions by mathematically integrating genetics with natural selection, thereby highlighting the role of genetics in trait inheritance. This theory identified new factors such as genetic drift, which helped explain the speed and breadth of biological changes, becoming the dominant paradigm in biology. Although evolutionary evidence has expanded, serious opposing views persist, and the theory has also become a subject of philosophical and ideological debate. The program then transitions to a theological critique of the theory before moving to religious segments and answers to jurisprudential questions. These answers cover rulings on night prayers, ablution, khums, and debt repayment at current rates, based on the fatwas of various religious authorities. The importance of consuming halal food for spiritual and physical growth is also emphasized. Finally, listeners are encouraged to submit their own jurisprudential questions via Radio Ma'aref's contact channels.
