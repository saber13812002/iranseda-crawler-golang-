This radio program reported that approximately 88,000 students have registered for the student Umrah, with the deadline extended to October 18. Several conferences are being held in Qom, including a seminar for religious propagandists to enhance their educational skills and a national conference on the pioneers of the Islamic Revolution marking the anniversary of Ayatollah Yazdi's passing. The second international conference on comparative ethics in Islam and Christianity also commenced in Qom with participants from various countries. The Ministry of Sports announced the distribution of educational packages regarding the 12-day war to students in November and the signing of a memorandum to establish a virtual bank for youth to facilitate marriage loans. The book "Under That Head" by Soheil Karimi, a biography of Martyr Nasrallah, has been released in the publishing market. In the "Zamzam Ahkam" segment, religious questions regarding wudu, recitation, khums, and selecting a marja were addressed. The program concluded with announcements of upcoming radio broadcasts and the playing of musical pieces.
