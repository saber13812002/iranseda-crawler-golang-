In this program, Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallah emphasizes the high status of language in manifesting male beauty based on religious narrations. He argues that a man's beauty lies in the eloquence and clarity of his speech, serving as a vital tool for conveying sensory and internal perceptions. While bad language can destroy physical attractiveness, beautiful and refined words can captivate hearts and make a person appealing regardless of their physical appearance. Imam Ali (AS) is quoted stating that God placed a woman's beauty in her face and a man's beauty in his words. In the second section, the necessity of moderation in worship and daily life is discussed using hadiths from the book of Kafi. The Prophet (PBUH) and Imam Sadiq (AS) stress the importance of balancing the initial intensity of worship with subsequent periods of rest or fatigue. They warn against extremism in worship that leads to neglecting daily life or recreation, as this contradicts the Prophetic tradition. The final conclusion is that fatigue in worship should not lead to abandoning obligatory duties, and maintaining balance is the key to guidance and spiritual stability.
