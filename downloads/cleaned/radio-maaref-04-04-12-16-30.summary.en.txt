While Iran keeps the door open for negotiations, its diplomacy never conveys weakness and firmly defends its interests against aggression from a stance of strength. The program subsequently transitioned to the special "Red Line" segment marking the seventh day of Muharram, focusing on the events of Karbala and the concept of "Jihad of Clarification" in historical narration. Dr. Jafari highlighted that fear of death and attachment to the material world are the primary obstacles to supporting truth, identifying martyrdom as the ultimate path to eternal life.
