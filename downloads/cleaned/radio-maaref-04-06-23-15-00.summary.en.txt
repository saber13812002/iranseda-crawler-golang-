This radio program examined the jurisprudential foundations of civil liability for compensating personal and financial damages resulting from war.
According to Islamic jurisprudence, any harm inflicted on life or property is generally claimable, except in specific exempted cases.
Key principles such as "La Zarar" (no harm), "Istilaaf" (destruction), and "Tasbib" (causation) serve as the primary bases for determining liability.
Civil liability encompasses not only direct actions but also indirect or causal damages inflicted upon others.
However, liability is waived in scenarios governed by the "Rule of Kindness" or "Rule of Self-Action," where individuals are harmed while saving others or voluntarily facing risks.
Regarding legitimate defense, jurists and legal experts agree that supporting the oppressed is a religious and legal duty.
Consequently, damages sustained by legitimate defenders against an aggressor are the responsibility of the aggressor, not the defender.
