The program first addressed the jurisprudential ruling on consuming suspicious food, stating that it is permissible based on the principle of validity until proven haram, though negative spiritual effects may persist. It was explained that believers can visit each other in the Barzakh heaven. The impact of prayers for the deceased was discussed, noting that while beneficial, it does not guarantee the complete forgiveness of all sins, especially those involving the rights of others. The necessity of seeking intercession (Tawassul) through the Infallibles was justified by reason and the Quran as a divine system of mediation. The recitation of "La ilaha illa Allah" during prostration was highlighted as the most effective dhikr, while caution was advised against excessive repetition without guidance due to potential spiritual risks. Finally, the program reviewed the life of Ayatollah Kashani, emphasizing his significant role in the anti-British struggle, the nationalization of oil, and support for the Iranian national movement.
