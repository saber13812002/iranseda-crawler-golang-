In the radio program "Persman Ateghadi," Professor Mohammadi Sharoudi addressed various theological and philosophical questions from listeners. He clarified that repentance and seeking forgiveness must be personal, as reciting prayers on behalf of others does not absolve their sins unless they repent themselves. The professor described the concept of "Khalifatullah" (Vicegerent of God) as a potential capacity in humans that becomes actualized through the four steps of Islam, faith, piety, and certainty. He emphasized that human free will is limited by one's power and expands with spiritual growth. Responding to a historical query, he argued that the Muslim entry into Iran was a spiritual conquest of hearts by Islam rather than a military invasion. He also stressed the intrinsic unity of Iranian and Islamic cultures, labeling the creation of a dichotomy between them as a conspiracy by enemies. Finally, citing Quranic verses, he stated that deliberately abandoning prayer leads to hell and that humans can perceive the future through cognitive faculties beyond physical senses.
