The radio program begins with a narrative depicting a spiritual visit to an Imamzadeh shrine, highlighting the power of intercession for fulfilling needs. The main segment features a theological discussion by Hujjat al-Islam Falahati on the moral vice of gossiping and slander. Citing religious traditions, the speaker identifies gossiping as a malignant disease rooted in the ego-driven soul and a sign of spiritual deviation. The gossiper is described as the worst of God's creations, committing a triple sin by destroying their own faith, their brother's reputation, and the integrity of the governing system. This act is equated to a soft assassination or the killing of three people, causing severe damage to social trust and political stability. The speaker also warns that listening to gossip is a form of injustice and complicity in the sin. The program concludes by urging listeners to avoid these verbal vices and to protect the honor of others to prevent societal erosion.
