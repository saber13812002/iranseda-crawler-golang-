The radio program "Parsaiyan" emphasizes the necessity of reliance on God for achieving inner peace and overcoming life's challenges, citing a narration from Imam Javad.
The serene conduct of Ayatollah Al-Azm and Bahjed during periods of sanctions and financial hardship is presented as a practical model of this divine trust.
Despite lacking even bread for lunch, he remained completely confident in God's mercy, avoiding anxiety and leaving his sustenance entirely to divine will.
This approach highlights the distinction between merely preparing material means and possessing deep faith, which is the root of true inner tranquility.
The second part of the program features Ayatollah Seyed Ahmad Faqih, an ethics professor, discussing the concept of "Tawhidic Ethics."
The guest defines this ethics in two stages: guidance and leadership, with the ultimate goal of practicing servitude on the straight path.
Finally, listeners are invited to share their opinions and questions regarding Tawhidic ethics through the provided contact channels.
