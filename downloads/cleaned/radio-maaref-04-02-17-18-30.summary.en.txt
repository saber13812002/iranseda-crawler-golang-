In this program, Ayatollah Sadrani discusses the life and thoughts of Ayatollah Iraqi, emphasizing that he recognized the era's crises and insisted on training committed scholars and preachers as the society's anchor against challenges like the Constitutional Revolution and wars. Due to religious and political differences, he initially left Iraq for Qom and later returned in 1332 at the invitation of Iraqi scholars to manage the seminary. His scholarly works, particularly his book on Usul al-Fiqh, are praised for their concise expression and depth, distinguishing them from other reports. His ethical traits, including asceticism, humility, and precision in answering questions, are highlighted in the book "A'yan al-Shi'a." The second part of the program highlights the significance of congregational prayer and spiritual unity during the Karameh decade and the birth of Imam Reza (PBUH). It also recounts the story of Zainab al-Kubra's (SA) self-sacrifice in the night of Karbala to protect the captives and care for Imam Sajjad (PBUH). The program concludes with an invitation to join the Maghrib prayer and a tribute to the martyrs of Karbala.
