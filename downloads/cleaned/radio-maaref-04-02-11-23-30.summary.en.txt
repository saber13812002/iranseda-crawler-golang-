This radio program highlights the city of Qom as a sacred sanctuary for Shias and a pivotal center for the expansion of Islam, citing narrations from Imam Sadiq (AS).
Imam Sadiq (AS) referred to Qom as the "support of the Fatimids" and described it as a holy city where knowledge and virtue flourish near the time of the Imam of the Age's reappearance.
The historical role of Lady Fatima Masumeh (SA) in forging emotional and spiritual bonds between the Iranian people and the Ahl al-Bayt (AS), particularly following Imam Reza's (AS) arrival in Khorasan, is emphasized.
The Ziyarat of Lady Masumeh (SA), taught by Imam Reza (AS), includes significant supplications such as being gathered with the Ahl al-Bayt on Judgment Day and seeking relief for Muslims' affairs.
The program also addresses the political and social dimensions of Hajj, arguing that it serves as a crucial opportunity to assess Muslim issues and foster unity, rather than being merely a ritualistic act.
Concluding with greetings for Teacher's Day and the martyrdom anniversary of Allameh Tabatabaei, the broadcast ends with morning prayers and an overview of other programs on Radio Ma'aref.
