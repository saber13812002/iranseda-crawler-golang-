This lecture, delivered on the martyrdom anniversary of Ayatollah Motahhari, explores the concept of "truth-seeking" and the role of doubt in achieving certainty. The speaker argues that while doubt may initially appear as hesitation, it is a sacred and necessary step to break the chains of blind imitation from parents and society. The Quran encourages research and criticizes unfounded adherence to tradition, urging humans to doubt in order to discover the truth. Al-Ghazali serves as a model for this path, having renounced worldly status to investigate inherited beliefs and seek genuine truth. In this context, doubt functions like physical pain, revealing the soul's need for truth and compelling the individual to act. Ultimately, the Quran promises that God will open the paths of guidance for those who strive in His way.
