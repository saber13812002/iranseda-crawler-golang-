Hassan Mirzaaghabibi, former head of the Tehran Province Sports and Youth Organization and former Rugby Federation president, discusses his decade-long managerial experience in basketball and other sports. He highlights the inefficient and rent-seeking structures in Iranian sports while recounting his efforts to organize events like the Iran Olympics and prepare Takhthi Stadium for the Super League under difficult conditions. Mirzaaghabibi details his strategic disagreements with former officials and the controversial basketball federation election, from which he withdrew due to illegal interference. He also shares a rare anecdote about awarding original gold medals to Super League basketball players in 2000, a move considered historic in Iranian sports. Emphasizing that money alone cannot secure Olympic success, he stresses the importance of training, planning, and fair player selection. Expressing satisfaction with his personal and professional life, he states his readiness to run for the basketball federation presidency again if appropriate conditions are met.
