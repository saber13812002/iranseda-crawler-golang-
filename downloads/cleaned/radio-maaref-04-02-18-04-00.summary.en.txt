In the radio program "Zamzame Ahkam," Hujjat al-Islam Tabatabai explained the practical rules of Tawaf during Hajj, emphasizing the importance of maintaining calm and avoiding disruption to other pilgrims. He recommended reciting specific supplications for each round of Tawaf to facilitate counting and increase the likelihood of prayers being accepted. In the Q&A section, Hujjat al-Islam Falasadeh stated that dedicating the reward of prayers and recommended acts to others, including strangers, is permissible and does not diminish one's own reward. Hujjat al-Islam Najat confirmed that reciting different surahs in the first and second rak'ahs of prayer is valid, although repeating surahs is considered less desirable. Hujjat al-Islam Sheikh Shabani ruled that selling the installment rights of a car before delivery is permissible and the income is halal, provided there are no legal obstacles. Hujjat al-Islam Jawaheri clarified that a wife may only withdraw money equal to her unpaid maintenance if her husband fails to provide it, and she must not exceed his financial capacity. Additionally, Hujjat al-Islam Falasadeh noted that the kaffarah for a child's missed fasts is not the father's responsibility unless the child has specifically willed it. Finally, the program concluded with a discussion from the book Kafi, emphasizing the principle of moderation and maintaining a respectful outward demeanor with wrongdoers without engaging in direct conflict.
