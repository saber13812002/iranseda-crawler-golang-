In the radio program "Tales of Freedom," Esmail Nakhai, a Basij fighter and former prisoner of war, recounts his experiences during the Iran-Iraq War. He describes the harsh conditions on the front lines, including severe dehydration and a surprise enemy tank attack that resulted in heavy casualties. After being captured, Nakhai details the brutal torture in Iraqi camps, such as beatings with cables and inhumane prison conditions. Despite this suffering, he emphasizes the importance of maintaining faith and reliance on God while in captivity, noting that prisoners continued to pray despite their wounds and restrictions. An Iraqi guard later admitted that the prisoners' steadfast faith had led to his own spiritual salvation. The program concludes by referencing other freed prisoners' stories and offering praise to Lady Zahra (SA).
