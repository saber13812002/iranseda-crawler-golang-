The program begins with a narrative from the book "Uways Qarni," where the protagonist travels to Medina to meet the Prophet but returns without seeing him to honor his mother's condition for a quick return. The second segment, titled "Parsaiyan," discusses the ethical conduct of scholars, emphasizing the importance of self-discipline and avoiding sins like backbiting through anecdotes from Imam Khomeini and Mulla Sadra. The final section features the Minister of Science, who analyzes the Zionist regime's assassination of Iranian scientists as an attempt to undermine the country's scientific and military authority. The minister highlights that the enemy's goal was to censor academic freedom and misjudged Iran's robust scientific production and defense capabilities. He points to the international collaborations of the martyred professors as evidence of Iran's significant scientific standing. The segment also notes the strong national unity and the solidarity of universities in the face of external threats. Finally, the "100 Professors, One Look" initiative is introduced as a symbol of scientific and national cohesion in defense of Iran's interests.
