The program began by congratulating the birthday of Hazrat Zainab (SA) before transitioning to a discussion on Islamic traditional medicine.
Dr. Pourhosseini emphasized the vital role of massage and "delk" in maintaining health, particularly for the elderly population.
He described massage as an effective method for eliminating excess body fluids, relieving muscle weakness, and improving blood circulation in seniors.
This therapeutic approach not only helps rehabilitate worn-out limbs but also prepares the body for more active physical exercises.
In the Quranic segment, Hojjat al-Islam Rasoul explained the concept of self-sacrifice (Ithar) by interpreting verses from Surah Al-Insan.
He highlighted the story of Imam Hassan and Imam Hussein (AS), who shared their simple Iftar meals with the needy during their illness.
This noble act of generosity led to the revelation of divine verses, illustrating the high value of altruism in Islamic culture.
Finally, the program concluded by emphasizing the Iranian nation's uprising as a symbol of Islam and resistance against global hegemony.
