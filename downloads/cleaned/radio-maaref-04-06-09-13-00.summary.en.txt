The twentieth specialized session for Quran reciters focused on the innovation of melody in recitation, with researchers given until the end of the month of Mehr to submit their papers. In the main segment, Hojjat al-Islam Loghmani delivered a speech on "Peace, Growth, and Increase in Life." He identified faith, trust, and hope as the three pillars of a complete personality, emphasizing that faith is rooted in security and frees individuals from life's fears. Trusting in God as the host and provider creates peace and joy, distancing people from worldly regrets and anxieties. Hope for the bright future of the Shia community, from Karbala to the reappearance, prevents despair and empowers individuals to face hardships. The speaker highlighted the power of prayer and Quran recitation as essential tools for warding off misfortune and enhancing spiritual peace. The program concluded with collective prayers for the Supreme Leader's health, the early reappearance of Imam Mahdi, and the defeat of enemies.
