This radio program analyzes verses 117 to 120 of Surah Al-Ma'idah based on the interpretations of Ayatollah Javadi Amoli.
The central focus is the phrase "I was a witness over them," indicating that Jesus (PBUH) witnessed human deeds during his life and will testify in the Hereafter.
The concept of divine witness and rivalry is reconciled with the Quranic principle of "restriction," asserting that God is the sole absolute agent while other beings are manifestations of His perfection.
The distinction between the "near witness" (the Infallibles) and the "distant witness" (God) is clarified, emphasizing that divine attributes in humans are identical to God's own attributes.
The discussion then shifts to the "Knowledge of Names" and divine names, noting that some are concealed from creation and reserved solely for God.
The verse "He is not questioned about what He does" demonstrates that God is beyond human judicial or scientific scrutiny and is not bound by any external law.
Jesus's reference to "what is in My self" underscores absolute submission to divine will and the lack of any right for humans to object.
The state of "Divine Pleasure" is achieved when a person abandons the attempt to judge God's actions with limited human reason.
