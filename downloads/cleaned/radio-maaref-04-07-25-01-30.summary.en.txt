In this program, Sayyid Hassan Nasrallah recalls a memory from 27 years ago when he resigned from his heavy responsibilities due to extreme exhaustion. The Supreme Leader of Iran advised him that human fatigue is natural and that one must rely on a direct and confident connection with God to overcome such challenges. He emphasized that true revolutionaries, grounded in faith, stand firm against major powers like Israel and the US, whereas enemies lack deep conviction and resort to terror and psychological warfare. Nasrallah recounted the defeat of Western armies in Lebanon by the resistance and student protests in the West, highlighting the ideological defeat of the West against Islamic revolutionary ideals. He described this as the third phase of World War III, where Israel is reaching its limits and facing defeat. The program then transitions to an introduction of Fars province and its historical figures, followed by the radio drama "Bagh-e Tamasha." This drama depicts the life of a young doctor during the Iran-Iraq war, focusing on his family conflicts and the moral dilemmas of serving in the war.
