The radio program began by highlighting the value of Ali Safa Airo's works on family law and hijab. It then addressed a married man's struggle with secret extramarital relationships, recommending practical exercises such as maintaining digital boundaries, using respectful language with non-mahram individuals, and seeking professional counseling. The discussion emphasized that inappropriate jokes about outside relationships or divorce can severely damage a spouse's trust. Subsequently, the show detailed problem-solving skills for couples, advocating for constructive dialogue, brainstorming solutions, and pilot testing them to manage conflicts like caring for a sick parent. Finally, the impact of introversion and extroversion on marriage quality was explored, noting that extreme differences in these traits can cause conflict, whereas mild differences may enhance attraction.
