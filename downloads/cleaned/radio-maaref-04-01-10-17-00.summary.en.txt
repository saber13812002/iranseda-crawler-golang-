This segment of the program explores Sermon 175 of Nahj al-Balagha, highlighting Imam Ali's (AS) profound responses to challenging questions. The Imam uses powerful metaphors, describing baseless slander as greater than the sky and truth as wider than the earth. He characterizes gossip as weaker than a helpless orphan and envy as hotter than fire. Furthermore, he identifies contentment as the state most independent of the sea and the heart of a disbeliever as harder than stone. The discussion also emphasizes the necessity of repentance and avoiding sin in a fickle world that is close to the Day of Judgment. Finally, the host expresses a desire to find individuals with broad-mindedness and sufficient spiritual capacity to share knowledge and wisdom.
