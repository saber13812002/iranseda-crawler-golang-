In the radio program "Armaghan," the hosts celebrated the birth of Prophet Muhammad and Imam Sadiq (PBUH) while highlighting the role of martyrs' blood in securing Iran's current peace. The main segment featured the story of martyr Mohammad Fazeli, a Sharif University student and intelligence commander during the US Embassy takeover. Fazeli, who collaborated with martyr Olum-al-Hoda, emphasized the need to deeply understand America's malicious nature and resist its cultural infiltration. He viewed the embassy takeover as a natural response to anti-revolutionary policies, marking a new phase of resistance. After this operation, Fazeli participated in Operation Nasr and the fourth Karbala, where he was martyred. The final section analyzed Quranic verses regarding the concept of being "stupefied" or "dumbfounded." This included the disbelief of the disbelievers when faced with Abraham's (AS) logical arguments and the moment of death when humans confront the consequences of their actions. These insights demonstrate that truth and reasoning render the disbelievers helpless, while death is the moment when one is stunned by the ultimate reality of their life.
