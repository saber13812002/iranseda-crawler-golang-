The Ma'aref network announced the martyrdom of Iran's Supreme Leader, Ali Khamenei, allegedly at the hands of the Zionist regime and the United States. Across Iran, citizens gathered in mosques and public squares to mourn, demanding revenge against their enemies and emphasizing national unity. The temporary leadership council was established under Article 110 of the constitution, with President Pezeshkian vowing to continue the late leader's path and thwart destabilization plots. Senior clerical figures issued statements urging unity and resistance against military aggression while honoring the leader's legacy. In the United States, thousands protested in major cities against the military intervention in Iran. Several members of Congress, including Thomas Massie and Sarah Jacobs, condemned the Trump administration's actions as illegal and contrary to the will of the American people. These protests and political criticisms highlight significant opposition within the US to re-entering a conflict in the Middle East.
