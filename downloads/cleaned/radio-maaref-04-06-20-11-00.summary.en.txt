The International Conference on Islamic Unity concluded with a declaration stating that returning to the merciful conduct of the Prophet (PBUH) is the only path to achieving lasting peace and unity among the Islamic Ummah. Participants condemned the crimes of the Zionist regime and emphasized the necessity of Muslim solidarity to counter aggression and provide practical support to the oppressed people of Palestine and Gaza. The "Iran-Noush" exhibition also offered a wide range of domestic products and online purchasing options, promoting national symbols and local manufacturing.
