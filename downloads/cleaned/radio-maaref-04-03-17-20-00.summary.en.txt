The radio program reported on the ongoing Israeli attacks in Gaza, highlighting the deaths of dozens of Palestinians, particularly while receiving humanitarian aid. A new Pew survey indicates that over 75% of people in 24 countries express negative views toward the Israeli regime, coinciding with widespread global protests in support of Palestine. Israel is pursuing a policy of annexation and geographic disconnection in the West Bank by planning to construct 22 new settlements to prevent the establishment of an independent Palestinian state. Meanwhile, Yemeni forces successfully prevented a US military transport plane from landing in Israel by launching missiles. The broadcast also covered the second day of the Hajj pilgrimage in Mina and the 36th anniversary of Imam Khomeini's passing in Kashmir, which was attended by various religious communities. The program concluded with a documentary segment detailing the life and academic career of Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi.
