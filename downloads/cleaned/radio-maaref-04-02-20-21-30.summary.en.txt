This radio program identifies Hajj and Umrah as the fundamental pillars of societal security and stability, establishing a mutual sanctity that prevents violence from pilgrims and protects them from harm.
The Quran emphasizes respect for divine symbols, such as sacrificial animals and their collars, illustrating that true peace is achieved when humans abandon their predatory instincts.
While the economy is recognized as a factor in societal standing, religious faith and adherence to divine rituals are considered superior to material wealth in the Islamic hierarchy.
Historical evidence suggests that economic blockades cannot defeat a community that relies on God and maintains a firm belief in its faith.
The Kaaba serves not only as the direction of prayer but as the central axis of Muslim life and death, requiring its sanctity to be preserved in all circumstances.
God has designated specific safe zones and detailed laws to guide humanity from animalistic behavior toward spiritual servitude and ethical conduct.
Ultimately, the interconnection between faith, economy, and security demonstrates that a society lacking a religious foundation and respect for rituals cannot achieve lasting stability.
