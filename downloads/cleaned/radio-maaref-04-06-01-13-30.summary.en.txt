In this program, Dr. Lali Jafari critiques political and media currents that, following the end of the Iran-Iraq war and during sanctions, advocate for a "change in the governance paradigm" toward secularism and submission to the United States. He argues that this approach stems from a defeatist and inefficient understanding of the situation, effectively attempting to impose the enemy's will on the Iranian nation. Conversely, Dr. Jafari emphasizes the country's resilience and capabilities by citing the Islamic Republic's scientific, military, and diplomatic advancements. He asserts that the unity of the people, support for the armed forces, and the role of the Supreme Leader were the three main pillars of Iran's victory against aggression, and political opportunists must not undermine national confidence through negative narratives. Furthermore, he stresses that negotiations with the US are only logical when conducted from a position of strength to protect national interests, not as a tool for surrender. The program concludes by highlighting the revolution's achievements in fostering self-confidence, expanding public services, and cultivating world-class scientists.
