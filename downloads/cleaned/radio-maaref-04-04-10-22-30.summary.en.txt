The core message of the program is that true faith requires complete submission to divine laws and the authority of the Guardian Jurist.
Personal and social failures, such as relationship issues, stem from deviating from God's guidance and relying on self-interest rather than faith.
The speaker emphasizes the importance of national unity and trust in God's protection against the enemy's psychological warfare and recent military threats.
The Iranian people's participation in Friday prayers and funeral processions demonstrates their courage and unwavering loyalty to the leadership.
The slogan "We are not the people of Kufa" signifies the nation's readiness to support the leader at the right time, unlike those who failed to do so historically.
Citing the works of Professor Mohammad Reza Hakim, the uprising of Ashura is described as a Quranic and revolutionary movement to revive Islamic values.
The ultimate goal is to cultivate "Husaini consciousness" in society to prepare for the reappearance of the Imam and the liberation of Jerusalem.
