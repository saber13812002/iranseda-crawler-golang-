In this theology lesson, the commentator uses verse 132 of Surah Tawbah to elucidate the station of infallibility and superiority of the Prophet and the Ahl al-Bayt.
The Quran's specific advice to the Prophet to be steadfast in prayer indicates his elevated, infallible status that transcends mere prophethood.
The commentator emphasizes that steadfastness in action requires complete knowledge and iron will, which are distinctive traits of the Ahl al-Bayt.
Regarding the verse concerning disbelievers, while its occasion of revelation is limited to Jews and Christians, its implication extends to all stubborn disbelievers.
Based on usuli principles, the verse applies to anyone who, despite witnessing miracles, remains obstinate in disbelief such that the Prophet's warning has no effect on them.
The commentator distinguishes between ignorance-based disbelief and knowledge-based disbelief, identifying the latter as the primary focus of the verse.
The concept of "sealing" (khatm) on hearts and ears is interpreted as spiritual deprivation and an inability to perceive metaphysical truths due to repeated sins.
This condition prevents individuals from truly comprehending divine realities, leaving them unable to internalize these truths or let them influence their actions.
