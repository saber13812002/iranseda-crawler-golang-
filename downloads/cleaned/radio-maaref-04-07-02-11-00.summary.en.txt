The Supreme Leader emphasized that the unity of the Iranian nation was the primary factor in defeating the enemy during the twelve-day war, describing it as a steel shield against adversaries. He rejected claims that national unity was temporary, asserting that political and ethnic differences must not compromise social cohesion in the face of external threats. The Leader also stressed the importance of maintaining Iran's nuclear enrichment capabilities, noting that Iran is one of only ten countries with this global expertise. He characterized the US shift from demanding reduced enrichment to a complete ban as an attempt to destroy this national achievement and impose its will on Iran. Consequently, he deemed negotiations with the US futile and coercive, as the American side had pre-determined the outcome to be the cessation of nuclear activities. The head of the Sacred Defense Foundation announced over 290 cultural and educational programs aimed at transmitting the values of martyrdom and resistance to the younger generation. Additionally, the official of Imam Reza's shrine detailed special programs for Wednesday commemorations, including memorials for the Mina tragedy and Sacred Defense martyrs. The broadcast concluded with religious music and an overview of upcoming radio programs.
