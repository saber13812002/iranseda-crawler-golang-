The Islamic Propaganda Coordination invited the public to attend the funeral processions of unidentified martyrs in Tehran and Golestan. Simultaneously, the audiobooks "Tab-e Natamam" and "Khanom-e Ma" were released during the 21st Celebration of Jihad and Resistance Literature. The Supreme Leader offered condolences for the passing of Ayatollah Sadeghi's son. The research deputy of the Ahl al-Bayt Fiqh Center announced a specialized exhibition and the Fazel Book of the Year conference. The Minister of Cultural Heritage declared religious tourism the country's top priority and highlighted plans for health tourism. The program also featured jurisprudential answers regarding khums, congregational prayer, and other religious rulings. The broadcast concluded with an invitation to Friday prayers and closing music.
