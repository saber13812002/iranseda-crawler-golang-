This radio program began with a military analysis of Operation Khorramshahr, highlighting the crucial roles of the Air Force and Navy in liberating the city. It then featured the memoirs of Abdollah Rajabi, a veteran who spent eight years as a prisoner of war in Iraq. Rajabi recounted how an Iraqi doctor, who was actually an Iranian spy, helped the prisoners by administering drugs to keep them unconscious during transfers. He described the journey to Baghdad and the presence of foreign military advisors at the Iraqi Ministry of Defense. A remarkable moment occurred when the prisoners identified Aboud Ravi, a commander martyred in the war, as one of their fellow captives in the bus. This unexpected recognition brought immense joy and hope to the Iranian prisoners. The program concluded with a book recommendation and an emphasis on the resilience and faith of the freed prisoners during their captivity.
