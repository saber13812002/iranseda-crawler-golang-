In this radio program, Haja Abadineh discussed the concept of the "mission of women," emphasizing their pivotal role in strengthening families and building a divinely guided society. She described the family as a microcosm of the ultimate society of the Mahdi, where love and service take precedence over rigid obligation. Citing the Quran, she stressed that adhering to Islamic guidelines regarding modesty and behavior is essential for the health of both the family and the broader community. The guest identified facilitating marriage and fostering domestic peace as the primary solutions to counter enemy plots and current social challenges. She argued that when women act with awareness of their divine status as God's agents, they can effectively address issues such as delayed marriages and social disorder. The discussion concluded with a call to leverage love for Imam Hussein (AS) and family unity to take meaningful steps toward spiritual perfection and the era of the Mahdi.
