The program begins by emphasizing the concept of sincerity in worship, citing narrations and stories from Ayatollah Ahmadi Mianjii and Allameh Jafari to highlight the necessity of acting solely for God's sake. It then transitions to a dramatic narration from the book "Neshan-e Hassan," which explores the lives of Imam Hassan's children and their decision to join Imam Hussein's migration from Medina. The narrative depicts Qasim's dialogue with his uncle regarding the wisdom behind their father's peace treaty and Nafila's emotional recollection of Imam Hassan's martyrdom. The latter part of the show addresses a jurisprudential question concerning the payment of restitution to dependents. Listeners are also invited to participate in Radio Mahram's "Novgaran-e Hosseini" campaign by submitting audio works. The broadcast concludes with condolences for the Muharram period and a musical piece commemorating the martyrdom of Imam Sajjad.
