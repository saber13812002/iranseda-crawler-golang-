In this radio program, experts emphasized the importance of constructive and scientific criticism of officials, deeming it compatible with national unity and diplomacy.
They argued that well-intentioned and logical critiques do not hinder cohesion but rather help solve problems and prevent crises.
The second part of the show praised the global "Sumud" movement to break the Gaza blockade as a people-driven and non-political action.
This movement exerts significant pressure on the Israeli regime and its allies by fostering global solidarity and raising public awareness.
The experts also highlighted the decisive role of the people in the twelve-day war, whose unity neutralized the enemy's plans to create chaos.
Finally, Imam Sajjad's prayer for rain and blessings was recited, seeking divine grace for the people of Iran and the world.
