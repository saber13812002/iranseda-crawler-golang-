Ali Ruzbehani, a veteran football and futsal player, recounts his career with military and army teams in this interview. He discusses winning the Golden Boot in the first official Iran Futsal Ramadan Cup and leading the Armed Forces team to victory against Persepolis. Ruzbehani also recalls finishing as runner-up at the World Army Games in Rome and receiving a bronze star for his participation in four consecutive tournaments. He addresses the challenges of playing both football and futsal simultaneously and the military restrictions that prevented him from joining the national team. In the final segment, he shares his experiences as a coach and manager of the Ground Forces club and his recent efforts to develop grassroots football in Tehran. He expresses satisfaction with his career path and thanks his wife and the show's production team.
