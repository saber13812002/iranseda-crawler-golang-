This radio program provided religious rulings based on the fatwas of various Islamic scholars in response to listener questions. According to Ayatollah Khamenei, a wife may use money given to her by her husband without specific conditions to support her own parents. Ayatollah Sistani clarified that brushing teeth while fasting is permissible only if all traces of toothpaste are completely removed. Regarding the omission of the testimony (Shahada) in prayer, Ayatollah Khamenei instructed that it must be compensated for after the prayer by reciting the Shahada and performing the prostration of forgetfulness. The program also addressed the issue of abortion, where Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi ruled that the perpetrator must pay blood money to the fetus's heirs and seek sincere repentance. Additionally, Ayatollah Sistani noted that adult children are independent in choosing their place of residence and are not bound by their father's location. The final segment discussed the reasons for Fatima Masumeh's migration from Medina to Qom, citing her desire to visit her brother Imam Reza and to escape Abbasid oppression. The broadcast concluded with Quranic recitation and religious supplications.
