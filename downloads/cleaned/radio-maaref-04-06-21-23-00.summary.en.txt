The radio program begins by emphasizing verbal gratitude and acknowledgment of the virtues of the Ahl al-Bayt before addressing the theological question of how diversity in creation aligns with divine justice.
The core objection raised is why God selects specific individuals for roles like Imamate, seemingly contradicting the concept of absolute fairness.
It is clarified that this issue extends far beyond Imamate, encompassing all existential differences from inanimate objects and plants to animals and humans.
To resolve this ambiguity, the program establishes the rational certainty of God as the "Necessary Being" possessing all absolute perfections as a fixed premise.
The general answer relies on the principle of congruence between the agent and the action, suggesting that every phenomenon reflects the divine system of its Creator.
Consequently, the differences in the world are not signs of injustice but rather manifestations of God's infinite wisdom, generosity, and mercy.
These variations stem from the essential perfection of God's nature and are fully compatible with His divine attributes.
Ultimately, understanding that every entity is of the same nature as its Creator is the key to solving this theological puzzle and accepting divine justice in creation.
