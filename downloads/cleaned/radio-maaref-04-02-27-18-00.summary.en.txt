The radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat" features a discussion by Hojjat al-Islam Fallah on the severe moral danger of anger.
Citing Imam Ali (AS), the speaker identifies anger not just as a bad habit, but as the "most dangerous enemy" within a person.
This internal enemy disguises itself as a familiar companion, systematically destroying virtues, peace, and spiritual credibility.
Anger and lust are described as the primary tools of the ego that trick humans into losing their divine potential.
The second part of the program analyzes a Quranic verse from Surah Al-Imran regarding the concept of "Habt."
This term refers to the nullification of all good deeds due to major sins, such as killing prophets or oppressors.
The discussion concludes by emphasizing the need for constant vigilance against internal vices that can erase one's spiritual capital.
