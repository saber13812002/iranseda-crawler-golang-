The program began with Quranic recitations and prayers for the martyrs of the recent Israeli attack and the health of Iran's Supreme Leader.
The Leader's message threatened the enemy with severe punishment and emphasized that successors would immediately assume the duties of the fallen commanders.
Subsequently, Hujjat al-Islam Fallah discussed the causes and factors behind the vice of arrogance.
He identified ignorance of self and God, false parental indoctrination regarding superiority, and associating with arrogant individuals as key contributors.
The instructor also highlighted rebellion, sin, lack of piety, and the self-admiration of officials as factors that foster arrogance in individuals.
Finally, he described internal inferiority and a sense of lack as the root of arrogance, viewing it as a way to mask inner smallness and spiritual disorientation.
The episode concluded with advice to avoid this vice and focus on cultivating moral virtues.
