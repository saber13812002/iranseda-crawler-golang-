Krim decided to compete with his rival, Haji Roghani, by stocking similar goods and selling them at lower prices, despite his father's strong objections regarding their limited capital and reputation. This strategy successfully diverted the rival's customers to their shop, causing Haji Roghani to stop his threats as he realized he was suffering losses. Eventually, the rival visited their shop to propose an end to the competition, suggesting that they respect their neighborhood by selling only their own specific products. Krim accepted this proposal and relayed the peace message to his father, who initially doubted its authenticity but was convinced when a customer arrived sent by the rival. The father ultimately acknowledged that his son's education and attention to detail were crucial for their business success, admitting he could not have handled the rival without Krim. The story illustrates that combining traditional reputation with modern knowledge can resolve commercial conflicts and lead to sustainable cooperation.
