The speaker emphasizes the principle of "primacy of existence" and its degrees based on Sadrian philosophy, arguing that existential differences are a requirement of absolute divine justice. Through rational inquiry, he demonstrates that varying levels of perfection among individuals of the same species are logically possible and necessary. He then highlights "faith in the unseen" and "establishing prayer" as two supreme spiritual stations, citing authentic narrations to support this view. A story about Abu Zar is recounted, illustrating how maintaining prayer with full devotion led to a divine miracle where a lion protected his flock. This narrative serves to show that perfect prayer elevates a believer to a high spiritual rank, validated by the Ahl al-Bayt. The speaker concludes by urging believers to view obligatory prayer not merely as a ritual, but as a vital opportunity for spiritual ascent and deepening their faith.
