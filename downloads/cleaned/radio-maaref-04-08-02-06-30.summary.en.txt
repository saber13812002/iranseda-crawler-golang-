In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Rabani elucidates the concept of "Ahl al-Bayt" in the Verse of Purification using authentic narrations. Citing sources such as "Al-Durr al-Manthur" and "Majma' al-Zawaid," he demonstrates that the Prophet (PBUH) identified the house of Ali (AS) and Fatimah (SA) as the most noble among the "houses of prophets." The primary criterion for membership in the Ahl al-Bayt is established as Wilayah and Imamate, rather than mere kinship or physical residence. The discussion also extensively covers the virtues of "Bismillah al-Rahman al-Rahim," which is described as the closest phrase to God's Greatest Name. Narrations indicate that reciting this phrase with sincerity repels Satan, preserves the blessing of food, and illuminates the believer's face on the Day of Judgment. Imam Ali (AS) further emphasized pure monotheism, warning against worshipping the name without understanding its true meaning. The program concluded with prayers for the reappearance of Imam Mahdi (AJ) and a song expressing anticipation for his arrival.
