The radio program "Pick of Guidance," featuring Hojjat al-Islam Najafi Najad, examines the continuation of Imam Sadiq's (AS) dialogue with an Indian physician.
The Imam argues that human intellect is limited in understanding celestial sciences, proving that such knowledge is bestowed by the Creator of the heavens and the earth.
Consequently, the Indian physician acknowledges the oneness of God and the absence of any partners in His divinity.
The discussion then shifts to the science of medicine and the creation of drugs to highlight the divine role in discovering plant properties.
By illustrating the limitations of the five senses in evaluating all plants on Earth, the Imam demonstrates that humans cannot reach this knowledge solely through material experience.
The ultimate conclusion is that accessing scientific truths beyond apparent senses requires spiritual powers and divine inspiration.
This dialogue reveals that astronomy and medicine are rooted in revelation and divine assistance rather than merely empirical experiments.
The program concludes by announcing the contact channels for Radio Ma'arif and previewing upcoming segments.
