The program begins by emphasizing the Islamic principle of justice and the prohibition of nepotism, using the story of Prophet Noah and his son as a model for upholding truth over familial ties.
Practical examples of this principle are illustrated through the lives of clerics like Ayatollah Qods and Ayatollah Hasani, who strictly enforced revolutionary laws even against their own children.
The second part of the broadcast features a narrative from the book "Galaxy of Nothingness," where Sheikh Mohammad Bahari discusses the true nature of miracles with Sayyid Ali Qazi.
In this dialogue, the cessation of sin and constant mindfulness during spiritual recitation are identified as essential prerequisites for achieving high spiritual ranks and self-knowledge.
Sheikh Bahari references a letter by Mola Hossein Qoli Hamedani to stress the importance of self-purification for understanding the teachings of the Ahl al-Bayt.
The program concludes with practical advice on listening to Friday sermon speeches and water conservation tips for air coolers.
Additionally, a call is made for the public to submit documents related to the Sacred Defense, and a contact number is provided for listener feedback.
By combining ethical, mystical, and social topics, the broadcast underscores the necessity of practicing faith and maintaining spiritual vigilance in all aspects of life.
