The program began with the recitation of a rain prayer from the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya before transitioning to a discussion on the ethics of academic supervision and evaluation. The guest expert, Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallah, emphasized the critical importance of adhering to ethical principles in the peer-review process. He outlined key indicators for ethical evaluation, including impartiality, scientific expertise, technical proficiency, and the avoidance of personal biases or projections. Fallah warned that evaluating works without proper technical skills or under the influence of personal prejudices leads to unfairness and undermines the credibility of academic outputs. He also stressed the need for fairness, acknowledging the strengths of a work, and avoiding absolutism when dealing with novice authors. Finally, he asserted that value-based alignment with human and religious principles must govern all evaluation stages, concluding the session by reciting a ghazal he had composed.
