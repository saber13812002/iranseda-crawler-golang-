In this radio program, Professor Faqih discussed the concept of monotheistic ethics and the role of prayer in fostering a spiritual connection with God. He emphasized that prayer is not merely a means to fulfill needs but serves as a pedagogical tool that instills divine attributes in humans through the intercession of the Prophet and his family. Responding to listener questions, he identified sincere affection and mercy, along with avoiding fault-finding, as the most crucial ethical recommendations for improving spousal relationships. The expert stressed that family life should be governed by ethics and tolerance rather than relying solely on legal rights and obligations. Regarding sins, he explained that the boundary between major and minor sins is intentionally ambiguous to ensure constant vigilance against disobedience. He noted that persisting in minor sins and disregarding divine commands are themselves considered major sins. Finally, the discussion highlighted the necessity of national unity and a unified voice among the three branches of government in addressing fundamental national issues.
