In the news segment of Radio Ma'aref, the manager of Radio Quran Two announced a return to the station's original focus on specialized Quranic recitation education, noting the positive reception from the audience. The program then transitioned to "Zamzam Ahkam," which addressed various religious questions from listeners. Regarding charity, it was clarified that obligatory charity must be given to the poor, while recommended charity may be given to non-poor individuals, though the latter may lack certain spiritual blessings. On the topic of Khums, paying it immediately upon receiving income is considered the most virtuous, although paying it at the end of the year is permitted as a facilitation. A listener asked about withdrawing money from a spouse's card for medicine; the ruling stated that limited usage for customary medical needs is permissible if done respectfully. Concerning the Qunut prayer, Ayatollah Sadrani emphasized that it should be recited in Arabic, and repeating phrases to catch up with the imam is not allowed. Finally, it was explained that a traveler who has passed the distance limit for shortening prayers must perform them in shortened form, and praying at the first opportunity at the destination negates the need to repeat the prayer upon returning home.
