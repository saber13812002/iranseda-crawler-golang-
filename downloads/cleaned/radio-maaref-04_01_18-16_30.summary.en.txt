In this radio program, Professor Tofiqi emphasized the crucial role of emotional and innate parental companionship in fostering children's interest in prayer and religious duties. He warned that using harsh methods, repetitive preaching, and pressure to complete homework is ineffective and instead leads to children's dependency on their parents while diminishing their own will. Two main reasons for students' failure to complete assignments were identified: incorrect parenting styles and potential disorders such as ADHD. Signs of such disorders, including sleep irregularities, frequent forgetfulness, weakness in spelling, and acting before thinking, were listed, noting that their simultaneous presence necessitates a consultation with a specialist. It was recommended that responsibility for homework should be reinforced in the school environment to prevent parents from feeling pressured to force their children. Finally, listeners were encouraged to utilize the audio archive of the "Educational Q&A" program via the Iran Radio app to access educational content at their convenience. This educational approach is built on love, patience, and understanding the child's nature to ensure healthy and religious growth in today's challenging era.
