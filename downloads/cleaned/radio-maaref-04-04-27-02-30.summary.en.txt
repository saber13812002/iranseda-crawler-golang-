The radio program "Zamzam Ahkam" addressed various jurisprudential questions from followers of different Maraji. Regarding wudu, Hujjat al-Islam Fallahzadeh stated that if a wound has healed and is covered by dead skin, normal wudu is valid, and wudu al-jabira is not required. Hujjat al-Islam Alianajadi clarified that impurity in the prostration area invalidates prayer only if it transfers to the body or clothes, or if the specific spot where the forehead touches is impure. On the topic of Khums, Hujjat al-Islam Mohammadi, citing Imam Khomeini, explained that selling agricultural land is exempt from Khums if it is the individual's sole source of livelihood and paying the tax would disrupt their life. Hujjat al-Islam Jawahiri noted that there is no specific order for making up missed prayers, except that Zuhr must be performed before Asr if both are missed from the same day. Regarding Hadiah, Hujjat al-Islam Zeinalabedini, referencing Ayatollah Javadi Amoli, stated that a significant amount of money that increases from the beginning of the year is subject to Khums, including any accumulated interest. Finally, Hujjat al-Islam Mohammadi Sheikh Shabani advised seeking forgiveness and, if possible, obtaining forgiveness from those harmed by telephone harassment. The program concluded by providing contact numbers and SMS codes for listeners to submit new questions.
