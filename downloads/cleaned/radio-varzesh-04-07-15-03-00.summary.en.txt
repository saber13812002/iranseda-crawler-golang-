Hasan Mirza Aghabik, a sports veteran and administrator, discusses his career as a player and manager at Esteghlal Club and the Tehran Sports and Youth Directorate. He highlights the successful organization of the Iranian Olympics and Asian championships in rugby as his primary achievements. Mirza Aghabik criticizes the country's sports system, particularly the lack of planning and excessive reliance on money in football. He shares a unique anecdote about awarding real gold medals to basketball players in 2000. The sports manager also details his efforts to secure Iran's membership in global rugby federations and his support for young talents in national teams. He expresses frustration over his failure in the basketball federation elections due to political interference and rent-seeking. Finally, Mirza Aghabik expresses satisfaction with his personal life while emphasizing the need for structural reforms and an end to corruption to advance the nation's sports sector.
