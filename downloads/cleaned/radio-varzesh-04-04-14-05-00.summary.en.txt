In this interview, Master Ali Reza Haghjati emphasized the necessity of orally transmitting the Zourkhaneh culture to future generations. He stated that a true master must possess a deep understanding of poetry rhythm, Persian literature, and authentic Iranian music. Haghjati criticized the blind imitation of other musical styles and the neglect of correct poetic versions, highlighting the importance of internal moral purification within the Zourkhaneh environment. He also outlined the administrative structure of the Masters' Association under the Federation and called for improved training and specialized judging standards. Finally, the guest shared his memories of listening to veteran masters and announced that his children are actively engaged in the field of masterhood and Zourkhaneh music.
