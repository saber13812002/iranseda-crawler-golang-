In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Mandegari emphasizes the necessity of establishing an Islamic government to implement divine laws, citing Quranic verses and narrations from Imam Sadiq (AS). He argues that all Infallibles sought to establish justice, but the public's acceptance and endurance of this justice have always been a significant challenge. Imam Sadiq (AS) rejected the offers of powerful figures like Abu Muslim al-Khurasani, demonstrating that an Islamic government requires "Mujahid fi Sabil Allah" rather than mere military or political force. In this context, jihad is defined as detaching oneself from worldly desires, maintaining physical and spiritual readiness for sacrifice, and adhering to truth over personal interests. The speaker highlights the role of Martyr Soleimani and the Freedom Fighters as the adornment of the Islamic Republic, urging the public to select ascetic and dedicated officials to become worthy of the Imam of the Time's (AJ) reappearance. It is also stressed that improper conduct in shrines and government offices tarnishes the image of Islam, necessitating a firm stance against oppression and corruption. Ultimately, the hope is that divine grace will foster a society of true mujahideen, paving the way for the appearance of the savior of humanity.
