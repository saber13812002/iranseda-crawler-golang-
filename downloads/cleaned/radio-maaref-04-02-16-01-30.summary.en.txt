The host criticizes the misuse of social media by youth, urging them to study history and the pilgrimage route of Imam Reza (PBUH) instead. He highlights the Imam's compassion by recounting his respectful visit to a deceased Shia in Tus, emphasizing the special bond between the Imam and his followers. A story is shared about Ayatollah Hashemi-Hayri, who, after seventy pilgrimages, wished to return to the world solely to visit Imam Reza (PBUH) one last time. The discussion then shifts to Imam Mahdi (AJ), describing him as a present helper who listens to the prayers of believers. Citing Sayyid Ibn Tawus’s book "Mahjub Du'at," the host recites a prayer allegedly heard from Imam Mahdi in Samarra. This prayer seeks healing for the sick, sustenance for the poor, and the safe return of refugees to their homelands. The program concludes with supplications for the hastening of the Imam's reappearance, economic relief, and the health of the ill.
