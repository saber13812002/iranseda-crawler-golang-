In the "Ganj-e Sa'adat" program on Radio Ma'arif, Hojjat al-Islam Fallah discusses anger as a dangerous vice, citing Imam Ali's narration that describes it as a "madness" that diverts humans from the path of reason and monotheism. He explains that anger is not merely an individual trait but can affect families, political parties, and society at large, leading to bad omens, incorrect judgments, and the infiltration of Satan into one's being. Anger is characterized as one of the soldiers of ignorance and Iblis, which prevents individuals from achieving their true spiritual goals and divine satisfaction. In the second segment of the program, the virtues of charity, humility, and forgiveness are explored based on a hadith from the book of Kafi. The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) stated that charity increases one's wealth and brings divine mercy, rather than causing financial ruin. It was also explained that humility and modesty do not diminish a person's status in the eyes of others; instead, God elevates their position. Finally, forgiving others is highlighted as a means to gain honor and superiority in the sight of God and creation.
