The Mar'efat radio network program began by commemorating the anniversary of Lady Fatima Masumeh's (SA) arrival in the holy city of Qom. During the ceremony, the public and shrine servants welcomed the Lady with religious rituals and recitations. In the news segment, the President returned to Tehran after attending the summit of Islamic and Arab leaders in Qatar, emphasizing the need for regional solidarity. The program also reported on the start of pre-registration for the 1405 Hajj and the decision to improve accommodation quality for Iranian pilgrims in Saudi Arabia. Other news included the launch of the International Imam Reza (AS) Festival and the revitalization of knowledge-based companies with endowment support. The reopening of the Bab al-Qibla basement in Karbala to facilitate pilgrim movement was also highlighted. In the final segment, Hujjat al-Islam Mayboud answered questions about the rulings for women visiting non-mahram dentists. He deemed such visits permissible if the medical gaze and touch are strictly professional and devoid of sinful intent.
