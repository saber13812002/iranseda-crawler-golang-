The radio program "Mashq-e Rah" focuses on monotheistic ethics, beginning with an emphasis on the simple lifestyle of the Prophet (PBUH) and Imam Ali (AS) to discourage attachment to worldly luxuries.
The narrative highlights the life of Ayatollah Marashi Najafi as a model of charity and detachment, noting that he relied on a taxi for transport and left no personal wealth behind.
Professor Faghihi stresses that counting God's blessings before making a supplication is essential for increasing divine knowledge and ensuring the prayer is answered.
He explains that acknowledging these blessings fosters love for God and prevents ingratitude, as supported by various religious narrations.
In response to listener questions, forgetting is sometimes framed as a blessing that helps individuals let go of past pains and sins.
To strengthen memory, the professor recommends cultivating piety, controlling desires, and reciting specific prayers found in religious texts like Mafatih al-Jinan.
Finally, he advises that preventing exposure to stimulating images and thoughts is the primary method for maintaining mental health and avoiding intrusive thoughts.
