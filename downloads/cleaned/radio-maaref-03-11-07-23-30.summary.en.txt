In this radio program, Professor Yousef Sadeqi emphasizes that children's religious and ethical challenges do not necessarily indicate parental failure in upbringing, and should be addressed with patience and maintained emotional connection. He advises encouraging teenagers to think and choose freely in a safe and respectful environment, rather than forcing them, and warns against letting belief issues interfere with the emotional bond between parent and child. To rebuild a relationship with a child who has been silent for years, identifying root causes, being prepared to hear criticism, and sending loving messages without pressure are effective strategies. In the second part, spousal distrust is interpreted as a sign of internal insecurity, and the solution is presented as creating behavioral transparency and engaging in intimate, respectful dialogue. The professor reminds parents that they are not a substitute for God and must understand the boundaries of their role to maintain a bridge of communication with their children with calmness and compassion.
