In this radio program, an economic expert emphasized the necessity of reducing reliance on the US dollar and replacing it with local currencies like the Rial, Ruble, and Yuan in trade. He identified the expansion of bilateral and multilateral agreements, particularly with Russia and BRICS nations, as the primary practical solution for de-dollarizing the Iranian economy. Despite structural challenges and market resistance, statistics indicate that a significant portion of Iran's economic relations is shifting away from the dollar. In the second segment, the discussion focused on factory closures and the Supreme Leader's emphasis on national self-reliance and supporting domestic production. The expert explained that bankruptcies are not necessarily the result of conspiracies but often stem from poor management, lack of government support, or artificial privatization processes. He called for stricter judicial oversight to identify true culprits and the provision of support packages for affected producers. Finally, news regarding Iran's missile strikes on enemy targets and the international condemnation of these aggressions was broadcast at the end of the program.
