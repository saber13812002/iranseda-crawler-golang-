This radio program addressed several religious questions with expert responses. First, regarding the recitation of "La nalam wa la khayr" to encourage prayer, the cleric noted it is permissible if understood as a sign of the person's Shia faith. Second, the ruling on communication between men and women emphasized that interactions must remain strictly within necessary limits to avoid any potential for sin or inappropriate relationships. Additionally, working on the days of Tashu'a and Ashura was deemed not strictly forbidden, though generally discouraged unless driven by necessity or to facilitate the needs of other believers. The program concluded by highlighting the spiritual importance of mindfulness and avoiding heedlessness in one's religious journey. Listeners were also provided with contact numbers for further religious inquiries.
