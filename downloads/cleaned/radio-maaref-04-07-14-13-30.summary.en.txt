The radio program "Tale of Freedom" features an interview with Naveed Tehrani, who shares his memories of captivity during the Iran-Iraq War. Tehrani recounts his heart injury in the Aboughor operation and his survival from a tank siege thanks to the sacrifice of his comrades, including Amir Asadi Afrouz and Nasim Panahian. He highlights the bravery of the Basij and IRGC soldiers and shares a humorous anecdote about distributing canned peaches in the POW camp. The program then shifts focus to the critical situation of children in Palestine and Gaza, condemning the Israeli regime's attacks. The host emphasizes that US and British support for the regime is the primary driver of these ongoing atrocities. He predicts that despite current challenges, ultimate victory will belong to the faithful nations and a free Palestine.
