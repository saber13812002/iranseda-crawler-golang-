This radio program narrates the story of Fakhr, a young man who leaves his university studies to defend Khorramshahr during the war. Amidst the hardships of the battlefield, he faces family conflicts regarding his sister Zainab's marriage to an Arab man named Iman. Despite his mother and aunts' concerns about cultural differences, Fakhr believes that national unity supersedes ethnic and religious boundaries in times of war. During a military operation, he rescues a girl from the enemy and is subsequently wounded. After recovering, he develops a close bond with Iman, a kind comrade-in-arms, and supports his proposal for his sister. The program concludes by emphasizing the importance of national solidarity against the enemy and condemning the human rights violations in Gaza.
