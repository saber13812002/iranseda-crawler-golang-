The first segment featured a speech emphasizing the importance of national unity and the avoidance of administrative corruption to serve the people sincerely.
The speaker warned that distancing oneself from revolutionary values and the public could lead to national collapse and vulnerability to enemy plots.
The program concluded with a call for a Friday march in support of Palestine and a condemnation of Israeli aggression.
The second part addressed religious questions from followers of various clerics, including Ayatollah Khamenei, Sistani, and Shabiri Zanjani.
Responses covered the ruling on missed morning prayers, the transfer of bank loans, the language of supplication in prayer, and the conditions for receiving Khums while in debt.
Additionally, rulings regarding doubts about purity, wudu, and joining congregational prayer during bowing were explained.
The key takeaway is the necessity of adhering to Islamic principles in governance and strictly observing religious laws in daily life.
