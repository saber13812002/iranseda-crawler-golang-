In this program, Dr. Mehdi Abutalibi criticized a recent statement proposing the lifting of sanctions in exchange for accepting a two-state solution for Palestine, labeling it a sign of political weakness and submission to the enemy. He emphasized that such an approach undermines Iran's national interests and dignity, urging the nation to maintain its sovereignty and avoid unilateral retreats based on a lack of understanding of international dynamics. The discussion then shifted to the tensions between the United States and Venezuela, attributing them to Washington's attempts to control oil resources and overthrow independent governments in Latin America. The analyst noted that Iran and Venezuela share a common interest in resisting American hegemony and warned against weakening relations with these nations under false pretenses. Furthermore, the program addressed US-backed legislative efforts in Iraq aimed at dismantling the Hashd al-Shaabi, a move intended to weaken the Axis of Resistance and eliminate the primary obstacles to American regional dominance. Despite political pressures and threats, these popular forces remain resilient due to their deep roots in society, rendering the enemy's attempts to eliminate them ineffective. The broadcast concluded with personal anecdotes about the life and moral character of the late Dr. Mohammad Javad Bahonar, the first Prime Minister and Minister of Education after the Islamic Revolution.
