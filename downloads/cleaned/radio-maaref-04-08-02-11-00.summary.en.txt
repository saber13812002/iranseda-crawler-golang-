The international conference on Mirza Naini commenced in Qom, where Ayatollah Sobhani highlighted his role in bridging the seminaries of Iran and Iraq and his exceptional ability to train prominent students. The Supreme Leader emphasized Naini's unique political thought, particularly in his book Tanbih al-Ummah, which outlines an Islamic government based on divine authority, public oversight, and the validation of laws by religious scholars. He noted that Naini's later regret over his initial support for the British-influenced Constitutional Revolution underscores the book's significance as an unparalleled jurisprudential and political model. Concurrently, the first national conference on the lives of Islamic scholars focused on the necessity of presenting moral role models to the youth to counter the negative impacts of the virtual space. The conference unveiled a comprehensive database of 240,000 religious heritage items and identified Islamic lifestyle as a key soft-power domain. Memorial ceremonies were also held for two prominent martyrs, Hajj Hossein Hamdani and Seyed Mostafa Sadrzadeh, in the presence of military and security officials. Other news included the start of the national Quran recitation competition in Sanandaj and the launch of a new fiber optic network at the shrine of Fatima Masumeh in Qom.
