This radio program began by explaining the rulings on using a toothbrush during Ramadan, noting that it is disliked and that swallowing toothpaste or moisture invalidates the fast, requiring expiation. Listeners were advised not to remove the toothbrush from the mouth to prevent accidental ingestion. In the second segment, a question was addressed regarding whether performing the funeral prayer for one's father also fulfills the obligation for one's mother. The cleric, citing Imam Khomeini's rulings, clarified that the prayer for the mother remains recommended and is not waived by performing the father's prayer. The broadcast concluded by providing contact numbers for submitting further religious questions. Finally, the program ended with the recitation of the Manajat Shakirin and special supplications.
