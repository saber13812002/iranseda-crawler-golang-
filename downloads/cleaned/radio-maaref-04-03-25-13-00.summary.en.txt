In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Hamid Kashani emphasized the necessity of unity and steadfastness against the Zionist regime while analyzing the historical and spiritual significance of the Day of Ghadir Khumm. Citing narrations and literary evidence, such as the poetry of Abu Tammam and the views of Orientalists, he argued that the Prophet (PBUH) practically declared Ali's (AS) Imamate through his marriage to Hazrat Zahra (SA). The speaker explained the concept of "rhetorical questioning" in Hazrat Zahra's (SA) sermons, identifying the betrayal of Ghadir as the primary cause of the Islamic society's stagnation and inability to achieve perfection. According to Kashani, had the community remained faithful to the covenant of Ghadir, the Islamic world would today be at the peak of power and material and spiritual prosperity. He stressed that returning to the principle of Ghadir and recognizing the status of Amir al-Mu'minin (AS) is a prerequisite for preparing society for the anticipated reappearance of Imam Mahdi (AJ). Finally, the speaker concluded with a prayer for the honor of martyrs, strengthening social bonds, and success in performing Hajj and pilgrimage.
