In this radio program, legal experts analyzed the hadd crimes of Moharebeh, Barqi, and Ifsad Fil-Ard, detailing their conceptual differences and associated punishments.
It was emphasized that for Ifsad Fil-Ard, mere knowledge of an action's effectiveness is sufficient for conviction, without requiring group collaboration or shared intent among the accused.
The issue of spreading rumors in cyberspace was discussed as a factor disrupting public order, with a suggestion for legislators to clarify legal boundaries through supplementary laws.
Judges were advised to exercise extreme caution in verifying the conditions of hadd crimes to avoid issuing incorrect verdicts under media or public opinion pressure.
Conversely, it was requested that judges act with courage to issue and execute sentences when all criminal conditions are fully met, thereby preserving societal security.
The program concluded with condolences for the passing of Ayatollah Shabiri Zanjani, reading a message from Ayatollah Sistani and highlighting the importance of maintaining order against enemy plots.
The broadcast ended with the recitation and translation of verses from Surah Al-Mumtahanah and Surah As-Saff, emphasizing themes of covenant, justice, and avoiding hypocrisy.
