Experienced goalkeeper coach Mehdi Heidari criticizes the lack of interest from clubs and the federation in utilizing specialized youth coaches for knowledge transfer. He argues that the current Iranian coaching system is too generic and lacks the specialized, age-specific training required for professional development. Heidari advises young coaches to maintain a primary job outside of football and to invest in international courses to update their skills. He highlights the absence of structured planning in football academies as a major reason for falling behind global standards. Despite the abundance of talented young goalkeepers in Iran, he warns that without proper investment in education and infrastructure, the country will lag behind even its regional neighbors. Ultimately, he calls for serious attention from administrators to cultivate specialized coaches and establish dedicated goalkeeper schools across the provinces.
