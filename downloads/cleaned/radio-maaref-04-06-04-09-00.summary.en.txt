The radio program begins by defining the "Haram" as a legally prescribed sanctuary where specific divine rules, such as the prohibition of hunting and fighting, must be strictly observed.
It highlights the example of Imam Sajjad, who prioritized maintaining religious sanctity over personal safety when facing assassination threats.
In the Q&A segment, the importance of showing respect when mentioning the names of the Infallibles and the severe sin of disrespecting parents are emphasized.
Listeners are warned against the dangers of casual curses from parents and advised to seek forgiveness and give charity to mitigate any negative effects.
The discussion then shifts to the historical figure Ayatollah Shahabadi, who opposed Reza Shah due to his anti-Islamic and oppressive policies.
Reza Shah aimed to destroy Shia institutions and consolidate power through military force, leading to the suppression of religious activities.
Despite Shahabadi's efforts to warn other clerics from the shrine of Abdol-Azim, their lack of solidarity forced him to end his seclusion after a year.
His return to Qom paved the way for Sheikh Abdolkarim Haeri Yazdi to establish the Qom Seminary, marking a significant step in religious resistance against the regime.
