In this radio program, Professor Mohammad Hadi Falah discussed the vice of stinginess from the perspective of Islamic narrations. Citing a hadith from the Prophet, he categorized people into four types, defining a miser as one who gives to no one and often denies even their own basic needs. The core argument posits that stinginess is fundamentally a theological issue, representing a lack of trust in God that undermines true devotion and faith. Imam Sadiq (AS) emphasized that a miser is essentially someone who suspects God's generosity, a trait incompatible with genuine belief. Furthermore, stinginess was described as the most comprehensive vice that, if unchecked, leads individuals into Satan's trap and away from spiritual submission. Therefore, overcoming this trait requires a deep reassessment of one's certainty in divine reward and a vigilant maintenance of practical monotheism.
