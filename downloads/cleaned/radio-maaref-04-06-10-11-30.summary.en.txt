In the "Zamzam Ahkam" program, Hujjat al-Islam Mahmoudi criticized the use of large, inappropriate photos in memorial ceremonies, arguing that it disrespects the deceased and constitutes a social harm. He also advised listeners not to act on baseless doubts regarding the impurity of washing machines, suggesting they consider the clothes clean. Hujjat al-Islam Jawahiri clarified that using a toothbrush does not invalidate fasting, provided that external moisture mixed with toothpaste does not enter the throat. Regarding frequent travel, Hujjat al-Islam Zani explained that traveling at least 16 days a month makes a person a permanent traveler, requiring full prayers and fasts. Hujjat al-Islam Fallahzade confirmed that paying off the injustices of a deceased person on their behalf is accepted by God and benefits their soul. The "Az-Anjahi be Omide Didar" program honored the martyrdom of Imam Hassan Askari (AS) and emphasized the spiritual and psychological importance of prayer. Dr. Mirbagheri highlighted the unique, unquantified reward of night prayers during the latter half of the night, while Hujjat al-Islam Javan described prayer as a primary tool for reducing anxiety and finding peace in the modern world.
