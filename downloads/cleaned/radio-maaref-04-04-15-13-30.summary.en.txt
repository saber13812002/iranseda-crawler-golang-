The program begins by highlighting the Supreme Leader's dignified presence during Ashura, emphasizing his courage and authority. It then transitions to an audio recitation of the martyrdom narrative of Imam Hussein, detailing his defense of the Prophet's family. The narration vividly describes the tragic killing of the Imam's children and companions, including Ali Akbar and Abdullah. Despite sustaining numerous wounds and facing relentless attacks, Imam Hussein stands firm, praying and protecting the women and children. The account reaches its peak with the cries of Zainab and other women witnessing the massacre and desecration by Yazid's army. The program concludes with a message on Wilayat, focusing on the martyrdom of Ali Akbar as one of the most beautiful and loyal companions of the Imam.
