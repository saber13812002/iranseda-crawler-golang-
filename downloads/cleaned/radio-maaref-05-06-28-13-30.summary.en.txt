The radio program Ma'aref began by celebrating the birth of Imam Hassan Askari and highlighting religious activities at the Shah Cheragh shrine in Shiraz. The second segment featured a radio drama titled "Mr. Judge," which depicted a drug-addicted taxi driver who injured a colleague with a knife during a dispute. The judge sentenced the offender to twelve years in prison and payment of blood money, emphasizing the critical importance of anger management and the legal risks of carrying cold weapons. In the final section, a political analyst stressed the necessity of rigorous follow-up on government decisions down to the execution level and reducing bureaucratic inefficiencies. The discussion also noted the current opportunity for consensus-building within the Islamic system to address fundamental issues like streamlining the state apparatus. The program effectively combined religious observances, legal education, and political analysis.
