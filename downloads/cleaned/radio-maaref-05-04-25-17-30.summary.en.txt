The program emphasizes the importance of eloquence, highlighting Zaynab (SA) as the embodiment of intellect and rhetorical power. Her speeches in Kufa and Sham, delivered while in chains, forced Yazid to retract his orders and apologize, effectively dismantling the legitimacy of his rule. This impact stemmed from Zaynab's intellectual credibility, composure, and mastery of ethical and political principles, earning her the title "Aqilah of Banu Hashim." The second part of the broadcast covers historical events such as the first nuclear bomb test and Dr. Mossadegh's resignation. It also mentions the establishment of the Welfare Organization and the life of the historian Abu Ishaq Safadi. Finally, the program invites listeners to participate in the Arbaeen caravan and a Quran recitation for the martyrs. The resistance of nations against the global hegemony is also assessed as a significant spiritual and political victory.
