The second night of Fatimiya mourning ceremonies was held at Imam Khomeini's Hosseiniyeh with the presence of the Supreme Leader, where Hujjat al-Islam Alizadeh emphasized the role of love for the Ahl al-Bayt in human salvation. Coinciding with the National Day of Unknown Martyrs, the bodies of 300 martyrs from the Sacred Defense were escorted in Tehran and other provinces, with a farewell ceremony at the National Museum of the Islamic Revolution. Special Fatimiya programs, including rain prayers and communal cooking, were organized in Imam Hossein Square. During Basij Week, legal service desks were activated in underprivileged areas, and Basij Sazandegi announced plans to build 76 schools and create 150,000 jobs by year-end. The Arbaeen Cultural Headquarters held a meeting in Baghdad with the Iranian delegation, and the fifth international conference of Arbaeen cultural activists was scheduled for Mashhad. The anniversary of the passing of Master Saeed Mosta'min Zadeh, a renowned Iranian eulogist, was commemorated with reflections on his unique artistic legacy. In the religious rulings segment, various jurisprudential questions were answered by maraji', including prayer rules for surgery patients and frequent travelers.
