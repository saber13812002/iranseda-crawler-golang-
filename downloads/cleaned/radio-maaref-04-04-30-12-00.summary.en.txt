In this radio program, the importance of prayer and the consequences of abandoning it on the Day of Judgment are emphasized based on verses from Surah Al-Muddaththir.
The core of the discussion is a hadith from Bihar al-Anwar regarding the "rights of prayer," interpreted by Dr. Seyed Mohsen Mirbagheri.
According to this hadith, a worshipper must realize that they are attending God's invitation and standing in His presence.
Therefore, the internal state of the worshipper should be a blend of hope, fear, humility, and dignity.
He describes prayer as an opportunity to establish the heart and stand before God with tranquility and grandeur.
The program also commemorated the martyrdom anniversary of Imam Sajjad (AS) and discussed the role of the Quran in alleviating loneliness.
The broadcast concluded with the recitation of Surah Al-Ahzab and the playing of religious and national musical pieces.
Finally, listeners were encouraged to call in to share their opinions and expectations regarding the radio media.
