Zahra Natiqi, a pioneer in women's martial arts, begins by recounting her early wushu training in the 1990s and obtaining the first third-degree coaching certificate in 1992. With significant support from officials, she successfully established the Women's Martial Arts Association and secured the first Chinese instructors to elevate the technical level of the sport. She subsequently introduced new disciplines such as rugby, cricket, and baseball to the Iranian women's sports scene by personally learning them before training others. Natiqi emphasizes the role of sports in strengthening families and society, highlighting achievements like national team dispatches and the presence of Iranian referees in international competitions. She also describes her managerial collaborations with her husband in the Wushu Federation and her return to the organization after a fifteen-year hiatus. Finally, Natiqi expresses her deep passion for wushu and her readiness to continue serving the sport and supporting future generations of athletes.
