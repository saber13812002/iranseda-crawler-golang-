Ali Ruzbahani, a football veteran and former captain of the military teams, recounts his memories of playing for the Armed Forces and winning the Golden Boot in futsal competitions. He discusses his participation in the World Military Games in Rome, where his team finished as the runner-up against France, and recalls collaborating with stars like Ali Daei and Karim Bagheri. Ruzbahani also details his coaching tenure at the Ground Forces team and the failed attempts to promote to the top league due to a lack of financial and managerial support. He attributes his absence from the national football team to military restrictions and the specific conditions of that era, expressing regret over missed opportunities. In the final section, he talks about his current activities in grassroots football in Tehran and his support for the younger generation. He mentions that he no longer intends to coach in the top league and expresses satisfaction with his contentment in retirement. This interview serves to honor the memories of military athletes and their role in the history of Iranian football.
