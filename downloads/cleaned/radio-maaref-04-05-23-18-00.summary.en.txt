The guest, drawing on Ayatollah Shariaty's thoughts, summarized the spiritual beauties of Arbaeen as rooted in intellect, allegiance to the Imam, forgiveness, sacrifice, and borderless brotherhood. He emphasized that to fully benefit from this pilgrimage, believers must deepen their faith through questioning experts and studying the Quran to remain resilient against enemy doubts. A crucial point was avoiding injustice and extravagancy in daily life, as Arbaeen serves as a school for fighting oppression and achieving global justice. In the second part, Hujjat al-Islam Vahidpour addressed the jurisprudential rulings regarding mourning gatherings, stressing the importance of respecting others' rights. He deemed the balanced use of sound systems necessary, provided the volume does not disturb neighbors or violate the rights of local residents. This jurisprudential approach aims to maintain public peace and order during religious gatherings. Ultimately, pilgrims were urged to preserve the spiritual atmosphere of Arbaeen by observing etiquette and respecting the rights of others.
