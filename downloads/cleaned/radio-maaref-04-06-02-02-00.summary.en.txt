The first segment of the program discussed the asceticism of Imam Ali and contemporary clerics like Ayatollah Shirazi and Bahjat, emphasizing the importance of detaching from worldly possessions. In the second segment, Hossein Shariatmadari analyzed the twelve-day conflict, declaring Iran the victor and Israel the loser due to its failure to achieve strategic goals. He highlighted global protests against the Zionist regime as a clear sign of Iran's diplomatic and moral victory. Shariatmadari introduced the concept of "artificial panic," describing it as a media tactic used to foster despair and promote negotiations with the US as a false solution. He distinguished between constructive criticism and security threats by noting that the latter often mirrors the enemy's demands in tone and repetition. Finally, he characterized negotiations with the US under current conditions not as a diplomatic tool, but as a psychological operation aimed at undermining national will.
