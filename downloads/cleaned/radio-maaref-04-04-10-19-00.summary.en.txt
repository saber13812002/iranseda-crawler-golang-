In this discussion, the expert claims that the Baha'i and Babi movements were established in Iran with support from Israel and Cyprus to undermine national identity. He argues that despite sectarian and political attempts to create division, Iran has reached its strongest form by adhering to the theory of Velayat-e Faqih. In this system, the distinction between national defense and Islamic jihad has vanished, making martyrdom for the homeland equivalent to Islamic combat. He describes the Iranian Revolution as a global event similar to the French Revolution, which has granted the country great prestige. The program concluded with the host of Radio Ma'aref offering condolences for the days of Muharram and paying tribute to the martyrs of Karbala.
