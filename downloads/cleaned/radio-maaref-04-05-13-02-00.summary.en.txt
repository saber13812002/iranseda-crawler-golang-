The radio program "Mashq-e Rah," featuring guest Seyyed Ahmad Faqihy, explores the concept of monotheistic ethics and methods for drawing closer to God.
The discussion emphasizes that seeking intercession through the Prophet and his family, along with reciting salutations, is the most effective path for repentance and divine mercy.
The guest distinguishes between Islamic asceticism and the practices of mystics, highlighting that religious disciplines are purposeful, reasonable, and aimed at spiritual purification.
Islamic asceticism involves controlling one's tongue, eyes, and wealth, as well as practicing moderate fasting, all designed to strengthen willpower and cleanse the soul.
Unlike the aimless and physically harmful practices found in other traditions, Islamic disciplines have clear boundaries to ensure the body is not damaged.
A pure intention for God's pleasure and service to the community is a defining characteristic of religious asceticism, contrasting with ego-driven motives.
Ultimately, following religious commands such as night prayers and charity not only avoids physical harm but also leads to spiritual refinement and proximity to God.
