In this radio program, Professor Jafari examines Aisha's behavior following the Battle of Jamal and the martyrdom of Amir al-Mu'minin. Citing reliable Sunni sources such as Sahih al-Bukhari and Tarikh al-Tabari, he argues that Aisha did not express remorse but rather recited verses of joy upon hearing of Ali's death. It is also reported that Aisha claimed ownership of the Prophet's house during Imam Hassan's funeral, thereby preventing others from paying their respects. These points suggest that Aisha maintained her hostility toward the Ahl al-Bayt even after the battle. In the second part of the program, jurisprudential answers were provided to listeners' religious questions. Topics included the validity of prostrating on stone, compensating for minor rights violations, repentance for slander, and how to perform prayers for the deceased. Additionally, the rules for traveler's prayers for those visiting their hometown for business and the ruling on the Prayer of Eclipses during menstrual periods were explained.
