In this radio program, the hosts celebrated World Elderly Day while emphasizing the need to address the specific needs of visually impaired seniors. A major focus was the failure to implement Article 27 of the Law on the Protection of Persons with Disabilities, which mandates the government to provide a living allowance for severely disabled individuals without income. Despite a unified ruling by the Administrative Justice Court requiring the government to comply, officials from the Welfare Organization have failed to provide clear answers or execute the law. In the second segment, Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallahati discussed the spiritual danger of pride stemming from others' praise and flattery. He warned that seeking social validation can lead to moral deviation and a disconnect from the Quran and divine principles. The speaker advised listeners to focus on self-improvement and strengthening their relationship with God rather than reacting to social threats or seeking approval. He also cautioned that withholding praise out of fear of making others arrogant is often rooted in jealousy and hypocrisy. The broadcast concluded with a religious song and an invitation for listeners to send their feedback to the radio station.
