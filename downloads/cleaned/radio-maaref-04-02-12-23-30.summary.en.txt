This program analyzes the event of the Cloak (Kisa) in the house of Hazrat Zahra, relying on authentic narrations from both Shia and Sunni sources. The Prophet (PBUH) gathered Ali, Fatima, Hasan, and Husayn (AS) and recited the Verse of Purification to explicitly define his intended "Ahl al-Bayt." The Prophet's prayer and actions during this incident demonstrate the infallibility and special status of these four individuals within the Prophet's lineage. Historical evidence and precise narrations confirm that no one else was present under the cloak at that specific moment. This event was not a casual act but a formal divine communication regarding the purity and leadership of this family. The discussion further addressed theological objections and emphasized the continuity of their infallibility. The program concluded with an invitation to cultural competitions and a call for spiritual jihad against political assassinations.
