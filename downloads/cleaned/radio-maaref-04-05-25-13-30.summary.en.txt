In this program, Dr. Ghaemmaghami analyzes how the enemy, despite knowing Iran's military strength, relied on surprise attacks to disrupt the command structure.
He argues that the enemy's strategy failed due to the social cohesion, faith, and 45-year resistance experience of the Iranian people, which prevented internal collapse.
According to this analysis, the main goal of the US and the Zionist regime is to weaken Iran's two pillars of national security: religion and science.
The enemy attempts to prevent Iran from becoming an international power and maintain its regional dominance by creating a nuclear crisis and inciting neighboring countries.
This conflict goes beyond strategic interests, rooted in a civilizational and ideological clash between the West's tribal worldview and Iran's revolutionary identity.
The recent US attack, rather than causing defeat, served to solidify Iran's position as an independent and uncontainable power.
Ultimately, the impact of the Islamic Revolution of Iran on shaping the world's multipolar order and the decline of major Western powers remains significant.
