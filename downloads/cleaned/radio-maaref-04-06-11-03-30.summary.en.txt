This radio program provided religious rulings in response to various listener inquiries. Regarding fasting, it is not obligatory if an illness is severe, but otherwise, one must follow the advice of a specialist doctor. Men are religiously prohibited from wearing short-sleeved shirts, and full body coverage is mandatory in public settings. Doubt about wudu during prayer does not invalidate the prayer, which should be continued. If a trustee is negligent and the entrusted item is lost, they must pay its current market value. Forgetting the Fatiha during prayer is remedied by reciting tasbihat or restarting the prayer, depending on the time remaining. Repaying debts in installments is obligatory if one has the financial means, unless the creditor forgives the debt. Additionally, peeling lips or mouth skin that results in bleeding is considered impure and should not be ingested. Finally, the program concluded with a lecture on the importance of constant spiritual awareness and mindfulness of God's presence.
