In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Javad Jafari explained the history of Imam Zaman's occultation, distinguishing between the Minor and Major Occultations based on the presence or absence of specific deputies. He emphasized the necessity of strong enemy awareness for the awaiters, warning against naivety and being deceived by superficial peace offers from adversaries. Professor Abazari also highlighted the importance of "Jihad of Clarification" and faith in divine promises, asserting that resistance and authority are the only paths to victory over global arrogance. He noted that belief in God and the afterlife provides peace and ensures ultimate victory, even in the most difficult circumstances. In the final section, the book "Let Us Help Him" was introduced as a practical guide for reviving the school of Shia Islam and supporting the Imam of the Age. Listeners expressed their anticipation and desire for the Arbaeen pilgrimage and the Imam's appearance by sending "Ya Mehdi" messages and prayers for relief.
