This radio broadcast began by discussing the UN Security Council's decision to extend sanctions against Iran and the strong reactions from Iranian officials. It also reported on the ongoing Israeli attacks in Gaza and the severe psychological crisis among Israeli soldiers due to their exposure to war scenes.
