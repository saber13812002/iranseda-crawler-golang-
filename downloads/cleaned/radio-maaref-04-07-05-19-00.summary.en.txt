This program examines the characteristics of the pious based on the fifth sermon of Nahj al-Balagha, focusing on their longing for martyrdom and their perception of God's greatness.
The pious are described as harmless and comforting to others, illustrated by stories of the gentle treatment of servants by Imam Sajjad and Imam Sadiq.
Their physical frailty and minimal needs are presented as signs of their dedication to worship and their lack of excessive demands.
The speaker highlights the Supreme Leader's recommendation to reduce the costs of unnecessary government agencies and increase their efficiency amidst sanctions.
He urges the national media to monitor institutional budgets and services to help fulfill the Leader's directives and alleviate public hardships.
The broadcast concludes with a prayer for rain and the recitation of the Quran, ending with well-wishes for the audience's health.
