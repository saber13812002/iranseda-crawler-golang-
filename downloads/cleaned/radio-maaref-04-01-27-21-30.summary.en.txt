This program examines verses from Surah Al-Ma'idah to refute the doctrine of the Trinity and affirm absolute monotheism. The speaker argues that God's essence encompasses all existence and, being beyond numerical count, cannot be ranked as one of three parts alongside other beings. Citing Surah Al-Mujadala and Al-Kahf, the text demonstrates that while God is present with every entity, He shares no genus or rank with them. It also references Fakhr Razi's view that describing God as "one of three" is invalid due to His non-numerical unity, regardless of whether divinity is intended. The discussion highlights the Quran's dual approach, using profound theological arguments alongside simple, accessible proofs, such as Christ's need for food versus God's absolute self-sufficiency. The speaker emphasizes that the miracles of Jesus and Moses were performed by God's permission, not through their own inherent creative power. Ultimately, the Quran clarifies the truth of monotheism for both deep thinkers and the general public through these varied yet consistent arguments.
