The radio program "Tales of Freedom" featured the memoirs of Abbas Bagheri, a veteran of the Iran-Iraq War. He recounted the humane behavior of the camp commander, Abu Tabari, and his influence on prisoners of various faiths and ethnicities. Bagheri described the moment the news of Imam Khomeini's death reached the camp and the prisoners' efforts to hide it from their captors. He also detailed the harsh living conditions, including severe food shortages and continuous torture. Bagheri shared the remarkable story of prisoners inventing yogurt using fig milk and powdered milk during their captivity. The program later introduced the book "Time Stood Still," highlighting the importance of resistance literature. The broadcast concluded with advice on water conservation and the necessity of national unity for Iran's development.
