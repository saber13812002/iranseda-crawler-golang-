The broadcast began with the song "Baran-e Mehrbani" and a discussion on the need for social solidarity to address housing and livelihood challenges. The main segment, "Sarmeshk," redefined the concept of "good" using Nahj al-Balagha and insights from Professor Javad Mohtadi. He argued that any pleasure or achievement leading to hell is not truly good, whereas hardships leading to paradise are not evil. Faith without patience was deemed incomplete, and piety was identified as the sole source of goodness in worldly possessions. The program then highlighted the 1404 slogan of "investment for production," emphasizing the necessity of government and public cooperation to resolve economic issues. Finally, the speaker stressed the importance of standing against injustice and enduring hardships to establish justice and preserve the Islamic school of thought.
