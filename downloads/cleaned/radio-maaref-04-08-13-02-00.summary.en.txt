This radio program recounts the bitter memories of Iranian soldiers held captive in Iraqi camps. The prisoners described severe physical violence, psychological torture, and harsh sanitary and food conditions. Despite these pressures, the freed captives maintained their resistance by praying and holding onto their religious beliefs. The enemy attempted to suppress prayer to erase the prisoners' religious identity, but their deep faith prevented this. The discussion then shifted to the concept of "love and hatred for the sake of God" as one of the strongest pillars of faith. According to religious traditions, friendship and enmity should be based solely on divine criteria and a person's Godliness. This approach is considered a sign of being chosen by God and is regarded as one of the greatest branches of faith.
