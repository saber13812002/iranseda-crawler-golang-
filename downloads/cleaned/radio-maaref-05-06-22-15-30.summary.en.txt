In this radio program, Professor Mohammadi analyzed the legal and Quranic concept of "ifsad fi al-ard" (corruption on earth), comparing it with the crimes of moharebeh and bri'at. He explained that this crime involves creating widespread disorder that severely disrupts public order, though it does not necessarily need to affect the entire country and can occur within a single city. A key distinction from moharebeh is that ifsad fi al-ard can be committed by a single individual, whereas moharebeh requires a group. The specific manifestations of this crime include creating insecurity, causing significant damage to property or life, and spreading corruption or immorality on a large scale. Determining the extent and severity of this disruption is a customary matter that must be assessed by a judge based on the specific case details. Following the legal discussion, recitations and spoken translations of verses from Surah Ar-Rahman and Surah Al-Waqi'ah were presented. The program concluded with practical tips for saving water at home and an invitation to join future broadcasts.
