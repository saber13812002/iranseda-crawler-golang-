The program begins by introducing "Hoseini Child," an initiative that teaches children religious concepts and problem-solving skills through martyr stories and practical games. It then reports on the 20th specialized conference for Quran reciters focusing on innovation in recitation, followed by tips for reducing water consumption in air coolers. The main segment features an interview with Davood Ghanbarian, a freedom fighter who was captured at age 16 during the Ramadan operation. He recounts his remarkable journey to Karbala while a prisoner, including efforts to prepare sweets for his fellow captives and the involuntary trembling he experienced in the presence of Imam Hussein (AS). Ghanbarian also describes the Iraqi soldiers' intense fear of entering Imam Hussein's shrine and the strict limitations placed on their worship. The program concludes with a recommendation of the book "When the Moon Was Lost," which narrates the spiritual transformation of a teenager at the front lines.
