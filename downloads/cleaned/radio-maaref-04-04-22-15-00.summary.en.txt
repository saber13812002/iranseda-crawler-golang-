In this radio program, legal experts and jurists discussed the right of self-defense following recent Israeli and American aggressions against Iran. According to international law and Islamic jurisprudence, victimized states have the right not only to repel aggression but also to take measures to prevent future attacks. Conversely, the theories of "preemptive" and "preventive" defense advocated by the US and Israel are deemed legally and religiously invalid, as they are prohibited in the Islamic tradition. The experts emphasized that the United States interprets international rules selfishly, prioritizing its own interests over global legal standards. From an Islamic perspective, maintaining dignity and military readiness for deterrence is a fundamental jurisprudential principle, as Islam supports both peace and defense. Finally, the necessity of unity among Islamic countries and a reevaluation of US military bases in Islamic lands were highlighted to preserve regional dignity and security.
