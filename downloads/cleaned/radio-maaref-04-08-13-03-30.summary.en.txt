In the radio program "Andisheh-ye Aftab," Professor Bagher Sabahi examined the philosophical and mystical views of Imam Khomeini. He emphasized that analyzing mystical content with a purely philosophical temperament is incompatible, arguing that the works of mystics like Rumi must be explained by a Sufi rather than a philosopher. Consequently, Imam Khomeini did not fully approve of Mulla Hadi Sabzevari's commentary on Rumi's Masnavi due to this mismatch in spiritual temperament. The Imam's philosophy was grounded in sacred law, aiming to elevate divine knowledge by integrating reason, intuition, and tradition. By training students and writing extensive annotations, the Imam created the necessary intellectual foundation for the acceptance of Transcendent Theosophy in seminary circles. His critical stance on figures like Fakhr al-Razi and his praise for Ibn Arabi demonstrated that his philosophy served the Sharia and addressed the intellectual challenges of his era. Finally, the discussion highlighted the importance of preserving the authentic place of mysticism and philosophy in seminaries while avoiding one-sided distortions.
