In the radio program "Mashq-e Rah," Professor Seyed Ahmad Faghihi discussed the concept of monotheistic ethics and the profound connection between prayer and knowing God. He explained that prayer is a reciprocal process where increased knowledge of God enhances a person's ability to ask for greater goods. The professor distinguished between "insisting on the act of prayer," which signifies humility and cures arrogance, and "insisting on a specific request," which is considered imposing conditions on God. To treat quick temper and despair, he recommended practicing endurance from a young age and reciting Surah Ar-Rahman. A key point was the importance of relying on God rather than solely on personal capabilities to overcome failures. Finally, the concept of "waiting for relief" (Intizar-e Faraj) was introduced as the best deed of the community, which not only instills hope but also motivates action and effort toward building a Mahdavi society.
