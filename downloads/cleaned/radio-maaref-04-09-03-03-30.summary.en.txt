In this program, Professor Shakarin critically analyzed Darwin's theory of evolution, arguing that the principle of "survival of the fittest" is merely a tautological and abstract statement that offers no practical solutions. He emphasized that science, like physics, should provide calculable laws enabling prediction and engineering, whereas evolutionary theory lacks such a tool. From the perspective of the philosophy of science, the empirical evidence for this theory was questioned, as scientific proof requires precise variable control and reproducibility, which are impossible in evolutionary studies. Furthermore, it was noted that the available evidence is extremely limited relative to the theory's broad claim to explain all biological diversity. The program then announced the funeral procession for 300 unidentified martyrs on the anniversary of Fatima Zahra's martyrdom. Following this, the "Zamzam Ahkam" segment addressed listeners' religious questions, covering rulings on coerced abortion, returning stolen property, a wife's authority over her husband's assets, the use of housing deposit loans, and the proper disposal of broken stamps.
