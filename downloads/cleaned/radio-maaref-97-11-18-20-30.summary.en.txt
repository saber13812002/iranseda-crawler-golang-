The radio program "I am Fatima" commenced during the mourning period of Hazrat Zahra (SA), focusing on her spiritual virtues and status. Hujjat al-Islam Hosini analyzed the Ziyarat Rabi, highlighting three key confessions: divine trial, validation of Wilayat, and patience with religious laws. He emphasized that accepting the Sadiqa Tahira (SA) is equivalent to accepting Tawhid, Prophethood, and the Imamate of Ali (AS). The program discussed the request to be connected to Prophethood and Imamate through Hazrat Zahra (SA), who serves as the link between people and the Prophet (PBUH). Accepting the Wilayat of the Ahl al-Bayt (AS) was presented as a means for self-purification and spiritual cleansing. Ayatollah Mazarari underscored the pivotal role of Hazrat Zahra (SA) in completing the religion and linking Prophethood to Imamate. The broadcast concluded with an invitation to follow the Fatimi lifestyle and a brief mention of the foundations of Marja'iyyat and Wilayat al-Faqih.
