This radio program first explained the Supreme Leader's ruling that depositing money in banks operating under approved parliamentary regulations is permissible. The religious ruling regarding stopping in halls adjacent to mosques was also discussed, noting it is allowed for those with excuses if the space is not part of the mosque. Additionally, the conditions for performing wudu with facial wounds and the obligation of khums on valuable gifts given to infants were detailed based on Ayatollah Javadi Amoli's fatwa. The permissibility of reciting the Qunut prayer in Persian, with a recommended precaution to use Arabic, and the requirements for making up missed fasts were also addressed. Finally, the program emphasized the necessity of detaching from worldly attachments and focusing on the afterlife, citing prophetic teachings. The Prophet (PBUH) viewed the world as a means for worship and earning a halal livelihood rather than an ultimate goal. The ideal state for a believer is to use worldly blessings for gratitude while simultaneously feeling a sense of poverty and need for God. This balanced approach helps individuals avoid sin and attain eternal happiness.
