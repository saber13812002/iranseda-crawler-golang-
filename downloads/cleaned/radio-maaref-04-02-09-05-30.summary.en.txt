The radio program begins with a jurisprudential discussion on the prohibition of selling properties dedicated to mosques and shrines.
Scholars like Muhaqqiq Naini argue that these properties possess a unique public ownership status that prevents their sale, though rebuilding is permitted if they are destroyed.
The conversation then shifts to the philosophy of prayer and intercession, emphasizing that unanswered prayers do not indicate divine indifference.
It is explained that God may reject a specific request to preserve a hidden benefit for the servant that is not immediately apparent.
Complaining to God or the Ahl al-Bayt when prayers are not answered is considered a grave error, potentially amounting to disbelief.
The broadcast concludes with congratulations for the birth anniversary of Lady Fatima Masumeh (SA) and the beginning of the Decade of Dignity.
