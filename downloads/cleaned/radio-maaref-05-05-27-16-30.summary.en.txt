In this program, Professor Mohammadi refutes the claim that sins delay the reappearance of the Imam, asserting that the martyrdom of Imam Hussein and the disclosure of secrets were the primary causes of the delay. He examines the influence of parents on their children within the framework of causality, emphasizing that while parents play a role, they are not the sole cause, and human free will remains intact. Citing the story of Abu Basir, he demonstrates that God maintains justice through spiritual compensation for physiological or upbringing-related deficiencies. In the second part, he critiques the concept of "innate knowledge," arguing that hidden knowledge is not among the causes of events and cannot prevent their occurrence. Comparing this to the story of Prophet Moses, he explains that governance is impossible without the people's cooperation, and Imam Hussein's martyrdom broke the wall of intimidation, paving the way for truth. Finally, addressing listener questions, he discussed topics such as the feeling of déjà vu, the timing of the delay, and the wisdom behind creating a spouse for human peace.
