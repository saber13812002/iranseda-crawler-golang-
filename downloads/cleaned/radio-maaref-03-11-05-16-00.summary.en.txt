In this radio program, Professor Jafari answers historical and religious questions from listeners. He cites Sheikh Mufid's book Irsad to state that Imam Ali (AS) had 27 children and eight permanent wives. The professor explains that Muhammad Hanafiya was named after his mother as a reaction to her unjust imprisonment by Abu Bakr's army during the Ridda wars. The claim by Wahhabis that building structures on graves is an innovation is refuted with historical evidence from the era of the Companions and Imams, such as the dome built over Ibn Abbas's grave. Limited historical information is noted regarding the Prophet's mother, Lady Amina (SA). In response to the query about Mary (SA) being addressed as "sister of Harun," the Prophet (PBUH) clarified that this was a symbolic title denoting piety among the Israelites. Finally, it is explained that the flood during Noah's (AS) time was local and only affected those who had received his message and disobeyed.
