This segment of the program discusses the philosophical nature of mental entities and the paradox of a single object simultaneously possessing the attributes of substance, accident, knowledge, and the known. The respondent argues that while distinguishing between two considerations (external and mental existence) can resolve some contradictions, it is insufficient to address the core issue of an entity being both a substance and an accident. The discussion also references a rule stating that every immaterial entity inherently knows its own existence, which implies the self-knowledge of God and angels. Furthermore, the text critiques the idea that the soul can act as an efficient cause to create an independent rational substance, as this would require transitioning from potentiality to actuality without a proper material or final cause. The program concludes with the recitation of verses from Surah Muhammad and Quranic supplications.
