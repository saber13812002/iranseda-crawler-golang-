The radio program "Narrative of Love" featured Fatemeh Hajimirza, the mother of martyrs Hossein and Majid Deljou. She shared memories of their life together, their martyrdom, and the peace she found afterward. The mother described her maternal longing and the intangible presence she feels from her martyred sons in daily life. She also read from her sons' writings, which expressed their desire for martyrdom and love for the system. In the final segment, the program discussed the economic capabilities of young people and the private sector's role in countering sanctions. The show concluded by emphasizing patience, love, and hope for a better future.
