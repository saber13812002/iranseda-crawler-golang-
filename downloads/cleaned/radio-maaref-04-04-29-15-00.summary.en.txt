In this radio program, jurisprudential experts refuted the claim that Iran is an illogical nation by emphasizing the strictly defensive nature of Islamic warfare. They clarified that while preparation and deterrence are religious obligations, preemptive aggression without an imminent, verified threat is not permitted in Islamic jurisprudence. Citing the Quranic verse "fight them until there is no fitna," the scholars defined the end of war as the complete elimination of the enemy's capacity to invade again, rather than a mere superficial ceasefire. They highlighted the central role of the Supreme Leader in making final decisions regarding the continuation or cessation of hostilities based on the principles of wisdom and public interest. Furthermore, they noted that for national security reasons, certain strategic details may remain undisclosed to the public to prevent adversaries from exploiting them. Finally, they interpreted military and technological investments as a form of "spending in the way of God," arguing that such expenditures are essential for ensuring public psychological security and maintaining effective deterrence against future threats.
