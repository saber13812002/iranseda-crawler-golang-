In this radio program, Professor Mehdi Yousefian from the Hazrat Ali Specialized Center for Mahdism discussed the significant role of women in supporting Imam Mahdi (aj). He cited historical figures such as Siyana, Sumayyah, and Nasaibah to demonstrate that women have taken steps toward faith and martyrdom throughout history and will accompany the Imam at the time of his reappearance. Addressing a listener's question about resurrection after death, he referenced a narration from Imam Sadiq (as) stating that pure believers will be revived in their graves to assist the Imam upon his rise. This return is conditional upon possessing spiritual readiness and progressing correctly on the path of anticipation, as highlighted in the Dua al-Ehd. The program continued with the recitation of the Ziyarat of Al-Yasin to enhance the spiritual atmosphere. The concluding remarks emphasized the individual responsibility of every person to perform their duties correctly. Relying on the constant supervision of God and angels, the necessity of righteous action was highlighted to avoid severe judgment on the Day of Judgment.
