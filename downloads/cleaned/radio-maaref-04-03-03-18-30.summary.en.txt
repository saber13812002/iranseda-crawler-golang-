This radio program discusses the interpretation of verses 8 to 10 of Surah Al-Layl, warning that stinginess, self-sufficiency, and denying divine rewards lead to the removal of God's grace and the difficulty of life. Conversely, generosity and belief in God's promises facilitate one's path and increase divine success. Imam Baqir (AS) emphasized that the intention to commit evil is facilitated by God, whereas true sinners may face unexpected obstacles. The program also referenced the Quranic verse stating that every soul will be recompensed exactly for what it has earned, highlighting the absolute justice of the Day of Judgment without any favoritism. In the second section, the rulings for the Sa'i between Safa and Marwah for Umrah al-Tamattu pilgrims were explained, stressing the importance of maintaining the path's continuity during breaks. Finally, religious questions from listeners were answered, including the invalidity of Ghusl if nail polish remains on the nails and the validity of a follower's prayer if they delay joining the Imam.
