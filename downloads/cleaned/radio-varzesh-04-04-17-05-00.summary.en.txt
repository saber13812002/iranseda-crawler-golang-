Football coach and instructor Hossein Saleh shared his career highlights in a radio interview, recalling collaborations with prominent figures like Ali Kazemi and Masoud Mehri. He described the humble beginnings of the Tehran provincial football education committee, where he worked to attract players to training courses despite limited resources. As the head coach of the Iranian U-16 national team in 2014, Saleh led the squad to a group championship in Asia, asserting Iran's strong position in the region. He also detailed his experience as the head coach of the Afghan U-23 team for the 2016 Olympics, noting that FIFA had offered him a technical director role there. Despite his international success, Saleh expressed frustration over being demoted within the Iranian Football Federation shortly after his team's victory. He discussed his retirement in 2022 and his ongoing efforts to establish a well-equipped football academy. Finally, Saleh stated his ambition to coach in the Iranian Premier League and eventually manage the national U-23 team.
