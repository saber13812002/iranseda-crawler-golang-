This radio program features Fatemeh Sheikhi, the sister of martyr Mohammad Sheikhi, who recounts the sweet and bitter memories of the Sacred Defense era. The martyr, a Taekwondo practitioner with a brown belt, joined the front lines at the age of fifteen and lost his right eye during Operation Walfajr 8. Despite his injuries, he returned to the battlefield with a prosthetic eye and was ultimately martyred in Operation Nasr 4. Fatemeh describes their deep emotional bond and the profound pain of receiving the news of his martyrdom. After her son recovered from a severe illness, the martyr's mother, relying on Imam Zaman, permitted him to go to the front, predicting that he would become a martyr. Mohammad Sheikhi was a kind and selfless individual who avoided causing distress to his friends and family, even while in the war zone. The program highlights the sacrifice and love between siblings, honoring the men and women who gave up everything for their ideals.
