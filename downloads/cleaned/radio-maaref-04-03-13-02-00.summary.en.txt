The radio program "Parsaiyan" emphasizes the spiritual value of midnight worship and supplication, citing the practices of great scholars who found peace and forgiveness through night prayers. The show highlights how these figures dedicated their nights to weeping and intimate dialogue with God, contrasting their daytime roles with their spiritual devotion. In the "Mashq-e Rah" segment, Professor Faghihi discusses the role of prayer in improving one's moral character within the framework of monotheistic ethics. He explains the distinction between praise and gratitude, stressing that praising God before making requests increases the likelihood of prayers being answered. The professor also offers practical advice for overcoming envy, recommending contentment and avoiding worldly desires. In response to listener questions, he identifies sincere repentance and genuine remorse as the primary steps for the forgiveness of sins. Finally, he advises seeking forgiveness in the presence of the Prophet and the Imams to attain divine mercy.
