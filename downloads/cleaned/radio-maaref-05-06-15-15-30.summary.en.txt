The radio program began by discussing the organized communication channels between the MEK and their families inside prisons during the early years of the revolution, which facilitated the transfer of information and images. A subsequent jurisprudential debate addressed the use of the term "muharib" (warrior against God) in Imam Khomeini's 1978 letter regarding the MEK. It was explained that at that time, the term was used in its broad sense, encompassing "baghy" (rebellion or terrorism), as the legal distinction between the two was not formally established until 2013. Therefore, the Imam's usage of the term was considered legally and jurisprudentially valid within the context of that era. The broadcast then featured recitations and translations from Surah Az-Zariyat and Surah At-Tur, emphasizing lessons from the fates of previous nations and the importance of monotheism. The program concluded with a call for energy conservation and a final prayer.
