The radio program "Strategy," featuring expert Bahmani, analyzed the strategic dimensions of the Supreme Leader's first statement following the martyrdom of General Soleimani. The central theme identified was the "preservation, continuity, and elevation of the revolution" based on the three pillars of the people, resistance, and spirituality. Key points included crisis management in difficult psychosocial conditions and the use of combined military and economic deterrence to instill anxiety in adversaries. The discussion also highlighted Iran's elevation from a "pawn" to a "game-maker" in the international arena and the emphasis on emotional justice in honoring martyrs' families. In the second segment, verses 9 through 40 of Surah Al-Qamar were recited and narratively translated. These verses recounted the stories of the peoples of Noah, 'Ad, Thamud, and Lot, emphasizing the consequences of rejecting divine messengers.
