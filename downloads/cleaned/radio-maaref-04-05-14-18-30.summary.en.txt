In the "Zamzam Ahkam" radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Vahidpour explained the prayer rulings for Arbaeen pilgrims. He emphasized that a traveler staying for ten days or more across multiple locations must pray in a shortened form, unless they intend to reside in one specific place for ten days. Four sacred sites, including Masjid al-Haram, Masjid al-Nabi, Kufa Mosque, and the Karbala shrine, allow the option to pray either fully or shortened. Responses to specific questions included Ayatollah Khamenei's ruling prohibiting menstruating women from stopping near the shrine's enclosure and Ayatollah Sistani's ruling that shoes do not need washing after mopping a carpet. The program also covered the impact of water barriers like spray paint on wudu and the purification of urine-stained car seats using hose water or limited water. Additionally, rulings on reserving prayer spots for others and making up missed prayers after a year of unawareness regarding the obligation of Qatl al-Muslimiyyah were discussed. The broadcast concluded with contact numbers for submitting religious queries and live reports from the Mehran border regarding the pilgrims' journey.
