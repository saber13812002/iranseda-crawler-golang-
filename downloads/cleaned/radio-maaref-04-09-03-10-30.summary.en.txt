The program began with religious music dedicated to the mourning of Hazrat Fatima Zahra (SA), followed by a segment on traditional medicine. A specialist discussed the causes of cysts and recommended regular sleep, healthy eating, and exercise as preventive measures. A family story illustrated how a daughter came to understand the importance of a healthy lifestyle through her mother's advice. Subsequently, the "A Drop from the Sea" segment analyzed a verse from Surah Ash-Shura regarding divine laws in human life. The speaker emphasized that God sometimes delays punishment for sins, warning listeners not to mistake the apparent success of sinners for divine approval. Finally, a narration from Imam Sadiq (AS) was shared about Hazrat Zahra's (SA) seventy-five days of life after the Prophet's martyrdom, highlighting the frequent visits of the Angel Gabriel to her.
