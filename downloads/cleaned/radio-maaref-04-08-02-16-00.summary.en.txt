In this radio program, Dr. Mostafa Sadeghi Kashani addressed various historical and narrational questions from listeners. He first discussed Roshayd Hujri, a companion of Imam Ali, noting his knowledge of future events and his tragic martyrdom at the hands of Ziyad ibn Abi Sufyan. The program then covered the Battle of Nahrawan, initiated by the Kharijites, and Imam Ali's role in conquering the fortress of Khaybar to secure Medina. The claims regarding Zuleikha's rejuvenation and her marriage to Prophet Joseph were evaluated as weak and doubtful narrations. Dr. Sadeghi refuted the narration suggesting that Prophet Joseph committed a sin that severed prophethood from his lineage, emphasizing Quranic teachings on respecting parents instead. He also explained the identity of Habib al-Najjar as the Believer of the Family of Yasin, detailing his story of faith and martyrdom in Antioch. Finally, the episode recounted the rescue of a besieged group of Imam Ali's companions during the Battle of Siffin, facilitated by the strategic plan of Abd al-Aziz ibn Haris al-Ju'fi.
