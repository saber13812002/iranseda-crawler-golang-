The program begins by attributing recent economic crises and bankruptcies in advanced countries to the entry of illicit income into the cycle of sustenance. In the main segment, Hujjat al-Islam Vahidi defines "tawakkul" (reliance on God) as the key to achieving high spiritual ranks and inner peace, citing various narrations. He emphasizes that true reliance does not mean abandoning one's duties but rather having absolute certainty in God's influence while still fulfilling responsibilities. The story of Prophet Ibrahim in the fire and the personal experience of Ayatollah Shahid Ahmad Tehrani in marrying with minimal resources are presented as practical examples of this concept. Furthermore, Imam Khomeini's calm demeanor and trust in divine support during the Iran-Iraq War and security threats are highlighted as a model of strong reliance. The episode concludes with an introduction to the novel "Let the Wind Blow," which narrates the life of Har ibn Yazid, accompanied by a reading of an excerpt from the book.
