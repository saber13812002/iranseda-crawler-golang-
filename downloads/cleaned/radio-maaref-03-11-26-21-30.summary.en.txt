The program began by dedicating the documentary "Bey-Neshan" to the anonymous forces of the Intelligence Ministry, followed by a detailed discussion on the fate of Jews during the era of the Mahdi. Relying on the interpretation of the Quranic verse "And to Him submit those in the heavens and the earth," narrations from Imam Kazim and Imam Sadiq were cited, indicating that Jews and other non-Muslims would either convert to Islam or be eliminated. A portion of Imam Ali's "Hidden Sermon" was also analyzed, referencing celestial signs and calls from the East and West that precede the appearance of the Imam. Both Shia and Sunni scholars interpret the verse regarding the "People of the Book" as referring to the descent of Jesus and the killing of the Dajjal, which leads to the conversion of the Jews. A key point of discussion was distinguishing between faith at the moment of death, which is futile, and faith during the period of accountability, which is accepted in the era of the Mahdi. The conclusion drawn is that during the Mahdi's appearance, Jews and other deviant groups will either embrace Islam or cease to exist. The program concluded by introducing the "Historical Puzzle" segment, which examines reliable historical sources from the pre-Islamic era and early Islamic history.
