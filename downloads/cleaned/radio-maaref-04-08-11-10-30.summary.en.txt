The radio program "Shifa" highlights the importance of foot massage in Islamic traditional medicine for alleviating fatigue and improving blood circulation among the elderly. It recommends using simple household items like rolling pins or glass bottles to perform gentle foot massages, which prepares the body for light exercises and prevents the accumulation of waste materials. The primary goal of these methods is to restore vitality and natural mobility to older individuals. Subsequently, the program "One Drop from the Sea" interprets verse 29 of Surah An-Nisa to discuss the prohibition of hoarding and ethical trade practices. The commentator cites Nahj al-Balagha to identify hoarding as a cause of inflation and economic injustice, urging officials to monitor the market. These segments demonstrate that adhering to religious and rational principles is essential for enhancing quality of life in both health and economics.
