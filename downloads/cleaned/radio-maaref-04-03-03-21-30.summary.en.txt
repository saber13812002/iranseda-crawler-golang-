In this program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets the Quranic concept of the "straight path" not as an external route, but as the very essence and spiritual attributes of the human self. He argues that the traveler and the path are one; if a person's beliefs, ethics, and actions are true, they themselves become the path. A central theme is the profound link between self-knowledge and knowing God, as forgetting God leads to forgetting oneself and ultimately results in spiritual blindness. This internal blindness manifests physically in the Hereafter, causing the book of deeds to be handed to the left hand. Conversely, remembering God and being grateful illuminates the inner eye and makes one's existence radiant. Piety and inner vigilance cure the blindness of the heart, ensuring that a person remains a guide and seer in the afterlife. Therefore, the ultimate goal of spiritual journeying is to return to the remembrance of God to avoid blindness and misguidance.
