In this radio program, the head of the Zourkhaneh and Pahlavani Wrestling Federation in South Khorasan province reported the successful hosting of a junior tournament, where the Isfahan team emerged as the champion. He emphasized the importance of social responsibility and utilizing the facilities of the Islamic Azad University to host national events, while thanking the provincial sports department for their cooperation. In the second segment, the interim president of the national federation announced that he would not run for election, focusing instead on holding a legal assembly and organizing international competitions in Urmia. He expressed satisfaction with the Ministry of Sports' support for developing the sport across various age groups and improving family engagement. The program concluded with a sports history segment reviewing events from August 15, including the passing of Gerd Müller and the Russian team's victory in cycling.
