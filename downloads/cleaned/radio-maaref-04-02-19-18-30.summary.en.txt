In the radio program "Zamzam Ahkam," religious rulings were provided in response to listeners' questions based on the fatwas of various Maraji. Hujjat al-Islam Mahdavi, citing Ayatollah Sistani's fatwa, stated that observing hijab is obligatory for women when reciting the Quran or making du'a. Regarding the difference between missed obligatory prayers and voluntary prayers, Hujjat al-Islam Mohammadi Sheikh Shabani explained that missed prayers are obligatory and require adhan and iqama, whereas voluntary prayers are recommended and can be performed without them, even while sitting. Hujjat al-Islam Jawaheri, referencing Ayatollah Shabiri Zanjani, clarified that the order of washing the right and left hands in wudu is not significant, and washing the entire body with the intention of wudu is sufficient. On the topic of Khums, Hujjat al-Islam Alian Najat emphasized that any financial surplus acquired by an individual is subject to Khums payment at the end of the year. Furthermore, Hujjat al-Islam Fallahzade, citing Ayatollah Khamenei, ruled that no one can perform fasting, whether obligatory or recommended, on behalf of a living person. The program concluded by announcing contact channels for submitting religious questions and playing a religious song.
