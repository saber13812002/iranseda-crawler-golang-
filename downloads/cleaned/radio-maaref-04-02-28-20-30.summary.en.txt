The program begins by introducing the book "Mizan al-Hikmah," which was initiated by Ayatollah Rishahri to promote interfaith dialogue by compiling shared wisdoms of Jesus (PBUH) from Islamic and Christian sources. This work, which started in the basement of Rishahri's home with initial support from Imam Khomeini, gained international recognition and led to the establishment of "Dar al-Hadith" and Iran's first virtual hadith education system. The second part of the show features the memoirs of Abdullah Rajabi, a veteran and former POW of the Iran-Iraq War, who recounts the severe hardships, physical torture, and human rights violations he endured in Iraqi camps. He describes the difficult interrogations and his intelligent responses regarding Iran's military structure, as well as the severe injuries and inadequate medical care he received during his captivity. The program concludes with a review of the book "You Are a General," which chronicles the life of Davoud Ameri, a veteran and mountaineer who achieved global success in sports after losing a leg in the war. The broadcast ends by emphasizing the spirit of freedom and resilience in the face of adversity.
