In the radio program "Persaman Quran," Professor Maleki addressed listeners' questions regarding Quranic concepts. He interpreted the verse "We created man in hardship" to explain that life's challenges are a prerequisite for spiritual growth and perfection, serving as a test of God's benevolence. Regarding divine guardianship, he distinguished between general mercy (Rahman), which encompasses all creation, and specific mercy (Rahim), which is exclusive to believers and involves special divine support and guidance. The rank of Imamate was described as superior to Prophethood, rooted in servitude and possessing metaphysical dimensions, even though the Quran primarily highlights the Prophet's role as a messenger. The professor also clarified the difference between "fixed death" (Ajel Musammay) and "suspended death" (Ajel Mu'allaq), noting that sins can hasten death while righteous acts like charity and maintaining family ties can extend life. Finally, he outlined the boundaries of kindness to parents, emphasizing respect and obedience in permissible matters, while clarifying that one must not obey parents if their commands contradict divine laws or religious obligations.
