The Supreme Council of the Quran is holding its twentieth specialized session focused on the requirements for Iranian Quran reciters. Interested individuals can submit their articles and ideas for this event by the end of the month of Mehr. For further details, the council's official website is the recommended source of information. Another segment of the program offers practical tips for reducing water consumption at home. Installing low-flow faucets can decrease kitchen water usage by up to fifty percent. Washing fruits and vegetables in a basin of water rather than under a running tap is an effective conservation method. It is also advised to use rice-cooking water for plant irrigation and to thaw frozen foods in the refrigerator overnight. Washing dishes in a basin with a small amount of detergent and avoiding unnecessary pre-rinsing are additional strategies to prevent water waste.
