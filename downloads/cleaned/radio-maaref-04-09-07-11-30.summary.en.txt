In the radio program "Persaman Mahdavi," expert Mehdi Yousefian addressed questions regarding the signs of the Mahdi's appearance and the role of industry. He emphasized that the appearance is a divine phenomenon that cannot be predicted solely by analyzing current events, as God values the process and human effort over immediate results. Citing the story of Prophet Yusuf and Ya'qub, he explained that a true awaiter is one who actively fulfills their worldly and social duties while maintaining spiritual anticipation, rather than passively waiting. Regarding industry during the era of the Mahdi, he noted that while scientific progress will experience a significant leap, the direction of industry will shift from material pleasure-seeking to serving spiritual and societal needs. Consequently, wasteful or harmful industries will be replaced by technologies dedicated to the well-being and spiritual welfare of the people. Finally, he stressed the importance of strengthening the country's scientific and industrial capabilities to prepare for the civilizational space preceding the appearance.
