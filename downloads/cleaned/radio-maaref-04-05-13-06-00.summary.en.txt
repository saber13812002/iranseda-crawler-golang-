This program analyzes the story of Prophet Noah and the flooding of his people through the interpretation of the Quran.
After receiving divine revelation that no more people would believe, Noah was commanded to build an ark and curse the disbelievers.
During the flood, Noah's son was excluded from salvation and drowned because he had not performed righteous deeds.
After his son's drowning, Noah asked a question that was interpreted as a scientific inquiry seeking clarification rather than an objection.
God responded that his son was not part of his family because he lacked righteousness, and that was not the time for discussion.
This analysis clarifies that Noah's question was neither a protest against God's decree nor a request for intercession, but a genuine inquiry.
Ultimately, God praised Noah with a unique salutation found nowhere else in the Quran, highlighting his humility and submission to divine will.
