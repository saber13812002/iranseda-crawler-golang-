In this program, Professor Rabani Golepayegani provides a theological analysis of the Prophet's (PBUH) delay in announcing the Imamate of Amir al-Mu'minin (AS). He argues that this delay was not a violation of infallibility but was executed with divine knowledge and permission. According to narrations, the Prophet (PBUH) initially refrained from immediate announcement to prevent hypocrites from exploiting the situation or suggesting personal motives. The apparent divine rebuke served to completely exonerate the Prophet's station from any worldly or personal intentions, proving his absolute obedience. In the third stage, upon the revelation of the verse commanding the delivery of the message, the Prophet (PBUH) officially announced the Imamate at Ghadir Khumm. Consequently, the Prophet's actions were entirely in accordance with divine wisdom, leaving no room for any claim of negligence or error.
