The radio program began by discussing the pre-Islamic practice of "Nasi'" and how some tribes attempted to circumvent the sanctity of forbidden months to wage war. It then addressed the claim that Lady Zainab (SA) made a condition to stay with Imam Hussein (AS), rejecting this narrative due to a lack of reliable historical evidence. A significant portion of the discussion focused on Rabia, the wife of Imam Sajjad (AS), confirming her presence in Karbala, the martyrdom of her son Ali Azhari, and her death in Medina one year after the event. The host also refuted historical inaccuracies regarding the age of Lady Sakina (SA) and her alleged marriage to Qasim (AS) during the tragedy. Additionally, the story of Zahhak was clarified as a symbolic myth related to the Damavand volcano rather than a historical fact. The program concluded by introducing the "Ma'aref-yaran" segment and playing poems and recitations related to Muharram, including criticism of Israel.
