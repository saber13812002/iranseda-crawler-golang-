This episode of the Parsayan program focuses on the importance of gratitude towards God and appreciation for the efforts of others. Citing narrations from the Infallibles, it emphasizes that one who does not thank people for their kindness cannot truly thank God. To illustrate this, a story is shared about Ayatollah Mohaghegh Damad, who recited a Fatiha daily for three individuals: his teacher in Yazd, his teacher in Qom, and a shoe-maker who financially supported him during his poverty. Another anecdote involves Ayatollah Shahid Al-Hashim, who, as the head of the army's ideological affairs, respectfully helped a common soldier, demonstrating the virtue of valuing others' contributions. These stories highlight that acknowledging the kindness of ordinary people is a noble character trait. The program concludes with the recitation of a prayer of gratitude and religious songs, reinforcing the necessity of being thankful for both divine and human blessings.
