The broadcast began with a live report from Karbala, highlighting the spiritual atmosphere and crowds during the Arbaeen pilgrimage. It then transitioned to a documentary titled "Window," which focused on the life and works of Shahid Sani, a prominent Shia scholar from the tenth century AH. The program emphasized his book "Maskan al-Fuad," written to provide solace and patience to those grieving the loss of loved ones. Two Persian translations of this book were introduced, both of which were undertaken by translators who had personally experienced the death of their children. One translator, Seyed Mohammad Maber, completed the work during the Iran-Iraq war to support the bereaved. The other, Abdolhamid Vastehi, began translating the book just two weeks after his daughter passed away from leukemia. The book offers practical spiritual guidance based on Quranic verses and narrations to help individuals find peace and accept God's will during crises. The episode concluded with a preview of upcoming programs, including a Quranic exegesis session by Ayatollah Javadi Amoli.
