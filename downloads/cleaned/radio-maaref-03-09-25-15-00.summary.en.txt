This radio program discussed the legal and jurisprudential frameworks for sentencing artificial intelligence systems. The four main punishment approaches—retribution, deterrence, rehabilitation, and incapacitation—were introduced, categorized in jurisprudence as either punitive or educational. Iranian legislation has recently shifted from purely punitive measures toward rehabilitation and alternatives to imprisonment. A key distinction was made between weak and strong AI; while weak AI can be technically reprogrammed, strong AI requires psychological and educational approaches due to its autonomous learning capabilities. It was emphasized that AI systems, with their access to big data, bear greater legal responsibilities and duties than ordinary humans. Consequently, punishments must be proportionate to the system's knowledge level and capabilities rather than just its apparent actions. Ultimately, the goal of punishment should be to uphold justice, protect social rights, and correct system behavior within jurisprudential principles.
