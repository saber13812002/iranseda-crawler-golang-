Saudi Arabia launched a major expansion project for the Grand Mosque, aiming to increase its capacity to 900,000 worshippers to establish Mecca as a global destination. Cultural events included international Quran recitation competitions in Gaza and Iraq, as well as an interfaith ethics conference that resulted in the publication of 18 joint books. The International Federation for Human Rights warned about the suppression of free speech and the persecution of Palestine supporters in Western and Arab nations. The Israeli regime has repeatedly violated the Gaza ceasefire and kept crossings closed, thereby obstructing the entry of humanitarian aid. Palestinian officials have estimated the cost of rebuilding the Gaza Strip at 100 billion dollars. The United Nations criticized Israel's continued obstruction of aid and described the humanitarian situation in Gaza as dire. Yemeni armed forces announced the martyrdom of their army chief of staff, reaffirming their commitment to resistance.
