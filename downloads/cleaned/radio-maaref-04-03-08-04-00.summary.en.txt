The radio program "Zamzam Ahkam" began by addressing various jurisprudential questions from followers of different marjas. The first segment clarified the obligation to pay khums on saved salaries intended for purchases and the conditions for valid wudu when the head is slightly wet. It also discussed the necessity of combining intention with action when renouncing one's homeland and the requirement to compensate for inflation when repaying old debts. The "Sarmash" segment then analyzed Nahj al-Balagha to identify the traits of God's most beloved servants, emphasizing humility, fear of the afterlife, and simplicity. This section highlighted the duty to support the infallible Imam through the heart, tongue, and actions. Finally, the "Andisheh" program featured a scholar discussing Imam Khomeini's exegetical views on Surah Al-Fatiha and the philosophical significance of its names.
