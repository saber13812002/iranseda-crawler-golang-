The program begins with a Quranic exegesis emphasizing that the Torah and Gospel predicted the Prophet Muhammad's specific characteristics.
The commentator argues that the People of the Book recognized the Prophet as clearly as they know their own children, yet they concealed the truth.
The discussion then expands to theological debates on monotheism and the denial of any partner to God, citing the views of the Shia Imams.
Subsequently, the show acknowledges daily occasions, including World Heart Day and the commemoration of martyred commanders from the Holy Defense.
The second segment focuses on the theme of "Being Kind," specifically addressing the metaphor of firefighting in spiritual and social life.
Guests and listeners discuss how to extinguish the fires of anger, conflict, and disputes through the power of words and patience.
The broadcast concludes with listener messages and an article about finding life's radiance during difficult times.
