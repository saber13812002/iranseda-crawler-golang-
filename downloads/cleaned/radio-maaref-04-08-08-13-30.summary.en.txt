The radio program "Narrative of Love" began by honoring martyrs and introduced the story of the martyr cleric Kamal Ghasemi. His sister, Shamsi Ghasemi, recounted her childhood memories and the profound influence her brother had on her religious and personal development. She described his decision to join the seminary in Tabriz, which was initially opposed by their father but eventually accepted. Ghasemi shared poignant memories of her brother's frequent deployments to the front lines during the Iran-Iraq War and their difficult farewells. She noted that he was martyred in 1988 after enduring four years of captivity and torture following the Badr Operation. In the final segment, a political analyst criticized the JCPOA as a failed agreement and deemed negotiations with the United States futile and dangerous. He emphasized that trust in the US is impossible due to its consistent breach of commitments regarding nuclear and regional issues.
