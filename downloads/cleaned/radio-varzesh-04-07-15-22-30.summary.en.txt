Veteran sports administrator Hassan Mirza Beik expressed dissatisfaction with his recent appointment to a position equivalent to the Deputy of Administrative and Financial Affairs, viewing it as an oversight of his 40-year career. He highlighted his successful management of Takhti Stadium and the Iran Olympiad while criticizing the sports system's lack of planning and reliance on money laundering. Mirza Beik detailed his decade-long efforts to elevate rugby to World Federation membership and win Asian medals, while also exposing political maneuvering that led to his defeat in the basketball federation elections. He recounted a unique historical incident where he awarded original gold medals to top basketball players in 2000, emphasizing the rarity of such a gesture. The administrator argued that Olympic success requires rigorous training and proper structure rather than just financial investment, lamenting the absence of performance review committees after the Paris Olympics. Despite various offers, he affirmed his integrity and stated that he would run for the next basketball federation election if the conditions permitted.
