In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Mohammad Hadi Falahat identifies stinginess as a theological root that fundamentally damages a person's faith and worship. Relying on religious narrations, he describes stinginess as the hub of all moral vices that creates severe challenges in both individual life and organizational management. Key signs of a stingy person include making excuses to avoid spending and disrespecting others' needs, which ultimately leaves the recipient feeling cold and undervalued. The expert also notes that stingy individuals often rely on free prayers and feigned generosity to escape actual financial costs, and that a partner's stinginess can be a critical factor in marital failure. In the second segment, Hujjat al-Islam Bagherian discusses the worldly effects of actions, emphasizing that arrogance and boasting are significant factors that cause one to lose divine favor.
