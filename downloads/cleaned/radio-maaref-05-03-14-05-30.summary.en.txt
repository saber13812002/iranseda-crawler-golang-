He demonstrated absolute bravery in battle, never showing weakness or hesitation, even as a young man. This innate courage meant that Imam Ali did not rely on the presence of others to feel secure in combat. Finally, the broadcast ends with prayers for the early return of the Imam of the Age and blessings upon the Ahl al-Bayt.
