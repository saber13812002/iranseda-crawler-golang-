This radio program discussed the vibrant growth of Zourkhaneh sports in Rafsanjan and Kerman province.
The local official in Rafsanjan reported the existence of about seventy active Zourkhanehs and municipal support for constructing two new facilities.
The Rafsanjan team successfully won the "Ethics Cup" at the first Junior Festival held in Hamedan.
The Hamedan federation head expressed satisfaction with the event's success, noting the participation of twenty provinces and the emphasis on social responsibility.
The federation's acting president highlighted the sport's expansion within families and announced upcoming competitions in Qom.
He also stated his decision not to run for the federation presidency, focusing instead on supporting the sport's development nationwide.
Finally, the program emphasized the role of Nahj al-Balagha in ethical training and the importance of learning from other coaches' experiences.
