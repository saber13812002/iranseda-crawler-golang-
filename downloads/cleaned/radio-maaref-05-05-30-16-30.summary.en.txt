In the first segment, the guest scholar discussed the high status of Imam Abbas and Hazrat Zainab, citing historical sources and the words of Imam Hussein and Imam Sajjad.
The discussion then covered the historical incident of the Black Stone being stolen by the Qarmatian group and its return after twenty-two years.
The program also addressed the religious rulings regarding vows and sacrifices, emphasizing the importance of fulfilling commitments and avoiding extremism.
The second part featured a dramatization based on a real criminal case where a couple drugged an elderly woman to steal her jewelry.
Tragically, the elderly woman died due to the heat and her age, while the perpetrators fled the scene after the theft.
The legal analyst highlighted three main crimes committed: incapacitation, theft, and involuntary manslaughter.
Finally, the method of identifying the suspects through surveillance cameras and bank records was explained to the listeners.
