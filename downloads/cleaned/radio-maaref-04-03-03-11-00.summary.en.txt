The news broadcast on the Ma'aref network commemorated the third of Khordad as the day of Khorramshahr's liberation and a symbol of resistance during the Operation Beit al-Moqaddas. International news reported on the ongoing operation to transport Iranian pilgrims to the Holy Land, noting satisfaction with the flight schedule. The first edition of the "Eight" short story literary prize, focused on human dignity and the life of Imam Reza (PBUH), was also announced. In cultural news, the film "Sabh Adam" directed by Behrouz Afghami, depicting the final moments of martyrs Teib and Esmail Rezaei, was unveiled. A significant incident involved the shooting of Hojjat al-Islam Samadi, a deputy at the Imam al-Mu'minin school in Iranshahr, prompting serious investigation by religious authorities. Socially, the "Golrizan" social celebration was held in Fars province to support working children and female heads of households. Finally, the results of the Resistance Film Festival were announced, with the film "Majnoon" leading the nominations with twelve awards.
