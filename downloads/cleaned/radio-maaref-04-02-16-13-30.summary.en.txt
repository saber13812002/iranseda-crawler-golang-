In this radio program, Professor Seyed Ahmad Faghihi emphasizes the importance of recognizing one's profound need for God to improve behavior, based on the concept of "Tawhidic ethics." He explains that humans are not one-dimensional beings and must address both worldly and spiritual needs in their prayers. Key spiritual needs include seeking God's mercy at the moment of death and on the Day of Judgment, as detailed in prayers like the Dua of Abu Hamza Thumali. In response to a listener's question, the first step in spiritual progress is identified as achieving a state of "awakening" or awareness from material heedlessness. Professor Faghihi highlights prayer as the primary factor that instills and maintains this state of attention and wakefulness. He also clarifies the fundamental difference between visiting ordinary graves and the shrines of the Infallibles, noting that the latter is for receiving divine grace and expressing dependence, not just for reciting blessings. Finally, the program touches on political and security issues, stressing the necessity of unity and practical action by the Islamic world against oppression.
