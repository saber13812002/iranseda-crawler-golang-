The program begins with religious supplications and an announcement for the 20th national Quran exam, followed by a dramatic retelling from the book "In the Ambush of the Red Rose." The narrative focuses on the life of General Ali Siyazi Shirazi and the security challenges in Kurdistan following the Islamic Revolution. Despite the revolution's victory, separatist attempts to create division led to the fall of military bases and the martyrdom of 52 guards in Sardasht. Shirazi, alongside Dr. Chamran, was dispatched to the region to assess the security situation, where he witnessed Chamran's bravery at the front lines. The segment concludes with Imam Khomeini emphasizing Iran's unique independence from superpowers and the necessity of continuing the struggle to maintain this freedom.
