The primary challenge in sports news broadcasting is accurately pronouncing foreign names and maintaining a positive demeanor even when delivering negative news. The speaker believes that sports culture should transcend mere winning and losing to become a source of joy for the public. While he considers the new generation of broadcasters competent, he advocates for merit-based hiring through public exams to ensure equal opportunities for talented individuals without connections. After 25 years in media, he entered the eighth parliament from the Izeh and Baghmalak district, driven by a desire to address macro-level sports issues. His most significant legislative achievement was the establishment of the Ministry of Sports and Youth, although he opposed merging the youth sector into this ministry. He criticizes Iran's lack of a clear sports strategy and the continued reliance on external figures to manage sports federations instead of developing internal human resources. Furthermore, he emphasizes that parliamentarians should focus more on oversight of the government's performance rather than solely on legislation.
