The radio program began by honoring the memory of Martyr Hujjat al-Islam Sharif Qanouti, a pious cleric known for his humility and dedication to social justice.
The narrator shared anecdotes about the martyr's eloquent speech, his efforts to mediate local disputes, and his deep compassion for the underprivileged.
The second part of the program focused on the harrowing experiences of Iranian POWs in Iraqi camps during the war.
A significant account detailed the escape of two prisoners from the camp's medical section and the collective resistance of the captives against forced shoulder-patting during the exchange.
The prisoners maintained their dignity by refusing to comply with degrading orders and by performing congregational prayers in the presence of armed Iraqi soldiers.
Ultimately, the freed captives returned to their homeland with their honor intact, receiving a warm welcome from the public.
The program concluded by highlighting the freedom fighters as models of patience and resilience, inviting listeners to share their feedback.
