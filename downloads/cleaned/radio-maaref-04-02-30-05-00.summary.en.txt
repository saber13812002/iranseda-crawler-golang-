This radio program identifies prayer as the most critical factor for preserving happiness in this world and the hereafter, as well as preventing moral deviation.
The Quran and narrations emphasize that prayer should not be a burdensome routine but must be performed with full presence of mind, in mosques, and in congregation.
According to a narration from Imam Sadiq (AS), prayer is the most important practice after the principles of faith, and the Quran's use of the verb "establish" underscores the need for its vitality in society.
People, especially the youth, must avoid negligence during prayer times, as abandoning prayer is considered "practical disbelief" that deprives an individual of divine protection.
The second part of the discussion addresses the jurisprudential aspect of the prohibition against changing the use of endowments for mosques and shrines.
According to the presented jurisprudential view, endowed assets such as carpets and buildings must be used strictly according to the donor's intent and in the specific location designated for the endowment.
Converting a mosque into a warehouse or school, or renting it out, is contrary to the principle of endowment and is religiously impermissible.
Therefore, prayer must be the center of one's spiritual life, while endowed assets must be preserved and utilized strictly within the framework of the donor's original stipulations.
