The program began by highlighting the crucial role of youth in Iran's development and the need for officials to support the people.
Hojjat al-Islam Fallah, referencing Ustad Ali, emphasized the necessity of training competent managers and soldiers for the future government of Imam Zaman.
He argued that Shia Muslims must establish a government before the Imam's return to practice management skills and loyalty to the deputy of the Imam.
The main discussion focused on the dangerous vice of gossiping and rumor-mongering, which Imam Ali (AS) identified as a cause of resentment and distance from God.
The speaker detailed the severe moral and religious consequences of this behavior, including disgrace, exclusion from Paradise, and the manifestation of sins in the Hereafter.
He cited narrations stating that gossiping leads to bloodshed, the destruction of homes, and severe punishments in the afterlife.
Suggested remedies included increasing awareness, abandoning envy and hypocrisy, adhering to Sharia, and avoiding earning money through spreading rumors.
Finally, the speaker advised learning from the bad behavior of others to avoid this moral flaw and maintain ethical conduct.
