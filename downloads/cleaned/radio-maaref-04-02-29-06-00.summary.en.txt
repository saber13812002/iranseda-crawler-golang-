This program begins with Ayatollah Javadi Amoli's interpretation of verse 105 of Surah Al-Ma'idah, emphasizing the concept of "watch over yourselves."
Citing the hadith "Whoever knows himself knows his Lord," the commentator explains that self-knowledge is a prerequisite for knowing God, and forgetting God leads to forgetting one's true self.
The internal structure of the human soul is analyzed by detailing its various levels, including the commanding, reproaching, tranquil, and deceptive souls.
The deceptive soul traps humans by beautifying sins, after which the commanding soul takes control and issues orders.
The primary responsibility lies with the "true self" or innate nature, which must supervise these internal forces.
The ultimate goal of the spiritual journey is the meeting with God, which signifies reaching infinite divine perfection and witnessing the Divine.
In this path, the human being is simultaneously the traveler, the path, and the destination, with piety serving as provisions and love as the vehicle for progress.
