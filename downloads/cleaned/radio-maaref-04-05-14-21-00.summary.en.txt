The program begins by interpreting the Quranic verse on charity, emphasizing the necessity of sending spiritual capital to the Day of Judgment by helping the poor.
Stories of the simplicity and generosity of Akhund Khorasani are shared, such as selling a gifted expensive cloak to distribute its value among students.
Despite his financial means, this religious authority drank salty water until all people in Najaf could access fresh water.
The second part of the program narrates a historical account from the book "Let Your Wound Bleed," continuing the story of the siege of Karbala.
Har ibn Yazid Rayahi, after receiving a threatening letter from Ubayd Allah, visits Imam Hussein's tent to seek reassurance.
Upon hearing the name of the land, Karbala, the Imam seeks refuge in God and purchases the land from its local inhabitants.
After witnessing the Imam's firm resolve and Khalid's return, Har, filled with fear of defeat and despair, issues orders to prepare for war.
