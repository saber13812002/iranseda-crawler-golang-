In this radio program, the Ayatollah emphasizes the importance of Tawhid and Wilayat in shaping a person's life and their final moments.
He explains, based on hadiths, that performing obligatory prayers on time is a crucial factor in illuminating the state of death and repelling Satan.
According to narrations, the Angel of Death reviews human destinies during prayer times, reassuring worshippers with the declaration of faith.
It is reported that Ayatollah Qomi, a marja, passed away by saying "The cursed one is gone" and reciting Tawhid, demonstrating the power of faith against Satan's whispers.
While Satan attempts to instill fear through wailing, angels comfort the believer's soul with good news and pleasant scents.
Maintaining family ties and donating clothes to the poor are also recommended as effective means to strengthen faith and prepare for death.
Finally, he advises listening to sermons and avoiding Satan's deceptions in religious settings to preserve spiritual health.
