In the radio program "Morning and Life," the host greeted listeners in Chaharmahal and Bakhtiari and Ilam provinces in Qashqai, Luri, and Persian, highlighting the shared cultural and geographical ties between these regions. The guest discussed Ilam's natural and historical beauty, emphasizing its significant role as a host for Shia pilgrims traveling to Karbala. Citing religious narrations, the guest argued that visiting the shrine of Lady Zainab (SA) is not merely recommended but obligatory and should be performed frequently, such as weekly or monthly. The main objective was to foster a culture of continuous pilgrimage outside of the Arbaeen period, ensuring Ilam remains a hub for visitors throughout the year. The guest also reported on the province's infrastructure developments, including gas distribution to villages and the smart transformation of schools. The program concluded with a religious song and prayers for the people of Ilam.
