The radio program begins by highlighting a mosque's successful strategy for engaging youth in the Quran through incentives and active family participation.
It emphasizes that shared recitation at home and understanding the Quran's meanings are crucial for fostering spiritual peace and interest in children.
The discussion then shifts to the concept of "slander" from a Quranic perspective, focusing primarily on Surah Al-Mumtahanah, verse 12.
The interpretation reveals that slander acts as a major barrier to establishing a spiritual covenant with the Prophet and receiving answered prayers.
God defended the status of believing women against social misconceptions of that era by setting conditions such as avoiding sin and refraining from slander.
Several verses from Surah An-Nisa, An-Nur, Al-Ahzab, and Al-Mumtahanah were cited to help listeners better understand this dangerous moral vice.
The program concluded with prayers for the listeners and an invitation to share feedback via the hotline number 162.
