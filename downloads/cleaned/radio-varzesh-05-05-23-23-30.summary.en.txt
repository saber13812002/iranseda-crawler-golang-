Veteran sports photographer Hassan Sabri shared his memories of legendary Iranian football and wrestling figures in a radio interview. He emphasized the importance of athletes recognizing their own worth, citing the tragic potential of talented players who did not reach their full height. Sabri advised aspiring sports photographers that genuine passion for the profession is the most critical factor for success. In the second segment, Mohabbat Karimi, head coach of the women's national wushu team, reported that the team is 80 percent ready for the Nagoya Asian Games. She identified China, South Korea, and Malaysia as the primary rivals, noting the extreme difficulty of the Taolu category due to precise judging. Karimi highlighted the team's focus on increasing the difficulty of their forms and utilizing a sports psychologist to help athletes manage pre-competition stress.
