In this radio program, the analyst emphasizes the Supreme Leader's firm stance, viewing nuclear negotiations as an opportunity to demonstrate Iran's good faith and achieve a temporary halt to sanctions. However, citing a history of distrust toward the United States, he rejects false hopes for the complete lifting of sanctions and stresses the need for economic independence to prevent internal unrest. The discussion also assesses the Axis of Resistance as remaining powerful despite the martyrdom of Hassan Nasrallah, noting that its strength is rooted in popular support and ethical constraints on civilian casualties, unlike Israel's tactics. The analyst argues that internal political differences and economic reliance on negotiations are the primary risks for potential internal strife, which can be mitigated by focusing on domestic production. Finally, the program highlights a positive social initiative in Tehran, where a free health insurance scheme has been implemented for 2,800 working children and their families, marking a significant step in social support and empowerment for this vulnerable group.
