In this program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli, while interpreting the Surah Al-Mu'ajjiz, emphasizes the unity and completeness of Islam, rejecting the notion that religion is limited solely to worship and ethics. He argues that reason is one of the four sources of religion and stands against transmitted texts, not against religion itself; thus, rational decisions in social administration carry religious weight. The concept of the Jurist's Guardianship (Velayat-e Faqih) is distinguished from the general completeness of religion, highlighting the necessity of a qualified jurist to manage political and social affairs. This jurist must be the "most knowledgeable about the present situation," possessing not only jurisprudential knowledge but also awareness of management, politics, and current events. In response to objections, it is explained that scientific experts provide expertise, but final decisions must be made by the jurist based on the fixed principles of religion. The necessity of a unified leadership in the Islamic system is stressed, noting that power cannot be divided among multiple jurists at the macro level. Finally, the Supreme Leader's consultation with the Expediency Discernment Council is presented as a legal and rational requirement for formulating general policies.
