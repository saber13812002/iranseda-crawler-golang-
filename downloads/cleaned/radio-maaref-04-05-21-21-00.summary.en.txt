The broadcast began by acknowledging a tragic event, emphasizing the need for spiritual resilience in the face of life's hardships. The main segment, titled "Parsaiyan," featured insights from Ayatollah Vahidi highlighting the spiritual significance of night prayers and midnight worship. Various narrations from Imam Sadiq and the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) were cited to describe night prayer as a special divine favor and a mark of honor for the faithful. Stories of prominent scholars like Akhund Khorasani and Sheikh Ansari were shared to illustrate that spiritual devotion complements rather than conflicts with academic excellence. Anecdotes involving Ayatollah Golpayegani and Ayatollah Javadi Amoli further underscored the high value these scholars placed on night worship. The program then transitioned to the fourth episode of the audio book "Seyyed Man Hosseini," narrating a young teacher's journey to Karbala and his eventual reunion with his cultural group after border delays. This section stressed the importance of building friendships with non-Iranian audiences to promote the global Husseini front against contemporary Yazidis. The broadcast concluded with an introduction to Ayatollah Javadi Amoli's interpretation of verse 120 of Surah Al-Ma'idah in the "Barkarane Noor" program.
