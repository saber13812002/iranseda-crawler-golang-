The 32nd National Prayer Establishment Conference commenced with a message from the Supreme Leader, while pre-registration for the 1405 Hajj pilgrimage began on the 20th of Mehr. The Innovation and Flourishing Fund reported a 146 percent growth in financial services to knowledge-based companies, totaling over 19 trillion tomans in the first half of the year. The head of Iran's Space Research Institute announced that the Nahid 2 satellite will be launched into space using a domestic launcher by the end of autumn. Cultural events included the fourth "Iran Jan" national event in Fars province and the first National Conference on Digital Islamic Sciences in Qom. Additionally, the National I'tikaf Congress was held in Mashhad with representatives from over sixty countries. News reports indicated that the death toll in Gaza has reached 67,160 since the start of the war. The program also honored the IRGC Week, paying tribute to the martyrs of the Islamic Republic of Iran's Police Force. Finally, the broadcast featured memoirs of former POWs from the Iran-Iraq War and an excerpt from the book "Taj al-Wisak."
