This radio program is broadcast from the Mehran border terminal, capturing the spiritual atmosphere of Friday night and the presence of Arbaeen pilgrims.
The guest discusses the spiritual impact of the pilgrimage on personal and social life, highlighting the central role of love for Imam Hussein in cultivating courageous and self-sacrificing individuals.
Citing religious narrations, he describes the pilgrimage as a "human-making factory" that purifies both past and future sins.
To fully benefit from the pilgrimage's rewards, three steps are deemed essential: sincerity of intention, adherence to rituals, and integrating the experience into one's daily actions.
The guest shares a moving anecdote about a devoted servant's loyalty to Imam Hussein, illustrating the depth of Shia devotion.
Finally, the program emphasizes the equal spiritual merit of pilgrims and servants, expressing hope that all listeners will be granted the opportunity to visit Karbala.
