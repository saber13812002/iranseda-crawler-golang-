Former national team and Pas FC player Mohsen Gharavi recounts his memories of his career in Iranian football during this interview. He discusses the frequent omissions from the national team roster and the tense conditions of that era, including conflicts with coaching staff and managers like Vira and Parvin. Gharavi details key matches in the 1998 World Cup qualifiers and the Asian Cup, highlighting tactical risks and critical decisions made during those games. He also shares his experience of military service at Pas Tehran and the club's victory in the 1997 AFC Champions League. Gharavi speaks about his financial decisions and transfers to Sepahan and Esteghlal, expressing dissatisfaction with certain technical and managerial conditions of the time. Finally, he reflects on his coaching career and the reasons for stepping away from the profession, emphasizing the importance of health and peace in life.
