On the sixth day of Muharram, mourning ceremonies were held in Karbala, London, and other global locations. Egypt's Ministry of Awqaf closed the shrine of Imam Hussein's head in Cairo on Ashura for maintenance and to prevent potential gatherings. In Kuwait, mourning rituals were conducted in 57 schools instead of husainiyyas due to security concerns. Four employees of a Turkish magazine were arrested for publishing a blasphemous caricature of the Prophet Muhammad. Iran condemned the violation of its airspace by the Zionist regime and filed a complaint with the UN Security Council. Officials and citizens in Afghanistan and Malaysia expressed solidarity with Iran by visiting embassies and signing condolence books. Reports from Syria detailed the detention of Shia Muslims in Sayyida Zaynab and the massacre of Alawites in March. The broadcast concluded with a documentary about the life and social services of Sheikh Mehdi Mazaheri, an Isfahan cleric.
