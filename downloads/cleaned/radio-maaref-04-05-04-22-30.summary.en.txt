In this radio program, Professor Maleki emphasizes the critical importance of engaging with the Quran, citing its role in intercession and complaint on the Day of Judgment. He references narrations from Imam Sadiq (AS) regarding the three ledgers of deeds in the afterlife, highlighting how the Quran can compensate for sins. The speaker advises Muslims to recite fifty verses daily and warns against relying on sorcery or superstition instead of God's word. The latter part of the discussion focuses on the Quranic characteristics of the companions of Sayyid and Shahada (AS), viewing their actions in Karbala as a practical manifestation of verses related to steadfastness and faith. Maleki notes that the Ahl al-Bayt (AS) serve as the practical interpreters of the Quran, making Karbala a profound arena for understanding the struggle between truth and falsehood. The program concludes with the recitation of page 506 of the Quran, which marks the end of Surah Al-Hijr.
