In the radio program "Until the Friday of Appearance," Professor Hassan Mahmoudi discussed the spiritual dangers of the long occultation of Imam Mahdi (AJ), specifically the concept of "Qasi al-Qalb" or a heart distant from God. He emphasized that neglecting the duties of a true awaiter can lead to spiritual alienation, citing Quranic verses and hadiths to support this view. To prevent and cure this condition, he stressed the importance of controlling sensory inputs, such as guarding the eyes, ears, and tongue from sin and inappropriate content. The professor recommended daily recitation of the Istighfar prayer, Quranic recitation, and frequent seeking of forgiveness to purify the heart. He also highlighted the spiritual benefits of caring for orphans and attending religious gatherings to maintain a vibrant sense of anticipation. Listeners shared their personal stories of the hardest waits in their lives, ranging from awaiting the Imam's return to missing loved ones. The program concluded with prayers for the Imam's early appearance and a hopeful outlook for the future.
