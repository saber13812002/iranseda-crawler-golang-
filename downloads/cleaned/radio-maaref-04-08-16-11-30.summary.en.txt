In the radio program "Persaman Mahdavi," expert Mehdi Yousefian discussed the letters from Imam Zaman to Sheikh Mufid. He explained that while the Imam's correspondence was typically handled by specific deputies, Sheikh Mufid lived after the era of special deputyship had ended. Consequently, researchers believe the Imam wrote directly to Sheikh Mufid due to his high scholarly status and significant influence on the Shia community. Some of these letters were published nearly two centuries after Sheikh Mufid's death, which has led some scholars to question their historical authenticity. In the second segment, the expert addressed a listener's question about how to connect with the Imam to better endure life's hardships. Yousefian emphasized that worldly difficulties are inherent and the goal is to find ways to navigate them. Citing a narration from Imam Hassan, he argued that God, being the most merciful, places the best possible conditions for a person's worldly and otherworldly growth. Therefore, believing in divine wisdom and maintaining a spiritual connection with the Imam enhances patience and resilience in the face of adversity.
