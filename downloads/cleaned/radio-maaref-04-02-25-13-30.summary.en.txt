This radio program features the final narrative of Mrs. Ebrahimpour, the wife of the martyr Mustafa Sadra-zadeh, a defender of the shrines. She recounts the emotional hardships of separation and the significant costs of maintaining phone contact while her husband was deployed to Syria. The story highlights their final meeting at the airport and her detailed memories of his kindness during their last trip together. The news of his martyrdom in 2015 profoundly changed her life, leaving her to face deep loneliness. However, she found strength and patience by drawing inspiration from the legacy of Lady Zainab and her belief in the spiritual value of martyrdom. The episode concludes with a brief discussion on the importance of preserving the integrity of the Persian language against the excessive use of foreign terms.
