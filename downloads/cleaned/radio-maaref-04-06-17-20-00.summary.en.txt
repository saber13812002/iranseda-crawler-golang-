The 39th International Conference on Islamic Unity commenced in Tehran with the participation of prominent scholars from across the Muslim world.
Iran's President emphasized the necessity of strengthening Islamic unity to counter the Zionist regime effectively.
Sheikh Naim Qassem also stressed the importance of consolidating unity and maintaining the resistance axis for Palestine's liberation.
In Palestine, the "Martyr Talabani" operation in occupied Jerusalem resulted in the deaths of six Israelis and two Palestinians.
The Zionist regime's intensified attacks on the Gaza Strip caused dozens of Palestinian casualties, including journalists.
The Israeli Prime Minister announced plans to escalate operations in Gaza and demanded the arming of all settlers.
In response, massive pro-Palestinian rallies were held in Belgium, Mexico, Sweden, Canada, and Syria to condemn the ongoing aggression.
