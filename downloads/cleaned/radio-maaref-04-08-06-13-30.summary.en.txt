In this radio program, veterans of the Iran-Iraq war share their memories of captivity in Iraqi camps. They describe the Iraqi regime's attempts to prevent them from performing the Karbala pilgrimage and using the event for propaganda against the Islamic Republic. Despite persistent pressure and threats, the captives, supported by the Supreme Leader's directive, successfully made the pilgrimage to the shrine of Imam Hussein (AS). During the journey, they faced violent and humiliating behavior from Iraqi soldiers, which led to several confrontations. Upon their return, the captives were subjected to gunfire and military displays in the streets of Baghdad. Another segment of the program discusses the concept of sincerity in righteous deeds and the dignified conduct of the captives under harsh conditions. Finally, the program concludes with criticism of US bullying policies and an emphasis on the Iranian nation's resistance against sanctions and threats.
