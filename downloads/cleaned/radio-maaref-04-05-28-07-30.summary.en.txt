In this radio program, Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallah continues the discussion on lying by examining its nature from the perspective of Islamic narrations. Citing the words of Prophet Muhammad and Imam Ali (AS), he emphasizes that lying is incompatible with faith and is rooted in hypocrisy and disbelief. According to the Infallibles, lying serves as a gateway to hypocrisy and constitutes a betrayal of God's trust, thereby diverting individuals from the path to Paradise. Conversely, truthfulness is identified as the key to salvation, honor, and entry into Heaven, with a true believer being a custodian of truth in both creation and legislation. The professor also shares a guest's anecdote highlighting the importance of relying on prayer, seeking forgiveness, and trusting in God during difficult times. It is further noted that lying alone leads to moral corruption and exacerbates other sins. Finally, listeners are encouraged to avoid lying and adhere to truthfulness to save themselves from the punishment of Hell and attain eternal bliss.
