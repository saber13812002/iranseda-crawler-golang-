In this program, a missile soldier recounts the experience of firing missiles at Israeli bases and the enemy drone strike that hit his launcher. Despite being injured and witnessing the martyrdom of his comrades, he refused to leave the battlefield out of a sense of responsibility for the people's security and continued with subsequent launches. The soldier refutes the Israeli regime's claim of destroying Iran's missile bases, emphasizing the military forces' steadfastness and readiness to defend the country until the very last moment. The second part of the program focuses on the concept of "halal livelihood," exploring the deep connection between pure sustenance and spiritual growth. Hujjat al-Islam Hosseini Ghomi, while interpreting hadiths from the book of Kafi, describes the pious as those who view the world as transient and constantly await their meeting with God. He advises that a person's standing before God depends on their level of obedience to divine commands and sincere servitude. The program concludes with the recitation of the Sajjadiya prayer and a supplication for divine mercy.
