In the radio program "Shifa," Dr. Pourasini discussed Islamic traditional medicine, specifically detailing various massage techniques. She described "rough massage" as a method to treat congenital or developmental limb deficiencies by using coarse fabric to increase local blood circulation. This technique aims to nourish and grow underdeveloped muscles, such as in cases of polio. Conversely, "smooth massage" uses soft fabrics like silk to gently stimulate the surface, improving skin complexion and beauty. Following the health segment, the program featured a Quranic interpretation by Hojjat al-Islam Rasoul, who emphasized the moral dangers of envy. He recommended reciting Surah Al-Falaq and adopting a monotheistic perspective on divine blessings as key remedies for jealousy. The broadcast concluded with remarks on the secrets of the Islamic Revolution's victory and the importance of national unity.
