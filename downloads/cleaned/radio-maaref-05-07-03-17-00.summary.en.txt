The radio program emphasizes the necessity of an ethical and ideological spirit in the Islamic army to distinguish it from Western and mercenary forces. Militarism is presented as a key pillar of great civilizations, requiring the simultaneous growth of spiritual and physical capabilities within the armed forces. Citing Nahj al-Balagha, the Islamic army is defined as a purposeful, duty-bound entity serving justice and national security rather than merely a war machine. The military is also described as the fortress of the nation and a symbol of national sovereignty against external threats. In the second segment, a hadith from Imam Sadiq (AS) highlights the severe consequences of refusing to help a believer in need. Finally, the program addresses religious queries from followers, providing clarifications on the rulings of Khums and property rights.
