On National Persian Gulf Day, the President warned that foreign interference threatens regional security and emphasized the strategic importance of the Gulf for energy transit and national sovereignty. He condemned attempts by adversaries to impose a naval blockade, asserting that Iran remains committed to freedom of navigation while holding the US and the Zionist regime responsible for any instability. Meanwhile, US Senator Bernie Sanders called for a shift in budget priorities from costly wars to social needs, citing the economic burden of previous conflicts. The Zionist regime attacked the "Sumud" maritime flotilla, seizing 21 boats and detaining their crews, an action condemned by Turkey as piracy. Israeli media reported that Hezbollah is deploying new fiber-optic guided drones that are highly resistant to electronic jamming, posing a significant challenge to defense systems. The Minister of Energy announced plans to expand renewable energy sources and battery storage to ensure grid stability during winter. The book "Friends of the Homeland" by Indian thinker Annaprasad was republished with research by Alireza Habib, highlighting resistance against colonialism. Finally, a Quranic program discussed the virtue of forgiveness, citing verses that link pardoning others to achieving piety and spiritual readiness for the afterlife.
