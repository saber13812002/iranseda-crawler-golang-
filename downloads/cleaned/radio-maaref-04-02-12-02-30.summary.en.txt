The radio program began by highlighting the compassionate approaches of Ayatollah Hujjat and Ayatollah Talaqani regarding their daughters' education and domestic skills. Subsequently, the guest scholar explained the rulings of Ihram and the rituals of Umrah al-Tamattu, emphasizing the necessary obligations and prohibitions. The Q&A section addressed various jurisprudential issues, including the permissible duration of menstrual cycles, the zakat on sheep, and the rules for managing a mother-in-law's assets. The scholars also clarified that breaking an oath sworn by the Quran does not require expiation, while smoking remains strictly prohibited. Furthermore, the ruling on insects that have contacted impurities was discussed, noting that they are only impure if actively transferring the substance. Finally, the conditions for paying Khums on the income of housewives were detailed, distinguishing between regular household expenses and significant savings.
