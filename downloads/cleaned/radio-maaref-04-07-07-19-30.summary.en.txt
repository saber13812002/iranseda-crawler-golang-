In this radio program, Professor Mohammad Hadi Falahati examines the causes and factors behind profanity and foul language in society. He identifies ignorance and a lack of spiritual knowledge as the primary cause, leading to a disregard for religious and human values. Misunderstanding religious concepts and defending them through offensive language is cited as the second common factor. Mental and spiritual decline, driven by the repetition of negative thoughts, also pushes individuals toward sin and abusive speech. Hypocrisy and hidden malice, particularly in the digital space, are highlighted as significant reasons for the prevalence of such behavior. The foul language of those claiming to be religious is considered especially damaging due to the contradiction with their pious appearance. Enmity, an inability to build relationships based on love, and the enemies' desire to create moral chaos among Muslims are also listed as key factors. Finally, the promotion of profanity in cyberspace is described as a plot against the community's religious and ethical identity, which must be countered through awareness and the cultivation of virtues.
