The Ma'aref network reported on mourning ceremonies for the anniversary of Fatima Zahra's martyrdom in Iraq and Afghanistan, emphasizing the importance of Islamic unity. Cultural highlights included the unveiling of the Turkish translation of Mulla Sadra's "Asfar" in Ankara and the Bosnian translation of Nizami's "Khazain al-Asrar" in Sarajevo. The program also covered the start of an international youth conference in Brazil and a poetry book fair in Venezuela featuring Iranian publishers. Political news featured Al-Azhar University's warning about rising attacks on mosques in the UK and Muslim backlash in Austria against a proposed school hijab ban. The removal of Tel Aviv from Ryanair's flight destinations due to the ongoing war in Gaza was also discussed. Analysts criticized Trump's UN peace plan for lacking executive clarity and failing to protect Palestinian rights. The broadcast concluded with Imam Khomeini's speeches on the power of faith and resistance against oppressive powers.
