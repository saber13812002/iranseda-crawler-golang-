In the radio program "Zamzam Ahkam," two jurisprudential questions from followers were addressed. In response to the first question, Hujjat al-Islam Mohammadi Sheikh Shabani emphasized that temporary marriage for unmarried women is invalid without their father's permission. Regarding the second question, Hujjat al-Islam Fallahzadeh explained that money lent out from one's income is subject to Khums. He added that the payment of Khums on this amount becomes obligatory when the loan is repaid to the lender. At the time of payment, any outstanding living expenses are deducted from the amount, and the remainder is paid as Khums. The program concluded by announcing contact details and a rebroadcast scheduled for 12:45. Additionally, segments of religious prayers and hymns were included in the broadcast.
