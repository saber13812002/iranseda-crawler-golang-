This radio program begins by introducing Hojjat al-Islam Rezaei Hamedani, a close student of Imam Khomeini and a founder of the Jami'at-e Darsin. He taught in Qom, focusing on ethics and jurisprudence, and trained prominent students in various Islamic sciences. The second part features the memoirs of Ali Reza Nikpour, a POW from the 1988 Shalamcheh battle. He describes the horrific medical treatment he received from the enemy, including surgeries performed without anesthesia and using unhygienic tools. Nikpour recounts the physical torture, frequent beatings, and the harsh conditions in overcrowded camps lacking basic hygiene and climate control. He details the suffering caused by infected wounds, multiple amputations, and severe food restrictions during his captivity. These accounts highlight the resilience of Iranian POWs in the face of enemy cruelty and inhumanity.
