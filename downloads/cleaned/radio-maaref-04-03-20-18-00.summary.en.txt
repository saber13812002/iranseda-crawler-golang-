In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Fallah examines the roots and consequences of arrogance based on the Holy Quran. He defines arrogance not merely as outward behavior, but as an internal deviation where an individual refuses to accept truth, reason, and God's commands within themselves. The speaker explains that arrogance is the common trait of all inhabitants of Hell, leading to the rejection of prophets, denial of Sharia laws, and even the abandonment of worship such as prayer. Citing verses from Surah Jasiyah, Yunus, and An-Nahl, it is demonstrated that arrogance causes individuals to consider themselves superior to God's messengers, ultimately resulting in divine punishment. The core message is that the arrogant person, through self-importance, fails to embrace moral virtues and faith, triggering a domino effect of other vices. The discussion concludes by highlighting the destructive effects of arrogance at both individual and social levels, emphasizing the necessity of overcoming this vice to achieve a virtue-centered life.
