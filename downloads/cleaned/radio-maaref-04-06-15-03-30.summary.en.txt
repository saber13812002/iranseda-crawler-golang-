The first segment of the program discusses the fatwa of Ayatollah Khamenei, declaring abortion haram and requiring the doctor and the woman to pay blood money, the amount of which depends on the stage of fetal development.
Subsequently, the rules of travel were explained, noting that the intention to stay for ten days is achieved by deciding to remain in the destination for at least ten full days, excluding travel time.
In response to another question, watching films containing inappropriate scenes was declared haram and harmful for women, making it obligatory to avoid them.
It was also clarified that mental dhikr does not replace verbal dhikr, and the latter is required to gain spiritual reward.
Buying and selling bank loan quotas is permissible if it does not violate bank regulations, but changing the loan's purpose or receiving extra profit is considered haram and usury.
Regarding Khums, debts incurred after receiving income are deductible, and individuals have one year to spend each profit on living expenses.
Finally, the spiritual segment emphasized the importance of guarding one's tongue and avoiding its sins in the Du'a Makarim al-Akhlaq, recommending that the tongue be constantly engaged in praising God.
