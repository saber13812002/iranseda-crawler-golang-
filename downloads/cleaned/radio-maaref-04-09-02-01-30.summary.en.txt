In the first segment, Hojjat al-Islam Ansarian emphasized the necessity of "seeing God" and absolute reliance on Him alongside using worldly means, citing a story by Fakhr Razi and Quranic verses. He referenced the events of Karbala and Imam Hussein's advice to Zainab to highlight that remembering God is the essence of monotheism and the key to overcoming hardships. The program then transitioned to veterans' memories from the Bit al-Muqaddas operation, detailing the intensity of the battle and the bravery of the fighters as they moved through enemy tanks. A short story titled "Martyrdom at Two Months Old" was also read, recounting a soldier's martyrdom by counter-revolutionaries and the survival of his infant son amidst gunfire. Additional testimonies described the incredible resistance of Iranian forces against enemy tank fire and the divine miracles witnessed on the battlefield. These narratives illustrated that the victories of the Sacred Defense were the result of combining human effort with deep spiritual reliance on God.
