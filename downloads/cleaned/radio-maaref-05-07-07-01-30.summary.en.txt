This radio program emphasizes the Islamic culture of work and time management based on the teachings of Imam Ali (PBUH).
According to a hadith from Imam Reza (PBUH), the day should be divided into four equal parts: spiritual devotion, earning a livelihood, social interaction, and rest.
Laziness and unemployment are strongly condemned, while working for a halal income is considered a noble act of worship comparable to jihad.
Officials and wealthy individuals are urged to create job opportunities to prevent social corruption and support the youth.
A person's true worth is determined by the quality and diligence of their work, not by their wealth or status, as God loves excellence in all actions.
Striving for a living is even cited as an expiation for sins that cannot be atoned for by prayer or fasting alone.
Ultimately, combining hard work with spiritual peace and Islamic etiquette is the key to success in both this world and the hereafter.
