This radio program distinguishes between individuals who are dominated by their ego, described as "ridden," and those who master it, described as "riders." The ego gradually leads humans toward financial greed, power-seeking, and moral corruption, often justifying sinful acts through deceptive religious rationalizations. Satan uses the promise of worldly life to isolate individuals from their families and spiritual values, ultimately destroying their lives. To counter this, humans must exercise rational control over their ego and prioritize spiritual duties, such as attending congregational prayers, even under difficult circumstances. The second part of the discussion addresses the jurisprudential requirements of justice for judges and witnesses, analyzing the difference between intrinsic and apparent justice. It emphasizes the necessity of caution in selecting a judge and ensuring their integrity when in doubt. The program concludes with prayers for the honorable longevity of Imam Zaman and blessings upon the Prophet and his family.
