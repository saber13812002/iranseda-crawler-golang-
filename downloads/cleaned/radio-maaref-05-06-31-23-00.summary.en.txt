In this radio program, the speaker warns against surrendering to the determinism of time, emphasizing the need to maintain self-dignity and independence. He highlights the spiritual status of prominent students of Ayatollah Javadi Amoli, such as Martyr Ahmad Niri, and stresses the importance of not asking trivial things from great figures. The main focus is on the greatness of Hazrat Fatima Masumeh (SA) and the holy shrine of Qom, described as a place where the sanctity of the Ahl al-Bayt was preserved without any transgression. The speaker analyzes the Ziyarat taught by Imam Sadiq (AS) for Hazrat Masumeh, noting that it includes greetings to all fourteen Infallibles, indicating her high rank. He also references the Fatimi lineage in narrations, arguing that modesty and chastity are not obstacles but rather factors that help talents flourish among Muslim women. The program concludes with a prayer for the reappearance of Imam Mahdi and a request for the success of Quranic families.
