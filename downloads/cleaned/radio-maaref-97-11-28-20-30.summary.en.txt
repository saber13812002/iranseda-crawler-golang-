The radio program "I am Fatima," featuring Hojjat al-Islam Hosseini Bagherabadi, explores the marital conduct and child-rearing practices of Hazrat Fatima Zahra (SA) and Imam Ali (AS).
The central moral lesson of this couple is prioritizing obedience to God, which Imam Ali (AS) cited as the primary reason for his satisfaction with his wife.
Mutual respect and the use of honorable titles, such as kunyas, in conversation are highlighted as key principles for fostering love and intimacy within a family.
Despite material scarcity, Hazrat Fatima (SA) exemplified modesty and hijab with her simple cloak while actively supporting her husband in his devotion to God.
Seeking intercession through Hazrat Fatima (SA) and invoking her name in prayers is described as an effective method for resolving personal issues and fulfilling needs.
The family of Ali and Fatima (AS) is presented as the only infallible and ideal family in history, serving as an unparalleled model for Muslim couples to avoid marital crises.
Being enslaved by ego and desires is the main obstacle to human perfection, and one must rely on the conduct of the Infallibles to break free from this bondage.
