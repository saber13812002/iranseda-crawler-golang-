The program begins by emphasizing the importance of repentance and self-discipline against desires, noting that true success in life's trials requires genuine faith.
It argues that the love for the Ahl al-Bayt and participation in Karbala are only possible through deep spiritual knowledge, not material promises.
Imam Reza (AS) defines faith not as a simple belief, but as a composite truth involving the heart, tongue, and actions, as described in the Quran.
The discussion transitions to a narrative about "Amaran" and "Rahleh," which illustrates the concept of divine guidance through a journey.
Rahleh poses questions about choosing between two paths (Paradise and Hell) to prove the necessity of a divine guide for humanity.
Amaran concludes that God's mercy necessitates sending prophets and Imams to guide people, as leaving them alone would contradict divine wisdom.
The segment concludes by asserting the necessity of accepting the Shia Imams as the primary guides to find the right path.
