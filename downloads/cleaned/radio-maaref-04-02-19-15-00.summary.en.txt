In an interview with the Deputy of Student Affairs of the Hawzas, it was emphasized that seminaries must go beyond mere knowledge production to actively shape social, economic, and cultural systems. Professor Qanabati highlighted the qualitative and quantitative changes in the Hawzas, stressing the need for university elites to join and for specialized centers to address contemporary challenges such as artificial intelligence and environmental crises. He argued that constructive interaction with state institutions and leveraging the potential of motivated youth are key to realizing Islamic civilization. In the second part of the program, Dr. Sarbaz Hosseini addressed listeners' questions regarding cracked skin on hands and morning nausea. He advised against the continuous consumption of lukewarm water to prevent stomach weakness, suggesting warm herbal teas or natural syrups like tamarisk instead. Additionally, he recommended preparing syrups from cooling plants like "Onab" to reduce body heat during the spring season.
