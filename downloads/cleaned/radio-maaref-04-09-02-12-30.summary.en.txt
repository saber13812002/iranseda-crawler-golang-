The radio program "Bar Bal-e Sokhan," featuring Sheikh Hossein Ansarian, was broadcast on the eve of Lady Zahra's martyrdom.
Key religious events mentioned include the Maghrib and Isha prayers at the Islamic Revolution Museum and the martyrs' body procession on Monday, Azar 3rd.
The speaker interpreted the concept of "kufr" alongside blessings in Lady Zahra's prayer as ingratitude and denial of the source of grace.
He emphasized that one must perceive God's presence and attributes, such as power and love, within every blessing.
Neglecting this divine source and forgetting God is considered a self-inflicted penalty for the individual.
Ansarian illustrated the necessity of reliance and God-consciousness in all moments by recounting stories of miraculous divine interventions.
The discussion concludes that true gratitude requires seeing God alongside blessings and avoiding spiritual negligence.
