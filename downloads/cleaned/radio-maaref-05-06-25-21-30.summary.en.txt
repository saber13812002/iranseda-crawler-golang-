Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verses 39 to 41 of Surah Al-A'raf, defining "Qassas" not merely as storytelling but as a means of teaching divine wisdom. He emphasizes that prophets are of human origin to facilitate the acceptance of guidance, distinguishing them from angels. The lecture clarifies the distinction between intellectually weak individuals who knowingly choose misguidance and those under compulsion, noting that the former face double punishment. Obedience to the Prophet is presented as a mandatory, independent command rather than a mere advisory, with disobedience incurring divine retribution. A believer is defined as one who constantly weeps and struggles against the self and Satan, earning the promise of no fear on Judgment Day. Finally, the entry of disbelievers into Paradise is described as impossible, akin to a camel passing through the eye of a needle. This impossibility is characterized as natural rather than logical, as God's absolute power could theoretically create a shrunken camel.
