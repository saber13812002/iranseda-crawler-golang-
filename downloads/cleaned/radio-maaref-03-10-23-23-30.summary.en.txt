In this program, the host psychologist argues that using physical illness as an excuse to avoid marital intimacy is unacceptable unless explicitly prescribed by a doctor. He emphasizes that proper management of health issues is crucial because physical well-being directly impacts the quality of relationships and family peace. The primary challenge after the birth of the first child is the growing distance between spouses caused by an excessive focus on the parental role at the expense of the spousal role. To overcome this crisis, couples must navigate the role adjustment process with patience and mutual support, much like learning to ride a bicycle, to restore balance in their lives. In the parenting discussion, the psychological differences between fathers and mothers in play and discipline are presented as complementary; the father represents courage and risk, while the mother embodies gentleness and security. The most fundamental principle of upbringing is creating a warm and loving environment through a healthy, respectful relationship between parents, which acts as a suitable garden for children's growth. Finally, the program concludes by stressing the importance of preserving family dignity and avoiding negative arguments in the presence of children.
