In this radio program, listeners are invited to participate in a financial support campaign for a needy woman and her daughter by sending a text message to 3552 with the name of Imam Reza (AS) or the number 8. The primary goal is to gather public donations to provide suitable shelter for this family and resolve their housing issues. The host emphasizes that the amount of the donation is not important and even small contributions are valuable, as the main objective is spiritual participation and solidarity. He encourages those worried about their budget not to hesitate and to donate whatever they can afford. Following this, the spiritual atmosphere is maintained by reciting the Ziyarat Al-Yasin and praising Imam Zaman (AJ). The discussion then shifts to the resistance of the youth against enemy plots and their attempts to discredit the revolution. The host reminds the audience that the fundamental principles of the revolution, such as independence, freedom, and the Islamic Republic, have been achieved. He urges the youth to stand firm against negative propaganda and rumors by being aware of these accomplishments.
