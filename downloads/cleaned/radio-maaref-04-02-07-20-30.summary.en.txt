The program began by honoring prominent martyrs such as Ayatollah Haghani Shirazi and Ayatollah Mahdavi Kani, emphasizing the role of martyrdom in preserving Islamic identity. It then transitioned to recollections from the period of captivity in Saddam Hussein's camps, highlighting the freedom fighters' resistance against torture and enemy propaganda. A central part of the narrative involved the captives' efforts to prevent Iraqi propaganda during their forced pilgrimage to Karbala, secured by a signed agreement from an Iraqi officer. Despite the Iraqis' breach of this promise, the captives maintained their dignity and faith, completing the visit and later exposing the enemy's deceit by presenting the signed document. These memories illustrate how captivity can serve as an opportunity for self-building and strengthening faith. The program concluded by recommending the book "Koocheh-ye Naghashha" as a resource for understanding the war's dynamics and urging society to appreciate the sacrifices of the freedom fighters.
