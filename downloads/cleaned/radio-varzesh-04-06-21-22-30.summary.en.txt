Ali Jamalki, a former national team and Pas FC player, recounts his football journey from his youth at Bank Sader and Bahman to his senior career. He highlights his time with Pas FC's Asian Champions team in 2004 and his experience as a captain at the age of 23 as his career peaks. Jamalki attributes his early retirement at around 29 to Pas FC's strict contract limitations and his refusal to play for teams below the top league. After retiring, he transitioned into coaching, achieving multiple championships with teams like Abfa, Afazla, and Esteghlal Tehran in lower divisions. He eventually continued his career in football development by serving as the academy manager for Saipa FC.
