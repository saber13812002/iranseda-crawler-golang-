The radio program "Hour Twenty" on the Ma'aref network began by introducing the 33rd Book Week of the Islamic Republic of Iran, which runs from October 15 to 21 under the slogan "Let's Read for Iran." The second segment provided practical tips for reducing water consumption in the kitchen to prevent resource waste. The show then featured Quranic recitations and a tafsir of verses from Surah Al-An'am by Ayatollah Javadi Amoli. The main focus of the final segment was the "Mashq-e Rah" program, featuring Professor Seyed Ahmad Faqih discussing theocentric ethics and the role of prayer. Professor Faqih explained that Satan tempts humans by instilling false pleasures and making deceptive promises of immortality and dominion. Referring to the mistake of Adam and Eve, he emphasized that repentance must be immediate and that prayer is one of the most miraculous ways to connect with God. Finally, listeners were invited to submit their questions on theocentric ethics via text message or phone call.
