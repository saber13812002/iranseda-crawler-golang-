The program began with an anecdote about Ayatollah Al-Azmeh Hujjat, highlighting his humility and reliance on God by refusing financial aid and gifting away a donated house.
Expert Tabatabai then explained the miqat locations for Iranian pilgrims, specifically Masjid Shajarah and Juhfah, and detailed the five main rituals of Umrah al-Tamattu.
He clarified that the prohibitions of Ihram are lifted after the Tawaf and Sa'y, and explained the different entry routes to Mecca for pilgrims.
In the Q&A section, Hujjat al-Islam Fallahzadeh provided guidance on how to perform missed prayers for the deceased, emphasizing the intention of representation.
Hujjat al-Islam Sheikh Shabani clarified that a marriage contract remains valid even if the wife refuses to comply with her marital duties.
Regarding Khums, Hujjat al-Islam Alian Jad advised listeners to contact the office of Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi directly to determine their specific obligations.
Hujjat al-Islam Jawaheri discussed the permissibility of dedicating prayers and fasts to the deceased and the correct intention for such acts.
Finally, the program addressed the prohibition of inappropriate contact with a married woman seeking divorce and confirmed the validity of a prayer where one rakaat was performed within the designated time.
