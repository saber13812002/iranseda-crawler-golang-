In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Ansarian uses the metaphor of a mason's plumb line to emphasize the guiding role of the Prophet, the Quran, and the Imams in keeping human life spiritually and morally upright. He introduces six key principles from Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) as divine plumb lines, including avoiding lies, keeping promises, not betraying trusts, avoiding illicit gazes, and refraining from oppression and backbiting. Adhering to these principles guarantees the acceptance of one's deeds and leads to happiness in both this world and the hereafter. Citing Quranic verses, the speaker stresses the importance of justice, moderation in describing personalities, and avoiding extremism in speech and behavior. Kindness to all humans, including non-Muslims and animals, is also highlighted as part of divine benevolence. Finally, four crucial advices from Imam Javad (AS) are presented: relying on patience, practicing contentment, abandoning forbidden desires, and having certainty in God's constant supervision. Accepting and acting upon these counsels is the key to preventing deviation in life and achieving moral perfection.
