The radio program begins with recitations of heroic and Ashura-themed poetry highlighting the pivotal roles of Fatima, Ali, and Zahra in the history of Karbala.
The main segment features interviews with listeners discussing their desire to visit Karbala and the spiritual emotions associated with this journey.
Some listeners who have not yet traveled to Karbala express their strong longing to participate in the Arbaeen pilgrimage and visit the shrines of Imam Hussein and Abbas.
Those who have already made the pilgrimage describe the profound sense of peace, closeness, and fulfillment of their needs experienced in the presence of the Ahl al-Bayt.
The program concludes with an invitation for all compatriots to join the Arbaeen pilgrimage and visit Karbala.
It ends with the recitation of Ziyarat Al-Yasin and the recitation of Hagh Mehdi Samavati, completing the spiritual atmosphere.
This collection of content illustrates the deep bond between the people and the Ahl al-Bayt, as well as the importance of collective pilgrimage.
