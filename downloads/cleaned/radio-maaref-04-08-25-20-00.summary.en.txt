A recent Harvard University survey reveals that a significant portion of American Gen Z youth prefer Hamas over the Zionist regime. These findings highlight a deep generational divide in American attitudes toward the Gaza crisis and a growing public empathy for Palestine. The second segment of the program discussed the results of the recent Iraqi elections and their impact on regional dynamics. The 56% voter turnout, despite the Sadr movement's boycott, indicates a renewed public acceptance of the political system and confidence in the government's services. Resistance movements, including Hezbollah and its allied forces, performed significantly better than expected in these elections. The martyrdom of Sayyid Hassan Nasrallah and Iran's military victories in the 12-day war were key factors in boosting the popularity of these groups. These developments demonstrate that the discourse of resistance and support for Iran has directly influenced the voting behavior of Iraqi citizens.
