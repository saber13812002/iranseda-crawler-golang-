A memorandum of understanding was signed between the country's seminaries and the National Broadcasting Organization to enhance scientific and cultural cooperation for promoting Islamic teachings. Simultaneously, the fourth version of the "Comprehensive Hadith" software was unveiled at the Noor Islamic Sciences Computer Research Center, utilizing artificial intelligence for semantic search across 1,600 hadith books. The sixth Mustafa Award ceremony honored three distinguished scientists from Iran, India, and Turkey for their significant achievements in nanotechnology and solar energy. The seventh National Hoda Clothing Exhibition opened in Tehran, showcasing the latest hijab designs alongside cultural services and parallel exhibitions for Iranian stationery and education. The National Charity Association for Young Marriages reported that over 7,000 young couples have received dowry assistance and various other services from the organization. A memorial ceremony was held at Behesht-e Zahra to honor the martyrs of the September 8, 1978, uprising, keeping their memory alive. In the jurisprudential segment, the principle of "respect for life" was emphasized, stipulating that doctors and medical officials are obligated to take immediate action to save a patient's life when it is in danger.
