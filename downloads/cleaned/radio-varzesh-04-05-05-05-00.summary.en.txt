Dr. Ali Gharadashi, the president of the Iranian Table Tennis Federation, discusses the sport's revitalization following Dr. Afazili's tenure and the introduction of foreign players to the national league. He highlights his decade as the federation's secretary, noting the crucial support of Mr. Shahnazari and key sponsors like Khodabad Company. After returning to the Islamic Republic of Iran Airline, Gharadashi ran for the presidency in 2014 with strong backing from clubs and a dedicated team of colleagues. He secured a decisive victory with 35 out of 39 valid votes, significantly outpolling his rivals, including the incumbent president. Now entering his tenth year in office, he has no intention of running for a fourth term due to the emergence of capable successors and personal fatigue. Despite ongoing sanctions and financial challenges, Gharadashi remains optimistic about the sport's future, pointing to a promising generation of young players. He believes that the continued efforts of the table tennis community will lead to historic achievements in upcoming international competitions.
