In the radio program "Doctrinal Questionnaire," Professor Mohammad Sharif addressed various theological topics and answered listener inquiries.
He emphasized the principle of learning, practicing, and teaching others, stressing the importance of relying on authentic hadiths during the absence of the Imams.
Professor Sharif refuted the notion that modern people are spiritually inferior or more deprived than previous generations, highlighting the abundance of current religious resources and the high value of faith in the present era.
He noted that while physical meetings with Imams were possible in the past, today one can establish a spiritual connection with the Imam of the Age through strict adherence to religious laws and worship.
In response to another question, it was explained that the duration of judgment on the Day of Resurrection varies for each individual and can be extremely long for sinners.
The final segment discussed the different levels of Paradise, emphasizing that "Firdaus" is the highest rank among them.
He also stated that the degrees of Paradise correspond to the number of verses in the Quran, with Shias holding special positions within it.
Finally, listeners were encouraged to prepare themselves for the reappearance of the Imam by adhering to religious principles.
