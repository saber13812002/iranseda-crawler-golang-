The most crucial quality for a successful referee is the courage to make correct and impartial decisions.
Unfortunately, the level of refereeing in Iran has significantly declined compared to ten years ago and global standards.
To improve quality, it is essential to exchange referees with neighboring and European countries to benefit from their experience.
Federation managers must be selected based on merit and management skills, rather than just playing history or political power.
None of the refereeing committee presidents in recent years have been able to introduce innovation or tangible progress.
Referees should be honest and financially independent to ensure they can judge without financial or political pressure.
The failure to invite veterans and experienced referees to national matches demotivates young people from pursuing this career.
The final hope is that future managers will research candidates' resumes and avoid envy and greed to save Iranian refereeing.
