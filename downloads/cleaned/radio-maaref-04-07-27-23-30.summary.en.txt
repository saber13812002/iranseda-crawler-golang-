Ayatollah Ali Doost shares his seminary experiences, highlighting the importance of preliminary study and mental preparation before formal education. He recommends his book "Question and Answer" as a guide to help students navigate their academic journey effectively. According to him, teaching while studying and engaging in continuous debate are fundamental pillars of effective learning in the seminary. He also identifies "self-reflection" and thinking about learned concepts as the best methods for retaining knowledge in the mind. The scholar praises the educational behavior of his teachers, particularly Ayatollah Karimi Jafari, as a model of moral instruction alongside religious studies. In his concluding remarks, he strongly emphasizes the necessity of maintaining a spiritual connection with Imam Zaman (AJ) through prayer and recitation.
