In this radio program, the characteristics of believers and the pious were examined based on the book of Kafi and Nahj al-Balagha.
The first trait is a soft and tolerant demeanor towards others, which stands in contrast to harshness and severity.
The second trait is avoiding hurting others with one's words, ensuring that no one is offended by a believer's speech.
The third characteristic is avoiding pretense and artificiality, meaning a believer presents themselves as they truly are without fabrication.
The fourth trait is refraining from being consumed by worldly affairs, although moderate effort for worldly gain is considered acceptable.
It was also emphasized that believers handle disagreements with dignity and honor, and they ask questions with respect and courtesy.
In the final section, the month of Ramadan was described as a divine guesthouse that invites people to abandon their desires and ego.
Finally, young people were encouraged to take advantage of their youth to reform themselves and draw closer to the divine realm.
