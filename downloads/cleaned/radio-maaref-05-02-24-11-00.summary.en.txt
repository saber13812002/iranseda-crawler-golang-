The two-day BRICS foreign ministers' meeting in New Delhi focused on the recent US war against Iran, emphasizing national sovereignty and multilateral cooperation. Despite unilateral sanctions, Iran highlighted its ongoing diplomatic efforts with India, particularly regarding the Chabahar port project. Economically, officials reported that the country is relying on domestic reserves and imports to mitigate damage to the steel and petrochemical sectors. The Deputy Minister of Health noted that while the population decline has slowed, mortality rates still exceed birth rates in provinces like Gilan and Mazandaran. The program commemorated the late Supreme Leader's historic trip to Kurdistan, which led to the approval of numerous development projects and redefined the region's cultural identity. In a religious segment, a Quranic expert interpreted a verse from Surah Ra'd to describe truth as enduring and falsehood as transient foam. The broadcast concluded by thanking emergency services like the Red Crescent for their crisis response and encouraging the public to conserve water and energy.
