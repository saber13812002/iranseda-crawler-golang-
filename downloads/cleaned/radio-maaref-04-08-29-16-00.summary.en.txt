In the radio program "Persaman Enghelab," expert Dr. Mohammad Malekzadeh analyzed the reasons for the Islamic Revolution's endurance. He identified the people's faith and insight, divine leadership based on the Guardianship of the Jurist, and the discourse of justice and independence as the three main pillars of the system's survival. Malekzadeh emphasized that despite external pressures and sanctions, the Islamic Republic has remained stronger than its enemies predicted. The expert also addressed Iran's strategic shift toward the East, explaining that cooperation with China and Russia is a response to the world's transition to a multipolar order. He stated that this partnership aims to break the West's financial monopoly and counter sanctions, based on long-term shared interests rather than absolute dependence. Furthermore, the internal conflict in Sudan was described as a proxy war supported by Israel and the UAE to pressure the legitimate government and encircle Yemen. Finally, the phenomenon of former politicians turning into internal opposition was attributed to a lack of political insight and excessive worldly ambition.
