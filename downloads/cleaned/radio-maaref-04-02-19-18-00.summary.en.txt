In the radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat," Professor Mohammad Hadi Falah Bastant, while congratulating the birth of Imam Reza (PBUH), emphasized the concept of "Rida" (divine pleasure) as the key to blessings and "Ghadab" (divine anger) as the cause of divine curse. Citing narrations, he explained that obedience in monotheism, ethics, and worship leads to God's satisfaction, while disobedience in these areas provokes His anger. A major point of discussion was the importance of controlling personal anger, which Imam Reza equated with divine punishment and identified as an obstacle to self-awareness. Professor Falah Bastant recommended that the solution to anger involves preserving one's soul, recognizing one's worth, and calming down to attain the rank of worship and fasting. The second part of the program, titled "Zamzameh Ahkam," addressed the rituals of Umrah al-Tamattu'. Professor Tava Tavai explained that the Tawaf prayer must be performed behind the Maqam Ibrahim while adhering to proper principles. He stressed the importance of reciting Surah Al-Ikhlas and Surah Al-Kafirun in the first and second rak'ahs, respectively, and advised maintaining calmness and avoiding haste to prevent disrupting other pilgrims.
