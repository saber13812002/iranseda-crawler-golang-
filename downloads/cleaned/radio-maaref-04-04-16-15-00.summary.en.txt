In this radio program, experts affirmed that the Islamic Republic of Iran's military response to Israeli and American aggression is a legitimate and obligatory act of self-defense, supported by both Shia jurisprudence and international law. They emphasized that defending against an attack is not only an inherent right but a religious duty for all Muslims, as derived from the Quran and prophetic traditions. Conversely, the concepts of "preemptive" and "preventive" defense promoted by Israel and the United States were deemed legally and religiously invalid, citing the prohibition of such actions in the Alawi tradition. The scholars warned that the US attempt to unilaterally interpret international rules to serve its own interests constitutes a blatant violation of the UN Charter and human rights. Furthermore, they stressed the necessity of Islamic unity and a critical review of the presence of US military bases in Muslim countries, which undermines regional security and dignity. The final conclusion was that Iran is on the right path by relying on its military strength and public support, and the global community must resist this self-serving approach to international norms.
