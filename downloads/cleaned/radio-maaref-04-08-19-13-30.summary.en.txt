The radio program "Hekayat-e Azadegi" featured Jamsheed Gholami-Natij, a veteran who participated in the Fath-Mobin operation. Gholami-Natij recounted the three-hour resistance of thirty comrades against Iraqi tanks, which resulted in the martyrdom of twenty-two of them to allow others to retreat. He shared memories of his captivity, highlighting how prisoners maintained their spiritual morale through hidden ceremonies and mutual Quranic education. In another segment, Abdolkarim Mazandarani emphasized the vital role of the Quran in preserving the identity and hope of the freedmen during their imprisonment. The program concluded with an analysis of the declining power of the United States and its inability to repeat past coups.
