In this radio program, Ayatollah Ostadi explained a hadith from Imam Javad (AS) regarding the pillars of repentance and the perfection of faith. He defined repentance as returning to God and recognizing the spiritual harm caused by sin, emphasizing the necessity of heartfelt remorse and verbal seeking of forgiveness. The other two pillars of repentance are compensating for sins through righteous deeds and making a firm decision not to repeat them in the future. The speaker also outlined four factors for perfecting faith: spending in God's path, avoiding spending on haram, and loving or hating for the sake of God. Finally, he highlighted three righteous actions that prepare a person for death: fulfilling obligatory duties, avoiding prohibitions, and guarding against negligence in religion.
