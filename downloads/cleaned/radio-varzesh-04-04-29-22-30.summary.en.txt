Fatemeh Abaqoli Asmi, the first female president of a sports federation in Iranian history, discussed her experiences with the Physical Fitness and Aerobics Federation in a radio interview. In 2004, she was appointed as the acting president and later elected president following a proposal from the Sports Organization and Mr. Kafashian. She described the extremely difficult start of her tenure, working in a small room with minimal resources, especially after administrative changes within the Sports Organization. Despite facing obstacles and budget constraints, she successfully improved the sport's scientific level by hiring foreign instructors and establishing national teams that achieved world rankings. She also highlighted her efforts to establish a National Physical Fitness Day and break gender taboos in sports management. Ultimately, she was removed from her position due to further structural changes in the federations, though she still expresses a strong desire to serve in the sports sector.
