This program features a lecture by Hojjat al-Islam Hosseini Ghomi on the scientific and social life of Imam Hasan Askari (PBUH).
A key aspect of his six-year Imamate was preparing the Shia community for the concept of occultation.
Numerous narrations highlight the Imam's miracles and his extraordinary generosity in helping the needy, often before they even asked.
One story describes a follower of the heretical Waqifiyya sect who reconsidered his beliefs after witnessing the Imam's miracles.
Imam Askari (PBUH) exemplified virtues such as forgiveness, patience, and prioritizing faith and service to the people.
The speaker emphasizes the importance of holding grand celebrations for the Imam's birth in Qom.
He warns against overshadowing the Imam's birth anniversary with the mourning events for Lady Fatima Masumeh (SA).
Finally, the lecture underscores the unparalleled status of the Infallible Imam in Islamic history and the need to preserve this dignity.
