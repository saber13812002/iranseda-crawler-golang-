The radio program "Mehrbān Bāshim" began by honoring the Sacred Defense Week and introducing student martyrs.
Listeners from various cities sent messages about student and combatant martyrs, which were read by the host.
The weather segment predicted that after today's intense heat, the weather in Tehran and Mashhad will cool down starting Friday.
The host emphasized the Iranian people's solidarity with their country and support for the leadership in current conditions.
The program then transitioned to the "Ganj-e Sa'adat" segment featuring Professor Mohammad Hadi Falahatpour.
Professor Falahatpour discussed the Quranic verse "What has deceived you concerning your Lord, the Generous?" and the causes of arrogance.
He stressed that humans should not deceive themselves by relying on outward actions and must believe in God's precise accounting of deeds.
Finally, listeners were encouraged to recognize the causes of arrogance to prevent self-deception and spiritual ruin.
