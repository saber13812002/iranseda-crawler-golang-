The radio program begins with a theological discussion on "innate knowledge" and the role of sacred traditions in understanding the Quran. The speaker argues that while the Quran is the primary source, relying on the narrations of the Ahl al-Bayt is essential for a comprehensive understanding of divine laws and wisdom. Following this, the show transitions into a morning segment featuring Quranic recitations and prayers from Imam Sajjad. Brief references are made to recent geopolitical events, such as the war in Gaza, and historical memories of the 1978 revolution in Iran. The main topic of the latter part of the broadcast is the concept of "role-playing" in daily social interactions. The host discusses the positive and negative aspects of masking one's true emotions to maintain social harmony. Finally, listeners are invited to share their personal experiences with role-playing or the characters they wish to emulate.
