In the radio program "Mashq-e Rah," Professor Seyed Ahmad Faghihi discussed the ethics of monotheism and the etiquette of supplication. He emphasized that when praying, one should have great and abundant requests, trusting in God's infinite power and avoiding selfishness. Using examples from prophets, the professor illustrated that the acceptance of prayers can sometimes be delayed, so one should not lose hope. In response to a question about losing spiritual motivation, he identified sins and minor mistakes as the primary cause of this issue. The suggested remedies include seeking forgiveness, engaging in honest self-criticism, and avoiding comparisons with others. He also advised against negligence in maintaining divine blessings and recommended seeking intercession through the Imams, particularly Imam Sajjad (PBUH). Finally, the spirit of awaiting the Savior was presented as a source of hope and strength for the Islamic community.
