In this radio program, Professor Mohammadi emphasized the role of spirituality in scientific progress, arguing that a healthy soul and prayer are essential for better understanding natural and experimental sciences. He refuted the claim that there is no time for worship, explaining that prayer does not hinder scientific speed but rather increases productivity by creating inner peace. During the Q&A segment, the discussion focused on the reliability of recurring dreams about the deceased; while multiple similar dreams may indicate the soul's peace, fulfilling religious duties such as forgiving rights and praying for the deceased remains mandatory. The barriers to the acceptance of prayers, including sin, consuming haram, and misunderstanding one's true desires, were also examined. Furthermore, the difference between genuine freedom and lawlessness was discussed, stressing that the clergy opposes moral corruption and chaos rather than progress and industry. Finally, the program concluded with references to the sacrifices of martyrs, the importance of raising the next generation for God, and the recitation of the Qunut prayer followed by religious music.
