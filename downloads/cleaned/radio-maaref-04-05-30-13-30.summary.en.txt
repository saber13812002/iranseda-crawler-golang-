The program began with a lecture by Ayatollah Dastgheib before transitioning to the "Narrative of Love" segment, which features interviews with the mothers and wives of martyrs.
The guest was Marzieh Yaqubi, the mother of martyr Mehdi Sabri, an Afghan defender of the shrine and a martyr.
She recounted her son's childhood, highlighting his deep devotion to the Quran and his active participation in local cultural and radio programs for youth.
Mrs. Yaqubi described Mehdi's academic journey in geology and his subsequent firm decision to defend the shrine of Imam Hussein in Syria.
She shared the emotional moments leading up to his departure, including her heartfelt prayers and her acceptance of his potential martyrdom, as well as his phone calls from the airport and Syria.
The narrative also included a memory from a pilgrimage to Karbala, where seeing the mound of Lady Zainab reinforced her son's commitment to the path of martyrdom.
The program concluded with a speech emphasizing the critical role of the mosque as a hub for social gathering, education, and the centrality of prayer in the lives of Muslims.
