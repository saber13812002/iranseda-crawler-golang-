This radio program analyzed the Supreme Leader's recent message, which expressed disagreement with certain negotiation trends while delegating the process to the Supreme National Security Council. Experts noted that while professional differences between the Leader and advisors are natural, insisting on a contrary view after the Leader's final decision is unacceptable. A key point is that Iranian negotiators must enforce the memorandum based on their own interpretation, strictly monitoring US commitments such as ending the occupation in Lebanon and releasing Iranian assets. Furthermore, managing the Strait of Hormuz implies Iran's authority over transit, not merely keeping the route open. The discussion also highlighted that officials using terms like "famine" or "weakness" in public statements may send negative signals to adversaries, weakening Iran's bargaining position. Finally, the program emphasized the importance of not concealing differences of opinion and urged the public to firmly support the new Leader during the farewell ceremonies for the late Imam.
