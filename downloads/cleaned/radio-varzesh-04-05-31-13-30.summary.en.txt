The guest, Ardalan, shares his memories of playing for the Ekteban team and representing national teams. He considers his decision to stay with Ekteban culturally and football-wise correct, though financially disadvantageous compared to offers from clubs like Persepolis and Shahin. Ekteban achieved the Tehran club championship in its early years by recruiting experienced players such as Khalakbari and Movini. Ardalan proudly highlights his 14 to 15-year loyalty to the club, noting that he declined numerous offers to leave. He recalls his time with the youth, junior, and hope national teams, particularly the memorable Great Wall match in Beijing. During that era, teams were sustained by personal investments from players and fans due to a lack of state or private corporate support. Ardalan emphasizes the strong camaraderie and deep friendships among Ekteban players, which helped preserve the club's legacy in football history.
