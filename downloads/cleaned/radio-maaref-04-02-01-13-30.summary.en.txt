In the radio program "Mashq-e Rah," Professor Seyed Ahmad Faqih discussed the concept of monotheistic ethics and the true nature of prayer. He emphasized that performing ablution with spiritual awareness and praying two units of prayer facilitates heartfelt focus and the acceptance of supplications. The professor also advised listeners to protect their reputation by avoiding situations that might lead to slander and to seek refuge in God through repentance if harmed. In response to audience questions, he recommended sending blessings, reciting the Quran, and praying the Ahda prayer in the morning to strengthen one's connection with the Imam of the Age. Furthermore, he highlighted the dangers of the tongue, stressing the importance of pausing before speaking and being concise to avoid causing lasting emotional harm to others. He noted that a single inappropriate word can inflict deep pain, making self-control a crucial ethical practice.
