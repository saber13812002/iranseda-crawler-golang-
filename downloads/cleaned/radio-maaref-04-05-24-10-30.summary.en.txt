In this radio program, Dr. Jafari distinguished between the forbidden act of predicting the exact time of the Mahdi's appearance and the permissible but specialized task of interpreting signs, warning against unqualified individuals making harmful interpretations. In the second segment, Dr. Erfan highlighted "self-sacrifice" (Ithar) as a core trait of Imam Hussein's companions and a fundamental requirement for the community of the Awaited One. Citing Quranic verses and narrations, he described self-sacrifice as a profitable transaction with God and a key to earning the satisfaction of Imam Mahdi. Dr. Erfan urged listeners to extend the spirit of altruism observed during the Arbaeen pilgrimage to their daily lives and social interactions, transcending ethnic and geographical boundaries. He also cited a narration from Imam Kazim emphasizing that breaking the hearts of believers prevents the Imam's entry into society. The program concluded by encouraging the promotion of Mahdavi prayers and engaging sermons to keep the memory of Imam Mahdi alive among the people.
