This radio program began by outlining the thirteen obligatory acts of Umrah al-Hajj, ranging from entering the state of Ihram to stoning the pillars at Mina. The ruling of Ayatollah Khamenei regarding Khums on online gold transactions was discussed, stating that if the transaction lacks valid Islamic conditions, it is void and the income is considered haram. The fiqh ruling on responding to greetings in virtual spaces was addressed, noting that it is obligatory if the voices of the participants can be heard. The ruling of Imam Khomeini concerning the prayer of drivers who make frequent short trips was also detailed with specific time conditions. Paying debts to government agencies through the "return of wrongs" account was deemed incorrect, with direct payment to the specific agency recommended. The issue of Mahramiyyat (prohibition of marriage) between spouses during long-term separation was clarified, affirming that it remains intact until a formal divorce occurs. Finally, reciting specific supplications addressed to Imams during the Qunut prayer was ruled incorrect, and the program concluded with general prayers and contact information.
