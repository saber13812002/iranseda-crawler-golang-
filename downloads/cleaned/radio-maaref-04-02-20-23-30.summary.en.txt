In this radio program, the importance of combining prayer with a focus on the afterlife and holding complete religious beliefs was emphasized through the poetic prayers of Imam Ali (AS).
The guest, Hajj Mehdi Foroughi, a renowned Quran reciter and international competition winner, brought the spiritual depth of these prayers to life through his recitation.
The guest shared a personal memory of reciting these verses in Karbala, highlighting their profound emotional impact on himself and his companions.
The host, Mortaza Najafi, argued that Imam Ali's lifestyle exemplified a perfect balance, being deeply immersed in night prayers while being active in politics and warfare during the day.
The main conclusion of the discussion is that prayer should not be viewed as a momentary act but as a permanent mindset integrated into all aspects of human life.
The program concluded with the recitation of a hadith from Imam Ali (AS) regarding the sufficiency of God's servitude and the desire for divine pleasure.
Finally, the host thanked the audience and offered prayers for the health and security of the country, as well as the appearance of the Imam of the Time (AS).
