The radio program "Bar-e Bal-e Sakhn," featuring Saeed Mirhashem Hosseini, explored the intellectual and practical legacies of Imam Hadi (PBUH).
A key point highlighted was the reference to over thirty Quranic verses in the Ziyarat Qadiriyah, all pointing to the virtue of the Wilayah of Amir al-Mu'minin and the occasion of Eid al-Ghadir.
The speaker refuted the common folk belief of reciting specific verses to ward off the evil eye, emphasizing that these verses are rooted in the Wilayah and connection to the Ahl al-Bayt.
The Ziyarat Jama'ah Kabirah was introduced as a source of blessing and a path to human perfection and monotheism.
A narration regarding Imam Hadi's illness was shared, where he recommended sending a worshipper to Karbala to pray under the dome of Imam Hussein instead of asking for his own healing.
This story underscores the sanctity of Husaini shrines and the high likelihood of prayers being answered there, particularly under the dome of Abu Abdullah.
The correct interpretation of the hadith "Do not be hostile to the days" is to avoid hostility toward the Infallible Imams and follow their path, rather than believing in superstitions about weekdays.
Finally, a prayer was offered for God to make this gathering servants of the Ahl al-Bayt school and to grant relief and health to all Muslims.
