This radio program discusses practical and preventive strategies for curing "ujb" (self-admiration) based on the teachings of Imam Ali (AS).
In his letter to Malik Ashtar, Imam Ali emphasizes avoiding self-praise, reliance on oneself, and seeking praise from others, warning that these traits invite Satan's influence.
Key recommendations include distancing oneself from narcissistic environments, refraining from boasting about achievements, and maintaining vigilance even after initial recovery.
It is stressed that individuals should not attribute spiritual progress solely to personal effort but must constantly seek refuge in God.
The second segment highlights the life and thoughts of Ayatollah Seyyed Mahmoud Taleqani, a symbol of sincerity and resistance against tyranny.
In a historic 1979 interview, Taleqani interpreted hijab not as a restriction but as a means of preserving dignity and Eastern traditions.
He argued that the enforced unveiling during the Pahlavi era was part of colonial programs aimed at weakening the national and Islamic identity of women.
Finally, the active role of women in the Islamic Revolution and the necessity of maintaining their revolutionary spirit alongside religious values were emphasized.
