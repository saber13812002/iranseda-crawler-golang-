In the radio program "Ganj-e Sadat," Professor Mohammad Hadi Falah discussed the severe consequences of stinginess based on Quranic verses and narrations. Citing a hadith addressed to Bilal, he emphasized that failing to be generous, even with guests, results in smoke in the fire of Hell in the afterlife. Referring to the Surah At-Tawbah, he warned that gold and silver not spent in the way of God will become hot iron bars that brand the foreheads and backs of the miserly on Judgment Day. Professor Falah characterized stinginess in one's love for God and His representatives as a form of disobedience, contrasting it with true devotion. He highlighted the story of Imam Hussein (AS) in Karbala to demonstrate that genuine love for God entails self-sacrifice and generosity. The second part of the program focused on investment in the agricultural sector, specifically fruit cold storage facilities in Balaw village, Urmia. This segment discussed the role of the public in supporting national production and preventing capital from remaining stagnant in non-productive markets such as gold and real estate.
