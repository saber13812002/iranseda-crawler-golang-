In this radio program, the guest emphasizes the necessity of understanding the essence of life, stating that the world is a transient place where absolute peace is unattainable.
Citing religious traditions, he identifies the primary human goal as recognizing one's origin, destination, and existential purpose, while aligning one's will with God's.
The guest also highlights the role of a correct worldview in achieving life satisfaction and managing the human psyche effectively.
The second part of the program examines the moral concept of "Ghadab" (anger) and distinguishes it from divine wrath and mere strictness.
It is explained that the power of anger serves to repel harm and danger, making it as sacred and essential for human survival as the power of desire.
The core issue lies not in the existence of this power, but in its mismanagement and deviation, which can lead to resentment and harm.
Referencing the story of Cain and Abel, controlling anger and mastering it is presented as the key to spiritual elevation and avoiding sin.
Finally, the Quran, as the final book of guidance, stresses the importance of managing anger and purifying the heart of rage to attain higher spiritual ranks.
