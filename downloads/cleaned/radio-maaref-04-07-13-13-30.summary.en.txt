The radio program "Enduring Memories" features poetry and music while recounting stories from the Iran-Iraq War by Supreme Leader Khamenei and Major General Gholam-Hosin Darbandi. In the first section, the Leader describes a young soldier who donated his salary to the front lines and shares a dangerous reconnaissance mission with the late General Chamran in the Duberdan area. Darbandi then narrates his experience during Operation Allah Akbar, where he rescued a wounded Iranian officer, Niknami, who prioritized saving two Iraqi soldiers over his own life. These accounts highlight the spirit of self-sacrifice, justice, and respect for human life, even towards enemies, which deeply moved the captured Iraqi soldiers. The program also includes a tragic story from the Topol operation, where a soldier named Seyed Saeed Rahimi was martyred due to dehydration and ignoring a comrade's warning. Finally, the head of the IRGC emphasizes the critical importance of public security, the fight against espionage, and the necessity of strict internal and external rule of law within security organizations.
