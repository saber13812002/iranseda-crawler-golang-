The program begins with the story of Salari, who, after blaming himself for selling his parents' memorial house, decides to buy a new home and take his children with him.
The second section emphasizes the importance of reciting the Quran as a source of spiritual life and forgiveness, citing narrations and the practices of prominent scholars.
Examples of the deep devotion of figures like Ayatollah Koohestani, Allameh Majlesi, Sheikh Ansari, and Imam Khomeini to daily Quran recitation, even during illness or political duties, are highlighted.
In the final segment, Fatemeh Rezaei, the wife of martyr pilot Bahman Masaei, recounts the last moments of their shared life and her heartfelt acceptance of his martyrdom.
She describes the hardships of her husband's frequent border missions in the Saravan region and her hidden anxieties during his final days.
Fatemeh recalls receiving his morning messages and the eerie silence of his unanswered calls on the day of the incident as ominous signs.
Finally, she shares the devastating moment she learned of his helicopter crash and martyrdom through her father's family group, a painful and unforgettable event in her life.
