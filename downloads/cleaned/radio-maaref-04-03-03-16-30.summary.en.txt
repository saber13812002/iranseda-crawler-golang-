This radio program explores the relationship between human will and God's will regarding guidance and misguidance. The commentator argues, citing Quranic verses, that God determines human destiny based on wisdom and their free choices, with misguidance resulting from humans misusing their free will. The discussion also references the story of Prophet Jesus and his disciples, highlighting that supporting God's religion requires effort, sincerity, and reviving the Prophet's traditions. In the second segment, the program addresses the importance of hard work and perseverance for achieving success, drawing from the book "Mafatih al-Hayat." A psychologist and listeners emphasize that without planning, persistence, and goal-oriented effort, reaching major life goals is impossible. The overall conclusion is that both religious and worldly success require continuous effort, sincerity in action, and the use of personal will within the framework of divine guidance.
