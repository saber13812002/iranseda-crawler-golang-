In this radio program, Professor Javadi Amoli interprets verses 101 and 102 of Surah Al-Ma'idah to categorize and define the boundaries of questioning. He emphasizes that questions driven by arrogance, provocation, or illogical demands are forbidden and may carry negative consequences in the afterlife. Conversely, pure inquisitive questioning, when accompanied by a receptive heart and a polite tongue, is considered a divine blessing for uncovering scientific and spiritual truths. Referring to the story of Moses and Khidr, he illustrates that even in the presence of divine saints, unlimited questioning is impermissible and must be conducted with proper etiquette and awareness of the other party's capacity. The verse "Do not ask about things" serves as a warning against inquiries that lead to personal disgrace or impose heavy, unnecessary obligations. Ultimately, the commentator notes that while God is forgiving of ignorance, humans must strive for truth through thoughtful and reasonable inquiry.
