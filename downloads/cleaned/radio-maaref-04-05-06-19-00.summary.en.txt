The radio program "Khaneh-ye Doost" began by announcing the time for Maghrib prayer and offering prayers for the sick.
The main segment featured a lecture by Seyyed Reza Tehrani on the significance of prayer based on Islamic narrations.
He compared the five daily prayers to a flowing river that purifies humans from the pollution of sins and disobedience.
It was emphasized that prayer with full presence of heart acts as an internal cleansing, freeing the soul from the burden of sin.
Subsequently, the concept of spiritual growth was explained using a famous hadith by Imam Ali (AS) regarding the necessity of daily progress.
A report was presented about the services provided by the caretaker of Imamzadeh Abdullah shrine to pilgrims on the Masouleh route.
The program concluded with advice to pay more attention to prayer and the recitation of verses from Surah Al-Ma'idah.
