The program begins by announcing the broadcast of the prayer for relief on the ninth of Dhu al-Hijjah from the shrine of Hazrat Masoumeh.
It then features Ayatollah Javadi Amoli's commentary on verse 105 of Surah Al-Ma'idah.
The human body is compared to a flowing river where the physical form changes, but the essence of the soul remains constant.
The distinction between true visions, false dreams, and genuine spiritual perception in both sleep and wakefulness is explained.
Ethics are described as the middle jihad, while practical mysticism is the greater jihad aimed at transforming knowledge into direct experience.
The spiritual stages from calling out to God to the state of silence and listening to the divine word are detailed based on the Shabaniyyah prayer.
Finally, the Hadith of Kumayl is referenced, noting that divine souls are suspended from the divine realm.
