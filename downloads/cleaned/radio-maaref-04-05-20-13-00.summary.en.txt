This radio program discusses the legacy of Imam Hussein (PBUH) during the Arbaeen period, focusing on his resistance against tyranny. The Imam refused to pledge allegiance to Yazid's unjust rule, prioritizing the revival of Islam over personal safety or power. The speaker contrasts the betrayal of the people of Kufa with the unwavering loyalty of true companions, emphasizing the need to stand by religious leaders in times of hardship. The core message urges society to remain vigilant against the spread of corruption and to actively oppose evil, just as Imam Hussein did. This historical uprising continues to inspire global movements against oppression, including student protests in the West supporting Palestine. The program concludes with a prayer inspired by Jabir ibn Hayyan, asking God to strengthen the followers of the Imam of the Time and increase the honor of Islam.
