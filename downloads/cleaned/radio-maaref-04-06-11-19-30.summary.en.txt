The program began with a call for citizens to upload documents related to the Sacred Defense to the "DefaDocs" system.
The first segment analyzed narrations from the book of Kafi, emphasizing that true gratitude to God requires believing that the ability to give thanks is itself a divine blessing.
It was highlighted that thanking intermediaries, such as parents and teachers, is a prerequisite for God accepting one's gratitude on the Day of Judgment.
In the second segment, Hujjat al-Islam Fallah discussed the virtue of speaking well of others and the vice of backbiting.
He described backbiting as a fatal disease for one's faith and the food of the dogs of Hell, which destroys spiritual credibility.
According to the narrations, backbiting is the act of the weak, whereas speaking well of others signifies inner strength and self-mastery.
The program concluded by warning that backbiting is worse than adultery because while adultery can be forgiven through repentance, backbiting remains unforgiven until the rights of the people are restored.
