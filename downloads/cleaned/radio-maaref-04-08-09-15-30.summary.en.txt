In the "Strategy" program on Radio Ma'aref, Dr. Mehdi Meshkibaf emphasized absolute distrust of the United States, citing historical documents and the Supreme Leader's statements regarding its hegemonic nature. He argued that three decades of interaction with dominant governments demonstrate that the US reneges on its promises even when Iran fully adheres to its commitments. Meshkibaf described negotiations with the US as "poisonous" due to its bullying nature, warning that such processes could contaminate the country's culture and social fabric. Referencing the US withdrawal from the JCPOA, he stressed that negotiations under the shadow of threats with a hegemonic system cannot yield a win-win outcome. The program subsequently featured the recitation and translation of verses from Surah Al-Imran, focusing on patience, steadfastness, and reliance on God in the face of enemies. Additionally, coinciding with the anniversary of the US Embassy takeover, listeners were invited to join the 13 Aban march to display the people's anti-hegemony stance.
