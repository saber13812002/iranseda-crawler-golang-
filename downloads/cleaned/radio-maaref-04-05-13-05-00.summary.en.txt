In the exegesis of Surah Al-Kahf, the term "young men" is reinterpreted from physical youth to spiritual maturity and strong faith. God validated the faith of the Cave's companions and rewarded their slight effort against their ego by strengthening their hearts. Their 309-year sleep serves as a divine sign of God's power to resurrect the dead and fulfill His promises. Following the theological discussion, the program addressed the importance of water conservation during the hot season. The session then transitioned to a Fiqh lecture on the third class of heirs, which includes uncles, aunts, and maternal relatives. These specific relatives are not explicitly named in the Quran but are included under the interpretation of "relatives" by Imam Ali (AS). Finally, the lecture highlighted the doctrinal differences between Shia and Sunni schools regarding the distribution of inheritance shares among these male and female relatives.
