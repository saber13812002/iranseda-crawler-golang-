The program begins by highlighting the virtue of humility in the conduct of Prophet Muhammad and Ayatollah Behjat, emphasizing their simplicity and lack of arrogance. The second part features the story of Ms. Hosseini, the mother of martyr Seyyed Mohammad Hosseini, a defender of the shrines from the Qeyor youth of Afghanistan. She recounts the agonizing days of waiting for news of her son's martyrdom, relying on patience and supplication to Hazrat Zainab (SA) to maintain her inner peace. She describes how her family subtly informed her of the tragedy by organizing a wedding ceremony instead of a mourning event to hide their grief from others. The narrative culminates in her final visit to her son, where she witnessed the wounds he sustained during his martyrdom. Finally, the mother emphasizes the importance of sincerity in serving God and the strength of patience in enduring maternal suffering.
