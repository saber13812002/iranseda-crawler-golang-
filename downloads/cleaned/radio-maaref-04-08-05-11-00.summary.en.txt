The broadcast began by celebrating the birth anniversary of Hazrat Zainab (SA) and Nurses' Day, emphasizing her spiritual strength and crisis management as a role model. News segments covered the Quran competition finale, Umrah registration for Sunnis, and a seminar on the concept of Maslaha in Shia jurisprancy. The program discussed the decline in reading rates due to excessive mobile phone usage and the need for families to promote physical books. Condolences were offered for the passing of a martyr's mother, while the head of the Statistics Center announced preparations for the 2026 census. The second half featured a Q&A session on religious rulings, addressing issues such as prostration with sweat, returning unjust gains, and the purity of blood stains. Fatwas from various clerics were also shared regarding the best time for Fajr prayer and the permissibility of clapping in religious gatherings.
