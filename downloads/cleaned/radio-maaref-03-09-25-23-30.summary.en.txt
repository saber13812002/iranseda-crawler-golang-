In this radio program, a family counselor addresses several key questions from listeners. Regarding the first case, it is advised that a young woman struggling with aggression and insecurity should focus on personal growth and counseling before marriage to reduce the risk of failure. For the second case, involving a daughter depressed by her husband's infidelity, the counselor emphasizes that medication only controls symptoms, while true healing requires individual and couples therapy. Listeners are also advised to remove external triggers, such as old messages and harmful media, to accelerate the recovery process. In the third question, concerning a spouse who is overly dependent on their family of origin, the importance of balancing duties between the partner and the extended family is highlighted. The counselor suggests writing down financial needs and goals visibly to foster a sense of participation and calm. Finally, the discussion concludes by stressing the importance of financial transparency and accepting a spouse's responsibilities toward their family to maintain peace in the marriage.
