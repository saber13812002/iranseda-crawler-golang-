This radio program initially addressed the religious rulings regarding women's participation in mourning gatherings and street processions. It was clarified that while women can attend separate sections in indoor venues, they must strictly observe full hijab and avoid makeup during outdoor processions due to the presence of non-mahram men. In response to specific fiqh questions, it was explained that if an obstacle like glue remains on the left hand during the ritual bath for major impurity, removing it and washing the area suffices, whereas an obstacle on the right hand requires completing the remaining parts of the bath in the correct order. The trading of football player cards was deemed permissible provided they possess value and reasonable utility. Regarding the act of pretending to pray, it was stated to be permissible only if it does not involve lying or cause others to lose trust, and is driven by necessity. The program also detailed the conditions for Friday prayers, specifically the requirement of connection between the imam and the congregation without physical barriers. Finally, rulings on doubt regarding the completion of ritual baths and the permissibility of selling found property were discussed.
