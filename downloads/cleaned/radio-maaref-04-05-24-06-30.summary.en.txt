The program provides a detailed analysis of the Quranic verse "Yaqtadun" and the concept of divine will within it.
A key distinction is made between the general legislative will, which includes all humans including disbelievers, and the specific obligatory will.
The primary argument is that since the Prophet's prayer for the Ahl al-Bayt was answered and the verse was revealed afterward, God's will here implies the actual removal of sin, not just legislative intent.
Consequently, the verse indicates a specific virtue and merit for the Ahl al-Bayt, a point agreed upon by both Shia and Sunni scholars.
In the second part, the concept of "Ghayb" (the unseen) in the Quran is analyzed, defined as that which lacks sensory perception and is known only through reason and faith.
Various narrations are cited to support the view that "Ghayb" includes the occultation of the Imam of the Time, making faith in his reappearance a form of faith in the unseen.
Finally, the difference between "faith" and "attainment" in prayer is discussed, emphasizing the necessity of performing prayer with perfect adherence to its prescribed conditions.
