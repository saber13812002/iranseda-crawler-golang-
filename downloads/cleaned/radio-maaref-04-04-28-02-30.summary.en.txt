In the first segment, Dr. Kazemi argued that Iran achieved its primary objective of inflicting irreparable damage on the enemy during the recent conflict, halting further escalation due to international and domestic considerations. He warned that any future aggression would face a more decisive strike, emphasizing the need for border control and economic stability to prevent imposed wars. In the second segment, Professor Vahidpour stressed that Ashura recitations must be based on authentic sources, permitting the use of "implied speech" only when explicitly clarified to avoid attributing falsehoods to the Infallibles. The program also addressed various jurisprudential questions, ruling that wudu is invalidated if residue remains on the face, eye drops do not break the fast unless one is certain they reach the throat, and a woman becomes obligated to perform Hajj only after receiving sufficient mahr to cover the pilgrimage costs.
