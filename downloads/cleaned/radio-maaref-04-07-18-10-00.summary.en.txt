In this radio program, Dr. Jafari and Professor Sadoughi compared the characteristics of Imam Zaman (AS) with previous prophets based on a hadith from Imam Hussein (AS). They emphasized that the Imam's primary mission is not to establish a new sharia, but to implement the Islamic laws globally and completely, a task prophets could not fully achieve due to people's disobedience. The Imam's fear and seclusion are not signs of weakness but measures to preserve the sanctity of his Imamate and prevent enemy exploitation. The similarity to Prophet Job offers hope for the resolution of current crises and the liberation of the oppressed. The misconception that Islam spread by the sword was refuted by highlighting the Prophet's (PBUH) thirteen years of peaceful preaching in Mecca. Finally, reciting the Ziyarat Al-Yasin and preparing for the reappearance were presented as essential steps for strengthening the bond with the Imam and achieving a healthy, valuable life.
