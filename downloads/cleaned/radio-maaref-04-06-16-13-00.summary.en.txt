Based on verse 164 of Surah Al-Imran, the program outlines the Prophet's three main duties: reciting verses, moral purification, and teaching wisdom.
Wisdom is defined as knowing God, understanding the world, and self-awareness, rooted in understanding one's origin, destination, and life path.
The Prophet fought against four pre-Islamic (Jahili) mindsets: independent will, independent power, worldly honor, and materialism.
The Quran emphasizes that human will is subordinate to God's will, and true power and honor belong solely to God, the Prophet, and the believers.
By adhering to these principles, the Prophet transformed from a state of defeat in Mecca to one of divine honor and victory in Medina.
Unfortunately, after the Prophet's passing, some apparent successors revived these Jahili mindsets, converting the divinely appointed leadership into a human choice.
This regression to ignorance replaced faith-based values with power-seeking and materialism, leading people away from the true path of Islam.
The discussion concludes that escaping these four false beliefs and returning to the Quran and the Ahl al-Bayt is the key to achieving honor and salvation in both this world and the hereafter.
