The radio program "Scent of I'tikaf" explores the philosophy and rulings of this worship during the month of Rajab. I'tikaf is described as a path to escape daily routine and focus on divine knowledge, combining presence in the mosque, fasting, prayer, and Quran recitation. Professor Fazeli Haidari emphasizes that I'tikaf liberates individuals from worldly slavery and brings them closer to the noble goal of servitude to God. Fiqh rulings are also discussed, noting that I'tikaf is recommended and fasting is one of its pillars, especially for travelers who have specific conditions. A listener shares a personal story illustrating how the experience of I'tikaf at Jamkaran Mosque led them to return to their innate nature and wear the hijab. Ayatollah Javadi Amoli also highlights that I'tikaf washes away the dust of the heart and leads to a deeper knowledge of God. Finally, the program concludes with prayers for those observing I'tikaf and news about a judicial power directive regarding prisoner leave for Nowruz.
