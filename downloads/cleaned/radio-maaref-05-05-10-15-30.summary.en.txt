In the radio program "Religion and Life," Ajar Falahzadeh analyzed the pathology of rituals honoring the Ahl al-Bayt by examining the various stages of worship. He outlined four distinct stages of honoring the Ahl al-Bayt: spiritual, verbal, individual practical, and social, each playing a crucial role in understanding correct practices. The spiritual stage involves pure love rooted in Quranic verses and serves as the foundation for all other actions. The verbal stage is manifested through reciting salutations on Muhammad and his family, which hold a special place in prayers and supplications. Individual practical actions, such as carrying a tasbih or wearing mourning clothes, are presented as external signs of respect. The social stage includes large-scale events like the Arbaeen walk, recognized as an international occasion that invites others to the faith. The program concluded with the recitation and translation of the opening verses of Surah Zumar to complement the discussion.
