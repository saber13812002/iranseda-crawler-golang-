In the radio program "Mashq-e Raah," Professor Faqih emphasized the concept of God's infinite grace, citing Du'a Kumayl and Du'a Mahrajab to explain that humans have no inherent right to spiritual ranks. He argued that relying solely on divine grace eliminates despair and depression, particularly for those facing the challenges of retirement. Addressing a listener's question about psychological distress, the professor highlighted that God sustains individuals from birth to old age, providing both sustenance and peace. Regarding the repulsion of Satan's whispers, especially doubts about ritual purity, he advised controlling the imagination and strengthening willpower through decisive actions and physical exercise. The recitation of "Dhikrullahi tatma'innul qulub" was recommended as a primary remedy for inner peace, provided it is done with sincere belief and consistency. Additionally, specific invocations like "La hawla wa la quwwata illa billah" were suggested to ward off both jinn and human temptations. The session concluded with the assertion that hope in God's grace is the fundamental key to solving both material and spiritual problems.
