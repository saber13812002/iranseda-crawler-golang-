The radio program "Tale of Freedom" begins with the testimony of Mr. Kazem Zarandi, a former prisoner of war from the Iran-Iraq conflict. He recounts the harsh conditions in Iraqi captivity camps, highlighting severe shortages of hygiene and medical supplies. Zarandi describes the humane and respectful behavior of an Iraqi doctor and guard soldiers who treated the Iranian prisoners with kindness. He also emphasizes how the prisoners maintained their morale through Quran recitation and strong bonds of brotherhood. The segment references the book "Those Twenty-Three" as a valuable source for understanding the experiences of captivity. The broadcast is accompanied by patriotic music and religious prayers. Towards the end, a short story is shared about a doctor bride who chose a spiritual and socially responsible dowry. This narrative conveys a positive message about shared life values and community service.
