The radio program begins with a theological discussion on the significance of prostration on the earth as a primary sign of Shia identity.
The host explains that this act symbolizes humility and the supremacy of God over all creation, citing relevant religious narrations.
The discussion then shifts to the two types of pilgrimage and highlights the Arbaeen visitation as the third essential sign of Shia faith.
The second part of the show features a dramatic narration from the book "Sayyidman Hosseini," depicting the Arbaeen walk.
This narrative recounts the spiritual conversations between two journalists and pilgrims from various countries, including Australia and Thailand.
These dialogues emphasize the pilgrims' deep hope for the Imam's reappearance and their emotional connection to Karbala.
The program concludes with prayers for peace, the victory of Muslims, and the preservation of Iran's cultural identity.
Additionally, the show announces the 20th specialized conference for Quran reciters, focusing on innovation in recitation styles.
