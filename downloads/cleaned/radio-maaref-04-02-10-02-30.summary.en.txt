The radio program began by highlighting the humility and avoidance of formalities in the lives of the late Ayatollah Hujjat and Imam Khomeini. Subsequently, Professor Tootbahi discussed the requirements for Hajj, emphasizing the need for spiritual, physical, and ritual preparation before the journey. He strongly advised pilgrims to purify their wealth by paying their Khums before traveling to ensure their funds are halal. In the Q&A section, Hujjat al-Islam Holsan explained the rulings regarding missed fasts and their expiation for the sick. Hujjat al-Islam Sheikh Shabani clarified the rules for prayer while traveling and how to estimate the number of missed prayers for a deceased person. Hujjat al-Islam Najati stated that the prayers of a living person cannot be performed by a proxy. Hujjat al-Islam Jawahiri explained that Khums is not due on assets currently in use or necessary for future essential needs. Finally, Hujjat al-Islam Zineldini ruled that paying restitution for wrongfully acquired wealth to licensed and reputable charities is permissible.
