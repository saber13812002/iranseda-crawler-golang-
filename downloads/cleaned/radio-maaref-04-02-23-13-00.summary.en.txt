In this radio program, ten signs of a rational human being are explained based on a narration from Imam Reza (AS), distinguishing innate intellect from mere knowledge or memory. The first two characteristics are consistently pursuing good and avoiding evil in both speech and action, rooted in piety and sincerity. The third and fourth signs involve magnifying the good deeds of others while minimizing one's own services to avoid arrogance and hypocrisy. A rational person does not tire of meeting people's needs, as noted in the fifth sign, and remains eager to acquire knowledge and study, as highlighted in the sixth. The seventh characteristic is feeling a sense of poverty and need before God, which leads to divine honor. In the eighth sign, the individual considers themselves humble and submissive before God, rather than before enemies. The ninth sign is humility and viewing others as superior to oneself, which prevents pride and hasty judgment. Finally, these signs provide a practical model for self-improvement and achieving moral excellence in daily life.
