The radio program "Mashq-e Rah," focusing on monotheistic ethics, featured Hujjat al-Islam Faqihy, a member of the Imam Khomeini Seminary faculty.
In the Q&A segment, Faqihy emphasized the importance of maintaining boundaries in humor and social interactions to avoid offending others.
He advised using educational stories to warm up gatherings instead of inappropriate jokes that might compromise personal dignity.
Addressing another question, he identified "intellect" as God's greatest gift and stressed the necessity of reflection before speaking or making decisions.
Citing the conduct of senior clerics, he noted that contemplation helps prevent regret and aligns one's actions with divine pleasure.
The program also highlighted the significance of Hajj as a unique opportunity to understand global issues and the Islamic principle of human equality.
Listeners were encouraged to use the "Iran Seda" app to access the program archive and submit their feedback.
