In this lecture, Ayatollah Estadi emphasizes the critical importance of focusing on the youth to secure the future religious and cultural integrity of society. Citing Islamic narrations, he explains that young people, due to their receptive hearts, represent both a golden opportunity for proper upbringing and a significant threat against enemy infiltration. The Prophet and Imam Sadiq (AS) warned that authorities must act swiftly to educate the youth before deviant groups can influence them. The responsibility for proper upbringing extends beyond the clergy to include families, the education system, media, and even sports facilities. Families are urged to create a supportive and intimate home environment to prevent emotional withdrawal and exposure to harmful external influences. Negligence by parents and responsible institutions could lead to the dominance of doubts and deviant ideologies among the younger generation. Ultimately, utilizing the opportunity of youth and establishing strong theological foundations are essential for maintaining the spiritual health of society in the future.
