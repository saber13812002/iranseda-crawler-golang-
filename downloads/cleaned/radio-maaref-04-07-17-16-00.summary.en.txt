In this program, the political expert discussed the necessity of holding officials accountable for administrative and economic errors, noting the legal challenges in defining such actions as crimes. He highlighted that while parliamentary oversight tools like impeachment exist, the technical complexity of executive decisions makes it difficult to determine culpability. The discussion also covered the debate surrounding Iran's potential withdrawal from the Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty (NPT), analyzing views on either full exit or merely reducing inspections. Additionally, the program addressed Imam Khomeini's book "Kashf al-Asrar" and the reasons behind his opposition to its republication after the revolution. Finally, the expert emphasized the critical importance of national unity and internal cohesion as the primary defense against sanctions and military threats. He argued that strengthening the domestic economy and resilience is the only viable solution to neutralize the impact of external aggression.
