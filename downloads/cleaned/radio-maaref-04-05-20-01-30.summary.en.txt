The program emphasizes the importance of standing by the Imam during difficult times, citing Imam Hussein's (PBUH) words and the loyalty of his companions.
History shows that many people regretted their absence after the martyrdom of Imam Hussein (PBUH), but true loyalty is tested in critical moments.
Imam Hussein (PBUH) rose up to command what is right and forbid what is wrong, serving as a model for all Muslims.
The second part narrates the journey of pilgrims from the Mehran border to the shrine of Imam Ali (AS) in Najaf.
The travelers face challenges such as fatigue, transportation issues, and losing track of their cultural group.
Ultimately, with the help of a local elderly man, they perform their prayers and hope to reconnect with their group using the internet.
This narrative combines religious reflections with the lived experience of pilgrims on their journey of worship.
