Yousef Danesh Sadegh, a veteran cameraman at IRIB, shared his 49-year career covering major sports and cultural events in a radio interview. He described the emotional peak of his career as the moment Hassan Zadeh won gold at the Sydney Olympics, highlighting the national pride it evoked in viewers. Danesh Sadegh expressed concern over the lack of recognition for retired staff, urging management to utilize their valuable experience and skills within the organization. He reflected on his apprenticeship under legends like Abbas Afshar and Ruhollah Ebrahimipour, crediting them for teaching him professional ethics and craft. Despite facing difficult conditions and limited resources, sports cameramen have consistently documented Iran's golden sporting moments with high dedication and responsibility. The veteran emphasized his pride in mentoring a new generation of cameramen and directors, stressing the need for institutional support for these artists. He concluded by expressing gratitude to his colleagues and listeners, reaffirming the importance of maintaining a positive and service-oriented spirit in sports media.
