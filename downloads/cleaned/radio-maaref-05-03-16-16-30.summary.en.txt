The radio program initially emphasized the necessity of possessing a special faith to achieve salvation in the afterlife and dedicating one's efforts accordingly. It then explored Quranic verses from Surah As-Saffat, interpreting the heavens as spiritual barriers preventing Satan from eavesdropping on divine secrets. Contemporary exegetes argued that these references to heavens denote spiritual ranks and the higher realm rather than physical skies. The discussion subsequently addressed the fate of Jesus (PBUH), refuting the claim that he was killed by the Jews. Based on Quranic verses and traditions, it was concluded that God kept Jesus alive and elevated him to the higher realm. In response to a listener's question, it was clarified that the Imam of the Time (AJ) lives among people in our world, unlike Jesus. Finally, the concept of Taqiyya was explained as a Quranic wisdom for preserving the lives of believers in dangerous circumstances.
