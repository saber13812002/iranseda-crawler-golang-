The radio program "Parsaiyan" emphasizes the central role of faith and remembrance of God in achieving inner peace, citing verses from the Quran. Hujjat al-Islam Vahidi explains that only through reliance on God can hearts be freed from worldly anxieties, referencing the Quran's assurance of tranquility for the faithful. To illustrate this concept, the program shares two historical anecdotes about the lives of Ayatollah Mohammad Hossein Sadr and Imam Khomeini. The first story describes the remarkable composure of Ayatollah Sadr's father during World War II in Tabriz, maintaining calm despite extreme poverty and fear. The second anecdote recounts Imam Khomeini's serene reaction to a sudden explosion under his pulpit, where he continued his lecture without any sign of distress. These examples demonstrate that deep faith renders individuals resilient and peaceful in the face of the most severe crises. The program concludes with the recitation of spiritual supplications, praying for a connection with the Infallibles and the attainment of heart's peace.
