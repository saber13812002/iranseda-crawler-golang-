This radio program emphasized the severe spiritual danger of backbiting and the immediate duty to defend one's brother in faith, citing narrations from Sheikh Saduq and Nahj al-Balagha.
Imam Ali (AS) warned that remaining silent in the face of slander makes one complicit in the sin and emboldens the offender to continue their harmful speech.
The wisdom of the First Imam highlights that incorrect words inflict wounds that never heal, whereas meaningful silence is far more valuable than empty talk.
The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) consistently identified guarding one's tongue as the primary sign of piety and the key to the forgiveness of sins during his thousands of sermons.
According to religious traditions, the true measure of a worshipper is their honesty and avoidance of slander, rather than merely the quantity of their prayers or fasts.
In the concluding segment, listeners were urged to take small but impactful steps for environmental conservation, such as saving water and energy, while observing the mourning days.
The program concluded with Quranic Q&A, prayers for the reappearance of the Imam, and a call for social solidarity and support for the oppressed people of Gaza.
