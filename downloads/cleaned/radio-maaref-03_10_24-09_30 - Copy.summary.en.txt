This radio program examines the educational and ethical style of Ayatollah Ahmad Mostahedi Tehrani. He exerted deep influence on the moral transformation of his students' families by closely monitoring their behavior, appearance, and worship. Mostahedi emphasized discipline, congregational prayer, and the quality of night prayers performed with humility and devotion. He also criticized irrelevant entertainments, such as music, within religious environments. His students, including Ayatollah Mohsen Atri Najad, recalled his unparalleled order and ethics in the seminary. The program also highlights the concise and engaging teaching style of Ayatollah Seyed Kazem Arafi in promoting religious knowledge. The key takeaway is that the effective sermons of these scholars were rooted in their own personal practice and self-discipline. This practical approach ensured that their words resonated deeply with their audience and left a lasting impact.
