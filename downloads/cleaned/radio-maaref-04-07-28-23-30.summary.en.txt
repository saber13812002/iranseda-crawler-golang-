Ayatollah Abdollahi recounts his student days at the Alavi School in Qom, which was managed by Martyr Beheshti and Ayatollah Rabani Shirazi. He describes the pre-revolutionary seminary environment as lacking modern administrative structures and formal examinations, noting that students had to independently choose their teachers and study plans. Prominent figures like Beheshti and Rabani Shirazi, who were active in the revolutionary struggle, closely supervised the educational quality during this period. After the revolution, the seminary system underwent a fundamental transformation by introducing age-based grades and regular exams, a stark contrast to the previous informal era. The program concludes with a religious song emphasizing the practical duty of the Imam's awaiters to prepare themselves in various fields. Additionally, Ayatollah Khomeini's speech criticizes Arab rulers for their negligence toward Islamic interests and calls for unity against American dominance.
