In this radio program broadcast from Abadan, the philosophy of visiting Imam Hussein (PBUH) and the importance of physically being in Karbala were discussed. The guest, Hajj Mr. Badri, explained that Karbala serves as a base for fighting tyranny, where martyrs reach a status in which their blood and bodies become sacred. Citing a narration from Imam Sadiq (PBUH), he noted that Imam Hussein (PBUH) looks upon the pilgrims and martyrs with greater awareness than they have of themselves. The program highlighted the deep connection between the martyrs of the Sacred Defense and Imam Hussein (PBUH), recounting memories of fighters in Shalamcheh and Abadan. Despite the harsh conditions of war, these martyrs carried Karbala in their hearts and entered the battlefield with love and faith. It was emphasized that loving Imam Hussein (PBUH) and following the path of the martyrs is the key to achieving spirituality and inner peace.
