In the first segment of the program, Hojjat al-Islam Dr. Irandoust interpreted the 97th wisdom of Saadi's Gulistan, emphasizing the avoidance of stinginess. Citing Quranic verses and narrations, he described stinginess as the root of worldly attachment and a cause of humiliation in both this life and the hereafter. Saadi compared extracting wealth from a miser to dying in the process, concluding that such individuals die in disgrace while their wealth remains in the hands of their enemies. The program then introduced the novel "Daughter of Shina" by Ahali Rostayi, which narrates the life of Mrs. Gadam-Kheir Mohammadi and her martyr husband. Following this, Majid Salimi, a former prisoner of war held by the Ba'ath forces, shared his memories of captivity and the harsh conditions in the camps. He recounted the bravery of fellow prisoners in organizing Muharram mourning and their successful escape from a hospital by wearing colored clothes to blend in. He also described how they were recaptured after escaping and how reciting salutations created chaos to help them flee an Iraqi officer's session.
