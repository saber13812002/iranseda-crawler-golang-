The radio program begins by discussing the spiritual rank and various levels of martyrdom according to Islamic jurisprudence and narrations.
It then addresses listener questions, clarifying that khums is not due on gifts given by a husband for his wife's clothing.
The program explains that zakat, khums, and kaffarah paid by a third party are only valid with the owner's permission and the individual's own intention for God.
Regarding surrogacy, the program notes that while Imam Khomeini deemed it impermissible, followers of other maraji' like Ayatollah Khamenei may act according to their specific rulings.
In the section on wudu, it is emphasized that the face must be washed completely from the hairline to the chin, including slightly below, to ensure validity.
Forgetting to mention God's name during animal slaughter does not render the meat haram, but ignorance of this rule does.
For individuals who are physically unable to perform Hajj and have no hope of recovery, sending a representative is mandatory.
Finally, the program addresses potential issues in religious processions, recommending preliminary meetings among officials to prevent such problems.
