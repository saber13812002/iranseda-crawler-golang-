In this segment of the program, a question is raised regarding the permissibility of receiving monthly interest from a father instead of an immediate repayment for a vehicle loan. Hujjat al-Islam Zain al-Abidini examines this financial arrangement by citing the fatwa of Ayatollah Javadi Amoli. He explains that if the father tells his son to receive a certain amount as monthly profit, there is no religious objection to this practice. The key point is that this financial relationship between father and son does not constitute usury. Therefore, there is no religious barrier to carrying out this transaction.
