In this radio program, a political analyst emphasized the role of the Islamic Republic of Iran as the primary challenge to American hegemony and its strategy of establishing a unipolar world. He argued that Iran, leveraging its geopolitical position, missile and nuclear capabilities, and the ideology of Velayat-e Faqih, has prevented the Middle East from becoming a network of US military bases and a consumer market. A retired French general also noted that Iran's steadfastness has extinguished the US military appetite for decades, placing Washington in a situation similar to the Vietnam syndrome. In the second segment, the Supreme Leader's message regarding the necessity of national unity and avoiding internal divisions in the face of enemies was discussed. It was stressed that criticism must be conducted within the framework of maintaining national cohesion without sending signals of weakness to adversaries. Finally, the program concluded with the recitation and translation of verses from the Surahs of Saba and Fatir, along with religious prayers and announcements of future schedules.
