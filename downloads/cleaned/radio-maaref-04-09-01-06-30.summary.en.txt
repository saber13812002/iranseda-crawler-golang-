The program begins with a Quranic interpretation emphasizing that martyrs are fully alive in the Barzakh and on Judgment Day.
The commentator explains that the soul and body reunite in both realms, and recognizing individuals on Judgment Day is a fundamental religious principle.
The discussion then shifts to the week's occasions, specifically approaching the anniversary of Fatima Zahra's martyrdom and Mother's Day.
The host invites listeners to express their gratitude to their mothers by sending short, affectionate messages via the program's hotline.
In the historical events segment, the UN Security Council Resolution 242 from 58 years ago and the assassination of John F. Kennedy from 62 years ago are reviewed.
The program concludes with greetings to internet taxi drivers and closing prayers.
