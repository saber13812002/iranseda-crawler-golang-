This radio program, dedicated to the night of Arbaeen, began with recitations of elegies to set a spiritual tone for the pilgrimage.
The core of the broadcast featured a live interview with a pilgrim from Qom who was on his fourteenth trip to Karbala.
The guest emphasized that the atmosphere of the shrine is indescribable and highlighted the selfless devotion of children and youth enduring harsh conditions.
He encouraged listeners who could not travel to participate spiritually through sincere prayers, advising them not to feel left out if their hearts are with the Imam.
The program concluded with the recitation of the Arbaeen ziyarat on behalf of all devotees, expressing hope for the reappearance of Imam Mahdi.
