In the "Persman Quran" radio program, expert Rostam Najat addressed listeners' questions regarding Quranic concepts. He clarified that while Jinn and humans are of different origins, they are addressed together in the Quran because both possess intellect, free will, and religious obligations. The expert also explained the grammatical nuances of the verb "falah" in the "Af'al" form, distinguishing between its usage for possession and transitivity. He emphasized the Quranic evidence for the awareness of inanimate objects, citing verses about the glorification of creation and the earth testifying on Judgment Day. Finally, he noted that most divine commands to celestial bodies are interpreted as cosmological acts of will, except for specific instances like mountains harmonizing with David, which imply genuine consciousness and obedience.
