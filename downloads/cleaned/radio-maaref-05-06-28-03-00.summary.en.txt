In this program, Ayatollah Mazaheri emphasizes the necessity for young people to attain deep certainty and avoid imitation in the principles of faith. He argues that reason alone cannot withstand cultural aggression or the whispers of Satan; only heartfelt belief leads to consistent action. To reach this spiritual station, one must progress from mere certainty to divine illumination, where worship is performed with love and full presence of heart. In the second segment, Masoudi narrates a hadith from the book of Kafi where Imam Sadiq (AS) writes a brief letter compelling a Shia governor named Najashi to forgive a man's taxes and honor him with valuable gifts. This story illustrates that making believers happy and assisting them also pleases God and His Messenger. The program concludes with the recitation of a supplication from the book of Tawhid, asking for connection with God's saints and divine guidance.
