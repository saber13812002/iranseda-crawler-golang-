In this discussion, Professor Mehdi Rasakhi analyzes Imam Khomeini's management strategies during the early years of the Islamic Revolution. He draws a significant parallel between the seizure of the US Embassy and the recent Operation True Promise, highlighting the continuity in managing external threats. The Imam utilized the hostage crisis to expose the US's consistent bad faith by setting four specific conditions for their release. Meanwhile, global powers attempted to destabilize the new regime through coups, assassinations, and the incitement of ethnic conflicts. The Imam countered these multifaceted plots by fostering public awareness and employing intelligent crisis management to solidify the system's foundations. This historical analysis underscores the importance of understanding these past strategies for the current generation to navigate future challenges.
