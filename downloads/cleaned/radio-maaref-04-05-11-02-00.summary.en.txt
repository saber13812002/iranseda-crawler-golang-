The program began by emphasizing the virtue of "accepting the truth," using anecdotes of scholars admitting their scientific errors to highlight the importance of abandoning stubbornness for mental and spiritual health. In the second part, Dr. Saeed Jalili analyzed the Supreme Leader's message regarding Iran's accelerated progress, stressing the need for a shared national understanding of the current geopolitical situation. He interpreted the recent conflict not merely as a military engagement, but as an attempt by a decaying global order to curb Iran's independent advancements. Jalili argued that the silence and support of major powers for attacks on scientists indicate that the international system is collapsing, with Iran standing as a primary agent of change through its resilience. Finally, he emphasized the necessity of adopting an active, "game-changing" approach in diplomacy and foreign policy to break the monopoly of Western powers and assert Iran's independent position in the emerging global order.
