On the occasion of Lady Masoumeh's birthday and Girls' Day, the radio program emphasized that God rewards individuals based on their personal merit and actions rather than mere family lineage.
The core of spiritual elevation is defined as submission to the religion and possessing a heartfelt faith rooted in absolute trust in God.
Obedience to divine commands and the control of one's body parts, viewed as divine trusts, are highlighted as essential pillars for spiritual growth.
The segment concluded with prayers for the guidance of youth and the provision of suitable spouses for young men and women.
In the final part, a speech by Imam Khomeini was aired, stressing the necessity of preserving Islamic institutions such as the Assembly of Experts and the Islamic government.
Imam Khomeini warned that covert attempts to weaken these religious structures are inherent to the revolution and must be firmly opposed.
These discussions were broadcast on Radio Ma'aref during the Decade of Karameh, under the slogan "Love of Iran, Imam Reza."
