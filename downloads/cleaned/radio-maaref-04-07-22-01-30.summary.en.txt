The program begins by recounting the historical Battle of the Trench, where Ali (AS) single-handedly defeated Umar ibn Abd Wud to save the honor of Islam.
During this critical moment, the Prophet (PBUH) declared Ali as his brother and successor, praying to God not to leave him alone.
The second part of the broadcast features testimonies from war prisoners describing the harsh conditions in Iraqi camps and their covert efforts to maintain their faith.
One prisoner narrates the martyrdom of his comrade during the initial prisoner exchange, highlighting his courage in his final moments.
Another story details the journey of Martyr Nabav and his companion from Najaf to Karbala, including his brave confrontation with an Arab robber.
These narratives demonstrate the spiritual authority and freedom of Iranian clerics and prisoners in the face of physical threats and hardships.
The program concludes with a final prayer dedicated to the lovers of the Ahl al-Bayt (AS) and the martyrs.
