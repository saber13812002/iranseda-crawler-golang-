This radio program utilizes the teachings of Hazrat Fatimah (SA) to explain the necessity of divine revelation to complement human reason and guide moral decision-making.
Prayer is presented as a vital tool for liberation from shirk and the three roots of disbelief: greed, envy, and arrogance, which are the sources of ethical corruption.
The text critiques deviant perspectives, including Ash'ari thought and positivism that view religious laws as mere social contracts, as well as "religious enlightenment" movements that claim the absolute sufficiency of reason.
It is emphasized that while divine commands possess wisdom, humans do not possess complete knowledge of all their underlying causes and should not claim total comprehension of their philosophy.
The Murji'ah view and religious liberalism, which separate faith from action and erase the boundaries between truth and falsehood, are strongly rejected as dangerous deviations.
Finally, hidden shirk is defined as the internal worship of multiple idols such as power and lust, while true monotheism is described as submission to the singular divine will rather than human-centric dominance.
