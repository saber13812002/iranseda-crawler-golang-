The core message of this sermon by Imam Ali emphasizes the necessity of avoiding religious innovations and strictly adhering to the Sunnah.
The speaker cites the hadith that a believer's intention is superior to their action, offering comfort to those who cannot physically travel to Karbala due to obstacles.
The program highlights a specific initiative to send 3,313 young pilgrims as first-time visitors, urging the audience to contribute financially and join the show's digital channels.
The broadcast includes the recitation of Surah Al-Fath and a tribute to the daily Quranic recitation habit of the late General Salami.
The episode concludes with prayers for the Mahdi and a call to maintain public morale and spiritual resilience in the face of economic and security challenges.
