Mohammad Hassan Fazel Golpayegani grew up in a rural village, facing the hardships of herding and farming from a young age. Despite these difficulties, his success in the final sixth-grade exams encouraged him to pursue higher education in Qom. There, he refined his linguistic and religious skills under teachers like Tofiqi Kashani, who taught him precise English pronunciation and comparative religion. He had a deep, intrinsic desire to become a cleric, rejecting other career paths such as teaching or continuing agricultural work. After his father's death, he faced financial and legal complexities regarding the family's share of water rights and land under the feudal system. He approached the landowner to request the transfer of his father's share to ensure the legitimacy of his future earnings. The landowner, impressed by his character, waived all debts and granted him full ownership of the rights. This narrative highlights his strong determination to achieve his religious goals while resolving material issues with integrity.
