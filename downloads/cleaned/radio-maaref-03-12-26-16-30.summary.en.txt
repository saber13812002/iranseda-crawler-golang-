In this radio program, experts emphasized the importance of interactive causality in marital relationships and the role of affection and gratitude in improving a spouse's well-being.
A listener complained about seven years of physical and verbal abuse from her husband, asking how to forgive and cope with such pain.
The respondent recommended starting with emotional catharsis through counseling or writing, followed by psychological empowerment via supportive and spiritual circles.
It was explained that forgiveness does not mean ignoring the other person's mistakes but rather releasing internal anger for one's own peace of mind.
In the second part, the discussion addressed a young man suffering from obsessive thoughts after a temporary marriage, advising him to see a psychologist first and a psychiatrist if necessary.
The program concluded with congratulations on the birth of Imam Hassan (PBUH) and a discussion on the profound concept of "patience" against material attachments and adapting to the audience's level of understanding.
These conversations demonstrate that managing emotions and deeply understanding ethical concepts are the keys to resolving many life challenges.
