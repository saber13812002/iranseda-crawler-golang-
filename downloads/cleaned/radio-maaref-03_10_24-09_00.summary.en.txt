She recommended strategies such as unconditional acceptance, two-way communication, granting freedom of action, and validating children's good deeds to enhance intimacy and self-confidence. The discussion also touched upon lying and taking money, advising parents to investigate root causes like shame in asking or overly strict financial rules. Finally, it was suggested that establishing a clear budget for the child's needs can help correct such behaviors effectively.
