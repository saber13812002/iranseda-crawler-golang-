The radio program began by congratulating the birth of Imam Reza (PBUH) and honoring the Ten Days of Dignity.
The host encouraged listeners to download the "Iran Sadar" app to enjoy its various services.
The national "Mirror and Suitcase" campaign was introduced to provide dowries for 8,888 couples via SMS.
A segment featured religious music and poetry dedicated to the love and reverence of Imam Reza (PBUH).
A narrative about Solomon and robbers was shared to highlight the importance of safety for travelers in trade.
It was recounted that Imam Reza (PBUH) once ended a drought in Khorasan by performing the rain prayer.
The program announced the start of the "100th Anniversary of Re-interpretation of Hoozi" conference with live coverage.
It concluded with blessings for newlyweds and wishes for all listeners to visit the holy shrine.
