In this program, Hojjat al-Islam Morteza Agha Tehrani, relying on Quranic verses and narrations, introduced Hazrat Zahra (SA) as the supreme role model for all men and women. He emphasized that knowing and following this model is the key to achieving human perfection and salvation in the afterlife. The speaker insisted that adhering to religious principles, maintaining hijab, avoiding sin, and being responsible for one's wealth and social conduct are essential ways to benefit from this model. He also criticized the incorrect behavior of some Muslims, urging them to be the adornment of religion and society rather than a source of disgrace for Islam. Finally, referencing the martyrdom of Hazrat Zahra and the Supreme Leader's presence at the mourning ceremony, he called for gratitude towards the Ahl al-Bayt and continuing their path.
