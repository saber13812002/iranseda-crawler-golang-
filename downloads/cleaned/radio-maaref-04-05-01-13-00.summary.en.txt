This speech interprets the slogan "Labbayk Ya Hussein" as a pledge of absolute allegiance to the Guardianship of the Islamic Jurist (Wilayat). The speaker emphasizes that the true greatness of Karbala lies in this spiritual loyalty rather than merely in the physical sacrifice. Stories of bravery from Karbala, including those of children and women, are cited as symbols of unwavering devotion to the cause. The recitation of war chants by Imam Hussein's companions is highlighted as a sign of their faith, courage, and clear purpose. The sermon further identifies the Guardian of the Islamic Jurist as the deputy of the Hidden Imam and the proof of God, equating obedience to him with obedience to Hussein. By referencing the betrayal of the people of Kufa, the speaker warns that abandoning this guardianship leads to spiritual defeat and isolation. Ultimately, the audience is urged to remain steadfast in their support of the Guardianship under all circumstances, no matter how difficult.
