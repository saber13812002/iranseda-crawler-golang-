This radio program emphasizes the necessity of avoiding lustful glances and envy, advocating instead for genuine happiness for others as a core characteristic of true Shi'ism. Citing narrations from the book of Kafi, the program defines acceptable worship as "good intention in obedience," which requires purity from hypocrisy and strict adherence to the teachings of the Ahl al-Bayt. Imam Sadiq (AS) interprets the concept of "Nasikh and Mansukh" as recognizing and obeying the Imam of the time in each era. The discussion also stresses the obligation to pay the Khums accurately, noting that it cannot be replaced by other charitable acts without following the specific religious framework. Finally, the program concludes with the recitation of supplications by Hajj Abbas Salehi, inviting listeners to deepen their spiritual connection and pure servitude to God.
