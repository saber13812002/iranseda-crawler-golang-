This radio program discussed the limitations of human cognition in understanding the metaphysical truths and spiritual ranks of the Prophets and the Ahl al-Bayt.
It emphasized that the best way to know God and His attributes is through the words of God and the Infallibles, as human understanding alone is insufficient.
The immense blessings of Imam Ali's existence in preserving Islam and preventing theological deviations and distortions were explained.
His critical role in battles such as the Trench and his impact on the survival of the true religion for future generations were highlighted.
In the second part, a hadith from Imam Hasan Askari (AS) regarding four traits of true believers—piety, worship, asceticism, and diligence in obedience—was presented.
It was explained that piety means avoiding doubtful matters and gray areas, not just abstaining from clear prohibitions.
Asceticism was defined as being free from worldly attachment, while diligence in obedience means abandoning both hidden and open sins.
Finally, the necessity of acting upon the commands of the Infallibles and following the Prophet's (PBUH) example as the complete model for Islamic life was reiterated.
