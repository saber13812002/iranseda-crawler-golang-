In this radio program, Professor Tavassir emphasized the spiritual status of Imam Ali (AS) as the perfect mirror of truth and the source of prophetic knowledge, referencing Ghadir ziyarat and Allameh Amini's works. He highlighted the subtle theological distinction regarding the rotation of truth and suggested producing a documentary series focused on "Imamate in Ghadir Ziyarat." The second part of the show featured an interview with Hojjat al-Islam Mousavibagh Moghaddam regarding social issues and the challenges surrounding hijab. Mousavibagh Moghaddam rejected the claim that the current situation is worse than the Pahlavi era, attributing the erosion of family structures and shifting attitudes primarily to the negative impact of social media and Western cultural aggression. He proposed a combination of administrative mandates for government employees and the promotion of role models among believing, educated women as key solutions for social reform. The discussion also touched upon economic challenges and executive management issues in the country. The program concluded with general prayers for the nation's health, security, and the acceleration of the Mahdi's reappearance.
