In this radio program, Mrs. Ebrahimipour, the wife of martyr Mostafa Sadrzadeh, recounts the immense hardships of separation caused by her husband's frequent deployments to Syria. She describes her fear of witnessing his departures and her efforts to hide her grief, as well as her paradoxical joy when he was injured, as it meant he would temporarily stay by her side. Memories from her pregnancy and childbirth highlight the deep mutual love and sacrifice, particularly when Mostafa visited the hospital despite his severe wounds. The purchase of an art piece by Mostafa served as his last gift before martyrdom, leaving a heavy emotional burden on his wife. The latter part of the program emphasizes the historical role of Tabriz and Azerbaijan in political movements and resistance against foreign invasions. It also highlights the region's enduring attachment to Islam and Iran, and its crucial role in preserving the country's unity. These narratives depict a profound story of love, patience, and resilience in the face of life's hardships and the defense of national and religious values.
