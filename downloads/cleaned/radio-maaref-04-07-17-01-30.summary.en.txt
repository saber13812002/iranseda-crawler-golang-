In this program, Ayatollah Ghalam-Ali Nimabadi uses the Quranic verse "Muhammad is not the father of any of your men" and a linguistic analysis of the term "Khatam" to prove that the Prophet of Islam is the final messenger. He argues that the flow of divine revelation and celestial news has ended with him, thereby refuting the claims of later figures like Qadiani and Baha'i as prophets. The speaker emphasizes that the twelve successors' role is solely to interpret and preserve the religion, not to claim prophethood. The second part of the show features the personal testimony of Fatemeh Hajimirsaei, the mother of martyrs Majid and Hossein Deljou. She recounts her prophetic dream about her sons' martyrdom and her husband's unwavering support for their participation in the war. By sharing the moment of their sacrifice and the peace she felt upon hearing the news, she illustrates the profound patience and love of martyrs' mothers. These narratives highlight how the families of the martyrs, through their acceptance of God's will, continue to uphold the values of the Islamic revolution.
