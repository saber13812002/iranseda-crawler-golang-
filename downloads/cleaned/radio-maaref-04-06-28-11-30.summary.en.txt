This radio program provided various jurisprudential answers to listeners' questions. In the rulings section, it was explained that blood between teeth is pure but forbidden to consume unless it is dissolved in saliva and imperceptible to the eye. Regarding eyebrow transplant by a non-mahram doctor, the procedure is impermissible if it is not medically necessary and involves unlawful touch or exposure. Financial aid received from welfare organizations like the Red Crescent or Behzisti is exempt from Khums, whereas income from personal work remains subject to it if saved. In matters of purity, a slight moisture on the feet that does not prevent water from penetrating does not invalidate wudu. The Friday prayer section emphasized the importance of attendance for staying informed about daily events and strengthening Muslim unity. Furthermore, while most maraji do not consider security barriers between rows to be a problem, followers of Ayatollah Sistani are advised to exercise caution in this regard.
