The radio program "Zamzam Ahkam" concluded by addressing various religious questions from its audience. Hujjat al-Islam Alian Najad clarified that followers must adhere to their own Marja's rulings, with limited exceptions allowing reliance on a spouse's Marja under specific conditions. Regarding family relations, Hujjat al-Islam Mohammadi Sheikh Shabani stated that the uncles and aunts of one's parents are considered mahram to their children and grandchildren. On the issue of blood spots in eggs, citing Ayatollah Javadi Amoli, the program advised that the entire egg should be discarded as a precautionary obligation. In the section on Khums, Hujjat al-Islam Jawahiri emphasized the annual payment of Khums on income, provided that funds are not saved for essential future needs. For missed prayers, the intention for a single prayer was deemed sufficient, and paying for the deceased's missed prayers without a will requires the consent of other heirs. The program ended with blessings and information on how to submit questions via phone or social media.
