In this radio program, Professor Bahmani analyzed the "open file" strategy outlined in the Supreme Leader's statements. He described this approach as a masterpiece of social and communicative management with internal, regional, and international dimensions. A key point of discussion was linking the open file for revenge against martyrs to "historical open files," meaning that recalling past enemy offenses keeps the nation's historical memory alive. Professor Bahmani cited events such as World War II and the 1953 coup to emphasize that a nation aware of these issues will not fall into negligence or deception. He also highlighted the crucial role of the people in realizing these strategies and the importance of continuous awareness. The final conclusion was that relying on the people's power and historical awareness guarantees the Islamic Republic's resilience and success against its enemies.
