The program begins by emphasizing the Quranic verse "Remember Me, and I will remember you," highlighting the importance of remembering God in both times of ease and hardship.
Narrations indicate that those who learn, act, and teach purely for God's sake are honored and remembered with greatness in the assemblies of angels.
Divine reminders may occur through events, dreams, or subtle signs to guide individuals toward perfection and divine satisfaction.
Remembering God acts as a rein that prevents believers from sin and deviation, thereby attracting divine mercy to them.
The discussion then shifts to the philosophy of the late Ayatollah Mischbah Yazdi and Mulla Sadra's rational inquiries.
The fourth objection, which suggests that perceiving the reality of objects should produce their physical effects (like heat) in the mind, is raised and addressed.
Responses include distinguishing between primary and secondary predication, existential standing in the world of imagination, and the difference between mental and external existence.
It is concluded that mental perception presents the essence of objects conceptually in the mind, rather than attributing their physical properties to the mind itself.
