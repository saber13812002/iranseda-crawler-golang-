This radio program examined the prelude to the Battle of Camel and the capture of Basra by the Nakithun group. Historical sources differ on the number of guards killed in Basra, but all agree that it was a mass execution and the first of its kind in Islamic history. Following the departure of Uthman ibn Hani and the martyrdom of Hakim ibn Jabala, the Basra treasury fell under the control of the opponents, sparking internal rivalries among them. Imam Ali (AS) mobilized forces in Medina and moved toward Basra, sending letters to the people of Kufa and Abu Musa al-Ash'ari. Abu Musa, cited as a prime example of the "Qa'idin" who remained neutral during the fitna, ignored the Imam's requests. The concept of Qa'idin refers to individuals who avoid conflict under various pretexts, failing to support the truth over falsehood. The program concluded by highlighting Abu Musa's disregard for the Imam's letters and announced that the discussion would continue in the next session.
