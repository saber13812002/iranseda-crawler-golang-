The radio program began by explaining the rules of entering holy shrines in Ihram for both regular individuals and those with valid excuses.
Subsequently, religious rulings were provided for listener questions, including the lack of need for verbal intention during Tayammum and the precise calculation of Khums on non-cash assets.
Other topics covered included the rules for paying Khums on missed prayers for the deceased and the permissibility of substituting "Subhan Allah" in prostration.
The program also addressed the ruling on touching impure objects with dry or wet hands and the discouragement of performing missed prayers while in a state of Janabah.
The spiritual role of Imamzadehs as guiding stars in the darkness of ignorance was discussed, highlighting their importance in leading society.
The guiding role of Lady Zainab (SA) after the event of Karbala and the significance of martyrdom and sacrifice were emphasized.
Finally, the program concluded with congratulations for the Ten of Karameh, the recitation of religious poetry, and an invitation for listeners to submit their questions.
