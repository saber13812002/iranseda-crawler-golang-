In the radio program "Historical Rummage," Dr. Mostafa Sadeghi addressed listeners' questions regarding early Islamic history. He detailed the life and decisive character of Malik Ashtar, highlighting his role in the battles of Jamal and Siffin. The discussion also covered Malik Ashtar's assassination on Muawiyah's orders and his significant influence on Imam Ali. In the second segment, citing the views of Martyr Motahhari, it was established that the stories in the Quran are historical facts rather than mere allegories. The program also confirmed the authenticity of the report that the Quran was recited while Imam Hussein's head was on a spear, citing both Shia and Sunni sources. The broadcast transitioned to the "Voice of Law" segment, featuring a dramatization based on a real case involving four young men transporting drugs. Dr. Majid Yazdi emphasized the critical importance of teaching teenagers self-esteem and the skill of saying "no" to prevent them from committing crimes.
