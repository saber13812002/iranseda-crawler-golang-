The program begins by highlighting the importance of maintaining a consistent and continuous night sleep schedule according to Islamic traditional medicine.
Key factors for healthy sleep include being deep, having an appropriate duration, occurring at night, and being uninterrupted.
Fragmented sleep or shifting sleep hours to the daytime can lead to serious physical and mental health consequences.
To fully benefit from sleep, one should rest only after food has been digested and the stomach feels light.
The second part of the program interprets a verse from Surah Yunus, emphasizing the necessity of monotheism and the constant remembrance of God.
The Quran criticizes those who seek God's help only in times of hardship and forget Him once the problem is resolved.
A true believer is one who relies on God in all circumstances, whether in peace or in distress.
Finally, the program addresses the role of foreign media in undermining the image of the Islamic Republic and the need to counter such propaganda.
