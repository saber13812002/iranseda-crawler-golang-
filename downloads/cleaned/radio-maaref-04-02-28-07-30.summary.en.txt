The radio program begins by highlighting the value of family bonds and spending time with grandparents. It then announces free museum visits and cultural tours in Qom province to mark International Museum Day and Heritage Week. Dr. Ahmad Gholam Ali emphasizes that true treasure lies in moral upbringing, stressing the importance of honesty and responsibility when facing unexpected opportunities. In the final segment, Professor Mohammad Hadi Falah identifies anger and lust as humanity's greatest enemies, based on the teachings of Imam Ali. He describes anger as an invisible, internal fire that cannot be escaped, as it originates from within the self. Consequently, gaining proper awareness of these psychological traits is essential for maintaining spiritual and moral health.
