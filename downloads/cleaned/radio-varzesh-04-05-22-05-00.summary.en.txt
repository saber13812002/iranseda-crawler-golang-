Former footballer Reza Kangarani discussed his career and his decision to retire in the late 1970s due to emotional issues and dissatisfaction with unfair practices in the sport. Despite possessing technical and physical talent, his sensitivity and refusal to accept certain coaching decisions prevented him from achieving greater success. He recounted his experience winning the Tehran First Division championship with the Azerbaijan team in 1982 and his subsequent local activities until 1986. Kangarani believes that modern football has become poorer in terms of aesthetics and technique, focusing more on results than skill. Although decades have passed, he continues to train regularly and maintains high physical fitness at the age of 53. He expressed his intention to participate in over-50 age group competitions and prepare for physique events. Finally, he raised concerns about the difficulties of coaching in the current environment, citing the lack of time and energy required to educate the younger generation.
