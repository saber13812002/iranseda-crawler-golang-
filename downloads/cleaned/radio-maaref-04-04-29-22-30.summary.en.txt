In this radio program, an instructor analyzed the enemy's strategies for countering truth and the Islamic Republic. He identified the eighth tactic as "creating negligence" among Muslims through political and military deceptions, citing the recent attack following negotiation announcements as an example. The ninth tactic was described as "economic sanctions," illustrated by the historical siege of Abwa and the critical need for domestic production and self-sufficiency to neutralize its effects. Additionally, "Tame'ie," or the corruption and bribery of commanders, was highlighted as a destructive method used by the enemy. The program concluded with the recitation of verses from Surah Al-Jasiyah and a eulogy for the martyrs of the imposed war. It was promised that countermeasures against these threats would be discussed in the next session.
