Following the Israeli airstrike on Iran that resulted in the martyrdom of General Hossein Salami, several nuclear scientists, and senior military commanders, the Supreme Leader appointed new successors for the fallen leaders. President Pezeshkian emphasized the necessity of national unity, stating that Iran's legitimate and powerful response would make the enemy regret its foolish action. The Iranian people held spontaneous protests across the country, demanding a decisive and crushing response from the armed forces to this crime. Simultaneously, governments and international bodies, including Iraq, China, Turkey, and the United Nations, condemned the attack as a violation of sovereignty and international law. While news sources report that Iran is preparing the largest missile attack in history against Israel, alarm sirens have been sounded in the occupied territories, reflecting intense anxiety over the impending Iranian retaliation. Amidst this tension, the holy day of Eid al-Ghadir was observed with special religious and cultural ceremonies across the country and holy shrines.
