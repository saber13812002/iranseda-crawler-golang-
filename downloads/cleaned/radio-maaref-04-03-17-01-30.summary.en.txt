The first part of the program begins with a Quranic interpretation emphasizing the Wilayah of Imam Ali (AS) and his role as the divider of Paradise and Hell.
Loving or hating Ali (AS) is presented as the primary criterion for faith or hypocrisy, with his virtues declared superior to those of all other companions.
The discussion highlights the Qadiriyah Ziyarat and the Ghadir sermon, stressing the covenant with the Quran, the martyrs, and the Islamic Revolution.
Resisting enemy plots and maintaining public unity within the Wilayati system are identified as the main duties of Muslims.
The second part features a narrative from the novel "Javan Dast-be-Sohr," set during the 1963 uprising.
The protagonist, Salār, who has estranged his family, becomes involved in the public protests against the arrest of Imam Khomeini (RA).
After his son Sohrab is wounded in the chaos, Salār transports him to Tehran and treats his wounds with gold leaf due to a lack of medicine.
This story illustrates the contrast between Salār's flawed life and the sacrifices made by the people for the revolution.
