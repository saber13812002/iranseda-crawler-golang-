In this radio program, Professor Mohammadi emphasizes that knowledge is the root of all good, citing Imam Ali (AS), and argues that most immoral acts and crimes stem from ignorance and lack of awareness. He explains that knowledge alone is insufficient; it must be applied within the framework of Islamic laws and with good intentions to avoid becoming harmful. Responding to listener questions, the professor discusses the concept of free will versus divine decree, asserting that while humans are responsible for their choices, God's will may sometimes alter the course of events for a greater good. He also highlights that the Prophet (PBUH) and the Infallibles (AS) are in a constant state of spiritual growth and perfection, never reaching a final limit in their knowledge. The program further identifies harming neighbors as a major sin with severe consequences, given the significant rights neighbors hold over individuals. The professor distinguishes between secular and divine knowledge, noting that even natural sciences become valuable when they lead one to recognize the Creator and serve humanity. Finally, he reminds listeners that the ultimate goal of human life is to defend justice and draw closer to God, rather than pursuing superficial statuses like martyrdom or power for their own sake.
