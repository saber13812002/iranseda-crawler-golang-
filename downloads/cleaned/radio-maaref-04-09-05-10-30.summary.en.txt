In the first segment, Dr. Pourhosseini outlined three golden rules from Islamic traditional medicine to maintain stomach health. He emphasized the prohibition of consuming water, dairy products, and fresh fruits alongside meals to prevent digestive conflicts and diseases. These recommendations were illustrated through the real-life story of a girl named Zahra who had the habit of drinking soda during meals. Subsequently, Hujjat al-Islam Rasoul interpreted verse 32 of Surah An-Nisa to discuss the dangers of envy and unrealistic desires. He warned that Satan uses material aspirations to lead individuals toward sin and improper paths. The core message of this section is to remain content with one's divine allotment and avoid harmful comparisons with others. Finally, the program highlighted the necessity of adhering to the principle of Velayat-e Faqih and defending Islamic interests against external threats.
