The radio program "Mashgh-e Rah" featured Seyyed Ahmad Faqih, a member of the Islamic seminary, discussing the concept of "theistic ethics" and the role of prayer in strengthening one's connection with God. Citing the Quranic verse "and remember His favors," the guest emphasized the importance of gratitude and acknowledging God's blessings before making requests to ensure prayers are effective. In response to listener questions, he described forgetfulness as a blessing that provides relief from past suffering and recommended piety and avoiding worldly distractions to improve memory. To control inappropriate thoughts, he suggested listening to the Quran, reciting specific supplications, and engaging in physical exercise as effective remedies. The program concluded by referencing the story of Prophet Moses and Pharaoh to highlight the necessity of perseverance and reliance on God in the struggle against evil. The guest noted that while the victory of truth often comes at a heavy cost involving the loss of martyrs, truth ultimately prevails over falsehood.
