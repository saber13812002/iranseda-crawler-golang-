On the eve of the martyrdom of Imam Reza, pilgrims from various parts of West Khorasan walked to the holy shrine to pay their respects. Concurrently, Saudi Arabia launched a new system allowing foreign pilgrims to apply directly for Umrah and Ziyarah visas without intermediaries. In a disturbing incident, armed attackers killed at least 27 worshippers in a mosque in northern Nigeria during morning prayers. In Germany, extremist and racist groups have united to exert greater pressure on Muslims and immigrants. These groups aim to restrict Muslim presence by exploiting fears regarding the spread of Islamic culture in the country. Sociologists argue that Muslim immigrants in Germany are law-abiding and beneficial citizens, making hostility towards them foolish. Despite nearly six million Muslims living in Germany, Muslim immigration to the country has decreased by 19 percent over the past five years. Finally, the Iranian army announced the start of the "Stable Authority 404" missile exercise, named after Prophet Muhammad.
