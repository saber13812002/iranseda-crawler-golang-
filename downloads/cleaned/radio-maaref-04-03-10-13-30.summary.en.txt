In this radio program, Dr. Hassan Hashemi Jazi analyzes the methods used to spread religious doubts and the resulting theological challenges within society. He compares emerging intellectual movements such as feminism, atheism, and primitivism to countries with distinct borders and personalities that establish independent identities through attractive discourses. These movements utilize key terms like "science" versus "superstition" and "freedom" versus "coercion" to sow doubt among the youth regarding their religious beliefs. Hashemi Jazi emphasizes the significant role of celebrities and social figures in amplifying these doubts and creating behavioral gaps between generations. He also advocates for shifting from a passive to an aggressive approach in responding to Western human rights violations, citing China's model as an effective strategy for active rebuttal. Finally, he warns that ignoring these challenges will lead to a collapse of faith among a large segment of the society, highlighting the necessity of thoroughly analyzing and understanding these discourses.
