In the first segment of the program, Hujjat al-Islam Rafii emphasized the necessity of forbearance in family relationships, criticizing bad temper and stinginess as destructive factors in married life. Citing the Prophetic tradition, he presented the Prophet's (PBUH) division of time among worship, personal work, and family as a model for maintaining boundaries and addressing the emotional needs of spouses and children. Furthermore, in child-rearing, he advised against comparison and blame, recommending that instead of confronting wrongdoers, society should identify the root causes of their issues and remove social barriers such as poverty and loneliness. Subsequently, the program aired enduring memories from the Holy Defense era, featuring the account of Brigadier General Hamid Shakiiba regarding the early days of the war. Shakiiba described his unit's entry into the Andimeshk region, enemy bombings, and the harsh operational conditions near the Karun Bridge. He detailed the efforts to identify enemy lines amidst heavy fire and the initial anxieties of newly arrived soldiers. These memories depicted the courage and prudence of commanders and fighters as they faced military threats and critical wartime conditions.
