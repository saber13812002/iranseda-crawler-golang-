The program begins with an interpretation of Nahj al-Balagha's 116th sermon, emphasizing the concept of "Yawm al-Taqabun" as the day when humans realize the deceptions of their worldly lives in the face of divine rewards and punishments.
Citing Quranic verses and the words of Imam Ali, the host warns that if the veils of the unseen were lifted, people would sacrifice their wealth and lives out of fear of punishment and regret over their lack of good deeds.
The discussion highlights the society's neglect of religious warnings and moral responsibilities, illustrated by a real-life story of a marriage scam.
To mark the anniversary of Operation Beit al-Maqdis and the liberation of Khorramshahr, the recitation of Surah Yasin was dedicated to the martyrs.
Listeners were also encouraged to resolve financial disputes and support charitable initiatives through specific text message campaigns.
The segment concluded with prayers for pilgrims to Karbala and Medina, as well as greetings to the holy shrines of Fatima Masumeh and Abdol-Azim.
Finally, the program introduced other radio segments and addressed Quranic questions from the audience.
