The radio program "Enduring Memories" begins by introducing recollections from Sardar Hossein Arooj regarding Martyr Mohammad Bonyadi, the commander of the Hazrat Masoumeh Brigade. These memories focus on Bonyadi's humble childhood and his high moral standards, such as his secret acts of charity and his refusal to abuse his authority for personal gain. Sardar Arooj also recounts Bonyadi's bravery in battle and the circumstances of his martyrdom during Operation Muharram, where he was killed by a shell fragment while engaging the enemy. Another segment features a memory from Sardar Hossein Aref about Operation Muharram, highlighting the soldiers' heroism in besieging Iraqi oil facilities and a fighter's swift return to the front after his sister's wedding. The program then shifts to an analysis of the recent conflict, emphasizing Iran's missile response to the Al-Udeid base and its deterrent power against American attempts to conceal the damage. These sections demonstrate that the Islamic Republic has remained the victor in the field, while the enemy failed to achieve its objectives. The program concludes by emphasizing the values of sacrifice and martyrdom, encouraging listeners to follow the path of the martyrs.
