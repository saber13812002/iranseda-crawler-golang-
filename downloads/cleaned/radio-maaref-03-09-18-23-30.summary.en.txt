In this radio program, the guest emphasized maintaining respect and avoiding retaliation in conflicts while strengthening self-control skills.
He introduced the STAR technique (Stop, Think, Review) to increase behavioral awareness and manage emotions during critical moments.
The guest explained the difference between intrinsic, authentic relationships and external, transactional ones, warning against judging family bonds solely by material benefits.
Addressing family challenges, he stressed the need for balance between helping relatives and focusing on the marital life, as well as patience in adapting to the spouse's family.
He also recommended using empathy and open dialogue to understand both parental concerns and the desires of young people when selecting a life partner.
Finally, the necessity of Islamic unity to counter foreign hegemony and the wise use of power for religious and national goals were discussed.
