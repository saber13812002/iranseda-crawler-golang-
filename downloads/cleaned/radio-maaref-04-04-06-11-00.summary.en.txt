The Ma'aref radio program commenced by marking the start of Muharram and the ninth season of Radio Muharram. The Hussaini Shir Khargan ceremony was held in thousands of locations across Iran and 45 countries, emphasizing the struggle against Zionism. The Tehran Sepah commander announced a grand funeral procession for resistance martyms scheduled for Saturday, July 28. After a 13-day suspension, the air repatriation of pilgrims from Medina resumed with five flights. Ayatollah Mazarani stressed the importance of pure love for the Imam of the Age and steadfastness in waiting for his reappearance. Hujjat al-Islam Vahidpour explained that wearing black and public mourning are recommended acts that honor divine symbols. These symbolic actions reflect the unity and commitment of the Islamic community to Hussaini ideals.
