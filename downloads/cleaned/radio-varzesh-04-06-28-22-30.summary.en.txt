Colonel Dr. Chingiz Mousavizadeh, the CEO of the Ground Forces Club and a national shooting champion, discussed his discovery by his mentor and his entry into the national team in 1991. He highlighted his silver medal at the Peace and Friendship Tournament as a pivotal moment in his athletic career while addressing the challenges of sanctions and ammunition shortages. After retiring from competition, he transitioned into managing the Ground Forces Club, leading it to league championships and Asian medals through strong institutional support. Mousavizadeh emphasized the club's policy of providing free access to talented youth without nepotism, a model he advocates for the broader sports community. He expressed deep gratitude for the commander's unwavering support in expanding sports opportunities for women and the general public. His ultimate goal remains achieving a world championship title, reflecting his continued passion for the sport. Finally, he stressed the importance of serving young athletes with integrity and avoiding the pitfalls of favoritism in Iranian sports.
