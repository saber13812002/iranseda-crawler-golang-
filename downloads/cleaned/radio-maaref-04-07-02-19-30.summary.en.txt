The radio program began by exploring the concept of "systems thinking" in business, emphasizing the need to build structures that operate independently of the founder's physical presence to free up time for reinvestment. A field report followed, highlighting the Elmast Dental Clinic in Damghan, established by a young entrepreneur to address local healthcare gaps and support domestic production. The final segment featured a scholarly discussion on the moral vice of cursing, analyzing Quranic verses and hadiths that declare it absolutely forbidden in all contexts. The interpretation revealed that cursing reflects the speaker's own lowliness and ignorance rather than the target's worthiness. The Prophet (PBUH) even prohibited cursing the bodies of polytheists after the Battle of Badr to avoid harming their families and maintaining moral dignity. The broadcast concluded with a segment of the Iranian national anthem and religious supplications, reinforcing a sense of national and spiritual identity.
