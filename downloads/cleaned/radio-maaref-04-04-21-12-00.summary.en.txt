The radio program begins by emphasizing the importance of prayer and gratitude, citing a hadith from Imam Sadiq (AS) that states God's acceptance of even a single prayer or good deed saves a person from eternal punishment.
Following the recitation of verses from Surah Al-Inshiqaq, the program highlights the Quran's role as a source of peace and enlightenment for believers.
The main discussion focuses on Islamic ethics, specifically the obligation to respect all of God's creations regardless of their financial or social status.
It is noted that a true believer, who submits to God, is respected even by animals, and that a person's worth is determined by their faith rather than wealth or rank.
Stories of Imam Hassan's (AS) humility with workers and the exemplary behavior of the late Ayatollah Rohani towards the poor and his own guards are shared as practical examples.
Ayatollah Rohani's decision to buy cheaper city water instead of expensive tanker water demonstrates the principle of not living above the means of ordinary people.
The program concludes by encouraging listeners to associate with the poor and avoid arrogance, as this conduct brings worldly blessings and is essential for spiritual success in the afterlife.
