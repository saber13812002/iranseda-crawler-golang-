In this radio program, the concepts of "wisdom" and "righteousness" are explained as unlimited and dynamic processes based on Quranic verses.
It is emphasized that even righteous individuals must pray to God for stability on the right path and elevation to higher spiritual degrees.
The program then distinguishes between "good stratagem" (constructive planning) and "evil stratagem" (enemy plotting) using a verse from Surah Fatir.
It is asserted that according to divine tradition, the evil plots of enemies ultimately backfire and lead to their own destruction.
The second part of the program focuses on the importance of "consultation" in individual and social decision-making within Islam.
The qualities of a qualified advisor are identified as God-fearing, experienced, knowledgeable, and independent-minded.
Finally, the duties of the person seeking advice are outlined, including trusting the advisor, using personal intellect, and ultimately accepting the benevolent opinion.
