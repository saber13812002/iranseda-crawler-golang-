In this interview, a football expert recounts his experiences during the 1996 and 1998 Asian Cups. He emphasizes the importance of thorough opponent analysis and recalls painful defeats against South Korea and Saudi Arabia, attributing them to refereeing errors and technical issues. He further highlights the managerial and nutritional problems faced by the national team under coach Blazevic, which led to their elimination from the 2002 World Cup qualifiers. The expert argues that ignoring scientific nutrition principles and failing to maintain player composure on match days were critical factors in these failures. He also praises the outstanding performances of goalkeepers and key players like Ali Daei and Mehdi Mahdavikia during that era. Finally, he stresses the necessity of separating politics from football and adhering to professional management standards in national teams.
