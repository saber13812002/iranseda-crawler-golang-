In this radio program, following the introduction of various segments and the announcement of the deadline for Quranic conference articles, the main discussion focused on a narration from Sheikh Tusi's "Ghaybah" regarding Fadl ibn Shadhan. Professor Tabasi emphasized the strength of the narration's text while analyzing the challenges in its chain of transmission from Sheikh Tusi to Fadl ibn Shadhan. Citing hagiographical sources like al-Najashi and al-Kashi, the scientific and spiritual status of Fadl ibn Shadhan as a prominent jurist and theologian who authored hundreds of books was established. A narration from Imam Jafar al-Sadiq (or Imam Javad, as contextually implied by the timeline) was cited, expressing regret over the absence of such a figure among the Shia of Khorasan. The discussion also covered anecdotes of Fadl ibn Shadhan's interactions with the Imam, including the Imam's approval of his books. Finally, it was concluded that although the chain of transmission requires further scrutiny, the narration's validity is accepted due to the strength of its content and multiple corroborations.
