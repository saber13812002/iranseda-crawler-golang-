In the radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat," Hujjat al-Islam Fallah described humility as a sign of intellect, strength, and inner peace, positioning it as the antithesis of arrogance. He emphasized that humility is not merely a spiritual virtue but the fundamental capital required for acquiring other perfections and achieving ultimate salvation. Subsequently, the program "For Iran," focusing on the annual slogan of "Investment in Production," featured Dr. Majid Rezaei explaining that producing new goods and services is the primary driver of economic growth and employment. He highlighted a practical case study in Khoy, demonstrating that mechanizing processes and utilizing hybrid seeds can significantly reduce reliance on imports. This approach leads to water conservation, increased agricultural yield, and added value for Iranian farmers. Ultimately, the discussion concluded that investing in production secures both personal benefits and national interests.
