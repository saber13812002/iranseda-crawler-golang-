The radio program began with a political analysis of the massive public attendance at mourning ceremonies.
The guest argued that this significant presence contradicts enemy narratives claiming the system's collapse and societal shift.
He emphasized that interpreting any public gathering as a sign of change is a tactic to justify political failures.
The discussion then shifted to the religious rulings regarding mourning gatherings and recitations.
Hojjat al-Islam Vahidpour stressed the importance of maintaining respect and avoiding insults to other faiths' sanctities.
He also strictly prohibited using terms that imply associating partners with God or deifying the Imams, deeming it haram.
In the Q&A section, the Supreme Leader's ruling on the precise calculation and payment of Khums was discussed.
Additionally, rulings on fasting for the elderly, proxy Hajj, and optional prayers during travel were explained by various clerics.
