The first segment features Melika, a young woman whose existential questions and deep emotions worry her father as he prepares to enter a war. The second part presents a historical narrative set in the Roman court, where two brothers, Matius and Maxim, compete to declare their love for the noble lady Melika. The main focus of the program is answering a theological question about the possibility of Imam Mahdi living for over a thousand years, supported by modern biological science, logic, and Quranic narratives such as the story of Uzair and Prophet Noah. Finally, the program introduces the book "Memoirs of Azat Shahi," which chronicles the revolutionary life of a fighter from his childhood in a poor, religious family through the Pahlavi era.
