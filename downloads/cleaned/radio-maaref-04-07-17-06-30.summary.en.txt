In the first segment, Hojjat-ol-Islam Baranjkar argues against the view that social utility is based on the consensus of the wise, asserting that rational commands stem directly from understanding reality rather than collective opinion. He clarifies that adherence to social laws is a consequence of implicitly signing a social contract by entering society, rather than a direct rational or divine command. The program then transitions to a morning greeting with religious prayers and an appreciation for the Iranian police force during their dedicated week. The host highlights historical events from this day, including Ayatollah Khamenei's appointment as president 44 years ago and the passing of philosopher Hakim Sahbai 137 years ago. The final segment focuses on the theme of "When did you last write a letter?", exploring the history of postal services in Iran and sharing listeners' nostalgic memories of letter writing, particularly during military service.
