In the radio program "Treasure of Happiness," Hujjat al-Islam Falaba, citing narrations from Imam Ali (AS), identifies anger as a form of madness that clouds the human mind and deprives individuals of logical decision-making. He emphasizes that anger is not only a personal psychological disorder but also manifests as collective madness at the social level, leading people from light into darkness. Furthermore, based on a narration from Imam Sadiq (AS), gentleness and kindness in social and financial interactions are presented as the key to achieving one's goals, while harshness is identified as a major obstacle to progress. The concept of humility is also explained as a sign of modesty in the face of divine blessings, illustrated through the story of the Negus and Ja'far ibn Abi Talib. The program aims to instill moral virtues and avoid vices by emphasizing the necessity of controlling anger and cultivating patience and kindness in daily life.
