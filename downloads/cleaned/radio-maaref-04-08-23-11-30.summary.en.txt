In the radio program "Persaman Mahdavi," an expert addressed questions regarding the conditions for the Mahdi's appearance and the viability of a righteous government in the modern world. He emphasized that the presence of 313 true companions is only one factor, and the general public's readiness to accept justice and humanity is equally essential. The Imam (AJ) does not come merely to materialize the world but to reform humanity and establish a moral order. Addressing concerns about the enemy's modern weaponry, he noted that the outcome of conflicts is not determined solely by arms. History demonstrates that factors such as psychological fear, divine aid, and widespread public support can overcome military superiority. Historical examples like the Battle of Badr and the Sacred Defense prove that faith and popular support can triumph over armed powers. Therefore, awaiting the appearance requires spiritual and social readiness from society, rather than mere anxiety over military tools.
