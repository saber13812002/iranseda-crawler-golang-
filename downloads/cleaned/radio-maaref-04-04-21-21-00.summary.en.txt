In the Parsaiyan program on Radio Ma'aref, based on a narration from Imam Sadiq (AS), it was emphasized that respecting believers and treating them kindly, including the poor, is a divine command, and a person's worth is determined by piety rather than wealth or status.
The ethical behavior of the late Ayatollah Rohani was highlighted as a model, noting his respectful and intimate interactions with everyone, from waiters to poor elderly women, and his treatment of his guards as if they were his own sons.
He also avoided personal luxury, instructing that only items affordable by the poor be purchased for him to maintain equality with them.
The second part of the program featured a narrative from the book "Nishan-e Hassan," depicting Nafisa's anxiety about her husband Abdullah ibn Zubayr's suspicious behavior following Imam Hussein's (AS) arrival in Mecca and the potential betrayal of the Alis.
Hassan (AS) advised his brother Qasim to keep these conversations secret and encouraged Nafisa to practice patience and endurance in her husband's house.
The segment concluded with the arrival of Imam Hussein's (AS) family, describing the emotional reunion between Hazrat Zainab (SA) and Nafisa.
Finally, the program addressed listeners' religious questions and ended with announcements for future shows and gratitude to the audience.
