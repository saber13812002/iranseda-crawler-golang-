This program features a tafsir of verses 90 to 93 of Surah Al-Ma'idah by Ayatollah Javadi Amoli, focusing on the prohibition of alcohol and gambling. The commentator clarifies that addressing believers does not imply the ruling is exclusive to them, but rather highlights their greater benefit from such divine guidance. Linguistically, "khamr" means covering, and any substance that obscures reason under the influence of lust or anger is considered haram. The prohibition is reinforced through eight distinct emphases in the Quran, including descriptions of these substances as abominable and works of Satan. Satan uses alcohol and gambling to destroy social bonds by fostering enmity and severing spiritual ties by distracting people from prayer and remembrance of God. The text notes that the prohibition was previously established in Surah Al-A'raf as a "great sin" and in Surah An-Nisa by forbidding prayer while intoxicated. Consequently, these rulings are essential for maintaining social and ethical order for all humans, not just Muslims. The repetition of these prohibitions across different surahs underscores the absolute certainty of the ban and the necessity of complete abstinence.
