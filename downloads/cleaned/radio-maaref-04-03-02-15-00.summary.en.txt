In this radio program, Dr. Mahdi Rast analyzes the evolution of internal unrest in Iran, noting that the enemy shifted focus from hard war to cultural aggression and internal subversion after the 1980s. The 1999 student protests marked a pivotal moment where the Supreme Leader adopted a strategy of "insight" and "enlightenment" rather than a purely security-based approach. By engaging directly with various societal segments, inspired by historical precedents of Imam Ali, the leadership successfully neutralized the enemy's attempts to create polarization and undermine the system's legitimacy. In the second segment, Dr. Zahra Sarbaz Hosseini emphasizes the critical role of lifestyle modifications in preventing chronic conditions like high cholesterol and diabetes. She warns that incomplete medical treatments can lead to drug resistance and strongly advises against relying on unscientific or alternative remedies for serious infections.
