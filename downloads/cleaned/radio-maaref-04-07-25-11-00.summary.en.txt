The President emphasized the practical implementation of Quranic and Nahj al-Balagha directives in governance and the unity of Muslims during the fourth international congress in Isfahan. He also stressed the importance of fostering a "we can do it" mindset and improving educational quality in meetings with education officials and school builders. The news segment reported the passing of Sardar Ali Reza Afshar, former Basij commander and head of the Sacred Defense Memorial Foundation. Concurrently, the new academic year for women's seminaries in Qom began, with a focus on training students to address the doubts of the younger generation. Additional specialized meetings were held to discuss reforms in the seminary system and to examine the works of Allameh Hasanzadeh Amoli. The religious rulings program provided answers to various jurisprudential questions, including the khums on retirement bonuses, the validity of wudu and prayer with wounds, and women's permissions to leave home.
