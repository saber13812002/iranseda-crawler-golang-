In this radio program, the discussion began with listener messages emphasizing the need for hope and improved infrastructure in various regions. The conversation then shifted to the perspective on war, highlighting the importance of finding beauty and hope amidst suffering, as exemplified by the words of Lady Zainab (SA). The core of the program focused on the concept of "Infaq" (charity), which the religious expert presented as a key to solving personal and social problems. Citing Quranic verses and narrations from Imam Ali (AS), the speaker defined proper Infaq as an act rooted in divine law that leads to God's pleasure and spiritual stability. It was emphasized that such charity is not a loss but a means to increase blessings and receive rewards in both this world and the hereafter. The program concluded by encouraging listeners to maintain hope, perform obligatory duties, and trust in God's promise to fully compensate for their charitable acts.
