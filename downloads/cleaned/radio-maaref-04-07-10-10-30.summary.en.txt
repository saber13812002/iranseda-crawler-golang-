The radio program began by highlighting the value of sibling bonds before introducing the "Shifa" segment on traditional medicine.
It explained that nettle aids in cell regeneration and improves insulin function, which helps regulate blood sugar levels.
The educational section then discussed effective methods for teaching hijab to teenagers, emphasizing its role in preserving dignity and social safety.
In the "One Drop from the Sea" segment, Quranic verses were used to explain that looking upward in spiritual matters and downward in material ones fosters gratitude.
A short dramatic skit depicted a woman's frustration with her financial situation and husband's indifference, which was resolved by her mother's advice to change her own efforts.
This part emphasized the Quranic principle that changing one's own behavior leads to a change in one's destiny and divine blessings.
Finally, the "Imam's Words" segment honored the Iranian people and doctors as striving for God, urging them to maintain divine intentions rather than materialistic goals.
