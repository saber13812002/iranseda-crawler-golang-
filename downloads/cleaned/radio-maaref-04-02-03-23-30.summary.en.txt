The radio program "Sarmashk," featuring Professor Javad Mohaddesi, explores Imam Ali's (PBUH) philosophical views on comparing the world and the afterlife as presented in Nahj al-Balagha. The discussion centers on the interpretation of the 31st letter to Imam Hassan (PBUH), where the world is depicted as a temporary and difficult stop for travelers. Imam Ali contrasts two groups: those who endure hardships with awareness of their ultimate destination, and those attached to worldly pleasures for whom death feels like a forced eviction from a palace to a ruin. The purpose of these metaphors is to detach listeners from materialism and inspire a desire to prepare for the afterlife. The segment concludes by emphasizing that a pure, divine motivation is the key to success in all endeavors, including education and public service. The program ends with religious prayers and a recommendation to focus on prayer and remembrance of God.
