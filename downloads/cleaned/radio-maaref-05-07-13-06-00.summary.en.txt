This program explains the concept of "Ta'wil" as the practical, external manifestation of the Quran in the Hereafter, contrasting it with intellectual interpretation.
The commentator emphasizes that the Quran is a luminous book that links action with knowledge by introducing the righteous and the wicked, with its Ta'wil realized in the afterlife.
Verses regarding blindness and deafness in the Hereafter are interpreted not as sensory loss, but as deprivation from witnessing divine mercy and hearing the truth.
Deviant individuals exploit the Quran's ambiguous verses to cloak sedition in religious colors, which is considered a negative form of Ta'wil in this world.
In the Hereafter, each person's character becomes their punishment, and the Quran's Ta'wil appears in the resurrection of sinners as animals or the blind.
The disbelievers in that day first seek intercession and then a return to the world, but both paths are closed to them.
Human life is like a trade that yields either profit or loss, with no opportunity for correction or return in the afterlife.
Ultimately, relying on worldly illusions and false idols leads to eternal helplessness and regret in the Hereafter.
