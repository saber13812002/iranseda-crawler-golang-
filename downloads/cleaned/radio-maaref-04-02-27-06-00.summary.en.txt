In this radio program, Seyyed Saeed Hosseini, relying on the Tafsir al-Mizan, identifies the two main messages of the Holy Quran as the declaration of monotheism and the cultivation of the perfect human being.
The commentator argues that verse 105 of Surah Al-Ma'idah, by emphasizing "taqwa" (piety), highlights individual responsibility and interprets "enjoining good" as a serious, practical command rather than mere preaching.
A central point of the discussion is defining humans as travelers in constant motion who, according to Surah Al-Inshiqaq, are created in hardship to reach their ultimate destination: meeting God.
The Quran confirms the existence of a specific, straight path through concepts like "term," "speed," and "guidance," where deviation from this path is considered misguidance.
The distinction between the "strong" and the "misguided" is also explained; the strong one has lost the goal but knows the path, while the misguided one knows the goal but has lost the correct way.
Ultimately, it is concluded that the path to God is not found in the heavens or solely in books, but within the human being through the practice of obligatory acts and the avoidance of prohibitions.
Therefore, a true traveler is one who, with faith in God and adherence to religious duties, moves along the path toward the divine encounter, making this internal journey the true essence of the spiritual path.
