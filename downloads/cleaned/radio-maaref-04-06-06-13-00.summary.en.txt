In this radio program, the late Ayatollah Morteza Motahhari analyzes the life and moral characteristics of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) prior to his prophethood. He highlights how the Prophet's spiritual solitude, orphanhood, and poverty created the foundation for deep contemplation and worship in the Cave of Hira. Citing reliable historical sources, Motahhari presents the Prophet's wise teachings on social responsibility, human equality, and the rejection of racism. The discussion also covers the Prophet's practical conduct in avoiding elitism and performing personal tasks without relying on others. By performing simple acts like tying his own saddle, the Prophet set a model of humility and practical independence for his companions. The primary goal of this analysis is to demonstrate that the miracle lies in the Prophet's entire existence, not just in the Quran. The speech concludes with a prayer for increased knowledge and love for the Ahl al-Bayt (AS).
