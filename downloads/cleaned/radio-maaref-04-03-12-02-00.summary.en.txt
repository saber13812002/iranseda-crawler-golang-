In the Parsian program, Hujjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the importance of seeking intercession and weeping for Imam Hussein (PBUH) as a path to purifying sins and achieving spiritual elevation. He cited narrations from Imam Sadiq (PBUH) indicating that reciting elegies that move even one person to tears makes Paradise obligatory for the reciter. Vahidi recounted stories of great scholars like Ayatollah Shahbazi and Ayatollah Marashi Najafi who, despite strict prohibitions during the first Pahlavi era, creatively and bravely held mourning ceremonies. In the second segment, the Mashq-e Rah program featured Professor Faqih discussing the ethics of monotheism. Professor Faqih stressed the necessity of observing proper etiquette before supplication and understanding God's closeness to humans to enhance the effectiveness of prayers. In response to listener questions, he advised on controlling behavior during jokes and adhering to ethical and social boundaries in social gatherings. Finally, Faqih insisted on the role of intellect and patience in avoiding inappropriate speech and preventing future regrets in daily life.
