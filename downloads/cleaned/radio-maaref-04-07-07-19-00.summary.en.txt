This radio program explores the Islamic concept of "considering things a gain" (ghanimat) in daily life. Citing narrations from Imam Sajjad and Imam Ali, the speaker explains that losing material possessions or opportunities should be accepted as a spiritual gain and a consolation for the hereafter. The discussion emphasizes the immense value of spiritual opportunities such as the Arbaeen pilgrimage and early morning prayers, warning against taking them for granted. The second part of the program analyzes a hadith from Imam Sajjad that identifies hostility towards the world as the highest deed after knowing God. Allameh Majlisi interprets this hostility not as abandoning worldly life, but as distancing oneself from moral vices like arrogance, envy, and greed. The speaker further references the Qasi'ah sermon from Nahj al-Balagha to highlight the severe danger of arrogance, using Iblis's fate as a stark warning. The program concludes by urging listeners to practically apply these teachings and guard their character to attain divine pleasure.
