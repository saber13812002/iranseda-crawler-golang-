In this radio program, as the month of Muharram approaches, the importance of promoting the culture of jihad and martyrdom as keys to dignity and eternal life was emphasized.
The people have revived the spirit of unity and self-sacrifice seen in the 1980s by demonstrating altruistic behavior amidst the country's current challenges.
Young revolutionary poets have responded swiftly to regional events, including tensions, by reciting committed poetry that supports the fighters and the Ahl al-Bayt.
Poems highlighting the defense of Imam Hussein's (PBUH) school of thought and the struggle against oppression were recited during the broadcast.
The guest stressed the pure, divine motivation of Iranian youth, distinguishing it from the power-seeking motives of their enemies.
In this perspective, martyrdom is not viewed as destruction but as a great divine victory rooted in service to Islamic teachings.
The program concluded with a prayer for God to remove the obstacles to the appearance of Imam Mahdi (AJ) and to increase the blessings of Husseini gatherings for the people of Iran.
