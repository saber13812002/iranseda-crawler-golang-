The radio program began by highlighting the West's double standards in supporting the Zionist regime and the silence of Islamic countries regarding their humiliation. A book detailing Ayatollah Kashani's role in the nationalization of the oil industry and his conflicts with Dr. Mossadegh was then introduced and analyzed. The main discussion focused on the Zangezur corridor, which aims to bypass Iran to connect Azerbaijan with Nakhchivan. Experts emphasized that this corridor would not only harm Iran's transit and economic interests but also facilitate the military presence of the US and NATO on the country's borders. Additionally, the potential for Pan-Turkism agitation and security threats to Iran's northwest region were discussed. Finally, the importance of public vigilance against enemy plots and the preservation of national unity were stressed.
