During this radio program, the martyrdom of Hazrat Zahra (SA) was mourned, and the sacred status of motherhood in Islamic traditions was highlighted.
The guest, citing a narration from Imam Sadiq (AS), identified mockery and foolishness as traits of the ego-driven soul that contradict religious law.
The distinction between healthy humor and excessive mockery was examined, urging listeners to actively oppose the disrespect of sacred values and individuals.
A historical incident involving a man's disrespect toward Imam Sajjad (AS) and the Imam's wise response was presented as a cautionary tale about maintaining decorum.
The program also expressed solidarity with the mothers of over 300 martyrs recently honored in Iran.
It was emphasized that making others laugh, particularly in digital spaces, must not come at the expense of ethical and religious boundaries.
The broadcast concluded with prayers for mothers and a call to apply moral teachings in our relationships with God, ourselves, others, and nature.
