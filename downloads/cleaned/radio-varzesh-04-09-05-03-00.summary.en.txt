Ali-Aziz Yazdani, a refereeing expert, emphasized the need to restructure the management and training systems of Iranian football refereeing in this radio program. He criticized the appointment of referees based on provincial pressure and advocated for a merit-based ranking system to eliminate political interference. Yazdani also highlighted the lack of technical infrastructure, such as sufficient cameras for the VAR system, and the failure to adhere to FIFA standards in training and supervision, which has caused Iranian referees to lag behind in Asia. From his perspective, Tehran football also faces issues such as the presence of unprofessional managers, a decline in match quality, and an excessive number of teams in lower divisions. He believes that to save Tehran football, the number of teams must be reduced and stricter oversight must be applied to club management to uncover genuine talent. Finally, Yazdani stressed that without fundamental changes to the infrastructure of the Tehran Football Federation and the proper implementation of FIFA principles, improving the level of refereeing and football in the country will not be possible in the long term.
