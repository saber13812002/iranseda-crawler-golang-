The host invites listeners to perform morning prayers and visit mosques, highlighting the spiritual significance of these acts.

He suggests practical solutions such as patience, formal correspondence, and legal pursuit instead of aggression or insults.

It also reviews the ratification of the oil consortium contract in the Senate and the birth of Jonas Salk, the polio vaccine inventor.
Finally, listeners are encouraged to maintain calm and pursue their rights through logical and appropriate channels.
