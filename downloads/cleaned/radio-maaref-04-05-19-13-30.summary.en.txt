In this radio program, Dr. Seyed Mostafa Khoshsham analyzes the recent imposed war, arguing that Iran emerged as the victor by preventing the enemy from achieving its goals of nuclear destruction and national disintegration. He refutes enemy claims of total success in the Fordow attack, emphasizing that Iran proved its defensive capabilities by altering missile tactics and significantly increasing strike accuracy against critical Israeli infrastructure. The analyst asserts that the adversary failed in both military and internal disruption phases, unable to destabilize Iranian society despite extensive espionage and soft war efforts. He also highlights a shift in public opinion, noting that some citizens now advocate for nuclear weapons due to fears of repeated attacks, although the state maintains its firm stance against nuclear armament as a non-negotiable red line. Ultimately, Dr. Khoshsham concludes that Iran neutralized the enemy's strategic scenario by maintaining national unity and delivering decisive military responses, thereby securing a strategic advantage in this confrontation.
