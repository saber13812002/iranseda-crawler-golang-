This radio segment began by honoring the fighters of the 1990s who sacrificed their lives to defend the shrines of the Ahl al-Bayt.
Names such as Ali, Sajjad, Ahmad, Mehdi, Hassan, and Mohsen Hajji were cited as symbols of this selfless devotion.
Mohsen Hajji was highlighted as a warrior who renounced worldly comforts to earn his mother's approval and protect the holy shrine.
The narrative emphasizes that detaching from material pleasures is the path to achieving higher spiritual ranks.
These defenders preserved the sanctity of Islam, humanity, and Shia identity by standing firm against oppression.
Through their sacrifices, they purged the stain of tyranny from the face of their enemies.
The program concluded with a hopeful message about the unity and cohesion of the Iranian people in thwarting enemy plots.
Finally, the host signed off with a farewell, accompanied by the anthem "To Independence and Freedom."
