The radio program "Sohragahi Sobh-e Niaz" began with Quran recitation and morning supplications, focusing on the profound secrets of prayer.
Hojjat-ol-Islam Mowahedsi explained that prayer is the first obligation for believers and a path to salvation from the tyranny of the ego and Satan.
He emphasized that true servitude to God liberates humans from falling into natural instincts and leads to genuine freedom.
Citing a narration from Imam Sadiq (AS), angels call upon people at prayer time to extinguish the fire of their sins through timely worship.
Prayer is described not merely as a mandatory act but as a passionate spiritual connection that guides the soul from darkness to light.
The program concluded by announcing the times for the Fajr adhan and sunrise on the 25th of Dhu al-Qi'dah, 1446, inviting listeners to devotion.
