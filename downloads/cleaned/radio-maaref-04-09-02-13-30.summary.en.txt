The radio program "Enduring Memories" began by sharing the recollections of three veterans from the Iran-Iraq War. Veteran Moradi detailed the Battle of Beit al-Maqdis and the liberation of Khorramshahr, highlighting a miraculous event where an enemy tank remained unharmed amidst heavy shelling. Sardar Davari described the profound spiritual atmosphere on the night of the Fath al-Mubin operation, emphasizing the soldiers' deep devotion in the desert. Haj Mohammad Hossein Yektaki recounted a tragic incident during the Badr operation where his platoon commander was martyred. Following these war stories, the program transitioned to a theological discussion on the virtues of Fatima Zahra (SA). The narrator interpreted Quranic verses to highlight her status as the leader of the women of Paradise and her model of selfless service to God. This segment illustrated the strong connection between religious faith and the heroic narratives of the war.
