In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Morteza Bahrami Khoshnoodi emphasized the central role of moderation and balance in the Prophet's (PBUH) conduct as a path to spiritual and mental health. He explained that true health extends beyond the absence of physical illness to include complete psychological and social well-being. The Prophet (PBUH) demonstrated a balanced approach, such as his gentle interaction with children and acceptance of gifts, thereby avoiding obsession and rigidity. Furthermore, Imam Ali (AS) identified piety and the purification of the heart as the primary cure for both spiritual and physical ailments. In the second segment, Hojjat al-Islam Falahati outlined practical strategies for abandoning slander and backbiting, citing narrations and the views of Imam Khomeini (RA) and the Supreme Leader. He stressed the necessity of avoiding toxic environments and controlling one's speech to preserve spiritual integrity. Finally, slandering individuals or the system was condemned as a grave sin that darkens the social atmosphere.
