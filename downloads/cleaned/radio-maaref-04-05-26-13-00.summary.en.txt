This lecture examines the pivotal role of Imam Sajjad (PBUH) and Hazrat Zainab (SA) in preserving the dignity of the Ahl al-Bayt during their return from Sham after Karbala. Over his thirty-four-year Imamate, Imam Sajjad (PBUH) navigated severe political and ideological pressures from the Umayyad dynasty through strategic taqiyya and careful planning. By promoting prayer as a spiritual language, he produced the enduring legacy of the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya for humanity. The Imam cultivated loyal companions and freed slaves to lay the foundational infrastructure for the future activities of Imam Baqir (PBUH) and Imam Sadiq (PBUH). Furthermore, he maintained the identity of the Shia community by keeping the memory of Imam Hussein (PBUH) alive through political mourning. This historical period demonstrates that Imam Sajjad (PBUH) was not a passive figure but the greatest mujahid among the Ahl al-Bayt for reviving Islamic teachings.
