This audio program narrates the book "In the Ambush of the Red Rose," recounting the battles of Martyr General Ali Siyazi Shirazi in Kurdistan. After learning of the siege of his forces at the Qalichan tower and Marivan, Major Shirazi deployed by helicopter and assumed command of the operations. He boosted the morale of the besieged troops by sending elite forces, including Ahmad Mousavian and Major Shahrani, and devised a plan to liberate the city of Marivan. The military convoy, through an unexpected route change and surprise attacks, successfully captured Marivan after fierce battles in the mountain passes. On the return journey, Major Shirazi won the trust of local villagers by rescuing survivors from an ambush and shelling the village of Sheikh Osman. This six-day operation in June 1980 concluded successfully and is regarded by military experts as a classic example of organized warfare. The program concludes with greetings to the Imam of the Age and an emphasis on the media's responsibility in promoting Islamic values.
