The first segment of the program emphasized the divine wisdom behind the delay in answering certain prayers, explaining that this delay is ultimately beneficial and compensated with great rewards in the afterlife.
Subsequently, Professor Jafari addressed historical inquiries, citing reliable sources to state that Imam Ali (AS) had 27 children and eight permanent wives.
He also explained that Muhammad Hanafiya was named after his mother as a reaction to her unjust imprisonment by Abu Bakr's forces during the Ridda wars.
In response to another question, the professor confirmed that building structures and domes over graves has a historical precedent dating back to the companions and successors, refuting Wahhabi claims that it is an innovation.
Professor Jafari expressed regret over the scarcity of historical information regarding Hazrat Amina, the Prophet's mother, due to the time gap between her life and the Islamic era.
Finally, the question about the Quranic address "Sister of Aaron" to Mary was answered by explaining that it was a symbolic title denoting her piety and righteousness.
This title was used among the Children of Israel for righteous individuals, and the Prophet (PBUH) clarified this symbolic meaning to the people of Najran to dispel their doubts.
