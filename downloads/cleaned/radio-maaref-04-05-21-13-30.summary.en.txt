In this program, Hojjat al-Islam Seyyed Ahmad Faqih emphasizes the importance of spiritual intimacy with God through prayer, recommending that it be performed both in congregation and privately with a soft voice to avoid ostentation. He cites Quranic verses and narrations to highlight that secret supplication fosters a more sincere connection with the Divine and shields the believer from the gaze of others. The discussion then shifts to the rights of parents, detailing duties such as kissing their hands, standing when they enter, expressing gratitude for their sacrifices, and reciting specific prayers for them. The guest stresses that full respect for parents, especially in old age or weakness, is a critical religious obligation, warning against even minor acts of disrespect like raising one's voice or using dismissive words. These behaviors are presented not only as divine commands but also as essential for achieving spiritual peace and God's pleasure. The segment concludes with a brief mention of the moral and cultural failures of the West regarding human rights and its support for the Zionist regime, which exposes the collapse of its claimed values.
