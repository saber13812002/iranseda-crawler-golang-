In the radio program "Mashgh-e Rah" on Radio Ma'aref, the host first apologized for a timing error in the broadcast schedule. Professor Seyed Ahmad Faghihi discussed monotheistic ethics, emphasizing that strengthening one's connection with God is the key to correcting one's behavior. He identified prayer as the simplest and most effective way to return to God after committing a sin, citing a hadith that states the voice of a sorrowful and repentant servant is highly beloved by God. The professor explained that even a short phrase like "Ya Rab" or "Astaghfirullah" can lead to the immediate forgiveness of sins and the acceptance of prayers. In the Q&A section, it was advised that keeping pets like dogs should be done in appropriate locations outside the living space to avoid hygiene and ethical issues. Furthermore, following the Quranic instruction to respond to evil with good and avoiding quick temper was recommended to resolve long-standing conflicts and grudges. Finally, listeners were encouraged to maintain a continuous connection with God and reconcile with one another to lead a healthier spiritual and social life.
