Goalkeeper Ardalan recounts his youth and the pivotal decision to join the newly formed Ekbatan team instead of Persepolis. Despite Persepolis offering a higher salary, he chose Ekbatan after being scouted by its managers and advised by his elder. Ekbatan, blending young talent with experienced players, won the Tehran club championship in its inaugural year and competed in the second division. Ardalan proudly recalls training alongside national team goalkeepers and playing with notable figures like Hadi Ahangaran and Saeed Tamadan. He also details his tenure with the national youth, junior, and hope teams, highlighting the historic Beijing Great Wall match. Although he acknowledges financial losses from rejecting Persepolis, he takes immense pride in his 15-year loyalty to Ekbatan. Finally, he expresses gratitude to the private investors and managers who funded the club during a time when state support was minimal.
