The radio program distinguishes between "Tashri" (apparent legal rulings) and "Takwin" (the hidden, cosmic order of existence), using examples like the Prophet's reliance on testimony despite knowing the truth.
It emphasizes that the Prophet was obligated to act according to the apparent law and Sharia, even when possessing hidden knowledge of the underlying reality.
The primary purpose of creation is the testing of humans, and direct divine intervention to eliminate oppressors would destroy the system of free will and trial.
In response to listener questions, it was advised to dedicate the reward of the Ja'far Tayyar prayer to a deceased child, noting that the soul is shielded from worldly grief.
It was clarified that secondary acts of worship, such as reciting Quran or dhikr, are not accepted in isolation without the foundation of daily prayer.
Regarding claims of contact with the Hidden Imam, the program cited Imams' letters to declare any such assertion during the Major Occultancy as a lie and slander.
Finally, giving charity and reciting specific supplications, such as "Masha'Allah" and the Harz of Imam Javad, were recommended as effective remedies for the evil eye and misfortune.
