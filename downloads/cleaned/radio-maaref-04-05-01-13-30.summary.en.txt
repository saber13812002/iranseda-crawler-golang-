The radio program "Narrative of Love" featured Zahra Nasiri, the wife of martyr Javad Akbari, a defender of the holy shrines.
The guest recounted her youth, her resistance to early marriage proposals, and her initial fears regarding commitment.
She described the hardships of her pregnancy and the supportive role her father played during the early challenges of their married life.
Zahra expressed her intense anxiety and recurring dreams when Javad decided to deploy to Syria, highlighting her deep emotional struggle.
She described the moment she learned of his sudden departure to Syria as one of the most terrifying and painful experiences of her life.
The program concluded with a message from the Supreme Leader emphasizing the responsibility of officials to maintain the revolution's identity and avoid past mistakes.
The broadcast ended with an invitation for listeners to send messages and tune in to upcoming programs on Radio Ma'aref.
