In this radio program, experts affirm that Iran's military response to Israeli and American aggression is a legitimate act of self-defense, grounded in both the UN Charter and Islamic jurisprudence. They distinguish between inherent "self-defense" against an ongoing attack and the unjustified "preemptive or preventive defense" concepts used by the US and Israel to rationalize their strikes. From a jurisprudential perspective, defending Islamic territory is not only permitted but obligatory for all Muslims, aligning with the historical precedent of Imam Ali who never initiated war. The experts argue that the US and Israel are distorting international law to serve their national interests, a behavior that contradicts the fundamental principles of the global order. Furthermore, the presence of US military bases in Muslim countries is criticized as incompatible with Quranic teachings that prohibit forming alliances with enemies. The discussion concludes by urging Muslims to maintain unity and military readiness to counter American hegemony while exercising their right to defense in accordance with divine and international laws.
