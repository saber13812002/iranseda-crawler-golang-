The program began by reporting on the continuous violation of the ceasefire in Gaza, highlighting airstrikes, a severe humanitarian crisis, and the destruction of refugee tents due to heavy rains. In the diplomatic section, Ali Larijani emphasized Iran's strategic and unrestricted cooperation with Pakistan in combating terrorism. Religious reports covered a conference of Latin American Muslims, the Fatima Zahra Mosque in Dagestan, and an international seminar on Mirza Naeini in Najaf. Additionally, news was shared about the construction of 3,000 free housing units by the Imam Hussein Shrine in Basra and the expansion of the Abbasid Shrine to accommodate more pilgrims. The death of "Jamal al-Amin," an American political prisoner whose innocence was proven, was analyzed as a symbol of structural racism in the United States. In the final segment, Dr. Madi identified the root of the recent war as the fundamental conflict between the fronts of truth and falsehood, arguing that resistance is the only path to preserving identity and progress, whereas compromise leads to stagnation.
