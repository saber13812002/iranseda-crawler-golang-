In this program, Professor Mohammadi Sharoudi, relying on the teachings of Imam Javad, identifies three main indicators of success: divine success, self-awareness, and loyalty to the Imamate. He emphasizes that by acknowledging "Bismillah" and relying on God, one can traverse the path of servitude and service to humanity, thereby benefiting from divine support. A key point is the fundamental difference between worldly and eschatological accounting; while worldly calculations are superficial and unfulfilling, the Day of Judgment's accounting is meticulous, internal, and based on absolute justice. Therefore, one must measure oneself against the moral and religious standards of the Hereafter, rather than worldly standards. Additionally, loyalty to the Imamate, defined as following the Imams and obeying religious directives, is explained as the third factor for success in life. The discussion then returns to proving the prophethood of the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH), citing his pure family lineage and the rational content of his call as definitive evidence. These characteristics include the nobility of his lineage, the purity of his parents, and the promotion of values such as monotheism, justice, and equality in a society that lacked these concepts.
