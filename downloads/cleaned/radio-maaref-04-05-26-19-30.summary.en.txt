The radio program began by emphasizing the scholars' duty to spread religion, even at the cost of their lives, to preserve the school of thought. It then cited verse 71 of Surah Al-Ma'idah to identify two main tools for misleading people: concealing the truth and mixing truth with falsehood. The segment transitioned into Quranic recitation and Sufi poetry, highlighting the spiritual purity and inner light achieved through reciting the Quran in a measured manner. Subsequently, the 20th specialized session of Quran reciters and memorizers was held, focusing on the theme of music, requirements, and innovation in Iranian recitation. Researchers and experts presented their papers and ideas regarding the challenges of music and creativity in Quranic recitation. The discussion also referenced previous debates on recitation issues and the importance of addressing audience needs. Finally, listeners were invited to call 162 to share their media expectations and help improve future programming.
