In this radio program, the hosts addressed various theological questions from listeners, clarifying that financial rights and forgiveness are not processed in the Barzakh (intermediate realm) but only during the Day of Judgment. It was explained that familial relationships in the afterlife are determined by faith and piety rather than mere blood ties, and the current age of Imam Mahdi was calculated. The importance of reciting the Quran even with errors was emphasized, as angels correct the recitation and consistent practice leads to gradual improvement. The concept of "Khawf and Raja" was defined as the necessary balance between fear of divine punishment and hope for mercy. The program also discussed why early Jews rejected Prophet Muhammad's prophethy, noting their expectation that the prophet would come from the lineage of Jacob, whereas the Prophet was from the lineage of Ishmael. The final segment focused on the detrimental effects of smartphone and internet addiction, including eye strain, reduced concentration, and social isolation. Experts stressed the importance of managing technology usage to protect both physical and mental health.
