The radio program "Andisheha," featuring Hujjat al-Islam Dr. Ali Reza Bahrami, explored the theological problem of evil and addressed related doubts.
A central point of discussion was the precise distinction between "divine will" and "God's love or satisfaction."
According to this perspective, all events in the world occur by God's will, but they are not necessarily loved or approved by Him.
To clarify this concept, the stories of Prophet Abraham (will without love) and Prophet Adam (love without will for sin) were used as illustrative examples.
Human duty is to align one's own will with God's love and to refrain from sins and oppression.
Believers can view hardships and calamities as opportunities for spiritual growth and strengthening their faith through trust in God.
Finally, it was emphasized that God will grant immense rewards to the oppressed on the Day of Judgment and punish the oppressors.
