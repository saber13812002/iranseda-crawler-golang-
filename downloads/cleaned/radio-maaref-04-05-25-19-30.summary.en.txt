The program begins by criticizing those who, despite knowing the Quran, neglect its interpretation in favor of other books.
The host emphasizes that the Quran is the book of the Creator, a source of light, and the law of the era, deserving of utmost reverence.
A segment of the broadcast is dedicated to reciting and singing religious verses about the Quran's guiding power.
The program then commemorates the anniversary of the return of the freed prisoners of war to Iran on August 17.
Freedom is defined as a trait of faithful men and women who are detached from worldly attachments.
Their return after years of captivity and suffering symbolizes a national and religious victory.
This day marks the end of waiting and separation, adding a new page of pride to Iran's history.
The broadcast concludes by congratulating Iran on this occasion and honoring the sacrifices of the freed POWs.
