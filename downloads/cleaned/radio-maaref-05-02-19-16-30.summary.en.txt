In this radio program, Professor Shaban Abdelaziz interprets Surah Al-Fath, describing the Treaty of Hudaybiyyah not as a defeat but as a clear victory that facilitated the spread of Islam without bloodshed. He emphasizes that this peace allowed the Prophet (PBUH) to overcome his rear concerns and focus on global invitations and internal management. In response to listeners' questions, the professor highlights the concept of "siyag al-mukhalefin" as a lesson in future studies and foresight for community leaders. He also criticizes "war profiteers" who exploit crises, labeling them as ignorant of the value of martyrs' sacrifices and spiritual principles. Furthermore, the reason for peace being decreed instead of war, despite the enemy's power, is attributed to the divine "Sakina" (tranquility) descending upon the believers and instilling fear in the enemy through their unity and courage. Finally, the "divine law" in verse 23 of Surah Al-Fath is discussed, stating that disbelievers will inevitably be defeated and flee when facing a front that fights with faith and reliance on God.
