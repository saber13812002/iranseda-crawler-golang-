In this radio program, the importance of silence and avoiding the sins of the tongue is emphasized, drawing on the wisdom of Nahj al-Balagha and ethical narrations. The speaker cites stories from great figures to illustrate that one's speech can lead to judgment on the Day of Judgment, and that a single inappropriate word can cause harm rather than healing. Referring to the words of Imam Ali, the speaker argues that intellect must govern the tongue, noting that thoughtful individuals tend to speak less. The program also highlights the sensitive role of speech in media and social spaces, urging public figures to be more cautious to prevent instability and protect public peace. Finally, the broadcast concludes by reflecting on the spiritual atmosphere of a pilgrimage journey to the holy shrines.
