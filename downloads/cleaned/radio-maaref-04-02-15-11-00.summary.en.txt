The representative of the Supreme Leader in Hajj and pilgrimage affairs emphasized the necessity of spiritual understanding of Hajj, describing it as a symbol of Muslim unity and solidarity with the people of Gaza. He called for a review of the reduced quota for pilgrim assistants and urged the maintenance of this year's allocation. Citing the increasing age of pilgrims, he stressed the need for enhanced services and constructive interaction with Saudi Arabia to facilitate travel for Iranian visitors. In other news, Mashhad is hosting the 21st International Imam Reza Festival, which features artistic exhibitions and poetry book launches on the night of Imam Reza's birth. The festival covers various artistic fields and honors outstanding artists and Razavi servants. Simultaneously, the international conference marking the 100th anniversary of the establishment of Qom seminaries has begun with the presence of prominent scholars. Research achievements from the seminaries were published during the event, and there is anticipation for the Supreme Leader's important charter on scientific and religious matters. These events highlight vibrant cultural and religious activities at both national and international levels.
