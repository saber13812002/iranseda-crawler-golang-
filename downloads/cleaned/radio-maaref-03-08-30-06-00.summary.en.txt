This segment of the program analyzes a Quranic verse where the word "al-muqeemineen" appears in the accusative case, unlike the surrounding nominative words.
The commentator argues that this grammatical shift is not an error but a rhetorical device intended to highlight the critical importance of prayer and almsgiving alongside faith.
A key point is that, unlike the general Quranic pattern of mentioning faith before righteous deeds, this verse lists the deeds (prayer and zakat) before faith.
This reversed order indicates that for believers, faith alone is insufficient and the performance of righteous deeds is also essential.
The decisive role of prayer in purifying individuals from moral vices and indecency is also emphasized.
The discussion then shifts to the topic of prophets being sent to all nations and peoples, even in the most remote parts of the world.
Based on rational proof and Quranic verses, God has never left any nation without sending a messenger or establishing a clear argument.
Consequently, no one can claim ignorance or lack of access to divine guidance as an excuse for their actions on the Day of Judgment.
