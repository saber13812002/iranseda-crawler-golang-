A conversation with a judicial official revealed that long-term statistics indicate less than 10% of Iranian marriages end in divorce, contrary to the exaggerated negative narratives often amplified by media. This constant bombardment of negative news leads to analytical paralysis, causing individuals to focus solely on risks while ignoring opportunities. The primary solution is shifting the mindset from seeking the right partner to becoming the right person, which includes actively considering one's spouse's happiness. In the second part, the discussion addressed parental disagreements over raising a four-year-old, emphasizing that conflicting parenting styles are more harmful than a unified but flawed approach. Parents are advised to form a cohesive leadership team by establishing shared fundamental rules and reaching at least a partial consensus. The segment concluded with advice to manage media consumption by avoiding negative content and to express gratitude for religious and spiritual blessings.
