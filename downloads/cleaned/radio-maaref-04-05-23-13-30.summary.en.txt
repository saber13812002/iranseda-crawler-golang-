The radio program "Narrative of Love" featured Ms. Hosseini, the mother of martyr Seyed Mohammad Hosseini, a defender of the shrines from Afghanistan. She recounted the painful moment of receiving news of her son's martyrdom in Syria and how she maintained her composure through faith and prayer. Instead of wearing black, she chose white and held a wedding ceremony for her son's body, expressing her belief in God's and her son's satisfaction. Ms. Hosseini described her final meeting with her martyr son, viewing his wounds as marks of honor and pride. She drew inspiration from the patience and endurance of Lady Zainab, comparing her own suffering to that of the great woman. In the program's conclusion, the Arbaeen pilgrimage was highlighted as a global media and a symbol of the Islamic Resistance's strength. This massive public movement is described as a powerful cry against oppression and a continuation of the Ahl al-Bayt's path in history.
