This radio program highlights the life and conduct of Hazrat Fatima Masumeh (SA) as the supreme model for expectant living and loyalty to the Imam during the period of occultation. The central theme is the concept of "Basirat," which involves recognizing truth from falsehood, understanding the timing of support, and knowing the correct manner of assisting the Imam. Hazrat Masumeh (SA) exemplified this through her spiritual proximity to the Imam, conscious worship, and attainment of acquired infallibility, serving as a model for avoiding sin and earning the Imam's pleasure. The significance of her Ziyarat was emphasized due to its key prayers for the reappearance and her unique intercessory status. The program stressed the necessity of behavioral adherence to her example, ensuring that visitors' actions align with the expectations of the Imam and the Holy Family. Practical ways to support the Imam, such as establishing educational centers with a Mahdavi approach to nurture an aware generation, were also discussed. Finally, listeners were encouraged to purify themselves from sin and strengthen their belief in Mahdaviyat to become true supporters of the Imam of the Time (AJ).
