The program begins with an exegesis of verses 103 and 104 of Surah Al-Ma'idah, examining pre-Islamic tribal customs regarding livestock and baseless prohibitions such as Bahirah and Sā'ibah. Ayatollah Javadi Amoli emphasizes that Islam abolished these irrational traditions to establish clear halal and haram boundaries based on divine revelation and reason. The speaker distinguishes between "Takbir" (free creation) and "Tashri'" (divine legislation), arguing that while God created humans with free will, He restricted them from following false paths through specific laws. He asserts that reason is not opposed to religion but works alongside revelation to form the two wings of faith, requiring that rational deductions align with Sharia principles. Blind imitation of ancestors and the ignorant is strongly condemned, highlighting the necessity of relying on genuine knowledge and intellect in religious and social matters. Finally, the speaker criticizes those who deny revelation and prophethood, accusing them of conflating the freedom of creation with the constraints of legislation to justify their baseless claims.
