In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Morteza Agha Tehrani, while congratulating Nurses' Day, emphasized the core Islamic concept of "steadfastness" and "resistance" in both personal ethics and politics. He argued that achieving any goal, from praying on time to self-improvement and defeating enemies, requires unwavering persistence and adherence to principles. Citing examples from the lives of the Prophet (PBUH) and Imam Hussein (AS), he demonstrated that spirituality and connection with God are the keys to overcoming major challenges. He criticized deceptive and fearful behaviors, urging honesty and courage in stating truths, such as condemning Zionism and America. The speaker also highlighted the role of nurses in supporting Lebanese casualties and the importance of maintaining hijab and Islamic ethics in society. He stated that resistance and standing firm in faith, even under enemy pressure, lead to blessings and martyrdom. Finally, he encouraged listeners to maintain hope and trust in God while staying committed to the path of Wilayat.
