The first night of mourning for the martyrdom of Hazrat Fatimah (SA) was held at Imam Khomeini Hosseiniyeh with the presence of the Supreme Leader, where Hujjat al-Islam Sharifi discussed the enemy's goals of creating division and doubt. The spokesperson of the armed forces visited Radio Ma'aref, praising it as a successful media outlet in the field of the Holy Defense. The program's jurisprudential section addressed various religious questions from followers of different maraji. A key ruling clarified that prayer on leased land is valid if the worshipper believes the owner consents, but becomes invalid if they know the owner does not consent. It was also explained that washing hands before wudu is not obligatory, and the face is the first mandatory part to wash. Regarding finding the Qibla in the desert without tools, one should pray in four directions to ensure one prayer is facing the correct direction. Downloading movies from unauthorized channels is considered impermissible due to copyright violations. To dispose of papers bearing the names of God or the Ahl al-Bayt, they should be placed in water or buried in the soil to decompose. Finally, the responsibility for compensating water damage in a building lies with the party who was negligent, and if neither party is at fault, the tenant is not liable.
