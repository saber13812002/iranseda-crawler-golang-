In this radio program, the speaker emphasizes that Iran's true power lies not in its missiles but in its reliance on God, citing divine support during the Iran-Iraq war and the recent twelve-day conflict. He stresses the importance of national unity and avoiding the enemy's attempts to create division, while urging listeners to maintain political insight and follow the leader's guidance to distinguish truth from falsehood. The second segment features a discussion by Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallah on spiritual charity and intellectual stinginess. He defines the best form of charity as acquiring and teaching monotheistic knowledge and religious laws, while identifying the worst stinginess as neglecting this spiritual growth for oneself and others. The professor highlights that those who seek true knowledge are supported by angels and the entire universe, with God removing obstacles from their path. The program concludes by reaffirming that the ultimate goal of the Islamic Revolution is to serve as a banner for the Imam of the Age, achieving dignity through spiritual and existential elevation.
