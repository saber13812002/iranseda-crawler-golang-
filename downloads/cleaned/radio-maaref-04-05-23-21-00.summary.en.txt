The radio program "Parsaiyan" begins by discussing the virtue of humility in the life of Prophet Muhammad. Hujjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasizes that humility is a chosen moral trait, citing the Prophet's simple lifestyle as a primary example. He further illustrates this concept by recounting anecdotes about Ayatollah Behjat, who maintained a humble and inconspicuous demeanor despite his high religious status. The second segment features a narrative from the book "Said Man Hosseini" about the Arbaeen pilgrimage. In this story, a teacher named Mohammad Taghi engages in a conversation with a young seminary student on the road to Karbala. They discuss the importance of authentic content in religious chanting to genuinely evoke spiritual passion. The segment concludes with an encounter with a group of Shia pilgrims from Australia who are dedicated to spreading awareness of the Ahl al-Bayt in their home country.
