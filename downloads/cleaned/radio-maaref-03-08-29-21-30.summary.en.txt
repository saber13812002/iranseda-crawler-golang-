This program discusses the interpretation of verses 162 to 165 of Surah An-Nisa, focusing on the concept of "Rasikhun fi al-Ilm."
According to the commentary, these are individuals with a sound heart and a deep understanding of the limits of their own knowledge.
They believe in all divine revelations, from the Torah and Gospel to the Quran, and follow the prophets and saints.
The text emphasizes that comprehending the essence of God's being is impossible for humans, and attempting to do so leads to spiritual ruin.
True depth in knowledge means accepting that our understanding is limited by our nature and relying on divine signs.
Respecting scholars and believers is considered in traditions to be as sacred as respecting the Prophet.
Finally, it is advised to prioritize those with greater knowledge and virtue in congregational prayers and social interactions.
This approach prevents the arrogance of scholars while also discouraging disrespect towards learned individuals.
