The program began with a call for financial aid to support the victims in Lebanon and Gaza before transitioning to a specialized discussion on the hadiths regarding the return of the Abbasid dynasty. The core of the debate focused on narrations describing the bloody conflict of the Qarqisiyya and the presence of birds of prey. A detailed analysis of the second narration's chain of transmission was conducted, specifically addressing the reliability of the narrator Muhammad ibn Sinan. While some prominent scholars like Sheikh Mufid considered him trustworthy, others such as Sheikh Tusi and Ibn Aqilah weakened his status due to accusations of extremism and deviation. The instructor, citing Ayatollah Khoei, explained that although Muhammad ibn Sinan was a loyal follower of the Ahl al-Bayt whose errors were rectified, the conflicting reports of praise and criticism prevent full reliance on his unique narrations. Consequently, it was concluded that resolving these biographical contradictions is essential to establish the authenticity of these specific hadiths.
