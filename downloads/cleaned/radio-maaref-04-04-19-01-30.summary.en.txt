In a radio sermon, the speaker criticizes Muslims for their lack of financial support for their Palestinian brethren, contrasting this with the billions of dollars donated annually by Jews worldwide to Israel. Citing Islamic narrations and the Prophet's hadith, he declares financial aid to be a religious obligation when Muslims call for help, arguing that failure to do so reflects a lack of solidarity and honor in the Muslim world. He references fatwas from religious authorities regarding martyrdom and urges Muslims to act on their religious duties to restore their dignity among nations. The second part of the program presents a historical narrative from the book "Nishan-e Hassan," depicting Imam Hassan's stay in Mecca after performing Hajj. The narrative details discussions among the Imam's brothers regarding potential threats from Abdullah ibn Zubayr and his relations with the Umayyads. Finally, a servant delivers an urgent message from the Imam's sister, Nafisa, requesting an immediate morning meeting with Imam Hassan.
