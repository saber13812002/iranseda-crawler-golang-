The radio program "Hekayat-e Azadegi" began with a hymn praising the Prophet of Islam before transitioning to the memoirs of Kazem Zarandi, a veteran of the Iran-Iraq war. Zarandi recounted the severe hardships of his captivity, including frequent beatings, a lack of basic necessities, and constant transfers between different camps. He highlighted the crucial role of prayer and reciting the Quran in maintaining morale and hope among the prisoners, particularly during Friday nights. The veteran also described a severe incident of abuse by enemy soldiers that resulted in lasting physical injuries and required heart surgery in 2011. The program emphasized the importance of documenting these experiences in books like "Azadeh" to help future generations understand the values of resistance and sacrifice. Finally, the broadcast concluded with a call for energy conservation and the avoidance of waste to preserve Iran's economic and environmental balance.
