The radio program began by highlighting the role of Iranian youth in achieving defense and missile self-sufficiency.
It reviewed the history of indigenous missile development since the 1980s and the leadership's support to prevent reliance on foreign purchases.
The Barakat Foundation reported on the implementation of 32,000 rural employment projects with an investment of 5,000 billion tomans.
The program also announced an international conference on Islamic humanities held in Tehran to honor Ayatollah Mesbah Yazdi.
The funeral ceremony for the mother of martyr Mehdi Rajab-Begi was held in Tehran, acknowledging her social services and support.
The "Zamzam Ahkam" segment addressed religious questions from followers of various maraji regarding prayer, purity, and illicit wealth.
The broadcast concluded with the announcement of prayer times and a preview of upcoming programs on Radio Ma'aref.
