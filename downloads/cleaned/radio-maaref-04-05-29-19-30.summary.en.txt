This segment of the program uses the story of Prophet Joseph (PBUH) as a model for patience in the face of hardship and oppression.
The key point is that God sent Joseph to Egypt, and through his grace, He showed mercy to the nation and resolved the famine crisis.
The Quran emphasizes in the verse "So be patient, for the outcome of patience is good" that the end result of patience is salvation.
According to narrations, a person must practice patience and forbearance to reach spiritual levels, even if they are not naturally patient.
Imam Ali (PBUH) also advises in Nahj al-Balagha to learn and practice patience if one does not inherently possess it.
These discussions are part of the sixth chapter of a collection of narrations, the continuation of which will be examined in future sessions.
The ultimate goal is to align oneself with patience to receive divine reward and achieve inner peace.
