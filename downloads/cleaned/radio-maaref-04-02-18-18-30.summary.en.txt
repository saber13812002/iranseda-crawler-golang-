This radio program addressed several religious questions and provided corresponding rulings. It was stated that dedicating the rewards of night prayers and recommended acts to others is permissible and does not diminish the individual's own merit, though it cannot substitute for settling financial debts. Performing prayer with different surahs in each rakaat is valid, although repeating surahs other than "Qul Huwa Allahu Ahad" is not recommended. For followers of Imam Khomeini, the pre-prayer takbirs are recommended, and omitting them does not invalidate the prayer. A wife may only take money from her capable husband's pocket to cover past unpaid maintenance, not future amounts or from an insolvent spouse. The father is not obligated to pay the expiation for his child's missed fasts unless the child left a will or sufficient estate. Selling a car reservation after paying half the price is religiously permissible if there is no legal prohibition. The program concluded by highlighting the night of Imam Reza's birth and encouraging a conscious pilgrimage.
