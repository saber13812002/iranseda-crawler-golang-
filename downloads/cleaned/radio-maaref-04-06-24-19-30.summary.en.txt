This radio program featured spiritual and ethical discussions emphasizing the necessity of internal purification and constant self-monitoring.
Hojjat al-Islam Mortaza Bahrami, referencing the Prophet's biography, highlighted the importance of continuous physical and spiritual hygiene.
He cited narrations about covering vessels and extinguishing lights to demonstrate the Prophet's (PBUH) vigilance over both external and internal health.
In another segment, Hojjat al-Islam Fala addressed the harms of speech, specifically identifying gossip and rumor-mongering as dangerous moral vices.
Interpreting Quranic verses, he declared that obeying gossips and fault-finders is forbidden and a primary cause of social relationship destruction.
Rumor-mongering not only harms people's reputation and lives but also renders the gossiping individual spiritually degraded and malicious.
The program concluded with a warning about divine punishment for those who facilitate evil and a strong emphasis on abandoning these character flaws.
