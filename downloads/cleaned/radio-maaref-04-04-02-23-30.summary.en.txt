In this radio program, Professor Tarashian emphasized the pivotal role of mothers in child rearing, stressing the need to manage teenagers' social environments and friendships to reduce behavioral harm. He addressed listener questions by suggesting solutions for children's boredom and destructive behavior, such as providing playmates, appropriate toys, and a conducive play environment. Regarding a teenager's obsessive-compulsive disorder, he explained that such conditions often stem from psychological stress, loneliness, and environmental pressures, requiring professional psychological intervention. The professor highlighted the social harms of having only one child, recommending that families have at least three children to prevent the isolation and stress associated with being an only child. Finally, he underscored the importance of early identification of stress roots and timely referral to specialists to prevent the escalation of behavioral and psychological issues in children.
