In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Jabbari addressed various historical questions from listeners. He traced the roots of Jewish presence in Palestine to the era of the Israelites and Pharaoh's oppression, declaring the "from the Nile to the Euphrates" claim invalid based on the Quran and the refutation of the corrupted Torah. He also detailed the story of Imam Ali's (AS) proposal to Hazrat Fatimah (SA), noting the rejection of other suitors and her implicit consent. The expert explained that while a distinction existed between formal and colloquial Arabic in early Islam, its exact intensity is difficult to determine historically. He further refuted the attribution of the flute to Prophet David (AS), emphasizing that his beautiful voice and recitation of the Psalms naturally attracted creatures. Finally, he identified the ancient land of Canaan as encompassing Palestine, Lebanon, and Syria, and clarified that Abu al-As ibn al-Rabi, the Prophet's son-in-law, had no lineage connection to Amr al-Qais.
