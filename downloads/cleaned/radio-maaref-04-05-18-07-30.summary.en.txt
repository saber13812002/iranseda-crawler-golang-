The program begins by referencing the events of Ashura and comparing them to recent attacks on children and women, emphasizing the enemies' injustice and the importance of Islamic awakening during the Arbaeen pilgrimage. The discussion then shifts to the cultivation of the soul and the dangers of the tongue at the level of the nafs, describing it as wild, aggressive, and predatory. The Prophet (PBUH) stated that most human sins and mistakes originate from the tongue, for which people will be held accountable on the Day of Judgment. The harmful effects of this tongue include the deprivation of blessings and the humiliation of the individual before God and the Infallibles, which may occur subtly or openly. Imam Ali (AS) considered the wound of the tongue more severe and harder to heal than the strike of a sword or spear. These verbal slips can destroy a person's honor and reputation, potentially leading to bloodshed and fitna. Finally, the destruction and ruin of people often result from the tongue, with the speaker themselves being the first victim of these misfired shots.
