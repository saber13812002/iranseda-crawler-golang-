The program begins by highlighting the virtue of humility in prominent religious figures, citing examples of their modest behavior. It then transitions to the sensitive topic of promoting ancientism in Iran, distinguishing it from scientific archaeology. Dr. Rasoul Rezaei explains that ancientism has often been a political tool used to counter the Abbasid Caliphate and establish a distinct national identity. He discusses the role of the Samanids and Ghaznavids in promoting the Persian language and pre-Islamic culture as a foundation for political power. Rezaei argues that if Islam had been properly explained, the ideological challenges posed by ancientism would have been less significant. The importance of a critical approach to history is emphasized to prevent distortion and foreign domination. Finally, the goal of studying ancient history should be to understand roots and strengthen identity, rather than creating a rift with religion.
