Mr. Javadi, the Director General of Sports and Youth in Tehran Province, discussed his background as a national referee and the ethical challenges of balancing refereeing with administrative duties. He stated that his primary goal is to increase the province's medal share to 40 percent at the Paris Olympics, focusing on disciplines such as wrestling, taekwondo, and weightlifting. Javadi highlighted the creation of a 42-kilometer walking and cycling ring in Tehran's parks as a significant infrastructure achievement and called for upgrading the Takhti Stadium to host top-tier league matches. He also reported substantial progress in provincial sports projects, noting that over 100 projects were completed this year, and expressed gratitude for the support of current and former governors. In the closing segment, Javadi mentioned his family's involvement in sports and emphasized the critical need for attention to health, education, and sports infrastructure for Tehran's youth.
