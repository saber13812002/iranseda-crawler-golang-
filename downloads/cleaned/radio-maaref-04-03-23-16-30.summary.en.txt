In this radio program, Professor Jafari first examines the identity of Islam ibn Amir Turk, confirming his Turkish origin while rejecting unsubstantiated claims. He then explains that Imam Reza (AS) was buried in front of his father's grave, facing the Qibla, after the ground behind the grave became impossibly hard to dig. The discussion also identifies the location of Noah's Ark construction and landing as the Najaf al-Kufa region and the Kufa Mosque, based on historical narrations and recent research. Furthermore, Ibn Muljam is traced to the Yemeni Banu Murad tribe, with notes on his mother's potential Jewish background despite the tribe's non-Jewish status. Finally, Shamir ibn Dhul-Joshan is identified as a member of the Hawazin tribe and Banu Kalab clan, establishing his kinship with Imam Hasan (AS) through their shared tribal lineage.
