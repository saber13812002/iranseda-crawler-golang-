In the first segment, a parenting expert emphasized reducing physical conflicts among children by fostering verbal communication and keeping them engaged in productive activities. He also advised parents to introduce mental games as alternatives to football and encouraged fathers to participate directly in play to model positive behavior. Additionally, strategies for preparing an older child for a new sibling were discussed, highlighting the importance of storytelling and avoiding comparisons that might trigger jealousy. The second part of the program addressed religious and historical topics, starting with the Supreme Leader's ruling on performing wudu after eye surgery. A documentary was then aired detailing the 1805 Wahhabi destruction of the Imam's shrine in Jannat al-Baqi and the ideological roots of this movement traced back to Ibn Taymiyyah and Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab. The documentary highlighted the extensive massacres of both Shia and Sunni Muslims by the Wahhabis and the looting of the Prophet's Mosque and the Baqi cemetery.
