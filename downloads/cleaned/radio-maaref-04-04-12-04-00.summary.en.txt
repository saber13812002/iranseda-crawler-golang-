The radio program began by addressing specific fiqh rulings, including the correct order of wudu, the conditions for referring to another marja when the primary one advises caution, and the recommendation of adhan and iqamah before prayer. It also clarified rulings on khums, making up missed fasts after reaching puberty, and the sufficiency of washing the face once during wudu. The second part of the show focused on interpreting Quranic verses about the "servants of the Most Merciful," emphasizing humility, avoiding sin at night, and the high value of night prayers. Using the metaphor of the Sirat bridge and eschatological stories, the importance of fearing the afterlife and avoiding sin for salvation was highlighted. Finally, the program urged listeners to make the most of their limited time by accumulating good deeds, comparing life to a fleeting journey that requires constant preparation.
