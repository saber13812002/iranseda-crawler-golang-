In this program, the commentator interprets the Quranic term "sufaha" not as financial weakness, but as the inability to discern true spiritual perfection and salvation. He argues that hypocrites publicly associated with sincere believers like Salman and Ammar, yet privately mocked their faith alongside their demonic influences. Rejecting Sheikh Tusi's view, the speaker defines the root of "istihza" as mockery, emphasizing that this act is serious and demeaning rather than mere joking. The concept of "shira" (buying) in the Quran is also explained metaphorically as the exchange of salvation for damnation. Finally, addressing the ongoing water crisis, the commentator prays for rain and urges listeners to practice water conservation while seeking the reappearance of Imam Mahdi.
