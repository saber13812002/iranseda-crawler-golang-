This radio program discussed the critical importance of teaching financial literacy to children and adolescents in both schools and homes.
The primary recommendation is to involve children in managing household expenses and keeping them informed about their parents' economic pressures.
To instill these skills, it is suggested that practical responsibilities, such as grocery shopping or managing a monthly budget, be assigned to teenagers.
Additionally, using simple tools like piggy banks and bank cards is recommended to teach children about saving and cost analysis.
The second part of the discussion focused on child-rearing during the first seven years, advocating for a reward system over punishment.
It was emphasized that parents should understand their child's age and teach problem-solving skills through patience and clear expectations rather than blame.
Finally, the concept of patience and self-control in the face of wrong desires and ethical challenges was explained from an Islamic perspective.
The program concluded with prayers for the appearance of Imam Mahdi and wishes for blessings during the holy month of Ramadan.
