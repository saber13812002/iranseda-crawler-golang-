In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasizes the importance of praying for others, citing narrations from Imam Hassan and Ayatollah Khomeini to show that this practice leads to answered prayers and spiritual growth.
He illustrates this virtue through stories involving Ayatollah Behjat and Sheikh Muhammad Hadi, presenting kindness and intercession for strangers and even doctors as a commendable model.
The second part of the program focuses on the exegesis of verses from Surah Yunus, explaining that "iftira" (ascription) means severing one's connection with God through lying about Him.
The commentator explains that "falaha" (success) implies breaking through the barriers of salvation, which liars fail to achieve by rejecting the truth.
The term "mata'" in the verse is defined as a meager and temporary worldly benefit, contrasted with the severe punishment of the Hereafter.
A subtle point is made regarding the use of the verb "nadhiguhum" (make them taste) instead of "na'adhibuhum" (punish them), indicating that sinners will taste the essence of their punishment.
Finally, it is stressed that lying about God and His Messenger is the worst form of falsehood, acting as the primary obstacle to eternal salvation.
