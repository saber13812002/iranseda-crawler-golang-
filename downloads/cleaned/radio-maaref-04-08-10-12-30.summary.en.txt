In this lecture, Hazrat Ehdollah Reza Ostadi emphasizes the principle of intercession and tawassul to the Ahl al-Bayt, citing Quranic verses and narrations to affirm its compatibility with pure monotheism. He clarifies that intercession is not independent of God but a status granted by Him to prophets and saints, thus refuting accusations of shirk. A critical warning is issued that intercession is denied to polytheists and oppressors, as stated by the Prophet (PBUH). Oppression against the vulnerable, particularly within families and society, is identified as a major barrier to receiving divine mercy. The speaker highlights that performing daily prayers, fasting, paying zakat, and maintaining lawful obedience to one's spouse are essential conditions for benefiting from the intercession of Fatima Zahra (SA). It is stressed that intercession is not an excuse for sinning but a refuge for those who have struggled against their lower selves. Finally, Imam Sadiq (AS) warned in his final testament that intercession will not reach those who abandon prayer and neglect the virtues of the Ahl al-Bayt.
