The program begins by highlighting the high statistics of single individuals in the 1970s and 1980s, then explores key criteria for choosing a spouse. According to the Quran and religious narrations, the primary criterion is the piety and chastity of both partners, which is emphasized for both men and women. Special importance is placed on selecting a spouse who brings joy and peace, allowing for long and intimate conversations. Family background is also identified as a crucial factor, with a preference for those from pious and righteous families. Physical health, fertility, and appearance are considered important, and it is recommended that prospective spouses see each other. The segment concludes by discussing the life of Lady Khadija and her age at marriage in celebration of the Prophet's birth. The program ends with prayers for the Mahdi and congratulations for the Prophet's birthday.
