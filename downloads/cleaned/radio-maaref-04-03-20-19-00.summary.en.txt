This radio program provided jurisprudential answers to various religious questions. First, it was clarified that the prayer of a thief is not invalid, although abandoning sin and maintaining trust are also his duties. Regarding activities in the digital currency market, no definitive ruling was given due to the ambiguity of these transactions, and consulting another scholar was recommended. Additionally, family raffles for interest-free loans were declared permissible if specific conditions are met. The necessity of informing a client about bleeding during a haircut was also emphasized. Finally, the concepts of order and continuity in prayer were explained, noting that any disruption in the sequence of parts or a customary gap between them can render the prayer invalid.
