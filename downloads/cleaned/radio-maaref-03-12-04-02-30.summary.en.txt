The radio broadcast utilizes verse 38 of Surah Yunus to distinguish between ignoring personal insults and defending the core principles of the system. While the speaker suggests that personal slurs can be met with dignified silence, any challenge to the revolution or the state's fundamental values demands a robust response. The Quran instructs believers to refute false accusations with truth and evidence, rather than leaving them unanswered. A key distinction is drawn between magic and miracles, noting that sorcerers lack the authority to challenge divine truth due to their reliance on limited material powers. In contrast, the Quran stands as an eternal miracle that has remained unchallenged for fourteen centuries despite all technological and scientific advancements. This enduring resilience highlights the Quran's inherent superiority over all powers other than God. The program concludes with a poetic tribute to Imam Ali (AS) and introduces the next segment, which explores his virtues through the lens of Nahj al-Balagha.
