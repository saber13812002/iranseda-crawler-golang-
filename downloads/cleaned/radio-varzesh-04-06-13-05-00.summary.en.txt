Ali Jamaleki, a veteran Iranian footballer, shared his memories of playing for Pas FC and the national team. He recalled the team's successes in the Bangkok Asian Games and the Asian Cup Winners' Cup, as well as the experience of performing pilgrimage in Iraq. Jamaleki attributed his short tenure with the national team to aging prematurely due to playing at the senior level at a young age. He criticized Pas FC's financial issues and strict contracts, which prevented him from transferring to other clubs. After retiring, Jamaleki transitioned into coaching, working with teams such as Abfa, Esteghlal, and Saipa. He emphasized that he never dealt with agents during his playing career and always maintained respectful interactions with clubs. He spoke fondly of his father, the late Ezzatollah Jamaleki, describing him as a reserved but proud figure who celebrated his son's achievements. Finally, Jamaleki highlighted the importance of proper training in youth football and the need for greater appreciation of forgotten football veterans.
