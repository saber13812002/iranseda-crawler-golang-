The first part of the broadcast emphasizes the necessity of internal movement and deep theological understanding to achieve spiritual perfection and divine proximity.
Based on Quranic verses and the teachings of the Imams, it is argued that humans must detach from worldly attachments to perceive the unity of God.
The Quran is highlighted as a major caller to faith, and students are urged to maintain a deep connection with the religious leaders and elders.
The second section delves into a complex philosophical discussion regarding the nature of intellectual perception and the relationship between the soul and intellectual forms.
The theory of "substantial species" and the existence of separate intellectual substances for each type of being are introduced and analyzed.
The debate over whether the soul creates sensory and imaginative forms or merely perceives them is examined in detail.
Finally, intellectual perception is interpreted not as a simple abstraction from material attributes, but as a distant and ambiguous observation of these separate substances by the soul.
