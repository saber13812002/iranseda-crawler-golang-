This radio program began by addressing specific jurisprudential questions for followers of Ayatollah Shabiri Zanjani and Ayatollah Javadi Amoli. It was clarified that saying only the final salam is sufficient to exit prayer during prostration, and dirt under fingernails does not invalidate wudu unless it prevents water from reaching the skin. Financial guidance was also provided, stating that khums is due on saved capital and its interest, although interest spent on living expenses during the khums year is exempt. The core of the program featured a tafsir of Imam Ali's sermons, emphasizing the steadfastness of Muslim faith against enemies, including unbelieving relatives, and asserting that ultimate victory for the side of truth is the result of sincerity and patience. A historical account of Imam Ali's confrontation with the Kharijites and his warnings against their rebellion were also discussed. The broadcast highlighted the current security situation in Iran following recent aggressions, urging citizens to avoid sharing sensitive information on social media. Finally, the program concluded with an interpretation of verse 55 of Surah Yunus, highlighting God's absolute power, the truth of His promises, and the inevitable return of all humans to Him.
