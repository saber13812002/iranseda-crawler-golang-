This radio program features Ayatollah Javadi Amoli's exegesis of verse 120 of Surah Al-Ma'idah, focusing on the concept of "following the best" and moral elevation from truth to excellence. Citing verses from Surah Az-Zumar and Al-Fatir, the exegete defines "the best" as adhering to the Prophet's words and actions and selecting the highest ranks within the realm of truth. He critiques pre-Islamic tribalism and social stratification, emphasizing the necessity of justice, benevolence, and humility in social interactions. The program explains that God rewards those who strive for the best, even if they occasionally fall short, as detailed in Surah An-Nahl. Furthermore, it highlights the distinction between internal and external conduct, advocating for "repelling evil with good" as a characteristic of great men and prophets. This approach not only mitigates hostility but also awakens hearts from worldly intoxication by fostering unity and affection. Ultimately, this path leads to true knowledge and spiritual delight in faith and a profound connection with God.
