In this radio program, Ayatollah Sifir Mazandarani analyzes the "Tathir" verse to demonstrate its implication for the Imamate of the Ahl al-Bayt. By consulting authoritative linguistic sources, he defines "Rijs" as impurity, sin, or actions leading to punishment, contrasting it with "Tathir" (purification). Scholars such as Zamakhshari and Bayzawi interpret the use of "Rijs" instead of "sin" as a rhetorical metaphor intended to evoke aversion to the spiritual pollution of the soul. This linguistic analysis establishes that the Ahl al-Bayt are free from all impurity and sin, thereby proving their infallibility. Since infallibility is a fundamental prerequisite for Imamate, the verse's reference to their purity directly supports their claim to leadership. Consequently, the Ahl al-Bayt's assertion of Imamate is considered a truthful claim based on their proven infallibility. The discussion concludes that the purification mentioned in the verse refers to an inherent spiritual purity that serves as the foundation for their divine authority.
