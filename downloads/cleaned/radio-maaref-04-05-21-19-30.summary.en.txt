The radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat" began with religious music and prayers before transitioning to a discussion on the importance of guarding one's tongue. Hujjat al-Islam Mohammad Hadi Falahati emphasized, citing narrations, that true faith and piety are unattainable without linguistic discipline, as the tongue reflects one's practical monotheism. He explained that preserving the tongue is itself a form of worship and that neglecting it invalidates other religious deeds. The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) identified the tongue as the key to entering Paradise and avoiding Hell, highlighting its central role in spiritual conduct. The speaker noted that in Islamic teachings, speech carries practical weight, meaning that gossip or lying constitutes a sinful action. Consequently, believers must flee from the ego-driven tongue by adhering to Sharia, using it as a shield to protect their other acts of worship. The program concluded with the prayer for the relief of Imam Mahdi and information on how to support the oppressed people of Gaza.
