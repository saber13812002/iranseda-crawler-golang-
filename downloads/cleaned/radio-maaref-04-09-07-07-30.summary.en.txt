In this radio program, Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallah analyzes the moral vice of flattery and sycophancy using Quranic verses and Hadiths from the Ahl al-Bayt. He describes flattery as a psychological trait stemming from the "commanding self" (nafs ammarah), which has become prevalent across all social classes, particularly among officials and the wealthy. A central point of the discussion is that authentic praise and glorification are exclusively reserved for God, while unwarranted praise of humans is considered foolishness and a form of mockery. Imam Ali (AS) in Nahj al-Balagha identifies flattery as a trap for Satan and a sign of irrationality, stating that praising someone for qualities they do not possess is actually ridiculing them. Consequently, both engaging in flattery and accepting it are indicators of intellectual weakness and a deviation from proper moral education. The program concludes by advising listeners to combat this vice to maintain moral and intellectual health, emphasizing that true perfection is achieved only through God's praise and sincere repentance.
