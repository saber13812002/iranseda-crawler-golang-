This segment of the program discusses the philosophical relationship between existence and essence, emphasizing the ontological priority of existence.
It is explained that this priority is not temporal but ontological, meaning that at the stage before essence is realized, the entity is neither human nor existent.
A critique is offered against views that prioritize essence, using the example of number and evenness to prove the necessity of existential priority.
The difference between external and mental existence is also examined, noting that essence in the mind is a quality and differs essentially from external existence.
Following the philosophical discussion, the Ziyarat al-Yasin was recited by a professional reciter.
Subsequently, statements by Imam Khomeini regarding the necessity of clerical engagement in political and governmental issues were broadcast.
The Imam emphasized that politics existed during the eras of the Prophet and the Imams, and ignoring it is a plot to remove religion from societal administration.
