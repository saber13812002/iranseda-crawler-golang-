In this radio program, the first guest emphasized the importance of choosing righteous companions based on Quranic teachings, urging listeners to support friends and relatives in their pilgrimage to Karbala during the Arbaeen season. He cited a narration from Imam Sadiq (AS) highlighting the immense reward of facilitating others' visits and expressed gratitude to the Iraqi people for preserving this sacred tradition. The second segment, featuring Professor Mohammad Hadi Fala, explored the concept of "Lisan" or the tongue in the Quran. The professor analyzed various verses to illustrate how language serves as a tool for preaching, prayer, and conversely, a source of major sins such as lying, slander, and harassment. A central point was the Quranic assertion that on the Day of Judgment, one's tongue will testify against them regarding their actions. The discussion concluded that after death, a person's inner self is revealed without disguise through their words, making the discipline of the tongue essential for spiritual growth and avoiding harm to others.
