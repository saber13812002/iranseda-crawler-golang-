In this radio program, counselors emphasized the importance of empathy with a spouse who is upset about their family, advising against taking on the role of a defense attorney.
They recommended accepting both the spouse and the family in their independent roles rather than making unfair comparisons, which helps prevent family conflicts.
The second section identified age differences in marriage, particularly when the woman is older, as a significant challenge requiring pre-marital counseling.
The risks of familial marriages were also discussed, noting that relatives often become more deeply involved when disputes arise.
Managing sulking was explored by distinguishing between insulting and affectionate sulking, with a recommendation to use "I-messages" to express needs clearly.
Counselors noted that men typically only understand explicit words and often miss indirect signs or sarcasm.
Finally, the influence of media in creating unrealistic expectations of love and marriage was criticized, and the normalcy of shared life was encouraged.
