In this radio program, the family counselor emphasized the importance of empathy with a spouse who is upset about their family, strongly advising against acting as a defense attorney or engaging in conflict with the in-laws. The expert clarified that comparing a spouse's role with that of parents is a mistake, as these relationships have distinct natures and should not be pitted against each other. Regarding age gaps in marriage, particularly when the woman is older and the couple is related, the counselor warned of cultural and psychological risks, making pre-marital counseling essential. On the topic of managing sulking, the advice was to replace insulting silence with affectionate withdrawal and the use of "I-messages" to express needs clearly. It was noted that men often do not interpret body language well, so requests must be stated verbally and directly. Finally, the program warned against unrealistic expectations of love created by media, encouraging listeners to view happiness as a normal, everyday occurrence rather than a dramatic event.
