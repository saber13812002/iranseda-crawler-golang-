The radio program begins with a discussion on the jurisprudential and political aspects of the Guardianship of the Jurist and governmental authorities.
A key segment introduces the book "The Position of Governmental Rulings and the Authority of the Jurist" by Ayatollah Momen, analyzing topics like defensive jihad and individual rights.
The conversation then shifts to the appointment and dismissal of officials, emphasizing that the Supreme Leader must respect the constitution and utilize the oversight mechanisms of the three branches of government.
Subsequently, the program highlights the Islamic importance of caring for orphans, illustrated by the stories of Ja'far Tayyar and Abdullah ibn Ja'far.
A field report from Farhang Street reveals that citizens stress the need to preserve the dignity of orphans and avoid any humiliating behavior.
Finally, the broadcast underscores the Quranic and hadith-based obligation to provide material and spiritual support to orphans until they are self-sufficient, strictly prohibiting the misappropriation of their wealth.
