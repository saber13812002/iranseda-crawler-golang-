In the radio program "Bar-e Bal-e Sakhn," Hujjat al-Islam Saeidi Aria discussed the concept of a virtuous society based on the teachings of Imam Sajjad (PBUH). He emphasized that a community lacking deep religious understanding is vulnerable to the influence of the ignorant and easily accepts injustice. The speaker argued that fearing God, rather than fearing other humans, grants individuals inner courage and freedom from baseless anxieties. To earn the love of God and people, one must practice benevolence through good intentions and righteous actions while avoiding greed for others' wealth. Paying Khums and Zakat as forms of wealth purification, along with giving charity to ward off calamities and maintain health, were highlighted as essential religious duties. The importance of continuous repentance for uncovering hidden sins and resolving financial and livelihood issues was also stressed. Finally, the talk concluded by underscoring the necessity of maintaining strong emotional bonds with parents and reviving religious traditions during events like Safar to foster social and spiritual cohesion.
