In this radio program, the wife of Martyr General Tehrani Moghaddam recounts the hardships of war and managing family life amidst resource scarcity. She emphasizes her husband's strict adherence to early morning prayers and his exemplary character within the family and community. The discussion also covers Operation Mersad and the martyr's collaboration with Seyyed Shirazi and Ahmad Kazemi against the MEK, which led to his inclusion on their terrorist list. The profound impact of Imam Khomeini's passing on the martyr's spirit and his swift acceptance of Ayatollah Khamenei as the new leader are also highlighted. Finally, the program explores the spiritual status of martyrs, distinguishing battlefield martyrdom from the sacrifices of Karbala in terms of defending divine boundaries and protecting family honor.
