This radio program emphasized the necessity of synergy and unity among Abrahamic religions to stand against the front of atheism and secularism.
The guest argued, citing a verse from the Quran, that Islam is not a new religion but a return to the faith of Abraham, highlighting the historical bond between Muslims, Christians, and Jews.
A personal anecdote about a deep friendship with an Italian Christian illustrated the practical aspect of selfless service and shared spirituality across different faiths.
Dr. Javad Jafari explained, referencing Nahj al-Balagha, that the Imam of the Time plays an active role during his occultation by dispersing groups of falsehood and supporting seekers of truth.
The guest recounted his experience of attending Catholic prayers in Italy, expressing amazement at the depth of their presence of heart and submission in their supplications to God.
He stressed that spirituality and connection with the Divine are not exclusive to one group and that common ground with other religions should be leveraged to build a just society.
The discussion concluded that waiting for the reappearance is not about passivity but requires active effort, cultural work, and the creation of heart-to-heart unity among Muslims and other believers worldwide.
