This radio program emphasized the active and dynamic nature of waiting for the Mahdi, highlighting the Imam's limited communication with Shia followers during his occultation. Dr. Erfan argued for the Imam's unlimited, innate knowledge by citing definitive texts, rational proofs, and the vast scientific legacy of the Ahl al-Bayt. He interpreted the Quranic verse regarding the elevation of the oppressed as a divine grace that will bring global justice and end tyranny for all humanity. A historical example of Imam Javad's debate with Yahya Nakkas was used to demonstrate that the Imams' knowledge transcends ordinary acquired learning. The discussion concluded with a call for listeners to promote the Imam's name through digital media and art to prepare the world for his reappearance. The program ended with prayers for the Mahdi's early return and the establishment of a global just government.
