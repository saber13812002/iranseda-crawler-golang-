Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the importance of protecting public funds and the ethical conduct of clerics in the Parsaiyan program. He cited Imam Ali's (AS) advice on financial prudence and accountability, highlighting the strictness of leaders like Ayatollah Khomeini and Ayatollah Tehrani in avoiding personal use of public money. The program then featured a dramatic retelling from the book "Neshan-e Hassan," focusing on the internal conflicts of Imam Hassan's (AS) children as they faced the decision to support Imam Hussein (AS). The narrative illustrated the tension between personal safety and religious duty, ultimately affirming their unwavering loyalty to the Ahl al-Bayt. The broadcast also addressed a fiqh question regarding the modification of vows and introduced a student-led audio campaign called "Novgaran-e Hosseini." The episode concluded with condolences for the Muharram mourning period and the martyrdom of Lady Zainab (SA). Finally, the host previewed the next program, which will discuss social justice in the light of the Hussaini movement.
