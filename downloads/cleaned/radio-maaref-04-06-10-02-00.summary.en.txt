In this radio program, the importance of praying for others and its impact on the acceptance of one's own prayers were discussed based on the traditions of the Ahl al-Bayt and the practices of great scholars like Ayatollah Behjat and Saduqi. Professor Faqih, a member of the faculty at the Imam Khomeini Educational and Research Institute, emphasized the heart's connection with God, presenting prayer as a key to deeper divine knowledge and moral improvement. He explained that prayer not only addresses material needs but also cures the trait of arrogance by fostering a sense of humility and dependence on the Divine. In response to listeners' questions, the distinction between insisting on specific personal desires and persisting in seeking God's general good was clarified. Professor Faqih advised that individuals should constantly raise their hands in supplication and seek absolute good from the Creator rather than fixating on specific details. The hidden wisdoms of prayer, including the enhancement of spiritual knowledge and the establishment of a reciprocal relationship between the servant and God, were also highlighted. Finally, the program concluded by emphasizing the necessity of persistence in prayer and supplication as the key to happiness and spiritual peace.
