Mehdi Monajati, a veteran of Iranian football, discussed his coaching career and participation in first-grade classes taught by foreign coaches. He recalled the national team's successful qualifying matches against North Korea and Australia for the 1986 World Cup, mentioning key players like Masoud Moini. Monajati expressed concern about the current state of Iranian football, emphasizing the need for unified management and leveraging domestic talent. He lamented the separation of the Pas team from Tehran, which he believes led to its decline, while sharing memories of Pas's Asian championship and intense derbies against Persepolis and Esteghlal. The veteran also noted the military background of many Pas players and the lack of adequate rewards during that era. Finally, Monajati mentioned his two children and stressed the importance of health and maintaining a passion for football in old age.
