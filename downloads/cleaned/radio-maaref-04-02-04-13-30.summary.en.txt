The radio program "Narrative of Love" begins by sharing the stories of martyrs' mothers and wives to highlight their enduring love and resilience.
The mother of martyr Javad speaks with dignity about her pride in her son's conscious choice to seek martyrdom.
She recounts his humane actions in Syria, such as offering water to enemy soldiers and helping the needy, as examples of Islamic ethics.
Despite her deep grief, she emphasizes the importance of maintaining strength and not displaying weakness to the public, viewing her son's sacrifice as a great honor.
She expresses frustration with people who misunderstand martyrdom, often associating it with foolishness or ignorance rather than a noble cause.
In the second part of the program, the occasion of Imam Sadiq's martyrdom anniversary is used to discuss the connection between his historical movement and today's Basij.
It is argued that the Infallible Imams were tasked with preserving religious values against the corruption of governance into tyranny and moral decay.
The discussion concludes that martyrdom and self-sacrifice are rooted in religious awareness and following the Infallible models to preserve the Islamic identity of society.
