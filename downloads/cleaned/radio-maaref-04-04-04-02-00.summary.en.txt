This program, dedicated to the mothers and wives of martyrs, features an interview with Elham Haidari, the wife of the late Sardar Hassan Tehrani Moghaddam. She recounts their nearly 28-year marriage, beginning with her husband's youth in Lavasan and his deep immersion in religious and revolutionary circles during 1979. Haidari shares a vivid anecdote about the martyr's determination to secure larger weapons during the revolution, including his role in the takeover of the state television. The discussion then shifts to his early war activities in Khuzestan, highlighting his collaboration with commanders like Kharazi and Qasemi. She emphasizes that their extraordinary bravery and strategic management stemmed from a profound spiritual connection with God and the system. This spiritual strength is credited with fostering a sense of national self-confidence and belief in the power of faith among the people. Finally, the summary touches upon their marriage in 1983, which was facilitated by mutual friends and family connections.
