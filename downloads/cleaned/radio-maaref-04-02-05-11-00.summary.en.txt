The Ma'aref radio program began by commemorating the anniversary of the US Delta Force defeat in the Tuba Desert. It then reported on the funeral of four newly identified martyrs and ceremonies marking the martyrdom of Imam Sadiq and the 1963 Fiziya incident. The broadcast highlighted student pilgrimages to the Bazi Daraz heights and the celebration of the Decade of Karamat in 50,000 mosques. The head of the Hajj organization announced the dispatch of 86,000 Iranian pilgrims and the formation of special groups for Iranians living abroad. Politically, the Foreign Minister and Quds Force Commander were appointed to the Foundation for Preserving Sacred Defense Heritage, which was renamed to include "Resistance." A memorial event for the wives of veterans featured the launch of a book titled "Alone with the Same Body." The 36th International Tehran Book Fair is set to open at the Imam Khomeini Mosque. Finally, the program covered anti-Israeli protests in three provinces and aired religious segments discussing the Islamic rulings on interest and loan penalties.
