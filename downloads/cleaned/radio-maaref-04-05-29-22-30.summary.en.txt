This radio program, centered on the Thursdays of Imam Reza (PBUH), addresses the economic and medical struggles faced by the public. The host urges listeners to appreciate the blessing of health and prioritize the urgent needs of cancer patients over costly pilgrimage trips. Real-life stories of low-income families struggling with the high costs of cancer treatment are shared to evoke empathy and encourage financial support. The primary goal is to raise funds for these patients' medication costs through text message donations. Listeners are invited to send the number 8 or the phrase "Ya Imam Reza" to the number 3552 to participate in this charitable effort. This initiative not only helps alleviate the suffering of needy families but also serves as a gratitude for the gift of health.
