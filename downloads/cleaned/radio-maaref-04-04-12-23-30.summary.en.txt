In this radio program featuring Hojjat al-Islam Sadeghi, the central role of Lady Zainab (SA) in Karbala and her support for her brother Imam Hussein (AS) were emphasized.
A key focus was the story of Lady Zainab's visit to Imam Hussein (AS) on the night of Ashura, where she heard about the Prophet's (PBUH) dream regarding Hussein's imminent martyrdom.
The high status of Imam Abbas (AS) as the Moon of the Banu Hashim and the phrase "Arakt bi nafsik antiaqi" spoken by Imam Hussein (AS) to his brother near the well were also discussed.
Citing Arabic poetry, the speaker highlighted how Imam Hussein's (AS) concerns shifted from the tents to his brother after Abbas's (AS) martyrdom.
The discussion then turned to the hypocritical criticism of human rights in Iran by the West, contrasting it with their silence on their own crimes.
The speaker argued that fear of the Islamic Republic's power and progress is the primary reason for this double standard and media slander.
The program concluded with a call for pilgrimage, seeking to soothe the heart of Imam Mahdi (AJ) through charity, and the recitation of Ziyarat Al-Yasin.
