The radio program begins by emphasizing the supreme value of mediating peace between conflicting parties, citing Nahj al-Balagha to state that this act is superior to all other acts of worship.
It then analyzes the story of Prophet David in the Quran to highlight the necessity of patience and impartiality for judges to hear both sides before rendering a verdict.
A narration from the Prophet is presented, declaring that loving Imam Hasan and Imam Hussein is equivalent to loving the Prophet and God, which guarantees entry into Paradise.
Conversely, harboring hatred toward Imam Hussein is identified as enmity toward the Prophet and God, leading to eternal damnation.
The broadcast concludes with the recitation of the Prophet's Ziyarat and supplications focused on the love of the Ahl al-Bayt.
The ultimate goal is to strengthen the faith of listeners through familiarity with the Prophetic and Quranic traditions.
The program stresses that spiritual enlightenment is only achievable by following the Prophet's example and avoiding the darkness of resentment.
