The radio program began with religious invocations and a focus on traditional medicine for health and wellness. Dr. Pourhosseini advised elderly listeners to use foot massages with a rolling pin or warm water bottle to alleviate fatigue and improve blood circulation. He emphasized that this practice prepares the body for light exercises like walking and prevents the accumulation of waste materials. In the second segment, a theological question comparing Islamic sacrifice with idolatry was addressed by expert Shahrudi. He clarified that Islamic sacrifice is an act of drawing closer to God and represents the perfection of the animal, unlike the irrational practices of idol worship. Subsequently, Hujjat al-Islam Rasoul Hafez discussed the Quranic concept of avoiding "takalluf," or unnecessary pretension and self-display. He urged listeners to maintain social etiquette without resorting to haram methods or excessive spending to impress others. The program concluded with a speech by Imam Khomeini highlighting the unity between the clergy and the nation as a divine blessing.
