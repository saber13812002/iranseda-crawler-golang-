The first anniversary of the martyrdom of President Raisi and Foreign Minister Amir-Abdollahian was commemorated in Basra, highlighting their revolutionary commitment. Simultaneously, an Islamic art exhibition featuring martyrs' works was opened at the holy shrine in Karbala. Internationally, a Quran desecration incident at the University of California and India's waqf law amendments sparked strong condemnation from Muslim communities and human rights groups. The 8th International Congress on the Quranic Thoughts of the Supreme Leader is being held in Qom, gathering scholars from over 21 languages. In Gaza, the regime's attacks on residential areas and hospitals have drawn global condemnation and pressure to lift the blockade. Yemeni armed forces declared Haifa port a military target in response to the ongoing siege of Gaza. In Turkey, students launched a campaign to boycott Zionist products and raise public awareness. Finally, special prayer facilities were provided for women in the Grand Mosque, and Quranic outreach initiatives began in Yemen.
