In this segment of the radio program "I am Fatima," Hujjat al-Islam Seyyed Abbas Hosseini Baghradi explored the Quranic concept of "Aswatun Hasanah" (a good example). Citing Imam Mahdi's statement that Fatima Zahra (SA) is his role model, he emphasized that this emulation involves patiently enduring hardships and oppression, rather than merely copying outward behaviors. Baghradi recounted a narrative from the book "Al-Ulum wal Ma'arif" about the miraculous grinding of wheat in Fatima's house, illustrating that God uses worldly difficulties to elevate a person's spiritual rank. He also highlighted Fatima Zahra's (SA) pivotal role as the bridge between prophethood and Imamate, urging both men and women to adopt her lifestyle. The discussion concluded by stressing the importance of following the path of the Infallibles in daily life, while also mentioning the significance of Friday prayers and upcoming broadcasts on Radio Ma'aref.
