The radio program begins by highlighting the high status of human dignity in Islamic culture, emphasizing the Quranic and narrated focus on human rights and defending the oppressed.
Imam Ali (AS) urged Muslims to support the oppressed and avoid indifference to injustice, a principle reinforced by the Ziyarat Ashura which condemns silence in the face of tyranny.
The second segment, featuring expert Hadi Fala, explores the critical role of language in human existence and its function in communication and identity.
According to narrations, language is an essential tool for conveying sensory and spiritual perceptions, without which a human being remains an ambiguous and unrecognized entity.
The beauty and attractiveness of men are primarily rooted in their eloquence and logic, whereas women's physical appearance plays a more significant role in initial attraction.
Using beautiful words and adhering to the etiquette of speech not only attracts hearts but also prevents alienation and creates a positive social impact.
Ultimately, the discussion stresses the necessity of mastering word choice and maintaining sincerity in speech to achieve human perfection and a transparent life.
