The radio program begins with local music and a casual, humorous conversation about everyday social topics. The main segment focuses on analyzing the concept of "arrogance" and the factors that lead to human self-deception from the perspective of the Quran and Hadith. The instructor identifies ignorance of the self, reliance on false beliefs, and attachment to the world as primary causes of arrogance. Special emphasis is placed on how people often deceive themselves through their own material wealth or by coveting the possessions of others. This self-deception causes the human body and soul to serve external worldly matters rather than achieving spiritual elevation. The program concludes with a warning about the dangers of greed and envy towards others' assets, urging listeners to abandon these moral vices.
