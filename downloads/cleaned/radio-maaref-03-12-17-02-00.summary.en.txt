After obtaining the license of ijtihad from Sheikh Ansari, Sayyid Jamal al-Din Ashtiani traveled to India to understand British colonialism and assess the status of Shia Islam. During his stay, he familiarized himself with modern sciences and other religions, later emphasizing the need to transform Mecca into a center of Islamic unity and power during his visit to the Hejaz. By establishing political and religious awakening circles across the globe, he sparked widespread revolutionary movements without any state support. He adopted an ethical and logical approach, engaging in dialogue with leaders of deviant movements to guide them back to the faith. Subsequently, he traveled to Afghanistan and Iran to resist colonial infiltration and the spread of heretical currents. The second part of the program examines a narration from the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) that introduces the twelve Imams as the "Naqibs." This narration, found in both Shia and some Sunni sources, refers to the return of the Imam of the Age and his followers. It also discusses the application of Quranic verses to Mahdism concepts, detailing the return of the oppressed and the fate of Satan's allies.
