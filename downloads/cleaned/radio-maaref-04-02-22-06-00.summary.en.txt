In this program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verses from Surah Al-Ma'idah to emphasize the fundamental difference between "khabith" (impure) and "tayyib" (pure), asserting that they are never equal. He argues that in spiritual matters, faith and knowledge are pure, while disbelief and ignorance are impure, and increasing the quantity of impurity does not make it equivalent to purity. The verse "Do not ask about things that will make you sad" is explained as a directive to avoid futile or secretive questions that lead to disbelief or despair. Conversely, asking about general principles and actionable knowledge revealed in the Quran is not only permitted but obligatory. The concepts of God's "forgiveness" and "forbearance" are interpreted as the cleansing of sins followed by divine pardon, similar to cleaning stains before painting a wall. Finally, the greeting of peace and mercy is described as a symbol of achieving a sound heart (a pure vessel) and then filling it with divine mercy (the content).
