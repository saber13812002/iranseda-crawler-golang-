The funeral processions for Iranian martyrs in Tehran were widely covered by global media, highlighting the nation's resilience against Israel and the United States. While President Trump reiterated claims of destroying Iran's nuclear facilities, US military officials admitted that bunker-buster bombs were not used on the Isfahan site due to its depth. Yemeni forces launched missiles at Israeli targets to support Gaza, while tens of thousands protested in England despite police crackdowns. In Tanzania, Indian-origin Shia Muslims held Muharram processions, demonstrating religious unity with other local faiths. Meanwhile, elements of the Jolani regime in Syria prevented the establishment of mourning tents in the Sayyida Zainab area. In Pakistan, the "World Day of Husaini Infants" was marked by mothers dressing their newborns in green to renew their pledge of loyalty to the Imam. Finally, the legacy of Martyr Sharif Qanuti, a pioneer in the anti-Pahlavi struggle and defender of Khorramshahr, was honored through the recollections of his comrades.
