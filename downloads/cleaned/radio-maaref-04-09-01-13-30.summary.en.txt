The radio program "Lasting Memories" began by sharing Sardar Nik-Sokhan's recollections from Operation Badr. He described the intense frontline battles and the martyrdom of his comrades during the Iraqi forces' advance. Nik-Sokhan recounted his desperate attempt to rescue a wounded student named Hadi Ziba amidst the danger of being captured. He highlighted Ziba's spiritual requests and his pledge to Imam Zaman during his final moments. The second part of the program featured Sardar Ali Sasan-Najad's account of evacuating wounded soldiers in the dark and the sacrifice of the fighters. The episode concluded by emphasizing the role of Basij thinking in overcoming historical humiliations and fostering national self-confidence.
