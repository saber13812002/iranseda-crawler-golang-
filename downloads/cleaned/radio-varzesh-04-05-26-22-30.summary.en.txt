The guest, Ali Rezaei, a veteran broadcaster and former parliamentarian, began by discussing the technical and linguistic challenges of reporting sports news and the importance of maintaining a calm demeanor when delivering negative updates. He emphasized the necessity of hiring based on merit rather than nepotism, expressing support for the new generation of broadcasters. Rezaei explained that his motivation for entering politics was to address major sports issues and influence legislation in this sector. He highlighted the establishment of the Ministry of Sports and Youth during the eighth parliamentary term as a key achievement, while criticizing the lack of a clear strategy and reliance on external personnel. The former representative of Izdخواست and Malek stressed the need for long-term, sustainable sports policies instead of frequent managerial changes. He also noted his refusal to sign protest letters regarding referee errors, advocating for the parliament to strengthen its oversight role over its legislative function. Finally, Rezaei underscored the importance of maintaining direct, face-to-face communication with the public and addressing their legitimate demands as a true representative.
