In the radio program "Panjereh," the challenges of creating engaging religious content while avoiding worn-out clichés were discussed. Guests emphasized that producers must simplify information and address current audience needs to effectively utilize radio for conveying new perspectives. They also stressed the importance of training creative producers to avoid producing content solely to fill broadcast hours. The second part of the program featured the memoirs of Hussein Khorshidi, a veteran of the Iran-Iraq War. He described the harsh conditions of his captivity, including psychological torture and the resistance against enemy pressure in Iraqi prisons. Khorshidi identified hearing the news of Imam Reza's martyrdom during his captivity as his most bitter memory. Finally, the book "Red Squares," which narrates the life of Hajj Hossein Yekta, was introduced to the listeners.
