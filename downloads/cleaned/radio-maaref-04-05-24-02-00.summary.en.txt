In the Parsayan program, Hujjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the necessity of humility towards teachers and students, citing narrations from Imam Sadiq (AS) and anecdotes from scholars like Ayatollah Borujerdi and Akhund Khorasani. He highlighted that even at high academic levels, modesty and mutual respect must be maintained, as illustrated by stories of past scholars apologizing to their students. In the second part, the story "The Right of a Muslim" was narrated, depicting a dispute between Zayd and his wife Hanieh regarding their neighbor, Samura ibn Jundub. Hanieh complained about the neighbor's disrespect for their home's privacy, accusing Zayd of inaction, while Zayd advocated for tolerance and respecting the rights of fellow Muslims. After extensive discussion, Zayd proposed taking the dispute to the Prophet (PBUH) to ensure God's judgment was implemented. Samura, confident in his ownership of the tree, agreed to refer the matter to the Prophet. The story illustrates that even in disputes over personal rights, referring to a just judge is the most appropriate solution.
