In response to a listener's question, Professor Pishvayi reviewed the history of conflicts between Muslims and the Eastern Roman Empire. He referenced the battles of Mu'tah and Tabuk, emphasizing that while no direct combat occurred at Tabuk, the massive military movement secured safety and led to widespread tribal conversions to Islam. He also mentioned the Army of Usama, which was deployed to Rome after the Prophet's death with Abu Bakr's approval and returned victoriously. The discussion then shifted to the story of Zayd ibn Harithah, who was initially adopted by the Prophet and later married to Zaynab bint Jahsh. After the Quranic abolition of adoption and their subsequent divorce, the Prophet married Zaynab to demonstrate that marrying a former adopted son's ex-wife is permissible. These historical and jurisprudential points were presented in the Ma'aref radio program to educate the audience.
