The program begins with Quranic recitation and eulogies before transitioning to an analysis of a sermon by Imam Hussein.
Imam Hussein cites the Prophet's declaration that anyone who witnesses tyranny and fails to oppose it will incur divine wrath.
The term "Sultan" is interpreted broadly to mean any oppressive power, including today's global hegemony and Zionism.
A crucial point is that the duty to resist injustice is triggered not only when believers are targeted, but whenever any human being is oppressed.
Opposition to tyranny can be verbal or physical, yet remaining silent in the face of injustice is considered a grave sin.
The discussion concludes by emphasizing that God has the right to punish those who remain passive in the presence of oppressors.
Therefore, being "Husseinian" entails adhering to this divine command and standing against all forms of global tyranny and oppression.
