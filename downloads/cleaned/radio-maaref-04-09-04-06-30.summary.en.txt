The program begins with a Quranic interpretation stating that conflict and rivalry weaken both parties, depleting their power and prestige as warned by the Prophet.
It then references verses from Surah Al-Ma'idah to highlight the difference in the intensity of divine enmity between Jews and Christians, emphasizing the Quran's role in guidance.
The second part of the broadcast is dedicated to Fatimiyyah, offering condolences for the martyrdom of Hazrat Zahra (SA) and encouraging kindness based on the Ahl al-Bayt's example.
Historical reports for "this day" include the martyrdom of Ali Chitazadeh in Operation Mus'ab ibn Uqba and Imam Khomeini's speech on establishing the Basij force.
The passing of Jafar Baghchan, the founder of special education schools in Iran, is also commemorated in this segment.
The show concludes by inviting listeners to participate in a discussion about their sorrows and send voice messages.
This collection of topics combines religious interpretation, historical remembrances, and audience interaction within a radio format.
