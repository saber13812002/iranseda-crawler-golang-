The broadcast began with news coverage of school supply shopping and children's excitement for the new academic year. Reports on the Government Week ceremony at the Imam Reza shrine and the call for the National Prayer Conference were also presented. In the main segment, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the importance of compassion and praying for others, recounting stories of Ayatollah Behjat's kindness. Subsequently, Professor Vaidburi explained the Islamic rulings regarding sperm tests, outlining permissible methods for married individuals and emergency conditions for singles. A fatwa was also issued confirming the validity of congregational prayer if the imam recites a dhikr after the Fatiha. The program concluded with answers to other religious questions and an invitation to listen to the following segments.
