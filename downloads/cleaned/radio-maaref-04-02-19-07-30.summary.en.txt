The program began with religious supplications and a plea for the early appearance of Imam Zaman, followed by a discussion on the 1404 national slogan of "investment for production." The core of the talk focused on the importance of controlling anger and obeying divine laws across four domains: theoretical and practical monotheism, ethics, and lifestyle. Citing narrations from Imam Reza (AS), the speaker explained that anger at God and disobedience lead to losing divine satisfaction and potentially committing hidden disbelief. It was emphasized that anger causes individuals to lose self-awareness and spiritual value, while the solution lies in self-preservation and recognizing one's worth. The Prophet (PBUH) also identified controlling anger as a means to remove divine punishment and attain the rank of those who fast by day and worship by night. Finally, the most appropriate greeting to Imam Reza (AS) was defined as emulating his gentle character and managing anger in personal and social relationships.
