Ahmad Sanjari, an Iranian football veteran and AFC instructor, discussed the evolution of the sport in a radio interview. He highlighted the significant increase in player running distance from 5 to over 13 kilometers per match, attributing this shift to technological and scientific advancements in sports. Sanjari argued that despite Iran's high potential in talent, the football system is stagnating due to a lack of professional culture, credible academies, and unprofessional interference. He shared his memories of playing for the national team and Homa club, emphasizing the profound impact of coach Amir Abutalib on his development. The veteran expressed dissatisfaction with the current state of coaching and club management, calling for greater attention to veterans and the establishment of more professional structures for training future generations. Finally, he expressed gratitude to his family and the football community, stressing the need to shift from "working harder" to "working smarter" in the modern era.
