This program narrates the life of Ali Siyazi Shirazi based on the book "In the Ambush of the Red Rose," focusing on his mission to document Iraqi mortar shelling at the Iranian border. Despite the refusal of the unit commander to provide troops, Ali entered Iraqi territory with eight men and mapped the exact impact sites amidst intense crossfire. Upon his return, the corps commander, lacking cartographic expertise, accused Ali of fraud and treated him with insulting disrespect. Ali maintained his principled stance despite the psychological pressure, and his integrity was eventually vindicated with the support of the staff chief. Due to his resistance against corruption and his religious activities, Ali was removed from his position but continued his life through marriage and the support of his soldiers. After ranking first in an English language course, he was sent to the United States for specialized training in ballistic meteorology. The program concludes with excerpts from Imam Khomeini's speeches criticizing the misconduct of certain commanders and discussing the state of the war.
