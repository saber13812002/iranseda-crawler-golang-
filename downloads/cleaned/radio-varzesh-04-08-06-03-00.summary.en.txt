Refereeing expert Alireza Yazdani discussed the challenges facing Iranian football and refereeing in a radio interview. He explained why he ceased his collaboration with the Tehran Football Federation, citing a lack of practical support despite verbal promises. Yazdani identified financial motivations, the entry of non-footballers into team management, and superficial coaching courses as the root causes of Tehran's football decline. He emphasized the need to reduce the number of teams to improve quality and called for stricter oversight by the sports organization. Regarding refereeing, he blamed the delayed implementation of VAR and insufficient training infrastructure for the decline of Iranian referees in Asia. Yazdani argued that without structural reforms and professional management, it is impossible to restore the country's football and refereeing to their former glory.
