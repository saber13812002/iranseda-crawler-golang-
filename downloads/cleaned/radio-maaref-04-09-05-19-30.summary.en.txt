In this radio program, Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallah discussed the vice of mockery and its severe consequences in the afterlife, drawing from the book of Kafi. He outlined several strategies to avoid this vice, emphasizing the importance of gaining knowledge, strengthening reason through moral virtues, and avoiding arrogance and self-centeredness. Other recommendations included practicing sincerity, refraining from mockery for financial gain, and maintaining dignity. The professor stressed that true reliance on God (Tawakkul) is achieved by strictly adhering to the rules of Sharia, while remembering death serves as a primary deterrent. He also advised seeking forgiveness from those who have been mocked as a form of repentance and compensation. The program highlighted the need to protect religious and revolutionary values from being ridiculed in both virtual and real spaces. The key takeaway was that focusing on one's own flaws and eliminating ignorance naturally removes the motivation to mock others.
