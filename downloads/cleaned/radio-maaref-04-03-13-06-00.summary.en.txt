The program begins with a tafsir of verse 105 of Surah Al-Ma'idah by Ayatollah Javadi Amoli, emphasizing the necessity of correctly understanding the "nafs" and distinguishing it from the physical body. Using the metaphor of a flowing river, the commentator explains that while the material components of the body constantly change, the human's fixed essence (soul) remains constant and should not be confused with material tools. The discussion then addresses the distinction between "ethics" and "mysticism"; ethics, which involves avoiding sins and performing duties, is considered the lesser jihad, whereas practical mysticism, aimed at witnessing hidden truths, is the greater jihad. To reach these levels, one must purify the self through adherence to revelation to remove internal obstacles, thereby achieving spiritual insight in both sleep and wakefulness. Finally, referencing Nahj al-Balagha and narrations, the stages of spiritual progression are described, from supplication to silence and hearing the divine word, concluding that the soul of a true mystic is suspended in the presence of the Divine.
