The radio program on the Ma'aref network began by congratulating the birth of Hazrat Fatima Masumeh (SA) and International Girl's Day, marking the start of the Ten Days of Karamat under the slogan "Love of Iran, Imam Reza." Officials from the Astan Quds Razavi announced extensive religious and cultural programs in Mashhad and Qom, including services for millions of pilgrims and the participation of public groups in approximately 50,000 mosques and religious sites. The closing ceremony of the 4th International Imam Reza Media Festival was highlighted, noting the selection of 72 winners and a significant increase in the participation of women and youth in producing Reza-related content. In the news section, the Maraji' of Taqlid offered condolences for the explosion at Shahid Rajaee Port, emphasizing the need to support the victims. The first International Human Rights Conference on the Eastern Strategy was held in Tehran with representatives from 22 countries, where attendees stressed the need to end the genocide in Gaza and support Palestine. The Saudi Ministry of Hajj announced that the "Nusuk" smart card is now mandatory for pilgrims to facilitate identification and services in holy cities. Finally, the book "Above the Sky," documenting the life and martyrdom of pilot Akbar Gharbani Shiroudi, reached its eighth edition on the 44th anniversary of his martyrdom.
