In this radio program, a narration from the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) is discussed, stating that believers characterized by humility, submission, and frequent remembrance of God will enter Paradise before others. This serves as a consolation for those who, due to poverty, jihad, or the pursuit of knowledge, have missed out on material wealth and may face regret or Satan's whispers. The Prophet (PBUH) confirms to Abu Dharr that these individuals, having no account of wealth or injustice, will swiftly pass through the crowd on Judgment Day to enter Paradise. The discussion then shifts to the jurisprudential rulings of Hajj, specifically the timing of moving from Mina to Arafat for the elderly and the sick. The program also analyzes the chains of narration and the reliability of various sources in fiqh books such as Wasail al-Shia. The primary goal is to encourage listeners to remain patient through religious hardships and to trust in God's special reward for sincere worshippers.
