In this radio program, experts discussed the impact of inflation on purchasing power and related jurisprudential issues.
It was explained that the devaluation of the national currency can affect the calculation of debts, mahr, and khums, with some jurists advocating for inflation compensation in long-term payments.
The difference between general and sectoral inflation was clarified, emphasizing that the specific sector's inflation rate must be considered to determine real profit in transactions.
In the second part, the status of cryptocurrencies was examined, noting that while they may be recognized as money in the future, they are currently traded primarily as assets.
Central banks' resistance to cryptocurrencies was explained due to the need for monetary policy control and preventing decentralized money creation.
Finally, tributes were paid to martyrs and the Iranian national wrestling team on the occasion of Sacred Defense Week and their world championship victory.
The program concluded with announcements for upcoming segments, including Quranic questions and late-night news.
