This radio program emphasizes the critical need to cultivate the "nafs amara" (the commanding self) and specifically its linguistic expression.
The core argument is that the tongue at this level is inherently rebellious, associated with disbelief, polytheism, and hypocrisy.
It is impossible to defend monotheism, moral virtues, or revolutionary values using this impure and untrained tongue.
An untrained tongue serves as a tool for Satan and poses a significant threat to religious and national beliefs.
The program distinguishes sharply between the tongue of the nafs amara and the refined tongue governed by reason.
Imam Ali (AS) described the tongue of the nafs amara as "rebellious," identifying it as the root of many sins.
Therefore, monitoring and training the tongue is a prerequisite for achieving spiritual virtues and effectively defending Islam.
