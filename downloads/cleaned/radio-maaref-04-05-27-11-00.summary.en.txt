The President expressed gratitude to the organizers of the Arbaeen ceremony, noting a four percent increase in domestic and foreign pilgrims this year. Reports indicated a forty percent decrease in deaths due to accidents and illnesses compared to the previous year. The head of the central Arbaeen committee evaluated the event as one of the most magnificent in recent years, despite the extreme heat. Simultaneously, a thousand walking caravans with a capacity of one hundred thousand people are en route to Mashhad for the Imam Reza pilgrimage. It is predicted that more than eight million pilgrims will visit Mashhad during the last ten days, supported by four hundred service tents along the five main routes. Ayatollah Javadi Amoli emphasized the role of martyrs in awakening society and strengthening Iran's power during a meeting with the Minister of Education. The body of the renowned Iranian miniature painter, Mahmoud Farshchian, was buried in Isfahan. Additionally, the Supreme Leader's commentary on the book "Pasid-e Pesar-e Khak" will be released at a national literature event in Qazvin.
