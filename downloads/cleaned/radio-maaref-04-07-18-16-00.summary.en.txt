In the radio program "Historical Perseman," Dr. Sadeghi addressed listeners' questions regarding key events in Islamic history. He confirmed the birth of Imam Ali (AS) in the Kaaba, citing both Shia and Sunni sources, and attributed its omission in some Sunni histories to selection bias or denial. Regarding the migration, the expert clarified that no prominent Muslims remained in Mecca, as staying behind was considered a denial of faith. He also confirmed that Imam Hasan Askari (AS) and Ja'far al-Kazzab shared the same mother, noting that the title "Askari" refers to a military garrison rather than sterility. The program detailed the betrayal of the judge Shurayh during the martyrdom of Muslim ibn Aqil, while firmly rejecting the unfounded claim that he issued a fatwa for Imam Hussein's (AS) death. Finally, Dr. Sadeghi explained that the Islamization of Iran was a gradual process and that exaggerated casualty figures from the conquest era are historically unreliable.
