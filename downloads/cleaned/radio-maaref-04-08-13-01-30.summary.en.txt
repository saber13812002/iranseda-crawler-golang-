In the first segment, Hujjat al-Islam Rostamnejad emphasized the decisive role of human actions in shaping one's destiny and ultimate fate. Citing Quranic verses, he explained that repeated minor sins can lead individuals to commit major offenses, such as fleeing from the battlefield. He also noted that persisting in wicked deeds obscures the innate light of the soul, blinding the heart to the truth. The second segment featured the memoirs of prisoner of war Dideban Kababiani from the Iran-Iraq War. He recounted the severe hardships in Ba'athist camps, including frequent whippings and physical torture by Iraqi guards. Despite these agonizing experiences, the prisoners of war maintained their faith and did not deviate from the path of truth. The Supreme Leader described these memoirs as a spiritual asset for the nation and a symbol of Islamic resistance.
