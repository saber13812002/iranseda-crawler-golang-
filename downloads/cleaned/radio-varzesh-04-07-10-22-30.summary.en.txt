In this interview, Rahim Yusefi, a veteran Persepolis player, recounts his experiences during the 1980s. He highlights his historic debut against Australia, where he entered the game as a substitute wearing the number 6 jersey. Yusefi also addresses financial disputes regarding contract checks with club officials during that era. He praises the rigorous physical training at Topeh Davoodi and the role of coaches like Salehniati in maintaining the team's consecutive championships. The former player explains that he rejected a transfer offer from Esteghlal to preserve his legacy and respect for Persepolis. He expresses regret over being overlooked by the national team in 1984 despite being initially selected. Finally, Yusefi criticizes the current state of Iranian football, emphasizing the need to prioritize local talent and youth prospects over relying exclusively on foreign players.
