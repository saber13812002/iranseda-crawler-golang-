In this radio program, Ayatollah Mazarari emphasizes the importance of full presence and focus during the night prayer, or Tahajjud. He describes prayer not merely as a ritual, but as a loving dialogue and intimate connection with God that leads to spiritual proximity. According to narrations cited, for those who are deeply absorbed in it, the night prayer is more valuable than the entire world and everything within it. This state of spiritual manifestation renders worldly pleasures insignificant and illuminates the heart with divine light. The speaker warns that the primary goal of prayer should not be simply avoiding hell or seeking paradise, but rather discovering truth and cultivating love for God. The program also included a segment from Ayatollah Sistani's jurisprudence class, discussing the legal rulings regarding the repair or sale of ruined endowments while preserving their charitable nature.
