The news segment of Radio Ma'arif reported that the final stoning of the Jamarat and the return of Iranian pilgrims, starting from the 21st of Khordad, were completed early this year without heatstroke due to adjusted schedules. Concurrently, the 10-kilometer Qadir Festival announced the establishment of 2,400 people's moqabs and a theme park hosting one million Tehran children, with services distributed across a five-kilometer zone. The "Million Bites" campaign was launched under the slogan "Every Family a Moqab," allowing participation via online registration or SMS. The "Haram to Haram" walking caravan also arrived in Karbala on the occasion of Arbaeen after a 72-day journey from Mashhad. The 5th International Conference on Fiqh, Law, and Artificial Intelligence is scheduled to be held in Tehran by the end of the year to address the jurisprudential implications of AI. Culturally, a course was held in Qom to introduce 80 university students to the seminary's academic system and lifestyle. Additionally, 65 student couples were married at Imam Hossein University to promote simple and affordable marriages. Finally, the "Man Ghadirim" program featured experts discussing the significance of the 13th of Dhu al-Hijjah and analyzing the sermon of Ghadir from historical sources.
