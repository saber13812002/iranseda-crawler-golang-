The radio program "Kimia," based on Imam Sajjad's (PBUH) treatise on rights, describes lying as a termite that erodes trust and family peace from within.
It is emphasized that even joking lies are sinful and stem from a lack of personal character rather than environmental conditions.
In management, honesty is presented as a key principle because a dishonest manager loses public trust even if they achieve significant results.
A narration from the Prophet (PBUH) illustrates that the virtue of truthfulness saved a non-Muslim from execution and led him to Islam.
In the "Zamzam Ahkam" section, various religious questions from listeners were answered.
Followers of the Supreme Leader are permitted to pay the fasting expiation at the current market price when due, without needing to store food.
According to Ayatollah Sistani, using the names of the Infallibles (PBUH) to decorate underwear is severely disliked.
Followers of Imam Khomeini (RA) can consult local clergymen or audio files of rulings to answer their religious questions.
