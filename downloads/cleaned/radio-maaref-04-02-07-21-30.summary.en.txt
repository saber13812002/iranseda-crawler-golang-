This program analyzes Quranic verses on monasticism, distinguishing between monasticism as a spiritual rank and as a profession of social isolation.
The Quran praises monasticism defined by the fear of God and adherence to divine laws within society, rather than seclusion in monasteries.
The occasion of revelation for the verse "Do not forbid" relates to companions who imposed extreme asceticism on themselves, forbidding God's lawful provisions.
The Prophet (PBUH) recited this verse to stop them from excess, emphasizing that a reasonable and moderate life is divinely approved.
Transgressing God's limits can mean either committing sins or abandoning permissible acts under the guise of piety.
Imam Ali (AS) describes the world not as a devil, but as an honest mirror that reveals both beauty and ugliness without concealment.
Therefore, renouncing the world means distancing oneself from unlawful pleasures while focusing on worship among people, not severing ties with creation.
