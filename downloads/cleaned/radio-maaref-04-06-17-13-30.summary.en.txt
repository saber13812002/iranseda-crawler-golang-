In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Faghi emphasizes the importance of balancing worldly and otherworldly requests in prayers, citing Quranic verses. He explains that hardships serve as divine tools to strengthen the human connection with God and remove spiritual negligence. Referring to Ayatollah Behjat's views, Faghi asserts that God ultimately manages all affairs, and humans must rely on divine will through trust and supplication. In response to listener queries, he advises improving family relationships by reducing unnecessary sensitivities and engaging in constructive dialogue during calm moments. He also highlights the devil's role in creating family discord and stresses the necessity of avoiding major sins to maintain household peace. Finally, marking the Shahrivar Week, the program discusses the Pahlavi regime's divisive tactics that labeled revolutionaries as extremists or moderates. This historical analysis illustrates how such strategies led to the massacre of September 8, 1978, and notes that enemies still use similar political labeling to sow discord today.
