In this narrative, Dalham, the wife of Zuhair ibn Qayn, recounts the final moments of her husband's life in the caravan of Imam Hussein (PBUH). Zuhair, who was deeply anxious upon hearing Hussein's name, decided to join him after receiving the Imam's summons, despite his inner fears. He dismantled his tent and told Dalham that she was free to leave so she would not be implicated in his actions. Dalham reminded him of a past conversation with Salman al-Farisi about his duty to the Prophet's grandson, which solidified Zuhair's resolve. With courage, Zuhair moved toward Hussein's camp, and Dalham spoke of his sacrifice with pride. The second part of the program emphasizes the value of time by sharing stories of great scholars who meticulously utilized every moment of their lives. It advises listeners to avoid wasting time and to dedicate their lives to worship and knowledge through careful planning.
