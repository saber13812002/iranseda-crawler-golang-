In this radio program, two jurists discussed the jurisprudential boundaries of a father killing his child in Islam.
Citing Ayatollah Sadr, the guests clarified that narrations suggesting a father's absolute authority over his child are not independent legal rulings but rather carry moral and educational significance.
It was explained that Islam combats pre-Islamic beliefs of ownership over humans, and while a father is exempt from qisas (retaliation) based on specific narrations, the act remains a major sin and serious crime.
Consequently, a father who kills his child faces severe discretionary punishment, disqualification from inheritance, and the obligation to pay expiation.
The program then transitioned to reciting and translating verses from Surah Qasas, narrating the story of Moses (PBUH) and Shu'ayb (PBUH).
This Quranic section emphasized the importance of trustworthiness, hard work, and honoring commitments in social and family relationships.
The broadcast also honored the martyrdom of General Mohammad Bagheri, his wife, and their daughter during the enemy's attack.
Finally, listeners were encouraged to participate in the Quran recitation ceremony and the national conference on religion and new media by sending text messages.
