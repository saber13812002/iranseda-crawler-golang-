The Radio Ma'aref program introduced the "Ayat Noor" contest, asking listeners to submit their answers to a Quranic interpretation question by 10 PM. The question, based on the Supreme Leader's teachings, asked about the key factor for Muslim unity according to the second verse of Surah Al-Ma'idah. Military personnel and their families were invited to participate via a dedicated SMS number for the Army's Political Organization, with a chance to win a three-million-rial gift card. The winners of this contest will be announced tomorrow at 8 AM, following the national news, on the radio and its Eitaa channel. The host bid farewell to the audience, offering prayers for their spiritual well-being and the acceptance of their worship. The program then featured a recitation of the sixth Juz of the Holy Quran by Qari Karim Mansouri. This recitation included verses from Surah Al-Ma'idah discussing division, the story of Jesus, and purification rules. The broadcast concluded with verses from Surah Al-Baqarah regarding God's promises to believers and the fate of disbelievers.
