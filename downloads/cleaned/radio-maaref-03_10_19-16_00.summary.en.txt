In the radio program "Persman-e Ateghadi," Professor Mohammadi-Sharoudi addressed listeners' questions regarding the spiritual benefits of the Infallibles during their physical presence versus their occultation. He refuted the notion that modern believers are deprived of divine grace, emphasizing that today's access to a vast cultural heritage results in superior faith and awareness compared to past eras. Regarding the Day of Judgment, he explained that the extended duration of judgment for sinners is due to the difficulty of the task in that realm, while miracles remain exceptional occurrences. The concept of the world as a place of retribution was rejected in its absolute form, with tribulations for believers often serving educational and elevating purposes. Finally, he clarified that harboring resentment is a personal spiritual sin distinct from the operational sin committed by the offender.
