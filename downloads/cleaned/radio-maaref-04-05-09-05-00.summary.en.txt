This program interprets Quranic verses to define the characteristics of "servants of God," emphasizing the avoidance of shirk, murder, moral corruption, and participation in environments of coercion and triviality.
The concept of "coercion" is explained as encompassing lies, drugs, and sins that lead to human regression and downfall.
The discussion highlights that pious individuals avoid corrupt gatherings and select their friends based on strict ethical and religious criteria.
To illustrate this virtue, the miraculous story of Hajj-Agha Ashghar-Jahbar is recounted, showing how his chastity saved him from death and led to the acceptance of his prayers.
The second part of the program addresses the jurisprudential issue of the judge's knowledge, asserting that it must be derived from logical deduction rather than rumors or baseless news.
A distinction is made between the judge's personal knowledge and the spread of unverified information in social media, referring to such spreaders as "al-Marjifun."
The overall conclusion underscores the necessity of maintaining chastity, avoiding harmful environments, and relying on sound, deductive knowledge in religious and social judgments.
