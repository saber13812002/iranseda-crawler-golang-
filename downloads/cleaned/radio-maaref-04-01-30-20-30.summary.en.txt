The program begins with a dream interpretation of the Noor prayer, linking it to eternal servitude to God. It then details the assassination of Zainab, a student suffocated by the MEK, and her mother's long struggle for justice. The main segment features the memoirs of Sayyid Mahmoud Pourmousavi, a veteran of the Iran-Iraq war. He recounts his capture during the Khorramshahr operation and his transfer to the Ardooba camp. Pourmousavi highlights the vital role of the spiritual council in managing the camp and saving prisoners from disease. The show concludes by introducing the book "Salam be Ibrahime" and emphasizing the importance of preserving the legacy of these freedom fighters.
