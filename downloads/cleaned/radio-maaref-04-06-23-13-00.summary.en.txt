This radio program outlines five economic recommendations from the Prophet of Islam to prevent poverty and lack of planning. The first recommendation is "Tadbir," or careful planning before taking any action, to avoid regret and financial loss. The second point emphasizes the necessity of "effort and hard work," clarifying that true reliance on God is not an excuse for laziness. The third recommendation is "knowledge and awareness," advising individuals not to enter professions for which they lack the necessary expertise or capability. Avoiding "waste and squandering resources" is the fourth point, citing Quranic verses that label wasteful people as brothers of Satan. Finally, "charity and distribution" are recommended, specifically through paying Khums and Zakat, and offering loans instead of alms to help regulate wealth and assist those in need.
