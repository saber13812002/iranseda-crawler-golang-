The radio program "Religion and Life" began by congratulating the birth of Imam Reza (PBUH) before narrating the historical story of Sayyid Hossein Razavi Qomi. Sayyid Hossein, who had traveled to Kashmir to preach religion, became acquainted with Ghazal Sadat, an artistic woman of noble lineage who attended his school disguised as a man. After Ghazal suddenly disappeared, the heartbroken Sayyid Hossein, who had fallen in love with her, retreated to a secluded spot to express his feelings. His close friend, Habib, went to the court of Sultan Zain-ul-Abidin to resolve the issue, presenting the proposal in the presence of Lady Gulab. The Sultan and Lady Gulab emphasized that Ghazal Sadat must make her own decision regarding her future and the man she would spend her life with. Lady Gulab stated that she would take Ghazal to her home to deliver the marriage proposal and convey her final answer to Sayyid Hossein.
