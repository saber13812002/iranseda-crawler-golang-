The program explains that true monotheism and complete jurisprudence involve perceiving humans as creatures whose reins are held by God, devoid of independent agency.
Relying on other people to meet one's needs or solve problems is contrary to the principle of Tawhid, which demands absolute trust in God.
Satan specifically targets those on the path of spiritual perfection by inducing arrogance and a sense of superiority to divert them from the truth.
The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) stated that a truly knowledgeable person sees others as helpless animals while also viewing themselves as insignificant and dependent on God.
The jurisprudential segment discusses the rule of "sharing in obligation" and whether it applies equally to men and women.
Contemporary scholars such as Allameh Bجنورdi and the author of "Anwar al-Faqih" have written treatises on this specific rule.
It is argued that using verses about the finality of prophethood to prove the sharing of obligations is logically and jurisprudentially incorrect.
Additionally, it is clarified that oral addresses, if considered as real propositions, include absent and future individuals, but this is distinct from the rule of sharing obligations.
