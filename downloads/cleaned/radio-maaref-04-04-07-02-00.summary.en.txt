The program began with a literary narrative depicting Zahra's profound loneliness and grief in the face of her husband's indifference. Subsequently, Hojjat-ol-Islam Vahidi emphasized the critical importance of safeguarding public funds, citing a hadith that condemns the misappropriation of communal wealth. He illustrated this principle by recounting how prominent religious leaders, including Imam Khomeini, strictly avoided using public funds for personal expenses such as purchasing telephones or printing their own religious texts. Imam Khomeini relied on public donations for his telephone and refused to use public funds for his legal opinions, ensuring that every expense was justified. Similarly, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli declined to eat public food while visiting the Supreme Leader, insisting on using his personal funds to respect the sanctity of public resources. Ayatollah Moshkini also demonstrated this integrity by seeking permission before using an official vehicle for his family's transportation. The episode concluded with religious music and supplications, urging listeners to remain vigilant in protecting public assets and adhering to divine commands.
