In this radio program, the concept of "Bazl" is explained as a fundamental trait of Shias and believers.
Bazl means spending whatever one possesses, such as life, wealth, reputation, pen, and art, for the sake of religion and the Ahl al-Bayt.
Social popularity and credibility can also be used as tools to help the needy and preserve human dignity in the path of religion.
The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) and Imam Ali (AS) emphasized maintaining this trait, describing Shias as people of Bazl.
Marriage, hijab, and avoiding sins are also considered individual forms of Bazl for preserving society and religion.
The uprising of Imam Hussein (AS) is the greatest example of sacrificing one's life, which sparked social awakenings and subsequent uprisings in Islamic history.
Finally, listeners are invited to spend for the sake of God's religion to the extent of their individual capabilities through any possible means.
