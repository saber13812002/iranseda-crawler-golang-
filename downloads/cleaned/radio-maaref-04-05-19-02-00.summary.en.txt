The program began by recounting the charitable deeds and discreet support for the needy provided by scholars like Ayatollah Marashi Najafi and Akhund Khorasani. The discussion then shifted to analyzing the recent imposed war and the conflicting claims of victory from both sides. Dr. Khoshchasm argued that Iran emerged as the victor because the enemy failed to achieve its primary goals of regime change and national disintegration. He refuted claims that bunker-buster bombs significantly damaged Iran's nuclear facilities, emphasizing the resilience of the country's defensive infrastructure. The analyst noted a tangible shift in public opinion, with increased demands for nuclear weapons emerging as a direct consequence of the conflict. Furthermore, he highlighted Iran's missile capabilities, pointing to improved accuracy and impact in the final days of the war compared to the initial phase. The failure of the adversary to incite internal unrest or instability was identified as the key factor behind the collapse of their broader destruction plans.
