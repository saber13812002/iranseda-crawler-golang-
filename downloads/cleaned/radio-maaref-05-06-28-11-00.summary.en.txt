The 313,000-person "Janfada" (self-sacrificers) exercise was held in Tehran with over 400,000 attendees, symbolizing national loyalty and deterrence under the country's leadership. The President praised this presence as a source of strength, urging citizens to support the government by conserving energy and boosting domestic production. The Friday prayer leader emphasized the crucial role of families in maintaining social cohesion and reaffirming their pledge to the revolution's ideals. In the cultural sector, the first novel titled "Day of the Attack" was released, depicting a security narrative involving electronic warfare and youth cooperation with armed forces. The broadcast also featured a tribute to the martyrdom of General Mehdiannafar, a commander of the IRGC Aerospace Force, and the resilience of his family. The "Jashn-e Atfah" charity campaign to provide school supplies for one million students will continue until mid-September. Finally, religious rulings were provided regarding book copyright, oath expiation, and the permissibility of machine slaughter.
