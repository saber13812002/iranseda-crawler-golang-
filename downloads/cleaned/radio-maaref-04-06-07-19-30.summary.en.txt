This radio program provided religious rulings in response to audience questions. According to Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi, buying gold for a wife's adornment is not subject to Khums, whereas coins become liable for Khums after a full year. It was clarified that proper Tajweed is not mandatory in prayer, and crying due to material issues or illness does not invalidate the prayer. Reserving a spot in the mosque for prayer is permissible as long as it does not obstruct other worshippers. The second segment condemned backbiting and slander against fellow believers, citing Quranic verses and prophetic traditions. The Prophet and Imam Kazim emphasized that one must not listen to gossip and should refute any false accusations heard. The program concluded by urging listeners to protect the honor of others and strictly avoid engaging in backbiting.
