The radio program "Narrative of Love" began by commemorating the birth of Imam Hassan (PBUH) and honoring veterans and freedom fighters. The main segment featured the mother of martyr Ghasem Hossein Afshordi, known as Hassan Bagheri, who shared memories of hosting military commanders during the war. She recounted her son's simple, long-distance marriage in Ahvaz and their brief one-and-a-half-year life together under difficult wartime conditions. The mother also described the birth of her granddaughter in her son's absence and the logistical efforts to send baby supplies via plane. She emphasized the profound patience and sacrifice of martyrs' families, noting her son was martyred just 19 days after his daughter's birth. The program concluded with an analysis of the significant defeat of Western civilization and the Zionist regime in Gaza. The host described the massacre of thousands of Palestinian children as a gross violation of human rights and a moral disgrace for the West. Despite these atrocities, he stressed the enduring strength and resilience of the resistance front against US and Israeli aggression.
