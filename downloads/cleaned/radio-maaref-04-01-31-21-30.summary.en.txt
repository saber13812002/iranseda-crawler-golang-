This radio program explores the difference in perception between materialists and spiritual seekers regarding the truths of existence. The Quran indicates that those whose knowledge is limited to sensory experiences and nature are incapable of understanding divine knowledge, advising Muslims to treat them with "kindly forbearance" without over-investing in their guidance. The core discussion focuses on the divine unity described in the Quran as "Al-Wahid Al-Qahhar," which signifies the infinite nature of God rather than mere numerical oneness. This specific type of unity makes the hypothesis of a second god inherently impossible, as God leaves no room for any other entity. Imam Ali (AS) in Nahj al-Balagha uses logical arguments to prove that God's unity transcends material numbers and limits, being absolute and indivisible. Consequently, true understanding of Tawhid involves recognizing this infinity, which liberates humans from polytheism and dependence on anything other than God.
