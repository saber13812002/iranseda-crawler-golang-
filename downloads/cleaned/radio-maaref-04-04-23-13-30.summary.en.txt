In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Seyyed Ahmad Faghi discussed the role of the Salawat prayer in divine education and monotheistic ethics. He emphasized that reciting Salawat is not merely for the forgiveness of sins but serves to create a spiritual bond with the Prophet (PBUH), akin to a coal catching fire from another. Using the metaphor of the sea, he explained the different levels of attention to the meaning of Salawat, ranging from mere verbal recitation to deep spiritual connection. In the Q&A section, he addressed the issue of lack of presence of mind during prayer, noting that it requires constant control of the imagination and mindfulness throughout the day. He also warned that Satan often induces doubt about one's intentions in good deeds to prevent action, advising believers to act promptly to avoid falling into this trap. The importance of "time awareness" was highlighted as a key to success in religious and social actions, with historical examples such as the Tawwabun uprising and the Islamic Revolution. The program concluded by encouraging listeners to recognize the right moment for action to ensure its effectiveness.
