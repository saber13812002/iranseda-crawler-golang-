Ali Reza Bahadori, a prominent Iranian volleyball setter, shared his memories of playing for Paykan and Pegah Urmia in a radio interview. He expressed deep resentment over his unjust exclusion from the national team in 2006, citing a personal conflict with a Paykan board member regarding a financial dispute. Bahadori emphasized that this individual's decision, rather than the club's official stance, kept him out of the national team during his physical peak and best professional years. Despite this, he praised Paykan as a principled and professional club that fulfilled its duties correctly. After this long hiatus, Hossein Madani invited him back to the national team, leading to his selection as the Most Valuable Player in Asia in 2018. Now 47 years old, Bahadori intends to continue playing professionally until he is 50, following the advice of veteran players. He also stressed the importance of integrity, professional ethics, and avoiding bias in sports relationships.
