In the first segment, Professor Sedaghi emphasized the educational importance of "waiting for the Savior" as a generator of righteous action and active motivation in all aspects of life. He urged listeners to manifest their hope for the reappearance in their daily behavior and to prepare themselves to serve as agents in the future government. Subsequently, Dr. Jafari addressed a listener's question regarding the burial history of Hazrat Masoumeh (SA) in Qom and the identity of the women interred in the adjacent crypts. Citing historical sources, he detailed the discovery of three intact coffins during the shrine's renovation in the Naseri era and correlated them with historical records. The final segment discussed a narration from Imam Hussein (AS) about the killing of prophets by the Children of Israel and the ultimate fate of oppressors. This narration was interpreted as a warning to modern oppressors, affirming the divine promise of their humiliation and defeat.
