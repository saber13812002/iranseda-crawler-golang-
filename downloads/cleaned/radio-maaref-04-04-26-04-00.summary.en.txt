Ayatollah Saffaee Bushehri discussed the fundamental characteristics of "the worshippers of the Most Merciful" in the "Makarem Khoban" radio program. He categorized people into three groups: those with divine behavior, those with hidden polytheism due to corrupted inner states, and those with satanic actions. A key point was the distinction between explicit and hidden shirk, where the latter involves forgetting God after a crisis is resolved and attributing independent power to material causes. The speaker emphasized that all material matters, such as medicine and nature, are merely divine instruments and should not be viewed as independent entities. The moral traits of true worshippers include purity, avoidance of corruption, and an otherworldly perspective focused on the afterlife. He warned that moral corruption leads to severe consequences, including early death and disgrace in this world and punishment in the hereafter. Finally, he prayed for divine protection against takfiri groups and moral decay, urging listeners to maintain a balanced and God-centered view of life.
