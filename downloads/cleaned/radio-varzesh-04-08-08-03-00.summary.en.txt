Ali Abadi, a Greco-Roman wrestling champion, shared his memories of winning a silver medal at the 1972 Munich Olympics in a radio interview. He described the harsh conditions of that era, including the lack of a coach, limited facilities, and extreme weight cutting using boiled water and sugar. Emphasizing the fundamental differences between his time and the present, Abadi called for officials to show more appreciation and attention to past champions who achieved success under difficult circumstances. He also noted the resurgence of Iranian Greco-Roman wrestling in the London and Paris Olympics, attributing this success to proper coaching and support. In the final segment of the program, Mehdi Rostami interpreted the first sermon of Nahj al-Balagha, explaining Imam Ali’s (AS) intellectual system regarding the knowledge of God and sincerity. The discussion highlighted the importance of preserving sports heritage and honoring veteran athletes.
