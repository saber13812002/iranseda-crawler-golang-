At the beginning of the program, a spiritual advisor sternly emphasizes that parental violence, anger, and harsh language suppress children's innate talents and create spiritual crimes within the home. He recommends treating children with calmness and respect, and suggests visiting Imam Reza (AS) to directly communicate with him for the cure of spiritual ailments like anger. The second part of the program features an audio narration from the book "Galaxy of Nothingness," detailing the life of Seyyed Ali Qazi Tabatabai during his stay in Najaf. He meets Sheikh Muhammad Bahari, who informs him that his time in Najaf is ending and he must continue his spiritual training under Seyyed Ahmad Karbala'i. After hearing Seyyed Ahmad's moving supplications in a mosque, Seyyed Jamal al-Din is guided by his teacher, Sheikh Ali Muhammad Najaf-Abadi, to seek Seyyed Ahmad's guidance. Ultimately, Seyyed Ahmad introduces Seyyed Jamal al-Din to Seyyed Ali Qazi, marking the beginning of a profound spiritual friendship and journey between these two figures.
