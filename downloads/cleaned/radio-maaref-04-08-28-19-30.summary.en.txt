The program began with a prayer for rain and divine mercy, followed by an introduction to the evening's radio schedule.
The main segment featured a discussion by Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallah on the verbal vice of mockery in the "Ganj-e Sa'adat" program.
Professor Fallah described mockery as a sin, an injustice, and a social illness rooted in human ego that hinders spiritual growth.
The scope of mockery extends from individuals and families to groups and even religious sanctities, and is prevalent in cyberspace.
Citing a verse from Surah Al-Hujurat, mocking others and using derogatory titles after embracing faith is deemed haram and characteristic of the wicked.
Imam Sajjad (AS) advised in a narration that one should respect others based on their faith or avoidance of sin, rather than mocking them.
Finally, it was emphasized that refraining from mockery and seeking repentance are keys to mental peace, reducing enemies, and increasing friends.
