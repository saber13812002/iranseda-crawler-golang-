The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) provided comprehensive teachings that guide human growth from infancy to spiritual perfection.
The core lesson is the clear distinction between the ultimate goal of spiritual growth and the material means of life.
Treating material wealth as an end rather than a means leads to internal corruption, arrogance, and societal decay.
Imam Sadiq (AS) attributed visible corruption to internal rot caused by greed, arrogance, and unrealistic long-term fantasies.
Separating ethics from economics reduces humans to predatory beings who view others merely as competitors.
Dog-like competition for power and wealth destroys humanity and exacerbates poverty and inequality within society.
To prevent this crisis, one must understand the true value of the world, avoid consumption excess, and integrate ethics into all aspects of life.
Finally, ensuring the timely supply of essential goods and focusing on production are practical necessities for economic stability.
