The news segment reported that over 7,000 people registered for the 1405 Hajj and that the number of active mosques in Iran is expected to increase significantly.
The program featured the testimony of Amir Eskari, a former POW captured in Shalamcheh in 1986, who described the harsh conditions in Iraqi camps.
He recounted the martyrdom of his fellow prisoner, Hassan Agha, who was shot by Ba'athist guards while being carried to the wire fence.
A story by Allameh Jafari about the bravery of Martyr Nabav Safavi during a pilgrimage to Karbala was also shared.
Hojjat al-Islam Nadem emphasized the importance of intention and foresight in life choices, citing the steadfastness of the freed POWs as a model for achieving spiritual success.
The broadcast concluded with a call for solidarity with the people of Gaza and a strong criticism of negotiations with the United States as detrimental to national interests.
Listeners were invited to share their own memories from the Sacred Defense era via text message.
