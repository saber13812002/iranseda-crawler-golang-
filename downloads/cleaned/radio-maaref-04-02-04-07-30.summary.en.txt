The program begins by commemorating the martyrdom of Imam Jafar Sadiq (AS) and explains that the Shia school of thought is known as "Ja'fari" due to the flourishing of religious knowledge during his time. The second segment introduces Martyr Mehdi Bagheri, the commander of the 31st Azar Division, highlighting his virtues such as silence, action-oriented behavior, and steadfastness during the Badr Operation. Subsequently, Hujjat al-Islam Falahati discusses the vice of "anger" (Ghadab), citing a narration from Imam Sadiq to distinguish between true Shias and false claimants. The Imam defines a true Ja'fari Shia as one who controls their tongue, fears God, and abstains from worldly pleasures, whereas false claimants exacerbate enmity through anger and provocation. The core message emphasizes that true Shias must avoid anger and control their behavior to steer clear of moral vices and remain faithful to the teachings of the Ahl al-Bayt. This approach not only preserves personal dignity but also prevents unnecessary provocations that harm the religious community. Finally, the program urges listeners to emulate the Imams' conduct and avoid moral flaws to become genuine followers of the Ahl al-Bayt.
