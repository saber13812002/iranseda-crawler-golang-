The radio program "Zamzam Ahkam" addressed various religious inquiries from its listeners. Regarding the copying of textbooks, it was stated that respecting intellectual property rights is obligatory out of caution. The program clarified that reciting the Takbirat after prayer does not conflict with reciting the Tasbihat of Hazrat Zahra (SA), and both are recommended. The ruling on the expiation for breaking an oath not to commit a sin due to necessity depends on the severity of the coercion and the necessity of the act. Consuming turtle eggs for medical treatment is only permissible in cases of absolute necessity when no halal alternative exists, and only to the extent required. Machine slaughter of animals is considered halal provided five specific conditions are met, including the operator being Muslim and the animal facing the Qibla. It was explained that personal gold and silver used for adornment are exempt from Khums unless intended for trade or investment. Finally, the program emphasized that creating intentional barriers for Wudu or Ghusl, such as nail polish or artificial nails, is impermissible, and if they cannot be removed, the method of Wudu al-Jabirah must be used.
