The program began with religious invocations and prayers before transitioning to a discussion on the virtues of humility and obedience to the Imams.
The speaker highlighted the importance of following Imam Baqir's (AS) commands by citing the story of Muhammad ibn Muslim.
In this narrative, Muhammad ibn Muslim changed his profession from trading to milling at the Imam's order to serve as a model of humility.
Humility was presented as the primary factor for nobility and piety, emphasizing its role as the foundation for spiritual growth and a connection with God.
The broadcast concluded with prayers for the reappearance of Imam Mahdi (AJ) and a request for martyrdom in his path, stressing the necessity of following the path of the Infallibles.
