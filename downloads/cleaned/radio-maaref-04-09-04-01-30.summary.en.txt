In this program, Hojjat al-Islam Morteza Agha Tehrani emphasized the importance of emulating Lady Zahra (SA) by strengthening family bonds, respecting the Ahl al-Bayt, and practicing the Quran rather than relying on superficial appearances or foreign languages. He urged Muslims to maintain their religious and moral identity to be an ornament to society, advising them not to feel ashamed of their faith. The second part of the program featured the memoirs of war prisoners, where Mr. Nakhaee described the unbearable torture in Iraqi camps and the heroic behavior of prisoners like Abutrabi in maintaining the morale of their group. These accounts demonstrated that the freed prisoners stood firm against the enemy's abuse through steadfastness and faith. Even in the harshest conditions, they prioritized preserving their dignity and supporting one another.
