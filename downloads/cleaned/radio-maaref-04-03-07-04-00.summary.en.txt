The program first addressed fiqh rulings, clarifying that seeking help for the preliminary steps of wudu is disliked unless necessary, while having another person perform wudu is valid if one is unable to do so. It was also specified that the kaffarah for missed fasts must be paid after a full year has passed and before the next Ramadan. Regarding the concept of homeland, one's birthplace and cities of long-term residence are considered home for prayer and fasting purposes. The discussion then shifted to Nahj al-Balagha, highlighting it as a comprehensive guide to understanding human nature and psychology. Imam Ali (AS) categorizes people based on their moral and practical traits, such as those focused on the world, those focused on the hereafter, and divine scholars. Self-knowledge is presented in the text as the key to achieving divine knowledge and accurately perceiving oneself and others. Finally, the program concluded by discussing Imam Khomeini's spiritual and Sufi approach to Quranic interpretation in his works.
