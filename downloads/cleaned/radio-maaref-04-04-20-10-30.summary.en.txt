The radio program begins by addressing claims of meeting the Imam of the Age, emphasizing that such claims require solid evidence and that physical searching for the Imam is prohibited during the Major Occultation, with only prayer and spiritual connection being recommended. The main discussion then shifts to drawing lessons from the companions of Imam Hussein (AS) for the waiters of Imam Mahdi (AJ). Dr. Javad Jafari highlights "religious zeal" (ghayrah diniyyah) as the most critical trait of the Ashura companions, defining it as the drive to protect the sanctity of religion and support the Imam. He argues that this religious zeal is the highest form of loyalty, surpassing national or personal honor, and was fully manifested on the day of Ashura. The core message is that waiters must embody this zeal by actively supporting and defending the Imam during the period of occultation. This support can be practiced by promoting Mahdavi culture in families, schools, and communities, and by making others familiar with the Imam's teachings. The segment concludes with stories of the companions' self-sacrifice, such as Sa'id ibn Abdullah, urging listeners to practically apply these models in their daily lives to become true supporters of the Imam.
