This radio program highlights the role of Fatima Masumeh (SA) as a central figure for reviving the teachings of the Ahl al-Bayt and as a specific model for women in the end times.
Citing a narration where Imam Kazim (AS) praised his daughter's virtue, the speaker emphasizes the importance of conscious pilgrimage and the dissemination of religious knowledge.
It is argued that the enemy has a malicious plan to harm women and youth in the end times, aiming to destroy their modesty and purity.
To counter this, forming circles of believers and emulating the wisdom of Moses' mother and sister, who thwarted Pharaoh's plots, is deemed essential.
The role of women as the embodiment of love and gentleness in the family and society is presented as complementary to men's role as the embodiment of power and defense.
The characteristics of the pious are also described from the book of Kafi and Nahj al-Balagha, including seeing Paradise and Hell with the eye of certainty and having hearts grieved by the distance from perfection.
Although their bodies may become thin due to worship, these individuals are spiritually sound, and their constant remembrance of the Fire of Hell is their defining trait.
Ultimately, it is concluded that women can neutralize the enemy's plans and build a healthier society by maintaining purity and seeking spiritual aid from Fatima Masumeh (SA).
