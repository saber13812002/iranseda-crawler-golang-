This radio broadcast reported on the start of pre-sales for Iran Air's extra flights and the organization of "Friday of Anger and Victory" rallies in three provinces. A significant portion of the program was dedicated to commemorating martyred generals Mohammad Saeed Izadi and Gholam-Ali Rashid, highlighting their pivotal roles in national defense against the Zionist regime. The report also covered the National Center for Answering Religious Questions' extensive activities during the crisis and the guilds' support for the government in meeting public needs. The book "Narrative of the Father," which details the life of the Supreme Leader's father, was introduced with an emphasis on his asceticism and contentment. In the jurisprudential segment, a ruling was provided stating that if the Friday prayer is invalidated, the Zuhr prayer must be performed as a make-up prayer. The program concluded with the recitation of elegies and an invitation for students to participate in the "Husaini Saplings" campaign.
