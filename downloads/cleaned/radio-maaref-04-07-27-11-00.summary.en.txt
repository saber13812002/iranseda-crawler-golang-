The Hajj and Pilgrimage Organization announced that holders of deposit receipts can register for the 2026 Hajj until the end of December 2025, with over 13,000 people already registered. The 48th National Quran Competition is underway in Sanandaj until early October, featuring 324 reciters under the slogan "The Quran is the Book of Unity." Simultaneously, the 31st International Quranic and Hadith Festival of Al-Mustafa University has begun in 20 countries, focusing on Islamic governance. In Shiraz, an international conference on the martyrs of the path to Quds is being held to honor the heroes of the Sacred Defense. The museums of the Astan Quds Razavi complex recorded approximately 400,000 visitors in the first half of the year, including over 12,000 non-Iranian tourists. The deadline for officials to resign in order to run for city council elections has passed, with registration scheduled for late November. Fiqh experts also addressed questions regarding the timing of night prayers, the permissibility of bank interest under Sharia contracts, and the rulings on ritual bathing.
