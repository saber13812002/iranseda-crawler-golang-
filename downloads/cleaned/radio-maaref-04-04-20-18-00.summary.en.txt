The radio program "Ma'aref" began by introducing the lunar year 1447 before transitioning to the "Ganj-e Sa'adat" segment featuring Hujjat al-Islam Fallahati. The guest emphasized the spiritual and practical importance of charity (infak) as an investment for the afterlife, citing narrations from Imam Ali (AS). He explained that true wealth is preserved through generosity, which liberates the soul from stinginess and aligns the believer with the Imam's guidance. The advice Imam Ali gave to Imam Hasan (AS)—that one's benefit from this world is solely building the hereafter—was highlighted as a key directive for Shia Muslims. The concept of a "light provision" was discussed, urging believers to spend their surplus on the needy rather than hoarding it, so it returns as spiritual capital on Judgment Day. The segment concluded with a call to send blessings for the early return of Imam Mahdi (AJ). Finally, the program aired a short story titled "Night Class," depicting the late Dr. Mostafa Chamran's dedication to educating his peers despite his demanding work schedule.
