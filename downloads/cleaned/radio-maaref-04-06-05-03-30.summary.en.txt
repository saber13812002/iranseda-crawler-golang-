The radio program began by addressing a fiqh question regarding the mahram status of a stepfather towards his wife's daughters from a previous marriage. According to Ayatollah Javadi Amoli's fatwa, if the stepfather breastfeeds these daughters after the marriage, he becomes a mahram to them, and marriage between them is strictly prohibited. Hujjat al-Islam Razianian then discussed the concept of self-monitoring and individual responsibility, citing a verse from the Quran that urges believers to reflect on their deeds. He emphasized that humans are created to strive for spiritual perfection and that enduring hardship is an essential part of this journey. The program also highlighted the significance of performing the morning prayer on time as a fundamental pillar of faith and a means of connecting with God. Following this, a recitation and translation of verses from Surah Shura were presented, focusing on divine mercy and God's power over nature. The broadcast concluded with prayers for the health of the Imam of the Time and a call for continuous self-improvement.
