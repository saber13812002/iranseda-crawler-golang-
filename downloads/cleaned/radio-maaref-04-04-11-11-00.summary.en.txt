The head of the Hajj and Pilgrimage Organization announced that the 1404 Hajj file was closed with the transfer of the last pilgrim group from Medina to Mashhad. He emphasized that no extra fees would be charged to the 64,000 pilgrims who were transported via land after air routes were cut off. Memorials were held for prominent martyrs, including General Kazemi, Hajizadeh, and Hossein Salami. The Network of Imams of the Country's Mosques condemned American threats as a declaration of war and called for a decisive response from the Maraji'. The traditional "Abbas in the Bazaar" ceremony was also held with the presence of numerous mourners. In the "Zamzam Ahkam" program, discussions covered the rules of mourning and the style of eulogizing. A religious query was answered, confirming that interest from deposits in interest-free banks is permissible according to approved regulations.
