In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Mohammad Hali-Fala discusses the virtue of humility (Tawadu) as a major act of worship, citing narrations from Imam Ali and Imam Sadiq (AS). He defines true humility not merely as sitting in a lower position, but as self-awareness, respectful interaction, and forgiving others' wrongs without seeking revenge. A key point is that humility does not mean consciously forgetting injuries, but rather refraining from retaliation and responding to evil with good or silence. In the second segment, Hujjat al-Islam Bagherian explores the "worldly reflections of actions" from a Quranic perspective, focusing on how behavior impacts mental peace and divine favor. He emphasizes that justice and fairness in dealing with everyone, including non-Muslims, are essential for earning God's love and inner tranquility, as highlighted in verses describing God as a lover of the just. Thus, the program concludes that the combination of ethical humility and social justice is the key to achieving divine satisfaction and spiritual well-being.
