The broadcast began with news reports covering the closure of the Iranian pilgrim file for Hajj, new clashes in the West Bank, and a surge in mask sales in France due to the Hanta virus. The second segment focused on the water crisis in agriculture, emphasizing the necessity of consumption management, modern irrigation, and reducing extraction from unauthorized wells. Subsequently, the "Zamzam Ahkam" program addressed listeners' religious questions through responses from representatives of the Maraji'. Topics included the Khums ruling on inheritance and gold, the order of prayer and Ziyarat Ashura, waqf during one's lifetime, and dedicating Quran recitation rewards. Rulings regarding purchasing Hajj tickets for a spouse and the conditions for following an Imam in prayer were also examined. The program concluded with the audio drama "Voice of Law," discussing the importance of preserving honor in Islam.
