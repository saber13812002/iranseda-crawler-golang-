The radio program "Zamzam Ahkam" began by explaining the conditions for stoning the Jamarat al-Aqabah on Eid al-Adha, emphasizing that based on the Supreme Leader's fatwa, seven stones must be thrown directly and individually with pure intention. It was confirmed that women and those with valid excuses are permitted to perform the stoning at night if they cannot manage it during the day. Fiqh responses addressed the fasting rules for pregnant and breastfeeding women, noting that if fasting harms the woman herself, the make-up fast is waived and a penalty is required, whereas if it only harms the fetus, the make-up fast remains obligatory. The ruling on finding lost items was discussed, stating that one must give charity on behalf of the owner to clear any doubt before using the item. The importance of prayer was highlighted, warning that neglecting it prevents intercession by the Imams, with strong recommendations for praying on time and in congregation. Regarding fasting invalidation, merely intending to break the fast invalidates it, but if one retracts the intention, only the make-up fast is required; however, actually committing the act adds a penalty. The presence of nail polish during prayer invalidates wudu and requires repeating the prayer. Finally, the criteria for legal maturity in boys were outlined, including reaching the age of 15 lunar years or the appearance of physical signs.
