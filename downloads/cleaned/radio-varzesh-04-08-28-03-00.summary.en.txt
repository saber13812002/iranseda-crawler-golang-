In this interview, the guest highlighted the urgent need for proper sports footwear for rural participants by sharing an anecdote about women competing in high heels. She proposed allocating part of the budget for rural sports houses to provide shoes to registrants, thereby boosting their motivation to participate. The guest also discussed the success of the Jam-e Parvaz tournament and the launch of the Rural Olympics in five regions, emphasizing the revival of indigenous games as a cultural treasure and a potential bridge for international competition. On a personal note, she described how witnessing the simple yet joyful lives of rural families profoundly influenced her own lifestyle, contrasting their happiness with the emptiness of material wealth. She further advocated for standardizing the rules of indigenous games to facilitate their recognition as official sports and to enable cross-border tournaments. The program concluded with the guest's gratitude and a brief educational segment on water conservation during car washing.
