In this radio program, an expert addresses several key questions regarding family dynamics. Regarding a father's anxiety about his daughter's potential divorce, the advice is to seek counseling rather than applying pressure. For a wife fearing her volatile and fragile husband, the first step is to improve his understanding of gender differences, with counseling suggested only during calm periods. On child-rearing, the expert emphasizes that conflicting parenting styles are harmful and parents must avoid arguing in front of their children. To resolve a spouse's disbelief in expressions of affection, hidden resentments and mismatched expectations of love must be identified and adjusted. Finally, listeners are invited to submit their own questions via the provided contact numbers.
