In the "Persaman Quran" radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Maleki addressed various theological questions from listeners. He interpreted the phrase "We breathed into him of Our spirit" as an honorific addition, clarifying that the spirit is a creation of God and this expression elevates the status of humanity. He also explained that the title "Spirit of God" for Jesus (PBUH) stems from his miraculous creation without a father. Regarding the verse "follow the best," the expert emphasized that avoiding falsehood and corruption is essential, arguing that content moderation is necessary to protect the minds of audiences, especially children. He further clarified that the term "servants" in the Quran refers to all humans or specific groups depending on the context. Addressing the concept of divine misguidance, he asserted that guidance and deviation are based on human free will, as God does not force anyone to go astray. Finally, he interpreted the repetition of "Which of your Lord's favors would you deny?" in Surah Ar-Rahman as an educational tool to reinforce gratitude and condemn ingratitude.
