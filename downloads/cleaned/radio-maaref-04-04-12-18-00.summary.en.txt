The host begins by greeting listeners for religious occasions and introduces a discussion on the roots of Western hostility toward Iran. He emphasizes that Iran, as an indigenous builder of the region, has been targeted by foreign powers seeking dominance. Dr. Haqqani explains that this enmity stems from 14th and 15th-century ideas aimed at creating a global system centered in Europe. Renaissance thinkers like Machiavelli laid the foundation for a world order where a few European nations would rule the globe. This system was established through geographical explorations and military superiority, particularly the use of firearms, during the 18th and 19th centuries. In this process, indigenous civilizations were destroyed, and a "center-periphery" economic structure was formed. Ultimately, Iran became a primary target of this hegemonic system, facing invasion and exploitation starting around 1823.
