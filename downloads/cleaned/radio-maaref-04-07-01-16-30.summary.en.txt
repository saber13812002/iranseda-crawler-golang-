In the radio program "Persaman Eteghadi," Professor Mofrad addressed listeners' questions regarding the Barzakh realm. He emphasized that Jinn, like humans, have a Barzakh where they continue their mischief, but there is no physical travel between the world and Barzakh for anyone. What is perceived in dreams or near-death experiences is the activation of Barzakh perceptions, not a physical entry into that realm. It was also explained that the angels witnessing human deeds in Barzakh are distinct from those in the world, and both will serve as witnesses on Judgment Day. In the second part, it was clarified that while believers' deeds are presented to Imam Zaman (AJ) every Tuesday and Thursday, he is aware of all actions and greetings at all times due to his unique spiritual status. Finally, the phrase "Ali Nabi Mahbur" in the Dua of Noor was identified as a reference to Prophet Muhammad (PBUH).
