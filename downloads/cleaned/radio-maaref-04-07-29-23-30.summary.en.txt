Ayatollah Abdollahi shares his memories of studying under prominent scholars such as Sheikh Morteza Ansari and the late Martyr Morteza Motahhari. He highlights their ascetic character, simple lifestyle, and active social presence in the Imam Mosque and seminary circles. The speaker emphasizes the importance of these teachers' instruction in jurisprudence and theology, particularly Motahhari's focus on theological foundations during the critical post-revolutionary period. He criticizes certain revolutionary groups who lacked a solid intellectual foundation and resorted to one-sided interpretations of Nahj al-Balagha. Additionally, he references the security issues faced by political activists who drifted into extremism after the revolution. These anecdotes underscore the great scholars' sensitivity to preserving religious and intellectual authenticity against distortions. The program concludes with a recitation of Imam Khomeini's words and a call for the younger generation to uphold the revolution's ideals.
