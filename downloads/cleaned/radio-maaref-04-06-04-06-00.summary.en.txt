In this program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli discusses the final topics of his tafsir on Surah Al-Ma'idah, highlighting the distinction between the human nature's inclination toward justice and its material tendencies. He argues that true freedom in Islam is reason-based and compassionate, rejecting absolute anarchy as "false freedom" that must be regulated by legal penalties. The speaker refutes the claim that the West originated human rights, asserting that Islam established these concepts earlier through reason and the Quran. Regarding the Guardianship of the Jurist, he emphasizes that reason is a primary source of religion and should not be opposed to revelation, as rational judgments in social and economic management carry religious weight. He also advocates for human brotherhood between Muslims and non-Muslims based on fairness and justice, encouraging respectful interaction with other faiths. Finally, the importance of addressing the doubts of young people and university students through intellectual and non-violent methods is stressed.
