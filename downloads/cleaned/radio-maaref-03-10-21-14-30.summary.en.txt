In the "Mahdavi Questions" segment, Hujjat al-Islam Yousefian refuted the claim that repentance is rejected during the Mahdi's appearance, explaining that it is only futile when a person has lost all agency and is on the brink of destruction. He distinguished between "questioning," which stems from ignorance, and "criticism," which implies a claim of superior knowledge, asserting that criticizing the infallible Imam is invalid while asking questions is appropriate. In the second segment, Dr. Meshkibaf discussed the recent developments in Syria, attributing the Supreme Leader's direct involvement in clarifying the situation to a lack of effective "Jihad of Clarification" by experts. He compared this scenario to Imam Khomeini's post-war efforts to address public doubts, emphasizing the need for precise responses to societal misconceptions. Meshkibaf also rejected the notion that Iran regrets its presence in Syria, noting that this involvement was based on specific strategic objectives rather than mere opportunism.
