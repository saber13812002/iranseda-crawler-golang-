This radio program began with an introduction to the book "Jihad of Clarification" by Saeed Souri Mirzadeh, which compiles the Supreme Leader's speeches on the importance of public enlightenment and countering anti-revolutionary currents. In the second segment, Ahmad Lakhzai addressed the question of why Islamic nations lack unity against Israel, attributing this weakness to the gap between religious claims and actions, fear of political costs, and the dependence of rulers on foreign powers. He expressed hope for positive changes in the future, citing the public's awakening and the loss of moral credibility by the Zionist regime. Subsequently, Dr. Sajjad Mirkhaleli discussed nuclear negotiations, emphasizing that a "step-by-step" approach is necessary to avoid repeating the bitter experiences of the JCPOA. He stressed that negotiations must remain strictly within the nuclear framework with the goal of lifting sanctions, while the system's red lines remain intact. The importance of strengthening domestic capabilities and reducing reliance on global markets to neutralize sanctions was also highlighted. The program concluded with a review of the week's political anniversaries and an anecdote about a wealthy Tabriz merchant's financial support for prominent clerics.
