During a program coinciding with the birth of Imam Reza and the centennial of the re-establishment of the Qom Seminary, Ayatollah Sadrani discussed the history of Shia seminaries. He highlighted the historical roots of seminaries in Medina, Kufa, and Khorasan, emphasizing Qom's significance as the fourth major center of Shia scholarship. By citing prominent scholars such as Sheikh Bahai and Sadir Mutallin, he illustrated the intellectual flourishing of Qom during the 11th and 12th centuries. The speaker also noted the significant growth in the number of students throughout various historical periods, underscoring Qom's role in preserving and promoting religious sciences. Ayatollah Haeri was mentioned as a pivotal figure in shaping the modern seminary, with his influence on subsequent generations being a key topic. The speech honored the legacy of pioneering scholars while stressing the necessity of continuing the educational and training path in religious seminaries.
