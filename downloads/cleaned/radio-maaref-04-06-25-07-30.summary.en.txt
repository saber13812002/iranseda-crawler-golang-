The radio program began by celebrating the anniversary of Lady Fatima Masumeh's (SA) arrival in Qom, highlighting her significant role in the history of Shia Islam.
The guest emphasized the importance of visiting her shrine to benefit from its spiritual blessings and praised the respectful hospitality shown by the people of Qom.
The main discussion focused on the ethical prohibition of gossip, rumor-mongering, and backbiting, which are considered dangerous vices according to the teachings of the Ahl al-Bayt (AS).
The Prophet (PBUH) and Imam Ali (AS) identified the rumor-monger as the worst of God's creations and instructed believers to neither engage in such acts nor permit others to do so.
Imam Ali (AS) specifically advised that any slander or gossip, even if perceived as true, should be treated as a lie and rejected outright.
The program concluded by stating that moral purification and the removal of such vices are essential prerequisites for accepting divine guardianship and achieving true Shia education.
Listeners were encouraged to avoid these behaviors to remain under the protective spiritual umbrella of the Imams' guidance.
