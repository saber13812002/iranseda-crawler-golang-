In this radio program, numerous narrations from the companions and the Ahl al-Bayt are cited to prove that the Verse of Purification was specifically revealed for Ali, Fatima, Hasan, and Husayn. After the revelation, the Prophet (PBUH) repeatedly greeted them during the five daily prayers for months to publicly establish this exclusive status. In contrast, none of the Prophet's wives claimed to be included in this verse, and even Aisha never asserted this virtue for herself. This silence and lack of claim from the wives, combined with the explicit claims of the Ahl al-Bayt, serve as the strongest evidence for the verse's exclusivity to the Prophet's family. Sunni commentators like Ibn Kathir, despite referencing the context of the verses, have been unable to refute the numerous and explicit narrations of the Ahl al-Bayt. Finally, the discussion regarding the context of the verses and responses to Sunni scholars' doubts is postponed to the next session.
