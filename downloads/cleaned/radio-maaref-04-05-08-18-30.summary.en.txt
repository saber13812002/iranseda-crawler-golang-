In this radio program, Dr. Bidar from Imam Khomeini University discussed strategies for a resistance economy, emphasizing the importance of small production workshops and cooperatives for job creation and reducing unemployment. He noted that the cooperative economy holds a special place in the constitution and Islamic teachings, suggesting that individuals with limited capital should operate in legal, group-based frameworks to prevent bankruptcy. The role of the government in providing administrative, judicial, and legislative support to protect these sectors from failure was also highlighted. In the second segment, fruit storage investors shared their experience in packaging and exporting high-quality fruit to foreign markets, creating employment for dozens of people. Dr. Bidar then explained the role of participation bonds in financing large-scale production and infrastructure projects, identifying them as a suitable tool for public participation in the Islamic economy. He stressed that strict oversight of the profitability of these projects is essential to maintain public trust. Finally, the program concluded with excerpts from the "Zamzame Ahkam" segment, which addressed the religious rulings regarding Ta'ziyeh and answered listeners' legal questions.
