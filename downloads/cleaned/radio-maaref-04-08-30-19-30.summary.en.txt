The program began with the recitation of the nineteenth supplication from the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya, seeking rain, before transitioning to a discussion on the moral vice of mockery. The instructor identified mockery as a sign of the dominance of the ego and a fundamental hostility towards monotheism and faith. Citing Quranic verses, he emphasized that mocking divine commands, prayer, and religious laws is a grave sin. The Quran not only condemns the act of mockery itself but also explicitly forbids befriending or associating with those who engage in it. Historical evidence shows that all prophets, from Noah to the Prophet Muhammad, faced ridicule from their communities. This mockery is essentially an attack on the entire religion and its sacred values, rooted in ignorance and disbelief. Finally, listeners were encouraged to study the relevant verses to avoid this moral corruption and maintain distance from those who mock religious matters.
