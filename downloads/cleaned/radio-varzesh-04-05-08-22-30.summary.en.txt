The program's guest, Dr. Sadeghi Hosseini, discussed the successful hosting of the World Wrestling Championship in Sari and its pivotal role in elevating the sport in Iran. Relying on precise task division and transparent financial management, he organized international events with a limited budget while settling previous debts without any objections. In the infrastructure sector, he leveraged public participation and inter-agency cooperation to complete 106 unfinished halls and construct 23 new ones across Mazandaran's villages and cities. By utilizing media and challenging TV programs, he highlighted infrastructure issues to the public and officials, thereby securing the necessary support for project completion. This approach led to a significant increase in organized athletes from 20,000 to 100,000 and the creation of sports facilities tailored to local needs. Hosseini emphasized that the key to success was establishing strong inter-departmental connections and gaining public trust through transparency and accountability. Finally, he expressed gratitude to the people and the media as the two primary factors behind achieving these sports and social milestones.
