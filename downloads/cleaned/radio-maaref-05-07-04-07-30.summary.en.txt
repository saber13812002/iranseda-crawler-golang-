The program begins with a humorous segment about hiring someone with "steel ears" to endure tense speeches at the United Nations. It then highlights the importance of global media accurately depicting diplomats leaving the hall during the Israeli Prime Minister's address. A touching story follows about a man named Peyman who, with a colleague's help, manages to buy his sick child's medication despite having insufficient funds, accompanied by a call for charity at the start of the school year. Listeners are also introduced to soldiers' hardships in border regions to foster appreciation for their service. In the main segment, Professor Mohammad Hadi Falahati discusses the factors that lead to being deceived by arrogance in the "Ganj-e Sa'adat" program. He identifies reliance on decorated lies, worldliness, uncontrolled desires, and worldly status as key causes of falling into these moral vices. Citing narrations from Imam Ali (AS), he explains that being fooled by the mirage of the world destroys the means of salvation and the illusion of invincibility is itself the cause of one's defeat. Finally, he advises abandoning moral vices and relying on the guardianship of the Imams to gain the power of discernment and mental freedom for achieving perfection.
