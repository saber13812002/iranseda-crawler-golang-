This radio program discussed the jurisprudential and legal validity of heavy and unconventional mahr (dowry).
It was emphasized that this is a modern issue not extensively addressed in traditional jurisprudential texts.
Some scholars cite the Quranic verse regarding the "qintar" to validate even exorbitant mahr if agreed upon by both parties.
However, contemporary views differ, with some arguing that mahr exceeding the husband's serious intent is invalid.
Another perspective holds that irrational or excessive mahr lacks religious validity due to the requirement of rationality in contracts.
Ultimately, the disagreement among contemporary scholars regarding the criteria for the validity of such mahr was highlighted.
The program concluded with an invitation to the 13th of Aban rally and the recitation of verses from Surah Al-Imran.
