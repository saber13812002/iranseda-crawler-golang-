The radio program "Zamzam Ahkam" began with Quranic recitation and prayers before addressing the jurisprudential rulings on sperm testing. The guest scholar explained that for single individuals, masturbation for testing is only permissible if it is the sole method for treatment and no halal alternative exists. For married men, obtaining sperm through their spouse at home or in a private clinic room is entirely permissible, whereas masturbation remains a major sin even if medically necessary if spousal access is unavailable. In the Q&A section, it was clarified that short dhikr between surahs in congregational prayer is acceptable and combining obligatory and recommended ghusls is valid. The program also addressed the fasting rules for patients who must take medication during the day, specifying when they must make up missed fasts or pay expiation. Finally, the scholars detailed the rulings on ghusl and the khums obligation on borrowed money that is repaid after the khums year has passed.
