In the radio program "Imam Reza Wednesdays," the story of a widowed mother of six children who is struggling to afford her surgical treatment due to financial hardship was shared.
The host, citing religious traditions and the significance of the first of the month, urged listeners to send the number 8 to 3552 to donate to the Hazrat Masoumeh shrine for her medical expenses.
It was emphasized that even small contributions, made with love for Imam Reza (PBUH), are valuable and can help alleviate this family's burdens.
At the end of the program, the host recited the Ziyarat Al-Yasin and religious poetry, stressing the importance of maintaining religious commitment and youth resistance against oppressive powers.
The host noted that Iranian youth have proven their ability to resist foreign plots and internal deviations through their dedication to Islam.
It is hoped that public participation will cover the mother's treatment costs, allowing for a positive update in the next episode.
These actions not only assist a needy family but also serve as an opportunity to strengthen spiritual bonds with the Ahl al-Bayt (PBUH) and honor the martyrs and parents.
