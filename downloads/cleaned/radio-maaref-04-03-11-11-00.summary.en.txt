The central committee for commemorating Imam Khomeini's passing announced details for the March 24th ceremony, titled "Following the Imam," which will culminate in a speech by the Supreme Leader at the Imam's shrine. In the cultural sector, the book "Ruh Allah" was released, offering a documented account of Imam Khomeini's life and his leadership role in the revolution. The first "Faith and Hope" conference was held to explore the Imam's ideologies and their continuation in the current Supreme Leader's perspective. Hajj reports indicate that over 53% of Iranian pilgrims this year are women, who have been provided with dedicated services such as permanent female counselors and exclusive sessions. The "Man Gadirim" program featured religious Q&A and the announcement of SMS contest winners. Guest Hujjat al-Islam Hosseini Ghomi addressed doubts regarding the Wilayah of Imam Ali (AS). He justified the Imam's silence at Saqifah by citing the short time gap since Ghadir Khumm and referencing numerous narrations in Nahj al-Balagha and Bihar al-Anwar.
