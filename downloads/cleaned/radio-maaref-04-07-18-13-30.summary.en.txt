The program begins by narrating the life story of martyr Ali Mardani, focusing on his marriage and military missions during the war. As a battalion commander on the Chazabe front, Mardani overcame severe shortages of troops and equipment by teaching creative tactical maneuvers to his soldiers. Despite being wounded and suffering from extreme thirst, he refused to leave his trench or accept rest, ensuring the defense line held until the final moment. He was ultimately martyred during combat, and his memory is honored as a symbol of self-sacrifice and loyalty to the revolution. The second part of the program discusses Iran's nuclear status, identifying uranium enrichment as the key to the country's industrial independence. The speaker emphasizes that while Iran does not possess nuclear weapons, it has the technical capability for enrichment, a result of domestic scientists' efforts under sanctions. The inability to enrich uranium would force the country to rely on others for power plant fuel, thereby threatening its energy independence.
