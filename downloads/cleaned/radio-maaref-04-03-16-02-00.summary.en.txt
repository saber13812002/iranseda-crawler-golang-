The radio program "Parsayan" emphasizes the religious importance of writing a will to protect others' rights and keep death in mind.
The show reads excerpts from the ethical and emotional wills of scholars such as Ayatollah Marashi Najafi, Allameh Majlisi, and Ayatollah Khonsari addressed to their children.
These wills include recommendations for night prayers, respecting parents, avoiding sin, and maintaining harmony among family members.
The second part of the program begins with a song by Ebrahim Hadi and transitions into a narrative about a character named Gholam Hossein.
In the story, Gholam Hossein has abstained from water and sleep for three days as penance for missing his prayers.
His friend, Ali Reza, is deeply moved by Gholam Hossein's weakened physical state and cracked lips, questioning why he is subjecting himself to such hardship.
Gholam Hossein explains that he is hoping for God's forgiveness and is fulfilling a personal vow to make up for his mistake.
Finally, upon hearing news of an enemy attack, Gholam Hossein prepares to defend the city using the community's weapons despite his physical weakness.
