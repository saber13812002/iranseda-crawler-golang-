In the Parsiyan program, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the spiritual and practical benefits of Istighfar and its specific prayer for resolving material and spiritual difficulties. He shared anecdotes from Ayatollah Khosrowshahi and Haj Agha Heydari Kashani, highlighting the miraculous efficacy of this prayer in alleviating financial hardships. Detailed instructions were provided on how to perform this two-rak'ah prayer, which includes specific recitations of Istighfar. The second part of the show featured a narrative from the book "Ocean of the East," where a lost man in the desert finds guidance and material aid from a cobbler, symbolizing the role of Imam Reza (AS) in guiding believers. This story serves as a metaphor for the Imam's intervention in saving Muslims from life's trials. The program also offered practical tips for reducing water consumption in the kitchen to prevent resource waste. It concluded by discussing the 1404 slogan of "Investment for Production," stressing the need for joint effort between the government and the people to revitalize the economy. Finally, the martyrs Rajai and Bahonar were honored on the anniversary of their martyrdom.
