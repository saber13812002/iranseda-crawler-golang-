In this radio program, jurisprudential answers to audience questions were provided by various religious authorities. Ayatollah Khamenei stated that hair does not prevent touching the scalp during wudu, but caution is required to wash hair and remove obstructive oils during ghusl. Hujjat al-Islam Mohammadi advised a person with a broken back who cannot perform leg wiping to perform tayammum and pray. It was also explained that performing wudu after ritual bathing for major impurity is valid with the intention of hope, though not obligatory. Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi emphasized observing the waiting period (iddah) after temporary marriage before remarrying. Ayatollah Sistani clarified that there is no jurisprudential priority in the order of reciting Surah Al-Qadr and Al-Ikhlas in prayer. Finally, Hujjat al-Islam Jawahiri explained Ayatollah Shabih Zanjani's ruling that washing the intended part of the body is sufficient in sequential ghusl.
