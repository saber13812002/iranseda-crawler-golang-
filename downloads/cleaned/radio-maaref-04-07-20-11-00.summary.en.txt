The pre-registration for the 1405 Umrah Hajj has begun, requiring a 200 million Toman payment and data updates via the official system. The Minister of Culture highlighted Hafez's role as Iran's spiritual memory, emphasizing the depth of his literary and mystical works during a commemorative event. The secretary of the International Imam Khomeini Conference announced over 150 academic sessions and preparations for the Ayatollah Safi Golpaygani conference. The Assembly of Poets of the Ahl al-Bayt issued a call for the 13th National Fatimi Poetry Conference, with a submission deadline of Aban 10. The "Iran Jan Fars" media event continues until Mehr 25 to showcase the province's cultural and economic potential. The registration deadline for university students' Umrah has also been extended to Mehr 25. The Minister of Economy announced a national credit scheme to stimulate demand by supporting low-income households. Finally, the program addressed various jurisprudential rulings, including the permissibility of a daughter performing Hajj on behalf of her father, the rules regarding music, and issues related to Adhan and Mafruz 'Alayha.
