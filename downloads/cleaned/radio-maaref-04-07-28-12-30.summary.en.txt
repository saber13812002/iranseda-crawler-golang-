In the radio program "Bar Bal-e Sakhn," Hujjat al-Islam Nazari Monfared discussed a hadith from Imam Reza (AS) narrated by Hazrat Abdolazim Hasani. The speaker first emphasized the necessity of respecting the Sayyids and highlighted the scholarly and spiritual status of Abdolazim as a great hadith scholar who met three Imams. A key point was Imam Hadi's (AS) validation of Abdolazim's correct beliefs, underscoring the importance of holding reasoned convictions in the principles of faith. Subsequently, Imam Reza (AS) urged the Shia community through this narration to prevent Satan from gaining dominion over their souls and to guard their inner sanctity. Citing Nahj al-Balagha and the Quran, the speaker explained that Satan misleads humans by beautifying sins and fostering destructive friendships. It was stressed that individuals must use reflection to distinguish between divine and satanic actions to avoid spiritual ruin. Finally, the virtue of visiting Hazrat Abdolazim in Rey was mentioned, noting its equivalence to visiting Imam Hussein (AS) in Karbala.
