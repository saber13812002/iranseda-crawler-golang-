The radio program emphasized the complete equality of men and women in performing righteous deeds and worship, stating that there is no difference in the value of these acts between the two genders. The concept of "Hayat Tayyib" was defined as a monotheistic life achieved solely through following the Imam and establishing order in one's life. Dr. Jafari, citing narrations from both Shia and Sunni sources, interpreted apostasy during the occultation not as a loss of theological belief, but as a loss of piety and the commission of practical sins. Practical ways to support the Imam of the Age, such as organizing Mahdavi book reading competitions and creating engaging cultural content for various social groups, were discussed. Professor Kafil interpreted the Quranic verse about God reviving the earth after its death as a reference to the Imam's appearance bringing life to the world through the establishment of justice. Finally, Professor Sadoughi concluded that civilization is the fruit of monotheistic culture, and that only by consciously choosing this culture and following the Guardianship of the Jurist can one attain the Imam's satisfaction and build a sustainable civilization.
