The radio program begins by recounting the historical narrative of Imam Hadi selecting Najas Khatun as the wife of Imam Hasan Askari and prophesying the birth of the Mahdi from her. Subsequently, expert Mehdi Yousefian addresses questions regarding the signs of the Mahdi's appearance, emphasizing that interpreting current events as definitive signs is a common error. Using the story of Prophet Yusuf and Ya'qub, he explains that the process of appearance is governed by divine wisdom and requires time for societal evolution. A key point in the discussion is the definition of "waiting" (Intizar) as an action-oriented mindset; a true waiter must be active and responsible in worldly life, much like Prophet Ya'qub. In the second part, the role of industry during the era of appearance is analyzed; while scientific progress will continue, its direction will shift from material pleasure-seeking to spiritual service and justice. Finally, the program concludes by sharing the memoirs of Azat Shahi, detailing his harsh imprisonment and torture under the Pahlavi regime while defending the Islamic Revolution.
