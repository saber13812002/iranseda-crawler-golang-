This program highlights the principle of "guidance" and "compassion" as the core of the Prophet's and Imams' conduct, citing historical accounts from Sheikh Mufid and Nahj al-Balagha. It explains that Imam Hussein and Imam Ali refrained from initiating war despite their military superiority to allow opponents the chance for spiritual guidance. Even after their companions were martyred in the battles of Jamal and Siffin, Imam Ali prayed to prevent further bloodshed and to guide the enemy. The second part features the mother of martyr Arman Alivardi, describing him as a compassionate and revolutionary youth dedicated to serving the people rather than engaging in conflict. Arman actively worked to educate and support the youth during the 2022 protests, where he was ultimately martyred. His story serves as a testament to his love for his homeland and his commitment to helping the marginalized. The program concludes by emphasizing that his martyrdom was the result of this selfless and guiding spirit.
