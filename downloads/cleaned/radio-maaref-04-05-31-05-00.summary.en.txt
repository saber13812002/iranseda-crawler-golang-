In this radio program, reason is introduced as the most fundamental divine blessing that serves as the foundation for human knowledge, faith, and moral virtue.
Citing narrations from the book of Usul al-Kafi, reason is described as an entity commanded by God to turn towards goodness and virtues while turning away from evil and superstition.
Imam Sadiq (AS) defines piety as being present wherever God commands and absent from places of sin.
Imam Kazim (AS) describes a wise person as one who is willing to sacrifice worldly possessions but never gives up on knowledge and wisdom.
Other characteristics of a wise individual include avoiding lies, not following the desires of the ego, and overcoming the base self against sin.
Prophet David (AS) also emphasized prioritizing God's command over the desires of the soul, which God acknowledged as a divine grace.
Finally, the discussion explores the philosophical and mystical dimensions of intellectual perception and the role of the imagination faculty in understanding abstract concepts and worldly details.
