In this radio program, Professor Maleki addressed various Quranic questions from listeners. He clarified that "sukara" in the Surah An-Nisa refers to intoxication from alcohol rather than sleep, highlighting it as part of the gradual prohibition of alcohol in Islam. He then explained that Surah Qaf features God speaking directly and fearfully to humanity to awaken them from spiritual negligence. Regarding the topic of sorcery, the professor emphasized the importance of investigating psychological or family issues before attributing problems to magic, while recommending protective Quranic recitations. He noted that sorcery has no effect on true believers and that many claims of magic are often delusions or scams. Finally, he interpreted the story of Prophet Solomon, explaining that the placement of a corpse on his throne was a divine test to prompt repentance and prevent arrogance. The program concluded with condolences for the Arbaeen mourning period and prayers for peace and the Mahdi's reappearance.
