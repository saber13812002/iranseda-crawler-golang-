The Iranian President addressed the UN General Assembly, accusing the Zionist regime of genocide in Gaza and Lebanon and emphasizing Iran's cultural influence over military power. During the same session, the President of Colombia called for an international military force to support Palestine, criticizing the US and NATO for undermining democracy. Concurrently, the killing of children in southern Lebanon, occurring while a US envoy was present, drew sharp criticism regarding the silence of international and Lebanese officials. Domestically, affordable pilgrimage trips to Iraq and a children's book fair at the Imam Hussein shrine were highlighted as significant cultural events. The new academic year began in Iranian schools abroad, particularly in Moscow, where students from various nations emphasized religious education alongside academics. In Europe, the trend of converting abandoned churches into mosques in the Netherlands was reported due to financial crises in Christian institutions and a growing Muslim population. The Supreme Leader's recent speech, focusing on national unity, uranium enrichment, and the futility of negotiations with the US, generated widespread discussion on social media. Analysts interpreted these remarks as an effort to consolidate public unity against enemy plots and to reaffirm the historical experience of resistance.
