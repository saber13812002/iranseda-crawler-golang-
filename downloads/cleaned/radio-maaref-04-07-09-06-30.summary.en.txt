The program begins with a theological analysis of the Quranic verse stating that body parts will testify against their owners on Judgment Day.
It emphasizes that this testimony is not driven by independent will but occurs solely by God's permission and power.
The discussion clarifies that while the testimony is truthful, humans cannot object to its validity since God authorized the speech.
The testimony of the earth and mosques is also mentioned as witnesses speaking under divine command.
The second part of the show celebrates the birth of Imam Hasan Askari (AS) and World Elderly Day.
The host highlights the importance of respecting elders and caring for the elderly within the community.
News segments cover the "Muslim bin Aqil" operation from 43 years ago and the 2000 martyrdom of Muhammad al-Durrah.
The program concludes by inviting listeners to participate in a daily challenge and introducing Dr. Mehdi Azari, head of the culture and society group.
