The head of the Hajj and Pilgrimage Organization announced the transfer of a thousand Zamzam water bottles to be gifted to Iranian pilgrims at airports, noting the presence of five thousand volunteers to assist the pilgrims. Coinciding with the Day of Marriage, a celebration was held for 236 student couples at the Tehran University of Medical Sciences, offering them a trip to Mashhad and financial grants. The groundbreaking ceremony for the fourth block of the married students' dormitory at Sharif University of Technology took place, with plans announced to construct 30,000 such units over the next five years. The fifth international Hooriyeh Ansayeh conference was held, focusing on the role of women in global changes and emphasizing adherence to Islamic thought. The 36th anniversary of Imam Khomeini's passing was commemorated by Tehran's Jewish community at the Youssef Abad synagogue, highlighting his role in the struggle against tyranny. The body of martyr Aboud Herab Nouri was identified after 43 years, and a ceremony was held to honor Martyr Sardar Siad Khodayi. The radio program "Man Gadirim" engaged listeners through SMS quizzes and announced winners, encouraging participation in religious and cultural activities.
