In this interview, veteran Zourkhaneh master Alireza Haghjati emphasizes the importance of preserving the authenticity of Zourkhaneh music and avoiding the imitation of other styles like Madaheh or Tasnif. He highlights the role of the Masters' Association, established under the Wrestling Federation, in providing specialized training, professional judging, and preventing unqualified individuals from assuming the role of a master. Haghjati argues that a master must cultivate spiritual purity and ethical conduct to maintain the sacred atmosphere of the Zourkhaneh and engage the audience with authentic rhythm and melody. He also addresses the economic challenges and lack of budget for training new artists, calling for greater support for this traditional art. Towards the end, the master recites a poem by the late Master Moradi, reflecting on his childhood memories and the importance of learning from great role models. He shares that his sons are continuing the tradition, underscoring the family's role in passing down this cultural heritage.
