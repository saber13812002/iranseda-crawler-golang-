This radio program emphasized the importance of respectful and emotional interaction between pilgrims and their children during the Arbaeen journey.
The central theme of the discussion is "respect" (Takrim), which is identified as the key to keeping the younger generation within the "tent of Imam Hussein."
Educational experts advised that parents and camp organizers should focus on thoughtful planning and cultural engagement rather than merely providing material amenities.
The Arbaeen pilgrimage is described as a golden opportunity to compensate for daily communication gaps and address the loneliness felt by teenagers in the digital age.
Camp organizers are encouraged to consult with experts to implement targeted cultural content and social interactions alongside their logistical services.
It was highlighted that simple acts like greeting, engaging in friendly conversation, and avoiding humiliation are among the most effective ways to build trust and friendship.
Ultimately, these positive interactions can foster the social, intellectual, and spiritual growth of children, protecting them from social harms.
