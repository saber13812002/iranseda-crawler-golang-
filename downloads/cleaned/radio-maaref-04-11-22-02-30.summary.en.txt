The program begins by recounting the covert efforts to distribute Ayatollah Khomeini's declarations against the State and Provincial Councils bill, highlighting the collaboration with religious groups in Tehran to reach the public. It marks the revolution's victory and the bill's repeal as a pivotal moment for uniting clerics from Tehran and Qom. Emphasizing the significance of February 11, the broadcast urges the public to participate in rallies to preserve the Islamic Revolution and its values. A segment dedicated to Mahdism features expert Mehdi Yusefian, who cites authentic narrations to distinguish between the specific and generic concepts of the Mahdi and confirms the Prophet's explicit mention of the Twelfth Imam. The discussion further explores the theological basis of the Imam's occultation and the spiritual benefits for his followers during this period. Finally, the show introduces the book "Memoirs of Ezzat-Shahri," detailing the struggles of revolutionary activists under the Shah's regime. It describes the political prisoners' release on the eve of the revolution and Ezzat-Shahri's role in maintaining order during the Ashura demonstrations.
