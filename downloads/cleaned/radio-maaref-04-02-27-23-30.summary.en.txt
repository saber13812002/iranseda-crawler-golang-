In this radio program, Hassan Salmi discusses the secret behind the impact of religious nasheeds on the audience. He believes that humans are innately drawn to truth and spirituality, and combining art with religious concepts presents life-saving messages in attractive and enduring forms. Salmi recalls his experience in producing the poetic munajat of Imam Ali (PBUH) by the "Ahl al-Bayt" group in the 1980s. He pays tribute to Master Arani, the group's supervisor and the main guide for this masterpiece, and sends condolences for the late Ay Kholhoro, the manager of the Tehran Pop Studio. Subsequently, the host cites Rumi to describe a mother's love as a part of divine mercy and a manifestation of God's grace. By recounting the story of Moses (PBUH) and a mother on Mount Sinai, he illustrates that mothers' prayers and sacrifices reflect God's boundless mercy. The program concludes with listener prayers and the broadcast of a piece from Imam Ali's (PBUH) munajats.
