The radio program "Hamneshin" was broadcast on Friday night, the commemorative night of Sayyid and Shahada, from Ma'aref Radio.
The host highlighted the Arbaeen pilgrimage and the unity of global visitors in their shared love for the Ahl al-Bayt.
A female listener shared her profound experience of visiting Karbala and her intense longing to return to the shrines.
The host encouraged listeners unable to travel due to personal issues to rely on Imam Reza (AS) and pray for the realization of their pilgrimage.
The program's poet, Dr. Ahour Hamed Ahour, recited a passionate and heroic poem in praise of Hazrat Zainab (SA) and the martyrs of Karbala.
Towards the end, the host emphasized gratitude towards the martyrs of the homeland defense and the parents who nurtured this love in their children.
The program concluded with extensive prayers for the appearance of Imam Mahdi (AJ) and special greetings to the Holy Family.
