Ashoor Gholamohammadi, a veteran basketball player and coach, recounts his playing career with national teams and various clubs in this interview. He highlights the golden era of university basketball, particularly the pivotal role of the Islamic Azad University under the support of Dr. Ghadimi and Dr. Dadkan. After retiring from playing, Gholamohammadi began his coaching career at Paykan, successfully guiding the team from the first league to the Super League through persistent effort. He discusses the hardships of his retirement period and credits his wife for helping him continue his professional activities. Gholamohammadi also reflects on Iran's golden basketball era featuring stars like Hamed Haddadi and expresses concern about the lack of a similar new generation. Finally, he emphasizes the importance of honoring veterans and supporting young athletes to ensure a bright future for Iranian basketball.
