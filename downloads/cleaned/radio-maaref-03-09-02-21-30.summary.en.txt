In this radio program, Teyta Allah Najm al-Din Tusi conducted a specialized analysis of Mahdism narrations and the signs of the Imam's appearance. The main focus was on a detailed narration by Jabir ibn Yazid al-Ju'fi from the book Ghaybat al-Nu'maniyya, which outlines the events surrounding the Saffari and the Imam's emergence. The narration identifies Qarqi Siyah as one of the Saffari's allies who is involved in a battle resulting in the massacre of one hundred thousand people, where the Saffari ultimately prevails. It also mentions specific signs such as a heavenly voice, an earthquake in the Golan region, and the entry of foreign forces into the Levant and Palestine. The text describes the Mahdi's departure from Medina and the subsequent destruction of the Saffari's pursuing forces by divine punishment and a ground collapse. This narration is cited in various Shia sources and some Sunni works, such as those by Salami al-Maqdisi, indicating its multiple chains of transmission. Finally, the speaker emphasized that while this narration details pre-appearance conflicts, it is not directly interpreted as a sign of the Abbasid return.
