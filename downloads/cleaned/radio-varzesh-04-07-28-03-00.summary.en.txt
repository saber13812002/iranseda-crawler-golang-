Seyyed Mehdi Seyyed Sadri, a wrestling champion and veteran, recounts his memories of his championship era and world competitions in this interview. He describes the critical moment of the world finals in Russia, where he defeated the powerful American team despite severe injuries and difficult medical conditions. Sadri emphasizes that he has never been discouraged and has overcome the hardships of life and sports through his trust in God. He believes that humans must rely on God in all circumstances and never forget their duty to help others. This athlete asserts that material possessions have no value and that the main goal of life is serving people and achieving divine satisfaction. He views his personal experiences with financial and physical crises as great life lessons. Finally, Sadri stresses the importance of gratitude and maintaining an ethical path in life.
