This radio program provided jurisprudential answers to listeners' questions based on the rulings of various Islamic authorities. According to Ayatollah Sistani, doubt regarding the impurity of an object does not obligate its purification, and a broken prayer intention is sufficient if made sincerely in the heart. Ayatollah Shabiri Zanjani ruled that those unable to fast must pay the equivalent of 900 grams of wheat or flour to a needy person for each missed day. Additionally, downloading software from enemy websites was deemed permissible as long as it does not violate local laws. Participation in mixed gatherings where women are improperly veiled was declared forbidden, with attendees advised to leave such settings. Ayatollah Javadi Amoli clarified that daughters from a previous marriage become mahram to their stepfather after their mother's remarriage, making marriage to them strictly prohibited. The latter part of the program focused on the moral interpretation of lying, detailing its negative consequences in both this world and the hereafter. Finally, the importance of repenting from lies, compensating for any wrongs committed, and embracing truthfulness to gain divine satisfaction and inner peace was emphasized.
