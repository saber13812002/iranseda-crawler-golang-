The radio program "Barbal Sakhn" featured a lecture by Hojjat al-Islam Hamed Kashani on how the Ahl al-Bayt introduced the virtues of Imam Ali (PBUH). The speaker emphasized that comparing Imam Ali to ordinary people or even angels is considered an act of arrogance unless there is a specific wisdom or a barrier to understanding within the community. Citing the Ziyarat Qadiriyah and historical accounts, he demonstrated that Imam Ali's unparalleled bravery in battles like Khaybar and the Trench not only defeated external enemies but also exposed the hypocrisy of internal traitors. During the Battle of the Trench, while many Muslims retreated in fear, Imam Ali stood alone against the enemy's strongest warrior, reviving the morale of the Islamic army with a single strike. The speaker highlighted Imam Ali's power and dignity, noting his refusal to take spoils of war even under extreme hardship, as praised by poets. Finally, he concluded with prayers for the preservation of the system and leadership, stressing the necessity of deeply knowing and loving Imam Ali as the guarantor of Muslims' salvation and victory.
