Fatemeh Abaqolaei, the first female president of a sports federation in Iranian history, recounts the severe challenges she faced while leading the Physical Fitness and Aerobics Federation. Due to a change in government and a lack of support from the Sports Organization, she was forced to launch the federation from scratch with minimal resources and only a single appointment letter. Despite these obstacles, Abaqolaei leveraged her high motivation and secured sponsors to organize world championships and achieve a bronze medal. However, she was eventually forced to resign due to unprofessional decisions made by new officials who replaced her with their own candidates. She argues that the absence of competent managers and a reliance on trial and error have hindered the progress of sports in the country. This bitter experience highlights the difficulty of breaking gender taboos in sports management without structural support. Despite these hardships, Abaqolaei remains willing to offer her specialized expertise to help resolve crises in other struggling federations.
