This radio program discussed Mahdavi issues, including the state of the world after the Imam Mahdi's (AJ) rule. Two perspectives were presented regarding whether life on Earth ends or continues after his governance. The respondent emphasized that, according to Shia beliefs and historical reports, the Imam of the Time (AJ) has already been born and currently serves as the twelfth Imam. In contrast, the majority of Sunni scholars believe he will be born in the years immediately preceding his appearance. The concepts of "Ruyubza" and "Makhl" before the Imam's uprising were also explained, referring to trivial and cunning individuals. These people play a negative role by deceiving and misleading the public during the pre-appearance period. Islamic narrations warn that in that era, liars will be validated while truth-tellers will be denied. The program concluded with a call to maintain faith and await the relief of the Imam.
