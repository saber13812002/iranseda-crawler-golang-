The program begins with a sharp critique of social and cultural deviations, condemning the lack of respect for the elderly, neglect of the poor, and the prioritization of wealth and fame over religious ethics.
The core of the discussion is a Quranic and narrational interpretation of the concept of Ali's (PBUH) guardianship, which is stated to be realized only through practical obedience to God and the Prophet and the revival of spiritual life.
Imam Baqir (PBUH) identifies two main indicators of Ali's (PBUH) true guardianship: establishing unity and cohesion in society and ensuring justice and the protection of everyone's rights.
According to this interpretation, a true Shia is one who, by acting on these two principles, transforms their animal existence into a human and divine one, experiencing genuine brotherhood beyond class and material boundaries.
Referring to the Prophet's (PBUH) hadith about "wearing Islam inside out," the speaker warns that religious priorities like piety and justice must not be devalued in favor of worldly and superficial interests.
Subsequently, a narrative from the book "Left Behind by the Son" is aired, depicting Salal, a father who, after his son Sohrab fled due to ideological differences, watches over him with regret and anxiety.
This literary segment highlights the generational gap and the internal conflicts of characters facing historical and social events, accompanied by a father's sorrow and longing.
The program concludes with prayers for the victory of the armed forces and a call for peace, inviting listeners to tune in for the broadcast's repeat.
