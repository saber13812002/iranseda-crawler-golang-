The program began by explaining the rules for praying at the Station of Abraham, emphasizing the importance of proper etiquette and reciting specific surahs to maintain the validity of the prayer without disturbing others. It then addressed various jurisprudential questions from followers of different maraji, covering topics such as the recommendation of seeking forgiveness during the Ghunut, the obligation of hijab for women during supplication, and the differences between making up missed prayers and voluntary morning prayers. Further rulings were provided regarding the khums on spousal allowances, the validity of transactions based on visual estimation, and the prohibition of delegating fasting for living individuals. The second segment focused on the spiritual and social impacts of Friday prayers, noting that attending them is considered equivalent to an accepted Hajj and fosters unity among Muslims. Experts highlighted the minimum number of male attendees required for the validity of the Friday prayer and the significance of active participation. The broadcast concluded with excerpts from Friday sermons in Qom and a reminder of the occasion marking the birth of the Eighth Imam.
