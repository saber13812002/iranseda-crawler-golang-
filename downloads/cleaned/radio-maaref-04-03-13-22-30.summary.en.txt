In this radio program, coinciding with the martyrdom of Imam Baqir (AS), the concept of "Tafakkur in Deen" (deep understanding of religion) is presented as the key to finding the right direction in history and life. Citing narrations, the speaker explains that this understanding requires a holistic view of the world and the hereafter, as well as the material and spiritual dimensions, to make correct decisions. He emphasizes the necessity of "scientific jihad" and warns against merely publicizing the system's flaws without acknowledging its strengths and capabilities. Furthermore, a religious lifestyle and logical planning based on the Quran are introduced as prerequisites for achieving divine proximity and success in life. The program concludes with Eid al-Adha greetings and prayers for pilgrims, followed by an introduction to the "Persaman Ateghadi" segment, where the host addresses listeners' questions regarding the proof of prophethood and the Prophet's (PBUH) pre-revelation faith.
