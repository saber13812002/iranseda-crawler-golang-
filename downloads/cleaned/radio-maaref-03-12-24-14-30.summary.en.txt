In the first segment, a Mahdism expert explained that the occultation of Imam Mahdi resulted from political pressure and the need for societal readiness for his reappearance.
He emphasized that, like the Kaaba, the Imam is a focal point, but it is the believers' duty to move toward the religion to facilitate this process.
The expert also described certain hardships as necessary catalysts for spiritual awakening and warned against diminishing the Imam's spiritual status with worldly titles.
In the second segment, the interpretation of Surah Ibrahim, verse 7, highlighted the importance of gratitude for both personal and social blessings, particularly the Islamic Revolution.
The Supreme Leader noted that ignoring the value of blessings is a form of ingratitude, as people often realize their worth only after losing them.
To maintain gratitude, it is suggested to meticulously list and deeply understand the achievements of the Revolution.
This approach helps avoid negative narratives and strengthens the spirit of appreciation for the country's progress.
Finally, a multiple-choice question was posed, identifying the Islamic Revolution as one of God's greatest blessings.
