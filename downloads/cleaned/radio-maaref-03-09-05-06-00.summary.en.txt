In this program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verses 166 to 170 of Surah An-Nisa, emphasizing the divine and angelic testimony to the truth of the Quran and the Prophet's mission. He refutes the Ash'ari view that knowledge is an attribute added to God's essence, arguing instead that the Quran, as a manifestation of divine knowledge, is protected from corruption and satanic interference. The interpretation of the term "sadda" reveals that the disbelievers not only turned away from the truth themselves but also misled others, using this deception to gain social status and material wealth. The Prophet (PBUH) is addressed to all of humanity in these verses, highlighting the universal nature of his mission and the Quran as a divine argument for all people. God warns that those who reject faith will have no path but to hell, where they will remain eternally. Ultimately, believing in the Prophet, who comes from the Creator and Manager of the universe, is a great benefit for humanity itself, rather than serving any need of God.
