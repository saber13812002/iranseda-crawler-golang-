Pilgrims in Arafat issued a statement demanding that all governments sever ties with the Zionist regime and support the Palestinian resistance. The declaration identified Palestine as the central issue of the Islamic world and called for the expulsion of Israel from international organizations. Simultaneously, thousands of Iranian pilgrims in Karbala prayed for the unity of Muslims and the support of the oppressed during the Day of Arafah. In Europe, the Al-Mustaqim Islamic Center was inaugurated in Brussels despite anti-Islamic pressures, while a man was arrested in France for desecrating the Quran. The aid ship Maudlin is moving toward Gaza under Israeli drone surveillance, with the Israeli army declaring readiness to intervene. At the United Nations, the US vetoed a ceasefire resolution, drawing sharp criticism from other members for enabling ongoing crimes in Gaza. The World Health Organization warned that hospitals in Gaza are nearly devoid of medical supplies, with thousands of aid trucks stuck at the borders.
