This radio program explores the concept of "basirat" (insight) through the life of Imam Hassan (PBUH), defining it as the key to distinguishing truth from falsehood in political and social contexts. The speaker highlights the spiritual suffering Imam Hassan endured during his peace treaty with Muawiyah, emphasizing that this decision was a divine wisdom to test the loyalty of his followers rather than a sign of weakness. The text warns that "insightless elites"—individuals with status but lacking awareness—can cause irreparable harm to society, potentially even more than external enemies. Piety is presented as the primary factor in acquiring insight, and the necessity of a conscious, non-blind belief in Imamate is stressed. The close relationship between insight and patience in the face of hardships and divine obedience is also discussed. Finally, the program concludes by referencing the martyrdom and secret burial of Imam Hassan, underscoring the importance of respecting the infallibles and maintaining vigilance against modern satanic plots.
