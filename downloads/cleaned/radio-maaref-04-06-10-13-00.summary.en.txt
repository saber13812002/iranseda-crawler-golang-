This lecture, delivered on the anniversary of Imam Hasan Askari's martyrdom, examines the severe oppression he faced under the Abbasid Caliphate. His enemies labeled him "Ibn al-Rida" to highlight the threat his leadership posed to their rule, subjecting him to repeated imprisonment and attempts on his life, including being placed in a lion's den. Despite these hardships, the Imam established a structured network of deputies and representatives to organize the Shia community for the long occultation of the Mahdi. This organizational framework preserved the community's faith and continuity, ultimately paving the way for the modern system of Velayat-e Faqih. The primary mission of this system is to combat global tyranny and prepare the world for the Mahdi's return. Consequently, contemporary Shias must maintain this unity and actively work to create the necessary conditions for the Imam's appearance worldwide.
