In this radio program, psychologist Dr. Mazrae Shahi emphasizes the importance of avoiding hasty judgments about others. He argues that we cannot truly understand someone's actions unless we have experienced their exact situation and circumstances. Judging based on a single moment or minor behavior is not only incorrect but can also lead to resentment, hurt feelings, and irreparable damage to a person's reputation. Therefore, we must distinguish a person's character from their momentary actions and question only the specific behavior, not their entire being. The expert advises that if we are compelled to share an opinion, we should do so cautiously and with sufficient evidence to prevent the need for later apologies and negative consequences. Ultimately, selecting judges requires great care and deep understanding due to the difficulty and sensitivity of this process.
