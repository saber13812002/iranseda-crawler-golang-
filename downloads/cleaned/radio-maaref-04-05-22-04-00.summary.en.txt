The program began with morning prayers and a sunrise update for Karbala. In the main segment, Professor Vahidpour emphasized the priority of performing obligatory acts, such as the first-time prayer, over recommended acts during the Arbaeen pilgrimage. He advised pilgrims to prepare for the Fajr prayer by waking up on time and having tayammum ready to avoid missing the prayer time. The ruling on reciting the Arbaeen ziyara from a distance was also discussed, noting that it carries significant reward with the proper intention. The Q&A section addressed various rulings, including khums payment, gender reassignment, prayer validity in former residences, and cosmetic surgery. Additionally, the conditions for reciting the iqama while sitting and the rules for women covering their hair or arms during prayer were explained. Finally, the intention for making up missed prayers for the deceased was described as a purely mental act that does not require verbalization.
