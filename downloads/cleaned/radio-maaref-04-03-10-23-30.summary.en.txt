The program begins by interpreting the Quranic verse stating that Satan descends upon liars and sinners, identifying deception as the primary gateway for spiritual misguidance.
Poets who use imaginative and provocative language to slander others are categorized among these misguided individuals who are susceptible to Satanic influence.
The discussion then shifts to the social destruction caused by lies, emphasizing the critical importance of "islah-e dhat al-bayn" (reconciliation) and warning against spreading rumors even under the guise of peace.
The second part of the broadcast includes prayers for the Day of Arafah and expresses gratitude for Iran's independence and strength against foreign powers.
Finally, the speaker criticizes the political apathy of some clerics, advocating for a "living Hajj" that actively addresses the injustices faced by Muslims worldwide.
He argues that Islam is not merely personal worship, and pilgrims must use the occasion to voice their protest against the oppression of America and its allied regimes.
This perspective contrasts with the silence of certain court clerics who avoid discussing the crimes committed against the Muslim community.
