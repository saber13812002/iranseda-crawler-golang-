The program begins by defining "flight" as a fresh start rooted in daily kindness and positive behavior.
The host greets the diverse ethnic groups of Iran, emphasizing national unity and moral conduct.
Guest Hujjat al-Islam Fallah identifies backbiting as a destructive act that is strictly forbidden in Islam.
He cites narrations stating that backbiting about believers who are known for honesty and have no confirmed sins is a grave sin.
According to Imam Sadiq (AS), engaging in backbiting causes a person to exit God's guardianship and fall under Satan's influence.
The primary solution is to avoid curiosity and keep one's inner self pure from the impurities of slander.
Finally, listeners are encouraged to abandon backbiting to regain the spiritual capacity for divine guidance.
