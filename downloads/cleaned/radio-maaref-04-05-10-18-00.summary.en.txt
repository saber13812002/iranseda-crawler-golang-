The radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat" features Hojjat al-Islam Fala discussing the concept of "language" from a Quranic and hadith perspective. The guest outlines a structure that includes general principles, the status of language in religious texts, and its moral harms such as lying and backbiting. He emphasizes the tripartite division of human existence into intellect, imagination, and matter, noting that each level has a corresponding mode of expression. In the physical realm, language is categorized into verbal speech, sensory perception, and body language, all of which require discipline and cultivation. Verbal speech is highlighted as a dynamic, tireless medium that distinguishes humans from animals by facilitating the transfer of intellectual and emotional concepts. The ultimate goal of this series is to prevent verbal vices and improve individual ethics through increased awareness. The program concludes with a literary segment narrating a young man's struggle for professional independence and his encounter with market competition.
