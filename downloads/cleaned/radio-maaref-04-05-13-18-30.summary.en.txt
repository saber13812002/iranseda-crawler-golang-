The radio program "Kolbar" focused on providing religious guidance and services for Arbaeen pilgrims, featuring a session with expert Vahidkooheh.
In the religious rulings segment, it was clarified that menstruation does not prevent women from walking the Arbaeen route or reciting the Quran, though entering the area under the shrine domes is prohibited during this time.
The restriction on entering shrines applies only to the space beneath the domes, while access to other parts of the holy sites and Imamzadeh shrines remains open to all.
The program introduced the six border crossings for exiting Iran, including Mehran, Shalamcheh, Qasr-e Shirin, Chazabeh, Basmaq, and Tamerchin.
A field report from the Chazabeh border highlighted the massive turnout of pilgrims braving extreme heat to reach Karbala for the ziyarat.
Listeners were also encouraged to support the Astan-e Hazrat Masoumeh service camp on the Najaf-Karbala route by sending the number 22 to the SMS code 301122.
The show aims to facilitate the spiritual journey of pilgrims by addressing their religious questions through representatives of the Maraji'.
