Freiba Soleimani, a veteran women's volleyball coach, discussed the structural challenges of the sport in a radio interview. She criticized the lack of commitment in women's volleyball investments, noting that coaches often handle multiple roles for inadequate compensation. Soleimani emphasized the need to standardize the talent scouting process, which currently lacks a unified model for discovering young players. She also pointed out the mistake of using foreign coaches for youth development instead of focusing on senior teams. Despite these issues, she acknowledged recent progress and the continued presence of experienced players in the national team. She argued that achieving self-sufficiency requires utilizing domestic coaches with up-to-date knowledge and increasing oversight of private academies. Finally, she highlighted the crucial role of girls in improving Iran's sports ranking and urged the younger generation to take over from the older generation.
