This radio program clarifies the fundamental distinction between personal financial investment and macroeconomic investment in national accounts. Personal investment, such as buying gold or foreign currency solely for price appreciation, does not necessarily increase the country's total capital stock or generate new production. The Supreme Leader's annual slogan emphasizes the critical need to channel idle domestic capital into productive and real sectors to combat inflation and preserve the value of the national currency. To achieve this, policymakers must remove legal barriers to production and establish transparent mechanisms to guide liquidity toward productive industries. Furthermore, ensuring economic stability and regulatory predictability is essential to encourage investors to accept production risks rather than seeking refuge in non-productive assets. Media outlets also play a vital role in educating citizens about small-scale, home-based entrepreneurship that is both profitable and beneficial to the national economy. Finally, the discussion highlights that distinguishing between calculable risk and unmeasurable uncertainty is key to understanding why economic stability is necessary to shift investment away from speculative assets.
