In this radio program, Dr. Hassan Raeisi Pour discusses the educational legacy of Imam Sajjad (AS) and his role in spiritual awakening after the tragedy of Karbala. The Imam reinterpreted familiar religious concepts to move his audience from mere linguistic knowledge to deep conviction and practical action, thereby preventing moral deviation. He emphasized that the primary purpose of life is spiritual growth and preparation for meeting God, offering a monotheistic philosophy of the human body and social relations. In the Risala al-Huquq, Imam Sajjad redefined the ethical and political framework of society by outlining reciprocal rights, all without directly provoking the ruling government. Although his statements appeared ethical, they carried a strong political message, condemning the world as "dog vomit" to prevent blind obedience to corrupt power. This subtle yet firm approach laid the cultural and ideological groundwork for the subsequent movements led by Imams Baqir and Sadiq. Ultimately, Imam Sajjad transformed the devastated landscape of Karbala into a garden of hope and resistance through his refined and impactful teachings.
