This radio program focused on the moral vice of "shamat," which involves blaming others for their hardships or sins.
A central point emphasized was that a person's problems or misfortunes are never evidence of their bad character or guilt.
According to narrations from the Ahl al-Bayt, anyone who blames others for their faults will eventually be afflicted with the same issue themselves.
Practical solutions to overcome this vice include gaining religious awareness, practicing patience and silence, and avoiding hypocrisy, envy, and arrogance.
It was stressed that while believers should view their own problems as a result of their shortcomings, they must never judge others based on their difficulties.
Finally, the program recommended repentance and seeking forgiveness as the final step to purify the soul and repair damaged relationships.
