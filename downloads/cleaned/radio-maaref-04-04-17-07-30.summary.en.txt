In this radio program, the guest identifies the primary lesson of the uprising of Sayyid and Shahid as "guidance and direction," citing letters from Imam Hasan Askari. The speaker emphasizes that true believers are scarce when it comes to practical action, urging listeners to follow the messages of the Ahl al-Bayt. The second major task of Imam Sajjad (PBUH) was rebuilding a society that had fallen into degradation due to the tyranny of Umayyad rulers. This reconstruction was achieved not through formal classes, but through prayer, love, intercession, and supplication. His third crucial duty was preserving the message of Ashura for history. Throughout his 34-year Imamate, Imam Sajjad kept the memory of Imam Hussein (PBUH) alive through constant weeping and enduring love. Stories of his grief upon seeing food or recalling Karbala symbolize this unwavering devotion. The program concludes by encouraging listeners to keep the sorrow of Hussein (PBUH) fresh in their hearts and to benefit from the conduct of the Ahl al-Bayt.
