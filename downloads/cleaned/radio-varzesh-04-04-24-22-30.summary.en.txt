Pegah Zangeneh, a karate champion and current head coach of the Iranian women's national team, shared her athletic journey in this interview. She recounted pivotal moments in her career, including winning a bronze medal at the 2018 Jakarta Asian Games at age 34 by strategically analyzing her Vietnamese opponent's footage. Zangeneh also described the traumatic accident at the 2010 Guangzhou Asian Games village, where she suffered a head injury and temporary memory loss. She detailed her forced emotional retirement following that incident and her subsequent return to competition, funding her own travel to secure a spot on the national team. As a coach, she highlighted the recent successes of the national team in the World League and the high global rankings of her current athletes. Zangeneh emphasized that her personal experience as a competitor allows her to deeply understand the emotions and challenges faced by her players. Her ultimate goal is to secure prominent gold medals for Iran at the upcoming Nagoya Asian Games and future world championships.
