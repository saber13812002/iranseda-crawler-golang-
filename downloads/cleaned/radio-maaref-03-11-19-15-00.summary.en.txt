This radio program analyzes the severe challenges of summer 1980 as the peak of the Islamic Revolution's crises. During this period, the Islamic Republic faced a combination of external war, continuous assassinations of key figures, and a political vacuum caused by Bani-Sadr's dismissal. The assassination of Ayatollah Beheshti, the martyrdom of Chamran, and the killing of Rajai and Bahonar placed the country on the brink of collapse. Despite these pressures, Imam Khomeini restored political stability by holding immediate elections and relying on spirituality. His calming speeches eliminated the enemies' fear and further unified the nation. This smart management demonstrated that religious beliefs could create resilience against the enemy's combined war.
