In the first segment, expert Mohammad Hadi Falahi discussed the severe consequences of arrogance based on religious narrations, highlighting "humiliation and degradation" as the primary outcome. He explained that arrogance not only severs a person's spiritual connection with God and the Holy Family but also leads to the downfall of high-ranking individuals, ultimately condemning them to hellfire. Citing a sacred hadith, Falahi emphasized that arrogance is the exclusive attribute of God, warning that anyone attempting to usurp this status will face divine wrath. The program then transitioned to the 1404 annual slogan, "Investment for Production," where Dr. Bidar outlined the government's responsibility to facilitate production by removing legal and administrative barriers. He stressed the need for streamlined licensing and tax incentives to encourage public participation in the economy. In the final segment, Mohammad Fekri, head of the beekeepers' union in Khoy, reported that the city has achieved the top rank in honey production. He highlighted the significant increase in honey yield from 7 kilograms to over 50 kilograms per hive and underscored the vital role of beekeeping in agricultural pollination and regional economic growth.
