This radio program invites listeners to send text messages to provide financial aid to a needy family in the name of Imam Reza (AS). The family consists of a mother who supports her bedridden husband and disabled child by selling handmade prayer beads. The host cites religious narrations to describe charity as the head of faith and a source of joy for God and the Prophet. It is emphasized that such acts of kindness should be performed quickly to prevent Satan from creating obstacles. Following the appeal, a segment featuring the recitation of the Ziyarat Al-Yasin and religious supplications was broadcast. The program concluded with a discussion on the necessity of countering political and cultural plots aimed at weakening Islam. It highlighted the enemy's strategy of using drugs to control the youth and undermine the nation's independence.
