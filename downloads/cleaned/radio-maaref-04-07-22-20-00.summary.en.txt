The people of Gaza welcomed the released Palestinian prisoners with enthusiasm, viewing this event as a victory of resistance against the Zionist regime. Simultaneously, the arrival of food supplies and the reopening of the Tawhid Mosque amidst the ruins symbolized the hope and resilience of the Palestinian people. In Pakistan, the live broadcast of an Israeli cabinet meeting on domestic media sparked strong reactions from religious activists and media figures. The inauguration of the Lady Mary Metro station in Tehran was praised as a sign of respect for religious minorities and peaceful coexistence in Iran. The second International Conference on Ethics in Islam and Christianity, attended by scholars from 25 countries, emphasized shared scientific collaboration. The Iranian Minister of Culture, during a meeting with his Turkish counterpart, stressed expanding cultural ties and preparing for a potential visit by Erdogan to Iran. Reports indicate a rise in Islamophobic attacks and the emigration of Muslims from France. Finally, Muslims in Mali continued to safeguard the UNESCO World Heritage site by repairing the historic Djinguereber Mosque.
