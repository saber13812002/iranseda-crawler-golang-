In this interview, Master Ali Akbar Daryabari recounts his memories of the revolution and the pivotal role played by local community members. He reflects on the unwavering support of his family and neighborhood during difficult times and recalls attending a tribute ceremony for Jahan Pahlavan Takti alongside Shahid Rajaee. Daryabari highlights the non-materialistic spirit of past athletes while contrasting it with the commercialized nature of modern sports. His eldest son, Amir Houshang Daryabari, speaks about his father's esteemed status among sports veterans in the 400 Dasteh neighborhood. He cites the attendance of prominent figures like Hassan Habib and Cyrus Jafari at a family memorial as evidence of the deep respect the sports community holds for his father. The son also mentions his father's social contributions as a vice president of the local association and a mentor to the youth. The program concludes by emphasizing Daryabari's profound love for his country and his lifelong dedication to serving the younger generation.
