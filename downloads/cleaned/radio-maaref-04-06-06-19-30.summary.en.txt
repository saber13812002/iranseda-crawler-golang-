This radio program provided jurisprudential answers to listener questions based on the fatwas of various religious authorities. It was explained that missed Ramadan fasts must be made up before the next Ramadan, with a specific expiation required if neglected without a valid excuse. Regarding marriage, Imam Khomeini's ruling was cited, stating that a Shia man may marry a Sunni woman provided the family remains Shia, while a Shia woman marrying a Sunni man is considered disliked. The necessity of intention in wudu was clarified, noting that the intention of proximity to God is sufficient without the need for verbal or precise mental articulation. According to Ayatollah Sistani's fatwa, listeners were advised not to act on doubts regarding the impurity of their clothing. For individuals unable to fast due to illness, the obligation is replaced by paying a fidya for each missed day. Finally, the rules for wudu and ghusl with long nails were discussed, distinguishing between conventional and non-conventional nail lengths regarding the requirement for water to reach the skin underneath.
