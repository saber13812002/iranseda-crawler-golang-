The guest, reflecting on his 25-year career as a sports news anchor, discussed the challenges of delivering sports news and the importance of a friendly tone in connecting with the audience. After leaving the media, he was elected to the eighth parliament from the Izeh and Bagh-e Malek district, achieving a historic record. He considered the establishment of the Ministry of Sports and Youth as the most significant achievement of that term, despite his opposition to including the youth sector. He criticized the lack of a clear, consistent strategy in Iranian sports and the reliance on external personnel for federation leadership. Furthermore, he emphasized the need to strengthen the supervisory role of MPs and avoid politicizing sports issues. The guest described his efforts to address public demands and maintain a direct connection with citizens from across the country during his parliamentary term.
