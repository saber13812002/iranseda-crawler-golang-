In the radio program "Persaman Ateghadi," Professor Mohammadi Shahroudi addressed various theological questions from listeners. He argued against a materialist view, asserting that Paradise and Hell were created beforehand and are visible to the spiritual eye. He emphasized that prayer is ineffective without action, recommending Istighfar and specific supplications for changing one's fate. The program also detailed the Imams' multifaceted roles in guidance, education, and political struggle, comparing their impact favorably to other sects. Expressing regret over the spread of superstition, the professor noted that most alleged cases of sorcery stem from ignorance and psychological factors. He clarified that sorcery cannot alter a person's destiny and can be nullified through devotion and prayer. Finally, listeners were encouraged to tune in to future episodes and share their feedback.
