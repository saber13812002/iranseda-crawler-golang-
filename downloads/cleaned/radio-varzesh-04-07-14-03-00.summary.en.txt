Reza Rajabi, a former Iranian football star and Esteghlal player, recounts his career highlights with the national youth and U-23 teams. He discusses his numerous championships with Esteghlal during the 1950s and 1960s and his international tournament experiences. Rajabi candidly shares his post-retirement struggles, including the loss of his father and a severe 12-year addiction. He credits his brother's support and a spiritual vision from his mother as pivotal in his recovery. Currently, he works as a coach and consultant in lower divisions, emphasizing the critical need for psychological counseling for athletes. He argues that Iranian football suffers from weak management structures and a lack of social support for retired players. Rajabi urges football authorities to establish better support systems for veterans and prioritize player mental health. His story serves as a cautionary tale about the importance of holistic care in professional sports.
