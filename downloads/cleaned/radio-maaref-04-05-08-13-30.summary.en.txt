In this episode of the radio program "Narrative of Love," Zahra Nasiri, the wife of martyr Javad Akbari from Afghanistan, recounts the painful final moments of her husband's life. She describes her intense anxiety after hearing news of a village siege in Syria and noticing the change in her husband's tone and appearance during video calls. Zahra Nasiri recalls her relentless efforts to stay connected with Javad and his repeated requests for prayers on the eve of the final operation. Despite the secrecy of those around her and the initial lies from Javad's brother claiming he was merely wounded, Zahra was eventually confronted with the shock and deep grief of learning about her husband's martyrdom. She speaks of the difficult hours of waiting and her endless tears during those nights as she struggled to accept the reality of her husband's death. At the end of the program, a Quranic commentator interprets a verse regarding the bond of faith among believers, emphasizing the importance of Muslim unity and solidarity to benefit from divine mercy.
