In this radio program, Dr. Ashgari Monfared highlighted the Prophet's birth as a divine investment intended to eliminate tribal grudges and foster brotherhood within the framework of monotheism. He emphasized that unity among Muslims is a vital necessity, arguing that division is the primary cause of weakness and the loss of collective power in the Islamic world. Citing the Quranic verse "do not dispute," he warned that destructive disagreements must be treated as a red line to prevent the collapse of shared strength. Dr. Monfared also noted that the globalization of Islam is an undeniable truth that has prompted enemies to devise new plots against the Muslim community. The program concluded with messages from listeners across Iran, who offered congratulations for Unity Week and stressed the importance of solidarity in countering enemy conspiracies.
