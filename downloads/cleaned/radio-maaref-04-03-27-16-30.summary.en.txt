In this radio program, the relationship between lethargy in worship and disbelief or hypocrisy was discussed.
The guest explained that fatigue in prayer and other religious acts does not necessarily stem from disbelief or hypocrisy, but can result from physiological factors such as insomnia or poor diet.
To illustrate this point, the story of Prophet Yahya and Iblis was narrated, showing that even ascetics may experience lethargy due to a heavy stomach.
Subsequently, the various degrees of hypocrisy and linguistic disbelief were examined, emphasizing that the higher the level of hypocrisy, the greater the lethargy in worship.
The key to achieving vitality in worship and eliminating lethargy was identified as acquiring knowledge and a deep understanding of God.
The second part of the program addressed theological questions from students, focusing on the reasons for the Quran's inimitability from the perspectives of its miraculous nature, historical authenticity, and logical content.
Finally, a listener's phone question regarding the benefit of charity for war martyrs was answered, confirming that such acts reach their spirits as spiritual blessings in the afterlife.
