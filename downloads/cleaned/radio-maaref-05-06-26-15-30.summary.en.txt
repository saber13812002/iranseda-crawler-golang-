This radio program analyzes the Supreme Leader's Nowruz message, emphasizing the decisive role of the people in the ongoing conflict. The speaker reviews three key phases of the previous year, including the Khordad war, the Dey coup, and the Esfand war, noting that the enemy calculated a precise strategy to create fear and despair. The adversary aimed to separate the public from the regime by targeting key commanders and exploiting internal fissures to cause systemic collapse. However, the people's jihad and the establishment of widespread defensive lines neutralized the enemy's strategic calculations. Although Iran suffered tactical losses and the martyrdom of commanders, it successfully prevented the regime's collapse and the loss of its defensive power. The enemy failed to achieve its ultimate goal of dominance over Iran, despite inflicting significant damage. Ultimately, the Leader's message is presented as a roadmap for safely navigating the coming year and achieving victory in this complex, multi-layered game.
