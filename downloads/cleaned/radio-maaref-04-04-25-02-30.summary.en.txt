The radio program began by mentioning the potential for religious events and speeches.
Hamatic and religious texts regarding freedom and sacrifice were recited.
The main focus was the story of Mrs. Hidari and her husband, the martyr Sardar Tehran.
This narrative was presented as a model of love and resilience in life's hardships.
The host emphasized that this story serves as a life lesson for the audience.
The goal of sharing these memories is to build spiritual resilience against daily challenges.
The program concluded with an invitation to learn from these life models and a farewell.
