This radio segment analyzes verses 109 and 110 of Surah Al-Ma'idah, focusing on the prophets' knowledge of deeds and the preservation of believers' dignity. The commentator argues that while God may hide certain sins from the sinners' memory to protect their honor, this does not imply that the prophets are unaware of these acts. The Quran and established traditions confirm that prophets witness the deeds of their communities, contradicting any claim that they lack such knowledge. While prayers like Dua Kumayl suggest that angels may be restricted from knowing all sins, this does not apply to the prophets, whose awareness is affirmed by the Quran. The discussion also highlights the global miracle of Jesus's birth without a father and his divine empowerment through the Holy Spirit. It is clarified that Jesus's miracles were direct manifestations of God's power rather than mere answered prayers. Finally, the distinction is made between the miraculous nature of his speech as an infant and the miraculous content of his words in adulthood.
