In this program, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the necessity of Shia-Sunni unity to counter imperialist plots, citing Imam Ali (AS) as a practical model for inter-sect interaction. He recounted a story of Ayatollah Naeini Shirazi, who sent a student to Karbala to prevent him from sowing sectarian discord in Samarra. In the second part, Professor Bagher Sahabi examined the status of Sheikh al-Ishraq in Imam Khomeini's thought, noting that the Imam regarded him as a rare sage who attained the rank of intellect and mystical insight. Sahabi stated that while Imam Khomeini critiqued certain views, he extensively utilized Sheikh al-Ishraq's philosophical and mystical works, even recommending his books to the President of Yugoslavia in a letter. It was also highlighted that Sheikh al-Ishraq's Shia beliefs are evident in his writings, and his martyrdom resulted from his opposition to the rulers' views on authority. Finally, it was emphasized that Imam Khomeini considered Wilayat (Guardianship) as the essential condition for the realization of the kingdom and spiritual elevation, deeming ascetic practices insufficient without this connection.
