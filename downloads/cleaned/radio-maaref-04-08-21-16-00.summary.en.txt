In the radio program "Pors-o-Pas Ateghadi," hosted by Professor Mohammadi Shadoudi, several key theological questions were addressed. The program clarified that the phrase "except what your Lord wills" in the Quran does not negate the permanence of Paradise and Hell, but rather emphasizes the unlimited power of God. It was explained that while eternal residence in Paradise is certain, God may forgive even disbelievers due to His infinite grace. The difference between the lunar and solar calendars was noted to be approximately ten days, with the lunar calendar serving as the primary basis for Islamic rulings such as fasting and puberty. Regarding the absence of a new prophet during contemporary injustices, the professor stated that the purpose of prophets is guidance rather than physical intervention, and the Quran and Sunnah provide a complete roadmap for humanity. He also highlighted that much of the current suffering is exacerbated by the silence of Muslim rulers, noting that the world is fundamentally a place of trial. On the topic of repentance, it was advised that making amends for wrongs is essential, and subsequent hardships are not necessarily direct punishments for specific sins. Finally, Prophet Joseph's prayer was interpreted as a conditional statement highlighting the need for divine assistance, rather than an expression of shame or a demand for a specific outcome.
