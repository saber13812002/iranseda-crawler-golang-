The guest, a veteran goalkeeper, shares his memories of playing for the Akbatan football team. He explains that Akbatan was formed with private financial support and a mix of young and experienced players, lacking government backing. The team achieved the championship in the Tehran club league in its first year before competing in the national second division. Despite better financial offers from larger clubs, the guest remained loyal to Akbatan for nearly fifteen years due to his bond with his teammates. He also recalls his participation in the national youth, junior, and hope teams, including matches during the Wall of China tournament. The outbreak of the Iran-Iraq war caused his generation to step away from professional football for a period. Finally, he expresses gratitude to the team's managers and private sponsors who kept the club alive through their personal investments.
