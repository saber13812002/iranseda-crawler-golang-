The Shurtat al-Khamis was introduced as the first organized military and police force in Islamic history, established by Imam Ali (AS) to maintain security and enforce laws.
The number of members in this group varies across sources, and their primary duties included vanguard roles in war and maintaining order during peace.
On the night of Imam Ali's martyrdom, the Shurtat members moved from their homes to the mosque to protect him but arrived late due to the Imam's unusual entry route.
