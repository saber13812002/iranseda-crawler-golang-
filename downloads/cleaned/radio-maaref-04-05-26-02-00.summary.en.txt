The first segment of the program highlights the spiritual significance of silence and language management, citing the teachings of Ayatollah Behjat as a path to divine growth.
Even when forbidden from performing night prayers by his father, Ayatollah Behjat maintained his spiritual progress through profound silence and avoiding idle talk.
In the second segment, Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi, speaking from Karbala, reported on the high motivation of the Iranian people and the explicit support from Iraqis following the recent conflict.
He dismissed rumors of an imminent war, emphasizing that strong military readiness serves as the most effective deterrent against aggression.
Araghchi explained the legal challenges arising from the European activation of the snapback mechanism, noting their failure to uphold their commitments under the JCPOA.
While expressing concern over the potential return of UN Security Council sanctions, he clarified that these are largely list-based and do not necessarily imply harsher economic restrictions than the current status quo.
Finally, regarding cooperation with the IAEA, Araghchi stressed the necessity of establishing a new inspection framework for bombed nuclear facilities, in accordance with the law passed by the Iranian Parliament.
