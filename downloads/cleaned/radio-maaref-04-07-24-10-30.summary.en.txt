In the radio program "Shafa Roo," Dr. Pourhosseini emphasized that consuming cold or iced water in autumn is harmful according to traditional medicine, advising listeners to avoid water purifiers due to potentially harmful minerals. He also noted that adding metals to water to increase mineral content is not scientifically or traditionally supported, although drinking from specific metal vessels may be beneficial. In the "One Drop from the Sea" segment, Hujjat al-Islam Rasoul stressed the importance of respectful and beautiful speech, citing Quran 17:53 to warn that bad language invites Satan's interference and conflict. He provided practical examples from married life to demonstrate how changing one's tone can reduce domestic tension. The program also highlighted the "Iran Jan, Fars Iran" campaign, praising Fars province and Shiraz for their rich cultural heritage and tourism potential, including being the birthplace of poets like Hafez and Saadi. The broadcast concluded with a musical piece and a speech by Imam Khomeini.
