In this segment, the story of Prophet Idris's occultation is examined as a parallel to the occultation of Imam Mahdi, based on narrations from Sheikh Saduq. The narrative begins with a tyrannical king who seizes Idris's land through false testimony and orders his execution. After receiving divine revelation of impending retribution, Idris leaves the city, and God withholds rain from the region for twenty years as a punishment. The people, suffering from severe drought and famine, repent for their sins and plead with God for rain. However, Idris, hiding in a cave, refrains from requesting rain to ensure the people undergo genuine spiritual transformation and complete repentance. His patience and insistence on true reform highlight the importance of sincere change in behavior. Ultimately, God instructs Idris to ask for rain, leading to the acceptance of the people's repentance and the return of rainfall. This story illustrates that occultation serves as an opportunity for self-improvement and returning to God.
