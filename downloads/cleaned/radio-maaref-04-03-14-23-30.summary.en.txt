In this radio broadcast aired on the eve of the Day of Arafah and Eid al-Adha, the host emphasizes the spiritual significance of visiting Imam Hussein (PBUH) and reciting the Arafah prayer. Listeners are encouraged to send a text message to a specific number to sponsor a sacrificial animal in the name of their own and their families' health, particularly on the night of Imam Reza's (PBUH) martyrdom. The host urges the audience to seize these fleeting spiritual opportunities, as the future is uncertain and time is short. The program includes recitations from the Ziyarat Al-Yasin and religious poetry praising Imam Reza (PBUH) and the Ahl al-Bayt. A segment of the discussion critiques religious and political sectarianism, framing it as a conspiracy by Islam's enemies to weaken Muslim unity. The host argues that differences between Sunni and Shia should not hinder solidarity against external threats. The broadcast concludes with prayers for the destruction of Islam's enemies and the preservation of the Islamic Republic, ending with final verses and supplications.
