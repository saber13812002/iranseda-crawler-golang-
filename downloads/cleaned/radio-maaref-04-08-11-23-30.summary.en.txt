Ayatollah Tabasi shares memories of his father, a scholar in Najaf, who raised twelve children under difficult economic conditions. His father gained the respect of prominent scholars like Ayatollah Na'ini by providing insightful answers to jurisprudential questions during his studies. The father's deep devotion to the Ahl al-Bayt, particularly Imam Hussein, was evident in his specific fasts and persistent visits to Karbala, even during government-imposed travel bans. He maintained his presence in Karbala every Thursday by reserving buses in advance, demonstrating both dedication and dignity. The narrator also highlights his father's humility and his role in resolving tribal conflicts in Najaf through his respected status. The program concludes with the recitation of Ziyarat al-Yasin and a critique of international organizations and the US role in fostering corruption in the Islamic world. The broadcast ends with a preview of upcoming night programs and prayers for the appearance of the Mahdi.
