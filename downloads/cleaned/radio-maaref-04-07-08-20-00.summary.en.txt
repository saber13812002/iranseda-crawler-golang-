The Global Sumud Flotilla has departed from Spain with the aim of breaking the Gaza blockade and delivering humanitarian aid, now comprising over fifty ships. Meanwhile, Qatar announced that it has delivered Trump's ceasefire plan for Gaza to Hamas, which the Israeli Prime Minister has also accepted. The Iraqi Prime Minister visited the Wadi Hauran region to confirm full security control over the western borders following the withdrawal of US forces. In Pakistan, thousands marched on the first anniversary of Sayed Hassan Nasrallah's martyrdom to express opposition to US and Israeli policies. Forty-eight European football players signed a petition demanding the exclusion of the Israeli team from international competitions. The French government further restricted religious freedom by closing a mosque in Marseille under the pretext of supporting Palestine. In Indonesia, construction has begun on the largest eco-friendly mosque, built from recycled materials. Additionally, an Islamic tourism exhibition is being held in the capital of Cambodia to promote the country to Muslim travelers.
