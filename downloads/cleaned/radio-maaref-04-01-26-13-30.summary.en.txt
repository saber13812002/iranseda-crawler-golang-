In this episode of the "Mashq-e Rah" program, Professor Seyed Ahmad Faqih discussed the true essence of prayer and its role in strengthening one's connection with God. He emphasized that supplication should not be merely a transactional tool for fulfilling material needs but should instead enhance spiritual awareness and devotion. Wudu (ablution) was described as a spiritual purification that removes internal barriers and improves the quality of prayer. In response to listener questions, abandoning sins was identified as the most critical step in self-improvement and drawing closer to the Divine. Performing the first-time prayer was highlighted as the best means of proximity to God, while reciting the Quran was noted as a powerful deterrent against sin. Additionally, seeking intercession from the holy Imams and weeping for Imam Hussein were cited as definitive paths to spiritual closeness. To address the issue of neglecting prayer, the professor recommended deeply understanding its significance and gradually studying the book "Towards You."
