In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Dr. Naser Rafiei interpreted verses 58 and 59 of Surah Ar-Rum, emphasizing the vital role of metaphor in the Holy Quran. He highlighted that the Quran uses simple and tangible examples, such as flies, mosquitoes, and spider webs, to help audiences better understand complex concepts. Dr. Rafiei cited Quranic metaphors, such as comparing shirk to a spider's web and Jewish scholars to a donkey carrying books, to demonstrate how the Quran conveys deep theological and ethical ideas in an accessible language. The speaker also referenced the story of Walid ibn Mughirah, who, despite recognizing the Quran's miraculous nature, rejected it due to stubbornness and prejudice, leading to his Quranic condemnation. He concluded by stressing the importance of reflection on the Quran's verses and recited parts of the prayer for the reappearance of the Imam and Ashura poetry. This program is part of the "Bar Bal-e Sakhn" series, aimed at promoting religious and cultural thinking.
