In the "Zamzam Ahkam" program, Professor Vahidpour emphasized the jurisprudential obligation for Arbaeen pilgrims to strictly adhere to the host country's laws, viewing non-compliance as a breach of the implicit contract formed upon entry. He urged respect for security inspections and prohibitions on photography within holy shrines to preserve the spiritual atmosphere and prevent negative perceptions of Iranian pilgrims. In the Q&A segment, participating in the Arbaeen walk was declared a frequently recommended act, and reciting the Arbaeen prayer on days close to the 40th was also considered meritorious. It was clarified that the Arbaeen prayer is not restricted to a specific location and can be recited anywhere in the world. Regarding mixed-gender sports, the activity is permissible provided there is no harm, such as improper gaze or inadequate clothing. Ayatollah Soubhani's rulings were cited, stating that cosmetic surgery is permissible outside of menstruation periods and that the prostration of gratitude is allowed in all conditions. Finally, it was explained that the Fitr Zakat is obligatory for those with financial means, but those who cannot afford it have no debt for past years and only need to pay for the current year once their financial situation improves.
