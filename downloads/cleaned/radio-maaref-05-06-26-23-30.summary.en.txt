The radio program begins with the narrative of a desert dweller who, after his camel and jewels are robbed by bandits, uses a trick to lure them into a well and recovers his wealth. He then engages in a transaction with an infertile woman who claims to be a sorceress, a story that concludes with religious advice on monotheism and abandoning magic. The second segment pays tribute to the martyrs of East Azerbaijan province and includes the recitation of the Ziyarat of Imam Mahdi. In the final part, a philosophical discussion on "white lies" is held with a guest scholar. It is argued that lying is only permissible when it saves innocent lives or prevents greater corruption, according to reason and religious law. The segment also emphasizes the importance of sincerity and divine motivation in performing good deeds to achieve true success.
