In the first segment, a philosophy professor explains the concept of "cosmic glorification" using the fictional story of termites and a real-life incident of a sparrow saving its chicks from a snake. He argues that creatures, despite their limited awareness, perceive the perfection of God's creation through instinct and gradual evolution, which constitutes this glorification. The discussion concludes by emphasizing that the knowledge of this cosmic praise belongs to God, not the creatures themselves. The second part of the program introduces the wife of Martyr Ali Aghazadeh, who comes from a family that produced multiple martyrs in the Holy Defense and shrine defense. She recounts their courtship, simple marriage, and deep religious and cultural compatibility during their student years. She shares memories of their initial concerns and shared patience and gratitude. This section ends by highlighting the pure love and commitment to religious values in their shared life.
