This radio program discusses the ethical recommendations of Imam Hasan Askari (AS) regarding interactions with others.
The Imam emphasized six key principles: piety, honesty, trustworthiness, prolonged prostration, good neighborliness, and good character.
It was stressed that Shia Muslims should maintain their doctrinal boundaries while treating all Muslims, including Sunnis, with love and respect.
The noble behavior and ethics of Shia are considered the best advertisement for attracting people's affection and pleasing the Imam of the Time (AJ).
The second part of the program focused on the concept of asceticism (Zuhd) and the remembrance of death, clarifying that it does not mean abandoning the world.
True asceticism means using the world as a bridge to the Hereafter and avoiding enslavement to wealth and status.
The Prophet and Imams view the world as a prelude to gaining divine pleasure, emphasizing lawful earning and serving humanity.
Finally, the remembrance of death was introduced as a tool for cultivating asceticism and focusing on spiritual values.
