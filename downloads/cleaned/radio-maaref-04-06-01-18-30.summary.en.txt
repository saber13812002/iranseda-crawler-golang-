In this radio program, the Quran is cited to rebuke the People of the Book for arguing over trivial matters while ignoring clear truths. The discussion emphasizes avoiding futile debates and focuses on the vital importance of prayer, citing a hadith where the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) states that prayer can serve as a shield against divine punishment on the Day of Judgment. It is highlighted that prayer not only forgives sins but also guides believers away from sin, a sentiment echoed in the ethical advice of Imam Khomeini’s daughter regarding the priority of prayer. The program also honors the life and martyrdom of Sardar Hassan Bagheri, a key commander in the Iran-Iraq War, noting his deep faith, calmness under pressure, and devotion to the Ahl al-Bayt. His leadership in major military operations and his personal piety are presented as exemplary traits for the audience. The broadcast concludes with the recitation of verses from the Quran, reinforcing the themes of divine unity and the spiritual light brought by prayer and scripture into one's life.
