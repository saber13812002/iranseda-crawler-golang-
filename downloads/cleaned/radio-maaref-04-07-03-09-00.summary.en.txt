In this program, Professor Abutalibi attributes the inaction of regional countries regarding Israel to their political and economic dependence on Western powers and the United States. He emphasizes that these regimes support Israel through silence or cooperation rather than aiding Palestine, and even confront Iran, the sole axis of resistance. In the second segment, Iran's potential withdrawal from the NPT was discussed as a tool to protect national interests and prevent the activation of the snapback mechanism. Professor Abutalibi clarified that this withdrawal does not imply nuclear weapon development but serves to reduce harmful IAEA oversight and preserve Iran's peaceful nuclear industry. Subsequently, the Supreme Leader's response to a query about procurement officials' misconduct was shared, stressing that discounts belong to the organization. The necessity of strengthening the country's military and scientific capabilities, along with the futility of negotiating with the US under current conditions, was also debated. Finally, the lack of appeal in textbooks for students was addressed, with modern solutions like podcasts and animations proposed to enhance the learning process.
