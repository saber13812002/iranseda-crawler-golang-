This radio segment highlights the central role of "religious zeal" in Iranian history and the defense of national identity.
Key historical figures such as Mirza Shirazi, Ayatollah Lari, and Ayatollah Kashani are presented as models of religious defense and resistance against colonialism.
The Prophet Muhammad is cited as having identified this zeal, inherited from Abraham, as one of the most important characteristics of divine prophets.
The contrast between a young Muslim's steadfast prayer and his polytheist parents illustrates how religious zeal serves as a powerful motivator even under severe hardship.
Listeners shared their daily prayers and supplications for the early return and well-being of the Imam of the Age.
The segment concludes with the story of a mother sending her son to the front lines with a greeting to Lady Zahra, symbolizing the cultivation of a resilient generation.
Ultimately, the discussion asserts that sensitivity to preserving one's faith is the key to achieving the status of supporting God's religion and the infallible Imam during the occultation.
