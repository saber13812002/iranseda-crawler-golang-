The radio program "Hekayat-e Azadegi" features the memoirs of Hassan Ali Shabani, a former POW from Qom who joined the front lines in 1984 despite his family's objections. He recounts his capture in the Qasr-e Shirin region and the severe hardships of interrogation, including blindfolding and physical abuse. In the prisoner camps, the enemy attempted to break the captives' will through humiliation and strict restrictions, yet Shabani endured through faith and patience. Another segment of the program discusses the spiritual and practical benefits of earning a halal livelihood, citing examples of divine protection for those who adhere to religious laws. The broadcast also highlights the significance of the recent mass gatherings of the Iranian people as a display of national unity and strength. The program concludes by emphasizing the enduring hope and resilience of the freed prisoners during their darkest moments.
