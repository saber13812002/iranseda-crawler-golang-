The program began with congratulations for the Holy Defense Week before analyzing a lecture by Hojjat al-Islam Rashed Yazdi on regretting the past and fearing the future. Psychologists argue that these two factors reduce both the duration and quality of human life, reducing existence to mere survival. In contrast, Islam defines life as a vertical process aimed at reaching God, which requires awareness and proper management. The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) and the Quran provide practical solutions for escaping futile grief by offering a model of joyful living and divine peace. Regretting the unchangeable past and fearing the unknown future only lead to spiritual and physical exhaustion. Therefore, individuals should focus on the present moment and enjoy its joys by relying on faith and effective self-management.
