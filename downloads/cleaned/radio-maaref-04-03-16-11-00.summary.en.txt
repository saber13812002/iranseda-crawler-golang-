The Eid al-Adha prayer was held across Iran with massive public attendance, led by Hujjat al-Islam Abotrabifard at the University of Tehran. Iranian pilgrims in Mina and at the Jamarat completed their Hajj rituals, including stoning the pillars, sacrificing animals, and shaving their heads. The Mehran border was the country's busiest crossing, with over 50,000 people traveling to Karbala in the last three days. The Jamkaran Mosque and the Welfare Organization encouraged the public to financially participate in a campaign for the health and reappearance of Imam Mahdi. The Veterinary Organization deployed thousands of experts across 468 counties to ensure the hygiene of animal slaughter. The fourth International Conference on the Theology of Arbaeen will be held at Qom University next week with international guests. Statistics from 2024 indicate that Fatemeh and Mohammad were the most popular names for newborns. In a radio program, Professor Farzad emphasized the importance of celebrating joy and festivals during the period between Eid al-Adha and Eid al-Mubahala.
