The radio program "Enduring Memories" began by honoring the martyrs of the Holy Defense and emphasizing spirituality and perseverance. Sardar Khalghi recounted the prayer of Martyr Bonyadi before the Fajr operation and the dramatic rescue of Martyr Zin-ol-Din from enemy lines. Sardar Kajee shared the moving story of Martyr Farahmarzi, who remained pain-free while reciting the Quran despite his body being torn apart by shrapnel. Wounded veteran Ali Maleki Nejad described Operation Karbala 5, highlighting Martyr Karimi's desire for a martyrdom that would allow him to stand before Imam Hussein with a fully devoted body. A short story titled "Alive Underwater" detailed the survival of a soldier who hid in the water for twelve days and was eventually rescued by a comrade. The program concluded by emphasizing the importance of reading books to reduce anxiety and invited listeners to join the Radio Ma'aref channel on the Eitaa messaging platform.
