In this Radio Ma'aref broadcast, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli analyzes the legal implications of marriage, lease, and loan contracts within the context of Surah Ma'ad. He argues that any condition contradicting the intrinsic nature of a contract, such as stipulating non-ownership in a sale, renders the contract void and invalid. Consequently, referendums and expert council elections are deemed legally void because they conflict with the divine principle of Wilayat and the acceptance of religious authority. The speaker clearly distinguishes between "wakalah" (agency), which terminates upon the principal's death, and "wilayat" (authority), which remains valid when appointed by the Imam. The authority of the Faqih is established not through popular vote or as an agent of the people, but as a deputy of the Imam of the Age based on divine command. Citizens are obligated to recognize and accept the Faqih's authority as a divine right rather than creating it through electoral processes. The program concludes by emphasizing that the Wilayat al-Faqih is rooted in the divine authority of "Anna Wali Allah" and is fundamentally distinct from the guardianship of minors described in the Quran.
