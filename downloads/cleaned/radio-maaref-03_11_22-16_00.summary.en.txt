In this radio program, Dr. Fatemeh Heydari emphasized the distinction between active and passive users in cyberspace, urging parents to transform their children from passive consumers into active content producers. She highlighted the necessity of fostering critical thinking and questioning in adolescents to help them resist the negative influences of media and blind imitation of digital role models. Heydari also stressed the importance of adjusting parental supervision according to the child's age and maturity, suggesting that independence within family values should replace rigid, authoritarian control. She offered creative strategies such as puppet shows, role-playing games, and fostering intimate family dialogue to manage anger and teach emotional skills effectively. The discussion also criticized the practice of teachers announcing grades aloud in class, identifying it as a source of significant stress and damage to students' self-esteem. Finally, to counter the rapid influence of peers on adolescents, the expert recommended strengthening self-confidence and the ability to logically analyze issues rather than blindly accepting the opinions of others.
