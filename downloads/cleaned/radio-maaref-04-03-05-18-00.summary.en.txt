In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Baqiryan emphasizes the necessity of abandoning anger and rage driven by ego to maintain individual and social security. Citing the hadith "Do not be angry at anyone," he explains that anger stemming from self-love damages one's relationship with God and must be eradicated through repentance and mercy. A key point is that the Prophet's (PBUH) command applies to all people regardless of ethnic, religious, or cultural differences, prohibiting ego-driven anger in any circumstance. The second part of the discussion focuses on the divine rewards (Hasana) in this world and the hereafter for performing righteous deeds. Referring to Quranic verses, Baqiryan states that God rewards at least tenfold for every good action. He cites Friday prayers and respecting parents as examples of such deeds that yield positive worldly consequences. Finally, it is emphasized that benevolence and kindness are the keys to receiving divine rewards and achieving inner and outer peace.
