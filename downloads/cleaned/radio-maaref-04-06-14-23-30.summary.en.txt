This radio program discusses the technical definition of "Ismah" (infallibility) and distinguishes it from "free will" and "compulsion." The speaker argues that infallibility does not mean the removal of free will or the forced creation of actions by God; rather, it is an intrinsic existential perfection stemming from the strength of the infallible's knowledge and will. The ontological structure of action and omission is identical for both the infallible and the non-infallible, as both operate through knowledge and will, but the infallible's faculties are perfected to prevent error. Consequently, infallibility is a qualitative internal trait that ensures the infallible's will and knowledge consistently align with obedience and away from sin. The speaker also clarifies that "knowledge of the unseen" does not imply knowing everything in an actual state, but rather that divine will determines when the infallible accesses this knowledge. Finally, the discussion concludes with the importance of Islamic unity, standing firm against external powers, and upholding the primary purpose of the prophets, which is the love of God.
