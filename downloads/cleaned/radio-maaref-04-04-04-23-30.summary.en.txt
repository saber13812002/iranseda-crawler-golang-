The radio broadcast began with recitations of Ashura poetry and religious prayers, transitioning into a tribute to recent martyrs.
The host highlighted the martyrdom of military commanders, nuclear scientists, and civilians during the Zionist regime's attack, urging the public to attend a rally on Saturday, July 28, at Tehran's Revolution Square.
This gathering aims to renew the pledge to the Islamic Revolution's ideals and demonstrate readiness to defend the homeland against enemies.
The program also featured religious narratives, speeches, and news updates from Iran and around the world.
The host outlined the full morning schedule for Radio Ma'aref, inviting listeners to tune in to various prayer and jurisprudence segments.
The broadcast concluded with the recitation of Imam Khomeini's words and morning prayers.
The overall tone of the program blended mourning for Muharram with solidarity for the martyrs of the defense.
