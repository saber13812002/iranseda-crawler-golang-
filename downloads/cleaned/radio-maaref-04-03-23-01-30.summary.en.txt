The text begins with a harsh critique of the corrupt ruling system, arguing that Islam has been reduced to a useless shell where injustice and discrimination have replaced justice.
Citing the words of Imam Ali (AS), it describes a society dominated by the poverty of the masses, the greed of capitalists, and the pursuit of power over religious values.
Referencing Islamic history and the role of Imam Sadiq (AS), the author warns that without true leadership and asceticism, religion becomes an empty name and corruption replaces divine values.
The second part of the text narrates the novel "Left Behind by the Son," set in 1963 during the 15 Khordad uprising.
Salar, the main character, faces financial and emotional struggles after his son Sohrab is wounded in the protests and seeks help from Farshad, a childhood friend.
Azar, Salar's wife, maintains a secret relationship with Colonel Rostami, a security officer, which is exposed when the Colonel's wife unexpectedly appears.
This revelation leaves Azar deeply ashamed and terrified of her secret being discovered, placing her in a difficult predicament.
In the end, Salar is forced to sell his assets to treat his son, grappling with despair and poverty as he loses hope for Sohrab's recovery.
