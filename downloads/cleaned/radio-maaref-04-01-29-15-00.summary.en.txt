The "Strategy" program, featuring Professor Rasakhi, examined the Supreme Leader's youth-oriented strategy and its impact on Iran's national progress.
The most significant achievement of this policy was the localization and replication of nuclear knowledge by the young, revolutionary generation during the era of sanctions.
Professor Rasakhi emphasized that trusting young people in key positions and supporting their creativity led to major developments in missile, satellite, and defense sectors.
The establishment of knowledge-based companies and science parks was also introduced as a direct product of this youth-oriented approach in the economic and industrial sectors.
The Supreme Leader prevented generational gaps and cycles of violence in power transitions by advancing this process calmly and gradually.
The six key principles of this strategy include trusting youth, appointing them to important posts, forming advisory councils, meritocracy, supporting innovation, and capacity building.
In the final segment of the program, Dr. Zahra Sarbaz Hosseini discussed traditional medicine methods for combating spring seasonal allergies.
She recommended using masks to prevent pollen intake, nasal irrigation with normal saline, and consuming almonds and figs to strengthen the brain.
