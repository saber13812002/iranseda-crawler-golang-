In this program, Imam Reza (PBUH) outlines five key characteristics that identify God's chosen servants. The first trait is serving others without expecting anything in return and forgetting the act of service. The second characteristic is refraining from making excuses or shifting blame when committing sins, and instead seeking immediate forgiveness. The third trait involves constant gratitude for God's blessings and focusing on what one has rather than what is missing. The fourth quality is patience and resilience in the face of life's inevitable ups and downs and hardships. Finally, the fifth trait is showing forgiveness and letting go of grudges during moments of anger. The speaker emphasizes the importance of prompt repentance using the analogy of washing a fresh blood stain before it sets. He also urges the audience to recognize their true standing before God and avoid denying His verses. These traits not only signify closeness to God but also pave the way for benefiting from the intercession of the Ahl al-Bayt (PBUH).
