In this program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verse 120 of Surah Al-Ma'idah, focusing on the spiritual rank of "Mukhlasin" and the life of Prophet Joseph. He explains that Joseph reached a level of monotheistic perfection where Satan had no power over him, viewing all existence as a manifestation of God's will. This state granted him profound inner peace and absolute trust in God, allowing him to remain calm even when thrown into a pit or imprisoned. Joseph demonstrated practical monotheism by forgiving his brothers and seeking forgiveness for them, serving as a model of divine mercy. The discussion also highlights the prayer of Prophet Moses in Surah Al-Qasas, where he asks God to use His blessings to support the oppressed. These prayers reflect the deep connection between prophets and God, specifically requesting the grace to die as a Muslim. The ultimate goal is to attain a state where one is constantly in God's presence, free from worldly fears.
