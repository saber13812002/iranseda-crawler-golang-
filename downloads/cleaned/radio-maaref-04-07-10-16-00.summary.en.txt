In the radio program "Revolution Q&A," Dr. Abutalibi argues that negotiating with the United States is neither rational nor honorable, citing the Supreme Leader's views. He asserts that America's true objective is Iran's submission rather than mutual agreement, noting that the JCPOA experience proved the US's lack of commitment to its obligations. The inaction of regional countries regarding Israel stems from their political and economic dependence on Western powers and their fear of losing power. The program also introduced a book on the methodology of understanding Imam Khomeini's political thought, which proposes an ijtihad-based approach to prevent misinterpretation. The relationship between Iran and the US is described as that of a wolf and a sheep, rooted in a fundamental clash between secular and divine systems. These differences are inherent and unsolvable due to Iran's geopolitical significance and America's desire for regional dominance. The only viable solution is maintaining independence and resisting Western hegemony, unless the US abandons its policy of coercion.
