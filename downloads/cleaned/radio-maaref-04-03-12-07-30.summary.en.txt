In this radio program, Dr. Ahmad Gholami discusses the significance of Hajj and the virtue of humility based on Islamic narrations. He explains that according to Imam Sadiq (AS), obligatory duties and social responsibilities take precedence over recommended acts like repeat Hajj if they conflict. The speaker warns that humility performed for worldly gain or non-divine purposes leads to spiritual loss and a lack of divine connection. Conversely, true humility for the sake of God, along with the poor relying on God rather than the wealthy, is highly praised. He emphasizes that following the spiritual guidance of the Infallibles is crucial to avoid spiritual pitfalls and social issues. The program concludes by urging listeners to maintain pure intentions in all social interactions to achieve closeness to God.
