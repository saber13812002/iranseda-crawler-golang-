Ashoori Golmohammadi, a former national team player and basketball coach, shared his career highlights in a radio interview. He recalled the success of the Azad University team in the top league and highlighted Dr. Ghadimi's crucial support for university sports. Golmohammadi also described his coaching experiences at Paykan and Homa clubs, mentioning innovations like video analysis for team improvement. He spoke about the hardships following his first wife's passing and how his second wife supported his professional journey. He identified the golden generation of Iranian basketball, featuring players like Hamad Hadadi and Mehdi Kamrani, as the sport's peak era. Golmohammadi emphasized that while coaching the national team is a distant goal, maintaining strong human relationships with players is paramount to him.
