In this broadcast from Radio Ma'aref, Dr. Fatemeh Heydari emphasizes the importance of media literacy and managing "information overload" among teenagers. She warns that unrealistic superhero movie archetypes can encourage dangerous behaviors in children, suggesting parents fill this gap by introducing real-life Iranian heroes. Additionally, she advises removing unnecessary channels to reduce the stress caused by the high volume of news. In the Q&A section, Heydari stresses the need for a balance between praise and disappointment to prevent teenage arrogance. She explains that appreciation should be expressed through gaze, words, and companionship rather than just material gifts. Regarding anger management, she recommends setting clear boundaries and engaging in calm dialogue after the teenager's emotions subside. Finally, parents must act as behavioral models by avoiding their own outbursts and maintaining realistic expectations while understanding the biological changes in a teenager's brain.
