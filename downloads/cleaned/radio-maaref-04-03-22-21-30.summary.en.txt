In this address, Imam Khomeini characterizes the imposed war not as an aggression but as a religious and human duty to defend the nation, faith, and sovereignty. He emphasizes that despite the hardships, the conflict has awakened the youth and fostered unprecedented unity and dynamism within the Iranian people. The Imam refutes Western media propaganda that portrayed Iran as chaotic, asserting that the country is more cohesive today than during the Pahlavi era and that recent victories prove the strength of faith and military unity. He warns that the primary objective of the enemy, supported by the United States, is to sow discord among Muslims and exploit regional resources for their own interests. The Imam urges regional governments to cease supporting Saddam Hussein and to resist American plots aimed at creating divisions among Islamic nations. Finally, he calls upon the Army and the Revolutionary Guards to maintain strict unity, avoid internal conflicts, and rely on God to navigate the enemy's traps while defending the Islamic revolution.
