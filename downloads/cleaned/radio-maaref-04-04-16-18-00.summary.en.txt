This radio program examines the distinction between the jurisprudential validity and the divine acceptance of charity.
It emphasizes that while an act may be legally valid, it is not accepted by God without faith and obedience to divine laws.
Neglecting prayer is identified as a primary indicator of apostasy that prevents charity from being accepted.
The Quran explicitly states in Surah Tawbah that the charity of those who deny God and the Prophet and do not pray is rejected.
Injustice, oppression, showing off, and hypocrisy are also cited as factors that invalidate charitable acts.
The conclusion is that true monotheistic charity must stem from faith, prayer, and a sincere intention free from hypocrisy.
The final segment of the program discusses the history of women's participation in mourning ceremonies since the Qajar era.
This involvement is recognized as a turning point in women's social and religious engagement in these rituals.
