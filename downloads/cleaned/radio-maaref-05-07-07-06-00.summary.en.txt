Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verses 47 to 51 of Surah Al-A'raf, focusing on the status of the "Mawzun" and the barrier between Paradise and Hell. Citing authentic narrations from both Shia and Sunni traditions, he identifies the Mawzun as Imam Ali (PBUH), emphasizing the honor of being God's spokesperson. A key point is that the barrier prevents physical entry but does not obstruct sight or conversation, as the men of Al-A'raf stand above it and recognize individuals by their signs. A novel interpretation suggests that "companions of Paradise" in these verses are not necessarily those already inside, but believers on the verge of entry whose hope is divinely validated. This resolves the grammatical issue of the demonstrative pronoun in "ah ulaa" by linking it to the previously mentioned group. Ultimately, these divine men use their knowledge to command the companions of Paradise to enter and warn the companions of Hell that their false oaths are void.
