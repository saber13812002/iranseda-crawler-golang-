Seyyed Mansour Daryabari shared his sports and revolutionary memories from the Charest Dashtak neighborhood and Piruzi Street in a radio interview. He recalled the presence of prominent figures like Hassan Habib in their local, family-oriented teams during his youth. Daryabari emphasized that despite a lack of facilities, the children of that era prepared their own fields and balls to play. He attributed the current decline in Tehran's sports performance to the conversion of playing fields into profitable construction projects and a loss of local motivation. In another part of the conversation, he described the active role of local teenagers in defending the Air Force base on the eve of the revolution. He believes that employing local coaches who understand the players is the key to success in sports like football. While noting relative progress in wrestling and volleyball, Daryabari stressed the need to return to a local, spirited sports culture.
