This radio program examined the jurisprudential and legal aspects of sports from an Islamic perspective. The central discussion focused on the concept of pre-emptive waiver of liability, where participants sign consent forms before competitions. While some scholars argue that consent is sufficient, the prevailing view, supported by the Supreme Leader, holds that one cannot waive liability for damages that have not yet occurred. Regarding the liability of coaches, particularly for children, the jurisprudential rule that a hired worker is liable for any damage caused was applied. This means a coach is responsible for any injury unless they can prove that all necessary safety precautions were taken. Sports law also confirms that coaches are obligated to ensure both the safety of the training intensity and the physical environment. The program further emphasized that sports are a social necessity and a means of preserving health, which aligns with Islamic jurisprudence rather than conflicting with it. Finally, other legal maxims such as "no harm," "action," and "priority" were mentioned as tools for resolving various sports-related legal issues.
