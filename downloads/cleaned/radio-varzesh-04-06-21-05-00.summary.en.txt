In this program, experts emphasized the pivotal role of artificial intelligence in analyzing demographic and environmental data to design long-term strategies for mass sports and preventing skeletal issues in children. They argued that leveraging technologies like virtual reality and wearables can transform home environments into smart gyms and accurately identify local sports needs. Furthermore, the necessity of revising urban policies to build efficient sports spaces and avoiding purely symbolic projects with no real utility was highlighted. In the second segment, the Judo Federation unveiled national team uniforms sewn by inmates, taking a step toward supporting domestic production and creating employment. This initiative not only reduced costs compared to foreign brands but also provided suitable quality and visual identity for international competition, as confirmed by national athletes.
