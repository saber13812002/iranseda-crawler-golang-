This radio program explored the concept of recognizing Imam Zaman (AJ) on two levels: basic and comprehensive.
The basic level involves knowing the Imam's identity and accepting the obligation to obey him.
The comprehensive level focuses on understanding his role as a divine refuge and a source of spiritual security during the occultation.
The key message to awaiters is to abandon sin and maintain continuous, steadfast obedience to the Imam.
The program emphasized that supporting the Imam is not limited to attending events but includes sincere conversations to guide others toward knowing him.
The interpretation of the Quranic verse "God has prepared for you a better thing" indicates that the Imam is a divine reserve that brings blessing and peace through faith.
Finally, the importance of living a "Mahdavi" life with good morals was highlighted to become a worthy supporter of the Imam at his reappearance.
