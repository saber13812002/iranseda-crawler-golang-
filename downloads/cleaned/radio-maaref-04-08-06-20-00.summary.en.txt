The Gaza information office reported that the Zionist regime has violated the ceasefire agreement over 125 times, resulting in hundreds of Palestinian casualties. Concurrently, a meeting of Sunni and Shia scholars in Islamabad was held to strengthen Islamic unity and counter division. The gathering emphasized the uprising of Imam Hussein (PBUH) as a symbol of unity and resistance against oppression. The head of the Pakistan Council of Muslims Unity condemned the potential involvement of the Pakistani army in international forces in Gaza as contrary to the Palestinian cause. Recent statistics indicate that 54 million pilgrims visited the Holy Shrines in the previous month. Peace negotiations between Afghanistan and Pakistan in Istanbul concluded without results, while an Iranian delegation in Kabul stressed the need for increased cooperation on water rights and border issues. Other reports included the opening of a Bosnian Muslim mosque in Norway and an Islamophobic attack on Scotland's largest mosque.
