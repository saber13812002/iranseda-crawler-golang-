The radio program "Zamzam Ahkam" addressed various jurisprudential questions from its listeners. Regarding wudu, if detached skin remains attached to the body, washing over it is sufficient, but if water can flow underneath, the area beneath the skin must also be washed. For housing loans, using the funds for a house down payment is permissible only if the bank's specific regulations and conditions are strictly followed. For the head wiping in wudu, it is a precautionary obligation to use the right hand and wipe from top to bottom, though a length of one finger and width of three fingers is sufficient. If a debtor cannot locate their creditor, paying the debt to a pious poor person as charity is an accepted jurisprudential solution. It is permissible to perform the Zuhr prayer after the Friday prayer. Smoking and using e-cigarettes are considered invalid and impermissible during menstruation or in a state of purity following sexual intercourse. Furthermore, according to Ayatollah Javadi Amoli's fatwa, riding motorcycles and bicycles is not permissible for women.
