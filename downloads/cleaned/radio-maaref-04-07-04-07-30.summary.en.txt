The radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat" began with the recitation of the Dua-e-Nezah and religious poetry before transitioning to a discussion on the prohibition of cursing and verbal abuse. Professor Mohammad Hadi Fala, citing Quranic verses and the narrations of the Ahl al-Bayt, emphasized that Islam strictly forbids cursing prophets, God's allies, and even Satan or opponents. He explained that responding to insults with silence and patience prevents Satan's provocation, earns God's pleasure, and ultimately leads to the punishment of the enemy. Imam Ali (AS) also urged Shias to maintain composure and forbearance in the face of provocation to preserve their dignity and safety from harm. The program concluded by urging listeners to protect their tongues and adhere to monotheistic ethics in daily interactions. Additionally, upcoming programs on the network, such as "Mr. Judge" and a specialized session for Quran reciters, were announced.
