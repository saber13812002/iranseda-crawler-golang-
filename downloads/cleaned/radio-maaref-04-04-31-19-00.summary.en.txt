In response to religious inquiries, the ruling on making up missed fasts depends on whether the harm affects the mother or the fetus. Performing cupping on pregnant women is permissible if done by a specialist while adhering to hygiene standards. Regarding missed prayers, if the exact number is forgotten, one should perform the lesser amount unless the forgetfulness resulted from negligence. It is also necessary to complete the Qunut prayer if remembered during bowing or prostration. Based on Imam Khomeini's fatwa, women with a valid religious excuse may perform the Friday bath. This recommended ritual can be performed during four specific times from dawn until the end of Friday. The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) emphasized the importance of this bath, even recommending selling food to buy water for it.
