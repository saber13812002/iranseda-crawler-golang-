Pegah Zangeneh, a former karate champion and current head coach of the Iranian women's national team, shared her athletic memories and challenges in an interview with Radio Sport. She discussed her psychological setback in the 2012 Asian Championship final after defeating the world champion, highlighting the critical role of sports psychology. Zangeneh recounted a severe accident in the 2010 Guangzhou Asian Games village that caused head injuries and forced her to take a break from competition. After returning, she participated in international tournaments at her own expense and won a bronze medal at the Jakarta Asian Games. She later transitioned into coaching, where she has achieved significant success, including world championship titles and high global rankings for her athletes. Zangeneh expressed confidence in the potential of Iranian karatekas, aiming to secure substantial quotas for the Nagoya Asian Games and future Olympic events.
