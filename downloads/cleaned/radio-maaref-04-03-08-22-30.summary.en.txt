This radio program explores the spiritual anxieties of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) regarding the Day of Judgment and his meeting with God.
The Prophet experienced such intense fear of the final reckoning that he would jump out of his bed in a panic during sleep.
When the verse "Mercy to all worlds" was revealed, the angel Gabriel informed the Prophet that divine mercy had encompassed everyone, bringing him peace.
In his final years, the Prophet wept out of gratitude and awe, feeling unable to express his thanks for God's immense blessings.
Encountering the majesty and beauty of God is described as a terrifying and weighty experience, even for the Prophet.
These anxieties demonstrate that prophets were never complacent in their worship and were always seeking God's pleasure.
The segment concludes with the recitation of verses from Surah As-Saffat to reinforce the discussed themes.
