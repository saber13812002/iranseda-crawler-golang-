In this radio program, Professor Mohammadi Sharodi explored the nature of knowledge and charity in Islam. He emphasized that true knowledge is of the nature of light, guiding humans through belief, ethics, and jurisprudence toward their ultimate destination. Regarding charity, he explained that it is not only a religious duty but also a means to bridge social gaps and nourish the human heart, fostering spiritual joy and community love. The program also described the Barzakh realm, where believers reside in a heavenly state, enjoying spiritual blessings and maintaining connections with their loved ones. In this realm, there are no rigid worldly obligations, but worship is performed voluntarily as a source of pleasure. Finally, the distinction between wealth production, which is encouraged, and hoarding, which is condemned, was clarified. The professor noted that Islam does not advocate for poverty; rather, individuals should earn lawful income and fulfill the obligatory and recommended rights of their wealth.
