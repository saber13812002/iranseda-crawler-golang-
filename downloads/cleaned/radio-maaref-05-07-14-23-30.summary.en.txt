This radio program introduces the book "In the Ambush of the Red Rose" and narrates the life of Martyr General Ali Siyazi Shirazi. The story focuses on the intense battle of the Banin garrison defenders against counter-revolutionary forces and Major Siyazi's efforts to break the siege and deliver reinforcements. After the armored units cleared the route, the relief column faced an enemy ambush, but Major Siyazi used helicopters and tactical awareness to guide the forces toward the garrison. His arrival at the garrison after forty-four days of siege significantly boosted the defenders' morale and revived their hope for victory. To capture the critical Arbabah height, which was the main obstacle, an initial helicopter operation failed and resulted in casualties. Major Siyazi changed tactics, using artillery fire and a ground assault to successfully seize the height and neutralize enemy fortifications. Ultimately, with the liberation of Banin city, the loyal forces overcame the enemy's resistance through strong will and faith, securing the city's freedom.
