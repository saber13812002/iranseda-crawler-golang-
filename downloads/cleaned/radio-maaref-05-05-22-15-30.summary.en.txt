This radio program analyzed the strategy of sustaining the Axis of Resistance in the first statement issued by the new leader of the Islamic Republic following the martyrdom of Imam Khamenei. The statement emphasized Iran's strength and its commitment to its allies, broadcasting a message of authority and resilience to the world. This approach not only reassures friends and neighbors but also refutes the alleged Iranian weakness propagated by adversaries. Within this framework, resistance is defined as a multidimensional phenomenon encompassing political, cultural, and economic aspects, rather than merely a military concept. The presence of the people as the system's primary support is crucial against enemy attacks that target spiritual and social infrastructure. By inviting neighbors to reconsider their relations with American bases, the new leader has paved the way for expanding the circle of allies and weakening the enemy front from within. Ultimately, the statement demonstrated that Iran remains capable and steadfast without reliance on external powers, proving that the miracle of this era is Iran's independent standing against superpowers.
