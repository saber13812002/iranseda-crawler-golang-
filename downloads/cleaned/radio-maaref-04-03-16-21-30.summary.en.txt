This radio program examines the fate of the Jews in the era of the Mahdi's reappearance and the validity of narrations regarding physical conflict with them. Citing a narration from the book Kharaj, the speaker argues that while the principle of Raj'ah and conflict with enemies is established, details such as the existence of an independent Jewish state during the Imam's time cannot be inferred from this text. It is explained that many narrations about war with the Jews actually refer to the era of the Dajjal, which occurs before the Imam's reappearance. The speaker also notes that the image of the Dajjal in Shia narrations is more limited than in Sunni sources, serving primarily as a symbol of the Imam's enemies. Many sources regarding the Dajjal are derived from Isra'iliyyat and People of the Book texts, with weak chains of transmission in Shia sources. Consequently, it is hypothesized that the political structure of the Jews will be dismantled before the Imam's reappearance, with the main conflict taking the form of the Dajjal's movement. Finally, there is insufficient evidence to prove the survival of a Jewish government until the time of reappearance and a direct conflict between the Imam and them.
