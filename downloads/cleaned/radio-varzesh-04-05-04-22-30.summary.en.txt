Retired Iranian footballer Seyed Mohammad Mehdi Seyed Salehi recounts the turbulent journey of his professional career in this interview. He highlights the conflicts he faced with coaches and managers at clubs like Sepahan and Tractor due to a lack of transparency and behind-the-scenes interference. Seyed Salehi expresses his frustration over the perceived injustice in national team selections and costly transfer decisions, despite his impressive performances at Tractor and Persepolis. He also admits to major mistakes during his peak years, particularly his move to Saipa and his departure from Persepolis. Towards the end, he shares the sorrow of his final days in football and his unsuccessful attempts to find a new team. Seyed Salehi concludes by stating his acceptance of God's will and expressing his hope to see his footballer son achieve success in the future.
