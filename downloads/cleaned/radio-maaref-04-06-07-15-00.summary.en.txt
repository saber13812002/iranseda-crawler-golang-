In a discussion with Professor Mehdi Rasakhi, the management strategies of the Supreme Leader in the Arbaeen statement following the twelve-day war with the Zionist regime were analyzed. The central theme was the pivotal role of leadership in fostering social cohesion and transforming the people into active, unified actors against the enemy. It was emphasized that national unity, as a red line for security, was a major achievement that prevented internal chaos and the enemy's destructive projects. The necessity of relying on internal capabilities, including scientific, missile, and drone advancements, was highlighted to maintain the country's authority and independence. Intellectual and media elites are also tasked with defending this authority through media diplomacy and resistance against the enemy's psychological warfare. Finally, preserving national dignity and utilizing a monotheistic spirit of trust in God combined with effort were identified as the keys to success in facing future challenges.
