In the first segment, Professor Mohammadi refutes the notion of being deprived of spiritual blessings, asserting that faith and prayer are the greatest successes and that worldly hardships serve as tests for spiritual growth. He explains that Imam Ali's decision not to draw his sword during the attack on Fatima's house was a strategic act of wisdom to prevent a civil war and protect the foundation of Islam from political conspiracies. Addressing another question, he emphasizes that sincere repentance purifies a person's past, and society should not judge individuals based on their previous mistakes. The second part of the program analyzes the historical context of the nationalization of the oil industry, highlighting the role of the D'Arcy contract in the exploitation of Iran's resources. Experts explain that this national movement was the result of a unity between religious forces and the National Front, supported by Ayatollah Kashani. Finally, the 1953 coup is identified as the outcome of British and American plots aimed at regaining control over oil and overthrowing the Mosaddegh government.
