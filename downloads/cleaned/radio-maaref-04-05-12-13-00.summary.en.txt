Hojjat al-Islam Haghijabaspasam interprets the three prayers of the mujahideen from the perspective of the Holy Quran in this radio program. He emphasizes the prayer "And help us against the disbelieving people," identifying it as the secret to victory alongside military advancements. The speaker argues that the root of problems in Islamic societies lies in the system of disbelief, urging unity against the enemy rather than internal division. He highlights that abandoning sins is a primary prerequisite for receiving divine support and maintaining steadfastness in the path of the Imam. Finally, the importance of winning the cognitive war and correctly recognizing the Guardianship of the Jurist as the sole fortress of resistance against global tyranny is stressed.
