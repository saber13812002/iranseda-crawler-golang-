In this program, Hojjat al-Islam Rabani Golpaygani uses authentic narrations to prove that the Verse of Purification is exclusively for the Ahl al-Bayt, noting that none of the Prophet's wives ever claimed to be included in it. By analyzing the views of Sunni scholars like Ibn Kathir and Alusi, he explains that while the wives were present at the verse's revelation, the Quranic context and hadiths confirm the Ahl al-Bayt's inclusion as well. Subsequently, Ayatollah Sifir Mazandarani elucidates the conceptual difference between "Rahman" and "Rahim" based on abundant narrations. He defines "Rahman" as a universal mercy encompassing all creation and their sustenance, whereas "Rahim" refers to specific mercy and spiritual guidance reserved for believers. The interpretation of the phrase "O Rahman of this world and the Hereafter" emphasizes the permanence of God's mercy for believers in the afterlife. This supplication seeks to ensure that both general and specific divine mercies remain enduring for the faithful in the next world. The discussion highlights the theological nuance that while general mercy may cease for disbelievers, the specific mercy for believers persists eternally.
