In the final episode of the "Persoman Tarbiyati" program featuring Dr. Fatemeh Heydari, the importance of fostering a sense of responsibility in adolescents and avoiding coercion in religious and personal duties was emphasized.
The expert advised parents to actively participate in their children's planning and time management rather than issuing top-down orders, ensuring the teenager feels a sense of ownership.
To boost motivation, positive feedback and rewards were recommended over punishment, so that actions are performed as personal choices rather than obligations to please parents.
Parents' expectations should be clearly communicated, realistic, and tailored to the adolescent's capabilities, with adjustments made during weekly family meetings.
Teaching "consideration" through practical exercises and situational analysis is essential for adolescents to understand others' emotions and maintain family privacy.
Finally, teaching basic financial management and economic concepts to adolescents at various ages was highlighted as a vital skill for their future independence.
