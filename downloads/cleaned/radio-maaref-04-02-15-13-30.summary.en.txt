In the radio program "Mashq-e Rah," Professor Seyed Ahmad Faghihi discusses the ethics of monotheism and the etiquette of supplication. He emphasizes that the best way to communicate with God is through Quranic prayers and texts from the Infallibles, such as the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya. Professor Faghihi advises focusing on the quality of actions and inner purification rather than external rituals or specific timeframes. In response to listener questions, kindness to parents is defined to include financial support, spiritual care, and protecting their honor. Furthermore, harming nature and neglecting cleanliness are condemned as violations of innate moral principles, and advice to others should be given with respect. Finally, the Supreme Leader stresses the importance of neutralizing sanctions by boosting domestic investment and expanding trade relations with neighboring countries.
