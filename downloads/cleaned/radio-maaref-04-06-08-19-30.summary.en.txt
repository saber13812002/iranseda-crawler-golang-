The radio program begins with an exegesis of verse 70 of Surah Al-Imran, which criticizes the People of the Book for concealing the truth despite their inner conviction.
The main discussion then shifts to the concept of backbiting (Gheebah) from the perspective of Shia narrations, identifying it as a dangerous moral vice rooted in ignorance.
Citing hadiths from the Prophet and the Imams, the speaker declares backbiting strictly forbidden and equates it with violating the honor of others, placing it alongside murder and theft.
It is emphasized that the person who backbites is actually projecting their own faults onto others, mistaking their internal thoughts for external reality.
Imam Hussein (AS) considered abandoning backbiting a prerequisite for entering his spiritual domain, while Imam Ali (AS) described it as a spiritual impurity requiring strict avoidance.
Listeners are urged to protect each other's honor and avoid toxic social media environments to escape divine punishment and the influence of the ego.
Finally, the program advises on energy conservation during the late summer and encourages maintaining a strong emotional connection with the Quran and authentic narrations.
