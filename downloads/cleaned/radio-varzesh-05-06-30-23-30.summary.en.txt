Veteran goalkeeper coach Mehdi Heydari criticized the concerning state of grassroots football education in Iran during a radio interview. He emphasized the necessity of separating coaching from primary employment and the mandatory participation in specialized, up-to-date courses abroad. Heydari lamented the lack of age-specific training classes and the absence of structured planning compared to global standards. He also highlighted the negative culture of knowledge hoarding among coaches and the neglect of domestic talent in favor of foreign goalkeepers. Despite the country's abundance of raw talent, he expressed deep concern over the lack of proper infrastructure and long-term development programs. Heydari warned that if this trajectory continues, Iran will fall behind neighboring countries like Tajikistan in youth categories in the future.
