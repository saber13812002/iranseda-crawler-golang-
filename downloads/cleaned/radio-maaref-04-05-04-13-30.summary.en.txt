In this radio program, Dr. Dehkhani Firouzabadi analyzes recent developments in Syria within the context of global geopolitical competition and Israel's attempt to create a "New Middle East." He argues that Israel's ultimate goal is to establish a hegemonic order based on absolute security and unilateral dominance over the entire West Asia. To achieve this, Israel employs both "hard hegemony" (coercion and force) and "soft hegemony" (incentives and normalization of relations). The analyst suggests that weakening Syria and creating buffer zones are steps to contain Iran and eventually pressure Turkey as a regional rival. Citing historical precedents, he warns that the pursuit of absolute security often leads to greater insecurity and broader conflicts. Finally, the host emphasizes Iran's national will and self-confidence in confronting major powers, asserting that the Iranian people, equipped with necessary tools, will enter both military and diplomatic arenas with strength.
