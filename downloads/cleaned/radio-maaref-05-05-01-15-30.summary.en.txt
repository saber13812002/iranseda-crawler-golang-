In this radio program, Professor Bahmani analyzes the Supreme Leader's first statement following his re-election. The primary strategy derived from this statement is focusing on God as the ultimate source of power and peace in the universe. This monotheistic perspective is highlighted as the key factor in managing the crisis and preventing social chaos in Iran after the recent war. Citing Imam Khomeini's letters, the expert argues that a weak belief in God leads to a misperception of global powers like the United States. The statement reinforced the people's historical and identity-based connection to religious teachings by greeting Imam Mahdi and quoting the Quran. This spiritual approach enabled the society to move forward with peace and hope for the Imam's reappearance rather than collapsing. Ultimately, the statement is presented as a model for maintaining national cohesion and achieving victory over enemies throughout history.
