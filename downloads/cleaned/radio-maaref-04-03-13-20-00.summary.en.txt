Radio Ma'aref's nightly broadcast announced the upcoming Fourth International Conference on the Theology of Arbaeen at Qom University, which will feature participation from Islamic universities worldwide and a written message from Ayatollah Sistani. The news segment reported on Belgian writers demanding political pressure on the Zionist regime, the introduction of Amsterdam's 360-year-old oldest mosque, and the conviction of a man for burning the Quran in London. Additionally, the opening of a new Islamic center building in Madison, USA, and the Supreme Leader's speech at the 36th anniversary of Imam Khomeini's passing were highlighted. The program concluded with the recitation of the Dua al-Tawassul and an overview of the evening's remaining segments, including book narrations and remarks by Ayatollah Javadi Amoli.
