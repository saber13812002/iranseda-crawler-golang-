The program begins by emphasizing the significance of the academic year's start as a spiritual and civilizational process, where learning is a lifelong religious duty. Drawing on the lives of prophets and Shia scholars, it highlights that seeking knowledge is a national obligation essential for societal progress. The main discussion critiques the vice of verbal abuse, which is deemed an ignorant and satanic act based on Quranic and hadith sources. The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) stated that two individuals who insult each other are like barking dogs, thereby losing their human dignity. Insulting another person's parents is considered an insult to one's own parents, reflecting a lack of intelligence and respect. This vice not only destroys the four fundamental relationships humans have with God, themselves, others, and the universe but also serves as a precursor to destruction and hellfire. Finally, the program advises avoiding foul language and cultivating good morals to prevent this vice from manifesting in one's character and appearance.
