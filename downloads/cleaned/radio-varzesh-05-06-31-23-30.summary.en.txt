Former footballer and coach Ardalan Ashtiani recounts his career-ending struggles with repeated ACL tears and bone fractures that forced him to retire early. After his playing days, he began his coaching career with the youth teams of Railway Club and later worked with various teams, including Pas Qom and national youth squads. Leveraging his advanced degree in sports science, Ashtiani unexpectedly joined the official ranks of national football instructors through persistent effort. He strongly criticizes the current landscape of professional football in Iran, highlighting the unfair burden of high costs and reliance on intermediaries for coaches seeking positions. Ashtiani argues that these inequitable conditions have distorted the sport, denying talented young coaches their rightful opportunities. He emphasizes that football is not everything and stresses the importance of trusting in God while supporting his children without imposing direct pressure. Finally, he names Ibrahim Ashtiani as the greatest player in Iranian football history and expresses his gratitude to Radio Varzesh for the interview.
