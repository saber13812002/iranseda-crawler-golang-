The radio program commemorated the martyrdom of Prophet Muhammad and Imam Hassan while reporting on extensive mourning ceremonies and processions in Tehran and Mashhad. The Minister of Interior announced special preparations to host over seven million pilgrims during the last ten days of Safar, including the allocation of 50 accommodation points and specific traffic plans in Mashhad. Simultaneously, the fourth "Iran Hamdel" campaign was launched to support the people of Gaza, urging citizens to donate via online platforms and a specified bank account. In the religious segment, Hujjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the importance of respecting people's rights for prayers to be answered, sharing anecdotes about the ethical conduct of Ayatollah Mesghini and Behjat. Finally, the discussion addressed the Sharia rulings regarding vows made specifically for the months of Muharram and Safar that were not fulfilled, explaining the religious obligations associated with such unfulfilled vows.
