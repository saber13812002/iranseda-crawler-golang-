The radio program "Hekayat-e Azadegi" began with contact information and a closing prayer. The first segment highlighted the spiritual benefits of visiting Imam Hussein (PBUH) and recounted a miraculous story of Ayatollah Haeri Yazdi stopping a flood in Qom using the Imam's relics. A narrative from the book "Neshan-e Hassan" followed, describing how Qasim ibn Hassan and his companions rescued an injured slave named Kanan on the road to Kufa. Qasim freed Kanan from slavery and corrected his misconceptions about Ali (PBUH) and his descendants through respectful dialogue. Qasim's mother shared her personal memory of being freed by Imam Hassan (PBUH) after he accepted a flower she picked for him. The program concluded with a jurisprudential ruling regarding the construction of commercial spaces in the basement or roof of a waqf mosque. It was clarified that such construction is religiously impermissible, even if the generated income is intended to cover the mosque's expenses.
