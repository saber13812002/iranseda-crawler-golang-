In this radio program, Professor Bahmani analyzed the rhetorical strategies of the Supreme Leader, emphasizing "emotional justice" and "empathy management" as core pillars. He demonstrated that the Leader consolidates political legitimacy by combining deep emotional resonance with firm accountability demands on officials. This approach, inspired by the legacy of Imam Khomeini and the late Imam, reflects a profound spiritual and operational alignment between the Leader and the nation. The discussion also covered social crisis management following the martyrdom of key commanders, highlighting deterrence strategies and the establishment of a balance of fear against adversaries. The statement was crafted to address diverse audiences, including military personnel, government officials, and neighbors, with distinct yet unified tones. This coherence results in a blend of emotional arousal, rational growth, and behavioral stability. The program concluded with the recitation and translation of verses from the Surahs of Al-Hujurat and Qaf, stressing the brotherhood of believers, the prohibition of slander, and the signs of God's power in creation.
