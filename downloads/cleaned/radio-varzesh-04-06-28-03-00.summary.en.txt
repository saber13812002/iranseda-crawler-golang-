Colonel Dr. Chingiz Mesibzadeh, a shooting champion and manager of the Army Ground Forces Club, recounts how his innate talent was discovered during national competitions, leading to his selection for the national team. He describes his first national team camp and winning a silver medal at the Peace and Friendship Tournament as pivotal moments in his athletic career. Mesibzadeh highlights the challenges posed by sanctions regarding equipment and ammunition, while celebrating his club's recent success in the Asian Championships in Thailand with gold and bronze medals. He emphasizes that the Army Ground Forces Club provides a fair and free environment for talented youth, strictly avoiding nepotism in athlete selection. As a manager, he believes his role is only meaningful while he remains impactful and serves the younger generation effectively. Mesibzadeh expresses pride in his club receiving the "Model Service Club" award for ten consecutive years from the Sports Industry. He acknowledges the crucial support of military commanders in developing the club's infrastructure. Finally, he underscores the importance of discovering pure talent and maintaining integrity in the path to sporting success.
