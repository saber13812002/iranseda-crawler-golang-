This year's Decade of Karamat is being held nationwide under the slogan "Mehri Iran," with the Astan Quds Razavi fully prepared to serve pilgrims. The first phase of Mashhad's metro line three, utilizing domestic components, will be operational before the decade to improve access to the shrine. The head of the Hajj and Pilgrimage Organization announced the refund of flight delay penalties and the conclusion of Umrah trips involving over 205,000 pilgrims. A specialized conference on artificial intelligence emphasized the need to align technological advancements with Islamic civilization and humanities. A national plan to renovate 7,200 schools in deprived provinces has begun, with 476 new schools set to be inaugurated by the President. Authorities have called for increased female participation in blood donation, which currently stands at only 4.5 percent. Special mourning ceremonies for the martyrdom of Imam Jafar Sadiq (AS) are being held in Mashhad with extensive religious programs.
