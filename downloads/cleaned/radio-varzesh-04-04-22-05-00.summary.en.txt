Seyyed Mohammad Mehdi Seyyed Sali shared his turbulent career journey in an intimate interview, recounting his time from Esteghlal to his retirement. He discussed the recurring injuries and conflicts with club managers at Esteghlal and Tractor Sazi that significantly undermined his confidence and motivation. Due to his refusal to accept a lucrative offer from a lower league team out of respect for fan sentiments, he changed his career path and joined Persepolis. He also revealed the hardships of his stints at Saipa and Esteghlal Khuzestan, including a bitter experience where Abdullah Vaisi cut off contact after Seyyed Sali helped the team during a crisis. The former player, who was repeatedly fined for wearing religious undergarments in Asian matches, spoke about the grief of his retirement and the lack of support for his footballer son. In conclusion, Seyyed Sali emphasized the importance of transparency in sports relationships and prayed for his son's success to be revealed to him before his passing.
