In this radio program, Professor Mohammad Hadi Fala continues his discussion by examining the nature of lying from the perspective of Islamic narrations. Citing hadiths from the Infallibles, he describes lying as a spiritual disease that is fundamentally incompatible with faith and rooted in disbelief and hypocrisy. According to these narrations, lying not only distances an individual from Paradise but also leads them toward sin and ultimately Hell. Conversely, truthfulness is presented as the key to entering Paradise and achieving divine security. Professor Fala defines lying as a betrayal of God's trust and a violation of the covenant established by creation and legislation. He emphasizes that a true believer is a custodian of truth who strictly avoids falsehood. The program concludes by highlighting that abstaining from lies and adhering to honesty are essential for salvation and honor.
