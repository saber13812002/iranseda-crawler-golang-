The program begins by quoting Imam Hassan to describe humans as living in a state of sleep and ignorance.
It emphasizes that the world is transient like a shadow, and clinging to it is a sign of the foolishness of worldly people.
The discussion then shifts to the life of Sulayman ibn Mهران, a prominent ascetic and jurist, and his intellectual debates with Abu Hanifa.
A narrative is presented where Abu Hanifa and two other jurists visited Sulayman during his final moments.
Sulayman recited authentic hadiths regarding the virtues of Amir al-Mu'minin to urge Abu Hanifa to repent and correct his beliefs.
These arguments profoundly affected Abu Hanifa, causing him to stand up, accept the truth, and leave with his companions.
The conclusion highlights the necessity of waking up from spiritual slumber and performing good deeds while alive, as knowledge without action is futile after death.
