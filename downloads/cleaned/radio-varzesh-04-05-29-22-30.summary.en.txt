Reza Kangarani, a former Iranian football star, recounts his career highlights and challenges in this interview. He discusses his time playing for clubs such as Persepolis, Keshavarz, and Iran Javan, including a notable heavy defeat against Persepolis. Kangarani also shares his experience in the second division, where his team achieved the championship title. He attributes his early retirement at age 29 to frustration with perceived injustices and the psychological pressure within the football environment. After leaving professional football, he worked as a border expert for the Tehran Municipality, while his son pursued a career in sports-related fields. The former player believes modern football has lost its former charm and technical depth due to an excessive focus on results. Currently, Kangarani maintains his physical fitness and aims to participate in sports competitions for the over-50 age group. He emphasizes the importance of both physical and mental readiness for continued athletic activity in later life.
