In this radio program, experts addressed several religious and theological inquiries. Regarding the first question, it was emphasized that individuals should consult professionals who integrate both religious and scientific perspectives, while avoiding unqualified practitioners for issues like curses. The second segment clarified that hunger and sleeplessness are not inherently acts of worship unless performed with the intention of drawing closer to God and within the bounds of Islamic law. Furthermore, while sending greetings to angels during night prayers is permissible, asking for their rank to be elevated is considered theologically incorrect. The third response clarified that Satan was expelled from his status among angels, not from the eternal Paradise, and that Adam and Eve were in a transitional realm rather than the final abode. The existence of Satan was explained as a necessary training opponent for human spiritual growth. Finally, the program highlighted the high status of knowledge in Islam, noting that scholarly discussions are highly valued by God.
