The universe is likened to a single body where prophets and saints serve as the heart and brain, essential for the existence's health. Lady Fatima Masumeh (SA) holds a sacred role in Qom, having demonstrated unwavering devotion to her father and brother's authority. A key eschatological mission of hers is the manifestation of divine knowledge, establishing Qom as a global center for spreading Islamic teachings. Religious narrations identify women and youth as the religion's reserves against enemy plots in the end times. The Prophet (PBUH) defined women's jihad as maintaining harmony and affection within the family, equating this domestic self-sacrifice to men's external battles. This perspective highlights that a woman's role in fostering family unity is the foundation of social cohesion. Therefore, a conscious visit to Lady Masumeh's shrine should inspire the fulfillment of maternal and sisterly roles in society. This approach aims to prevent social fragmentation by reinforcing the values of love and family integrity.
