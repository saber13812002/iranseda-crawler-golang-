The radio program began by emphasizing the importance of morning prayers and following the spiritual path of great scholars. After congratulating the birth of Imam Ali al-Ridha (AS), the main segment focused on the virtues of Imam Ali (AS). Dr. Hosseini cited Sermon 197 from Nahj al-Balagha to highlight Imam Ali's unique status alongside the Prophet (PBUH). The Imam described his unwavering loyalty, his defense of the Prophet, and his witnessing of the revelation in the Cave of Hira. A key point was the Prophet's passing while his head rested on Imam Ali's chest, a distinction unique to him. The Imam also recounted performing the Prophet's ritual washing and receiving assistance from angels during the funeral. The program concluded with prayers and an introduction to a tafsir segment on Surah Al-A'raf by Ayatollah Javadi Amoli.
