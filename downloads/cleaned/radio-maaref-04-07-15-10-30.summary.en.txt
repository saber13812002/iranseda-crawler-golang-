The radio program "Shifa" highlighted the importance of Islamic traditional medicine, specifically the role of massage in improving infant and child health.
A traditional medicine specialist explained that gentle abdominal massage with mild oils can effectively reduce infant colic, bloating, and digestive restlessness.
He emphasized that these massages must be performed with very light pressure and in the direction of natural bowel movement to prevent any potential harm.
The second segment, titled "A Drop from the Sea," focused on the moral obligation of keeping promises and commitments.
Hojjat al-Islam Rasoul Hafez, citing Quranic verses, stated that covenants with God, spouses, and others are critical and will be scrutinized on the Day of Judgment.
He noted that breaking promises not only has spiritual consequences but also leads to a loss of trust and social issues in this world.
The program also marked the International Day of Hamas Palestine, honoring the resilience of the people of Gaza and praying for the liberation of Jerusalem.
Finally, the broadcast concluded by emphasizing the superiority of spiritual well-being over material wealth and the blessings found in a simple, spiritual life.
