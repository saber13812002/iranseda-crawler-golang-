The guest reflects on the golden era of Iranian football in the late 1990s, highlighting the national team's third-place finish and individual awards at the Asian Cup. He praises the talented squad featuring stars like Ali Daei and Karim Bagheri, noting their high motivation despite an initial loss to Iraq. The team regained confidence after defeating Thailand and securing a historic 6-1 victory over Saudi Arabia. The guest expresses regret over the refereeing issues and penalty shootout loss in the bronze medal match against Kuwait. He also criticizes the managerial and nutritional mishaps during the 2002 World Cup qualifiers under coach Blazevic. Last-minute changes to the team's accommodation and diet on match day severely disrupted the players' focus and physical readiness. Ultimately, the guest emphasizes that these failures were not due to conspiracy but rather resulted from managerial weaknesses and a lack of scientific approach to football.
