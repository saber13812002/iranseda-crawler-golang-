In the first segment, a preacher highlighted the severe spiritual dangers of mobile phones and illicit relationships, equating such behaviors with the sin of adultery and urging youth to avoid inappropriate interactions.
In the "Jor'eh" program, Professor Masoudi interpreted hadiths from the Book of Kafi that emphasize the immense value of fulfilling a fellow believer's needs and respecting their trust.
He also cited a narration stating that God loves those who show kindness to His dependents and promises great rewards for those who strive to help others.
A police officer discussed the rising trend of mobile phone theft, attributing it largely to owners' negligence and recommending preventive measures like using car holders and tracking apps.
Subsequently, Javad Mohaddesi analyzed the 36th letter of Nahj al-Balagha, describing the united front of Quraysh against the Prophet and Imam Ali as a disguised front of disbelief.
He emphasized Imam Ali's complaint regarding the severance of kinship ties and the injustices committed by Quraysh, calling for justice against these oppressions.
Finally, a representative of a religious authority in the "Zamzam Ahkam" program explained the conditions for shortening prayers during a round-trip journey, specifying a minimum distance of forty kilometers.
