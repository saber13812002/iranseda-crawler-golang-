In the radio program "Narrative of Love," Zahra Nasiri, the wife of martyr Javad Akbari, discussed the importance of preserving the enduring love for Imam Hussein.
The guest emphasized the necessity of reviving the traditional practice of holding home gatherings during Muharram and playing recitations to create spiritual merit.
She recommended inviting educated and expert women knowledgeable in religious laws and history to lead these home gatherings to ensure accuracy.
The guest also stressed the importance of adhering to religious rules in women's gatherings, such as maintaining hijab and preventing non-mahram men from hearing the recitations.
In the Q&A section, Hujjat al-Islam Falahzadeh stated that performing tayammum instead of wudu solely to protect makeup is not religiously permissible.
Hujjat al-Islam Alian Najat clarified that reciting specific dhikr after rising from ruku in a seated prayer is valid if intended as a general remembrance.
Regarding water purity, Hujjat al-Islam Mohammadi Shakhshabani explained that sediment at the bottom of water does not make it impure unless the water itself is mixed with impurities.
Finally, Hujjat al-Islam Jawahdi confirmed, citing Ayatollah Shabiri Zanjani, that direct sunlight does not purify a najis (impure) sofa.
