The radio program "Love Story" featured Mrs. Ebrahimipour, the wife of martyr Mustafa Sadraei, as its guest.
She described her profound attachment to her husband and her constant effort to remain by his side.
The guest recounted memories of their shared life, noting that she refused to eat meals without his presence.
Her husband's mission in Syria introduced geographical distance and security concerns as new challenges for the couple.
Mrs. Ebrahimipour discussed the hardships of separation and the stress-induced illnesses she experienced in his absence.
The program concluded with an analysis of Iran's economic situation and foreign policy.
Listeners are invited to share their feedback via the radio station's SMS and phone systems.
