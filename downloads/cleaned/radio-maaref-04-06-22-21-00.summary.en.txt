The program begins with Quranic recitation and a sermon by Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi on the importance of good morals and helping the needy.
Three anecdotes are shared about the simple and compassionate lives of Ayatollah Salehi Mazandarani, Ayatollah Haeri, and Hajj Sheikh Rezaee Yazdi.
These stories emphasize the urgency of responding to people's suffering and preserving their dignity during difficult times.
The second part of the show continues the dramatic adaptation of the book "Ocean of the East" regarding Amiran's journey to Khorasan.
After escaping from bandits, Amiran accompanies Hajj Karim the cobbler and his daughter Rahleh to a doctor's house.
The doctor treats Hajj Karim's shoulder wound and stresses the necessity of prayer and reliance on God.
Amiran realizes that the name of Imam Reza (AS) is constantly mentioned, and his path is shifting towards divine guardianship.
The narrative illustrates how Amiran's spiritual quest is being redirected from seeking a mythical elixir to the truth of Wilayah.
