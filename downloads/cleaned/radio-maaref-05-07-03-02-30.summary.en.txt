The radio program begins by critiquing certain Mahdavi claims that rely on weak or unknown narrators, emphasizing that the consensus of twelve Imams is shared by both Shia and Sunni Muslims.
It is highlighted that anonymous individuals claiming exclusive knowledge of the Imam of the Time lack credibility and should be approached with caution.
The discussion then shifts to the significant role of women during the reappearance of Imam Mahdi, noting that 50 noble women will accompany the 313 male companions.
It is clarified that women's contributions will align with the Imam's commands and their dignity, rather than necessarily involving direct combat.
The program also underscores the importance of deep reflection as the highest form of worship for identifying personal vulnerabilities, hidden fears, and attachments.
By acknowledging human fragility and seeking guidance through faith and righteous action, individuals can achieve spiritual growth and salvation.
Finally, listeners are advised to consult authentic sources and avoid accepting baseless claims circulating in social media spaces.
