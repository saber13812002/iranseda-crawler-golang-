In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the importance of writing a will to settle financial affairs and pass on moral advice to children. He read excerpts from the wills of Ayatollah Marashi Najafi, Allameh Majlisi, and Ayatollah Mousavi Khonsari to demonstrate how such documents provide peace of mind and serve as lasting reminders for the bereaved. The second segment featured a narrative from the novel "Left Behind by the Son," which depicts the events of the 1963 uprising. The protagonist, Salār, faces security risks while trying to save his wounded son in a wheat field. With the help of his friends, Salār manages to hide his son in a pickup truck and evade the interrogation of the authorities. The program concluded with a report on the country's economic indicators, including decreased inflation and the revival of production units. Additionally, a religious ruling was presented stating that the Hajj sacrifice must be performed in the land of Mina and cannot be substituted elsewhere.
