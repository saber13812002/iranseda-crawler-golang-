The program began with the recitation of the nineteenth supplication from the Sahifa al-Sajjadiya, seeking rain to alleviate drought.
Subsequently, Professor Mohammad Hadi Fala discussed practical solutions for curing the moral vice of foul language.
He emphasized the necessity of public and courageous repentance for verbal sins committed in the presence of others.
Citing religious narrations, the professor identified bad speech as a major obstacle to the acceptance of prayers.
Practical advice included maintaining modesty, remaining silent in the face of insults, and associating with eloquent individuals.
Imam Ali (AS) also modeled patience and praying for enemies as alternatives to engaging in verbal abuse.
Finally, the program concluded with a prayer to God to prevent bloodshed and the harms caused by resentment.
