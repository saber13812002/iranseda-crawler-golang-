The radio program "Hekayat-e Azadegi" aired the war memories of Mr. Gholamreza Fallah, a former POW. He recounted the critical moments after being exposed to chemical weapons and the dangerous night crossing of a minefield. Fallah described the difficulty of identifying enemies among his own troops and the severe hunger and thirst endured during the retreat. He detailed how he recognized a trap marked by an Iranian flag, leading to a heavy enemy attack and the subsequent capture of his group. He emphasized the bravery of his commander and his own faith as the primary tools for survival amidst these hardships. The latter part of the program featured a Quranic interpretation of the Prophet's characteristics and the ease of Islam. Finally, it announced the upcoming commemorative ceremonies for the late Supreme Leader in Tehran and Mashhad.
