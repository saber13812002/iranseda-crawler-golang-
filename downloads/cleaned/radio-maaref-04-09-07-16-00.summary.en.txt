In this radio program, Dr. Mostafa Sadeghi Kashani answered historical questions from listeners. He first explained that the Prophet's aunt, Baraka, was a slave of Abdullah who married twice and eventually married the Prophet's adopted son. The expert emphasized that during the Meccan period, the Prophet (PBUH) did not have written treaties with tribes, relying instead on oral agreements like the Pledge of Aqabah. Regarding the Hijra, he detailed the companionship of Abdullah ibn Uraiqit and Abu Bakr, as well as Ali's (AS) mission to bring the Prophet's family to Medina. On the topic of Nowruz, the professor cited the Letter to Malik Ashtar to demonstrate that the Ahl al-Bayt did not oppose healthy local customs and even supported them. Finally, the objectives of early Islamic wars were examined; while the Prophet's (PBUH) battles were largely defensive, the conquests after his passing remain a debated topic regarding the political stance of the Ahl al-Bayt.
