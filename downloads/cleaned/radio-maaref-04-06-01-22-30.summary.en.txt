In this radio program, the commentator uses Quranic verses to define "La ilaha illa Allah" as the primary key to security and refuge against shirk and arrogance. Referring to the Prophet's public call from Mount Safa, he emphasizes that the main danger to society is distancing oneself from divine mercy and the establishment of a system of shirk, which corrupts social and family relations. The commentator cites examples of social isolation in modern societies to illustrate that severing family ties is a sign of being disconnected from God's mercy. He also references shocking statistics of casualties in Palestine to demonstrate that the root of such tragedies is the dominance of arrogance and the departure from monotheism. He argues that true security is achieved only by restraining the forces of shirk and refusing to submit to global arrogance, rather than through superficial compromises. The program then featured Quranic recitation and hymns in honor of the Muharram and Safar months. It concluded with an introduction to the Global Arbaeen Award and answers to religious questions regarding family ties and related rulings.
