The first segment of the program discusses the characteristics of true scholars based on Quranic verses, highlighting prostration, weeping, and humility when hearing divine verses.
The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) emphasized that a heart lacking sorrow and humility is distant from God, urging believers to cultivate these spiritual states.
The second part examines the reasons behind Imam Ali's (AS) acceptance of the caliphate following the Prophet's (PBUH) passing.
Imam Ali (AS) stated in Nahj al-Balagha that he had no personal desire for power but accepted the oath of allegiance due to the people's pressure and a divine covenant to defend the oppressed.
He considered the material world insignificant, valuing his worn-out shoes over the caliphate unless it served to establish justice and truth.
A narration from Ibn Abbas illustrated Imam Ali's (AS) simplicity, showing him repairing his own shoes and prioritizing spiritual duties over worldly status.
In Letter 62 of Nahj al-Balagha, the Imam explained that his primary motivation was the fear of Islam being forgotten and the community deviating from the divine path.
The broadcast concluded with announcements for upcoming programs on the Ma'aref Network, including a tafsir of verses from Surah Al-A'raf.
