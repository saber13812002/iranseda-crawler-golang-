The first segment of the program outlined fiqh rulings regarding wudu with a cast, cleaning the prayer mark from impurity, and paying alms to Sayyids based on the fatwas of religious authorities. It also clarified the ruling on holding a child with impure clothing during prayer, emphasizing the need to avoid excessive movement and prevent impurity from transferring to the worshipper. Subsequently, Hujjat al-Islam Fallah discussed lying as the most dangerous moral vice, describing it as a factor that destroys faith, religion, and manliness. He emphasized that habitual lying leads to a blackened heart, deprivation of divine guidance, and being labeled a "liar" by God. Lying also paves the way for other sins such as backbiting, hypocrisy, and pretense, ultimately leading both the liar and their followers to destruction. Therefore, avoiding lies and adhering to truthfulness is essential for maintaining spiritual and social health.
