This radio program, broadcast on the twentieth night of Muharram, began with religious laments and condolences for the upcoming Arbaeen.
The main segment focused on the necessity of training and guarding the tongue as a vital human asset.
Hojjat al-Islam Fala Barestan cited narrations from Imam Ali and Imam Sadiq to describe guarding the tongue as the key to a believer's salvation.
He emphasized that the greatest problems in the Shia community stem from verbal negligence and that monitoring speech brings personal and social peace.
Four main strategies for controlling the tongue were outlined: avoiding corrupt environments, strengthening belief, practicing linguistic piety, and speaking rationally.
Finally, guarding the tongue was described as a lifelong obligation in the stage of the lower self to ensure the safety of faith and society.
