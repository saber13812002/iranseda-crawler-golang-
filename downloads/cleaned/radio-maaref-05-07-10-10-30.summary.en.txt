In the final segment of the program, Professor Erfan emphasized the role of the Imam of the Time as the divine proof, explaining that this status has both epistemic and legal dimensions. Epistemically, the Imam reflects God's attributes, making his recognition a religious necessity, while legally, his existence leaves no excuse for deviation from the truth.
The program then transitioned to an analysis of Iran's economy by Dr. Mehdi Khatibi, who highlighted Iran's economic lag compared to the West and the devastating impact of the eight-year war.
The war caused approximately 440 billion dollars in damage, which severely hindered economic progress despite post-war reconstruction efforts.
Dr. Khatibi noted that incorrect policies, such as excessive dam construction, have led to environmental issues like dust storms in recent decades.
He attributed the average 20% inflation rate to the rapid growth of a developing economy, while noting that US sanctions after 2011 exacerbated this, pushing inflation to 40%.
The broadcast concluded with a feature on a successful family-run mushroom production business in Sistan and Baluchestan.
