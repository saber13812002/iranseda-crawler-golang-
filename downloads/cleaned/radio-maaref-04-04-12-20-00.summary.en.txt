Shiites around the world observed the seventh day of Muharram with mourning ceremonies in Iran, Iraq, and Europe to honor Imam Hussein. In the news segment, the Chief of Staff of Iran's Armed Forces warned the Zionist regime of a decisive response to any potential aggression. Economic reports indicate that Iran's missile strikes inflicted heavy damage on Israel, including infrastructure destruction and a significant decline in economic growth. Iraqi officials and citizens expressed solidarity with Iran by visiting the Iranian embassy in Baghdad and supporting the Supreme Leader. The Secretary-General of Hezbollah emphasized resistance, refusing to surrender to Israeli threats or hand over weapons. In Gaza, the government's TV office reported 300 Palestinian deaths in 48 hours, while Amnesty International criticized the regime for turning aid distribution centers into deadly traps. The program concluded with the recitation of Dua Kumayl, praying for the martyrs and the resilience of the bereaved.
