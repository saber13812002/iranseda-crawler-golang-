The program began with the recitation of the rain prayer from the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya before transitioning to a discussion on the moral vice of negative judgment. The guest emphasized that true nobility requires avoiding unfair criticism and increasing one's awareness of its harmful consequences. He cited the example of Abu Dharr to illustrate how asceticism and refraining from judging others elevate one's spiritual status. The second proposed solution is to ignore the approval or disapproval of others and focus exclusively on pleasing God. The story of Luqman the Wise and his son was detailed to demonstrate that it is impossible to satisfy everyone's opinions. The core message is that one must not be bound by public praise or criticism. Instead, the heart should be occupied with divine proximity rather than worldly validation. This approach liberates individuals from the psychological distress caused by others' judgments.
