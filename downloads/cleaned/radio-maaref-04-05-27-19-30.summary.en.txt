The radio program begins by emphasizing the need for Muslims to remain vigilant against political tactics and infiltration, noting that true faith must not be shaken by the superficial comings and goings of specific groups. The discussion then critiques improper behaviors during religious ceremonies, such as disturbing people with loud voices during Ashura nights and seeking fame through distinctive attire, which are deemed contrary to religious principles and the guidance of Maraji. The core of the program focuses on a Quranic analysis of lying, describing it as a heart disease rooted in a lack of faith. The commentator cites Quranic verses to identify lying as the primary cause of misguidance, hypocrisy, and deprivation from divine guidance, while also outlining the characteristics of liars. It is stressed that honesty is the practical manifestation of inner faith, and abandoning lies is a prerequisite for reaching the station of truth and achieving salvation. Finally, the severe consequences of lying, including painful punishment and distance from eternal salvation, are discussed, urging listeners to avoid falsehood and practice honesty even in difficult circumstances.
