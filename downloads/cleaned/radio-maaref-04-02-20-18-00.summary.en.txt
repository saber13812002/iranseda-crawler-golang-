This radio program discusses the concept of piety as a fundamental human need and a prerequisite for spiritual survival.
The main segment explores the signs of divine punishment in the Quran through the stories of past nations such as the Pharaohs, the people of Lot, and the Adites.
Hajeh Falsafeh cites Quranic verses to identify natural disasters, epidemics, and agricultural plagues as manifestations of God's wrath.
A key distinction is made between the physical punishments of ancient tribes and the spiritual punishments relevant to the final era.
While physical transformations are no longer common, internal or metaphysical punishments still exist for sinners and may manifest in the afterlife.
The second part features an exegesis of a verse from Surah Al-Imran, defining the prophets' duty as mere conveyance and invitation to Islam.
The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) is advised to avoid futile arguments with stubborn individuals and to answer only sincere inquiries.
The final conclusion emphasizes that knowledge alone is insufficient; one must submit to the truth and avoid arrogance and stubbornness.
