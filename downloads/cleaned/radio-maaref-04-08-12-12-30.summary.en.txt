In this Radio Ma'aref program, Hojjat al-Islam Rostamnejad explores the complex and reciprocal relationship between belief, inner ethics, and outward action. Using the story of Satan's refusal to prostrate, he demonstrates that every apparent action is rooted in three layers: external behavior, spiritual traits, and theological worldview. From a Quranic perspective, these three domains not only influence each other but also allow outward actions to feed back into and reshape a person's inner character. The Prophet (PBUH) initiated social reform by first correcting thought and establishing monotheism, as correct belief is the foundation of proper conduct. The Quran in Surah Ar-Rum illustrates that believing in God as the provider directly leads to generosity and liberation from stinginess. Thus, action is not merely a mirror of the inner self but an active agent in changing one's essence and destiny. The discussion emphasizes that true reform requires simultaneous attention to all these layers and an understanding of their bidirectional impact.
