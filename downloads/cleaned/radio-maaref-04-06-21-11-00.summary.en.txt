The radio broadcast began by highlighting a municipal initiative that leverages community participation and mosque cooperation to improve local urban services. Coinciding with Unity Week, the program reported on the wedding ceremonies of 210 young couples held at the shrine of Hazrat Masoumeh (SA). The literary segment featured the book "Ank An Yateem Nazar Kardeh," a narrative retelling of the Prophet's life that has been translated into several languages. Hujjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the importance of emulating the Prophet's noble character while avoiding negative traits, citing relevant religious narrations. He also discussed the profound devotion of Allameh Tabatabaei to the Ahl al-Bayt, noting his regular visits to the shrine in Qom. The program then addressed the critical water crisis in Iran, urging for strict conservation and the correction of consumption patterns in both agriculture and households. Finally, the jurisprudential segment clarified the religious ruling on disclosing specific medical conditions during pre-marital tests to prevent potential harm to the family unit.
