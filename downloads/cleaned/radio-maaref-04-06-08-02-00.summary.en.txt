The program initially emphasized the religious duty of preserving the honor of believers and avoiding backbiting. Dr. Shariat-Zarshenas then analyzed the concept of "paradigm shift," arguing that it is a strategy used by ruling factions to evade accountability. He asserted that these groups have controlled the country's policy-making for over four decades by implementing a neoliberal paradigm. The consequences of this approach include structural inflation, unemployment, widening class gaps, and foreign policy failures such as the JCPOA and failed negotiations with the US. Those advocating for a paradigm shift are actually the architects of the current crises and are now deflecting blame to avoid responsibility. The expert noted that the revolutionary paradigm was only operationalized in the military sector, while the economy and culture remained under neoliberal influence. Therefore, the true solution lies not in further submission, but in returning to a revolutionary mindset that fosters social responsibility across all domains.
