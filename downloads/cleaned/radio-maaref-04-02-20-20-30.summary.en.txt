This radio program begins by highlighting the extensive humanitarian services of Ayatollah Seyed Kemaleddin Faghihaimani in Isfahan. Relying on public trust, he constructed charitable hospitals such as Hazrat Zahra and Imam Musa Kazem in deprived areas, dedicating his life to healthcare and education. The second part of the show features the memoirs of Ali Azhar Meshayekhi, a veteran who was captured during Operation Khorramshahr and held in Iraqi captivity for over seven years. He recounts the harsh conditions of his imprisonment, including initial torture, staged transfers for enemy propaganda, and the dire state of the POW hospital with minimal resources. Meshayekhi also describes the prisoners' efforts to expose the truth to foreign journalists and counter the false narratives of the Iraqi regime. These memories illustrate the steadfast faith and courageous resistance of the freed prisoners against physical and psychological hardships.
