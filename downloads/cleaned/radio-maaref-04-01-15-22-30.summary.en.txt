In this radio program, Professor Kafil emphasized the necessity of linking authentic civilizations with a monotheistic culture based on the satisfaction of God's Proof, citing verse 18 of Surah Saba. Relying on narrations from Imam Sadiq (AS), he identified justice as the fundamental principle of life and the factor that revives the earth from the death caused by oppression and misguidance. Consequently, those awaiting the Mahdi must make justice the central axis of their lives to prepare the ground for his reappearance. In the second part, listeners declared their commitments to self-improvement, praying on time, obeying the Supreme Leader, and serving those in need. These pledges include avoiding sins, participating in ziyarat ceremonies, and translating hadiths from the Siyyer al-Sajjadiyya. Finally, referencing narrations from the Ahl al-Bayt (AS), the expectation of the Mahdi's return was highlighted as the best deed and a means to increase spiritual rewards.
