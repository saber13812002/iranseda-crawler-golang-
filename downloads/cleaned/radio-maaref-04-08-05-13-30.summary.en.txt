This radio program, titled "Tales of Freedom," features the memoirs of Hamidreza Jafari, a veteran who was wounded in the Muharram operation in 1982 and captured by Iraqi forces. Jafari recounts the hardships of interrogation, his temporary loss of vision, and his transfers to various Iraqi prisons, including Ali Amara and Baghdad. He describes the prisoners' hunger strike to alleviate pressure and their courage in breaking down cell doors to access water and better conditions. The narrative also highlights the severe physical violence, the martyrdom of fellow prisoners, and the covert efforts to maintain religious practices within the camps. Citing the Supreme Leader, the program characterizes the period of captivity as a significant spiritual reserve for the freed prisoners. The central theme is the concept of sincerity in action, which reaches its peak in the harsh conditions of captivity where only God is a witness. Stories of sharing food with fellow prisoners and caring for the wounded are presented as living examples of this pure devotion and servitude.
