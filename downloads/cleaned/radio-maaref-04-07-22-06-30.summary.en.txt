The program begins with an interpretation of a Quranic verse from Surah Al-Ankabut, describing the world as mere play and the Hereafter as the abode of permanence.
This contrast implies that the world is like a living animal, while the Hereafter represents a state of seriousness and piety.
Piety is defined as avoiding frivolous play and deception, supported by two narrations of the Prophet Muhammad regarding not deceiving animals or children.
Listeners participate by sending voice messages sharing memorable literary and personal quotes that have left a lasting impression on them.
The "History Calendar" segment highlights the coronation of Cyrus the Great in Babylon in 539 BC, marking the start of the Achaemenid Empire.
It also discusses the historical Portuguese occupation of the Strait of Hormuz and the British role in limiting Iran's naval power.
The broadcast concludes by mentioning World Standardization Day and inviting listeners from Fars province to join future programs.
