The cultural segment began with a memory of Samuel Khachikian, who was beaten while waiting in line for the premiere of his own film. Cinema critics identified Khachikian as the father of Iranian police cinema and the director of the blockbuster "Agapa." The film "Agapa," based on the true story of an Iranian pilot rescued by Kurds, holds the record for the highest number of viewers in Iranian cinema history. Khachikian's son also highlighted his father's deep love for Iran and how it was reflected in his works, particularly "Agapa."
