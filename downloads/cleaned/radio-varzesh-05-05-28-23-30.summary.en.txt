Fatemeh Ebrahimzadeh, the first female federation president in Iranian sports history, recounts her merit-based journey to this prestigious position. In 2005, she was appointed head of the Physical Fitness and Aerobics Federation by the Sports Organization and was subsequently elected with 100% of the votes. Despite initial support, a change in government led to broken promises regarding budget and facilities, forcing her to start the federation from scratch with minimal resources. Relying on personal motivation and her husband's support, she modernized the sport by hiring foreign experts and achieving international medals for the national teams. However, after five years of successful leadership, administrative changes resulted in the federation being downgraded back to an association. She criticizes the lack of professional expertise in sports management and expresses regret over her return to a zero-level position in retirement. Ebrahimzadeh concludes by emphasizing the vital importance of physical activity for women's health and urges society to prioritize this aspect of well-being.
