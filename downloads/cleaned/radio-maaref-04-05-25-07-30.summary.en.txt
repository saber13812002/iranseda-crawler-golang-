In this radio program, the spiritual passion of Arbaeen pilgrims is first highlighted as a symbol of Shia power and resistance against enemies. The discussion then shifts to criticizing the prevalence of lying and denial in social media and modern media, which is rooted in disbelief and the ego-driven self. It is explained that lying is not only a moral corruption but also an injustice to others and a tool of Satan, exacerbated by modern devices like mobile phones. From the Quranic perspective, liars are considered kin to all previous liars and will be judged alongside them on the Day of Judgment. The program emphasizes that a true Shia must strictly adhere to the path of Imam Ali (AS) in belief, ethics, and lifestyle. Finally, Imam Ali (AS) is presented as the divine scale by which a person's faith and actions are measured to determine their true closeness to God.
