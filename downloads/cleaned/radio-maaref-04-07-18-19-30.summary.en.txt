In this Radio Ma'aref program, Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallah discussed practical solutions for overcoming verbal abuse and foul language. He analyzed a narration from Imam Sadiq (AS) about a rude woman who was cured of her bad speech after eating a morsel from the Prophet's (PBUH) hand. The key takeaway is the importance of seeking spiritual guidance through the Quran and Hadith, rather than solely relying on the physical presence of the Imams. Professor Fallah emphasized that the Quran itself acts as a spiritual remedy, provided it is recited with sincere intention and a genuine desire for self-improvement. He noted that if believers continue to use foul language despite reciting the Quran, they must reevaluate their approach to these divine sources. The ultimate goal is to transform these teachings into a lifestyle, thereby avoiding moral vices and cultivating virtues in both personal and social spheres.
