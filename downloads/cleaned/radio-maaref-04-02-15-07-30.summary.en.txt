The radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat" began by emphasizing the importance of kindness and caring for others, with listeners discussing how to support colleagues and address social issues. The program's guest, Hujjat al-Islam Fala, explored the ethical concept of "Qadabi" and the consequences of abandoning one's post in the front line of justice. Citing Quranic verses, he stressed that neglecting religious and moral duties such as justice, prayer, and monotheism invites divine wrath. The guest corrected a previous classification error, moving the topic of "Haram-khari" from moral to practical deviations. He highlighted the inherent mission of Muslims to uphold monotheism, faith, and ethics, urging the audience to avoid shirk and injustice. The broadcast concluded with congratulations for the birth of Imam Hasan Askari (AS) and a call to practice moral virtues.
