The radio program "I am Fatima" on Radio Ma'aref began by discussing the virtues of Hazrat Zahra (SA) and interpreting her Ziyarat.
Key points included her status as "Raziya and Marziya," signifying total submission to divine decree, and her status as "Mazlum and Maghsuba" due to the usurpation of Fadak and her painful martyrdom.
It was emphasized that Hazrat Zahra (SA) and Imam Zaman (AJ) are witnesses to the actions of Shia, and following the Fatimi lifestyle is a sign of love for the Ahlulbayt and a source of spiritual peace.
Subsequently, the speeches of Martyr Baha'ei regarding the necessity of the revolutionary school and school-based justice in society were presented.
Martyr Baha'ei stressed the need to abandon personal favoritism and adhere to honesty and relationships based on Islamic values to achieve an ideal society.
The program concluded with congratulations on the 22nd of Bahman, commemorating the victory of the Islamic Revolution as a symbol of the nation's steadfastness and the manifestation of Imam Khomeini's (RA) ideals.
