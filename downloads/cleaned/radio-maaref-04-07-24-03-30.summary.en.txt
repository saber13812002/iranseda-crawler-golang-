This radio program examined the prelude to the Battle of Jamal and Imam Ali's (PBUH) efforts to prevent it. Imam Ali (PBUH) repeatedly sent letters and intermediaries, including Talha, Zubayr, and Ibn Abbas, urging Aisha and her allies to return to Medina and avoid conflict. Despite these diplomatic efforts, Aisha consolidated forces in Basra and sent provocative letters to Syria and Medina, demonstrating her resolve to confront Imam Ali (PBUH). The program recounted a narrative where Aisha and Hafsah celebrated, only to be confronted by Umm Kulthum, Ali's daughter, who rebuked them for their actions. Imam Ali (PBUH) marched from Kufa to Basra with twelve thousand troops and established his camp at Ziqar. He continued to seek peaceful resolution until the very end, but the opposing faction rejected all peace proposals, leading to the preparation for battle. The discussion highlighted Imam Ali's (PBUH) patience and diligence in seeking peace, contrasting it with the haste of his opponents, based on historical sources.
