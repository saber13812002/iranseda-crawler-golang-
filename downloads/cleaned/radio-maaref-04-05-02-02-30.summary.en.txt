In the first segment, Zahra Nasiri, the wife of martyr Javad Akbari from Afghanistan, recounts the pain of separation and her husband's journey to Syria to defend the shrine, including his farewell message on the Day of Arafah.
The second section features a jurisprudential discussion by Hujjat al-Islam Esfahani regarding the style of reciting Ruzh, emphasizing the need to maintain the dignity of the gathering and avoid imitating secular or non-authentic musical styles.
In the fatwa section, it was declared that placing conditions in family games, which resemble gambling, is haram according to Ayatollah Khamenei.
It was also explained that bleeding after performing Wudu does not invalidate the Wudu itself, but the body must be free from impurity for prayer.
Another response detailed the method of following the Imam in prayer during the final Tashahhud for followers of Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi.
Finally, the ruling on cleaning dried blood under the fingernails before Wudu was explained based on Imam Khomeini's fatwa.
The program concluded with the broadcast of the "Sabh-e Niaz" morning show and pre-dawn prayers as the Adhan for Fajr in Qom was approaching.
