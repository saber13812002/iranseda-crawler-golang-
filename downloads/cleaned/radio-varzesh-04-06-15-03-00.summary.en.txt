Ashouri Gholam-Mohammadi, a former national team player and basketball coach, shared his career highlights in a radio interview, covering his time with the Islamic Azad University, Paykan, and Homa teams. He recalled how the university's facility was acquired by the late Dr. Tehrani, leading to the formation of a dominant team that won multiple national championships. After the university team was dissolved, Gholam-Mohammadi transitioned to coaching at Paykan, where he rose through the ranks with the support of colleagues and club officials. He also described his unique experience with the Homa team, where he initially served as an assistant coach before becoming the head coach, utilizing video analysis to achieve impressive results. The retired coach highlighted the crucial role played by university officials like Dr. Ghadimi and Dr. Dadkan in promoting university sports and encouraging athletes to pursue higher education. Following his retirement, he held executive and coaching positions at the Shimidour, Nbuq in Iraq, and Paykan clubs. Gholam-Mohammadi reflected on personal hardships, including the loss of his first wife during his peak career, and credited his second wife for his continued success. He concluded by expressing concern over the lack of a new generation of star players comparable to Hamad Hady and Mehdi Kamrani, while thanking the Radio Sport listeners for their support.
