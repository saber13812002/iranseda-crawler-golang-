In this radio program, the necessity of protesting against oppression and cursing enemies of religion, such as America and Israel, was explained as a religious duty based on Quranic verses and narrations.
The guest clarified that disavowing oppressors and chanting slogans like "Death to America" are rooted in Islamic teachings, citing specific verses and prayers.
The discussion then shifted to the "rights of the tongue," emphasizing the importance of eloquence and purity in speech according to the teachings of Imam Ali and the Prophet.
It was stressed that language is a spiritual asset that must be protected from vulgarity and filled with beneficial words.
To enrich one's vocabulary, the guest suggested marking sophisticated words in books and recording them in a dedicated notebook.
Listeners were advised not to rush in using these new words but to first verify their precise meanings in dictionaries.
Finally, it was noted that eloquent speech should be context-appropriate to avoid appearing artificial, with the ultimate goal being the sincere transmission of profound concepts.
