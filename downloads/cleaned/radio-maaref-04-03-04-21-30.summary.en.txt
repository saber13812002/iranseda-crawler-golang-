Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets the Quran by describing the Day of Judgment as the manifestation of human inner realities rather than merely an external event. Using the metaphor of a marketplace, he posits that a human being is simultaneously the seller, the commodity, and the market, as one sells their soul to God through covenant while also becoming the fuel for hell if they commit injustice. The lecture further analyzes five categories of Quranic verses to demonstrate that all human deeds will be presented in their original quality, whether sincere or hypocritical. A central argument is that the individual is inherently the scribe, the book, and the written content of their own record of deeds. This internal record emerges from the soul itself, which does not contradict the role of angels in recording actions, as both perspectives are valid. Ultimately, the individual faces the full reality of their actions on the Day of Judgment, with nothing remaining hidden.
