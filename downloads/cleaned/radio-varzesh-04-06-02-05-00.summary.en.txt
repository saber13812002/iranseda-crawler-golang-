Saeed Morteza expresses deep regret over his emotional decisions to decline national team invitations during his peak years. He argues that stepping away from the national squad and his on-field protest against the coach cost him significant opportunities, despite being named the best player of the year. Morteza also discusses the non-sporting obstacles that prevented him from becoming the caretaker of Esteghlal and the coaching offers he rejected out of loyalty to the club. He criticizes the current football academy system, emphasizing the urgent need to support young, deserving players to prevent the collapse of Iranian football. Finally, Morteza voices his concern over the current state of Esteghlal and the national team, calling for structural reforms and the appointment of competent managers.
