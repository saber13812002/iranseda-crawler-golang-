Hamid Kashani's sermon highlights the Prophet's humility and his deep concern for guiding believers, citing Quranic verses.
He emphasizes the unique status of Imam Ali as the Prophet's sword and crown, based on Imam Sadiq's supplication.
The program then shifts to the war memoirs of Air Force Colonel Qalijkhani during the early days of the Iran-Iraq War.
His accounts include rescuing wounded soldiers in extreme heat and evading enemy aircraft in the rugged terrain of Kurdistan.
The narrative also tells the tragic love story of martyr Ali-Asghar Mahmoonia and nurse Parvaneh Shamaei-Zadeh.
These stories illustrate the purity, sincerity, and sacrifice of the Iranian defenders and the general public.
The broadcast concludes by encouraging listeners to emulate these virtues and honor the memory of the martyrs.
