In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Falahati examined the deviation from practical and devotional monotheism, emphasizing that theoretical belief in God's oneness must be accompanied by obedience to divine laws. Citing verse 61 of Surah Al-Baqarah, he explained that disregarding God's commands and ignoring His limits directly leads to divine wrath and punishment. Falahati asserted that theoretical monotheism without practical manifestation is a baseless claim, and true faith is only revealed through one's behavior, prayer, and personal ethics. He also noted that the assassination of prophets and deviation from the path of truth were primary reasons for God's anger toward the Children of Israel. The core message is that our lives are a collection of voluntary choices, where every action, good or bad, serves as a practical prayer that yields its result before God. Therefore, to avoid divine punishment and gain His satisfaction, one must remain steadfast in practical monotheism and abstain from prohibitions. In the final segment of the program, Professor Tabatabai also explained the rulings of Umrah Tamattu and the specific Miqat locations for Iranian pilgrims.
