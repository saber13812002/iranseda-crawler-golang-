In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Fala discussed the harms of lying from the perspective of the Quran and Islamic traditions. He described lying as a heart disease rooted in a lack of faith, which leads to the misguidance of both the liar and others. Signs of liars include insisting on appearing truthful while concealing their deceit by attributing lies to God and the Prophet. The program also highlighted that excessive listening to lies and gossip is a negative habit that must be controlled. The consequences of lying were listed as hypocrisy, deprivation of divine guidance, lack of salvation, and severe punishment in the afterlife. Towards the end, the importance of gratitude and reciting the supplication "Allah Majl Wali Kull Farjah" for spiritual peace was emphasized. Finally, honesty was presented as the true sign of faith and the key to salvation, urging listeners to abandon lying.
