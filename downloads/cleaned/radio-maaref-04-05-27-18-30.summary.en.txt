This radio program provided jurisprudential answers to several questions. According to Ayatollah Sobhani's fatwa, reciting the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya in the Qunut of the Eid prayer is permissible. Based on Ayatollah Javadi Amoli's view, performing wudu for mourning ceremonies is recommended but not obligatory, and omitting it is not a sin. To prolong prostration and bowing, using the specific supplications "Subhan Rabbi al-Azim" and "Subhan Rabbi al-A'la" is sufficient. The program also highlighted the spiritual merit of praying on Friday nights and in sacred places like mosques, where the reward is multiplied. It further discussed the significance of Takbirat al-Ihram in severing worldly attachments. The broadcast concluded by honoring notable martyrs and emphasizing the importance of visiting their shrines.
