In this radio interview, the guest emphasizes the economic and cultural value of handicrafts, warning that modernity has led to the neglect and potential loss of these traditional arts. He notes that despite the high global revenue of this industry, Iran's share is minimal, and these home-based occupations are unfortunately not properly recognized or supported. Citing the Supreme Leader's designation of the current year as the "Year of Investment for Production," he stresses the urgent need for investment in and support for local producers. Examples of handicrafts from the Shahroud region, such as pottery, carpet weaving, embroidery, and ceramics, are highlighted as strong economic and employment opportunities. The guest argues that supporting these artisans is not only economically beneficial but also rooted in Islamic culture, representing a spiritual heritage. He encourages the public to support these artisans by purchasing their products, thereby helping to preserve this cultural heritage and create jobs. Finally, he urges officials to pay closer attention to the Supreme Leader's statements on the importance of tourism and handicrafts, aiming to convert these potentials into marketable products.
