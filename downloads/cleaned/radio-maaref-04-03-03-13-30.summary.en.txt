In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Sadeghi examines the epistemological relationship between religion and ethics, emphasizing the independence of rational moral laws from divine law. He argues that no divine ruling can contradict rational justice, and any apparent conflict must be resolved by interpreting the text allegorically. The story of the sacrifice of Ismail is presented as a primary challenge to the compatibility of divine command and ethics. The speaker explains that God, as the absolute owner of life, has the inherent right to take lives whenever He deems it necessary, which is not inherently immoral. The uniqueness of this event lies in its nature as a severe test for Abraham, rather than being a violation of moral principles. Finally, the program highlights the liberation of Khorramshahr during Operation Beit al-Moqaddas, asserting that faith and reliance on God, rather than material calculations, were the decisive factors in achieving victory against superior military powers.
