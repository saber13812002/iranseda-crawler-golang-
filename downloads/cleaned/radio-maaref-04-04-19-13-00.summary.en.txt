The radio program begins by broadcasting a historic 1969 speech by Ayatollah Shahid Allameh Tabatabaei regarding the historical and political roots of the Palestine and Jewish issues. The speaker refutes ancient Jewish claims to the land, demonstrating that Palestine historically belonged to Muslims and Christians, and that Jews only migrated there after World War I through British conspiracies and the establishment of the United Nations. He highlights the extensive financial support provided by the global Jewish diaspora to Israel, contrasting it with the lack of solidarity among Muslims, and calls for both financial and spiritual aid to the Palestinians. Allameh Tabatabaei emphasizes that indifference to the oppression in Palestine is a violation of religious and human duties, urging Muslims to stand against Zionist aggression with courage and empathy. In the closing segment, the speech is compared to the bravery of Iranian fighters in recent conflicts against Israel, honoring the martyrs of the defensive war. The program also invites the public to attend a memorial ceremony for newly deceased martyrs, including Hajj Ramazan, at the Prophet Muhammad Mosque in Tehran. The final message underscores Iran's resilience and unity against its enemies, instilling hope for ultimate victory in the hearts of the audience.
