The program began by commemorating the anniversary of the passing of Lady Fatima Masumeh, highlighting her status as a scholar and intercessor. The main segment focused on the criminal case of Mohammadreza Ghasemi, who defrauded 85 elderly victims by falsely claiming family ties and exploiting fears related to the pandemic. The suspect, who operated in 18 different cities, amassed approximately 35 billion tomans, which he converted into gold and foreign currency. The judge ordered the suspect to be sent to a psychological rehabilitation center and encouraged other victims to file formal complaints. Practical advice was then provided to prevent such scams, including asking probing questions, requesting time to verify identities, and ensuring the elderly are not left alone. The broadcast concluded with a discussion on how the Sacred Defense served as a catalyst for revealing the hidden talents of young Iranians, transforming them into capable military commanders.
