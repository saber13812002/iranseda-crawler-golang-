The program begins by analyzing Abu Dharr al-Ghifari's inappropriate joke with Bilal and his subsequent apology, emphasizing the importance of ethical boundaries in humor to avoid hurting others' feelings. Abu Dharr is portrayed as a courageous and justice-seeking figure from the Prophet's household, whom the Prophet identified as the closest to him on Judgment Day due to his steadfastness. The historical section highlights Abu Dharr's role in the conquest of Mecca and his fair treatment of prisoners, including granting safety to polytheists. The Prophet offered Abu Dharr fifty-six pieces of advice, urging him to worship God with true knowledge, appreciate the blessings of youth and health, and avoid procrastination. After the Prophet's passing, Abu Dharr was exiled for criticizing the rulers and eventually passed away in the desert of Rabadh. He died with confidence in the Prophet's promises and was buried by loyal companions like Malik Ashtar and Abdullah ibn Masud. The episode concludes with the recitation of verses from Surah Yasin and an invitation to join the program's digital channels.
