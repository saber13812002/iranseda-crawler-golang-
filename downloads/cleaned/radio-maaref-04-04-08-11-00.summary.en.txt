The funeral of Major General Amir Ali Hajizadeh, commander of the IRGC Air Force and known as the father of Iran's drone industry, was held at Behesht-e Zahra with massive public attendance. He was martyred in an attack on an IRGC facility, having previously overseen significant advancements in Iran's missile and drone capabilities. The Foreign Minister emphasized the Iranian people's resolve to resist foreign interference and determine their own destiny in response to these martyrdoms. Over 1,300 prominent Sunni scholars issued a statement declaring resistance against the Zionist regime a religious obligation. A book titled "Life and Intellectual Legacy of the Supreme Leader" was published in English by Algoritm London. The Red Crescent announced that over 2,200 holy shrines would host mourners during the Muharram and Safar months. The program's jurisprudential segment discussed the rulings on mourning rituals and the necessity of repentance before death, citing the story of Pharaoh. Finally, fatwas regarding the validity of wudu (ablution) when one is uncertain about having performed it were addressed.
