This radio program emphasizes, based on the Verse of Affection and Islamic narrations, that love for the Ahl al-Bayt, particularly Imam Hussein (PBUH), is the root of Islam's survival and salvation for Muslims. The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) highlighted the high status of Imams Hasan and Hussein (PBUH) as the main branches of the tree of prophethood and as moral models for all humanity. Poets and eulogists also depicted the longing of pilgrims for Karbala and the noble character of Imam Hasan (PBUH) through their verses. The program then discussed the recitation of Ziyarat Al-Yasin and acknowledging the guardianship of the twelve Imams as a path to peace and salvation. The final section criticized the isolation of Islam within purely ritualistic frameworks, arguing that it was a comprehensive religion during the Prophet's time. It was noted that mosques served as centers for political and social decision-making, not just worship. Therefore, restoring Islam to its original comprehensive role and preventing its reduction to an isolated religion is an undeniable necessity for the Islamic community.
