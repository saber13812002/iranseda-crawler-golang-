In this radio program, Professor Reimpour analyzes the consequences of Ali (AS) being denied the caliphate, relying on Fatima's (SA) Shiqshqiyya sermon. He argues that this decision planted the seeds of violence and betrayal in Islamic history, paving the way for dictatorial and corrupt regimes in subsequent centuries. The speaker emphasizes that Ali, with his complete understanding of the path, could have guided society toward perfection with peace and justice, but deviating from this course led to a regression to pre-Islamic values and spiritual apostasy. The discussion also highlights Ali's role in battles and his tense relationship with certain tribes, which was the primary reason for their resistance to his leadership. Finally, the speaker expresses regret for the fate of future generations, complains to God, and underscores the necessity of adhering to the path of truth and justice.
