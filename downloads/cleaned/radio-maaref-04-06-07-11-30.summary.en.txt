The program "Zamzam Ahkam" initially addressed the ethical and financial dilemmas physicians face regarding expensive diagnostics, emphasizing the necessity of prudence in patient costs. It then answered fiqh questions about the prayer and fasting obligations of couples residing in different cities, based on Ayatollah Khamenei's rulings. The purity of blister fluid and the khums rules for purchasing gold or coins for a spouse were also clarified. Furthermore, the program explained that tajweed is not mandatory in prayer and that crying during prayer does not invalidate it according to Ayatollah Shaberi Zanjani. The impropriety of reserving specific spots in the mosque for congregational prayer was highlighted, stressing the public nature of the space. In the "Friday Call" segment, Ayatollah Saffai of Bushehr described Friday prayer as a school for knowledge, unity, and social and economic services. Finally, Hojjat al-Islam Fallahzadeh stressed the importance of maintaining silence and full attention during the Friday sermon to maximize its spiritual benefits.
