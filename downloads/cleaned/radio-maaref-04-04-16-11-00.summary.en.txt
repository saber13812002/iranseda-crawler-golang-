The program began by covering the Ashura evening ceremony at Imam Khomeini's Hosseiniyeh and in Zanjan, where Hujjat al-Islam Rafiei discussed the causes of the Karbala event. News reports highlighted the successful traffic management for 12 million pilgrims in Karbala and the reissue of the book "Hamaseh-ye Hosseini" by Martyr Motahari, emphasizing its heroic aspects. Additionally, the start of ticket sales for the Arbaeen trains at stable prices with increased capacity was announced. The news segment continued with the death of Major General Mohammad Saeed Izadi, a key resistance commander and victim of an Israeli assassination. In the cultural section, the title "Qateel al-Abra" for Imam Hussein (PBUH) was interpreted as the martyr who brings tears and establishes a school of lessons. The pivotal role of Lady Zainab (SA) in conveying the message of Karbala and related mourning songs were also discussed. Finally, jurisprudential discussions on the rulings of jihad and defense based on Quranic verses were presented, along with answers to religious questions regarding women's movement in the shrine during menstruation and the effect of bird feathers on prayer.
