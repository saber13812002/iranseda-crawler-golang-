The radio program "Enduring Memories" begins by introducing two veterans from the Jihad-e Sazandegi organization. The first segment recounts a story from Operation Fajr-5 where an enemy helicopter was hit by an RPG, though the identity of the shooter was never determined. The second part features the memoirs of wounded veteran Mohammad Ghasemi, who describes his deployment to the front lines in 1985 as a loader driver during Operations Fajr-8 and Karbala-5. He details dangerous encounters with shrapnel, an ammunition truck explosion, and the harsh conditions that led to his injury. The program concludes with a discussion on the role of students in creating social and political change, as well as the importance of volunteer forces in national crises and the resistance front.
