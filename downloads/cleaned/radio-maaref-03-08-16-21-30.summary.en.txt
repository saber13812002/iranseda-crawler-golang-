The program began by congratulating the birthday of Hazrat Zahra (SA) and honoring nurses, then proceeded to interpret verses 148 to 152 of Surah An-Nisa. The commentator highlighted the fundamental difference between "faith and disbelief" and "obedience and disobedience," noting that while good and bad deeds can be mixed, faith and disbelief are like purity and impurity, which cannot be blended. Consequently, accepting some prophets while rejecting others, as seen among the People of the Book, is considered absolute disbelief rather than a middle path, as denying prophethood inherently denies God's word. The Quran describes this group as "true disbelievers," emphasizing that there is no intermediate position between accepting and rejecting prophethood. In contrast, true believers are those who accept all prophets and heavenly books without distinguishing between them in the principle of prophethood. God promises forgiveness and mercy to these believers, even if they commit operational mistakes.
