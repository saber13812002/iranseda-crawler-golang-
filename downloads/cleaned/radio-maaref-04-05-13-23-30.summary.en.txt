In this radio program, the expert emphasizes the importance of building reciprocal and positive relationships between parents and children, urging parents to improve the home's social and emotional atmosphere by changing their own behavior. Responding to a listener concerned about their eight-year-old son's media addiction, the expert explains that children at this age require physical activity and peer interaction rather than passive television viewing. The expert warns that prolonged inactivity and excessive media consumption can harm a child's physical and mental health, potentially even reducing their intelligence. To address this issue, he suggests forming friendship groups among families so that children can play in safe, active environments. A book titled "Media Literate Parenting" is also mentioned, which examines the educational harms caused by media. Finally, the primary responsibility for raising a child lies with the parents, and media should not replace human interaction.
