In the radio program "Zamzam Ahkam," jurisprudential answers were provided to listeners' questions.
Hunting with a firearm is permissible if the name of God is invoked and the animal is killed.
Performing one ghusl janabat with the proper intention suffices for other obligatory ghusls.
Travelers whose workplace is in another city pray shortened prayers and do not fast.
Resting one's feet during Quran recitation is permissible if it does not constitute disrespect according to custom.
Using moisturizing cream while fasting is allowed without any restriction.
The time for Maghrib and Isha prayers extends until the middle of the night, or until dawn if there is a valid excuse.
In the "Makarim Khuban" segment, Ayatollah Mazarani emphasized the importance of practicing mutual support and self-sacrifice in daily life.
