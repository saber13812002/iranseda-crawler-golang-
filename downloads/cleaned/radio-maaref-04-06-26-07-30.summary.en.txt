The program begins with a visit to the shrine of Hajeh Arefim Arabab in Shede, highlighting his spiritual significance as a prominent student of Akhund Kashi.
The main discussion then shifts to the vice of gossiping and rumor-mongering, which is described as detrimental to social trust and healthy human relationships.
Imam Ali (AS) is cited as stating that a gossip is despised if truthful and punishable if lying, yet the path to repentance remains open.
Imam Sadiq (AS) also warned officials in a letter to the governor of Ahvaz to strictly avoid and not listen to gossips and bad-mouthing individuals.
Leaders and those in power must act with prudence and avoid quickly believing rumors, as these individuals often operate out of envy and a desire to cause harm.
Gossiping is likened to weeds in the garden of life, obstructing the growth of moral virtues and trust within the community.
Finally, the importance of avoiding this vice and repenting if committed is emphasized, with a prayer for divine protection against such individuals.
