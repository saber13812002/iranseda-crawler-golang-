Seyyed Ahmad Kavianpour, a former defender for Esteghlal, Sepahan, and Paykan, discusses his playing career and the challenges he faced. He recounts being unexpectedly dropped from the national team at his peak by coach Ali-Dousti, an event that significantly impacted his professional trajectory. Kavianpour admits that due to the specific conditions of Iranian football during his era, he could not earn enough to sustain a luxurious lifestyle and transitioned into coaching after retiring. He gained experience under coaches like Mahmoud Yavari and Abdollah Bisi while obtaining international licenses, and he is currently the head coach of Esteghlal's youth team. Kavianpour emphasizes that football has become an industry and players must view their futures realistically. He advises young athletes to seek proper counseling and work hard in any field where they have genuine talent. His ultimate goal as a coach is to transfer mental and motivational values to the new generation to prevent costly failures in football.
