In this radio program, religious rulings were provided in response to various listener inquiries. According to Ayatollah Khamenei's fatwa, fasting is not obligatory if it worsens a person's illness or delays their recovery. Conversing with non-mahram individuals is permissible provided that seclusion is avoided and inappropriate jokes are not made. Khums is due on parental stipends that have been held for a year without being spent on living expenses. If bleeding occurs after the end of menstruation but before the tenth day, it is considered part of the menstrual cycle, rendering previous prayers invalid. Traveling during Ramadan does not invalidate fasting if one arrives at the destination before sunset. Neglecting mosque attendance due to laziness or disregard for God's command is sinful, though valid excuses prevent this sin. Furthermore, running in a galloping manner during mourning ceremonies is discouraged if it causes disrespect or distress to the mourners.
