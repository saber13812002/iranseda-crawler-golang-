In this radio program, Seyyed Ahmad Faqih discusses the concept of monotheistic ethics and the importance of prayer as a means to deepen one's connection with God. He emphasizes that the core of prayer is recognizing the absolute human dependence on God in all matters, rather than merely asking for the resolution of specific problems. Faqih explains, citing Quranic verses and narrations, that a person must view themselves as poor and needy before God for prayer to hold true spiritual significance. In the second part, the topic of repentance is addressed, describing it as a noble and highly valuable station. The instructor warns that committing sins with the intention of repenting is a trap set by Satan and should not be viewed as a path to spiritual elevation. He reminds listeners that the ability to repent is itself a divine grace, and God loves those who turn back to Him. Finally, he advises that individuals should always seek God's protection and avoid the devil's temptations to sin.
