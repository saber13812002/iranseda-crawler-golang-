In this radio program, Dr. Javad Jafari argues that the presence of figures like Safian before the reappearance is necessary to expose the falsehood of global systems and awaken society to the need for justice. He cites the atrocities committed by the Zionist regime as a catalyst that has led to a global awakening and a rejection of hypocritical human rights claims. In the second segment, Dr. Amir Mohsen Erfan interprets a hadith from Imam Sadiq (AS) stating that God will extend the final day of the world to ensure the Mahdi's appearance. He emphasizes that this narrative provides certainty, dispels fear and despair among the waiting, and motivates believers to maintain steadfastness and perform righteous deeds against the dominance of falsehood. Additionally, the guest scholar explains that the Imam's will serves as a sign of God's will, urging believers to find peace through complete submission to the Imams' path. The program concludes by encouraging listeners to promote the Imam's name through digital media and art, and to send the phrase "Ya Mahdi" as a form of spiritual support.
