In this radio program, Professor Fallah explores the causes of divine anger and its manifestations in the material world. Citing Quranic verses, he identifies deviations in theoretical, ethical, and practical monotheism as the primary factors that provoke God's wrath. Divine anger is manifested in the Quran through natural disasters such as floods, earthquakes, the drowning of Pharaoh, and agricultural droughts. These punishments serve not only as retribution for major sins like idolatry and denying prophets but also as reminders to return to the path of truth and avoid moral corruption. A key point in the discussion is the internalization of these concepts, meaning that every individual possesses both "Abraham" and "Moses" as well as "Nimrod" and "Pharaoh" within themselves. Therefore, the main duty of a person is to strengthen the monotheistic aspects within and weaken the ego and internal disbelief to remain safe from divine punishment. The program concludes with advice on properly managing Hajj rituals and focusing on their spiritual virtues rather than turning them into purely political or social rallies.
