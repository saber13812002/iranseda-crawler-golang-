In this program, Professor Rasakhi analyzes the Supreme Leader's managerial strategies, focusing on the principles of republicanism and Islamism alongside cultural strategy. He emphasizes the Leader's crucial role in safeguarding elections and preventing their postponement, even during crises such as the president's martyrdom, thereby preserving the system's republican nature. A key point is the Leader's early warning in 1988 regarding a "massive cultural front" and foreign cultural aggression, which was unfortunately overlooked by some officials at the time. Despite the emphasis on "Jihadi management" and reconstruction after the war, subsequent governments' rigid and inappropriate responses to the "soft war" led to significant cultural damages and corruption. Professor Rasakhi argues that true national independence is impossible without cultural independence, and the Leader has consistently warned officials about these cultural and economic threats with precise foresight. Ultimately, he believes that fully adhering to the Leader's macro-level policies is the key to the system's success and the prevention of social and cultural harms.
