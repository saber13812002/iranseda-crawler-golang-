In this radio program, Professor Mohammadi addresses various theological questions from listeners. He clarifies that while repentance must be a personal act, dedicating the spiritual rewards of recitations to others is permissible. Regarding the concept of human vicegerency, he emphasizes that individuals must actualize their innate potential through worship and knowledge to reach perfection. The professor asserts that human authority is strictly limited by their capabilities and never equates to God's absolute power. Addressing historical queries, he rejects the narrative of a military invasion of Iran, describing the event as a cultural conflict between Islam and the Sassanid caste system. He argues that Iranians embraced Islam before the armies arrived and have been the greatest contributors to Islamic civilization. Finally, citing Quranic verses, he identifies the deliberate abandonment of prayer as a major sin that leads to hellfire in the afterlife.
