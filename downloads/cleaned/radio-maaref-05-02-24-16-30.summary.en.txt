In this radio program, Dr. Abutalibi emphasized the necessity for officials to provide honest explanations of the country's situation while avoiding populist approaches. He highlighted the critical role of major petrochemical and steel companies in the economy, demanding strict action against their commercial exploitation of the public. The expert then analyzed negotiations with the West, arguing that while adversaries use these talks for hidden agendas, Iran also leverages them to gain time and strengthen its military capabilities. Furthermore, Iran's decision not to pursue nuclear weapons was interpreted as a conscious choice driven by religious and political constraints to prevent a global consensus against the nation. Finally, Trump's trip to China amidst ongoing tensions was assessed as a sign of American weakness, with the prediction that China will not easily yield to Washington's demands due to the balance of power.
