This radio program primarily discusses the choice between studying in Iran or abroad, emphasizing the importance of serving one's homeland. The host cites the story of Dr. Chamran and his mother's advice to highlight the necessity of maintaining identity and using knowledge for Iran's benefit. Hujjat al-Islam Ghalamali references narrations from Imam Baqir and Imam Javad to stress the value of associating with righteous people and acquiring knowledge to solve societal problems. He warns that foreign environments may pose moral and religious challenges, requiring a firm faith to navigate them successfully. The second part of the program focuses on humility, which Professor Falah defines as focusing on one's own faults and serving others for God's pleasure. He illustrates true humility through the story of Imam Ali (AS) washing a guest's hand, showing that it involves serving without expecting rewards or feeling superior. Signs of humility include sitting in the lower part of a gathering, greeting others first, avoiding arguments, and staying away from hypocrisy and seeking praise.
