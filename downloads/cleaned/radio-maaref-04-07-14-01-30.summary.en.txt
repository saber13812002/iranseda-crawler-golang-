The program begins with an interpretation of Imam Ali's first sermon, emphasizing the crucial stages of self-knowledge, self-discovery, and self-building.
The main obstacles to spiritual and moral growth are identified as five negative traits: complacency in ignorance, stinginess with virtues, greed for the world, hypocrisy in actions, and arrogance in one's own opinion.
The broadcast then transitions to the personal memoirs of Mr. Naveed Tehrani regarding his captivity during the Iran-Iraq war and the Tang-e Ab Ghor operation.
He recounts his heart injury, his bravery in the face of enemy tanks, and how two fellow soldiers sacrificed their lives to save him.
These memories vividly portray the pure spirit, deep faith, and selfless dedication of the Basij and IRGC fighters under extreme wartime conditions.
The program concludes with a humorous anecdote from his time in captivity and special prayers for the Supreme Leader's health and the early reappearance of the Imam.
