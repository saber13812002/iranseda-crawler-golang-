In this radio interview, the guest emphasized the need to restructure football education in Iran, warning that young coaches must gain extensive experience in youth academies before managing senior teams. He identified the lack of fundamental training and outdated coaching knowledge as the primary reasons for the technical deficiencies seen in the top league, criticizing the absence of long-term planning compared to successful Asian nations. Drawing on his personal experiences playing alongside legends at Persepolis and the national team, he highlighted the crucial role of high-quality friendly matches against strong opponents in developing young players' mental and technical resilience. Furthermore, he stressed the importance of teaching players professional life skills to prevent their early decline and social isolation after their playing careers end. The guest concluded by urging coaches to show more courage in giving opportunities to young talents in major clubs, arguing that such risk-taking is essential for Iranian football to align with global standards and overcome its current stagnation.
