In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Fala continues his discussion on the vice of Qadabi and the reasons for God's anger towards humans. He corrects a previous classification error by moving the topic of consuming forbidden items from moral to practical deviations, emphasizing that abandoning one's post in the front of truth is a major cause of divine wrath. Citing verses from the Holy Quran, the scholar explains that believers are under specific mandates ranging from Islam and faith to monotheism, sincerity, justice, and prayer. He stresses that neglecting these duties equates to abandoning one's position in the front of truth, which inevitably leads to God's anger. The core message of the program is that humans must be aware of their missions in the realms of monotheism and ethics, avoiding shirk and hypocrisy. The importance of praying for the strengthening of faith and monotheism in hearts and actions is also highlighted. The program concludes with greetings for the days of Karamat and the birth of Imam Reza (PBUH), accompanied by repeated salutations to him.
