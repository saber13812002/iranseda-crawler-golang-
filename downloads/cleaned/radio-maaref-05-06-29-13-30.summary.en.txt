This radio broadcast began by commemorating the birth of Imam Hasan Askari (PBUH) and mentioning the holy shrine of Hazrat Abdol-Azim Hasani (PBUH). The main segment featured a legal case where a trading company manager sued an employee for negligence in customs clearance, which resulted in the spoilage of perishable goods worth two billion tomans. The plaintiff alleged that the employee deliberately delayed the process by wasting time at a cafe instead of arriving at the customs office on time. The defendant claimed the delay was due to exhaustion and forgetting the power of attorney, but the judge ruled the negligence was intentional based on the evidence. Citing legal statutes and the jurisprudential principle of "no harm," the judge held the employee liable for compensating the damages. The program concluded by highlighting Imam Hasan Askari's (PBUH) exemplary life and virtues as a role model for the youth.
