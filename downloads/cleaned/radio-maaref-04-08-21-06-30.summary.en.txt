The program begins by interpreting Quranic verses that emphasize God's absolute knowledge of the finest details of existence, such as a grain in the darkness of the earth.
The commentator cites narrations to explain the difference between divine "descent" and material transfer, clarifying that God's bestowal of knowledge or grace is a manifestation rather than a separation.
This means that when God grants knowledge, it does not leave His eternal storehouse, unlike material objects which are transferred from one owner to another.
The discussion divides the verse into three main chapters: first, that only God knows the unseen; second, that God is all-knowing; and third, that everything is recorded in a clear book.
Questions are raised about whether this clear book refers to the Perfect Man or the Preserved Tablet, noting that this requires deeper theological discussion.
The latter part of the program includes the prayer "Ya Hayy ya Qayyum" and sending blessings upon Muhammad and his family.
The broadcast concludes with descriptions of morning scenes and an invitation to spirituality in various Iranian cities.
The discourse emphasizes the unity of existence and God's eternal presence, denying any change in the Divine Essence.
