The radio program "Zamzam Ahkam" began by addressing various religious inquiries from its listeners. The program cited Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi's ruling that prayer remains valid for individuals suffering from urinary incontinence. Ayatollah Sadrani clarified that minor bleeding from a finger for blood sugar testing does not invalidate wudu. According to Ayatollah Shabiri Zanjani, facial makeup does not invalidate prayer, provided there is no barrier between the forehead and the prostration surface. Ayatollah Javadi Amoli stated that reciting the Zuhr and Asr prayers aloud on Fridays is recommended and permissible, unlike other daily prayers. The program also confirmed that network marketing is permissible if it involves real products, is not a pyramid scheme, and complies with the law. Ayatollah Khamenei's ruling was shared, stating that tubal ligation is permissible if it does not cause permanent sterility and with the husband's consent. The show then featured a historical account of the martyrdom of Imamzadeh Soltan Ali in Ardhal, highlighting the efforts of Umayyad and Abbasid rulers to suppress the light of the Ahl al-Bayt. The program concluded with religious music and a reminder to perform prayers on time.
