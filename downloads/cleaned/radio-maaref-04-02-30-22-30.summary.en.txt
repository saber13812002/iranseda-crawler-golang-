This program emphasizes the concept of the "Divine Sunnah" and the unchangeable nature of God's laws, citing verses from the Quran. The speaker highlights the figure of Ayatollah Raisi to illustrate the characteristics of the "station of witnessing," which includes detachment from worldly attachments, positions, and material ties. It is argued that martyrs, by shedding worldly burdens and serving creation sincerely, reach a state where they remain witnesses to the truth even after death. The text distinguishes between being a "martyr" during one's lifetime and becoming a "witness" after death, a stage from which there is no return. The speaker portrays Ayatollah Raisi as a model of humble leadership who prioritized the needs of workers and followed through on promises. Furthermore, Muslims are advised to attain this station of witnessing before death through the pilgrimage to Imams and adherence to religious traditions. This spiritual preparation is presented as essential for salvation on the Day of Judgment.
