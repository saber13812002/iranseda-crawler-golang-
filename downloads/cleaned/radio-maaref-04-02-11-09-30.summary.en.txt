Adolescents often turn to smoking and e-cigarettes as an escape from family tensions and due to the influence of media role models.
Early initiation of smoking increases the risk of progressing to other drugs, while physical and psychological nicotine dependence makes quitting difficult.
The depiction of smoking in television series and hidden advertising are identified as significant factors promoting this habit among youth.
A campaign supported by the Ministry of Health in Zahedan encourages stores to voluntarily stop selling tobacco products.
Experts emphasize the necessity of consulting specialized psychologists for addiction recovery and the importance of parents modeling positive behavior.
Providing accurate information without inducing negative curiosity or excitement is key to preventing tobacco use among teenagers.
Parents must continuously update their educational knowledge to effectively manage the impact of social media and digital spaces.
Effective child-rearing requires attention to individual differences and the application of scientific, age-appropriate methods.
