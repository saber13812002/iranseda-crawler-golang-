This radio program explores the philosophy behind the hardships of worship and Hajj, citing Imam Ali's words and Quranic verses.
Imam Ali stated that if the Kaaba were located in a pleasant climate, the divine test would be nullified and the merit of deeds would decrease.
According to narrations, the more difficult the worship, the greater its reward and virtue in the eyes of God.
By placing the Kaaba in a challenging region, God removes arrogance from hearts and opens the doors of divine favor for His servants.
Verses from Surah Al-Baqarah and Ibrahim indicate that the Station of Ibrahim is a symbol of sincerity and the reward for striving for God.
Hajj is meaningless without hardship, and the true grandeur of this worship lies in its physical struggles and efforts.
Pilgrims are advised to perform Hajj with lawful wealth and without encroaching on the rights of others to ensure their worship is accepted.
Imam Musa ibn Ja'fer (AS) also emphasized that shrouds, dowries, and Hajj expenses must be sourced from one's purest personal assets.
