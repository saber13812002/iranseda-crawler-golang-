The program begins with a Quranic interpretation discussing how human limbs will testify against their owners on Judgment Day if they were unjust or greedy in life. It explains that even if a sinner is resurrected as an animal, their essence will reveal their sins, contrasting the tightness of the grave for the disbeliever with the peace of death for the believer. The show then transitions to morning greetings and prayers for the Prophet and Imam Zaman. Following this, the host invites listeners to share their personal symbols and memories associated with the autumn season. A historical segment reviews key events such as Imam Khomeini's migration, Operation Zafar 2, the discovery of penicillin, and the birth of Louis Lumiere. The broadcast concludes by reading listener messages that connect the beauty of autumn to personal life experiences and nature.
