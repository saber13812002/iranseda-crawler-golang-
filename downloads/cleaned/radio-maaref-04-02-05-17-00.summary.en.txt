The radio program "Beyond the Eyes" began with prayers for the resistance front's victory and the liberation of Gaza's people. In the successful blind individual segment, Sadegh Rahimi, a member of the national blind football team, shared his journey of perseverance in sports and professional life. The program's expert emphasized the importance of gratitude and its role in spiritual growth and the increase of divine blessings, citing Quranic verses. A short story from Imam Sadiq (AS) was broadcast, illustrating that continuous gratitude to God is the key to receiving further grace and support. Listeners engaged with the program via SMS and phone calls, requesting free services for the blind community. A listener inquired about how the host reads messages, which was answered by explaining the use of screen reader software. The program concluded with thanks to the audience and an invitation to send more messages in upcoming weeks.
