This radio broadcast covered the annual mourning ceremonies for Lady Fatima Masumeh (SA) in Qom and Mashhad, attended by numerous pilgrims and shrine servants.
The Speaker of the Iranian Parliament emphasized the role of the Shia school and seminaries in training the martyrs Hassan Nasrallah and Hashem Safieddine, highlighting the resistance's victory over the Zionist regime.
News segments reported on the first National Congress of "Hayat Tayyibah" for Islamic scholars in Qom and the start of the judging process for the National Youth and Population Award, which received approximately 2,000 entries.
A group of lawyers condemned France's detention of Mehdi Esfandiyari as illegal and launched a campaign to secure his release through international legal channels.
The "Zamzam Ahkam" segment provided religious rulings on various issues, including the permissibility of buying a house with a housing loan and the conditions for a seated person to lead congregational prayer.
Further questions addressed the purity status of newborns in hospitals, the obligation of Salat al-Ayat during severe thunderstorms, and the proper method for cleaning contaminated pillows.
Finally, the ruling on Khums for working women whose annual income is entirely spent on medical expenses was clarified, stating they are exempt from payment if no surplus remains at the end of the year.
