In this radio program, Professor Yousef Estadi addresses various family and educational questions from listeners. A key inquiry concerns a husband whose wife has been diagnosed with clinical depression by a professional. The expert emphasizes that depression is a genuine psychological disorder distinct from ordinary sadness or stress. He advises the husband to actively support his wife in taking her medication and attending therapy sessions consistently. Additionally, he should coordinate with the therapist to implement recommended techniques, such as behavioral activation, at home. It is crucial for the caregiver to monitor his own mental health to prevent burnout and the spread of depressive symptoms. Finally, recognizing danger signs like suicidal ideation and immediately informing the therapist is highlighted as a critical safety measure.
