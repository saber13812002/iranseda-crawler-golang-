In the radio program "Parsaman Enghelab," Dr. Abutalevi Moayyed emphasized the importance of maintaining national unity following the twelve-day war. He argued that a shared cultural identity rooted in the school of Imam Hussein and the pursuit of justice serves as an inseparable bond within the Iranian nation. This common spirit transcends religious and ideological boundaries, acting as a primary motivator for resistance even among non-Muslim minorities and Marxist activists. Moayyed described the direct US attack on Iran as the first significant defeat of the unipolar system since World War II. He characterized the resulting ceasefire not as a sign of fear, but as a victory and a "good news of conquest" that solidified Iran's global authority. The second segment of the program addressed the issue of academic demotivation among the youth, citing alarming statistics regarding the high percentage of young people who are neither studying nor working. Experts identified negative influences from social media, parenting styles, and educational system flaws as key causes of this lack of motivation. Finally, the discussion highlighted the necessity of returning to educational values and avoiding doctrinal deviations caused by inappropriate media content, drawing on religious narratives and the wisdom of Saadi.
