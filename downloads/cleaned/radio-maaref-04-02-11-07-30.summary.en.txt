The program began by honoring teachers on Labor Day, emphasizing their crucial role in shaping human character and knowledge.
Hojjat al-Islam Fallah then discussed "deviation in practical monotheism" as a primary cause of divine anger.
Citing verse 61 of Surah Al-Baqarah, he explained that the Children of Israel incurred God's wrath by violating divine limits and assassinating prophets.
The speaker clarified that theoretical belief in God's oneness is only validated through practical adherence to Sharia, prayer, and justice.
He argued that a person's life choices essentially constitute their prayer, meaning our actions are our requests from God.
Consequently, any deviation from divine commands is interpreted as a self-request for punishment rather than a blessing.
The segment concluded with an urging to maintain true monotheism by strictly following religious laws and avoiding disobedience.
