This radio program first explains the concept of "Islam covers what came before," indicating that embracing Islam purifies past sins and shirk, effectively resetting a person's spiritual status, though personal financial debts must still be repaid. The discussion then addresses the frequent question of why Surah At-Tawbah (Al-Bara'ah) lacks the Basmala at its beginning. Scholars argue that this is due to the surah's severe tone of declaring war against polytheists, which contrasts with the attribute of mercy, or because it is considered a continuation of the previous surah, Al-Anfal. The final segment highlights the Quran's unique scientific and mystical insights, urging the audience to actively disseminate Islamic knowledge to the public. The program concludes with congratulations for the birth anniversaries of Prophet Muhammad and Imam Jafar Sadiq, followed by the recitation of the Dua for the reappearance of the Mahdi.
