The program began by honoring the martyrdom of Imam Jawad (AS) before delving into Quranic interpretations of the soul and the record of deeds.
A key point is that on Judgment Day, humans recall their actions not from angels' writings, but by witnessing the acts themselves within their own souls.
Repentance and divine grace can either erase sins or transform them into good deeds, ensuring that believers remain free from shame and sorrow in Paradise.
The fundamental difference between humans and God is that God can remove inner pains and memories from the heart, a capability humans do not possess.
Self-knowledge is the most beneficial path to knowing God because, unlike external knowledge, it is accompanied by practical spiritual purification.
God's essence is a reality that does not fit within the mental space and cannot be fully comprehended through external concepts or logical arguments.
Knowing God is a necessary, innate reality, meaning every human being inherently recognizes the Divine Truth even if they claim ignorance.
