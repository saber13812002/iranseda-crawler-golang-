The program begins by interpreting a Quranic verse that predicts the emergence of a group beloved by God, who are humble toward believers and mighty against disbelievers.
Citing a hadith from the Prophet Muhammad, the speaker identifies this group with Salman al-Farsi and his companions, suggesting that these virtues are also applicable to Iranians and Muslims today.
The central theme of the discussion is "humility" (tawadu), which is described as a psychological and spiritual trait that must manifest in a person's outward behavior.
According to Imam Sadiq in the book Misbah al-Shari'ah, humility is the source of all good deeds and a prerequisite for the acceptance of worship, making the individual known to the inhabitants of the heavens.
In contrast to arrogance and self-praise, true humility is defined by modesty before believers and avoiding the desire for praise.
The Prophet is also commanded in the Quran to be humble toward believers, acting like a mother hen who lowers her wings in tenderness and submission to her chicks.
Finally, the host encourages listeners to read the Quran and adopt divine ethics, emphasizing that acquiring wisdom and emulating the Prophet's character are essential for achieving spiritual success.
