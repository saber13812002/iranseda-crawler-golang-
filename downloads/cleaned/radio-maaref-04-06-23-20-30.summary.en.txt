The program begins by narrating the transformation of Mirza Jahangir Khan Qashqai, who abandoned his life as a tar player at the age of forty to become a student in a seminary, eventually rising to the status of a Marja. He maintained his tribal identity by wearing a felt cap and refused to accept the Imam's share, ultimately being buried in the Takht-e Foulad cemetery. The second part of the broadcast features the captivity memoirs of Mr. Nouri during the Fath al-Mubin operation, detailing his physical and spiritual suffering in a pit and his eventual rescue with the help of fellow captives. These narratives highlight a deep commitment to faith and homeland, illustrating the journey through hardship to achieve spiritual perfection.
