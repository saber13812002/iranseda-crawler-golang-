This audio transcript is a religious and educational speech focusing on anger management and proper child-rearing techniques. The speaker cites anecdotes from Gohar Chadeh Mosque to argue that many behavioral issues in children stem from parental stress and anger rather than the child's nature. He warns that verbal and physical violence stifles a child's potential and will be considered a sin on the Day of Judgment. Referring to Hafez's poetry, the speaker urges parents to act as gentle gardeners and strictly avoid using profanity or harsh language at home. He believes that irritability and bad temper drive divine mercy away from the household and render the family's prayers ineffective. Finally, the speaker advises listeners to seek healing for their anger by relying on Imam Reza (AS) and reciting specific supplications. He recommends approaching the Imam with a sincere and simple heart for inner healing rather than undertaking long pilgrimages.
