In this radio segment, as the clock approaches three o'clock, the discussion centers on the awakening and the uprising of Imam Hussein (PBUH).
The primary focus is on countering the rumors and conspiracies orchestrated by enemies against the awakening movement.
The adversaries have explicitly stated their opposition to the awakening of the Iranian nation.
They aim to dismantle this movement by spreading misinformation and creating chaos.
Therefore, public awareness and vigilance are essential to identify and neutralize these plots.
The program emphasizes the importance of preserving the eternal glory and ideals of the Ashura uprising.
It concludes with the repeated slogan of "House of Awakening" and an expression of hope for a bright future.
