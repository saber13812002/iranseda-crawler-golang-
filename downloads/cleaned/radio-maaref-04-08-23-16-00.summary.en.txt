In this radio program, Professor Jafari initially dismissed the book "Aisha After the Prophet" by Kurt Frischler and its translator as a novel lacking any historical validity. He also clarified that the legend of Zahhak is a literary allegory based on the Damavand volcano rather than a historical fact. Responding to a listener's query, the professor confirmed the authenticity of the story regarding three thirsty wounded soldiers in the Battle of Yarmouk, interpreting it as a symbol of moral training in early Islam. He then detailed the Prophet's life before his prophethood, covering events from his birth to the reconstruction of the Kaaba and his marriage to Khadija. The professor further refuted the claim that Harith ibn Ziyad was the brother of the companion Kamal ibn Ziyad, noting that the killer of Muslim's children remains unidentified in reliable sources. Finally, he explained that the Imams' participation in Friday prayers led by Caliphs was not merely a precautionary measure but a strategic effort to maintain social cohesion and community relations. He noted that the Imams attended these prayers while maintaining their individual intention and performing their own recitation.
