In this radio program, counselors emphasized the importance of fostering positive behaviors and strengthening emotional bonds with spouses through communication and awareness. A key point discussed was managing expenses during the engagement period, which requires balance and dialogue between both families to help spouses gradually learn responsibility. It was also stressed that staying in a marriage solely for the sake of children is not a sufficient reason to endure cold and affectionless relationships; instead, couples should seek root-cause solutions through professional counseling. Advisors recommended that couples use the presence of their children as a source of hope to drive positive changes rather than focusing on failures. In the final segment, the recitation of Ziyarat Al-Yasin and a quote from Imam Khomeini highlighted the necessity of Muslim awakening and avoiding division during sensitive times. The ultimate goal of the program is to provide practical solutions for keeping the family hearth warm and resolving marital challenges.
