The radio program begins with an interpretation of Nahj al-Balagha Sermon 149, where Imam Ali (PBUH) describes his impending death as the ultimate lesson for his companions. The Imam emphasizes that witnessing the silence and lifelessness of his body after his martyrdom will be more impactful than any speech, revealing his true status to those who will lose him. The second part of the show features a lecture by Hojjat al-Islam Fala Bastant on the virtue of patience. He defines patience in three contexts: obedience, sincerity, and affliction, highlighting its importance during the worship of the Nights of Power. The speaker asserts that Imam Ali's patience transcends these standard categories, existing at a higher spiritual level. The program concludes with prayers for the Imam's followers and an introduction to the commemorative ceremonies held at the shrine of Hazrat Fatima Masumeh (SA).
