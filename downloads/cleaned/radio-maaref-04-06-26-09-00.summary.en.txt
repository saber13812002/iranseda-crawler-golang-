The radio program begins with a theological discussion on the nature of the world as a place of trial and the distinction between the infallible knowledge of Imams and general occult knowledge.
It is emphasized that this divine knowledge does not alter religious obligations, as Imams must still act based on apparent evidence and legal proofs.
Listeners' questions are addressed, including the idea that illness may serve a divine purpose and that believers can visit the Imam of the Age in the intermediate world.
Fiqh rulings clarify that minor sins are forgiven by good deeds, while major sins require sincere repentance and a firm intention not to repeat them.
The program also highlights the urgent humanitarian crisis in Gaza, urging listeners to support the oppressed people through donations.
The final segment features a historical account of the Iranian air operation against Baghdad during the Iran-Iraq War.
Iranian pilots flew at extremely low altitudes to demonstrate the vulnerability of Baghdad's airspace and disrupt a planned summit of non-aligned countries.
This strategic mission, led by pilots like Abbas Doran, is presented as a significant victory that showcased Iran's air power and deterrence capabilities.
