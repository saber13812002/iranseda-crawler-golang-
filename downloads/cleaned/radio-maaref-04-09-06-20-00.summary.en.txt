Over 66 million pilgrims visited the Holy Shrines last month, marking a 12 million increase compared to the previous year. The Student Affairs Organization announced the launch of Umrah missions for university students and faculty, supported by 40 million Toman bank facilities. The Supreme Leader is scheduled to address the nation at 8:30 PM regarding current national and global issues. Other news includes the restoration of the historic Al-Muqarram Mosque in Jeddah and the arrest of Sheikh Abdullah al-Nimr in Saudi Arabia. The International Mirza Naeini Conference was held in Najaf, featuring the unveiling of a 40-volume encyclopedia of the scholar. The Arab League declared International Day of Solidarity with Palestine, while Egypt emphasized the need to halt Israeli aggression against Lebanon. Finally, the ongoing ceasefire violations in Gaza were reported, with airstrikes targeting Khan Younis and Rafah.
