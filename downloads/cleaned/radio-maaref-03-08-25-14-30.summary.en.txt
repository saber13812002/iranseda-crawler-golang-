In this radio segment, after introducing the daily schedule and calling for support for Lebanon and Gaza, the program addressed questions regarding the Mahdi.
The main discussion focused on the possibility of Imam Mahdi falling ill and how he would be treated.
The expert emphasized that the Imam, like other humans, has a physical body and is therefore susceptible to illness.
However, citing religious narrations, the use of medicine and adherence to hygiene, alongside prayer, are the natural and approved methods for treatment.
The key point is that God, in His infinite power, can prevent severe illness or death for the Imam.
Thus, while the possibility of illness exists, the preservation of his health and longevity holds a special place in divine will.
The program concluded by inviting listeners to submit new questions and playing music.
