The first segment of the program distinguished between pathological obsessions and constructive thinking aimed at removing ignorance, emphasizing that religious principles are investigative in nature.
It was clarified that accepting beliefs based on evaluating the research of others is not blind imitation, as it involves a preliminary understanding of the researcher's credibility.
The discussion then addressed verse 101 of Surah Tawbah, where commentators identified three stages of punishment for hypocrites: public humiliation in this world, internal torment or punishment in the Barzakh, and severe punishment in the Hereafter.
The second segment provided a live report from the Hajj, noting that this year's pilgrimage involved 1.8 million people from 181 countries with improved organization and safety measures compared to previous years.
The remaining key rituals include the stoning of the pillars in the coming days, after which pilgrims are expected to begin their return journey from the 20th of the month.
The correspondent shared moving anecdotes from Medina, including visits to the Prophet's birthplace and the grave of Imam Reza's mother, while highlighting the spiritual dangers of pride and self-importance during the pilgrimage.
Finally, pilgrims were encouraged to maintain humility to avoid Satan's traps and to offer prayers for the well-being of their compatriots and listeners in these sacred sites.
