The program began by honoring the memory of the late President Raisi before delving into the exegesis of verse 105 of Surah Al-Ma'idah based on Ayatollah Javadi Amoli's views. The interpretation emphasized that night prayer is not driven by fear of hell but rather by a spiritual longing for the Truth and inner movement. It was explained that in spiritual progression, the traveler, the path, the vehicle, and time are all intrinsic to the human soul, meaning true life is measured by spiritual motion rather than earthly time. The discussion distinguished between theoretical knowledge and actualized knowledge, highlighting the importance of practical realization. The core of the talk focused on the "Greater Jihad," where reason must act as a just leader to manage the soul's faculties, preventing them from being enslaved by lust or anger. Ultimately, recognizing oneself as the path is a prerequisite for knowing God as the goal, as forgetting the destination inevitably leads to losing one's way.
