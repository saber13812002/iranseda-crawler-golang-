This program critically examines the view of Sheikh Muhammad Abduh and Rashid Rida that the Verse of Tabligh was revealed at the beginning of the Prophet's mission.
The primary critique is that the supporting narration conflicts with multiple other reports, including those related to the event of Ghadir, thereby losing its credibility.
Allameh Tabatabaei argues that interpreting the verse as a threat due to the Prophet's fear for his life is incompatible with the dignity of prophethood and the courage of true believers.
The Quran demonstrates that believers, when faced with enemy threats, did not fear but rather had their faith strengthened.
Linguistically, if the pronoun in "his message" refers to the new mission, the sentence becomes tautological; if it refers to previous missions, it contradicts the claim of early revelation.
Therefore, the preference lies with narrations that attribute the revelation of this verse to the announcement of Imamate at the event of Ghadir.
This analysis indicates that interpretations based on fear of enemies are unacceptable both from a narrational and a logical, Quranic perspective.
