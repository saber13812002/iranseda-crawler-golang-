In this radio program, the greatness of God and the role of the Perfect Human in the universe were explained based on Quranic verses.
The main discussion focused on practical advice for Karbala pilgrims to align their spirits with the martyrs, shedding pure tears that extinguish the fire of hell and forgive sins.
The guest emphasized maintaining the Ashura atmosphere throughout the pilgrimage, aiming to become a true "Karbali" as the ultimate goal of the journey.
For those unable to travel physically, reciting specific salutations after morning and evening prayers was recommended as a spiritual compensation.
The virtues of the Arbaeen pilgrimage were then discussed, citing narrations from Allameh Majlisi and Imam Sadiq (AS).
It was highlighted that praying for the pilgrims of Imam Hussein (AS) is a place of answered prayers and holds a special status with God.
Finally, the program's SMS and phone numbers were announced, inviting listeners to send "Ya Hussein" or call to express their devotion.
