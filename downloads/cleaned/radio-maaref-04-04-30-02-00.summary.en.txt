The Parsaiyan program emphasizes the importance of maintaining self-dignity and avoiding financial dependence on others, citing Islamic narrations.
Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi uses hadiths from the Prophet and Imams to illustrate that true believers never humiliate themselves, even in dire circumstances.
Real-life stories of Ayatollah Tabrizi and Shahid Dastgheib serve as models of self-reliance and preserving dignity in the face of material needs.
The second part of the program features the testimony of Zainab Hosseini, the wife of an Afghan martyr who defended the shrines.
Zainab recounts her final communications with her husband, Seyed Ahmad, and his promise to contact her after an operation in the Lazkiye hills.
As time passed without any contact, Zainab experienced intense anxiety and searched hospitals for news of her husband.
This segment highlights the spiritual hardships and patience of martyrs' wives during the agonizing period of waiting and grief.
