Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verses 6 through 9 of Surah Al-An'am, highlighting the significance of the seventy thousand angels that greeted the surah as a symbol of its unique status. He analyzes the Quranic principle that nations are destroyed due to their oppression, categorizing this injustice into two types: exploitation based on wealth and tyranny based on brute force. The lecture then addresses the common skepticism of why the Prophet was not an angel and why angels did not appear alongside him to provide proof. The Quranic response is that a messenger must be of the same species as the recipients to serve as a complete argument and share their culture and language. It is further explained that while seeing God is impossible, seeing angels is only possible after death or in specific spiritual states. Finally, the text notes that even if all the demands of the skeptics were met, their faith would not be restored because their hearts have been darkened by their own sins.
