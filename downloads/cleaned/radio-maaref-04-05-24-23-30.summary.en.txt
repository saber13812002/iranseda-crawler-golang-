This program discusses the theological concept of "divine will" in the Verse of Purification and its specific application to the Ahl al-Bayt. The central argument posits that the will mentioned in the verse is not a general legislative will, but rather a cosmic will that necessitates the actual removal of sin from the Prophet's family. Evidence such as the story of Umm Salama, who was not included in this specific honor, demonstrates that the verse highlights a unique virtue and distinction for the Ahl al-Bayt. Furthermore, referencing narrations, it is explained that the Prophet requested God to remove impurity from his household, and God accepted this prayer by revealing the verse and effecting their purification. Consequently, the will in the verse is one whose outcome has been realized, confirming the high status of the Ahl al-Bayt. The discussion also highlights the scholarly contributions of Sayyid Murtada and Sheikh Tusi in clarifying these issues, noting that their works remain authoritative and uncensored sources for understanding Islamic jurisprudence. Finally, the program concludes with specific supplications for the Ahl al-Bayt, emphasizing the importance of divine motivation in human actions and revolutions over purely material or worldly ambitions.
