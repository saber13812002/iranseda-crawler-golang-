The program clarified the distinction between "creation" (khalq), which involves time, and the "command" (kalam), which is timeless, noting that God accounts for time in material creation despite His infinite power. It was explained that only God is both essentially and temporally eternal, whereas all other entities, including abstract ones, are essentially created. Responding to listener questions, the host emphasized that having children does not inherently cause sorrow; rather, it is human attachment and anxiety that lead to distress. The concept of repentance was also discussed, highlighting that the opportunity to repent is a divine grace, and major sins may hinder its acceptance. In the section on moral education, the path to preserving modesty in a corrupt society was described as requiring theoretical awareness followed by practical training to form a moral habit. Additionally, it was stressed that the Quran itself emphasizes equipping oneself with modern military power to deter enemies.
