The Governor of Karbala announced that the province is fully prepared to host 25 million Arbaeen pilgrims this year, while Iraq's Ministry of Planning launched a new initiative to provide services to visitors along the routes. At the Chazabe border, pilgrims from various countries continue their journey despite extreme heat, with Iran's Hajj and Pilgrimage Organization reporting that 450,000 visitors have already returned home while 1.4 million remain in Iraq. Meanwhile, Human Rights Watch documented the systematic bombing of 50 schools in Gaza using American weapons, and the World Food Programme warned that half of Gaza's residents are on the brink of famine. In London, civil activists criticized the continuous financial and military support provided by European governments to Israel, demanding an immediate halt to arms exports. Israeli security officials discussed the potential full occupation of Gaza, though the Chief of Staff warned that such a move would replicate the Vietnam War disaster for Israel. In Yemen, the Humanitarian Coordination Center sanctioned 64 companies for violating the naval blockade, while in Pakistan, protests by Shia pilgrims over the ban on land travel to Arbaeen were temporarily suspended after officials promised to address the issue urgently.
