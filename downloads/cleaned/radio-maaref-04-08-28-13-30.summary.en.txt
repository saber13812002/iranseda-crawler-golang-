In this radio program, the mother of martyr Arman Ali-Bardi recounts the moral and religious character of her son, a revolutionary youth from the 1980s generation. Arman was admired by his family for his politeness and noble behavior, and his interest in martyrs and Islamic history deepened through his studies at a seminary. He was constantly concerned with serving the people and defending the country, often spending his time helping homeless youth in parks even in his final days. Arman was martyred in 2022 during the protests in Tehran while intervening to protect a woman from a group of attackers. His mother painfully describes the last phone call with her son and her sudden realization of his martyrdom, which became clear when his phone went silent and his father exhibited unusual behavior. This narrative highlights Arman's jihadist spirit, patience, and love for his homeland and people, which transformed him into a symbol of freedom and sacrifice.
