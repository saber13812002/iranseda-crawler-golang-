The radio program begins by highlighting the virtue of enjoining good and forbidding evil, citing the courage of Ayatollah Behjat and Ayatollah Baqaei during their imprisonments. Dr. Kachooian, a sociologist, then analyzes the recent war and the strategic goals of the enemy. He argues that the enemy's primary objective was regime change and national disintegration through social collapse. Kachooian asserts that the enemy operated under the false assumption that the people were alienated from the system and ready to revolt. However, the widespread public support for the system thwarted the enemy's calculations. He emphasizes that some domestic analyses are also influenced by this collapse narrative, effectively moving in the enemy's direction by exaggerating problems. Finally, he believes that the true identity of the Iranian people is loyalty to the system, and any analysis based on public apathy is a fundamental error.
