This radio program argues that in the path of human perfection and closeness to God, apparent differences such as gender and age are irrelevant, as the human spirit transcends these limitations. God assigns duties based on each individual's innate capabilities, and young people bear specific responsibilities due to their intellectual, physical, and transformative potential. On the Day of Judgment, individuals will be specifically questioned about how they spent their youth, whether they devoted it to truth and serving the oppressed or squandered it in sin. Today's youth can achieve a high spiritual status by standing at the right point in history and defending Islamic values, much like the great martyrs. The program also emphasizes that allegiance to the Ahl al-Bayt is the key to salvation and guidance in religion. Therefore, young people must be aware of the value of time and their abilities to play an effective role in the fields of knowledge and action.
