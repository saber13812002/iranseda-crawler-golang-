The radio program began by highlighting the significant role of Sayyid Muhammad Tabatabai and Kashf al-Zuhm in issuing fatwas for jihad during the Iran-Russia wars. It then addressed the jurisprudential distinction between permissible natural sounds and the prohibition of musical instruments used in sinful gatherings. The use of drums and cymbals in mourning ceremonies was clarified as permissible since they are considered tools of war and emotion rather than music. Subsequent segments answered listener questions regarding the purity of peeled lip skin, the invalidation of fasting by therapeutic injections, and the timing for making up missed Ramadan fasts. The program also confirmed that reciting the Quran for multiple individuals is valid and that washing body parts separately is not required for ghusl. Finally, it discussed the necessity of a husband's permission for women's social activities and the ruling on listening to prostration verses for women with valid excuses.
