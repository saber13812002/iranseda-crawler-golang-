The program began with a lecture by Hojjat-ol-Islam Madani Bajestani on the need to change attitudes towards marriage and support it. In the "Message from the Shrine" segment, the host discussed spiritual programs for pilgrims with the official of the Shah Cheragh shrine. The legal section addressed a labor dispute where a worker claimed unpaid wages from his employer. The judge emphasized that under Article 11 of the Labor Law, employers must pay all dues if they terminate the contract, even during the probationary period. The importance of work in Islam and the Quran was also highlighted. The final segment detailed the history of US hostility towards Iran, including the 1953 coup and the establishment of SAVAK. The Shah's regime's support for the Zionist regime with US aid was strongly criticized. The show concluded by inviting listeners to share their feedback via the hotline number 162.
