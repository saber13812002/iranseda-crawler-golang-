The radio program begins by emphasizing Imam Ali's dictum that "human reason is religion," arguing that a person's intellectual maturity is measured by the school of thought they choose.
A comparative analysis was presented, criticizing the Torah and Gospel for their anthropomorphic or contradictory depictions of God, such as the Trinity.
In contrast, Islam was highlighted as the only school that presents a singular, merciful God, making it the sole choice acceptable to true reason.
The second part of the broadcast delved into Mulla Sadra's philosophical treatise, specifically distinguishing between "conceptual inclusion" in a definition and "subordination to a category."
The instructor clarified that merely including a concept in a definition does not imply subordination to that category unless the category's specific effects are applicable to the subject.
It was explained that abstract universal concepts cannot be subordinated to categories because they lack external existence, whereas specific natures can be, given their real existence.
The session concluded with an emphasis on gratitude for the divine gift of reason and wisdom, along with prayers for the appearance of the Mahdi and the triumph of Islam.
