In the radio program "Until Friday of Appearance," researcher Ali Sadoughi discussed a hadith from Imam Zaman (AJ) regarding divine trials and tribulations. He emphasized that hardships are an immutable divine law designed to expand human spiritual capacity and reveal hidden talents. Citing a verse from Surah Al-Baqarah, Sadoughi explained that God uses afflictions to identify the truly patient and those with broad hearts. He noted that managing these trials requires strong religious foundations and resistance against the pressures of Satan and the ego. The discussion also highlighted that excessive attachment to the world and children is the root of many sins, and that trials help liberate humans from such dependencies. In another segment, Hojjat al-Islam Kafil discussed the concept of "Yum al-Husrah" (the Day of Regret) and the necessity of connecting to Imam Zaman to avoid regret on Judgment Day. The program concluded with listener messages and an invitation to participate in the "Mahdi" campaign.
