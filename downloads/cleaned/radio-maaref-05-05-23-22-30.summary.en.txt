This radio program highlights Imam Hassan Askari's (AS) emphasis on the necessity of filling the earth with justice after the Mahdi's (AJ) appearance, contrasting it with the current systematic injustice. The discussion cites authentic narrations and direct testimonies from forty-six witnesses to affirm the existence and authority of Imam Zaman (AJ). A significant portion focuses on the security measures taken by Imam Hassan (AS) during the severe Abbasid repression to protect the Mahdi's birth and early life. Through historical wisdom, the Imam simultaneously proved the Mahdi's authority to devoted Shias while convincing the rulers and spies that he had no son. These actions not only saved the savior of humanity but also prepared the Shia community mentally for the era of occultation and their reliance on scholars. Finally, the program reads from the book "Let Us Help Him," emphasizing the spiritual merit of praying for the Mahdi's early return during specific times like prayer and the Karbala pilgrimage.
