In this radio program, Dr. Rafei discusses the unique characteristics of Imam Ali al-Ridha's era on the occasion of his birth anniversary. He highlights that the Imam had the shortest period of leadership, spent in Samarra, and was the last visible Imam before the era of occultation began. His key actions included protecting the Imam of the Age, establishing a network of deputies to maintain communication with the people, and sending numerous letters to guide the Shia community. Imam al-Ridha defined the true identity of Shias by emphasizing moral traits such as altruism and avoiding sin. He also stressed the importance of unity with other Islamic sects and respecting their rights. Finally, the discussion concluded with the necessity of referring religious and financial matters to a qualified jurist as the Imam's representative during the occultation.
