This program analyzes verses 140 to 142 of Surah An-Nisa, focusing on the Quranic concept of "khawz" (initiating an action) which, in a negative context, refers to mocking divine verses. Based on Hadith and Nahj al-Balagha, associating with sinners or approving their actions is described as a cause for forgetting faith and allowing Satan to dominate the soul. The discussion then shifts to the characteristics of hypocrites who opportunistically wait for the Islamic system to fail and engage in espionage during conflicts. It is highlighted that hypocrites claim allegiance to Muslims when they win but align with disbelievers when they lose, seeking benefit from both sides. The theological distinction between committing a sin and merely approving it is also examined, noting that approval darkens the soul even if it lacks specific legal rulings. Finally, the program affirms the Quranic principle that truth and Islam always prevail over falsehood and disbelief. Apparent military defeats are interpreted not as the failure of the religion, but as divine tests to verify the sincerity of believers' intentions before granting ultimate victory.
