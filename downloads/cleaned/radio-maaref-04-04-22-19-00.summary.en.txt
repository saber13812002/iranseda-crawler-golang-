The radio program began by emphasizing the importance of maintaining independence and avoiding reliance on oppressors. It then addressed various jurisprudential questions, including the rules for paying the Sadat share without permission, the impact of water on wudu, and the ownership of dowry based on parental intent. The validity of prostration on clothing or rings was also discussed, along with the ruling that artificial nails are forbidden as they obstruct ritual purity. Additionally, it was clarified that reciting the Quran is permissible even without full mastery of Tajweed rules. In the second segment, a cleric provided guidelines for religious groups to enhance their activities in mosques. These recommendations included adhering to Sharia standards, ensuring active participation in congregational prayers, and avoiding unhealthy competition or political deviations. The program concluded with the recitation of prayers and the playing of musical pieces.
