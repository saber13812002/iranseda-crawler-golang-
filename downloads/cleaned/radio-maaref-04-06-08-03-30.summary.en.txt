This radio program provided jurisprudential answers to listener questions based on the rulings of Islamic scholars. According to Imam Khomeini, a woman's physical presence is not required for divorce, as it is valid if her agent pronounces it in the presence of two just witnesses. Regarding ablution, any substance like dirt or long nails that prevents water from reaching the skin invalidates the ritual and must be removed. Chewing gum does not break the fast unless its moisture is swallowed or the gum is removed and reinserted. Prayers missed during travel or residence must be performed in the same format they were originally obligatory, regardless of the current location. Selling a post-dated check at a discount to its owner is permissible, though some scholars allow selling it to third parties as well. The program concluded with a discussion on the concept of Ihsan, emphasizing spiritual mindfulness and the presence of God in worship. A recitation of Quranic verses from Surahs Zukhruf and Dukhan was also broadcast.
