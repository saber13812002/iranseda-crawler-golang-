This program examines the scientific and argumentative methodology of Allameh Tabatabaei in addressing modern intellectual challenges. By meticulously dissecting the claims of Marxist and materialist currents, he exposed their internal contradictions. A key critique of his was directed at materialist epistemology, demonstrating the contradiction between claiming that knowledge is entirely material and asserting realism. He argued that if knowledge is merely a combination of external stimuli and internal brain structures, it can never align with external reality. This argument applies not only to Marxism but also to Kantian epistemology. In the fields of anthropology and biology, he distinguished between empirical and philosophical propositions to highlight the limitations of Darwinism in explaining the origin and end of existence. He emphasized that explaining phenomena through natural causes does not necessarily negate supernatural or theological explanations. Ultimately, his method provided the intellectual foundation for many contemporary Islamic thinkers to offer rational and balanced responses to modern questions.
