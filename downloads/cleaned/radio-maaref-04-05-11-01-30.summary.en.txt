The program begins by emphasizing the concept of "basirat" (insight) as the ability to distinguish truth from falsehood and understand political and social conditions.
Piety is introduced as the primary factor for acquiring insight, with a strong emphasis on standing firmly on the side of truth and avoiding indifference during times of fitna.
Referring to Imam Ali's sermons, it is stated that only those with insight and patience can uphold the banner of Wilayah in critical situations.
The painful history of Imam Hasan and the martyrdom of Imam Hussein are cited as examples of the suffering caused by the lack of insight among companions and enemies.
The text then transitions to a literary narrative of Imam Hussein's encounter with Har ibn Yazid's army on the way to Kufa.
Imam Hussein reveals his intention to avoid war and follow the people's invitation by leading a joint congregational prayer.
Har ibn Yazid, upon seeing the people's letters and understanding the situation, ultimately refrains from killing the Imam and joins him.
The final conclusion of the discussion is the necessity of awakening, piety, and insight to escape Satan's traps and preserve religious identity against tyranny.
