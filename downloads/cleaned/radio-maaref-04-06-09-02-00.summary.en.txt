The program began by announcing the Supreme Council of the Quran's call for academic papers and emphasizing the role of Radio Ma'aref in public guidance.
The religious segment highlighted the importance of compassion and praying for others as a means of salvation on Judgment Day and social peace.
In the analytical section, Dr. Sadrul Hosseini examined the disarmament of Hezbollah within the context of simultaneous US and Israeli pressures on the region.
He stated that the primary goal of this pressure is to eliminate the resistance's deterrence and facilitate military entry into Lebanon, rather than merely collecting light weapons.
Hosseini noted that the recent reduction in pressure, following Barack's trip to Beirut and Tel Aviv, stems from Washington's recognition of Hezbollah's social power and broad public support.
The analyst clarified that media portrayals of Palestinian camp weapons handovers are symbolic actions intended to mislead public opinion outside of Lebanon.
Finally, citing Sheikh Naim Qassem, he warned that if pressures and civil war continue, Hezbollah is prepared for a decisive confrontation and even a full-scale war against the Zionist regime.
