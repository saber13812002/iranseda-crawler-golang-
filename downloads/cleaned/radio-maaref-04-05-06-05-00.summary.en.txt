The program begins by interpreting Quranic verses that highlight God's power to resurrect the dead.
Using the story of Prophet Abraham and his observation of a ruined city, the concept of the afterlife is explained.
It is noted that even with faith and logic, humans may face inner doubts which are resolved by witnessing divine miracles.
The discussion then shifts to the jurisprudence of inheritance and the classification of heirs in Islamic law.
The first class includes children, the second includes siblings and grandparents, and the third includes uncles and aunts.
The primary criterion for this classification is the number of intermediate links between the deceased and the heir.
In the third class, uncles and aunts are connected through the father, while maternal aunts are connected through the mother.
Finally, the various subdivisions of the grandfather's children and their inheritance rights in different scenarios are outlined.
