The International Mirza Naini Congress commenced in Qom with a message from the Supreme Leader, with its closing ceremony scheduled for Mashhad. Ali Reza Rashidian was introduced as the new head of the Hajj Organization, emphasizing the adoption of smart services and new technologies. The Shia Quran Charter was drafted as a national and seminary document and received approval from the Maraji. The Supreme Audit Court of Iran intervened to monitor the implementation of the Population Youth Law and the allocation of related budgets. At the international conference on reconstruction and development of religious sites, the integration of technical and cultural knowledge in shrine restoration was highlighted. The Deputy for Youth Affairs announced a joint meeting between banks and parliament to remove obstacles to marriage. The Zamzam Ahkam program addressed various jurisprudential questions from followers of different Maraji regarding purity, Khums, and transactions. These segments reflect extensive cultural, religious, and social activities across the country.
