The program begins with a jurisprudential critique of Ayatollah Khurasani's view that real rulings are conditional upon knowledge.
The main argument is that there is no valid textual evidence to support the rational assumption that divine laws depend on human awareness.
The discussion then shifts to the mysticism of the self, emphasizing that self-knowledge is the prerequisite for knowing God and feeling responsible.
Spiritual education is described as a gradual process where sudden leaps are impossible without traversing the necessary stages of time.
Consistency in small acts of worship and perseverance in good deeds are identified as the keys to spiritual evolution.
In the final section, following one's desires and making long-term wishes are highlighted as the greatest obstacles to personal growth.
Controlling inner impulses and avoiding gossip or wasting time are presented as practical solutions for achieving peace and perfection.
