In the radio program "Treasure of Happiness," Hojjat al-Islam Fallah describes anger as an army of Satan's soldiers that enslaves humans and turns them into colonies of the ego. Citing a prophetic hadith, he emphasizes that refraining from anger is essential for maintaining a healthy spiritual relationship with God and avoiding divine wrath. The second segment, titled "Baastab," features Hojjat al-Islam Bagherian analyzing verses from Surah Al-Layl. He explains that charity combined with piety and belief in divine promises leads to the facilitation of tasks and the removal of obstacles from a believer's path. Consequently, combining righteous deeds with faith in afterlife rewards brings divine success into daily life.
