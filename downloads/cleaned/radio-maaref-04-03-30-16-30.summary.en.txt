This radio program begins by examining the event of Mubahala and the verse in Surah Al-Imran, explaining how the Prophet (PBUH) demonstrated the truth of his mission by bringing Fatima, Hasan, Husayn, and Ali (AS) as representatives. Upon witnessing these signs of divine truth, the Christian delegation from Najran withdrew from the debate and agreed to pay the Jizya tax while remaining under the protection of the Islamic government. The second part of the show addresses the question of whether two prophets can exist simultaneously, a concept affirmed by scholars through the Quranic narrative of Abraham and Lot in Surah Hud. The instructor clarified that while a prophet with a new Sharia might be accompanied by other prophets, the latter served to promote the primary law rather than establish a separate one. The discussion highlights the significance of the Ahl al-Bayt as the central symbols of the faith and the gratitude owed to God for this guidance. The program concludes with Quranic supplications and a brief update on regional news, emphasizing the spiritual and historical lessons derived from these prophetic events.
