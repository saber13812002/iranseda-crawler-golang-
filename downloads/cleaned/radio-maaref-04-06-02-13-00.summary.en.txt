This lecture, delivered on the martyrdom day of Imam Reza (PBUH), examines Harun al-Rashid's historical role in spreading the "Waqifiyya" rumor to undermine the eighth Imam's authority. The speaker illustrates the Imam's spiritual power and knowledge of the unseen through the story of Hasan ibn Ali Wahsha, who was convinced of the Imam's divinity by supernatural signs. The concept of the "King of Hearts" is contrasted with worldly rulers to highlight the unique influence of God's saints on human souls. A narrative from Sheikh Ibrahim Ansari demonstrates how a handful of soil from the Imam's shrine transformed a young man's life, leading to a destined marriage. This anecdote underscores the profound spiritual impact of visiting and seeking refuge in Imam Reza (PBUH) during times of hardship. The sermon concludes by citing the Imam's directive to "seek help from us" when facing severe difficulties. Finally, the Imam's ethical advice regarding honesty, trustworthiness, and the practice of silence is presented as practical guidance for a devout life.
