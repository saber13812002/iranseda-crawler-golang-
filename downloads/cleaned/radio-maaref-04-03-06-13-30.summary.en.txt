In this radio program, Professor Faghihi discussed methods for eliminating negative moral traits such as arrogance, pride, and self-admiration. He emphasized that these traits are among the most dangerous vices and require continuous effort and constant vigilance to overcome. Key recommended solutions include greeting everyone with respect and comparing one's own faults to those of others to gain better self-awareness. Additionally, seeking intercession from the Ahl al-Bayt and comparing oneself to religious scholars were presented as effective ways to reduce arrogance and increase humility. In the second part, the host addressed a question about the habit of looking at non-mahram individuals, suggesting that higher spiritual pleasures from gazing at the faces of the pious and sacred sites should replace fleeting worldly desires. Reciting Quranic verses with deep attention was also proposed as a creative method to control the gaze and purify the soul. Finally, the importance of loving the Ahl al-Bayt and the role of poets and eulogists in preserving this emotional and spiritual current in Shia history was highlighted.
