Ali Jamalki, a football veteran and former captain of the Pas club, recounts his memories of playing for this historic rival of Esteghlal. He praises Pas's organized structure and long-term contracts in the past, expressing sadness over the club's dissolution and the fading memory of its legacy. Jamalki, who stopped playing at a young age due to financial issues and a lack of agreement with the club, is now active as a coach in youth categories. He takes pride in discovering and developing current successful players in the top league through his own testing processes in academies. He complains about the lack of media attention to old football veterans and emphasizes the importance of appreciating their contributions. Finally, he expresses his deep love for teaching fundamental football skills to the younger generation, hoping for better supervision to elevate Iran's youth football.
