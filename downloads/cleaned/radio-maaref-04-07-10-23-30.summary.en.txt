This radio segment covered a criminal case involving license plate tampering and fleeing the scene of an accident. The defendant claimed innocence and relied on the testimony of a friend, but the judge found the evidence conclusive. The judge warned that perjury is a crime and that fleeing an accident scene incurs significant financial penalties for the offender. Ultimately, the defendant was sentenced to six months of suspended imprisonment and ordered to pay all damages and blood money to the victim. In an educational section, the judge emphasized the importance of maintaining decorum in court and avoiding flight from accident scenes. He also explained that covering or altering license plates is a crime punishable by six months to one year of imprisonment. Finally, listeners were advised to immediately contact police 110 if they notice fraudulent traffic fines.
