The radio program began with a theological discussion clarifying that references to God's right and left sides in the Quran are metaphorical, as both sides of the divine throne are equally blessed. It then highlighted narrations about the "Beloved of God," who will stand in the shade of the Throne on Judgment Day, distinguished by their divine criteria for love and enmity in this world. In the jurisprudential segment, a scholar emphasized that doctors must be absolutely certain a medication is safe for pregnant women and their fetuses, as prescribing drugs with even a probable risk of harm is strictly forbidden. The discussion on abortion noted that while it is generally prohibited, it is permissible only in rare, exceptional cases where the mother's life is at risk. The Q&A section addressed various rulings, including the obligation of a son to perform missed prayers for his deceased father and the insufficiency of repentance prayers to replace missed obligatory prayers. It also confirmed the permissibility of sharing water blessed with specific Nisan month recitations and using meat from a personal vow for a family gathering. Finally, the program clarified that washing fingers during ablution is sufficient if water passes through them, according to different marjas.
