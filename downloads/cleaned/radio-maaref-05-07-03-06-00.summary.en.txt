This session of the theology course continues the discussion on the occasion of revelation of the verse of Tabligh and its implication for Imamate.
Ibn Munir al-Iskandari's theory regarding the conveyance of the divine message was presented as a valid defense against the charge of redundancy.
The primary justification distinguishes between the exclusive conveyance of revelation, specific to the Prophet, and the general propagation of religion, shared by the Prophet and the Imam.
To reconcile conflicting narrations, the hypothesis of the verse's multiple revelations during the proclamation of Tawhid and Imamate was proposed and supported by reputable exegesis.
The session also referenced Sheikh Salim's letter to Sharaf al-Din Yazdi in al-Muraja'at, praising the verses concerning the virtues of Ali (AS).
A key point established is that conveying the divine message is an exclusive attribute of prophethood, which prevents the argument of redundancy from holding.
This analysis demonstrates that the verse of Tabligh can simultaneously imply both the proclamation of prophethood and Imamate without creating any contradiction.
The session concluded by introducing the thirteenth reference in al-Muraja'at and promising to discuss it in the next session.
