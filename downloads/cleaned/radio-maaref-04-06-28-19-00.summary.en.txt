This radio program featured jurisprudential answers to listener questions from various religious authorities. First, the permissibility of eyebrow transplant by a non-same-sex doctor was discussed, deemed acceptable if necessary and strict privacy boundaries are maintained. It was clarified that financial aid from welfare organizations is not subject to Khums unless it is converted into personal income. Regarding adoption from an orphanage, listeners were advised to consult specialists to establish a valid legal guardianship. The validity of prayer with minor nail contamination and wudu with slight foot moisture was also confirmed. In a financial dispute with a spouse, the importance of caution and preserving family harmony was emphasized, allowing the retrieval of one's own money only with certainty of ownership. Finally, two short stories titled "Zalal Vaghef" and "Toranj" were broadcast, highlighting themes of charity, patience, and human resilience.
