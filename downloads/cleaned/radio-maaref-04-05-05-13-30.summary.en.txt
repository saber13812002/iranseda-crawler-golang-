In this radio program, experts characterize the recent conflict as a strategic defeat for the United States and Israel in their attempt to impose a "New Middle East" order on West Asia. Dr. Torabi argues that the enemy, believing in the false narrative of Iran's rapid collapse, was instead confronted by the military resistance and political cohesion of the Islamic Republic, which highlights its people-centric power. The analysis indicates that advanced Western missile defense systems proved ineffective against Iranian missiles, marking a practical defeat for Israel and weakening America's regional standing. Within the US, this war has deepened the rift between Trump and the "America First" movement, while independent experts acknowledge Tel Aviv's failure in direct combat. The experts believe that Iran's policy of supporting popular resistance, contrasted with the West's divisive and clan-based approach, will ultimately lead to the destruction of the Zionist regime and the US withdrawal from the region. It is also emphasized that while Iran did not seek war, its crushing response to the attack demonstrated the nation's significant military and security capabilities.
