The program began by introducing autumn and encouraging listeners to maintain hope and a positive outlook. In the health segment, a traditional medicine expert advised consuming ripe, warm fruits while avoiding unripe or sour ones. To counteract the cold nature of white rice, the expert recommended using brown rice or mixing it with warm legumes like chickpeas and mung beans. Olive oil was suggested as a healthy alternative to animal fats, and the excessive use of hot spices was discouraged. In the Quranic section, the scholar emphasized the importance of performing good deeds and prayers promptly before misfortune strikes. Citing the Surah Al-Ma'arij, he described prayer as the pillar of religion that prevents the forgetfulness of God and the Day of Judgment. Finally, the program highlighted the significance of teaching the Quran to children as a crucial aspect of their spiritual upbringing.
