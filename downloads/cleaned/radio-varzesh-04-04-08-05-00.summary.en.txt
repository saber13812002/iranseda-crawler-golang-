Dr. Ali Gharadashi, the current president of the Iranian Table Tennis Federation, discusses his tenure as secretary-general and his key collaborations with Aytollah Shahnazari and Foad Kasab to develop the sport. He highlights a pivotal 1999 trip to North Korea, where he observed high technical skills despite limited facilities, which inspired the federation's strategic shift. Gharadashi emphasizes that bringing North Korean coaches and players to Iran to participate in the local league was a turning point that significantly improved the national team's performance. He also recounts the 2014 election process, noting his victory with 35 out of 39 valid votes to become the federation's president. In conclusion, Gharadashi states that he has no intention of running for re-election, expressing confidence in the capable next generation of players and his colleagues' team efforts. He believes that despite sanctions and financial challenges, winning an Olympic medal in table tennis is an achievable goal for the future.
