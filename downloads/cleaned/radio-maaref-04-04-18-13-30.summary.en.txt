In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Saeidi Aria initially discussed the importance of friendship with Imam Hussein (PBUH) and its role in bringing blessings.
The main segment, titled "Narrative of Love," featured the wife of Martyr General Tehrani Moghaddam recounting her husband's life and character.
Mrs. Haidari highlighted his multidimensional personality and his high popularity among various social groups, including youth, officials, and intellectuals.
She mentioned his remarkable athletic achievements, such as organizing a five-thousand-person camp on Mount Dena and setting records in rock climbing, as examples of his creative efforts.
The martyr's deep love for worship, particularly night and Friday prayers, and his natural influence on his family and surroundings without any coercion were also discussed.
A notable point was his constant habit of maintaining ritual ablution and performing prayers in all conditions, even during road trips.
The program concluded with the broadcast of religious music pieces and an introduction to religious media outlets.
