This program explores the vital role of speech in conveying divine messages, citing Quranic verses that emphasize the importance of eloquence and prayer for ease of expression.
A central theme is the severe Quranic warning against the misuse of language, including lying, slander, and verbal harassment, which are strongly condemned.
The discussion clarifies that blunt speech without the foundation of knowledge and wisdom is a moral vice rather than a virtue, as it causes harm to others.
The concept of the "testimony of the tongue" on the Day of Judgment is explained, where one's words and deeds will serve as evidence against or in favor of them.
The segment concludes with an analysis of the Verse of Mutual Cursing (Mubahala), which highlights the high status of the Prophet's household and proves the truth of Islam.
This verse is recognized in both Shia and Sunni sources as a sign of the greatness of the Prophet and his family.
Finally, the necessity of self-discipline and learning to control one's tongue to avoid verbal sins in religious and social life is emphasized.
