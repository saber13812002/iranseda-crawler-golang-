The radio program began by describing the physical appearance of Imam Mahdi (AJ) based on shared Shia and Sunni narrations, noting features such as a wheat-colored complexion, medium height, and long hair. Dr. Erfan-Khah then emphasized the concepts of "basirat" (insight) and "opportunity recognition" in supporting the Mahdi, using historical examples from the companions of Imam Hussein to illustrate how seizing critical moments leads to eternal legacy. He distinguished between mere "mourning" and truly becoming "Husaini," arguing that genuine supporters must embody ethical behavior in all daily activities, including driving and workplace interactions. A practical method for aiding the Imam was proposed: establishing Mahdi-oriented kindergartens and schools to nurture children with love and hope for the appearance from a young age. The discussion concluded with an interpretation of Quranic verses affirming that falsehood is destined to perish throughout history, reinforcing the certainty of the Imam's eventual rise.
