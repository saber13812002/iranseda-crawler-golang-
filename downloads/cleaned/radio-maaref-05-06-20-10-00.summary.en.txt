This radio program focuses on the concept of gratitude for the blessing of Wilayah and the preparation for the reappearance of Imam Mahdi. The guest expert, Dr. Erfan Hajari, categorizes gratitude into spiritual, behavioral, and verbal forms, emphasizing the necessity of aligning actions with beliefs. Citing Quranic verses and hadiths, he identifies the unity and solidarity of Muslims as a critical prerequisite for the Imam's return. A central point is that true Shia must adorn their faith with ethical conduct and avoid sins that would bring disrepute to the Ahl al-Bayt. Verbal gratitude is defined as publicly acknowledging and promoting the virtues of the Holy Family. The discussion concludes by urging listeners to eliminate grudges and emulate the Prophet's and the Imams' exemplary social behavior.
