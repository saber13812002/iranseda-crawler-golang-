The program begins by addressing various jurisprudential rulings related to Hajj and prayer, including the requirement to prostrate on earth rather than metals and the correct method for performing tayammum. It also clarifies the rules regarding the buying and selling of women's hair, emphasizing caution when looking at the hair of non-mahram women, and the validity of substitute prayers for individuals with obsessive doubts. Additional rulings cover the permissible deviation from the Qibla direction during urination, the permissibility of wearing a tie at weddings, and the option for sinusitis patients to perform tayammum instead of ghusl. In the second segment, Dr. Seyed Mohsen Mirbagheri discusses the wisdom of prayer based on the narrations of Imam Reza (AS). He describes prayer as a means to acknowledge God's lordship, seek forgiveness for sins, and cultivate humility and reverence. The primary goal of prayer is to maintain a constant connection with God and prevent spiritual negligence in worldly affairs. The program concludes with the recitation of verses from Surah Ash-Shura by Master Mansour Ghasemzadeh.
