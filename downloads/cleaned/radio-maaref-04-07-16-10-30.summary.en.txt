In the radio program "Shifa," Dr. Pourhosseini explained that consuming jelly immediately after meals disrupts digestion due to artificial dyes and chemical sweeteners. She recommended using jelly that meets religious and health standards and advised maintaining a two-hour gap between meals and jelly consumption.
