Hojjat al-Islam Kashani delivered a lecture titled "Four Pillars of Civilization" on the radio program "Bar Bal Sakhn." He identified the month of Rabi al-Awwal as the month of the end-times civilization, marked by the births of the Prophet, Imam Sadiq, and Lady Masoumeh. Kashani emphasized that the Prophet established a great civilization without capital, force, or reliance on foreign powers, relying solely on divine will. By contrasting the Treaty of Hudaybiyyah with Meccan polytheists and the Prophet's decisive action against treacherous Jews in Medina and Khaybar, he argued that peace with true enemies of Islam is impermissible. The four main pillars of this civilization were identified as wisdom, justice, chastity, and courage. He stressed that maintaining chastity in speech, wealth, and social relations is essential to eliminating societal abnormalities. Finally, he deemed military readiness and courage necessary to deter enemies and defend Islam. He expressed hope that these four principles will enable the Islamic Revolution to prepare the ground for the reappearance of the Imam and fill the earth with justice and courage.
