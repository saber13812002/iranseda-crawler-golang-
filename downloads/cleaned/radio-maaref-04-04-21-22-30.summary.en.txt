In this radio program, based on authentic Shia narrations, the traits that God loves and those He dislikes were explained. Among the divine preferences are honesty, seeking knowledge, maintaining etiquette and a soft voice in the presence of elders, moderation in behavior, beauty and grooming, and gratitude for blessings. It was emphasized that God dislikes waste and the squandering of resources, particularly food and water, which is prevalent in our society. Other qualities favored by God include high ambition, performing noble deeds, reciting the Quran with proper rhythm and a beautiful voice, and having a profession with integrity in trade. Conversely, God detaches from stinginess, laziness, and rude or ill-mannered behavior. The speaker highlighted the importance of environmental conservation and preventing waste, urging listeners to apply these teachings. The program concluded with the recitation of verses from Surah Zukhruf and the playing of Hussaini hymns in observance of the month of Muharram.
