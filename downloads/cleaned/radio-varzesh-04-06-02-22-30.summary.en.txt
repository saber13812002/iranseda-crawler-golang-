Seyyed Mansour Daryabari shared his youth memories from the Chahardasht neighborhood during the early days of the Islamic Revolution. He recounted how local youths actively defended the Air Force base by constructing Molotov cocktails against enemy tanks. Daryabari also discussed his efforts to establish sports spaces in the village of Vashdan and organize local football leagues. He highlighted the surprising visit of football legend Goudars Habibi, who served as a coach for his family team. The poet's verses describing the neighborhood and national champions were also featured in the conversation. He attributed the decline in producing sports heroes in Tehran to the conversion of dirt fields into construction sites and a lack of local motivation. Daryabari expressed concern about Iran's current football status while acknowledging significant progress in sports like wrestling and volleyball.
