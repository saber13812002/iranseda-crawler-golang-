This radio program begins with a tafsir of verses from Surah An-Nisa by Ayatollah Javadi Amoli, focusing on the distinction between the direct reception of revelation by the Prophet and its mediated reception by angels. The commentator explains that in the highest stages, the Perfect Man receives divine grace directly from God, a moment when even Gabriel is absent, whereas in other stages, angels act as intermediaries. The discussion also addresses the concept of hidden prophets and the necessity of a divine proof for every nation, although it is not required for every city or village to have an independent prophet. During periods of interregnum, if the voice of a prophet does not reach a people, God does not punish them because the divine proof is not complete for them. Finally, the concept of "God's testimony" in the Quran is explained as a declaration of certainty that ends the debate with deniers, rather than merely stating God's knowledge. This testimony signifies the presentation of miracles and God's word as definitive proof of prophethood.
