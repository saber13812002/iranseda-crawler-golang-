This radio program provided religious rulings in response to various listener inquiries. Regarding a smoky vehicle, mechanical defects must be repaired, while inherent smoke requires avoiding public nuisance. Khums is obligatory on salary saved during the Khums year for purchasing an asset. Wudu is invalidated if the head's moisture is excessive enough to erase the wetness of the hand during the wiping ritual. To atone for backbiting, one must seek forgiveness from the victim or perform good deeds on their behalf if that is impossible. Renouncing one's legal homeland requires a sincere intention accompanied by the actual act of leaving the city. Selling inherited land and converting it into capital makes it subject to Khums at the end of the year. Old debts must be repaid with inflation adjustments to the creditor or to the poor as public rights if the creditor is unreachable. The program concluded by emphasizing the moral conduct of believers and introducing the shrine of Bibi Hayat in Baku.
