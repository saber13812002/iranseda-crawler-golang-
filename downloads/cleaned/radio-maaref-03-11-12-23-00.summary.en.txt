In the first segment of the program, Dr. Hassan Molaei emphasized the necessity of strengthening the absolute belief in the appearance of Imam Mahdi (AJ). He warned that enemies are using media to weaken this faith and called upon broadcasters, experts, and youth to reinforce it through knowledge and righteous action. The program then transitioned to "Historical Persman," hosted by Professor Mehdi Pishvayi, who addressed historical inquiries from listeners. In response to a question about the Prophet's (PBUH) worship in the Cave of Hira, it was explained that he was raised under the guidance of angels before his prophethood, engaging in contemplation, devotion, and prayer. The distinction between a prophet (Nabi) and a messenger (Rasul) was also clarified, noting that the Prophet received his mission at the age of forty. The discussion further analyzed verses by Abu Firas al-Hamdani regarding the tragedies of the Ahl al-Bayt and the burial sites of their martyrs. Finally, the identity of the "Martyr of Bakhmarah" was identified as Ibrahim ibn Abdullah, the brother of Muhammad al-Nafs al-Zakiyya, who was killed near Kufa during his uprising against the Abbasid caliph.
