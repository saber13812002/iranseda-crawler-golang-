Master Ruben Shahvordi recounts his bold decision at age 25 to travel to South Korea to find his original Taekwondo master and advance his rank to third Dan. Upon returning to Iran, he recognized a lack of reliable scientific resources in the field, which led him to author and translate specialized martial arts books for future generations. He discusses the challenges of operating as a one-person publisher and the recent decline in public interest for specialized sports literature. Shahvordi emphasizes the martial arts philosophy as a lifestyle approach where respect, discipline, and self-improvement take precedence over combat techniques. Although a distinguished student of Master Kang Shin Chul, he has decided not to pursue the eighth Dan due to physical limitations and age. He concludes by encouraging listeners to support hidden talents in the field and to use martial arts for personal and social growth.
