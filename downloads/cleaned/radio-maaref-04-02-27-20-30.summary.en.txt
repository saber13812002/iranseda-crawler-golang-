The program begins by examining the intellectual and cultural legacy of Martyr Ayatollah Motahhari, who emphasized time-consciousness and addressing societal needs through his book "Dastan-e Rastan." This work, which won a UNESCO award, was designed to introduce the general public to authentic Islam and combat syncretism while maintaining an engaging narrative style. It gained significant respect from Imam Khomeini and the public for its role in clarifying Islamic identity. The second part of the show features the testimony of Abdullah Rajabi, a freedom fighter and war veteran, recounting his experiences during the Iran-Iraq War and his captivity in Iraq. He describes his severe injuries during border operations and the harsh conditions of his imprisonment, including torture and his determination to maintain his prayers despite extreme hardship. Rajabi also details the psychological pressures and attempts to preserve his Islamic identity against enemy humiliation. The program concludes with a recommendation of the book "Autumn Has Come," a biography of Martyr Ahmad Yusefi, and invites listeners to share their feedback.
