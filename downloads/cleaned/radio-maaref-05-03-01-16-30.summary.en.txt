In this radio program, Professor Jafari first discusses the betrayal of the Banu Nadir tribe and Sa'd ibn Mu'adh's judgment during the Battle of the Trench, which resulted in the execution of their combatants. He then recounts the historical account of the Battle of Siffin, explaining how Imam Ali (AS) entered the battlefield after three of his warriors were killed by Quraib ibn Shabab and defeated him with a single strike. Responding to listener questions, the program clarifies the approximately 350-year gap between Imam Ali's martyrdom and Sayyid Razi's birth, highlighting Razi's role in compiling Nahj al-Balagha. The discussion also analyzes Imam Ali's statement regarding the pledge of allegiance with Talha and Zubayr, noting that it was intended to expose their lack of qualification for leadership. A significant portion of the episode is dedicated to the details of the Battle of the Confederates, including the digging of the trench based on Salman al-Farisi's suggestion and Imam Ali's heroic duel with Amr ibn al-Walid. Finally, the program references the verses in Surah Al-Ahzab concerning this battle and the punishment of Banu Quraizah, concluding with a poem by Rumi about Imam Ali's bravery.
