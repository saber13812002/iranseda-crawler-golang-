In the radio program "Mashq-e Rah," Hujjat al-Islam Seyyed Ahmad Faqih emphasized the fundamental importance of the phrase "La ilaha illa Allah" as the core of monotheism and the key to salvation. He refuted the notion that this dhikr is burdensome, describing it instead as a source of divine peace and blessing in one's life. Addressing listener questions, the expert explained that family conflicts and severing ties with relatives often stem from envy, excessive worldly attachment, and Satan's whispers. He advised addressing these issues through open dialogue with one's spouse and recognizing how Satan beautifies sinful actions. To control the tongue and avoid verbal sins, he recommended practicing reflection before speaking and habituating the mouth to reciting and memorizing the Quran. Finally, he identified religious study circles as the modern arena for "natural jihad" and the revival of enjoining good and forbidding evil.
