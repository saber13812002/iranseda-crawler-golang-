The news segment announced Hassan Najat's return to the boxing ring to face opponents from Kazakhstan and Turkey.
The program then transitioned to a religious discussion in "Mahdavi Questions" featuring Professor Yousefian.
The central question addressed was why the exact time of Imam Mahdi's reappearance remains unknown.
Professor Yousefian explained that this secrecy is a divine wisdom designed to keep humanity in active anticipation.
If the timing were known, the motivation for effort, patience, and resistance against hardships would vanish.
This state of waiting fosters the development of human talents and provides hope amidst oppression and injustice.
The discussion also noted scholarly disagreement regarding whether the Imam himself knows the specific time of reappearance.
Ultimately, the conclusion was that the uncertainty of the timing is the primary driver of societal movement and resilience.
