The program begins with a theological discussion on the Quran's role as a guardian and intercessor, emphasizing that these attributes are granted by God's permission and denying them constitutes a rejection of divine law. The concept of "Hukm" (judgment) is then explored as a stabilizing force that resolves uncertainty in scientific, judicial, and belief-related matters, identifying God as the sole source of wisdom and the "best argument." The broadcast transitions to a spiritual segment highlighting the historical significance of Bab al-Hat in Najaf and its connection to repentance, followed by morning prayers and greetings to the Imam. The show also marks National Quality Day and commemorates the birth of Muhammad Iqbal, while simultaneously refuting enemy claims about Iran's production capabilities and encouraging domestic manufacturers. Finally, the main focus shifts to the health benefits of eating breakfast, discussing its impact on metabolism and overall well-being through live interactions with listeners.
