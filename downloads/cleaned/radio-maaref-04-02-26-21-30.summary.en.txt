This segment of the Mahdism course discusses a narration claiming that Imam Mahdi (aj) will enter Jerusalem without conflict. The hadith, attributed to Jabir ibn Abdullah Ansari, is cited in major collections like Wasail al-Shia and Awaid al-Athar. Its primary source is the book "Al-Majmu' al-Ra'iq" by Sadh al-Allah Musawi, a scholar from the eighth century AH. Although Allameh Majlisi did not directly quote this book in Bihar al-Anwar, Allameh Nuri later identified it as a reliable source in his supplementary work. Research confirms that the book was not authored by Sheikh Saduq or Mufid, but rather by a contemporary of Allameh Hili who cited later sources. Crucially, the specific narration in question was found in an unconnected manuscript (wajada) within the Ibn Tawus library. Consequently, while the source book is considered credible, this particular hadith lacks a continuous chain of transmission and requires further investigation in future sessions.
