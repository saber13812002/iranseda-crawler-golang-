On the first anniversary of President Raisi's martyrdom, scientific sessions were held to examine his views on religion, media, and management. The head of the Women's Seminaries announced the start of admissions across 450 schools, noting a 30 percent increase in registrations. The National Cinema Document was finalized by the Supreme Council of the Cultural Revolution to incorporate cultural values. A dental jihad camp in Shiraz provided free services to over two thousand needy individuals. Special religious ceremonies were scheduled at Jamkaran Mosque for the anniversary of Imam Sadiq's martyrdom. In the "Zamzam Ahkam" program, Professor Vahidpour explained the jurisprudential difference between interest-free loans and bank facilities. He emphasized that bank facilities are a type of Sharia-compliant transaction and are distinct from usury.
