The radio program "Narrative of Love" featured Shamseghasemi, the sister of the martyr cleric Kamal Ghasemi.
The guest recounted the four separate funeral ceremonies held for her brother in Iraq and Iran.
She described the difficult process of receiving his remains and the secret, nighttime burial in Urmia.
This clandestine burial was arranged to spare her parents from the shock of a second loss.
Ms. Ghasemi revealed that her mother remained unaware of the truth for years, while her father learned of it later.
She drew parallels between her brother's martyrdom and the story of Lady Zahra, highlighting the shared suffering.
The segment concluded with listener messages expressing deep emotion and respect for the family.
Additionally, a separate discussion addressed meritocracy and the role of women in managerial positions.
