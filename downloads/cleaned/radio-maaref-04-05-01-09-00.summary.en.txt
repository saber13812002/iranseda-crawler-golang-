In the "Persaman Ateghadi" program on Radio Ma'aref, various religious and theological questions from listeners were addressed. The host clarified that the prayer for breaking sorcery has no negative effects, as it simply unties knots and removes harmful influences. It was explained that the freedom of deceased souls to visit their families depends on their spiritual rank in the Barzakh realm, rather than being restricted to specific days. Regarding the end times, the program noted that while good and bad deeds are revealed in all eras, maintaining faith is more difficult in the final period due to increased awareness and the absence of excuses for ignorance. The scholar emphasized that Imam Hussein's uprising was inevitable and aimed at reforming the Ummah and defending Islam, with the pressure to pledge allegiance or the invitation from Kufa serving only as contextual factors rather than the primary cause. Another response highlighted that performing recommended acts, such as night prayers, should not disturb others, as respecting the rights of neighbors is an obligatory duty. The broadcast concluded by honoring the martyrdom of nuclear scientist Dariush Rezaei Najaf and discussing the engineering efforts of Seyed Mohammad Sani Khani in rebuilding bridges destroyed during the Iran-Iraq war.
