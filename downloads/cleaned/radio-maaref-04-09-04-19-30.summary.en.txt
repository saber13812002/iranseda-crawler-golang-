The program began with the recitation of a rain prayer from the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya before transitioning to a discussion on the vice of mockery and its consequences.
Key causes of mockery, such as arrogance, heedlessness of God, envy, and self-centeredness, were identified as factors that destroy others' self-confidence and create social discord.
The worldly consequences of this vice include the darkening of the heart and acting as a significant barrier to spiritual progress and religious self-improvement.
Regarding the afterlife, the concept of the manifestation of deeds was explained, indicating that mockers will face a painful punishment that is intrinsically linked to their own existence.
On the Day of Judgment, they will be mocked in return for their actions, and the gates of Paradise will be closed to them, leading to eternal regret and lamentation.
Conversely, believers who avoided this vice and sought divine pleasure will be rewarded with joy and happiness in contrast to the sorrow of the mockers.
