In this radio program, Dr. Mehdi Amidi emphasized Iran's firm resolve to maintain and advance its nuclear industry, refusing to yield to external threats and recent bombings of nuclear facilities. He dismissed rumors regarding the legal division of the Caspian Sea, clarifying that the process is solely for defining coastal boundaries and does not imply ceding territory to other nations. Regarding negotiations with the United States, Amidi argued that dialogue is only logical if the adversary's stance shifts from seeking destruction and disintegration to narrower issues, provided Iran's preconditions, such as the return of assets and troop withdrawal, are met. Citing the failure of past treaties, he warned that negotiations must not lead to zero enrichment or grant dangerous concessions to the enemy. Furthermore, Amidi highlighted the critical importance of Iran exercising sovereignty over the Strait of Hormuz, asserting that full Iranian oversight ensures regional security and limits US military influence. Finally, he stressed the necessity of maintaining military capability and the nation's will to stand firm against pressure, preventing the realization of US objectives in the region.
