Ayatollah Fazel Golpayegani recounts his childhood memories in Qom, highlighting the severe water shortages and the lack of modern plumbing infrastructure. He describes how residents relied on collecting water from open ditches for both drinking and washing purposes. The speaker praises the late martyr Mohammad Sadegh Sedoq for his proactive role in solving community issues, such as clearing qanats after floods and digging deep wells for agriculture. He emphasizes that clergymen of that era stood alongside the people against injustice with high ambition, independent of government support. The discussion also touches upon the unique virtues of Lady Zahra (SA) and her special connection with the Angel Gabriel during the seventy-five days following the Prophet's passing. The segment concludes with congratulations on the anniversary of the Basij establishment and tribute to its martyrs.
