The radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat," featuring Professor Mohammad Hadi Falahati, explores the concept of "anger" and its psychological roots in the Quran and Hadith.
Citing a narration from Imam Sadiq (AS) regarding Mufaddal, the professor distinguishes between true Shias and false claimants.
Imam Sadiq (AS) defines a Ja'fari Shia as one who controls their tongue, fears God, and works for His sake, rather than acting out of rage.
The second segment discusses the impact of "fear of God" on human behavior through a narration from Imam Sajjad (AS).
The story of a chaste woman whose fear of God prevents a sinful man from committing adultery exemplifies the transformative power of faith.
After repenting, the man's prayer for shade is answered, symbolizing the acceptance of his repentance.
The discussion concludes that fearing God and controlling anger are the keys to moral and social salvation.
Listeners are encouraged to follow the path of the Ahl al-Bayt (AS) to avoid moral vices.
