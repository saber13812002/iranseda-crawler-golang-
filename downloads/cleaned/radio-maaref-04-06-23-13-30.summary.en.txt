In this discussion, the Iranian representative emphasized the new agreement signed with the IAEA in Cairo, which redefines the framework of cooperation following attacks on nuclear facilities. Under this deal, the IAEA acknowledged the attacks on Iran's facilities as illegal and accepted that cooperation must proceed under new conditions while respecting Iran's domestic laws. The core of the agreement mandates that any IAEA access request must be reviewed and approved by Iran's Supreme National Security Council to address national security concerns. Currently, no inspections are scheduled, and even for unattacked facilities, access must be granted on a case-by-case basis through this council. Iran stated that reports on enriched materials at bombed sites will also be submitted to the IAEA via the Supreme National Security Council after final assessment. The differing statements by Iranian and IAEA officials stem merely from each side highlighting different aspects of the same clear text, rather than conflicting interpretations. Finally, the speaker stressed the importance of maintaining national unity and public solidarity against enemy plots to create division, urging media to avoid undermining the system's foundations.
