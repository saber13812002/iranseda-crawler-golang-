In the radio program "Persoman Quran," Hujjat al-Islam Molaei addressed listeners' questions regarding Quranic verses. He explained that while the creation of the heavens and earth is physically vast, God calls humans "the best of creation" because humans possess spiritual potential and the capacity for divine proximity, which celestial bodies lack. Responding to another query, he emphasized that the Prophet's (PBUH) Ascension was a physical event that occurred in a single night, not a dream or spiritual journey, during which great divine signs were revealed to him. He also noted that the exact number of angels is unknown to humans, though they are deployed to assist believers at various times. In the final segment, the discussion focused on why prayers for ending the oppression in Palestine seem unanswered. The expert stressed that no prayer goes unanswered, but acceptance may take the form of averting disaster or being stored for the Hereafter. He identified the primary reason for delayed acceptance as the lack of practical action and unity within the Islamic world, emphasizing that prayer must be accompanied by effort and the awakening of Islamic leaders.
