In this program, Dr. Bahmani emphasizes the Supreme Leader's declaration that America is the "Great Satan," arguing that negotiations with the US are meaningless and akin to dealing with Pharaoh. He asserts that Iran is engaged in a comprehensive war with America across military, economic, and cultural fronts, clarifying that the current pause in hostilities does not signify a ceasefire or the end of the conflict. Dr. Bahmani warns that after failing on the military battlefield, the enemy may attempt to undermine social resilience by creating severe economic fluctuations and instilling fear. He believes that countering these threats requires "Greater Jihad," which involves strengthening solidarity, hope, and vigilance among the people and officials. The expert stresses that internal disagreements or the enemy's misinformation must not be allowed to disrupt national unity, and vigilance is crucial to prevent the adversary from exploiting any weaknesses. Finally, he highlights the power of the people in the streets and underscores the importance of maintaining calm and trusting the leadership's promises to achieve ultimate victory.
