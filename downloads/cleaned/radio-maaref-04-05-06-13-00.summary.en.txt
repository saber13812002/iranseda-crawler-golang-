This radio program emphasizes the importance of "self-accounting" as a directive from Imam Sadiq (AS). Evaluating good deeds requires gratitude and attributing success to God to avoid the pitfalls of negligence, arrogance, and hidden shirk. Conversely, assessing bad deeds demands honest acceptance of mistakes, avoiding excuses, and refusing to blame others or God. True repentance occurs when, after personal effort to reform, one seeks divine mercy and forgiveness from God. During the months of Muharram and Safar, a specific form of self-accounting focuses on "intentional success" and "ta'assuf" (regret for missing the chance to aid Imam Hussein). It is believed that a person can share in the martyrdom and support of Imam Hussein through their heart and intention, even without physical presence. This process enables the believer to continuously progress spiritually and draw closer to God by being aware of their shortcomings.
