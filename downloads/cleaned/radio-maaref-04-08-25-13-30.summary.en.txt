The radio program "Enduring Memories" began by sharing combatants' recollections from the Badr, Fath al-Mubin, and Tariq al-Quds operations. Sardar Lakzai described the rigorous winter water training conducted for the Badr operation in the marshlands of Hawizeh and Al-Azimeh. He also recounted a meeting with an elderly guardian who was amazed by the moral transformation of the people following the Islamic Revolution. Sardar Rasouli shared the story of student martyr Alireza Zare'i, who continued his studies at the front and was engaged in worship the night before the Fath al-Mubin operation. Davoud Moushiri recalled the martyrdom of a soldier who had fled his post only to return to the front line during the Tariq al-Quds operation. The final segment focused on the Moharram operation and the funeral of 360 martyrs in Isfahan, highlighting the city's resilient and revolutionary spirit. These memories collectively emphasize the bravery, divine trust, and self-sacrifice of the combatants during the Sacred Defense.
