The broadcast began with the song "Iran," followed by a segment in "Parsaiyan" where Hujjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the virtue of humility and the avoidance of arrogance. Citing religious narrations, he described humble behavior as a sign of divine love and a source of public respect, highlighting Ayatollah Mirza Javad Tabrizi as a model who maintained a lifestyle equal to that of ordinary people. In the "Book Narrative" section, the story of Hasan ibn Ali and his brother Qasim during their Hajj journey was recounted; Hasan expressed his resolve to martyrdom alongside Imam Hussein and calmed Qasim, who was distressed by his horse's agitation. The "Did You Know?" segment clarified the legal distinction between lying with serious intent and harmless jokes where the listener is aware of the speaker's intent. The program concluded with announcements for upcoming shows, including Ayatollah Javadi Amoli's lecture on social justice, and provided contact information for Radio Ma'aref.
