In this segment of the program, a hadith from the Prophet is discussed, emphasizing the importance of praying with a sense of farewell and closeness to God.
The Prophet stated that one should pray as if it were their last prayer, feeling as though they are about to stand before their Creator.
A man who practiced this spiritual state for a week after hearing the hadith reported experiencing profound positive effects in his life.
Another key point from the hadith is the necessity of feeling shame before God, even in private, which increases a believer's certainty.
The program also highlighted the high status of the Quran and the Prophet's family in guiding humanity.
Subsequently, verses 48 to 55 of Surah Al-An'am were recited by the respected Qari, Mr. Mohammad Hossein Sabzali.
The segment concluded with prayers for the listeners and a reference to the mourning period for the martyrdom of Imam Hussein (PBUH).
