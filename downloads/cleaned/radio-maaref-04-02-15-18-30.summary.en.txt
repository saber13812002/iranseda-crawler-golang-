The "Zamzam Ahkam" program addressed various religious inquiries from listeners. Regarding the use of alcohol in food, caution and avoidance were recommended due to doubts about its impurity. It was ruled that child support remains the father's responsibility after divorce, even if the children choose to live with their mother, unless otherwise agreed. A guitar should be destroyed if used for forbidden music, but can be sold if it is a shared instrument. Making up missed prayers for others requires full compliance with all pillars without any excuse. The presence of men in a woman's operating room is considered problematic if the surgery is on the woman. The second segment emphasized the importance of investment in production to resolve Iran's economic issues in 2025. Finally, the virtues of Lady Zainab and her station of contentment were highlighted as a model for submission to God's will.
