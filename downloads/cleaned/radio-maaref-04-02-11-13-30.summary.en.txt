The program began by honoring the memory of Martyr Ayatollah Mortazavi and celebrating Teachers' Day.
It then transitioned to the "Narrative of Love" segment, featuring Hajeh Parva Javad, the wife of Martyr Hossein Amini.
She recounted their shared memories, highlighting the final farewell where the martyr concealed his departure to maintain his resolve.
Hajeh Parva described receiving the martyrdom letter and visiting his body at the martyrs' cemetery, where she was the sole family member present.
She detailed the hardships of the eight years of uncertainty and the impact of his absence on their children and daily life.
She concluded by sharing miraculous experiences that reinforced her belief in her husband's spiritual presence after his martyrdom.
The broadcast ended with music and fragmented discussions regarding the status of workers in Islam and a critique of the labor system.
