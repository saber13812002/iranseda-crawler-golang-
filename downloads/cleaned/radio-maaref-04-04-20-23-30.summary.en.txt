This radio program examines Shia narrations regarding the exclusive scientific and spiritual resources of the Infallible Imams.
Key texts such as the "Mushaf of Fatima" and the "Andi al-Jafar" (White and Red) are highlighted as containing divine laws and future predictions.
It is emphasized that these sacred books belong solely to the Ahl al-Bayt, refuting claims by other groups like the Zaydis.
The narrations indicate that the Red Jafar symbolizes the declaration of war, while the White Jafar contains fundamental human needs and rulings.
The speaker criticizes certain political-religious currents for seeking power through false claims and denying the Imamate via illegitimate means.
It is stressed that legitimate uprisings must occur with the Imam's permission and within the framework of Wilayah, not through independent claims.
Finally, the importance of practical follow-up on religious and social issues after public speeches is discussed to avoid mere sloganism.
