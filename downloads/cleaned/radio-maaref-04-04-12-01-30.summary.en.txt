This radio program details Iran's retaliatory operation following the assassination of General Soleimani, which involved launching 30 missiles and executing an electronic warfare campaign to disable US drones at the Al-Asad base. The speaker emphasizes that the primary objective was to demonstrate military capability and shatter the false sense of security among US forces, rather than to inflict mass casualties. By highlighting the concentrated nature of US bases in limited spaces, the speaker argues that Iran can effectively target these vulnerable points with a limited number of missiles, whereas the US would need to strike thousands of dispersed targets within Iran. The speaker also urges the American people to pressure their government to end its costly and futile presence in the region. Finally, the program concludes by warning that any retaliatory response from the US would trigger a more decisive and severe reaction from Iran, before transitioning to a literary narrative titled "The Sign of Hassan."
