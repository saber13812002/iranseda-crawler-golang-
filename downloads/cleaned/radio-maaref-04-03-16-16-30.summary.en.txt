In the radio program "Historical Riddle," the host addressed listeners' questions regarding historical events and figures. He identified the "Lion and Faze" site in Karbala as stemming from a weak narration where a lion protected Imam Hussein's (PBUH) body from enemies. The host also confirmed Imam Ali's (PBUH) reference to the event of Ghadir Khumm to prove his legitimacy, citing testimonies recorded in the book Al-Ghadir. Regarding Imam Hassan's (PBUH) peace treaty, he attributed it to the disloyalty of companions, the bribery of a military commander, and the public's exhaustion from war. He clarified that Hazrat Abdol-Azim Haseeni was a descendant of Imam Hassan (PBUH) who lived in Rey, and explained that the high number of Imams' descendants is due to indirect lineage. In the final segment, the host emphasized the virtue of humility and the danger of arrogance based on hadiths from the book of Kafi. The story of Noah's (PBUH) ark and the humility of Mount Judi were presented as a practical model against social competition and ostentation.
