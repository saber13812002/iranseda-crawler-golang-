Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verses 52 and 53 of Surah Al-A'raf by defining "ta'wil" as the return of the Quran's news and rulings to their objective reality on the Day of Judgment. He emphasizes that ta'wil is of the nature of external existence, fundamentally differing from interpretation, which belongs to the realm of mental concepts and knowledge. The term "they wait" is explained as a threat, indicating that disbelievers are awaiting the manifestation of the Quran's truth. On that day, those who neglected the Quran will acknowledge the truth of prophethood and God's lordship. They will express two desires: seeking intercession or returning to the world to perform righteous deeds, both of which are rejected by God. The rejection of returning to the world is based on the fact that their lifespan and existential capital are exhausted, leaving no possibility for a new start. Furthermore, intercession is futile for those who had no relationship with God's saints and relied on false idols. Finally, God points out that even if a return were possible, these individuals would revert to their previous misguidance due to repeated divine trials.
