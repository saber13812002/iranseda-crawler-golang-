In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Rostamnejad first discusses the Quranic concept of the resurrection of deeds and the return of their effects on the Day of Judgment. He emphasizes that every action, from prayer and writing to social media activity, leaves a lasting impact that will be accounted for. The second part of the program features the memoirs of Air Force Colonel Ahmad Mehrnia regarding the heavy Iraqi air raids in November 1986. Mehrnia recounts the bombing of the Desful air base and Andimeshk city, which resulted in the martyrdom of hundreds, including guests and base personnel. He describes the intense psychological pressure of that day and his efforts to protect his family by taking shelter in dugouts. He also praises the bravery of the demolition team in collecting cluster and time-bombs from the streets to prevent secondary explosions. Finally, the program highlights the miraculous discovery of two missing martyrs' bodies in the Afghan desert, which remained perfectly preserved after years.
