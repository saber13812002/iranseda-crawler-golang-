In this program, Professor Mohammadi outlines five characteristics of God's best servants, including forgiveness, gratitude to the intermediaries of blessings, patience during adversity, and controlling anger.
He then discusses the proof of Prophet Muhammad's prophethood, citing Quranic verses and the Torah to show that the People of the Book recognized his attributes before his arrival.
The professor attributes their refusal to accept him to racial prejudice and tribalism rather than a lack of knowledge about his identity.
Addressing a listener's question about human history, he clarifies that the current human lineage, starting from Adam and Eve, is approximately ten to twelve thousand years old.
He argues that seven previous generations existed before this one but became extinct, as they lacked the divine spirit and rational soul found in modern humans.
Quranic evidence, such as the angels' objection to Adam's creation, supports the existence of these prior generations and their known behaviors.
The distinction between biological evolution and the specific history of the human lineage endowed with God's spirit is clearly explained.
This analysis reconciles religious narratives with demographic calculations regarding the population growth from the first pair to the current eight billion people.
