This text is the audio transcript of the radio play "The Desert Shipwright," based on the story of Prophet Noah (PBUH), broadcast on the Bagh-e Tamasha program. In the play, Noah has built a massive ark in the desert, while his wife, Helwan, worries about his hereditary madness and erratic behavior. Despite her emotional attachment, Helwan refuses to accompany Noah on his journey, accusing him of idolatry and being tied to the temple. Noah warns his people of divine punishment and a coming storm due to their disbelief and ingratitude. He believes they are incapable of accepting the truth and that their fate is destruction. At the end of the play, Noah orders the animals to be brought into the ark in preparation for the flood. Following the play, the text continues with a Ziyarat (prayer) and elegy for Imam Mahdi (AJ), accompanied by poetic verses. Finally, a short speech is delivered regarding the importance of serving on the front lines and self-building in contrast to worldly pursuits.
