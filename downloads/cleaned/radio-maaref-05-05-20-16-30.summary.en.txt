In this program, Professor Reza Mohammadi Shahroudi addresses various theological and social questions from listeners. He refutes the notion that the return of the Imam and participation in his movement are exclusive to men or limited to warfare, emphasizing that women are present among his companions and commanders. The professor highlights that women's active roles in protests and support have historically stabilized and strengthened these movements. Citing Quranic verses, he asserts that human merit is determined by piety, faith, and righteous deeds rather than gender, as physical differences do not affect one's spiritual worth. The concept of "similarity" and the impossibility of "recreating the non-existent" are explained using a football match analogy, proving that time and space cannot be perfectly replicated. Regarding God's names, he clarifies that goodness is intrinsic to existence, while negative phenomena like diseases are relative and serve beneficial purposes within the divine system. Finally, the program covers the prohibition of grave desecration, the adjusted religious duties for those with congenital disabilities, the immediate recording of proxy prayers in the afterlife, and the testimony of the body's members based on the soul's identity in the Hereafter.
