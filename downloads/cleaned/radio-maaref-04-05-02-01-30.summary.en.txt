The text begins by emphasizing the principle of Velayat-e Faqih and recalling the sacrifices made by the people during the Islamic Revolution and the Iran-Iraq War.
It highlights the Islamic system's victories over global powers, attributing these successes to divine support and the guidance of the infallible Imams.
Citing a hadith from Imam Sadiq (AS), the narrator distinguishes the patience of Shias from that of other Muslims by their "awareness of the ultimate reward" and "certainty of the outcome."
To illustrate this concept, a historical narrative is presented about a difficult childbirth in the caravan of the Ahl al-Bayt, assisted by Khola and Nafila.
In this story, Hassan ibn Ali (AS) performs the Adhan in the newborn's ear and names him, symbolizing the spiritual role of the Imams in saving lives.
This narrative serves as a symbol showing that Shias act with calm and hope in the most difficult conditions due to their deep awareness of the Ahl al-Bayt's spiritual status.
The final conclusion is that this level of patience and courage is rooted in a profound understanding of Velayat and faith in divine support.
The presence of the Ahl al-Bayt in critical moments not only saves lives but also consolidates the spirituality and religious identity of future generations.
