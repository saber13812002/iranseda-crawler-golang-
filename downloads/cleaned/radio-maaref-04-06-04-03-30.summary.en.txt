This radio program initially outlined the Islamic rulings regarding the Khums tax on surplus income and bank interest, stating that unused surplus from the current year becomes subject to Khums if it remains until the following year. In the jurisprudential section, it was clarified that in a revocable divorce, a husband kissing his wife constitutes a valid return to the marriage, even without explicit verbal intent. Subsequently, Hujjat al-Islam Razzani discussed the concept of spiritual stagnation, citing a narration from Imam Jafar al-Sadiq (AS) that emphasizes the necessity of continuous existential growth. He explained that remaining static in one's current state is considered a loss, while deteriorating morally is a sign of falling away from divine mercy. Therefore, individuals must constantly strive to acquire spiritual perfections and noble moral traits to avoid deficiency. The speaker concluded that for those who do not pursue spiritual elevation, death is preferable to life, as a life devoid of moral and spiritual progress lacks true value.
