This radio program explores ethical teachings from Ayatollah Mousavi regarding the concepts of asceticism and worldly attachment. The interpretation of Quranic verses reveals that those whose hearts are tied to the world fail to see its flaws, whereas ascetics perceive its ugliness and attain spiritual health and wisdom. The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) described the characteristics of ascetics as remembering graves, abandoning excessive adornment, and prioritizing eternal matters over transient ones. Subsequently, narrations from Imam Sadiq (AS) and Imam Baqir (AS) were examined to highlight the necessity of piety and righteousness among Shias. According to these narrations, a Shia living in a large city must possess greater piety than others to be considered a true follower of the Ahl al-Bayt. The discussion emphasized that piety is the primary key to achieving divine satisfaction and happiness in the afterlife. Finally, it was stressed that meeting God in a state of piety leads to relief and joy in His presence.
