Former karate head coach Seyed Shahraneh Mehri discussed the intense pressure and efforts to secure a gold medal for Iran at the Tokyo Olympics. He highlighted the significant loss caused by Bahman Eskari's one-year ban for using a therapeutic medication without proper disclosure, despite WADA eventually clearing him of intentional doping. Mehri recounted the dramatic final match of Sajjad Ghanbzadeh, who suffered a concussion from an illegal strike but was declared the winner after Japanese doctors confirmed his integrity and lack of malingering. This victory marked the first and only gold medal in the history of Iranian karate at the Olympic Games. The coach also expressed concern about karate's potential removal from future Olympics due to financial and political disputes between the World Federation and the IOC. Mehri emphasized his deep reliance on faith and the Ahlulbayt during critical moments, noting that this spiritual support was crucial for his team's success. Finally, he reflected on his humble lifestyle and dedication to the sport, stating that he has never pursued personal wealth or luxury despite his long career.
