In this program, Professor Mohammadi emphasized the concept of divine agency, stating that God is the only true effective force and that Shia supplications are the richest sources of theological knowledge. Addressing the incident of Al-Hura, he clarified that Imam Sajjad (AS) did not support this unauthorized uprising and actually provided refuge to the people of Medina. It was also noted that the non-acceptance of some prayers does not necessarily mean they are rejected, as there may be a divine wisdom behind it. In the Q&A section, the importance of maintaining etiquette in worship without excessive anxiety and the continuous duty of parents to guide their children were highlighted. A major warning was issued regarding the grave sin of misleading people through social media and inventing false religions for profit, which carries severe consequences in the afterlife. Finally, the distinction between superficial faith (Iman Mustawad) and rooted faith (Iman Mustaqir) was explained, emphasizing the need to solidify beliefs to prevent them from being stolen by Satan.
