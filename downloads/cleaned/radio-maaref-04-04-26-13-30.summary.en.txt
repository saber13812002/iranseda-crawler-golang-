In this program, Zeinab and Sadat Hosseini, the wife of martyr and defender of the shrine Sayed Ahmad Hosseini, recount the final moments of their three-year marriage and his courageous decision to deploy to Syria. Despite his youth and his family's concerns, Sayed Ahmad went to the battlefield with full awareness and a strong will to defend his ideals, writing a will beforehand that emphasized his mental and spiritual soundness and his gratitude for his wife's support. Zeinab describes the farewell day at the Shrine of Hazrat Masoumeh, where she asked for his intercession in the afterlife, and the profound grief and isolation she experienced during the first three days after his departure. Over time, she has gained a deeper understanding of the greatness of her husband's sacrifice and expresses satisfaction with her own patience and resilience in the face of loneliness and social pressure. The final segment of the program analyzes the danger of forgetting the revolution's foundations after victory, emphasizing that the Islamic Revolution's leadership, through divine wisdom, prevented emotions from turning into tyranny and preserved the revolution's original path.
