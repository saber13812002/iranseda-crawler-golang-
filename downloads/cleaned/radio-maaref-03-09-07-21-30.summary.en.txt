This program analyzes verse 171 of Surah An-Nisa, drawing on Ayatollah Javadi Amoli's insights to refute Christian theological claims.
The text emphasizes that Jesus (PBUH) is merely a word and spirit from God, and attributing parenthood to the Divine Essence is a grave theological error.
A fundamental distinction is made between "Rabi' al-Thalatha" (the fourth of three), which represents true monotheism, and "Thalatha al-Thalatha" (three of three), which is considered disbelief.
In the monotheistic model, God is the encompassing reality that does not fit into numerical counting, whereas in the trinitarian model, God is placed alongside other entities as a third element.
The Quran refutes the concept of incarnation by interpreting the preposition "from" in Surah Al-Jathiyah as emanation rather than partition or division.
Comparing the creation of Jesus to that of Adam demonstrates that being created without a father does not imply divinity, as God is absolutely free from having a spouse or offspring.
Various forms of the Trinity, including combinations of God, the Holy Spirit, Jesus, or Mary, are explicitly identified as false and blasphemous.
The discussion concludes that Islam is the singular divine religion, and any form of polytheism or partnership in God's essence constitutes a direct violation of pure monotheism.
