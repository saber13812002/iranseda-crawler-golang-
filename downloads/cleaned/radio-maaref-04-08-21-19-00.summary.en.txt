This radio program identifies seeking forgiveness and repentance as the primary causes for rainfall, increased sustenance, and divine mercy. True repentance goes beyond verbal recitation, requiring deep remorse, a firm decision to abandon sin, and the restitution of people's rights. The speaker advises combining personal supplication with intercession through the Ahl al-Bayt, particularly Lady Fatimah (SA) and the Imam of the Time (AJ), to secure divine intercession. The rain prayer should be performed with hope and certainty in God's mercy, avoiding despair while requesting beneficial and nourishing rain. In the final section, asceticism is defined as detachment from worldly desires rather than poverty, which plants wisdom in the heart and tongue. Ultimately, ascetics are described as those who leave the world with a sound heart and complete faith, heading toward paradise and the abode of peace.
