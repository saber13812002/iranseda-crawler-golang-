The radio program "Let's Be Kind" began by observing the Fatimiyyah mourning period and offering condolences for the martyrdom of Hazrat Zahra.
The first section focused on interpreting Quranic verses to distinguish God's "Lordship" for believers versus disbelievers.
It was emphasized that God is not a Lord in the sense of affection or grace for disbelievers, but acts with justice.
The concept of "Fawq al-Ilah bi al-Harakat" was explained as a sign of the speed and immediacy of divine actions.
The second section reviewed historical events of the second of Azar, including the Constitutional Revolution pact and the shooting in Mashhad.
It also commemorated the passing of the renowned reciter Soleiman Mozanzadeh on this date in previous years.
The main theme of the program was inviting listeners to share the last prayer they had made for others.
Listener messages indicated prayers for the healing of the sick and the reappearance of the Imam of the Time.
