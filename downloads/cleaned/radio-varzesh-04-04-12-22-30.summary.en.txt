Former footballer Ardalan views his decision to stay with Eghtesad Novin (referred to as Eghtesad or Akbatan in the transcript) over a move to Persepolis as a culturally honorable choice, despite the financial loss. He remained with the same club for nearly fifteen years, rejecting numerous offers, while also representing Iran in youth, junior, and national teams. His career spanned the post-revolution era, a period marked by significant structural changes and challenges for footballers of his generation. The team, supported by private investors and managed by dedicated figures like Nicheh and Nemati, achieved the Tehran championship and competed in the second division. Ardalan expresses deep gratitude to the club's financial backers and managers for their unwavering support during a time when state funding was limited. He recalls his time with the national team in Beijing, highlighting the strong camaraderie and talent of that squad. Ultimately, he reflects on his career with pride and health, expressing thanks for the opportunity to continue serving the football community.
