In this radio program, the birth of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) and Unity Week were celebrated, emphasizing that following the Prophet's example and strengthening spiritual bonds are key to overcoming global crises. Religious leaders from BRICS nations issued a statement highlighting the importance of preserving family values and promoting peace. The broadcast also covered art exhibitions and Quranic manuscript displays in Iran and the United States, while introducing the Moscow Cathedral Mosque as a significant Islamic architectural landmark in Europe. International news segments reported on the departure of a humanitarian flotilla for Gaza and a solidarity meeting in Islamabad supporting the Palestinian cause. Additionally, the program detailed Israel's military operations in Gaza City and Denmark's diplomatic shift regarding the recognition of Palestine. The broadcast concluded with criticism of the UK government's stance on Gaza and an invitation to listeners to participate in the special programming for the Prophet's birthday.
