In this radio program, the presenter discusses historical Islamic events, distinguishing between Ghazwa and Sariya and highlighting the role of the Prophet's deputies in battles.
The central argument refutes the common claim that the companions would not have disputed the succession if the Prophet had explicitly announced Ali's (AS) caliphate at Ghadir.
By citing numerous historical and Quranic examples, the speaker demonstrates that disagreements persisted even when the Prophet explicitly clarified matters such as wudu, adhan, prayer, and the execution of divine laws.
To support this point, the story of the Israelites worshipping the calf during Moses' (AS) brief absence and the companions' resistance during the Treaty of Hudaybiyya are referenced.
The discussion also touches upon Fakhr Razi's interpretation of the Basmala, where he advocates for reciting it aloud in prayer by following the religious practice of Ali (AS).
Towards the end, the book "Sijil Qibla Kaaba" by Hamed Kashani is recommended as a suitable gift for the days of Ghadir, followed by a recitation from Surah Maryam.
This analysis suggests that accepting the Wilayah and succession requires faith and adherence to the Ahl al-Bayt (AS), going beyond merely hearing a public announcement.
