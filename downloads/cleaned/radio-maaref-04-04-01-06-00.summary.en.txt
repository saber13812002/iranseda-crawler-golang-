The Iranian Atomic Energy Organization affirmed its commitment to continuing nuclear development despite enemy threats, stating that it has initiated legal proceedings and expects global support. The broadcast emphasized the Iranian people's steel-willed resistance against 46 years of sanctions and aggression from the US and Israel, rejecting any form of submission. Patriotic songs were played, and listeners were invited to suggest names for a punitive operation against the United States. The program then reported on strong reactions from US Congress members regarding a recent military strike on Iran. Representatives Thomas Massie and Sara Jacobs condemned the action as unconstitutional and a dangerous escalation of regional tensions. Yasmin Ansari described Trump's attack as illegal and unauthorized by Congress. These lawmakers warned that such moves risk dragging the US into another endless and deadly war.
