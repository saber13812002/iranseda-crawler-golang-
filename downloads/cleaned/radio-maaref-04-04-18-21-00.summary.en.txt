The program began by inviting students to participate in the "Novgolane Hosseini" audio contest and sharing their recitations. In the main segment, Hujjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the spiritual necessity of weeping and seeking intercession through the Ahl al-Bayt, particularly Imam Hussein (PBUH). He cited narrations from Imam Sadiq (PBUH) and shared anecdotes from scholars like Ayatollah Ahmadi Mianji and Ayatollah Haeri Shirazi to illustrate how grief and devotion purify the soul. The broadcast then featured a narrative from the book "Neshane-ye Hassan," depicting the Hajj journey of Imam Hassan's (PBUH) children. The story detailed the emotional bond between Qasim and Hassan, their political concerns regarding Medina, and Imam Hussein's (PBUH) instructions to his brothers. The narrative concluded with the warm hospitality extended to Imam Hussein's (PBUH) caravan by Abdullah ibn Abbas in Mecca. The episode ended with an introduction to the next program and an acknowledgment of the upcoming guest, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli.
