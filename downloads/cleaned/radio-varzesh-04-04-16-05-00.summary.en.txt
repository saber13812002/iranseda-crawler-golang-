In this interview with Ismail Sarparast, a veteran of the Zourkhaneh sport, the current status of Zourkhaneh halls in Tehran was discussed.
The Tehran Urban Renewal Organization reduced the number of halls from 60 to about 25 by demolishing and renovating 30 of them.
The guest emphasized the severe shortage of halls relative to Tehran's population and the need for greater financial and administrative support.
He also addressed the challenges facing the International Zourkhaneh Federation, whose activities have stalled due to internal conflicts.
Sarparast believes that internal issues must be resolved first so that the sport can be presented cohesively on the global stage.
He highlighted the vital role of masters in transmitting the sport's culture and ethics to the new generation through specialized training.
Finally, the guest thanked the Ministry of Sports for its support and described the Zourkhaneh as a place for spiritual purification and moral education.
