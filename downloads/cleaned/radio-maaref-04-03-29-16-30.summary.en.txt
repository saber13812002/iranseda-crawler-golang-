This radio program discussed Iran's missile strikes on Israel and the death of Qasem Soleimani, emphasizing national unity and a jihadist spirit. The guest, Professor Abutalavi, defined the people's wartime duties as including economic jihad, boosting public morale, and mutual support. He stressed the importance of addressing public doubts in the cognitive and media war to maintain societal psychological stability. The benefits of this conflict were identified as fostering national unity across different groups and solidifying a defensive strategy beyond borders. The show concluded with news reports on officials condemning the aggression and the funeral of martyrs. Finally, the broadcast ended with patriotic songs and Quran recitation, highlighting the Iranian identity and the nation's steadfast faith.
