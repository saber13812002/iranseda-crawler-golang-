The program begins by emphasizing the historical and identity-based significance of the American Embassy takeover on Aban 13, framing it as a day that revealed the true nature of the Islamic Revolution. Following the production credits, the narrative of the series "Pahlavan Paytakht" unfolds, focusing on the young wrestler Seyed Ali. After his father's passing, Ali is encouraged to pursue a marriage proposal involving the daughter of a wrestler from Shiraz. With the support of his family and the Zourkhaneh master, Ali prepares to travel for the proposal ceremony and a subsequent wrestling match against a rival from Khorasan. The storyline builds tension through the mother's concerns and rumors regarding the opponent's tactics, setting the stage for the upcoming competition.
