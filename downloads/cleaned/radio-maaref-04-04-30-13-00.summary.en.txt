This radio program features a lecture by Hojjat al-Islam Mirbagheri on the transformative role of Imam Sajjad (PBUH) in the Islamic community following the tragedy of Karbala. The speaker identifies two fundamental interventions by the Imam: the revival of spirituality through the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya and the reactivation of the emotional bond with the Ahl al-Bayt through public mourning. The Sahifa is described not merely as a book of prayers, but as a vessel of spiritual truths descending from the Imam's heart to reveal humanity's true needs before God. Conversely, the Imam's thirty-five years of public weeping symbolize the creation of a "valley of grief" that paves the path for believers to connect with Karbala. This mourning is presented as a tool for purifying the soul, achieving spiritual growth, and reaching higher stations of divine proximity. The speaker emphasizes that in the modern era, facing materialistic distractions makes this spiritual grief and attachment to Imam Hussein (PBUH) more essential than ever. Ultimately, Imam Sajjad opened the doors of hearts toward Karbala, effectively turning the world into a Hussainiyya and providing a model for spiritual progression toward God.
