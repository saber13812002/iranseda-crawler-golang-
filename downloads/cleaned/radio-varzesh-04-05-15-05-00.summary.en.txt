Ali Eshkani, a former Greco-Roman wrestling champion, shared his memories of the 2000 Sydney Olympics in a radio interview. He discussed his semifinal loss to Kure and his fifth-place finish, criticizing the lack of support for Greco-Roman wrestling during that era. Eshkani highlighted his 2005 World Championship silver medal as a pivotal moment for proving his worth and fulfilling his captaincy duties. He also detailed his coaching tenure with the national team, noting the success of the young squad in 2015 and 2017. Financial challenges faced by the federation and the Ministry of Sport in 2018 and 2019 were also addressed. Eshkani concluded by emphasizing the importance of athletes' mental health and expressing optimism for the future of Iranian Greco-Roman wrestling.
