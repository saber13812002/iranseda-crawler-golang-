The first segment recounts a historical narrative where the brothers of Imam Hassan (AS) discuss the death of Muawiyah, with Hassan emphasizing that they should not rejoice over an enemy's death and urging restraint based on Imam Hussein's wisdom. The second part highlights the religious obligation to respect scholars, citing hadiths and anecdotes about the profound reverence shown by Shia leaders like Ayatollah Esfahani and Imam Khomeini toward their teachers. The final section presents the story of Mahmoud, a Basij soldier, who speaks with his mother before heading to the battlefield, asking her to celebrate rather than mourn if he is martyred. On the train to the front, Mahmoud asks his friend Hossein to deliver the news of his potential martyrdom to his mother and performs a divination using prayer beads to seek guidance for his fate.
