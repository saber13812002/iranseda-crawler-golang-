The program began by emphasizing the spiritual significance of visiting the Imams, citing the daily devotion of scholars like Allameh Amini and Ayatollah Behjat. It then transitioned to the "Story of Freedom" segment, featuring Seyed Kamil Manavi, a veteran who endured eight years of captivity in Iraq. Manavi recounted the severe torture and harsh conditions he faced in prisons in Kirkuk, Baghdad, and camps in Ramadi and Mosul. He described his steadfast resistance against interrogators and the brutal beatings inflicted by Iraqi officers. The broadcast also highlighted the resilience of the captives' wives, who raised their children alone during their husbands' absence. Additionally, the program introduced the book "Jashn-e Hanabandan," which documents the events of the Karbala 5 and Beit al-Moqaddas 4 operations. The episode concluded with a call to maintain spiritual connections with the holy shrines and to honor the sacrifices of the freed captives and their families.
