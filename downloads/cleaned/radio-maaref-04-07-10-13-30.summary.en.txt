The radio program "Narrative of Love" is dedicated to the mothers and wives of martyrs, featuring the memories of the mother of martyr Hassan Bagheri.
She describes her son's pious, action-oriented character and his efforts to organize community service and resistance.
The mother recounts the final moments of her son's life during the Fajr Operation and his martyrdom caused by a shell fragment.
She shares her experience of receiving the news of his death and the extraordinary patience granted to her by God in those moments.
In her closing remarks, she urges the youth to value their youth, adhere to their faith, and avoid misleading distractions.
The program concludes by highlighting the success of Iran's astronomy team in the international olympiad and the need to motivate experts.
Officials emphasize the importance of better engagement with young people and providing educational and employment opportunities to prevent the brain drain of experts.
