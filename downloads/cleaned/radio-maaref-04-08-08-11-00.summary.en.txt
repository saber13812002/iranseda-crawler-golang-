The Supreme Council of the Cultural Revolution officially added National Aerospace Day and the Day of Sangan Island in the Persian Gulf to the national calendar. Concurrently, benefactors participating in a school construction campaign have allocated 1,500 billion tomans for this purpose. The "Zamzam Ahkam" program on Radio Ma'aref addressed various religious questions based on the rulings of clerical authorities. It was clarified that Equity Shares are subject to Khums if they are acquired and generate profit. Giving money with the condition that it be used for mourning ceremonies after the donor's death is considered valid if the recipient accepts the condition. The concept of "Red Muzalim," referring to people's rights where the owners are unknown, was explained as requiring distribution to the poor with the permission of the religious authority. The use of kohl and moisturizing cream does not invalidate wudu unless it prevents water from reaching the skin, a determination based on common understanding. Finally, the program discussed the jurisprudential rulings regarding finding a decorative bird and using medications containing haram ingredients for kidney stone treatment.
