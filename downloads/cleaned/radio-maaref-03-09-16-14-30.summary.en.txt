In this radio program, the "Iran Empathy" campaign to support the oppressed people of Lebanon and Palestine was introduced alongside theological discussions regarding the occultation and reappearance of Imam Mahdi. Experts emphasized that the Imam continues to assist humanity through spiritual blessings and that his absence does not imply indifference. The reappearance is intended not merely to solve material problems but to establish divine justice, which requires the moral and spiritual readiness of society. According to narrations, on the morning of his return, the Imam will move to Mecca with his companions and deliver a speech in the Grand Mosque, calling for allegiance. The angel Gabriel will descend from the sky to pledge allegiance first, followed by a celestial call that awakens and astonishes the entire world. This event, while initially causing shock, is accompanied by divine mercy and guidance. The program concluded by analyzing Imam Khomeini's management strategies after the Islamic Revolution and the challenges faced by the provisional government in consolidating the new system.
