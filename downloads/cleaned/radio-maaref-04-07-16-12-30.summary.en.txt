In a speech at the shrine of Imam Reza (PBUH), Hojjat al-Islam Mohammad Javad Nazafat emphasized that maintaining unity among Muslims is the most critical expectation during the period of occultation. He identified Satan as the primary agent of division, warning that true enemies like Israel and the US exploit internal conflicts to distract Muslims from their common adversary. The speaker outlined the characteristics of those who break unity, including an excessive focus on history rather than current threats, insulting other sects' sanctities, and misinterpreting the concept of "baraa" (disassociation). Imam Sadiq (PBUH) clarified that one has no right to disassociate from anyone who loves the Ahl al-Bayt, regardless of theological differences, as disassociation is only permissible from explicit enemies of God. The Quranic example of Prophet Abraham (PBUH) illustrates that one should initially approach others with kindness and invitation, only declaring disassociation after enmity becomes evident. Unity does not mean abandoning one's beliefs but rather avoiding actions that cause division or harm the reputation of fellow Muslims, thereby preparing the ground for the reappearance of Imam Mahdi (ATFJ). Finally, the speaker urged the audience to pray for sinners and the misguided, and to refrain from divisive behaviors at all social levels.
