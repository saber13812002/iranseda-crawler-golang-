The radio program first analyzed the true motivation behind the Rashidun Caliphs' ban on narrating and writing down Hadith, arguing that it was politically driven to suppress narrations supporting Ali's succession. The discussion then highlighted Salman al-Farsi's crucial role in the conquest of Iran, particularly in encouraging Muslims to cross the Tigris River and face the Sassanid army. In the second segment, a Hadith from Imam Sadiq (AS) regarding moderation and dealing with wicked people was interpreted, emphasizing the importance of maintaining a good appearance while avoiding direct confrontation. The Imam predicted that in an era of tyranny, only those who exercise patience and wisdom, while refusing to imitate corrupt practices, would be saved. Finally, the program addressed the burial of the martyrs of the Battle of Uhud in Medina and recommended historical books detailing Salman's life and contributions.
