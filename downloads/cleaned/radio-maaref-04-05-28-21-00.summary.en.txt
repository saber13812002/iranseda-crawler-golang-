This radio program began by highlighting the tranquility and reliance on God exhibited by Ayatollah Sadr during his youth while facing material and political hardships. It then featured a narrative from the book "Said Man Hosseini" about the extraordinary hospitality and generosity of Iraqi pilgrims during the Arbaeen walk, despite their own poverty. A segment addressed a jurisprudential question, clarifying that steam cleaning is insufficient for removing impurities and that water washing is required according to religious rulings. The broadcast also provided practical tips for reducing water consumption at home, such as using low-flow faucets and washing vegetables in a basin. Interspersed messages emphasized support for the people of Gaza and the importance of protecting Iran's water resources from external threats. The program concluded with announcements for upcoming broadcasts on Radio Ma'aref, including a tafsir of Surah Muddaththir.
