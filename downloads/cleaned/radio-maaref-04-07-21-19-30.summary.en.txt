The program began with the recitation of the rain prayer before transitioning to a discussion on the ethics of criticism. The instructor explained that self-criticism is a moral virtue, whereas criticizing others beyond appropriate limits is a forbidden vice. Key factors contributing to this vice include ignorance, hypocrisy, poor religious practice, gossip, arrogance, envy, and habitual behavior. To overcome this, one must first recognize their own flaws before judging others. Appropriate and ethical criticism must be conducted with gentleness, patience, love, and strict adherence to religious boundaries. The primary goal of such criticism should be education and correction, not humiliation or displaying one's own piety. Supporting individuals who are conveying the truth, even in difficult circumstances, is a crucial religious principle. Ultimately, abandoning criticism rooted in vice and practicing it with proper etiquette is essential for leading a morally upright life.
