This radio program provided religious rulings in response to several listener questions. According to Ayatollah Sistani's fatwa, a Friday prayer performed under valid Sharia conditions suffices for the Dhuhr prayer, eliminating the need to perform Dhuhr separately. Regarding wudu, if the toilet and the wudu area are in the same location, the ruling of sufficiency applies; however, if they are separate, it does not. In response to a question about a nine-year-old girl who has reached puberty and is taking medication, Ayatollah Sistani's view was cited, stating that making up missed prayers is not obligatory for her. In another segment, Hujjat al-Islam Badini explained that in ghusl, the order of washing the head and neck, followed by the right side and then the left side of the body, is mandatory. He clarified that stepping away from the shower to ensure each side is washed is merely a recommended practice (mustahab) and not a religious obligation. The program concluded by providing contact numbers for submitting further religious inquiries. Finally, religious songs and salutations were played to close the broadcast.
