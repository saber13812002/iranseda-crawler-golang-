In this radio program, Dr. Hassan Akbari discusses "psychosomatic conditions" and their profound impact on physical health. Citing Avicenna and Zakariya Razi, he emphasizes that providing patients with peace and reassurance is a physician's primary duty. Dr. Akbari introduces the concept of "postmodernism in medicine," which combines modern medical advancements with the revival of forgotten human traditions to alleviate social stress. He warns that industrialized, apartment-based living can weaken children's immune systems through anxiety and depression, making them susceptible to diseases like Type 1 diabetes. In the second segment, Hujjat al-Islam Rasoul uses the story of Prophet Joseph and Quranic verses to argue that lies inevitably unravel due to the interconnected nature of the universe. The program concludes by stressing the importance of telling the truth to prevent further harm and to maintain spiritual and social well-being.
