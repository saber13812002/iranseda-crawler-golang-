The program began by explaining the rituals of Umrah Hajj, including entering the state of Ihram, reciting the Talbiyah, and standing at Arafat, emphasizing spiritual presence during the expected reappearance of the Imam. Subsequently, various religious questions from listeners were addressed, covering topics such as the permissibility of eating until full, preventing food waste, and the ownership of gifts given to children. The ruling on the mahram status of an aunt to her brother's daughter and the validity of prayer in a personal villa considered a contractual homeland were also discussed. Additionally, the financial obligations regarding the divorce of mentally disabled couples were clarified. In the concluding segment, a hadith from the Eighth Imam was cited to define prayer as an acknowledgment of God's lordship and a plea for forgiveness. Listeners were advised to forgive others before praying so that God may forgive them. The importance of prostration and humility before God was highlighted. The broadcast ended with the recitation of verses from Surah Al-Buruj and the Du'a of Arafat.
