In this program, the expert emphasizes the importance of making conscious partner choices with the help of counseling and avoiding unhealthy perfectionism. He also warns that parents speaking negatively about each other in front of their children destroys the children's personality and damages the parent-child relationship. To prevent this harm, couples should resolve their conflicts through healthy communication or professional counseling, keeping children away from their emotional disputes. In the second part, transparency, good manners, and understanding each other's needs are presented as essential for preventing married life from becoming repetitive. The expert believes that true happiness lies in normalizing life and enjoying simple moments, rather than in artificial complexities. He stresses that love takes different forms at various stages of life, and one should not expect the initial feelings to remain identical. Finally, the "divine family" is introduced as the foundation of a healthy society, where members regard each other as a trust and ensure peace and growth through mutual care.
