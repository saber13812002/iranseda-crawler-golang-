In this radio program, the expert emphasized the importance of making conscious partner choices with professional counseling and avoiding unhealthy perfectionism. He warned that parents badmouthing each other in front of children destroys their personality and damages the parent-child relationship. Couples were advised to resolve their conflicts through healthy communication with their children and by seeking counseling to keep kids out of marital disputes. To prevent marriage from becoming monotonous, transparency, good manners, and understanding each other's emotional needs are as essential as water is to humans. True happiness lies in normalizing life and avoiding unnecessary harshness or overthinking. A divine family is one where members act as "water" for each other, providing mutual care, peace, and growth. Ultimately, building a healthy family was described as the fundamental step toward creating a dynamic society that embraces divine values.
