In this program, Professor Maleki emphasizes the absolute clarity and precision of God's creation, citing Allameh Tabatabaei's interpretation that the Quran itself is a clear manifestation.
He then addresses a question regarding a verse from Surah Tawbah, explaining that God guides the hearts of believers by manifesting virtues such as patience, steadfastness, and gratitude.
The professor distinguishes between temporary and established faith, urging listeners to seek a stable belief that yields tangible results in their lives.
He stresses the importance of returning to the practical lifestyle of the Prophet and the Ahl al-Bayt to rectify modern societal behaviors.
Despite limited historical records of some Imams, he asserts that the remaining narrations provide sufficient guidance for ethical and jurisprudential matters.
The program concludes with the recitation of the Ziyarat of Al-Yasin, followed by Imam Khomeini's statements on the broken promises of global powers.
Imam Khomeini's segment highlights the need for the oppressed to unite against the corrupt influence of major powers and international organizations.
