The text emphasizes the necessity of supporting jurists and religious scholars against oppressors while criticizing societal indifference toward the oppressed.
By citing historical precedents and narrations, it argues that abandoning the defeated and failing to support justice constitutes a form of enmity and divine abandonment.
It is stressed that replacing qualified jurists with ignorant individuals leads to the distortion of religion and the emergence of extremist groups like ISIS.
Indifference in religious and social matters is considered a sin, even if one lacks financial means, and must be compensated by sincere spiritual commitment.
Accepting the authority of Satan and falling for media misguidance weakens faith and creates confusion within the community.
Finally, referencing the events of Karbala and the behavior of the people of Kufa, the speaker prays for forgiveness and an increased devotion to the Quran and the Ahl al-Bayt.
