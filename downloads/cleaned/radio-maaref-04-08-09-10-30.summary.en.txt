In the radio program "Shifa," based on Dr. Pourhosseini's insights, the importance of managing sleep according to traditional and Islamic medicine was emphasized. According to these teachings, sleep primarily belongs to the night, and short naps during the day are recommended only to relieve fatigue, not as a seasonal habit. In autumn, due to the earlier sunset, it is advised that people go to bed earlier to properly meet the body's need for rest. Additionally, based on individual temperaments, those with a dry constitution must manage their sleep more precisely to prevent further dryness. The second part of the program featured the recitation performance "A Drop from the Sea," which interpreted verse 223 of Surah Al-Baqarah. This section used everyday life examples to demonstrate that sometimes not achieving one's desires or enduring hardships is ultimately for one's own good. The main message of this segment was patience and reliance on God in accepting divine decrees, as God knows better than humans what is truly beneficial or harmful.
