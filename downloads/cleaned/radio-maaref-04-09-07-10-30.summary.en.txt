The radio program "Shifa" explores Islamic traditional medicine, offering advice on correcting sleep habits and maintaining health during the autumn season. It emphasizes the importance of gradually changing unhealthy habits to achieve a secondary nature without applying excessive pressure. The segment also discusses the liver's regenerative capabilities and the differing perspectives between traditional and modern medicine regarding chronic diseases. Following this, the broadcast highlights the national slogan of "investment for production," urging both the public and the government to collaborate in revitalizing the economy. The "A Drop from the Sea" segment interprets the Quranic verse "Do not follow the footsteps of Satan" from Surah Al-Baqarah, positioning the Quran as a life navigator. This interpretation stresses the necessity of avoiding Satan's path and promptly returning to the divine route after making mistakes. Finally, the program discusses the Quran's role as a comprehensive source of knowledge and the key to understanding the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH).
