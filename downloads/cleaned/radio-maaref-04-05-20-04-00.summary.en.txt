The "Zamzam Ahkam" program began by addressing the religious rulings for Arbaeen pilgrims, emphasizing that public areas are considered pure unless there is certainty of impurity and that wudu can be performed over bandaged wounds.
It was stated that reciting the Arbaeen ziyara on the designated day and as close to Imam Hussein's shrine as possible is a highly recommended act.
Ayatollah Sistani's ruling was discussed, clarifying that khums is not due on Umrah tickets used in the same year, but is required if the pilgrimage is postponed to future years.
The program also covered rulings on female reciters using loudspeakers, the khums obligation on monthly salaries, and the permissibility of bathing during Ramadan.
Regarding marital obedience, scholars affirmed that a wife must comply even if her husband fails to provide maintenance, unless she has a valid religious excuse.
The latter part of the broadcast focused on critiquing modern theology, specifically analyzing eternalist views in cosmology and their challenges to the concept of God.
Professor Shakarin examined the criteria for the need for a cause and the concept of novelty to address the theoretical flaws in eternalist theories and their relationship with divine existence.
