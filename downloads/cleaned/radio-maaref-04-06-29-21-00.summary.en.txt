The program begins by emphasizing the virtue of praying for others, citing narrations from Imam Hasan Askari and Ayatollah Khoei regarding its positive impact on spiritual growth and the acceptance of prayers.
Practical examples of scholars' noble character are shared, including Ayatollah Khoei's prayers for Iran during the war and Ayatollah Sheikh Mohammad Hadi's gratitude for a fulfilled prayer for a child.
A story about Hajj Molaft Ali Soltan Abadi is also told, illustrating how night prayers for the deceased, even strangers, can save them from punishment.
The second part of the program features a narrative from the book "Ikhwan al-Mashreq," where the character "Amir" travels to Khorasan with "Hajj Karim" and his daughter "Rahleh."
In this story, a man named Ahmad invites Hajj Karim to engage in a theological debate with a Sunni soldier who is seeking the truth.
The host of the debate, while respecting Imam Reza's character, does not believe in his Imamate and asks Hajj Karim to answer his brief questions.
Hajj Karim, identifying himself as a follower of the opposing school, agrees to the discussion, and the host promises to gift him a staff if he answers correctly.
The program concludes with a call for viewers to submit documents related to the Sacred Defense and a response to a legal question regarding full rent deposits for housing.
