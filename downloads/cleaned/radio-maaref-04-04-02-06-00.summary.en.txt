In this program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verses 114 to 116 of the Holy Quran's Surah Al-Ma'idah. He explains that divine sustenance encompasses both material and spiritual blessings, and the miracle of the descending food strengthened the faith of the disciples. The discussion highlights the degrees of monotheism, noting that the highest level is for a human to become a manifestation of divine attributes. The text also analyzes the verse regarding unprecedented punishment for those who deny miracles after witnessing them. Furthermore, God's question to Jesus (PBUH) about the deification of himself and his mother by some Christians is examined. The phrase "other than Allah" does not imply the denial of God, as His existence is undeniable to all, including materialists. Human disagreements concern God's attributes rather than the essence of the Necessary Being.
