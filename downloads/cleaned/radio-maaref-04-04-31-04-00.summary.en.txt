Ayatollah Mazaheri emphasized the importance of the "Mabasat" law and respecting the dignity of others, citing Quranic verses and hadiths. He defined insulting any individual, regardless of their social status, as a war against God and the Prophet, referencing Surah Abasa and a narration from Imam Sadiq (AS). The speaker warned that disrespecting elders, parents, or religious leaders leads to personal downfall and a miserable life, urging listeners to avoid political and social insults. He highlighted that neglecting people's feelings can result in one's own destruction, using anecdotes to illustrate the severe consequences of such behavior. In the second segment, Hojjat al-Islam Hosseini Qomi interpreted a narration from the Book of Kafi to define the essence of faith. He identified three core components of true belief: acceptance of God's decree, complete reliance on Him, and absolute submission to His commands. The program concluded by stressing the vital role of ethical conduct and deep theological understanding in daily life.
