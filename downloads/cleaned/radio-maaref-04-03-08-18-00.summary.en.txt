In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Falah introduces humility as an innate and natural virtue of humans, supported by Quranic verses and narrations. He describes humility as a perspective that arrogant people are naturally drawn to, despite their outward pretensions of superiority. Citing a narration about the land of Hijaz being chosen by God due to its humility, the program emphasizes that God pays special attention to humble individuals. Prostration in prayer is highlighted as the pinnacle of humility and the key to connecting with God, while stressing the importance of avoiding arrogance in the face of divine verses. In the second part, Hojjat al-Islam Irandoust interprets a wisdom from Saadi's Gulistan, which advises against showing mercy to oppressors and tyrants. Relying on religious and literary teachings, he outlines four scenarios where forgiveness and kindness toward oppressors are not permissible. These include the battlefield, those who harm national rights, violations of social rights, and cases where forgiveness emboldens the oppressor. Finally, humility is presented as the primary path to gaining God's pleasure and achieving spirituality.
