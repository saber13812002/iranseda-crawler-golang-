This radio program provided religious rulings on three key questions. First, fraud in transactions, such as selling fake gold, is deemed haram, and the customer has the right to annul the deal. Second, regarding the khums on loans, unused loans in the current year are not subject to khums, but unpaid installments of current-year loans can be deducted from the capital. Third, using human or animal hair transplants for medical treatment is considered permissible. The program concluded by announcing contact channels for further inquiries and ended with a collective prayer and recitation.
