In this radio broadcast, following the recitation of the fourteenth prayer from the Sahifa al-Stajadiyya, Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallah discusses the phenomenon of mocking others in the "Ganj-e Sa'adat" program. He describes mockery as a global affliction rooted in ignorance, heedlessness, and arrogance. Citing Quranic verses and narrations, Fallah identifies sixteen primary causes of this behavior, including fault-finding, pride in worldly appearances, wealth, hypocrisy, envy, and forgetting death. The program also highlights the destructive effects of mockery on the victim's self-confidence and the severe worldly and otherworldly consequences for the mocker. A key warning is issued regarding the loss of sincere friendship from people and, more critically, the loss of God's love due to such actions. Finally, listeners are urged to avoid this sin and practice empathy to remain safe from divine punishment.
