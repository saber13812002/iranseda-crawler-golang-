In the health segment, Dr. Sarbaz Hosseini emphasized that, according to Islamic traditional medicine, sleep primarily belongs to the night, with only short naps recommended during the day. He stressed the importance of managing sleep schedules for individuals with dry or moist constitutions and warned against staying up late for work, as it damages the body's balance. The doctor advised performing precise tasks during the midday or just before dawn to maximize energy efficiency. In the Quranic section, Hujjat al-Islam Rasoul interpreted a verse from Surah An-Nahl, highlighting the necessity of repentance and making amends for ignorant mistakes. He expressed hope that God accepts the repentance of those who seek forgiveness and trust in Him. The segment concluded with a message from Imam Khomeini urging national economic, industrial, and agricultural independence to eliminate foreign dependency. Finally, the program announced the start of the new pre-registration phase for the 1405 Umrah Hajj.
