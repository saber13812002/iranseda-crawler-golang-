In this discussion, Dr. Mohammad Morandi analyzes global shifts, emphasizing Iran's increased power and credibility on the international stage following the recent conflict. He argues that Western media and influential American figures have acknowledged Israel's strategic defeat and Iran's victory. Given the high costs of US involvement in Ukraine and tensions with China, Iran has transitioned from the "axis of evil" to a key player in groups like BRICS and the Shanghai Cooperation Organization. Regarding the snapback mechanism, he views it as ineffective and primarily a psychological pressure on the public with no significant economic impact. Morandi also stresses the importance of maintaining strong ties with Russia and China as strategic alternatives to reduce dependence on Europe and the United States. Finally, he urges domestic officials to avoid irresponsible statements that weaken Iran's position and to focus on self-sufficiency and transparent management.
