The radio program begins by commemorating the martyrdom of Hazrat Fatima Zahra (SA), recounting the historical siege of her home.
It cites historical sources to describe the fire at the door and the severely weakened physical condition of Hazrat Fatima in her final days.
The text emphasizes that her martyrdom was kept secret to prevent chaos and fitna in the early Islamic community.
The second segment, titled "Tales of Freedom," shifts focus to the memoirs of war prisoners, specifically narrating the story of Asadollah Nakhaei.
Nakhaei describes the harsh conditions on the front lines, extreme thirst, enemy tank attacks, and his eventual capture in the Rumadiyeh region.
He details the horrific torture in prison and the nightly fears of the captives, identifying the recitation of Dua-e-Tawassul as their spiritual coping mechanism.
By combining the mourning of Hazrat Fatima with the remembrance of the freed prisoners, the program underscores the steadfastness of faith and endurance in the path of truth.
