Reza Kangarani, a former Iranian footballer, discussed his career decisions with Radio Varzesh, acknowledging that staying in military teams for financial reasons cost him opportunities at larger clubs. Despite his age, he maintains good physical fitness and believes that lower-league football relies more on intelligence and technique than raw athleticism. Kangarani shared his unsuccessful attempts to move abroad, noting that advice from his friend Akbar Mohammadi discouraged him due to his age. After retiring, he transitioned into a role as a sports expert for the Tehran Municipality in District 12. He criticized the modern game for losing its technical appeal and becoming overly result-oriented. Kangarani also expressed the difficulties of coaching youth, citing the lack of time and energy required to manage players' diverse needs. Finally, he revealed his current goal of competing in physical fitness and bodybuilding events for the over-50 age group.
