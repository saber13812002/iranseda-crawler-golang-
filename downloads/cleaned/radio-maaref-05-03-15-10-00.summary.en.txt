The radio program begins by citing authentic narrations, such as those in the book of Kafi and sayings of Imam Sadiq (PBUH), to establish that the two occultations of Imam Mahdi (PBUH) are rooted in religious texts rather than being merely scholarly interpretations. The discussion then shifts to the concept of making a covenant with the Imam, specifically focusing on the ranks of "Nasrat" (support) and "Awanah" (assistance). It is explained that supporting the Imam requires both intellectual and practical efforts; intellectually, one must understand religious foundations and recognize enemies, while practically, one should perform positive social actions like honesty in business and kindness to neighbors, which are considered forms of jihad during the waiting period. The speaker emphasizes that despite recent challenges and trials, believers must not lose hope, relying instead on the Quran and the Prayer of Covenant to maintain their faith in the imminent appearance of the Imam and his just government.
