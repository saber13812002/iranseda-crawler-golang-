This radio program explores suffering as an inherent part of existence and a governing law of the universe. The speaker argues, citing religious wisdom, that receiving new blessings often comes at the cost of losing others, urging listeners to avoid excessive attachment to the material world. Two primary strategies for managing suffering are presented: accepting the logic of pain as a natural consequence of human choices and external events, and relying on God as a compassionate companion during difficult times. It is emphasized that suffering can lead to spiritual expansion, increased wisdom, and stronger character, provided one does not let fear or resentment corrupt the soul. The speaker also introduces a mental exercise based on a narration from Imam Baqir (AS), where one imagines themselves in a coffin and asks God for a return to life to perform good deeds. Ultimately, the message encourages individuals to focus their efforts on creativity and the beauty of life rather than merely seeking wealth or avoiding risk. By trusting in God, one can transform suffering into an opportunity for spiritual growth and personal development.
