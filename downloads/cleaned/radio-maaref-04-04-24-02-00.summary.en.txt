In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Faqih emphasized the importance of caring for believers and meeting the needs of religious brothers as a condition for the acceptance of one's deeds, citing narrations from Imam Kazim and the Prophet. He illustrated this principle with stories of Ayatollah Tabrizi's compassionate service to the needy, highlighting a practical model of social responsibility. The guest also discussed the theological ethics of supplication, advising listeners to seek refuge in God through the intercession of the Prophet and his family rather than relying on worldly connections during times of hardship. Furthermore, he provided practical advice for managing workplace and family stress, suggesting that individuals avoid internalizing difficulties, reduce mutual expectations, and perform tasks with genuine love and pure intention. To resolve misunderstandings and suspicion, he recommended always interpreting others' actions in the best light and avoiding premature judgment. The discussion concluded with the importance of transparency in communication and avoiding ambiguity to prevent Satan from exploiting misinterpretations.
