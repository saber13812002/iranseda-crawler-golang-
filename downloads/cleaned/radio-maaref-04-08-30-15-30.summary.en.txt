In the radio program "Strategy," Dr. Mehdi Moshkibaf criticized past de-escalation policies, emphasizing the necessity of maintaining national power and avoiding intimidation by superpowers. Citing the leadership's views, he argued that Iran's tactical retreats failed to reduce tensions and instead provided opportunities for adversaries to inflict heavier blows. Moshkibaf highlighted the European breach of commitments in the Sarab negotiations and the activation of the snapback mechanism in the JCPOA, demonstrating that the West seeks to weaken Iran rather than reach a genuine agreement. He warned that the greatest error for a nation is to display passivity, which emboldens the enemy, and that true deterrence stems solely from strength and authority. Finally, he stressed the importance of continuing Iran's anti-hegemonic and dignity-based policies when confronting the global system of domination.
