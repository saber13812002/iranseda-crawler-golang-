This radio program focuses on managing societal consciousness and countering the enemy's psychological operations. The speaker cites the Quran to demonstrate that the primary accusations against prophets and believers have historically been "madness" and "foolishness." These insults stem from the opponent's inability to comprehend the rational depth of religious discourse and their impulsive emotional reactions. To counter this threat, the speaker emphasizes the necessity of teaching "emotional intelligence" and rationality to the younger generation. True rationality is achieved when an individual's emotions are directed toward grand, long-term concepts like the Day of Judgment rather than immediate, short-term gains. The martyrdom of leaders, such as Sayyid Hassan Nasrallah, exemplifies this profound union of intellect and emotion, which awakens the conscience and fosters intellectual growth within society. Ultimately, the goal of religion is to cultivate a rationality where emotions serve as a guide to understanding deeper truths.
