This radio program explored the complex secrecy surrounding the birth of Imam Mahdi during the era of Imam Hasan Askari and how Shias were informed, citing narrations from the book Kamil al-Din.
The guest defined the expectation of the Mahdi's return not as passive waiting, but as an active movement to establish global justice and support the promised Imam.
Several proofs, including definitive texts, rational arguments, the rich intellectual heritage of the Ahl al-Bayt, and historical evidence, were presented to demonstrate the Imams' unique and innate knowledge.
A specific historical example was cited where the young Imam Javad displayed profound jurisprudential knowledge in a debate with Yahya Naqib, proving the divine nature of his intellect.
Promoting Mahdavi culture through artistic content and social media was highlighted as a crucial method for supporting Imam Mahdi and preparing society for his return.
It was emphasized that Imam Mahdi is the leader of all humanity, and his appearance will mark the victory of the oppressed over the oppressors worldwide.
The program concluded with prayers for the early return of the Imam and hopes for the realization of divine justice and his global presence.
