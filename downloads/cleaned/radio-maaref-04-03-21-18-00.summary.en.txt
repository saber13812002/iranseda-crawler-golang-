The program begins with a religious discussion where Hujjat al-Islam Fala highlights the vice of arrogance based on Quranic verses and hadiths. He describes arrogance as the greatest sin and the devil's garment, which distances humans from servitude and humility, ultimately leading to damnation. The Prophet and Imam Ali (AS) emphasized avoiding pride, as it deprives individuals of divine mercy and spiritual elevation. In the second segment, economic expert Dr. Mohammad Bidar explains the impacts of public participation in production investment. He identifies increasing gross domestic product and controlling inflation as the two key economic benefits of this collaboration. The show also features a field report on entrepreneurs who have developed freeze-drying machines to preserve food quality and extend shelf life. These segments collectively demonstrate that improving life quality and solving societal problems require effort and awareness in both moral and economic spheres.
