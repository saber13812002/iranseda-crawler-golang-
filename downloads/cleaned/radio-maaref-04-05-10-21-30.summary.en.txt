In this lecture on Mahdism, the discussion begins by clarifying that a Jewish government will not exist during the era of the Mahdi's reappearance. The main focus then shifts to the reasons and wisdom behind the occultation of Imam Mahdi, citing Sayyid Murtada's arguments. It is established through rational deduction that the existence of an infallible Imam is necessary for guiding the obligated people. Consequently, the Imam's occultation must be based on divine wisdom and a specific necessity, even if the detailed reason is not fully known to us. This situation is compared to the ambiguous verses in the Quran, which possess valid interpretations despite their apparent ambiguity. Just as the wisdom behind certain religious rituals may be hidden, the specific reason for the occultation remains a matter of divine discretion. One cited reason for the occultation is the fear for the Imam's life, which necessitates enduring hardship to ensure the delivery of divine grace to humanity. These arguments are presented to refute the doubts of opponents and demonstrate the rational strength of the Mahdist perspective.
