This radio program provided jurisprudential answers to various religious questions from listeners. Ayatollah Javadi Amoli emphasized that prayer is invalid if performed in clothing whose ownership or the owner's heartfelt consent has not been verified. Hojjatolislam Mohammadi Sheikhabani clarified that a dog's breath alone does not render clothing impure, unless its saliva touches it or the fabric is wet. Regarding the khums on shrouds and graves, Hojjatolislam Elyan Najat, citing Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi, stated that khums is due if a full lunar year has passed and the items remain unused. The program also explained the conditions for expiating missed prayers by providing a specific amount of grain to the destitute, rather than just cash, and the order of reciting Adhan and Qada based on Ayatollah Sadr's fatwa. Ayatollah Khamenei permitted paying the return of usurped wealth to jihad groups only if their members are poor or if the funds are guaranteed to be distributed to the needy. The broadcast concluded with special prayers for Yalda night and Imam Reza (AS), highlighting the spiritual significance of prostration in prayer.
