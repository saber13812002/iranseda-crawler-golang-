In this radio program, the speaker emphasizes God's constant surveillance over human actions, urging listeners to maintain proper etiquette and accountability before divine judgment. He highlights the impact of the Supreme Leader's martyrdom on national awakening, arguing that Iran's steadfast resistance against enemies not only dispels fear but also accelerates the reappearance of Imam Mahdi. Drawing on the story of Imam Hussein, the speaker presents resistance and martyrdom as the highest spiritual states and key drivers of personal and social transformation. In the second part, he stresses the necessity of closing the scientific gap with the global frontier of knowledge to build a new Islamic civilization. The speaker believes that goal-oriented research and the cultivation of elites are essential for overcoming underdevelopment and achieving sustainable progress. Finally, he calls for unity, spiritual reliance, and firm standing in the path of elevating Islam and Iran, blending religious and scientific imperatives.
