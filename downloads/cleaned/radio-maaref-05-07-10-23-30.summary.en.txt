This program is an audio narration of the book "In the Ambush of the Red Rose," based on the life of General Ali Siyazi Shirazi. The story begins with an anti-revolutionary ambush in Sardasht, where Shirazi uses his intelligence to lead his forces to safety. He subsequently meets Dr. Chamran, forging a deep personal and professional bond between the two. After returning to Isfahan, Shirazi proposes a plan to secure the western borders and cut off the rebels' external connections. He presents this strategy to President Bani-Sadr, who dismisses it due to the ongoing crisis in Samandaj. Despite the rejection, Shirazi declares his readiness to go to Samandaj to help liberate the city. The narrative highlights Shirazi's bravery, faith, and pivotal role in establishing his partnership with Chamran. It also underscores his strategic contributions during the early post-revolutionary conflicts.
