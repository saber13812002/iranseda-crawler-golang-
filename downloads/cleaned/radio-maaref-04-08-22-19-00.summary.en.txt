The program refutes Satan's false fears regarding poverty and the future, emphasizing reliance on God and Tawhid as the solution to life's problems.
God's love and divine justice should bring peace and hope, rather than fear, while humans should fear sin and injustice, not God.
Islamic narrations advise believers to avoid expressing their needs to others as much as possible to preserve their dignity and honor.
Imam Sadiq (AS) stated that asking for help from people destroys one's dignity and modesty, noting that greed is itself a form of permanent poverty.
Imam Reza (AS) also emphasized the dignity of his followers by refusing to write a letter requesting help from a specific individual, instead offering his own assistance.
Therefore, trust in God and contentment are suitable alternatives to begging and dependence on others.
This approach allows individuals to manage their lives with self-respect and inner peace.
Ultimately, preserving honor and dignity in religion is a fundamental principle that the Prophets and Imams consistently upheld.
