In this radio program marking the birth of Imam Reza (PBUH), the focus was on the intuitive and indescribable nature of divine love compared to rational knowledge.
The central argument emphasized that purifying the heart from sins and lying is a prerequisite for accepting divine guidance and preventing Satan's influence.
The guest, Master Falah, enhanced the spiritual atmosphere by reciting love poetry and sharing his personal experiences of visiting Imam Reza's shrine.
He also discussed his cultural and poetic activities in literary and religious circles, highlighting the role of art in promoting religious teachings.
The program concluded with prayers for the Prophet and the Ahlulbayt, celebrating the occasion and offering good wishes for the listeners and martyrs.
