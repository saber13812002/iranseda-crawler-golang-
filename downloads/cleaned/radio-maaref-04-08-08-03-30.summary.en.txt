This radio program examined the details of the Battle of Camel and the fate of its prisoners, particularly Marwan ibn al-Hakam. Imam Hasan and Imam Hussein (PBUH) interceded with Imam Ali (PBUH) to pardon Marwan, who was subsequently released. Imam Ali (PBUH) dismissed Marwan's pledge of allegiance due to his history of breaking oaths and likened his brief rule to a dog licking its nose. He also warned that Marwan's descendants, especially Abd al-Malik, would cause significant bloodshed among the Muslim community. The exact duration of the battle was debated, with Ya'qubi's account of four hours being considered more credible than the three-day narrative due to the rapid collapse of the enemy front. After the battle, Imam Ali (PBUH) ordered that martyrs be buried in their clothes and allowed both sides to bury their respective dead. By establishing new rules for internal conflicts, such as prohibiting the taking of civilian property and prisoners, he distinguished this war from previous battles. This approach introduced Muslims to new jurisprudential rulings regarding civil wars.
