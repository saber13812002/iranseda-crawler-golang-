The radio program first emphasized the religious and moral obligation of doctors and financially capable individuals to save patients in need of expensive or rare medications, classifying it as a collective duty. In response to legal questions, it was clarified that the prayer for earthquakes is only mandatory if the tremor is felt at the person's current location, regardless of whether they are a traveler or resident. The program explained that house deposit money and bank loans are not subject to Khums as long as they are considered loans. The specific method for performing the prayer of fear (Namaz-e Wahshat) was detailed, including the required verses for each rakaat. Regarding ablution, it was stated that performing Ghusl is sufficient for prayer after the end of menstrual periods, making Wudu unnecessary. The sequential order for Ghusl was defined as washing the head, then the right side, and finally the left side of the body. Finally, the principle of purity applies to doubtful items unless impurity is proven, and prayer is invalidated if hair is intentionally left outside the head covering.
