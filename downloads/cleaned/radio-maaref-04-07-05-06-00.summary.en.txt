The "Ma'aref" radio program introduces its new schedule and features a tafsir of Surah Al-An'am by Ayatollah Javadi Amoli. The commentator emphasizes human inherent weakness, arguing that only God can alleviate minor afflictions or bestow small blessings. This analysis suggests that humans are generally heedless and only turn to God when facing minor hardships or receiving slight favors. The Quranic concept of God being "above His servants" signifies absolute existential dominance rather than physical spatial superiority. God protects humans from various events through guardian angels, and this divine encompassing power applies to both punishment and mercy. Ultimately, the tafsir concludes that God's oneness and uniqueness are proven through both rational arguments and Quranic revelation.
