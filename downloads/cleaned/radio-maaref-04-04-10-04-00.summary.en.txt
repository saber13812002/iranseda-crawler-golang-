This radio program provided religious rulings to audience questions from various Maraji'. Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi clarified that women may recite the Fajr, Maghrib, and Isha prayers aloud, but must whisper during Zuhr and Asr. Ayatollah Sistani ruled that his followers must pay past Zakat al-Fitrah by adding half the price difference to the previous year's amount. Ayatollah Sobhani stated that killing harmful insects is permissible, whereas killing small, harmless creatures is considered injustice. Regarding prostration, using the back of the hand is only valid in cases of extreme necessity when no other suitable surface is available. Ayatollah Javadi Amoli explained that reading the Quran from a tablet or phone does not require Wudu, but touching the physical text of a book does. The program concluded by emphasizing the Islamic principle of mutual support and encouraging listeners to submit their questions via the provided contact numbers.
