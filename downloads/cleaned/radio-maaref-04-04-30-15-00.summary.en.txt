In this radio program, experts compared Islamic war ethics based on Imami jurisprudence with the Western realist approach, emphasizing that the end does not justify the means. They stressed that Islamic Sharia strictly prohibits actions such as torture, killing prisoners, and harming civilians, even during wartime, deeming excessive violence beyond defensive necessity as impermissible. In contrast, the Zionist regime is placed on the side of the wrong by ignoring human rights and employing indiscriminate violence. A key point was the necessity of maintaining national unity against external threats, as the enemy aims to break the people's will by sowing discord. Consequently, any action that weakens the side of justice or creates despair among the people is considered religiously forbidden. The unified presence of all societal sectors, from Muslims to non-Muslims, under the Islamic government, was identified as the primary factor for victory. Ultimately, adhering to ethical and jurisprudential standards in war is not only a religious duty but also the main factor legitimizing the side of justice on the international stage.
