In the final segment of the program, it was emphasized that the waiting community must prepare for the Mahdi's appearance by earning his practical satisfaction and eliminating ethnic and geographical barriers.
Citing the narrative of Ali ibn Yaqtin, breaking the hearts of believers and showing disrespect were identified as major obstacles to the Imam's entry into society.
The discussion then shifted to a theological analysis of the Verse of Purification and its implication for the Imamate and infallibility of the Ahl al-Bayt.
The primary argument posits that the divine will in this verse is "Tawqifi" (ontological) and necessitates certain realization, rather than "Tashri'i" (legislative) which may not be fulfilled.
The word "Innama" in the verse indicates that God's will is exclusive to the Ahl al-Bayt, distinguishing them from other humans.
Since legislative will applies to all humans, including disbelievers, its exclusivity to the Ahl al-Bayt negates the legislative interpretation and affirms the ontological one.
The final conclusion is that purification from "Rijs" signifies the permanent and absolute purity of the Ahl al-Bayt from sin, thereby confirming their infallibility.
