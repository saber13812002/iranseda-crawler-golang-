On the anniversary of the martyrdom of Imam Hassan Askari, pilgrims gathered in Samarra for mourning ceremonies, with Iraqi security forces ensuring public safety. The Supreme Leader of Iran emphasized the necessity of implementing the strategic agreement between Iran and China to drive transformation on the global stage. The Iranian President traveled to China for the SCO summit, advocating for multilateralism to counter Western blocs. In Yemen, the assassination of the Prime Minister and several ministers in an Israeli airstrike triggered widespread reactions. The Houthis and the Yemeni government vowed to continue their resistance and seek revenge, while Iran's Foreign Ministry condemned the attack. Lebanese and Palestinian officials also issued statements condemning the killings and expressing solidarity with Yemen. Analysts believe these assassinations will not alter Yemen's path of resistance but may create new opportunities for future actions.
