The program begins by interpreting the story of Yusuf and an old man to explain the concept of "the right of the self" and avoiding arrogance.
Professor Khatami, citing Imam Sajjad's "Risalat al-Huquq," emphasizes the need to guard the tongue, ears, and eyes as divine blessings.
He states that fulfilling the rights of these faculties means abstaining from sin and aligning behavior with divine will.
The second part of the program is dedicated to answering religious questions from followers of various maraji'.
The discussed issues include the ruling on individual prayer during Friday prayers, consuming blood, and ritual bathing from an unlawful source.
A fatwa regarding doubtful wealth and how to handle it is also presented.
Another question about the impurity of hands after touching an animal and the conditions for purification is answered.
The program concludes with the announcement of contact numbers and a prayer for the listeners.
