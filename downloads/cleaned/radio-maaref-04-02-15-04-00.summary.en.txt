The radio program "Zamzam Ahkam" began by explaining the rules of Umrah al-Tamattu, detailing the prohibitions for men and women during Ihram.
Men are forbidden from covering their heads, fully covering their feet, walking under moving shadows, and wearing sewn garments.
Women are prohibited from covering their faces beyond the amount washed in Wudu and from wearing visible jewelry or adornments after entering Ihram.
In the Q&A section, the use of unidentified alcohol-containing liquids was deemed impermissible out of caution due to uncertainty.
Child support remains the father's responsibility after divorce, even if the children live with the mother, unless otherwise agreed upon.
Performing missed prayers on two sandals is permissible, while the presence of men in a woman's operating room is problematic if the surgery is on her.
The program concluded with a tafsir of verse 19 of Surah Al-Imran, emphasizing that the People of the Book recognized the Prophet but were obstinate.
