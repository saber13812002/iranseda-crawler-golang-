In this program, Professor Mohammadi refutes the concept of fatalism, emphasizing that human destiny is shaped by both material and spiritual factors such as prayer and charity. He argues that certain knowledge about the afterlife is attainable only through revelation and reliable narrations, as other methods like sensory perception are not transferable and prone to error. Addressing a question about pilgrimage, he clarifies that performing the rituals and etiquette of ziyarat remains valid even without achieving a state of spiritual presence or weeping. He advises listeners to deepen their knowledge of the Infallibles through studying their virtues to enhance the quality of their worship. The program also features recollections of Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi's political struggles and exile during the Pahlavi era. These anecdotes highlight his steadfastness and extensive activities in preaching and political resistance. The broadcast concludes by inviting listeners to share their feedback and promoting upcoming segments.
