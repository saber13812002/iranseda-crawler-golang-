This radio program discusses the interpretation of the Verse of Purification and the debate over whether "Ahl al-Bayt" refers exclusively to the Prophet's wives or also includes the Ahl al-Kisa. The speaker argues that relying solely on the context of the verse to claim exclusivity to the wives is insufficient and must be reconciled with authentic and mutawatir narrations. It is noted that this exclusive view is not unique to Shia Islam, as some Sunni scholars have also proposed it, though the vast majority of reputable scholars reject it. A significant critique is leveled at Ibn Kathir for mistakenly conflating the "cause of revelation" with the "context," incorrectly asserting that the wives were the cause of the verse's revelation rather than the event of the Kisa. The shift in pronouns within the verse from feminine to masculine is highlighted as evidence supporting the shared application of the verse to both the wives and the Ahl al-Kisa. Ultimately, the program concludes that the theory of shared application, supported by mutawatir narrations and a more precise analysis, is the more correct and robust perspective.
