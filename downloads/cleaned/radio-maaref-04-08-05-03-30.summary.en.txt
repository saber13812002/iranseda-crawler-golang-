In this radio program, Professor Shakerin analyzed the appropriate Persian terminology for Darwin's theory of evolution, arguing that "evolution" is misleading due to its connotation of positive progress. He suggested that terms like "transformation" or "development" are more accurate scientific equivalents that better reflect the theory's neutral nature. The discussion also highlighted how Darwin was influenced by Malthus's population theory and Charles Lyell's geology in formulating his ideas. The historical roots of these concepts in ancient Greece and the various criticisms from physics and biology were also briefly addressed. The program then transitioned to a segment answering religious questions based on the rulings of Islamic scholars. Topics included the validity of prostration with sweat, repaying debts for unauthorized construction, and the purity of blood-stained clothing. Additionally, the virtues of the morning prayer and the permissibility of clapping or drumming in religious ceremonies were clarified.
