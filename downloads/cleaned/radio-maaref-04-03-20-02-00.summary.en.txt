The radio program "Parsaiyan," featuring Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi, explores the virtue of concealing poverty and enduring hardships with patience.
The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) stated that poverty is a trust from God, and hiding it earns a reward comparable to performing night prayers.
Islamic narrations emphasize that a believer who is poor but keeps this status hidden is more beloved to God.
Ayatollah Mirza Khalil Kermi shared a memory from his studies in Iraq, where he endured two days without food until his father traveled to help him.
Ayatollah Ali Iraqi also recalled the severe financial struggles of students in Qom, who could not even afford coal for heating during cold winters.
The key point is that scholars, despite their severe poverty, never displayed it to others and fulfilled their religious duties through patience.
The main lesson of the program is the recommendation to be patient and discreet about material difficulties to preserve one's spiritual status.
