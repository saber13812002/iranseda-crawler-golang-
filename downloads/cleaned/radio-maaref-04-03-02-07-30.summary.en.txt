In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Falahbastan, citing narrations from the Ahl al-Bayt, identifies anger and rage as a "vast army of Satan's soldiers" and the antithesis of mercy. He emphasizes that anger is not a single enemy but a complex, multi-faceted force that attacks humans from six directions, aiming to destroy reason, ethics, and monotheism. By creating internal and external division, anger disrupts psychological peace and paves the way for the infiltration of jinn and human devils. The scholar warns that succumbing to this vice is equivalent to opening the doors of one's heart and mind to Satan, thereby accepting his malicious schemes against humanity. Therefore, deeply understanding the dangerous nature of anger and avoiding it is the key to maintaining spiritual health and preventing moral corruption. This discussion continues previous sessions with the goal of rooting out moral vices from the audience's lives.
