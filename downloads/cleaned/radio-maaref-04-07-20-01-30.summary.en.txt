In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Shariatitabar emphasized the necessity of observing hijab and appropriate attire for both men and women within an Islamic lifestyle, citing Quranic verses. He highlighted the story of Prophet Moses and the daughter of Khathir to illustrate that trustworthiness and capability are essential traits for selecting officials and employees. The speaker explained that proper attire is not only a sign of piety and dignity but also prevents moral corruption and voyeurism, thereby enhancing social psychological security. Observing hijab was identified as a key factor in strengthening family stability and reducing divorce rates, leading to the realization of a pure and peaceful life. In the second part of the program, Sardar Mohammad Bagher Nikkhah narrated his memories from the Sacred Defense, including his presence in Susangard and Khorramshahr. Additionally, the story of martyr Ali Reza Allahyari, a young Pasdaran who was executed by the enemy for refusing to deny his identity, was recounted. These narratives demonstrate the sacrifice and loyalty of the war veterans to Islamic values and the system during the most challenging wartime conditions.
