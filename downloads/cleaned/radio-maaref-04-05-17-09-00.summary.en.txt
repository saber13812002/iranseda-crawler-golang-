In response to a listener's query, Professor Jafari confirmed the historical accuracy of Sa'd ibn Mu'az's arbitration during the Battle of Banu Qurayza. Citing verses 26 and 27 of the Quran, he explained that the execution of the combatants was based on the Torah's judgment and divine approval, rather than personal whim. The professor refuted the claim of a thousand casualties, estimating the number of executed men to be between 300 and 700 based on demographic analysis and historical sources, while noting that women and children were taken as captives. In the second part of the discussion, the concept of the Mu'tazila was explained as a sect originating from the debate on the status of a major sinner in Hasan al-Basri's circle. The distinction was drawn between the theological Mu'tazila, which emphasizes rationalism, and the political Mu'tazila, referring to those who abstained from internal conflicts. Finally, the discussion touched upon the continuation of this intellectual tradition among contemporary Sunni thinkers who advocate for rational analysis of religious issues.
