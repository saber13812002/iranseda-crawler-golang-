The radio program began by emphasizing the importance of secret charity and the seven groups who will be in God's shade, citing relevant religious narrations.
Examples of the hidden generosity of Ayatollah Ghaadi Khorabadi and Ayatollah Hadaiq were shared, highlighting their anonymous assistance to the poor and sick.
Guest expert Seyed Ahmad Faghihi then discussed prayer as a tool for moral education and strengthening one's spiritual connection with God.
He explained that sending blessings upon the Prophet and his family is essential for prayers to be accepted, describing it as a "passport" and "purification" for supplication.
In response to listener questions, the expert advised teachers to combine firmness with kindness and a smile, rather than using bad temper or fear to manage classrooms.
He noted that love and trust are more effective in education than fear, urging educators to follow the compassionate yet firm example of the Prophet.
The discussion also briefly touched upon the prohibition of backbiting and mentioning others' sins, even when referring to non-believers or sinners.
The program concluded with a focus on instilling monotheistic ethics and addressing various religious and educational inquiries from the audience.
