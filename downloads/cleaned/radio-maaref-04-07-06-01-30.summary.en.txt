The most essential quality for serving in an Islamic government is a jihadist spirit combined with asceticism, meaning a detachment from worldly status and wealth alongside readiness to sacrifice one's life.
Citing narrations from Imam Sadiq (AS), the author emphasizes that officials must be free from the love of the world, and the public must make wise choices to prevent corruption and injustice in administration.
Visiting holy shrines imposes a doubled moral responsibility, as any injustice or unfairness committed by pilgrims is considered worse than the actions of others.
The second part of the program recounts memories from the Supreme Leader regarding the early days of the Iran-Iraq war and the brave resistance of the people in Abadan during Operation Karbala 5.
These anecdotes demonstrate that the jihadist spirit and readiness for martyrdom were deeply rooted even among ordinary citizens and merchants, serving as a key factor in military victories.
Ultimately, the hope is that by cultivating a jihadist spirit and fighting injustice, the ground will be prepared for the appearance of Imam Mahdi (AJ) and the Islamic society will reach moral and political perfection.
