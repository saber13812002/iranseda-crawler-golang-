In this program, Professor Masoudi emphasizes the superiority of serving God's creation and fulfilling the needs of believers over performing twenty optional pilgrimages, citing narrations from the book of Kafi. He explains that a person who feels pain for others' suffering and joy in their happiness is already in a worldly paradise, whereas arrogance and unrealistic expectations create an internal hell. The program also highlights that the requests of the needy are a divine mercy, and rejecting them deprives one of God's grace. In the second section, religious rulings were provided in response to listeners' questions, including the prohibition of copying rented films without permission or paying the licensing fee. The conditions for paying Khums to poor or stranded Sayyids, and the necessity of obtaining permission from the Marja's office for spending the Sayyids' share, were also clarified. Regarding divorce, it is invalid if it occurs during menstruation or during a period of purity without intercourse, except in specific cases such as pregnancy or the husband's lack of knowledge. Waqf of property is invalid if it prevents a debtor from paying their debts, and the missed prayers and fasts of parents are obligatory only for the eldest son.
