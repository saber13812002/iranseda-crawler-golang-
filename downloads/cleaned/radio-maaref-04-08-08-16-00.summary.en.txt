In the radio program "Persaman Enghelab," Dr. Mohammad Ali Loli discussed Iran's political and economic challenges. He described trade relations with Russia and China as a result of national interests and Western sanctions, emphasizing that while Iran trades globally, the West is not a primary partner due to economic warfare. Loli stressed that Iran's missile capabilities are non-negotiable, viewing them as a crucial guarantee of national security derived from indigenous knowledge. Addressing the question of why some revolutionaries change their identity, he considered this a natural phenomenon in any system, citing psychological and political factors, but noted that the revolution itself remains resilient against such attrition. Finally, he viewed the approval of Iran's accession to the CFTC convention by the Expediency Discernment Council as a positive step, although he expressed skepticism about whether Western-influenced international bodies would genuinely accept Iran.
