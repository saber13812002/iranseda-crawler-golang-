In this program, the speaker emphasizes the importance of avoiding arrogance and learning moral lessons from the fate of past powerful figures, citing Nahj al-Balagha and narrations from Imam Kazim (AS). He reminds the audience that only righteous deeds and good character accompany a person in the afterlife, not status or wealth. Referring to the Dua of Sabba, he highlights the necessity of balancing hope in God's grace with fear of His punishment for a true believer. The speaker also stresses the importance of "obedience through knowledge" and a deep understanding of religious laws, rather than merely reading books. On a social level, he discusses the need for Muslim unity and standing against bullies to preserve religious identity and achieve global power. The program concludes with prayers for the reappearance of the Imam and a celebration of Imam Kazim's birth. The show then transitions to the book narration "Left Behind by the Son," where Salal, whose son Sohrab was injured during protests, seeks help from Maluk and Afthana Khanum to treat his child. As Afthana Khanum enters the house with her specific tools, Salal reflects on his past while anxiously awaiting the outcome of his son's treatment.
