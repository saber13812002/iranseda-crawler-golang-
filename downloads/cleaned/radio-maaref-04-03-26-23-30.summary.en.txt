This radio program offered practical strategies for reducing child stubbornness and improving parent-child communication. A key point emphasized was the use of pure speech, a gentle tone, and beautiful words, which are more effective than force due to human nature. Parents, particularly mothers, were advised to record and review their conversations with their children to evaluate the impact of their tone and words. It was highlighted that parents should avoid creating tension when the child is tired or hungry, as these states lower the child's behavioral tolerance. Parents should avoid looking down on their children and instead interact with them as equals to respect their sense of dignity. Maintaining a strict schedule for sleep and meals was also identified as a crucial factor in preventing stubbornness and restlessness. The program concluded by thanking the listeners and emphasizing the importance of scientific and psychological education in child-rearing.
