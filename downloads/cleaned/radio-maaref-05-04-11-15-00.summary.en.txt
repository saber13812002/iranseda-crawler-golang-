The Langley documents reveal that the US and Israel launched covert operations to destabilize the Islamic Republic just six months after the revolution. These files highlight Washington's active role in inciting ethnic and religious unrest to create chaos and shut down oil regions. A key strategy involved using human rights rhetoric to justify executions and pressure the government. The documents also detail efforts to spread the English language and Western media as tools for cultural infiltration and cultivating dependent elites. It is indicated that the US supported leftist and nationalist groups to create a rift between the people and the religious leadership. The ultimate goal of these plots was to weaken Iran's economy and bring it back into Western orbit. These documents demonstrate that anti-Iranian policies by the US began from the very first days of the revolution and have continued to this day.
