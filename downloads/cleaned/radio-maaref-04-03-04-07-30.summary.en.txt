In this radio program, the guest emphasizes the significance of Resistance and Resilience Day, highlighting the courage of people who maintained their urban life despite heavy bombings during the war. The main discussion focuses on managing anger as a critical obstacle to maintaining a healthy spiritual connection with God. According to religious narrations, controlling anger is essential for mastering one's inner state and is a prerequisite for emulating divine ethics and merciful attributes. Anger not only destroys individual inner security but also spreads like a domino effect to families and society, creating widespread insecurity. Therefore, to achieve peace and preserve one's religious relationship, individuals must avoid the vice of anger and repent through kindness and mercy. This individual process serves as the foundation for building secure and dynamic families and communities. Ultimately, refraining from anger is identified as the key to achieving both internal and external security and strengthening the bond with God.
