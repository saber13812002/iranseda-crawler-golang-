This radio segment discusses the status of the Jewish community during the reappearance of Imam Mahdi and the likelihood of a direct governmental confrontation. Citing various narrations, the speaker argues that Jewish political sovereignty will cease to exist before the Imam's emergence, meaning he will not face them as an independent state. According to a narration from Imam Hussein (AS), people of the book will be presented with only two options during this era: accepting Islam or preparing for war, with no provision for paying the Jizya tax. The narration also highlights immense material and spiritual blessings that will descend from the sky, including the healing of all diseases and the revival of barren lands. Furthermore, the Shia community will be granted special divine favors, such as knowledge of hidden matters and mastery over the world. The speaker emphasizes that these blessings are contingent upon sincere repentance and a complete spiritual return to the Ahl al-Bayt.
