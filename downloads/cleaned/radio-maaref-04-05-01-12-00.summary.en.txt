This radio program explained the essence of prayer as a dual connection between God and the servant, representing divine mercy and the servant's effort to reach their Lord.
The most important etiquette of prayer is the presence of the heart and detachment from the five senses, allowing the worshipper to stand before God with humility.
To achieve this presence, it is recommended that the worshipper mentally visualize Paradise on the right, Hell on the left, and God directly in front of them.
After completing the prayer, the state of fear and hope should prevail, reflecting both the fear of rejection and the hope for God's grace.
The second part of the program introduced the proxy pilgrimage ceremonies at the Mutahhar Shrine and how to register for them via the website and a dedicated phone number.
Special religious and cultural programs for the months of Muharram and Safar, including lectures and mourning rituals in various shrine halls, were also announced.
For foreign and non-Persian-speaking pilgrims, multilingual programs are available in different parts of the shrine, with international information provided through embassies and tourism organizations.
The program concluded with prayers for the reappearance of the Imam and greetings to the Ahl al-Bayt.
