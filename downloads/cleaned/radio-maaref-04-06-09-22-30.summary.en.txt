In this radio program, the distinction between a temporary sulk and a complete severance of ties is clarified while examining the root causes of resentment. The speaker cites Quranic verses to identify six major sins—mockery, prying, suspicion, backbiting, using bad names, and public criticism—as primary drivers of conflict. Additionally, the consumption of intoxicants and gambling is highlighted as factors that cloud judgment and lead to violence and lasting grudges. The importance of avoiding public reprimands of children and utilizing short-term sulking as an effective educational tool is also emphasized. To resolve conflicts, the speaker suggests that the person initiating peace should use methods such as greeting, giving gifts, seeking advice, and forgiving. It is stressed that seeking reconciliation carries greater spiritual merit, and one's duty is fulfilled even if the other party does not accept the olive branch. The program concludes with prayers for the appearance of Imam Mahdi and condolences for the martyrdom of Imam Hasan Askari.
