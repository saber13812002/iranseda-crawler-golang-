In this radio program, Professor Mohammad Sharoudi emphasizes the comprehensiveness of Islamic Sharia and the role of finality in completing previous teachings, focusing on the concept of "neglecting God." He identifies neglecting God as the root of all misfortunes, loss of trust and servitude, and historical political failures such as the nuclear deal. According to the arguments presented, relying on external powers instead of God not only fails to solve problems but also allows devils to take advantage of humans and deprives them of inner peace. Conversely, trusting in God and remembering Him provides peace and divine protection, even in the harshest conditions like illness or war. The professor uses the story of Prophet Moses to illustrate that the greatest asset is remembering God, not material wealth. It is also emphasized that economic and daily life calculations must consider God's blessing and support to avoid hardships and delays. Finally, listeners are encouraged to join the ranks of those who remember God, as mentioned in Nahj al-Balagha, rather than remaining in the company of the negligent.
