Former Persepolis and Damash player Ardalan Ashtiani discusses his playing career and the structural financial issues within Iranian football clubs. He recalls the harsh training conditions and lack of supervision in older Iranian leagues. Ashtiani highlights the severe ACL injuries he suffered in both legs, which led to the early end of his playing career. After retiring, he transitioned into coaching, working with youth and junior teams. Despite holding degrees in jurisprudence and sports science, he joined the national football instructors' group. He criticizes the financial challenges and corruption in grassroots football, emphasizing the need for transparency and fairness in the sector. Finally, he shares his future aspirations and the importance of his children's well-being in other sports disciplines.
