In this radio program, two jurisprudential questions from listeners were addressed and answered. The first question concerned the ruling on prostrating on a carpet, with the response stating that doing so intentionally invalidates the prayer unless under extreme necessity or lack of access to permissible items. The second question asked about price increases for purchased goods, with the answer clarifying that the seller cannot demand more money if there is no legal prohibition. Following the responses, the program's contact channels were reminded to the audience for submitting new inquiries. The broadcast concluded with the recitation of religious pieces and mystical supplications. This program aims to resolve religious doubts and foster a spiritual atmosphere for its listeners.
