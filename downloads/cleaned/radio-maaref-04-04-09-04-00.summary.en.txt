The first segment of the program addressed specific jurisprudential rulings regarding prayer validity, such as the prohibition of praying on moving carpets and the requirement for permission to use shrine seals, citing Ayatollah Sistani. Fiqh answers were also provided concerning the expiation for missed fasts and the permissibility of hair dyeing if it interferes with wudu. The discussion further clarified the rules of spousal maintenance (nafaqah), emphasizing that a wife may only take what is strictly necessary if her husband refuses to pay, ideally under the guidance of a religious authority. The second part featured a lecture by Ayatollah Bahrami on the concept of the "assured soul" and strategies to overcome base desires. He identified the remembrance of God and the remembrance of death as the primary tools for maintaining spiritual peace and religious integrity. The serene and courageous behavior of Imam Hussein (AS) during the events of Ashura was highlighted as the ultimate example of inner tranquility in the face of death. Finally, listeners were advised to recite Surah Al-Fajr, known as the "Surah of Hussein," in their prayers to attain similar spiritual calmness during difficult times.
