In this program, Hojjat-ol-Islam Seyyed Mohammad Hossein Rajour emphasized the need for a respectful and engaging approach to youth in mosques, strongly criticizing the exclusion of teenagers for trivial reasons. He identified good temper and humanism as the most essential tools for cultural and propagandistic jihad, defining them as a lifestyle rooted in faith in God and serving the people. Citing the repeated messages of the Supreme Leader, Rajour stressed the critical importance of mosques in nurturing the next generation and creating a warm environment for young people. In the second segment, the mother of martyr Arman Alibardi recounted her son's moral character, intellectual maturity, and deep passion for seminary studies and jihadist activities. She described the final moments of Arman's life and his martyrdom during the 2022 protests, highlighting his quiet efforts to guide others with kindness and logical argumentation. Her narrative illustrates that Arman, despite his young age, was a model of piety, study, and responsibility who deeply influenced even his own family.
