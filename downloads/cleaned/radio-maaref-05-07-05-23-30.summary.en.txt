The program began by emphasizing the importance of raising public consciousness and praying for the acceleration of the Imam's reappearance. It then introduced the narrative from the book "In the Ambush of the Red Rose," focusing on Ali Siad Shirazi's studies in the United States. Despite the rigorous curriculum and language barriers, Ali excelled in his ballistic courses, securing the top rank among American and international students. He utilized various social opportunities to discuss Islam and address the doubts of his American peers. During a heated debate about the Vietnam War, Ali's critique of military atrocities moved an American pilot to tears and confession. Although warned by his Iranian supervisor to avoid political activities, Ali persisted in his religious dialogues. Ultimately, he was honored as the outstanding student of the course from fifteen countries and returned to Iran with distinction.
