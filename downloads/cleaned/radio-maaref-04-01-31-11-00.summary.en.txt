The radio program "Qoumeh" began with cultural and religious news, highlighting the 11th Poetry of the Islamic Revolution conference in Mashhad where selected poets received the "Ambassador of Poetry" award.
The broadcast also reported on the publication of over 200 new books about army martyrs and the production of ten high-quality novels detailing their lives.
The head of the seminary domains announced the dispatch of approximately 35,000 religious propagandists across the country during Ramadan and a five-year effort to train students with specific goals.
A cultural hub named "Nilou Faraneh" was opened in Qom to educate and entertain teenage girls, while a national exam for memorizing 25 parts of the Quran is scheduled for early May.
The segment featured Dr. Alipour, a physics teacher in Abadan, who creatively teaches Nahj al-Balagha by integrating religious concepts with contemporary issues to engage students.
Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the severe prohibition of backbiting in the "Parsaiyan" program, citing hadiths and the conduct of Imam Khomeini to describe it as a sin greater than usury.
The program concluded with announcements for upcoming shows on the Ma'aref network and an invitation for listeners to tune in to the "Zamzam Ahkam" program.
