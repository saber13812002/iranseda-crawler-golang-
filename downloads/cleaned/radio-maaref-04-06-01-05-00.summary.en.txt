This program emphasizes the spiritual danger of seeking status and superiority on earth, citing Quranic verses that warn against such ambitions. The speaker argues that those who pursue power and demand obedience from others are destined for hellfire and will be deprived of divine mercy. In contrast, the truly pious are those who fear God, engage in self-accountability, and remain humble among people. To illustrate this virtue, the humility of great scholars like Ayatollah Haqshenas and Ayatollah Na'ini is highlighted, as they rejected fame and even asked their students to correct their mistakes. The sermon concludes with a prayer for well-being and a strong reminder to maintain piety and avoid causing corruption in society.
