This radio program primarily discusses the concept of divine justice and the limitations of its realization in this world.
The host emphasizes that the world lacks the capacity for complete justice, meaning the full vindication of the oppressed is not guaranteed here.
He argues that worldly punishments, such as retribution, are necessary but insufficient to fully compensate for the suffering caused by injustice.
Conversely, oppressors suffer in this life not only through legal consequences but also through internal spiritual turmoil and guilt.
The conclusion is that absolute justice and precise accounting will only be fully realized in the afterlife following the resurrection.
In the Q&A segment, the topic of sharing the rewards of good deeds with others was addressed, clarifying that proxy worship for obligatory acts like prayer is not permissible.
However, intending to share the spiritual rewards of voluntary deeds, or even the rewards of obligatory acts, with others is considered valid in jurisprudence.
The program concluded with congratulations for Unity Week and the Prophet's birthday, followed by a prayer for rain to alleviate the drought.
