The radio program "Zamzam Ahkam" addressed various jurisprudential questions, emphasizing the importance of specifying one's marja in inquiries. Ayatollah Fallahzadeh clarified that following a prayer leader is permissible if their recitation is considered correct by Arabic-speaking commoners, regardless of minor tajweed details. The use of gold and silver vessels for eating and drinking was declared haram, while washing blood-stained clothes once after removing the impurity was deemed sufficient. Regarding ritual washing, Ayatollah Moeini stated that in sequential ghusl, the head and neck must be washed before the body, though the order of right and left sides is not mandatory. The purification of impure land by sunlight requires specific conditions, including direct exposure and drying solely by the sun. Financially, purchasing securities with monthly interest is permissible for informed individuals, though investment via mudarabah or agency is considered more compliant. Finally, Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi discussed the sixth station of spiritual progression, "Tajalli," where God's light manifests in the heart, eliminating compound ignorance and enabling true discernment between right and wrong.
