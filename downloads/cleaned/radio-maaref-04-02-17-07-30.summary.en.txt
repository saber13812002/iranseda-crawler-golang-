In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Ghasem Souri from the Qom scholars emphasized the importance of becoming a means for Imam Reza (PBUH) to untie the knots of people's problems. He explained that God often uses humans as intermediaries to solve others' issues and that believers should not hesitate to offer their help. The second part of the program, featuring Hojjat al-Islam Falah, focused on the vice of divine anger and its causes. Citing Quranic verses, Falah warned that intimate friendship and relations with God's enemies and disbelievers are among the primary reasons for divine wrath. He interpreted verses from Surah Al-Mujadila, Al-Ma'idah, Al-Mumtahanah, and Al-Imran to stress that Muslims must avoid relationships that could harm the faith community. The core message of the broadcast was the necessity of being cautious in choosing friends and strictly avoiding any actions that might undermine the interests of Islam and the religious community.
