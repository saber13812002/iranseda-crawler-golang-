In the radio program "Persoman Quran," Hujjat al-Islam Maleki addressed listeners' religious questions. The first topic discussed the angels' objection to Adam's creation, explaining that they anticipated potential corruption based on the free will observed in Jinn. God responded by highlighting the role of prophets and the righteous in guiding humanity toward virtue. The program then compared the forgiveness shown by Prophet Joseph to his brothers and Prophet Muhammad to his enemies during the conquest of Mecca. These actions demonstrate the unified prophetic tradition of mercy and forgiveness, even towards those who caused harm. It was emphasized that the Prophet never initiated war, engaging in conflict only in self-defense against aggression. The final question addressed why Prophet Jacob favored Joseph, with the answer being Joseph's inherent virtues and the involuntary nature of parental affection. The episode concluded by encouraging listeners to emulate the prophets' patience and forgiveness in their own lives.
