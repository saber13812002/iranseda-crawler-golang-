Ali Roozbehani, a former player and coach for military teams, recounts his experiences playing for the Ground Forces and in futsal competitions. He discusses winning the top scorer title in Iran's first official futsal cup by defeating his rival in the final, as well as finishing as runner-up at the World Army Games in Rome. Roozbehani shares insights into national team selection, recounting a story about Ali Parvin and Jalal Cheraaghpour regarding how a knee injury affected his chances of making the national team. He also reflects on his coaching tenure at Niroo-ye Emineh, noting the team's failure to reach the Premier League due to insufficient support from the command. In the final segment, he highlights his recent efforts to develop grassroots football and school programs through the Tehran Football Federation. At the age of 61, he states that he no longer seeks higher administrative positions and expresses deep gratitude to his wife for her unwavering support throughout his career.
