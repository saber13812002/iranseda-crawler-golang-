The program began by emphasizing the virtue of humility and the importance of maintaining a balanced, respectful attitude towards parents, regardless of one's academic or social status. In the religious rulings section, Hujjat al-Islam Vahidpour clarified that Arbaeen pilgrims who stay in multiple locations during their journey must pray shortened prayers, except in four specific places: Masjid al-Haram, Masjid al-Nabawi, Kufa Mosque, and the Karbala shrine itself, where they have the option to pray either shortened or full prayers. It was also ruled that women with menstrual issues may pass through the shrine via different doors but should avoid stopping near the shrine's enclosure to touch it, as a precautionary obligation. Other responses indicated that washing shoes is not required after cleaning a carpet contaminated with impurities, and wudu is invalid if a barrier like spray paint was not removed before performing it. Additionally, individuals who forgot to perform the "Qatl al-Muslimiyyah" must do so now and make up their past prayers, unless they had previously made a general intention to remove all such barriers. The program concluded with rulings on reserving spots in congregational prayer rows and purifying car seats, ending with blessings and contact information for listeners.
