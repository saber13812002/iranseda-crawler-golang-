Simayeh Khosrowshir, a track and field coach and federation employee, recounted the tense experience of securing an Olympic qualification for her student and the emotional celebration that followed. She highlighted the major challenges of her athletic career, particularly the lack of clarity regarding future prospects and economic instability, alongside severe limitations in training facilities. Khosrowshir emphasized the critical need for long-term support for grassroots sports and the recruitment of strong financial sponsors to develop the discipline. Referring to the national team's success at the London Olympics, she described the professional atmosphere of the event and how it inspired her coaching career. She also discussed her active role in the Asian Walking Committee and efforts to expand this sport across the continent. Finally, she advised families to encourage their children to participate in foundational sports like track and field at a young age to build strong physical and moral skills.
