The narrator recounts the memory of holding a grand congregational prayer with about 600 participants in the courtyard of a nursing home. At that time, military forces and the Red Cross were present but failed to prevent the ceremony from taking place. This event is described as an unprecedented occurrence in the history of that camp. The speaker emphasizes the memorable nature of the prayer, mentioning that its spiritual fragrance is still felt after many years. The latter part of the text consists of religious and national anthems praising Iran and Imam Ali (AS). These songs highlight a sense of belonging to the homeland and pride in living in a country devoted to the Commander of the Faithful. The martyrs and historical victories are also mentioned as symbols of strength and resistance. Overall, the text is a combination of a personal memory of a religious ceremony and patriotic-religious chants.
