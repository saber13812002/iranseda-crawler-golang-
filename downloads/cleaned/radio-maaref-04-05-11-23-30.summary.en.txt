The radio program begins by emphasizing the necessity of relying on authentic sources and avoiding personal, unverified interpretations of the Quran, particularly regarding its miracles. The concept of "Muhaajizin" is explained as the enemies of religion attempting to hinder God from executing His divine laws, such as the triumph of truth over falsehood. The story of Moses and Pharaoh is presented as a historical example of how such plots ultimately fail and how God protects the faithful. The discussion then shifts to the Islamic Revolution in Iran, highlighting its role in awakening Muslims and defeating major global powers despite material shortages. The speaker stresses that Muslims must distance themselves from Western cultural influences and return to their authentic Islamic identity and personality. Furthermore, the importance of unity among Muslims is highlighted as a crucial strategy to counter imperial powers and prevent historical fragmentation. The program concludes with condolences for the martyrdom of Imam Javad (AS) and the playing of a musical piece.
