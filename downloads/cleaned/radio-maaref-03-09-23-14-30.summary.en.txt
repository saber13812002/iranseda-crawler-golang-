In the Radio Maarif program, expert Yusefian analyzed the claims of Ahmad al-Hasan al-Yamani, identifying him as a fraud who changed his name and progressively escalated his claims to present himself as the deputy and son of the Imam of the Time. The expert deemed these claims baseless due to the lack of rational and narrated evidence, the absence of support from religious scholars, and the ambiguity surrounding the individual's true identity. It was emphasized that dreams and divination are not valid grounds for proving the position of Imamate. In the second segment, the topic of the return of the Imams after the appearance of the Imam of the Time was discussed, with narrations confirming the return of all Imams during the Mahdi's rule. Although the exact order of this return is not definitively specified in sources, some narrations suggest the subsequent rule of Imam Hussein (PBUH) after the Imam of the Time. These discussions aimed to clarify correct Shia beliefs in response to Mahdavi questions. The program concluded with an invitation for listeners to submit their questions and a prayer for the Prophet and his family.
