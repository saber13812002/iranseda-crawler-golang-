This radio program features a Quranic exegesis by Ayatollah Javadi Amoli, focusing on the centrality of monotheism in both belief and worship. The commentator emphasizes that all prophets were sent based on the principle of "La ilaha illa Allah," and true worship requires recognizing that no being is superior or inferior to another. Intentional sin stems from claiming divinity or independence from God's will, whereas the best form of worship is seeking forgiveness and accepting divine unity. Fear of God and hope in Him must be rooted in monotheism to free individuals from reliance on anything other than the Divine. Prophets, having preserved internal monotheism, call society to unity, a concept that transcends sectarian differences and encompasses all followers of revelation. The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) demonstrated that societal reform is only possible through monotheistic action and reliance on divine grace.
