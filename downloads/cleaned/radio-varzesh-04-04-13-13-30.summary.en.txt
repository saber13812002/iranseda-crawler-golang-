In this interview, a father reflects on the golden era of wrestling in Iran, which was driven by passion rather than financial contracts. He highlights the achievements of his sons, Amir Roshan and Amir Bahram, who became national champions and members of the national team. Having experienced hardship and orphanhood himself, he emphasizes that sports helped his children escape life's struggles and remain independent. He contrasts the current sports environment, which he views as heavily materialistic and conditional, with the past when athletes trained solely for the sake of winning. He expresses pride in how his sons have built their futures through their dedication to the sport. The program concludes by introducing Amir Hesheng Daryabari, another son of the family, for a subsequent discussion.
