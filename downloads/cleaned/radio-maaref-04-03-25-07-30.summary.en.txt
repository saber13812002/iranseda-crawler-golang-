In this radio program, guests emphasized the importance of recognizing Iran's correct historical position against the injustices of Israel and the United States, citing the Supreme Leader's statements.
The martyrs of this conflict were described as devastating blows to the enemy's cunning, and God's presence alongside the fighters was highlighted to instill confidence in ultimate victory.
Hojjat al-Islam Gholamali, referencing Imam Ali's teachings, identified the source of calmness during war as "fear of God" in the heart and wearing the "robe of tranquility" in appearance.
He further defined true faith in God's power and spiritual discipline through jihad and moderation in eating as the other pillars of this composure and authority.
In the second segment, Hojjat al-Islam Fala examined the devastating consequences of arrogance (Kibr) based on numerous Quranic verses.
These consequences include deprivation of divine love, the closing of the gates of heaven, and condemnation to eternal residence in Hell.
Arrogance not only deprives a person of intercession and God's mercy but also places them in a state where even the fire of Hell cannot purify them.
Finally, humility was presented as the only path to salvation and the key to entering the divine realm, urging listeners to abandon arrogance and cultivate moral virtues.
