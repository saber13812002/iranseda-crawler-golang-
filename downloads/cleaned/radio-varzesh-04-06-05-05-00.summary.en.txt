Dr. Ali Gharadashi, the president of the Iranian Table Tennis Federation, discussed his playing career and the travel restrictions of that era in an interview with the Oral History of Iranian Sports program. He highlighted his crucial role as the federation secretary from 1999 to 2010 and his close collaboration with Shahnazari. A key initiative during his tenure was inviting Korean coaches and players to train with the national team, which significantly improved the technical level of Iranian table tennis. Gharadashi also noted the beginning of foreign player participation in the domestic league, which helped raise the competitive standard. He was elected as federation president in 2014 with a high vote count and discussed the team efforts to secure sponsors despite economic sanctions. Despite recent medal successes in Asia, Gharadashi stated that he has no intention of running for a fourth term as president. He believes that with the continued dedication of the new generation of players, winning an Olympic medal in the coming years is achievable.
