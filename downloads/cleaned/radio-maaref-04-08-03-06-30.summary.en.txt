The program begins with an interpretation of verses from Surah Al-Baqarah, explaining that divine misguidance is a punishment for the wicked while guidance is a reward for the faithful. Following a recitation of "Ya Rabb al-Alameen" and expressions of gratitude, the show honors the memory of Hajj Ahmad Mousavian and emphasizes the mercy of the Prophet's family. The "Salam Sar-khat" segment highlights the warmth of family life and the supportive roles of parents. The host then engages the audience by asking who the "kind detective" in their household is, drawing a parallel to literary detective characters. Listener responses reveal that young daughters often fulfill this role through their curiosity and attentiveness. The broadcast also reviews historical events of Aban 3rd, including Reza Khan's premiership, the opening of Karaj Dam, the death of poet Hallet, and the birth of Pablo Picasso. The program concludes with thanks to listeners for their messages and a final emphasis on kindness in family relationships.
