This radio program began by reporting the martyrdom of three law enforcement officers in Chabahar and condemning the recent killings of Shia scholars in Syria. It then commemorated the Day of Modesty and Hijab by recalling the historical resistance of Mashhad's people against Reza Shah's policies at the Goharshad Mosque. The broadcast also highlighted the Imam Ali Fiqh Center, which has published over 480 books on contemporary issues such as medical and political jurisprudence. Furthermore, the program discussed the fate of those who have not been exposed to the truth, distinguishing between hostile deniers and the oppressed based on Quranic verses. The violation of the Geneva Conventions by the Zionist regime, specifically through attacks on Iranian Red Crescent ambulances, was strongly condemned. Finally, in an interview, Hujjat al-Islam Vahidpour explained the jurisprudential concept of "Kaf'ah" obligation in national defense. He emphasized the urgent need for the younger generation to fill the void left by martyred nuclear and missile experts to ensure the country's security.
