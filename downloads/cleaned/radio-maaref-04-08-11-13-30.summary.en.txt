The program begins with a reflection on the purity and faith of Basij fighters, contrasting their sincerity with the claims of intellectuals. Colonel Qalijkhani shared memories from the early days of the war, including a narrow escape from landmines at Susangar and hiding under a rock in Kurdistan to evade enemy pursuit. The narrative also covered the tragic love story and martyrdom of Ali-Asghar Mahoudnia and his wife, Parvaneh, on the western front and in Kermanshah. Colonel Abbasi described the tactical maneuvers used to shoot down an Iraqi helicopter during Operation Beit al-Moqaddas and the humane treatment of prisoners of war during Operation Fath al-Mubin. The final segment criticized American officials' demands for Iran's surrender, interpreting them as a continuation of the US's long-standing opposition to the Islamic Revolution.
