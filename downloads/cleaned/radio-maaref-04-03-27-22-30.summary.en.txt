In this radio program, speakers emphasized the importance of national unity and resilience, urging the public to remain steadfast against external threats and destructive viruses by learning first aid and relying on spiritual practices like prayer and remembrance.

Citing Quranic verses and narrations, he explained that a human being without knowledge and insight is like a traveler in darkness who cannot reach their destination.
The speaker noted that inquiring and seeking the truth are the keys to saving humanity from misguidance and achieving perfection.
The program concluded with an invitation for listeners to participate in the theological Q&A section and the recitation of the Holy Quran.
