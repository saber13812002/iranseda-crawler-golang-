This radio program commemorated the martyrdom of Imam Hasan Askari (AS) while analyzing his dual efforts to protect and introduce Imam Zaman (AS).
The discussion highlighted the Imam's protective measures, such as concealing the birth from the government by using different names for the mother and feigning the death of another child.
Conversely, he took affirmative steps to ensure the Shia community was aware of the Imam's birth and allowed them to meet him during his childhood.
After Imam Hasan's martyrdom, the four deputies continued to safeguard Imam Zaman (AS) through strict security protocols and the practice of taqiyya.
The program emphasized that the primary threat to the Imam has shifted from physical assassination to the ideological and conceptual assassination of the concept of Mahdism.
This modern threat is driven both by uninformed internal individuals and by Western media and academic centers seeking to undermine Shia beliefs.
The core message is that the duty of awaiters is to defend the theological integrity of Mahdism against these intellectual attacks.
Ultimately, preserving this faith is presented as the most critical way to support the Imam in the current era of occultation.
