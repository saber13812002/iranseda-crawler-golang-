In the radio program "Persaman Mahdavi," expert Mehdi Yousefian addresses listeners' questions regarding Mahdism and religious matters. He cites the Hadith of Fatima's Tablet to highlight the Imam of the Time's attributes and the exalted status of his companions as God's true allies. Responding to a query about mosque demolitions during the Mahdi's appearance, he clarifies that this refers specifically to four mosques built after Imam Hussein's martyrdom, aiming to rectify their spiritual context. He also explains that while the core events of the Mahdi's signs are fixed, their specific timing may vary, emphasizing the importance of the phenomenon over exact dates. The program concludes with a segment introducing the memoirs of Ezzat Shahi, a revolutionary fighter. These memoirs vividly depict the harsh interrogations and torture he endured during the Islamic Revolution.
