In the radio program "Perso-man Quran," Ayatollah Javadi Amoli argued that the Prophet's Mi'raj was entirely physical rather than merely spiritual, citing Quranic verses. He emphasized that God's power allows Him to perform miracles that defy natural laws, such as a physical ascension. In the second segment, the scholar analyzed the final verse of Surah Al-Imran, distinguishing between "Ithbira" and "Sabira." "Ithbira" refers to individual patience against sin, adversity, and obedience, while "Sabira" denotes collective endurance against the hardships inflicted by the enemy. He stressed the importance of maintaining ideological and physical boundaries and strengthening the bond of believers around the Imamate. In the "Khyaban Farhang" program, Hojjat-ol-Islam Ghami highlighted the right to knowledge and the necessity of engaging with religious and scientific elders. Citing hadiths, he considered intellectual discourse superior to material worship and identified divine knowledge as the noblest form of learning. The ultimate goal of this knowledge is to achieve closeness to God and true servitude.
