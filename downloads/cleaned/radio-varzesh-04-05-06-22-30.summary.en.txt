Freiba Soleimani, a veteran women's volleyball player and coach, discussed the challenges facing the sport in a radio interview. She criticized the Saipa club for failing to pay her full salary and championship bonuses, emphasizing the need for financial transparency in contracts. Soleimani also highlighted structural issues in talent scouting, arguing that Iran lacks a unified and coherent model for discovering young athletes. She insisted on utilizing specialized domestic coaches rather than relying solely on foreign experts and warned against signing blank contracts with young players. The coach stated that her primary motivation is her passion for the sport and serving the younger generation. Finally, she called for stricter oversight of private academies and improved rankings for Iran in Asian and Olympic competitions.
