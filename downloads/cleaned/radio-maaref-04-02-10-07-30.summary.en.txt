The radio program began by celebrating the National Day of the Persian Gulf and honoring the military forces for their service to the nation.
The discussion then centered on the famous poem "All my body is from you, Iran is my heart," referencing Nizami Ganjavi as a symbol of patriotic devotion.
Listeners contributed their own verses, connecting the poem's themes to national leadership, martyrs, and historical tragedies.
The second segment focused on divine anger, with a guest scholar explaining that moral deviations are primary causes of God's wrath according to the Quran.
The guest described practical lies, such as worshipping other than God or following one's ego, as a major form of deception that leads to spiritual and worldly confusion.
Additionally, the misuse of God's blessings by crossing legal and rational boundaries was identified as another significant trigger for divine anger and human downfall.
The program concluded by urging the audience to avoid moral vices and utilize God's pure provisions correctly, ending with prayers for the Holy Family.
