The program begins with a tafsir of verse 120 of Surah Al-Ma'idah by Ayatollah Javadi Amoli, emphasizing that "adab" is an action-based attribute reflecting a society's moral code.
Tawhid is the fundamental pillar of religion, standing in opposition to shirk, and thus all ethics and actions must reflect this divine unity.
The Quran is not merely a scientific text but a guide for spiritual purification that combines knowledge with practice, requiring continuous daily training.
In the pursuit of understanding, the faculties of sense, imagination, and illusion hinder reason, while in action, lust and anger obstruct pure will by demanding their own share.
Struggling against these internal forces is known as the Greater Jihad, which is far more difficult than external combat and requires rigorous self-discipline.
True spiritual guides must embody their teachings through personal action before instructing others, ensuring that hypocrisy remains hidden and trust is established.
By choosing between the invitation of angels (good) and Satan (evil), humans determine whether they become a source of good or evil, shaping their ultimate moral destiny.
