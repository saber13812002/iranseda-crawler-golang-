In this radio program, listeners first shared their personal experiences of sacrifice and letting go of personal desires for the sake of their loved ones. The news segment then reported on the growth of domestic medical equipment production and the issuance of a support card for mothers, which provides a monthly stipend of two million tomans. Additionally, experts warned about the serious risks of sleep deprivation to heart health and blood pressure. Guest Dr. Mohammad Javad Fallah began the ethical discussion by emphasizing the virtue of giving away one's most cherished possessions. Citing Quranic verses, he explained that true righteousness is achieved only when a person donates from what they genuinely love. In the second part, Fallah examined the verbal vice of "meddling" or nosiness, defining it as more than simple curiosity and a violation of others' privacy. He described meddling as a work of the ego that destroys relationships, extinguishes spiritual light, and creates darkness in both individual and social life. Finally, he advised believers to avoid interfering in others' affairs and focus on self-improvement to overcome this moral affliction.
