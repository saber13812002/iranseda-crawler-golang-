In this radio program, Professor Maleki emphasized the necessity of acting upon the content of Quranic verses to achieve spiritual and material desires, while criticizing the commercial exploitation of verses in social media. He warned that many narrations regarding the specific effects of surahs are fabricated and should be approached with caution based on reliable sources. The concept of "Mu'ajjizin" in the Quran was interpreted as those who attempt to incapacitate God from executing His divine laws and the triumph of truth over falsehood. Citing the story of Moses and Pharaoh, it was demonstrated that any resistance against God's will ultimately leads to the defeat and destruction of the opponents. In the concluding section, the importance of security as a fundamental infrastructure for economic, scientific, and social progress was highlighted. It was also explained that chaos resulting from weak order, poverty, and corruption poses a serious threat to societal stability. The program concluded with condolences for the martyrdom of Imam Javad (AS) and prayers for the listeners.
