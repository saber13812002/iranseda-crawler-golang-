This radio program addressed various theological and practical questions from listeners. It began by emphasizing the existence of awareness beyond the five senses, using dreams and the order of creation as evidence for knowing God through diverse paths. The segment then explained the difference between the cold of hell and paradise, along with the four types of death described in religious traditions. The impact of charity on souls in the barzakh and the necessity of thorough research before performing istikhara for marriage were also discussed. A theological doubt regarding the relationship between human will and God's will in remembrance was resolved through the concept of divine agency. The ruling on making up missed prayers for individuals with obsessive doubts was clarified, advising them to focus only on what they are certain about. Finally, a documentary was aired highlighting the efforts of IRIB documentarians during the Iran-Iraq war and the unique "Illuminationist documentary" style of the late Shahid Avini.
