This special radio program, dedicated to the birth of Imam Ali (PBUH) and Father's Day, began with the recitation of various poems and eulogies.
The guest, Mr. Iran-Doust, emphasized the moral and spiritual characteristics of Imam Ali (PBUH), including his bravery, generosity, and comprehensive scientific and practical knowledge.
Citing narrations, he described the love for Imam Ali (PBUH) as a universal and human sentiment that transcends religion, noting that even non-Muslim writers have praised him.
To highlight the unique status of Imam Ali (PBUH), verses by classical poets such as Saib Tabrizi and Seyed Hamid Barghi were recited and interpreted.
In the final section, the "Bafaqeh Sabzeh" charity foundation and its social activities in rural areas were mentioned, and listeners were encouraged to participate.
The names of three contest winners from the cities of Ilam, Qom, and Maku were announced, and they were congratulated.
The program concluded with a quote from Imam Khomeini (RA) regarding the convergence of contradictory attributes in Imam Ali's (PBUH) personality and a prayer for all fathers.
