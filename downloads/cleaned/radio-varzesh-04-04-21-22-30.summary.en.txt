Ali Esfahani, a former Iranian Greco-Roman wrestling champion, recounts his journey to winning the silver medal at the 2005 World Championships and his participation in the 2000 Sydney Olympics in a radio interview. He discusses the intense competition against top global wrestlers and the bitter experience of missing a medal in Sydney due to a loss in the repechage round. Esfahani also reflects on his tenure as a national team coach, during which he helped elevate the team from ninth place to world champions. He details the financial challenges and conflicts with the federation in 2018 and 2019 that led to his departure from the national team. In the final segment, he emphasizes the importance of athletes' mental preparation and the supportive role of his family in his success. Now a coach, he expresses pride in the achievements of his students in recent Olympic Games. He also mentions his personal passion for football and his support for the Esteghlal team. This interview offers a portrait of dedication, hard work, and the transition from athlete to coach in Iranian wrestling history.
