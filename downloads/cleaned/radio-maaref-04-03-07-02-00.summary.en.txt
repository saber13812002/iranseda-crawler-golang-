The program begins by emphasizing the virtue of good manners in Islam, citing examples of the compassionate behavior of religious leaders. It then transitions to a segment titled "Love Narrative," featuring the mother of martyr Ahmad Makian. She recounts the final moments of her son's life, mentioning recurring dreams and a premonition of his martyrdom. The mother describes the urgent journey to Karbala to see her son's body and how his father received the news of his death. Her testimony vividly portrays the profound grief of a mother facing the loss of her son in the Defender of the Shrine operations. The program concludes with a tribute to the martyrs and an emphasis on the patience and resilience of their families.
