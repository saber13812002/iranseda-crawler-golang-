The radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat," featuring Hujjat al-Islam Fallah, identifies humility as one of the greatest acts of worship and a mandatory duty for Shias.
Citing narrations from Imam Ali and Imam Baqir (AS), the speaker describes humility as the primary source of inner peace and spiritual elevation.
The discussion links humility to the concept of Taqwa, presenting it as the key to human dignity and worth in the eyes of God.
The story of Muhammad ibn Muslim, who sold dates in the market despite his high status and wealth, serves as a practical example of self-abasement following the Imam's command.
Humility does not replace prayer but rather elevates the prostration, facilitating a deeper connection with God and others.
The program concludes with the historical story of Zayd ibn Thabit, who refrained from retaliating against a hostile neighbor to uphold the rights of fellow Muslims.
This narrative demonstrates that forbearance and forgiveness are not signs of weakness but are rooted in faith and adherence to Quranic and prophetic teachings.
