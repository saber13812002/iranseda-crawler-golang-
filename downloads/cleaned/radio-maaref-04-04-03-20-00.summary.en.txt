Following twelve days of resistance and Iranian missile strikes on US military bases in Qatar, the Zionist regime was forced to accept a ceasefire and halt its attacks. The Emir of Qatar emphasized that this reaction was solely due to the US's direct involvement in the aggression against Iran and does not harm the friendly relations between the two nations. People in Pakistan and Afghanistan expressed their firm support for Iran's resistance through widespread protests and official statements. Arab media also shifted their perspective, praising Iran's military capabilities, while Arab countries refused to allow the use of their bases for attacks on Iran. The second part of the program examined the historical terrorist group Farghan and the role of Akbar Goudarzi in assassinating key revolutionary figures. This group, influenced by Marxist ideologies and misinterpretations of the Quran, engaged in the killing of elites and innocent civilians. Ultimately, intelligence operations led to the arrest of the leaders and the trial and execution of a small number of the group's members.
