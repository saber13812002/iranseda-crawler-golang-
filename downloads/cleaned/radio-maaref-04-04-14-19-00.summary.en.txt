The radio program begins by emphasizing the importance of maintaining proper etiquette and decorum during Muharram mourning ceremonies, warning against both negligence and exaggeration in describing the martyrs' status. Practical advice for Ashura is provided, including fasting until evening and showing solidarity with Imam Abbas by experiencing hunger and thirst. The second segment addresses various jurisprudential questions, covering the rules of wudu with peeled skin, the conditions for using bank loans, the obligatory and recommended amounts for head wiping, and rulings on henna and cycling for women. Subsequently, a cleric outlines twelve strategies to attract youth to mosques, such as keeping programs lively, ensuring cleanliness, maintaining good conduct, and establishing counseling and cultural spaces. The broadcast concludes by reiterating Imam Hussein's (PBUH) statement about his uprising's goal to reform the Ummah, followed by a biographical sketch of Martyr Majid Hasanzadeh, who served as a teacher in remote areas after the Islamic Revolution.
