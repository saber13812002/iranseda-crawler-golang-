In this radio program, the host emphasizes the importance of good public relations in religious settings, urging listeners to warmly welcome newcomers to prevent them from feeling alienated. Dr. Ahmad Gholam Ali outlines four key principles for successfully joining new groups: having a clear goal, avoiding arrogance, fostering cooperation, and maintaining a spirit of humility. He cites religious narrations to explain that kindness and closeness to others are signs of proximity to the Prophet (PBUH) on the Day of Judgment. The second part of the show focuses on the interpretation of the hadith "Anger is an enemy," identifying anger and lust as the most dangerous internal enemies. The guest explains that anger is not merely a moral flaw but the key to all evil, directly contradicting monotheism, prayer, and other religious virtues. He stresses that internal enemies are far more perilous than external ones because they are constantly present and intimately familiar with the individual's weaknesses. Finally, listeners are warned that anger and lust act like spies of the ego, capable of depriving a person of divine blessings and spiritual credibility.
