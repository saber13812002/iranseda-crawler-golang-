The program begins by introducing the Firouzabadi Hospital, which has become a trusted medical center due to the pure intentions and endowments of Seyyed Vahid Firouzabadi, son of Ayatollah Firouzabadi.
Supported by charitable donations and an active social work department, the hospital provides free or low-cost medical services to needy patients and even shelters homeless individuals at night.
Dr. Seyyed Vahid Firouzabadi continues his father's legacy by actively supporting patients and acquiring medical equipment through a charity named "For the People."
The second part of the program features the memoirs of Hassan Nouri, a former prisoner of war who was captured during the Fath al-Mubin operation.
He recounts getting lost among enemy forces, taking refuge in a dugout, and enduring heavy rocket and bullet fire from Iraqi soldiers who were trying to flush them out.
Nouri describes the terrifying moments after being discovered, the martyrdom of his comrades, and his survival despite severe injuries and brain hemorrhage.
The program concludes with a recommendation of the book "Autumn Came," which narrates the memories of the wife of Martyr General Ahmad Yusefi, and invites listeners to share their feedback.
