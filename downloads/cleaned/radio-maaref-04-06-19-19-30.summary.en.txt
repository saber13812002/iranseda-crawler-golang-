In this discussion with business consultant Mohammad Amin Dakhili, the importance of specialized knowledge and market awareness for entering the investment market was emphasized.
The most critical trait of a successful entrepreneur is perseverance and consistency in working without fatigue or giving up.
The concept of "overnight success" is an illusion, actually resulting from years of hidden effort, risk-taking, and enduring hardships.
The virtual space is filled with false promises and deceptive advertisements that promise quick profits without hard work, which should be avoided.
True entrepreneurs leverage the potential of others, but not out of fear or flattery.
No business exists without risk, challenge, and difficulty, and expecting immediate results is meaningless.
Therefore, the key to success lies in the combination of knowledge, strong connections, and continuous perseverance over time.
