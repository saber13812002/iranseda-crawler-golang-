The program begins by emphasizing the importance of piety and prayer in society, warning that social sins diminish the effectiveness of supplications and intercession.
According to religious narrations, if a community lacks piety, blessings are removed from life, wealth, and offspring, leading to poverty and insecurity.
To restore divine mercy and blessings, piety must be institutionalized at all levels of society, including universities, and individual and social sins must be eradicated.
A complete Shia must not only believe in the Ahl al-Bayt but also practice Shia principles in daily life and maintain piety for their faith to be considered complete.
Otherwise, mere outward display of religiosity without adherence to religious laws is condemned in the Quran and narrations, rendering one's Islam incomplete.
The second part of the program addresses the jurisprudential concept of "waqf manfa'a" (endowment of benefits) and distinguishes it from the endowment of the asset itself.
In this type of endowment, the owner may use the benefits of the property but does not have the right to convert or change its nature.
The discussion concludes with examples of endowed properties and the challenges associated with changing their designated use.
