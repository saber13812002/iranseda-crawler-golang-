In the radio program "Porsaman," Professor Maleki addressed various Quranic questions from listeners. He interpreted the verse "We created man in hardship" to explain that life's difficulties are essential for spiritual growth and perfection, rather than mere punishment. He distinguished between "Rahman," which encompasses all creation, and "Rahim," which is a special mercy reserved for believers in the form of divine guidance. The professor also clarified that the rank of Imamate is higher than Prophethood, noting that the Prophet (PBUH) possessed both but the Quran emphasizes his public role as a messenger. Regarding the concepts of "Ajel Musamma" and "Ajel Muallaq," he explained that sins can hasten death, while righteous deeds like maintaining family ties can delay it. Finally, he defined kindness to parents as showing respect and obedience, provided it does not conflict with divine commands, establishing that obedience to God always takes precedence over parental demands.
