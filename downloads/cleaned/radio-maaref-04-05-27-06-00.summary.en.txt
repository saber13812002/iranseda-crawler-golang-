In this program, human perfection is defined as servitude to God, which serves as the foundation for understanding the authority of prophets and imams. True and primary authority belongs solely to God, while the authority of the infallibles is an intermediary in manifestation rather than in existence, meaning they are signs of divine authority. A fundamental distinction is drawn between the guardianship of the incapacitated, which is a legal duty, and the religious authority of the infallibles, which reverts to God's authority. In religious authority, the infallible holds the status of "Mawla Alihi," and obedience to their rulings is obligatory even for themselves. This analysis defines the Guardianship of the Jurist not as rule over the incapacitated, but as a return to the authority of justice and jurisprudence. A religious leader must adhere to their own rulings before others to preserve monotheism in practice. Consequently, accepting the Guardianship of the Jurist is accepting the authority of religion and returning to the principle of monotheism in servitude to God.
