In this episode of the "Ganj-e Sa'adat" program, Hujjat al-Islam Falah examines the vice of anger based on the narrations of the Ahl al-Bayt. He identifies anger and lust as humanity's greatest enemies, noting that they deceive us by masquerading as part of our own selves. Allameh Tabatabaei warns that ignorance leads people to justify the "commanding soul," which actually operates against reason and faith to satisfy its own desires. Anger is described as an internal, invisible fire that threatens the core of human existence and ignites from within. This inner fire signifies the infiltration of Satan into the human heart, manifesting as embodied evil when anger is expressed. Since this fire is internal, physical escape is impossible; the only solution is conscious recognition and active struggle against it. Poets have also highlighted this painful truth, stating that the fire of hell is so internal that changing one's location offers no refuge.
