In this interview with veteran zor khaneh master Ali Reza Haghjati, the importance of preserving the authenticity of zor khaneh music and avoiding its commercialization was emphasized. Haghjati explained that the audience seeks the spiritual and historical atmosphere of this art, requiring masters to strictly adhere to traditional principles. He detailed the administrative structure of the Wrestling and Zor Khaneh Federation and the role of the Masters' Association in organizing and training practitioners. The guest highlighted financial challenges in education and the need for greater effort to elevate the artistic level of the community. He shared how the late master Maradi's unique style influenced his own artistic path and recited a poem to convey the emotional depth of the tradition. Haghjati also mentioned that all his children are actively involved in the art of zor khaneh. Finally, he expressed his commitment to serving public health and preserving this cultural heritage through three decades of professional dedication.
