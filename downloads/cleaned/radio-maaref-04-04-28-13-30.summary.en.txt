Political analyst Janoubi Kazemi discusses the recent imposed war and the objectives of the Zionist regime and the United States in the radio program "Borhan." He argues that the enemy miscalculated the strength of the resistance front and Iran's internal stability, aiming to incite chaos and regime change, but ultimately failed. Kazemi emphasizes that Iran's decisive response, particularly targeting the US base in Qatar, sent a strong deterrent message, and the current ceasefire is a result of the enemy's compulsion. He highlights the critical need to strengthen internal security, strictly control borders, and counter infiltration and illegal migration as key lessons from this conflict. Furthermore, he notes that Iran retains its military readiness and enjoys broad support from the people and the Axis of Resistance, enabling it to repel any future aggression. Finally, he asserts that the enemy's cognitive warfare and economic pressures have been ineffective without the cooperation of the Iranian people.
