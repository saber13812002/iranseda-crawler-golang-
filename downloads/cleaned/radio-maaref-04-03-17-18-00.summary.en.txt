In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Fallahati identifies humility as the primary weapon against Satan and his forces, citing various religious narrations. He explains that humility not only limits the power of evil but also serves as the foundation for acquiring wisdom and fresh understanding. A humble heart is compared to fertile soil where virtues grow, whereas an arrogant heart is like stone that prevents spiritual development. Furthermore, humility elevates a person's status across seven existential layers and grants them a high rank in God's eyes. Conversely, arrogance leads to a person's degradation in the eyes of society, making them lower than animals. The session concluded by reviewing twelve benefits of humility and announced that future episodes will focus on the opposing concept of arrogance.
