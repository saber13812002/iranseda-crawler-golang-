In the radio program "Persman Ateghadi" on the Ma'aref network, Professor Mohammadi Sharoudi addressed various theological and religious questions from listeners. He clarified that the phrase "except what your Lord wills" in Surah Hud signifies the boundless power of God rather than an exception to the eternity of Paradise and Hell, distinguishing between divine promises and threats. The professor also explained the difference between lunar and solar years, emphasizing that Islamic rulings such as puberty and fasting are strictly based on the lunar calendar. Regarding the question of why prophets do not intervene to stop current injustices, he argued that the purpose of prophethood is guidance, and direct divine interference would negate human free will and the purpose of worldly trials. He further discussed that some post-repentance hardships may be residual effects of past actions or opportunities for spiritual awakening. Finally, he interpreted Prophet Yusuf's prayer as a conditional statement highlighting total reliance on God's help, rather than an inappropriate demand or ultimatum to the Divine.
