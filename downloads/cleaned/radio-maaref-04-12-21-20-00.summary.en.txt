The radio program begins by emphasizing the critical need to revive reading habits, asserting that no medium can replace the unique value of books in people's lives and schedules. The host expresses deep admiration for literary works and war memoirs, specifically praising titles like "My Commander" and "Step by Step with the Rain," while urging the translation and production of valuable content for Iranian youth. The second part of the show analyzes the inaugural message of Iran's new Supreme Leader, Ayatollah Seyed Mojtaba Khamenei. A guest expert describes this message as a reflection of the late Imam Khomeini's spirit and a strategic roadmap for the revolution, highlighting the leader's strong emphasis on the decisive role of the people in governing the country. The analysis also notes the leader's wise choice to introduce himself as the deputy of Imam Zaman and his condolences to martyr families as signs of a profound connection between the leader and the nation. Finally, referencing global media reactions to the leader's pledge of retribution and support for the resistance front, the program calls on the public to participate in the upcoming International Day of Jerusalem march.
