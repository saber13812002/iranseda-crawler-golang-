The program began by inviting listeners to share their feedback and previewing upcoming segments. The main segment featured the testimony of Farzam Chehregani, a former POW captured during the Koushk Telleh operation. He described the harsh conditions of Iraqi captivity, including frequent beatings and severe deprivation in the camps. Despite the lack of paper and pens, he and his fellow prisoners creatively taught English and organized cultural competitions outdoors. One of his most painful memories was the day of Imam Khomeini's death, when their mourning rituals were brutally suppressed by Iraqi guards. The broadcast then covered the historic first circumnavigation of the globe by the Iranian naval vessel "Naav 86." This significant voyage, which crossed multiple oceans, greatly enhanced Iran's international and military prestige. The program concluded with a public service announcement about water conservation and an introduction to a specialized conference for Quran reciters.
