This program defines righteous deeds as unconditional companionship with the Prophet and Imams, where hardships are part of a divine test leading to certain victory. Citing verses from the Quran, the speaker emphasizes that piety means adhering to a covenant with God and remaining steadfast with the Imam, even during sieges and conflicts. Historical narratives from the Battle of the Trench and Karbala illustrate that the "Sadiqeen" are those who accept suffering and martyrdom to uphold their divine covenant and guide the community. The program then recounts a story from the book "Nishan-e Hassan" regarding the anxieties of Imam Hassan and Qasim ibn Muhammad after Imam Hussein's arrival in Mecca and the threats posed by Abdullah ibn Zubayr. This narrative depicts the tense atmosphere preceding the events of Ashura, highlighting reliance on God and patience in the face of rumors and threats. Ultimately, the core message is that Muslims must stay on the path of the Imam of the Time and the Sadiqeen, guarding themselves against misguidance to pass through history's severe trials.
