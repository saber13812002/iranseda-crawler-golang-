In the radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat," Hojjat al-Islam Fallahi discussed the virtue of sincere charity, emphasizing that wealth ultimately belongs to God and humans are merely nominal owners. He stressed that for charity to be blessed, it must be free from hypocrisy, showing off, and must be directed solely toward legitimate religious paths. Fallahi further advised that individuals should dedicate themselves to the cause of Imam Hussein (PBUH), a commitment achievable only through strict adherence to Islamic law, including performing obligatory acts and avoiding prohibitions. In the second segment of the program "For Iran," Dr. Mohammad Bidar addressed the 1404 national slogan, "Investment for Production," highlighting the critical role of cultural and religious institutions in fostering the necessary mindset for economic growth. He argued that sustainable economic progress requires robust cultural preparation alongside institutional efforts. The program then featured a report on the "Iran Pas" factory in Urmia, identified as the world's largest producer of monolithic polyethylene tanks. This facility exports to 35 countries and emphasizes the use of safe, domestic raw materials to protect public health from harmful substances.
