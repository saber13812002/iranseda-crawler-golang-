Dr. Sadi Hosseini discusses the successful organization of the World Wrestling Championship in Mazandaran, highlighting the crucial role of community participation and efficient resource management. He explains that by clearly defining roles and maintaining strict financial oversight, the event was completed without any debt and with high satisfaction among participants. The guest details the extensive development of sports infrastructure, including the construction and renovation of dozens of gyms in rural and urban areas using state budgets and public contributions. He emphasizes innovative strategies, such as resolving local conflicts by building sports halls and leveraging media to generate public support. Dr. Hosseini also mentions close collaboration with universities for needs assessment and sports program design. This approach led to a significant increase in sports facilities and fostered a spirit of championship in rural regions. Ultimately, he attributes his achievements to the support of the people, the media, and his dedicated colleagues.
