Memorial ceremonies for the first anniversary of Ayatollah Raisi's martyrdom were held at Imam Khomeini's Hosseiniyeh and the holy shrine with the presence of the Supreme Leader. The second meeting of Islamic education ministers concluded with the adoption of the "Tehran Declaration," the first shared document on artificial intelligence in the Muslim world. The President emphasized the necessity of scientific collaboration among Islamic nations to achieve a leading global position in technology. Meanwhile, more than half of the Iranian pilgrims have arrived in the Holy Land, with their welfare status in Mecca and Medina reported as satisfactory. Quranic festivals organized by the Ministry of Health and mosques are underway, featuring tens of thousands of participants in various fields including AI and arts. Extensive programs, including exhibitions and specialized seminars, have been arranged nationwide to commemorate the anniversary of Khorramshahr's liberation. The 8th International Resistance Film Festival is currently screening films and featuring a special section on AI-generated productions. Finally, special religious and cultural programs centered on the concept of waiting for the Savior have been prepared for visitors to the Holy Jamkaran Mosque.
