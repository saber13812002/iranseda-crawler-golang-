This radio program defines arrogance as the human claim of "I am your Lord" across various levels, from family dynamics to global power structures. Global arrogance is characterized by Western powers imposing their discourse and lifestyle on the world, leveraging their military and economic superiority. The Islamic Revolution disrupted the bipolar world order with its "Neither East nor West" slogan and weakened imperialist powers by establishing the Axis of Resistance. Recent analyses suggest that the potential alliance of Iran, China, and Russia poses a significant threat to Western interests. Despite its military might, the United States faces deep economic, social, and moral crises, leading to a loss of global credibility. The recent defeats of Israel serve as a sign of the gradual decline of imperialist powers and the ideological victory of the Islamic Revolution. Even Western theorists like Fukuyama now express concern over the severe social collapse and internal crises within American society.
