In the first segment, Ayatollah Mazaheri uses the story of Prophet Joseph to explain that prophetic infallibility stems from a profound awareness of God's presence and the dominance of faith over the ego. Citing Allameh Tabatabaei and Imam Ali, he emphasizes that true certainty and spiritual illumination, achieved through consistent worship and night prayers, protect a person from all errors and sins. Subsequently, in the "Jor'eh" program, Professor Masoudi references hadiths from the Book of Kafi to assert that fulfilling a believer's need is more virtuous than performing ten circumambulations of the Kaaba. He highlights the immense divine reward for serving others, urging listeners to apply these teachings in their daily lives. The final section features the "Zamzam Ahkam" program, where religious scholars answer various jurisprudential questions. These include rulings on Khums and Zakat for prisoners, the option to annul a contract due to defects, the age of menopause for women, and the conditions for medical examinations. Additionally, rulings on the prayer of a traveler intending to stay for ten days and the purity of rainwater were addressed. The program aims to provide moral guidance and resolve religious doubts by combining theological, ethical, and jurisprudential discussions.
