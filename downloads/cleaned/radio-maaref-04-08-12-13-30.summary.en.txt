The radio program "Hekayat-e Azadegi" introduces Nasrollah Kababiani, a soldier captured by Iraqi forces in 1988 during Operation Karbala 5. He recounts his role as an observer, his efforts to destroy sensitive documents before capture, and the physical and psychological torture endured in Iraqi POW camps. The show also highlights the book "Those Twenty-Three," a notable work on captivity literature by Ahmad Yusefzadeh. Abdul Karim Mazandarani then discusses how the faith of the freed prisoners was a unified combination of monotheism, perseverance, and righteous action, as described in the Quran. This spiritual strength allowed them to endure the harsh conditions of captivity, which the Supreme Leader has described as a significant spiritual asset. The program concludes with a message encouraging small daily acts of service to the country and a critique of Western interests.
