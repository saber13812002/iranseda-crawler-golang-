The program began with the recitation of a rain prayer from the Sahifa Sajjadiyya before transitioning to a discussion on the roots and consequences of swearing. Relying on Quranic verses and Transcendent Wisdom, the instructor explained that human actions transform into energy that becomes united with the self. Consequently, every action, whether good or bad, returns to the individual in a manifested form as their own reward or punishment on the Day of Judgment. Swearing not only renders all other virtues and personal merits worthless but also causes people to avoid the individual's company. This vice is rooted in enmity, which it further exacerbates, while also leading to a lack of blessing in one's livelihood. The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) warned that abusing a Muslim brother results in the removal of divine blessings from one's sustenance. Finally, abandoning this vice and remembering God were presented as the primary solutions to restore spiritual and material well-being.
