This program emphasizes the unique status of Imam Ali (AS) alongside the Prophet, Fatima, and the two Imams, citing Quranic verses and authentic narrations from both Shia and Sunni sources.
Loving or hating Ali (AS) is presented as the primary criterion for distinguishing true faith from hypocrisy, with Ali described as the Prophet's successor and companion, analogous to Harun to Musa.
Historical narrations, such as the Hadith of Manzilat and events from the battles of Khaybar and Khandaq, highlight the Prophet's consistent selection of Ali (AS) as a leader and standard-bearer in critical military and political situations.
The Qadiriyya and Jami'a Kaba prayers are referenced as authoritative sources confirming that the Quran speaks of Ali's Wilayah and that his words are perfectly aligned with the divine text.
The concept of jihad and self-sacrifice is defined as a "trade with God," honoring the families of martyrs who offered their lives for divine satisfaction as per the Quranic verse regarding the believers' souls.
The speaker stresses the importance of adhering to the covenant with the Islamic Revolution and its leadership, warning that enemies will continue to strive to create despair until the very end.
Public participation is deemed essential for the legitimacy of the Wilayati system, and religious leaders are urged to remain vigilant against social and cultural challenges, including issues related to dress codes.
The program concludes with a prayer for martyrdom in the path of Imam Mahdi (AJ) and a reaffirmation of support for the Supreme Leader and the continuation of the Prophet's path.
