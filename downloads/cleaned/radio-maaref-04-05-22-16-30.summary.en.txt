In the "Persaman" program, Professor Mohammadi emphasized the necessity of linking the Quran with the Wilayat of the Ahl al-Bayt to achieve human salvation, citing verse 38 of Surah Al-An'am. He explained that while the Quran provides a complete roadmap for happiness, it requires the practical interpretation of the Ahl al-Bayt to be fully realized. The concept of the world was then described as a farm and a school where individuals can convert material wealth into spiritual capital through charity and service. Subsequently, the "Kiyaban-e Farhang" program defined public security as the fundamental infrastructure for societal progress and peace. Hujjat al-Islam Aleyegomi, referencing the conduct of Imam Sadiq (AS), argued that security extends beyond physical safety to include justice in trade and the protection of citizens' rights. Field reports highlighted that education, cultural awareness, and active public participation are the primary keys to building a secure and dynamic society. Finally, chaos resulting from corruption and injustice was identified as a serious threat to social stability, underscoring the need for preventive measures.
