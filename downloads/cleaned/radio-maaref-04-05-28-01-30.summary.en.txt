The program begins with an interpretation of Imam Sajjad's prayer, emphasizing noble virtues such as responding to hostility with love and deception with benevolence.
In this prayer, the Imam asks God for a strong hand and eloquent tongue to stand against oppressors and secure the rights of the oppressed.
The second part of the program features a narrative about a pilgrimage walk to Karbala, highlighting the pure hospitality of a poor elderly man and an old woman.
These stories illustrate that despite material poverty, the people of Iraq welcome pilgrims with their entire being, even considering their homes blessed by the pilgrims' footsteps.
The contrast between the pilgrims' initial sensitivities regarding the hosts' poverty and their ultimate admiration for the hosts' generosity creates a profound emotional impact.
The main message is that good morals and forgiveness in social relations can soften hearts and create deep bonds, even under the most difficult circumstances.
The program concludes with a prayer for the early return of Imam Mahdi and the victory of the resistance front against global oppressors.
