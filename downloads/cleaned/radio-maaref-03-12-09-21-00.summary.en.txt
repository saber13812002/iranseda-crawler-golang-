The radio program "Rain of Kindness" began by introducing Saadi as a shining star in Iranian literary history.
The host emphasized the impact of Saadi's thoughts on people's minds and tongues for over eight centuries.
He described Saadi's words as sweet advice and a remedy for human happiness and suffering.
Saadi's perspective in his works is based on optimism about humanity's ability to solve individual and collective problems.
In the main segment, a wisdom from the eighth chapter of the Gulistan was recited regarding avoiding conflict.
This wisdom advises choosing the easier path with people and refraining from violence in difficult situations.
The host then referred to the moral and educational points of this attractive wisdom, which were repeated.
Finally, a letter from a listener was mentioned, whose content was read in an encrypted and ambiguous manner.
