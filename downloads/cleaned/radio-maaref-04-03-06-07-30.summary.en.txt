The radio program begins by commemorating the martyrdom of Imam Javad, highlighting his leadership during the Abbasid suppression and emphasizing the importance of relying on God rather than people. The main discussion then shifts to the moral virtue of humility, defined as an internal and spiritual quality that stands in contrast to arrogance. The speaker explains that humility is a characteristic of all prophets and saints, serving as a sign of true wisdom and spiritual rank. The Quranic connection to humility is explored through concepts like khushu (reverence) and sujud (prostration), which are presented as external manifestations of this inner state. References are made to specific verses in the Quran, such as in Surah Al-A'raf and Al-Isra, to illustrate how believers should display humility before divine miracles and towards one another. The earth is used as a metaphor, suggesting that its humility allows it to be filled with blessings, and humans should emulate this trait to achieve spiritual growth. The session concludes with the advice that practicing humility and honoring one's parents are essential steps toward receiving divine mercy and attaining paradise.
