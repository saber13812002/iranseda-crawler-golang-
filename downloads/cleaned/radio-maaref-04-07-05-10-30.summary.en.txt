The radio program "Shifa," featuring traditional medicine expert Dr. Hassan Akbari, discusses religious recommendations for maintaining health during the cold season.
The expert emphasizes avoiding the simultaneous consumption of cold-natured foods, such as fish and yogurt, in winter to prevent exacerbating conditions like rheumatism.
Instead of expensive or industrial snacks, the consumption of affordable and nutritious dried fruits like raisins, jujube, and dates is recommended for both children and adults.
The discussion also highlights the importance of planting native fruit-bearing trees, such as jujube, instead of pine trees in urban areas and religious shrines to preserve the environment and local culture.
The Quranic segment interprets the verse "Do not insult one another with bad names" from Surah Al-Hujurat, stressing the necessity of respecting individuals and avoiding labeling in social and family relationships.
The key message of this section is the negative impact of insults and humiliation on personal identity and the need to address one another with dignity and respect.
Finally, the program critiques the political logic of the United States regarding the concept of "savagery," emphasizing the importance of logical thinking and avoiding hasty judgments in international relations.
