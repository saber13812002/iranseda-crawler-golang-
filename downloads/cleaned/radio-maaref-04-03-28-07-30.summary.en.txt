In this radio program, listeners' messages were shared, advising the public to ignore enemy rumors, trust national media, and rely on God's help to see the collapse of the Zionist regime. Subsequently, Hujjat al-Islam Rasoulpour emphasized the Quran's role in providing peace during difficult times, citing verses from Surah Al-Anfal. He explained that God destroys the enemies' apparent power and air defenses from within by instilling deep fear in their hearts. The speaker then discussed arrogance as the root of Satan's rebellion and the cause of Iblis's fall from grace. He stressed that arrogance is exclusive to God, and anyone who dons the garment of pride is stripped of divine honor and spiritual rank. Quoting a holy hadith, he warned that those who challenge God's majesty will be destroyed by the fire of their own arrogance. Finally, the Prophet (PBUH) was presented as the model of humility, and arrogant, harsh individuals were identified as the inhabitants of hell.
