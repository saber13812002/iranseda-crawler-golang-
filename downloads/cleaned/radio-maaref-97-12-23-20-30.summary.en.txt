The radio program "Shajareh Tobe" opened by highlighting the Basij's role in alleviating poverty and maintaining social justice.
The host, citing Imam Khomeini's thoughts, described the Basij as a mass movement dedicated to serving the oppressed and fighting global arrogance.
Cultural and social activities of local bases, such as choir groups and religious book publishing, were presented as practical examples of this ideology.
The second part of the program focused on the Quranic concept of monotheism in actions, emphasizing that life and death are entirely subject to divine will.
Memories from Operation Beit al-Moqaddas 3, specifically the liberation of heights in winter conditions by IRGC fighters, were also recounted.
The program concluded with an invitation for listeners to participate in the Nowruz "Asrane" contest and wishes for a spiritually fulfilling new year.
