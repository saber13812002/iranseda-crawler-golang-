Babak Sinori, a veteran of Iranian football, shared his memories of the sport's golden era in the 1950s and 1960s during a radio interview. He discussed his career with clubs such as Persepolis, Homa, Keshavarz, and Keshirani, emphasizing that ethics, discipline, and respect for elders were far more valued than material wealth at the time. Sinori recounted his unique experience of serving in the military football team and being introduced to it by Ali Parvin. He also highlighted the challenges faced by foreign coaches like Bahman Foroutan in implementing modern tactical systems in Iran due to limited infrastructure. The veteran noted that while the technical level of players has not changed drastically, the living conditions and facilities have improved significantly. He expressed concern that modern clubs now prioritize financial backgrounds over talent when recruiting young players. Finally, he urged a return to the human and professional values that once defined Iranian football.
