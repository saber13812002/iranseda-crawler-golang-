The radio broadcast began with news updates regarding the establishment of a virtual bank for youth, the "Iran Jan Fars" event, and increased pilgrim readiness for the 2026 Hajj. It also reported on the ceasefire agreement in Gaza and the rising number of martyrs in the region. The main segment featured the testimony of Amir Eskari, a former POW, recounting his capture during the 1986 Shalamcheh operation. He described the desperate resistance of his eight-man group against Iraqi forces and their efforts to treat wounded comrades with limited resources. Eskari detailed the physical torture and harsh interrogations he endured in captivity, including the tragic death of two comrades from thirst during transport to Baghdad. In the closing remarks, a cleric emphasized that these veterans exemplified true freedom and steadfastness despite the isolation of solitary confinement. Their stories were presented as a powerful lesson in courage and loyalty to Islamic values for the listeners.
