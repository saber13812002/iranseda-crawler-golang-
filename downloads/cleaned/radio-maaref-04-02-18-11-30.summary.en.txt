The radio program began by explaining the practical rules of Tawaf during Hajj, noting that talking and drinking water are permissible as long as the pilgrim's left shoulder remains oriented toward the Kaaba.
Pilgrims are advised to exit the Tawaf circle calmly to avoid disturbing others before proceeding to perform the associated prayer.
Subsequent sections addressed various jurisprudential questions, confirming that dedicating the reward of night prayers to others, including strangers, is valid and does not diminish the individual's own reward.
It was clarified that while reading specific surahs in different rakats is acceptable, repeating the same surah in both rakats is not recommended.
The seven takbirs in prayer were identified as a recommended act rather than an obligatory one, meaning their omission does not invalidate the prayer.
Regarding financial rights, a wife is not permitted to take money from her husband's pocket if he lacks the financial capacity to pay her maintenance, and she must wait like any other creditor.
The program concluded by emphasizing the spiritual significance of prayer as a means to establish sincerity and eliminate arrogance before God.
The broadcast ended with the recitation of the Holy Quran and religious supplications.
