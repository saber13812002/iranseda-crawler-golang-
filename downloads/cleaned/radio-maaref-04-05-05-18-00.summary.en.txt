The radio program initially examines stinginess as a vice that severs family ties and distances individuals from divine mercy.
According to religious narrations, a stingy person is considered far from God and cuts off their connection with moral virtues and the Holy Family.
The discussion then shifts to the concept of "good reward" or hasana for those who perform acts of kindness in this world and the hereafter.
The Quran promises that God will reward good deeds at least tenfold or replace them with something even better.
An example of such a reward is the expiation of sins achieved by attending Friday prayers with silence and tranquility.
Performing good deeds, such as respecting parents and making others happy, leads to receiving these worldly and otherworldly rewards.
Finally, the program emphasizes that one must avoid moral vices and follow the path of goodness to benefit from God's blessings.
