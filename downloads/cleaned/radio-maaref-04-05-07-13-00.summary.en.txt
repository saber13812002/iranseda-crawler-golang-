The program begins by interpreting verse 146 of Surah Al-Imran, which praises the steadfastness of those who strive in the path of God.
A narration from Ibn Da'ab is cited, describing how Ali (AS) refused to show weakness or surrender despite sustaining eighty wounds in the Battle of Uhud.
The Prophet (PBUH) promised Ali martyrdom, and Ali volunteered to fight Abu Sufyan at Hirah al-Asad, demonstrating his unwavering commitment.
Five key characteristics of true mujahideen are outlined: God-consciousness, resilience against adversity, avoiding the display of weakness, refusing to submit to enemies, and patience.
The text emphasizes that instilling fear of the enemy is the work of Satan, while believers must rely on God to demonstrate courage and initiative.
The experience of the Iranian nation during the Sacred Defense, particularly the patience of martyrs' families, is presented as a practical model of these Quranic traits.
Finally, Imam Hussein (AS) and his loyal companions, especially Zainab (SA), are highlighted as the ultimate role models of steadfastness and patience against oppression.
