The radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat" began with the recitation of the fourteenth supplication from the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya, followed by a discussion on the moral vices of nitpicking and criticizing others. Hojjat al-Islam Mohammad Hali emphasized that un-Islamic nitpicking reduces community self-confidence and diminishes collective virtues. Citing narrations from Imam Sadiq (AS) and Imam Ali (AS), he warned that anyone who faults others will inevitably be afflicted with the same errors. It was also noted that believers are the honor of God, and criticizing them leads to both worldly and otherworldly punishment. The scholar explained, based on Quranic verses, that God grants humans a respite to repent and rectify their shortcomings. Finally, it was advised to focus on self-improvement and acquiring virtues rather than dwelling on others' mistakes, as divine blessings can sometimes serve as a subtle divine strategy to awaken individuals.
