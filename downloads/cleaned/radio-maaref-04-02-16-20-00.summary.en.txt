The radio program "Music at 20:00" on the Ma'aref network began its news segment by highlighting the violation of humanitarian principles.
UN officials announced that more than three thousand trucks carrying essential aid for the people of Gaza are ready.
Despite this preparation, the entry of these supplies into Gaza has been blocked by the Zionist regime.
Consequently, the aid remains stuck at the entry checkpoints and fails to reach the needy population.
The program then featured an invitation to participate in the national "Ain" festival and donate 8,888 devices to long-married couples.
A special short code with the prefix 3888 was announced to support this specific initiative.
The broadcast concluded with religious prayers and music dedicated to the occasion of Imam Reza's birth.
