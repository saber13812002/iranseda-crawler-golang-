This radio program examines the distinction between superficial differences in taste and fundamental disagreements in life values before marriage.
Critical life issues such as residence, career, number of children, and family dynamics must be carefully evaluated.
The technique of "shared future visualization" is recommended to reveal each partner's mental image of their life ten years from now.
The second technique, "practical tests," involves observing the partner's genuine reactions to everyday situations and challenges.
If agreement cannot be reached on these core issues, the likelihood of facing serious conflicts in the future is high.
The most important point is accepting human differences as part of divine creation and avoiding the imposition of personal mental frameworks.
Successful relationships are those that, by embracing differences, enter a spiral of mutual virtue and growth.
