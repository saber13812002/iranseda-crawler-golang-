This radio program explores the concept of "mind management," emphasizing that inner peace and reliance on God are the keys to successful decision-making.
The most significant error for managers and officials is identified as ignoring expert opinions and lacking a receptive ear for professional advice.
Individual efforts, Istikhara, and consulting others are not negated but are presented as essential complements to decision-making, provided they do not replace a direct connection with God.
The religious segment highlights the importance of maintaining mental tranquility to perceive life's truths and the spiritual impact of Quranic recitations.
Practical advice was also given on resolving personal and state injustices by assisting the poor through specific religious permissions.
With the arrival of autumn, critical safety tips regarding the proper use of gas appliances to prevent suffocation accidents during the cold season were reiterated.
Finally, the program transitioned to a segment on child-rearing and answering educational questions with a specialist, following the broadcast of autumn-themed music and literature.
