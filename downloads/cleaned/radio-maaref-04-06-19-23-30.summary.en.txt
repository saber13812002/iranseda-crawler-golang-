In this radio program, listeners are invited to send text messages to the number 1552 to donate funds in the name of Imam Reza (AS) for the educational needs of orphaned children.
The host emphasizes the importance of even the smallest acts of charity and good deeds, citing narrations from Imam Sadiq (AS) that promise great rewards for such actions on the Day of Judgment.
By sharing stories of the impact of small donations, such as an elderly woman's contribution to light a candle, the host illustrates that no service is worthless in the eyes of God.
The program also highlights the virtues of good character and generosity in Islam, advising listeners to bring joy to orphans through affectionate gestures like kissing their heads.
Towards the end, while reciting the Ziyarat of Al-Yasin and performing mourning, the show discusses a historical narration regarding the fall of the Ctesiphon palace during the appearance of Imam Mahdi (AJ).
This historical analysis stresses that the palace symbolized tyranny and its future destruction signifies the end of oppression and the beginning of a just government.
Finally, the program concludes with the names of the Infallibles, prayers for all listeners, and an encouragement to continue serving the community.
