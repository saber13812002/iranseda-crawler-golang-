This program analyzes the final verses of Surah An-Nisa to clarify the inheritance rules regarding siblings, known as Kalalah.
The commentator emphasizes that the Quranic term "child" includes both sons and daughters, meaning the presence of either disqualifies siblings from inheriting.
According to authentic narrations, siblings can only inherit if the deceased has no children and no living parents.
The distribution of wealth among paternal or maternal-paternal siblings follows the principle that a male receives twice the share of a female.
In contrast, maternal siblings mentioned in verse 12 of the same surah receive equal shares and are excluded if paternal siblings are present.
The commentator refutes the claim that Islam always favors men in inheritance, demonstrating that women sometimes receive equal or even greater shares depending on the relationship.
Finally, the structural coherence of the surah is highlighted by noting that it begins and ends with references to God's power and knowledge.
