The program began by congratulating students on the start of the new academic year and the Sacred Defense Week, with listeners sharing advice on cultivating good habits and strengthening faith.
In the main segment, Hujjat al-Islam Fallahati examined the vice of cursing from the perspective of the Quran and Hadith, declaring it absolutely forbidden in all circumstances.
Citing verses from the Surahs of Al-A'raf, An-Nur, and An-Nahl, he emphasized that foul language harms the speaker as much as the target and is a sign of moral degradation.
The scholar warned, based on a narration from the Prophet (PBUH), that cursing even non-believers is impermissible because it ultimately reflects back on the individual and disturbs the deceased's family.
The concept of "Sabb" (insulting) was also analyzed in relation to Surah Al-An'am, highlighting that responding to insults with insults only perpetuates ignorance.
The episode concluded with advice for students to use their knowledge in service to society and to rely on faith to achieve noble character.
Finally, listeners were encouraged to abandon moral vices and embrace virtues to achieve higher devotion and contribute to a stronger society.
