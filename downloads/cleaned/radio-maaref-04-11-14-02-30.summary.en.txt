The first segment features a narrative about Melika, who receives divine messages through dreams involving Jesus, Muhammad, and Fatimah Zahra. She learns of her destined marriage to Hasan ibn Ali and the necessity of embracing Islam to connect with him. Following this revelation, she requests her father to release Muslim prisoners held in the Roman Empire. The second segment features expert Mehdi Yusefian discussing the letters of the Imam of the Age. He explains that the Imam's correspondence with Sheikh Mufid was direct, even during the period without a specific deputy. The expert also offers spiritual remedies, such as prayer and ziyarat, for finding peace during hardships. Finally, the program concludes with excerpts from Ezzat Shahi's memoirs, highlighting the bravery of Sheikh Gholam-Hossein Jafari during the 1940s.
