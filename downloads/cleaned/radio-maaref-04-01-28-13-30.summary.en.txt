The radio program "Narrative of Love" features the story of Zahra Sigdin, the wife of the martyr pilot Ahmad. Zahra recounts their marriage in 2006 and the birth of their son, Hossein, who was only seven months old when Ahmad was martyred in the Rigi operation. Following this devastating loss, Zahra faced a new and severe challenge when her son was diagnosed with leukemia at the age of six. She describes the immense hardships of his treatment and the painful moments of his final days in the hospital. Despite these profound tragedies, Zahra has persevered by relying on her faith and the memories of her late husband. Her story highlights the resilience and endurance of women who bear the heavy burden of raising a sick child after losing their spouse.
