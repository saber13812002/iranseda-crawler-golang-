The radio program began by emphasizing the importance of preserving the spiritual atmosphere of pilgrimages and avoiding disruptive photography. Fiqh questions were then addressed, confirming that the Arbaeen walk is a strongly recommended act for both men and women, provided women observe hijab. It was clarified that the Arbaeen prayer is not restricted to the specific day of Arbaeen and remains rewarding on nearby days, although the full recommendation applies to the main day. Regarding mixed-gender sports in parks, it is permissible as long as there is no harm, such as improper gaze or lack of modesty. The program also cited Ayatollah Sistani's ruling that saying "Subhan Allah" suffices for the obligatory prostration, and that cosmetic surgery is permissible for women outside their menstrual period. Furthermore, it was stated that those without financial means are not obligated to pay the Zakat al-Fitr, nor do they owe it later. Finally, the Friday sermon highlighted the significance of attending Jumu'ah prayer for maintaining national unity and staying informed about current social and political issues.
