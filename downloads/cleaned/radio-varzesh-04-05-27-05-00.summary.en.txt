In this interview, Dr. Seyed Hadi Hosseini discusses the successful organization of the World Wrestling Championship in Mazandaran, highlighting the crucial role of community participation and inter-departmental cooperation. He shares anecdotes about the dedication of staff, such as a janitor’s extreme efforts to maintain cleanliness and local officials helping to prepare parking lots during heavy rain. Hosseini explains that through precise task division, academic consulting, and media challenges, they increased the per capita sports space sevenfold compared to the previous seventy years. A key achievement was constructing sports halls in rural areas by leveraging the resources of provincial directors to create local landmarks and foster community identity. He also describes how he resolved local conflicts by offering to build sports facilities instead of unwanted infrastructure projects, ensuring transparent financial management with no post-event debts. Ultimately, Hosseini attributes his success to the combined efforts of the people and the media, emphasizing the importance of cross-sectoral synergy in sports development.
