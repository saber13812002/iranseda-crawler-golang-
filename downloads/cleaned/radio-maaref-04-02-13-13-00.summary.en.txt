The program began by congratulating the birth of Hazrat Fatima Masumeh and Imam Reza, focusing on the former's spiritual role as a model for both girls and boys. The speaker cited verse 35 of Surah Al-Ahzab to outline ten spiritual competitions that God has established between Muslim men and women. These competitions include Islam (submission to divine commands), Faith (practicing obligations and avoiding prohibitions), Obedience to authority, Truthfulness, Patience, Humility, Charity, Fasting, Chastity, and Remembrance of God. It was emphasized that physical differences between genders should not hinder spiritual competition, where women can actually surpass men in piety. Hazrat Fatima Masumeh was presented as the ultimate exemplar who excelled in all these areas, earning a special reward from God for her devotion. The speaker urged the audience to participate in these spiritual contests to achieve divine forgiveness and great rewards. The session concluded with prayers for spiritual growth, healing, and the well-being of the community, invoking the intercession of Hazrat Fatima Masumeh.
