The radio program began by discussing proxy pilgrimages to the shrine of Hazrat Masoumeh and the high status of prayer in Imam Khomeini's thought. It then introduced the 33rd National Prayer Conference, which focuses on the practice of prayer in the late Imam's life and work. A significant portion of the broadcast was dedicated to a criminal case involving an actor accused of blackmail, privacy violations, and money laundering. The presiding judge detailed the severe legal penalties for these crimes, including imprisonment and heavy fines. Subsequently, a cleric emphasized the religious and social necessity of earning a halal livelihood, citing Quranic verses and hadiths. The discussion concluded with an analysis of the diminishing impact of US sanctions and the declining military and political power of the United States.
