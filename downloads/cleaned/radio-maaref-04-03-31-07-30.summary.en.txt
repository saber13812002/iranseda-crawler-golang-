In the radio program "Mehraman," featuring Haja Barzoui, the importance of Tawhid and resistance against the Zionist regime and global arrogance was emphasized.
The key messages from the audience highlighted complete trust in the Supreme Leader and Iran's inevitable victory against external threats.
In the second segment, Professor Mohammad Hadi Fala interpreted Quranic verses and narrations regarding the cure for arrogance.
He recommended negative practical steps, such as abandoning arrogant walking styles and severing ties with arrogant individuals.
Positive steps included cultivating humility, maintaining consistency in these traits, and seeking refuge in God against the sin of arrogance.
True humility is defined as internal engagement and following God's plan, rather than mere external displays or affected behavior.
Arrogance was identified as a calamity and a curse for oppressors, while silence in the face of injustice was deemed a sign of ignorance.
The program concluded with a poem by Nader Ebrahimi and a prayer for the Iranian nation.
