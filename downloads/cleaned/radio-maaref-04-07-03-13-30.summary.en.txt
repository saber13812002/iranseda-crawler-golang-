The radio program "Narrative of Love" began with hymns honoring martyrs before transitioning to an interview with the mother of martyr Gholamhossein Afshordi, known as Hassan Bagheri. The mother recounted her son's early efforts to educate and mobilize his peers during his high school and university years. She detailed his covert political and media activities, including his work for the Islamic Republic newspaper and the IRGC, which ultimately led to his expulsion from university. After the war began, Hassan Bagheri initially joined the front lines as a journalist but quickly rose to command operations and military intelligence, becoming a strategic commander within just two years. The final segment, "Message of Wilayat," addressed managerial challenges, emphasizing the need to bridge the gap between decision-making and execution. It also highlighted the importance of overcoming the fear of making decisions and fully utilizing the country's vast natural and human resources.
