Pilgrims are required to register via the Samaneh system for pilgrimage passports, while the Moallem Insurance Company offers special coverage services for the journey.
Radio Ma'aref launched a dedicated "Arbaeen Radio" network on July 25 to provide narrative programming and information services for pilgrims.
