In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Mohammad Hadi Falah discusses practical and scientific methods for overcoming the vice of arrogance. He emphasizes that arrogance is a major sin and a Satanic whisper that deprives individuals of God's love and spiritual connection. Key strategies include recognizing that pride belongs solely to God and believing in the Prophet's role as a healer for this spiritual ailment. The speaker highlights the importance of "self-dialogue" and continuous self-affirmation of moral virtues to instill positive beliefs deeply within the soul. Furthermore, drawing lessons from the fate of Satan and past arrogant nations serves as a serious warning, noting how Satan's six thousand years of worship were destroyed in a single moment of pride. Finally, he urges listeners to accept the Prophet's call as a divine physician, abandoning self-centeredness and pretense to move toward humility and sincere devotion to God.
