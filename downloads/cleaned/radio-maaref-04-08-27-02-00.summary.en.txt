In the "Tales of Freedom" program, a former prisoner of war shared memories of fasting in Iraqi camps despite severe hunger and heat. He highlighted the extraordinary faith of the freedmen who maintained their worship under such harsh conditions. The second segment criticized how short social media posts reduce attention spans, encouraging a return to reading books. The program then discussed narrations from the Book of Kafi emphasizing the duty to help Muslims in distress, particularly praising firefighters and volunteers. It also interpreted the Quranic verse "Speak to people kindly," urging viewers to avoid gossip and unfounded rumors. The core message was to treat others with fairness and wish for them the same good one desires for oneself. Finally, a prayer for rain from the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya was recited to seek divine mercy.
