In this radio program, the expert emphasized the historical challenges of fitna and hypocrisy in Islam before addressing current national issues. A primary focus was the over-seven-month suspension of the Islamic Consultative Assembly, which the expert deemed inefficient and contrary to the principle of government oversight, urging a return to normal legislative functions. The discussion also covered a judicial ruling against a parliament member, stressing the need for prudence and magnanimity to preserve social unity and avoid personal feuds. Another major topic was the hijab crisis and social disorder, where the expert called for strict enforcement of laws by the judiciary and the government to prevent societal division and address street violence. Finally, the surge in currency rates and inflation was analyzed, noting that while the war has an impact, flawed domestic currency policies are also a significant factor that the government must address based on expert advice.
