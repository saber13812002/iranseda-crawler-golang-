This radio broadcast begins with an introduction to upcoming programs and the airing of a religious song before introducing a dramatic series titled "Pāyegāhi-ye Kome Shokāri." The narrative centers on Major Babaei, a top fighter pilot, who successfully executes a dangerous nighttime refueling mission with an F-14 despite technical limitations and external threats. Following this achievement, he is selected to participate in an aerial parade in Isfahan, but he is forced to make an emergency landing due to a sudden hydraulic system failure during the flight. This incident leads senior commanders, particularly a Colonel, to detain and interrogate Babaei, driven by political sensitivities and suspicions regarding his loyalty. Ultimately, Babaei's direct commander expresses support for him while warning that higher authorities are closely monitoring him due to his religious and traditional background. The broadcast concludes with a speech by the Supreme Leader regarding the Iranian nation's willpower against its enemies and the playing of a religious song.
