The program refutes the notion of ethnic privilege by highlighting that the Abbasid Caliphate was established with Iranian support, leading to a golden age of science and justice. During this period of Iranian influence, Shia Islam became an official state ideology, a context in which Ferdowsi sought to revive "Islamic Iran" rather than oppose Islam itself. The narrative also rejects the myth of forced conversion, arguing that Iranians embraced Islam voluntarily due to its social equality and freedom from Sassanid oppression. The broadcast further designates 2025 as a year for investment and production to improve the livelihood of the people. Finally, the discussion shifts to theological matters, specifically addressing the succession of Imamate from Imam Reza to Imam Javad and the principles of lineage within the Ahl al-Bayt.
