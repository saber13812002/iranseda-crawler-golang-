In this program, Professor Mohammadi refutes the claim that the Prophet (PBUH) was martyred, emphasizing that God preserved him, prioritizing His divine custom over breaking it. He explains that death does not dissolve the marital bond, meaning spouses remain each other's mahram in the afterlife. The lecture defines five levels of the human soul, with the soul of faith and the Holy Spirit being the highest. It is noted that jinn and Satan acquire knowledge through observation, reason, nature, and transmission, similar to humans. In contrast, angels like Gabriel (AS) only know what God teaches them and lack independent analytical capabilities. The professor highlights that God revealed His oneness to humans in the pre-existence world, where they testified to it. Finally, sending blessings upon the Prophet and his family is presented as an inherent act of worship and a means to resolve worldly and otherworldly issues.
