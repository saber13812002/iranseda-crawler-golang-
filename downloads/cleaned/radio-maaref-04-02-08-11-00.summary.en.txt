The Supreme Leader of Iran expressed condolences for the devastating fire at Shahid Rajaee Port, ordering authorities to investigate the cause and hold all responsible parties accountable. The Minister of Interior confirmed that an investigation committee has been formed and initial evidence collected, promising strict action against any negligence or intent. In Qom, the Razavi Service Centers have prepared extensive cultural programs for the "Day of Karim" to mark the birth of Hazrat Masoumeh and Imam Reza. While celebratory events for Girls' Day at the Hazrat Masoumeh shrine were postponed out of respect for the victims, religious lectures continue with prayers dedicated to the deceased. A conference on the re-establishment of the Qom Seminary and its historical significance in Shia scholarship was also held in the city. The book "Biographies of Islamic Mathematicians" was selected for the National Library's list of 100 enduring books of the century. The 36th International Tehran Book Fair is set to open next week, focusing on promoting reading and supporting publishers. Finally, a cleric discussed the jurisprudential rulings regarding bank late-payment penalties based on contractual obligations.
