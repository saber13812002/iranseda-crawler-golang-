The program begins with an interpretation of Imam Sajjad's words, emphasizing that Islamic society must be led by God's righteous and trustworthy servants.
It then transitions to a historical narrative from the book "Neshan-e Hassan," depicting the atmosphere in Imam Hussein's house before his journey to Kufa.
The text highlights the concerns of Muhammad Hanafiya, who attempted to dissuade the Imam from traveling due to the known treachery of the people of Kufa.
Imam Hussein (PBUH) calmly reaffirms his divine wisdom and the necessity of obedience to his authority, despite the warnings.
The intimate dialogue between the Imam and his sons, Ali Akbar and Qasim, reveals their spirit of martyrdom and deep trust in God.
The presence of Nafila and Fatima further illustrates the family's anxiety regarding political conspiracies and the shifting political landscape.
Ultimately, the Imam insists on the righteousness of his path, citing the Quran and relying on his unique spiritual insight.
This segment of the program highlights the profound spiritual depth and political strategy of Imam Hussein (PBUH) on the eve of the event of Karbala.
