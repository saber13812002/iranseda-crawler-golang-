This radio program examines why Imam Ali (PBUH) selected Kufa as his capital after the Battle of Jamal. The first reason was gratitude towards the people of Kufa, who had supported him during the battle. Secondly, Kufa served as a crucial military hub where Ansar and various tribes were stationed, which was essential for confronting Muawiyah. The economic wealth and fertile land of Iraq also offered significant advantages over the arid regions of Medina. Geographically, Kufa's proximity to Syria allowed for a faster response to Muawiyah's potential movements. In contrast, Medina was not a secure or supportive environment due to the presence of hostile remnants and hypocrites. Historical accounts indicate that Ali had very few supporters in Medina during the Battle of Jamal. Consequently, Kufa was the logical choice due to its combined military, economic, and security benefits. This strategic decision was vital for consolidating power and preparing for future conflicts.
