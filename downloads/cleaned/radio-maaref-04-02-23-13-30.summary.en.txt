In the radio program "Mashq-e Rah," Hujjat al-Islam Seyyed Ahmad Faqih discussed the importance of spiritual presence and self-reflection during prayer. He emphasized that supplication requires a sincere intention and a clean appearance, illustrating this with a story of a drunkard who found spiritual awakening when he realized the contradiction between his sins and his prayer. Addressing a listener's question, the expert advised reducing material desires to make room for the genuine spiritual need for God in one's heart. He also distinguished between the obsession with academic degrees and a true love for knowledge, suggesting that reciting specific dhikr can purify one's intentions without inducing harmful anxiety. The program concluded with a brief historical reference to Imam Khomeini's protest against the capitulation treaty imposed by the United States during the Pahlavi era.
