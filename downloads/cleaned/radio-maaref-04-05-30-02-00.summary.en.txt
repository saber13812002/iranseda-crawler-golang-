The radio program begins by highlighting the solidarity of Iranians with the people of Gaza, urging listeners to contribute via a provided bank card number. Hujjat al-Islam Vahidi then emphasizes three pillars of a believer's pride based on Imam Sadiq's teachings: night prayer, indifference to others' material wealth, and adherence to the Wilayah of the Ahl al-Bayt. The core of the broadcast features the moving testimony of Mrs. Yaqoubi, the mother of martyr Mehdi, who recounts her son's decision to join the resistance in Syria. She describes the emotional farewell, her maternal prayers, and the anxious wait for his return during the Day of Arafah. The narrative concludes with the announcement of Mehdi's martyrdom while serving the resistance, portraying it as the ultimate sacrifice. This story serves to honor the dedication and selflessness of the mothers and wives of martyrs in the path of Imam Hussein and Lady Zainab.
