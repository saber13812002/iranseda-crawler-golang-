The radio program "Mashq-e Rah," featuring ethics professor Seyed Ahmad Faghihi, explored the concept of monotheistic ethics and the central role of prayer and salawat in spiritual cultivation. Professor Faghihi emphasized that sending salawat to the Prophet and his family is not merely a ritual but a vital path to fostering divine love and connecting with God. Citing the Quran, he identified love for the Ahl al-Bayt as the essential condition for benefiting from divine education and moral reform. In the Q&A segment, lack of patience was attributed to internal chaos and life disorganization, with focusing on God and establishing routine suggested as effective remedies. Furthermore, having high ambition was linked to relying on God's infinite power rather than one's own limited capabilities. Finally, the program highlighted the innate human ability to distinguish ugliness through intuition and reason, stressing the need for strong will to resist base desires and adhere to moral truths.
