In this program, the speaker emphasizes the importance of maintaining etiquette and mutual respect within families and society, citing religious narrations. He strongly advises against insulting children or using vulgar language, describing such behavior as a sign of a lack of personality and inner emptiness. Using the metaphor of an empty versus a full glass, he explains that individuals lacking character react aggressively to minor provocations. The speaker also highlights the necessity of controlling anger and avoiding making decisions while upset, as these actions cause deep emotional wounds and potential physical health issues. Subsequently, stories from the lives of Imam Hassan and Habib ibn Mazahir are narrated, focusing on the betrayal of the people of Kufa and the tragedy of Muslim ibn Awsaja. These historical accounts are presented to teach lessons on patience and resilience in the face of adversity. Finally, the speaker prays to God for eloquent speech, soft hearts, and enduring patience, urging Muslims to avoid sinful speech and improper behavior.
