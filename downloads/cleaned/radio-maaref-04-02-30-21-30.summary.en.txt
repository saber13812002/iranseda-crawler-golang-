This program interprets the Quranic verse "O you who believe, take care of your own souls" to explain the spiritual journey as an internal process. In this path, the human soul serves simultaneously as the traveler, the distance, the vehicle, and the provisions, with night prayer acting as a vehicle driven by desire for God or paradise. True life is defined not by chronological time but by spiritual progress and movement in this inner journey. The core of the discussion is the rational self, which must triumph over lust and anger in the internal struggle to maintain balance. A just intellect manages the soul's faculties, including imagination, to create order in both waking life and dreams. Forgetting God equates to forgetting one's authentic human self, leading to the dominance of animal instincts. Finally, the presence of a just leader is deemed essential for guiding groups of people toward their ultimate destination.
