The program begins with the recitation of the "Amraz" prayer and the repeated slogan "Al-Islam is the only one." It then highlights the spiritual significance of feeding the faithful, citing anecdotes from Ayatollah Marashi Najafi and Ayatollah Shahbadi regarding hospitality and divine intervention. The main segment features a narrative from the book "Hadith of the Sun," depicting a man named Aman stranded in a desert. Aman, on the verge of death from thirst and despair, encounters a mysterious man who claims to be sent by Imam Reza (AS). The stranger provides him with water and reveals the existence of a "Spring of Life" within a fortress. He instructs Aman that eternal life can only be achieved by reaching this fortress and seeking guidance from a tailor. The stranger then vanishes, leaving Aman with the water gourd and a newfound realization of the truth.
