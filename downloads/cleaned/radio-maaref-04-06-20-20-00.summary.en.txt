The "War on Terror" defense exhibition opened in London with 1,600 participating companies and Israeli officials, sparking severe protests by Palestine supporters that were met with police crackdowns. The UK government faces human rights criticism and declining popularity due to its support for Israel and suppression of demonstrators. Concurrently, the Israeli regime launched a missile strike on the Hamas headquarters in Qatar, targeting its leaders. This attack, which drew strong international reactions and comparisons to Iran's stance, highlighted the alleged betrayal of Qatar by its ally, the United States. Yemen retaliated against Israeli attacks on Gaza and Yemen by targeting occupied settlements with missiles. In Gaza, intense aerial bombardments have killed dozens of civilians and created an urgent humanitarian crisis for over two million people. The UN warned that no safe place remains in Gaza, and the humanitarian situation has become critically dire.
