In this radio program, Professor Tala'at Javadi, relying on the Tafsir al-Mizan, identifies the two main messages of the Quran as the explanation of monotheism and the cultivation of the perfect human being. Citing verses from the Surahs of Al-Inshiqaq and Al-Ma'ad, he describes humans as travelers in constant motion who must engage in self-purification and continuous effort to reach their ultimate destination: meeting God. A key point in the discussion is the clear distinction between true "enjoining good," which involves practical struggle against corruption, and mere preaching or sermons, which should not be confused. It is also explained that Quranic concepts such as speed, haste, and guidance indicate the existence of a specific and straight path for spiritual movement. Finally, it is emphasized that the main path of spiritual journeying is internal, and external acts of worship like prayer and fasting are merely prerequisites for finding this inner path.
