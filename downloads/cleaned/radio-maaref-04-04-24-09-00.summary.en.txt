In this radio program, Professor Mohammadi emphasizes the necessity of standing on the side of justice and opposing tyranny, drawing parallels with the narrative of Karbala. He argues that reason and human nature, rather than race or nationality, are the true criteria for distinguishing right from wrong, noting that even non-Muslims can align with justice. The speaker highlights the enduring impact of Imam Hussein's uprising on human history and its role in Iran's victories in recent conflicts. In the second segment, a listener's question is addressed regarding the distinction between "Zabur" as a generic term for heavenly books and "Zabur of David" as a specific title. The program concludes with an introduction to the audio documentary "The Liar Shepherd," which uses historical documents to debunk the European origins of this myth and refute false claims about liars entering Iran.
