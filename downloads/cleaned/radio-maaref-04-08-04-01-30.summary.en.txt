Hojjat al-Islam Agha Tehrani emphasized the necessity of resistance, steadfastness, and maintaining hijab as both religious and political duties, drawing upon the legacy of Imam Hussein and Lady Zainab. He cited a narration stating that the Prophet's resistance was the cause of his spiritual aging and urged the audience to rely on God and follow the Wilayat to stand against enemies. The speaker recounted international protests against Israel's presence, highlighting Iran's strengthened resistance power compared to the past, and expressed appreciation for supporting the Lebanese people and their martyrs. In the second segment, Sardar Haj Hossein Arooj shared memories from the early days of the Iran-Iraq War, including the arrival of 700 untrained and unarmed volunteers in Khuzestan. He described the harsh frontline conditions, such as long night marches and tactical deceptions used to mislead the enemy in the Susangerd region. The program also featured the story of Gholamali Javan, who tore the Shah's crown, lost his sons during exile and war, and was later martyred. The broadcast concluded with prayers for the reappearance of the Imam and tribute to the veterans of the eight-year war.
