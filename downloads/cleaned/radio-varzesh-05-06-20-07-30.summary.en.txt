Seyyed Mohammad Mehdi Seyedsalaei recounts his peak years at Esteghlal and the devastating ACL injury he sustained while in top form. He discusses the frustrations of his time at Tractor Sazi, highlighting conflicts with coaches who favored training players over experienced match players. Seyedsalaei explains his decision to reject a move to Persepolis out of respect for fans and his personal integrity, despite his impressive goal-scoring record. He admits to making significant transfer mistakes with Saipa and Gostaresh Foolad, while expressing disappointment at the lack of respect from some club officials after helping Esteghlal Khuzestan avoid relegation. The former player announces his retirement from football and his acquisition of a coaching license, lamenting the lack of opportunities to manage major Iranian clubs. Finally, he shares his regret about not being visible to his young son during his playing days and expresses his acceptance of his current fate.
