This radio program began by recounting the memories of Iranian POWs in Iraqi camps, highlighting their steadfastness in prayer and Quran recitation amidst torture and psychological pressure.
The captives demonstrated remarkable resilience by using creative methods, such as tying knots in strings to count verses and writing Quranic verses on cigarette paper, to evade enemy inspections.
Their deep faith in God was identified as the primary source of their patience and endurance, allowing them to maintain their spiritual integrity despite harsh conditions.
The second part of the program analyzed narrations from the book of Kafi regarding the concepts of contentment and sufficiency.
These narrations emphasize a direct correlation between the amount of sustenance one receives and the volume of religious obligations they must fulfill.
The core message is that contentment is not passivity but a conscious acceptance of God's decree, which leads to pure earnings and a distance from sin.
The program concluded with the recitation of the 14th supplication from the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya and religious poetry, urging listeners to pray for the freedom and well-being of all.
