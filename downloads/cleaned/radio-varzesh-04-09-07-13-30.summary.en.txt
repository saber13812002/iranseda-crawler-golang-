Ashoor Gholamohammadi, a veteran basketball player and coach, shared his career highlights in a radio interview. He recalled playing alongside stars like Mohammad Kasaeipour and Ibrahimi in the Premier League and Azad University teams. Gholamohammadi highlighted the golden era of university sports under the leadership of Dr. Ghadimi and Dr. Dadkan, which saw multiple championships. He emphasized that Hamed Haddadi was instrumental in the national team's success, noting that achieving such honors without him would have been extremely difficult. The guest also expressed gratitude for the strong support provided to university teams for international competitions during that period. After retiring in 2018, he took on managerial roles at clubs like Pیکان and Noobagh. He identified the Asian Championship victory with the national team as his proudest moment. Finally, he stressed the importance of preserving the golden generation of Iranian basketball players.
