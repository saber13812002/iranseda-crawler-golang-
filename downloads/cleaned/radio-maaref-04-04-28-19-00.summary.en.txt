In the "Zamzam Ahkam" program, several religious questions were addressed. A follower of Ayatollah Makarem asked if raising one's voice for dhikr during prayer to inform someone else is permissible; the answer was that it is allowed if the dhikr is in Arabic and intended as such. Regarding eye drops during Ramadan, Hujjat al-Islam Mohammadi Sheikh Shabani, citing Imam Khomeini, stated that the fast remains valid if one is not certain that the taste reached the throat. On the topic of mahr and Hajj, Hujjat al-Islam Rabdini clarified that a wife is not considered financially capable (mustata') for Hajj until her mahr is actually paid to her. Additionally, Hujjat al-Islam Falsadeh emphasized that if any part of the face, such as the lips, is not wetted during wudu, the wudu is invalid and the subsequent prayer must be repeated. In the final segment, Seyyed Reza Tehrani, referencing a narration from Imam Sadiq, identified three key criteria for identifying a true Shia: praying at the first time, keeping secrets, and showing financial solidarity with fellow believers. The program aimed to resolve religious doubts and promote the importance of early prayer on Radio Ma'aref.
