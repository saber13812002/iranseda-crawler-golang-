This radio program commemorated the birth of Imam Ali ibn Musa al-Ridha and Eid Qadir, highlighting the massive public turnout in Mashhad and the celebration of the holiday in six thousand mosques across Iran. Expert Seyyed Mohammad Taghi Qadri discussed the unique features of the "Ziyarat Qadir," a prayer composed by Imam Hadi during the peak of Imam Ali's oppression. The President emphasized the need to enhance psychological security and counter cognitive warfare in cyberspace by leveraging community resources for positive content. The return of Iranian pilgrims from Mecca began with 343 scheduled flights, while 95 percent of container schools are set to be dismantled by the end of the month. Commemorative events were held in Qom for Martyr Ayatollah Saeedi and 28 other martyrs, and an international conference on Martyr Nasrallah was finalized. The Endowments Organization announced the start of Arbaeen services from August 5, focusing on accommodation, food, and cultural activities. Finally, listeners were encouraged to participate in cultural contests and receive related books by sending text messages to the specified numbers.
