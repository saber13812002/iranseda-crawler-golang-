The program began with a narrative highlighting the courage and steadfastness of Ayatollah Qazi Tabatabai against the Pahlavi regime, which led to his exile and subsequent triumphant return to Tabriz. In the jurisprudential segment, the importance of respecting public rights and avoiding disturbances during mourning ceremonies and Mawlid gatherings was emphasized. Religious rulings addressed listener questions regarding the impurity of sinks, the disposal of profits from fraud, accepting food from children, and the correct criteria for selecting a Marja. Additionally, rulings on selling old inventory and adding unknown numbers to channels were explained based on the fatwas of religious authorities. The program concluded with a research discussion on the reasons for the Imam of the Age's occultation and the absence of a Jewish government during the era of reappearance.
