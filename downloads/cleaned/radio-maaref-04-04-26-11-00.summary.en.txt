The tenth gathering of people-oriented activists commenced in Khuzestan with 600 participants aiming to combat the Zionist regime. Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi condemned the martyrdom of Sheikh Roushoul Shohoud, a prominent Syrian scholar, by terrorists. The head of the Martyrs Foundation announced the final count of martyrs in the twelve-day war as 1,062. The secretary of the Guardian Council emphasized the necessity and wisdom of the body in overseeing elections and interpreting the constitution. The Supreme Leader, during a meeting with the Judiciary, stressed the importance of international pursuit of recent crimes and building public trust through anti-corruption efforts. He attributed Iran's national resistance against the US and Zionism to the nation's will and self-confidence. The Leader declared the enemy's calculations to weaken the system through internal chaos as nullified. He viewed Iran's crushing response as a testament to the country's diplomatic and military strength.
