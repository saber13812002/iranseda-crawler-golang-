This radio program features a lecture on a letter from Imam Mahdi to Sheikh Mufid, addressing all Shia followers.
The Imam issues two primary commands: to perform deeds that foster love and closeness to him, and to avoid actions that cause divine displeasure.
Acts of kindness, sincere worship, and good character are highlighted as the means to earn the Imam's affection and joy.
Conversely, hypocrisy, envy, oppression, and disrespect towards believers are identified as behaviors that must be strictly avoided.
The Imam emphasizes that his reappearance will be sudden and unexpected, leaving no time for last-minute repentance to escape punishment.
Therefore, believers are urged to repent and improve their conduct while they still have the opportunity to receive the Imam's prayers and divine success.
The second part of the program includes a brief Quranic commentary on verses from Surah Al-Imran regarding the miracle of Prophet Jesus speaking in his cradle.
This interpretation illustrates that God's will is not limited by age, as even infants can use divine speech to prove their innocence and truth.
