At the 33rd International Quran Exhibition, a specialized book from the Holy Shrine of Imam Hussein containing five thousand questions on Quranic ambiguities was unveiled. In Paris, the trial of women supporting Palestine continued, with one defendant wearing a dress made of Palestinian scarves and inviting eyewitnesses to testify against Zionist crimes. The Al-Rahman Mosque in Cairo was inaugurated after four decades of construction, featuring a 124-meter minaret and architecture inspired by the Hagia Sophia. Muslims in Scotland and Venezuela actively observed Ramadan through communal iftars and prayers in major mosques, highlighting their religious presence. Border tensions between Afghanistan and Pakistan escalated along the Durand Line, with both sides blaming each other for initiating military clashes and airstrikes. In Bulgaria and the Netherlands, initiatives were launched to support Islamic education and introduce the faith to non-Muslims to combat Islamophobia and foster coexistence. The program concluded with a Quranic exegesis of Surah Al-Anfal, emphasizing the concept of "But it was Allah who threw" and the role of divine will in victory and the testimony of body parts on Judgment Day.
