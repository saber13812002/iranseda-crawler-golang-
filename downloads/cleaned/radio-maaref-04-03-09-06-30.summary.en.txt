In this radio program, Ayatollah Sifii Mazandarani utilizes the principle of contextual clues to interpret verses from the Quran. He illustrates how ambiguous or polysemous words can only be understood by analyzing the surrounding sentences. The discussion then focuses on the verse "Iyyaka na'budu wa iyyaka nasta'in," which is cited in authentic narrations as the most effective means for fulfilling needs and drawing closer to God. The speaker explains that worship, in its literal sense, signifies ultimate humility and submission devoid of any show or external motivation. Furthermore, he emphasizes that obedience to God is a primary manifestation of worship, requiring divine assistance to overcome internal obstacles. The danger of mistaking one's inner enemy, the ego or Satan, for one's own desires is also highlighted. Consequently, the program concludes that total reliance on God and avoiding dependence on other sources are essential for spiritual success.
