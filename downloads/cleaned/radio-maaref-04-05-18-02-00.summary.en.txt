In the first segment of the program, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the importance of eating moderately and living simply to maintain both physical health and spiritual clarity, citing the practices of Islamic leaders like Ayatollah Khorasani and Imam Khomeini. In the second segment, Dr. Majid Behestani described the recent Iran-Europe talks in Istanbul as a continuation of indirect negotiations with the US aimed at containing Iran. He explained that the "snapback" mechanism poses a psychological and economic threat by potentially revoking UN Security Council resolutions and imposing new military and trade restrictions. According to him, European involvement serves both the shared Western project of containment and their desire to assert their global relevance. The expert advised Iran to "vaccinate" its domestic economy against external shocks by reducing dependence on the US dollar and promoting bilateral trade agreements. Furthermore, he suggested that strengthening national spirit and focusing on domestic capacities rather than relying on transit corridors are essential strategies to mitigate the impact of sanctions and political pressure.
