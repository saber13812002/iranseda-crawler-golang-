In this radio program, Dr. Mohammad Ali Laval discussed the political and economic challenges facing the Islamic Republic of Iran. He emphasized that expanding trade relations with China and Russia is a strategic necessity driven by the unilateral sanctions imposed by the US and Europe. Citing the decline of the US dollar and the rise of the Chinese Yuan, Laval argued that aligning with the East better secures Iran's national interests than the West. He also described Iran's missile program as a non-negotiable achievement and a primary deterrent that has preserved the country's national security. Furthermore, he characterized the defection of some revolutionaries as a natural phenomenon in any system, noting that it does not harm the revolution itself. Finally, he viewed the approval of Iran's accession to the CFT Convention as a crucial step to remove Western pretexts for financial sanctions and to integrate into the international banking system.
