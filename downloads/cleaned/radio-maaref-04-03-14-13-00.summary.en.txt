Hojjat al-Islam Mirbagheri outlines the fundamental difference between Imam Khomeini's strategy and previous reformers in addressing the crises of the Islamic world. While earlier reformers sought modernization through liberal and Marxist ideologies, the Imam identified these very schools of thought as the root of moral decay and spiritual alienation. The Imam's approach was to return to the teachings of the prophets and move toward the realization of the Age of the Mahdi, which required confronting global arrogance and internal deviations. He believed that the hardships and costs of this path were not signs of failure but rather catalysts for greater divine success. Citing the hadith of "a man from a nation," the speaker describes the followers of this movement as steadfast, unyielding against enemy storms, and fully reliant on God. Mirbagheri concludes that this steadfastness has weakened the West's soft power and led to the failure of sanctions. Ultimately, the future of this movement is destined to be in favor of the pious.
