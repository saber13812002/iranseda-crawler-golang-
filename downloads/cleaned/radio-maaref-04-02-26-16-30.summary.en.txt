The radio program begins by discussing the lineage and life of Lady Hakeema, the mother of Imam Reza (PBUH), who is traditionally believed to be of Roman origin. According to narrations, she was captured during wars between Muslims and the Eastern Roman Empire, purchased by Imam Hadi (PBUH), and later became the mother of Imam Reza (PBUH). She is noted as a key witness to the secret birth of Imam Reza (PBUH) in Samarra and played a crucial role in raising Imam Mahdi (PBUH) during his early years. The instructor then addressed why few people witnessed the birth of Imam Mahdi (PBUH), attributing this to the severe political threats and security risks of the time. The discussion also covered the life of Prophet Zechariah (PBUH), who was appointed as the guardian of Maryam (PBUH) and later became the father of Prophet Yahya (PBUH), both of whom were eventually martyred. Furthermore, several hadiths from the book of Kafi were presented to highlight the virtue of gentleness and patience in social interactions. The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) emphasized that kindness prevents anger from leading to disbelief, using the analogy of caring for exhausted animals to illustrate the need for compassion toward vulnerable individuals. The segment concluded by urging listeners to respond to others' mistakes with patience and courtesy rather than aggression.
