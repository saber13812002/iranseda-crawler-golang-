In the radio program "Bar Bal Sakhn," Hojjat al-Islam Rezaei Tehrani outlined five fundamental characteristics of a true believer based on verses 2 to 4 of Surah Al-Anfal. The first condition is the deep rooting of faith in the heart, which trembles at the mention of God, acting as a solid foundation that prevents spiritual collapse during life's crises. The second trait is the continuous growth of faith when engaging with divine verses, ensuring that one's spiritual state remains dynamic rather than stagnant. The third pillar is proper reliance on God, defined not as abandoning responsibility but as aligning one's life with the Absolute Attorney while actively fulfilling one's duties. The fourth requirement is establishing prayer as the pillar of religion, which must be performed with presence of mind and substance, not merely as a superficial habit. The fifth feature is spending from what God has provided, as recognizing God's ownership over one's wealth makes generosity effortless. The speaker emphasized that these five pillars constitute the core infrastructure of true faith, without which outward religious appearances are ineffective against challenges. The discussion concluded with a call to evaluate oneself and others against these criteria, along with prayers for the Mahdi's reappearance and the victory of the resistance front.
