This program provided theological answers to listeners' questions regarding fate, suffering, and the relationship between science and religion. The first response clarified that human destiny is determined by free will rather than divine compulsion, while acknowledging the significant influence of the prenatal period and spouse selection on an individual's spiritual path. Regarding accidents and misfortunes, the speaker explained that these events have multiple causes and should not be attributed solely to God's will, though prayers and charity remain spiritually effective. Listeners were advised to practice patience during grief and consult the book "Maskan al-Fuad" for emotional healing. The discussion on science and religion distinguished between the formation of the universe and the emergence of the current human lineage, noting that scientific theories like the Big Bang are hypotheses that do not contradict established religious principles. It was emphasized that Islam is compatible with verified science and highlights divine wisdom in the evolution of the cosmos. Finally, the segment on ethics described the believer as a wise and vigilant individual, illustrated by stories of respectful interactions between pious merchants and religious scholars.
