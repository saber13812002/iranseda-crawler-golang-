In this program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verses 43 to 45 of Surah Al-A'raf, focusing on the spiritual status of the inhabitants of Paradise. He explains that gratitude and greetings in Paradise are driven by intrinsic pleasure rather than religious obligation, unlike in the worldly life. The blessed individuals possess an angelic nature, free from envy, and find joy in striving for higher spiritual ranks. The sermon also highlights how divine revelation guides human intellect to understand both general principles and hidden details, with both being divine gifts. The concept of "inheritance" in the Quran signifies a profound connection with God and divine vicegerency, rather than merely receiving free property. Finally, the dialogue between the people of Paradise and Hell occurs after the judgment is complete and with God's explicit permission. This interaction serves as a moral vindication and a confirmation of God's promises, where the blessed express their wonder at fulfilling divine assurances.
