This discussion introduces reliance on God as a crucial tool for mental management and reducing anxiety. Tawakkul does not mean abandoning effort; rather, it is like children who act with complete peace because they trust their parents' support. A key point is that during adolescence, this trust must be transferred from parents to God to prevent feelings of helplessness. If the desired outcome is not achieved despite reliance and effort, the next step is "acceptance of divine decree." Accepting scarcity and lawful sustenance makes life easier and allows more blessings to flow toward the individual. Islamic narrations emphasize that submitting to God's will is the key to achieving one's true desires. Ultimately, accepting divine decree frees the mind from rumination and brings inner peace.
