The program began by congratulating Iran's armed forces for their missile operation, followed by a tafsir of verses 114 to 116 of Surah Al-Ma'idah. The commentator explained that divine sustenance encompasses both material and spiritual needs, noting that the miracle of the Ma'ida reinforced the disciples' faith after receiving revelation. The discussion focused on the verse where God asks Jesus if he invited people to worship him and his mother. Citing Zamakhshari's Kashshaf, the speaker argued that "man duna Allah" does not imply the denial of God, as God's essence is undeniable and differences lie only in His attributes. He asserted that early Christianity deviated due to Jewish slander against Mary and the deification of Jesus. The commentator highlighted the Quran's role in purifying religions and guiding Christians back to monotheism, observing that deviant groups are gradually disappearing. Finally, he distinguished the philosophical concept of the Necessary Being from the Dahrists' view, which sees the universe as a transient and changing aggregate.
