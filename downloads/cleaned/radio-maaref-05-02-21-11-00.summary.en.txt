Parliamentary committee members emphasized that despite enemy propaganda, the stock market and production sectors remained stable during the third imposed war, even witnessing capital inflows and supply surges in certain industries. The head of the Article 90 Commission attributed car price hikes to the opportunism of some groups and announced strict market controls to prevent exploitation. Regarding reconstruction, the head of the Housing Foundation in Tehran Province reported that over 90 percent of damaged residential units in Tehran and Pardis have begun the rebuilding process. The Power Holding Company supported the industrial sector by waiving over-consumption penalties and increasing electricity allocations for factories. The Basij Construction Organization facilitated employment for 500 people through loans, while local producers demonstrated economic resilience by exporting goods to Iraq and Turkey. In the diplomatic sphere, Iranian officials stressed their commitment to national interests and condemned the United States' repeated breaches of good faith in negotiations. Furthermore, the Taliban's stance of neutrality regarding the Iran-US conflict was criticized, with a strong call for the international community to take a firm stand against the violations of international law by the US and the Zionist regime.
