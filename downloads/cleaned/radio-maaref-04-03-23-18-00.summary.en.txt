In this program, Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallah discusses the dangerous vice of arrogance and its underlying causes. He explains that arrogance stems from ignorance of oneself, lack of piety, self-admiration, and an attempt to compensate for internal feelings of inferiority. Fallah emphasizes that officials who display arrogance are actually rooted in sin and lack of piety, which damages their social standing. Citing narrations from Imam Ali and Imam Sadiq, he clarifies that every arrogant person is inherently humble and uses arrogance to mask their internal deficiencies. The discussion also notes that environment, friends, and nature can play a role in shaping this negative trait. At the beginning of the program, there is a reference to the difficult situation of people in shelters and prayers for the reappearance of the Imam and the destruction of the Zionist regime. Finally, listeners are encouraged to guard their souls and practice piety to avoid falling into the trap of arrogance.
