In this radio program, Dr. Mohammad Shojayan emphasizes the cultural and religious roots of hijab, viewing it as a religious obligation that the global hegemony attempts to politicize to weaken the family institution. He argues that alongside cultural efforts, the strict enforcement of Islamic laws by the state's branches is essential, noting that officials' recent negligence has exacerbated the issue. Shojayan also highlights the capture of an American smart submarine as a testament to Iran's military prowess and a blow to Trump's psychological warfare, revealing enemy technology through reverse engineering. Addressing public inquiries, he affirms the fundamental role of the people in the Velayat system, asserting that governance rests with the people's elected representatives rather than a ruling elite. He attributes economic hardships and inflation primarily to the government's flawed policies, such as currency floating and adherence to IMF programs, rather than just sanctions. Finally, he calls for unity among officials in enforcing hijab laws and correcting economic strategies to alleviate the public's financial burdens.
