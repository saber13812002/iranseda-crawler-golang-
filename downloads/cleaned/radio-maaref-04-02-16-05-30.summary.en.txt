The program begins with a jurisprudential discussion regarding the ownership of properties and the role of the religious authority in managing endowments.
It then highlights the days of Karamat and the birth of Imam Reza (PBUH), mentioning pilgrimages to Mashhad and Qom as well as charitable initiatives for needy couples.
The main segment features Ayatollah Mazaheri, who emphasizes the importance of seeking intercession from the Ahl al-Bayt and participating in mourning gatherings for healing and blessings.
He cites narrations illustrating how intercession with Imam Ali (PBUH) has led to the recovery of patients and the fulfillment of needs.
The speaker attributes the diminished effectiveness of these practices to social sins and moral corruption within society.
According to the Quran, widespread social sins prevent divine mercy from descending, causing even righteous individuals to suffer from general calamities.
Finally, the audience is advised to avoid sins and recite prayers like Kumayl to restore the conditions for divine blessings and answered prayers.
