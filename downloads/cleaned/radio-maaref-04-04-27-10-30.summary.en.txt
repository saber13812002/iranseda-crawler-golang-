In response to doubts regarding the long life of Imam Mahdi, the discussion cites Quranic verses and authentic narrations confirming that God has granted extended lifespans to figures like Noah, Jonah, and Khidr. Allameh Tabatabaei describes this phenomenon as a miracle that is not logically impossible, while modern science also acknowledges the potential to halt aging processes. The second part of the program emphasizes the critical need for steadfastness in supporting the Imam during the period of occultation. Current challenges posed by the virtual space and cognitive colonization have made maintaining faith more difficult than in previous eras. Therefore, believers are urged to emulate the resilience of Imam Hussein's companions, such as Habib ibn Mazahir, to withstand contemporary cognitive and worldly harms.
