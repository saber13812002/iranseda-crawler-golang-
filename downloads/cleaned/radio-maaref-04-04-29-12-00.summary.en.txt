This radio program began by interpreting a hadith from Imam Ali regarding the meaning of the prayer call (Iqamah), describing prayer as a visit, supplication, fulfillment of needs, and a connection to God. Following a Quran recitation and the Adhan, the discussion shifted to the Islamic duty of enjoining good and forbidding evil. The program recounted the story of the late Sheikh Mohammad Taghi Bahouli, who, while imprisoned in Kabul, agreed to preach at a gathering only after ensuring that men and women were separated according to Islamic rules. It also highlighted the bravery of Ayatollah Bafghi, who defied the Pahlavi government's ban on this religious duty by publicly reading their decree and asserting that it is a divine command no tyrannical state can suppress. These narratives emphasized the importance of religious courage and adherence to Sharia in difficult circumstances.
