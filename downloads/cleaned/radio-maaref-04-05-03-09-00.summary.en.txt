In this radio program, Professor Jabbari analyzes the symbolism of clothing colors in Islamic history. He explains that the Abbasids adopted black as their symbol to leverage the public's desire for revenge from Karbala and legitimize their rule. Conversely, Al-Ma'mun used green, the symbol of Ali (PBUH), to demonstrate his connection to the Ahl al-Bayt. Following the martyrdom of Imam Reza (PBUH) and the return to Baghdad, Al-Ma'mun reverted to the black symbol of the Abbasids. The program also discussed the Supreme Leader's fatwa prohibiting shaving or machine-trimming beards for his followers. Additionally, the family of Martyr Sardar Nozari shared their memories of his martyrdom and its impact on national unity. Finally, listeners were encouraged to participate in the "I am the Voice of Iran" campaign by submitting their personal stories.
