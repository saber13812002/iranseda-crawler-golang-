This radio program discusses the concept of knowing God through the clear mirror of the soul, free from the dust of sin.
Citing Imam Ali's sermons and authentic narrations, the text emphasizes that the Divine Essence cannot be fully grasped by pure intellect or mental analogies.
God is known only through Himself, and the entire creation serves as a constant, ontological sign pointing to Him.
Unlike limited human conventions, divine signs are permanent and truthful in all circumstances.
Self-knowledge is presented as the primary key to knowing God, while forgetting oneself is considered the greatest form of ignorance.
Achieving this awareness requires daily self-monitoring to keep the soul's mirror clean and prevent spiritual negligence.
Ultimately, resolving theological and practical issues depends on overcoming cognitive barriers and returning to the correct path of recognition.
