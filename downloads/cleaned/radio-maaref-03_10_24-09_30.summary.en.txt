The radio program initially explored the psychological aspects of lying in children and adolescents, noting that it often signifies cognitive development and imagination unless it becomes a repeated strategy to evade responsibility.
Parents were advised to create a safe environment that accepts mistakes, using interactive and play-based methods to correct behavior rather than relying on punishment.
The discussion also highlighted the importance of setting realistic expectations and teaching children cognitive-emotional skills to manage stress and stubbornness effectively.
The second part of the program focused on the educational and upbringing methods of Ayatollah Moshtahed Tehrani, who emphasized order, simplicity, and meticulous attention to the ethics of his students.
His students recalled his intense dedication to night prayers and his strict monitoring of their behavior, dress, and daily conduct.
His teaching approach for Arabic and Quranic interpretation relied on practical exercises and analyzing verses, which enhanced his students' language proficiency and spiritual depth.
This upbringing style resulted in his students being recognized as pious, disciplined individuals who transformed their families and communities.
Ultimately, the program concluded that a mentor's practical example and sincerity in behavior are the primary keys to influencing hearts and raising the next generation.
