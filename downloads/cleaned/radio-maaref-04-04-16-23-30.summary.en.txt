In this radio program, the expert criticizes Western parenting models, such as assigning private rooms to children, emphasizing the need to avoid fostering a sense of ownership in offspring. He argues that dedicating rooms, phones, or even bathrooms to a child creates harmful boundaries that negatively impact their upbringing. Instead, he suggests that spaces and devices should be viewed as belonging to the entire family to teach children the concepts of sharing and household transparency. Furthermore, parents' behavior regarding their own privacy, such as locking phones, serves as a direct model for children, so invisible walls within the home must be avoided. The expert stresses that proper upbringing requires familiarity with its principles, and one cannot expect appropriate behavior from teenagers without adhering to these guidelines. Finally, families are advised to gradually and calmly dismantle these incorrect boundaries to raise healthier children.
