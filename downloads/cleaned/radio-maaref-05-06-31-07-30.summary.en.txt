In the radio program "Mehrbān Bāshim," the broadcast honored the memory of Hazrat Fatima Masumeh and the Sacred Defense Week, emphasizing the need for constant vigilance against enemies and the affirmation of Iranian identity. A segment on meteorology reported strong winds in Tehran and issued an orange marine warning for the northern coasts. The main feature, "Ganj-e Sa'adat," focused on the third factor of arrogance: the whispers of Jinn devils. The cleric explained that the Quran identifies Satan as an open enemy who operates through worldly decorations and by exploiting the human ego. The core message was that the solution to resisting Satan lies in internal self-management and spiritual struggle to prevent humans from becoming tools of the enemy. Finally, it was noted that on Judgment Day, Satan will attribute the cause of misguidance to humans themselves, who willingly accepted his invitation and thus brought about their own punishment.
