The program begins with an exegesis of the Mubahala verse from Surah Al-Imran, recounting the debate with the Christians of Najran and the presence of Ali, Fatima, Hasan, and Husayn as the Prophet's "souls."
The speaker emphasizes Ali's unique status as the Prophet's soul and the core of Wilayat, citing interpretations from both Shia and Sunni scholars.
A narration from the Prophet's Mi'raj is shared, describing how Ali's virtues were carried on camels and his name was repeated in the gates of Paradise.
In this account, God speaks to the Prophet in Ali's tone, and angels recite tasbih out of their love for Ali.
The story of Ali purifying the Kaaba of idols and his subsequent weeping is presented as a symbol of his deep devotion and proximity to the Prophet.
The speaker concludes by praying for the early return of Imam Zaman and stressing the necessity of adhering to Wilayat to strengthen resistance against enemies.
The discourse ends with greetings to Imam Zaman and blessings for the Shia community.
