The program begins by emphasizing the importance of preserving self-esteem and avoiding financial dependence on others, citing the lives of scholars like Sheikh Zahed and Ayatollah Marashi Najafi as examples of dignity in poverty. The second segment features a narrative from the book "Let the Storm Blow," where Hur al-Karam, on the eve of the Battle of Karbala, learns of Umar ibn Sa'd's army and the fear among the people of Kufa. Hur attempts to negotiate with Umar to prevent an unequal war against Imam Hussein (AS), advocating for ruling over hearts rather than using force. In the religious Q&A section, the ruling on heirs using a deceased person's assets before settling debts is explained, noting it is permissible if there is an immediate intention to pay. Practical tips for saving water and electricity, such as checking for leaks and adjusting air conditioning temperatures, are also provided. The broadcast concludes by announcing the next program, "Barakane Noor," which will feature a tafsir of a verse from Surah Al-Ma'idah by Ayatollah Javadi Amoli.
