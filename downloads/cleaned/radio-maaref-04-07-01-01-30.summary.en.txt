In this radio program, the host cites Quranic teachings and narrations to identify worry and grief as the primary causes of premature aging and reduced spiritual life.
He criticizes baseless fears about the future and regret over the past, emphasizing the need for proper emotional management and a focus on the present moment.
Referring to Quranic stories and hadiths, he presents faith and steadfastness as the only solutions for finding peace and receiving divine blessings.
The program then features a narrative from the book "Ocean of the East" about the journey of Amran and Hajj Karim the cobbler in search of the water of life.
In this story, Hajj Karim uses logical and historical arguments to enlighten a Sunni man about the virtues of the Ahl al-Bayt and the necessity of knowing Imam Reza (PBUH).
Hajj Karim recounts a miracle where Imam Reza (PBUH) prayed for rain, demonstrating his power and high status.
This narrative causes the listener to question his beliefs and ultimately leads him to embrace the school of the Ahl al-Bayt.
Ultimately, the program's main message is that knowing the Ahl al-Bayt and managing one's life correctly are the keys to achieving peace and worldly and otherworldly happiness.
