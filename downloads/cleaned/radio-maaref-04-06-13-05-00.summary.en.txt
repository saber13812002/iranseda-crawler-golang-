Modesty is an innate divine trait that reflects true knowledge and deters humans from sinning before God, angels, and the Holy Family.
Religious texts emphasize the constant presence of angels who record human deeds and assist in removing calamities.
Achieving the rank of modesty and piety brings divine blessings and angelic respect, as illustrated by the story of Sheikh Abbas Qumi.
The jurisprudential section discusses the validity of testimony and the conditions of a witness's justice during the incident and the testimony.
Muhakk al-Hilli's view holds that justice is required at the time of giving testimony, but the witness need not have been just when the incident occurred.
Conversely, intellectual maturity and sound mind must be present both when witnessing the event and when providing the testimony.
This distinction between different conditions facilitates the resolution of judicial issues and prevents unnecessary strictness.
The final conclusion is that a judge's ruling based on testimony given while the witness was just remains valid, even if the witness later becomes sinful.
