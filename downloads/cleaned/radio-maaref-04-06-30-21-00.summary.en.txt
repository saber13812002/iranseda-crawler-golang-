In the Parsaiyan program, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the necessity of asceticism and detachment from the world to achieve true faith, citing relevant narrations. He illustrated this concept through the simple and pious lifestyle of Imam Khomeini, clarifying that asceticism means avoiding attachment to wealth beyond one's basic needs rather than absolute deprivation. Imam Khomeini's refusal to travel to Kufa for health reasons, out of solidarity with the suffering people, highlighted his profound detachment from worldly comforts. The program then transitioned to a book narrative from "Ocean of the East," featuring a debate between a cobbler and a Sunni man regarding the concept of Imamate. The cobbler argued logically that the Imam is essential for interpreting divine laws and maintaining the Sharia after the Prophet, distinguishing between the living and absent Imam. Finally, the broadcast included a call to free debtors on the occasion of the Prophet's birthday and addressed a religious query stating that the Prayer of Eclipses is obligatory as a precaution when solar or lunar eclipses are visible only through instruments.
