The program begins with a Quranic interpretation emphasizing the Prophet's exclusive knowledge of the unseen and the Day of Judgment. The morning segment encourages listeners to practice gratitude, recite Ayat al-Kursi, and focus on spirituality during the early hours. Listeners share their experiences regarding the difficulties of honesty and ethical challenges in work and academic settings via text and phone. The historical section commemorates the 10th of Aban by reviewing the martyrdom of Ayatollah Ghaedi, the start of Operation Muharram, and the Battle of Jamal. The broadcast concludes by highlighting the importance of integrity and the courage to speak the truth, even under difficult circumstances.
