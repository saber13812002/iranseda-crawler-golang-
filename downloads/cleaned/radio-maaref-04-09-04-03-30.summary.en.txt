This program analyzes Imam Khomeini's critiques of Ghazali and other Ash'ari theologians. The Imam refutes Ghazali's classification of knowledge, asserting that the science of poverty is a noble esoteric discipline rather than a worldly one. He also rejects Ghazali's interpretation of Paradise and Hell as mere psychological states, arguing that this view contradicts rational proofs and prophetic traditions. The Imam attributes Ghazali's denial of the resurrection of the dead and the parallel existence of worlds to a lack of understanding of philosophy and logic. The second part of the broadcast provides religious rulings on issues such as modesty during prostration, the obligation of Khums, the procedure for guiding the deceased, and the permissibility of eye kohl. It is clarified that unintentionally hearing forbidden music is permissible, while parents are urged to protect their children from negative online content. Finally, the importance of night prayer and avoiding excessive sleep to prevent spiritual poverty on the Day of Judgment is emphasized.
