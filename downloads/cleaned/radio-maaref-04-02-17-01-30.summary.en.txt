In this radio program, Professor Mohammad Sharafi outlines the expectations of Imam Reza (AS) from his followers, focusing on three key pillars: practicality, moderation, and rationality. He emphasizes the central role of "intellect" in the Quran and narrations, strongly criticizing the turn towards superstition, magic, and unfounded claims prevalent in social media. The concept of "Ziyarat" (pilgrimage) is explained as a detachment from the material world to achieve spiritual connection with the Imam. Furthermore, the importance of being Quran-centric and adhering to the Wilayat (guardianship) is highlighted, stressing that acting upon the Quran is as vital as reading it. The second part of the program addresses theological questions and explores the miraculous aspects of the Quran. Professor Sharafi refutes claims that the Quran is a copy of previous texts, pointing to its unique internal coherence, prophetic predictions, and literary appeal. He also discusses the Prophet's miracles and prophetic news as other proofs of his prophethood, concluding with the timeless and ever-renewing nature of the Quranic message.
