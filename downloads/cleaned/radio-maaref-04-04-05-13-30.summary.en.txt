The program began by praising the performance of the late President Raisi's government and urging future leaders to emulate its successful policies.
Listeners were informed that a video message from Iran's Supreme Leader would be broadcast later that day.
The main segment, titled "Love Story," featured an interview with Elham Heidari, the wife of Martyr General Hassan Tehrani Moghaddam.
Mrs. Heidari recounted the details of their courtship, their marriage ceremony officiated by Imam Khomeini, and their early life together during the war.
She described the immense psychological pressure caused by Saddam Hussein's missile attacks and her husband's secret missions to bring missiles into the country.
The interview highlighted the long periods of separation her husband endured in 1984 and 1985, and the constant fear of receiving news of his martyrdom.
She expressed gratitude for the 28 years she spent by his side before his martyrdom, emphasizing the divine nature of their bond.
The program concluded with announcements regarding global events condemning Israeli crimes and supporting Palestine in various locations.
