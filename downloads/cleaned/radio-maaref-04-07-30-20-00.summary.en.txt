The Assembly of Muslim Scholars in Lebanon condemned Trump's threats to bomb Iran's nuclear facilities, emphasizing the Islamic Republic's resilience against global challenges. Contradicting the US president's claim of destroying Iran's nuclear program, reports from the Pentagon and IAEA indicate that the strikes merely delayed the program, with Iran still possessing enriched uranium. Concurrently, the Israeli Knesset passed a bill to impose official sovereignty over the West Bank, sparking widespread international reaction. In Gaza, the handover of freed prisoners' bodies and UNICEF reports on severe child malnutrition highlighted the region's dire humanitarian crisis. Freed Palestinian prisoners recounted horrific torture in Israeli prisons, noting their forced deportation to Egypt and the ban on holding welcome celebrations. In other news, the UK government removed the Hayat Tahrir al-Sham group from its terrorist list, while the Pew Research Center confirmed a significant rise in Islam among French youth. Finally, Iran's Supreme Leader issued a firm response to Trump, emphasizing the concepts of "authority" and "national self-confidence" over raw power, urging a focus on the main enemy rather than internal distractions.
