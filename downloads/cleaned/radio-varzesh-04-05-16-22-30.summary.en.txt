Ruben Shahverdian, a grandmaster of Taekwondo and the founder of Elm-o-Harakat Publishing, recounts his journey to Kuwait to master the art and achieve high ranks such as the 7th Dan. He highlights the lack of reliable Persian resources in the field, which motivated him to publish a reference book on Poomsae in 1980, a move that significantly boosted his reputation in the sports community. Shahverdian shares anecdotes about the successful sales of his books and the challenges of establishing a specialized sports publishing house in the past. He emphasizes the deep philosophy of martial arts as a way of life rather than mere combat techniques, expressing satisfaction with the impact of his works on subsequent generations. The master criticizes the recent decline in the specialized sports book market, attributing it to a cultural deficit and the failure of instructors to promote literature. Despite these challenges, he continues his work with passion, aiming to elevate the scientific and ethical standards of athletes. Finally, he expresses his desire to attain the 8th Dan at his master's dojo and acknowledges the enduring support of his esteemed teachers.
