In this radio program, listeners' theological questions were addressed first. It was explained that while the Prophet (PBUH) inherited material wealth from his father like any other human, he did not inherit scientific knowledge from him due to possessing innate divine knowledge. The nature of angels was also discussed, clarifying that they lack gender, age, and physical bodies, though they are visible in the Hereafter through spiritual sight. Regarding the Day of Judgment, it was noted that followers of Ali (AS) will be resurrected in their shrouds, whereas others will be naked. The existence and impact of the evil eye were affirmed based on Quranic verses and narrations, rejecting scientific denial. Finally, Hujjat al-Islam Qalamari emphasized the importance of obedience to God using the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya. He explained that obedience is the key to accessing divine grace and achieving human dignity. He also advised that consistent obedience prevents us from looking down upon others with contempt.
