In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Lughmani uses Quranic narratives and the words of Imam Sajjad to define "living Karbala" as a model for contemporary life. By recounting the story of the German football coach who joined the mourning rituals, he demonstrated that love for Imam Hussein transcends religious and ethnic boundaries, being rooted in human nature. Citing the Dua of Abu Hamza Thumali, Lughmani emphasized the importance of a peaceful, pure life free from sin, urging listeners to seek inner tranquility through devotion. He highlighted the Prophet's hadith regarding kindness and fostering love in others' hearts as a defining trait of the most virtuous individuals, whom even prophets aspire to emulate. The speaker warned against the harms of inappropriate gazes and bad habits, stressing the need to keep the eyes and heart pure to reach God. Finally, Lughmani led the audience in a collective prayer for healing, national security, and the preservation of the memory of Karbala's martyrs in daily life.
