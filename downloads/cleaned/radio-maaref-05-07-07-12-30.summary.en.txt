This radio program began by interpreting the concept of "Jihad al-Kabir" as the refusal to obey infidels in political, economic, and cultural spheres.
A segment of the late Iranian leader's speech at the Imam Hossein Command and Staff University was then broadcast, emphasizing resistance and reliance on God.
The main focus was on the memoirs of a former POW from the Iran-Iraq War, who was held in a camp in Romania.
The veteran described the hardships of camp life, including strict surveillance and severe limitations on clothing and food.
He recounted the creative methods prisoners used to receive news from Iran and maintain a spirit of resistance among the group.
The most bitter memory of his captivity was hearing the news of Imam Khomeini's passing via smuggled televisions, which caused profound grief.
Despite these difficulties, the prisoners endured the harsh days of imprisonment by finding small comforts and maintaining hope for freedom.
The program concluded by congratulating the day of the prisoners' release and honoring the sacrifices of the war veterans.
