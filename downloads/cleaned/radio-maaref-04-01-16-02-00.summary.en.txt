This radio program addresses Quranic questions regarding divine guidance and human free will.
The key point is that God does not guide the wicked because they consciously choose sin and corruption, not because they lack a God-seeking nature.
Arrogance, negligence, and denial of divine signs cause individuals to alter their spiritual taste and choose misguidance over growth.
Verse 146 of Surah Al-A'raf clearly indicates that arrogant people turn away from God's signs and select the path of evil for themselves.
In the second section, Verse 85 of Surah An-Nisa is interpreted as the foundation for child rearing and mutual influence in society.
Intercession here means guidance and upbringing, where the mentor shares in the reward of the student's good deeds.
Furthermore, silence in the face of sin is considered a form of approval and complicity, making it a source of responsibility.
The discussion concludes that humans shape their path to salvation or destruction through their choices, and proper upbringing plays a crucial role in guidance.
