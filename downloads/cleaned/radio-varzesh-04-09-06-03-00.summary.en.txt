In this discussion, Dr. Hossein Tabousi highlights the success of the 1980s and 1990s sports generations, emphasizing the need for the education system to return to its role in cultivating elite talent from millions of students. He criticizes the current educational focus on championship medals, arguing that the communal and joyous aspects of sports have been neglected. Drawing on past experiences, Tabousi asserts that student competitions are essential not just for competition, but for fostering motivation, cultural exchange, and updating teachers' knowledge. He also addresses the structural and financial challenges facing Iranian skiing, pointing out the paradox of limited financial resources in areas with high athletic potential. Finally, he recommends that sports become an institutionalized part of life at all ages to ensure healthy aging and muscle retention. His core message is that Iranian youth possess the potential to reach the pinnacle of success, provided the society creates the right conditions to nurture their talents.
