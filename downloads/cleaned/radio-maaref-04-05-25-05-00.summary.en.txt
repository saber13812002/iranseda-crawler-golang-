The program explains that true anticipation of the Imam's reappearance requires a profound internal transformation, including moral purity, careful speech, and spiritual readiness. It emphasizes that genuine expectation involves perceiving the Imam's absence as a presence through deep intellect, thereby avoiding any actions that would displease him. The discussion then shifts to a complex jurisprudential debate regarding the principle of "Istishab" (presumption of continuity) and the differences between Sheikh Ansari and Akhund Khurasani. The analysis focuses on the nuances of logical structures like "Kan" and "Laisa" and the conditions for applying Istishab when prior certainty is lacking. The conclusion supports Sheikh Ansari's view, arguing that the effect of the final moment is sufficient for applying the presumption in these specific cases. This detailed examination highlights the critical role of scholarly precision in interpreting Islamic legal rulings.
