The radio program begins by citing narrations from Imam Sadiq (AS) that emphasize good character as the most beloved act to God after fulfilling religious obligations.
It is highlighted that good manners yield the rewards of fasting and night prayers, melt away sins, and extend one's lifespan.
While natural kindness is a divine gift, those who strive to cultivate good character through effort are considered superior in their spiritual reward.
The second part of the discussion focuses on the immense benefits of avoiding backbitting, which is described as a negative worship more valuable than ten thousand units of voluntary prayer.
Listeners are reminded that keeping one's ears away from gossip is a right, and refraining from it protects against a thousand calamities and hellfire.
Furthermore, defending a believer against slander in their absence is a collective virtue that earns divine assistance in both this world and the hereafter.
The program concludes by urging the audience to look beyond individual merit and actively combat backbitting to build a healthy society filled with moral and divine virtues.
