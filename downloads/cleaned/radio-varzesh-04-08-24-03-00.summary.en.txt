In this interview with veteran Zourkhaneh master Alireza Oudati, the importance of a master's deep knowledge of literature, poetic meter, and authentic Iranian music is emphasized. Oudati criticizes the blind imitation of eulogy and classical song styles in Zourkhaneh, advocating for the preservation of the venue's distinct musical identity. He recalls that historically, reaching the "Sardam" platform required rigorous moral and technical training, a standard that is sometimes overlooked today. The Association of Masters, operating under the Zourkhaneh Sports Federation, is working to organize and elevate the art through specialized committees and professional standards. Despite economic challenges and limited budgets for education, the association strives to improve the quality of masters' work through dedicated training and refereeing courses. Oudati also discusses the influence of historical figures like Master Moradi on his artistic development and the preferences of subsequent generations. Finally, he shares that his children are actively continuing the profession, highlighting the importance of passing this cultural heritage to the next generation.
