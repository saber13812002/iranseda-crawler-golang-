In this program, Hujjat al-Islam Falah introduces humility as an innate virtue and a form of worship dedicated to God, warning against using it as a tool for hypocrisy or masking personal flaws. He emphasizes that true humility requires introspection and self-improvement, urging individuals to focus on correcting their own inner state rather than criticizing others. In the second segment, Hujjat al-Islam Bagherian discusses the worldly impact of actions and the concept of repentance, explaining that God loves those who sincerely turn back to Him. Citing religious traditions, he defines "Tawbah Nasuha" as a definitive return from sin and cautions against despairing of divine mercy. The discussion highlights that even in Islamic history, the repentance of sinners was accepted and cherished by the Ahl al-Bayt. Ultimately, purity and repentance are presented as key factors for gaining God's favor and achieving spiritual peace.
