The radio program begins by emphasizing the necessity of social life and the Islamic guidelines for selecting spouses, business partners, and friends.
Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) and Imam Ali (AS) issued strict warnings about the detrimental effects of associating with immoral or irreligious individuals.
The Quranic verse regarding the regret of the oppressor is illustrated through the historical story of Akbah and Ubayy, showing how a bad friend can lead a person to eternal damnation.
The second part of the discussion shifts to jurisprudential principles, specifically analyzing the concept of Ijma (consensus) in the Shia school of thought.
The concepts of "Ijma Kashf" (revealing consensus) and "Ijma Hads" (presumptive consensus) are examined, revealing that they ultimately rely on the same logical and customary reasoning.
Finally, Ayatollah Borujerdi's interpretation of "Relative Consensus" is presented as the most precise and logical explanation for how the opinions of scholars reflect the ruling of the Infallible Imam.
