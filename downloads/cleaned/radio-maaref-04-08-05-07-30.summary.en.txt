The program began by celebrating Nurses' Day and the birth of Hazrat Zainab (SA), featuring heartfelt messages from listeners, particularly those in Zaynabiyeh, Isfahan. In the health segment, it was announced that 18 new drugs have been added to Iran's official list, while high drug consumption rates compared to global standards were criticized. The importance of strengthening the immune system for a healthy pregnancy and preventing infections was also emphasized. The religious section highlighted the high status of nurses based on narrations, citing Prophet Yusuf (AS) as a model for caring for the sick. Subsequently, Professor Mohammad Hadi Afla discussed the causes of negative judgment in the "Ganj-e Sa'adat" program. He identified factors such as love and hate, lack of spiritual guidance, injustice, envy, self-centeredness, and nosiness as the roots of this moral vice. The speaker advised avoiding unjust judgment by seeking refuge in the Quran, refraining from suspicion, and focusing on self-improvement.
