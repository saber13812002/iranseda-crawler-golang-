In this radio program, Professor Fatemeh examines the dangerous consequences of the vice of arrogance from the perspective of Islamic narrations. She identifies arrogance as Satan's greatest trap, which targets the human heart and intellect to pull individuals from spiritual light into darkness. A major consequence is the gradual reduction of practical reason and humanity, as arrogance conflicts with the rational faculty and leads to moral decline. Contrary to common belief, arrogance does not elevate a person but instead traps them in existential pressure by misusing their innate divine capacities. Furthermore, arrogance serves as a catalyst for other sins such as envy and malice, ultimately destroying a person's dignity and social standing. The vice leads to spiritual death and an increase in errors, cutting the individual off from divine guidance and intercession.
