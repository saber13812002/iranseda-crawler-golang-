In the radio program "Mahrab-e Del," Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the importance of time management by citing the conduct of great Shia scholars. He highlighted examples from Sheikh Javadi, Sheikh Muhammad Tehrani, and Ayatollah Behjat, who utilized every moment for study and spiritual remembrance, even during travel or brief intervals. Ayatollah Behjat notably interrupted his prayers to answer questions, demonstrating his strict avoidance of wasting time. The program then featured a narrative from the book "Okyanos-e Mashreq," where Hajj Karim Pinduz defended his dignity and innocence against armed robbers with grace and courage. This story illustrated the preservation of human honor in critical situations. Later, the program discussed the Supreme Leader's fatwa regarding the permissibility of artificial nails and hair extensions if they can be easily removed. Practical tips for conserving water at home were also shared to promote resource efficiency. The broadcast concluded with acknowledgments to the presenters and an announcement of upcoming programs.
