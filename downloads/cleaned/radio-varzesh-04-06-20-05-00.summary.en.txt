In this program, Dr. Moshetdi emphasized the vital role of exercise in women's health, particularly during menopause and old age. She recommends moderate-intensity resistance training to manage hot flashes and lower cortisol, as high intensity can exacerbate stress and sleep disturbances. Group activities and swimming are also highly beneficial for reducing stress and improving mood. The false belief that exercises like rope jumping are forbidden for women is baseless, and specialized sports can even prevent pelvic floor issues and uterine prolapse. Kegel exercises and plyometric movements significantly strengthen the pelvic floor and improve childbirth quality. Furthermore, maternal exercise can improve offspring genetics through epigenetics, passing these positive effects to future generations. In the second segment, a father and son who manufacture equestrian footwear discussed the challenges of domestic production and Iran's high potential to compete in the global shoe industry.
