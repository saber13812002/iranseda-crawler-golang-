This program reviewed recent Israeli attacks on the Gaza Strip and the West Bank, which have resulted in Palestinian casualties and a worsening humanitarian crisis. The assassination of Hisam al-Tawil, a senior Hezbollah commander, was condemned, highlighting his significant role in the resistance history. Mourning ceremonies for the anniversary of Fatima Zahra's martyrdom were held across various Islamic countries with widespread participation. The Fatima Zahra Mosque in Sharjah, UAE, was introduced as a symbol of unity and Islamic architecture. Sudan's Sovereignty Council rejected the US-proposed ceasefire plan, citing its bias toward the Rapid Support Forces. The International Halal Economy Conference in London concluded with an emphasis on Islamic financial cooperation. The first international Quran recitation competition in Pakistan commenced with Iranian representatives in attendance. An international congress honoring Ayatollah Habat al-Din Shahrani is also scheduled to be held in Iran and Iraq.
