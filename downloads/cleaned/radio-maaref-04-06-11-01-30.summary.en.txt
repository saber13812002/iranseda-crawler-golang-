In this lecture, Hojjat al-Islam Hosseini Qomi emphasizes the importance of prayer and ziyarat to maintain a connection with Imam Mahdi (AJ). Citing a narration from the second deputy of the Imam, he explains that during the occultation, submitting to God's plan is superior to insisting on a physical meeting. The appearance of the Imam is characterized by the peak of altruism, justice, and knowledge, and a lack of readiness in these areas may render the appearance non-beneficial for some. Referencing Ayatollah Behjat, he advises believers to pray for the readiness to accept the Imam's return rather than solely for its acceleration. The program then transitions to a reading from the book "Okyanos-e Mashreq," where the character Aman seeks the water of life and meets Hajj Karim. Hajj Karim teaches him that faith should not rely solely on physical sight, but rather on the Quran as a more reliable source of truth. This dialogue illustrates that religious knowledge and allegiance to the Imam are the keys to finding the ultimate truth.
