In the program's exegesis segment, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli emphasizes monotheism and the necessity of avoiding injustice, citing verses from the Quran. He explains that lying and denying the truth represent the peak of oppression, ensuring that the oppressor never attains eternal salvation. The analysis of practical wisdom reveals that all sins ultimately reduce to injustice, and every action, whether good or evil, reflects back upon the actor. The host then discusses seasonal events and World Deaf Day, honoring Rumi and Imam Sajjad (AS) on their respective commemorative days. The main segment, titled "Let's Be Kind," focuses on the challenge of self-perception through the eyes of others. Listeners shared messages identifying traits such as honesty, good nature, and high energy as how they are perceived by their communities. The program's ultimate goal is to reinforce positive character traits and correct negative ones within society.
