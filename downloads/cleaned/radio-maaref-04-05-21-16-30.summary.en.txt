This radio program discusses Imam Sajjad's knowledge of the Greatest Name of God and its relation to the station of Imamate. The expert emphasizes that Imams acquire their knowledge from God through prayer and worship, distinguishing between theoretical knowledge and the practical manifestation of the Divine Name. It is explained that while Imams possess the power to know the future, they may choose not to exercise this knowledge to align with God's will and wisdom, as illustrated by the stories of Imam Sajjad's martyrdom and Abraham's sacrifice. The segment also addresses written and phone questions regarding funeral rites, congregational prayer, and the theological differences between Akhbari and Usuli schools of thought. Finally, the program offers practical tips for conserving water and electricity, concluding with the recitation of Imam Sajjad's prayer for rain and its Persian translation.
