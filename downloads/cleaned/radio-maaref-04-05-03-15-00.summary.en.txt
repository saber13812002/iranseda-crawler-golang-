The program identifies the success of the Palestinian resistance as a result of the power balance shift orchestrated by the Islamic Republic and Hezbollah against the Zionist regime's siege. By transferring missile and weapons manufacturing technology to Gaza, the resistance evolved from stone-throwing to launching long-range missiles during the 2008 war. The construction of an extensive underground tunnel network allowed the resistance to bypass strict blockades and strengthen their forces within enemy territory. The leadership of Hamas, featuring historical figures like Yahya Sinwar who were released from prison, transformed the movement into a unified and heroic force. Logistical support via Syria and Hezbollah was instrumental in enabling the Al-Aqsa Flood operation and defeating the enemy's technological superiority in an asymmetric war. These achievements demonstrate the effectiveness of the Supreme Leader's strategic approach in exposing the regime's nature and elevating the status of the Palestinian resistance.
