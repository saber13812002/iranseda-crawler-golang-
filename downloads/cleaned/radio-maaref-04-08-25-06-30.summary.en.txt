The radio program begins with a mystical discussion on how divine names serve as keys to the unseen and are essential for prayers to be answered.
It is emphasized that a supplicant must embody a divine name to truly hold the key to the mysteries of the unseen.
The host then offers salutations to Imam Zaman and Imam Ali, referencing Ali's description of paradise in Nahj al-Balagha.
Marking the International Day of Tolerance, the host invites listeners to share their personal experiences of patience and forbearance.
The historical calendar segment recalls the passing of scholar Mohaghegh Jafari and freedom fighter Sattar Khan on this day in previous years.
It also commemorates the mass funeral of 370 martyrs in Isfahan and the invention of artificial rain on November 16.
The broadcast concludes with religious and patriotic music, encouraging audience participation through text messages.
