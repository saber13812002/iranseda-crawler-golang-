In this radio program, experts emphasized the importance of utilizing the Arbaeen pilgrimage as a golden opportunity for raising the younger generation and strengthening family bonds. They believe parents should use this journey to compensate for the lack of daily interaction and build genuine friendship with their teenage children. Muezzins and pilgrims are also responsible for paying attention to the educational and spiritual dimensions of their audience alongside providing material services. Establishing real social connections and engaging in dialogue with diverse individuals was presented as a key solution to combat the loneliness and digital isolation of today's generation. The ultimate goal is to honor children in the spiritual atmosphere of Karbala so that they remain immune to social harms. These interactions can foster social, intellectual, and civilization-building growth for the future generation of Shia Muslims. Finally, Arbaeen was described as a spring for the growth and deepening of human and religious ties.
