The radio program "Zamzam Ahkam" began with religious chants and a song before addressing listeners' jurisprudential questions. Regarding fasting while suffering from Vitamin D deficiency, the ruling emphasized consulting a specialist doctor to determine if fasting would cause harm. It was clarified that while men may wear shorts, they must ensure full coverage in public and professional settings. A listener's doubt about wudu during prayer was dismissed, confirming that the prayer remains valid if one does not act on the doubt. In cases of lost trust property, the trustee is liable for compensation at current market rates only if negligence or misuse occurred. If the Fatiha is forgotten, the prayer should be restarted if time permits, or completed with known verses and tasbih if time is running out. Finally, paying a portion of a debt is obligatory if the debtor has the financial means, unless the creditor grants a waiver.
