The text begins with Imam Khomeini's speech emphasizing the necessity of judicial decisiveness and the strict implementation of Islamic limits in courts and prisons. He calls for the removal of laws incompatible with Islam and demands justice that is neither lenient toward criminals nor unjust toward the innocent. The second part features the "Zamzam Ahkam" program, which addresses listeners' religious questions. One ruling states that breaking prayer for a child's routine needs is not permissible unless under specific emergency conditions. Additionally, worshippers with severe physical pain may pray while sitting if standing causes them hardship. Another fatwa clarifies that a child can have two homelands, and changing residence with the intention of permanent stay establishes a new one. Regarding impurity, prayer remains valid if blood is below the specified limit and discovered after the prayer is completed. The fidya for fasting must be paid in food items rather than cash. Finally, converting accumulated coins into a car before the end of the Khums year exempts the item from paying Khums.
