Fereba Soleimani, a veteran women's volleyball coach, emphasized the importance of supporting young players and strictly prohibiting the use of blank contracts. She argued that combining experienced athletes with the younger generation is the key to the national team's success and progress. Soleimani highlighted structural issues in talent scouting, calling for scientific and organized models to identify tall and gifted athletes. She also stressed the need to utilize domestic coaches with international certifications and update training methods rather than relying solely on foreign coaches. The coach expressed concern about the lack of supervision in private academies and the declining financial returns in this sector. Finally, Soleimani mentioned her personal dream of participating in the Paralympics and underscored the crucial role of female athletes in improving Iran's global sports ranking.
