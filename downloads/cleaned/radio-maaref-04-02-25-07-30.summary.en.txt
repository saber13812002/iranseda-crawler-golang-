In this radio program, coinciding with Ferdowsi Day, the importance of preserving the Persian language and resisting foreign-imposed changes was emphasized. The guest, referencing the Shahnameh and historical efforts to protect the language, urged the preservation of local dialects and the avoidance of excessive foreign vocabulary in media and daily speech. The second part of the program focused on the moral discussion of "Ghadab" or anger, which was identified as the worst companion for a human being. Relying on the theory of the unity of agent and action in Transcendent Theosophy, it was explained that anger is inherently evil and that repeating it transforms the individual into anger itself. Finally, avoiding irritability and controlling the ego were deemed essential for achieving moral perfection and respecting oneself and others.
