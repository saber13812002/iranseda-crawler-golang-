The program begins with a mystical interpretation of a sacred narration, describing prayer as a dialogue and intimate communion with God.
In this interpretation, the reward for reaching the station of annihilation and divine love is God's direct dominion over the servant's heart.
Hojjat al-Islam Masoudi then explains hadiths from the Book of Kafi regarding the chapter on "bringing joy to a believer."
He emphasizes that making a believer happy is a form of worship that God loves more than any other act of devotion.
According to the narrations, simple acts like smiling at a friend or performing small kindnesses are easy ways to bring joy to believers.
Imam Baqir (AS) also equates making a believer happy with making the Prophet and ultimately God happy.
The program concludes by highlighting the spiritual opportunities of night prayer and the supplication for the reappearance of the Imam, ending with a recitation of dawn prayers.
