In this program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verse 176 of Surah An-Nisa, focusing on inheritance rules and the concept of "Kalalah." He explains that Kalalah refers to collateral relatives like siblings, who inherit only if the deceased has no children or living parents. A key point is the refutation of the claim that men always receive double the share of women, as women sometimes inherit more or an equal amount depending on the circumstances. The interpretation clarifies that this specific verse applies to paternal and maternal siblings, whereas verse 12 of the same chapter is limited to maternal siblings. Islamic narrations further confirm that the presence of a father or mother disqualifies siblings from inheriting. The discussion highlights the structural coherence of the chapter, which begins and ends with themes of divine power and knowledge. Finally, the tafsir emphasizes God's absolute knowledge of hidden benefits, discouraging human bias in the distribution of inheritance.
