The radio program introduces the guest, Mrs. Seyed Rahimeh Hashemi, the mother of martyr Ahmad Makian, a defender of the shrines. The mother recounts her son's childhood, noting his intelligence, Quranic memorization, and early desire to join the seminary and the war in Syria. Despite his young age and frailty, Ahmad physically prepared himself for the front lines through rigorous training. He secretly obtained his father's consent, a cleric, to sign the deployment form for the Fatemiyoun Brigade while keeping his mother in the dark. On the day of his departure, Ahmad asked his mother to pack his bag under the pretext of visiting Imam Reza, though he was actually heading to Syria. His mother expresses her shock and deep admiration for his unwavering commitment to martyrdom upon realizing the truth. The program concludes with a discussion on the necessity of addressing the spiritual and intellectual questions of the new generation through Islamic teachings.
