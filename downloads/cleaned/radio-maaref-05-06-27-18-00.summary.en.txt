In this radio program, the concept of "ujb" or self-admiration is explained as the root of arrogance and pride. The speaker explains that individuals afflicted with ujb derive moral credit even from their own flaws and misfortunes, viewing themselves as superior to others. This trait causes them to distance themselves from others in social interactions, including religious gatherings, and to convey contempt through their body language. To better understand this spiritual harm, the historical story of Ibn Hamdani and Umar Saad in Karbala is recounted. In this narrative, Ibn Hamdani exposes Umar Saad's hidden arrogance by not greeting him, demonstrating how the pursuit of power can lead a person to grave sins. The speaker emphasizes that the curse on Umar Saad in the Ziyarat Ashura is a symbol of the struggle against the "internal Umar Saad" that forms within us. This "internal Umar Saad" represents the arrogance and self-centeredness that can block the path to truth and lead us to destruction. Therefore, the main goal of the program is to warn listeners to avoid negative moral traits and to guard their inner selves against internal satanic influences.
