In the Radio Ma'aref broadcast, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the necessity of patience and gratitude during illness, citing the lives of Ayatollah Langaroudi and Martyr Bahonar as exemplary models. He explained that for believers, sickness is a divine mercy and a means of purifying sins, while complaining to others is strictly discouraged. The program then transitioned to a narrative segment from the book "Okeanos Mashreq," recounting the journey of Amirtia Emran in search of the water of life. Through philosophical dialogue with Raha, the daughter of the cobbler, Emran realized that a merciful God would not leave humanity to choose between heaven and hell without guidance. This realization led him to accept the necessity of following divine leaders and the authority of the Imams to find the correct path. The broadcast concluded with a jurisprudential answer regarding the purchase of second-hand software and practical tips for saving water at home.
