In this radio program, experts emphasized the importance of limiting children's media usage, recommending that toddlers under two years old avoid screens entirely.
The general guideline suggests that digital playtime for children should not exceed one hour per day.
To compensate for this restriction, parents are advised to organize group games and physical activities to prevent their children from feeling bored or neglected.
At religious and social gatherings, creating dedicated spaces for children alongside shared family areas was proposed as an effective strategy for their engagement.
Regarding a listener's question about encouraging a teenage girl to pray, the experts suggested leveraging peer groups and fostering a strong emotional bond between parents and children.
In the final segment, the claim that the public is exhausted by war was refuted, asserting that the Iranian people remain dynamic and committed to defending their country and Islamic values.
The program concluded with religious prayers, anthems, and an announcement of upcoming broadcasts.
