The program begins with a political analysis asserting that Iran's national unity and collective piety have strategically defeated external threats and enemy attempts to create social divisions. The speaker emphasizes that the tactical and media victories of Israel and the United States are rendered ineffective by the Iranian people's cohesion and the country's deterrent power, which prevents internal collapse. The second part of the broadcast addresses the jurisprudential necessity of military and defensive readiness, citing Quranic verses that mandate acquiring modern knowledge, such as nuclear energy, to instill fear in enemies and preserve national independence. Subsequently, the program provides jurisprudential answers to listener questions, including the ruling on paying the Sadat share to a non-Sadid, the conditions for wudu when extra water is present, and the permissibility of prostrating on non-pure objects in emergencies. The ruling on artificial nails is declared invalid as a permanent obstacle to wudu and ghusl, with a strong emphasis on removing them. Finally, the broadcast concludes by stating that reciting the Quran without full mastery of Tajweed rules is permissible and that fasting remains valid if incense smoke reaches the throat, ending with morning prayers and acknowledgments of the virtues of Muharram.
