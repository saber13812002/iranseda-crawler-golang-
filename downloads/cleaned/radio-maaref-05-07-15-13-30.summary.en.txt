The program began with a lecture by Hojjat-ol-Islam Hosseini Araaki on cultivating individuals for martyrdom, followed by a segment dedicated to the spiritual longing for Imam Reza during Thursdays and an interview with a servant of the holy shrine in Mashhad. The main feature was a radio drama titled "Mr. Judge," which detailed the fraud case of Ahmad Mehrabi, a man who used fake identities and claims of living in Canada to deceive women online. By promising marriage and car purchases, he extorted significant sums of money from his victims, who eventually exposed his true identity during the trial. Judge Mohsen Akbari emphasized the importance of using domestic digital platforms to reduce cybercrime and highlighted common traits of fraudsters, such as false confidence and exploiting victims' weaknesses. He stressed the need for increased media literacy among citizens to detect online scams and sentenced the defendant to three years in prison along with restitution. The broadcast concluded with the Supreme Leader's remarks on the significance of Palestinian resistance and the decisive impact of the Al-Aqsa Flood operation on the Zionist regime.
