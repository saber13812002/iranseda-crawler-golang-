In this radio program, following the announcement of other show schedules, the main discussion focuses on ethics and the management of anger. Professor Mohammad Hadi Falahati, relying on the narrations of Imam Ali (PBUH), introduces anger as a dangerous and rootless flaw in the system of character. He emphasizes that anger pulls a person from the noble lineage of faith and monotheism into degradation, and no apparent or family honor can compensate for this moral defect. Anger is described as a "bad companion" that, like Satan, serves as an inappropriate substitute for virtues and reason. This trait, like a constant shadow, can divert a person from a healthy life path and the hereafter. Therefore, to achieve steadfastness and peace, one must draw upon moral virtues and avoid anger. The program concludes with a prayer for God's grace to stay away from anger.
