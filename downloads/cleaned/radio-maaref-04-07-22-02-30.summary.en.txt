In this radio program, Professor Mohammadi addressed various theological and practical questions from listeners. He emphasized the spiritual value of visiting parents' graves and reciting specific Quranic verses like Surah Al-Mulk and Yasin for their peace. Regarding workplace discrimination against the disabled, he advised individuals to assert their rights while encouraging employers to support them by recognizing the spiritual rewards of such assistance. On the topic of diet, he clarified that a complete abstinence from meat is not mandatory, but moderation and communal support for the poor to afford meat are highly recommended. The professor refuted the concept of fatalism, asserting that human free will in the physical world is distinct from the pre-determined nature of the spiritual realm. He analyzed the historical corruption of the Israelites, suggesting that the current actions of Israel may represent the second phase of corruption that will lead to their destruction. Finally, he distinguished between true divine love and base instinctual desire, urging listeners to focus on the authentic meaning of affection as presented in Islamic texts.
