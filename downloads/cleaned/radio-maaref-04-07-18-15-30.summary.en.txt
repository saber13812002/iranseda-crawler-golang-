In this program, a political analyst examines the McFarlane affair as a case study of how an external crisis was transformed into an opportunity for internal structural purification. He argues that Imam Khomeini's firm refusal to negotiate directly with the United States not only prevented enemy infiltration but also facilitated the exposure and removal of parallel networks and deviant internal currents. This analysis highlights that the slogan "Neither East nor West" served as an effective tool for preventing colonialism and maintaining political independence. In the second segment, another expert emphasizes the futility of negotiating with the US government under current conditions. He contends that such negotiations offer no benefit to national interests and instead incur irreparable damages. Subsequently, recitations and translations of verses from Surah Al-Baqarah were broadcast, focusing on the rules of fasting, worship, and the prohibition of aggression and unjust war. The program concluded with the playback of religious music and a call for listeners to support the people of Gaza.
