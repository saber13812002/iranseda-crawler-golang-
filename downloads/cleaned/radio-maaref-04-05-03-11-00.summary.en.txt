Ayatollah Nouri Hamedani and the Assembly of Experts described the Gaza crisis as a historic test for the Islamic world, warning against silence in the face of ongoing violence. In response, massive "Friday of Anger" rallies were held across Iran to express solidarity with Palestine and condemn Israel. Concurrently, border authorities from Iran and Iraq confirmed their full readiness to facilitate the safe passage of Arbaeen pilgrims through the Khorramshahr border. In a significant technological achievement, the Nahid-2 communications satellite, developed by Iranian experts, was successfully launched into space. The radio program also featured the recitation of the Imam Mahdi's Ziyarat and emphasized the crucial role of mothers in raising children with a Mahdavi mindset. Scholar Rafiei drew parallels between the uprising of Ashura and the concept of waiting for the Imam, urging listeners to connect these two spiritual pillars. Finally, the broadcast concluded with jurisprudential answers to listener questions regarding the rulings on divorce and religious vows.
