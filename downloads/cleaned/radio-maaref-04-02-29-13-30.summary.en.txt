The radio program "Mashq-e Rah," featuring Professor Seyed Ahmad Faqih, explored ethical and mystical topics centered on prayer and monotheism.
The professor emphasized that acknowledging sins and purifying the inner self from hypocrisy and envy are prerequisites for establishing a spiritual connection with God before praying.
In response to listener questions, he recommended studying the profound spiritual significance of prayer to revive neglected worship practices.
Regarding the sin of backbiting, the necessity of complete repentance, practical compensation for the harm caused, and seeking intercession from Imam Zaman (AJ) to repair broken relationships were highlighted.
The latter part of the program analyzed the situation in Palestine and the regional resistance against the Zionist regime.
The professor underscored the strategic depth of Iran-Syria relations by recalling Syria's vital support for Iran during the Iran-Iraq war.
The program concluded by inviting listeners to participate in surveys and access the archive through the Iran Sound application.
