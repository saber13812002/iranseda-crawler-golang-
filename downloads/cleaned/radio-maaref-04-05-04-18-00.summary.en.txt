In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Flaba Estan, relying on Quranic verses and narrations, examined the vice of stinginess and its destructive effects. He defined stinginess not only in terms of money but also in behavior, such as withholding greetings or neglecting duties. One of the major consequences of stinginess is the loss of inner peace, as the Prophet (PBUH) described the miser as the least comfortable person. Furthermore, a miser loses their wealth, which God then forcibly grants to wicked people. Instead of gaining honor, stinginess leads to humiliation before God and the Ahl al-Bayt, and is ultimately considered a form of sin. In the second part, Hujjat al-Islam Baqiryan emphasized the importance of greeting upon entering a home and making space for newcomers in gatherings. He explained that these actions invite divine blessings, the presence of angels, and expansion in both worldly and hereafter life.
