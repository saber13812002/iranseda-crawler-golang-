In this radio program, a man from Tabriz was interviewed who has been waxing the shoes of pilgrims at Jamkaran Shrine for a year out of pure devotion to Imam Zaman.
He emphasized that this humble service has profoundly impacted his character, making him a more compassionate and God-conscious individual.
Dr. Jafari then addressed the question of why the Imam was not born shortly before his reappearance, explaining that the Imam is actively managing the world's affairs during his occultation.
Hojjat al-Islam Nasiri highlighted listener messages, stressing the importance of linking one's life and economy to the Imam to receive divine blessings.
He also noted that true anticipation is not merely about prayer but involves acting according to the Quran, obeying the Imam, and building a society aligned with Islamic standards.
In the final segment, Hojjat al-Islam Kafili interpreted the concept of "Gate of God" in the Al-Yasin supplication, viewing the kissing of shrine doors as a symbol of love and respect toward God.
The overall conclusion is that a true awaiter is one who prepares for the reappearance through knowledge, love, and practical obedience.
