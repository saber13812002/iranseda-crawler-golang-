The mourning ceremony for the night of Ashura was held at Imam Khomeini's Hosseiniyeh with the presence of the Supreme Leader. In his address, Hujjat al-Islam Ali emphasized the lessons of Imam Hussein's uprising, identifying Iran as the core of the global resistance front. He stressed that the Iranian nation will never abandon the path of truth, citing the slogan "Life is in submission" as their guiding principle. Mahmoud Karimi further heightened the spiritual atmosphere by reciting poetry and elegies in praise of Imam Abbas and his martyred companions. The Supreme Leader's attendance was widely reported by international media and sparked intensified chants of "Death to America and Israel" among the mourners. This symbolic presence was interpreted as a firm message to adversaries, affirming the resilience of the Iranian people against conspiracies. Additionally, the President participated in the Ashura mourning ceremony in Tabriz, underscoring the national significance of this religious occasion.
