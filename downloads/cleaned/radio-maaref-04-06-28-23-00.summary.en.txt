At the end of the "Ta Jome Zehoor" program, the host emphasized the necessity of strengthening knowledge of the Imam and obeying him to achieve a pure life.
Subsequently, in the "Mabna" segment, Hojjat al-Islam Rabani Golepaygani discussed the theological issue of Imamate and divine cosmic justice.
He argued that the differences among created beings are not contrary to divine justice but are actually a manifestation of it.
From a rational perspective, creating a world without diversity would require eliminating many beings and wasting their potential, which contradicts God's absolute generosity.
As the Absolute Generous, God has granted existence to all beings with inherent and situational potential, making diversity a sign of the perfection of creation.
Removing differences or limiting creation to the Infallibles would constitute a form of injustice and a flaw in the cosmic system.
The speaker then addressed religious pluralism, rejecting pluralism regarding truth and falsehood.
He stressed that while there is only one true religion, Islam provides a suitable model for peaceful coexistence by respecting the rights of minorities in social life.
