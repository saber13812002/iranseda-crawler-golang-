This radio program focuses on the significance of the Arbaeen pilgrimage, highlighting the role of Lady Zainab (SA) in globalizing the message of Karbala.
The guest emphasizes that visiting Imam Hussein's shrine is superior to Hajj, citing Quranic verses and narrations to describe it as a sign of spiritual purity and global justice.
The Arbaeen march is portrayed as a powerful media tool that counters enemy narratives and serves as a definitive precursor to the Mahdi's reappearance.
The diverse participation of pilgrims from various ethnicities and religions symbolizes a profound, unconditional love for Imam Hussein (AS).
The shrine of Lady Fatima Masumeh (SA) is recognized as a key supporter, providing extensive services such as accommodation and meals for thousands of pilgrims.
The program featured a poetic recitation praising Lady Zainab and shared listener messages, stressing the importance of preserving the Ashura culture for future generations.
The broadcast concluded with prayers for the Mahdi's return and gratitude towards the pilgrims and the shrine's support teams.
