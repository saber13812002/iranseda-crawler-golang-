The program began by addressing various jurisprudential questions, including the rules of impurity when washing objects, the validity of prayers and fasting for traveling students, and the permissibility of wudu with an artificial lens. It also clarified the specific rulings regarding infant urine and the forgiveness of small amounts of blood on clothing. In the second segment, Professor Javad Mohtadi, referencing Nahj al-Balagha, emphasized the importance of seeking, knowing, and speaking the truth under difficult circumstances. He noted that attaining truth requires seriousness and endurance, and that truth-tellers face a severe test in eras where truthfulness is rare. The section concluded with Imam Ali's advice to select advisors who speak the truth rather than flatterers. The final part of the program focused on a historical analysis of Musaylim ibn Aqil's life. The researcher cited historical sources indicating his participation in the conquest of Egypt and his command of troops during the Battle of Siffin. These pieces of evidence suggest that Musaylim held a significant political and military position before the events of Karbala and was likely born in the early years of the Hijra.
