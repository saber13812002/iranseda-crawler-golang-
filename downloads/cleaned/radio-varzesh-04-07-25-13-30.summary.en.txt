The guest, a sports and social veteran from Tehran's Piruzi neighborhood, shared memories of his youth and the early days of the revolution. He described his active role in defending the Air Force headquarters and making Molotov cocktails to counter tanks. In the sports section, he recounted his efforts to establish a family football field and invited Goudars Habibie as a coach. The guest emphasized the fundamental changes in local sports, attributing the decline in football quality to the conversion of dirt fields into profitable gardens and a lack of non-material motivation. He argued that utilizing domestic coaches is key to success due to their better understanding of local players. Comparing different sports, he assessed wrestling and judo as more advanced than football. Finally, while praising national achievements in individual sports, he insisted on the necessity of localizing football to reach a global level.
