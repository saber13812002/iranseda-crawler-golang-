This text provides a tafsir of verses from Surah Yunus, focusing on the status of the "People of the A'raf" on the Day of Judgment. These individuals, positioned between the inhabitants of Paradise and Hell, are described as divine men who recognize people by their inner moral signs rather than just physical appearances. On that day, they greet the people of Paradise and ask the people of Hell to share their water and food. A key theological point is that actions like greeting and supplication are not considered religious obligations or worship in the afterlife, but rather sources of pleasure and signs of submission to God. The People of the A'raf remind the damned of their arrogance and the false oaths they swore in this world regarding the fate of the deprived. Ultimately, these individuals are invited to enter Paradise by divine command, finding peace and freedom from fear.
