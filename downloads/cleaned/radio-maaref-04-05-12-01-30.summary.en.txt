This radio program analyzes three key Quranic supplications of the martyrs as essential for defeating the global system of oppression. The first prayer, asking for victory over the disbelievers, is interpreted as a call to dismantle the unipolar Western hegemony, which the Supreme Leader describes as transitioning into a multipolar order led by the Islamic Ummah. The second prayer, seeking forgiveness for sins, highlights the necessity of abandoning sin and countering the enemy's cultural and media corruption to maintain social cohesion. The third prayer, requesting steadfastness, emphasizes the importance of loyalty to the Guardian Jurist and winning the cognitive war by correctly recognizing God's allies. The speaker notes that the people's awakening from the enemy's plots has strengthened the foundation for steadfastness and resistance. The discussion concludes with the recitation of Ziyarat Ashura and mourning for Lady Zainab, linking these acts to the ultimate fulfillment of these prayers through the appearance of Imam Mahdi. The segment ends with a historical narrative of Imam Hussein's encounter with Har ibn Riyahi, who warned him of the Kufans' betrayal and the approaching enemy army.
