In this radio program, Professor Jafari examines the life of Rabab, the wife of Imam Ali, using historical sources such as Al-Aghani and Al-Kamil fi al-Tarikh. He asserts that Rabab was the only confirmed wife of Imam Hussein present at Karbala, where their children, Ali Asghar and Sakineh, were also present. Historical evidence suggests that Sakineh was an adult at the time of the event, refuting common claims about her being a teenager or married to Abdullah Akbar. After her husband's martyrdom, Rabab lived under the open sky for a year out of grief and devotion before passing away. The program also addressed a listener's question about Zahhak, clarifying that he is a mythological figure rooted in the symbolic interpretation of the Damavand volcano rather than a historical person. Finally, a discussion from the book of Kafi explored the concept of being "deficient" in worship and the necessity of acknowledging one's shortcomings before God.
