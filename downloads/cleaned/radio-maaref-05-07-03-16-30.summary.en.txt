In this program, Dr. Mostafa Sadeghi first highlights Imam Ali's (PBUH) advisory role in protecting Iranian farmers from enslavement. He then addresses a listener's question regarding Imam Reza's (PBUH) migration, clarifying that it was not a voluntary hijra but a forced exile by Caliph Ma'mun to monitor the Imam. The scholar also describes the forced marriage of Imam Javad (PBUH) to Ma'mun's daughter as a political maneuver to establish a lineage with the Prophet's family, which ultimately did not result in offspring. The second part of the program analyzes the revolutionary perspective on the military, emphasizing the fundamental difference between the Islamic army and colonial or Pahlavi forces. Sadeghi argues that the revolutionary army fights based on spiritual and political convictions, whereas Western and Pahlvi armies were merely tools of oppression dependent on foreign powers. By comparing the performance of the Iranian army during the imposed war with the collapse of the Pahlvi army in 1941, he underscores the authenticity and loyalty of the Islamic Republic's armed forces. Finally, the importance of ideological education and military discipline in shaping the identity of a genuine army, independent of foreign influence, is discussed.
