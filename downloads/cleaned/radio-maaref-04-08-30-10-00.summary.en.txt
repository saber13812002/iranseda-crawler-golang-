In this radio program, Professor Amir-Mohsen Erfan emphasized the exemplary role of Hazrat Zahra (SA) in Fatimi culture and its impact on the community of the Awaited One. He identified "social altruism," empathy, and unconditional charity as key lessons from her conduct for those in expectation. Citing a narration where Hazrat Zahra (SA) considered serving creation as one of her most beloved acts alongside Quran recitation, he stressed the importance of transcending personal gain to help others, even those who offer no benefit in return. Referencing Imam Hasan Askari's (AS) statement that benefiting fellow believers is second only to faith, he called for "social mutual support" and collective responsibility among believers. In the second segment, Dr. Jafari interpreted narrations regarding the decline of religion before the reappearance, clarifying that this refers to neglecting religious principles in social management and policy rather than abandoning personal worship. He highlighted that social sins and corruption can lead to natural phenomena like drought, citing Quranic verses and Tafsir al-Mizan. Finally, by reading from the book "Let Us Help Him," the importance of maintaining good conduct and protecting the reputation of Imam Mahdi (AJ) as a form of assistance during the occultation was underscored.
