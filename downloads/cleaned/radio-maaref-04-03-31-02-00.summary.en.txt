The program begins by emphasizing the virtue of reliance on God, stressing the importance of fulfilling worldly duties while maintaining complete trust in divine will.
It then warns, citing Imam Khomeini's poetry, that academic knowledge and philosophy should not hinder spiritual progress or the pursuit of God.
The main segment analyzes the efficacy of Islamic jurisprudence (Fiqh) in managing both individual lives and society, presenting various scholarly perspectives.
Some critics argue that Fiqh is limited to minimum requirements and that ethics are necessary for ideal social management, or that Fiqh is entirely insufficient.
Conversely, the program defends Imam Khomeini's view that Fiqh possesses the complete capacity to govern human life from cradle to grave.
The speaker refutes the critics by arguing that social Fiqh complements individual Fiqh and remains dynamic by addressing contemporary issues within traditional frameworks.
Ultimately, it is concluded that Islamic jurisprudence is a vibrant and comprehensive system capable of effectively managing both personal and societal affairs.
