In the radio program "Historical Q&A," Professor Jafari answered various listener questions regarding Islamic history and religions. He first clarified that Umm al-Banin, the second wife of Imam Ali (AS), married him about a week after the martyrdom of Fatimah (SA) in accordance with her will. The professor also discussed the short mourning period for women in Islam, contrasting it with pre-Islamic pagan traditions. In response to another query, he explained that Qarun was a contemporary of Moses (AS) who amassed wealth through trade and knowledge but was destroyed by God due to his arrogance and failure to pay Zakat. Regarding Uthman ibn Affan, it was noted that he did not have the opportunity to appoint a successor, although Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf played a pivotal role in his selection. The professor identified Mecca as the place of Adam's (AS) descent and the area near Kufa as his burial site, suggesting his language was either Arabic or Old Syriac. Finally, he affirmed that Greek philosophers like Socrates and Plato were influenced by the monotheistic teachings of Abraham and Moses (AS), as evidenced by the presence of monotheism in their philosophical works.
