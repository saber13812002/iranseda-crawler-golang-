In the first segment, the program clarified that inheritance from relatives is not subject to Khums, rendering the concept of a Khums year start date based on such funds invalid.
A story from Saadi's Gulistan was then narrated, illustrating the importance of giving hope to others and refraining from causing harm as key moral virtues.
The second part featured the memoirs of Mr. Nazari, a former POW, who described the covert efforts to acquire a radio to stay informed about events in Iran.
He recounted how prisoners maintained their morale through secret fasting, martial arts training, and cultural activities like poetry and painting.
These activities were crucial for preserving the spiritual resilience and national identity of the captives during their difficult years in captivity.
The program also recommended the "Yadgaran" book series by Ravayat Fath as a valuable resource for learning about the lives of war martyrs.
Ultimately, the broadcast highlighted the enduring lessons of perseverance, sacrifice, and love for the homeland imparted by the freed prisoners.
