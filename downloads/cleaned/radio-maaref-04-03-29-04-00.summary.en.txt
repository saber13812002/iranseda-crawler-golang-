In the religious rulings segment, Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi clarified that paying the expiation for missed fasts due to illness is not immediately obligatory, provided there is hope for recovery before the next Ramadan. Ayatollah Sistani confirmed that investing in interest-bearing loan funds is permissible, with the profits belonging to the investor. He also refuted the claim that missing Friday prayers for three weeks constitutes disbelief, affirming the prayer's obligatory nature for able-bodied men. Regarding eye surgery, Ayatollah Khamenei ruled that patients should not fast if it causes harm, paying fidya if they cannot make up the missed days later. The sermon section emphasized the importance of government officials maintaining accessibility to the public, citing Nahj al-Balagha to warn against prolonged isolation from the people's grievances. Finally, the history program debunked the historical myth that Abdullah ibn Saba, who was involved in Uthman's assassination, was a Shia, labeling it a fabricated narrative.
