This radio program examined the recommendations of Imam Hasan Askari (AS) to his followers, emphasizing piety, righteousness, striving in the path of God, truthfulness, and trustworthiness.
Piety was presented as the primary condition for benefiting from the Quran's guidance and achieving individual and social peace, contrasted with historical examples of impiety.
Striving in the path of God was interpreted as sincere effort and fulfilling one's duty without expecting specific outcomes, similar to the lesson of duty in Karbala.
Truthfulness and trustworthiness were identified as two main ethical pillars, essential in all interactions regardless of the other person's moral standing.
The perspective of viewing life and the world as a trust was explained as a way to free oneself from material attachments and maintain peace during hardships, illustrated by the remarkable story of Ayatollah Mirza Javad Agha Maleki Tabrizi.
The characteristics of a successful Mahdavi society were listed as peace derived from piety, Quran recitation, honesty, trustworthiness, and respecting each other's rights.
Finally, based on Nahj al-Balagha, fairness and treating others as one would wish to be treated were proposed as the key to proper social interactions, concluding with prayers for the Imam's reappearance and success in following these directives.
