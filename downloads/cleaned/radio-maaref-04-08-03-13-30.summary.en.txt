The radio program "Enduring Memories" from Radio Ma'aref featured the recollections of commanders and veterans from the Iran-Iraq War. Brigadier General Hamid Shokiba recounted the early days of the conflict, including the bombing of Mehrabad Airport and the urgent deployment of forces to Andimeshk and the Karun River. He described reconnaissance missions under equipment shortages and the rescue of trapped soldiers in the Hendli area, which helped stabilize the front line for a year and a half. In the second segment, Colonel Ghasem Karimi detailed the operations of Fath al-Mubin, specifically the liberation of the radar site on the asphalt road axis. Karimi shared the story of a soldier named Hosseinzadeh who insisted on staying for the Khorramshahr operation despite being discharged, eventually fulfilling his wish for martyrdom in the Muslim ibn Aqil operation. The program concluded with a dedication to the martyrs of security and border forces, accompanied by poetic tributes to the warriors.
