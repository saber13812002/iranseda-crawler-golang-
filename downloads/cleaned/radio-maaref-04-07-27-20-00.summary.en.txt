Hamas reaffirmed its commitment to the Gaza ceasefire agreement, accusing the Israeli regime of repeatedly violating the truce and evading its obligations. Concurrently, Israeli airstrikes on various parts of Gaza continue, with reports indicating that dozens of civilians have been killed or injured since the ceasefire began. Hamas has proposed a committee of forty independent national figures to manage Gaza and is demanding its immediate formation within a purely Palestinian dialogue framework. Analysts suggest that while Israel and the US are not reliable partners, there are better grounds than in previous rounds to advance to the second phase of the agreement. In other news, Quranic activities commenced at shrines in Iraq, and the second edition of the Martyr Sadr International Prize is set to be held in Najaf. The report also highlighted the significant growth of Islamic schools in the Netherlands following legal changes and the booming halal meat trade in Canada. The broadcast concluded with a call to support the people of Gaza, accompanied by motivational and religious messages.
