The program begins by citing Imam Hasan Askari to identify two major behavioral pitfalls: premature action and inaction at critical moments.
It critiques the haste of Salman al-Farsi at Karbala as an example of acting at the wrong time while failing to act when necessary.
The discussion highlights Imam Hasan's strategy in Medina, where he continuously exposed the corruption of the Umayyad government to prevent the public from forgetting their ongoing enmity.
Referencing the Quranic verse on verification, the program emphasizes the necessity of investigating news from untrustworthy sources to avoid being misled by modern media rumors.
The historical narrative is revisited to show that Imam Reza (AS) fought against deviant currents and the Abbasid regime through a global network called "Wakalat."
Imam Reza is depicted as strategically exploiting internal Abbasid conflicts to organize Shia movements, while his forced exile by Mamun is framed as a plot to control Shia leadership.
The broadcast concludes with a narrative from the book "Galaxy of Nothingness," where Sayyid Ali in Najaf guides a young beggar toward honest work and finds spiritual certainty in the shrine of Imam Ali (AS).
