Iran's Foreign Minister discussed the strong Iranian presence at Arbaeen and the Iraqi people's support for Iran's resistance during the recent conflict. He emphasized that military readiness is the most effective deterrent against war and cautioned against overestimating the threat posed by the snapback mechanism compared to existing sanctions. Regarding the nuclear program, he called for a new framework for IAEA inspections based on the new parliamentary law following the destruction of nuclear facilities. The minister dismissed the presence of an American engineering company in Armenia as a military threat but stressed the importance of preserving borders and avoiding foreign military presence. He described the relationship between military power and diplomacy as complementary, noting that both must work together to achieve national interests. Finally, he highlighted the importance of utilizing the memoirs of former POWs to strengthen the spiritual identity and resilience of the Iranian nation.
