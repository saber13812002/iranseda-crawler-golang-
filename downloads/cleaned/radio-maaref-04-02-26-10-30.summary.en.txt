In this radio program, the existence of Imam Zaman (AJ) was proven as the divine expert and guide of religion using rational and scriptural arguments.
The Imam's key attributes were identified as infallibility, stemming from his detachment from worldly dependencies, and divine knowledge covering the past, present, and future.
This divine knowledge and oversight enable the Imam, as the manifestation of God on earth, to manage human lives with comprehensive precision.
Hojjat-ol-Islam Kafil emphasized the necessity of hope for the future and the emergence of a global government based on Wilayah, citing Quranic verses.
He predicted that after the appearance, religious fears will transform into absolute security, and Islam will become dominant throughout the world.
The main conclusion is that active waiting means entrusting the holistic management of one's life to the Imam, rather than merely praying and visiting shrines.
By accepting the Imam as the helmsman of the ship of life, one can navigate through the storms of tribulations to reach lasting peace and joy.
This approach ensures that social and economic interactions become healthy and meaningful under divine supervision.
