In a Radio Ma'aref broadcast, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the importance of avoiding waste and caring for God's blessings, citing narrations from Imam Sadiq (AS) and the meticulous habits of Imam Khomeini (RA). He illustrated that waste differs from stinginess by sharing anecdotes of Imam Khomeini's careful use of tissues, turning off unnecessary lights, and collecting fallen fruits. The second segment featured a narrative from the book "Ocean of the East," where Hajj Karim the cobbler argues to Abdul Rahman that the Imam must be infallible to warrant unconditional obedience. Using Quranic verses and the Hadith of Jabir, he demonstrated that human intellect is insignificant compared to the Imam's divine status. The program concluded with a jurisprudential answer stating that deferred Khums should be paid from the new year's income or other non-Khums assets, as per obligatory precaution. The broadcast ended with wishes for a season filled with goodness and blessings for the listeners.
