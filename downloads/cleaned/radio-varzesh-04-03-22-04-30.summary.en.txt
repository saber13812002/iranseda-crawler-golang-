Mehdad Hojafard, a former player for Homa FC, shares his childhood memories of playing street football in Tehran neighborhoods. He emphasizes that coaches like Ahmad Tusi and Ali Kazemi prioritized moral and human development over technical skills before teaching football tactics. Joining Homa FC was a turning point for him, as the team's strict discipline and order transformed not only his football career but also his approach to life and education. Hojafard describes Homa's unique culture of loyalty, friendship, and mutual respect, which led players to reject lucrative offers from bigger clubs. He recounts a memorable anecdote about his coach demanding that players pursue university education, which significantly impacted their life paths. Finally, he identifies the 1980 championship as the team's peak success, while noting that this achievement raised fan expectations and kept the team under constant comparison with larger rivals.
