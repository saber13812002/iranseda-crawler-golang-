In this program, Professor Tabatabai explained the rulings regarding the stoning of the Jamarat and the sacrificial ritual in Mina, emphasizing the necessity of adhering to Sharia conditions for selecting a proxy and the sacrificial animal. He clarified that pilgrims unable to physically attend the Jamarat may appoint a proxy, provided the proxy performs the stoning during the day rather than at night. Regarding the sacrifice, it was noted that the ritual must be conducted by authorized representatives in mechanized slaughterhouses, after which the pilgrim proceeds to shaving or trimming the hair. In the Q&A section, Hojjat al-Islam Falsadeh permitted the recitation of non-Arabic supplications in the Ghunut of prayer, while Hojjat al-Islam Mahdavi stated that a small amount of oil residue on the feet does not invalidate Wudu. Hojjat al-Islam Mohammadi Sheikh-Shabani advised paying the debts of deceased creditors to the poor, and Hojjat al-Islam Jawari considered delaying Khums payment as usurping others' property. Furthermore, Hojjat al-Islam Alian Najad ruled that delaying the performance of Qada prayers does not invalidate the fast, and Hojjat al-Islam Zino Labadine explained the difference between Fidyah and Kaffarah, noting that one Kaffarah of choice suffices for multiple years of delay.
