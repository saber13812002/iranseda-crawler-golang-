This radio program focuses on the tragic events of Ashura, describing the most painful moments of the Karbala tragedy.
The narrator uses the metaphor of weaning a child to express the immense suffering of mothers and children on that day.
The story highlights the bravery and sacrifice of Ali Akbar (AS), who shielded his father with a smile.
Emotional descriptions of his martyrdom and the reactions of his family complete the somber and heroic atmosphere.
The program concludes by inviting listeners to visit Imam Reza (AS) and Hazrat Zahra (SA).
The ultimate goal is to seek blessings and peace for listeners' troubled lives through the remembrance of the Ahl al-Bayt.
Finally, prayers are recited to alleviate sorrows and bring joy to the hearts of orphans and mothers.
