The 33rd International Quran Exhibition is being held in Tehran, featuring artists and scholars from twenty countries, with Sheikh Soltani honored as a special guest. Meanwhile, Muslims in Utrecht, Netherlands, organized an Iftar tent and congregational prayer to promote Islam and combat Islamophobia. In Afghanistan, the tradition of Iftar, marked by cooking Qabuli Palaw in mosques, remains a significant symbol of solidarity among Muslims. Reports also highlighted the history of Al-Azhar University in Egypt as the oldest university in the Islamic world and its role in Islamic education. Politically, the head of the Malaysia Quds Foundation called on Muslims worldwide to participate in the International Day of Quds march, while the Islamic Cooperation Union condemned recent Israeli decisions in the West Bank. The visit of Trump’s special envoy to Iraq was analyzed as an effort to pressure political groups to prevent Nouri al-Maliki’s return to the premiership and curb the influence of the Coordination Framework. The UN Secretary-General emphasized the Israeli regime's violation of human rights in Palestine and warned against the failure of the two-state solution. Finally, a Hezbollah parliamentary member in Lebanon stressed the legitimacy of the resistance's weapons and the need to respond to border violations.
