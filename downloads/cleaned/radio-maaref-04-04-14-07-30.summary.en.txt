The radio program began with a noha reciting the virtues of Imam Abbas (AS) before transitioning to a discussion on the concept of charity and generosity in his life. The guest speaker, Hujjat al-Islam Falahati, identified Imam Abbas as the supreme model of complete and perfect charity, emphasizing that he sacrificed his entire being to protect Imam Hussein (AS) at Karbala. By referencing historical accounts, such as his efforts to secure water for the tents and his martyrdom in the path of monotheism, the speaker highlighted that Imam Abbas serves as an unparalleled role model for all ages. The speaker stressed that to benefit from these virtues, one must approach his sacred presence with a spiritual mindset and full reliance on God, seeking forgiveness and generosity. It was noted that there is no stinginess on the part of Imam Abbas, and the door to monotheism and moral excellence is open to anyone who approaches with an open heart. The program concluded by encouraging listeners to emulate this virtuous lifestyle and to include the Radio Ma'aref team in their prayers.
