The program begins with a Quranic analysis emphasizing the distinct nature of "tawaf" in death versus sleep.
Scholars argue that in death, the essence of the soul departs to God, whereas in sleep, the soul's animal and human aspects ascend to a higher stage.
This process indicates that sleep is a temporary death where the soul is present with God at that moment.
Consequently, reciting the prayer "Allahumma inni astawdi'uka nafsi" before sleep is recommended to entrust a pure soul to God.
The second part of the program highlights historical events of Aban 27, including the martyrdom of Sardar Mehdi Zin-ol-Din and the passing of Ayatollah Ashtiani.
It also recalls the release of American female and Black hostages by Imam Khomeini's order and the birth of Louis Daguerre, the father of photography.
Finally, the morning discussion focuses on the concept of being a "supporter," with listeners sharing their personal experiences of receiving support.
The program concludes with morning prayers and expressions of gratitude to the audience.
