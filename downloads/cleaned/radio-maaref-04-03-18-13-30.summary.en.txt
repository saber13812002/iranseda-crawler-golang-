The radio program "Thoughts," featuring Dr. Rasoul Rezaei, examines the political dimensions of promoting ancient history in Iran.
The primary driver of this trend is identified as the activities of Western Orientalists who distorted history to create a divide between Iran and the Islamic world.
Dr. Rezaei argues that the claim of Achaemenid Zoroastrianism lacks historical evidence and that ancient history was exploited during the Pahlavi era to weaken religion.
Citing Edward Said, the speaker describes cultural aggression by Orientalists as more destructive than military colonialism and a source of historical misrepresentation.
He critiques irrational and superstitious laws in ancient texts like the Vendidad, demonstrating their contradiction with reason and Islamic teachings.
Furthermore, the cultural inconsistencies of the ancient era, such as rigid class systems, are contrasted with the justice and equality of Islam.
The conclusion is that ancient history should not conflict with religion, and Iranians must use reason to distinguish compatible heritage from false beliefs.
