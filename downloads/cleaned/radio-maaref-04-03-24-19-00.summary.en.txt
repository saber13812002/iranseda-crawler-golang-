In the Zamzam Ahkam program, Imam Khomeini's fatwa prioritizing settlement in blood money payments was discussed, noting that the expert's valuation applies if no agreement is reached, unless specific laws dictate otherwise. Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi's ruling on Zakat al-Fitr, requiring the payment of three kilograms of the local staple food such as bread or rice, was also presented. Regarding gender determination via IVF, Ayatollah Sistani ruled it impermissible without necessity due to the prohibition of looking at or touching private parts. Ayatollah Soubhani advised consulting the marja's office for Khums payments during Hajj, specifying that the Ihram and Hasyafandi portions must be paid before departure. Ayatollah Shabih Zanjani clarified that swallowing a significant amount of food invalidates prayer, whereas minor droplets of sweat or rose water do not. The Khaneh Doost segment honored Eid Qadir by discussing the historical roles of Imam Baqir and Imam Sadiq in the Fin and Kashan region, including a letter from Imam Sadiq to his representative. A reporter from the Gilan center highlighted the cultural and social services provided by Imamzadeh Hashem in Rasht and its positive impact on the local economy.
