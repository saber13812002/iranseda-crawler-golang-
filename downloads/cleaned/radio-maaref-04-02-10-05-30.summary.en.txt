The radio program began with a fiqh lecture by Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi, focusing on the distinction between obligatory and situational rulings in the components of worship and transactions.
The instructor used narrations to explain the necessity of repetition and expiation for intentional or ignorant omissions in Hajj rituals, emphasizing the rule of situational implication in conditions.
The second part featured ethical sermons by Ayatollah Misbah Yazdi, highlighting the importance of the believer's sorrow in this world.
He argued that while entering Hell is certain for all, exiting it depends on divine grace, which should instill a sense of caution and prevent worldly arrogance.
This uncertainty regarding salvation is meant to keep believers away from negligence and excessive joy, fostering a state of spiritual alertness.
Ayatollah Misbah also defined beneficial knowledge as that which induces weeping and humility when hearing divine verses, softening hardened hearts.
The program concluded by honoring the Ten Days of Karamat, encouraging women to emulate Hazrat Masoumeh (SA), and ended with prayers for the Imam's reappearance.
