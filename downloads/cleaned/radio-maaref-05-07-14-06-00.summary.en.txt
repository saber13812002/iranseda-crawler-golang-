Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets the concept of "six days" in the creation of the heavens and earth as distinct cosmic eras rather than standard 24-hour days. Using the method of interpreting the Quran by the Quran, he argues that the term "heavens and earth" encompasses the entire cosmic system, including the space and layers between them. Since other verses specify that the heavens and the earth were each created in two days, it logically follows that the remaining two days were dedicated to creating the intermediate space. This analysis is derived purely from the semantic connection between verses without relying on external dictionaries or linguistic tools. The commentator distinguishes between empirical scientific statements, which may evolve over time, and the immutable philosophical and theological principles of the Quran. He asserts that Quranic philosophical arguments, such as the necessity of a mover for motion, remain constant regardless of scientific shifts like the Copernican revolution. Thus, while human understanding of astronomy has changed from geocentric to heliocentric models, the theological truth that the created requires a Creator remains unaltered.
