The program begins with listener messages expressing their desire to visit Imam Reza (PBUH) and praying for the health of their loved ones.
Guest Mohammad Hadi Fallah then discusses the vice of lying and its underlying causes.
He identifies ignorance, spiritual inferiority, and a lack of manliness as the primary drivers of dishonesty.
Repeated lying and insisting on falsehoods lead to being labeled a liar by God.
Listening to lies and spreading them, especially on social media, acts as a catalyst for multiplying this vice.
Believers are advised to avoid listening to worldly sinners to protect their faith from corruption.
The episode concludes with prayers and blessings upon the Ahl al-Bayt (PBUH).
