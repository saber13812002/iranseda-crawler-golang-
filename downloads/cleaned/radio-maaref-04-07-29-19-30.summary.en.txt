In this radio program, following the recitation of a rain prayer from the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya, Hujjat al-Islam Falahati discussed the harms of verbal sins, specifically fault-finding and criticizing others. Citing religious narrations, he emphasized that anyone who faults others will first be afflicted with the same defect and ultimately face divine punishment. To prevent these vices, observing piety and restraining oneself from engaging in backbiting or condemnation is strongly recommended. If a person has committed this sin, the cure involves two steps: first seeking forgiveness from those harmed, and then repenting and weeping before God. For those who are subjected to criticism by others, patience and endurance are advised so that divine intervention may resolve their issues. Finally, verses from the Quran were recited, asking God for forgiveness and assistance to be relieved of the heavy burden of sins.
