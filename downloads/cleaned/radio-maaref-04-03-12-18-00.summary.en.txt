In this radio program, Professor Fallah explains the authentic concept of humility based on Quranic verses and narrations, emphasizing that it must be practiced solely for God's sake and for self-improvement. He argues that humility driven by material needs or a desire for status is not only undignified but also leads to spiritual regression and the loss of one's faith. A key point in the discussion is that the poor should maintain their self-respect by relying on God rather than making material demands on the wealthy, prioritizing divine pleasure over worldly gains. The program also highlights that being humble towards believers while remaining firm against enemies are characteristics of those beloved by God. Practical signs of humility, such as sitting in the lower part of a gathering without expectation, are cited from narrations as commendable behaviors. Ultimately, adhering to the spiritual guidance of the Infallibles and avoiding social prejudices is presented as the key to achieving divine proximity and inner peace.
