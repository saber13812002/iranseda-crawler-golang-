This radio program began by celebrating the birth of Hazrat Fatimah Zahra (SA) and exploring her virtues and high status. Hujjat al-Islam Bagherabadi explained the title "Mudathira" based on narrations from Imam Sadiq (AS), noting that angels addressed her similarly to how they spoke to Maryam (SA). He emphasized that her superiority extends to all women of all eras, not just her own time. The program also recounted the miraculous birth of Hazrat Fatimah (SA), attended by angels and four heavenly women. The "Enduring Thoughts" segment featured quotes from Shahid Motahhari on the importance of spiritual insight over mere instruction. A listener shared a personal story of how attending a sermon by Sayyid Mehdi Ghavam transformed his life and reconciled him with his father. This narrative highlights the profound power of religious discourse in guiding individuals and changing their destinies.
