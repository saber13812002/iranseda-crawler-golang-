This radio program explores the concept of "self-loss" as a failure in mental management and incorrect human calculations. Citing the Shabaniyya supplication and religious narrations, the speaker explains that spiritual negligence acts like intoxication, causing individuals to overlook the value of their youth and life opportunities. A narration from the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) highlights that the most valuable prayer is one where a person, through personal reflection, asks to be of the same rank as the Prophet in Paradise. Quranic verses emphasize that the greatest losers are those who believe they are doing good in this world but are actually making miscalculations. In the second part, based on narrations from the book of Kafi, the direct relationship between one's sustenance and religious obligations is examined. Imam Sadiq (AS) stated that the more sustenance a person has, the more financial and social responsibilities they bear. Therefore, being content with less sustenance does not imply laziness but rather signifies a lighter burden of duties and God's acceptance of fewer actions.
