In this interview, Professor Javad Mohdasi discusses the profound impact of Jalal Al-e-Ahmad's works and literature related to Palestine and Sayyid Jamal al-Din Afghani on the pre-revolutionary youth. He recalls the study of books such as "Avaye Roozha" and "Bidayat al-Hikmah" and "Nihayat al-Hikmah" by Allameh Tabatabai, which were taught using innovative methods and manually reproduced in the Haqq al-Qom seminary. Mohdasi emphasizes the crucial role these texts played in awakening political consciousness, exposing the mechanisms of colonialism, and fostering a revolutionary spirit among young people, including ordinary citizens. The segment concludes with the recitation of Ziyarat Al-Yasin and a speech by Imam Khomeini, stressing the necessity of sincerity in serving Islam and relying entirely on God rather than material or military power. The final message underscores the importance of following the school of thought of the Ahl al-Bayt and strengthening Islamic foundations against global challenges. This historical narrative illustrates how resistance literature and Islamic thought laid the groundwork for the political and religious identity of the revolutionary generation.
