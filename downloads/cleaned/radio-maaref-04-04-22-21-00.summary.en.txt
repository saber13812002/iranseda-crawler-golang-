The radio broadcast began with a spiritual atmosphere for Muharram, featuring elegies and an invitation to mourn for the Ahl al-Bayt.
A narrative from the book "Neshan-e Hasan" was then presented, depicting Fatima, the mother of Imam Hasan, performing Tawaf around the Kaaba at night with Qasim.
The story highlights Fatima's encounter with Zaynab, who is longing for Medina, and Qasim's concerns regarding the political threats posed by Yazid in Mecca.
Imam Hasan reassures Qasim, emphasizing reliance on God and the wisdom of their uncles, Ali and Abbas, rather than personal anxiety.
In the second segment, a listener's question about the permissibility of holding religious gatherings in apartments was addressed.
The response clarified that while such gatherings are highly recommended, it is obligatory to avoid causing disturbance to neighbors.
The program concluded by providing contact channels for listener feedback and encouraging the audience to tune in via the Iran Sound app and specific radio frequencies.
