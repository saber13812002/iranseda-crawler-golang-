The program began with Quranic recitations and prayers emphasizing the importance of fear of God and weeping in worship, citing the habits of the Imams and scholars regarding night prayers. It then continued the dramatic retelling of the book "Green Blue," where the Muslim protagonist, Sayyid Farid, falls in love with Jacqueline, a Christian girl, while working at a library in Tripoli. After completing his carpentry work, Farid writes a love letter to Jacqueline and hides it inside a book he has borrowed. Jacqueline, fearing her strict father, replies that in-person meetings are dangerous and suggests they continue their relationship through letters. However, Jacqueline's father, Dawood, discovers the letter and, filled with anger, decides to confront Farid firmly to stop the relationship. In the final segment, the program addressed a religious ruling stating that applying oil to hair for adornment while in the state of Ihram is prohibited. The broadcast concluded with an invitation to follow Radio Ma'aref's official channels on the Eitaa and Virasty messaging platforms.
