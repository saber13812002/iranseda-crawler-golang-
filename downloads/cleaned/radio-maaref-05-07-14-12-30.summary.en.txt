This radio program begins by emphasizing the importance of "basirat" (insight) and environmental awareness for students, citing the Supreme Leader's teachings. It then transitions to the testimony of Hossein Haghjati-Zadeh, a veteran of the Iran-Iraq War, who recounts his harrowing experience of captivity. He describes the brutal transfer to enemy camps in Iraq, including the psychological torture of the "Tunnel of Terror" in Basra and Baghdad. His narrative highlights the resilience of Iranian POWs who endured severe physical hardships and humiliation while maintaining their faith. The broadcast also features a brief introduction to the book "Chand o Chand Jaye Khali" and promotes various cultural and Quranic events.
