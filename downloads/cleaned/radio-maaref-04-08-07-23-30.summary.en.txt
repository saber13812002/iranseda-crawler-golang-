The program began with religious songs and prayers dedicated to Imam Reza (PBUH), followed by a discussion on the importance of charity and helping those in need.
The host cited a narration from the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) emphasizing that even if one lacks financial means, they should borrow money to assist the needy.
Referencing the story of Prophet Ibrahim (AS) and a bird dropping water on fire, the host explained that no act of kindness is insignificant in the eyes of God.
The discussion then highlighted the role of unity and faith in the success of the Islamic Revolution, identifying them as the key to the Iranian nation's victory.
The host warned that despite past achievements, Iran remains vulnerable to economic and military threats from major powers, particularly the United States.
He stressed that true victory will only be achieved when the roots of division between people and religion are eliminated and national unity is preserved.
Finally, listeners were encouraged to send supportive messages and special prayers, and the program concluded with closing supplications.
