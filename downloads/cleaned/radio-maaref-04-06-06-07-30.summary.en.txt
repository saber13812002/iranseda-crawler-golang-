In this radio program, verse 40 of Surah At-Tawbah is used to explore the concept of relying on God and the divine tranquility revealed to the Prophet in the Cave of Thawr.
The guest emphasizes that this peace is not exclusive to the Prophet but is granted to anyone who seeks refuge in God during moments of fear or anxiety.
The discussion then shifts to the sin of backbiting, which the Quran compares to eating the flesh of one's dead brother.
Imam Sadiq (AS) describes backbiting as the act of those who are spiritually deficient in monotheism and rely on the reputation of others for their own survival.
Character destruction is rooted in ignorance and polytheism, and is considered a major verbal sin that God dislikes.
The program concludes with the recitation of Quranic verses prohibiting backbiting and a closing prayer, urging listeners to avoid slander and rely on divine power.
