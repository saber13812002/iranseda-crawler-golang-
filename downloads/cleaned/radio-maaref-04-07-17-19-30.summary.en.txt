In this episode of the radio program "Ma'aref," Hujjat al-Islam Mohammad Hadi Falah continued discussing methods for treating cursing and foul language. Citing a narration from Imam Sadiq (AS), he emphasized that cursing is a grave injustice and has no place in the practical conduct of the Infallibles. The primary remedies for overcoming this vice include seeking forgiveness from God, compensating the harmed individual, and committing to never repeat the behavior. The program also highlighted the importance of seeking intercession through the Ahl al-Bayt (AS) and their narrations as a means of healing. Finally, a story was shared about a foul-mouthed woman who was cured of her habit after receiving a morsel of food from the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH). This narrative illustrates the profound, healing power of the Infallibles' presence and words in reforming human character.
