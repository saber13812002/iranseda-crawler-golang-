This radio program featured the speeches of Martyr Ayatollah Bahشتی regarding the tripartite role of prophets in guidance, leadership, and justice. He described prophets as illuminators who use divine signs to clarify ambiguities and establish the criteria for distinguishing truth from falsehood. Bahشتی distinguished between "guidance" as showing the path and "imamate" as practical leadership involving active participation with the people. He warned that an excessive gap between the leader and the followers undermines effective leadership, emphasizing that a true leader must remain accessible and serve as a practical model. Regarding justice, he stressed the inseparable link between individual moral integrity and social or economic justice in an Islamic society. Bahشتی urged officials to accelerate their efforts toward achieving justice to prevent deviation from the main path. Finally, he called for complete alignment between the thoughts, words, and actions of officials to realize the lofty ideals of Islam.
