The program begins by emphasizing the importance of learning from past mistakes, illustrated by a story of Ayatollah Yazdi who wept for not learning from history like an animal does. The main segment features a dramatic retelling from the book "Ocean of the East," where a character named Amran, after being saved from bandits, is urged by a doctor and a girl to seek the intercession of Imam Reza and remember his kindness. In the Q&A section, it is clarified that the Supreme Leader's fatwa prohibits buying lottery tickets universally, regardless of whether the purchase occurs in Muslim or non-Muslim countries. The broadcast concludes by highlighting the significance of Rabi al-Awal, encouraging listeners to forgive grudges and treat their families kindly to achieve closeness to Imam Reza on the Day of Judgment.
