In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Nazariyan emphasizes the necessity of prayer for everyone, including those who are materially wealthy, citing the teachings of Imam Ali and the Quran. He argues that since humans are unaware of their future, they must constantly seek divine connection to preserve their blessings and prepare for the afterlife. Prayer is presented not only as a means to fulfill material needs but also as a crucial path to inner peace and a remedy for depression that worldly supports cannot address. The speaker explains that God is the responder of prayers, ensuring that every supplication is answered either immediately, in the hereafter, or by averting a greater calamity. Furthermore, he highlights that purity of speech and action, along with avoiding sins like severing family ties, are essential conditions for prayers to be accepted. By referencing the Prophet's and Imam Hussein's devotion, he illustrates that seeking refuge in God remains the primary shelter even during extreme hardships. Ultimately, Muslims are advised to maintain a continuous relationship with God through remembrance and prayer to remain under His divine protection.
