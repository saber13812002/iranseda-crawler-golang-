In the closing segment of the "Mehrbān Bāsim" program, the importance of national unity and supporting the government despite potential criticisms was emphasized amidst sensitive economic and security conditions.
The guest, Mr. Mohammadian Shahrudi, cited Quranic verses and the Supreme Leader's statements to urge avoiding a rift between the government and the people in the face of enemy plots.
Referencing the concept of "do not divide," he argued that tolerating one another and setting aside political differences is the highest moral and religious value at this time.
Subsequently, the "Ganj-e Sa'adat" program began with Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallah, focusing on the topic of backbiting from the perspective of the Quran and narrations.
Professor Fallah described backbiting as a chronic moral disease rooted in ignorance and neglect of the Quran, which has intensified in the virtual space.
Quoting hadiths from the Prophet and the Imams, he compared backbiting to eating the flesh of one's brother and labeled it as haram and harmful to society.
The key point was that the backbiter actually reveals their own flaws and, by repeating this act, becomes accustomed to it and distances themselves from moral virtues.
Finally, complete abstention from backbiting was emphasized as a condition for entering the domain of Wilayah and maintaining the psychological security of the Islamic society.
