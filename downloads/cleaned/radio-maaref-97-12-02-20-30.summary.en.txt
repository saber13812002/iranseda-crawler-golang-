In the radio program "I Am Fatima," Hojjat al-Islam Hosseini Bagheri explored the social conduct of Hazrat Fatima Zahra (SA), focusing on neighborliness and charity. Citing a narration from Imam Hassan (AS), he highlighted the Prophet's daughter's habit of praying for her neighbors and prioritizing them over herself, presenting this as a model for both spiritual and material interactions. The discussion also recounted the famous story of her wedding night, where she gifted her new dress to a poor man and attended the ceremony in an old one, demonstrating the true essence of selfless giving. In the second segment, Martyr Chamran emphasized the necessity of internal and spiritual transformation for a successful revolution, arguing that the Islamic Revolution's victory was achieved through the people's readiness for martyrdom rather than material power. He illustrated this point with a historical anecdote from Tabriz, where a cleric's call for martyrdom caused soldiers to surrender twelve tanks without a fight. The overall conclusion underscores the vital connection between social actions based on the Imams' example and the internal revolution of the human soul as the key to achieving perfection and ultimate success.
