The radio program begins by emphasizing the importance of engaging with the youth and utilizing their spiritual potential. It then features the testimony of Zahra Imami, the daughter of a Holy War martyr and the wife of martyr Hossein Moradi. She recounts her difficult childhood following her father's disappearance in Operation Karbala 5 and the subsequent deaths of her grandparents. Zahra describes the miraculous moment her mother recognized her father's coffin and the years of grief and loneliness they endured together. She shares her story of marrying Hossein Moradi, who, despite initial disagreements about their living conditions, persisted until they were united. Hossein, a member of the IRGC, traveled to Syria in 2014 to defend the holy shrines and participated in various operations. He was severely wounded in his final mission and, after a period in a coma, was martyred on November 19, 2014. This narrative serves as a testament to the love, patience, and sacrifice of martyrs' wives in the face of life's hardships and the loss of their loved ones.
