In this radio program, editors and legal experts discussed a new parliamentary resolution stating that financially capable men will not be imprisoned for failing to pay mahr (dowry) amounts under fourteen gold coins. A key point clarified is that the exemption for financially unable men has existed since 2015, while the new innovation extends this protection to capable men who refuse to pay small mahr amounts. Instead of imprisonment, these individuals will be placed under electronic monitoring, a move critics argue dangerously facilitates the evasion of financial obligations. One professor emphasized that while prison is ineffective for genuine debtors, stronger mechanisms are needed to identify hidden assets and ensure creditors' rights are upheld. He also warned that this law could create new challenges in financial relationships and encourage asset concealment. The program subsequently featured the recitation and translation of verses from the Quran's Surah Qiyamah. The broadcast concluded with an invitation to listen to the next episode.
