In this radio program, the speaker emphasizes the sacred fear of the Hereafter and the necessity of prayer as a means of submission to divine greatness, while highlighting the importance of ethics and social upbringing. He strongly criticizes takfiri currents that have weakened Sunni Islam by declaring its theological, jurisprudential, and Sufi branches as heretical, stressing the need to avoid superficial judgments and to maintain mutual respect among Islamic schools of thought. By referencing the Prophet's (PBUH) sensitivities regarding education, the fight against superstition, and tribalism, the speaker underscores the pivotal role of the family, particularly mothers, in raising a healthy generation and preserving the community's religious identity. In the concluding section, while honoring the Sacred Defense Week and the martyrs, listeners are encouraged to maintain their emotional connection with Imam Hussein (AS) and the culture of martyrdom by sending salutations and performing remote ziyarat.
