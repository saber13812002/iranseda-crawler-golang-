In this radio program, the manager of the Fatima Masoumeh Mawqif reported on the commencement of their services in Karbala, highlighting the extensive facilities prepared for pilgrims. Located at the Jisr Fatima, the Mawqif operates in a space exceeding 14,000 square meters, offering three daily meals, prayer services, and religious programs. To combat the intense heat, numerous air conditioners have been installed, with all costs covered by public donations. Listeners were encouraged to support the Mawqif by sending the text "Labbaik Ya Hussein" or "Ya Hussein" to the number 3552. Mahmoud Parchibaf, a volunteer, reflected on the historical sacrifices of the people in 1404 AH, contrasting that era with the present day. He emphasized that worldly power is transient, and only service to God constitutes a lasting investment for the afterlife. The segment concluded by stressing the importance of serving God sincerely to fulfill His promise of support, rather than relying on earthly powers.
