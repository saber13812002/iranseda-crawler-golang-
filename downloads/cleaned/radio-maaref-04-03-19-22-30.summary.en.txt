In this radio program, Professor Panahian outlines three Islamic approaches to dealing with life's hardships: prevention, practical action, and the conscious acceptance of suffering. He emphasizes that many fears are merely mental constructs, and maintaining tranquility through trust in God helps mitigate their impact. A central theme is "embracing suffering," which involves willingly accepting beneficial hardships like exercise, worship, or sacrifice to prevent greater pains in the future. Fleeing from problems does not solve them but rather doubles the suffering by creating persistent anxiety and mental preoccupation. In the second segment, expert Dr. Tarashoon discusses the importance of a mother's mental health in child-rearing, noting that emotional stability is crucial for effective parenting. He highlights that a mother's prolonged psychological issues can negatively affect the emotional atmosphere of the home and the quality of education provided to children. Therefore, maintaining inner peace and responsibly facing challenges are fundamental to both navigating life's difficulties and raising well-adjusted children.
