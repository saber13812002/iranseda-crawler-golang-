In the radio program "Porsoman-e Enghelab," Dr. Laksazai analyzed Iran's political and diplomatic challenges. He emphasized that negotiations with the United States do not imply accepting its hegemony but serve as a tool to defend national interests and alleviate unjust sanctions. The expert highlighted that the only sustainable solution to counter sanctions is relying on domestic capabilities and self-sufficiency in strategic sectors. Addressing the lack of unity among Islamic states against Israel, he attributed this weakness to the gap between claiming to be Muslim and actually practicing Islamic values. Many Islamic leaders avoid supporting Palestine due to fears of losing power or their dependence on the West. However, he noted the global awakening of people and the loss of moral credibility by Western powers and their Israeli allies following recent events. Finally, he characterized nuclear talks as strictly limited to peaceful issues and demanded firm guarantees that sanctions would not return under different pretexts.
