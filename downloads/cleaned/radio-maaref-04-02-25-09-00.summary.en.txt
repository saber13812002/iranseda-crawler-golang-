In the first segment, Professor Laksay emphasized the importance of safeguarding the education system and prayed for protection against the influence of Satan and the system of domination.
He argued, citing historical evidence and statements from clerics like Ayatollah Khomeini, that the clergy and scholars were consistently aligned with the Islamic Revolution and the Holy Defense.
The professor clarified that while methodological differences existed among scholars, there was no fundamental opposition to the revolution or religious leadership among the religious elite.
The second segment focused on the phenomenon of "reverse migration," specifically the return of Iranian doctors and nurses to their home country.
Returned migrants stated that their primary motivation was the sense of purpose in building the nation and a deep cultural belonging.
Psychologists and experts identified job burnout, loneliness abroad, and the lack of genuine service in Western systems as key drivers for this return.
Additionally, improved supportive conditions and family responsibilities played a significant role in their decision to stay in Iran.
