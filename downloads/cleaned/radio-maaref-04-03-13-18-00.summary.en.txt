In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Falah emphasizes that humility is only valuable when performed for the sake of God rather than for personal gain. Citing a narration from Imam Hasan Askari, he highlights Imam Ali's humility in serving a guest by washing his hands, presenting it as a supreme model of conduct. The key point is that Imam Ali viewed the guest as his brother, an act that earned him immense divine reward. The speaker stresses that a true Shia is one who follows this humble tradition, particularly by sitting in the lower rows of religious gatherings to avoid ostentation. Other signs of humility include greeting others first, refraining from argumentation even when right, and rejecting excessive praise. The program concludes by introducing an active Quranic session in Mashhad and discussing the blessings of reciting the Quran in one's home.
