In this program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli provides an exegesis of verses 115 to 119 of Surah Al-Ma'idah, emphasizing the distinction between bestowed blessings and answered prayers. He explains that if a prayer is answered, the individual is obligated to remain grateful and steadfast for the rest of their life, as ingratitude in such cases carries a greater risk of severe punishment. A significant literary point discussed is the use of the past tense for events of the Day of Judgment, which signifies their absolute certainty and their nature as being detached from time rather than being in the past. The special relationship between God and Jesus (PBUH) is also highlighted, noting that God is his tongue and that Jesus sold his soul to God, making it a divine property. Consequently, Jesus (PBUH) confidently disavows inviting people to polytheism, as it is impossible to do so using God's own tongue and a soul that belongs to God. Finally, the difference between the tone of objection and intercession in Jesus's (PBUH) speech compared to Abraham's (PBUH) is clarified. Jesus speaks not in a state of intercession or complaint, but to affirm the truth and demonstrate God's justice and wisdom.
