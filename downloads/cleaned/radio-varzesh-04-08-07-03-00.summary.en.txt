Former national basketball player Farzamarkhaliqi recounts his journey from being selected for the national youth team to competing in the Asian Games in Manila and subsequently serving in the military in Urmia. After completing his service, he balanced his athletic career with academic pursuits, passing the national university entrance exam to study electrical engineering at the Tehran Polytechnic University. Although he received a professional offer from Turkey, he declined it to stay in Iran with his mother, eventually graduating and transitioning into the information technology sector. He held senior management positions at major organizations, including IBM and the Central Bank, while also serving as the head of the national handball teams. In this role, he played a key part in preparing the handball team for the Rio Olympics and securing a silver medal at the Qatar qualifiers. During his tenure as CEO of Sadad Data Processing, he was a pioneer in developing the "Bale" messaging app, overseeing its launch and branding. This sports veteran defines success not as reaching a destination, but as the continuous effort to achieve multiple goals, viewing life as a rewarding journey rather than just the end result.
