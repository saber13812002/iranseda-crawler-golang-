The radio program "Zamzam Ahkam" began by outlining the rituals of Umrah Hajj, including the stances at Arafat and Muzdalifah, followed by the movement to Mina and the stoning of the Jamarat. In the fatwa section, based on Ayatollah Khamenei's ruling, it was clarified that failing to pay Khums does not invalidate correct prayers or fasts, though it may hinder their full acceptance. The program also cited Ayatollah Shabiri Zanjani's views on the impropriety of handshakes in mosques and the prohibition of Muslim women marrying non-Muslim men. Other responses addressed the permissibility of exchanging small change for large bills for charity with authorization, and noted that the expiation for a broken fast by a single adult child is their own responsibility. Ayatollah Sistani's ruling was also shared, stating that obligatory ghusl does not need to be performed before Fajr prayer outside of Ramadan. The broadcast concluded by emphasizing the importance of prayer as the believer's ascension and the necessity of seeking intercession through the Ahl al-Bayt, particularly Lady Zainab (SA), for spiritual guidance and relief from difficulties.
