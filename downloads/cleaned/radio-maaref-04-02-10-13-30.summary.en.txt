The radio program "Roayat-e Ashefteh," hosted by Arash Darabi, features the love story of Hajjar Pourvazeh, the wife of martyr Hossein Amini. Hajjar recounts their meeting in 1978 and the symbolic gift of a plane tree leaf during their first encounter. Despite her father's initial objection and their class differences, the young couple married and began a simple life together. Hossein Amini was martyred in the Karbala 5 operation in 1986, and Hajjar describes his final moments and his dream of martyrdom before heading to the front. In the program's final segment, a political analyst discusses America's role in creating instability in the region and its attempt to divert attention from the true enemy. He emphasizes that the Islamic Republic's strength lies in its reliance on the will of tens of millions of people, a voice that cannot be silenced. The program combines personal memories with political analysis to present a portrait of love, sacrifice, and resistance.
