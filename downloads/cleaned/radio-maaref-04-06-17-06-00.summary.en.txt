This radio program discusses the interpretation of Surah Al-An'am and the reasons for its single revelation in Mecca.
Numerous narrations from both Shia and Sunni sources confirm the revelation of this surah, accompanied by thousands of angels.
Allama Tabataba'i suggested the name "Surah of Argument" due to the presence of forty distinct proofs within the text.
Memorizing and compiling these forty proofs qualifies one for the reward mentioned in the hadith about preserving forty narrations.
The interpretation of verses regarding night and day reveals that night is essentially the shadow of the Earth, ontologically following the light of the sun.
The Quran's statement "We made the sun a sign for it" refers to the Earth's rotation around the sun, rather than the sun's movement.
This scientific interpretation refutes the ancient theory of a stationary Earth and demonstrates the Quran's alignment with modern astronomy.
Finally, the program explains the existential relationship between revelation and divine manifestations in the Prophet's heart.
