In this segment of the radio program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verses from Surah An-Nisa to clarify the fundamental difference between "confusion" and "hypocrisy." He argues that a hypocrite, unlike a confused individual, has made a definitive decision to be two-faced, and this intentional act is caused by the person themselves rather than God. The commentator refutes Fakhr Razi's view that hypocrisy is a result of divine compulsion, explaining that God leaves the hypocrite in darkness by withdrawing specific grace, yet does not destroy their innate divine nature. Consequently, the hypocrite remains religiously obligated and can return to the light through repentance and the effort to revive their innate faith. A key point is that God has no argument against believers and will not send them to hell, whereas He has a complete argument against disbelievers and hypocrites who neglect their basic duties. Finally, believers are warned not to establish secret relationships with disbelievers, as doing so would allow God to establish a valid argument against them.
