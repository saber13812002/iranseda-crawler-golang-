The program begins by recounting the 1978 Pahlavi military attack on the Imam Reza shrine, which resulted in civilian casualties and significantly fueled the revolutionary spirit in Mashhad. The second part features the testimony of Sayed Hassan Miresad, a veteran captured during the Battle of Beit al-Moqaddas. He describes how prisoners maintained their morale and unity by secretly sharing hadiths in a chain-like manner within the camps. Miresad also details the clandestine efforts to compile a basic fiqh manual to guide injured and sick fellow prisoners in their religious duties. He recounts the firm resistance of the captives against the guards' humiliating demands to insult Imam Khomeini, which ultimately broke the psychological taboo of submission. The narrative concludes with the release of the POWs following Iraq's invasion of Kuwait and their eventual return to Iran.
