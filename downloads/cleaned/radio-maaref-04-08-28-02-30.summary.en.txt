In the radio program "Persaman Ateghadi" featuring Professor Mohammadi, several listener questions were addressed. A key discussion focused on why the bodies of some scholars and martyrs remain intact, which the professor attributed to the profound influence of their powerful souls rather than purely material causes. Regarding the appearance of Imam Zaman, it was emphasized that he will utilize just and advanced weaponry that prevents indiscriminate mass killings of innocent people. The professor also explained that the experience of death varies, being pleasant for the faithful and difficult for sinners, depending on their spiritual state. Furthermore, it was assured that God compensates for unjust or unmerited suffering in this world or the hereafter. Finally, the prostration in the Ziyarat Ashura was discussed, highlighting that expressing gratitude during affliction is a duty because it invites divine mercy and immense reward.
