In this segment of the radio program "Barkarane Noor," the interpretation of verses 727 to 822 of Surah Al-Ma'idah by Ayatollah Javadi Amoli was broadcast.
The commentator analyzes the Quran's logical argument, stating that if the People of the Book truly believed in God and the Prophet, they would never consider disbelievers as their allies.
Based on this analysis, those who adopt disbelievers as their friends are actually considered hypocrites and non-believers.
The Quran preserves the rights of righteous minorities by avoiding generalizing corruption to all People of the Book, criticizing only the corrupt among them.
Subsequently, a verse regarding the hostility of Jews and the closeness of Christians to Muslims is interpreted.
The closeness of Christians to Muslims is attributed to the presence of devout and humble priests and monks among them, as well as their lack of arrogance toward Muslims.
These moral and religious characteristics have caused Christians to show more kindness toward Muslims compared to other groups.
