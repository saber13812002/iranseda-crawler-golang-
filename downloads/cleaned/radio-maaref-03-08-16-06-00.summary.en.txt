The program began with the recitation of the Holy Quran, followed by an exegesis of verses 148 to 152 of Surah An-Nisa. The commentator analyzed verse 284 to illustrate the spiritual connection between the soul and the heavens, and the body and the earth, emphasizing God's absolute knowledge of all actions. The central discussion focused on the concept of "true disbelievers," specifically those who accept some prophets while rejecting others. The analysis argues that denying any part of the prophetic mission is equivalent to denying God's message, thus constituting absolute disbelief rather than partial rejection. In the legislative system, the acceptance of prophethood is an inseparable extension of the divine essence, making any attempt to differentiate among prophets a fundamental rejection of God's word. By comparing this with verses from Surah Al-Baqarah and Al-Imran, the commentator highlighted the comprehensive faith of Muslims versus the selective belief of the People of the Book. Ultimately, the Quran warns of eternal punishment for disbelievers while encouraging Muslims to maintain unwavering faith in God and all His messengers without distinction.
