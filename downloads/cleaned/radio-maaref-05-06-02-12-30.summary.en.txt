The radio program "Tales of Freedom" featured the memoirs of Mr. Fallah, a former POW from the Khorramshahr operation. He recounted the severe hardships of his captivity, including repeated torture by enemy intelligence and extreme deprivation of water and hygiene in Mosul camps. Prisoners suffered from skin diseases and lice, often enduring beatings just to drink dirty water. Access to information was strictly limited to a two-page newspaper distributed every few days under heavy surveillance. Even smoking was used as a pretext for punishment, with prisoners facing beatings regardless of their actions. These stories highlight the resilience and patience of the fighters who maintained their hope despite harsh conditions. The program concluded with a call for listener support and well-wishes for the freedom fighters.
