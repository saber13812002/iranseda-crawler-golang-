The speech began by emphasizing the virtues of forgiveness, generosity, and service to people as exemplified by Imam Ali and Imam Askari.
The speaker illustrated these ethical principles through stories of Imam Askari's compassionate interactions with his companions, such as forgiving an enemy and sending financial aid to a prisoner.
The importance of preserving the high status of Imam Askari as the father of Imam Mahdi and avoiding inappropriate comparisons with other scholars was also discussed.
It was stressed that the celebration of Imam Askari's birth must be held with appropriate grandeur to maintain his spiritual rank.
The second part of the program featured the memories of the mother of martyr Hassan Bagheri, who spoke about hosting Supreme Council commanders at her home.
She described the simple, non-ceremonial marriage of her son to a woman from Ahvaz and their short but meaningful married life during the war.
The mother recounted her practical support in providing basic necessities for her son and daughter-in-law at the start of their marriage.
These narratives highlight the deep connection between the personal lives of martyrs and religious and ethical values during the Holy Defense.
