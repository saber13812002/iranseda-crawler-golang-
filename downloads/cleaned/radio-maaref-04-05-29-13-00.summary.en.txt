In this program, Hojjat al-Islam Kazrouni describes the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) as a physician of the human soul, emphasizing the distinction between worldly intellect and divine revelation. He presents ten key points from the prophetic message as a healing prescription for society, starting with absolute monotheism and the rejection of shirk. The core tenets include kindness to parents, the prohibition of killing, respecting the property of orphans, and maintaining justice in all transactions. The speaker also highlights the necessity of honesty in speech and keeping promises to God as essential tools to prevent social corruption and reduce prison populations. By asserting that the Prophet was a mercy to the worlds, he argues that any failure to improve stems from human error rather than a flaw in the message. The discussion concludes with prayers for the martyrs and a call for responsible resource management to address current national challenges.
