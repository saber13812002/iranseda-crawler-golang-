This radio program examined the historical challenges surrounding the event of Ghadir Khumm, focusing on witness credibility and narrative details.
The guest explained that discrepancies in specific details, such as the identity of Imam Hussein's killer or the exact attendance count, do not undermine the event's authenticity.
Astronomical calculations placed the event in late winter, while the presence of thousands of companions and Sunni scholars corroborates its validity.
The question of how the Prophet's voice reached the large crowd was answered by referencing historical methods where individuals with loud voices repeated the sermon.
The program also addressed a post-event objection and its outcome, citing the interpretation of the third verse of Surah Al-Ma'arij.
It was emphasized that all attendees pledged allegiance to Imam Ali voluntarily, without any coercion.
The final segment discussed the global influence of Islam and the necessity of Muslim unity and active participation in social and political spheres.
