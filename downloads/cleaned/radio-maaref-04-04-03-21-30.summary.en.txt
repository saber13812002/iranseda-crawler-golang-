The program begins by interpreting verses from Surah Al-Ma'idah, where Jesus (PBUH) refutes the accusation of promoting polytheism or deifying himself and his mother.
He uses logical and exceptional syllogisms to prove that he never called people to shirk but only guided them towards divine monotheism.
The concept of "Alim al-Ghaib" is explained to mean that God is aware of what is hidden from others, representing a state of direct presence and knowledge.
A subtle distinction is made between "witness" and "witnessed" in the verse "You are a witness over all things," clarifying that God acts as a witness to actions in this context.
The phrase "when You took me" does not definitively imply death, as the term "tawaffa" is used in the Quran for both sleep and death.
Consequently, this verse alone is not sufficient to determine whether Jesus (PBUH) has ascended or remains alive, requiring correlation with other verses.
Finally, it is emphasized that the call to monotheism was made solely by God's command, and the Prophet never exceeded the limits of his divine mission.
