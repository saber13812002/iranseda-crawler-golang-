The radio program began by discussing the Zionist regime's targeting of women and children and its censorship of resistance news. It then featured an interview with Dr. Dariush Farajnejad, a defense expert, regarding the final stage of the certainty of victory. The majority of the broadcast focused on the biography of Martyr Mahmoud Kavah, starting from his childhood and his father's revolutionary influence. Kavah described his early enlistment in the IRGC and his volunteer work supporting the families of martyrs and veterans. He recounted his marriage to Fatemeh Amadel Islami, noting that their shared life was limited to less than one hundred days due to his constant military missions. The narrative detailed his intense battles in Kurdistan and the Operation Gorgan, where he led from the front despite sustaining thirteen shrapnel wounds. His comrades highlighted his exceptional physical fitness and fearless leadership on the battlefield.
