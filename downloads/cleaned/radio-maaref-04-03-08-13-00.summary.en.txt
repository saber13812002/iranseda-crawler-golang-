This program explores the concept of culture as a collection of knowledge, insights, and values, specifically focusing on the culture of marriage. The speaker contrasts pre-Islamic marriage customs, such as forced unions and bride exchange, with the Islamic approach that combats these harmful traditions. In Islam, marriage is viewed not merely as a physical union but as a process of purification, spiritual growth, and closeness to God. The fundamental difference between Islamic culture and others, like in India where women bear the financial burden of marriage leading to social crises, is highlighted. The speaker emphasizes the distinction between local Muslim customs and the authentic Islamic rulings regarding marriage. The concept of marriage in Islam is defined as the pairing of two beings created for each other in the cosmic order, which is the primary purpose of human creation. Finally, the speaker warns that neglecting the Islamic culture of family and spousal ethics can lead to social disintegration and widespread psychological crises.
