In this discussion, sports lawyer Dr. Nader Shokri emphasized the legal vacuum and the obsolescence of current legal tools in Iran's sports system. He pointed out that the existing law designating federations as non-governmental entities contradicts the reality of them receiving government funding and facing administrative interference. Shokri also highlighted the structural injustice in federation disciplinary committees, where the complaining entity and the judge are effectively the same body. He noted that the Iranian Scientific Association of Sports Law, despite repeatedly offering revised legal drafts to the Ministry of Sports, has faced indifference from administrators. Established in 2011, the association has successfully led to the creation of official judicial experts in sports and the approval of a master's degree in sports law by the Ministry of Science. However, Shokri believes that many judges and sports managers still fail to utilize up-to-date legal knowledge in this field. Finally, he announced his continued involvement in sports advocacy and research, stressing the urgent need for legal reforms to resolve the current challenges facing Iranian sports.
