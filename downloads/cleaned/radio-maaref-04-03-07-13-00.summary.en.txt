This radio program commemorates the anniversary of the marriage of Imam Ali and Fatimah by recounting historical and religious narratives surrounding this sacred union. The story describes how Imam Ali visits the mosque to deliver Fatimah's sermon and receives her approval for the marriage proposal, which brings joy to the Prophet. The discussion focuses on the concept of Mahr (dowry) as a symbol of the groom's love and gift, where Imam Ali offers his sword and armor as his dowry. The Prophet advises Imam Ali to sell his armor to purchase essential household items for his wife, emphasizing practical support over material wealth. The program also highlights the Prophet's insistence on mutual consent and knowledge between spouses, rejecting forced marriages without the bride's awareness. Ultimately, Imam Ali secures Fatimah's consent by promising to teach her four Quranic surahs as his dowry. The broadcast concludes by emphasizing love, tranquility, and affection in Islamic marriage, ending with prayers for the youth and the martyrs.
