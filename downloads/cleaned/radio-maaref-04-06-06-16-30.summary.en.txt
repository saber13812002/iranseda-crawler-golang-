In this radio program, Dr. Abutalibi warned that despite the relative weakness of the United States, its coup-like interventions in Latin America and other parts of the world could set a precedent for similar actions in the Middle East. He emphasized the necessity of preserving people's governments and preventing foreign interference, highlighting the importance of free and legal elections to maintain internal stability. In the second part, the analyst discussed the Basij model and its export as a popular resistance force in Lebanon, Iraq, Yemen, and Syria, considering it a social asset of the Islamic Republic. He argued that the US is attempting to weaken and eliminate the Axis of Resistance by coercing regional governments, particularly in Iraq and Lebanon, to pass anti-resistance laws. The experience of Syria was examined as an example of failure in maintaining these popular forces and their subsequent weakening by the state. Finally, while introducing a book on strategies for the continuity of the Islamic Revolution, the discussion stressed the need to preserve divine motivations, intellectual independence, and the upbringing of future generations.
