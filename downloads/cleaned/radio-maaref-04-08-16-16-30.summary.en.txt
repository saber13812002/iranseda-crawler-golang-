In this radio program, the speaker analyzes the political and social messages of Hazrat Fatimah (SA) following the Prophet's (PBUH) passing, emphasizing her role as a timeless and practical model. The argument posits that Fatimah's (SA) objections were not merely personal disputes over power but a profound concern about Islam's deviation and a regression to pre-Islamic ignorance. She warned future generations that sidelining Ali (AS) as the most qualified leader would inevitably lead to tyranny, violence, and oppression within Islamic governance. The speech highlights that Ali (AS) would have guided society with justice and gentleness, whereas his absence resulted in dictatorship and internal conflicts. By citing Quranic verses and historical narratives, the speaker illustrates Fatimah's (SA) full awareness of the historical consequences of this political shift. Ultimately, the program calls for a re-examination of history to understand the critical importance of justice and qualified leadership in preserving Islamic identity.
