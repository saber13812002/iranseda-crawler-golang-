The radio program "Historical Persaman," featuring Dr. Mostafa Sadeghi Kashani, addressed various historical inquiries from listeners. In response to a question about the film "Mokhtarnameh," the professor emphasized that viewers should not expect a perfect alignment between the movie and authentic historical sources. He explained that Abdullah ibn Muti' did not assist Mukhtar because of his loyalty to Abdullah ibn Zubayr and his physical presence in Mecca, which prevented him from intervening in Kufa. Regarding the high spiritual status of Fatimah (SA) despite her young age, the expert noted that age is not a barrier to spiritual rank and highlighted the existing discrepancies between Shia and Sunni views on her exact age. The program also detailed Fatimah's (SA) defense of Imam Ali (AS), including her public speeches, her demand for the rights to Fadak, and her active support during the early days of the Caliphate. Furthermore, it was clarified that the sword Zulfiqar was a war trophy from the Battle of Badr that was transferred to Ali (AS) before the Battle of Uhud. Finally, the professor acknowledged that the precise physical shape of the three-pronged arrow used in Karbala is not explicitly documented in ancient historical texts.
