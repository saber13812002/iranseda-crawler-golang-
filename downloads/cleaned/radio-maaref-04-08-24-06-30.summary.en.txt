The radio program begins with a Quranic exegesis emphasizing God's eternal knowledge and the predestination of human destinies in the Preserved Tablet. The commentator clarifies that this divine foreknowledge does not imply compulsion, as God records the consequences of humans' free choices with full awareness. The second part of the broadcast is dedicated to National Book and Library Day, highlighting the vital role of books in knowledge transfer and spiritual growth. It also commemorates the approval of the Islamic Republic's constitution on October 15, 1979, stressing the importance of rule of law based on divine principles. The program includes a tribute to the late scholar Allameh Tabatabaei and mentions the 1988 declaration of Palestinian independence. Finally, listeners shared the most influential books they have read via SMS and phone, concluding the show with an encouragement to read and start the week with hope.
