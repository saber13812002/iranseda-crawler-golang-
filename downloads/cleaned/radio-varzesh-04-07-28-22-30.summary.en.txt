Saeed Sadri, a veteran wrestler, recounts his athletic journey, highlighting the severe obstacles he faced, such as travel bans and serious injuries. He describes how he competed in the World Championships despite a broken hand and a bad leg, driven by a desire to defend Iran's name rather than just win medals. Sadri emphasizes that his motivation during those intense moments was purely patriotic, leading him to defeat strong opponents like those from the USA and Bulgaria. He also shares his experience in Russia, where he won a bronze medal through intelligence and divine help, despite lacking a coach and facing unfair conditions. The athlete asserts that despair has no place in his life, viewing all hardships as divine lessons for achieving spirituality and serving others. Sadri believes that worldly wealth is a trust that must be spent in the path of God and to help those in need. He shares a personal story of how financially assisting a stranger brought him true spiritual peace and abundance. Finally, he advises people to overcome the fear of death and worldly struggles by relying on God and serving humanity with sincerity.
