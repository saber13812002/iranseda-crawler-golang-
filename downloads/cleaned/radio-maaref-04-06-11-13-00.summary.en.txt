The speaker in this radio program outlines the methods for connecting with Imam Zaman (AJ), emphasizing the daily recitation of Surah Yasin and the Ziyarat Al-Yasin. Citing a narration from Sheikh Saduq, he highlights that Imam Reza (AS) recited ten parts of the Quran daily, equating this to three hours of dedicated worship. He contrasts this spiritual discipline with the excessive time modern people spend on smartphones, urging listeners to prioritize their connection with the divine. The speaker shares a story about Allameh Tabataba'i, who wrote his famous tafsir after a dream in which his mother asked him to do work that would have value in the afterlife. A key point is that if Muslims dedicate a daily gift of Surah Yasin and Ziyarat Al-Yasin to the Imam for a year, they will receive his special attention on the Night of Qadr. In the second part, referencing the story of the second deputy of the Imam, the speaker advises that submitting to the program of occultation is more important than insisting on a physical meeting. Therefore, focusing on prayer, ziyarat, and strengthening faith in the occultation are considered more effective ways to maintain a connection with the Imam.
