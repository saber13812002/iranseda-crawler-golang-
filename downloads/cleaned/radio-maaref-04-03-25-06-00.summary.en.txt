This program analyzes verse 109 of Surah Al-Ma'idah to discuss the status of prophets and angels on the Day of Judgment. The commentator explains that on that day, even angels cannot speak without divine permission, and only those granted knowledge act as God's official spokespersons. The prophets' response of "we have no knowledge" is not an admission of ignorance, but an acknowledgment of God's exclusive knowledge of the hidden secrets of people. God protects the honor of those who repent, and even some sinners, by preventing the disclosure of their inner secrets before the prophets and the public. This divine mercy ensures that individuals may even forget their past sins, sparing them from shame in Paradise or on Judgment Day. Conversely, the honor of disbelievers and hypocrites, who are resurrected as fierce beasts, will be completely exposed. Thus, the verse highlights God's justice and mercy in preserving the privacy of believers while revealing the fate of the oppressors.
