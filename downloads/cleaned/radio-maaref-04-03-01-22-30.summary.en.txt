The radio program begins by emphasizing the concept of "fear" and "awe" before God's greatness, explaining that true fear stems from a deep understanding of the Divine rather than mere punishment.
The speaker cites Quranic verses and narrations to demonstrate that prophets, the Ahl al-Bayt, and even angels all fear God, viewing this fear as a sign of devotion and true knowledge.
By referencing historical stories and supplications like "La nur illa nuruk," the speaker explains that humans must feel a sense of helplessness before divine majesty and avoid arrogance.
The discussion also highlights the importance of righteous deeds for salvation on the Day of Judgment and their role in gaining God's pleasure.
The speaker recites comprehensive prayers from the Prophet and Husaini ziyarat texts, stressing the necessity of supplication and reliance on the Ahl al-Bayt during significant religious days.
Concluding with congratulations for Teacher's Day and Husaini holidays, listeners are invited to participate in the spiritual campaign for the "Ayyam of Qadir Khum" by sharing their plans for honoring these occasions.
The program aims to foster a sense of holy fear and hope for divine mercy, encouraging listeners to engage in pure worship and strengthen their bond with the Ahl al-Bayt.
