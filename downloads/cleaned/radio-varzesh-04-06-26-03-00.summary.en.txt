Ashoor Gholam-Mohammadi, a former national team player and prominent basketball coach, recounts his career journey from playing for teams like Rahavand, Naft, and Homa to his roles as a coach and administrator. He highlights the golden era of Iranian basketball, specifically the national team's Asian championship victory, as his most cherished professional memory, emphasizing the pivotal role of Hamed Haddadi in those successes. Gholam-Mohammadi also shares personal challenges, including the loss of his first wife and the crucial support of his second wife in helping him return to his professional life. He details his managerial experiences at clubs such as Pیکان, Shemidour, and Novabugh, while underscoring the significant impact of key administrators at the Islamic Azad University on the development of university sports. Now active as a coach and radio expert, he stresses the importance of strong interpersonal relationships within sports environments. Expressing concern about the lack of a generation comparable to the golden era, he discusses the future of basketball in Iran. Finally, he thanks all the elders and colleagues who supported his career, hoping for continued support to achieve further successes in Iranian sports.
