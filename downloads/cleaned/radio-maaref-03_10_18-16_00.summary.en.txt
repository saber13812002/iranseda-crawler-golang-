In this radio program, Professor Mohammadi Sharoudi addresses various theological and social questions from listeners. He refutes the notion of divine indifference to injustice, arguing that direct divine intervention would negate human free will, and emphasizes that God's tradition relies on internal societal change. A key point is that the prayers and charity of believers act as causes to alter destiny, potentially accelerating the reappearance of Imam Mahdi. The program also clarifies the difference in the afterlife for believers and disbelievers, noting that believers are relieved of worldly hardships while disbelievers face punishment. In the ethical section, Western imitative behaviors, such as keeping pets in beds as substitutes for human affection, are criticized. Finally, the impact of multiple factors like heredity, environment, and governance on human character is analyzed, defining individual moral responsibility within the limits of free will.
