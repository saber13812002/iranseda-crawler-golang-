This program utilizes narrations and sermons of Imam Ali (AS) to describe life as a long and arduous journey toward the afterlife.
Preparing for death involves fulfilling religious obligations, avoiding prohibitions, and adorning oneself with virtues such as generosity and forgiveness.
The concept of contentment and sufficiency from the book of Kafi is examined, emphasizing that "having little but enough" is superior to "having much but being distracted."
The Prophet's (PBUH) story with two men in the desert illustrates that God prayed for sufficiency for the generous helper and for increased wealth for the greedy one.
This narration suggests that excessive wealth can sometimes distance a person from worship and spiritual joy, whereas sufficiency brings peace and the opportunity to remember God.
The program also highlights social solidarity during crises and the role of believers in divine support, recommending that one seek death with trust and readiness.
Finally, the Prophet's prayer for the Ahl al-Bayt for sufficiency is presented as a model for a balanced and spiritual life.
