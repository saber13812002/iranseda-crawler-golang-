In the radio program "Porsaman Quran," Hujjat al-Islam Molaei addressed listeners' questions regarding Quranic nuances. The first response analyzed the verse "fa-basarat bihi in jounubn" in Surah Qasas, explaining that the word "jounubn" implies a moderate distance and a specific angle, allowing Moses' sister to observe without being seen. Next, the use of "saydha" instead of "ba'lahu" in Surah Yusuf was discussed, attributed to preserving Egyptian cultural context and highlighting the Pharaoh's royal status at that moment. The program also addressed Surah Yasin, citing the verse "Layta qawmi ya'lamun" as evidence for the existence of the Barzakh heaven and the divine honor bestowed upon martyrs. Furthermore, the verse "wa-dribuhunna" in Surah An-Nisa was interpreted not as physical violence, but as a deterrent gesture based on the teachings of the Ahl al-Bayt and the absence of any fatwa permitting actual striking. Finally, referencing Surah Al-Isra, the divine promise of the destruction of oppressors, specifically the Zionist regime, was highlighted. This promise was interpreted as a prophecy of their eventual downfall and humiliation following their second phase of corruption.
