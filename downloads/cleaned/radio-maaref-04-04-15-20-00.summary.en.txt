The night of Ashura was observed with mourning ceremonies in Karbala, attended by tens of thousands of pilgrims from around the world, particularly Iranians. In Lebanon, Hezbollah Secretary-General Sheikh Naim Qassem emphasized the steadfastness of the resistance and the refusal to submit to the Zionist regime, defining national defense as an undeniable duty. He also called for the full implementation of the ceasefire agreement through the cessation of aggression and Israel's withdrawal from Lebanese soil. The presence of the Supreme Leader of Iran at the mourning ceremony in Tehran received extensive international media coverage, interpreted as his first public appearance since the recent war with Israel. The spokesperson for the Ministry of Roads announced the start of ticket sales for Arbaeen trains at stable prices, with increased capacity on various routes. The report also highlighted historical sites known as "Rasoul Hossein" in Damascus, Cairo, and Baalbek, where the head of Imam Hussein (PBUH) is reported to have been temporarily kept. These reports underscore the deep religious and political connection of Shia Muslims worldwide to the events of Karbala and regional resistance.
