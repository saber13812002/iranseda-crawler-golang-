Hassan Habibzadeh shared his memories of local sports activities in Tehran and Firuzkuh during a radio interview. He emphasized that in the past, children developed into national champions through personal effort and community solidarity, despite lacking proper facilities and equipment. Habibzadeh argues that the decline in Tehran's sports performance is due to the commercialization of local fields and the loss of non-material motivation among the younger generation. Although physical resources are more abundant today, he believes the self-made spirit and genuine passion for neighborhood sports have disappeared. The second part of the program reviewed historical sports events from July 2nd, including France's victory in the 2000 European Championship and the birthdays of players like Lee Chung-yong and Danny Rose. Key results of the Iranian national teams in football and basketball over the years were also highlighted. The program concluded with a focus on transferring the experiences of the previous generation to today's youth and reviewing the history of Iranian sports.
