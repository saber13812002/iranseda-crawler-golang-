The program discusses the theological distinction between innate divine infallibility and acquired infallibility in Islamic thought.
It argues that individuals endowed with innate infallibility possess a higher existential status than those who achieve it through years of effort.
A key objection is raised regarding whether innate infallibility negates human free will, which would logically invalidate religious obligations.
The response asserts that divine infallibility is compatible with free will, serving as a special grace to prevent sin and maintain justice.
The discussion then shifts to the Quranic verse "Guidance for the God-fearing," explaining that the Quran offers a deeper level of guidance to the pious.
Faith and piety are described as having various degrees, where human effort and struggle elevate one from initial stages to higher spiritual ranks.
Ultimately, God grants special success to those who actively pursue piety, enabling them to reach advanced levels of knowledge and infallibility.
