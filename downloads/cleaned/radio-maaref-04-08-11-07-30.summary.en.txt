The radio program began by emphasizing the importance of forgiveness and respecting neighbors' rights in social interactions. An interactive segment followed, where listeners shared stories of forgiving one another and resolving family disputes. The Deputy of Treatment at the Ministry of Health stressed the need to improve the health system's resilience and support healthcare workers' livelihoods. Dr. Rezaei highlighted the gradual and scientific implementation of the family physician plan as a key priority. He also advocated for evidence-based decision-making to ensure system sustainability and public satisfaction. In the health section, a condition affecting women after menopause and men aged 65 to 70 was discussed. These points formed the core topics of the broadcast.
