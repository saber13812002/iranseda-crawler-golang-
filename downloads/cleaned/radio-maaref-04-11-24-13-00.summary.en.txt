The Friday prayers highlighted the massive public turnout on February 11 as a demonstration of Iran's true deterrent power and national unity. The Supreme Leader stated that this presence thwarted the enemy's projects of fear and marked a transition from a defensive posture to an offensive of will against global hegemony. He described the bond between the people and the leadership as a sacred, faith-based connection that serves as a model for the oppressed worldwide. Regarding diplomacy, negotiations with the United States were strictly limited to the nuclear weapons issue, with a warning that any deviation would result in severe strikes on the enemy's regional interests. The speech reaffirmed that the Palestinian cause remains the top priority for the Islamic world, promising continued resistance against the Zionist regime. Finally, government officials were urged to reciprocate this public trust by providing transparent services, addressing grievances, and working with unity and sincerity to improve the nation's welfare.
