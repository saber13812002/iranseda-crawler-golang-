In this program, Salman, who had distanced himself from the Ghazaleh family due to old conflicts, is confronted with his painful past upon seeing their son at a gathering. Fearing a recurrence of the bitter memories and betrayals he experienced during his captivity and the war, he decides to avoid attending his daughter Ghazaleh's wedding. Ghazaleh's mother is upset by Salman's secrecy and accuses him of disrespecting their guests and failing to respond to the proposal. Salman eventually confesses that the family has dark and dangerous roots, and he refused them to protect his family's safety. This confession allows Ghazaleh to understand the harsh truth about her father and his hidden grievances. Ultimately, Salman accepts his paternal responsibilities and decides to stay by his family's side to protect them, despite all the hardships.
