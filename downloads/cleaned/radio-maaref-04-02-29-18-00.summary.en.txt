In this radio program, anger and rage are identified as primary tools used by Satan to dominate human beings. The speaker explains that Satan is created from fire, and human anger is essentially a fragment of that fire ignited within us. This negative emotion not only destroys personal, family, and social relationships but also reduces one's connection with God and self to ashes. Anger is described as a sign of human weakness, opening the door for Satan to enter and control the individual's existence. Therefore, controlling anger is crucial to depriving Satan of the opportunity to act in our lives. Ultimately, listeners are urged to guard their inner selves to avoid falling into this destructive fire.
