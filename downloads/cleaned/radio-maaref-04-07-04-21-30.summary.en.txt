This radio program examines the narrative of Prophet Idris's occultation and its relevance to the debate surrounding the Imam of the Age's absence. According to the story, Idris remained hidden from the people for twenty years due to a tyrannical king's cruelty, during which time rain ceased to fall. God commanded Idris to pray for rain, but he initially refused due to his own severe hunger and a sense of neglect toward the suffering populace. After witnessing the extreme poverty of a woman and her son, Idris revealed his identity by resurrecting the boy, thereby guiding the people to confront the unjust ruler. The king, acknowledging his fault, joined the people in a state of humility and desperation before Idris. Upon Idris's prayer and the collective repentance of the group, a torrential rain descended, resolving both the famine and the tyranny. This narrative is presented as evidence to validate the possibility of a righteous leader's occultation and to dispel related theological doubts.
