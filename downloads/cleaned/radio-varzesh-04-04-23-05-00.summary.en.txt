In this interview, Mohsen Grousi recounts his memories of playing for the Iranian national football team and the 1990 Asian Games in Beijing. He expresses his disappointment at being cut from the final squad for the Beijing games due to an ankle injury and the coaching staff's decisions. Grousi also shares his experience in the 1989 Futsal World Cup in Hong Kong, where the Iranian team finished fourth despite facing innovative tactics from their opponents. He criticizes the administrative and technical issues during the Viera era, noting their negative impact on the national team's performance in crucial matches against Japan and Australia. Grousi highlights his military service at Pas Tehran and the team's Asian Championship as significant achievements in his playing career. He explains his decision to leave Pas and join Sepahan to resolve contract issues before eventually returning to Esteghlal to continue his professional journey. Finally, Grousi reflects on his coaching career and his lingering dissatisfaction with not reaching his deserved status in professional football.
