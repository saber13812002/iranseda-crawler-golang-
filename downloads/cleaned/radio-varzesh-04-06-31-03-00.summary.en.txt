Mohsen Ashoori, a karate veteran, discussed the historic 2003 world championship victory of the Iranian team wearing the Islamic hijab in a radio interview. He criticized the current federation management, emphasizing the need for an independent national team organization with modern management principles and proper delegation of authority. Ashoori argued that technical committees should consist of up-to-date experts but often serve as tools for managers' personal agendas rather than technical guidance. He mentioned past conflicts with federation presidents who failed to recognize the full potential of Iranian karate and did not provide adequate support. On a personal note, Ashoori shared his early marriage and how his children followed in his footsteps to pursue karate. He also expressed his passion for boxing and football, particularly his long-standing support for Esteghlal FC and its legendary players. Finally, he highlighted the core values of discipline and respect in karate and reaffirmed his willingness to serve the sport again if invited.
