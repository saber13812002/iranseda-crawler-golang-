Saudi Arabia's passport authority announced that nearly 1.5 million pilgrims have entered the country for Hajj, with the rituals scheduled to begin on the fourth of Dhu al-Hijjah. Concurrently, an Iranology hall was inaugurated at India's National Islamic University to strengthen cultural and diplomatic ties between the two nations. In Iraq, the ninth national Amir al-Quran competition commenced across sixteen provinces, focusing on Quranic recitation and memorization workshops. In France, protests against rising Islamophobia highlighted a growing Muslim population, driven largely by young, educated converts. In Palestine, Israeli settlers incited violence against Al-Aqsa Mosque, while Hamas rejected the US ceasefire proposal as insufficient and a continuation of the killing. Military attacks on Gaza continue, resulting in the closure of the last active hospital in the north. Despite anti-war rhetoric, Germany remains the second-largest arms supplier to Israel, and Yemen has warned it will intensify military operations until the Gaza blockade is lifted.
