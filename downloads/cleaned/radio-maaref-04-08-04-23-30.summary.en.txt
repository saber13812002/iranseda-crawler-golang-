In this segment of his memoirs, Ayatollah Abdollahi reflects on his seminary years at the Fayziyeh school and his early writings in the 1960s and 1970s. He discusses his books "Ethics for All" and "Monotheism and Intercession," which were published with the encouragement of prominent scholars like Ayatollah Javadi Amoli and Ayatollah Yazdi. Following the revolution, his focus shifted to the jurisprudential foundations of the economy and teaching in specialized fields of propaganda and Quranic studies. He highlights his unique pedagogical innovations in teaching tafsir, such as group study of Al-Mizan and independent presentations by students. These methods resulted in graduates who were more successful in Quranic and interpretive discussions. The program continued with the recitation of the Dua al-Faraj and religious songs for Nurses' Day and the birth anniversary of Hazrat Zainab (SA). Finally, a speech by Imam Khomeini (RA) was broadcast, emphasizing the preservation of the revolution's victory and the solidarity of Islamic nations with Iran.
