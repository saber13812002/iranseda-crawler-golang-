The broadcast began with news updates, including the conclusion of the World Assembly of Muslim Jurists in Qatar, which emphasized Islamic responsibilities toward Palestine. It also reported on the honoring of seminary students in Najaf for Imam Reza's birthday and Yemen's missile strikes on Israel, which halted flights at Ben Gurion airport. Additionally, the program covered the Iranian Foreign Minister's upcoming visits to Saudi Arabia and Qatar. A segment analyzed the expansion of ISIS in Southern Africa following the withdrawal of Western forces and the UN's appointment of a special envoy to combat Islamophobia. The show then featured religious music and supplications dedicated to Imam Reza. The main segment, titled "Religion and Life," focused on the nature of cryptocurrencies and Bitcoin from jurisprudential and economic perspectives. Experts discussed the history of money and the necessity of defining the jurisprudential rulings for digital assets. The discussion aimed to clarify the religious and economic foundations for engaging in the cryptocurrency market.
