This radio program reported on Ashura mourning ceremonies in London and the growing security concerns among British Muslims due to rising Islamophobia. It also introduced the 21st Alexandria Book Fair, which focuses on introducing Islamic resources to the younger generation, and the first Halal Food Festival in the United States. The main segment featured a documentary about Sheikh Jafar Shoushtari, detailing his initial struggles with public speaking and a prophetic dream in Karbala that empowered his oratory skills. Invited by Naser al-Din Shah to Tehran, the Sheikh captivated a massive audience of forty thousand people, demonstrating his profound impact on the public. The documentary illustrates how a weak speaker transformed into a significant religious figure through perseverance and divine inspiration. The broadcast concluded by inviting listeners to submit recitations for the "Novgaran Hosseini" campaign and offering condolences for the anniversary of Imam Sajjad's martyrdom.
