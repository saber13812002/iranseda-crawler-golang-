In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Mohammadi Sheikh Shabani emphasized the prohibition of shaving the beard entirely, citing Imam Khomeini's fatwa and relevant narrations regarding the required length. Subsequently, Hujjat al-Islam Jawaheri clarified the ruling for a person who moves between locations with different prayer conditions during prayer time, stating that the prayer must be performed according to the condition of the location where the prayer time ends. Hujjat al-Islam Zirnabidini, referencing Ayatollah Javadi Amoli, specified that the kaffarah for missed fasts cannot be paid to those for whom financial support is obligatory, though paying it to other relatives is considered meritorious. In another segment, Hujjat al-Islam Alian Natij, citing Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi, confirmed that khums applies to any surplus funds remaining at the end of the khums year, regardless of when they were acquired. Regarding the return of usurped wealth, it was stated that amounts less than thirty US dollars should be given to the poor, while larger amounts require a specific inquiry from the marja's office. The program concluded with recommendations to perform the first-time congregational prayer and a narration from Imam Ali (AS) encouraging devotion and presence in mosques.
