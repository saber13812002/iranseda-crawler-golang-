The radio program begins with a Quranic exegesis of Surah Al-An'am, emphasizing absolute monotheism and the special status of the Prophet and Imam Ali as divine signs. This is followed by recitations of the Dua al-Faraj and morning supplications, inviting listeners to remember God and await the Savior. The show then transitions to a segment on kindness, highlighting how compassion and gratitude for God's blessings enhance the beauty of life. The host discusses the aesthetic appeal of autumn, encouraging listeners to share their unique memories and observations of the season. A historical review covers the birth of Dostoevsky and significant events related to World Wars that occurred on this date. Listener messages express personal joys and appreciation for nature, contributing to the program's intimate atmosphere. The broadcast concludes with morning prayers and a reminder to strive for a halal livelihood through daily effort.
