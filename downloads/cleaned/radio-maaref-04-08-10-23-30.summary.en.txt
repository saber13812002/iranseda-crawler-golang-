Ayatollah Najm al-Din Tusi shares his memories of his seminary education and the process of writing his books. He highlights that his book on the Shia perspective of return was written under his father's strict supervision during the latter's illness. Tusi emphasizes that a true student of knowledge must maintain vitality, seriousness, and spiritual momentum in their studies. He recounts his extensive teaching career in jurisprudence and principles, as well as the respectful interactions with his teacher, Ayatollah Fazel Larijani. The interview also details the exemplary punctuality and etiquette of his mentor, Ayatollah Wahid Khorasani, during his advanced classes. The program concludes with the recitation of the Ziyarat of Al-Yasin and poems about the Awaited Imam. Finally, a segment featuring Imam Khomeini's speeches on the importance of economic independence and agriculture in Iran is played.
