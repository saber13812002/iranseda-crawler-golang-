The Parsaiyan program began by emphasizing the spiritual significance of night prayers, citing stories of prominent clerics who attributed their academic and spiritual success to this practice. The second segment featured the testimony of Ali Azhar Moshayekhi, a veteran who was captured during the Khorramshahr operation and spent over seven years in Iraqi POW camps. He described the harsh conditions of his captivity, including severe injuries that received no medical attention and the physical and psychological torture inflicted by Ba'athist forces. Moshayekhi also recounted how the enemy staged deceptive propaganda for foreign media to falsely portray the well-being of prisoners, which the captives resisted by revealing the truth. The program concluded by introducing the book "Group of 8th Esfand," which details the hardships of captivity through the account of another veteran, Jamsheed Sarmastani. The broadcast ended with a call to maintain spiritual awareness and honor the sacrifices of the freed prisoners.
