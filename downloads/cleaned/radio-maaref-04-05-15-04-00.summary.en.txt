The program begins by emphasizing the concept of "Hasan Sira," which means maintaining good character in all moments of life, not just in specific situations.
In the rulings section, answers were provided regarding the repayment of old debts, including adjusting for inflation and notifying the creditor.
The ruling on picking fruit or leaves from street trees was discussed, deemed permissible if it does not harm the tree and the owner has not objected.
It was clarified that funeral and burial costs paid from funds given by a husband to his wife are not subject to Khums.
The difference between Fidyah and Kaffarah for missed fasts and how to pay them to the needy was also explained.
The ruling on broken oaths was addressed, stating that if one is unable to fulfill them, the oath is void and no Kaffarah is required.
The final part of the program focused on early Islamic history, specifically the dialogue between Imam Ali (AS) and Talha and Zubayr.
Professor Jafari explained that Imam Ali (AS) acted based on the Quran and Sunnah, consulting people only on matters not explicitly covered in religious sources.
