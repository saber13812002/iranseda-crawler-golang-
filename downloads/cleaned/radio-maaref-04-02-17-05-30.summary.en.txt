The program initially addressed a fiqh issue regarding the validity of repeating ihram and taqsir after tawaf, which the editor found difficult to justify. The discussion then shifted to the concepts of "huzn" (sorrow) and "khawf" (fear) based on a narration from Imam Ali. It was emphasized that psychological states like sorrow and fear serve as catalysts for spiritual awakening and attention to religious duties. Conversely, the heedlessness caused by worldly pleasures hinders the acceptance of eschatological truths. The Prophet (PBUH) stated that God does not combine fear and security in a single servant; thus, one who fears God in this world will be secure on the Day of Judgment. In contrast, those who are complacent in this life will face fear and punishment in the hereafter. Additionally, it was noted that fearing sin in this world can lead to its forgiveness in the afterlife, whereas relying on good deeds and trivializing sins invites divine wrath.
