In the first segment, the instructor emphasizes that prayer is only accepted by God when performed with full presence of heart and through the intercession of the Ahl al-Bayt. He explains that true spiritual connection removes existential veils, making the joy of prayer, especially night prayer, superior to the entire world and material paradise. This state elevates the soul to a high station of divine proximity. In the second segment, the scholar interprets hadiths from the book of Kafi, stating that making a believer happy is equivalent to pleasing the Prophet and God. Conversely, harming a Muslim incurs God's wrath, as He is jealous of His friends and followers. Acts of kindness, such as feeding the hungry, relieving sorrow, or paying off debts, are considered the most beloved deeds to God. These actions not only bring joy to the believer but also secure divine happiness and reward for the doer.
