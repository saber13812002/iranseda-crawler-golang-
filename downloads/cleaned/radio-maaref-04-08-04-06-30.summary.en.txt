The program began with a Quranic interpretation emphasizing absolute monotheism and the dependence of all afterlife events on God's will.
It then commemorated historical events, including Imam Khomeini's speech against capitulation and his subsequent exile to Turkey.
The anniversary of the Iraqi occupation of Khorramshahr and the heroic resistance of its people during the Battle of the Blood City were also reviewed.
The program further marked the 45th anniversary of the experimental launch of Iran's national television.
In the second segment, the concept of reaching a "dead end" in life was discussed, along with strategies for overcoming such crises.
Psychologists noted the impact of the autumn season on mood and the potential for seasonal affective disorder.
The primary advice was to seek refuge in God and maintain hope during difficult times.
Additionally, kindness and changing communication styles were suggested as practical solutions for family conflicts.
