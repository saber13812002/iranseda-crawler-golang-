In this radio program, the importance of fiqh principles such as the presumption of purity, permissibility, and innocence was emphasized to avoid unnecessary doubts and excessive caution in religious matters.
Scholars and maraji' stressed the necessity of utilizing legal dispensations and avoiding undue strictness, which is considered a source of divine anger.
The practice of making vows to perform recommended acts was criticized for creating religious burden and transforming optional deeds into obligations.
By citing Quranic verses and the words of Imam Reza (AS), it was demonstrated that Islam is a religion of ease where permissible matters far outweigh prohibitions.
Contrary to Western media claims about Islamic strictness, the Prophet (PBUH) and the Imams (AS) consistently encouraged enjoying lawful blessings and the best of life.
The final section addressed the political ignorance of enemies who, due to a misunderstanding of Iran's social cohesion and faith, fail in their attempts to weaken the country.
The overall conclusion calls for mental peace, avoiding religious obsessions, and trusting in the easy and merciful rulings of Islam.
