The program begins with an exegesis of verses 69 and 70 of Surah Yusuf, highlighting that lying about God and the Prophet is a major barrier to spiritual success.
The commentator emphasizes that divine punishment in the afterlife does not become normalized through repetition, regardless of a disbeliever's material wealth in this world.
The second segment focuses on the importance of night prayer, describing it as a crucial means to overcome spiritual poverty and gain divine reward on Judgment Day.
In the "Zamzam Ahkam" section, jurisprudential answers to listeners' questions were provided by representatives of various Maraji'.
Key rulings included the permissibility of a daughter acting as a proxy for her father in Hajj and the conditions for listening to permissible music.
Further clarifications were given regarding wudu with previous moisture, consuming part of a specific zakat fund, and broadcasting the adhan with a loudspeaker.
Finally, the concept of "ma fi al-zimmah" in worship intentions and the ruling on using goods whose profits benefit enemies were explained.
