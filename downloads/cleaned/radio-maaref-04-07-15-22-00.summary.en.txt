The program begins by examining Quranic and narrational evidence affirming the existence of Bark Abi Taleb as a manifestation of monotheism and the Prophet's truth. The news segment highlights gratitude towards national media during the Sacred Defense, Hezbollah's pledge to the resistance, and the IRGC's warning regarding the martyrdom of two staff members in Sarbabad. It also reports on upcoming academic conferences, the start of Umrah registration, and marriage statistics for the first half of the year. The main segment, titled "Tale of Freedom," features interviews with former POWs who describe the harsh conditions and continuous torture in Iraqi prisons. The freed prisoners recount their remarkable self-sacrifice, their efforts to maintain hope, and their ingenuity in building batteries from pomegranate peels to stay connected with Iran. A touching anecdote about the close relationship between Martyr Rostami and the battalion's cleric on the eve of Operation Khorramshahr is also shared. The program concludes by emphasizing the spiritual growth of the prisoners during their captivity and reading Quranic stories from the book "Taj al-Qasas."
