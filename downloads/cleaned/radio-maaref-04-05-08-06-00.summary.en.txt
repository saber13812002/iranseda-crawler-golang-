The program begins with a reminder to manage energy consumption efficiently before transitioning to Ayatollah Javadi Amoli's interpretation of verse 122 of Surah Al-Ma'idah. He defines the guidance of the prophets as "monotheistic guidance," which stands against polytheism and is rooted in the belief that God solely manages the entire universe. In this perspective, humans who engage in divine remembrance eventually synchronize with the world and perceive the praise of other creatures, thereby moving away from the selfishness of Qarun. The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) is described as the "first of the Muslims" in terms of existential and hierarchical priority, continuing the monotheistic path of previous prophets. Imamate and guardianship are linked to intuitive knowledge and practical education, requiring attention to both the content and the credibility of the speaker. Finally, the master emphasizes the Quran's unique style as "light," criticizing purely logical or scientific interpretations that overlook its spiritual essence.
