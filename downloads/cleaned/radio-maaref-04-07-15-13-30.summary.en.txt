The radio program "Hekayat-e Azadegi" began by honoring the memory of the freed POWs and revisiting their captivity experiences. Lieutenant Colonel Mohammad Hossein Ansari recounted the harsh conditions of their secret detention in Iraqi pits, including a prolonged deprivation of fresh air. He described the prisoners' ingenuity in creating batteries from pomegranate peels to listen to Iranian news and communicate with other detainees. The program also featured stories from the Holy Defense, highlighting the close bond between a battalion's chaplain and the soldiers, including a humorous incident involving a prayer for the chaplain's father. Ansari further detailed the mutual support and resistance against severe torture among the prisoners, emphasizing their solidarity in the face of adversity. Hujjat al-Islam Nadim noted that the freed POWs transformed their captivity into an opportunity for spiritual and intellectual growth, emerging with greater stability. The broadcast concluded with a message from the Supreme Leader condemning the Zionist regime's unprecedented atrocities against children in Gaza. He called for all countries, especially Islamic nations, to sever all political and economic ties with the regime to hold it accountable.
