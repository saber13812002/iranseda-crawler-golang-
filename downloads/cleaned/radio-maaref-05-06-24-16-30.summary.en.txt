In this radio program, Professor Mohammadi clarifies that severe regret occurs on the Day of Judgment rather than in Paradise, as inhabitants of lower levels cannot perceive the pleasures of higher ones. He emphasizes the importance of paying off personal rights and giving recommended charity to alleviate sins and benefit the deceased, noting that good intentions alone do not yield reward until acted upon. Responding to listener questions, he explains that the funeral prayer requires no wudu due to its nature as a supplication, and that children raised in addicted families are innocent victims of their circumstances. The discussion also covers the awareness between the saved and the damned during the Judgment, where knowledge of each other's fate causes either joy or regret. Professor Mohammadi highlights the spiritual value of reciting Surah Al-Hashr nightly and in night prayers to avoid poverty and dependence on others. He further argues that faith in the current era is more valuable than in the Prophet's time because it is based on intellect rather than witnessing miracles. Finally, he advises listeners to rely on epistemological methods beyond sensory perception to understand metaphysical concepts like the Barzakh.
