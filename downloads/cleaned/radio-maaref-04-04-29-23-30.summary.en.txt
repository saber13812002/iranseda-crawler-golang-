This radio program addresses two primary challenges in family relationships. The first issue concerns enduring a spouse's parallel relationship for the sake of reputation and children, which the expert deems incorrect and harmful. Instead of silence, it is advised to firmly establish religious and ethical boundaries using the "I-message" technique and demand clarity on the spouse's commitment. A crucial point is that before marriage, one must ensure previous relationships are fully resolved and negative feelings toward the past are eliminated. The second part discusses bitterness and disrespect in long-term marriages. The expert emphasizes that passive endurance does not solve problems; rather, one must act like a scientist to uncover the root causes of conflict and address them through active dialogue. Finally, referencing Imam Khomeini's teachings, the program stresses the importance of sacrifice for the faith and avoiding the divisive tactics of enemies.
