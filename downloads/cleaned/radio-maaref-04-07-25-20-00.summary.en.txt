This news program reported on the competition among over 1,400 candidates for Hajj service roles and the upcoming Umrah lottery for university students in Iran. Meanwhile, Pakistani officials reaffirmed their rejection of recognizing Israel and announced that the COMSTECH organization would provide 5,000 scholarships to Palestinian students. Internationally, the report highlighted hacker attacks on US and Canadian airports by Hamas supporters and large-scale protests in Malaysia, Indonesia, and Australia against Israeli and American policies. It also covered the arrest of nearly 30 people in England for pro-Palestinian activities and the ongoing boycott of Israeli goods by the Turkish public. In Gaza, despite the ceasefire, the Israeli army has repeatedly violated the agreement and refused to fully reopen crossings, leading to severe food shortages. Tensions on the Pakistan-Afghanistan border escalated after a suicide attack killed several Pakistani soldiers. The segment concluded with a remembrance of the 1961 Paris massacre of Algerian protesters and a speech by Imam Khomeini emphasizing the high moral value of nursing and serving the sick.
