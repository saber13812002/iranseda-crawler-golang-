This radio program emphasized the importance of action quality and following the Imam of the Age and Sayyid al-Shuhada, noting that even the Imam's companions reached the rank of martyrdom.
Citing narrations from Imam Sadiq and Imam Hadi, the broadcast strongly recommended the blessings of the Arbaeen pilgrimage and financial assistance for pilgrims who cannot attend physically.
The most crucial factor in this endeavor is a pure intention and participation in the pilgrims' expenses, rather than the specific amount of money paid.
By providing the SMS number 3552, the program invited listeners to send the message "Yar-o-Gheyr" and transfer desired amounts to become sponsors of Karbala pilgrims.
The spiritual atmosphere was enhanced by reciting the Al-Yasin Ziyarat and specific prayers, with a strong emphasis on unity and hope for the Imam's reappearance.
The program concluded with a patriotic song and speeches highlighting the necessity of the nation's collective participation in rebuilding the country and continuing the martyrs' path.
This approach demonstrates that Radio Ma'aref aims to create a deeper connection between the audience and religious occasions by combining religious and social content.
Finally, the call to pray for martyrs and officials, along with the hope for a successful Arbaeen pilgrimage, served as the main and final message of this segment.
