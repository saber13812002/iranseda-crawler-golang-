Seyyed Mohammad Mehdi Seyyed Salehi recounts his early career, highlighting his brief but impressive trial with Sepahan under Stankovic before moving to Saipa. He details his return to Sepahan, where he achieved significant success, including winning the league title and the AFC Asian Cup runner-up spot under coaches Farhad Kazemi and Mahmoud Karimi. The player discusses his tenure at Esteghlal, marked by persistent injuries and conflicts with the club's management under Parviz Fattah. His career saw a resurgence at Tractor Sazi, where he became the league's second-top scorer, proving his enduring value in Iranian football. Seyyed Salehi explains his decision to reject a lucrative offer from a lower-division team out of respect for his fans and his commitment to top-level competition. He also reflects on his final moves to Persepolis and Saipa, noting the challenges and controversies he faced with certain coaches like Majid Jalali. The interview concludes with his thoughts on his national team experiences and the personal sacrifices he made throughout his professional journey.
