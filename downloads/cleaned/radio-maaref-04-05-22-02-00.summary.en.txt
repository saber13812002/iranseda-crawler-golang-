The first segment recounts a story from the book "Said Man Hosseini," where Mohammad Taghi and Raza are working in a pilgrimage crowd after the morning prayer.
Mohammad Taghi follows a barefoot young man holding a prayer bead, a scene that evokes a gentle moral reminder by Ayatollah Boushehri regarding the importance of night prayer.
The second segment features Mrs. Hosseini, the mother of Afghan martyr Seyed Mohammad Hosseini, on the "Narrative of Love" radio program.
She describes how her son used playful actions and practical help to comfort her during her illness before his departure.
Mrs. Hosseini shares the painful moments of his farewell as he traveled to Syria to defend the shrine of Lady Zainab (SA).
She also recounts a disturbing dream she had the night before his martyrdom, in which she saw her husband wounded.
These memories highlight the martyr's strong determination to serve and his mother's patience and reliance on God in the face of tragedy.
