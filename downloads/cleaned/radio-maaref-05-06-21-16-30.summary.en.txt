The radio program began by discussing the exalted status of martyrs in the Barzakh and their spiritual joy. It then addressed the question of the Quran's ease of understanding, explaining that individuals benefit from the scripture according to their personal capacity. The host also explained Imam Ali's advice to Ibn Abbas to rely on the Sunnah rather than the Quran when debating the Khawarij due to their misinterpretations. Furthermore, the program explored the Quranic foundations of the Wilayat al-Faqih, citing verses that prohibit the rule of disbelievers and oppressors while emphasizing the need for justice and knowledge in governance. A question regarding the verse "Hala 'ata 'ala al-insan" was answered by interpreting it as the scattered state of human particles in nature before creation. Finally, the positive and healing effects of listening to Quran recitation on both the human soul and physical health were highlighted.
