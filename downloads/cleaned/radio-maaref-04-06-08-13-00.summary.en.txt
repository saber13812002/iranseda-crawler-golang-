The program begins by interpreting a Quranic verse to emphasize the concept of being a "leader of the pious" and the importance of officials serving as moral role models. Citing a hadith that God will use the conduct of the righteous as an argument against others on Judgment Day, the speaker highlights Martyrs Rajai, Bahonar, and Raisi as exemplary committed leaders. Their shared characteristics include a deep belief in the Guardianship of the Jurist, a strong commitment to prayer and spirituality, and viewing political power as a trust rather than an opportunity for exploitation. The speaker praises their simple lifestyles, rejection of extravagance, and their effort to live on par with the underprivileged to avoid social alienation. Furthermore, their dedication to serving the marginalized through provincial trips and their history of enduring torture during the previous regime are highlighted as key virtues. The discussion also commends their religious insight and efforts to promote Islamic education and youth upbringing within the education system. Ultimately, the program honors these martyrs as enduring symbols of integrity and calls for future officials to emulate their selfless service and spiritual depth.
