In this radio program, Professor Fala categorizes the concept of patience into two types based on Quranic verses: praiseworthy and false. False patience is defined as resisting the acceptance of truth and monotheism, which is condemned in the Quran and leads to spiritual darkness. Conversely, praiseworthy patience includes enduring hardships, performing acts of worship, and abstaining from sins. The instructor emphasizes "beautiful patience," where one endures without complaint, seeking only God's pleasure. It was explained that patience against sins is a virtue that prevents moral deviation. In the Q&A section, the belief in reincarnation was refuted using rational and religious arguments. Solutions for alleviating grief, such as focusing on the afterlife and comparing one's situation to those less fortunate, were also discussed. Finally, the concept of curses was dismissed, and listeners were advised to rely on prayer and trust in God to resolve their problems.
