The head of the Qom Seminary emphasized the mission of training students who do not lose the truth in the face of falsehood and the necessity of pure intention in learning. At the gathering of new students, officials reported a significant increase in enrollment, with 800 new students joining this year. Simultaneously, a call was issued for an international conference on Imam Khomeini's school of ijtihad to provide a comprehensive analysis of his intellectual system. The Institute for Compilation and Publication of Imam Khomeini's Works unveiled the seven-volume book "Miftah al-Hidaya" by Ayatollah Moqtadaei and announced the publication of 34 new works in the past year. The closing ceremony of the second national "I Write" campaign was held, honoring twenty selected works by students from martyr and veteran families. Additionally, a national conference on "The Philosophy of Logic of Understanding Religion" was held at the Islamic Culture and Thought Institute to coincide with World Philosophy Day. In the religious Q&A segment, rulings were provided regarding missed prayers during travel, the permissibility of consuming donkey milk, and the conditions for performing prayers on behalf of the deceased.
