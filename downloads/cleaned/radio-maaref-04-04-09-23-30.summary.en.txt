In this radio program, featuring Professor Tarashoon, practical strategies for managing parenting challenges and reducing parental stress were discussed.
The expert explained the importance of children identifying with positive characters and gradually guiding them toward constructive activities through storytelling.
Key signs of attention deficit, such as restlessness, forgetfulness, inability to finish tasks, and quick distraction by sounds, were examined in detail.
It was emphasized that observing multiple signs simultaneously requires consultation with a specialist for proper diagnosis.
A major recommendation was to avoid relying on potentially incorrect answers from artificial intelligence for sensitive parenting or health issues.
Parents were encouraged to consult with qualified experts and professional counselors rather than attempting self-treatment.
The program concluded by inviting listeners to send questions via the SMS system and expressing gratitude for the guest's participation.
