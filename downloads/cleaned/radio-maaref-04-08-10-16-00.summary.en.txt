In this radio program, expert Hajj Qasem Maleki addressed various Quranic questions from listeners. He first clarified the relationship between divine will and human free will regarding misfortunes, distinguishing between God's permission and human choice. The expert then explained that the Prophet's act of wrapping himself in a cloak in Surah Muzzammil symbolized his nightly seclusion for spiritual self-improvement. A linguistic distinction was drawn between "dhunub" (heavy, persistent sins) and "siyaa't" (bad deeds or the consequences of sins). Regarding visually impaired listeners, the focus was placed on the importance of spiritual reflection over the physical act of reading or touching the Quran. The difference between the physical difficulty of dying and a believer's spiritual longing for reunion with God was explained using the analogy of the hardships of a pilgrimage. Finally, the program concluded with an encouragement to deepen one's spiritual connection with the Quran and the teachings of the Ahl al-Bayt as a guide for life.
