Seyyed Mahmoud Nabavian, Vice President of the National Security Commission, visited Baghdad to discuss regional developments with Iraqi officials. The recent Israeli aggression against Iran was condemned, and Iraq's official complaint to the United Nations was highlighted. Iranian representatives emphasized the shared enemy of the Zionist regime and the US, stressing the importance of maintaining the resistance axis. In other news, Malaysians protested against the US-proposed ambassador due to his pro-Israeli and Islamophobic stance. The solidarity of Kashmiris with Palestine during the martyrdom ceremony of Imam Sajjad (AS) and the police investigation into an attack on a mosque in Copenhagen were also reported. Finally, the critical role of Seyyed Mohammad Sani-Khani in establishing the IRGC's transportation unit was detailed. His innovative use of public resources helped resolve the logistical crisis caused by the destruction of the Qator bridge during the Iran-Iraq war.
