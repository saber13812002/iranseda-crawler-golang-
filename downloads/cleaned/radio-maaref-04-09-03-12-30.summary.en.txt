In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Kashani argues that Lady Zahra's (SA) struggle was not driven by worldly interests but by a divine mandate to protect the rights of Imam Ali (AS) and prevent the community from deviating from true guidance. He cites second-century historical documents and poetry to demonstrate that this Shia belief was preserved at great cost, including the torture and execution of the Ahl al-Bayt's companions. The speaker highlights authentic narrations indicating that Lady Zahra (SA) led the resistance against injustice, with Imam Ali (AS) himself acknowledging her authority in this specific context. By contrasting the current behavior of Arab rulers with historical oppression, he emphasizes that her protest was a necessary measure to save society from corruption and spiritual decay. The lecture concludes by describing the severe physical toll of the oppression on Lady Zahra (SA) and the necessity of her secret burial, underscoring the profound significance of this legacy for Shia Muslims.
