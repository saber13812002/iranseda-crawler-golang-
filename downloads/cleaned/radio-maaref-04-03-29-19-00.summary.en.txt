In this radio program, jurisprudential answers were provided to various listener questions. It was first emphasized that receiving profits from investment funds is permissible if Sharia conditions are met. Regarding Friday prayers, Ayatollah Sadrani deemed them obligatory for non-exempt individuals, while noting that some specific effects are unique to the era of the Imam's presence. Ayatollah Shabiri Zanjani stated that reciting Surah Al-Qadr in the first unit of prayer is recommended. On the topic of Aqiqah, Ayatollah Chowadi Amadi explained that it is a recommended act for each individual separately, though one Aqiqah for multiple children is acceptable if financial constraints exist. Concerning fasting after eye surgery, Hojjatol-Islam Falsade, citing Ayatollah Khamenei, stated that fasting is not obligatory if harm is certain; otherwise, one must make up the fasts or pay fidya. Regarding making up missed prayers, Hojjatol-Islam Mahdavi, citing Ayatollah Sistani, confirmed that performing Qada prayers between Zuhr and Asr or Maghrib and Isha is valid. In the final segment, Hojjatol-Islam Taheri emphasized prayer as a primary sign of faith and Islam and a source of divine light, concluding with an introduction to the cultural and religious activities at the Imamzadeh Molat shrine in Langroud.
