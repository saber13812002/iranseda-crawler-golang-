In the first segment, Professor Mohammadi explained that while material work is unnecessary in Paradise, spiritual activities like dhikr, scientific discussions, and worship continue for the believer's spiritual perfection. He clarified that wishing for death is not a sin but is considered a reprehensible act because it implies rejecting God's blessings. Regarding the Prophet's migration, the discussion highlighted that Imam Ali sleeping in the Prophet's bed was a strategic move to deceive enemies and ensure a safe exit, illustrating God's use of natural causes. The science of Tajweed was described as a conventional linguistic discipline essential for correct Quran recitation, rather than a purely rational or transmitted science. In the second part, Dr. Saleh Ghasemi reported a significant increase in the birth of third, fourth, and fifth children following the implementation of the Population Youth Law. He and other guests emphasized the benefits of large families, such as stronger parental authority and improved social skills in children. Finally, the program refuted the misconception that having fewer children leads to better upbringing, stressing that sustenance is in God's hands and citing Quranic verses that praise a large population.
