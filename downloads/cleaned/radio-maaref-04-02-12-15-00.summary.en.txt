This radio program discussed the pivotal role of the Qom Seminary in maintaining the leadership of the Islamic world following the death of Sheikh Haeri. It emphasized that Imam Khomeini's leadership and the training of prominent students like Shahid Beheshti established a revolutionary school combining science and action. The martyrdom and exile of senior clerics were highlighted as Quranic examples of jihad, demonstrating that the seminary remains inseparable from the revolution. Iran's military and nuclear authority was assessed as a result of the Supreme Leader's policies and domestic scientific advancements. This strength allows Iran to enter international negotiations with greater power and self-respect. In the second part of the program, the importance of traditional medicine principles and avoiding certain food combinations for health maintenance was discussed. Finally, the program concluded by introducing the edible plant "Zarbal" and answering medical questions, encouraging listeners to maintain a healthy lifestyle through exercise and proper diet.
