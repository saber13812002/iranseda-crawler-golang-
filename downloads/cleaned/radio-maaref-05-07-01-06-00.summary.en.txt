In this program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets the Quranic concept of Paradise as an "inheritance" not through death, but as the transfer of a divine right from disbelievers, who forfeited it through their own choices, to the believers. He argues that Paradise belongs to God, and the share of those who rejected Him is granted to the faithful. The discussion then focuses on the verse "And on the 'Araf are men," describing a spiritual, invisible barrier between the people of Paradise and Hell. Standing atop this barrier are "men" who are neither angels nor jinn, but complete human beings endowed with knowledge and faith. These divine men recognize all individuals on the Day of Judgment by their specific signs, or "simas," which are only fully understood by these spiritual experts. They interact with the people of Paradise through greetings and hopeful anticipation, while also observing the people of Hell. Like Prophet Abraham, these men possess a praiseworthy longing for the Divine presence, and despite their position on the 'Araf, they are ultimately considered heirs to the Garden of Delight. The core insight is that the precise understanding of these signs and fates is exclusive to these "mutawassitin," or spiritual sign-readers, while others perceive only general indicators.
