The radio program "Zarb-o-Zang" emphasizes the importance of collective ethics and the proper use of physical power in Zourkhaneh sports through classical poetry.
In the first section, the Morshed recites verses warning athletes against arrogance and urging them to practice charity and avoid oppression.
The show then visits Yazd to explore the historical role of Zourkhanehs as shelters for the needy and centers for local conflict resolution.
A subsequent interview is held with Amir Arab, the head of the Zourkhaneh and Pahlavani Wrestling Federation in Tehran Province.
Mr. Arab reports success in rejuvenating the athlete demographic by lowering the average age in Tehran.
He also highlights a signed memorandum with the Welfare Organization to support disabled and needy athletes in southern Tehran.
Finally, a request is made to expand these cultural and support activities to the eastern and western regions of Tehran.
The program concludes by reiterating that ethics is the fundamental pillar of Zourkhaneh sports.
