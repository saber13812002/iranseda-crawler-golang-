In this discussion, Dr. Bahmani emphasizes that the martyrdom of the Supreme Leader has revealed the intrinsic identity of the Iranian people and validated the revolution's core slogans. He argues that supporting the Axis of Resistance and pursuing a resistance economy were correct strategies, further proven by Iran's demonstrated deterrence power in the Strait of Hormuz. Geopolitically, Iran is shaping a "New Middle East" where the United States no longer holds central dominance, leveraging its strategic waterways and self-sufficient defense industry. Despite the enemy's superior military budget, Iran stands firm against global hegemony by relying on its ancient civilization, popular will, and strategic location. The current conflict is described as an existential war rooted in the opposition of global powers to Iran's independence and identity. Ultimately, it is predicted that as US power gradually declines and Muslim nations awaken, Iran will position itself at the center of global transformation.
