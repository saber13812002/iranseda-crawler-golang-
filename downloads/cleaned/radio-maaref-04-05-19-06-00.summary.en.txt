This program features Ayatollah Javadi Amoli's exegesis on the scientific and practical journey of Prophet Moses (PBUH) in the Quran.
Scientifically, the verse "Our Lord who gave everything its creation" is presented as the core principle of monotheism, explaining the active, internal, and teleological systems of existence.
Practically, being "Mukhlas" (sincere) is identified as the firmest operational principle, preventing both intentional and inadvertent sins.
Moses' (PBUH) prayers during his confrontation with Pharaoh demonstrate absolute reliance on God and a request for eloquence to effectively guide the people.
The request for his brother Aaron as an assistant is highlighted not as a sign of weakness, but as a strategic need for smoother preaching and clearer expression.
The program also notes that God, through Khidr, reminded Moses of his three previous good deeds to reinforce his understanding of divine protection.
Finally, Moses' prayer for the punishment of Pharaoh's people due to their disbelief mirrors the prayer of Prophet Noah (PBUH), reflecting a serious stance against deviation.
The discussion concludes that all of Moses' (PBUH) actions and words are rooted in monotheism and sincerity, serving as a model for correct faith and practice.
