In this radio program, child and adolescent psychologist Dr. Fatemeh Heydari emphasizes the importance of parents managing their own stress during child-rearing. She warns that maternal anxiety can sever the emotional bond with teenagers, driving them to seek refuge in the virtual world. Responding to listener questions, she advises against leaving adolescents alone during exam periods unless their readiness and sense of responsibility are confirmed. Additionally, forgetting friends' names in six-year-olds may be normal, but persistent issues warrant a check for learning disorders. To address messiness in five-year-olds, she recommends using play and positive reinforcement rather than threats or confiscating items. Parents should maintain the appeal of play by periodically rotating toys and reducing the overall volume of belongings. Finally, monitoring the social relationships of teenagers and intervening promptly in inappropriate discussions are essential aspects of upbringing at this age.
