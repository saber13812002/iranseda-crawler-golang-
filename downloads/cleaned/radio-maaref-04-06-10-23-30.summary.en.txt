In this radio program, the expert emphasized the need for smart, non-coercive media management for children, warning that sudden deprivation can lead to negative psychological effects. He recommended that media usage for children under two should be zero, while screen time for other ages should be limited to less than one hour per day. Instead of imposing bans, parents should engage their children in group activities and interactive games to shift their focus from screens to real-world interactions. Regarding religious ceremonies, it was suggested to create separate, age-appropriate spaces so that children can actively participate alongside their parents. In response to a question about encouraging a teenage girl to pray, leveraging peer groups and fostering a strong emotional bond between parents and children were identified as key strategies. Finally, a brief discussion addressed the necessity of reforming the banking system to eliminate usury, ensuring that the implementation of Islamic laws frees society from financial and moral harms.
