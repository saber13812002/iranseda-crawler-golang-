In this program, Professor Seyed Ahmad Faqih emphasizes two main points: recognizing God's unlimited power as a fundamental pillar of prayer and guarding the heart against internal sins. He explains that perceiving God's power as limited shrinks a person's existential capacity, whereas divine generosity is not diminished by increased requests. A key distinction is made between physical sins and heart sins, the latter being more critical because Satan enters through mental and spiritual temptations. To protect the heart, reciting the Dua-e-Muqallib al-Qulub and the phrase "La hawla wa la quwwata illa billah" is recommended to achieve a sound heart and divine refuge. The segment concludes by highlighting Qom's historical role as the primary hub for Ahl al-Bayt teachings and the center for producing jurisprudential and hadith literature. Today, the city continues to serve as the beating heart of knowledge, responsible for spreading the light of insight and awakening throughout the Muslim community.
