The program begins with a Quranic interpretation emphasizing the divine mercy poured upon worshippers and the angels' message encouraging them to continue their prayer. It then transitions to the virtues of Imam Ali (PBUH), highlighting his status as the first male Muslim and the first person to pray alongside the Prophet. Ayatollah Seyed Abbas Hosseini cites numerous narrations from companions and both Shia and Sunni sources to substantiate this historical fact. He references Nahj al-Balagha and Imam Ali's own statements to underscore his precedence in faith and prayer. Prominent poets have also celebrated this distinction in their verses. The segment concludes with prayers for spiritual success and an introduction to the morning show, which praises the Iranian flag and wishes listeners a blessed day.
