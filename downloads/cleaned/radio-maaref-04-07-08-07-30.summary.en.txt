This radio program primarily discusses the central role of kindness in shaping how others perceive our character.
The most essential trait for earning love and a good reputation in society is kindness, which is rooted in human nature.
In the second part, Hujjat al-Islam Falah analyzed the vice of foul language and cursing.
He identified fifteen main causes for such behavior, including envy, shamelessness, belittling others, and associating with abusive individuals.
The instructor emphasized that bluntness is never a valid justification for cursing and must be exercised with strict limits.
Finally, it was advised to release oneself from the cycle of hostility and foul language to maintain peace and dignity.
