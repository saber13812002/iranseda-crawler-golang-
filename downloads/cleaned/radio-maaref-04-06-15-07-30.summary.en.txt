During this radio program, the importance of giving gifts to enhance love and peace in family relationships was highlighted during the Week of Unity.
The guest, Hujjat al-Islam Falahati, then examined the severe consequences of backbiting, identifying it as rooted in hypocrisy and envy.
He warned that backbiting not only destroys social bonds but also leads to divine distance and the invalidation of worship.
Among the outcomes of backbiting is the erasure of all good deeds on the Day of Judgment and the transfer of the victim's sins to the backbiter.
In the afterlife, backbiters are punished by scratching their own faces, which serves as a manifestation of their worldly actions.
Repentance and seeking forgiveness from the victim were presented as the only way to escape these divine punishments and return to God's protection.
Finally, it was advised that those who are backbitten should remain patient, knowing that their suffering is rewarded by God.
