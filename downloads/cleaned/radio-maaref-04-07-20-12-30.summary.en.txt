In this lecture, Hojjat al-Islam Shariatitabar examines the role of appropriate attire and hijab in the Islamic lifestyle. He emphasizes that modesty is a shared responsibility for both men and women, rooted in inner purity and moral integrity. According to religious narrations, chastity is considered the highest form of worship, while hijab serves as the visible symbol of this inner character. The Quran instructs believing women to wear full coverage to display their religious identity and protect themselves from harassment. Furthermore, men are urged to control their gaze as a complementary duty to preserve social peace and honor. Adhering to modest dress is identified as a crucial factor in strengthening family bonds and preventing social crises such as divorce. Ultimately, implementing a Quranic lifestyle requires the simultaneous observance of external modesty and internal purity within the community.
