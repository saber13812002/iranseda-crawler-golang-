The radio program "House of the Beloved" began with Quranic recitation and religious songs, emphasizing the importance of prayer and spiritual connection with God on the fourth of Mordad. The historical segment detailed the Battle of Siffin, specifically the intense night known as "Laylat al-Hirir," describing the brutal combat between the armies. Amidst this chaos, the composure of Imam Ali (AS) in performing his prayer while arrows flew around him was highlighted as a model of serenity in the face of adversity. The core message of the show asserts that prayer is not merely a ritual but a path to knowing God and avoiding sin. It is believed that those nurtured in the school of prayer gain insight into hidden spiritual truths and rely solely on God in their daily lives. The program concluded with the repeated refrain of the song "Open the Window," inviting listeners to open their hearts to the divine.
