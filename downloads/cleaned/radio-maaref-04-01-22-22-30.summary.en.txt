In the radio program "Until Friday of the Appearance," Dr. Amir Mohsen Erfan emphasized the necessity of avoiding setting a specific date for the appearance of Imam Mahdi (AJ), based on the teachings of the Ahl al-Bayt. He explained that predicting a specific time not only fosters despair and lethargy in society but has also historically been exploited by deviant sects such as the Babis and Bahais. The primary causes of this issue were identified as ignorance of Mahdavi teachings, impatience for salvation, and the dominance of unstructured superstitions over scholarly knowledge. In the second segment, Hojjat al-Islam Mahmoudi introduced his book, "In His Air," which outlines practical methods for enhancing love and devotion to the Imam of the Time (AJ). The book presents eleven actionable steps and analyzes the obstacles to spiritual love, advocating for a dynamic life rooted in divine affection during the period of occultation. The program concluded by inviting listeners to participate in the "Ya Mahdi" campaign and to perform small but impactful actions to prepare the ground for the Imam's return.
