Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets Quranic verses to distinguish between "ajal musamma" and "ajal maghzi." The former refers to a fixed, unchangeable lifespan that marks the end of an individual's life. In contrast, the latter refers to initial conditions that are subject to change based on human actions. Relying on authentic narrations, he emphasizes that the fixed lifespan is the ultimate destiny, and no one dies based solely on the initial conditions. Humans can influence their initial lifespan through prayer, charity, and avoiding sin, thereby extending their lives. The universe operates on a precise, mathematical divine system where there is no ambiguity in God's foreknowledge. Nevertheless, humans possess free will to impact this system and shape their own destiny.
