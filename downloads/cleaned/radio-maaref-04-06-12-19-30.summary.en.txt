This radio program examines narrations from the book of Kafi, emphasizing that good character is the heaviest deed on the scale of judgment and a sign of perfect faith.
Imam Sadiq (AS) identified honesty, trustworthiness, modesty, and good character as four traits that preserve faith even in the presence of sins.
Allama Majlisi explained that these four virtues inherently prevent the commission of many sins, effectively shielding the individual from moral corruption.
The second part of the program analyzes the causes of backbiting, including ignorance, hypocrisy, personal flaws, and projection.
Additionally, anger, associating with backbiters, suspicion, envy, mockery, and boredom are highlighted as primary drivers of this grave sin.
Imam Ali (AS) described backbiting as a distinct sign of hypocrites and identified reason as the main factor in avoiding it.
Finally, envy is characterized as an incurable disease, while boredom is noted as a common reason for tearing down others.
