In this radio program, Professor Mohammad Hadi Falahat discusses the dangerous vice of gossiping and backbiting based on Quranic verses and hadiths. He describes gossiping as a malignant disease rooted in the ego, which indicates a lack of faith and apostasy. According to the narrations, a gossip is considered the worst of God's creations and the most wicked person on earth. The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) equated gossiping with the murder of three people, describing it as a soft terror that destroys the credibility of the individual, their brother, and the governing authority. Accepting such gossip is also considered an act of injustice and participation in sin. The professor emphasizes that gossips are morally bankrupt and unworthy of professional promotion or public funds. Finally, listeners are urged to avoid gossiping themselves and to refuse to listen to others who engage in this harmful behavior.
