In the "Zamzam Ahkam" program, jurisprudential answers were provided to listeners' questions, including recommendations for adhan and iqamah before prayer, the permissibility of using a phone screen during congregational prayer provided it does not disrupt obligatory recitations, and the application of khums to government subsidies if any amount remains after annual expenses. The ruling on the expiation for missed fasts for those unable to pay was also clarified. In the "Sarmashk" segment, Professor Mohtashebi, relying on Nahj al-Balagha, described jihad as the pinnacle of Islam and a gateway to paradise, emphasizing its role in protecting society from humiliation and defeat. He illustrated the concept of self-transcendence and generosity through a narrative involving Imam Ali and Imam Hasan. Finally, an interpretation of a verse from Surah Yunus was presented, highlighting the regret of disbelievers on the Day of Judgment and their fear of witnessing the punishment of their enemies in hell.
