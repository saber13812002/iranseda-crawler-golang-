This radio program provided jurisprudential answers to listeners' questions from various religious authorities. Ayatollah Sadrani clarified that using money specifically designated for personal use is not permissible for parents without permission, and independent children are not subject to their father's legal guardianship. The method for compensating a forgotten tashahhud in prayer was explained based on the risalas of Imam Khomeini and Ayatollah Sadrani, involving specific intentions and prostrations. It was emphasized that toothpaste residue must be completely removed to ensure the validity of fasting. Ayatollah Javadi Amoli ruled that a person who retains a home and emotional ties to their birthplace is considered a resident there, making their prayers and fasts valid. Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi detailed the obligation of repentance and paying blood money (diyah) for intentional abortion, specifying the distribution among heirs and the amount based on the fetus's age. The program concluded with Quranic recitation and spiritual discourses on the virtues of prayer, ablution, and the status of Hazrat Fatimah Zahra (SA).
