The radio program "Narrative of Love," dedicated to martyr Reza Parsa Moghaddam, featured Fatemeh Haghighi Mirza, the mother of martyrs Majid and Hossein Deljou. She recounted her prophetic dream about her sons' martyrdom, which Ayatollah Yazdi interpreted as a sign related to the appearance of Imam Mahdi. Fatemeh described her remarkable composure upon receiving the news of their deaths, including her calm preparation for Majid's farewell ceremony. She also shared Hossein's request to spare his younger sisters from hardship and the sacrifice of a cousin who took his place at the front. In the final segment, a political analyst argued that negotiations with the United States are currently futile and untrustworthy. Citing the US history of broken promises and assassinations, he asserted that such interactions offer no benefit to Iran's national interests in the present situation.
