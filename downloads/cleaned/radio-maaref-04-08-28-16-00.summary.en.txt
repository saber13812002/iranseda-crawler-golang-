In this radio program, Professor Mohammadi Sharoudi addressed various theological questions from listeners. He first refuted the concept of physical pressure in the grave, attributing the preservation of martyrs' and scholars' bodies to the profound influence of their souls. Next, he explained the virtues of the Ghafila prayer, recommending that listeners combine the intention of the Maghrib sunnah prayer with the form of the Ghafila prayer to maximize its benefits. Regarding the return of Imam Mahdi's companions, he emphasized that their piety and faith are paramount, while the Imam will utilize advanced, just technology to neutralize weapons of mass destruction. He also interpreted the Quranic verse on death, clarifying that the experience of dying varies based on one's spiritual state and can be pleasant for the faithful. Finally, he assured that worldly hardships are compensated by God either in this life or the hereafter, and that patience in the face of such tribulations yields immense divine rewards.
