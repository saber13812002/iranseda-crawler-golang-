The program began with the recitation of the prayer for rain from the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya, followed by a discussion on verbal vices, specifically blame and censure. Citing narrations from Imam Ali (AS), the guest emphasized that repeated blame is more painful than physical beating and severely damages an individual's self-confidence. The negative effects of censure on the blamed person include increased stubbornness, boldness in sinning, and the eventual ineffectiveness of future moral reprimands. It was clarified that blame is only beneficial when delivered in the appropriate context, with moderation, and proper etiquette. For the person who censures, there are severe consequences, and it is advised to avoid confrontation with brave or noble individuals, retreating if necessary. A key point highlighted is the importance of distancing oneself after delivering a reprimand to prevent the complete destruction of the relationship. Finally, listeners were encouraged to monitor their speech and cultivate ethical behavior to achieve a spiritual life worthy of awaiting the Imam of the Age.
