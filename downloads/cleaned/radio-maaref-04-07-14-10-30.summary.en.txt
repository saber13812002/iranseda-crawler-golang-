The radio program "Shifa" featured Dr. Pourhosseini discussing the role of traditional medicine in improving lifestyle and preventing chronic diseases.
The doctor identified persistent phlegm, known as "Nazla," as the root cause of many resistant illnesses that spread from the throat to other organs.
He emphasized that heavy mental work, sleep deprivation, and excessive consumption of vapor-producing foods are primary factors in worsening this condition.
Adhering to preventive principles such as maintaining a regular sleep schedule and a balanced diet is crucial for avoiding the recurrence of these ailments.
In the second segment, Hujjat al-Islam Rasoul Hafez interpreted verse 78 of Surah Qasas to highlight the dangers of arrogance and attributing blessings to oneself.
Referencing the story of Qarun, he reminded listeners that all abilities and wealth are trusts from God and must be met with gratitude and humility.
The program concluded with a tribute to Imam Khomeini and a reflection on national pride, specifically mentioning the historic seizure of the US embassy.
