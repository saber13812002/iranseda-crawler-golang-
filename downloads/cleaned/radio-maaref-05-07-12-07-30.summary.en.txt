The program begins with a poetic description of autumn as a symbol of maturity and calm, followed by a casual dialogue with listeners. Various segments discuss daily life issues, such as children's routines, and security concerns regarding ISIS threats and the situation in France. The core of the broadcast is a religious lecture warning against being deceived by the superficial aspects of life and neglecting self-improvement. Imam Sadiq (AS) cautions that health, wealth, longevity, and having children should not distract individuals from monitoring their own deeds. It is emphasized that salvation before God is achieved solely through personal faith, piety, and righteous action, rather than relying on social status or relationships. The lecture also highlights the danger of imagination and mental deception, which can distance a person from the truth. Finally, listeners are encouraged to focus on their own moral development and self-correction instead of preoccupied with the sins of others.
