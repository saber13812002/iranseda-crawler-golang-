In this radio program, the speaker emphasizes the fundamental difference between the "Kalimim" (true followers of the Torah) and the "Jews" who opposed Prophet Moses and the Prophet of Islam, citing Quranic verses. He argues that modern enemies are essentially engaged in a conflict with God, and the primary objective is to strike at their "power claws" and main capabilities. The concept of "Divine Support" (Nasr) is presented as a reward for steadfastness, which may lead to the military defeat of the enemy or a peace treaty from a position of strength. Referring to the Treaty of Hudaybiyyah, the speaker explains that lasting peace is achieved only after proving steadfastness and breaking the enemy's power equations, rather than through surrender. He also stresses the importance of not fearing Satan's army of fear and relying on God's spiritual and material support in this great arena. Finally, the program concludes with special prayers for the victory of the Resistance Front and the nearness of the Imam's reappearance.
