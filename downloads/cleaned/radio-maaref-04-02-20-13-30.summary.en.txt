The radio program "Andisheha," featuring Dr. Hamid Mohseni, examined the various dimensions of artificial intelligence from both technical and civilizational perspectives.
A key point emphasized was the necessity of moving beyond a purely instrumental view of AI to address its fundamental challenges in epistemology, ethics, and governance.
The Supreme Leader also stressed the importance of mastering the deep layers of AI technology rather than merely relying on other nations.
The program explained different definitions of AI, including narrow, general, and strong AI, analyzing the differences between these systems and human intelligence.
The Turing Test and the definitions by Russell and Norvig were introduced as the philosophical and technical foundations of artificial intelligence.
It was highlighted that Islamic societies must critically address the ethical and legal challenges of this technology based on religious principles.
Finally, the spiritual and social danger of self-forgetfulness and forgetting God in the age of artificial intelligence was discussed.
