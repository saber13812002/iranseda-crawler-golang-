In this radio program, the unity of the criterion and the plurality of degrees in existence are emphasized, citing Quranic verses and Mulla Sadra's philosophical foundations.
It is explained that existence is a single reality but possesses degrees of richness and poverty, much like light has varying intensities.
This perspective demonstrates that there is no conflict between mystical, philosophical, and jurisprudential principles, as they all fit within a coherent system.
The concept of "Al" in prayers is defined as broader than the Prophet's blood relatives, encompassing all who sincerely adhere to his prophethood.
The primary examples of this "Al" are the Ahl al-Kisa (Ali, Fatima, Hasan, and Husayn), who hold the highest degrees of perfection in this criterion.
In the narrative interpretation section, the term "Rabb" is interpreted as the nurturer and manager of all beings in existence, not just humans.
The word "Alameen" refers to the system of existence and all material and spiritual entities under God's management.
These interpretations indicate that God is the manager and nurturer of all levels of existence, from material particles to spirits and angels.
