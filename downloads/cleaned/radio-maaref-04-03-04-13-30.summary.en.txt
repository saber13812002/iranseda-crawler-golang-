The radio program "Andisheha" on Radio Ma'aref explored how to resolve the apparent contradiction between religion and ethics in the story of the sacrifice of Prophet Ismail. Dr. Hadi Sadeghi rejected secular and Ash'ari theories, arguing that divine commands are inherently ethical and cannot conflict with reason or morality. He dismissed common justifications such as the command being a mere "test," "abrogated," or having a "mystical interpretation," as these views either escape the issue of unethics or contradict the infallibility of prophets. The core argument is that this was a specific divine exception within God's rights over life, making the obedience to it ethically sound due to hidden wisdom. This reasoning is supported by analogies to judicial executions based on apparent evidence and ethical permissions to kill innocents in specific wartime scenarios. The program concluded with a personal account of Imam Khomeini and Chamran's early war efforts in Khuzestan, emphasizing the importance of leadership presence and criticizing unrealistic war novels.
