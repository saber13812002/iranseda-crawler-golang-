In the radio program "Mashq-e Rah," Professor Seyed Ahmad Faqih discussed the concept of "Tawhidic ethics" and the pivotal role of prayer in refining human behavior. He emphasized that knowing God and maintaining a heartfelt connection with Him automatically improves one's character, and that prayer should begin with gratitude for divine blessings. Citing religious narrations, the professor explained that praising God before making requests increases the likelihood of acceptance through the intercession of prophets and avoids arrogance. In the Q&A section, he recommended contentment, avoiding worldly desires, and seeking God's grace as remedies for envy. Furthermore, he identified sincere repentance, seeking forgiveness, and relying on the Prophet and the Ahl al-Bayt as the primary solutions for the forgiveness of sins. The episode concluded with a historical overview of the transition of Imamate from Imam Sajjad to Imam Baqir during the political era of Hushaym ibn Abd al-Malik.
