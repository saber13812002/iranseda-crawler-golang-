The program began with Quranic recitation and a strong emphasis on avoiding lies, followed by anecdotes highlighting the former leader's meticulous attention to truthfulness. The "Book Narrative" segment then introduced the novel "Glass Marbles," which indirectly critiques the "fewer children, better life" slogan by depicting the joyful life of a large family. The story of Sara and her friends sneaking into the basement to see their brothers' strange invention was narrated with excitement and warmth. Following the story, an announcement was made regarding student admissions for media and communication studies at Qom's IRIB University. In the "Short Answers" section, a father explained the causes of global poverty and wealth to his son, emphasizing the roles of colonialism, individual effort, and spiritual sustenance. The broadcast concluded with a Hadith from Imam Ali (AS) regarding social ethics and mutual respect.
