In the radio program "Zamzam Ahkam," two significant jurisprudential questions were addressed and answered. Responding to the first question, Hujjat al-Islam Fallahzadeh stated that a man who has not paid the mahr of his deceased wife remains in debt and is obligated to pay it to her heirs. However, if he lacks financial capacity, the heirs must grant him time according to the Quran's directive until he can afford it, unless they choose to forgive the debt. In response to the second question, Hujjat al-Islam Aliannejad explained the ruling for fasting among elderly men and women who are unable to fast. He noted that these individuals must give a specific amount of wheat, barley, or bread to the poor for each day of missed fasting. It was also recommended that they eat during permissible hours in mild seasons as a precaution. The program concluded by providing contact details for submitting questions and announcing the national Quran competition.
