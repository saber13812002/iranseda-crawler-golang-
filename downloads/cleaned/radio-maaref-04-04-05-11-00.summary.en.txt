This radio program, held on the eve of Muharram, featured Hujjat al-Islam Najm al-Din Tabbasi, a professor from Qom Seminary. The guest praised media efforts to keep the culture of Imam Hussein alive while analyzing recent events and military threats against Iran. He argued that enemies, driven by envy over Iran's scientific progress and political independence, seek to create a "scorched earth" scenario rather than accepting defeat. Tabbasi highlighted Israel's historical military failures, warning that despite its aggressive propaganda, the regime has revealed its actual weakness and impotence. He also emphasized the importance of national unity, stating that the Iranian people's cohesion is the primary factor in strategically defeating the enemy. Finally, the guest recited passionate poetry, urging the public to uphold Islamic values and welcome the month of Muharram.
