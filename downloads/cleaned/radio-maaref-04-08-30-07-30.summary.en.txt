In this radio program, Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallah examines the moral vice of "mockery" based on Quranic verses. He identifies mockery as a sign of inner ignorance and following one's ego, which is essentially a hidden hostility toward God, religion, and sacred values. The Quran strictly prohibits not only mocking divine verses and rulings but also sitting with or befriending those who ridicule the faith. History shows that prophets, from Noah to the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH), consistently faced ridicule and mockery from their people. This mockery is actually an attack on the entire system of faith and God's messengers, rooted in a failure to understand the truth. Therefore, believers must maintain distance from such individuals through piety and self-restraint, avoiding any close relationships with them.
