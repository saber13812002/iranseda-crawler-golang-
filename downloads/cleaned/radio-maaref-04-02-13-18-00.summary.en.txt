The program began with a quiz segment where participants answered general knowledge questions and won prizes.
In the "Ganj-e Sa'adat" segment, a scholar discussed practical monotheism and the reasons for divine anger.
He emphasized that seeking the pleasure of other than God and denying monotheism in Lordship, such as believing in the independent influence of other entities, corrupts one's deeds.
This denial of Lordship affects not only non-believers but also nominal Muslims, leading them to worship their ego and tyranny.
The "Zamzam Ahkam" program then explained the recommended practices for the Umrah Tamattu' ihram.
The expert clarified that the ritual bath, wudu, and the ihram prayer are recommended but not mandatory for the validity of the ihram.
After wearing the ihram clothes and reciting the Talbiyah, 24 prohibitions apply to the pilgrim until the completion of Umrah.
Finally, a religious question regarding the prayer method for patients with speech impairments was addressed.
