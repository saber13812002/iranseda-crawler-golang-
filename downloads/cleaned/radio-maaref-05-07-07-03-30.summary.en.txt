In the radio program "Zamzam Ahkam," jurisprudential answers were provided to listeners' questions.
According to Ayatollah Sistani's ruling, consuming tea or food with a mere suspicion of containing haram substances is permissible if there is no certainty of their presence.
Ayatollah Javadi Amoli stated that buying and selling pork or alcohol, even for industrial or medical use, is strictly haram and void.
It was explained that if water is harmful to a wound, the ritual ablution (wudu) must be performed using the "jibireh" method.
The program concluded by reminding listeners of the contact channels for submitting new questions.
An advertisement for the 20th National Quran Competition was also aired, detailing the registration and exam dates.
The final segment included the recitation of Munajat Muta'in and religious songs.
