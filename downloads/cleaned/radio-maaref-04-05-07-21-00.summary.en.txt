The radio program "Parsaiyan," featuring Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi, emphasizes the necessity of seeking intercession from the Infallibles and visiting their shrines as paths to closeness with God. Ayatollah Al-Azmjowadi Amoli and Ayatollah Zanjani then recount stories of their teachers' devotion and a miracle where Sayyid Fakhreddin Jazairi was healed through intercession with Imam Abbas. The main segment features a narrative from the book "Neshan-e Hossein," depicting the brave battles of Imam Hassan's sons at the Battle of Karbala. This account details the martyrdom of Ali Akbar, Hassan, Ghasem, Amir, and Qasem, along with the suffering of their mother, Nafisa. Following the narrative, the program discusses the jurisprudential rulings regarding the ownership of sports competition prizes and the necessary legal conditions for them. Finally, the show concludes with advice on performing small acts for environmental and social well-being, ending with a song praising Iran and the sign-off.
