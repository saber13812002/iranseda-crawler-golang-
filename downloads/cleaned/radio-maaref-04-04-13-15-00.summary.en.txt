In this radio program, expert Mehdi Rasakhi argues that the enemy's calculations for the collapse of the Islamic Republic have failed due to the unpredictable nature of the Iranian people. He emphasizes that the inherent sensitivity of the Iranian public to foreign aggression has fostered unprecedented social cohesion, effectively thwarting the enemy's divisive schemes. Rasakhi asserts that no external force can bring down the system, noting that internal challenges are the only potential threat, yet religious beliefs continue to serve as a protective barrier. He points out that recent conflicts and unilateral actions by the United States have clearly exposed the ineffectiveness of international organizations and global legal frameworks. These analyses suggest that American soft power is declining, revealing the true face of arrogance to the world. Ultimately, the discussion concludes that these events have provided an opportunity to demonstrate the legitimacy of the Islamic Republic and the benefits of resistance against falsehood.
