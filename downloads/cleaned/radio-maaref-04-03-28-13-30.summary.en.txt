The radio program "Narrative of Love" began by announcing the upcoming broadcast of the Supreme Leader's televised message before transitioning to the story of Zahra Imami, daughter of a war martyr and wife of martyr Hossein Moradi. Zahra recounted her difficult childhood following her father's martyrdom in 1986 and the subsequent loss of her grandparents, which deeply affected her mother. She described her mother's spiritual experiences, including a vision of her father before his death, and the intense anxiety she felt upon receiving the news of his martyrdom. Zahra shared her journey to marriage with Hossein Moradi, a Pasdaran member and geography student, highlighting the financial challenges and initial hurdles they faced in their shared life. In 2013, her husband was deployed to Syria to defend the holy shrines, participating in various operations. Hossein Moradi was injured in his final mission and, after a 15-day coma, achieved martyrdom on November 19, 2013. The program concluded by emphasizing the patience and love of martyrs' families while inviting listeners to tune in to the Supreme Leader's address.
