This radio program addressed several jurisprudential questions from Islamic scholars. First, it was clarified that breastfeeding does not establish a permanent prohibition of marriage unless it reaches the specific legal threshold. Regarding gifts of doubtful origin, using them is permissible unless there is certainty that the money itself is haram. The ruling on prayer for a family visiting their original hometown was explained, noting that while the parents pray full prayers, their daughter is considered a traveler and prays shortened. It was also stated that one cannot take fruit from a neighbor's tree that falls into their yard without the owner's explicit consent. Finally, the segment emphasized the importance of spiritual humility and focus during prayer.
