This radio program focuses on the concept of the "walking pilgrim" and the Arbaeen journey, identifying Imam Hussein (PBUH) as the primary guide and destination.
The guest emphasizes that the ultimate goal of this journey is to draw closer to God and to cultivate the pilgrim's vision, hearing, and actions.
Arbaeen is presented not merely as a religious ritual but as a vital opportunity for social networking and unity among diverse groups under the banner of Wilayah.
Serving the pilgrims in this journey should transcend the geographical boundaries of Karbala and Iran, becoming a global model for the defenders of the Ahl al-Bayt.
The program highlights the importance of maintaining solidarity and preparing for the reappearance of the Imam by adhering to the teachings of Arbaeen.
The broadcast concludes with the recitation of Hussaini poetry and mourning, bringing the spiritual and emotional atmosphere of the show to a climax.
