This radio program, broadcast on the martyrdom day of Imam Reza (PBUH), explores his ethical and spiritual characteristics.
The central theme is "untying the knots" of people's problems, which narrations and the Quran describe as a cause for ease in both this world and the Hereafter.
The speaker emphasizes the importance of charity and service by recounting stories of Imam Reza's secret assistance to the needy and his distribution of gifts to the poor.
A key distinction is made between "arrogance" (sawt) and "self-sufficiency" (istighna), with wealth accumulated through greed and stinginess being identified as harmful.
The concept of divine "cause-making" is explained, where God inspires others to fulfill the needs of believers, thereby answering their prayers.
The program concludes by reflecting on the Imam's martyrdom through poisoning by Caliph Mamun and offering salutations to his memory.
