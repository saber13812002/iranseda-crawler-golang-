In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Mehdi Yousefian addresses various Mahdavi questions. He identifies the return of Imam Hussein (AS) as a fundamental belief in Shia Islam, noting that he will be the first to return to earth after the reappearance. Yousefian explains that Imam Hussein (AS) will govern after Imam Mahdi (AJ) and will even perform the latter's funeral rites. The discussion also clarifies that the Imam's name is not hidden, although some traditions advise against mentioning it. This caution is attributed either to a general ruling until the reappearance or to preventing enemy provocation during the Imam's early years. The program concludes with the broadcast of Friday prayers from Tehran, emphasizing the importance of jihad and justice in Islam.
