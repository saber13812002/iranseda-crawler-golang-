In the "Quranic Q&A" radio program, the host addressed several theological questions from listeners. The commentator analyzed a verse in Surah Qasas, explaining that the word "south" was used instead of "far" to indicate the moderate distance and hidden angle of Moses' sister's observation. Regarding Surah Yusuf, the use of the title "Lord" for the Egyptian vizier was attributed to his royal status and ancient Egyptian cultural norms. In response to a question about Surah Yasin, it was clarified that entering Paradise after martyrdom refers to the Barzakh realm in a symbolic form, not a physical one. The discussion on the verse regarding striking wives in Surah An-Nisa was interpreted as a deterrent measure rather than violence, citing the conduct of the Infallibles. Finally, the program referenced Surah Al-Isra to predict the downfall of the Zionist regime due to its oppression. The broadcast concluded with an introduction to the "Model" segment, focusing on the virtue of honesty in the school of Imam Ali (PBUH).
