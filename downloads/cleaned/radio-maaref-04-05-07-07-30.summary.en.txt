The radio program began by sharing listeners' messages expressing nostalgia for Iran, highlighting the nation's unique hospitality and compassion.
The guest expert then emphasized the critical importance of preserving the Persian language as a national asset and a tool for cultural resistance against enemies.
He pointed to the Supreme Leader's consistent use of Persian vocabulary as a model, urging the public to prioritize this language in daily conversations.
The second part of the show focused on the religious and ethical interpretation of stinginess, which was identified as a major sin.
According to Islamic narrations, a stingy person wrongly perceives God's trust as personal property and refuses to share it with others.
The expert cited a narration from Imam Sadiq (AS) to clarify that the entire world and the hereafter belong to the Imams, with believers acting merely as trustees.
Finally, the severe consequences of stinginess were detailed, including the curse of Imam Ali (AS), the prayers of angels, poverty in this life, and the punishment of hellfire in the afterlife.
