In this radio program, the speaker emphasizes the role of spirituality and divine grace in military victories, arguing that material tools like missiles and public unity are merely means through which God bestows His support. Citing Quranic verses, he warns against attributing achievements solely to human effort, as this can lead to arrogance and the forgetting of God. The speaker also stresses the importance of national unity achieved by highlighting the virtues of rivals and avoiding division, viewing this unity as a divine gift. He further explains the concept of proper charity, which follows Quranic priorities starting with family and neighbors, ensuring that basic needs are met before giving away excess. The program concludes with the commemoration of martyrs from Isfahan province and the performance of religious music in honor of the national event "Iran Jan, Isfahan Iran."
