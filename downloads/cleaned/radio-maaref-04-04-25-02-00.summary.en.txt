The program began with religious music for Muharram, followed by a sermon from Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi on the virtue of humility and the dangers of arrogance. Citing religious narrations, he described humility as the fruit of love and arrogance as a source of public disdain. To illustrate this, anecdotes were shared about a religious leader who lived a simple life, refusing a private shelter during the war and a luxury car in his later years. The second segment, titled "Love Story," featured the wife of Martyr General Tehrani Moghaddam. She recounted the hardships of their shared life in the early 1980s and the loneliness she felt during his frequent absences. She highlighted the general's complete trust in her regarding child-rearing and household management. Despite the difficulties, she emphasized that she would choose him again if given the chance. These memories reflect deep love, sacrifice, and a positive perspective on life with a great and compassionate man.
