The radio program "Zamzam Ahkam" began by addressing various religious questions from listeners. The first section detailed rulings on blood impurity, following the imam during prostration and the Qunut prayer, and wudu with blood under the fingernails. It also clarified the validity of women's prayers in the front row and the calculation of Khums when facing immediate debts. Subsequently, a lecture was broadcast on the importance of prayer and its role in preventing immorality and sin. This was followed by a narration regarding the virtues of prayer and the status of worshippers in Paradise. The final segment of the program focused on the martyrdom of children in recent attacks on Iran. The story of Taha Bahrouzi and Alisan Jabari, two children from Tabriz who lost their lives, was recounted to highlight their innocence. The program concluded with blessings and a closing prayer.
