In the "Mashgh-e Rah" program on Radio Ma'aref, Professor Seyed Ahmad Faghi discussed the stages of the spiritual journey toward God, emphasizing that piety is achieved through fear, hope, and love. He explained that fear of God is the initial and most universal step that fosters hope in divine mercy, while highlighting the importance of wudu, prayer, and sincere supplication for spiritual growth. The professor cited a hadith stating that spiritual hunger can be immediately alleviated by performing these rituals with genuine devotion. In response to a listener's question about weak willpower and the inability to act on moral advice, he advised taking small, practical steps to resist one's ego rather than passively listening to lectures. He recommended gradual, minor changes in habits, such as slightly reducing food intake, to strengthen willpower, following the advice of Ayatollah Behjat. The program concluded by highlighting the unique formation of the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) during the Iranian Revolution. Unlike self-serving groups in other revolutions, the IRGC was established with centralized command and exceptional moral and spiritual integrity from the outset. Its rapid growth into a well-organized military force with divisional units within two years is described as an unprecedented phenomenon in Iran's military history.
