Reza Rajabi, a former Iranian football legend and captain of the national youth and U23 teams, recounts his journey from his 11-year tenure at Esteghlal to his retirement. Despite his on-field success and multiple championships, he faced severe depression following his father's death and the lack of social support after leaving professional football. Rajabi admits that he fell into addiction during his early retirement years, a period he describes as a dark chapter in his life. He credits his personal willpower and spiritual guidance for overcoming this crisis, eventually transitioning into a role as a social and sports expert. The former player criticizes the Iranian football system for neglecting retired players and failing to utilize technical directors effectively. He argues that the sport needs to value the experience of technical managers and counselors rather than relying solely on head coaches. Rajabi expresses gratitude for recent support from football figures and emphasizes the importance of hope and community assistance for those facing social issues.
