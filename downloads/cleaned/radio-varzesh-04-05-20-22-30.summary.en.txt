The show's guest, veteran referee Qahraman Jafari, discussed his 25-year refereeing career and 18 years of administrative experience within the Football Federation. He acknowledged that while refereeing errors are inevitable, he never made decisions out of fear, citing a specific mistake in a match between Railways and Foolad Khuzestan. Jafari described the final match between Sepahan and Saipa, which determined the league champion, as the most memorable moment of his career. Addressing concerns about potential corruption in refereeing, he emphasized the urgent need for an independent, organized "Refereeing System" similar to medical or engineering boards. He argued that the current committee structure is insufficient and that a dedicated organization is necessary to ensure integrity and professional standards. Finally, citing his comprehensive background in teaching, coaching, and refereeing, Jafari stated that he would run for the presidency of the Football Federation if Dr. Majidi did not stand as a candidate.
