In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Mohammad Hali-Fala identified stinginess as a moral vice and a sign of distance from God, citing Quranic verses and narrations. He attributed the causes of stinginess to ignorance, a misunderstanding of wealth, listening to Satan's whispers, and excessive attachment to the material world. The primary solutions for overcoming this vice include gaining awareness of the virtues of charity, correcting misconceptions about future planning, and transforming doubt into certainty regarding divine promises. The speaker emphasized that charity is the only cure for stinginess and the path to achieving true dignity and greatness. Practical advice included avoiding the company of stingy people, consistently practicing generosity, and refraining from coveting others' possessions. In the final segment, Hujjat al-Islam Irandoust analyzed a story from Saadi's Gulistan to highlight the importance of avoiding hypocrisy in worship. He explained that hypocrisy invalidates religious acts and that a sincere servant must focus solely on God.
