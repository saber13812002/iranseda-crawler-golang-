The program begins by emphasizing the concept of "Mercy to the World," citing Quranic verses to present the Prophet's (PBUH) ethics and forgiveness as the primary model for Muslims.
Stories of the Prophet's patience and miracles, such as forgiving his enemies and responding to a Jewish creditor, are recounted to illustrate the depth of his compassion.
The second part of the program follows a narrative from the book "Akhyanous Mashreq," where the character "Amaran" travels to Khorasan in search of the water of life.
During this journey, Amaran is accompanied by Hajj Karim the cobbler and his daughter Rahleh, and after being rescued from bandits, they arrive at the tent of a man named Ahmad.
Ahmad, who is himself a Shia, invites Hajj Karim to participate in a friendly discussion with the tent's host, a moderate Sunni.
The host acknowledges his respect for Imam Reza (AS) but believes he was the fourth caliph rather than an Imam.
By posing short questions, he intends to engage in dialogue with Hajj Karim and Amaran to better understand the truth of the Shia faith.
The ultimate goal of this discussion is to achieve unity and brotherhood among Islamic sects without denying the inherent beliefs of each.
