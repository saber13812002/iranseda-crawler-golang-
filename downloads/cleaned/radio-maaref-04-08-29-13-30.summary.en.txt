In this episode of the "Narrative of Love" program, the mother of Arman Alivardi recounts the final moments of her son's life and his martyrdom during the 2022 protests in Tehran. She details the last phone calls with Arman and her maternal anxiety, leading up to the moment she learned he was wounded and her presence at the hospital until his martyrdom on Friday morning. The mother describes the intense grief of her family, particularly her brother, after losing this young man from the 1980s generation who deeply desired martyrdom. Arman Alivardi was a young man with great ideals and good character who achieved his wish for martyrdom despite difficult circumstances. Reflecting on her patience and sacrifice, the mother speaks about the hardships of life after her son's death and her constant longing for him. This narrative highlights the profound pain of martyrs' mothers and the role of the Basij in creating opportunities from threats. Listener messages also express gratitude for the program and solidarity with the families of the martyrs.
