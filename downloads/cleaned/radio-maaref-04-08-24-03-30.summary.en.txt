The radio program initially discusses the sixth and seventh linguistic vices, specifically enmity, cursing, and slander.
Citing the Quran and the conduct of Imam Ali, it emphasizes that Muslims should avoid using abusive language against enemies and instead address their errors calmly.
The discussion then addresses slogans like "Death to America," justifying them as a religious declaration of dissociation from God's enemies.
The second part of the broadcast provides jurisprudential answers to listeners' questions regarding the details and recommended practices of the night prayer.
It also clarifies the rulings on peacock meat and lists fifteen specific parts of halal animals that are forbidden to consume.
Guidance is offered on performing specific prayers such as Laylat al-Dafn and night prayers while on security duty, including recommended supplications.
Finally, the program addresses the validity of praying with a long chador dragging on the ground and the permissibility of prostrating on Karbala soil for those with legal excuses.
