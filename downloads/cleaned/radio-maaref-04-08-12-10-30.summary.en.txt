In this radio program, the principles of exercise in Islamic traditional medicine were first discussed, citing Dr. Pourhosseini and Ibn Sina. These principles emphasize that physical activity must be balanced, with proper breathing and without excess or deficiency, to prevent bodily harm. Subsequently, in the "A Drop from the Sea" segment, Hujjat al-Islam Rasoul interpreted verse 86 of Surah Hud to stress the importance of earning a halal livelihood. He explained, based on narrations and Imam Ali's sermons, that pursuing haram income does not increase one's sustenance but rather diminishes their halal share and leads to negative consequences in this world and the hereafter. The core message is that a believer should rely on God and strive for halal earnings to achieve peace and blessing. Finally, upcoming programs on Radio Ma'aref, including a lecture on the relationship between belief and action and legal case reviews, were announced.
