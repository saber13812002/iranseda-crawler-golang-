In the "Porsoman-e Qur'ani" program, the theory of Quranic alteration was refuted by emphasizing the principle of Tawatur, which ensures the text's integrity through mass transmission.
While individual narrations may not always meet the strict criteria of Tawatur, the collective preservation of the Quran by the global Muslim community eliminates any possibility of tampering.
The discussion also highlighted the social responsibility of guidance, specifically praising the crucial role of mothers in maintaining their children's religious identity against cultural threats.
With the approaching month of Muharram, the importance of embracing Alawi culture and Karbala's teachings to strengthen resilience and unity was underscored.
In the news segment, support for the Supreme Leader was declared in response to the US President's provocations, with Basij members pledging to defend the system.
The Ministry of Health reported injuries among medical staff following recent attacks, while the Ministry of Communications announced restored access for Iranians abroad to domestic messaging apps.
Ali Shamkhani reaffirmed his commitment to the Iranian people and the path of resistance through a social media post.
The program concluded with the recitation of Surah Fatir and a call for listeners to attend the next session focused on the event of Ashura.
