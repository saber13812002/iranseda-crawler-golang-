The news segment reported that over 5,000 people have pre-registered for the 1405 Hajj, urging existing ticket holders to use the remaining deadline. It also highlighted the training of over 3,000 complete Quran memorizers at the Bait al-Hussein institute and the Qom Alawi Institute's mission to train theologians capable of addressing societal doubts. Yemen's Ansar Allah movement celebrated the release of nearly 2,000 Palestinian prisoners as a result of resistance in Gaza, while an international solidarity conference in Tehran emphasized the unity of Islamic sects. The religious Q&A program addressed various jurisprudential issues, including the validity of congregational prayer for those who cannot sit fully and the permissibility of praying after partial ghusl without wudu according to Imam Khomeini's fatwa. Further rulings were provided regarding the khums on savings for debts, the prohibition of fasting for the sick, and the conditions for performing full prayers while residing in a city. The broadcast concluded by emphasizing the spiritual strength and peace derived from the remembrance of God.
