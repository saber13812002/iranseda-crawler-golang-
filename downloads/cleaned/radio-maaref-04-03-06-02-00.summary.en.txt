The radio program "Parsaiyan" began by highlighting the virtues of congregational prayer and the consistent practice of scholars like Ayatollah Izadi and Akhund Khorasani, who maintained this ritual even in harsh winter conditions. The show then transitioned to the "Mashgh-e Rah" segment, where Hujjat al-Islam Faqih discussed the ethics of monotheism and the proper etiquette of supplication. Citing a hadith addressed to Salman al-Farisi, the guest emphasized that reciting Surah Al-Fatiha, Surah Al-Ikhlas, and specific tasbih phrases are essential prerequisites for effective prayer. These actions are intended to strengthen one's focus on God and cultivate positive moral traits in daily behavior. The discussion also addressed ethical flaws such as arrogance and self-admiration, suggesting remedies like greeting others and comparing oneself to great scholars to overcome these vices. The educational benefits of tawassul, or seeking intercession through the Imams, were highlighted as a means to reduce pride and increase humility. Finally, the program addressed a listener's question about the habit of unlawful gaze, recommending that it be replaced with loving and respectful looks toward family members.
