In this radio program, the host celebrates the birth of Imam Abdul-Azim al-Hasani while emphasizing that trust in God and self-confidence are the two pillars of success and inner peace. Using a sports metaphor, the speaker argues that fear of enemies leads to stagnation, whereas reliance on God and belief in one's inner potential pave the way for progress and victory. The second part of the show focuses on practical solutions for curing "ujb," or self-admiration, as explained by the guest expert based on the teachings of Imam Ali. The expert stresses that preventing self-admiration is easier than curing it, and officials must avoid seeking praise to prevent being perceived as self-centered. Additional recommendations include avoiding the company of self-admiring individuals and remaining vigilant after achieving success, as comfort can easily lead to a relapse of arrogance. Finally, the program concludes that relying on the guidance of the Ahl al-Bayt and constantly seeking refuge in God is the only way to remain safe from Satan's arrows and the desires of the ego.
