This radio program uses the metaphor of a "divine school" to explain the three stages of faith: verbal submission, internalizing faith in the heart, and practical action through one's limbs.
The core argument emphasizes that merely reciting the testimony of faith is insufficient; God must become the commander of the heart to control the ego and base desires.
Relying on Quranic verses and Nahj al-Balagha, the program identifies religious obligations like prayer and fasting as primary tools for testing a believer's obedience and sincerity.
A fundamental distinction is drawn between true believers, who are purified through sincere repentance, and hypocrites who only display Islam on the surface.
In the second segment, scholar Javad Mohaddesi interprets Imam Ali's wisdom to define divine trials as tests of sincerity, noting that even angels were tested through their obedience to God's commands.
The program warns, using historical examples like the Battle of Siffin, that Satan may disrupt the execution of divine laws by influencing the companions of the Infallibles.
Ultimately, the discussion concludes that achieving spiritual perfection and eternal rewards requires continuous effort, charity, seeking forgiveness, and complete submission to God's command.
