In this segment of the radio program "Asr-e Javani," the concept of arrogance is examined as an internal moral vice and psychological trait. Arrogance is described as a gradable characteristic that serves as the common denominator among the inhabitants of Hell, whereas humility is the shared trait of those destined for Paradise. This vice acts as a severe barrier to spiritual elevation and divine proximity, trapping individuals within their own limited self-perceptions. In contrast, the illuminated faculty of reason manifests through humility, bringing light and clarity, while the commanding ego spreads darkness and corruption through arrogance. Arrogance functions as a domino effect, triggering other vices and leading to exploitation and extreme self-centeredness. The speaker identifies global Zionism as a concrete embodiment of arrogance and tyranny, citing Quranic verses to warn of the significant corruption it is causing in Palestine. Ultimately, the discussion emphasizes the necessity of overcoming this vice and cultivating humility to achieve spiritual salvation and enter Paradise.
