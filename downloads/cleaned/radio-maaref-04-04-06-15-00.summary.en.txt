In the radio program "Rahbord," analyst Mehdi Rasakhi examines the Zionist regime's attack on Iran on Khordad 23 and the Islamic Republic's military and intelligence responses. He attributes this attack to the regime's desperation and weakness following the exposure of classified intelligence documents by the Imam's (AJ) unknown soldiers. Rasakhi highlights the structural difference between Iran, a vast country with open borders, and the Zionist regime, which is a closed and controlled territory. Domestic unity and the people's enthusiastic participation in religious events like Arbaeen and Qods Day are presented as the system's most crucial support and the key factor in thwarting the enemy's calculations. He describes the ongoing conflict as a battle between truth and falsehood, emphasizing divine assistance and Iran's ultimate victory in these new regional dynamics based on the Quranic verse "Allah will surely help you." The analysis also notes that the cost of resistance is always lower than the cost of passivity, and these developments will alter the world's perception of the Zionist regime's military power.
