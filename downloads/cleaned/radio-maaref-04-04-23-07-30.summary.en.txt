This radio program discussed the role of cyberspace in strengthening national heroes and countering the enemy's anti-heroes.
The main focus was the "Day of Dialogue and Constructive Interaction with the World," featuring an interview with Hujjat al-Islam Baba Najat.
He highlighted the enemy's failed calculations following General Soleimani's martyrdom and identified arrogance as the primary source of global tension.
It was suggested that Iran's core message to the world should be reliance on monotheism and rejection of oppressive powers, following the Prophet's example.
The second segment, with Professor Falahati, explored the spiritual benefits of charity and emphasized that its reward is not necessarily material.
It was explained that God provides the return of charity in various forms, such as averting misfortune or granting inner peace, according to divine wisdom.
Charity was presented as a path to spiritual closeness to God, earning the Prophet's prayers, and receiving shade on the Day of Judgment.
Finally, it was noted that even giving half a date or speaking a kind word can serve as a shield against the fire of Hell.
