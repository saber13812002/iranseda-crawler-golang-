Prominent basketball coach Abbas Koochaki discussed his extensive career with national and club teams from 1979 to 2017, highlighting the 2003 Asian Championship victory. He played a pivotal role in developing Iran's wheelchair basketball program, ending the team's initial deprivation by securing equipment and training within four years. Through his presence at the 2014 World Cup in South Korea, Iran qualified for the top eight teams and secured an Olympic berth for the first time. As the wheelchair basketball development officer for Asia, Koochaki leveraged his position to facilitate Iran's participation in international competitions. He also voluntarily guided the national teams of Malaysia, Thailand, and Afghanistan, helping them achieve top Asian rankings despite challenging conditions. These efforts resulted in him receiving the National Hero award from Afghanistan and significantly boosting Iran's global reputation. Koochaki emphasized that holding international positions serves as a gateway for Iranian athletes to gain visibility and success in Europe and worldwide.
