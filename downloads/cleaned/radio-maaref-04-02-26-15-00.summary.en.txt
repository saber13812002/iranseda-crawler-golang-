In this radio program, an expert outlined the Islamic Republic's strategy of "conditional negotiation," emphasizing the necessity of pairing diplomacy with the strengthening of the resistance economy. The expert distinguished the resistance economy from austerity measures, defining it as a system based on domestic self-sufficiency and reduced external dependency. A key point is that negotiators must not focus solely on talks but should neutralize the enemy's primary weapon, sanctions, by leveraging domestic capacities and regional agreements like the Shanghai Cooperation Organization. The importance of full transparency in the negotiation process and informing the public to prevent a gap between the people and the system was also highlighted. In the second segment, a traditional medicine specialist discussed treatments for varicose veins and perioral bumps, stressing the importance of exercise and proper nutrition. He also advised on managing childhood anorexia by checking for anemia and infections, and using herbal remedies to reduce bile and stimulate appetite.
