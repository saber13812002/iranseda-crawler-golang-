The program begins by defining true manhood as trustworthiness, providing calm during crises, and exercising compassionate authority, citing the Prophet, Imam Hussein, and Allameh Tabatabaei as prime examples. The news segment highlights the inclusion of safety and crisis management in school curricula, financial support for students in need, and the introduction of indigenous educational robots. Listeners share messages defining real men and expressing gratitude to fathers, which forms the emotional core of the broadcast. Subsequently, Professor Mohammad Hadi Falahatian discusses the verbal sin of mockery and ridicule, identifying it as a vice rooted in the ego that hinders spiritual and social progress. Citing the Quran, he explains that mocking believers or using derogatory titles is forbidden, and those who persist in this behavior without repentance are considered oppressors. A narration from Imam Sajjad (AS) advises humility toward elders and acknowledging one's own faults over others to avoid the temptation of ridicule. Ultimately, the speaker concludes that abandoning this moral flaw leads to an easier life, more friends, and fewer enemies.
