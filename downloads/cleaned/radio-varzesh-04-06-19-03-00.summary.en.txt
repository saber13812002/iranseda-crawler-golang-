Ali Reza Boudvari, a veteran setter for the Iranian national volleyball team, discussed his grievances regarding personal decisions made by officials at Paykan club and the national team that caused significant gaps in his career. He expressed regret over the group decision to leave Foolad Sirjan for Shahrdari Urmia in 2020, labeling it a major mistake. Boudvari stated that he was excluded from the national team for eight to nine years during his peak physical condition, a period he refuses to forgive those responsible for. Despite being selected as the league's best setter multiple times, he missed the opportunity to win Asian or World championships during his prime years. Although he was eventually recalled to the national team by Hossein Madani, he continued to face systemic challenges but remained committed to playing for Iran. Boudvari, citing advice from veteran Yadollah Kargar, has set a goal to continue playing competitively until the age of 50, provided he remains useful to his team.
