This radio program begins by addressing Islamic rulings regarding the consumption of leftover food at event halls by workers and the obligation of Khums on personal assets. It then explores the status of the Quran as a healing guide and the ultimate criterion for distinguishing truth from falsehood, based on the teachings of Imam Ali in Nahj al-Balagha. The discussion emphasizes that Muslims must actively read, understand, and implement the Quran in their personal and social lives. Finally, the program concludes with a tafsir of Surah Yunus, highlighting the futility of faith adopted only in moments of distress and the impatience of disbelievers regarding divine punishment.
