This radio program commemorated the birth of Lady Fatima Masumeh (SA) by reporting on recitation ceremonies in Mashhad and the participation of over two thousand students in Qom. The head of the Hajj and Pilgrimage Organization announced that 15,000 Iranian pilgrims would be deployed to Mecca with free internet packages and Saudi airline services. Simultaneously, the first International Conference on Human Rights in the East was held in Qom, emphasizing the use of civilizational resources to advance human rights. The Khesrowshahr border in Qasr-e Shirin is also preparing its infrastructure to host approximately two million Arab pilgrims. In another segment, the children of martyrs were honored on Girl's Day, and the passing of a martyr's mother and two young martyrs was reported. The head of the Election Headquarters announced that the seventh round of council elections and mid-term parliamentary elections would be held on May 6, 2026. Finally, the program highlighted the enthusiastic participation of youth in the Imam Reza (SA) Media Festival, celebrating their significant contribution to cultural and artistic content production.
