The radio program began by celebrating the birth of Imam Mahdi (AJ) and highlighting the grand ceremonies held at Jamkaran Mosque.
Following a recitation and interpretation of verses from Surah An-Naml, the stories of Prophet Solomon and Job were shared as examples of divine mercy and servitude.
The core discussion focused on the linguistic and theological analysis of the word "Rabb" in "Rabb al-Alameen," interpreting it as the Nurturer and Manager of the universe based on scholarly views.
God's role as a Nurturer was examined on two levels: the cosmic level involving the essential and accidental movements of beings, and the legislative level involving the guidance of humans and jinn.
It was explained that cosmic laws, such as gravity and essential motion, are immutable and reflect God's absolute sovereignty over the system of creation.
The distinction was drawn between the mutable nature of material beings and the unchangeable divine laws that originate from God's will.
The discussion demonstrated that God is both the Creator of natural laws and the Manager of living beings' destinies through spiritual guidance.
The program concluded by emphasizing the unity of divine management and the importance of understanding these dimensions of Lordship to strengthen faith.
