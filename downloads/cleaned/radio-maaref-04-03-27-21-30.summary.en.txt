This program analyzes verses from Surah Al-Ma'idah, detailing the divine blessings granted to Jesus (PBUH) by God.
The mentioned blessings include support by the Holy Spirit, teaching of the Book and Wisdom, and miracles such as creating a bird from clay.
The interpretation clarifies that healing the blind and raising the dead were miracles performed by Jesus solely by God's permission.
The discussion also covers the plots of the Children of Israel against Jesus and God's response as the best of those who scheme.
God inspired the disciples to renew their faith and pledge to defend the religion against their enemies.
This renewal of the covenant indicates that early Christians were prepared to fight and protect their faith.
The program concludes by emphasizing that all miracles and resurrections were acts of God, not independent powers of the Prophet.
