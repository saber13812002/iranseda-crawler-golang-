In this program, Professor Jabbari first interprets the story of the Hajar al-Aswad, explaining how the Prophet (PBUH) resolved tribal disputes by placing the stone under his own trust, thereby affirming the Kaaba's divine status. He then discusses the pre-Islamic beliefs of the Arabs, noting that while they practiced idolatry, they still believed in the Creator's oneness and revered the House of God. Responding to listener questions, the expert distinguishes between three historical figures: Anas ibn Malik, Sinan ibn Anas (who severed Imam Hussein's head at Karbala), and Malik ibn Anas (a student of Imam Sadiq and founder of the Maliki school). The details of the Treaty of Hudaybiyyah and the Pact of the Tree are also examined, highlighting the Prophet's bravery in battle and his deliberate avoidance of direct killing to prevent lasting tribal enmity. In the linguistic section, the Arabic language of the Quran is traced back to Semitic roots, specifically Aramaic and Nabataean, while noting that only a few Persian words appear in the Quran. Finally, the professor emphasizes that although the four Imams of Sunni jurisprudence did not write specific martyrdom books, prominent Sunni scholars like Tabari and Baladhuri recorded extensive reports of the Karbala event in their historical works.
