This radio program highlights the plight of a mother and her four orphaned children of noble lineage who are facing eviction from their home.
The host appeals to listeners for financial support, invoking the intercession of Imam Reza and Lady Zahra to alleviate the family's suffering.
A guest speaker emphasizes the religious obligation of charity, citing hadiths that state humans will need their charitable deeds more than their accumulated wealth on Judgment Day.
He explains that giving to orphans not only increases one's blessings but also softens the heart and protects against hellfire.
The speaker advises that aid should empower the needy to become self-sufficient rather than creating a cycle of dependency.
In the latter part of the broadcast, the host addresses external military threats, arguing that intimidating Iranians with the prospect of war is a fundamental misunderstanding of their culture.
He asserts that Iranians view martyrdom as an honor, and maintaining faith and national unity is the true defense against enemies.
Listeners are encouraged to send text messages to specific numbers to donate funds for the family's relief in the hope of the Imam's reappearance.
