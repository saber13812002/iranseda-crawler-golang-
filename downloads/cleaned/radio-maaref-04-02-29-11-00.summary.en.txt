The President visited the families of the late President Raisi and his companions on the eve of the first anniversary of their martyrdom, emphasizing the importance of keeping their memory alive. The Foreign Minister stated at a memorial event that he would continue the diplomatic path of the late Amir-Abdollahian and acknowledged the heavy responsibility ahead. The Islamic Propaganda Organization announced commemorative programs, including ceremonies at the Fatima Masumeh Shrine and a walk in Tehran. Medical students at Shiraz University of Medical Sciences held an Islamic studies class at the Khan School to connect the youth with national and religious identity. The International Resistance Film Festival opened with screenings of films and animations related to the Sacred Defense. The rural and tribal insurance scheme for mothers with three or more children has been implemented, with 60,000 new mothers enrolled last year. Officials also highlighted Iran's small share in the global halal market and the efforts of the Shahid Mardani-Pour volunteer group in serving the needy.
