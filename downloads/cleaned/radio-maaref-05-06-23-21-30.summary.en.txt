Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verses 37 to 39 of Surah Al-Alaq, emphasizing the unified message of all prophets regarding monotheism and moral reform. He explains that mere faith or outward Islam is insufficient for salvation; a person must be both theologically pious and practically righteous. On the Day of Judgment, the universe will undergo severe upheaval, yet true believers will remain immune to all fear and sorrow. Conversely, those who deny the truth through arrogance or ignorance will be condemned to Hell for rejecting divine blessings and misleading others. In this fire, the fuel and kindling are the very oppressors and disbelievers who led people astray during their lifetimes. The followers will complain to God about their leaders, requesting that the punishment be doubled upon those who caused their misguidance. This process illustrates divine justice, where every individual is punished according to their actions and their specific role in leading others astray.
