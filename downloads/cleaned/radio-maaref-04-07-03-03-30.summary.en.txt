The radio program begins by clarifying the concept of asceticism through Nahj al-Balagha and the Quran, emphasizing that the pious enjoy lawful worldly benefits while securing their afterlife. The discussion then shifts to the serious jurisprudential issue of abortion, highlighting the religious prohibition, the severity of the sin, and the liability of doctors and counselors involved. Various religious rulings are addressed, including the determination that merely introducing a doctor for abortion without direct encouragement does not make one a partner in the sin, though it violates the duty to enjoin good and forbid evil. Other fatwas cover the validity of wudu with medical adhesives, the permissibility of grandparents gifting or bequeathing wealth to grandchildren, and the precautionary measures required when the specific object of a forgotten vow is unknown. The program concludes with a call to support the oppressed people of Gaza and a recommendation to recite the seventh prayer from the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya during quiet hours.
