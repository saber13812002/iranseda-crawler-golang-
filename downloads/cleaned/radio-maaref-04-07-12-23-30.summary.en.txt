The program highlights the distinction between hard and soft war, emphasizing the need for effective cultural activities to counter enemy infiltration. Ayatollah Al-Hashem, the Friday Prayer Leader of Tabriz, praised the city's people for their bravery and loyalty during critical historical periods. He stressed the importance of utilizing art and media to convey religious and revolutionary messages more effectively than mere sermons. The discussion also covered the critical role of the education system in nurturing vulnerable generations and addressing diverse societal segments. In the second part, the situation in Gaza and Israeli crimes were addressed, calling for Muslim awakening and unity under the banner of Islam. He identified Western cultural dominance in educational centers as the primary cause of Muslims losing their identity. Finally, the necessity of Muslim nations and governments awakening to counter superpower plots and preserve their religious identity was emphasized.
