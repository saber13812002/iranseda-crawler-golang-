A former Iranian handball coach recounts his experience as the manager of Afghanistan's national teams in 2022, prior to the Taliban's takeover. After forming a team of migrants in Iran, he traveled to Kabul to oversee all national squads, including men's, women's, and youth categories. Despite the players' high potential, official activities were halted due to political changes and the cancellation of the Asian Championship. He subsequently participated in the Islamic Solidarity Games in Turkey with a limited eight-player squad representing Afghanistan. The coach highlights the cultural challenges and the public's lack of recognition for handball as a serious, established sport. He shares anecdotes about family indifference and even refereeing misunderstandings during official ceremonies. Finally, he emphasizes his pure passion for the game and his hope to establish a specialized training base in Tehran to nurture future generations.
