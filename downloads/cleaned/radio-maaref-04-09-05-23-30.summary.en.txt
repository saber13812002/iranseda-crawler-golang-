In this radio broadcast, the host highlights the severe financial struggles of a widow named Masoumeh, who is raising two orphans, Amir Abbas and Zainab, in a small rented apartment. Listeners are urged to support this family by sending the number 8 to 30552, while wealthier donors are encouraged to cover their rent or fund a pilgrimage to Mashhad. The program emphasizes that such acts of charity are a way to honor Imam Reza and alleviate the suffering of the vulnerable. In the second segment, the discussion shifts to the social and political responsibilities of Shia Muslims regarding the preservation of Islam. The host critiques the passivity of many who claim allegiance to the Wilayah (divine authority) while remaining silent in the face of threats to the Islamic community. By referencing the historical actions of Imam Ali, the speaker argues that true loyalty requires active defense of Islamic interests rather than mere verbal claims.
