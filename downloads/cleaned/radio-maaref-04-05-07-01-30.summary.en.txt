This text is a religious speech that outlines five characteristics of the true fighters for God's path, based on verse 146 of Surah Al-Imran. These traits include devotion to God, resilience in the face of hardship, avoiding the display of weakness, refusing to surrender to enemies, and maintaining patience. The speaker emphasizes the mutual support between humans and God, citing the Supreme Leader's frequent reference to the verse "God will surely help those who help Him." Historical examples from the Iran-Iraq War, such as the sacrifices of martyrs' mothers, are presented as practical models of this resilience. The speech then details the Battle of Karbala, highlighting the bravery and steadfastness of Imam Hussein's companions like Ali Akbar, Hassan, Qasim, and Abbas. Despite immense suffering and the loss of loved ones, these figures fought with courage until their final moments. Finally, Zainab (SA) is highlighted as a model of patience and endurance against tyranny. The core message encourages the audience to follow the path of the martyrs and uphold the spirit of resistance.
