In this program, Ayatollah Hosseini Bushrari highlights Zaynab (SA) as the ultimate model of nursing and guardianship based on religious narrations. He emphasizes her critical role in caring for the sick and prisoners after the event of Karbala and her courageous speeches in preserving Imam Hussein's (AS) objectives. The main message urges modern nurses to emulate Zaynab's (SA) patience, hope-giving, and ethics to improve patients' mental well-being. The program then transitions to "The Tale of Freedom," featuring the memories of Hamidreza Jafari, a freedom fighter captured during the Muharram operation. He describes the enemy's attempts to send prisoners to Karbala for negative propaganda and the prisoners' resistance against this plot. Jafari recounts the night transfer of prisoners in curtained buses toward the Iraqi border. This narrative illustrates the spiritual resilience of the freedom fighters against psychological pressure and threats during their captivity.
