The program begins by interpreting Quranic verses, emphasizing the unity of glorification and praise in the divine essence.
The concept that "mercy precedes punishment" implies that even punishment and hell serve as tools for guidance and maintaining public order.
Prophets, described as mirrors of God, are complete messengers endowed with divine ethics.
In delivering their message, prophets initially use a gentle and emotional tone to soften people's hearts.
If faced with resistance or insults, the prophets' tone shifts to severity, focusing on refuting falsehood.
The principle that "the end does not justify the means" is evident in the Quran, stating that pure goals require pure methods.
Prophet Noah exemplifies humility and reliance on God, attributing all power and influence to the Divine rather than himself.
Finally, it is stressed that human guidance depends on their own will, with the prophet serving only as a guide.
