In this program, a parenting advisor recommends that a mother set clear and enforceable time limits for her teenager's video game usage. He emphasizes the importance of "firmness combined with kindness" in enforcing these rules, warning against aggression or physical punishment as ineffective methods. Using everyday examples, the advisor explains that parents must actively monitor and verify chores to teach children that enjoyment requires patience and responsibility. The second part of the program details the biography of Ayatollah Mohammad Momen Aamoz, recounting his exile in Shahdad and his revolutionary activities in the 1970s. It also describes his role in establishing the Revolutionary Courts and selecting qualified judges based on Ayatollah Mousavi's approval. Finally, his membership in the Guardian Council and the Supreme Leader's insistence on retaining him due to his justice and attention to detail are discussed. His academic works and teaching of Islamic jurisprudence are also highlighted as evidence of his intellectual depth and religious commitment.
