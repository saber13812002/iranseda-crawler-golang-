In this radio program, several religious questions were addressed. Based on Ayatollah Sistani's ruling, Hujjat al-Islam Mahdavi advised listeners to ignore obsessive thoughts regarding the impurity of objects like washing machine doors and to trust in their inherent purity. Regarding women leading prayers, Hujjat al-Islam Neshad cited Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi, stating that a woman can lead other women if she meets the necessary conditions such as maturity, intelligence, justice, and correct recitation. Additionally, Hujjat al-Islam Jawaheri, referencing Ayatollah Shabiri Zanjani, clarified that using a toothbrush does not invalidate fasting unless external moisture mixed with toothpaste or saliva is swallowed. The latter part of the broadcast was dedicated to the anniversary of the martyrdom of Imam Hasan Askari (AS) and the beginning of Muharram, featuring Quranic recitation and morning prayers. The program concluded by providing contact numbers for listeners to submit their own religious inquiries.
