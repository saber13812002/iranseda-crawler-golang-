In this radio program, Dr. Mohammadi explains the criteria for identifying the crime of Moharebeh based on Article 281 of the Islamic Penal Code. He emphasizes that merely displaying a weapon, even if empty, is sufficient to create fear and intimidation, regardless of the weapon's authenticity. A key point is that fear must be induced at the moment of the crime; thus, fear generated later by the distribution of crime footage does not constitute Moharebeh. The legal term "people" includes any individual, even a single person or a government official, and is not limited to a large crowd. The distinction between Moharebeh and personal crimes lies in the public nature of the act and the intent to undermine societal security, rather than personal motives. The program also briefly discussed water conservation tips, specifically regarding the efficient use of evaporative coolers. The broadcast concluded with the recitation and translation of verses from Surah Az-Zukhruf and the beginning of Surah Ad-Dukhan.
