This radio program distinguishes between true hope and false hope, defining the former by its alignment with reality and actionable effort. False hope is likened to the ethnocentric salvation narratives found in some religions, which lack a solid foundation and universal scope. In contrast, the hope for the appearance of Imam Mahdi (AJ) in Islam is rooted in authentic beliefs and aims for the salvation of humanity in both this world and the hereafter. This sincere hope possesses characteristics such as cohesion, stability, and the power to build civilization. Key benefits of this hope include increased resilience against hardships and a firm stance against corruption and support for the forces of evil. Furthermore, this belief addresses the modern crisis of meaning in life by providing a transcendent understanding of human purpose. Ultimately, this hope reduces future anxiety and boosts individual and social motivation, preparing society for the establishment of a global government based on justice.
