Former footballer Reza Kangarani recounts his professional career in this interview, highlighting his time with teams such as Farsh Shahid, Iran Javan Bushehr, and the Azerbaijan team in Tehran. He concluded his official playing career in 2003 after winning the Tehran First Division championship and securing promotion to the Second Division. Kangarani cites his strong sensitivity to unfairness and the psychological pressure of that era as the primary reasons for his retirement from football. After retiring, he joined the Tehran Municipality as a border expert while maintaining his physical fitness through consistent personal training. He believes that modern football has become poorer in terms of beauty and technique, becoming overly result-oriented. Kangarani expresses no regret about not moving abroad in his youth, emphasizing the importance of making conscious decisions. Finally, he shares his current interest in participating in bodybuilding and physical fitness competitions for the older age group.
