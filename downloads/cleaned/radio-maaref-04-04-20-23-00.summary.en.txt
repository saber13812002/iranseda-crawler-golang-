This radio segment began by thanking guest Dr. Amir Mohsen Erfan and previewing upcoming programs on Radio Ma'aref, including a show on the status of Jews during the occultation. The discussion then shifted to theological narratives, concluding that there is no reliable evidence supporting Jewish dominance during the era of the Mahdi's appearance. The program examined the concept of the "Jafar," a sacred book of knowledge, citing Al-Kulayni's hadiths to assert that this divine wisdom is exclusively reserved for the Infallible Imams. It was explained that the Imams' knowledge encompasses thousands of categories and general principles, though they may occasionally consult texts or the Holy Spirit for specific details. Furthermore, a hadith regarding the emergence of "Zanadiqa" in 128 AH was analyzed, identifying them as intellectually deviant groups like Ibn Mqaffa and followers of Greek philosophy. These individuals were noted to be at the peak of their rebellion and anti-Islamic propaganda during the time of Imam Sadiq.
