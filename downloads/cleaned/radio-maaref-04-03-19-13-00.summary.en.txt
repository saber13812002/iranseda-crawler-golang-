In this program, Hojjat al-Islam Dr. Naser Rafiei interprets verse 18 of Surah Al-Imran, known as the "Verse of Testimony." He highlights the repetition of the word for monotheism in the verse and identifies three divine witnesses to God's oneness: God Himself, the angels, and the scholars. Rafiei emphasizes the proof of monotheism and divine justice by citing the precise order of the universe and the human body. He strongly recommends reciting this verse after prayers and before sleep to strengthen faith and gain spiritual rewards. The speaker also underscores the high value of knowledge and attending educational gatherings, particularly those associated with the Ahl al-Bayt. He argues that a lack of religious practice often stems from weak beliefs, urging the cultivation of certainty and faith among the youth. Finally, as the occasion of Ghadir approaches, he stresses its importance and encourages seeking intercession through Imam Ali to resolve issues and gain salvation.
