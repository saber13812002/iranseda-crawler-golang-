In the "Zamzam Ahkam" program on Radio Ma'aref, Ayatollah Tabatabai detailed the rulings of Umrah al-Tamattu, emphasizing recommended acts such as visiting graves and praying in Masjid al-Haram until the eighth of Dhul-Hijjah. He outlined the thirteen obligatory acts of Hajj, ranging from entering Ihram and standing at Arafat to stoning the pillars and staying in Mina. In the Q&A segment, Ayatollah Soubhani clarified that buying gold online is invalid and the income haram if standard transaction conditions are not met. He also ruled that saying salam is obligatory in live voice conversations and explained the conditions under which a driver's prayer becomes shortened based on travel duration. Ayatollah Zanjani stated that debts owed to government agencies cannot be settled via "Radd al-Mazalim" and must be paid directly to the designated accounts. Finally, the program highlighted the spiritual significance of Imamzadehs as guiding stars, specifically praising Hazrat Zainab's (SA) role in countering the negative propaganda of the enemies of Islam.
