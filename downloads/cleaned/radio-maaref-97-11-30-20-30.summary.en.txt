The radio program "I am Fatima," featuring Hujjat al-Islam Hosseini Bagherabadi, explores the educational legacy of Hazrat Fatima Zahra (SA) in raising her children. The speaker emphasizes that Muslim mothers should utilize early childhood opportunities, such as lullabies, to impart religious and psychological teachings to their offspring. Hazrat Fatima (SA) provided a unique model of verbal education by composing verses that encouraged worshipping God and avoiding negative traits like resentment. A crucial aspect highlighted is the importance of practical education, as parents' behavior has a deeper impact on children than their words. By keeping her children awake during the nights of Qadr and Friday to witness her worship, Hazrat Fatima (SA) taught the concept of servitude through practical role modeling. This approach demonstrates that a mother's active and devotional presence in the home is the most effective tool for shaping children's religious character. Finally, the program underscores Hazrat Fatima's (SA) pivotal role in preserving Islamic teachings and raising the eleven Imams, affirming her status as the supreme educational role model.
