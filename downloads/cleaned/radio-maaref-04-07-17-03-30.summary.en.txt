This program examines the prelude to the Battle of Jamal and the movement of the Ahl al-Jamal towards Basra. It recounts a historical narrative where Aisha, upon hearing dogs barking in the Hawab region, recalled the Prophet's hadith about this place and requested to turn back. Despite her insistence, Talha and Zubayr swore false oaths and gathered witnesses to confirm it was not Hawab, forcing the group to continue their journey. Upon reaching Basra, the governor, Uthman ibn Hunayf, consulted with advisors who advised resistance, leading him to send envoys to negotiate with the rebels. The envoys attempted to persuade Aisha, Talha, and Zubayr, but their justifications and aggressive responses caused the negotiations to fail, resulting in the preparation for war. The second part of the program addressed various jurisprudential questions from listeners regarding fasting, wudu, the washing of the deceased, congregational prayer, and zakat.
