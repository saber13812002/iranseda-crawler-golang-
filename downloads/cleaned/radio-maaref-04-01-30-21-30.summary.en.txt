This program utilizes verses from Surah Ma'idah to reinterpret monotheism as an evolutionary journey of understanding human needs rather than a mere doctrinal belief. The speaker argues that while humans initially rely on God for material sustenance, spiritual growth reveals a deeper need for knowledge, justice, and the direct perception of the Divine Essence. A nuanced distinction is drawn between "Wahid" (One) and "Ahad" (Absolute One), with the latter representing God's unique, peerless nature, often correlated with the concept of "Al-Wahid Al-Qahhar" (The One, The Subduer). The discussion critically examines concepts like liberty, independence, and contentment, labeling them as political constructs used to manipulate or suppress the populace rather than genuine ethical virtues. Prophets are portrayed as the most courageous and knowledgeable figures who awakened human intuition, guiding people from reliance on ignorance and weakness to a true recognition of God. Ultimately, God is presented as "Al-Wahid Al-Qahhar," a singular entity that encompasses all existence, leaving no room for any partner or equal in the cosmic order.
