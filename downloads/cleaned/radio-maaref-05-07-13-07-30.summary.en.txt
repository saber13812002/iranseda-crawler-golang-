The program began by honoring the National Police on their day, acknowledging their extensive role in maintaining public security and stability. Following news reports about price stabilization of essential goods and free public transportation in Tehran, a humorous discussion on the economic and social situation ensued. The success of Iran's national team in the International Astronomy and Astrophysics Olympiad, which won five gold medals including one for a female participant, was also celebrated. The main segment focused on interpreting a narration from Imam Sadiq (PBUH) that outlined various causes of arrogance and hypocrisy. These factors included feigning repentance, showing off in worship, relying on public opinion, boasting about knowledge or lineage, and using prayers or charity to gain fame. The speaker emphasized that individuals should not use false humility or self-criticism as tools to attract attention from others. Finally, the audience was advised to place their trust solely in God and to avoid the deceptions of superficial behavior and hypocrisy in both worship and daily conduct.
