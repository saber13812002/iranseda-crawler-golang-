The IRGC announced that it targeted the Mossad intelligence center in Tel Aviv with ballistic missiles, stating that the facility is currently on fire. Simultaneously, Iran's air defense command reported the downing of 28 drones and cruise missiles in the country's airspace. The Iranian Foreign Ministry spokesperson welcomed a joint statement from Arab countries condemning Israel's aggression, labeling it a blatant violation of international law. Turkish President Erdogan and UAE President bin Zayed emphasized the need for diplomacy to end the conflict and called for global action against Israel. Political and media figures praised the courage of IRIB staff during the attack on their headquarters, viewing it as a symbol of the nation's resilience. International analysts expressed concern over Iran's missile capabilities and the failure of Israel's air defense systems. In Gaza, the Israeli army attacked civilians waiting for humanitarian aid, resulting in at least 50 deaths.
