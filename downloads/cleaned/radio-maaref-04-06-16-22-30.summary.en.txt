This radio program explored the ethical conduct of Prophet Muhammad and Ali ibn Abi Talib when facing their abusers, relying on historical narrations.
It highlighted the Prophet's forgiveness of a man who insulted him, citing three divine reasons for his mercy and restraint.
The story of Harith al-Amawi, who repented after the conquest of Mecca and was forgiven by the Prophet based on a Quranic verse about Joseph, was also discussed.
The Prophet's social behaviors, such as greeting children, shortening prayers to soothe a crying infant, and consistently checking on his companions, were presented as social models.
It was emphasized that the response to a greeting must be identical for both the poor and the rich, as differentiating based on financial status contradicts Islamic principles.
The program concluded with prayers for fertility and health, followed by the recitation of verses from Surah al-Mumtahanah and praises of the Prophet.
