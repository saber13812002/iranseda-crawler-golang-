In the "Persaman Enghelab" program, Dr. Mehdi Abutalibi argues that after decades of failed military and economic strategies, Western powers and Israel now rely solely on internal collapse and public protests to undermine the Islamic Republic. He emphasizes that the recent twelve-day war, despite causing damage, ultimately strengthened Iran's military and social standing on the global stage rather than breaking its backbone. From an international relations perspective, Iran, Russia, and China are engaged in strategic cooperation driven by their shared opposition to US dominance and mutual national interests. This alliance is based on pragmatic national benefits rather than permanent friendship, serving as a tool to counter Western influence in the region. The program also featured a book review of "Re-recognizing the System of Hegemony," which analyzes the structure of the international system and the Islamic Revolution's resistance against it. Finally, the expert stressed that Iran's resilience has been proven, highlighting the importance of national unity and reliance on domestic capabilities to face future threats.
