Seyyed Hassan Hosseini, a veteran and international handball referee, discussed the structural challenges facing handball in Asia and Iran during this interview. He highlighted the issues with the Kuwait Handball Federation, which was suspended due to legal violations, and noted Qatar's strategy of recruiting foreign players to secure Asian championships. Hosseini shared anecdotes about his efforts to maintain fairness in officiating and his resistance to illegal pressures aimed at manipulating match results. Reflecting on his student days and entry into physical education, he emphasized that sports have been an inseparable part of his life. The veteran also pointed out the weak presence of Tehran teams in the top handball league, contrasting it with the financial focus on Isfahan clubs like Foolad Mobarakeh. At 72 years old, Hosseini expressed his continued passion for sports and mentioned that he has always corrected minor officiating errors immediately. He concluded by hoping that increased attention from the federation to specific details would help Iranian handball achieve a better standing in Asia.
