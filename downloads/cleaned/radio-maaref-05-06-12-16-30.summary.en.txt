In this radio program, the expert emphasized that deterrence is not limited to nuclear weapons but also relies on strong national will, effective internal management, and appropriate responses to threats. Citing Russia's experience in the Ukraine war, he argued that proper management of banks, exchange rates, and de-dollarization can prevent economic weakening under sanctions. The repeated claims by domestic officials regarding a return to the JCPOA were described as a weakness in political rhetoric that ignores America's consistent bad faith. The second part of the discussion analyzed the outcomes of the President's trip to Kyrgyzstan and the Shanghai Cooperation Organization summit, viewing Iran's presence as a break from isolation and a chance to develop land transit corridors. The expert stressed the importance of maintaining a balance in relations with India and Pakistan to create a power equilibrium in the region. Finally, support for the Mecca Agreement was deemed justifiable only if its goal is to reduce regional countries' dependence on the United States rather than posing a threat to Iran.
