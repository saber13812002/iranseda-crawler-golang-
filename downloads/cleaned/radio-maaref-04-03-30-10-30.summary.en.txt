The radio program "Until the Friday of Appearance" began by analyzing the civilizational dimensions of the Zionist occupation of Palestine.
The guest, Dr. Erfan, argued that Zionism justifies the occupation of Palestinian land as a prerequisite for the appearance of the Messiah or a savior, relying on false eschatological narratives.
He highlighted the role of Hollywood in Judaizing the concept of salvation and creating a link between Jewish Zionism and Evangelical Christianity.
Concepts such as "returnism" and "seeking blessings from Israel" were introduced as ideological tools to falsely legitimize the presence of Jews in Palestine.
The second guest, Dr. Javad Jafari, congratulated the victories of the Islamic fighters and addressed religious narrations regarding the cultivation of hope and anticipation among Shia.
Citing authentic hadiths from Imam Kazim (AS), he emphasized that hope for the future and waiting for the reappearance are the main pillars of Shia identity and patience throughout history.
The program concluded that a deep understanding of the enemy's eschatological plots, combined with loyalty to the leadership and maintaining hope for the appearance, is the key to the ultimate victory of Islam.
