In this radio broadcast, Amnesty International condemned recent US sanctions against the UN special rapporteur on Palestine. The Ansar Allah movement in Yemen reported a successful missile strike on Ben Gurion Airport and the destruction of two major commercial ships in the region. Iran's Foreign Minister reaffirmed the country's unwavering support for Palestine and the oppressed, drawing parallels to the Srebrenica genocide. Additionally, a controversial visit by a group of European imams to the Israeli regime was widely condemned by Islamic scholars and activists. The program also highlighted the violation of the Geneva Conventions, specifically condemning Israeli drone attacks on Iranian Red Crescent ambulances as war crimes. The broadcast concluded with an invitation for students to participate in the "Servants of Hussein" campaign and the recitation of Dua Kumayl during the nights of Muharram.
