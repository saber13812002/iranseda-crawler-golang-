Sayed Mehdi Sayed Sadri, a world veteran wrestling champion, shares his memories of international competitions and the challenges of refereeing in this interview. He recounts his dramatic victory in Russia, where he won a world gold medal despite a severe leg injury and protests from his opponents. Sadri emphasizes that he has never felt despair during his sports career, believing that life's hardships bring one closer to God and service to humanity. He asserts that the primary goal of life is to be a good human being, pray on time, and help those in need, rather than pursuing wealth or status. The athlete believes that all possessions are God's trust and should be used for charity and alleviating others' problems. By sharing a story about financially helping a needy driver, he highlights the importance of being attentive to one's surroundings and responding to God's call in difficult moments. Finally, Sadri thanks God for his guidance and hopes that his model of service will inspire the younger generation.
