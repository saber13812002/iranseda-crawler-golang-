This program explores the virtues of character from the perspective of Imam Sadiq, focusing on the 20th supplication of the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya.
Ethical issues are categorized into four domains: the relationship with God, oneself, the world, and others, with a primary emphasis on social ethics.
The Imam emphasizes bringing good to people but warns against spoiling this benevolence by keeping score or showing off.
He advises responding to ignorance-driven hostility with love and forgiveness, and actively restoring severed social and familial bonds.
The Imam's practical example of forgiving a person who insulted him is presented as a model of divine pardon and patience.
Conversely, he prays for a strong hand and a powerful tongue to confront oppressors and ensure justice for the oppressed.
This dual approach highlights the balance between tolerance toward fellow humans and firmness against tyranny in Shia thought.
