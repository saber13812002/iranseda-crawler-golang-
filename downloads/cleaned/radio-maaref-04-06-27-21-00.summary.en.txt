In the radio program Ma'aref, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the importance of avoiding overeating, oversleeping, and excessive talking to maintain physical and spiritual health, citing religious narrations. He illustrated the correct Islamic lifestyle by contrasting the simple dining habits of Imam Khomeini and Imam Musa Sadr with the lavish meals of the Pope. The second segment continued the narrative from the book "Ocean of the East," where Hajj Karim Pin Doz discusses the Imamate of Ali ibn Musa al-Rida with Amran. This dialogue highlights the necessity of recognizing God's proof for true faith, referencing authentic hadiths and Quranic verses regarding the Imam's miraculous knowledge of his enemy's intentions. The program concluded with a discussion on the fiqh rulings concerning the khums of retirement funds and charitable campaigns aimed at freeing debtors from prison.
