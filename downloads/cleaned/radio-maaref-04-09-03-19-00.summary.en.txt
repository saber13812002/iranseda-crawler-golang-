In this radio program, the status of Fatima Zahra (SA) is presented as extending beyond defending the Imamate to preserving the core of Prophethood and the religion from distortion.
Her most significant action was opening the door to jurisprudence and using Quranic arguments in the Fadak sermon to prevent lies about the Prophet (PBUH) and the alteration of laws.
Her uprising, by recording events in history and shaping public opinion, ensured the survival of the Ahl al-Bayt lineage and prevented the Islamic community from ideological deviation.
Imam Ali (AS) also defended this historical and spiritual legacy by drawing his sword against those attempting to uncover Fatima's (SA) grave.
The second part of the discussion emphasizes the importance of Taqiyya (dissimulation) as the ninth-tenth of religion and a means to preserve the lives of believers during times of peace with opponents.
Imam Sadiq (AS) and Imam Baqir (AS) described Taqiyya in the Book of Kafi as the most beloved action under severe security conditions.
Observing Taqiyya during the period of occultation is considered a sign of faith and preservation of religion, while abandoning it is deemed a lack of faith.
These discussions demonstrate that both the historical uprisings of the Ahl al-Bayt and jurisprudential principles like Taqiyya have been vital tools for the survival of Islam against distortion and threats.
