The "Jeshfara" exhibition, featuring avant-garde fashion inspired by Iranian civilization, is open at the Asman Cultural Center in Tehran until May 12. Concurrently, the fourth International Conference on the Theology of Arbaeen was held at Qom University, analyzing the spiritual and social dimensions of the pilgrimage through 99 accepted papers. In academic circles, the International Conference on the Quranic Thoughts of the Supreme Leader has received over 3,000 papers and held eight sessions across various provinces. A new textbook on Quranic Sciences was also unveiled as a mandatory course for first-level seminary students nationwide. Regarding economic support, the head of the Parliament's Family Support Commission stated that 84% of marriage and childbirth loan applicants have successfully received their funds. Despite a 63% increase in loan disbursements this year, 16% of applicants remain in the waiting queue. Finally, the deadline for master's degree program selection has been extended to May 10, with 162,000 candidates having already registered.
