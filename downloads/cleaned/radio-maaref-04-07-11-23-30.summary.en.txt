The program begins with a philosophical discussion on the nature of true knowledge versus rational concepts of non-existent entities.
It emphasizes that while the intellect can construct various concepts, these do not necessarily reflect ontological reality.
The broadcast then transitions to introducing various segments of Radio Ma'aref, including cinema reviews and a specialized session for Quran reciters.
A religious section features the Ziyarat Al-Yasin recited by a mourid, highlighting the importance of allegiance to the Ahl al-Bayt.
Finally, a speech is aired that stresses the unity between the clergy, armed forces, and the people as the key to Iran's victory.
The speaker describes this solidarity as a divine blessing and the primary cause of fear among enemies.
The program concludes by referencing God's promise to increase blessings for those who show gratitude and follow the path of guidance.
