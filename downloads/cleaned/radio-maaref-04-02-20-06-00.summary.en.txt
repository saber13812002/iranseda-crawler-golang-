This program interprets verses 97 and 98 of Surah Al-Ma'idah, emphasizing the dual-sided security established by religion during Hajj and pilgrimage.
The Quran instructs pilgrims to refrain from any harm while simultaneously commanding the general public to show respect and non-interference toward the visitors of the House of God.
This approach views security not merely as a passive state but as a social and spiritual training process for all individuals.
The discussion then explores the relationship between faith and the economy as the two main pillars of societal stability.
Although the economy is vital for survival, faith and the connection to the Kaaba are considered the primary foundation, with the economy being secondary.
God guarantees that economic pressures cannot weaken the faith of believers, as He holds the keys to the treasures of the heavens and the earth.
The Kaaba, as the axis of Muslim life and death, symbolizes a divine security and identity that must be respected in all circumstances.
Ultimately, Hajj is a process for breaking human egoism and training individuals toward servitude and mutual respect.
