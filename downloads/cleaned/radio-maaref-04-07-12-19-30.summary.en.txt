In this radio program, Professor Mohammad Hadi Falan emphasized the necessity of balancing night worship with daytime charity, citing Quranic verses and Hadiths. He warned that using abusive language leads to the rejection of prayers and the loss of the companionship of the Ahl al-Bayt, as highlighted in a narration from the Prophet. The professor refuted common justifications that misinterpret the stern statements of the Infallibles during Karbala and Nahj al-Balagha as insults, clarifying that these were not vulgarities. He noted that despite the extreme hardships faced by the Ahl al-Bayt, they never resorted to foul language, unlike their enemies who frequently used abusive terms. Therefore, Shias should not use vulgar or offensive language in the name of defending their faith, as it contradicts their high spiritual status. The discussion concludes that abstaining from abuse is a sign of moral elevation and is essential for maintaining a spiritual connection with the Ahl al-Bayt.
