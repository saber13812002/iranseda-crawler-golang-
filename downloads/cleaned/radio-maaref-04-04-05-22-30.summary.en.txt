In this radio program, listeners are urged to maintain piety and avoid the trials and doubts created by enemies on the path to the Mahdi's reappearance.
The primary duty of Muslims is to hold firmly to the rope of Allah, defined as the Quran and the Imam, while strictly avoiding division to preserve the unity of the Ummah.
The historical transformation of tribal enmity into genuine brotherhood under the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) is cited as a divine blessing that must be preserved.
The program warns that enemies, including certain groups of People of the Book and their supporters, constantly seek to mislead people from the truth and create ambiguity in public opinion.
Concluding with prayers for the Mahdi's reappearance and gratitude for divine support, the broadcast transitions to the first night of Muharram.
Listeners are invited to observe mourning rituals, follow the Husaini conduct, and recite specific supplications recommended by Imam Reza (AS) for this holy month.
