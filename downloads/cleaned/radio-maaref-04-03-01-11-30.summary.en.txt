The radio program began by detailing the rulings for stoning the Jamarat on the 11th and 12th of Dhu al-Hijjah, emphasizing the necessity of leaving Mina only after the Zuhr prayer.
It was highlighted that pilgrims must possess adequate physical stamina for the rituals and strictly follow the caravan leaders' instructions during peak congestion times.
The Q&A section addressed various jurisprudential issues, including the permissibility of playing with non-pubescent children, the status of the Zuhr prayer after Jumu'ah, and hygiene rules regarding diapers.
The use of cologne while fasting and wearing clothes made from camel hair were both deemed permissible according to the cited fatwas.
Regarding taking permission from a host, it was clarified that implicit consent is sufficient for minor items like cookies, provided no specific restriction exists.
The program transitioned into a special segment titled "In Hope of Meeting," featuring Quran recitation coinciding with the Zuhr adhan in Mecca.
Finally, the etiquette and recommended practices of prayer, including pre- and post-prayer supplications and the Tasbihat of Zahra (SA), were explained by a seminary professor.
