The radio program "I am Fatima" examined parts of the Fatimiyya sermon to highlight the virtues of Hazrat Fatima Zahra (SA) and her defense of Ali's (AS) authority.
The host explained that the Prophet's (PBUH) public introduction of Fatima (SA) serves as the basis for her self-identification in the sermon.
This self-introduction was a subtle yet powerful rebuke to the people of Medina for violating her sanctity and breaking the Prophet's heart.
Hojjat al-Islam Hosseini Bagherabadi emphasized that Fatima's (SA) praise of Ali's (AS) bravery and devotion to the faith proves his right to succession and Imamate.
He pointed out that the people's neglect of the Ghadir Khumm appointment and their selection of a caliph without Ali's (AS) presence constituted a major injustice.
Furthermore, the program cited Imam Mahdi's (AJ) statement about following his mother to establish Fatima (SA) as a universal role model for all humanity.
The broadcast concluded with excerpts from Martyr Baha'udi's speeches on the necessity of patience, unity, and viewing the revolution's progress as a mountain climb with inevitable ups and downs.
