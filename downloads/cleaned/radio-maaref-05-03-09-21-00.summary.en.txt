In the radio program "Rahbord," the Supreme Leader's message for the anniversary of the Islamic Consultative Assembly's founding was analyzed across seven key axes. A central theme was the warning against the enemy's plot to create social division following military defeats, emphasizing the critical need to preserve national unity. Dr. Hamid Souri utilized Noam Chomsky's theories and media framing concepts to demonstrate how Western media distorts reality to portray Iran as a threat and limit its influence. The ethical dimension highlighted piety as a guarantee against corruption and the necessity for representatives to prioritize national interests over factional ones. The discussion also noted the continuity of the current Leader's discourse with the late Leader, utilizing shared value-laden terminology. Ultimately, the message was interpreted as an operational guide for the public and parliament, defining the Iranian nation as a "missionary community." The final goal is to navigate crises through hope-building, avoiding trivial political disputes, and fostering collaborative governance.
