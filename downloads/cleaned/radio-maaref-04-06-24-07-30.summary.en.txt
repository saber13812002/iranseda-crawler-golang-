This radio program honored prominent martyrs from Isfahan, such as Shahid Mostafa Radanipour, highlighting their significant impact during the Holy Defense and the 12-day operation.
The main discussion focused on practical teachings regarding proper supplication and gratitude during the morning hours.
The guest advised listeners to recall God's previous blessings before making requests, noting that morning prayers help alleviate anxiety and promote spiritual peace.
Scientific evidence was also cited to support the positive effects of morning gratitude on reducing stress and improving social relationships.
In the second segment, the 21st prayer from the chapter on sustenance in the book Mafatih al-Jazaer was recited to seek blessings for livelihood, particularly for the people of Yemen.
The host also promised to visit the Isfahan Martyrs' Cemetery the next day to pray for the listeners at the shrine of Shahid Radanipour.
The program concluded with an inspirational song and prayers for the reappearance of the Imam and ultimate victory.
