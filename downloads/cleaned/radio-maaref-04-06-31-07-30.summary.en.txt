The radio program "Ma'rif" began by discussing the concept of courage, featuring listener messages about their most brave life experiences.
The show honored the martyrs of the Sacred Defense, specifically highlighting the spiritual power of "Allahu Akbar" in achieving victory.
Expert Hujjat al-Islam Falahati then addressed the linguistic vice of cursing and verbal abuse.
He defined cursing as a polytheistic act, a sign of hypocrisy and ignorance, and a violation of divine commands.
Based on Quranic verses and narrations, cursing is rooted in following Satan's path and distances individuals from God's guardianship.
Imam Sadiq (AS) identified cursing as one of the primary signs of Satan's partnership in a person's life.
Finally, the program recommended avoiding verbal abuse and maintaining linguistic etiquette to preserve faith and avoid Satan's influence.
