In this radio program, experts in international law and jurisprudence condemned recent attacks by the Zionist regime and the United States on Iran as a blatant violation of the UN Charter and the fundamental principle of the prohibition of the use of force.
They emphasized that the international community, particularly major powers, has failed its commitment to justice and equality before international law by practicing discrimination and supporting Israel.
It was noted that attacking nuclear facilities is illegal and condemnable under the clause prohibiting "any other method inconsistent with the purposes of the United Nations," even if it does not involve direct territorial invasion.
Citing the Holy Quran, the experts identified state self-interest and selfishness as being in direct conflict with the principles of peace and goodness, labeling them as the root of current injustices.
Regarding enforcement mechanisms, the weakness of the UN Security Council due to the veto power of its five permanent members and US dominance was identified as the primary obstacle to international justice.
It was suggested that states utilize the UN General Assembly and permissible non-military pressure tools to condemn aggressors, though these instruments are currently viewed as weak and insufficient.
The final conclusion was that without sufficient military, economic, and political power, adhering to international rules against aggressive powers is difficult, and ignoring these principles places the global order on the brink of collapse.
