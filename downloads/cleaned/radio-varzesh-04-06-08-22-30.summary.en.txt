Colonel Dr. Chingiz Mesibzadeh, the manager of the Army Ground Forces sports club and a former national shooting team member, discusses his return to competition after years of absence. He highlights the club's recent success in winning the league championship and securing silver and bronze medals at the Asian Games in Thailand despite limited training time. Mesibzadeh emphasizes the crucial role of the Ground Forces' command in providing equipment and facilitating athletes' participation. He also speaks about the club's efforts to eliminate nepotism by offering free and transparent testing opportunities for young athletes. His ultimate goal is to make a positive impact in sports management and nurture a new generation of champions. Mesibzadeh believes that athletes should be selected for national teams based solely on their talent and hard work. He expresses gratitude for the unwavering support of the Army commanders in developing sports for both men and women. Finally, he underscores the necessity of daily improvements to ensure the growth and success of athletes within the club.
