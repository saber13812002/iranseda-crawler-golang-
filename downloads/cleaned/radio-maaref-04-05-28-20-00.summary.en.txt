The Israeli regime's attacks on the Gaza Strip continue, resulting in Palestinian casualties and the widespread destruction of residential buildings. Meanwhile, the United States and Iraq have reached an agreement to alter the nature of Washington's military presence in Baghdad after two years of negotiations. Analysts view this deal not as a true end to occupation, but as a tactical shift designed to maintain American influence in the region. Najaf is currently preparing to host millions of pilgrims for the annual commemoration of the Prophet Muhammad's passing. In Taiwan, the broadcasting of the Adhan at the capital's metro station has drawn attention as a symbol of peaceful coexistence and religious freedom for the Muslim minority. The Islamic Art Museum in Cairo has also put on display ancient pages of the Holy Quran, some dating back more than eight hundred years. Russia is aggressively expanding its exports to Islamic markets by focusing on obtaining Halal certifications for its products. In Turkey, a new wave of arrests of mayors from the opposition party on financial corruption charges has sparked strong reactions and accusations that the cases are politically motivated.
