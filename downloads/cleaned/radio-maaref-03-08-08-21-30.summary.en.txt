In this Radio Ma'aref program, Professor Javadi Amoli interprets verses 141 to 143 of Surah An-Nisa. The central theme is the Quranic concept of "deception" and "stratagem," illustrating that hypocrites, by deceiving the religion and the Prophet, are actually deceiving themselves and falling into divine punishment. The professor explains that divine misguidance is not an existential gift but a punitive consequence for those who voluntarily deviate from the path of guidance. Reference is made to Surah Al-Hadid, which describes a wall separating believers and hypocrites on the Day of Judgment, revealing their hidden truths. The sermon also highlights the importance of prayer as a means of connection with God, noting its difficulty for hypocrites due to their lack of genuine faith and their practice of show. The interpretation of the verse "they do not remember Allah except a little" indicates that the remembrance of hypocrites is ineffective because it lacks sincerity and piety. Finally, the concept of "wavering" is explained as a sign of hidden disbelief and ultimate damnation in Hellfire.
