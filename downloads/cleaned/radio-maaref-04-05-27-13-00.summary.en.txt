In this program, Hojjat al-Islam Heydari Kashani emphasizes "divine perseverance" as the most crucial lesson from the conduct of prophets and Imams. He describes perseverance as the head of faith, positioning it against Satan's whispers that urge haste and ingratitude. By citing the stories of Prophet Noah and Prophet Abraham, the speaker illustrates that steadfastness in the face of hardship and absolute reliance on God are the keys to divine miracles and salvation. Referring to the promises of Imam Khomeini and the Supreme Leader, he identifies the elimination of Israel as the direct result of faith-based perseverance and jihad in the path of truth. The resistance of the people of Gaza and Palestine is presented as a living example of this Hussaini and Karbala-style perseverance. Ultimately, the final victory of the Islamic Ummah and the appearance of Imam Mahdi (aj) depend on maintaining this resilience and hoping for divine support.
