This radio program began by commemorating the 46th anniversary of the Holy Defense Week and honoring the late Ayatollah Shabestari Zanjan. The main segment featured an interview with Hossein Khorshidi, a proud freedman, who recounted his captivity experiences starting in 1985. He described the harsh prison conditions, physical and psychological torture, and the neglect of medical needs for himself and his fellow prisoners. Khorshidi also shared his most bitter memory, the news of Imam Khomeini's passing while he was in captivity, and its profound impact on the prisoners' morale. The program then introduced the book "Red Squares," which narrates the memories of martyr Hossein Yekta and his battalion, recommending it to the audience. The broadcast concluded by emphasizing the importance of emulating the patience and resilience of the freedmen and inviting listeners to share their feedback.
