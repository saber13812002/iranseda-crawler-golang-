This radio program addressed several religious questions with answers from Islamic authorities. First, doctors must obtain a liability waiver from patients before surgery, and relying solely on the probability of recovery without full disclosure is impermissible. Second, buying and playing the piano is forbidden if it distracts one from God and religious duties, though calm and permissible use is allowed. Third, attending music classes that are not aligned with the system's goals or under official supervision is considered impermissible by Ayatollah Khamenei. Fourth, a woman wearing her husband's clothes for prayer is permissible as long as complete modesty is maintained. Fifth, the expiation for missed fasts by pregnant or nursing women is their personal responsibility and must be paid from their estate if they pass away. Sixth, taking loans from benevolent loan banks is not considered usury if the fees are less than operational costs or compensate for high inflation. Finally, touching a plate or writing with the names of the Infallibles while in a state of ritual impurity is forbidden, whereas common names are not.
