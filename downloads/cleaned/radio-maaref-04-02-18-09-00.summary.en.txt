He also criticized certain Islamic countries for economically supporting the Zionist regime, thereby indirectly enabling its crimes against the oppressed. The second segment featured a documentary addressing the physical and psychological dangers of illegal and self-administered abortions. Medical and psychological experts detailed the life-threatening risks of using abortion drugs without medical supervision, as well as the severe mental health crises that often follow such decisions.
