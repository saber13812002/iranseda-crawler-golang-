In the first segment, Hojjat al-Islam Mohammadi clarified the prayer rules for travelers based on Imam Khomeini's fatwa, stating that only those whose primary occupation is travel must perform full prayers.
Hojjat al-Islam Alian detailed the correct etiquette of prostration, emphasizing the recitation of the Istighfar between the two prostrations and proper hand placement.
Other questions regarding vows, fasting for the sick, and calculating khums on agricultural products were addressed with specific rulings for the followers.
The second segment focused on the topic of lying, with Professor Fala reviewing causes such as ignorance and spiritual degradation.
Citing Imam Ali's narrations, he described listening to and spreading lies as a gateway to hell that weakens intellect and strengthens ignorance.
The professor stressed the importance of avoiding the company of liars and not accepting news hastily, advising listeners to choose their companions before choosing their path.
The program concluded with the prayer for the relief of the Imam and condolences for the martyrdom anniversary of Imam Reza (AS).
