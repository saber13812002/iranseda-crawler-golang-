The program "Porsman Quran" began by discussing the signs of the Mahdi's appearance and the characteristics of the minor Day of Judgment, such as the difficulty of practicing faith and the revelation of new levels of knowledge. It then analyzed verses 62 and 63 of Surah Qaf, arguing that those who reject the truth despite knowing it are driven away from the right path by stubbornness and a lack of reflection. The discussion subsequently shifted to the concept of justice, drawing from Allameh Javadi Amoli's book "Mafatih al-Hayat," emphasizing that justice is a fundamental pillar of faith and social well-being, not just a legal obligation. A narration from Imam Ali (AS) was cited to highlight the importance of maintaining fairness even in minor, everyday judgments, such as comparing children's handwriting. The speakers also addressed the prevalence of injustice in social media and personal interactions, attributing it to a lack of integrity and respect for truth. Finally, the program honored the Sacred Defense week, expressing gratitude to the martyrs and veterans whose sacrifices secured the nation's current dignity and life.
