The radio program "Ma'aref" began by emphasizing the importance of rational energy consumption before transitioning to a discussion on Mahdism. The expert clarified that genuine love for Imam Mahdi (AJ) must be accompanied by the fulfillment of religious obligations and behavioral reform, as his guardianship encompasses all humanity. He cited a narration regarding houses in Paradise and Hell to warn that neglecting religious duties can lead to the loss of both spiritual and worldly opportunities. The discussion then addressed the role of scholars during the Imam's reappearance, noting that scholars of false sects will be among the first to oppose him due to their inherent conflict with the truth. Finally, the program analyzed recent developments in Syria, stressing the need to correctly understand these events and map the region's future based on the resistance strategies of the Islamic Republic.
