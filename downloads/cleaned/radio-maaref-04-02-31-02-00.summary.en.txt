In this radio program, the expert clarifies that an illicit relationship or temporary marriage with a person who already has a spouse is not considered "friendship" but rather a form of marriage that is religiously forbidden and sinful. Furthermore, keeping dogs inside the home was condemned from a religious and ethical perspective, as it is believed to drive angels away, render the house impure, and constitute an imitation of non-Muslims contrary to authentic Iranian and Islamic culture. In response to listener questions, it was stated that the exact time of the Day of Judgment is hidden even from the Imam of the Time, and the Prophet Muhammad's rank is supreme in the entire creation, transcending time and space.
