A report from the Khwarizmi martyrdom book regarding Ali Akbar's death and his horse was also verified. The program then covered news about the farewell ceremony for the Supreme Leader, noting the presence of foreign delegations in Tehran and specific restrictions at the Razavi shrine. The final part introduced the legal program "Voice of Law" and aired a dramatic segment based on a real case. This segment highlighted the dangers of seclusion between unrelated men and women and the importance of adhering to Islamic family boundaries.
