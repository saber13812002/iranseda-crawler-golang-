Hojjat al-Islam Kazrouni emphasizes the importance of seeking refuge in the Eighth Imam during times of hardship, citing narrations and real-life stories. He highlights the Imam's title "Kafi lil-Khalq" and his advice to seek help in times of calamity, explaining the spiritual power of visiting the shrine in resolving life's knots. A documented anecdote from Sheikh Ibrahim Ansari is shared, where soil from the Imam's shrine served as a spiritual bridge, leading to the successful marriage of his son to a girl who had written a letter to the Imam. This story demonstrates the profound impact of sincere devotion on changing destinies and connecting hearts. The program then transitions to a literary segment narrating the book "The Galaxy is Not," which reconstructs the life of Ayatollah Seyyed Ali Ghazi Tabatabai. In this narrative, Seyyed Ali and his wife, Rakhshand Sادات, live in Najaf, continuing their studies and visits despite financial hardships. The news of their teacher, Mulla Hossein Qoli Hamedani, passing away brings deep sorrow, reminding them of death and spirituality. Amidst his scholarly duties and jurisprudential debates, Seyyed Ali remains committed to his spiritual path, supported by his wife's loyalty to the simple life of a student.
