The radio program begins with the recitation of the fourteenth supplication from the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya by Hajj Mahmoud Karimi, which focuses on asking God to remove oppression and injustice. Following the recitation, the host discusses the virtue of patience as a fundamental pillar of faith and a sign of piety. Citing the words of Imam Ali, the host describes patience as the first pillar of the house of faith and believes that enduring hardships with patience invites divine assistance. The host then prays for the improvement of conditions in Islamic countries, particularly Palestine, Syria, Lebanon, and Sudan, hoping for peace and stability across the world. The program concludes with an invitation for listeners to join the "Treasure of Happiness" segment and an expression of gratitude for the blessings of Islam and faith. This religious and supplicatory broadcast aired during the final hours of Friday.
