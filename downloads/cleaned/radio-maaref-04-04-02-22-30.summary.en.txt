The core argument of this program distinguishes between a war of resources and a strategic war of intellect, rooted in Quranic verses.
According to the interpretation presented, Iran's victory over its enemies stems not from military superiority, but from possessing deep understanding and proper mental management.
Conversely, despite having abundant resources, the enemies are declining due to a lack of this deep insight and strategic analytical weakness.
Comparing the current situation with the Holy Defense era reveals that Iran now enjoys national cohesion, deterrent power, and clarity in identifying the aggressor.
This national unity is not a new phenomenon but rather the manifestation of Iran's inherent and permanent characteristics during crises.
Ultimately, based on religious narrations, these conflicts are interpreted as an opportunity to eliminate tyrants and purify the earth from corruption.
The discussion concludes that Iran, relying on faith and strategic intelligence, stands on the verge of a definitive victory over humanity's enemies.
