In this radio program, the host first emphasized the resilience of the Iranian people and the enduring memory of martyrs following recent conflicts.
The guest cited the historical speech of Zainab (SA) to Yazid to demonstrate that no military power or enemy can break the iron will of the nation.
The discussion then shifted to the virtue of charity (infak) and the condemnation of stinginess, labeling the latter as a delusion based on Quranic verses.
It was explained that God replaces the stingy with those who are charitable, and that charity elevates a person to the status of God's beloved and vicegerent.
Finally, the program stressed the importance of adorning oneself with the virtue of charity and avoiding stinginess to achieve eternal happiness and spiritual perfection.
