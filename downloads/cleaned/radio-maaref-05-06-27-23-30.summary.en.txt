The text is a transcript of a diverse radio program that begins with the dramatic performance "Kashtiban." In this play, characters named Kanan and an old man are trapped in a deep well, struggling with fear and the threat of falling. The old man encourages Kanan to rely on his willpower and courage to climb out of the well safely. Following the performance, the program invites listeners to the 21st specialized Quran reciters' conference focused on Imam Khamenei's thoughts. It then features the recitation of Ziyarat Al-Yasin and plays religious and national songs, including "Iran Jan Azarbaijan." In the final segment, the host emphasizes the importance of small, daily actions for environmental conservation and social peace. He argues, citing the stories of prophets, that any action performed with divine intention and proper motivation is valuable and effective, no matter how minor it may seem.
