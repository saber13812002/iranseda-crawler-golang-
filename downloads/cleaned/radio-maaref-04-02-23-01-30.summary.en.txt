In this program, Hujjat al-Islam Rafiei outlines ten signs of wise individuals based on the words of Imam Reza (AS), emphasizing traits such as avoiding evil, valuing others' good deeds, and humility before God. He stresses that intellect is a divine restraining power and that the wise are perpetually seeking knowledge while avoiding the pursuit of fame. In the second segment, Hujjat al-Islam Mohammadi-Sharoudi discusses the proof of Prophet Muhammad's (PBUH) prophethood, citing three main avenues: continuous reports, consensus among religions, and the Quran. He references Quranic verses to affirm the Prophet's universal mission and the finality of prophethood. The discussion also highlights the role of previous prophets' predictions in validating the Prophet's status, with a promise to explore ancient scriptures in a future episode. The dialogue focuses on the importance of reflecting on religious knowledge and correctly understanding the characteristics of wise people and divine prophets.
