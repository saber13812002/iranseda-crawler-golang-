In this radio interview, Master Alireza Haditi, a veteran of traditional Iranian wrestling, speaks about his deep passion for reciting poetry and the spiritual atmosphere of the Zurkhaneh. After performing a live recitation of mystical verses, he addresses the host's question about following in his father's footsteps, revealing that all his children are interested in this ancient sport. Master Haditi emphasizes that both his sons and daughters are actively engaged in the fields of Zurkhaneh leadership and music, possessing the necessary talent for the craft. He also explains that after completing his education, he professionally dedicated himself to this vocation out of love, serving the public's health for thirty years. Towards the end of the program, the host invites the master to visit Yazd in the future to explore its historical fabric. The conversation concludes with well-wishes for the master's health and longevity, along with thanks to the listeners.
