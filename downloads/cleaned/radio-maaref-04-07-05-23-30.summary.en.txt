In this program, Hujjat al-Islam Muthahhari recounts his childhood memories and religious upbringing. He speaks about his father's simple, ascetic life and the profound impact of his father's piety and calligraphy on his character. Muthahhari also recalls the pivotal moment when he entered Haghani School for high school registration and chose the path of clerical studies over secular education. He highlights the role of his mother's family and his participation in religious gatherings in fostering his interest in the clergy. The program featured the recitation of the Ziyarat of Al-Yasin with a soothing melody. It concluded with an introduction to the morning radio schedule and a segment of Imam Khomeini's (RA) statements criticizing US policies and the concept of savagery.
