Ahmad Sanjari, a football veteran and instructor, discussed the evolution of the sport in a radio interview, emphasizing the critical role of repetition, precise planning, and technology in player development. He highlighted the significant increase in running distances and physical demands in modern football, stressing the need for coaches to update their knowledge and utilize advanced medical and nutritional facilities. Sanjari expressed concern over the stagnation in Iran's grassroots and professional leagues, attributing it to generational changes, decreased work ethic, and a lack of managerial stability in clubs. He also pointed out the scarcity of reputable academies and the absence of proper cultural development for both fans and players, arguing that Iran's inherent talent is hindered by flawed methodologies and intermediaries. In the final segment, Sanjari reflected on his playing career with the national team and Homa club, identifying Amir Abutalebi as his most influential coach. He expressed satisfaction with his football journey and called for greater respect for veterans and support for young players to achieve better horizons in Iranian football.
