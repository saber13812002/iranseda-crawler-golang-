In this radio program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli provides a profound exegesis of the opening verses of Surah Al-An'am, defining "Hamd" (praise) not merely as gratitude for blessings but as an acknowledgment of God's intrinsic perfection and absolute freedom from deficiency. Citing the Sajjadiyyah supplications and Quranic verses, he argues that a true human being is one who praises God with their heart, soul, and tongue, utilizing divine blessings in their proper context. The lecture distinguishes between "creation" (Khalaq) applied to the heavens and "making" (Ja'al) applied to darkness and light, explaining that light is singular while darknesses are multiple and relative. The concept of the "Makam Mahmud" is interpreted as reaching a state of intrinsic perfection and purity from flaws, emphasizing that gratitude itself is a blessing that necessitates further thanks. The Quranic term "yad'ulun" is interpreted as replacing God with other entities for polytheists and denying God for atheists. Finally, the creation of humans from clay and water is presented as a sign of divine power, clarifying that death signifies only the separation of the soul from the body, while the soul remains eternal.
