In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Panahian emphasizes that struggle is an inherent aspect of human life, ranging from battling nature for survival to economic competition and jihad against enemies. He views complacency as a political and social sin that leads to moral decay, contrasting it with the culture of resistance which he presents as a model for raising strong, responsible individuals. By referencing memories from the Sacred Defense, Panahian illustrates that spirituality and collective ethics, such as self-sacrifice, are rooted in this culture of struggle and should be promoted in schools and families. He argues that religion does not impose struggle but rather provides the logical and spiritual framework necessary to navigate these natural conflicts effectively. The program then transitions to a book narration segment from "Ocean of the East," where characters on a journey to Khorasan engage in deep theological debates regarding the infallibility of Imams. These discussions, citing Quranic verses and hadiths, describe the exalted status of the Infallible Imam as transcending human intellect and the entirety of existence. The broadcast concludes with religious prayers and an introduction to upcoming programs on Radio Ma'aref.
