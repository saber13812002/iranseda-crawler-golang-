The Hajj and Pilgrimage Organization announced that its kitchens in Najaf and Karbala will provide part of the food for Arbaeen pilgrims. Pre-sales for Arbaeen bus tickets begin on July 16, with the Tehran-Mehran route priced at 1,482,000 Tomans. The Arbaeen Committee has completely banned the sale of bus tickets to non-Arbaeen destinations from August 9 to 17. The Deputy of the Civil Aviation Organization stated that flight ticket prices will be announced after final approval by the Ministry of Roads and Urban Development. The Deputy of the Supreme Leader's Office for Propagation reported that over 12,000 propagandists will be dispatched across the country during Muharram. These propagandists are all selected from seminary students with advanced degrees. Additionally, the activity of 1,722 propaganda groups was confirmed to promote religion and utilize the expertise of seminary elites.
