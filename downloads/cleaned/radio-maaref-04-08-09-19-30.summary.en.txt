The radio program "Ma'aref" began with the recitation of the prayer for rain from the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya before transitioning to the "Treasure of Happiness" segment. In this segment, Professor Mohammad Hadi Falah emphasized the importance of ethics in supervision and evaluation processes, citing Quranic verses and narrations. He identified negative judgment and bias in selection procedures as ethical violations that can profoundly impact the destinies of individuals and families. Falah argued that healthy evaluations must be grounded in human and religious principles, going beyond mere adherence to administrative regulations. He warned that subjective and unjust interventions in supervisory processes carry severe consequences in the afterlife for those who commit such errors. The discussion concluded with a call for a comprehensive ethical code for supervision, after which the program moved on to the news section.
