In this radio program, Professor Mohammad Hadi Falah discusses "blame and reproach" as a linguistic vice. He explains that while criticism is necessary for moral correction, it becomes a vice when it exceeds moderation and is intended to hurt or offend others. Related concepts such as arrogance, condemnation, humiliation, and verbal abuse are also examined within this framework. Citing Quranic verses, the professor emphasizes that God reproaches humans not for unintentional slips, but for sins committed out of free will and injustice. It is also noted that rejoicing in the sorrow of enemies or even allies is impermissible if it lacks boundaries; true joy should stem solely from the victory of truth and the defeat of falsehood. The delay in divine punishment is not a sign of forgiveness but rather a grace period for repentance and return. Finally, listeners are encouraged to avoid harsh and hurtful speech to steer clear of moral harms and cultivate a life based on balance and mutual respect.
