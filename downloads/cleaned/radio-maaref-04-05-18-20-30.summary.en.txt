The program begins by introducing the book "Zayn al-Din Amili" by Shahid Thani, which offers solace to those grieving the loss of loved ones, particularly children. The book, written in fluent and literary Persian, explores concepts like causality, acceptance, and submission to God's will through real-life anecdotes from predecessors. The author, who was martyred in Mecca on the orders of the Ottoman Sultan, left this work as a gift for the bereaved. The second part of the program features the memories of Behrouz Emadi, a former POW captured during the Khatam al-Anbiya operation. Emadi recounts being hit by three bullets and his critical condition immediately after being taken prisoner. He describes the Iraqi officers' attempts to extract military intelligence and his intelligent responses to their questions. This segment serves as a symbol of the patience and resilience of Iranian POWs during the harshest conditions of the war.
