This radio program begins by recounting the inspiring story of a blind man who memorized the Quran with his daughter's help after losing his sight. The show then features Seyed Ali Ajaghi, a freedom fighter captured by the Ba'ath regime in Iraq in early 1983, who shares his memories of captivity and psychological torture. Ajaghi describes how the noble character of a cleric in the camp profoundly affected a harsh Iraqi soldier, demonstrating the power of spiritual resilience against enemies. The final segment emphasizes the importance of maintaining family ties, or "Sila-e-Rahim," based on the teachings of Imam Ali (AS). The core message of the broadcast highlights the strength of faith in extreme adversity and the necessity of nurturing emotional connections with relatives.
