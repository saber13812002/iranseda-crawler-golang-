This radio program discussed the jurisprudential and legal distinctions between the crimes of Baghy (rebellion) and Moharebeh (waging war).
A key point of debate was the interpretation of the law prescribing the death penalty for members of groups that wage armed rebellion against the fundamental system of the Islamic Republic.
The central question is whether the death penalty applies to all group members or only those who directly used weapons.
The prevailing legal opinion suggests that if the group as a whole uses weapons, all members are subject to the death penalty, even if some held non-combatant roles like driving.
The program also highlighted how new penal codes have better distinguished between Baghy and Moharebeh compared to previous laws.
If group members are arrested before engaging in armed conflict, their punishment is reduced from the death penalty to discretionary imprisonment.
Following the legal discussion, the program featured the recitation and dramatic translation of the final verses of Surah Muhammad and the beginning of Surah Fath.
The broadcast concluded with an invitation to a cultural event in Qazvin and advice on managing energy consumption.
