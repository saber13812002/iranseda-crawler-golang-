In the radio program "Persaman-e Ateghadi," Hujjat al-Islam Mohammadi Shahrudi addresses various theological and jurisprudential questions from listeners. The discussion begins with the interpretation of the word "day" in the context of creation, clarifying that it refers to vast geological eras rather than a standard twenty-four-hour period. The program then explores the concept of cursing Satan, explaining that this act symbolizes opposing the "Party of Satan" and anti-divine currents throughout history. Furthermore, the distinction between God's essential eternity and temporal eternity is explained, emphasizing that God is the Creator of time and space, possessing no physical limitations. The speaker warns against attempting to comprehend God's essence through limited human reasoning, as this can lead to confusion or the creation of mental idols. Instead, the focus should remain on God's pure existence, which transcends all definable attributes. Finally, the session concludes with a spiritual reminder to look beyond outward appearances and judge people by their inner sincerity and love, aligning with divine wisdom.
