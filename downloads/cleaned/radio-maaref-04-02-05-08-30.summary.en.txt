The radio program "Armaghan" from Radio Ma'aref explored the Islamic concept of "Silat al-Rahim," or maintaining family ties. The program's expert highlighted Quranic verses and hadiths that emphasize this practice as a means to extend life, increase blessings, and draw closer to God. It was clarified that maintaining these bonds does not require significant financial expenditure and can be achieved through simple gestures like greetings. The discussion also stressed the moral superiority of initiating reconciliation during family disputes. In the report section, listeners shared their personal experiences, noting the positive impact of family connections on happiness and peace. The program further discussed the importance of maintaining ties with in-laws and even relatives with differing beliefs.
