This program emphasizes the superiority of "self-knowledge" over knowing God through external signs and logical reasoning, based on mystical traditions. It argues that external knowledge is merely theoretical and prone to forgetfulness, whereas self-knowledge is a direct, intuitive science where the seeker, the path, and the destination are unified. Citing the hadith "Know God through God," the text explains that true divine knowledge is achieved only by purifying the heart's mirror from sins and ego. In this state, recognizing one's own inherent poverty and need leads to the realization of God's absolute existence. Consequently, attempting to know God through mental concepts or analogies is considered a form of shirk. The ultimate path to salvation lies in spiritual purification and witnessing the divine light within oneself.
