In this program, Ayatollah Rafiei emphasized the importance of social etiquette in families and society, relying on narrations and the Du'a Makarim al-Akhlaq. He identified principles such as expressing affection, covering a spouse's faults, serving them, showing appreciation, and maintaining neatness as the main pillars of family stability. He also stressed the necessity of gaining a spouse's satisfaction by understanding their preferences and avoiding insults to their loved ones. In the second part, Ayatollah Hosseini Qomi interpreted the chapter on "Self-Sufficiency" from the book of Kafi, emphasizing the preservation of self-esteem and avoiding dependence on others. Citing a poem by Hatim Tai, he stated that the greatest wealth is being independent of people's hands and maintaining one's dignity. This approach aligns with the principle of patience and endurance in hardships, showing that maintaining honor by concealing needs is a great virtue. Ultimately, both speakers focused on the balance between helping those in need and preserving personal dignity when facing difficulties.
