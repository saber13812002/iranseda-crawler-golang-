This radio program analyzed the Supreme Leader's second message regarding the Zionist regime's military aggression. Dr. Ali Bahadori Jafari, a legal scholar, emphasized the pivotal role of the people as the foundation of the Islamic system and the primary driver of its success. He highlighted the people's dignified behavior and solidarity during the crisis, such as massive attendance at events and continued economic activities, as symbols of national unity and victory. A key point was the fundamental distinction between Iran's system, based on peace and self-determination, and the Zionist regime, which is built on war and imposition. Bahadori argued that this crisis provides an opportunity to better understand national identity and clarify social dualities. It was also predicted that following the decline of American power, Iran will be recognized as a powerful and respected global force. Finally, the importance of preserving revolutionary ideals, expressing opinions with courage, and setting aside personal calculations in decision-making was stressed.
