Following the declaration of war and blockade, Imam Khomeini ordered the mobilization of truck drivers to urgently unload ships at the ports and resolve the logistical crisis. Despite the lack of proper infrastructure in Bandar Abbas, thousands of drivers worked tirelessly with great enthusiasm to transport goods and supply the front lines. In the second part, former POW Naser Karimi recounts his experiences during captivity, including a mourning ceremony for the Imam's death held in the camp. The presence of MEK members at this event angered the prisoners, leading to a confrontation aimed at countering their influence. Karimi also describes the process of prisoner release following Iraq's invasion of Kuwait and the subsequent exchange of remaining detainees from the Tikrit camp. These narratives highlight the prisoners' spiritual resilience against torture and the enemy's attempts to break their morale. Ultimately, the release of the POWs and their return to Iran symbolized the victory of the people's will and the fighters against the enemy's aggression.
