In this dialogue with sociologist Dr. Kachouyan, the central role of "national cohesion" in countering external and internal plots is emphasized. He argues that the enemy's primary goal in the recent war was regime change and the disintegration of Iran, a strategy based on the false assumption that the people had distanced themselves from the system. Kachouyan contends that the discourse of "social collapse" in recent decades was an attempt to construct an "artificial nature" for the people as opponents of the system. The widespread public support for the regime refuted this enemy assumption, demonstrating that the people's true identity is intertwined with the Islamic system. He warns that some domestic forces, by advocating for fundamental changes during wartime, are inadvertently moving in the direction of the enemy. Finally, the uprising of Imam Hussein (PBUH) is presented as a model for resisting the transformation of Imamate into tyranny and preserving the banner of truth.
