In this radio program, Professor Mohammadi addresses various theological and jurisprudential questions from listeners. He first distinguishes between faith and certainty, outlining four methods to strengthen faith: exercising reason, purifying the soul, reciting specific supplications, and adhering to the Quran and the Ahl al-Bayt. In the second section, referencing the story of Moses and Khidr, he emphasizes the necessity of patience regarding divine mysteries and trusting in God's wisdom in destiny. Regarding marriage without prior investigation, he advises that enduring a spouse's difficulties can lead to spiritual perfection. In response to a question about the impurity of dogs, he clarifies that ritual impurity differs from physical cleanliness and suggests using legal arguments and kindness to persuade others. He also considers the disapproval of reciting poetry in religious gatherings acceptable due to the significant spiritual benefits of praising the Ahl al-Bayt. Finally, he recommends reciting Surah Yasin, Malik, and Tawhid, and dedicating the rewards of good deeds to the souls of deceased parents for their peace.
