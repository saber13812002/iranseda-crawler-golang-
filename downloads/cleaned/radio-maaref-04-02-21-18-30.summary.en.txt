The radio program "Zamzam Ahkam" began by addressing various religious inquiries from its audience. The first topic clarified the rules of Tawaf, stating that a short break of three to five minutes after the fourth or fifth round is permissible, and the ritual must resume from the exact point of interruption. The program then discussed Ayatollah Khamenei's ruling on missed fasts due to long-term addiction, noting that while making up the fasts is not obligatory if the inability is permanent, paying the Mut'ahm for each day is required. It was also explained that performing prostration for Quranic verses without Wudu is sufficient, provided there is no obligatory Ghusl pending. Other rulings included the permissibility of marrying a widowed aunt or uncle's wife after the waiting period, as well as detailed explanations on Khums regarding bank interest and leftover food items. The broadcast concluded with a spiritual reflection on the importance of prayer and a special prayer for the holy city of Qom.
