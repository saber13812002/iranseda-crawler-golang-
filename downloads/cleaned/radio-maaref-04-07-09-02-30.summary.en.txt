In the "Persaman Eteghadi" program on Radio Ma'arif, Hojjat al-Islam Mohammad Sharoudi addressed various theological and ethical questions from listeners. After congratulating the birth of Imam Hassan Askari (AS), he emphasized the importance of seeking truth rather than engaging in argumentative debates. He clarified that dedicating the rewards of good deeds to others or performing them on behalf of the Infallibles is a commendable practice. The scholar advised against wishing for death for oneself or others, recommending prayers for the guidance of oppressors instead of curses. He also highlighted the necessity of maintaining family ties with non-praying relatives to guide them, while discouraging marriage to them. Finally, he provided a historical analysis of Imam Hassan's (AS) presence in Medina, arguing that his presence was crucial in preventing Muawiyah's plots and preserving the sanctity of the Prophet's Mosque.
