In this program, Professor Maleki emphasized the necessity of military strength and deterrence for the Islamic society, citing Quranic verses. He explained that accepting peace is only legitimate when done from a position of power after the enemy has been intimidated, rather than from weakness or fatigue. Referring to verse 35 of Surah Al-Mumtahanah, he stated that seeking peace out of weakness is a sign of defeat and ruin. The miracle of transferring the throne of the Queen of Sheba was discussed to illustrate that divine power transcends human physical and logical laws. In response to questions, it was clarified that hostility towards Jews or Christians is based on their actions, betrayal, and obstruction, not their religious beliefs. Verse 44 of Surah Al-Fath was interpreted to show that the Quran is a guide and healing for believers, while disbelievers fail to comprehend it. Finally, it was advised that severe physiological anxiety should be managed through medical treatment alongside faith and Quran recitation.
