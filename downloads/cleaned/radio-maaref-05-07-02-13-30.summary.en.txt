The program began by honoring the memory of Martyr General Musavi and inviting the public to attend Friday prayers in Tehran, scheduled to be held at the University of Tehran. The main segment focused on the criminal case of a mother who administered unapproved diet pills to her five-year-old daughter, Asal, for child modeling purposes, resulting in severe malnutrition and digestive issues. Although the mother claimed the pills were herbal and harmless, social emergency services identified her actions as economic exploitation of a minor. The judge, citing the Law on the Protection of Children and Adolescents, sentenced the mother to a suspended one-year prison term, contingent on her promise not to repeat the behavior. The judge emphasized that parents are strictly prohibited from engaging in any conduct that harms the physical or mental well-being of their children. The broadcast concluded by reflecting on the tragic dimensions of the Mina disaster and the profound grief experienced by the victims' families. Finally, the importance of proper child upbringing in Islam was highlighted, urging guardians to protect the spiritual and physical health of their children.
