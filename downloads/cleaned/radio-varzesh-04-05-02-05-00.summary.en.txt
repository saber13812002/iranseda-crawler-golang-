Seyyed Sharam Heravi, the head coach of the national karate team, discussed Iran's sporting successes in 2010 and Sajjad Gharzadeh's historic first Olympic gold medal in Tokyo. He highlighted the challenging conditions of the Tokyo Olympics due to the pandemic and Gharzadeh's injury just two days before the final match, emphasizing the athlete's resilient spirit. Heravi also addressed the impact of Bahman Eskri's doping ban on Iran's qualification slots and shared how his personal faith and spiritual support played a crucial role during high-pressure moments. He praised the golden generation of Iranian karateka and stressed the importance of investing in human resources and effective management within the federation. Heravi revealed that despite his long career, his financial rewards were minimal, and he has maintained a simple lifestyle without purchasing luxury items. He expressed his hope for karate's continued presence in future Olympics and concluded by prioritizing the health and happiness of his fellow citizens as his ultimate life goal.
