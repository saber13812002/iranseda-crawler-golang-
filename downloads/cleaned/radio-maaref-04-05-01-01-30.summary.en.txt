The program begins by analyzing the recitation of poetry in the Battle of Karbala, highlighting it as a symbol of faith, courage, and purpose among the fighters. The speaker emphasizes that the Guardian Jurist is the legitimate deputy of the Imam of the Age and a divine proof, urging listeners to follow this authority. The slogan "Labbayk Ya Hussein" is interpreted as an absolute commitment to the guardianship and a refusal to deviate from the path of truth. The second part features a literary narrative depicting the moments before Imam Hussein's departure to Kufa. Messengers reported the betrayal of the people of Kufa and the enemy's military readiness, yet the Imam remained steadfast in his decision, trusting in God's will. Meanwhile, Hasan (AS) expressed his anxiety about the journey's outcome through prayer and reliance on God.
