This radio program explores the divine interpretation of history through the Quran, presenting prophets as pivotal turning points and embodiments of divine unity.
The evolutionary path of prophets from prophecy to apostleship and imamate is explained, emphasizing their intrinsic unity and organic connection to the Final Prophet (PBUH).
A key insight is that each prophet fulfilled the peak needs of their era, and all prophetic traditions are manifestations of the complete existence of the Prophet of Islam and the Ahl al-Bayt (AS).
Thus, studying the biographies of prophets is essentially studying the various facets of the Perfect Man of the final era, linking all previous nations to the final community.
In the second part, the concept of Zuhd (asceticism) is defined via Imam Reza (AS) and Jesus (AS) as not grieving over lost worldly gains nor becoming arrogant over acquired ones.
Zuhd does not mean abandoning the world entirely, but rather prioritizing divine pleasure over egoistic desires and remaining indifferent to worldly fluctuations.
God promises to secure the livelihood of those who prioritize divine will over personal desires, granting them a benefit superior to the trade of any merchant.
This approach allows individuals to achieve a deeper understanding of Tawhid (divine unity) and inner peace through a profound knowledge of prophetic unity and ascetic practice.
