In this radio program, the dichotomy between religious values and people's livelihood is rejected as a media conspiracy.
The key point is that supporting the economy and resistance is itself a religious and Islamic duty.
The experience of the last decade shows that compromising with the West and abandoning values has only intensified pressure rather than lifting sanctions.
Iran's economic problems stem from internal weaknesses and management failures, not just sanctions, and require internal planning and wisdom to solve.
Yemen's resistance against Israel and the US serves as an example of people's will overcoming economic shortages and sanctions.
The elimination of resistance leaders does not destroy the spirit of confrontation, and the path of truth continues.
Therefore, improving livelihood is achieved through resistance, greater effort, and internal management reform, not through compromise.
