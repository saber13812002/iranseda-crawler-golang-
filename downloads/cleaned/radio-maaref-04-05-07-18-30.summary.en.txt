The radio program "Zamzam Ahkam" addressed two main themes: Quranic education and jurisprudential rulings. The first segment highlighted the impact of Quranic games on students' enthusiasm and moral development. Mr. Zare'in, the founder of this initiative, emphasized that clinging to the Quran and establishing prayer are the two pillars of societal reform, inspired by Imam Hussein's uprising. He noted the success of these programs is due to creative instructors, parental involvement, and students independently organizing Quranic sessions in their homes. In the second segment, Hojjat al-Islam Vaeidpour discussed the Sharia-compliant conditions for Ta'ziyeh and Shabih-khani. Citing the Supreme Leader's fatwas, he stressed the necessity of avoiding falsehood, disrespecting the Ahl al-Bayt, and relying on authentic historical sources in these performances. The program also provided jurisprudential answers to listeners' questions regarding making up missed prayers while traveling, the correct method of wiping the head during Wudu, the prohibition of handshakes with non-mahrams, and the etiquette of listening to Friday sermon speeches.
