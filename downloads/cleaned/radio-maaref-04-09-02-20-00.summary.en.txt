The martyrdom anniversary of Hazrat Fatimah Zahra (SA) was observed with mourning ceremonies in the shrines of Karbala and Najaf, hosting thousands of pilgrims from around the world. Simultaneously, the Zionist regime assassinated a senior Hezbollah commander in a strike on southern Beirut, increasing the casualty count. In Gaza, despite the ceasefire, attacks continue, and the recovery of thousands of martyrs' bodies from the rubble remains difficult due to a severe lack of heavy equipment. A survey in Israel revealed that a quarter of settlers, particularly the youth, are seriously considering emigration. Analysts highlighted the extensive influence of Zionist lobbies like AIPAC on US government institutions and their impact on foreign policy. Other news included the launch of a tree-planting initiative on the Arbaeen route and the administration of Persian language exams for Iraqi students in Baghdad. The story of Dr. Alfred Heber, a German orientalist who converted to Islam and produced a precise German translation of the Quran, was also shared. The broadcast concluded with the special program "In Mourning of Yas," inviting listeners to participate in a competition and send condolence messages.
