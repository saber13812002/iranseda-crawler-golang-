In this radio program, the mother and wife of martyr Mostafa Ahmadi Roshan shared memories of his childhood, education, and married life. The martyr's mother described his birth in December 1979 and highlighted his brave and independent character from a young age. His wife recounted their meeting at Sharif University and emphasized his devout yet kind nature within the family. Although active in the university's Basij, Mostafa remained steadfast in his beliefs despite professional and academic pressures. He was martyred in December 2011, leaving a lasting legacy for his family and society. The program concluded by stressing the importance of keeping the martyrs' names alive and using their revolutionary spirit as a model for the younger generation.
