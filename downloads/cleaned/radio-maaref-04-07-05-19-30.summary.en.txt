In this radio program, the concept of asceticism (Zuhd) is explained as the key to experiencing the sweetness of faith, based on narrations from the book of Kafi.
According to the Prophet and Imam Sadiq (AS), asceticism does not mean completely abandoning worldly benefits, but rather avoiding excessive attachment and envy towards others.
A narration from the Prophet (PBUH) states that a person cannot feel the sweetness of faith in their heart until they stop being envious of what others possess.
In the second part, the instructor discusses the vice of cursing and foul language, citing narrations that classify this behavior as a major sin.
Imam Ali (AS) instructed his companions during the Battle of Siffin to refute their enemies by citing their false actions rather than using abusive language.
Imam Sadiq (AS) emphasized in a narration that cursing, even in response to injustice, constitutes a compounded wrong and creates a barrier between the servant and God.
The conclusion of the discussion is that believers must protect themselves from moral vices by maintaining politeness and engaging with opponents through logical argumentation rather than insults.
