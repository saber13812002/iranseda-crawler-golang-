This radio program provided jurisprudential answers to various listener questions.
Consulting a non-mahram doctor or sonographer is permissible only in cases of necessity when a same-gender alternative is unavailable.
Using lip balm to heal cracks does not invalidate fasting unless part of it is swallowed.
A woman's physical presence is not required for divorce; appointing a trustworthy agent who fulfills the witness conditions is sufficient.
Obstacles under toenails must be removed for wudu and ghusl, although minor pus under the nail is not an impediment.
Chewing fresh gum does not break the fast, but reinserting wet, previously removed gum does invalidate it.
Missed prayers must be performed exactly as they were missed, whether shortened or full.
The final segment emphasized the importance of prayer for spiritual growth and its individual and social impacts.
