Alireza Bahouei, a former national team setter, discusses his grievances regarding non-technical exclusions from the Iranian national team during his peak years. He criticizes financial disputes with the Paykan club and personal decisions by certain managers that prevented his participation in the Asian Championship. Bahouei emphasizes that loyalty to promises and ethical principles are more important to him than formal contract signatures. He expresses regret over the group decision to leave Foolad Sirjan and join Shahrdari Orumieh, viewing it as a mistake. Despite his criticisms of the national team's management system, he remains committed to Iranian volleyball and intends to play as long as he is useful to his team. Based on advice from veterans, he has set a goal to remain in top form until the age of fifty. He also takes pride in the recent national team medals, viewing them as a strong response to the enemy's soft war.
