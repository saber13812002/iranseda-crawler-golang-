The radio program "Ma'aref" began by reciting the prayer for rain from the Sahifa al-Sajjadiya to seek divine mercy and blessings. In the main segment, Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallah discussed the verbal vice of "Ta'yeer," specifically focusing on unwarranted nitpicking and fault-finding. He emphasized that reason does not criticize reason, noting that prophets never disagreed because they had conquered their lower selves. Unjustified nitpicking is contrary to Islamic law and weakens the front of truth while creating social tension. Professor Fallah warned that many political and social conflicts stem from this verbal flaw and hidden negative intentions. He called for a return to Quranic unity and the avoidance of baseless destruction in all aspects of life. The ultimate goal is to purify speech from vices and foster a peaceful environment based on monotheistic ethics.
