This radio program, dedicated to the anniversary of Fatima Masumeh's (SA) arrival in Qom, explores her life, virtues, and practical examples for believers. The speaker highlights her migration from Medina to Khorasan and her temporary stay in Qom, emphasizing the city's historical significance as a hub of Shia Islam and her burial site. Key attributes of Fatima Masumeh discussed include her infallibility, her role as a narrator of hadiths, and her unique status of intercession on the Day of Judgment. The importance of preserving the name "Fatima" among Muslims, despite historical political pressures to erase it, is also noted. The speaker underscores the spiritual and historical impact of her presence in Iran, particularly in the establishment of the Qom Seminary and its role in the Islamic Revolution. The segment concludes with prayers for the liberation of Palestine and the healing of the wounded, followed by practical tips for water conservation at home.
