In this radio program, citizens expressed their readiness to assist military forces during wartime and reported the safe return of Iranian pilgrims from Saudi Arabia. Government officials reassured the public that essential goods, medicine, and blood supplies are sufficient, alleviating concerns about shortages. The guest speaker emphasized the importance of supporting the armed forces and avoiding criticism during this crisis, urging citizens to report suspicious activities to the police. He identified arrogance as the root of all moral vices and a primary barrier to acquiring knowledge and spiritual insight. According to religious narrations, arrogant individuals lose sincere companionship and deserve condemnation because arrogance is incompatible with the light of knowledge. The program concluded with condolences for three media colleagues who were martyred in a recent attack. Listeners were encouraged to remain steadfast in their faith and avoid arrogance to navigate this challenging period.
