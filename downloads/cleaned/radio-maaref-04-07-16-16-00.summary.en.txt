In this radio program, Professor Mohammad Sharoudi addressed various theological questions from listeners. He explained that the Throne (Arsh) is not a physical location but a symbol of God's infinite knowledge and power, which transcends space and time. Regarding political-religious issues, he emphasized that Islam is inherently authoritative and that dividing it into different types is incorrect, as dignity and resistance against oppression are fundamental principles. The concept of self-accounting was described as a daily review of one's actions and speech to achieve spiritual perfection. On the topic of guardianship (Wilayah), he noted that this status was offered to all humans, though acceptance depends on individual will. The pressure of the grave was clarified as a spiritual and barzakhic pressure resulting from sins and moral deficiencies, rather than a physical punishment. Finally, Lady Zahra's visitation to Imam Hussein's grave was interpreted as a form of spiritual attention and illumination in the barzakh realm.
