As the anniversary of Imam Reza's martyrdom approaches, Mashhad is hosting domestic and international pilgrims, with authorities preparing necessary accommodations and traffic management. Afghan pilgrims have also entered Iran via the Dogharon border, where they are being accommodated in local mosques. In the UAE, a large mosque in Sharjah has been inaugurated, featuring a capacity of three thousand worshippers and a design without internal columns. In occupied Jerusalem, the Citadel Mosque remains a symbol of Islamic and Arab identity despite being converted into a museum by the Zionist regime. In Palestine, the foreign minister resigned over disagreements regarding sanctions against Israel, while heavy regime attacks on Gaza have caused dozens of casualties and exacerbated the famine crisis. Turkey has blocked the last remaining direct trade routes with Israel by prohibiting the docking of commercial ships associated with the regime. Finally, the "Arbaeen Narrator" campaign continues to invite pilgrims to share their experiences by sending short videos or audio clips to the designated numbers.
