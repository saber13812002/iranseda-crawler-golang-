This radio program discussed the theological relationship between essence, substance, and mental versus external realities.
It was explained that essence exists as a non-subject entity externally and as a subject-based entity within the mind.
A common misconception is that the external essence is transferred directly to the mind with only a change in state.
In reality, the unity between mental and external essence is merely a conceptual or classificatory unity, not a real existential one.
An analogy involving a magnet was used to illustrate that the rational essence is universal and distinct from the particular external one.
This analogy demonstrates that the mental essence cannot be identical to the external particular essence.
The program then proceeded to interpret verses from Surah Zukhruf regarding the miracles of Jesus and the promise of Paradise for believers.
The broadcast concluded with Quranic supplications and news about the admission process for Khorasan seminaries for the upcoming academic year.
