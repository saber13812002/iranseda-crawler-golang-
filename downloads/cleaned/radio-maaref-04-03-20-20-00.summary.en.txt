This radio broadcast from the Ma'aref network aired on the occasion of the birth of Imam Ali ibn Musa al-Rida (AS). The program began with news reports highlighting Afghanistan's economic progress and its readiness to increase trade with Iran to ten billion dollars. It also announced the publication of the fifth volume of the Astan Quds Razavi library's manuscript catalog. The main segment was the special program "Hadi Ammat," featuring experts and a live report from the Holy Shrine in Mashhad. The reporter described the enthusiastic presence of pilgrims and the religious ceremonies held in the shrine's arcades. The program aimed to deepen religious knowledge and express devotion to the Eighth Imam. It concluded by inviting listeners to send messages and participate in a contest.
