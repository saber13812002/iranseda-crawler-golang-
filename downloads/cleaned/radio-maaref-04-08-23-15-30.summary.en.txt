In this discussion, Dr. Mehdi Meshkibaf analyzes the post-9/11 developments, demonstrating that despite Iran's tactical and covert cooperation with the US in Afghanistan and Iraq, the de-escalation strategy ultimately failed. George Bush's designation of Iran as part of the "Axis of Evil" exposed the US government's hegemonic nature and lack of trust, proving that it only leveraged cooperation to advance its own regional objectives. The expert emphasizes that the illusion of a "win-win" policy with the global hegemony has harmed Iran for over four decades, leading to significant security crises. He argues that in an international system based on power, any nation lacking adequate deterrence and military capability is destined for elimination and should not expect flexibility from the United States. Meshkibaf warns that accepting American rules of the game without sufficient power places the country under serious threat, necessitating a departure from past mistakes. Ultimately, the importance of accurately understanding the nature of the hegemonic system and avoiding conciliatory illusions to preserve national interests and security was highlighted.
