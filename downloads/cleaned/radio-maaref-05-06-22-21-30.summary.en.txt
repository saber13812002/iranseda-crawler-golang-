This program features a tafsir of verses 35 to 39 of Surah Al-A'raf by Ayatollah Javadi Amoli, focusing on the shared theme of religious obligation across Surahs Al-Baqarah, Al-A'raf, and Ta-Ha.
The concept of "Qissa" here is defined as the transmission of verses, teaching of the Book and wisdom, accompanied by spiritual purification, rather than mere storytelling.
Piety (Taqwa) is described as abstaining from falsehood and denial of truth, while righteousness (Islaah) refers to performing good deeds.
Denying divine verses and attributing lies to God are considered the worst forms of injustice because they violate the core principles of faith and God's rights.
On the Day of Judgment, disbelievers are forced to testify to their own disbelief and sins after witnessing the destruction of their idols and the collapse of their false claims.
This confession takes the form of a testimony because denial is impossible in a scene where the sin is evident and the idols are gone.
Believers who accept revelation and perform good deeds are freed from fear and sorrow, whereas disbelievers, despite receiving their worldly sustenance decreed by God, remain subject to divine punishment.
Ultimately, various groups of disbelievers are gathered in the Hellfire, where the later arrivals blame the earlier ones for leading them astray and demand double punishment for them.
