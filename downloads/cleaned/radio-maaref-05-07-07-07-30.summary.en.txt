In this radio program, following listener introductions, a fictional narrative of a fire hose is presented, where it speaks of its loyalty and the support of its colleagues during difficult operations. This symbolic story portrays the firefighter as a person of wisdom who patiently untangles the knots of others' confusion and distress during life's crises. The main discussion focuses on the concepts of "keeping one's promise" and "sincerity" in covenants made with oneself, God, and others. The speaker emphasizes that the true value of a promise is revealed in moments of hardship and intense trials, requiring one to stand by their word even in storms. Citing a Quranic verse and the words of Imam Ali (AS), keeping one's promise is introduced as a pillar of human honor and integrity. The program also references Imam Sajjad's (AS) supplications in Makarim al-Akhlaq regarding wearing the garment of piety and calming the hearts of the people of Iran. Finally, the show concludes with an invitation to kindness in speech and behavior, hoping to meet again the next day.
