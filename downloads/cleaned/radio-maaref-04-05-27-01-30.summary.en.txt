The program begins by emphasizing that steadfast faith and patience are the keys to the victory of the Islamic nation.
It revisits the crucial messages from religious leaders, including Imam Khomeini and the current Supreme Leader, regarding the destruction of Israel and support for Palestine.
The resistance fighters in Gaza are presented as the practical model of this Hussaini and Karbala-style steadfastness against oppression.
The broadcast then transitions to a narrative from the book "My Master is Hussain," detailing pilgrims' journey to the Mehran border and their return to Iran.
The protagonist, Mohammad Taghi, travels toward Mehran city with a cultural group after visiting the holy shrines.
He faces challenges such as heavy rain and a lack of transportation but continues his journey with patience.
Ultimately, Mohammad Taghi arrives in his hometown and is warmly welcomed by his family and friends.
The segment concludes with an invitation to listen to the final part of the story in the next broadcast, accompanied by religious songs.
