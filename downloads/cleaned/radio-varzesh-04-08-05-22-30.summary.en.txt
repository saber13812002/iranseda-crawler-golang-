The discussion highlights the critical importance of mass sports as a primary strategy for disease prevention and reducing the national expenditure on imported medicines.
The guest emphasized the vital role of physical activity in enhancing community well-being, citing successful examples from developed countries and personal experiences of morning exercises in hospitals.
Criticism was directed at the lack of coordination between the mass sports federation and university experts, with a call for a joint think tank to leverage professional insights.
The proposal to launch a specialized media channel dedicated to health and mass sports was made, focusing particularly on the pivotal role of mothers in raising a healthy generation.
The guest also stressed the need for media to address the issues of physical inactivity and the resulting bodily damages prevalent in society.
Further criticism was raised regarding bureaucratic parallel efforts and the absence of a national will to truly popularize sports across the country.
Finally, the guest requested that the Minister of Sports and Youth allocate time for a dedicated discussion to support and promote mass sports initiatives.
