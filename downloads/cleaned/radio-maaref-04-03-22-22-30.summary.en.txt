In this segment of the program, a hadith from the Kanz al-Umal is cited to emphasize the necessity of distinguishing true followers of the faith from others. The Prophet (PBUH) instructed Imam Ali (AS) to remain on the path of guidance and distance himself from the masses if they were seen moving towards misguidance. This highlights God's extreme sensitivity regarding the purity of religion and the need to prevent it from being mixed with familial or political ties. The role of the "Sirefa Mush'ama" is also mentioned as a document confirming the Imamate that was protected against the plots of the Umayyad and Abbasid dynasties. The speaker emphasizes the blessings of Ali's (AS) footsteps and his martyrdom, stressing the importance of recognizing his authority for spiritual salvation. Finally, the audience is invited to participate in the feeding of the poor during the days of Ghadir and to join a live discussion with Hujjat al-Islam Farahzad on the Eitaa channel.
