The guest highlights the 44-year wait for an Olympic appearance, criticizing structural issues such as clubs withholding players and non-technical conflicts within the football system. He describes the golden era of 1996, when the selection of Mohammad Momeni as head coach led to a third-place finish in the Asian Cup, a result driven by a smart mix of young and experienced players. This success facilitated the transfer of 18 players to European leagues and secured qualification for the 1998 World Cup. Conversely, he criticizes the team's failure in the 2002 World Cup qualifiers under Blazevic, attributing it to poor management decisions and a lack of attention to player nutrition and psychological comfort on match days. He dismisses claims of foreign conspiracies or technical failures as excuses for avoiding responsibility. Finally, he emphasizes the importance of focusing on youth development and fostering coordination between clubs and the federation to ensure sustainable progress in Iranian football.
