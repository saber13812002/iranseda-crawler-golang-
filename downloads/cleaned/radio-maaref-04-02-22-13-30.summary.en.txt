In the "Mashq-e Rah" program on Radio Ma'aref, Hujjat al-Islam Seyyed Ahmad Faqih discussed the ethics of monotheism and the etiquette of prayer. He emphasized that prayer must be accompanied by a genuine expression of need and heartfelt belief, rather than mere verbal recitation. Faqih explained that sins cause the heart and tongue to become hardened, hindering spiritual connection with God; therefore, repentance is a prerequisite for prayers to be answered. Responding to listener questions, he stressed the importance of making amends for disrespect towards parents and reciting Surah Yasin for family peace. He also advised avoiding sins and performing night prayers before sleep to ensure waking up for them. Finally, the discussion touched upon women's employment, highlighting that jobs should align with women's divine nature and that family security remains the primary priority.
