In this radio program, Professor Javadi critiques the materialistic worldview and self-centeredness of contemporary society using Nahj al-Balagha. Citing Letter 78 and Sermons 113 and 233, he identifies the love of the world and forgetting death as the root of moral and social corruption. The analysis of Letter 45 to Uthman ibn Hanif highlights the contrast between aristocratic behavior and the revolutionary principles of Imam Ali. In the second segment, scholars answered various jurisprudential questions from followers of different Maraji. Topics included organ donation, temporary marriage, prayer following, and the prohibition of usury. Clarifications were also provided regarding the permissibility of sharing book content online and divorce rulings in specific circumstances. Finally, the program announced the 20th specialized conference for Quran reciters focusing on innovation in recitation.
