In this program, Ayatollah Khatami, relying on Imam Sajjad's Risalah al-Huquq, defines tawakkul as acknowledging God's absolute power and disregarding the independent influence of creation. He emphasizes that tawakkul does not mean abandoning means, but rather using them without believing they are the ultimate cause. According to narrations, tawakkul and satisfaction with God's decree are superior to all other deeds and revive the spirit of Tawhid in the believer. In the jurisprudential section, Hojjat al-Islam Fallahzadeh clarified that using a gifted item is forbidden without permission if it is certain that Khums is due on it. Regarding another question, selling surplus medication is permissible if it is not legally prohibited and has not expired. It was also ruled that in case of doubt regarding the number of missed prayers, one must perform the certain amount. Finally, intentionally omitting the salam in prayer invalidates it, whereas omitting it by mistake does not.
