In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the importance of deep respect for teachers and scholars by recounting stories of Ayatollah Khorasani and Mirza Shirazi. He highlighted their humility before their own masters as a model for students to follow. In the second segment, Hojjat al-Islam Faqih discussed "monotheistic ethics" and identified the phrase "La ilaha illa Allah" as the core of monotheism and the key to salvation. He refuted the notion that this recitation is burdensome, describing it instead as a source of peace and blessing in both worldly and afterlife contexts. Responding to listener questions, Faqih stressed the spiritual benefits of maintaining family ties and resolving conflicts. He attributed the severing of family relationships to envy and Satan's whispers. Finally, he advised listeners to control their tongues by pausing and reflecting with reason and heart before speaking.
