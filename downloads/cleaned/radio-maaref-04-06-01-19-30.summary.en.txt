The radio program initially addressed jurisprudential rulings regarding the requirements for a prayer leader and the validity of fasting during Ramadan, citing Ayatollah Sadrani's fatwas. It briefly discussed the prohibition of deception in pricing and the necessity of mutual agreement in commercial transactions. The main segment focused on the psychological and moral factors behind lying, such as ignorance, spiritual degradation, lack of honor, and the persistence in repeating falsehoods. The speaker emphasized that listening to and spreading lies, particularly on social media, acts as a catalyst for multiplying deceit and damaging one's faith. Quoting Nahj al-Balagha and the Quran, listeners were advised to avoid heeding the words of worldly and unreliable individuals. Ultimately, understanding these root causes was presented as the primary solution for purifying society and individual lives from dishonesty.
