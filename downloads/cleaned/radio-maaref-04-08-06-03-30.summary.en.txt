In this program, Professor Sahabi examined the profound impact of Mulla Sadra's Transcendent Theosophy on Imam Khomeini's thought. He demonstrated that the Imam utilized concepts such as existential poverty, the rule of corporeality in origin, and the unity of agent and action to construct his spiritual and mystical foundations with philosophical precision. Furthermore, the Imam supported Mulla Sadra's rational arguments for bodily resurrection while critically addressing and clarifying certain ambiguities in Sadra's work. The second part of the program provided jurisprudential answers to listeners' questions, covering the khums status of educational stipends, the validity of prayers with different surahs, and the conditions for optional prayers during travel. Additional points addressed the etiquette of Dhuhr and Asr prayers and the religious obligations when traveling in environments containing prohibited activities. Finally, the importance of promoting Transcendent Theosophy in seminaries and the role of commentators like Mulla Hadi Sabzevari were highlighted. This analysis reveals that Imam Khomeini's thought is a coherent synthesis of philosophy, mysticism, and jurisprudence.
