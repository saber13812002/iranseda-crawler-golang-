In this interview, Master Ali Akbar Daryabari and his nephew Seyed Mansour discuss the golden era of football in the Charestan Dasht neighborhood and the master's pivotal role in developing players for the Iranian national team. They recount memories of collaborating with prominent sports figures like Hossein and Goudarz Habib, as well as their social and revolutionary activities during the 1970s. Daryabari recites a poem honoring local champions, highlighting a time when 12 of the 15 national team players originated from their specific neighborhood. However, he criticizes the conversion of dirt fields into commercial properties and the decline in community sports motivation driven by financial interests. He argues that utilizing domestic coaches is essential due to their better understanding of local players, while acknowledging recent positive progress in sports like wrestling and volleyball. The conversation also covers successful activities in the sport of Pirsco in the Firuzkuh region, where nearby villages have achieved county championships. The program concludes by emphasizing the importance of passing these sports memories and honors to future generations and expressing gratitude for the veterans' lifelong contributions.
