This radio program explains that the spiritual path relies on maintaining a connection with God as both the primary mover and the ultimate goal.
The Quran emphasizes the necessity of constant remembrance of God, warning that forgetting Him leads to spiritual blindness and misguidance in the Hereafter.
Conversely, remembering God grants "basirah" (insight), enabling a person to perceive inner truths and navigate the correct path.
A fundamental distinction is drawn between the external "path" and the internal "journey," noting that one may appear healthy yet fail to digest spiritual truths due to internal ailments.
Unlike the rationalist approach that derives knowledge of God from self-knowledge, the Quranic perspective asserts that anyone in motion must inherently maintain a link with their source and destination.
The Infallible (AS), being constantly remembered by God, stands on the "straight path" and clearly sees the way forward.
To sustain this journey, the program highlights the essential stages of vigilance (taqwa) and self-accounting (muhāsaba) to prevent forgetfulness of both the self and God.
