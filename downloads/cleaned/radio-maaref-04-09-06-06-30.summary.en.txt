The program's fiqh segment discussed the concept of obligatory means and the probability of an actual religious duty.
It critiqued the views of Ayatollah Khoei and others, arguing that the probability of an actual duty does not necessarily imply the probability of punishment.
The core argument is that without a specific enforcer, the probability of potential harm alone does not trigger the rule of repelling potential harm.
The respondent distinguished between direct implication and sequence, suggesting that the probability of punishment can arise without being directly implied by the duty.
