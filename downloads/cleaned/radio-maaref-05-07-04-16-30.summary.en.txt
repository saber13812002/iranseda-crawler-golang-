The radio program began by discussing the Quran's immunity from alteration, referencing the book "Sanat al-Quran." The host then addressed listener questions, starting with the story of Prophet Shu'ayb and Moses to explain the father's guardianship over his daughter and the practical nature of the mahr in that era. The concept of divine wisdom (Hikmah) was explored as a higher form of knowledge than mere science, illustrated by the story of Luqman. The discussion also covered the application of verses like "Baqiyatullah" to the Imam of the Age, relying on hadith and contextual interpretation. A fiqh segment clarified the essential differences between the prayer of fear and the traveler's prayer, specifically regarding when they must be repeated. Finally, the program identified the people of Tubba' as a historical group mentioned in the Quran who were destroyed for rejecting their prophets.
