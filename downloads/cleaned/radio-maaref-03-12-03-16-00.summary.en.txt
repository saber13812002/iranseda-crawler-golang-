In this radio program, Professor Pishvay addresses and refutes false claims regarding the treaty of Salman al-Farisi and its alleged impact on Iranian history. Citing reliable historical sources, he rejects the authenticity of this treaty due to weak documentation and contradictions among various manuscripts. The professor emphasizes that the claim of granting permanent, special privileges to Zoroastrians through Salman lacks historical and logical support. He also dismisses the suspicion that the Quran aligns with Zoroastrian beliefs, labeling it as a baseless intellectual conspiracy raised by certain Orientalists. The professor characterizes the methodology of Western Orientalists as rooted in ignorance or malice, urging listeners to rely on authentic Islamic and historical sources. Finally, he clarifies the distinction between Zoroastrians and idolaters, explaining their status as People of the Book within the Islamic framework.
