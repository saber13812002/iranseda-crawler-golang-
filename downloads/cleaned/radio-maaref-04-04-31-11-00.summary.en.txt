The program began by introducing a book on Sardar Amil Ali Haghighzadeh's pivotal role in establishing Iran's missile power. It then detailed extensive preparations for the Arbaeen pilgrimage, including temporary suspension of the Hajj organization, the deployment of over 300 service tents, and the creation of a joint operations room with Iraq to enhance border security. In the second segment, Professor Mohammad Shahroudi explained the concept of the "World of Gold," linking it to the spiritual realm and the human soul. Subsequently, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidpour emphasized the need for comprehensive Arbaeen gatherings that combine emotional mourning with religious education on prayer, hijab, and national pride. Fiqh questions were addressed regarding fasting during pregnancy and breastfeeding, as well as the rulings on hair removal. The broadcast also announced the identification and upcoming burial of Martyr Sadegh Poshtyafteh in Dezful after 43 years. The program concluded with a call to appreciate the blessings of life in Iran and a transition to a special segment for answering religious queries.
