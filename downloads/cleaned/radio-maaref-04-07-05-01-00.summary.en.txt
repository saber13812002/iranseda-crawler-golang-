The program began with the recitation of the Arifa prayer, followed by a lecture by Fallahzadeh in the third segment of Radio Ma'aref.
The speaker commemorated the birth anniversary of Hazrat Abdol-Azim Hasani and explored the reasons behind his high status and the rewards of visiting his shrine.
A key point was the direct referral by the Infallible Imams to prominent jurists like Hazrat Abdol-Azim and Zakariya ibn Adam as sources of religious guidance.
These narrations demonstrate that the institution of jurisprudential authority has roots in the era of the Imams' presence, not just the period of occultation.
Additionally, Imam Hadi equated the visitation of Hazrat Abdol-Azim with that of Imam Hussein for those unable to travel to Karbala.
Referencing a narration where Hazrat Abdol-Azim presented his faith to Imam Hadi, the speaker emphasized the importance of reviewing one's beliefs and practices.
Finally, it was advised that Muslims, especially the youth, should verify their prayers and recitations with religious scholars to ensure the validity of their worship.
