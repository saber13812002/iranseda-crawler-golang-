This radio program discussed the connection with Imam Mahdi (AJ) and the distinction between the minor and major occultations based on the presence of specific deputies.
The primary criterion for distinguishing friends from enemies is allegiance to the Wilayat (divine authority), rather than personal relationships or fleeting emotions.
Relying on verse 64 of Surah Al-Isra, the guest explained the enemy's combined war strategy, which includes creating chaos, promoting sin, and exploiting moral weaknesses to weaken the community.
The enemy aims to create internal divisions and induce negligence, thereby diverting the power of the faithful from fighting injustice to inflicting harm upon themselves.
To counter these plots, the importance of political insight, accurately identifying the enemy, and avoiding arrogance or negligence while adhering to the guidance of the Supreme Leader was emphasized.
Active participation in the Arbaeen pilgrimage and serving the pilgrims were highlighted as models of unity and solidarity that can neutralize the enemy's schemes.
Finally, the program concluded that trusting in divine promises rather than the enemy's deceptive offers is the key to victory and peace during the period of waiting.
