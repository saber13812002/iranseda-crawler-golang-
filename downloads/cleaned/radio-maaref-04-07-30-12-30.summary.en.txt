In this lecture, Ayatollah Ziyabadi outlines four fundamental principles of a believer's life based on a narration from Imam Sadiq (AS). The first principle is the realization that religious and moral deeds are strictly personal, meaning heirs cannot substitute for an individual's own actions during their lifetime. The second principle is "modesty before God," which involves being conscious of divine surveillance in both private and public settings to prevent sin. The third principle is trusting God as the ultimate provider, which frees individuals from the anxiety of excessive material accumulation and unnecessary worries. The fourth principle is being prepared for death and avoiding idleness, as working by God's command is a religious duty rather than merely a means to secure a livelihood. The Imam also emphasizes maintaining boundaries in social and family relationships, warning that inappropriate jokes with non-mahram individuals carry severe consequences in the afterlife. Finally, serving within the family, such as men helping with household chores and women serving their husbands, yields immense rewards like prolonged worship and pilgrimages when mutual respect is upheld.
