The program began with a listener discussing financial struggles and family conflicts regarding housing and marriage, which transitioned into a broader discussion on the importance of reading.
Guest Haja Barzoui emphasized the need for selecting high-quality books and warned against the negative impact of social media on reading habits, recommending the book "Tareh Keli" as a valuable resource.
The main segment featured Hujjat al-Islam Falahati, who provided an in-depth analysis of the vice of anger and irritability based on Islamic narrations.
Citing a hadith from Imam Ali (AS) that describes anger as rootless and invalid, the scholar positioned this vice as a product of disbelief, in contrast to moral virtues rooted in faith.
He explained that anger is so destructive that no family honor, status, or social credit can compensate for or hide it, effectively dragging a person from noble moral lineage into degradation.
Anger was further described as a "bad companion" and the deputy of ignorance, acting like a constant shadow that diverts individuals from the path of reason and monotheism.
The program concluded with advice to avoid anger, repent from vices, and cultivate moral virtues to achieve inner peace and social harmony.
