The radio program began by discussing human will, emphasizing that individual choice is the complete cause of events, while other factors are incomplete. Listeners' questions addressed the possibility of visiting the Ahl al-Bayt in Paradise without prior awareness of its joy, and the reasons why tyrants fail to learn from history. The responses attributed the behavior of oppressors to ignorance, lack of faith, and moral weaknesses. The necessity of holding religious gatherings was defended against cost-saving arguments, highlighting their educational value and support for the poor. It was clarified that the term "fourteen infallibles" signifies the peak of infallibility within our specific jurisprudence, rather than excluding other prophets. Finally, the program addressed the theological and legal status of those who abandon prayer, affirming they remain sinful believers for whom good deeds still hold spiritual merit.
