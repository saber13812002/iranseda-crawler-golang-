In this radio program, Sayyid Hassan Miresad, a veteran of the Iran-Iraq war, recounts his captivity following the Bit al-Muqaddas operation in 1983. He describes his transfer to the Al-Anbar and Tikrit camps in Iraq, detailing the harsh initial conditions such as severe torture, extreme scarcity of water and food, and overcrowding in the dormitories. Miresad also highlights the contemptuous and hostile attitude of Iraqi soldiers toward the Iranian captives' Islamic identity and their attempts to undermine their religious beliefs. His testimony illustrates how the prisoners maintained their morale and hope through faith and patience despite physical deprivation and psychological pressure. This narrative is part of the "Story of Freedom" series on Radio Ma'aref, which honors the memory and suffering of the freed prisoners during their years of captivity.
