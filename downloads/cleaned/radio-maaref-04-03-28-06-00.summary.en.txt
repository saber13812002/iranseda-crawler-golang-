This radio program provides a theological interpretation of verses from Surah Al-Imran regarding the miracles of Prophet Jesus (PBUH). The speaker refutes the literal interpretation of creating a bird from clay, emphasizing that this miracle involved granting actual life and internal structure, not merely crafting a statue. Citing rational and Quranic arguments, he asserts that the animal soul is immaterial and that the term "breathing" in the Quran signifies the infusion of the soul rather than physical exhalation. The distinction between Jesus's miracle and the Samiri's deception is clarified by contrasting true resurrection with the creation of an inanimate idol. Additionally, the concept of "inspired revelation" to the disciples for renewing their pledge of loyalty is explained and distinguished from legislative revelation. Finally, the status of Fatima Zahra (SA) as a knower of the unseen and a participant in the debate with the liars is established through the verse of Mubahala. These analyses demonstrate the depth of exegetical and theological understanding in interpreting the Holy Quran.
