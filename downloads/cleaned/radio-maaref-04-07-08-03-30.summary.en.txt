In the radio program "Andisheh Aftab," expert Baqer Sahabi analyzed Imam Khomeini's views on the philosophical foundations of the Shiraz school, specifically focusing on the works of Mohaghegh Davani. The Imam endorsed Davani's distinction between the derivative and its source in jurisprudence, rejecting the criticisms raised by Sadr al-Din Shirazi. Regarding mystical concepts, the Imam critiqued Davani's assertion of the primacy of essence in contingent beings, arguing that it contradicts the principle of similarity and the primacy of existence. Furthermore, the Imam validated Davani's objections to Qutb al-Din Razi on the divisibility of bodies, deeming the defense offered by Davani's student insufficient. The second segment, "Zamzam Ahkam," addressed various jurisprudential questions from listeners. Topics included the permissibility of mobile phone charging fees, the rules for selling a deceased person's assets, and the requirements for wudu and prayer when water is scarce. The program also clarified the rulings on adhan and iqamah for daily prayers and the obligations regarding lost property.
