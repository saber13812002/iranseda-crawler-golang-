In the first segment, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the religious obligation to avoid waste, citing narrations from Imam Sadiq (AS) and the meticulous practices of Imam Khomeini (RA). He illustrated how the late Imam carefully managed resources like paper towels and lighting to prevent even minor extravagance, distinguishing between necessary use and sinful waste. In the second segment, Hojjat al-Islam Shakarin discussed the "Fine-Tuning" of the universe within the context of modern theology. He critically analyzed the theory that physical laws are necessary, arguing that it lacks empirical evidence and fails to explain the origin of the laws themselves. Shakarin noted that scientific evidence suggests the universe had alternative possible paths, undermining the claim of physical necessity. Finally, the discussion began to address the random chance theory, highlighting its statistical improbability in explaining the universe's intricate order.
