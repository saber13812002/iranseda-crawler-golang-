In this radio program, anger is described as the "ember of Satan" and the gateway for his influence to enter a person's soul. Islamic narrations state that rage acts like a fireball that first harms the individual and then damages those around them, weakening reason and allowing Satan to dominate decision-making. Anger is not just a negative emotion but a manifestation of hell in the material world, destroying inner security and social relationships. The solution to this affliction is controlling anger, avoiding quarrels, and restraining the ego to eliminate the ground for Satan's infiltration. In the second part, the virtue of "gentleness" and softness in behavior was emphasized, citing the Prophet (PBUH) who said that if gentleness were a visible creature, it would be the most beautiful creation of God. It was also mentioned that in any interaction, the person who behaves with more gentleness is more beloved to God and receives a greater reward. These points indicate that managing anger and cultivating the character of gentleness are the main keys to inner peace and improving interpersonal relationships.
