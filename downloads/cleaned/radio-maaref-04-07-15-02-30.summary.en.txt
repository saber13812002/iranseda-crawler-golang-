In this radio program, Professor Mohammad Sharoudi addresses various theological and social questions from listeners. He first explains the nature of the Barzakh body, describing it as an ethereal entity free from physical mass and spatiotemporal limitations, where human perception is significantly enhanced. Regarding the situation in Gaza, he rejects the comparison with Imam Hasan's truce, emphasizing that the ultimate goal of life is preserving faith and that resistance is necessary against oppression. The professor argues that in the global test of life, the oppressed are successful, while both oppressors and silent bystanders, including certain Islamic nations, are condemned. He also characterizes public immodesty not only as a moral sin but as a significant political and security threat to the Islamic system. Finally, he highlights the fulfillment of promises as a fundamental principle of faith, describing the breach of oaths as a grave sin with divine consequences known only to God.
