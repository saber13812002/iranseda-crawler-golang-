In this interview, Major General Sadegh Shirzad emphasizes the vital role of mass sports and daily walking in maintaining the physical and mental health of society. Citing World Health Organization guidelines, he argues that forty minutes of daily walking can prevent many diseases and significantly reduce healthcare costs. Shirzad recalls the golden era of mass sports under Amir Majidara's management and identifies the lack of proper infrastructure, such as sports parks and cycling paths, as the main challenge. He shares memories of collaborating with prominent figures like Qavam-oddin Shahkar and Ehsan Hadadi, stressing the necessity of investor support for sports development. Finally, Shirzad reveals administrative issues, including his son's dismissal from the Housing Organization due to managerial conflicts, and expresses concern about the current state of track and field in Iran.
