The program's expert highlighted the fundamental differences between the recent attacks on Iran and the Lebanon experience, emphasizing the role of Western intelligence agencies in designing this multi-faceted operation. The enemy's objective was described as creating military and political collapse by assassinating key commanders and suppressing air defenses to force negotiations from a position of weakness. However, the Supreme Leader's swift and strategic crisis management, which involved calming the public and immediately appointing experienced replacements, neutralized this plot. The selection of new commanders from within the General Staff and the IRGC demonstrated the cohesive and dynamic structure of Iran's armed forces, eliminating reliance on specific individuals. This rapid response not only prevented internal chaos and the erosion of public morale but also showcased the system's deterrence power and resilience. Ultimately, this event is recognized as a strategic lesson in international crisis management, illustrating that the will of the people and strong leadership can defeat the enemy's miscalculations.
