In this discussion, Dr. Ghalibaf emphasized the necessity of providing a logical response to global public opinion following the military aggression and insisted on receiving compensation before engaging in any negotiations. He argued that talks must be based on guaranteeing regional security and lifting sanctions to achieve free economic growth, rather than capitulating to one-sided demands. Ghalibaf viewed the twelve-day war as an opportunity to demonstrate deterrence power and reach a balance of power, highlighting its moral and social achievements. The most significant outcome of this period was described as the unprecedented national unity and comprehensive solidarity of all social classes against external threats. He characterized this unity as a sign of the living culture of manliness and defense of the homeland in the younger generation, which acted even more firmly than during the Sacred Defense era. In the second part of the program, Mowlana Nouri offered strategies for the economic self-sufficiency of mosques. He emphasized creating endowments, utilizing public waqfs, and encouraging permanent donations over temporary ones. It was also recommended that mosque financial management be transparent and avoid excessive dependence on government budgets to preserve the people-oriented identity of these institutions.
