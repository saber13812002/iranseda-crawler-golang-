In this radio program, counselors addressed various parenting and psychological challenges faced by teenagers and their parents. For shy adolescents, practical strategies such as rehearsing difficult scenarios in front of a mirror, maintaining eye contact, and adopting assertive body language were recommended to help them defend their rights. The experts also emphasized the importance of fostering a loving communication environment at home and using the technique of "praise before correction" to minimize family tension. In response to mothers who tend to focus on the negative aspects of situations, the session advised identifying and correcting cognitive distortions, such as black-and-white thinking, while focusing on the child's positive traits. Regarding a twelve-year-old girl's habit of slouching, the experts suggested explaining the physical consequences of this posture scientifically and avoiding constant criticism to build internal motivation. Finally, the program concluded by highlighting the slogan of "production leap through people's participation," stressing the critical role of self-confidence and mobilizing human capital and public initiative in resolving the country's economic challenges.
