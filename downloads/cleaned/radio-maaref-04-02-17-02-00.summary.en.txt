In the "Persaman E'teghadi" program, the discussion focused on the initial abode of Adam and Eve, favoring the theory that it was a Barzakh realm where time and space are meaningless.
The guest argued that while Adam's physical body was on Earth, his life was Barzakh-like, similar to the state of sleep where temporal and spatial constraints do not apply.
It was explained that humans before Adam were not obligated to religious duties due to the lack of a divine soul and full intellect, with the verse about messengers applying specifically to Adam's lineage.
In the short answers section, listeners were advised to maintain ties with relatives who have cut them off, emphasizing patience and forgiveness to avoid spiritual harm.
The ruling on fasting was clarified, stating that submerging the entire head in water invalidates the fast, whereas partial submersion does not.
The guest stressed the importance of obeying religious laws without necessarily understanding all their wisdom, noting that human reason cannot grasp every divine rationale.
Finally, while seeking wisdom can strengthen religious motivation, worship must remain pure for God's sake and should not be conditioned on fully comprehending its underlying reasons.
