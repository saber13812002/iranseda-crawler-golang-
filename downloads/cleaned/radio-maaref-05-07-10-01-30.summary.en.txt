The program begins by emphasizing God's constant surveillance, suggesting that maintaining decorum in His presence is a true sign of faith. The speaker reflects on past expectations of the Mahdi's appearance, concluding that the people's steadfastness and purity are the key factors accelerating his return. The martyrdom of the Supreme Leader and the Arbaeen pilgrimage of Imam Hussein are highlighted as symbols of the Iranian nation's awakening and strength against global arrogance. The importance of maintaining calm, avoiding baseless fears, and relying on intercession and congregational prayer in mosques is discussed to bolster a resistant spirit. The speaker asserts that the mosque is not merely a place of worship but a comprehensive hub for social management and education. Citing the Prophet's example in Medina, the mosque is described as the center for political, military, and social decision-making. Finally, the audience is urged to maintain unity and firm standing to facilitate the ultimate victory of truth and the Mahdi's appearance.
