In this radio program, the security inspection of suspicious individuals is deemed not only permissible but obligatory under Islamic law when there is a strong probability of espionage.
Spreading unverified rumors that cause public anxiety and fear is declared strictly forbidden and a serious crime from both religious and legal perspectives.
The program addressed various jurisprudential rulings, including the stoning of the Jamarat for blind pilgrims and the requirement for women to cover their feet completely during prayer.
It was clarified that one may begin prayer immediately upon hearing the Adhan without needing to wait, based on the fatwa of Imam Khomeini.
Tasting a small amount of Imam Hussein's turbat dissolved in water for healing purposes is permitted, whereas eating soil is generally prohibited.
Endowed lands (waqf) are not subject to inheritance, but the benefits derived from them are distributed among heirs according to Islamic law after the owner's death.
Women are given the option to recite the Quran quietly or aloud in loud prayers, while men are required to recite loudly.
Finally, the importance of respecting mosque caretakers and observing local customs in religious ceremonies was emphasized.
