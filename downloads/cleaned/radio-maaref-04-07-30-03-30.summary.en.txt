This radio program examines Imam Ali's (AS) efforts to prevent the Battle of Jamal by dispatching Ibn Abbas to the opposition leaders. Ali (AS), noting Talha's bad temper and stubbornness, instructed Ibn Abbas to meet with him, as well as Aisha and Zubayr. During the negotiation with Talha, who initially claimed he was forced to pledge allegiance, the discussion ended with Talha demanding the handover of the killers and the transfer of the caliphate to a public vote. After failing to convince Talha, Ibn Abbas visited Aisha on Ali's (AS) orders, urging her to return home and refrain from political interference. Aisha refused to accept the advice, citing the superiority of her supporters' numbers and acknowledging Ali's (AS) bravery, which led to a dead end in the conversation. The meeting with Zubayr also failed to yield results due to the presence of his son and the repetition of previous accusations against Ali. Ultimately, Ali (AS) prayed for a solution and asked Ibn Abbas to return to Aisha to warn her against defying divine command and causing bloodshed.
