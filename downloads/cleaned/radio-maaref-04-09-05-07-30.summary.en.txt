In this radio program, the three key characteristics of the Basij—insight, sincerity, and timely action—were highlighted on the occasion of Basij Day.
Subsequently, in the "Treasure of Felicity" segment, Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallah discussed the vice of mockery and its painful consequences in the Hereafter.
He offered several solutions to overcome this vice, emphasizing the importance of gaining knowledge, strengthening reason, remembering death, and avoiding arrogance.
The professor stressed the necessity of weeping for one's own sins, avoiding hypocrisy and financial motives in mockery, and maintaining dignity and modesty.
True reliance on God was defined as adhering to Sharia and servitude, and listeners were encouraged to seek refuge from mockery through prayer and intercession of the Ahl al-Bayt.
Finally, it was advised to refrain from mocking religious sanctities, martyrs, and values, and to focus on one's own faults rather than the flaws of others.
The program concluded with blessings and a reminder of the importance of ethical conduct in daily life.
