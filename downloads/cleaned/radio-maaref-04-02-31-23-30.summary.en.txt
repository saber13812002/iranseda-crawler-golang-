The radio program began by urging listeners to send a text message to support a needy family in Mashhad who are struggling with rent and medical expenses. In the "Sarmashk" segment, Professor Javad Mohaddesi analyzed Imam Ali's letters to Muawiyah, emphasizing the superior status of the Muhajireen and Ansar compared to those freed during the conquest of Mecca. He argued that Muawiyah's claims were baseless and asserted that even if migration were the sole criterion for leadership, the right belonged to Imam Ali, while the Ansar were superior to those who fought against the Prophet. The discussion also highlighted the tradition of pledging allegiance to the Imams, as referenced in the Ghadir Khumm sermon and the Ahad prayer. Finally, the program critiqued the disconnect between the claims of modern political leaders and the actual teachings of prophets, stressing the importance of aligning actions with Islamic principles rather than mere verbal assertions.
