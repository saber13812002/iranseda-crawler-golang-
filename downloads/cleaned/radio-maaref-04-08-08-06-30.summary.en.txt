The radio program begins with a jurisprudential and philosophical discussion on the foundations of the rule for repelling probable harm, validating the "rational consensus" perspective while rejecting the "circumstantial evidence" view. It then commemorates Youth and Student Mobilization Day by recalling the martyrdom of Mohammad Hossein Fahimi as a heroic role model for the younger generation. Additionally, the importance of passive defense is highlighted to explain how national resilience and deterrence can be strengthened without relying on military weapons. The show also pays tribute to the late poet Gheysar Aminpour, honoring his literary contributions and expressing condolences. The host engages the audience by inviting them to share memories from their own adolescence, fostering a warm and interactive atmosphere. The broadcast concludes by emphasizing the vital role of youth in building Iran's future and encourages listeners to recite specific religious invocations.
