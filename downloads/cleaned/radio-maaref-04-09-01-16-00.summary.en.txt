In the radio program "Persaman Quran" featuring Professor Maleki, listeners' questions regarding Quranic concepts were addressed. The first question concerned why God swears by creations like the sun and moon, which the expert explained is not out of necessity but to emphasize the importance of the message and draw human attention to divine signs. He noted that these oaths have a logical connection to their answers, aiming to guide humans toward spiritual purification and perfection. The second question addressed the meaning of "six days" in the creation of the universe, which was interpreted as "six stages" of creation rather than literal 24-hour days. Regarding the third question about Prophet Noah's global mission and the extent of the flood, it was explained that the human population at that time was limited to a specific region where Noah was the prophet. Consequently, the divine punishment was directed at that specific community, with only those in the ark surviving. These discussions highlight that a precise understanding of the Quran requires deep reflection and an awareness of its historical and linguistic context.
