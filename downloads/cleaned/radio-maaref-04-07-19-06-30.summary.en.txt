The radio program began by discussing divine knowledge and free will, citing narrations that God is aware of both human actions and the hypothetical effects of unperformed deeds. The broadcast then commemorated the historical events of September 19, including the birth of the scholar Mohaghegh Helli, the passing of Ayatollah Shirazi, and key moments of the Iran-Iraq War such as the siege of Khorramshahr. The latter part of the show focused on the importance of starting the day with a positive mindset, prayer, and gratitude. It also highlighted the neurological process of habituation, advising listeners to avoid looking at undesirable things to maintain mental peace. The program concluded with a tribute to border guards and a call for listeners to live a happy, high-quality life.
