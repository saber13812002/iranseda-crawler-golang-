In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Fala discusses the dangerous vice of arrogance and its remedies. He emphasizes that recognizing God's greatness compels humans to humility, as grandeur is exclusively an attribute of the Divine. The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) was sent to save people from pride, and reason serves as the primary tool for understanding this truth. Emulating the Ahl al-Bayt (AS), who were devoid of arrogance and superiority, is highlighted as a crucial practical solution. Furthermore, acknowledging the material beginning and end of human life, from conception to decay, prevents self-aggrandizement. The program concludes by explaining the Quranic concept of "Ajl," or the predetermined end of all entities. This perspective on the finitude of existence liberates humans from the illusion of immortality and pride.
