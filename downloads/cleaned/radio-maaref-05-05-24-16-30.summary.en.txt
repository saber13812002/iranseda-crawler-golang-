In this radio program, Professor Maleki interprets verse 268 of Surah Al-Baqarah, emphasizing the importance of recognizing internal and external enemies. He defines Satan's promises of "poverty" and "immorality" as inducing despair, negativity, and committing major sins. Conversely, the voice of divine mercy offers forgiveness and grace, encouraging hope, joy, and contentment with God's provisions. Citing the story of Prophet David and two brothers, the professor warns judges against hasty decisions, presenting the narrative as a divine test. The root "Kara" is analyzed across various verses, signifying hardship in pregnancy, ugliness in disbelief, or unpleasantness in the context of jihad. In response to a listener's query, it is suggested that the "seven verses" in Surah Al-Hujurat likely refer to a seven-verse surah revealed twice. Finally, based on narrations, it is explained that human creation and guidance continue in subsequent cycles, with God appointing a guide for every created being.
