In this program, Hojjat al-Islam Saeidi Aria, relying on the Quranic verse "Do not think," introduces martyrdom as divine sustenance and eternal life, arguing that human worldly calculations regarding martyrs are fundamentally flawed. He emphasizes, based on narrations, that even the sincere desire for martyrdom grants its reward, positioning it as the highest level of goodness. The speaker warns that Satan diverts people from martyrdom by promoting materialism and the desire for a long life, replacing spiritual values with worldly substitutes. Conversely, he states that relying on God and magnifying Him in one's heart brings peace and certainty, as illustrated by the stories of Prophet Moses, Imam Khomeini, and General Soleimani. He highlights a narration suggesting that martyrs may return early to fight alongside the Imam of the Time, framing martyrdom as the ultimate life goal. The program concludes with prayers for the martyrs and the reappearance of the Imam, stressing the importance of maintaining devotion to the Quran and the Ahl al-Bayt while avoiding materialism.
