Ali Reza Bahoudi, a veteran volleyball player, discussed in a radio interview why the national team has not fully realized its potential to win more gold medals. He argued that flawed mindsets and the lack of complete professionalism in the sports chain, from coaches to managers, have hindered Iran's greater success. Bahoudi expressed regret over missed opportunities to play in reputable European leagues due to the short-sightedness of domestic administrators. He also criticized a seven-to-eight-year period during which he was excluded from the national team for non-technical reasons, stating that he does not forgive those responsible. Despite these challenges, he emphasized the importance of loyalty and professional ethics in club relationships. Bahoudi shared his memories of playing for Paykan and Urmia, expressing regret over a group decision to leave Foolad Sirjan for Urmia Municipality. Finally, he expressed his desire to continue playing until the age of 55, stressing the need to maintain health and readiness to serve the country's volleyball.
