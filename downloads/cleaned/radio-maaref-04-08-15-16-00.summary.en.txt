The radio program "Perso-man Enghelab" addressed listener questions regarding political and social issues. In the first segment, the guest explained that the perception of impunity for corrupt officials is partly due to the judiciary's weak media presence in publicizing its rulings. While acknowledging potential administrative flaws, he emphasized the judiciary's serious commitment to fighting corruption and encouraged citizens to follow up through official spokespersons. In the second segment, the analyst attributed US global influence to its military and economic power, noting that this influence is gradually declining due to war costs and the rise of rival currencies. He also highlighted the de-dollarization trend and the US government's massive debt. In the final segment, the discussion shifted to internet filtering, with the expert arguing for comprehensive "cyber governance" rather than focusing solely on filtering or its removal. He compared the virtual space to geographical borders, urging the government to establish robust security principles to protect national interests and citizens from cyber threats.
