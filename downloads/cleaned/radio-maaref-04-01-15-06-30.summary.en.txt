In this radio program, it is demonstrated through authentic narrations and the verse of Anfas that Ali ibn Abi Talib and the Ahl al-Bayt are infallible and possess prophetic ranks.
The Quranic concept of "Muthani" is specifically applied to the Ahl al-Bayt, indicating their unique status and companionship with the Quran.
Non-Shia scholars, such as Ibn Abd al-Hayd and the author of Sharh al-Aqa'id, have explicitly affirmed the infallibility of Ali, Fatima, and their descendants in their works.
This infallibility entails abstaining from major sins and vile morals, although there are minor differences regarding specific jurisprudential rulings.
The final conclusion is that the "Ahl" in the verse of Salawat refers to these specific infallible individuals identified in the narrations.
The program concluded with the recitation of verses from Surah Fussilat and Quranic supplications, followed by a discussion on the narrated interpretation of the verses.
