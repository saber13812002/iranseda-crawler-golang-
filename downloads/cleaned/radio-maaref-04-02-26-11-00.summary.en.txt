The 36th Tehran International Book Fair concluded with a record sales figure of 424 billion tomans and five million visitors, marking a 66 percent increase compared to the previous year. Concurrently, the second international conference on Allameh Mola Abdullah Bahabadi Yazdi was held in Mashhad to revive the works of this prominent scholar. In the Quranic sector, the head of the Islamic Propagation Organization announced the national "Living with Verses" project, aiming for ten million people to memorize 200 key verses within three years. The 48th National Quran Competition has also begun, accompanied by a specialized meeting of judges to update national and international competition regulations. In the religious education segment, Hojjat-ol-Islam Vahidi emphasized the importance of accepting advice and the profound impact of sincere reminders on believers. He illustrated through anecdotes from great scholars that pure speech and the Quran can soften hearts and guide individuals toward piety even in challenging circumstances.
