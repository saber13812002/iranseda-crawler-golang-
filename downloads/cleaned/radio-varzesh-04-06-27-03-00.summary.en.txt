Colonel Dr. Chingiz Mousavizadeh, manager of the Army Ground Forces shooting club, recounted his athletic career and his controversial selection as the first-choice team member at age thirty. He discussed his successes in the Peace and Friendship Cup and the Asian Games, noting that sanctions and ammunition shortages have always been major hurdles for Iranian shooters. Transitioning into management, he leveraged military support to transform the Ground Forces club into a primary hub for developing professional talent. His club team recently achieved significant success by winning gold and bronze medals at the Asian Championships in Thailand. Mousavizadeh emphasized the importance of eliminating nepotism and providing equal, free opportunities for young athletes through transparent testing processes. While his ultimate goal remains winning a world championship or Olympic medal, his management philosophy focuses on staying in roles where he can remain impactful.
