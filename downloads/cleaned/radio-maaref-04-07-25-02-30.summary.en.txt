In this program, Dr. Mostafa Sadeghi Kashani addressed various historical questions from the audience. He explained that Abdullah ibn al-Zubayr, despite not being part of the six-member council, secured the caliphate for about 11 years due to his close familial ties to the Prophet and the political influence of the Zubayrid clan. The program also examined the uprising of Mukhtar al-Thaqafi, which lasted approximately one and a half years and concluded with his martyrdom, while his commander Ibrahim al-Ashtar continued fighting alongside al-Zubayr's son. Dr. Sadeghi traced the etymology of the term "Shia" back to the Prophet's era and the period following Uthman's assassination, identifying Kufa as the primary historical center of Shia Islam. Finally, he discussed the sword Zulfikar, noting that it was either a divine gift or a war trophy from Badr, and clarified that it was not a double-edged blade but featured a central ridge.
