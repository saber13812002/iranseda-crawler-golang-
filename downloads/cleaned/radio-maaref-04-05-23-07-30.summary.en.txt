At the beginning of the program, the mother of martyrs Hossein and Esmail Biabani recounted her brothers' journey to Syria to defend the shrine of Hazrat Zeinab (SA) and their subsequent martyrdom.
She shared the news of her younger brother's martyrdom in Tadmur and his body being torn apart by enemies, emphasizing their steadfastness against oppression.
The guest then interpreted the concept of "conscious ziyarat" (pilgrimage), stressing the necessity of shedding one's ego to reach a state of nearness to the Ahl al-Bayt (AS).
Referring to the extensive linguistic harms mentioned in religious narrations, the guest presented a list of sins associated with the "tongue of the nafs" (ego).
Among these sins, lying was highlighted as one of the most significant linguistic transgressions, with its detailed discussion postponed to the next episode.
The guest also emphasized the role of the Perfect Human as the "physician of circles" in healing spiritual ailments and the importance of sincerity in following religious teachings.
Finally, listeners were urged to monitor their speech and avoid linguistic sins to take steps toward martyrdom and service to the Ahl al-Bayt (AS).
This program was broadcast during the thirty days of Arbaeen, aiming to promote the culture of martyrdom and ethical conduct.
