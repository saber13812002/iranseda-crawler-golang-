The radio program "Ma'aref Yaran" introduced its "People's Club," inviting listeners to join by sending the number 58 to 105882 or searching for the channel on the Telegram app. The host reflected on the program's successful participation in the 36th International Book Fair in Tehran, highlighting the enthusiastic response from young attendees. Niko Beshiri, a young girl from Mazandaran, performed a literary piece titled "Stone Back," which explores themes of hope and perseverance. Fatemeh Navabi, a reporter from Chenaran, provided a live report from a resistance economy exhibition, showcasing local handicrafts and home-based employment opportunities for women. Atif Naeimi shared insights on how changing our mindset can help us recreate our life experiences for a better future. The broadcast concluded with a recitation of a Rumi poem dedicated to the Eid al-Adha holiday. Finally, the host encouraged listeners to contribute to societal peace and environmental care through small, consistent daily actions.
