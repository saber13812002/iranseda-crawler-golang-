The radio program "Zamzam Ahkam" began by addressing listeners' religious inquiries. Regarding keeping a dog at home, Hujjat al-Islam Alian Natij, citing Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi, deemed it culturally inappropriate and a cause of ritual impurity that invalidates prayer. On the issue of blood spreading to a carpet, Ayatollah Sistani stated that there is no obligation unless one is certain of the spread, in which case cleaning is required. Ayatollah Sadrani permitted using surplus income or a specific amount from an orphan's assets for hosting guests. Hujjat al-Islam Jawaheri, referencing Ayatollah Shabiri Zanjani, explained that a grandmother breastfeeding her grandchild creates a permanent mahram relationship, thereby invalidating the child's marriage. Hujjat al-Islam Zin al-Abidin, citing Ayatollah Javadi Amoli, ruled that long-lasting nail extensions are impermissible as they hinder wudu and must be removed at the earliest opportunity. The second segment, "Sarmashk," featured a lecture by Hujjat al-Islam Mahdasi on fulfilling divine rights and implementing Islamic laws. He emphasized, based on Nahj al-Balagha and the Quran, that obeying God's commands and executing justice are essential acts of gratitude for divine blessings and necessary for maintaining social order.
