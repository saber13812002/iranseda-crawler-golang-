The program began with a lecture by Hojjat al-Islam Mirbagheri on the societal impact of Imam Hussein and Imam Sajjad's martyrdom. The main segment featured Zainab and Sadat Hosseini, the wife of martyr Seyed Ahmad Hosseini from Afghanistan. Zainab recounted her husband's final emotional calls and his last will before his final operation in the Lazqiyeh hills. She described the agonizing wait for news and her unsuccessful attempts to identify his body among the wounded in hospitals. The broadcast concluded by referencing Imam Hussein's uprising, emphasizing that its primary goal was to preserve truth and faith rather than merely seeking political power. The program ended with expressions of solidarity with martyrs' families and a call to follow the path of the Infallibles.
