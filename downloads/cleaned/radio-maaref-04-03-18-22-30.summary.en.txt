This program focuses on the figure of Maysam Tammar, a monotheist hero and narrator of the Hadith of Ghadir, emphasizing the importance of trust in transmitting Islamic teachings. Maysam, known for his accurate prophecies regarding his own martyrdom, engages in a courageous dialogue with Ibn Ziyad in prison, refusing to compromise his principles even under the threat of execution. The text highlights that Islam is a comprehensive package consisting of the Quran, Sunnah, consensus, and reason, with hadiths being an inseparable part of this religious framework. It also discusses eloquence and expression as a divine gift essential for defending truth and attracting hearts. Finally, the program offers recommendations for giving gifts on the Day of Ghadir and stresses the importance of transparency and alignment between spouses in building a spiritual family.
