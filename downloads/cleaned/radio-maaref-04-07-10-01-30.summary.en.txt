The program begins by highlighting the spiritual and political significance of Hazrat Masoumeh (SA), citing how prominent scholars like Sadr al-Muta'allihin and Ayatollah Najafi Mar'ashi sought her intercession for philosophical solutions and protection from oppressors.
Historical accounts reveal that her shrine served as a safe haven for political asylum seekers, officially recognized as such even during the Qajar era.
The second part of the show features a heartfelt narrative from the mother of Martyr Hassan Bagheri, describing his virtuous character, practical faith, and deep commitment to serving the community.
She recounts the tragic final moments of her son's life during the Fajr 1 operation, detailing his martyrdom and the miraculous repetition of Imam Mahdi's name while he was in a semi-coma.
The mother expresses the profound peace she felt upon receiving the news of his martyrdom, attributing it to divine mercy despite the immense grief.
She also laments the loss of her son's will, which was misplaced amidst the chaotic and massive public reception of his funeral procession.
These stories collectively portray the self-sacrifice of the war veterans and the remarkable patience and resilience of the martyrs' families in defending Islamic values.
