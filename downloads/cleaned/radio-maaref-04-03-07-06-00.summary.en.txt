This radio program argues that God cannot be known through mental concepts, analogies, or logical reasoning, as the divine essence transcends the realm of the mind.
Citing the Quranic verse stating that most believers are polytheists, the speaker explains that this occurs when people perceive God through limited material intermediaries or abstract ideas.
The solution to this conceptual polytheism is shifting from acquired knowledge to direct, intuitive perception, where God is known through Himself rather than external tools.
According to Islamic narrations, God is closer to humans than they are to themselves, and the only barrier between the human soul and the Divine is the self's own ignorance and distraction.
True faith requires recognizing God as the sole, absolute cause of all events, avoiding the attribution of direct effects to secondary agents or intermediaries.
There are different levels of spiritual knowledge, and as a person progresses in self-awareness and divine proximity, their intuitive vision of God becomes deeper and stronger.
Ultimately, the text concludes that authentic knowledge of God requires self-discovery and the removal of mental veils to perceive the Divine essence directly and without mediation.
