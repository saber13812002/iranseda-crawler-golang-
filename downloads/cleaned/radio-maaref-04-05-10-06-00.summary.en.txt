In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Rabbani Golpaygani analyzes the theological placement of the Verse of Purification within the context of the Prophet's wives. He refutes the objection that the Prophet's prayer for the Ahl al-Bayt was redundant, arguing that it served to emphasize the issue's importance and request the continuity of divine grace. The core argument distinguishes between initial guidance and its sustained preservation, noting that even the Infallibles require God's constant support to maintain their spiritual state. The scholar also addresses the critique by Dehlawi regarding the timing of the prayer relative to the verse's revelation, clarifying that the supplication reflects a perpetual dependence on divine mercy. Finally, the discussion highlights the profound difference between worship driven by fear of punishment and that rooted in spiritual love and awareness.
