In the radio program "Persman Ateghadi" on Radio Ma'aref, Professor Mohammad Sharoudi addressed various theological and religious questions from listeners. He emphasized the importance of seeking knowledge as a primary means of becoming beloved to God and discussed the five different scholarly views regarding the reproduction of Adam's descendants. Responding to inquiries about the relationship between calamities and sins, he explained that hardships can serve either as punishment for past sins or as a necessary process for spiritual purification and growth. He also affirmed the positive impact of sending blessings to the deceased for the forgiveness of their debts, while noting that making forgiveness conditional on the value of the blessings is not logically sound. The professor clarified the theological distinction between attributing good and evil to God in the contexts of creation (Tawqin) and legislation (Tashri'), asserting that evil is legislatively the result of human free will. Finally, regarding concentration in prayer, he advised against closing the eyes as a primary method, instead recommending practices such as focusing on the meanings of the recitations and visualizing one's presence before God.
