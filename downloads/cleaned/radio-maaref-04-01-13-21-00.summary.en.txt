The radio program "Parsaiyan" began with a Quranic interpretation of Surah Al-Ma'idah, followed by a discussion on the importance of knowledge and goal-setting in spiritual growth. Hujjat al-Islam Vahidi highlighted the life of Ayatollah Mirza Hashem Aghili, emphasizing the need to focus on scientific objectives while avoiding political distractions. The main segment featured the captivity memoirs of Mr. Nazeri, who described his experiences in Iraqi prisons, including interrogation tactics and strategies for resisting torture. A significant part of his account involved the prisoners' covert efforts to acquire a radio to stay informed, using clever methods to hide it from Iraqi forces. The program concluded with a book review of "The Last Seven Days" by Mohammad Reza Bayrami, underscoring the lessons of freedom, hope, and resilience learned during the war.
