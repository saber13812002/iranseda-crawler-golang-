In this radio program, Dr. Pourhosseini emphasized the importance of dietary and hydration principles during autumn based on traditional medicine. He explained that autumn is characterized by a cold and dry temperament, making the consumption of ice-cold water harmful as it triggers bad humors. It was recommended that water temperature be adjusted according to individual constitution, with even warm-temperament individuals drinking less cold water than in summer. The speaker clarified that ice is not considered a food item in traditional medicine and should only be used indirectly to cool water. In the second segment, Hujjat al-Islam Rasol Hafez discussed the concept of trust (Amanah) in Islam, citing the relevant verse from the Quran. He stressed that humans are responsible for safeguarding divine trusts, including their bodies and property, and must avoid any personal misuse. Finally, the program touched upon the peaceful nature of the Islamic Revolution in Iran compared to other historical upheavals.
