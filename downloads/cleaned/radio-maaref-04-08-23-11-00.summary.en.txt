The Supreme Religious Authorities met with the head of the Judiciary, emphasizing the need for special attention to cultural, economic, and social issues. The 22nd International Conference of Husseini Servants was held in Mashhad, gathering participants from Iran and other countries. A ceremony was held to commemorate the martyrdom of Hassan Tehrani Moghaddam, highlighting his role in Iran's defensive power. An academic session at the Imam Foundation focused on the history of the Prophetic Legacy and launched a new book on the subject. A nationwide mobile phone alert drill was conducted to test public warning systems in several cities. The Prayer for Rain was performed in Mashhad and other locations due to the prevailing drought. The "Zamzam Ahkam" program addressed various jurisprudential questions from followers of different clerics, covering topics such as backbiting, khums, and inheritance.
