Dr. Khosrow Ebrahimi Nia, referencing the German philosopher Hegel, argues that true leadership requires deep thinking and continuous learning rather than relying on nepotism or past credentials. He emphasizes that officials must upgrade their capabilities through study and action, avoiding negative criticism that offers no constructive solutions. Ebrahimi Nia describes rescuing Nader Ivaizi, a paralyzed Asian medalist who had withdrawn from public life, as the most rewarding moment of his career. By supporting Ivaizi, he helped the athlete return to the sport and win a gold medal for Iran at an international competition. This experience illustrates how a human-centric approach and proper thinking can unlock hidden talents within society for national glory. He concludes by stressing the importance of genuine cultural development and raising children who achieve maturity through experience and effort.
