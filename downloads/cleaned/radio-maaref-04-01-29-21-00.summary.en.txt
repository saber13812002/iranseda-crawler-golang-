In the Parsaiyan program, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the importance of critical thinking and awareness of current events, citing Imam Sadiq and Ayatollah Misbah Yazdi. He highlighted the role of religious scholars in guiding people out of intellectual confusion and referenced Ayatollah Hakim's letter to Shahid Sadr regarding the need to distinguish Islam from communism. The discussion also included Shahid Motahhari's firm stance against communists before the revolution and his rebuttal to Ayatollah Lahuti's concerns about maintaining unity against the Shah. The program then transitioned to a narrative story titled "Glass Marbles," where a student named Sara is caught in a riot and discovers a connection to an injured Basij youth. Despite being wounded while defending him, both are eventually rescued by medical personnel. The episode concluded with a message encouraging listeners to contribute to national peace through small, wise daily actions.
