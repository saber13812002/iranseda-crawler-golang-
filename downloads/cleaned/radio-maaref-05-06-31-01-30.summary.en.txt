The speech examines ten distinct characteristics of Hazrat Fatima Masumeh (SA) that set her apart from other Imams' descendants.
Her primary feature is the certainty and uniqueness of her burial site in Qom, unlike other figures with multiple shrines.
Four infallible Imams, including Imam Sadiq (AS) before her birth, recommended visiting her, equating her visitation to that of Imam Reza (AS).
Her specific ziyarat text was directly transmitted by Imam Reza (AS), and she is recognized as a hadith narrator who transmitted significant narrations.
Her migration from Medina to Qom to see her brother is considered a divine act explicitly referenced in the Quran.
Her presence in Qom facilitated the establishment of the Qom seminary, leading to the training of hundreds of major clerics and scholars.
Other attributes include her loyalty to the Imamate, her global intercession, and numerous miracles confirmed by high-ranking clerics.
The speech concludes by emphasizing the importance of visiting with true knowledge and praying for the joy and relief of the Ahl al-Bayt.
