This radio program examined the theological question of why the Prophet (PBUH) delayed the announcement of the Hadith of Ghadir.
Critics argue that this hesitation and the request for time from Gabriel contradict the Prophet's absolute submission and infallibility.
The response, citing Sayyid Ibn Tawus, draws a parallel to the story of Prophet Musa (AS) and his fear of being killed by Pharaoh.
According to this analysis, the Prophet (PBUH) was not concerned for his own safety but feared that hypocrites would frame the event as tribal favoritism.
This caution stemmed from his deep compassion for the Ummah and his awareness of the unprecedented sensitivity of the Ghadir gathering.
The discussion also referenced a verse from Surah Al-Ahzab, illustrating the Prophet's prudent approach to sensitive social matters to prevent fitna.
The conclusion is that this delay was not a sign of weak faith but a wise strategy to maintain unity and prevent enemy exploitation.
