The program began by congratulating the birth of Imam Hassan Askari (PBUH) and then reviewed the weather forecast, highlighting stable conditions in most regions with potential rain in the north and dust storms in the west and center. The main segment focused on the ethical roots of arrogance and how humans are deceived by the glitter of worldly life. Professor Fatemi, citing Quranic verses, identified four primary causes of arrogance: internal psychological illusions, worldly decorations, Satan's temptations, and the influence of social media. He defined "decoration" as the act of making a defective or worthless thing appear good, labeling it a key factor in deceiving people. A crucial point was that humans are not deceived by life itself, but by its superficial appearances and byproducts, which constitutes a moral vice. Finally, it was advised that to escape arrogance, one must understand the concept of asceticism and avoid being enslaved by material displays and false praise.
