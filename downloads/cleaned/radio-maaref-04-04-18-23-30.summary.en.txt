The radio program "Baran-e Mehrbani" began with an emotional song depicting the suffering of mothers and the cries of children.
The host expressed gratitude to the listeners, praising the generosity and empathy of the Iranian people in helping those in need.
Referencing messages from listeners facing their own hardships, the host emphasized the importance of even small financial contributions.
He explained that these donations are made completely anonymously to ensure they are free from ostentation and carry greater spiritual merit.
Listeners were invited to participate by sending a text message to 5152 to receive a bank card for transferring their donations.
Towards the end, the program announced a memorial ceremony for Martyr Sardar Mohammad Saeed Izadi (Haj Ramadan) at the Prophet's Mosque in Tehran.
The broadcast concluded with the recitation of the Ziyarat of Al-Yasin and prayers for the martyrs and the audience.
