Ashoor Gholamohammadi, a veteran basketball player and coach, shared his career highlights with teams such as Homa, Islamic Azad University, and Pikan during the interview. He highlighted the pivotal role of Dr. Ghadimi and Ghorbani Vafa in establishing the Azad University basketball team and achieving multiple domestic and international championships. Gholamohammadi also discussed his coaching tenure at Pikan and Homa, noting the organizational challenges and the significant support provided by club administrators. He emphasized that Hamed Aghdaei's presence was the primary factor behind the national team's success and their Asian Championship victory, describing him as a rare exception in Iranian basketball history. Finally, Gholamohammadi expressed gratitude for his collaborations with distinguished professors and friends at Radio Varzesh, stressing the importance of maintaining strong human relationships in the sports world.
