In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Mandegari outlines the characteristics of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) as a bringer of glad tidings and a warner, based on Quranic verses. He emphasizes that the Prophet removes the chains of slavery to other than God, guiding humanity toward true freedom and divine servitude. The core argument is that accepting external oppression, such as that from the US and Israel, stems from self-oppression and the rejection of God's sovereignty, which leads to servitude to Satan and tyrants. The speaker highlights the global wave of resistance against injustice as a sign of people finally responding to the Prophet's 1,400-year-old call and awakening from spiritual slumber. He stresses that witnessing to the Prophet's mission and the Imamate, particularly that of Imam Mahdi (AJ), is essential for complete faith and achieving eternal salvation. Finally, listeners are urged to follow the Prophet's example and support the system of Wilayat to avoid unforgivable sins, such as sowing division within the Islamic Ummah.
