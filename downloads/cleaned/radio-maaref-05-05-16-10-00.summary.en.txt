In this radio program, Professor Amir-Mohsen Erfan analyzed the social relationships within the culture of Arbaeen, describing them as pure, responsible, conscious, and based on self-sacrifice. He emphasized that Arbaeen is not merely a mourning ceremony but a voluntary spiritual experience that must translate into faithful action and behavior throughout the year. A significant threat to this culture is the "sacrificial" or Christian-like perspective that views Imam Hussein's martyrdom solely as atonement for the sins of the community, which hinders practical behavioral change. Conversely, the correct Islamic view holds that mourning is a path to God and a model for emulating Imam Hussein's self-sacrifice to save humanity from ignorance. The discussion also referenced historical and narrational evidence regarding the direct sighting of Imam Zaman in infancy and testimonies related to his birth. The implication of this analysis is that expectants should embody the Husseini model in their daily lives through altruism, serving others, and avoiding material expectations. The ultimate goal is to transform the culture of Arbaeen into a sustainable value system where love for the Imam is manifested in social actions and conduct.
