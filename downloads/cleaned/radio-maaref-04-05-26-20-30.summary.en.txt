The radio program "Tale of Freedom" features the memoirs of Seyyed Ibrahim Bagheri, a veteran captured during the Karbala 2 operation. He describes the severe physical and psychological torture, extreme starvation, and inhumane conditions faced by Iranian prisoners of war in Iraq. Bagheri recounts the martyrdom of two fellow prisoners due to enemy brutality and the captors' relentless attempts to break their will through humiliation. Despite these immense pressures, faith in God and the memory of Imam Khomeini served as the primary spiritual support for the prisoners. Following the acceptance of Resolution 598, negotiations led to the honorable return of the POWs to their homeland in 1989. This return was met with warm national celebrations, symbolizing the spiritual victory of the Iranian people in the eight-year war. The program concludes by recommending the book "Nights of Power in Karbala 5" as a documented account of the soldiers' bravery.
