In this program, Professor Hosseini Qomi emphasizes the supreme status of Tawhid and the role of prostration in prayer as the peak of humility and willpower. Relying on narrations, he demonstrates that God controls all apparent causes and is the savior of believers even in the most difficult circumstances. The central point of the discussion is the distinction between the world, which is given to everyone, and piety, which is reserved only for God's chosen ones. The Imams (AS) advised that one should sacrifice one's life to preserve one's faith, as losing one's religion signifies spiritual destruction and plunder. The health of one's faith is considered the most important asset, even in the case of martyrdom. Finally, the Quran is introduced as a light and guide in all hardships, emphasizing the necessity of relying on God's absolute power rather than fearing apparent causes.
