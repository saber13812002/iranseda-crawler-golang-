At the closing ceremony of the Khorasan Razavi Martyrs' Congress, Ghalibaf emphasized that the Iranian people's resistance is deeply rooted in the culture of sacrifice and martyrdom. Other news items included the start of pre-registration for the 1405 Umrah Hajj and the publication of the first volume of the Biographies of Mahdavi Authors by the Maboud Cultural Institute. The President also offered condolences following a helicopter crash in Ashtian that resulted in two fatalities. The head of the Endowments Organization announced the launch of an innovation fund to ensure the financial sustainability of Quranic activities. The Tehran Prosecutor stressed the importance of maintaining cultural norms and social security by cracking down on illegal cafes and events. The program also featured religious rulings from various Maraji' regarding proxy Hajj, fasting expiation, the khums of books, and the Aqiqah ritual.
