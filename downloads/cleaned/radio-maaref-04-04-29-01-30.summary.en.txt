This radio program presents an emotional narrative of the Battle of Karbala, focusing on the martyrdom of Ali Akbar, the eldest son of Imam Hussein (PBUH). The speaker highlights the similarities between Ali Akbar and Abbas (PBUH) regarding their roles as water carriers and their manner of martyrdom. The story emphasizes the profound impact of this tragedy on their father, Ali (PBUH), describing his intense fear and anxiety during his son's absence from the battlefield. After observing a group of enemies attacking, Ali Akbar returns to his father with a broken heart upon seeing his wounded brother. In these final moments, Ali (PBUH) bids a sorrowful farewell to his son, who is on the verge of martyrdom. The narrative concludes with prayers for the reappearance of Imam Mahdi (AJ) and for peace and stability in the country.
