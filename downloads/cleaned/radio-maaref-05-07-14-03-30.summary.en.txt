The radio program "Zamzam Ahkam" addressed various religious inquiries from its listeners. Regarding prayer, it was clarified that repeating a recitation from the beginning after stopping halfway does not invalidate the prayer. A fast is considered valid if a person forgets to perform the ritual bath for major impurity, provided they had no opportunity to do so before the morning prayer. In matters of inheritance, paternal half-siblings do not inherit as long as full siblings are alive, whereas maternal half-siblings are entitled to a share. Surrogacy is deemed permissible only if it avoids illicit touching or looking and is driven by necessity. The expiation for intentionally breaking an oath involves feeding or clothing ten poor people, or fasting for three days if financially unable. Finally, a buyer has the right to cancel a transaction if a hidden defect is discovered, unless they were already aware of the defect at the time of purchase.
