The first segment of the broadcast narrates the story of Owis, a kind shepherd in the Yemen valley who finds joy in making children happy despite being mocked as a madman. Despite societal ridicule, Owis remains steadfast in his pursuit of truth and eventually departs for Mecca upon hearing news of the Prophet's emergence. The second segment features an interview with Dr. Mohsen Rezaei regarding Iran's defense against Israeli aggression. Rezaei asserts that Iran has successfully invalidated Israel's strategic calculations by breaching its air defense shield, placing the country in a position of strength. He describes the ongoing retaliation as a necessary measure to ensure future security and deterrence. Rezaei concludes that a decisive victory would not only elevate Iran's global prestige but also pave the way for a regional security union among neighboring nations.
