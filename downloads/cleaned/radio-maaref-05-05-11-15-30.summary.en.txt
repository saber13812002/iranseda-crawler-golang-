This radio program examined the principles governing Islamic criminal policy from jurisprudential and ethical perspectives.
The guests emphasized the connection between belief, ethics, and legal rulings, noting that piety and good character result from internalizing these three domains.
A key principle discussed is the belief in the afterlife, which serves as a stronger deterrent than worldly punishments.
It was explained that eternal punishments in hell for intentional murderers are incomparable to worldly retribution, and repentance is required for their remission.
The principle of presumption of innocence was also presented as a fundamental jurisprudential and legal rule.
This principle governs both legislation and judicial proceedings, requiring judges to prove guilt with conclusive evidence before conviction.
The program concluded with a live report on the spiritual atmosphere of the Arbaeen walk in Karbala, highlighting Islamic unity.
The broadcast ended with the recitation and translation of verses from Surah Zumar, serving as a divine reminder and warning against the hereafter's punishment.
