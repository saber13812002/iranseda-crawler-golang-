This radio program provided jurisprudential answers to audience questions based on the rulings of various religious authorities. The discussion began with the rules of prayer during travel and the obligation of a follower to select a living marja after the death of their current one. The validity of a specific type of commercial transaction was also confirmed from a jurisprudential perspective. Regarding women's rulings, the program explained that bleeding lasting more than ten days is considered istihadah, requiring specific prayer and fasting obligations. It was stated that a fasting person may apply cream or ointment to their skin, but actions like eye drops or collyrium that might reach the throat are discouraged or forbidden. Finally, the two essential pillars of a temporary marriage contract, which are the specified duration and the mahr, were identified as necessary for its validity. The program concluded by sharing contact information for future questions and reciting Quranic verses and supplications.
