In this radio program, Dr. Mashkibaf emphasized the necessity of reviving "collaborative governance" in the fifth decade of the revolution, based on the Supreme Leader's messages. He argued that public participation must not lead to structural disruption but should operate within specific models aligned with the government's macro policies. An example of this synergy is the cooperation between universities in providing novel technical solutions and local communities in accelerating infrastructure projects. The ultimate goal of this approach is to transform social capital into institutional capital and move toward realizing an Islamic society and a new Islamic civilization. The discussion also stressed the importance of preserving collective memory by documenting oral and written narratives of recent events for future generations. Finally, citing resource limitations, citizens were urged to manage their consumption of water, electricity, and gasoline to support the national economy while standing by the system.
