In this program, Ayatollah Karimi Jafari emphasizes the importance of choosing a pious and purified preacher to illuminate hearts, citing Nahj al-Balagha. He recounts a story by Ayatollah Farid Iraqi to warn that attending scholarly gatherings should aim for self-purification rather than merely collecting material for sermons. The Quran, Nahj al-Balagha, and the words of the Infallibles are identified as pure sources that cleanse the soul from impurities. The second part of the broadcast focuses on a jurisprudential discussion regarding the principle of "Asl al-Masibat" in Usul al-Fiqh. It critically examines the objections raised by Muhaqqiq Naeini against Sheikh Ansari concerning the concept of "customary leniency" or "masamihat." The discussion revolves around the distinction between noticeable and inadvertent errors in public understanding. The conclusion suggests that when the public makes inadvertent errors regarding hidden intermediaries, one must rely on the literal scope of the text. This analysis highlights that accurate jurisprudential understanding requires careful attention to the boundaries of public comprehension and common errors.
