This radio program primarily discusses the nature of the Imam's knowledge and how it differs from God's infinite, intrinsic knowledge. The instructor emphasizes that the Imam's knowledge is not innate or limitless but is acquired through sources such as the Quran, the Prophet's teachings, and divine inspiration. Reaching high spiritual levels, such as knowing the "Greatest Name" of God, requires both the spiritual station of manifestation and the knowledge of specific words, a process achieved through persistent prayer and worship. The example of Imam Sجاد (AS) illustrates that he prayed for a year to receive this knowledge and the divine permission to use it, demonstrating that even Imams must seek knowledge from God. It was also explained that the Imam's acquired knowledge is sometimes withheld from action for the sake of expediency; for instance, Imam Reza (AS) chose not to explicitly predict his martyrdom to preserve the interests of the community. In the Q&A section, the differences between the Akhbari and Usuli schools of thought regarding the interpretation of religious texts were addressed, highlighting the necessity of rational analysis. The program concluded with a sermon on controlling one's ego and prioritizing the interests of Islam over personal desires and the pursuit of power.
