The radio program began by honoring the 16th anniversary of Ayatollah Behjat's passing, highlighting his deep devotion to the Ahlulbayt and his emphasis on visiting Karbala.
Guest Mr. Mostafa Vafi introduced the international "Martyr of Service" campaign aimed at preserving the legacy of the late President Ebrahim Raisi.
The campaign features a book translated into eight languages, which has already been downloaded by over 1,500 participants from more than 70 countries.
Winners of the campaign will receive free travel to Mashhad and Qom to visit the holy shrines, with the initiative continuing until the Eid of Qurban.
The guest also detailed Raisi's profound spiritual connection to Imam Hussein, noting his personal recitations of Rawda and his efforts to facilitate pilgrims' passage through borders.
His emotional tears during mourning ceremonies and his consistent presence in religious gatherings were cited as evidence of his sincere faith.
The program concluded with an invitation for listeners to join the campaign and a prayer for the martyrs, including President Raisi and his companions.
