In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Mohammad Hadi Fallah emphasizes the importance of "change" and seizing life's fleeting moments to achieve true devotion before it is too late. Citing the Quranic verse "Rabb arji'ni," he warns that late-life regrets are irreversible and urges listeners to press the "change button" every morning with the intention of returning to God. The core of the discussion focuses on the vice of lying, which is identified as the most dangerous moral flaw that destroys faith, religion, and manliness. The speaker explains that lying not only leads to spiritual darkness and divine disguidance but also acts as a chain reaction, driving individuals toward hypocrisy and other sins. He distinguishes between an occasional liar and a "Kadhab" (a habitual liar whose nature is rooted in falsehood), stressing the absolute necessity of avoiding this vice. The program concludes with a strong call for honesty and truthfulness, advising the audience to guard their tongues to avoid the severe consequences of lying and to remain on the path of spiritual salvation.
