Yemeni armed forces have halted Israeli and American shipping in the Red Sea using missiles and drones, forcing Washington to alter its military doctrine. Simultaneously, the direct involvement of British fighter jets in bombing Yemen has raised serious questions in the UK Parliament regarding the use of Cyprus bases. In occupied Palestine, widespread fires near Jerusalem have displaced tens of thousands and led to the arrest of 18 individuals for allegedly starting the blaze. The works of Martyr Motahhari, translated into Malay, are enjoying significant popularity in Southeast Asia, while Malaysia has proposed a joint halal industry council with Indonesia. In Vietnam, the historic Noor Mosque in the north requires renovation to accommodate the growing Muslim population. Thailand hosted an Islamic finance conference, and protests continue in Turkey over the arrest of Istanbul's mayor. Finally, Seoul's Central Mosque was reopened after renovations featuring Islamic calligraphy and tile work.
