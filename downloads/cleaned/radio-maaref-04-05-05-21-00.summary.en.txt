The program begins by emphasizing the importance of serving believers and appreciating divine blessings. Hujjat al-Islam Vahidi cites narrations from the Ahl al-Bayt to highlight that relieving the needs of others is a fundamental duty for Muslims. To illustrate this, stories of the generosity of Ayatollah Tiareh and Ayatollah Shafie in helping the needy, including non-Muslims, are recounted. The literary segment then presents a narrative from the book "Neshan-e Hossein," depicting the tense moments of Hazrat Zainab and Nafigh before Karbala. This story highlights the mother's anxiety about impending tragedies and her sons' readiness to accompany Imam Hussein (AS), emphasizing the martyrdom of servants and children. In the final section, the difference between lying and "Turi" (evasive answering) in daily speech is explained based on the Supreme Leader's fatwa. The program concludes with announcements of future episodes and thanks to the listeners.
