In this radio program, Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallah examines the moral vice of "negative judgment" and its destructive impact on social relationships. He defines incorrect judgment not as a legal ruling, but as a major sin rooted in suspicion, snooping, and nosiness, stemming from educational deficits and self-centeredness. Citing Quranic verses and Prophetic traditions, the discussion emphasizes that believers must avoid baseless assumptions and strictly protect the privacy and honor of their fellow Muslims. A key point highlighted is the necessity of practical reparation and seeking forgiveness, which goes beyond verbal apologies and may require restoring the victim's reputation or providing financial compensation. The program also warns that listening to others' judgments fosters a negative culture, urging listeners to break this cycle through piety and self-improvement. Finally, the stark contrast between the richness of Shia educational resources and the poverty of practical application in many individuals is criticized, calling for a life that aligns with religious beliefs through ethical action.
