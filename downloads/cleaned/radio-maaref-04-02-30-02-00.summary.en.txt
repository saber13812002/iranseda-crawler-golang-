In this radio program, responses were provided to listeners' theological and jurisprudential questions. First, it was explained that no book, including "Tasakirat al-Awliya," is absolutely endorsed without flaws and must be examined with critical scrutiny. Regarding the training of the nafs (ego), the analogy of a truck on a steep hill was used to emphasize that one must neither completely abandon the nafs nor overburden it to the point of losing control; instead, a balance between restraint and keeping it alive is essential. The practical solution for achieving this balance was identified as adhering to the Quran, the traditions of the Ahl al-Bayt, and consulting reliable religious scholars. Additionally, the program affirmed the concept of bodily resurrection, clarified the correct performance of the Eid al-Fitr night prayer, and stressed the importance of rule of law over personal revenge. Finally, it was stated that backbiting officials is generally haram, and the jurisprudential exception regarding judges does not imply that backbiting any official is permissible, except in specific cases such as public sin or professional consultation.
