In this program, Dr. Javad Jafari emphasizes the Quranic prohibition against predicting the time of the Mahdi's appearance, warning listeners against believing such false claims. He analyzes the historical failures during the era of Imam Hasan (AS) as the result of a "soft war" and "cognitive war" waged by the Umayyads. Muawiyah weakened the will of the elite and the public through psychological manipulation, enemy glamorization, and the distortion of narrations to isolate the Imam. This psychological tactic even affected loyal commanders like Ubaydullah ibn Abbas, who were influenced by the engineered environment. Dr. Jafari warns that the enemy is currently repeating this strategy through media dominance and media imperialism to exaggerate the enemy's power and diminish the truth. Therefore, awaiters must maintain vigilance and correct perception of right and wrong to avoid calculation errors and preserve their loyalty. The program also highlights that the key to meeting the Imam of the Time (AJ) is purity, abstention from sin, and possessing deep faith and insight.
