This radio program first addressed the structural changes to the Jamarat al-Aqaba, emphasizing the need to follow one's marja's ruling regarding the new 35-meter wall for stone throwing. The discussion then covered the rules of wudu, clarifying that impurity in the washing area invalidates the ritual, while impurity in the wiping area does not if the wiping is performed on a clean part. Subsequent Q&A segments addressed prayer rules for travelers intending to continue their journey, the permissibility of praying the night prayer while sitting for those with health issues, and the fact that lying does not invalidate daily prayers or fasting. The program also explained wudu and tayammum requirements for individuals with casts or bandages, as well as how to perform precautionary prayers while seated. Regarding the duty of enjoining good, advising a spouse to pay khums or lead prayer is considered sufficient unless one is certain they have not acted. Finally, the minimum clothing required for men praying in a bathroom was discussed, along with the significance of prayer as the primary criterion for the acceptance of other deeds.
