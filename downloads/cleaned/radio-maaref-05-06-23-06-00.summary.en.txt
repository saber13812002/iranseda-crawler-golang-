Ayatollah Javadi Amoli, in his exegesis of verses 37 to 39 of Surah Al-A'raf, emphasizes that the divine call "O children of Adam" is a shared message among all prophets, not exclusive to the Prophet of Islam. He explains that salvation from the terrors of the Day of Judgment and cosmic upheavals requires not just faith or formal Islam, but genuine piety and righteous action. On that day, the followers of disbelief will protest against their leaders, cursing them and blaming them for their misguidance. The Quranic interpretation reveals that the fuel of the Hellfire is the disbelievers themselves; the leaders of disbelief act as the explosive material, while their followers serve as the wood. This social structure of disbelief in this world is replicated in the Hereafter, maintaining the same dynamics of leadership and following. Ultimately, God allows the followers to demand that the punishment of their leaders be doubled, as they led society toward destruction through arrogance and the rejection of divine signs.
