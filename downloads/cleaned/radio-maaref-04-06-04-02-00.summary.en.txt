In the Parsiyan program, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the necessity of justice and the rejection of nepotism, even towards close relatives, citing the story of Noah and the conduct of scholars like Ayatollah Qodsavi and Hasani. He illustrated that in Islam, truth and rights take precedence over familial ties, as demonstrated by these scholars' firmness against their own children. Subsequently, the Mashq-e Rah program featured Professor Faghih discussing the concept of monotheistic ethics. He explained that creation is a continuous and perpetual act of God, and humans must cultivate awareness of this constant divine grace within themselves. Relying on the Quranic verse "Attend to your own selves," he stressed the importance of self-knowledge and recognizing absolute dependence on God to rectify behavior. He also offered solutions for vices such as keeping score and having expectations from others, advising that all service should be performed solely for God's sake without expecting rewards from people. Finally, he recommended strengthening reason, adhering to religious principles, and constantly seeking refuge in God through prayer and remembrance to prevent errors.
