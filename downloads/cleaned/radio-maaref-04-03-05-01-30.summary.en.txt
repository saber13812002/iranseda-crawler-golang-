The program begins with a sermon emphasizing the necessity of obedience to the Supreme Leader and criticizing the choice of leaders less qualified than the people.
Citing a hadith from the Prophet, it is argued that a community which ignores its most knowledgeable and sincere leader will inevitably decline.
The speaker asserts that returning to the path of Velayat-e Faqih is the key to reviving the Axis of Resistance and preparing for the reappearance of the Imam.
The narrative then shifts to the story from the book "Sobz Abi," where a Muslim carpenter named Seyed Fereydoun falls in love with Jacqueline, a Christian girl.
Jacqueline's father, David, orders Fereydoun to be kidnapped and crucified due to their religious differences and his own resentment.
With the help of Master Fakhr, Jacqueline rescues Fereydoun and hides him at the master's house to recover from his injuries.
Seeking a solution, Jacqueline visits Judge Saad, the head of the library, to secretly complain about her father's injustice.
Finally, Jacqueline confronts her father with courage, defending Fereydoun's right to life, while David admits he intended to kill him.
