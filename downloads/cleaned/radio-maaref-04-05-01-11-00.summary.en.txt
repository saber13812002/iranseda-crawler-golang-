The radio program begins by urging the public to join the Friday march on August 21 to condemn Israeli crimes in Gaza. Reports highlight the severe humanitarian crisis in Gaza, including extreme hunger and a death toll exceeding 59,000 Palestinians. The President affirmed that uranium enrichment will continue within international legal frameworks and that Iran is prepared to defend itself. The Head of the Judiciary emphasized the importance of national unity and the Supreme Leader's pivotal role in crisis management. The program also reported the martyrdom of three IRGC aerospace forces personnel during a recent attack. Additionally, it was announced that over 35,000 people have registered for the Arbaeen walk via the Samah system. In the jurisprudential segment, a cleric clarified that performing tayammum solely to preserve makeup is not religiously permissible.
