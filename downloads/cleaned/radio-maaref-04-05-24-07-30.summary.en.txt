The program began with religious supplications and praise for the Ahl al-Bayt before transitioning to a detailed discussion on the vice of lying. Lying was defined as the distortion of truth that endangers the material and spiritual interests of others. Citing religious narrations, the instructor identified lying as a major sin rooted in the animalistic aspect of human nature, which conflicts with reason and divine law. The destructive effects of lying extend beyond personal ethics, leading to the ruin of the heart and the core of human existence. A liar is considered an enemy of God due to their opposition to monotheistic truths, a hostility that extends to all creation. Consequently, the Shia community is advised to distance itself from liars and embrace truthfulness as a path to self-improvement. The discussion on the consequences of lying was postponed to the next episode, and the program concluded with blessings upon the Ahl al-Bayt.
