In this radio program, the speaker emphasizes the necessity of accepting God's wisdom in the face of painful and unknown events, citing the 31st letter of Imam Ali.
Through the story of Moses and Khidr, the speaker illustrates that many seemingly incorrect actions or occurrences are actually hidden behind divine wisdom.
The speaker warns against hasty judgment, advising that when we lack knowledge, we should attribute our confusion to our own ignorance rather than questioning God's will.
It is highlighted that admitting to not knowing is not shameful but rather a sign of humility and a pathway to further growth and learning.
The program also discusses the historical and revolutionary role of the bazaar in defending Islam and supporting the Islamic Revolution, from the Tobacco Protest to the present day.
The bazaar is portrayed as a leading class in national and religious resistance, consistently standing alongside other societal groups against tyranny and imperialism.
Finally, the speaker calls for patience and trust in God's mercy, urging listeners to hold fast to the guardianship of the Imam and remain hopeful in times of hardship.
