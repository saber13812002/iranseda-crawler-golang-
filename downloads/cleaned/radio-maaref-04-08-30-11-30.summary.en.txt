In the radio program "Porsaman Mahdavi," expert Mehdi Yusefian addressed listeners' questions by emphasizing the significant and certain role of women during the reappearance of the Imam of the Time. Citing religious narrations, he highlighted the presence of fifty distinguished women among the Imam's highest-ranking companions, naming figures like Siyana, Sumayya, and Naysiba as examples of these courageous believers. Another key point discussed was the possibility of "raj'ah," or returning to life, for believers who pass away before the reappearance but have genuinely prepared for it. Yusefian explained that angels will inform these individuals in their graves that they may return to the world to assist the Imam. This return is not limited to a specific group but is available to any believer who has cultivated the readiness to support the Imam. He also referenced the Dua al-Ehd, stressing the importance of practical preparation and striving to fulfill the Imam's will to seize this divine opportunity. The program concluded by discussing the importance of Friday prayers as a symbol of Islam's political and social power and its role in neutralizing enemy plots.
