In the first segment of the program, Professor Barjkar rejects the theoretical foundations of "rational good and evil," arguing that human intellect inherently perceives the goodness and badness of actions without needing complex theological justifications. He asserts that this innate rational perception serves as God's primary proof over His servants, establishing the deservingness of punishment for sinners on a rational rather than abstract basis. Following this theological discussion, the program shifts focus to the "Day of Parents and Teachers" and the national "Iran Jan Fars Iran" campaign. The hosts, broadcasting live from Shiraz, introduced the region's culture and art, inviting listeners to participate by completing verses of Hafez Shirazi and sending warm messages. The segment also commemorated historical events of September 15, including the burning of Kerman's Grand Mosque and the start of the Khorramshahr operation, to keep the memory of Iran's resistance alive.
