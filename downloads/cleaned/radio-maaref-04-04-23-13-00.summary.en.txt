In this program, Seyyed Ahmad Khatami interprets the slogan "Labbaik ya Hussein" as a pledge of absolute obedience and sacrifice for the sake of religion. He emphasizes that everything, including life, wealth, and reputation, must be sacrificed to preserve faith, drawing parallels to Imam Hussein's divine command to march to Kufa. Khatami highlights steadfastness in religion, family responsibility in upholding values, and the observance of hijab as fundamental components of this devotion. He also asserts the identity of the Ja'fari Twelver Shia school as the official and immutable religion of Iran, citing the Constitution to defend this heritage. The segment concludes with a poignant account of Zaynab's (SA) resilience and courage in the aftermath of Karbala, urging listeners to keep their Hussaini and Shia identity alive.
