The program began by announcing the funeral procession of Ayatollah Shabiri Zanjani in Qom. The main segment featured a criminal case involving academic plagiarism between two master's students. The judge, relying on a university expert's testimony, confirmed that the article belonged to the complainant. The defendant and his accomplice were sentenced to heavy fines for violating intellectual property rights. Hujjat al-Islam Razaqi provided a legal interpretation emphasizing the Islamic and legal protection of individual rights. The broadcast concluded by discussing the historical role of Imam Reza and the Shia Imams in spreading Shia culture in Khorasan and Samarkand.
