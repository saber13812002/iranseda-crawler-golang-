This radio program covered key news from the Islamic world and domestic developments in Iran. The Iranian public demonstrated widespread support for Palestinian resistance, condemning the Israeli regime's violation of the Gaza ceasefire. Simultaneously, Yemen emphasized its readiness to counter any delays in implementing agreements, while reports indicated the gradual withdrawal of Israeli forces and the entry of humanitarian aid into Gaza. Other news segments analyzed the alarming rise in hate crimes against Muslims in the UK and the brain drain in Indonesia driven by youth unemployment. The broadcast also highlighted the construction of the North Kamil Mosque at the North Pole and the destruction of over 800 mosques in Gaza during the recent conflict. In a discussion segment, an expert deemed negotiations with the US futile and dangerous, citing the lack of trust in Western commitments following the JCPOA experience. The program concluded with a speech by Imam Khomeini emphasizing the necessity of officials being people-oriented and avoiding aristocratic tendencies within the Islamic system.
