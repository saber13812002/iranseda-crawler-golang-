The radio program announced a special live broadcast from Qom titled "Backpack" dedicated to the Arbaeen pilgrimage.
Faqih Vahidpour clarified that women's participation in the Arbaeen walk is religiously permissible without any doubt.
He argued that observing hijab and Islamic boundaries is fully maintained in these gatherings, citing precedents like Umrah.
It was emphasized that married women must obtain their husband's permission for such recommended travels to avoid religious issues.
The Q&A section addressed rulings on Quranic prostration, the conditions for Istikhara, and the impurity of certain animals.
Additionally, the obligation of Khums on funds set aside for funeral expenses but unused within the fiscal year was explained.
The program concluded by inviting listeners to financially support the Hazrat Masoumeh Mookab along the Arbaeen route.
