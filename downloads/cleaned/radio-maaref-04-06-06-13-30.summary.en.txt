The radio program "Narrative of Love" featured Mrs. Yaghoubi, the mother of martyr Mehdi Saberi, sharing memories of his final days in Syria. She recounted his request for patience from Hazrat Zainab (SA) and his heroic efforts to continue fighting despite severe injuries to his side and chest. The news of his martyrdom reached the family with a sense of peace, as his mother viewed his sacrifice as a source of pride and joy. The second segment analyzed Iran's military and diplomatic stance, emphasizing that Iran delivered a crushing response to enemy attacks. The discussion highlighted the national will and Iran's readiness to counter external threats while explicitly stating that the country did not seek war. The program concluded by affirming Iran's capabilities in various fields and expressing hope for a brighter future.
