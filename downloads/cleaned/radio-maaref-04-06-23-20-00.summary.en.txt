The Islamic Cooperation Organization's foreign ministers' meeting began in Qatar, focusing on recent Israeli aggression and serving as a precursor to the upcoming summit attended by Iran's president. Concurrently, an international conference on the Islamic resistance discourse in humanities is being held in Qom, highlighting the 77 years of Palestinian occupation and the legacy of martyr Nourallah. Global efforts to break the Gaza blockade reached a peak with a fleet of humanitarian aid ships departing from Italy and other nations. Alarming reports of rising Islamophobia in Australia were released, while the Iranian commander of the paramilitary forces visited Iraq to strengthen bilateral security cooperation. Al-Azhar established a museum to revive the scientific heritage of Muslim scholars, and Bangladesh requested technical assistance from the ICG for issuing halal certificates. In the cultural sector, the United Civilizations Forum in Russia emphasized preserving cultural diversity, and the Timbuktu manuscripts were recognized as a global heritage.
