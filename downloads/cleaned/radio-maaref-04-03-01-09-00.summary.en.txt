The radio program begins by explaining the concept of strategic location and various geopolitical positions, such as mathematical, relative, maritime, and central locations.
The West Asian region is highlighted for its central position at the intersection of three major continents, providing access to vital straits and energy resources.
This central location has led to the region being recognized as the "heart of the earth," making it a constant focus for global powers.
The second part of the program addresses a fake news story circulating on social media claiming that Iran ranks third in the world for average IQ.
This false claim was accompanied by fabricated images and interviews with officials and ordinary people, aiming to create a false sense of pride in society.
Experts subsequently explained that intelligence is a complex concept involving a combination of innate factors and environmental influences, such as education and social conditions.
The program concludes by warning about the dangers of fake news and emphasizing the importance of media literacy to distinguish truth from falsehood.
