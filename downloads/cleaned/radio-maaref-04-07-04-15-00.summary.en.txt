In this radio program, Professor Mehdi Meshkibaf analyzes Imam Khomeini's managerial strategy regarding the seizure of the US Embassy. The Imam labeled this popular action the "Second Revolution," supporting it to maintain revolutionary cohesion while creating a strategic basis for negotiations. Following the incident, Bazargan resigned, and the Imam accepted this to remove the National Front from power, thereby providing breathing room for the revolutionary current. During the hostage crisis, Iran leveraged pressure tactics to force the US to accept the Algiers Accords, even though Washington later violated its commitments. Brzezinski, the US National Security Advisor, admitted that the Imam defeated American foreign policy through steadfastness and profound international insight. The Imam's behavioral model, characterized by careful consultation, support for popular initiatives, and emphasis on national rights, demonstrated his political acumen and deep understanding. This approach enabled Iran to withstand global hegemony and clearly identify the root of US hostility, which stems from a desire to dominate Iran.
