In this radio program, the expert refutes the common belief that a spouse's infidelity or mistake signifies the end of the relationship, emphasizing that marriage can be rebuilt through counseling and awareness. He warns that social media promotes separation, causing significant cognitive damage to families, and suggests approaching a resistant spouse with kindness and a focus on improving the bond rather than assigning blame. In response to a listener, the discussion addressed how long-term family conflicts erode trust and the importance of healing old wounds. The expert advised against giving unsolicited advice, recommending instead that partners listen empathetically to their spouse's pain and express love and respect to foster healing. Patience is highlighted as crucial in the recovery process, with the suggestion to seek a mediator or counselor if challenges become overwhelming. Finally, the program concluded by recommending spiritual solace through visiting shrines and confiding in Imam Reza (AS) to find peace and hope.
