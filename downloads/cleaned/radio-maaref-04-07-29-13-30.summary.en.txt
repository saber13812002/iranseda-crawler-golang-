The radio program "Tale of Freedom" emphasizes the importance of patience and reliance on God during the Holy Defense and captivity, citing Quranic verses.
Mohammad Hossein Keshani, a freed prisoner, recounts his experiences in Iraqi camps, including the harsh conditions at Qasr-e Shibein.
He describes creative methods used by prisoners to memorize the Quran with needles and how they united to counter enemy and Meccarist attempts to create division.
Keshani highlights the physical suffering caused by diseases and water shortages, as well as the martyrdom of fellow prisoners due to the Iraqis' neglect of hygiene.
The program also narrates the story of Ali Moheddin's martyrdom in the Bostan operation, where he sacrificed himself to clear a path through barbed wire.
The guest stresses that performing prayers under the most severe captivity conditions represented the ultimate reliance on God and was a source of spiritual victory.
Finally, the program highlights the role of Jihadist groups in developing deprived areas and calls for the continuation of this movement to enhance the managerial understanding of the Islamic Revolution's generation.
