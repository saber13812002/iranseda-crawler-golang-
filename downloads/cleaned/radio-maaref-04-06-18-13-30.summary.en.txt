In the radio program "Mashq-e Rah," Professor Faqih discussed the importance of high aspirations in prayer within the context of monotheistic ethics. He emphasized that one should ask God for things that encompass all good, such as proximity to the divine and companionship with the saints, citing a narration from the Prophet (PBUH). Addressing listener questions, he advised relying on the intercession of the Ahl al-Bayt (AS), reciting the Quran, and writing Ayat al-Kursi with wudu and attention rather than resorting to superstition. Regarding depression, he suggested reducing false needs and practicing trust in God through divine remembrance and the recitation of Ayat al-Kursi as a spiritual remedy. The discussion concluded by highlighting the Prophet's (PBUH) role as a skilled physician who provided the ultimate cure for humanity's social and spiritual ailments.
