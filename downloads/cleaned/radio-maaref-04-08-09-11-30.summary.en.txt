The program begins with a literary narrative depicting the suffering and famine in a drought-stricken land, where a hungry man sells a valuable gem to meet the people's needs. Following this segment, the atmosphere shifts to longing for the appearance of Imam Mahdi, accompanied by religious poetry. The broadcast then reports on the grand Friday prayer held at the University of Tehran, led by the preacher, Hajj Ali Akbari. The preacher emphasizes the importance of Friday prayer and Muslim unity, citing hadiths and urging enemies to observe the unified rows of the congregation. Finally, the preacher concludes his sermon by calling for piety and spiritual readiness for the journey of life.
