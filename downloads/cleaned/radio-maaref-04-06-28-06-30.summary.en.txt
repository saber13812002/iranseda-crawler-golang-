The radio program begins with a discussion on divine justice and the inherent differences among humans. The speaker argues that ontological justice requires God to grant existence to any being with the potential for it, regardless of its level. The discussion then shifts to legislative and retributive justice, explaining that duties and responsibilities are assigned according to individual capabilities to ensure fairness. Subsequently, the topic transitions to the Quranic interpretation by Ayatollah Sifi Mazandaran. He interprets a verse referencing previous heavenly books, such as the Torah, the Gospel, and the Scrolls of Abraham. The speaker clarifies that the Scrolls of Abraham were originally separate sheets that later compiled into a book. The linguistic and technical meaning of the word "ajab" in the Quran is also examined. Finally, the program touches upon the occasion of revelation for a specific verse involving a black slave.
