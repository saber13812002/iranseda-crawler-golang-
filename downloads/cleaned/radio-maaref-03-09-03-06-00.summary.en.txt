The "Ma'aref" radio program began by introducing the day's schedule and encouraging listeners to participate in the "Iran Hamdel" charity campaign.
The host greeted the audience with a hadith from the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) regarding the divine love for those who bring benefit to God's family.
A detailed list of the day's broadcasts was read, covering topics from Quranic exegesis and news to religious rulings and lectures.
The main segment focused on a theological discussion about the necessity of prophethood and the mission of the prophets.
The speaker explained that prophets were sent to bring good news of truth and warn against falsehood, thereby establishing a complete argument for humanity.
It was argued that while human reason can grasp general moral principles, divine revelation is essential for understanding specific laws and the consequences of actions.
The discussion also compared the views of the Ash'ari and Mu'tazila schools on rational good and evil, highlighting the perspective of Transcendent Wisdom.
The conclusion emphasized that God, being the embodiment of pure good, inevitably performs righteous deeds and does not commit evil.
