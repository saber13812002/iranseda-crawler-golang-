During the 21st celebration of Jihad and Resistance literature, the Supreme Leader highlighted three new books, which were unveiled at the Vahdat Hall in Tehran. The President's Science Deputy announced that knowledge-based exports grew by ten percent despite restrictions, reaching approximately 550 million dollars. The Hajj and Pilgrimage Organization reported that over 8,000 students and professors would be dispatched for Umrah in 70 caravans. Culturally, the series "Pouriya Vali," directed by Masoud Jafari Jozani, is nearing pre-production and will depict the Mongol invasion of Iran. The government's information manager noted that 208,000 families in Qazvin are facing severe conditions due to adverse weather. The radio program "Tales of Freedom" featured Jamshid Gholami Nejad, who recounted his experiences of captivity in Iraq and escape attempts from Mosul and Anbar camps. The discussion emphasized the vital role of prayer and supplication in maintaining the morale of the freed prisoners. It highlighted how spiritual devotion helped transform suffering into hope during the harsh conditions of imprisonment.
