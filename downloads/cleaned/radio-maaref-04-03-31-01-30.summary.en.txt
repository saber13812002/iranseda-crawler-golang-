The program begins by citing authentic narrations from both Shia and Sunni sources to highlight the virtues of Imam Ali (AS) during the Prophet's (PBUH) ascension. In this account, the Prophet witnesses a caravan of camels in the fourth heaven carrying the virtues of Ali, which never cease until the Day of Judgment. Furthermore, God is described as speaking to the Prophet in the tone and voice of Ali, whose beauty serves as a source of comfort during moments of fear. The text then abruptly shifts to an unrelated dramatic narrative about a father named Salari who is suffering from a psychological crisis after an incident involving his son, Sohrab. Salari's wife, Azar, expresses her feelings of loneliness and fear regarding her husband's changed behavior and their son's future. This narrative section concludes with an emphasis on grief, recitation of elegies, and the family's effort to rebuild their emotional bond.
