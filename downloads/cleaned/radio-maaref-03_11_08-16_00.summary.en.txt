In this segment of the "Persaman Tarbiyati" program, Dr. Fatemeh Heydari explores the psychological reasons behind children's and parents' addiction to computer games and social media. She attributes this behavior to the "dopamine trap," where the brain releases pleasure hormones in response to immediate digital rewards like virtual coins or likes, creating a compulsive cycle. Heydari emphasizes that parents must understand this reward structure and use real-life emotional encouragement to foster sustainable happiness in their children. Addressing listener questions, she stresses the importance of maintaining a healthy emotional connection between a child and both parents after a divorce, while strictly avoiding speaking negatively about the other parent. Regarding exam season, she advises parents to avoid hosting gatherings that disrupt the home environment and to help their children accurately estimate their study workload. She recommends using the Pomodoro technique to enhance focus and manage sleep schedules effectively during high-stress periods. Finally, identifying a child's specific learning style—whether auditory, visual, or kinesthetic—is presented as the key to improving study efficiency and reducing academic anxiety.
