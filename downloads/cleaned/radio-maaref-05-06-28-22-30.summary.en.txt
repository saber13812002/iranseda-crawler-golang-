In this radio program, the concept of lowering one's gaze from visual prohibitions is explained based on Imam Ali's (PBUH) hadith, distinguishing it from simply closing one's eyes.
The most significant consequences of an illicit gaze are identified as the destruction of good deeds, the firing of Satan's poisoned arrows, and planting the seed of lust in the heart.
Numerous narrations indicate that even a single contaminated look can lead to severe repercussions, including punishment in the Hereafter and the loss of honor in this world.
Conversely, controlling the gaze and avoiding looking at non-mahram individuals is described as the gateway to opening the spiritual eyes and tasting the sweetness of faith.
Practical examples from religious figures, such as Martyr Ahmad Ali Niri and Hajj al-Rahim Arbab, demonstrate the spiritual rewards of this practice in both worldly and afterlife contexts.
The discussion concluded by emphasizing the importance of preserving modesty and chastity within families and the digital space.
