In this program, Professor Mohammadi distinguishes between God's creative and legislative will to demonstrate that divine foreknowledge does not imply determinism, noting that Shamar acted against legislative commands by misusing his free will. The discussion also affirmed bodily resurrection, explaining that humans will be revived with bodies purified of worldly defects. Other questions addressed the necessity of protecting the Quran from disrespectful handling by children and the infallibility of the Infallibles from both sin and error. The professor refuted the claim of a material difference in the clay of Shias, interpreting it instead as a distinction in innate nature and character. Regarding Karbala, Imam Hussein's choice of martyrdom over victory was justified as a means to preserve the integrity of the divine test and ensure the lasting impact of his school of thought. Finally, the existence of God was affirmed through reason, revelation, and intuition, emphasizing that the inability to see God directly does not negate His existence.
