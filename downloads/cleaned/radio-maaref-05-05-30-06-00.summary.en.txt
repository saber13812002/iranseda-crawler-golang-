In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Rabani Golepaygani examines the reason behind the Prophet's (PBUH) delay in immediately announcing the Imamate of Amir al-Mu'minin (AS) at Ghadir Khumm. Citing narrations, he attributes this hesitation to the Prophet's concern that hypocrites and lingering pre-Islamic tribalism might exploit the event for their own agendas. To demonstrate that this caution does not contradict prophetic infallibility, two Quranic and historical examples are presented: Prophet Musa's (AS) fear of Pharaoh and the Prophet's (PBUH) secrecy regarding his marriage to Zaynab bint Jahsh. In the latter case, the Quranic verse "you fear the people" is interpreted to mean that the Prophet was not afraid of people themselves, but rather that their slander could damage his prophetic status and, consequently, the divine religion. Allameh Tabatabaei emphasizes in his tafsir that this fear was divine in nature rather than personal, as the Prophet's primary objective was to protect the integrity of the message. Thus, these delays were not signs of weakness or fear of death, but rather divinely inspired prudence to prevent misinterpretation and preserve the credibility of the faith.
