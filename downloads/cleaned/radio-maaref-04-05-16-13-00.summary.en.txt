This segment of the program explores the spiritual significance of Arbaeen and the story of Ashura as a manifestation of both human misery and felicity. Citing the Quranic verse on human creation, the speaker explains that God placed ignorance within humans so that by fighting it, they can ascend from the earth to the highest heavens, a journey angels are unable to undertake due to their lack of free will and ignorance. The enemies of Karbala, such as Yazid and Ibn Saad, are viewed as having followed their base desires and ignorance, thereby becoming obstacles that paradoxically served as the ladder for the ascension of the martyrs. The martyrs of Karbala reached divine stations by adhering to their innate light and opposing their inner darkness, demonstrating that loving Imam Hussein means self-knowledge and distancing oneself from the ego. Consequently, Ashura is not merely a historical event but an eternal symbol of the internal struggle between ignorance and light that repeats in every time and place. The ultimate conclusion is that by consciously choosing to follow reason and light, humans can escape worldly and otherworldly punishment and attain true felicity.
