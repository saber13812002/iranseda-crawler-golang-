The first segment of the program provided jurisprudential answers to listener questions, covering topics such as the khums on salaries consumed within the same year, etiquette regarding relatives' events, and prayer rules for travelers who interrupt their ten-day intention. It was clarified that only the Takbirat al-Ihram is obligatory in prayer, and listening to the Quran does not carry the same reward as reciting it. The discussion then shifted to Nahj al-Balagha, emphasizing the necessity of discerning truth through insight rather than focusing on specific individuals. The segment concluded with religious poetry and stories about Imam Hasan and Ali (PBUH). Finally, the "Andisheh Aftab" program began with expert Bagher Sabei discussing the interpretation of Surah Tawhid from Imam Khomeini's perspective. This section continues previous discussions on Surah Al-Fatiha, highlighting the Imam's mystical and esoteric approach to Quranic exegesis.
