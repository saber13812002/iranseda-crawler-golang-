The radio program "Parsayan" explores the concept of altruism and self-sacrifice in the path of God, grounded in Quranic verses. Hojjatoleslam Vahidi illustrates this virtue by recounting the story of Martyr Madani, who donated his savings for marriage to fund the travel expenses of Martyr Navaab Safavi. Another narrative highlights Seyyed Ali Akbar Abutrabee, who declined to use family funds for buying a house and instead used them to save a bankrupt baker from eviction. These stories emphasize that true salvation lies in overcoming greed and helping others, even when one is in need. The program transitions into a spiritual atmosphere with religious music and recitations expressing deep love for the Ahl al-Bayt. It specifically honors Imam Hassan and Imam Hussein through poetic verses and chants. The broadcast concludes by urging listeners to emulate the path of the Infallibles and cultivate a spirit of selflessness in their daily lives.
