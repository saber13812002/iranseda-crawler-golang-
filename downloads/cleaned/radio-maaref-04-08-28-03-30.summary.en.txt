In this radio program, Professor Javali discussed Imam Ali's (PBUH) letters following the Battle of Jamal and his treatment of prisoners. The letters sent to Medina, Kufa, and his aunt Umm Habiba were nearly identical in announcing the victory over the rebels and listing the casualties, including Talha and Zubayr. Sheikh Mufid reported that Imam Ali treated prisoners based on evidence and a general amnesty, even freeing the sons of Uthman. He kindly received young Quraysh men who sought safety, addressing their envy and forgiving their sins. During this, Imam Ali warned Marwan that he would break his pledge and predicted that Marwan would survive and eventually rule. The second part of the program featured the "Zamzam Ahkam" segment, answering religious questions. Topics included the rules of Khums, Wudu, the first-day-of-the-month prayer, Ashura Ziyarat, and touching a corpse. The permissibility of touching items with the names of the Infallibles without Wudu was also clarified. The program concluded by inviting listeners to send questions and providing the contact numbers for Radio Ma'aref.
