In this radio program, the speaker emphasizes the distinction between God's enmity toward bad deeds and His love for His servants, relying on Islamic narrations.
Citing the hadith that "Paradise is veiled with hardships and Hell with desires," he explains that spiritual perfection is unattainable without enduring difficulties and struggling against one's ego.
Through the story of Gabriel's visit to Paradise and Hell, the speaker illustrates how humans are initially deterred by the hardships of Paradise but are ultimately drawn to the ease of sinful desires.
Referring to Nahj al-Balagha, he defines the characteristics of a true believer as self-awareness, continuous self-criticism, and avoiding self-praise.
According to this perspective, a genuine believer is always anxious about whether their actions are performed correctly and fears the praise of others.
The speaker advises seeking true knowledge of God and proper worship rather than relying on superficial justifications.
The program concludes with the recitation of the opening verses of Surah Jathiyah, dedicated to the martyrs of Karbala and the Sacred Defense.
