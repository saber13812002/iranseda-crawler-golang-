Babak Simbari, a former Iranian footballer, shares his career memories in this interview. He recalls the hardships of the past, such as vehicle breakdowns during travel and a lack of proper gear for cold-weather matches. Simbari emphasizes that the Homa team's strong ethical and social training shaped his character and instilled a deep respect for elders. He attributes his exclusion from the national team to the quota system favoring players from the two major clubs, Esteghlal and Persepolis. After retiring at age 38, Simbari left coaching due to the organizational collapse of Homa and shifted his focus to business. He criticizes the current state of Iranian football, arguing that young players are selected based on family wealth and connections rather than genuine talent. Finally, Simbari urges modern coaches to evaluate young athletes fairly to prevent the loss of potential talent in Iranian football.
