In this radio program, Professor Seyed Ahmad Faghihi discusses the concept of "monotheistic ethics" and the role of prayer in strengthening the human connection with God. He emphasizes that before making any requests, one must acknowledge their sins, apologize, and seek refuge in the Ahl al-Bayt to truly understand their position as a servant before the Divine. In response to a listener's complaint about feeling distant from God and their prayers being unanswered, the professor recommended prioritizing prayer, Quran recitation, and seeking intercession through Lady Zahra (SA), while avoiding the devil's whispers of despair. Furthermore, controlling one's speech was identified as crucial, with the use of intellect, habituating the tongue to divine remembrance, and choosing healthy social circles presented as key solutions. Finally, the program highlighted the significant impact of friends and social environments on shaping individual ethics and determining one's destiny in both this world and the hereafter.
