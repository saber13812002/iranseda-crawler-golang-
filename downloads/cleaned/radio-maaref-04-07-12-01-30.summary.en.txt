In this program, first, Professor Fateminia emphasizes the metaphysical and hidden dimensions of religious rulings such as wudu and repentance, relying on narrations and hadiths. He describes istighfar not merely as a verbal formula, but as a heartfelt apology and a form of humble pleading before God, while also noting the recommended strictness of a judge regarding divine rights. The main message of this section is the hope for God's boundless mercy towards sinners who return to Him with genuine repentance. In the second part, memorable anecdotes from the Sacred Defense era are recounted by Generals Ali Akbar Khaleghi, Mohsen Karimi, and Hossein Salimani. General Khaleghi describes the profound impact of Imam Khomeini's personal greetings on the morale of the front commanders. General Karimi also recalls the vital role of the leader's voice messages in boosting the motivation and resistance of the fighters during the Badr and Karbala 5 operations. These narratives demonstrate that loyalty to the leader and a spiritual connection with him were the primary factors behind the military forces' success and steadfastness against the enemy.
