Iranian armed forces launched retaliatory missile and drone strikes against US military bases in the Persian Gulf and Israeli targets. The IRGC announced the destruction of two US THAAD systems and reported damage to Patriot missile stockpiles in Qatar, emphasizing that attacks are strictly limited to military objectives. Funerals were held across Iran for victims of the strikes, including students in Minab and air defense personnel, drawing large public crowds. Resistance groups in Lebanon and Iraq supported Iran by targeting Israeli air bases and hotels housing US troops. Widespread protests condemning the assassination of Iran's leader took place in Iraq and Bahrain, with clashes occurring in the latter. The Iranian ambassador in Riyadh announced the coordinated return of Iranian pilgrims from Mecca and Medina. Finally, listeners were advised to follow safety protocols during wartime and refrain from sharing images of strike locations.
