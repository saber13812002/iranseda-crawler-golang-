This program discusses excerpts from Ibn Qasim Muhammad's "Mabai'" regarding the virtue of weeping out of fear of God or longing for His presence.
According to narrations, weeping for God is a unique reward that cannot be matched by thousands of other acts of worship.
A believer's wisdom is defined by a constant awareness of death and preparation for it, as the ultimate destination is the afterlife.
Divine light illuminating the heart expands its capacity, allowing the individual to distinguish truth from falsehood.
Signs of this spiritual light include focusing on the hereafter, disregarding the deceptive allure of the world, and being perpetually ready for death.
While the world deceives humans with its material attractions, an illuminated heart recognizes it merely as a tool rather than the ultimate goal.
The second part of the broadcast shifts to a jurisprudential discussion on the rules of Hajj, specifically for those with valid excuses.
A key legal point is that women fearing menstruation can perform Tawaf and Sa'i before going to Arafat, provided the proper sequence of rituals is maintained.
