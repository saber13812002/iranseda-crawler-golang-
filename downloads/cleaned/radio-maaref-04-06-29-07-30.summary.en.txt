In the radio program "Let's Be Kind," Hujjat al-Islam Fozuli discussed the concept of the "cotton hero," describing individuals who appear physically strong and make grand claims but lack actual resilience or capability in practice. Citing the Quran and hadiths, he defined a true hero as one who masters their anger and ego, rather than someone who merely projects strength. Subsequently, Hujjat al-Islam Falah Ba addressed the vice of "gossiping" and "spying," characterizing it as a form of sorcery that harms the perpetrator, the victim, and society at large. The primary drivers of this behavior were identified as religious ignorance, hypocrisy, envy, malice, and material needs. The consequences of such actions include divine wrath, public humiliation, social discord, and a weakening of the community's connection to religion and the Ahl al-Bayt. Finally, it was emphasized that a believer is the honor of God, and any violation of their privacy invites the exposure of secrets and divine punishment for the offender.
