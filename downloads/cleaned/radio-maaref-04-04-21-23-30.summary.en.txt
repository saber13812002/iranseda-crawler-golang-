In this segment of the program, Hujjat al-Islam Maleki addresses the theological and legal discussion regarding the obligation of Khums, explaining why the Quran does not explicitly list all its specific sources. He argues that based on the principle of analogy and the narrations of the Ahl al-Bayt, Quranic verses encompass similar cases, and complex financial details are clarified through the teachings of the Infallibles. The speaker emphasizes that Khums is a fundamental pillar of the faith, and denying it constitutes apostasy, as the authority of the Infallibles' words is equivalent to that of the Quran. He also highlights the linguistic meaning of "ghanimah" as any form of profit or benefit, indicating that the scope of Khums extends beyond war spoils. Furthermore, the moral and social consequences of failing to pay Khums are examined, including the rendering of wealth haram, the corruption of future generations, and the creation of hidden enmity in one's life. Citing a real-life example, he asserts that paying Khums can lead to the removal of calamities and an increase in life's blessings. Finally, he appreciates the efforts of religious propagators in raising awareness through social media and underscores the crucial role of women in preserving dignity and promoting Islamic values.
