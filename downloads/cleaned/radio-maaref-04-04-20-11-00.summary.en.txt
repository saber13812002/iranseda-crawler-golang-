The Speaker of the Iranian Parliament identified national unity as the most significant achievement of the twelve-day war, crediting the Supreme Leader's wise command for the country's robust military response.
He emphasized that over 90 percent of missiles hit their targets during the final days of the conflict, demonstrating the effectiveness of the defense strategy.
The First Vice President stated that the government successfully managed the crisis through precise planning and decentralization of authority to provincial governors without depleting strategic reserves.
The broadcast also covered the commemoration of martyr Sardar Izadi and the organization of Friday processions in several provinces to show public solidarity.
Additionally, the first national conference on "Digital Islamic and Human Sciences" was announced to be held in Qom, focusing on the digitalization of academic research.
The head of the National Emergency Organization confirmed full medical readiness for Arbaeen pilgrims, predicting that two million people would cross the Khorramshahr border this year.
In the "Zamzame Ahkam" segment, a cleric explained that defending the homeland is a collective religious obligation that serves as a defense of Islam itself.
The program concluded with jurisprudential answers to listener questions regarding the proper payment of fasting expiation and the validity of prayers in rural areas.
