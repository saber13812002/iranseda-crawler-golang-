The program begins with a Quranic interpretation emphasizing the sanctity of the Ahl al-Bayt and rejecting the idea of the Prophet excluding the poor.
The commentator argues that the Quran not only forbids distancing oneself from the poor but encourages their companionship to gain divine grace.
Subsequently, the show shifts to mourning the martyrdom of Fatimah Zahra (SA) and highlighting maternal virtues.
The core segment invites listeners to answer the challenge of "the greatest service to one's mother" by sending their mothers' names.
Participants share their mothers' names and personal memories via SMS and phone calls, creating a live interaction.
This engagement underscores the deep sense of empathy and gratitude towards the irreplaceable role of mothers in listeners' lives.
The program concludes with the recitation of Quranic verses and religious supplications.
