The speech emphasizes the pivotal role of Hazrat Zainab (SA) in the jihad of clarification and awakening, which follows a three-stage process of Husaini personality, family, and society building.
The speaker argues that Zainab's (SA) sermons and actions, such as her confrontation with Umar ibn Saad and the establishment of Arbaeen, serve as a model for breaking arrogance and fostering unity in a monotheistic society.
The talk also highlights end-times signs, such as spiritual awakenings in the West and protests for Palestine, indicating the proximity of the Mahdi's appearance and the necessity of intellectual jihad against brainwashing.
The second part of the program presents a literary narrative from the book "Bughaz Asbat Betazad" regarding Har ibn Yazid al-Riyahi.
This narrative depicts the critical moments of the caravan's arrival in Karbala and the receipt of threatening letters from Ibn Ziyad by Imam Husain (AS) and Har.
Despite his fear and hesitation, Har ultimately distances himself from doubt after witnessing the Imam's steadfast resolve and hearing his warnings.
The story illustrates how faith and insight overcome human fears when confronting oppressive power, even under the most difficult circumstances.
