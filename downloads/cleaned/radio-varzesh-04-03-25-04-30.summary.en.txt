Ali Hamoudi, a former national team player and Esteghlal Tehran star, recounts his early career at Esteghlal Ahvaz, where he played for both the youth and reserve teams simultaneously. After three seasons in Ahvaz, he moved to Mes Kerman under coach Parviz Mazloomi, followed by a stint at Foolad Khuzestan. Hamoudi shares an anecdote about a clerical error in a letter from coach Luka Bonačić, which led Sepahan to sign Mohsen Hamidi instead of him. Despite interest from Persepolis, he chose Esteghlal Tehran due to his loyalty and the persistent efforts of club manager Ali Ahmadi. He reveals that he received no salary for two years at Esteghlal due to the club's financial issues and the priority given to senior stars, forcing him to take loans for personal expenses. After resolving part of his unpaid dues with the help of MP Omidvar Rezaei, he accepted a transfer to Sepahan. This move was driven by the significant financial and psychological pressure he faced during his time in Tehran.
