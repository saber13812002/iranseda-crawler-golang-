In the radio program "Parsaiyan," Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the duty of believers to stand against tyranny and global arrogance, citing the historical resistance of clerics against British colonialism in Iraq and Iran. He highlighted the martyrdom of Ayatollah Khalasi and his continued scholarly efforts during exile as a model for defending justice. The discussion also covered the jihad of Sayyid Mujahid during the Iran-Russia war and Kashf al-Qutub's fatwa for national defense. The second part of the program featured a narrative from the book "Neshan-e Hassan," depicting the lives of Imam Hassan's children and their presence at the event of Karbala. This story illustrated their patience and faith through the intimate dialogue between Imam Hassan and his wife Fatima regarding their fears for the caravan's future. The broadcast concluded with a jurisprudential discussion on the prohibition of fraudulent sick leave and the importance of public investment for economic growth in 2025. The program ended by introducing a Quranic exegesis segment on Surah Al-Ma'idah and providing contact details for audience feedback.
