On the second day of the Sacred Defense Week, the program "Barkarane Noor" began with a tafsir of verses 14 to 18 of Surah Al-An'am by Ayatollah Javadi Amoli. The commentator distinguished between absolute and special mercy, explaining that the recording of absolute mercy encompasses both punishment and reward for all humans, while special mercy is reserved for believers. The verse "Say, other than Allah, whom shall I take as a protector?" serves as both a rational and scriptural proof of the harmony between logic and revelation in establishing monotheism. This rational argument is based on the inherent human need for a self-sufficient Creator who is the originator of the heavens and the earth and the provider of all needs. The distinction between "creation" and "origination" was also clarified, with origination signifying innovative creation without a prior model, reflecting God's absolute power. The concept of divine guardianship in these verses goes beyond mere worship, serving as a guidance that calls the intellect to submission after understanding the truth. Finally, the Prophet's primacy among Muslims was emphasized as a rank-based and spiritual priority, distinct from the temporal priorities of others.
