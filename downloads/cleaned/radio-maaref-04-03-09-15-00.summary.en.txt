This radio program analyzed the Supreme Leader's management strategies during the 1999 and 2009 crises. The expert emphasized that the core strategy relied on enhancing public insight and providing clear explanations to foster independent analysis among citizens. This approach countered the enemy's miscalculation that the system had collapsed after a decade of planned opposition. By presenting evidence and maintaining transparency, the leadership revealed the truth behind the fabricated narratives of the crises. A key aspect of this strategy was recognizing the "soft war" nature of these uprisings and avoiding the use of violent or hard-power tools in response. The leadership allowed time for the public to reflect, enabling the falsity of the opposition to become apparent while the truth of the system was highlighted. This method successfully broke the polarization created by the enemy and led to a widespread public awakening. Ultimately, the program concluded that the system's legitimacy and the people's commitment to it were the primary guarantees of resilience against these internal and external plots.
