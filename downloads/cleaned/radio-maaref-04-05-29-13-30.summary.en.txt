In the radio program "Narrative of Love," the mother of martyr Seyed Mohammad Hosseini, a defender of the holy shrines, shares her memories from after her son's martyrdom. She describes her emotional journey to Syria and the profound impact of visiting the shrine of Lady Zainab (SA). The mother recounts the story of a necklace she gifted her son before his death, which was later found stained with blood. She also shares a vivid dream in which she saw her son reciting elegies near the shrine of Lady Masoumeh (SA). Emphasizing the patience of Lady Zainab (SA), she highlights the importance of steadfastness and trust in God during difficult times. She urges the audience to pray for the stability of their faith and protection from misguidance. The program concludes with a message from the Supreme Leader, stressing the vital role of religion and knowledge in the progress and resistance of the Islamic Republic.
