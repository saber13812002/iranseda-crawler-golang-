In this radio program, the guest emphasizes that technology must serve humanity rather than the other way around, highlighting its cultural and psychological harms. He warns that excessive reliance on tools like artificial intelligence and mobile phones weakens human thinking, memory, and inner peace. The core of the discussion is the need to maintain a "technology culture" that prevents humans from becoming slaves to technology. Citing Islamic narrations, the guest identifies controlling anger and ego as the primary conditions for individual and social security. He stresses that anger driven by personal ego, even toward close relatives, must be abandoned to remain safe from divine wrath. Ultimately, the program's ethical message focuses on prioritizing reflection and contemplation before using technology and cultivating Islamic virtues such as mercy and justice.
