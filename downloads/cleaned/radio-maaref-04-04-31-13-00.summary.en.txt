In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Sadeghi explores the ethical and spiritual teachings of Imam Sajjad (PBUH) based on the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya. He emphasizes the role of its supplications in fostering dignity, gratitude, and salvation from sin, highlighting the 41st prayer as a model for rejecting oppression and supporting the oppressed. A significant portion of the discussion focuses on the Imam's virtue of forbearance, illustrated by his forgiveness of enemies and his protection of Marwan bin al-Hakam despite the latter's tyrannical history. The lecture also recounts the Imam's secret assistance to the needy and his profound spiritual impact on the public, citing the famous narrative of the poet Farazdaq in the Masjid al-Haram. Finally, the hardships following the events of Karbala and the Imam's martyrdom are revisited, concluding that his deepest suffering stemmed from the humiliation of his spiritual status rather than physical pain.
