Dr. Saeed Hosseini discussed the successful organization of the World Wrestling Championship in Mazandaran, highlighting the crucial role of community participation and inter-agency cooperation. He emphasized that fostering a sense of belonging and solidarity among officials and staff was key to the event's success despite a limited budget. By implementing strict task division and transparent financial oversight, he ensured the competition was completed without any outstanding debts. A major achievement was the sevenfold increase in per capita sports space, accomplished by constructing and renovating facilities in both urban and rural areas. This infrastructure development was driven by university consultations and active public involvement in providing materials and labor. Hosseini also credited the media and public support for maintaining motivation and facilitating various sports projects. Furthermore, he underscored the importance of inter-departmental communication and resolving local conflicts through constructive alternative proposals.
