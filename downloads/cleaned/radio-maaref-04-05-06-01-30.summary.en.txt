The program begins by explaining the concept of self-accounting, emphasizing the acceptance of flaws and avoiding false justifications.
The most crucial step for spiritual correction is sincere repentance, which involves remorse, the decision to abandon sin, and compensating for others' rights.
The text further elaborates on the role of divine will in purifying sins after human effort and the acceptance of one's own weakness.
The second part focuses on the months of Muharram and Safar, highlighting the necessity of specific self-reflection during these sacred times.
Participation with the companions of Imam Hussein (PBUH) is possible through "intention" and "passion" rather than physical presence.
The narrative continues with a literary account of Qasim ibn Hasan's encounter with Qasid, Yazid's envoy, showcasing his bravery.
Finally, the scene depicts the night before the Battle of Karbala and the intimate dialogue between Imam Hussein (PBUH) and his companions.
These conversations demonstrate the companions' complete readiness for martyrdom and their unwavering commitment to the path of truth.
