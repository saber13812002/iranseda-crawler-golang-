In this program, Imam Khomeini critiques both secular materialist and superficial religious currents, asserting that human rights are inherently religious and lack philosophical validity without a divine interpretation. He argues that Islam comprehensively defines freedom and democracy, respecting not only material but also spiritual rights, while prohibiting any form of tyranny over others. By highlighting the massive public participation in the Islamic Revolution, the Imam demonstrates that true democracy in the Islamic system means the people's right to determine their destiny and oversee their rulers, rather than the dominance of behind-the-scenes powers. By subjecting the system's principles and constitution to a referendum, he proved that governmental legitimacy stems directly from the people's will, a foundation unmatched by any other revolution in history. Finally, the Imam emphasizes the equal rights of all social classes and religions in Islam, warning that Western interpretations of freedom often serve as a pretext for corruption and oppression.
