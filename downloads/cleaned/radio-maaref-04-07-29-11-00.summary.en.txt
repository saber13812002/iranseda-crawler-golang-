The Supreme Leader of Iran praised sports and science champions as symbols of national power and a practical response to the US President's insults. The Hajj and Pilgrimage Organization announced new deadlines for pre-registration and payment for the 1405 Hajj, varying based on the deposit receipt date. The Shia Quranic Charter was unveiled with the endorsement of top clerics to serve as a credible document for promoting the Quran. Approximately 1,800 academic works were received for the Seminary Book of the Year conference, with 337 advancing to detailed evaluation. The Ministry of Education and the Imam's Executive Headquarters signed a two-trillion-toman agreement to improve schools in underprivileged areas. The IRGC's Deprivation Removal Headquarters allocated about 298 billion tomans to support welfare beneficiaries. The "Zamzam Ahkam" program addressed various jurisprudential questions from followers of different marjas regarding taxes, missed prayers, and financial rulings. Finally, the Adhan times for Qom were announced, and listeners were invited to tune in to upcoming Radio Ma'aref programs.
