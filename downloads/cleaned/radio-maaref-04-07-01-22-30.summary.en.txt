The core message of this program relies on the 47-year experience of the Sacred Defense as a living model for religious and social life. The speaker emphasizes the concept of "Sibghat Allah" (being colored by God) in all aspects of life, from clothing and food to management and economics, urging the adoption of Quranic models over Western ones. He criticizes certain officials who, by leaving lifestyle choices entirely open, have effectively wronged the religion, stressing the need to preserve Islamic identity against cultural infiltration. A key point is the combination of "strength" and "spirituality," meaning that physical and scientific power must be accompanied by faith and ethics to instill fear in the enemy. By referencing the behavior of commanders and martyrs who served as models of sacrifice and service in the harshest conditions, the speaker calls on the younger generation and officials to implement these models in the "Greater Jihad" (struggle against the self) and the "Greatest Jihad" (struggle against false paradigms). Finally, the program concludes with an invitation for listeners to participate in charitable activities and recite the Quran, emphasizing hope for the appearance of the Imam and victory under the shadow of Wilayat and Tawhid.
