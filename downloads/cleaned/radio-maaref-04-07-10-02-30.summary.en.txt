In the "Porsoman Enghelab" program, Professor Abutalibi argues that negotiating with the United States is neither rational nor dignified, citing the Supreme Leader's views. He asserts that America's true objective is Iran's complete submission rather than achieving a mutual win-win agreement. The failure of the JCPOA and the US violation of commitments demonstrate that the US resorts to war and sanctions even during negotiations. Therefore, the only viable strategy is to strengthen Iran's internal economic and military resilience rather than engaging in futile talks. The program also explains that the inaction of regional countries regarding Israel stems from their political and economic dependence on Western powers. Additionally, the book "Methodology of Understanding Imam Khomeini's Political Thought" was introduced, emphasizing the need for indigenous methods to accurately interpret the Imam's ideology. Finally, the relationship between Iran and the US is deemed unsolvable due to fundamental systemic and geopolitical conflicts, unless the US abandons its hegemonic policies.
