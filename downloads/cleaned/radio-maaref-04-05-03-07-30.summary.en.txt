In the radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat," Professor Mohammad Radi analyzed the concept of stinginess from the perspective of the Quran and Islamic traditions. He described stinginess as a disease that leaves harmful and bitter consequences in individual, family, and social dimensions, particularly within management and organizational structures. Citing Quranic verses, he emphasized that God intends for leaders and managers to be chosen from among generous individuals to prevent the creation of "organizational stinginess." The discussion also referenced a narration from Imam Ali stating that looking at stingy officials leads to heartlessness and hardness of heart for the people. Professor Radi clarified that stinginess ultimately harms the individual, as God is always rich, but generosity is essential in social responsibilities. Finally, he stressed the necessity of replacing stinginess with generosity in both inner spiritual practice and external conduct to build a society based on the ethical values of the Ahl al-Bayt.
