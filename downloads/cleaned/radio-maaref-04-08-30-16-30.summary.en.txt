In this radio program, Dr. Raympour utilizes narrations from Hazrat Zahra (SA) to elucidate human values and the wisdom-based logic behind divine laws. He emphasizes that God communicates with humans, who possess the capacity to be divine addressees; therefore, religious laws are neither arbitrary contracts nor purposeless, but are designed to foster spiritual evolution and liberate individuals from root sins such as shirk, arrogance, and envy. Prayer and faith serve as primary tools to free humans from the illusion of self-centeredness and attachment to internal, scattered deities, a concept referred to as hidden shirk. The speaker refutes deviant theological views, including the Ash'ari stance that laws lack wisdom and the Mu'tazilite claim of complete knowledge of all causes, while stressing the necessity of revelation to guide human reason. Furthermore, religious liberalism, which blurs the lines between right and wrong and separates action from faith, is identified as a dangerous deviation. Ultimately, religion is described as a train guided by prophets, which must not be controlled by anti-value currents but must operate within the framework of divine monotheism and justice.
