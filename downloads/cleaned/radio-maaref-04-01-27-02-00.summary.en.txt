This radio program addressed various theological topics with expert insights. The discussion began by clarifying that some prophets were law-givers while others were merely messengers, and they did not necessarily appear in a strict chronological order. Regarding the fate of officials who have both served and harmed the country, it was explained that in the afterlife, sins and rights must be settled first, and one's ultimate destination depends on their faith and repentance. The nature of creation was also discussed, noting that while God's essence is eternal, created beings can be temporary, and creation will continue even after the Day of Judgment. The concept of God's manifestation in Paradise was explained as being proportional to each individual's spiritual capacity, with God's merciful gaze being the greatest blessing for the inhabitants of Heaven. It was further clarified that Jinn are obligated to believe in human prophets and do not have independent prophets of their own kind. Finally, the relationship between God's foreknowledge and human free will was addressed, emphasizing that trials are necessary for human growth and the preservation of free choice, even though God already knows the outcome.
