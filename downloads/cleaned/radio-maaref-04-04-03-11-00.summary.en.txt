In the radio program "Man Hosseinam," the IRGC's statement and Iran's missile strikes on Israeli targets and a US military base in Qatar were highlighted as a response to recent aggression.
The Foreign Minister stated that Iran has no intention of continuing its military response if Israel ceases its attacks by 4:00 AM.
The program's guest, Ayatollah Sadeghi, emphasized the role of "steadfastness" and "reliance on God" in historical and contemporary victories, citing the battles of Badr and Karbala.
He argued that the courage and resilience of Iran's armed forces and people are rooted in their deep faith in the justice of their cause.
The broadcast featured patriotic poetry and invited listeners to send supportive messages regarding the military victories.
These victories were interpreted as consecutive defeats for the US and Israel, symbolizing the power of Iran's resistance.
The host and guest stressed the unity of the people and the armed forces in defending Iran's soil and Islamic identity.
