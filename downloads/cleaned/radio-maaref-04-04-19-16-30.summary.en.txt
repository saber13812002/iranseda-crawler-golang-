In this radio program, Dr. Abutalebi argues that Western accusations regarding Iran's nuclear program, human rights, and missiles are merely pretexts for US hegemony and fear of Iranian independence. He points to the collapse of independent nations like Iraq and Libya to demonstrate that the US cannot tolerate any sovereign power, whether religious or secular, in the region. The analyst warns that even if Iran abandons its nuclear program, new excuses will be fabricated to justify continued sanctions and aggression. Conversely, he highlights the tangible benefits of nuclear technology in electricity, industry, and medicine, cautioning that surrendering this technology to the West would only increase dependency without resolving the underlying geopolitical conflicts. In the second segment, Dr. Jafari emphasizes the pivotal role of Lady Zainab's speech in Kufa in exposing the truth of Karbala and discrediting the oppressive regime. He explains that the martyrdom of Imam Hussein and the severe suffering of the captives, particularly the ill Imam Sajjad, served as a catalyst for public awakening and a comprehensive societal critique. This historical narrative is presented as a model for understanding social pathology and preventing the repetition of past mistakes during the occultation of the Imam of the Time.
