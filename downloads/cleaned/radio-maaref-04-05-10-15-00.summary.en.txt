The program opens with a patriotic anthem praising Ali and asserting that those who do not stand with him are doomed. Professor Mehdi Rasaei emphasizes the necessity of strengthening the "will to resist" against the illegitimate Zionist regime, a stance firmly supported by Iran's Supreme Leader. He highlights the resilience of the Palestinian and Yemeni resistance, demonstrating that strong willpower can challenge the enemy even amidst siege and technological inferiority. Rasaei explains that despite the adversary's material and technological superiority, the resistance axis has successfully altered the international landscape through asymmetric warfare and diverse tactics, including intelligence operations and missile strikes. Iran's military, media, and diplomatic support has been crucial in breaking the isolation of these groups and proving their non-terrorist nature to the world. The broadcast concludes with medical advice for Arbaeen pilgrims, focusing on managing digestion, preventing gastrointestinal issues, and using herbal teas to maintain health during the long journey.
