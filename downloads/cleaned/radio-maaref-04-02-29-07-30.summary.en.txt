In this radio program, the importance of patience and forgiveness when facing daily rudeness or delays is highlighted, emphasizing the need to remain strong and avoid judging others. Subsequently, Hujjat al-Islam Ahmad Gholamali, citing Quranic verses and narrations, identifies keeping promises and honesty as fundamental pillars of humanity and faith. He explains that failing to honor commitments indicates a lack of human perfection, which can be cultivated through consistent practice. In the second segment, Hujjat al-Islam Fallah discusses the nature of anger as a dangerous internal fire. He describes anger as a sign of human weakness and the devil's dominance over the soul, capable of destroying all relationships and righteous deeds. Finally, the program advises listeners to recognize the satanic roots of anger and control their impulses to avoid being consumed by this internal fire and harming themselves or others.
