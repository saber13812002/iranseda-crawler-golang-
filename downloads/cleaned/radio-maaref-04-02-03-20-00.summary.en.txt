On the eve of the martyrdom of Imam Sadiq (AS), the holy shrines in Najaf, Karbala, and Kazimayn were draped in mourning flags, accompanied by extensive religious programs. Concurrently, the head of Iran's Judiciary presented eight operational strategies at the Shanghai Cooperation Organization summit to enhance international legal cooperation, including combating transnational and cyber crimes. The first international human rights conference in the Middle East is set to be held in Tehran and Isfahan with representatives from 23 countries, aiming to improve Iran's global image through cultural diplomacy. Internationally, reports highlight the normalization of discrimination against Muslims in the Netherlands and increasing police pressure on Palestine supporters in the UK. Additionally, Iranian-French journalist Shahin Hazami was arrested in France for his support of Palestine, with security forces raiding his home. Militarily, the armed forces of "Gheman" targeted vital assets in the occupied Gaza region using ballistic missiles and drones. Finally, the Islamic Resistance Movement in Palestine called on citizens to intensify their presence at Al-Aqsa Mosque to counter plans for the Judaization of Jerusalem.
