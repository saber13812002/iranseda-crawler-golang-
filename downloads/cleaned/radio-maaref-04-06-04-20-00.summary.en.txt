The Pakistani Foreign Ministry spokesperson condemned recent Israeli attacks on Gaza as a blatant violation of international law, urging the global community to take immediate action to stop the aggression. Concurrently, Qatari officials stated that the Israeli regime is not seeking a deal, leaving the ball in their court regarding negotiations. In another development, the US and Israel are at odds over the renewal of the UN peacekeeping mission (UNIFIL) in southern Lebanon, although analysts believe Israel actually wants the force to remain despite its apparent opposition. The US special envoy for Lebanon and Syria also sparked strong media backlash in Lebanon by insulting journalists and comparing them to animals. Recent polls indicate that hatred toward the Israeli regime has peaked among young people in the UK and many European and Asian countries. Finally, an American military analyst, referencing the Iranian Supreme Leader's fatwa declaring nuclear weapons haram, suggested that Iran should completely halt its nuclear program to prevent war.
