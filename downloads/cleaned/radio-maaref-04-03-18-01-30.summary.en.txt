This program highlights the virtues and exalted status of Imam Ali (AS) as the first believer and martyr for the faith, based on the Prophet's sermons. The speaker criticizes the historical neglect of Imam Ali (AS) by some companions after the Prophet's death, emphasizing the necessity of adhering to his authority. The role of Imam Khomeini in reviving religious rituals and preserving revolutionary ideals after a period of collective amnesia is also discussed. The final section underscores the critical role of Muharram and Safar ceremonies in attracting and guiding new generations to Shia Islam. Statistics are cited indicating that these rituals significantly influence non-Shia Muslims to convert or deepen their faith. The program concludes by urging the audience to maintain the reverence for these rituals and resist the enemy's attempts to erase the legacy of the Ahl al-Bayt (AS).
