This radio program recounts the imprisonment of Imam Hasan Askari (AS) by the Abbasid regime and the selection of Ali ibn Narmash, a hostile Nasibi jailer, to guard him. Upon seeing the Imam, Ali ibn Narmash was spiritually transformed into a devoted Shia, demonstrating the Imam's power over his oppressors. The primary reason for the Abbasids' severe persecution was their fear of the birth and future leadership of Imam Mahdi (AJ), who would eradicate tyranny. Consequently, spies were placed in the Imam's household, and the birth of Imam Mahdi was kept strictly secret to prevent its discovery. To counter these threats, Imam Askari (AS) established a structured network of deputies (Nawab) and gates (Bawab) to maintain contact with Shias during the occultation. This organizational framework enabled Shias to preserve their faith throughout the long occultation and eventually facilitated the establishment of a Wilayat-based government in the modern era. The program concludes by emphasizing that the current Wilayat system's primary mission is to prepare the ground for the Imam's reappearance and to combat global oppression.
