Holding mourning gatherings for Imam Hussein (PBUH) carries immense virtue and reward, as emphasized by the Prophet and the Imams, particularly Imam Reza (PBUH).
Imam Reza (PBUH) stated in a hadith that weeping for the tragedy of Karbala leads to the forgiveness of sins and sharing in the rewards of the martyrs.
These gatherings are not merely for seeking reward; their primary purpose is to keep religious teachings alive, strengthen Husseini ethics, and foster social education.
The intercession of the Imams on Judgment Day applies to those who benefited from their guidance and education in this world and took steps toward justice.
The history of Karbala reveals that despite the Prophet's explicit recommendations to love the Ahl al-Bayt, their enemies committed unprecedented oppression.
Mourning gatherings should be viewed as a school for learning life lessons, pure living, and resistance against tyranny.
The ultimate goal of participating in these gatherings is to achieve deep spiritual knowledge, adherence to Islamic values, and worthiness of intercession in the afterlife.
