The "Zamzam Ahkam" program began by addressing various religious inquiries from its listeners. A question regarding the validity of forward contracts on melon and cucumber plants was raised, noting that it requires specific reference to the follower's marja. It was clarified that menstruating women are prohibited from stopping in mosques or touching the Quran, although passing through is permitted. Regarding vows, it was explained that making a vow in Persian is just as valid as in Arabic, provided the person is of age, sane, and acting voluntarily. On the topic of divorce, the experts emphasized that repeating the word "divorce" three times in a single session counts as only one divorce. Three actual divorces occur only when the pronouncement is made in three separate sessions with intervening periods of reconciliation. The program then shifted to security, offering five tips to prevent theft in mobile phone shops. Finally, the broadcast concluded with the recitation of Munajat al-Zahiri from Imam Sajjad's supplications and a narrative about a young man's desire for martyrdom.
