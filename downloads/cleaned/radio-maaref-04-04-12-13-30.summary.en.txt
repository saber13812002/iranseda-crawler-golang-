The radio program "Ma'rif" began by honoring the memory of Martyr Hajizadeh before narrating the martyrdom of Ali Akbar, the son of Imam Hussein (PBUH). The segment highlighted Ali Akbar's bravery in battle and Imam Hussein's powerful prayers for him and curses upon their enemies. Following Ali Akbar's martyrdom, the deaths of Abdullah ibn Ja'far and other companions in Yazid's army were also recounted. Another part of the program focused on the martyrdom of Raml, the son of Hasan ibn Ali (PBUH), and his severe thirst in Karbala. Finally, a message from the Supreme Leader was aired, referencing the severe US sanctions against Iran and their support for the 2009 unrest. The message also emphasized the US assassination of Martyr Soleimani and Martyr Mughniyeh, highlighting their unparalleled role in the region and in resolving the issues of neighboring countries.
