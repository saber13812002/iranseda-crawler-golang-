The program begins by emphasizing reliance on God as the key to eliminating anxiety and stress. Hujjat al-Islam Vahidi then cites religious narrations to explain that detachment from worldly desires leads to divine guidance and knowledge that does not require a teacher. To illustrate this principle, the story of the late Ayatollah Hujjat is shared, highlighting his extremely simple and humble lifestyle despite his high status. Although he had access to significant religious funds, he refused to use them to repair his broken heating stove in his home. He explained that these funds were a trust belonging to the Imam of the Time and using them for personal expenses was religiously impermissible. This anecdote serves as a powerful example of strict stewardship and the importance of avoiding material attachment. The broadcast concludes with a call to gratitude and remembrance of God.
