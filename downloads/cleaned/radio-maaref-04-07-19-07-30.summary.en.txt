This radio program discusses the methods for starting a good day with a moral and ethical approach.
Key recommendations include performing morning prayers, reciting dhikr, maintaining optimism, and greeting others.
Based on religious narrations, it is emphasized that beginning the day with a good deed keeps the entire day on a righteous path.
The specialized section of the program focuses on the vice of "blaming and reproach."
The instructor explains that while moderate criticism is necessary for upbringing, excessive blame is considered a moral vice.
Taking pleasure in the sorrow of others, even enemies, is prohibited in Islamic law, and only the victory of truth is a source of joy.
The Quran describes the delay of divine punishment as an opportunity for repentance and returning to God.
Ultimately, avoiding harsh words and adhering to religious limits in speech are the keys to maintaining peace and good character.
