In this radio program, the instructor refutes the claim that sins are excusable due to a lack of knowledge of Sharia, emphasizing that many sins are fundamentally incompatible with human nature and reason. He clarifies the significant difference between the high heavenly ranks of believers who practice true faith and worship, and those who enter Paradise merely through the merit of their ancestors or Islamic environment. The instructor also explains that physical differences among humans are not direct divine wills to create defects, but rather results of material and spiritual factors with social and evolutionary purposes. In response to listener questions, the topic of seeking healing through intercession with the Imams was discussed, presenting conflicting testimonies from listeners regarding its efficacy. Finally, the distinction between true poor who refrain from begging due to dignity and professional beggars was examined, with recommendations for appropriate assistance to genuine needy individuals by civil and state organizations.
