The radio program "Let's Be Kind" began on the fifth of Mehr with the theme of "Heroes of Life," where listeners identified teachers, martyrs, and mothers as their heroes via text messages.
The primary heroes discussed are mothers and fathers, who remove initial life obstacles through sacrifice, and teachers, who provide the necessary tools for progress with patience and knowledge.
Martyrs and fighters are also highlighted as selfless heroes who stood against enemies, with the anniversary of the Abadan siege serving as a symbol of this heroism.
The Supreme Leader and sports champions are also mentioned as inspiring role models in various fields of life.
The program concluded by congratulating the birth of Hazrat Abdol-Azim Hasani, who is also recognized as a hero.
The second part of the show introduces Master Mohammad Hadi Afar for presenting ethical insights from the Quran and Hadith.
The ultimate goal is to instill moral virtues and purify daily life from vices among the audience.
