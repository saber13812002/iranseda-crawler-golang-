The radio program first emphasized that defending the Islamic system during times of crisis is a rational and religious obligation that does not require a specific ruling from the Supreme Leader. It was clarified that, according to Ayatollah Khamenei, freeing a prisoner cannot replace freeing a slave as the expiation for breaking a fast. The use of two separate prayer mats for the forehead and nose during prostration was deemed a recommended precaution. Regarding financial issues, taking a loan with interest is strictly forbidden even under duress, and listeners were advised to repay the principal and repent for the excess. Travelers must pray shortened prayers until they reach the distance limit, after which they must pray full prayers. If an obstacle prevents proper ablution, the prayer remains valid if the obstacle is removed within the time limit; otherwise, it must be made up later. Finally, performing daily optional prayers after congregational prayer is permissible with the intention of seeking nearness to God, and feeding a poor person is recommended if one cannot perform these prayers.
