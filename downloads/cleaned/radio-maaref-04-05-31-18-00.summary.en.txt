The program begins with religious music and an overview of upcoming shows before introducing the weekly story, "Moon on the Ship." The narrative follows Sayyid Qutb's 1948 voyage from Alexandria, Egypt, to the United States. During the journey, Qutb reflects on the ocean's majesty as a sign of God's power and resolves to dedicate his stay to promoting Islam. He faces moral tests aboard the ship, demonstrating his steadfast faith by resisting temptation. With the captain's permission, Qutb leads Friday prayers on the deck, a scene that deeply impacts other passengers, including a Christian woman. The story highlights Qutb's strengthened faith and his unwavering commitment to serving his religion.
