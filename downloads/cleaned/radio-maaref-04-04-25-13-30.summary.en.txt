In this radio program, Zainab and Sadat, the wife of martyr Seyed Ahmad Hosseini, recount their shared life and the reasons behind his decision to deploy to Syria. Seyed Ahmad was a devout young Afghan who, inspired by the stories of Lady Zainab and the sacrifice of Qasim ibn al-Hasan, chose to defend the shrine of Imam Hussein. Although Zainab initially resisted with tears and concerns about their family's well-being, Ahmad persuaded her by vividly describing the suffering of the Syrian people and his religious duty. He promised to return within two months with a definitive outcome, leading Zainab to trust his decision despite the pain of separation. The latter part of the program shifts to a political analysis of the continuous plots against the Islamic Republic of Iran since the revolution. These threats include ethnic provocations, targeted assassinations, economic sanctions, and direct military attacks by global powers and intelligence agencies. The narrator emphasizes that the intensity and variety of these threats are unprecedented, highlighting the significance of the revolutionary system in the eyes of its adversaries.
