The radio program began by discussing how to manage unrealistic expectations in marriage, emphasizing that life skills are acquired gradually and initial ignorance is not a fault. The discussion then shifted to the challenges faced by an 18-year-old boy suffering from depression and confusion due to his parents' divorce. The counselor advised parents to provide emotional security and support without blaming each other, and strongly recommended professional psychological help if signs of depression are observed. Finally, the program highlighted the importance of social responsibility by encouraging listeners to participate in the "Iran Hamdel" campaign to support the people of Lebanon affected by recent conflicts.
