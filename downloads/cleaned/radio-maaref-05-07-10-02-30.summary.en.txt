In this radio program, a Mahdism expert addresses the question of whether the reappearance of Imam Zaman is imminent, clarifying that predicting a specific time or relying on superficial analysis is impermissible. He emphasizes the necessity of constant readiness and warns against complacency or unrealistic expectations, noting that the reappearance will not align with human forecasts. The expert also explains that while the timing is divine, humans can facilitate the conditions for reappearance by preparing society. In the second segment, Professor Ali Saffai analyzes Imam Ali's (PBUH) testament, highlighting the importance of integrating the concept of Wilayat into daily life. By translating poetic verses from the testament, he illustrates that Ali's (PBUH) silence and stillness after death serve as a more profound lesson than his living words. Saffai warns that if people do not remain steadfast in their actions, Ali's (PBUH) presence will be as fleeting and ineffective as a passing shadow. Ultimately, the program underscores the critical need to act upon these testaments and develop a deeper understanding of Imam Ali's (PBUH) role in everyday existence.
