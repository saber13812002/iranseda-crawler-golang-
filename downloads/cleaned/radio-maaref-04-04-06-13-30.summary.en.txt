This audio narration from the book "You Have Forgotten" reconstructs the final and heroic scenes of the Battle of Karbala. After the morning prayer, Imam Hussein (PBUH) addresses his companions, emphasizing the necessity of martyrdom in God's path and patience against the enemy. Despite having few forces, he arranges a precise military formation to defend the tents and protect the family. The enemy army, led by Umar Saad and Shimr ibn Dhi al-Jawshan, launches a surprise attack from the rear, with Shimr hurling severe insults at the Imam and his companions. Imam Hussein (PBUH) calmly reminds the people of Kufa that he is the grandson of the Prophet and has no personal enmity with them. He validates his claims by referencing prominent companions of Islam and firmly rejects the proposal to surrender to the Umayyads. Ultimately, the Imam seeks refuge in God, refusing to flee or submit to the oppressors, and stands firm on the righteousness of his cause.
