The radio program "Ma'arif" began by honoring Student Day and highlighting how love for revolutionary ideals drives fundamental societal change. The speaker emphasized that love for the Ahl al-Bayt, particularly Imam Husayn, acts as the alchemy of life, freeing individuals from material suffering. Citing a hadith from Imam Reza, it was stated that devotion to the Infallibles is more valuable than resolving all financial and worldly problems. This love is considered humanity's primary asset and reaches its spiritual peak during pilgrimage routes like Arbaeen. The ultimate goal is to replicate this beauty in daily life and pass this love on to future generations. The program concluded with a call to preserve this devotion and prayers for the listeners.
