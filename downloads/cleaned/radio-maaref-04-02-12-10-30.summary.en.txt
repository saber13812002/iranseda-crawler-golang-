The radio program began by celebrating Teachers' Day, highlighting the role of educators as saviors who guide humanity from ignorance toward the light of knowledge. In the Q&A segment, Dr. Javad Jafari explained that the Savior's name is not explicitly mentioned in the Quran to prevent historical disputes and protect the scripture's integrity, although his characteristics are clearly described in verses and traditions. Engineer Mohammad Sadeghi Kafili interpreted the fifth verse of Surah Ibrahim, describing the Imam's appearance as "Yawm Allah," a day when obstacles are removed and people move from darkness to the light of monotheism and devotion. He emphasized that a deeper understanding of this concept motivates individuals to take proactive steps in preparing for the reappearance. One of the most effective ways to support the Imam was identified as engaging in sincere, face-to-face conversations in daily life to introduce others to Mahdism. The program concluded with the recommendation that expectants should facilitate the conditions for the appearance through pure deeds and prayer, serving as true allies of the Imam in every moment of their lives.
