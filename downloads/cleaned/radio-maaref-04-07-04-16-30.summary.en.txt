This radio program explains the imposition of the truce and arbitration after the Battle of Siffin as a political plot by Muawiyah and his allies against Imam Ali (AS). Despite the Imam's objection, Abu Musa al-Ash'ari was selected as his representative, whereas Imam Ali preferred Abdullah ibn Abbas for this role. Abu Musa, known for his pacifism and previous failure at the Battle of Jamal, reached a deceptive agreement with Muawiyah during negotiations. He initially declared that both sides were deposed so the people could choose a new caliph, but ultimately appointed Muawiyah as caliph and declared Ali (AS) deposed. This blatant betrayal severely damaged Imam Ali's political legitimacy and the outcome of the arbitration. Although Muawiyah's army suffered a tactical defeat on the battlefield, he achieved a political victory through this deception. The event highlights the critical role of the lack of insight among certain figures in creating fitna and harming the cause of Islam.
