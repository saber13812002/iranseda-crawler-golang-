The program began with reciting verses by Saadi that emphasized empathy and viewing humans as parts of a single body.
Host Amirali Ramiyani discussed the importance of mutual understanding and compassion in today's world, criticizing indifference to others' suffering.
He announced that starting next week, reciting poetry in various Iranian dialects, such as Kurdish, Balochi, Khorasani, and Isfahani, would receive special scoring points.
Listeners were invited to submit their audio files via the Shad app or Eitaa to participate in the show.
The host also thanked Shad's managers for their cooperation in promoting the program in the events section and provided contact numbers.
The show concluded with a song featuring spring and kindness themes, which ended with a repetitive loop of a specific line.
The program aims to build a bridge between audiences and diverse Iranian cultures by promoting dialectal diversity and solidarity.
The ultimate goal is to create a more intimate and dynamic space for participants from across the country.
