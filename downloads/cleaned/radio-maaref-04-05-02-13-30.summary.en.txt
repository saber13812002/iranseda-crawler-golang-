In the radio program "Narrative of Love," Zahra Nasiri, the wife of martyr Javad Akbari from Afghanistan, recounts the painful memories of separation and her deep concerns. She describes the difficult period when her husband went to Syria without prior notice, leaving her without news for months, and shares his emotional letters and promises to return. Despite Zahra's repeated objections and fear of losing him, Javad Akbari, influenced by the words of other martyrs and a sense of religious duty, went to the battlefield again to defend the shrines. In the second part of the program, speeches by the Supreme Leader of Iran are broadcast, emphasizing the necessity of adhering to Quranic principles and Islamic ideals. He criticizes Western civilization for being based on flawed foundations and double standards regarding human rights and women's issues. These segments illustrate that the martyrs' sacrifices are rooted in deep religious beliefs, while the Islamic system stands firmly on authentic Quranic values.
