In this segment of the "Barkarane Noor" program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verses 112 to 115 of Surah Al-Ma'idah.
He discusses the request of the Children of Israel for a heavenly table and explains how Jesus (PBUH) refined this request with spiritual corrections before presenting it to God.
Among these corrections was changing the request from "eating" to seeking "provision" and praising God as the "Best of Providers."
Jesus (PBUH) also requested that this event become an annual "Id" (festival) for the Christian community to serve as a perpetual remembrance.
God responded by descending the table but warned of severe punishment for anyone who disbelieved after witnessing this miracle.
The commentary then analyzes the phrase "I have purified you above the women of the worlds" from Surah Maryam.
Through grammatical analysis, the interpreter demonstrates that this phrase signifies both inherent sanctity and a specific selection among all women in history.
This unique selection highlights the status of Fatimah Zahra (SA) as the only woman to become a mother without a husband, distinguishing her among all women of the past and future.
