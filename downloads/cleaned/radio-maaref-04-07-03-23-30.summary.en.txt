The radio program "Hamneshin" opened with religious hymns honoring the martyrs, coinciding with the Sacred Defense Week and Friday night.
Guest Hosseinzadeh, speaking from Lavar Khash village in Fars province, shared his presence at a memorial for seven local martyrs.
He drew a parallel between the martyrdom of Karbala and the Sacred Defense, identifying Imam Hussein (AS) as the open gate of martyrdom and resistance against tyranny.
Hosseinzadeh emphasized that the spirit of Karbala remains alive and serves as a practical model for standing firm against enemies in the modern era.
He also highlighted the role of love and waiting for Imam Mahdi (AJ) in preserving this spiritual legacy and connecting generations.
Towards the end, a speech by the Supreme Leader was aired, stressing the necessity of "supporting God" and performing deeds with divine intent for eternal salvation.
The program concluded with prayer for visiting Imam Hussein's shrine, and the host thanked the listeners for accompanying them in the final episode of this season.
