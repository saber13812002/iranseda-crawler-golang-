This radio program began by addressing various jurisprudential questions, including prayer rules for individuals with physical limitations and the permissibility of repeating the "Subhan Allah" invocation, which Ayatollah Sistani's fatwa allows without limit. The discussion also covered the wife's obligation of obedience if the husband fails to provide financial support, as well as the rules for reciting the Quran and performing ziyarat while in a state of janabah or menstruation, emphasizing that touching sacred verses is prohibited in these states. Additionally, selling goods with illegal profits was deemed haram based on Ayatollah Sistani's ruling. The second part of the program was dedicated to the biography of Martyr Mohammad Mehdi Khademi Shari'eh, a pre-revolution activist and a brave commander in the Battle of Beit al-Moqaddas. Known as the "Mus'ab of the Prophet" due to his courage, he was martyred in 1983 and buried in the shrine of Imam Reza (AS). The program concluded with hadiths highlighting the true characteristics of a Shia, defining a believer as one with pure earnings, good morals, and a sound inner self.
