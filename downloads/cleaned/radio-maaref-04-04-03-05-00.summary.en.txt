This radio program emphasizes the essential balance between individual worship and serving God's creation as the two main pillars of faith.
Citing a narration from Imam Sadiq (AS), it is stated that resolving a Muslim's problem carries the reward of an accepted and approved Hajj and Umrah.
The concept of "Muwasat" is defined in four parts: financial aid within one's means, problem-solving, mutual respect, and defending Muslims' lives, honor, and reputation.
Contrary to popular belief, small acts of charity are sufficient for the needy, and there is no need for ostentatious displays of wealth.
Protecting people's reputation is considered even more important than their life or chastity, and gossiping or listening to it is deemed a grave sin.
The second part of the program delves into a detailed jurisprudential discussion regarding the rulings of waqf (endowments) and the sale of endowed properties.
The distinction between waqf for "benefit" (income) and waqf for "use" (occupancy) is examined in relation to the permissibility of selling the property and buying a replacement.
The final conclusion is that selling and replacing the property is permissible if the endowment is for income, but changing the nature of the property is not allowed if the intent is direct use.
