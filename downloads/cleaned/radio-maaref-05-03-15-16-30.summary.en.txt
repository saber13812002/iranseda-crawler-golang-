The radio program began by examining historical debates regarding the Shura and Imam Ali's status in both Shia and Sunni sources. The guest expert then introduced Sheikh Saduq, a prominent 4th-century hadith scholar, highlighting his extensive works and their enduring reference value in Islamic jurisprudence and history. The discussion subsequently analyzed the figure of Zulqarnain, exploring the Quranic basis for his name and his role as a just ruler who traveled to the far corners of the earth. The expert also addressed the lineage of Ja'far al-Kadhdhab, noting the scholarly ambiguity surrounding the repentance of his descendants based on historical genealogies and religious texts. Finally, the program focused on the sects active during the era of Imam Hadi, particularly the Ghulat and Mu'tazila. The expert emphasized the Ahl al-Bayt's strict stance against extremism and theological deviations, urging listeners to maintain a balanced and moderate approach to their faith.
