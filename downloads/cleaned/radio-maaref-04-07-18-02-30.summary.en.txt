In this radio program, Dr. Sadeghi addresses various historical questions from listeners. He first confirms the birth of Imam Ali (AS) in the Kaaba, citing both Shia and Sunni sources, and attributes its omission in some histories to historian bias or selection. Regarding the second question, he explains that nearly all prominent Muslims in Mecca migrated, while the few who remained, such as Abbas or Abu Rafi, did so for specific reasons or due to their dependent status. Dr. Sadeghi also clarifies that Imam Hasan Askari (AS) and Ja'far al-Kazzab shared the same mother, noting that ambiguities in the behavior of some Imams' children are not unusual. In the following section, he identifies Shurayha as the judge of Kufa during Imam Hussein's (AS) time; while condemning Shurayha's betrayal during the incident of Hani ibn Urawa, he dismisses the claim that he issued a fatwa for the Imam's murder as lacking credible evidence. Finally, he explains that the Islamization of Iran was a gradual process and that exaggerated casualty figures from that era are unreliable. He attributes Iran's emergence as a center of Islamic science and Shiaism to its geographical position, civilizational background, and the efforts of Iranian scholars in compiling Islamic texts.
