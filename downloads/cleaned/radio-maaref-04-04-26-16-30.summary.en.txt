The radio program emphasized the necessity of avoiding divisive ethnic and religious perspectives to maintain social unity and cohesion.
It was highlighted that scientific criticism and theological discussions must occur within a framework of mutual respect, avoiding polarization.
The guest attributed the global support for Iran to its discourse of human nature, which he identified as the key to defeating enemies and preserving national power.
The second segment focused on the concept of freedom, defining it in religious teachings as liberation from base desires and worldly dependencies.
Quranic interpretations indicated that true freedom is achieved when an individual is released from the bondage of sin and respects the rights of others.
It was stressed that freedom is not absolute but is defined within an ethical and divine framework, balancing individual rights with societal interests.
Guest Hujjat al-Islam Ali Gohari linked freedom to a sense of independence, critical thinking, and better mental and physical health.
Ultimately, the program concluded by discussing citizen rights and the essential balance between individual freedoms and collective benefits.
