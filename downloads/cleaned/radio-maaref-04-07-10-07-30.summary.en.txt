The program began by emphasizing that supplements should only be taken under medical supervision to address nutritional deficiencies. The discussion then shifted to the role of elites, stressing that they must offer solutions and actively participate in production and technology rather than merely criticizing. Officials are urged to create employment opportunities and utilize domestic products to foster a sense of utility among elites, thereby preventing brain drain. The main segment, led by Hujjat al-Islam Falahati, detailed the severe consequences of foul language and cursing based on religious narrations. These consequences include financial hardship, loss of divine love, being viewed as demonic, and being deprived of the Prophet's intercession on Judgment Day. It was also noted that habitual cursing leads to increased aggression and poor social and family relationships. The program concluded by urging listeners to guard their tongues and avoid insulting others.
