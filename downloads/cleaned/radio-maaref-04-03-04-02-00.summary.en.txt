The program begins by emphasizing the importance of seeking intercession through the Ahl al-Bayt, citing stories of devotion from prominent religious figures. The main discussion then focuses on the relationship between religion and ethics in the story of the sacrifice of Prophet Ismail. Dr. Sadeghi refutes theories such as absolute divine command, the complete separation of religion from ethics, and symbolic interpretations. He argues that the divine command was inherently ethical, relying on the infallibility of the prophets to demonstrate that obedience was a rational and religious duty. To illustrate this point, he uses the analogy of executing judicial rulings based on apparent evidence, even if a potential error exists. The final conclusion is that within the framework of religion, obedience to divine laws is compatible with ethical principles, and no contradiction exists between them.
