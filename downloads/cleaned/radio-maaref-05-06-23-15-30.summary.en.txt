The six-part series on hudud crimes against the Islamic system concluded by analyzing the jurisprudential and legal distinctions between concepts such as Moharebeh, Barqi, and Ifsad-fil-Ard.
The final recommendation to judges is to meticulously verify all conditions of these crimes to prevent unjust rulings influenced by media pressure or public opinion.
Conversely, when the conditions for a crime are fully met, it is urged that judgments be issued and executed with courage and without hesitation.
The primary objective of applying these divine rulings is to establish order, security, and peace in the Islamic society, which is desired by all members of the community.
The judicial system must remain unaffected by the noise of the system's enemies or false news, and should explain its rulings to the public with transparency and confidence.
The second part of the program featured the recitation and translation of verses 41 to 96 from Surah Al-Waqi'ah, emphasizing the Day of Judgment and God's creative power.
These verses challenge humans with questions about God's creation versus their denial, highlighting the necessity of gratitude and remembrance of the Resurrection.
Finally, the Prophet is instructed to ask the disbelievers to return the soul of a dying person to prove their truthfulness, a task they are incapable of performing.
