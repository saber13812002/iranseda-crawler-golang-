This segment of the program discusses the validity of historical narrations and the implications of accepting them.
The core of the discussion analyzes the behavior of a person close to God when facing the duty of accepting guardianship.
According to the arguments presented, this individual committed a lesser sin by hesitating to accept the guardianship.
This lesser sin granted him the choice between worldly punishment and afterlife punishment.
He chose the worldly punishment and was exiled to a remote island.
With the birth of the infallible Imam, this lesser sin was forgiven, altering his status.
At the end of the show, listeners were invited to submit their theological questions via phone or voice message.
These questions will be answered by Professor Mohammadi Sharodi in future episodes.
