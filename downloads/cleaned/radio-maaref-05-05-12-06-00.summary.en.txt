Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verses 16 to 19 of Surah Al-A'raf, clarifying that Satan's request for a respite until the Day of Judgment was not for eternity but until the time of death and resurrection. He refutes the doctrines of fatalism and absolute free will by explaining that Satan's attribution of misguidance to God stems from his ignorance of the difference between initial and punitive divine misguidance. God never initially misleads humans; rather, He abandons them to their own fate after they reject repentance, which is a punitive measure rather than a forced one. Satan attacks humans through four main avenues: deceiving them about the afterlife, promoting worldly desires, relying on power, and inciting lust. Humans must defend themselves against these temptations by relying on Quranic verses and the principle of monotheism. The ultimate path to salvation lies in seeking refuge through prayer and supplication from above and prostration and humility from below. Satan attempts to sow doubt and hesitation at every stage of the religious journey, from embracing Islam to engaging in jihad.
