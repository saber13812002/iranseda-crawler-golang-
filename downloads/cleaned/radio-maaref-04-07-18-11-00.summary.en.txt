The 32nd National Prayer Conference concluded in Rasht, emphasizing the role of mothers and modern technologies in spiritual growth. The Vice President of the Assembly of Experts highlighted the Supreme Leader's direct management of recent crises and his role in restoring national calm. An international conference poster dedicated to Martyr Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah was unveiled, with the event scheduled for late this year in Mashhad. The head of the country's seminaries criticized the lack of creativity in architecture, while the head of the Welfare Organization announced a policy shift toward family empowerment. Fiqh sections addressed questions regarding missed morning prayers, the transfer of interest-free loans, and rulings on doubts concerning purity and wudu. Additionally, rulings were provided on the proper method of washing wudu limbs and the conditions for joining congregational prayer.
