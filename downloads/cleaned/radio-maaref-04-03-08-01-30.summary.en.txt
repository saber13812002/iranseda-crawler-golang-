The program begins by distinguishing between local Muslim customs and authentic Islamic rulings, noting that many wedding traditions are non-Islamic cultural practices. The speaker defines marriage not as a chemical combination, but as the pairing of two beings created as a pair by God. In this view, human spiritual evolution depends on maintaining this duality, and Islam preserves this cosmic principle through its legal framework. Upcoming topics include marriage impediments, criteria for choosing a spouse based on religious guidance, and the causes of family dysfunction. Citing Toffler and Western statistics, the speaker warns that the collapse of the family institution leads to widespread psychological and social crises. Examples from Greece and Germany are provided, where declining marriage rates and rising divorce rates have put the population at risk of extinction. Finally, the necessity of teaching Islamic marriage culture and spousal ethics is emphasized to save families from the Western fate and prevent widespread divorce in Iran.
