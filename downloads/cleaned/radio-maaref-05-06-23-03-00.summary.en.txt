In this radio program, prayer is first described as a loving and enjoyable dialogue between God and the servant, based on the ethical teachings of Ayatollah Mazaheri.
According to narrations, worship should not be driven solely by fear or the desire for reward; rather, the highest form is the worship of the free, performed out of love for the Creator.
In the second part, Hojjat al-Islam Hosseini Qomi interprets hadiths from the book of Kafi, explaining the three categories of worship: slaves, hired workers, and free people.
He also emphasizes the moral ugliness of becoming poor after wealth, sinning after repentance, and abandoning worship after practicing it.
Imam Sajjad (AS) states in a narration that the most devout people are those who correctly fulfill their religious obligations.
The program concludes with a pre-dawn supplication, inviting listeners to deepen their spiritual connection and seek divine mercy.
It highlights that true worship requires worthiness, intercession through the Ahl al-Bayt, and a living, loving heart.
The ultimate goal is to transform from a fearful servant or wage-earner into a free lover of God.
