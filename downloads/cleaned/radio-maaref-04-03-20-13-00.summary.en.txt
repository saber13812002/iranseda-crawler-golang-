In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Mirbagheri uses Quranic verses to explain the danger of deviation from the path of truth and following the enemies of religion. He emphasizes that the only way to save oneself from these threats is to hold fast to the rope of God and follow the Quran and the Infallible Imams, particularly Imam Ali (AS). Referring to the divine gift of prophethood, he explains how the Prophet (PBUH) transformed tense tribal relations into genuine brotherhood and affection. The speaker warns that separating from this spiritual axis leads society toward hell and worldly deviations. Conversely, following the conduct of Imam Ali (AS) creates a heavenly society where sacrifice, love, and forgiveness replace competition and envy. Citing Imam Khomeini's views, he defines pure Islam as the Islam of Amir al-Mu'minin and corrupt Islam as that of the Umayyads. Finally, he stresses the necessity of true piety and reciting prayers to hasten the reappearance of Imam Mahdi (AJ) and strengthen the front of the believers.
