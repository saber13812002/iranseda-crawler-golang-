This radio program addressed several jurisprudential questions based on the rulings of Islamic scholars. It began by discussing the financial independence of a child from their father and its impact on maintenance obligations. The method for making up a forgotten tashahhud in prayer was explained according to Imam Khomeini's legal treatise. The ruling on using a toothbrush with residual toothpaste while fasting and the conditions for determining one's domicile for prayer and fasting were also examined. Additionally, the obligation of a person who intentionally harmed a fetus to pay blood money to the fetus's heirs was detailed, including the distribution of inheritance. Finally, a narration from Imam Javad (AS) regarding kindness to fellow believers and avoiding arrogance was presented.
