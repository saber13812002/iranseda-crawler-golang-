This radio program highlights the divine character of Imam Hussein (PBUH) and his Ar-Rafat prayer to explore the concept of "Qawl Thabit" (firm word) versus fleeting human motivations. The speaker argues that Imam Hussein reached an absolute level of faith and love for God, maintaining his composure even amidst the extreme hardships of Karbala. The Quranic concept of "Jamil" (beautiful) is interpreted as the divine wisdom within suffering, suggesting that the tragedies of Karbala were beautiful and justified within God's plan. Relying on Quranic verses, the program emphasizes that humans should not despair of the past or fear the future, as God is always present with His servants. The importance of prayer is stressed as a means to clear the mind of illusions and establish a transparent connection with the Divine. The speaker also critiques human self-centeredness, which acts as a barrier to perceiving God's inherent closeness to humanity. Ultimately, the core message is a call for submission to divine will and the abandonment of egoism to find true inner peace.
