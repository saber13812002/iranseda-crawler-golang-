Ali Roozbehani, a veteran Iranian football player and coach, shares his memories of playing for military teams and winning the top scorer title in futsal. He recounts his experience with the national futsal team, including participation in the Rome Olympics and international tournaments in Italy. Roozbehani attributes his absence from the national football team to military restrictions and the specific conditions of that era, while sharing an anecdote about Jalal Cheraghpour's suggestion for his inclusion. He also discusses his coaching tenure with the Ground Forces team and their unsuccessful attempts to reach the Premier League. Highlighting the lack of strong military teams in the league, he proposes forming a new squad comprising experienced and young players. In the final section, he talks about his post-retirement life and his sons' activities in football and education. Roozbehani emphasizes the importance of developing grassroots football in Tehran and expresses gratitude for the current officials' efforts in this field.
