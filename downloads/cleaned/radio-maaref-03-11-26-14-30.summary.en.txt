In the first segment, Professor Yosefian cited authentic narrations to identify the Mahdi as a specific individual rather than a general concept, emphasizing the impact of his light on the faithful during his occultation. He described the pre-apocalyptic era as a period of global instability and political confusion, highlighting Iran's social stability and its central role in spreading Mahdavi teachings from Qom. Subsequently, the program "Rahbord" analyzed the managerial strategies of the Supreme Leader, reviewing Imam Khomeini's political actions during the struggle and the establishment of the new system. Professor Rasakhi stressed that Imam Khomeini's formulation of a new political system was an unprecedented experience in Shia jurisprudence that requires deep analysis. This segment focuses on understanding the characteristics of leadership following Imam Khomeini and examining the managerial strategies of the current Supreme Leader in recent decades. The ultimate goal is to better comprehend the transfer of power and the maintenance of the Islamic system's stability in the new era.
