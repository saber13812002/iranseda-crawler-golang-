This text narrates the martyrdom of Ali Akbar, Qasim, and Abbas (AS) during the event of Karbala. Ali Akbar, after suffering extreme thirst, was martyred and his pure body was returned to the tents. Qasim, a youth who shone like the full moon, was martyred in the battlefield, causing Imam Hussein (AS) to grieve deeply. After the martyrdom of his brothers, Abbas (AS) was granted permission by the Imam to fetch water from the Euphrates but was ambushed by the enemy. Despite having both his right and left hands severed, he continued to defend the Imam until his final breath. The news of Abbas's martyrdom deeply grieved Imam Hussein (AS), who wept bitterly over his brother. Finally, the virtues of Imam Hussein's (AS) martyrdom, including his intercession and the high status of the Imamate in his lineage, were emphasized.
