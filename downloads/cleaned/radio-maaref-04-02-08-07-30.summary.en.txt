Following the tragic incident at Shahid Rajaee Port in Bandar Abbas, which resulted in hundreds of casualties, a three-day public mourning was declared in Hormozgan province and a one-day mourning across the country. The Supreme Leader and the President have emphasized the need for a serious investigation into any negligence and the identification of the accident's causes. A key point of discussion is the necessity of storing over 30,000 containers at this port complex and finding safer management solutions for such a high volume of goods. In the second part of the program, the importance of deep empathy with the victims and understanding the pain of fellow citizens was highlighted based on Quranic verses and narrations. The concept of apostasy from faith and the dangers of doctrinal deviations were also discussed. Parents were warned to monitor their children's religious beliefs against the risks of disbelief and polytheism.
