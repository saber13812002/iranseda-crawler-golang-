The radio program "Zamzam Ahkam" began with morning music and prayers before addressing various jurisprudential questions from listeners. The first segment clarified the rulings on oral fluids and post-extraction bleeding during dental procedures, stating that clothing remains pure if blood is absent or covers less than the width of a finger. It was also explained that while blood in the mouth is pure, consuming it is forbidden unless it is completely dissolved in saliva. Subsequent answers covered the obligation to respond to greetings during prayer, the conditions for cosmetic surgery by a non-mahram doctor, and the exemption of government welfare payments from Khums. Other inquiries addressed the process of making an adopted child a mahram, the validity of prayer with ink on nails, and the right to reclaim gifted money held by a spouse. The program concluded by detailing the rules of Wudu with slight moisture on the feet and announcing the specific times for Fajr and sunrise in Qom. Finally, the broadcast ended with a special supplication and an introduction to the next program, "Sobh-e Niaz."
