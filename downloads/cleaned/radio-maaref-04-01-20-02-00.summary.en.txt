In this radio program, the instructor addressed listeners' theological questions by first outlining two primary paths to knowing God: rational deduction based on the order of creation and spiritual, heart-based knowledge emphasized in prayers. Responding to a parent's concern about a teenager's skepticism regarding God's existence, the speaker stressed that knowledge is not limited to the five senses and that rational arguments, such as the "footprint" and "building" analogies, effectively prove God's existence. The role of Istikhara in marriage was clarified, noting that it is not a substitute for thorough research, consultation, and verification, but is only permissible when a person remains undecided between two equally suitable options after complete investigation. The apparent contradiction between the hadith stating that remembrance of God originates from God and the Quranic verse "Remember Me, and I will remember you" was resolved through the concept of divine agency, explaining that human will operates within the scope of God's will. The discussion then shifted to the Barzakh realm, where it was explained that the soul's acquired attributes and feelings are transferred, although material tools and specific worldly actions are absent in that dimension. Finally, the instructor explained that the negative effects of sins on the soul can be purified through good deeds or punishment in subsequent realms, whereas fundamental sins like shirk leave lasting impacts on the spirit.
