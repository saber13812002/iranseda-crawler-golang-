This program features the memories and conversations of veterans regarding the life and martyrdom of Alireza Mahini, the commander of the Unconventional Warfare Headquarters in the South. After the martyrdom of Shahid Chamran, Mahini assumed command of this headquarters and played an active role in operations such as Hoveyzeh and Dehlaveh. Following the dissolution of the Unconventional Warfare Headquarters and the deployment of troops to training courses, he was sent to the Chazabeh area in Bahman 1360 to counter an Iraqi army invasion. During this battle, Mahini resisted the enemy heavily and was ultimately martyred by a mortar shell fragment on the 23rd of Bahman 1360. The final section of the program highlights the achievements of the Army's Navy, including the Dena ship's complete circumnavigation of the globe. Additionally, scattered messages regarding support for the people of Gaza and encouragement to perform small acts for environmental preservation and social peace are discussed in the program.
