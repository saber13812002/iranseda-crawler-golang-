The first segment of the program emphasized the necessity for mourners and mobq operators to maintain order and cleanliness on sidewalks to avoid disrupting pedestrians and overburdening sanitation workers.
Sharia rulings were provided regarding the impurity of washing machine sinks, the financial restitution required for fraud victims, and the need to verify parental consent for children's food.
It was also clarified that changing one's Marja must be based on independent research, as following family tradition alone lacks religious validity.
In the second part, Hujjat al-Islam Ibrahimi discussed the conditions for the validity of Friday prayers, specifically addressing the ruling on safety railings that might obstruct the view of the Imam.
Hujjat al-Islam Hamidi highlighted the mandatory nature of Friday prayers by citing Quranic verses and historical narratives about the Prophet's insistence on attending prayer despite economic hardships.
The program featured a sermon by the late Ayatollah Imam Kashani criticizing the humiliation of America and rulers who ignore their people.
Finally, the broadcast concluded with religious anthems and an invitation to join Friday processions, transitioning to the Friday prayer in Tehran led by Ayatollah Khatami.
