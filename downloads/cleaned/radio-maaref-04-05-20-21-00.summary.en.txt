The program began with advice on the optimal consumption of water and electricity during the summer season. Following Quranic recitation and poetry, the main segment featured a lecture by Hujjat al-Islam Vahidi on the importance of seeking knowledge and respecting teachers. He illustrated practical examples of reverence towards educators through stories of Ayatollah Khorasani and Mirza Shirazi. Subsequently, a report highlighted the national slogan of "production leap with people's participation," emphasizing investment for economic growth in 2024. The final section presented a narrative from the book "Seyyed Man Hosseini," detailing Mohammad Taghi's journey to Karbala. The story depicted the challenges at the Mehran border, the travel by van, and the visit to Najaf. It concluded with Mohammad Taghi's efforts to locate his cultural group and receiving assistance from an elderly Iraqi man at the shrine of Imam Ali.
