This radio program discusses the pivotal role of scholars and ascetics in preventing arrogance and ignorance within human societies. The speaker argues that early Christians avoided arrogance and developed compassionate hearts due to the presence of dedicated monks, whereas Jews became arrogant and hardened because their scholars were either eliminated or corrupted. The Quran highlights this moral distinction through verses describing Christians as non-arrogant and their hearts filled with mercy, contrasting them with the Jews. The concept of innovation (bid'ah) is also examined, clarifying that positive innovations, such as building Husainiyyas, can be recommended or obligatory, unlike forbidden innovations. The speaker emphasizes that if Christians deviate from the teachings of Jesus (PBUH), they will become as arrogant and warlike as the Jews. Finally, the importance of faith in the fundamental principles of religion and the desire to join the ranks of the martyrs are explained as the highest spiritual achievements.
