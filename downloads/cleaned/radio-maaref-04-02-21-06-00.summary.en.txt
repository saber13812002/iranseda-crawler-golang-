In this radio program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli provides a profound interpretation of verses 97 to 99 of Surah Al-Ma'idah, presenting the Kaaba as a symbol of spiritual elevation and the axis of the cosmic order. He critiques Fakhr Razi's terrestrial view that the Kaaba serves only Arab interests, emphasizing instead that it is a source of guidance and blessing for all humanity. The discussion centers on the relationship between the severity of divine punishment and God's mercy, arguing that divine mercy precedes wrath and guides it. His exegesis reveals that even hell and punishment ultimately serve God's mercy by acting as catalysts for human awakening and moral correction. The conclusion of this interpretation is that worldly and otherworldly security and prosperity are achieved through faith in this divine system and a deep understanding of God's attributes.
