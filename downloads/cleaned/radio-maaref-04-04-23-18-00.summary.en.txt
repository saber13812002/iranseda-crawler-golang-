In the "Ganj-e Sa'adat" program, Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallah emphasized the blessings of charity based on Quranic verses and narrations. He explained that the reward for charity is not necessarily immediate or material; rather, God grants it according to His wisdom and the servant's needs, sometimes in the future or for future generations. Key benefits of charity include drawing closer to God, achieving true peace, receiving the Prophet's prayers, and gaining shade on the Day of Judgment. Charity is also described as a shield against the Hellfire, where even giving half a date or speaking a kind word can save one from divine punishment. In the second segment, Ayatollah Fazel-Dankari cited verse 59 of Surah Al-Ahzab to assert that hijab is an explicit and obligatory divine command for all believing women until the Day of Judgment. By interpreting the word "jilbab" as covering the entire body, he argued that loose or inappropriate clothing does not fulfill this requirement, emphasizing that hijab must prevent the gaze of non-mahram men.
