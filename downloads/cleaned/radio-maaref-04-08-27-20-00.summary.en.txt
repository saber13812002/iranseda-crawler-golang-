The Shia Coordination Framework declared itself the largest parliamentary faction in Iran, turning Baghdad into a hub for coalition negotiations to form the new government. While resistance-aligned currents maintained and strengthened their political influence, opposing factions faced a decline in public support. Concurrently, a US-backed UN Security Council resolution was passed to establish a transitional government in Gaza led by Trump, with China and Russia abstaining from vetoing it. A friendly football match between Catalonia and Palestine was held to honor the martyred athletes of Gaza. In Afghanistan, the international Kabul exhibition opened with a focus on Iran's transport capacities, despite Pakistan closing its border crossings. The 48th Kuwait International Book Fair launched with the slogan "Capital of Culture," offering free books to visitors. In Iran, the Supreme Leader's forewords for three books on the Holy Defense were unveiled during the 21st Celebration of Jihad and Resistance Literature. The 33rd Islamic Republic Book Week was also held under the slogan "Let's Read for Iran."
