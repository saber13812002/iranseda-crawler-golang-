In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Fallah explores the concept of "language" and its dimensions, including verbal, sensory, and body language, based on Quranic verses. He defines language as a tool for transmitting perceptions and emphasizes the importance of cultivating it to prevent moral harm. A key point in the discussion is the distinction between the innate and fascinating nature of body language and the need for managing verbal speech. Citing verses such as "He taught man eloquence," the speaker highlights eloquence as a unique human privilege and a sign of divine creation. The Quranic advice "Do not move your tongue hastily" is presented as a crucial principle for patience in understanding and avoiding haste in conveying messages. The speaker stresses the necessity of using simple, comprehensible language in religious preaching to ensure divine messages are transmitted without barriers. In the concluding section, the verse "The truth is from your Lord" is interpreted to contrast the permanence of divine truth with the transience of worldly values and materialistic relationships.
