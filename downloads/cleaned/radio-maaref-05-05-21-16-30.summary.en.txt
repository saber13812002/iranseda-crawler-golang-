In this radio program, the permissibility of reciting general ziyarat for all Imams from a distance, even outside specific times, was explained based on narrations from Imam Sadiq and Imam Hasan. The discussion then addressed the concept of hidden knowledge (Ilm al-Ghaib), distinguishing between independent knowledge denied in the Quran and knowledge granted through divine revelation to prophets and Imams. Regarding jurisprudence, the preference for wearing a ring on the right hand over the left was emphasized, citing caution during the Imam's occultation. Questions about the temporal and ontological nature of the universe's creation, as well as the ruling on cursing animals, were also answered. The absolute authority (Wilayat) of the Imams and their ability to distinguish halal from haram in difficult conditions, such as imprisonment, were clarified. Finally, highlighting Iran's scientific progress under sanctions, it was emphasized that returning to Islamic principles can lead to the country's scientific and cultural flourishing.
