The radio program "Andisheha," featuring Dr. Hamid Mohseni, explored the epistemological and ethical challenges posed by artificial intelligence.
Key concerns include the technology's autonomous learning capabilities and self-directed systems, which challenge traditional definitions of moral responsibility.
Privacy violations through the collection of personal data and user chats are identified as significant security threats.
Determining liability in autonomous vehicle accidents and the reliability of AI in jurisprudential and medical matters require a re-evaluation of Islamic jurisprudence.
While AI can serve as a "jurisprudential assistant" for searching resources, it cannot replace human judgment due to data limitations and epistemological errors.
Recent research indicates that AI models lack sufficient data in Islamic humanities, and publishing AI-generated articles faces legal and ethical hurdles.
Finally, it was emphasized that in critical situations such as surgery or life-and-death decisions, the final decision-maker must remain human rather than an algorithm.
