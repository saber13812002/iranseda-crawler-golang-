This radio program begins by explaining the philosophy of prayer as a means to remember God and avoid immorality, emphasizing that divine remembrance is the highest purpose of worship. The discussion then analyzes verse 82 of Surah An-Naml, interpreting the "beast from the earth" as a symbol of the Imam Mahdi's appearance during the Raj'ah (return) rather than the Day of Judgment. Scholars argue this event aims to revive truth and falsehood, allowing believers and sinners to return to the world to complete their spiritual development. The program also highlights the significance of the Arbaeen pilgrimage, urging participants to emulate the behavior of the martyrs to achieve salvation and loyalty to the Ahl al-Bayt. Furthermore, it stresses the importance of maintaining faith and unity in the face of external threats and unfounded fears. Finally, the life lessons of Arbaeen are presented as a way to keep the truth of martyrdom alive against the enemy's propaganda.
