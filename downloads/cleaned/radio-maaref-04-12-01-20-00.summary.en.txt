Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi emphasized the need for cultural and scientific exchanges between Muslims and Christians to mitigate the negative legacy of the Crusades during a meeting with the Iranian ambassador to the Vatican. In Gaza, Palestinians revived Ramadan traditions by praying at Al-Aqsa Mosque and crafting lanterns from recycled materials despite widespread destruction. In Turkey, the "Alu Iftar" program was launched to distribute free iftar meals to needy families in Istanbul. Egypt celebrated the holy month with extensive Quran recitation sessions and city decorations, viewing Ramadan as a significant social and civilizational phenomenon. A Quran exhibition was held in Kabul, Afghanistan, attracting many young people, while the Nour Mosque with its distinctive glass dome was inaugurated in the UAE. Bosnian Muslims welcomed Ramadan by decorating Sarajevo and holding collective prayers and events. Pakistan faced domestic criticism for sending a delegation to the Gaza peace committee in Washington, which was seen as contradicting the country's historical support for Palestine. Finally, the 21st "Mercy for Gaza" relief ship, loaded with food and supplies, departed from Mersin, Turkey, bound for the Gaza Strip.
