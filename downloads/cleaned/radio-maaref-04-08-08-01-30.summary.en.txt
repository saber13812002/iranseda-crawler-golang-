The first segment features excerpts from a lecture by the late Allameh Jafari on the Quran's role in history and its driving forces. He cites Quranic verses to describe humans as the drivers of history and stable truths as its raw materials. The lecture also highlights the Quran's unique classification of worldly life and the good life, emphasizing human dignity. The second segment introduces the radio program "Narrative of Love," featuring Shamsi Ghasemi, the sister of martyr Kamal Ghasemi. Ms. Ghasemi recalls her brother's profound influence on her intellectual and religious path during their youth. She describes the moment she first felt the weight of being a martyr's sister when he played a martyr in a school play. She also shares memories of his frequent deployments to the front lines and the painful farewells before his martyrdom in the Fath al-Imam operation.
