The program emphasizes the priority of respecting believers over the Kaaba, highlighting the necessity of paying zakat and charity while one has the means.
Listeners face three critical risks: losing wealth, missing the opportunity to help the poor, and sudden death that eliminates the chance to act.
To warn against these dangers, the stories of the garden owners in Surah Al-Qalam and the two gardeners in Surah Al-Kahf are revisited.
In these narratives, arrogance and denial of the Day of Judgment lead to the destruction of wealth and deep regret in the final moments of life.
The program also cites hadiths from Imam Sadiq (AS) that identify friendship for the sake of God as a fundamental pillar of faith.
This type of divine friendship illuminates a person's face and pulpit on Judgment Day, signifying true spiritual commitment.
Finally, the broadcast mentions recent droughts, calling for prayers for mercy rain and urging support for the people of Gaza.
