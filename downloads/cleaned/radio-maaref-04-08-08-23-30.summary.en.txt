This episode of the radio program "Mr. Judge" examines a medical malpractice case involving a dentist. The dentist extracted four healthy teeth in addition to the damaged ones without conducting necessary tests or X-rays. Forensic experts classified this action as a criminal offense under Article 495 of the Islamic Penal Code. The patient suffered compounded psychological distress due to the doctor's and his secretary's subsequent neglect. Through the judge's mediation, the parties agreed that the dentist would cover the cost of dental implants and pay compensation for the healthy teeth. Ultimately, the dentist secured the patient's forgiveness to avoid the initiation of disciplinary proceedings. This case highlights the critical importance of medical professionals adhering to technical and ethical standards to protect patient rights.
