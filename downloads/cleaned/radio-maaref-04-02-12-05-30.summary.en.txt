The program begins by exploring the philosophical concept of how the will of souls with spiritual miracles can manifest external effects, replacing mere imagination with reality.
It then highlights the occasion of the 300th anniversary of the Qom Seminary, emphasizing the economic slogan of "investment for production" to improve livelihoods.
The core of the lecture focuses on the virtue of knowledge and the specific ethical and methodological conditions required for teachers and students in Islam.
Teachers are advised to organize their content, eliminate unnecessary details, and start their sessions with the remembrance of God to create a worshipful atmosphere.
They must treat students with respect and patience, addressing their questions with kindness and integrating moral education with academic instruction.
The ultimate goal is for the teacher to act as a "human-making factory," inspired by the Prophet's pedagogical methods to cultivate complete and high-quality individuals.
