In this radio program, Professor Moradi analyzes the verses of Surah Al-Fath, focusing on the Prophet's role as the commander of the Islamic army. He emphasizes that the Pledge of Aqabah signifies a direct covenant with God, asserting that God's power supersedes all human authority. The fundamental difference between believers and hypocrites lies in their faith in the unseen and divine power, whereas pagans rely solely on material calculations. By referencing the Eight-Year War and Imam Khomeini's uprising, the speaker demonstrates that victories defying material logic stem from divine assistance and allegiance to the Wilayah. The discussion also criticizes Arab leaders who, through misplaced suspicion of God and collusion with enemies, have abandoned Muslim solidarity. Furthermore, the program addresses the "Mukhaleefon," those who refused to join the Prophet in the journey to Hudaybiyah, noting their false excuses and insincere repentance. The Quran warns that if God wills harm or benefit, no one can stop it, highlighting that reliance on divine power is the only path to salvation.
