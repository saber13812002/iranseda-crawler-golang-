In this radio program, following a call for aid to the victims of the conflict in Lebanon, a tafsir of Surah An-Nisa by Ayatollah Javadi Amoli was broadcast. The commentator used the metaphor of a mirror to explain the relationship between monotheism and intercession, emphasizing the role of practical intellect in choosing one's life path. He described humans as "animals in reality and humans in potential," noting that a wrong choice leads to a descent from humanity to pure animal nature. This process of decline occurs in three stages—animal, plant, and mineral—each containing three sub-levels of degradation. Hypocrites are considered lower than any animal or mineral because they consciously misuse their God-given intellect, ultimately reaching the lowest depth of Hell. On the Day of Judgment, these individuals will find no intercessors, and their buried innate nature will be exposed as a punishment in the lowest level of the Fire. However, the possibility of return through repentance and seeking refuge in the Quran and the Prophet's family remains. This spiritual revival leads to the emergence of wisdom and sincerity within the individual.
