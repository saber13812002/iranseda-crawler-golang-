In the Friday sermon, it was emphasized that the majority of students are faithful and revolutionary, while recent university violence was committed by a minority of outsiders.
Scientific debate is the proper tool for university discourse, and the use of barbaric instruments like knives and stones is a violation of this principle.
Authorities are urged to impose exemplary and public punishments on these individuals to prevent the recurrence of such behavior.
The media war, characterized by the spread of false rumors in cyberspace, was identified as a serious threat rooted in hypocrisy and aimed at manipulating public opinion.
The Quran explicitly condemns "marghifoon" (rumor-mongers) and calls on people not to believe the news circulating in social media.
In the environmental segment, clean air was described as a divine blessing and a necessity for both physical and mental health.
Environmental psychology suggests that nature serves as a bridge between humans and inner peace, making its preservation a practical form of gratitude.
The program concluded with musical pieces and a radio drama focusing on emotional and family-related themes.
