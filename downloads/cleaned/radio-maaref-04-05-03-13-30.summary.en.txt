The text examines Reza Pahlavi's Caucasian roots and the structure of the Qajar army, which was heavily influenced by Russia. During this era, various government sectors were controlled by European advisors, while the Cossack Brigade served as the official army under Russian command. Following the Bolshevik Revolution and the collapse of the Russian Empire, Britain seized control of the brigade using loyalist officers and Reza Pahlavi. Reza, who had previously suppressed the Constitutional Revolution and killed its leaders, seized power in the 1921 coup to establish the Pahlavi regime. The speaker criticizes historical distortions and highlights the religious motivations of constitutional leaders like Sattar Khan and Bagher Khan. Finally, the US President's call for Iran's surrender is interpreted as an admission of Washington's true hostile intentions. The speaker expresses pride in Iran's historical resistance and emphasizes the nation's unity and resilience against external pressures.
