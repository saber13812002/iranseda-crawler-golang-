In the radio program "Zamzam Ahkam," key points regarding hygiene and health during the Arbaeen pilgrimage were discussed. Due to the large crowds and hot weather, preventing illness and maintaining personal cleanliness are religiously essential for the well-being of oneself and others. The importance of avoiding waste in food and beverages was emphasized, and it was clarified that sanitary water is strictly for drinking and cannot be used for wudu or washing. In response to religious inquiries, it was stated that performing the Arbaeen ziyara on days before or after the main day still carries significant reward and participation in the gathering is valued. Additionally, the salary earned from a job obtained through past cheating is considered halal if the individual currently possesses the necessary qualifications and has not wronged others. Rulings on wudu after ghusl, praying in clothes with blood stains, and calculating zakat on gold were also explained based on the fatwas of religious authorities. Finally, it was explained that varying prices for different customers is permissible as long as it does not involve deception or fraud.
