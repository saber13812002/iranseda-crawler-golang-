The radio broadcast from the Ma'aref network began with news coverage, announcing that registration for the 1405 Hajj Umrah will start in the second half of the month of Mehr. The program also reported on the first-anniversary memorial ceremony for Sayyid Hassan Nasrallah held in Qom and the arrival of 44 newly identified martyrs from the Sacred Defense at the Shalamcheh border. The second congress honoring the 3,000 martyrs of Semnan province concluded with an emphasis on the culture of sacrifice and economic resistance. Additionally, the Endowments Organization unveiled the "Amian Asset Card," and the 13th National Student Marriage Plan launched, citing high marriage stability rates. The spokesperson for the Parliament's Education Commission announced the approval of mandatory pre-kindergarten education starting at age five and a gradual restructuring of the national education system. The final segment of the broadcast featured the "Zamzam Ahkam" program, which provided answers to listeners' religious questions regarding congregational prayer, khums, and purity laws.
