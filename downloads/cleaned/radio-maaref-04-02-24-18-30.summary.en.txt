The program began by explaining the jurisprudential rules for standing at Arafat and Muzdalifah during Hajj, emphasizing the correct intention and the performance of recommended prayers.
Various religious questions were answered, including the permissibility of prostrating on non-edible materials and metals, the correct method of tayammum requiring only one strike, and the rules regarding the buying and selling of women's hair.
The ruling on wearing a tie at weddings and the option for sinusitis patients to perform tayammum instead of ghusl if water is harmful were also addressed.
Financial rulings clarified that, according to Imam Khomeini, pensions and survivor benefits received by a deceased person's family are not subject to Khums.
A sermon followed, highlighting the importance of praying at the earliest time and avoiding hypocrisy in worship, illustrated by the story of a man who prayed in the front row for thirty years.
The discussion then covered the guiding effects of Lady Zainab (SA) in the downfall of the Umayyad dynasty and the necessity of promptly answering God's call during prayer.
The program concluded by providing contact numbers for submitting religious questions and inviting listeners to join future episodes.
