In this episode of the radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat," Hujjat al-Islam Falah Ba identifies anger as one of Satan's most formidable armies based on the narrations of the Ahl al-Bayt. Citing a hadith from Imam Ali, he describes anger not as a single enemy but as a vast, multi-faceted legion that constitutes an independent nation of ignorance. Anger is portrayed as a grand, sinister plot by Satan, ongoing since the creation of Adam, aimed at seizing control of human identity, beliefs, and ethics. By infiltrating the human psyche from six different directions, anger creates internal conflict and disrupts reason and peace. Satan utilizes this vice to sow discord among individuals, families, and societies, ultimately tarnishing human dignity before God. The speaker argues that our lack of awareness regarding the true nature of anger leads us to treat it as a natural part of ourselves, thereby opening the doors of our hearts to Satan. The discussion concludes that recognizing anger as a complex, multi-layered enemy is essential for eradicating it and preserving spiritual unity and inner tranquility.
