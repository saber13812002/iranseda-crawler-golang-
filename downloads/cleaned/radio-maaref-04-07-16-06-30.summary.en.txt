In this program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verses from the Surah Al-An'am, emphasizing the difficulty of recognizing one's true identity. He explains that forgetting God leads to forgetting one's true self, rendering human arguments futile in the face of the Hereafter. Even if a person were to return from the afterlife to the world, they would still be under Satan's influence and prone to forgetting again. God has tested humans through various conditions such as prosperity, adversity, youth, and old age to reveal their existential truth. Therefore, individuals must constantly review their identity through remembrance and mindfulness to avoid misguidance and denial of truth. The second part of the program celebrated World Children's Day by greeting children and sharing listener messages about their "inner child." Additionally, significant historical events like the chemical bombing of Halabja and the exile of Ayatollah Madaris were commemorated on this date.
