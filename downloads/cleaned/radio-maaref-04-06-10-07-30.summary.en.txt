In this radio program, the occasion of Imam Hasan Askari's martyrdom was used to emphasize the importance of increasing patience and controlling anger, which is described as the key to all evil. The program then honored the memory of martyr Abbas Duran, a brave air force pilot, highlighting his heroic actions during the Iran-Iraq war and his martyrdom in the Baghdad operation. The final segment focused on a detailed discussion of backbiting, providing various definitions based on the narrations of the Ahl al-Bayt. According to these narrations, backbiting includes mentioning a person's true flaws, revealing secrets that God has concealed, or stating things that the individual dislikes hearing. It was also clarified that attributing a flaw to someone who does not possess it constitutes slander rather than backbiting. Ultimately, backbiting was defined as a major sin and a form of war against God, which removes a person from divine guardianship and places them under the influence of Satan.
