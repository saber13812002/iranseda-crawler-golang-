In this radio program, the wife of martyr Mostafa Saed Rozayi recounts the story of her meeting and marriage to her late husband. She describes the specific conditions of his family, who were accustomed to familial marriages, and the initial hesitation of his father. Their story begins on a Fatimiyya day when Mostafa visited her home to help move a heavy stone model. During the proposal meeting, the conversation was difficult due to her shyness until Mostafa explicitly stated that he was seeking a true companion and intellectual equal. This honesty and deep perspective created a strong emotional bond, leading to a shared life built on peace and mutual trust. In the final segment of the program, the Supreme Leader's speeches on the necessity of unity among Islamic nations are broadcast. He emphasizes that unity based on shared interests is the only way to counter aggressive powers and extortion.
