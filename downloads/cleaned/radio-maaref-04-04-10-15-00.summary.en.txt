In this radio program, experts analyzed the sources of the Islamic Republic of Iran's soft power in the face of Israeli aggression and US support. They argued that universal values such as anti-hegemony, justice-seeking, and belief in Mahdism are the primary drivers of both domestic unity and international solidarity. These values have enabled Iranians to unite despite political and religious differences, while also attracting support from other nations who identify with these anti-imperialist ideals. Furthermore, the enemy's strategic miscalculations regarding Iran's military and social resilience led to a failure in predicting the public's reaction and the regime's enduring authority. The distinct behavior of the Iranian people compared to Israeli citizens is attributed to deep-seated beliefs in martyrdom and the afterlife, which foster courage and steadfastness in the face of danger. Ultimately, these cultural and ideological factors are recognized as a strong foundation for maintaining national cohesion and countering the enemy's soft power warfare.
