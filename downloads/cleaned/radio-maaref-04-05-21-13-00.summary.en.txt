Hojjat al-Islam Rashed Yazir presents patience as the fundamental pillar of ethics and the key to success in this lecture. He defines patience not merely as enduring pain, but as managing anger and controlling inner emotions to achieve spiritual perfection. Citing the story of Lady Zainab's (SA) captivity in Kufa, he highlights her resilience and composure in the face of oppression as a supreme model for Muslims. The speaker emphasizes that humans are like gold mines, and only through patience and effort can we extract the hidden values within ourselves. He advises the audience to strengthen their internal "brake" to avoid anger and impulsive reactions. Finally, he recalls the martyrs of Karbala and the martyrdom of Imam Hussein (AS) as motivation for adhering to religious principles and overcoming life's hardships.
