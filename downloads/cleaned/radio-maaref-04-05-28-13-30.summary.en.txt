In the radio program "Mashq-e Rah," Hojjat al-Islam Seyyed Ahmad Faqih discussed the concept of monotheistic ethics and strengthening one's connection with God. He emphasized the importance of practicing a monotheistic perspective, viewing all worldly possessions as God's property and recognizing oneself as merely a trustee during prayer. In the Q&A section, he addressed a teenager's complaint about the lack of direct access to the Imam of the Age. Faqih interpreted this objection as a sign of spiritual thirst and love, recommending alternative paths such as prayer, visitation, and spiritual pursuit. He stressed that a heartfelt, spiritual connection with the Imam is more valuable than a physical meeting, even if the latter is not possible. Finally, the program marked the anniversary of the attack on the US embassy by reviewing the history of Iran-US tensions. This history was traced back to the 1953 coup d'état, where the US played a key role in overthrowing the national government of Prime Minister Mossadegh.
