In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the importance of accepting the truth and avoiding stubbornness, citing stories of great scholars like Mulla Ali Zanjan and Mirza Shirazi to illustrate that admitting mistakes is a sign of spiritual maturity. The second part of the show featured a dramatic retelling from the book "Let It Be," depicting the siege of Imam Hussein's caravan at the Zuhaism camp. In this narrative, the army commander Hur ibn Riyah attempted to prevent war by insisting on performing the congregational prayer together with the Imam's companions, revealing his strategic intent to his son Bakr. Imam Hussein (PBUH) responded to Hur's request with silence and the performance of prayer, subsequently displaying the invitation letters from Kufa as proof of their summons. Ultimately, despite internal pressure and threats from his subordinates, Hur declared that his mission was to escort the Imam to Kufa rather than kill him. The program concluded with a jurisprudential note regarding the Islamic ruling on necklaces inscribed with Quranic verses touching the skin.
