The program begins by emphasizing moral values and respect for parents before discussing the physical and spiritual nature of fire.
The program's expert, Hojjat al-Islam Falahati, analyzes the causes and factors behind profanity and foul language in society.
He identifies ignorance and a lack of rationality as the primary and most significant causes of this moral vice.
Misunderstanding religious and national values, along with defending them incorrectly, is cited as another major reason for such behavior.
Hypocrisy, the bad language of those claiming religious devotion, and hidden enmity from adversaries, especially in cyberspace, are highlighted as aggravating factors.
The expert stresses that profanity not only deprives an individual of moral virtues but also harms religious and social values.
Finally, the necessity of avoiding this vice and striving to build a moral and united society based on Quranic and hadith teachings is emphasized.
