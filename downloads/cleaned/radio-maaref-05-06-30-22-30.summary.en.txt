The program began by offering condolences for the martyrdom of Hazrat Fatima Masumeh (SA) and then discussed the enemy's psychological operations aimed at managing society's mindset. The guest, Haj Agha Panahian, identified mockery and ridicule as primary tools used by enemies to weaken the resistance of believers. Citing Quranic verses, he emphasized that all divine prophets were mocked, which actually validates their path. One reason for being mocked is the long-term vision and rationality of believers, which contrasts with the short-sightedness of their critics. Courage in action and speaking the truth, even when one is certain of its correctness, often leads to ridicule. Additionally, intense love and self-sacrifice are other reasons for being mocked, as illustrated by the story of Layla and Majnun. Finally, believers are advised not to refrain from speaking the truth or taking courageous actions out of fear of being mocked.
