This program narrates the story of Ali-Sayad Shirazi, a military officer and martyr, based on the book "In the Ambush of the Red Rose." In December 1975, the army's counter-intelligence placed him under strict surveillance and ordered the censorship of his correspondence following a religious letter. Despite positive reports from commanders regarding his faith and competence, the monitoring continued, and a classmate even warned him about the security risks. Ali was once detained by Brigadier Naji for praying in the barracks and removing his weapon, but he successfully proved his innocence through logical and religious arguments. Despite his lower rank, he possessed the courage to debate senior commanders and insisted on his religious rights. After the massacre of September 17, two young officers who had become disillusioned with the army trusted Ali due to his committed and religious behavior. These two men decided to speak with Ali to determine whether to continue serving in the army or to leave. The program concluded with the recitation of the Ziyarat of Al-Yasin and religious songs.
