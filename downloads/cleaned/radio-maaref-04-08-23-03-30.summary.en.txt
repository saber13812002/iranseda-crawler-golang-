The text begins with a speech urging fighters to rely on divine support and unity to overcome their enemies and Saddam's backers.
Advice is given to the Iraqi army and regional governments to stop supporting Saddam and to fear divine wrath.
It also warns that American plans aim to create division among Muslims and exploit regional resources.
The second part of the program features answers to listeners' religious questions in the "Zamzam Ahkam" segment.
One answer states that listening to backbiting without objecting is considered sinful.
Ayatollah Sadr's ruling on paying Khums for forgotten clothing when previous payment is uncertain is explained.
The religious rulings on music, the purity of the body after insects sit on it, and breastfeeding conditions for mahram status are detailed.
Finally, the distribution of inheritance and the payment of mahr for a deceased individual are outlined based on Imami jurisprudence.
