In the radio program "Tales of Freedom," former POWs recount the harsh realities of their captivity in Iraqi camps. They describe the severe lack of hygiene facilities, the prevalence of skin diseases, and the physical endurance required to survive in small, crowded rooms. The recitation of the Quran is highlighted as the sole spiritual refuge and the primary factor that sustained their mental resilience. In that dark environment, the Quran was secretly taught and served to keep their religious identity and hope for liberation alive. The second part of the program features an interpretation of hadiths from Imam Sadiq (AS) in the book of Kafi, emphasizing the difference between worldly and divine friendship. According to these narrations, pure friendship for the sake of God carries an eternal reward, and a person's spiritual rank depends on the depth of their heartfelt affection. The program concludes with the recitation of the fourteenth supplication from the Sahifa al-Sajjadiya and religious poetry, ending with prayers for the listeners' health and liberation.
