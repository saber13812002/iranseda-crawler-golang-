Prophets and Imams bear the heavy burden of guidance, leading their followers from worldly dust to the station of Tawhid.
Some followers, despite their flaws and base traits, remain on the path of guidance and ultimately find their way to Paradise.
Intercession on the Day of Judgment is not a direct entry for sinners but a process of purification and compensating for their deficiencies.
In the scene of Araf, intercessors purify believers by removing base traits like envy, preparing them for entry into Paradise.
Imam Hussein (AS) and Hazrat Zainab (SA), as the "Habeels" of Imam Reza (AS), connected the Ummah to the Imamate through their martyrdom.
This spiritual connection allows their followers to intercede on the Day of Judgment and guide their companions to Paradise.
Ultimately, intercession is a process where prophets and Imams strive to compensate for and complete the deficiencies of their followers.
