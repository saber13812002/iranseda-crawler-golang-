This radio program narrates the story of Maryam, a student who finds a forgotten notebook titled "Kood Sobavam" at a Tehran metro station. This discovery coincides with news that Iran has secured the second-highest average IQ globally. Mohammad Fallah Alipour, head of the Iranian IQ Association, attributes this achievement to recent organizational efforts and the use of credible global data. In this ranking, South Korea leads with an average of 175.4, followed by China at 106.99, while Iran ranks second with 106.82. The discussion also highlights Tara Sharifi, an 11-year-old Iranian girl who broke Albert Einstein's estimated IQ record by scoring 162 on the Mensa test. Maryam visits the association to verify these claims, and Dr. Fallah Alipour confirms the validity of the "International IQ Test" ranking. Ultimately, reports indicate that Iran holds the third position in the top 23 countries list, following South Korea and China.
