The radio program begins with a blend of jurisprudential and literary discussions, highlighting the role of experts in verifying the authenticity of narrations.
The literary segment features morning poetry and prayers for daily mindfulness and attention to the Imam of the Age.
The main news section reviews historical events of the eighth of Azar, including the start of Operation Karbala 5, the passing of Ayatollah Araaki, and the martyrdom of Dr. Majid Shahriari.
It also mentions the Iranian national football team's success in the 1998 World Cup.
The final part of the show focuses on the ethics of privacy and the proper use of others' personal belongings.
Listeners shared their views on privacy violations through text messages and phone calls.
The program concludes by emphasizing kindness, smiling, and respecting the personal boundaries of others.
