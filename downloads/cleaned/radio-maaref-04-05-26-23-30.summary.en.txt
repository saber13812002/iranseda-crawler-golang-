This radio program discussed strategies for living with a spouse who exhibits obsessive-compulsive or hoarding behaviors. The primary step is accepting the partner's behavior without validating it, while fostering empathy instead of confrontation or pressure. Logical explanations are often ineffective for such individuals, so it is crucial to avoid causing sudden stress. The recommended strategy involves gradual organization and categorizing items into essential, sentimental, and low-use groups. Subsequently, one can reduce resistance by removing low-use items one by one. A clever alternative is donating items to those in need, which helps the individual avoid the feeling of discarding possessions. The program concluded with the recitation of the Al-Yasin ziyarat and a reference to Imam Khomeini's speeches on standing firm against external threats to Islam.
