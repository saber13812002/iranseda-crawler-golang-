The program began by honoring the anniversary of Lady Fatimah's martyrdom before transitioning to a discussion on Islamic traditional medicine and dietary principles. Experts emphasized that consuming highly hot foods like red meat simultaneously with garlic, onions, and mustard can damage the stomach and should be avoided. It was also warned that cold-temperament individuals should not self-prescribe hot foods without proper diagnosis to prevent adverse side effects. In the second segment, the importance of rationality, foresight, and honesty in human life was discussed based on Quranic verses. It was interpreted that honesty not only benefits one in the afterlife but also builds trust and yields long-term profits in this world. Finally, referencing the history of the Islamic Revolution, it was highlighted that the Iranian people started from zero and, through collective will and unity, managed to defeat foreign powers.
