This radio segment featured the Supreme Leader's Friday sermon in Tehran, emphasizing the principles of dignity, wisdom, and expediency. He called for preserving national unity and holding vibrant elections to strengthen the country's power and security. The Leader stressed that Iran's defensive power must be strong enough to deter any enemy from attacking its territory. He also highlighted the necessity of linking foreign policy with academic circles to enhance a wisdom-based discourse. Regarding negotiations, he defined national interests as a red line and reiterated the lack of trust in the United States based on past experiences. The Leader urged the lifting of sanctions while protecting nuclear rights and utilizing human capital to resolve economic challenges. Finally, he presented the combination of active diplomacy and military power as the primary strategy for maintaining Iran's prestige.
