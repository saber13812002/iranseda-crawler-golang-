The radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat," featuring Professor Mohammad Hadi Fala, discussed the vice of judging others and strategies to overcome it. Citing the story of Luqman the Wise and his son, the professor advised focusing on earning God's pleasure rather than seeking public approval. He emphasized that self-awareness of one's inner worth renders the baseless judgments of others ineffective, as illustrated by Imam Kazim's hadith. The third strategy involved applying the principle of wishing for others what one desires for oneself, thereby avoiding negative judgments. Additionally, the professor recommended maintaining piety and avoiding situations that might provoke bad judgments from others. Ultimately, the priority should be God's judgment, as divine accounting differs fundamentally from superficial human assessments.
