In this radio program, various theological and ethical concepts were discussed with a religious expert. The role of religious recitation in creating a spiritual barrier and distinguishing actual jinn affliction from delusion were addressed, with a warning against consulting fraudulent exorcists. The claim that Sayyid lineage is fabricated was refuted, emphasizing that it is a verifiable historical and genealogical reality. The virtues of visiting graves and the spiritual presence of Imams in shrines were also explained. The program highlighted the severe sin of disobeying parents, even after their death, urging listeners to seek forgiveness through prayer. Questions regarding why Imams and Prophets did not use divine power to avoid martyrdom were answered. The correlation between rainfall and religious belief was dismissed, attributing weather to geography while stressing that social order depends on practicing Islamic principles. Finally, the fate of a believer with correct faith but poor deeds was analyzed, concluding that faith ensures eventual salvation unless sins lead to disbelief.
