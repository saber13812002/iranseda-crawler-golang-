In this radio program, Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi's fatwa was discussed, stating that animal slaughter by women is valid, though it is more recommended for men to perform it if available. According to Ayatollah Khomeini, hair falling on the face during wudu does not invalidate the ritual ablution. Regarding travel during Ramadan, Ayatollah Sistani clarified that if a trip exceeds ten days, the rules for prayer and fasting depend on the initial intention for the first ten days and subsequent residence. Ayatollah Shabiri Zanjani explained that performing Jumu'ah prayer on behalf of the deceased is not legally valid, although dedicating its reward to them is permissible. Concerning wearing black clothes in respect of the Ahl al-Bayt, scholars noted that while it is generally disliked, it is acceptable during Muharram and Safar to honor religious symbols. Finally, it was emphasized that for the Jumu'ah prayer, water must reach the scalp, and merely washing the hair is insufficient. The program concluded by providing contact numbers and SMS codes for listeners to submit new questions.
