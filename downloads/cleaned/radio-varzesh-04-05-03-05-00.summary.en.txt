In this radio program, Hamid Sadeghi-Zadeh, a Zourkhaneh master from Andimeshk, discusses his journey starting at age six and his training under senior mentors. He emphasizes that the Zourkhaneh is a spiritual space where the recitation of epic, religious, and mystical poetry transforms the athletes' mindset. Sadeghi-Zadeh explains how the poetry is curated for each session, blending Persian literature with religious verses to enhance the workout experience. He also highlights the presence of three active Zourkhanehs in Andimeshk and their efforts to register them as national heritage sites. Additionally, the head of Zourkhaneh activities in Khuzestan province reports on the operation of 23 active facilities and the organization of competitions in universities and schools. The primary goal of these initiatives is to preserve the cultural legacy of Pahlavani and transmit it to the younger generation through both formal and informal educational channels.
