Mahdad Hafezipour, a former player for the Homa team, shared his childhood memories and entry into local football in Tehran during the "Mahat-e Gotohoo" program. He highlighted the profound impact of coaches like Ahmad Tusi and Ali Kazemi on his character and skills development, emphasizing the central role of ethics in the Homa team. Hafezipour explained that Homa focused on human upbringing and fostering a collective, disciplined spirit before tactical training, which fostered strong player loyalty to the club. He recounted his unique experience of being accepted into university after his coach insisted on his education, noting how this shift transformed his lifestyle. He also shared his memory of seeing Proiz Dehdari and his admiration for the football etiquette and rituals of the Homa team. Finally, he discussed the post-revolution era when Homa players transitioned into coaching and sports teaching, playing a key role in training new generations of athletes.
