The program begins with safety and weather alerts, advising citizens to avoid parking near withered trees and unfinished buildings due to the risk of falling debris, especially for respiratory patients and the elderly. Forecasts indicate that the southern regions and the Caspian Sea will experience rough and stormy conditions for the next five days. Mashhad is expected to reach a high of 35 degrees and a low of 17, while Qom is the hottest city at 42 degrees and Dasht-e Neman in Fars is the coldest at 3 degrees. Tehran will also face high temperatures of 38 degrees with a significant risk of severe dust storms. The second part of the show features musical performances followed by an ethical discussion with Professor Mohammad Hadi Falahbayest. The professor identifies lying as the greatest sin, a form of injustice, and a betrayal of the divine systems of creation and legislation. Citing narrations from the Infallibles, he describes lying as the key to entering the house of wickedness and the lowest form of character. He strongly emphasizes the necessity of complete abstinence from lying, even in the context of jokes or casual conversation.
