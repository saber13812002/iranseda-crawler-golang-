This radio program discusses the necessity of establishing jurisprudential frameworks for sports, given their widespread social impact. From an Islamic legal perspective, sports are inherently permissible, but their ruling can change based on intent, context, and specific circumstances. Key criteria for legitimacy include the intention of worship, adherence to Sharia principles, and the avoidance of gambling or collusion. Jurists emphasize that gradual or tolerable injuries for professional athletes do not automatically render a sport haram, unless there is a risk of irreversible bodily harm. In combat sports, mutual consent and waiver of blood money can alter the initial ruling, while military necessity may justify participation despite risks. Therefore, a precise jurisprudential evaluation of sports requires careful attention to external realities and individual differences among athletes.
