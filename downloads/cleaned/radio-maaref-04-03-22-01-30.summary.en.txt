The first part of the broadcast features a religious discourse emphasizing the virtues of Ali ibn Abi Talib and the spiritual necessity of loving him for purification.
The narrative then shifts to the novel "Left Behind by the Son," focusing on Salār, a man involved in the 1963 uprising due to poverty and support for the people of Pishva and Varamin.
After his son Sohrāb is wounded during the uprising, Salār seeks help from Farishte, a kind woman who raised him in his childhood.
He is shocked to learn of Farishte's death from the elderly caretaker of her house, a revelation that deeply affects him.
Salār laments his ignorance of her passing and his son's political troubles, believing that earlier contact with Farishte might have changed Sohrāb's fate.
Meanwhile, Salār's wife, Azar, asks their relative Dāy Jamshid to help locate Salār so she can urge him to find Sohrāb, who has grown into a politically active young man.
The story highlights the complex family dynamics and the heavy impact of historical political events on the personal lives of the characters during that era.
