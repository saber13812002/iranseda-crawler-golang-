The program began by congratulating the audience on the new moon of Rabi al-Thani and commemorating the birth of Imam Reza (AS).
The guest interpreted Imam Sadiq's prayer "Wadri'ani bi-lutfik" as seeking divine protection from natural disasters, oppressors, and internal conflicts.
Citing the Quran, he explained three types of divine punishment: calamities from above, from below, and internal discord.
He emphasized avoiding obsessive-compulsive thoughts and behaviors as internal plagues caused by Satan's whispers.
The second part of the discussion focused on the prayer "Wa-qizni bi-ni'matik" and the importance of lawful sustenance and food security.
Through stories of the Prophet (PBUH) and Imam Ali (AS), the necessity of earning a halal living for life's blessings was highlighted.
Finally, the guest stressed the payment of Khums as a fundamental religious obligation and recommended studying relevant texts.
