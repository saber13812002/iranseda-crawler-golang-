The radio program begins by discussing the story of the People of the Cave as a symbol of divine resurrection and identifying Jordan as the likely location of their cave. It then examines the historical evidence for the marriage of Imam Hasan Askari (AS) to Lady Narjis, citing authoritative sources such as Kamil al-Din and Ghaybah. The segment refutes claims denying the birth of Imam Mahdi (AJ), asserting that reports of his birth to Imam Askari (AS) are mutawatir and widely accepted. It explains that despite strict surveillance by the Abbasid Caliphate, the Imam's birth remained hidden by divine will and was protected by loyal guardians. The program concludes by addressing the humanitarian crisis in Gaza, urging listeners to support the oppressed people there. Finally, the Du'a for rain by Imam Sajjad (AS) is recited to seek relief from drought.
