The speaker refutes Western demands to halt all uranium enrichment, affirming Iran's right to its nuclear achievements. He argues that true faith requires active opposition to injustice and a refusal to remain silent against oppression. Silence in the face of the suffering of Palestine and historical injustices against Imam Ali is described as a sign of spiritual weakness. The talk highlights the historical role of Jewish tribes in sowing division and the necessity of Muslim solidarity. Citing the Ziyarat Ashura, the speaker warns that accepting injustice is a grave sin on the Day of Judgment. He emphasizes that believers must align with the authority of the Imam and stand against tyranny even during the occultation.
