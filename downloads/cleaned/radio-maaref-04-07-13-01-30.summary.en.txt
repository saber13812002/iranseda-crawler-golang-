In this program, Ayatollah Ostadi, citing a narration from Imam Javad (AS), outlined four pillars of perfect faith: spending in God's path, refraining from spending on sin, and maintaining love or enmity for divine reasons. He emphasized that on Judgment Day, individuals will be questioned not only about their income sources but also about how they spent their money, noting that youth is a golden opportunity for moral transformation. Additionally, three righteous acts were identified as preparation for death: fulfilling religious obligations, avoiding prohibitions, and guarding against spiritual negligence. The second part featured a memory shared by Ayatollah Khamenei about a young soldier who donated his salary to support the front lines. Subsequently, a story was recounted about the Supreme Leader accompanying Martyr Chamran during a reconnaissance mission where enemy missiles targeted their shelter. These narratives highlighted the sacrifices of the war fighters and the importance of religious mindfulness during the conflict.
