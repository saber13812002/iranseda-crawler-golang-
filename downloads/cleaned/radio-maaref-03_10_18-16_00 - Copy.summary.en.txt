In this radio program, Professor Mohammadi Sharodi addresses various theological and social questions from listeners. He rejects the notion of God "tolerating" injustice, explaining that divine intervention is withheld to preserve human free will. The professor argues that the acceptance of foreign occupation by some Muslim rulers is the primary reason for the presence of external powers in the region, emphasizing that change requires self-correction by the people. The program also distinguishes between the afterlife fates of believers and disbelievers, noting that while believers are relieved of worldly hardships, disbelievers face the trials of the Barzakh. Furthermore, the impact of prayer and charity in hastening the reappearance of Imam Mahdi is highlighted as a means to alter destiny. Finally, the discussion concludes that while factors like genetics, environment, and history shape human character, moral responsibility is strictly limited to the degree of an individual's free will and choice.
