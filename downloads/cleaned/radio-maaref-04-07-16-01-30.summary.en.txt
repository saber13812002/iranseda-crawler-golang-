Hojjat al-Islam Mohammad Javad Nazafat discussed the characteristics of those who break unity, emphasizing the necessity of mutual respect among Islamic sects. Citing the Quran and narrations from Imam Sadiq (AS), he clarified that disavowal is only permissible for clear enemies of God and the Ahl al-Bayt, not for Muslim brothers with differing beliefs. Imam Sadiq (AS) instructed believers to maintain affection and guide those who love the Ahl al-Bayt, rather than treating them as enemies. The speaker referenced the story of Prophet Abraham (AS) to illustrate that even he initially treated his idolatrous guardian with kindness, only disavowing him after his enmity became evident. He warned that sowing discord among Muslims is a major obstacle to the reappearance of Imam Mahdi (AJ) and is a strategy used by Satan to cause destruction. Finally, he urged Shias to promote the teachings of the Ahl al-Bayt through love and attraction, thereby preparing the ground for the Imam's return while avoiding internal division.
