This radio program explores the Islamic concept of intercession, emphasizing that while the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) reserved his intercession for sinners, oppressors and those who neglect prayer are excluded. Fatima Zahra (SA) is also highlighted as an intercessor for women who observe daily prayers, fasting, zakat, and obedience to their husbands. The discussion further notes that the Quran, martyrs, scholars, and even living believers can intercede for others. The second part of the program features memories of Martyr Mohammad Bonyadi, commander of the Hazrat Masoumeh Brigade, shared by Sardar Hossein Orooj. These anecdotes underscore Bonyadi's humility, high moral character, and selfless service to the needy, even towards enemy prisoners. The stories collectively present a model of true leadership and sacrifice in the path of God and the people.
