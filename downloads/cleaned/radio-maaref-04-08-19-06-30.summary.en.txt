In the first segment, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interpreted verses from the Quran to emphasize that the right to legislate belongs exclusively to God. He explained that attributing authority to other rulers is merely a sign of divine will, using the analogy of a mirror reflection to distinguish between direct and indirect attributes. The host then shifted the focus to the importance of vitality in daily life, inviting listeners to share who the most energetic person in their lives is. The program concluded with a review of historical and political news, including France's support for Iraq during the war, the martyrdom of Hossein Fatemi, and the UN resolution supporting Palestine.
