This program features a theological and philosophical discussion on divine justice and the concept of human salvation. The speaker references the works of Shahid Mottahari and Sheikh Mufid to argue that God's mercy is vast, extending even to the good deeds of non-Muslims. The concept of "cosmic justice" is explained as God granting existence to all entities with the potential for being, while differences in intellect and piety lead to varying destinies. After analyzing legislative and retributive justice, it is concluded that inherent human differences are compatible with divine fairness. The discussion then shifts to the role of the people in the Islamic Revolution of Iran, highlighting how their monotheistic will overthrew the Pahlavi regime. It is emphasized that the Iranian people, despite lacking weapons and organized structures, started from zero and defeated foreign powers through faith. Finally, a call is made for other nations to awaken and form a national Islamic army to resist global hegemony.
