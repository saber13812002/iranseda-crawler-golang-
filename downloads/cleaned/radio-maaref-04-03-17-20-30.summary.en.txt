This radio program begins by highlighting Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi as a prominent religious figure with extensive works translated into over ten languages and a highly popular teaching style in seminaries. It then transitions to the war-time and captivity memories of Ali Bakhshizade, who recounts the compassionate behavior of a Shia Iraqi soldier towards him and other prisoners amidst the battlefield. The freedom fighter describes the dangerous moments of his capture, medical care in Iraqi camps, and human interactions with other captives. A segment is dedicated to introducing the book "Gol Kashati Abbas," which narrates the captivity and release of Abbas Hosseinzadeh Khalidi. The book consists of four chapters covering his childhood, the war, his captivity, and the circumstances of his freedom. The program concludes by inviting listeners to stay connected with Radio Ma'aref through its official applications and channels. It also emphasizes the collective responsibility of Iranians to preserve the Persian language by using native words instead of foreign terms.
