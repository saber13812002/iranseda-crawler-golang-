The program began with a discussion on the ethical necessity of avoiding nepotism and prioritizing merit over personal connections.
The guest, Hojjat al-Islam Fallah, identified anger as one of the most dangerous psychological vices based on the narrations of the Infallibles.
The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) described anger as a spark from Satan that ignites a fire within the human soul.
Imam Ali (AS) characterized anger as a form of temporary madness and light-mindedness that obscures reason and leads to regret.
This vice not only harms the individual but also inflicts severe damage on families, organizations, and society as a whole.
The guest emphasized that anger is essentially an army of Satan's soldiers, which must be prevented from entering one's heart through vigilance.
The program concluded by stressing the importance of controlling anger and cultivating good morals to achieve a just and peaceful society.
