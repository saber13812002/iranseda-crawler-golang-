In this broadcast from Radio Ma'aref, the Sunni argument for the validity of consensus (Ijma) based on the Quranic verse of "the path of the believers" was examined.
Proponents argue that since opposing the Prophet and following a path other than that of the believers are both condemned with the threat of punishment, following the consensus of believers must be obligatory.
However, the primary critique asserts that "the path of the believers" does not mean collective consensus, but rather the path of faith, which implies following and supporting the Prophet.
The apparent meaning of the verse indicates that the "path of the believers" is the path defined by the Prophet, and opposing it is essentially a rejection of divine revelation.
Consequently, the verse does not validate a consensus that might contradict the Prophet's explicit instructions, but rather emphasizes the necessity of adhering to the religion.
Additional objections were raised, including the ambiguity of the term "believers" and the lack of definitive proof that the verse mandates following every decision made by the community.
The final conclusion is that the consensus-based interpretation of this verse is weak, and its core meaning is the inseparable link between faith and practical obedience to the Prophet.
