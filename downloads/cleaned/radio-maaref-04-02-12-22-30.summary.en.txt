In this radio program, the vital role of teachers in preparing society for the reappearance of Imam Mahdi (AJ) and introducing him to the public was emphasized on Teacher's Day. Hujjat al-Islam Abbazari, referencing a letter from Imam Mahdi to Muhammad ibn Ismail ibn Sa'id, highlighted key traits of the Imam's supporters, including insight, analytical power, self-management, and strict obedience to the rule of jurists during the occultation. He stressed that following a qualified jurist in the absence of the Imam is equivalent to obeying the Imam himself, and separating from this authority exposes individuals to Satan's influence. The program also discussed the story of Khidr in the Quran, explaining that specific names were omitted to protect the scripture from historical disputes and sensitivities. In another segment, Professor Kafil interpreted Quranic verses to describe the reappearance as "Yawm Allah," a manifestation of God's power to save humanity from the darkness of polytheism into the light of monotheism. The broadcast concluded by emphasizing that supporting the Imam is achievable through daily life and sincere conversations with people.
