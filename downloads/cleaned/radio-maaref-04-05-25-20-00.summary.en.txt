Recent Israeli attacks on Gaza resulted in at least thirteen Palestinian martyrs and over twenty injuries, with the Ministry of Health warning that fifteen people may die daily from starvation and thirst if the siege continues. In response, resistance groups targeted Israeli military assets and downed a reconnaissance drone. Global support for Palestine persisted through protests at American and Swedish universities and demands to ban the Israeli team from the youth football tournament in Spain. The revelation that Microsoft's infrastructure was used for surveillance and bombing in Gaza sparked backlash, prompting the company to launch an investigation. Analysts warned that the Lebanese government might be forced to hand over the disarmament of Hezbollah to Israel due to the army's inability to do so. In Iraq, the Abbasid Shrine announced that over 21 million pilgrims attended the Arbaeen ceremony, with the Interior Minister praising the security plan's success. The fourth anniversary of the US withdrawal from Afghanistan was marked by public celebrations in Kabul and strong criticism of two decades of American crimes in the country.
