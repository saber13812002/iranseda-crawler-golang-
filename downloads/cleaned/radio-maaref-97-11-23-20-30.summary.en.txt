The radio program "I am Fatima" began by examining the worship and ethical conduct of Hazrat Fatima Zahra (SA).
Hojjat al-Islam Hosseini emphasized her exceptional status in servitude to God and her deep presence in prayer.
Narrations indicate that her prayer was so radiant that angels were astonished by its light.
The Prophet (PBUH) boasted to the angels about his daughter's devotion and promised protection for her true followers.
In the second segment, Ayatollah Motahhari distinguished between superficial "reform" and fundamental "revolution."
He defined revolution as a structural transformation in society and the human spirit, surpassing mere improvement.
According to this perspective, a social revolution is impossible without a fundamental change in the people's mindset.
The program concluded by encouraging listeners to emulate the infallibles and continue exploring religious knowledge.
