The radio program "Shajareh Tibe" focused on the role of the Basij organization in preserving and reviving the authentic ideas of the Islamic Revolution. Hujjat al-Islam Nadem, a representative of the Sirat-e Shahada Institute, emphasized that Basij has prevented the dilution of revolutionary slogans against enemy plots by maintaining a stable structure. He identified Imamate, Ummah, and Justice as the core concepts that Basij actively defends against attempts to replace them with human-made notions like Caliphate, citizenship, and purely material development. In the second segment, Ahmad Panahian, a member of the Amir al-Mu'minin resistance base in Qom, discussed the base's operational, cultural, and educational activities in deprived areas. He highlighted the importance of following the Supreme Leader's directives and fostering a revolutionary generation through clerical circles and jihad camps. The program also featured an introduction to the "Soldiers of Islam" exhibition and aired a song by the Defenders of the Haram. Finally, the "Mandegar Thoughts" segment concluded with a tafsir of the Quranic verse "Fear Allah, and He will teach you," stressing that moral self-purification is a prerequisite for teaching others.
