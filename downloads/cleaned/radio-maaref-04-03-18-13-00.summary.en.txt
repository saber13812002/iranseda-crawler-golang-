In the "Barbal Sakhan" program, Hojjat al-Islam Saeidi Aria celebrated the Eid of Ghadir by emphasizing the critical importance of Imam Ali's (AS) authority as the continuation of the Prophet's (PBUH) divine knowledge and guidance. The speaker highlighted the profound transformation of pre-Islamic Arab society under the Prophet's leadership and warned that rejecting Imam Ali's guardianship led to significant spiritual loss and persistent misguidance for humanity. Citing Quranic verses and hadiths, he noted that despite possessing knowledge, some individuals remained indifferent to the truth due to spiritual hardness and negligence. Saeidi Aria also underscored the vital role of Muharram and Safar in preserving Shia identity, noting that over 80% of converts to Shia Islam were influenced by witnessing these mourning rituals and the reverence for the Ahl al-Bayt (AS). He concluded by stressing the need to adhere to the ideals of Imam Khomeini (RA) and to dedicate one's entire life and emotions to the service of the Ahl al-Bayt, while avoiding waste and deviation from the religious path.
