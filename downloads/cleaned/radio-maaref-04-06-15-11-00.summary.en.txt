The Mar'ef radio program began by covering the start of the academic year in seminaries and the seventh edition of the "Mizban" scientific award. Three scholars from Iran, Turkey, and India were selected as the winners of this prestigious award. Extensive special programs were also held at the Astan Quds Razavi and various Iranian cities to mark the 1500th anniversary of the Prophet's birth and Unity Week. These events aimed to strengthen social cohesion and unity between Shia and Sunni communities, drawing enthusiastic participation from the public and religious leaders. The seventh Iranian-Islamic clothing exhibition was also held in Tehran, featuring producers and offering services such as free hijab photography. The cultural segment discussed the Prophet's and Ayatollah Bahjat's exemplary kindness towards children and the importance of engaging with the younger generation. Finally, the jurisprudential section addressed the religious and legal prohibition of abortion and the responsibility of medical professionals in this matter.
