In the radio program "Persaman Enghelab," Dr. Mohammad Ali Layali analyzed the goals and achievements of the Islamic Revolution, noting that while significant progress has been made in independence and science, cultural aggression by enemies remains a major obstacle to achieving ethical ideals. He rejected the claim that Iran missed an opportunity by not attending the Sharm El-Sheikh summit, describing the absence as a wise and dignified diplomatic decision since participating in a show hosted by the US and Israel would contradict the Islamic Republic's principles. Layali also characterized the "Al-Aqsa Flood" operation as a historic turning point that shattered the myth of Israel's invincibility and shifted the regional balance of power in favor of the Axis of Resistance. This operation not only hindered the "Abraham Accords" and the normalization of Arab-Israeli relations but also sparked a global awakening against the Zionist regime. The expert concluded that Hamas has transformed from a defensive force into a key decision-maker, creating a strong leverage over Israel and the United States. Finally, he emphasized the necessity of a collective struggle against cultural vices and the continuation of the path of the martyrs of the resistance front to preserve the Islamic identity of the society.
