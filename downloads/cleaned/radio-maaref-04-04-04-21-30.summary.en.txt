This radio program analyzes the verses in Surah Al-Ma'idah regarding the divine dialogue with Jesus (PBUH) on the Day of Judgment.
The commentator refutes the common interpretation that the past tense verbs in these verses merely signify a "certain future," arguing instead that they are timeless because time and motion cease when the cosmic system is dismantled.
Citing narrations, he highlights three specific attributes of Jesus (PBUH) known intimately by God: his divine vicegerency, the fact that God purchased his soul, and his heart being connected to the Holy Spirit.
Based on these attributes, it is argued that Jesus (PBUH) could never have invited people to polytheism, as it is impossible to do so with God's tongue, a soul owned by God, and a heart linked to the divine spirit.
The text distinguishes between the station of intercession and the station of argumentation, clarifying that this specific dialogue is a form of rebuttal rather than complaint or intercession.
It is emphasized that while hope for divine mercy exists for sins, polytheists have no right to claim forgiveness for this major creedal sin, which is forgiven only as a pure divine grace.
Finally, the phrase "You are the Mighty and the Wise" is interpreted to mean that God's absolute power is impervious to any influence and is firmly grounded in eternal wisdom.
