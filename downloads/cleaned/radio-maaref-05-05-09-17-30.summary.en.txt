The "For Awakening" program begins by emphasizing the necessity of public vigilance against enemy plots and widespread rumors.
The central theme analyzes how Imam Ali (PBUH) attained the caliphate, unlike his three predecessors, through the direct pressure and mass oath of the people.
Despite his initial reluctance to accept governance, Imam Ali (PBUH) shouldered the responsibility due to the people's overwhelming presence, establishing the most people-centric government in history.
A key insight is that the legitimacy and sustainability of a religious government depend not only on its formation but also on the continuous endurance, oversight, and active participation of the people.
Islamic governance is not a dictatorial relationship but a brotherly division of labor, where authority is a trust from God and the people that requires constant public scrutiny to prevent corruption.
Imam Ali (PBUH) stressed that no one, regardless of their virtue or knowledge, is exempt from the need for consultation and assistance, and everyone must contribute to establishing justice within their capacity.
Historical defeats, such as the Battle of Siffin, resulted from the people's failure to act on their justice slogans rather than the commander's incompetence, making the people responsible for the outcome.
Finally, the program concludes by linking the remembrance of martyrs and Quranic ideals as a path to spiritual enlightenment and maintaining this sacred covenant.
