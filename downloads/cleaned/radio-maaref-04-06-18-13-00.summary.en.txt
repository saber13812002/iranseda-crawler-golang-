Hojjat al-Islam Rafiei emphasizes the voluntary nature of faith and guidance by interpreting the Quranic verse "It is not upon you to guide them." He explains that the Prophet (PBUH) cannot force anyone to believe, as faith is a matter of the heart and intellect that requires personal acceptance. The verse was revealed to prevent Muslims from pressuring non-Muslims economically to force them into Islam, highlighting that charity should be given for God's sake regardless of the recipient's religion. The speaker uses the analogy of university education to distinguish between accepting the core principles of religion, which requires rational persuasion, and adhering to its branches, which becomes obligatory after initial acceptance. He also stresses the importance of strengthening the theological foundations of the youth to counter the doubts and misinformation prevalent in social media. Finally, he concludes with a prayer asking God to preserve the beliefs of the younger generation and facilitate their religious practices.
