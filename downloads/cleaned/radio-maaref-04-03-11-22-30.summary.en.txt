This radio program examines the concept of "asabiyyah" or tribal bias, distinguishing it from the legitimate defense of values. The speaker uses historical events like the battles of Karbala and Siffin to illustrate how blood ties can lead individuals to remain silent against or even support injustice. Key signs of this bias include arrogance, inciting conflict over trivial matters, and denying the truth due to familial attachments. Hadiths from the Prophet and Imam Sajjad are cited to emphasize that even a grain of bias in the heart separates a person from the Prophet's community and leads to resurrection with the polytheists. Conversely, standing firm on the truth and defending religious and human values, even against relatives, is praiseworthy rather than condemned. The story of Imam Ali during the conquest of Mecca serves as an example of prioritizing divine duty over family relations. Finally, the solution to eradicating this moral corruption is identified as breaking the ego and arrogance, and avoiding blind imitation of incorrect ancestral customs.
