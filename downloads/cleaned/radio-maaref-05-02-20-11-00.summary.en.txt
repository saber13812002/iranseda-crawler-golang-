In the radio program "A Drop from the Sea," Professor Ruhollah Rasoul revisited the Battle of Badr based on Quranic verses. He explained that Muslims in the second year of Hijra initially aimed to seize Abu Sufyan's trade caravan but were forced into battle when Mecca's military caravan arrived. Divine intervention ensured the Muslims' victory, marking it as Islam's first major triumph against infidelity. This event was presented as the precursor to the ultimate destruction of falsehood and the victory of truth. Following this narrative, the program featured a series of patriotic and religious anthems. These songs emphasized Iran's sovereignty, resistance against enemies, and the anticipation of the Mahdi's appearance. Chants of "Ali" and "Haidar" were repeatedly recited, highlighting the audience's Shia and revolutionary identity. Ultimately, the program's core message focused on patience, steadfastness, and faith in the imminent victory of truth under the Quran's guidance.
