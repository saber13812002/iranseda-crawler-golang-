The first segment narrates Qasim ibn Muhammad's preparation to defend Imam Hussein and Zayd's warning regarding the plots of Yazid and Muawiyah.
Subsequently, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasizes the necessity of enduring hardships for spiritual and physical growth, citing Quranic verses and Nahj al-Balagha.
He illustrates how great scholars like Shahid Qazi Tabatabai and Ayatollah Yazdi achieved their revolutionary goals through patience during their exiles and trials.
This section concludes by encouraging listeners to practice patience and trust in God, highlighting the role of endurance in the historical victories of the Shia.
The program then transitions to the recitation of Hussaini and Ashura poetry accompanied by religious music.
Radio reports on the activities of religious networks and the schedule of morning programs are also included in this segment.
Finally, the spiritual atmosphere is completed with the recitation of dawn prayers and Arabic supplications, inviting listeners to continue with the morning broadcasts.
