This radio program analyzes verses 50 to 52 of Surah Al-A'raf, drawing on the exegesis of Ayatollah Javadi Amoli. The commentator explains that the verse order is not strictly logical due to the surah's fragmented revelation, which responds to specific contextual needs. A key distinction is made between the internal relationships of the people of Hell, characterized by mutual curses, and those of the people of Paradise, defined by peace and affection. The role of the "men of the A'raf" is highlighted, as they greet those with balanced good and bad deeds with a message of consolation and good news. The core of the discussion focuses on the concept of "forgetting" in the phrase "today We will forget them," arguing through rational and textual evidence that God does not literally forget. Instead, this term signifies a deliberate divine abandonment, mirroring the way the disbelievers intentionally neglected God and the Day of Judgment in this world. Finally, the Quran is described as a detailed and clear book that offers guidance and mercy to all, though its true benefit is realized only by the believers.
