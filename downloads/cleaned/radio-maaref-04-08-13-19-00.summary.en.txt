This radio program emphasizes the necessity of accepting Islam as a complete and unified system, strongly rejecting the selective adoption of its rulings known as "talaqqat."
The special status of Imam Ali (AS) is highlighted through Quranic verses and historical consensus, identifying him as the first believer, the first to pray behind the Prophet, and the first to pay zakat.
Loving and hating for the sake of God is presented as one of the most solid anchors of faith, where personal or worldly criteria must be completely disregarded.
According to narrations from Imam Sadiq (AS) and Imam Baqir (AS), these four attributes—loving, hating, giving, and withholding for God—are among the greatest branches of faith and signs of God's chosen servants.
The program warns that mixing religious duties with personal or political preferences is akin to taking medication without a doctor's prescription, rendering one's actions ineffective.
Ultimately, the program asserts that linking religious practices to the concept of Wilayat (divine authority) is the source of dignity and the key to overcoming global arrogance.
