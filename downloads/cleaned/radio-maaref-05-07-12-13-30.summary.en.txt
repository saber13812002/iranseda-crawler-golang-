The program began by highlighting the spiritual and social significance of Hazrat Abdol-Azim Hasani in Rey, emphasizing the role of his shrine in promoting piety and community services.
A court report followed, detailing the trial of Dr. Fariborz Kazemi, a hospital director, charged with smuggling and using expired medications, which posed a threat to public health.
Dr. Kazemi's defense, citing medical necessity and the drugs' efficacy, was rejected based on evidence of smuggling and financial exploitation of patients.
The judge sentenced him to six years in prison and a heavy fine, temporarily suspending the hospital's operations due to the severe danger posed by these violations.
Hojjat-ol-Islam Razaqi, head of a Supreme Court branch, analyzed the legal implications, stressing severe penalties for distributing spoiled drugs and the requirement of qisas if a patient dies.
The final segment discussed Imam Khomeini's return to Iran and the unprecedented public reception he received from the masses.
The Imam immediately declared the abolition of the monarchy, initiating two fundamental transformations: converting the autocratic system to a people's government and Islamizing the country's processes.
These actions demonstrated that the Imam's leadership was not merely ceremonial but focused on structural change and direct public participation in governance.
