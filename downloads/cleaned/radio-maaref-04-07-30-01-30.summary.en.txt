The radio program begins with a lecture by Ayatollah Ziabadi on the foundations of life according to Imam Jafar Sadiq (AS), emphasizing three key principles: personal responsibility for one's actions, awareness of God's constant surveillance to avoid sin in private, and trust in divine sustenance rather than material accumulation. He cites narrations to highlight that working is a religious duty and warns against hoarding food or housing beyond one's actual needs. The lecture also stresses the importance of family ethics, noting that helping one's spouse in household chores carries immense spiritual rewards. The second part of the show introduces Nafiseh Kermani, the wife of Martyr Colonel Ali Aghazadeh, who shares memories from their 23 years of marriage. She describes the challenges of living apart due to her husband's missions in Syria and the difficulties of residing abroad away from their families. Despite the hardships, she found peace in supporting her husband's religious and military duties. The segment concludes with her poignant account of the day her husband was martyred in an Israeli terrorist attack in Syria.
