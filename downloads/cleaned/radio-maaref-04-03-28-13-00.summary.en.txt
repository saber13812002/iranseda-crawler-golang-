In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Alavi Tehrani argues that the unique governance style of Imam Ali (AS) is inimitable and will only be fully realized by Imam Mahdi (AJ). He cites historical accounts where even Ali's enemies acknowledged that no one could replicate his specific leadership qualities, which stem from his divine station. However, the speaker emphasizes that while imitating Ali's governance style is impossible, his ethical teachings and principles of justice are universally applicable. A just leader, according to this perspective, must aim to elevate society to spiritual perfection rather than merely consolidating power or exploiting public resources. The sermon highlights Ali's firm rejection of a proposal to distribute wealth unequally to secure political loyalty, prioritizing divine justice over pragmatic political gains. Ali (AS) insisted on equal distribution of public funds, refusing to use state resources for personal or factional benefit. The speaker concludes that the core duty of leaders and citizens alike is to study and demand the implementation of these ethical and justice-oriented teachings. Ultimately, the message calls for a society guided by the moral principles of the Ahl al-Bayt, focusing on fairness and the rejection of oppression.
