This radio program discussed the psychological and behavioral challenges faced by adolescents due to their parents' divorce.
The primary advice for divorced parents is to avoid mutual destruction and maintain a calm environment for their child in each other's presence.
The presence of a healthy peer group and engaging in board games were deemed essential for mental construction and reducing negative fantasies.
Occupying the child with skill-based and artistic activities, such as woodworking or painting, was introduced as an effective solution to prevent stagnation and loneliness.
To improve adolescents' speech and behavior, associating with positive individuals and studying the Quran and Hadith were recommended.
Avoiding association with ill-mannered and misbehaving people was emphasized due to their negative impact on the adolescent's developing personality.
In the final section, the spiritual and political status of Imam Ali and the relationship between religion and politics in narrations were addressed.
The program concluded with religious prayers and the playback of music.
