Ali Roozbehani, a veteran football and futsal player, recounts his career with military teams and the World Army Games in this interview. He highlights the national armed forces team's achievements, including a runner-up finish at the World Army Games in Rome and winning the Golden Boot at the Iran Ramadan Cup. Roozbehani mentions his collaboration with prominent players like Ali Daei and Karim Bagheri within the military squads. He attributes his absence from the national football team to military restrictions and the specific conditions of that era. The former captain also discusses his tenure as a coach and manager of the Ground Forces club, detailing their efforts to advance in the first division. Currently, Roozbehani is active in grassroots football development and school football programs in Tehran. He expresses contentment with his current life and thanks his wife for her unwavering support over the years. This conversation offers a glimpse into the dedication of military sports pioneers and their role in Iranian football history.
