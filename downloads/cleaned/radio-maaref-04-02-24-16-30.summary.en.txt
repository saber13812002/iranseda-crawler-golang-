In the radio program "Persman Etteghadi," responses were provided to listeners' questions regarding spiritual and social perspectives.
The instructor explained that during the Mi'raj, the Prophet (PBUH) transcended time and space, observing all levels of existence from the past to the future.
Therefore, seeing unveiled women in hellfire refers to observing future individuals, not necessarily women from the pre-Islamic era.
In the second section, claims by some individuals regarding their connection to Imam Mahdi (AJ) were strongly rejected as lies and slander based on narrations.
It was emphasized that claiming a meeting or connection with the Imam during his occultation is not only unsourced but can also lead to ideological deviations and harm to the youth.
The "Kiyaban-e Farhang" program also discussed the crucial role of youth in societal transformation and the necessity of utilizing their intellectual and physical capacities.
Key recommendations for the youth included continuous learning, self-monitoring of actions, sincerity in worship, and awareness of the digital space.
Finally, the importance of respectful and consultative behavior by adults towards the younger generation, avoiding punishment and humiliation, was highlighted.
