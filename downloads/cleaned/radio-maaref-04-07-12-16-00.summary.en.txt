In the "Persman Quran" program, Hujjat al-Islam Maleki addressed listeners' questions regarding Quranic verses. He explained that while the creation of the heavens and earth is physically vast, God calls humans "the best of creation" because they possess spiritual potential and proximity to God that transcend material quantity. The scholar also referenced the concept of "Tabaqarun," noting that the heavens are layered and our world is lower in spiritual rank compared to higher celestial spheres. Regarding the Prophet's Mi'raj, he affirmed it was a physical event occurring in a single night, during which great divine signs were revealed to the Prophet. In response to questions about why prayers for Gaza are not immediately answered, he emphasized that no prayer is ignored, but acceptance requires practical action and fulfilling necessary prerequisites. He added that collective prayer without the coordinated action of believers, as seen in historical battles, will not yield results. Therefore, lifting oppression requires the awakening of leaders and the unity of the Islamic world alongside prayer and tangible action.
