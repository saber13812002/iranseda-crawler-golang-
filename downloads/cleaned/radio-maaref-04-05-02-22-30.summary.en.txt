This radio program, centered on the Friday nights of Muharram, emphasized the spiritual bond between Shias and Imam Hussein (PBUH).
The guest cited a narration from Imam Sadiq (PBUH) warning that neglecting the pilgrimage to Karbala for more than three years severs one's spiritual connection to the Prophet (PBUH).
He criticized Arab rulers who align with the US and Israel, attributing their actions to a lack of religious thought and loyalty to divine authority.
Referencing the Day of Arafah as a time when hearts are opened, the guest urged listeners to return to the path of justice and avoid following unjust leaders.
The broadcast included the recitation of the "Opening the Treasures of Mercy" prayer and eulogies for Imam Hussein (PBUH) and Imam Abbas (PBUH).
Listeners were encouraged to participate in the "Sacrifice of the Travel Month" project by submitting their names to the designated SMS systems.
Finally, the program invited the audience to join the pilgrimage to Karbala or send proxies for the visitation by texting specific numbers.
