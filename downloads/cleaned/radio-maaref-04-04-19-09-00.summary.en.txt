In this program, political analyst Aboud Talebi describes the recent conflict as a strategic defeat for the US and the Zionist regime, evidenced by Washington's direct intervention and a ceasefire request from a position of weakness. Conversely, Iran achieved significant moral and political gains, including increased leadership popularity, national unity, and the revival of the Holy Defense spirit, while successfully defending its territory. Talebi emphasizes that US hostility is rooted in a desire for regional dominance and will not end even if Iran surrenders its nuclear program, as other obstacles like the Velayat-e Faqih system will be targeted. He attributes the fall of Syria to its internal weaknesses and the army's inability to sustain resistance, rather than Iranian negligence. The second part of the show examines the state of religious propagation; while institutional growth has occurred, the main challenges remain hardware infrastructure and the need for specialized approaches to engage the younger generation.
