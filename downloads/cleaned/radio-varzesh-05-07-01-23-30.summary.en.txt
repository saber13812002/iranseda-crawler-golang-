In this interview, Kamyar Kardar recounts memorable moments from his playing career, highlighting his championship goal in the Tehran Club Champions Cup in 1975 as the turning point of his life. He recalls the positive reactions from fans and media, which validated his hard work during a challenging period. Kardar credits the moral and technical support of senior Esteghlal players like Abbas Sarakhob and Samand Harifi for his development. He emphasizes the significant technical gap between Iranian and European football, citing advanced training methods introduced by foreign coaches such as Sukumarokhov. The player expresses regret over the wasted potential of young talents like Amirfarhad Yaghoubi, arguing that the current system prioritizes star worship over investing in youth academies. He attributes his failure to be called up to the senior national team to limited playing time and a lack of assertiveness in advocating for himself at the time. Finally, Kardar stresses the urgent need for the federation and clubs to focus on youth development to ensure a bright future for Iranian football.
