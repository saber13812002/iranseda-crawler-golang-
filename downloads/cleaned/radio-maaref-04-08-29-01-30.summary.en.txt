This text combines a religious lecture with an emotional narrative from the radio program "Love Story." The first section discusses the spiritual status of Imam Hassan (AS), presenting his silence and pursuit of peace as the primary factors in preserving the Quran and the faith against the Umayyads. The speaker cites concepts like "Uyub al-Ilm" and "Qalb al-Wujud" to describe Imam Hassan as the true source of divine knowledge and spiritual secrets. The second part features the testimony of the mother of martyr Arman Ali-Verdi, who was killed during the 2022 protests. The mother recounts the final moments of her son, describing her maternal intuition and her husband's attempt to hide the news to spare her emotional shock. She speaks of Arman's deep desire for martyrdom and his strong emotional bond with his younger brother, Mohammad, who suffered severe grief after Arman's death. The narrative highlights the patience, sacrifice, and profound pain experienced by the families of martyrs in the face of such loss.
