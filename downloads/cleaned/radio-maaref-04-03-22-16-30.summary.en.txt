In this radio program, the expert compares living standards before and after the revolution, highlighting significant improvements in housing, health, and infrastructure despite sanctions and population growth. While acknowledging current management challenges and inflation, he argues that the system's efficiency is undeniable compared to the Pahlavi era, especially given the harsher conditions. The discussion then shifts to the potential consequences of failed nuclear negotiations and the Supreme Leader's concerns about internal unrest. The expert believes that war is too costly for the US and that negotiation failures do not necessarily lead to military conflict, though they may cause economic fluctuations. He emphasizes that many economic issues are internal and that proper public communication and strengthening domestic production can prevent unrest and widespread protests. Finally, the program concludes with a discussion on the etiquette of hospitality and the importance of respecting social and family interactions.
