This radio program provided religious rulings in response to several listener questions. According to Ayatollah Khomeini, a traveler's prayer remains shortened for trips under ten days and upon returning home. Performing ghusl with a bucket of water is permissible if the correct intention is made and water reaches all body parts. A follower of Ayatollah Sistani who falls asleep during prayer must restart both wudu and prayer, unless it is a minor state that does not invalidate wudu. Women with light istihadah must perform wudu for each prayer and pray immediately. While wearing a tala is forbidden for men, it does not invalidate wudu if the ritual was performed correctly. Collected kaffarah funds in a mosque are strictly for feeding the needy and cannot be used for mosque expenses. Finally, listeners were encouraged to submit high-quality questions and utilize the program's communication channels.
