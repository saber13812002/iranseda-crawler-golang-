In this radio program, the analyst highlights Israel's strategic failure to disintegrate Iran, emphasizing the Islamic Republic's military and moral victory in the recent conflict. He dismisses the claims of Netanyahu and Trump as invalid, acknowledging the heavy but honorable cost of 18 senior generals' martyrdom. Despite the damages, Iran maintained its missile capabilities and, with widespread public support, not only prevented collapse but also strengthened its regional standing. The analyst criticizes the inaction of the IAEA and the UN Security Council in condemning Israel's aggression, viewing this neglect as a factor in weakening international institutions' credibility. From a diplomatic perspective, the US betrayal in negotiations and Europe's disregard for JCPOA obligations underscore the need to reassess foreign policies and rely on domestic capacities. Finally, he warns of the potential for repeated attacks by the defeated regime, stressing the importance of Iran's continuous military and social readiness and the central role of leadership in maintaining national stability.
