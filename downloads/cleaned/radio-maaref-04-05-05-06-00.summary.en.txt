This program features a tafsir of verses 119 to 120 of Surah Al-Ma'idah by Ayatollah Javadi Amoli, emphasizing the inseparable nature of "truthfulness" and "truth." The interpretation asserts that God is the absolute Truth and Truthful, with no equal in sincerity. On the Day of Judgment, absolute transparency eliminates the possibility of lying, forcing even Satan to admit that God's promises were true while his own were false. The concept of God's "Ridwan" (pleasure) is defined at two levels: through actions and attributes, and at a higher level, through the essence of the soul. The tafsir distinguishes between "righteous deeds" and "intrinsic righteousness," noting that God's acceptance of a person's essence is superior to the acceptance of their actions. Fulfilling the divine covenant is presented as the primary indicator of faith, with Jesus (PBUH) cited as the supreme model of such fidelity. Finally, the program warns that rejecting divine law and ruling by other means leads to ignorance, disbelief, oppression, and transgression.
