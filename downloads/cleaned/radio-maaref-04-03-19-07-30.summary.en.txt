In this radio program, the concept of gratitude was explained as a three-stage process involving the heart, tongue, and actions.
The highest level of gratitude was identified as practical action, such as helping the needy and utilizing divine blessings wisely.
The guest cited Quranic verses to describe gratitude as a sign of righteous servants and a factor in increasing blessings and longevity.
The second part of the discussion focused on arrogance and its destructive effects on humans and Satan.
Arrogance was examined as the trait that caused Iblis to be expelled from heaven and his status among the angels.
It was emphasized that majesty belongs solely to God, and humans must avoid this moral vice through humility and gratitude.
The program concluded with a call to be thankful for the blessing of guardianship and the playing of a religious song.
