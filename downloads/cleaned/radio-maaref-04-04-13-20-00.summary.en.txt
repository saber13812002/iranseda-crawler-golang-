This radio broadcast began by covering the mourning rituals for Hazrat Qasim in Karbala and the Iraqi tradition of "Hass-khani" as a form of religious and political protest. The Iranian President emphasized that the country's armed forces defended national sovereignty, delivering a significant lesson to Zionist aggressors and preventing regional war escalation. Field reports highlighted the severe fear among Israeli citizens due to continuous missile attacks from Iran and Yemen, forcing them to seek constant shelter. International news segments addressed the Indonesian government's support for Islamic schools, the global condemnation of Muslim persecution in India, and a 75 percent rise in anti-Islamic incidents in France. The program concluded with the announcement of prayer times and the recitation of Surah Fajr, known as the "Surah of Hussein." According to narrations, this surah symbolizes Imam Hussein's uprising and the revival of life and justice within the Muslim community.
