This radio program discussed the concept of trustworthiness as a fundamental pillar of Muslim unity and social capital. By referencing the Prophet Muhammad's title "Al-Amin" and stories like that of Hassan the cobbler in Zanjan, it was emphasized that safeguarding trust, even from enemies, builds public confidence. The occasion of the Day of Endowments was also noted, highlighting that waqf is a divine trust requiring careful management by its administrators. The second part of the show analyzed the vice of "slander," defined as a combination of lies and backbiting that serves as character assassination. The guest explained that slander stems from envy and an inability to tolerate others' superiority, carrying severe worldly and otherworldly consequences. The most dangerous individuals were identified as those who facilitate slander by listening to and accepting these lies, thereby aiding social destruction. Finally, the program advised distancing oneself from slanderers and fostering a society based on trust and virtue by upholding integrity and avoiding defamation.
