The radio program began by addressing Islamic rulings regarding the prayer of gratitude and the calculation of old debts based on current or original rates. It then covered Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi's fatwas on the amount of Zakat al-Fitr and the permissibility of determining fetal gender via IVF. The discussion also clarified that consuming small amounts of food residue or water droplets during prayer does not invalidate it, unlike in fasting. The second segment featured a lecture by Professor Javad Mohtadi on the concepts of rights and duties in Nahj al-Balagha. He emphasized that divine rights include obedience, gratitude, and piety, noting that few people fully fulfill these obligations. The lecture highlighted Imam Ali's just treatment of prisoners after the Battle of Jamal as an example of fulfilling human rights. Finally, the speaker stressed the importance of believing in God's promises and the role of intellectual leaders in resolving societal doubts and ambiguities.
