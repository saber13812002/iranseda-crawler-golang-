In the radio program "Ma'arif," Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the spiritual importance of alleviating the needs of believers, citing religious narrations and the compassionate life of Ayatollah Niazia Tabrizi. He illustrated how kindness and attention to others' needs can profoundly impact their hearts and strengthen community bonds. The second segment introduced the book "Neshan-e Hassan" by Leila Mohtavai, which offers a literary and historical narrative of Imam Hassan's children and their presence during the events of Karbala. The story depicted the emotional and intellectual exchanges between Imam Hassan and his brother Umar in Mecca, focusing on their concerns regarding Yazid's threats and discussions about marriage and piety. The narrative also highlighted the enthusiastic letters sent by the Shia of Kufa to Imam Hussein, expressing their desire to follow him. However, doubts were raised about the Kufans' reliability, referencing their past betrayal of Imam Ali. The segment concluded with Imam Hussein contemplating these letters and the political situation, leaving the gathering in a state of solemn reflection.
