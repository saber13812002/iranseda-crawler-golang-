In this radio program, experts refute the claim that the strictness of the 1980s caused recent crises, arguing instead that the root of the problems lies in the gradual laxity in law enforcement and foreign conspiracies. They assert that social disorder and the hijab controversy are not the result of extremism but rather stem from neglecting the sanctity of the law and the political exploitation of this vacuum by enemies. In the second part, Trump's shift from military to economic warfare is interpreted as an admission of US failure on the military front. In response to this threat, experts emphasize the need for novel economic tactics to neutralize sanctions and apply reciprocal pressure on US economic chokepoints, rejecting the false dichotomy of war or surrender. The discussion also addresses the December coup attempt and the presence of infiltrators within the governance system, calling for authorities to take decisive action based on solid evidence without leniency. Finally, it is stated that the media and the public have a duty to demand accountability from officials to maintain and strengthen public trust in the system through transparency and firm action.
