The first part of the program analyzes the historical figure Reza Pahlavi, portraying him as a violent agent serving Russian and British imperial interests. As the commander of the Cossack machine gun units, he played a pivotal role in the siege of Tabriz and the suppression of the Constitutionalists, contributing to the martyrdom of leaders like Sattar Khan and Baqir Khan. The text argues that the history of the Constitutional Revolution has been distorted by various political currents, while emphasizing that its leaders, including Amir Kabir, were driven by Islamic motivations to fight internal tyranny and foreign occupation. The second section discusses Quranic verses regarding the fate of the Jews during the era of the Mahdi. While some interpretations do not relate to this specific period, certain narrations identify the "servants of God" mentioned in the text as the Imam Mahdi and his companions. These narrations predict that after two instances of corruption, the Jews will ultimately be defeated by the divine forces and the Imam of the Time.
