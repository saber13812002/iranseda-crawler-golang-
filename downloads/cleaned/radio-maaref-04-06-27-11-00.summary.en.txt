The first interaction meeting between the seminary and the Hajj delegation emphasized utilizing seminary resources for international affairs and increasing Iran's Hajj quota. Coinciding with Persian Poetry and Literature Day, the legacy of Shahrir, a prominent poet who honored the Ahl al-Bayt in his works, was commemorated. The Islamic Revolution Housing Foundation highlighted its support for lower-income households through 400-million-toman loans and lease-to-own schemes, stressing the importance of public participation. The Imam Khomeini Relief Committee is supporting 850,000 needy students by distributing educational packages and organizing supportive camps. The Shahid Etemad Plan, driven by volunteer groups and public participation, has accelerated the renovation of over 30,000 schools across the country. The 30-volume research collection "Iranian Colonialism Studies," translated into three languages, analyzes the role of colonialism in Iran's modern history. In the religious segment, the sermon emphasized avoiding overeating and excessive talking as virtues loved by God, based on the conduct of the Infallibles. Finally, the religious ruling regarding the impurity of dental instruments that become contaminated with blood after being removed from a patient's mouth was explained.
