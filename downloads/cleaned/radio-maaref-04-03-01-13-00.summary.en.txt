This program interprets the Verse of Mutual Cursing in Surah Al-Imran to identify the People of the Cloak as the Prophet, Ali, Fatima, Hasan, and Husayn.
According to narrations, the Prophet placed these four individuals under his cloak to demonstrate that they are the true Ahl al-Bayt.
God purifies these five from all sin in this verse, thereby confirming their divine infallibility.
Imam Ali is presented as the Prophet's successor and caliph, a status that transcends the Prophet's personal will.
The speaker emphasizes the human need for an infallible guide, viewing the silence of Imam Ali and Imam Hasan as wise.
This silence was strategic to preserve the fragile thread of Islam and prevent the complete disintegration of the Muslim community.
Had they risen in revolt, the religion might have been destroyed, so their primary goal was preserving Islam for future generations.
Ultimately, Imamate is presented as a necessity for interpreting the Quran and solving human problems after the Prophet's era.
