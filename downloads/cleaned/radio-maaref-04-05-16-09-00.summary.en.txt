In this radio program, the analyst argues that the primary objective of the US and the West is to prevent the emergence of any independent political power in the region, citing historical interventions such as the 1953 coup against Mossadegh. He emphasizes that the nuclear enrichment issue is merely a pretext and that sanctions will not be lifted unless Iran submits completely, regardless of any diplomatic agreements. By referencing the fates of countries like Libya and Syria, which faced destruction after capitulating or being weakened, he warns that pressure on Iran will continue as long as it maintains its sovereignty. In response to listener questions regarding the lack of global action against Israel's actions in Gaza, the discussion highlighted the UN's impotence due to the US veto power and the dependence of many governments on the West. Consequently, the practical solution lies in mobilizing the masses and strengthening the Axis of Resistance. Specifically, the proposal by Malaysia to send a thousand aid ships was presented as a viable strategy to break the blockade and exert tangible pressure on Israel through organized public action.
