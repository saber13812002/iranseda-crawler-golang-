This radio program began by discussing the final preparations to facilitate Arbaeen pilgrim traffic at the Mehran border, involving the Iranian IRGC commander and the Iraqi Minister of Interior. General Radan emphasized the full readiness of the borders and the importance of pilgrims managing their travel time, while noting that domestic airline tickets are being sold through official systems. It was also announced that approximately 14,000 male and female preachers will be deployed to Iranian and Iraqi camps to conduct cultural activities under the slogan "Ana Lillahi." In the academic segment, the submission of 575 papers for the first National Conference on Islamic and Digital Humanities, scheduled for September, was reported. The main segment, titled "Parsaiyan," focused on the courage and conduct of clerics, recounting the story of Allameh Amini in Arafat. In this narrative, Allameh Amini stood firmly against an Arab prince, asserting the rights of God's servants in the sacred land of Arafat. Additionally, the bravery of Ayatollah Qazi Tabatabai against the Shah's regime and his firm response to a disrespectful official during his exile in Kerman were highlighted. These stories illustrate that faith and steadfastness empower believers to remain courageous and resilient in the face of oppressive powers and injustice.
