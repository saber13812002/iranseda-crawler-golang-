The program begins by highlighting the establishment of the Qom Seminary by Ayatollah Haeri Yazdi as the most significant cultural action during the Pahlavi era. Despite severe pressure from Reza Shah and attempts to dismantle it, the seminary was preserved through the political acumen of scholars like Sheikh Baqiri, eventually becoming the foundation for training revolutionary leaders such as Imam Khomeini. The discussion then shifts to the resistance of the people and clergy against Pahlavi cultural policies, notably the Goharshad Mosque uprising and the resulting martyrdoms. The second part of the program features the memoirs of freed POWs from the Iran-Iraq War, recounting their spiritual and physical resilience against enemy and Meccarist abuses in captivity camps. The book "Nordin, Son of Iran" is also introduced as a sincere work in resistance literature that avoids hagiography. The program concludes by emphasizing the spirit of freedom and spirituality, paying tribute to the martyrs and honoring their distinguished families.
