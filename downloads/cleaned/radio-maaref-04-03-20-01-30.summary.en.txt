This radio program interprets the concept of "Hablullah" as a vital connection between humans and God that prevents falling into the abyss of sin and hell.
The divine grace manifested through the Prophet's mission transformed hostile tribal relations into a profound brotherhood deeper than blood ties.
This authentic brotherhood was established around the guardianship of Imam Ali, fostering virtues such as self-sacrifice, forgiveness, and deep affection within the community.
The program emphasizes that detaching from this divine axis leads to deviation, materialism, and hell, rendering worship without allegiance to the Imams devoid of true value.
True piety and seeking refuge in the Infallible Imams are the only ways to save the community from Satan's plots and preserve its religious identity.
The Imams act as the shade, sun, and rain of divine mercy, reviving dead hearts and transforming society into a terrestrial paradise.
The broadcast concluded with specific prayers for the hastening of Imam Mahdi's reappearance and the strengthening of the believers' front.
