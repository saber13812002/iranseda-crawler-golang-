This radio program initially addressed the ruling on performing full prayers in three types of homes (original, chosen, and legal) according to the Supreme Leader, advising listeners to consult their marja for personal details. The simple method of visiting graves for followers of Imam Khomeini was explained, emphasizing the importance of dedicating Quranic rewards to the deceased. In response to specific questions, lying to obtain a bank card was declared haram even out of necessity, and a wife's obligation to her husband was prioritized over recommended acts like night prayers. The conditions for the legality of buying and selling loan privileges and the sufficiency of one ghusl with the intention of janabat were also clarified. The program concluded by commemorating the birth of Imam Ali (AS) and discussing the virtues of Hazrat Abdol-Azim al-Hasani (AS).
