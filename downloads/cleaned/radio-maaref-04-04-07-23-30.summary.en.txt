In this radio program, the commentator interprets a Quranic verse regarding the permissibility of marrying the wives of one's adopted sons. He explains that the Prophet's marriage to Zaynab bint Jahsh aimed to break a pre-Islamic custom and remove legal restrictions for believers. By referencing the story of Zayd ibn Harithah, the speaker highlights how this divine action established a crucial social and legal principle. Following the exegesis, the host congratulates listeners on the days of Muharram and encourages them to utilize the spiritual opportunities of this period. The program then features religious and patriotic songs dedicated to Imam Ali, Imam Hussein, and the defense of Iran. These lyrics emphasize national values, resistance against external threats, and deep love for the homeland. The broadcast concludes with a repeated musical segment and the end of the audio transmission.
