In this radio program, expert Mehdi Yousefian addresses listener questions regarding the duties of those awaiting the Imam and the concept of the "End Times." He emphasizes that the primary duty for women is to raise future generations and prepare their children to support the Imam of the Time. Citing religious narrations, Yousefian notes that fifty women will be among the first rank of the Imam's companions, many of whom are mothers of martyrs. In the second part, he discusses the term "End Times," clarifying that while it appears in traditions, it requires careful interpretation. He argues that the world will face natural and social chaos before the appearance, but the savior's ultimate goal is to establish justice. Yousefian also highlights the West's role in promoting ignorance and denying the true savior, arguing that despite their hidden knowledge of the Imam's characteristics, they refuse to accept him. Finally, he describes the Imam's appearance as the beginning of a new phase in humanity's journey toward perfection and global justice.
