In this radio program, the concept of "trading with God" is explained as a three-part contract involving servitude, service to creation, and the preservation of self-dignity. The speaker emphasizes that God guarantees divine insurance and protection against ideological and practical deviations in exchange for sincere servitude. A significant portion of the discussion is dedicated to the importance of women maintaining modesty and chastity, which is described as the highest form of trading with God and the "zakat of beauty." It is also stressed that serving others should not be limited by specific times, places, or individuals, but must be performed without expectation of reward. By citing narrations from the Prophet and the Imams, the speaker highlights the importance of charity and almsgiving to secure spiritual profits in the afterlife. The program concludes by offering condolences for the mourning period of Safar and the martyrdom of Imam Reza (AS), encouraging listeners to adhere to these principles to draw closer to the appearance of Imam Mahdi (AJ). Finally, the show transitions to the "Creed Inquiry" segment, inviting listeners to submit questions to Professor Mohammad Sharoudi.
