This radio program explores the pilgrimage memories of visitors to Imam Reza (AS) in Mashhad, emphasizing the importance of families traveling together.
The reporter interviews a family from Isfahan who made a spontaneous decision to undertake this spiritual journey.
The mother of the family speaks about the spiritual atmosphere of the trip and Imam Reza's prayers for the pilgrims.
The second part of the show is dedicated to reviewing memories sent in by listeners via phone and text messages.
One listener shares a childhood memory of visiting the shrine with his father, who is now ill in bed.
These stories illustrate that the pilgrimage to Imam Reza (AS) has been a unique and meaningful moment for many people.
The program concludes with prayers for the sick and thanks to the participants.
