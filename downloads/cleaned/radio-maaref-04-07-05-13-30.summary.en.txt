The radio program begins by honoring the memory of Sayyid Hassan Nasrallah and martyr Nil Foroushaneh before transitioning to recollections from the Sacred Defense era. The first section features the Supreme Leader's account of the initial moments of the Iran-Iraq War and the immediate military and political responses. By citing precise statistics on 289 border violations by Iraq in 1979 and 1980, the Leader demonstrated that the war was pre-planned. He also explained Iraq's strategy of launching supporting attacks to divert Iranian forces from the main offensive. Subsequently, the program shares the story of a young martyr and spiritual predictions regarding his future. The final section emphasizes the Leader's firm rejection of negotiations and ceasefires while Iranian territory was occupied. Drawing on historical lessons, he argued that submission and negotiation would only lead to the loss of more land.
