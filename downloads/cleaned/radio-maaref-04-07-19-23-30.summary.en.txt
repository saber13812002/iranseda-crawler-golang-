Ayatollah Ramezani discusses the pivotal role of his parents and the local religious atmosphere in shaping his passion for the clergy. He explains that attending religious gatherings and the moral example of his hardworking parents naturally guided him toward seminary studies without direct coercion. Despite a low high school average and initial rejection from Qom, he moved to Mashhad where he spent four fruitful years of study. The program also contrasts the nature of universities and seminaries in the pre-revolutionary era. During that time, seminaries were strictly focused on religious and jurisprudential sciences, remaining completely detached from political and social debates. In contrast, universities were influenced by various external currents, sometimes leading students to adopt views contrary to their religious upbringing. This structural difference helped preserve the independent identity of the seminaries against external pressures and social changes. The broadcast concludes with the recitation of the Ziyarat of Imam Mahdi (AJ) and religious poetry.
