Ashoor Gol-Mohammadi, a former basketball player and coach, recounts his career highlights with teams like Homa and Azad University. He recalls multiple championships in the top league and international university tournaments featuring foreign teams. Gol-Mohammadi highlights the crucial financial support and foundational efforts of Azad University's team founders in developing university sports. He shares the personal hardship of his first wife's passing and credits his second wife for helping him return to his professional life. After retiring from playing, he transitioned to coaching at Paykan and later served as head coach for Azad University teams. He also discusses his experience as a radio commentator and host for basketball programs. Finally, he emphasizes the importance of maintaining strong, sincere relationships with players and his enduring passion for the sport.
