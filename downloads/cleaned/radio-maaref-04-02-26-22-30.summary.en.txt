This radio program examined the rational, philosophical, and mystical proofs for the existence of the Imam in every era.
The guest interpreted verse 55 of Surah An-Nur, highlighting God's promise of global governance for believers and the transformation of fear into security after the reappearance.
The attributes of the Imam's infallibility were defined as comprehensive knowledge of the past, present, and future, along with absolute freedom from worldly dependencies.
These traits ensure the Imam is free from error and sin, enabling him to guide society effectively through his complete awareness of existence.
According to this perspective, submitting to the Imam and entrusting life's management to him is the prerequisite for achieving lasting peace and joy.
Listeners also expressed their longing for the reappearance and requested its acceleration through sent messages and poetry.
Finally, it was emphasized that maintaining hope for the future and preparing the ground for the reappearance is the primary duty of Shias during the occultation.
