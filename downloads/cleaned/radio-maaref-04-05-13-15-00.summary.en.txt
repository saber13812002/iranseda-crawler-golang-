This radio program examined the legal and jurisprudential challenges of supporting the elderly, emphasizing the potential of Islamic jurisprudence. A key point was the underutilization of legal tools like "wasia bil-sils" by men, as the statutory one-eighth inheritance share is often insufficient for a widow's needs. Islamic law mandates financial and ethical support for the elderly based on their inherent dignity, even in cases of war or parental sin. Statistics indicate that over half of the harms inflicted on the elderly are psychological and emotional, stemming largely from family neglect. Urbanization and apartment living were criticized for weakening family support systems, raising concerns about children's negligence toward aging parents. Given the projected sharp increase in Iran's elderly population by 2051, the need for precise support policies and cultural awareness to honor the elderly in both family and society was highlighted.
