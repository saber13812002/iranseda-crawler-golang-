This radio program discussed the strategic importance of Iran as the "Heart of the World" and "Heart of Asia" from a political geography perspective.
Key reasons for this status include access to vital straits like Hormuz and Bab-el-Mandeb, as well as the vast energy reserves of the Persian Gulf.
This location ensures that regional developments, including the Islamic Revolution, acquire global dimensions and influence world destiny.
In the book review segment, "The Islamic Revolution and Its Roots" was analyzed, emphasizing the religious, political, and economic roots of the revolution.
The primary motivations for the revolution were identified as the Shah's regime's secularization, political dependence on foreign powers, and economic issues stemming from unfair contracts.
The program then transitioned to the topic of neighborly ethics and kindness, citing Quranic verses and narrations from the Ahl al-Bayt.
It was emphasized that praying for neighbors, protecting their privacy and honor, and avoiding harm are crucial moral duties for Muslims.
Finally, the role of resistance in shifting the regional balance of power was highlighted, underscoring the future impact of the Islamic Revolution's current in the world.
