The program began by analyzing the twelve-day war, emphasizing that resisting oppression is essential for achieving global justice, even at a high cost. By referencing historical narratives like Karbala and the lives of Yasir and Sumayyah, the speaker argued that accepting humiliation contradicts human nature and faith. In the second part, General Mosavi praised the heroic performance of the IRGC Aerospace Force in defending Iran's territorial integrity, deeming it greater than achievements during the Sacred Defense. He highlighted the strong will and determination of the forces, stating they will not allow the country's security to be threatened. Finally, the program stressed the importance of national unity, adherence to the Supreme Leader, and readiness for the Imam's reappearance. Listeners were encouraged to support the defenders through prayers and visits to holy sites.
