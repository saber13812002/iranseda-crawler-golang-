In this Radio Ma'aref program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verse 105 of Surah Al-Ma'idah and discusses Sufi concepts regarding the knowledge of God. Citing a narration from Imam Sadiq (AS), he categorizes love for the Ahl al-Bayt into three levels and emphasizes the importance of truly knowing God. The commentator argues that knowing God involves two stages: first recognizing His essence as a present witness, and then understanding His attributes. By referencing the Quranic stories of Joseph and Moses, he demonstrates that God introduces Himself through the "I" (the listener's self) rather than through names or titles. The key point is that one must first recognize God as a reality present within oneself before proceeding to know His attributes. This process, unlike the usual bottom-up approach, moves from top to bottom through genuine self-knowledge. Ultimately, knowing God means realizing that the Divine Essence is present in human existence prior to any description.
