In the first segment of the program, Hojjat al-Islam Dr. Naser Rafiei outlined ten key principles of charity based on the Quran. He emphasized that charity must be given from lawful and pure wealth, without hypocrisy or expectation of reward, and should prioritize family members and essential societal needs such as housing and education. The final point highlighted the virtue of giving secretly, which carries greater reward, noting that God replaces what is given with something better. The discussion included the story of Imam Sadiq (AS), who gave away money intended for buying a house in Medina to the poor, offering the recipient a deed to a house in Paradise instead. The second segment, titled "Enduring Memories," featured accounts from the Holy Defense era. Sardar Mohammad Bagher Nikkhah Bohrami recounted the early days of the war, including the aerial bombardment of Ahvaz and the efforts of students and clerics to establish themselves at the front lines. He described the initial cooperation between the Reconstruction Jihad and the IRGC, highlighting the urgent need for cultural content and war chants in the trenches during the conflict's early stages.
