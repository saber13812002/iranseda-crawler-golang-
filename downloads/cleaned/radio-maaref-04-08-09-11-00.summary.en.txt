The secretary of the National Quran Memorization Plan announced the drafting of a strategy to train ten million memorizers, aiming to address a fourteen-year delay. This initiative, supported by 35 billion Tomans allocated to the Ministry of Culture and Islamic Propagation, is being developed with the help of experts and managers. The program also reported that 1,800 works were submitted to the 27th Hawza Book of the Year Conference, whose final ceremony will be held in Qom. The 13th of Aban was commemorated as a symbol of Iran's resistance against global arrogance, recalling the 1964 capitulation bill and the 1979 embassy takeover. The head of the Environment Protection Organization emphasized the need for legal reforms to support environmental rangers. Additionally, the "Hope Card" was introduced to provide monthly financial support to mothers to encourage childbirth. The 39th National Student Quran and Hadith Festival will be hosted in Isfahan, and the Qaysar Aminpour Literary Prize was unveiled. Finally, religious scholars provided jurisprudential answers regarding prayer validity with concentration issues, partnership with alcoholics, and the calculation of Khums.
