Former footballer Reza Kangarani recounts his professional career and the reasons behind his early retirement. He discusses memorable matches involving Persepolis and the performance of goalkeepers during that era. Kangarani admits that he quit football at the age of 28 due to moral sensitivities and frustration with the injustice prevalent in the sport. After a three-year hiatus, he played for the Azerbaijan team, helping them win the Tehran league and secure promotion to the second division. However, he declined to continue playing in the second division after the team was transferred to Ali Parvin, citing financial and emotional disagreements. Kangarani believes that modern football has become poorer in terms of technique and aesthetics, with a dominant focus on results. After retiring, he worked at the Tehran Municipality and now maintains his physical fitness through regular training for senior age-group competitions.
