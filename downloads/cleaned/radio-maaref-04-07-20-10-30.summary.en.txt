The radio program "Beautiful Life" featured Dr. Pourhosseini discussing the importance of maintaining children's happiness in traditional medicine.
He warned that frightening children or depriving them of harmless pleasures can lead to heart weakness and chronic fear.
In the "A Drop from the Sea" segment, Hojjat al-Islam Rasoul interpreted a verse from Surah Yusuf to condemn hasty judgment and slander against believers.
Referencing the story of Benjamin and Joseph's brothers, he emphasized the necessity of assuming the best in fellow Muslims and avoiding pessimism.
The final section highlighted the critical role of foreign languages in global Islamic propaganda and the expansion of the Islamic Republic's influence.
Training competent propagandists and the active participation of women in scientific and preaching fields were identified as current necessities.
The program aimed to educate listeners on religious, educational, and social concepts, broadcasting from Qom's Ma'aref Radio.
