Former Persepolis player Rahimi Yusefi shared his memories of playing in the 1980s during a radio interview. He described the rigorous training sessions at Topeh Davoodi and the high competitive spirit of the team during that era. Yusefi recalled his debut appearance as a substitute for Malkuan in a match against Sharox, which ended in a championship victory. He also discussed rejecting a two-year contract offer from Esteghlal to join Port Darreh before eventually returning to Persepolis. The player recounted his selection for the national team in 1984 and his participation in the Asian Cup. Finally, he criticized the current state of Iranian football for neglecting local talents and emphasized the importance of ethics in the sport.
