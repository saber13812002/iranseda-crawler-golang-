The guest, reflecting on his decade as president of the Rugby Federation, highlighted his efforts to secure Asian and global memberships and attract private investment to develop the sport. He revealed the origins of his entry into sports management and his conflicts with former basketball federation officials over flawed strategies and an over-reliance on financial power. Criticizing Iran's inefficient sports structure, he emphasized that Olympic success cannot be bought but requires rigorous training, precise planning, and internal structural reforms. He also criticized the authorities' neglect of analyzing Olympic failures and the lack of a long-term vision for high-medal sports. Finally, he shared a memorable anecdote about awarding real gold medals to the champion basketball team in 2000 as a symbol of respect for athletes.
