In the radio program "Mabna," Professor Rabani Golepayegani interpreted the Quranic metaphor of hypocrites using authentic narrations from Imam Sajjad (AS). He explained that hypocrites derived only a temporary and superficial benefit from the Prophet's (PBUH) guidance, similar to a person using a lightning flash in the darkness before falling back into it. The narrations indicate that these individuals gained social and material benefits in this world through their apparent pledge of allegiance, even receiving protection from Muslims. However, at the moment of death, their apparent light vanishes, and Hell is manifested before them in all its terrifying forms. The Angel of Death reminds them that had they remained steadfast in true faith, Paradise would have been their destination, but their hypocrisy condemns them to eternal punishment. It was also noted that hypocrites feared their secrets being exposed and mistakenly attributed their worldly blessings to their superficial pledge. This discussion highlights the profound difference between genuine faith and apparent hypocrisy in the Hereafter.
