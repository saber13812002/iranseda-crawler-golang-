This program examines the pivotal roles of Imam Sajjad and Hazrat Zainab in Syria following the events of Karbala. Imam Sajjad delivered a historic sermon in Yazid's court, highlighting the virtues of the Ahl al-Bayt and sparking widespread grief and protest among the attendees. This powerful speech caused Yazid to regret bringing the captives to Syria, leading him to order their dignified return to Medina. Upon their return, Imam Sajjad kept the memory of Imam Hussein alive by establishing days of mourning and public weeping. Hazrat Zainab, as a fundamental pillar of this epic, played a crucial role in preserving the identity and message of Karbala through her insight and courage. These actions successfully solidified the religious and political standing of the Ahl al-Bayt within Islamic society. The program concludes with prayers for the appearance of the Imam and support for the resistance front, emphasizing the importance of the Arbaeen pilgrimage and the continuous remembrance of Imam Hussein.
