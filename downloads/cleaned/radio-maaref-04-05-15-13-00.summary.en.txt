This program introduces the second chapter of the "Husaini Epic," led by Imam Sajjad (AS) with the pivotal participation of Hazrat Zainab (SA). Imam Sajjad (AS) assumed leadership at the age of 22 under the most difficult circumstances following his father's martyrdom. A severe illness served as a divine cover to protect him from the battlefield, ensuring his survival for the continuation of the path. Hazrat Zainab (SA) played a crucial role in safeguarding the Imam and transforming the political atmosphere in Kufa and Sham through her powerful sermons. In Kufa, the Ahl al-Bayt (AS) faced humiliation and imprisonment, yet Zainab (SA) successfully defended the Imam against Ibn Ziyad's threats. This 34-year period of Imamate marked the beginning of the spiritual and political reconstruction of the Ahl al-Bayt (AS) after Karbala. The ultimate goal of this movement was to preserve the great legacy of Karbala and transmit it until the appearance of Imam Mahdi (AJ).
