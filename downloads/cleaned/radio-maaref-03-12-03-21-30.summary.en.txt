This radio program examines the connection between Quranic verses and the fate of the Jews during the era of the Mahdi's appearance. The interpretation of the verse "And to Him belongs [the submission of] all in the heavens and the earth" by scholars like Tabataba'i primarily refers to historical context and general monotheism, lacking a direct link to the eschatological discussion of the Jews. However, narrations from Imam Baqir and Imam Sadiq regarding the verse "And We decreed for the Children of Israel" describe two major corruptions and the establishment of a Jewish government before the appearance. According to these reports, a group of God's servants will overthrow the Jewish power and dismantle their regime before the Mahdi emerges. A detailed narration also references the return of Imam Hussein (PBUH) after the natural death of the Imam of the Time, where the Mahdi reveals Imam Hussein's identity to the people. Ultimately, following the passing of the Imam of the Time, Imam Hussein (PBUH) assumes governance as the leader of the Ummah. These analyses indicate that while some interpretations are historical, specific narrations detail the role of the Jews and the post-appearance governmental structure.
