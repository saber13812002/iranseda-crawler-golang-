The former Speaker of the US House of Representatives warned that keeping the Strait of Hormuz closed would lead to a swift US defeat, while the US Secretary of War claimed that civilians are not targeted in strikes against Iran. However, field reports from Tehran reveal that the shockwaves of these attacks have caused severe damage to residential buildings, grocery stores, and a specialized women's health center on Dardasht Street. The cultural segment of the broadcast introduced the book "Me and the Book," which compiles the Supreme Leader's reflections on literary and artistic works related to resistance. The Leader emphasized the critical importance of promoting reading and the profound impact of literature on the lifestyle of young people, urging writers to provide authentic narratives of the war and resistance periods. He praised outstanding works such as "My Commander" and "Step by Step with the Rain" for their role in documenting contemporary history and preserving national identity. The program concluded on the 21st night of Ramadan, the night of the martyrdom of Imam Ali, with prayers for the victory of the armed forces and the spiritual success of the listeners.
