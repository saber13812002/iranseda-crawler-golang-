The memorial ceremony for Hazrat Zahra (SA) was held at Imam Khomeini's Husainiyah, where speakers addressed the community's distance from the path of truth. The head of the Hajj and Pilgrimage Organization announced that Umrah registration for Rajab and Sha'ban begins on November 20, noting that over 80,000 pilgrims have already been dispatched. The fifth of Azar, marking the anniversary of the Basij's formation, highlighted the organization's contributions in military, cultural, and scientific fields. An international conference on the rights of nations and legitimate freedoms is scheduled for November 23 at the IRIB International Conferences Center. The Minister of Culture and Islamic Guidance emphasized the need for inter-agency cooperation to enhance the religious devotion of adolescents. A conference for managers of Islamic Sciences and Doctrine schools began in Qom to improve educational quality. The head of the Dispute Resolution Councils reported that over 1.5 million legal cases were resolved through reconciliation in the first half of the year. Finally, the program provided jurisprudential answers regarding prostration on stone, prayer rules for travelers, and the obligations of the Prayer of Signs.
