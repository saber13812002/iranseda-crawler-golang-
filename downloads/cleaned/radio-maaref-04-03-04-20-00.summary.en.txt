This radio broadcast discussed the deprivation of Gaza's Palestinians from performing Hajj due to the ongoing siege and war, alongside global condemnation of Israeli crimes. The decisive victory of Hezbollah and its allies in Lebanon's municipal elections, particularly in Shia areas, was celebrated as a defeat for the US and Israel. Yemen's military spokesperson announced a successful ballistic missile attack on Ben Gurion Airport, which halted its aviation activities. Reports also covered discriminatory policies in Kuwait targeting Kuwaiti women of Cuban origin, as well as the status of Muslims in Bulgaria and India. In the final segment, marking the Day of Resistance and the resilience of Dastgerd's people, two citizens shared harrowing memories of the city's missile bombardment and the loss of their family members at the start of the Iran-Iraq war.
