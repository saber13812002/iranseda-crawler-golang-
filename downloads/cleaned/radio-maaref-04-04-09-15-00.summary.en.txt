This unprecedented jurisprudential action expands the definition of "moharebeh" beyond physical violence, effectively constituting a targeted and international jihad. Experts argue that this fatwa exposes the enemy's true intent to undermine the essence of Islam, thereby strengthening the unity of the Muslim ummah and imposing new responsibilities on states and media. From an international law perspective, threatening religious leaders is a clear violation of civil and political rights, including freedom of belief, which has drawn global condemnation. This jurisprudential move creates an opportunity for a cognitive war and mobilizes wills against the front of arrogance. The ultimate goal of this movement is to establish a civilizational revolution and defend Islamic identity on the global stage.
