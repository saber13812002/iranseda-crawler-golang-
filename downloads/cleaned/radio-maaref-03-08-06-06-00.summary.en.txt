The program begins with Ayatollah Javadi Amoli's interpretation of verses 139 to 141 of Surah An-Nisa, emphasizing the necessity of detailed faith and the danger of accepting the authority of disbelievers. The commentator analyzes the verse regarding sitting with those who mock divine verses, distinguishing it from other sins and stating that leaving such gatherings is obligatory to avoid complicity in sin. He compares the opportunistic behavior of hypocrites, who seek spoils from both sides in conflicts, to the work of human demons who seize hearts to make people forget God. The verse "Allah will never give the disbelievers a way over the believers" is interpreted through three possibilities: the superiority of believers in the Hereafter, the logical superiority of believers' arguments, and the lack of political dominance by disbelievers. The commentator refutes historical misuses of this verse to deny the martyrdom of Imam Hussein (AS), clarifying that the verse does not imply the impossibility of martyring prophets or Imams, as their killing is explicitly mentioned in the Quran.
