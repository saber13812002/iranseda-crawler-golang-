In this radio program, analysts examined the key messages of the Supreme Leader's statement following the martyrdom of Ayatollah Khamenei. The statement, grounded in Quranic tradition, urges the public to continue the struggle and manage the crisis without succumbing to despair or enemy provocations. Experts emphasized that this message provides psychological stability to the society, alleviating fears of internal chaos or disintegration. The selection of Ayatollah Seyed Mojtaba Khamenei as the new leader is viewed as a symbol of authority, wisdom, and the continuity of the Imamate against global challenges. By warning officials to address victims' needs and maintain national unity, the statement raises the cost of any potential enemy miscalculation. The discussion highlighted deterrence strategies and crisis management, stressing reliance on God and solidarity among all segments of the nation. The program concluded with the recitation of verses from Surah Sad and prayers for the Arbaeen pilgrimage.
