In this radio program, following the martyrdom of the Supreme Leader, the focus was on national unity and continuing the revolution's path based on the school of Imam Hussein.
The people demonstrated their solidarity against external and internal threats through their massive presence in Friday prayers and public gatherings.
Dr. Ali Akbar Jalali, Secretary of the Supreme National Security Council, analyzed the enemy's strategy to create division and strike at the leadership in an interview with IRIB News.
He emphasized the nation's inherent awareness and the resilience of Iran's defense system, noting the failure of US and Israeli military and economic plans.
Jalali warned separatist groups that the armed forces would not allow any fragmentation of the country.
According to the constitution, a temporary leadership council comprising the President, the Head of the Judiciary, and a Guardian Council jurist will soon be formed.
This council will assume the duties of the Supreme Leader until a new leader is elected to prevent a power vacuum.
Finally, the program highlighted the spirit of martyrdom, resistance against economic sanctions, and hope for the victory of the Islamic front in the future.
