In this radio program, the discussion highlights the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) as the ultimate model of human perfection, focusing on the hygienic and spiritual aspects of his conduct.
Expert Morteza Bahrami Khoshnoudi emphasized the importance of oral hygiene, including rinsing the mouth and nose, and maintaining clean clothing, especially during prayer.
He explained that keeping clothes clean is not only a health requirement but also a spiritual necessity that preserves the garment's spiritual purity.
A significant portion of the talk addressed the vice of backbiting, describing it as a form of spiritual pollution and moral decay.
Practical solutions for abandoning backbiting include gaining awareness, exercising patience, remembering God instead of creation, and covering the faults of others.
The speaker advised defending those who are backbitten and leaving gatherings where such behavior occurs to prevent the spread of this negative culture.
Finally, seeking forgiveness from God for the backbitten individual and avoiding the company of habitual backbiters were presented as key ethical and religious remedies.
