In this radio program, Professor Jafari analyzed the Battle of Camel and its prerequisites using both Shia and Sunni sources. Referring to the 600th sermon of Nahj al-Balagha, he discussed the forced allegiance of Imam Ali (PBUH) and the subsequent breach of trust by a group of companions. The battle was primarily driven by four key figures, each with distinct motives, such as Muawiyah's desire to secure his rule in Syria and Talha and Zubayr's ambition for political power. Imam Ali (PBUH), recognizing their true intentions and fearing corruption, refused to grant them governmental positions and warned them against betrayal before allowing them to leave Medina. In the second segment, the cleric answered various jurisprudential questions from listeners. He clarified that zakat al-mal is not due on money specifically saved for essential medical expenses. Additionally, it was explained that a prayer started with certainty that the time has entered remains valid, even if it is later discovered that part of it was performed outside the designated time.
