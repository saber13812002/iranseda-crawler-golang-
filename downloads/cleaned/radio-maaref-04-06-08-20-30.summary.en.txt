This radio program begins by recounting the martyrdom of Rajai and Bahonar in the Prime Minister's office explosion, featuring eyewitness accounts of the incident and Imam Khomeini's steadfast commitment to the revolution. The main segment focuses on the memoirs of Seyyed Khamal Moini, a freedom fighter who spent eight years in Iraqi captivity. He describes the severe torture, frequent beatings, and inhumane conditions in the prisons of Sulaymaniyah and Baghdad under the Iraqi intelligence forces. Moini details the religious insults and the enemy's attempts to break the prisoners' will using theological ambiguities and targeted violence. He also recounts the transfer of prisoners to the Mosul camp and their passage through the "Tunnel of Terror," where the abuse continued. Finally, the book "Jashn-e Hanabandan" is introduced as a source of Holy Defense memoirs, and the program concludes with a call to maintain hope and resistance against the enemy.
