In this radio program, the physical and mental health of parents is identified as a fundamental prerequisite for effective child rearing. The speaker compares past and present lifestyles, noting that the disappearance of traditional daily activities has eliminated natural educational opportunities, requiring parents to actively fill this gap today. In response to listener questions, constant fighting between siblings or twins is viewed not as a problem to be suppressed, but as an opportunity to develop problem-solving skills and self-confidence. It is recommended that parents refrain from intervening in minor conflicts and instead reduce tension by engaging children in meaningful activities. To curb excessive interest in football and prevent the use of inappropriate language, the promotion of mental games and alternative sports with adult supervision is suggested. Finally, to foster religious motivation in teenagers, the program advises against coercion and punishment, instead emphasizing the importance of parental role modeling and creating a dynamic, affectionate environment.
