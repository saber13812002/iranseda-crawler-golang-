In this radio program, Professor Maleki discusses the Quranic concept of "Tabyan," arguing based on Allameh Tabatabaei's interpretation that God has created everything with clear details and without ambiguity.
He addresses a listener's question about the verse "And whoever believes in Allah, He will guide his heart," explaining that this guidance manifests as patience, steadfastness, and gratitude.
The professor emphasizes that true faith must be stable and established to provide resilience against life's hardships and blessings.
Towards the end, he stresses the importance of returning to the practical lifestyle of the Prophet Muhammad as the ultimate model for living.
While noting that historical records of some Imams are limited, he asserts that their ethical and jurisprudential teachings remain sufficient for guiding human life.
Finally, he congratulates the audience on the birth of the Prophet and Imam Sadiq, highlighting security as the fundamental infrastructure for economic, scientific, and social progress in the country.
