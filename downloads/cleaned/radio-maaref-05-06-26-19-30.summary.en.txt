The program identifies arrogance as a moral vice and a form of self-deception, citing Imam Ali's description of it as the worst form of foolishness.
It distinguishes between negative arrogance, which hinders spiritual growth, and positive pride, which celebrates identity and achievements.
Divine piety and self-restraint are presented as the primary solutions to prevent arrogance from overpowering human reason and will.
The second part analyzes Shahid Motahhari's book "Wilayaat," defining the root of "Wilaya" as a deep, seamless bond between entities.
Negative Wilaya is interpreted as an identity firewall to maintain independence from foreign cultures, while positive Wilaya fosters internal social cohesion.
During the Imam's occultation, the Guardianship of the Jurist is deemed essential as specialized management to preserve order and justice.
Ultimately, Wilaya is described as an evolutionary process starting from individual identity preservation and culminating in self-mastery and divine power.
