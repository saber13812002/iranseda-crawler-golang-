This radio program began by explaining the jurisprudential details of the Hajj pilgrimage, specifically focusing on the standing at Arafat and the importance of attending the ceremony of disavowal of polytheists.
Subsequently, listeners' religious questions were addressed, including the ruling on not eating to fullness to prevent waste and the recommendation to utilize leftover food.
It was clarified that if a necklace was given as a gift, it belongs to the recipient, and there is no obligation to return it to the sister-in-law.
The permanent and relative mahram status of a paternal or maternal uncle towards his nephews and nieces was also confirmed.
The concept of a contractual homeland was explained with reference to seasonal migration areas, detailing the conditions for the validity of prayer and fasting there.
Regarding the marriage of individuals with mental disabilities, it was stated that the mahr and maintenance remain the husband's responsibility, regardless of the disability status of either party.
Finally, the program concluded by emphasizing the importance of praying on time and the special respect Imam Mahdi (aj) holds for prayer, ending with a religious song and an introduction to the next segment.
