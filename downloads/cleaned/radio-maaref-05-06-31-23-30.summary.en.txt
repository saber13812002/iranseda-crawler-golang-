This radio program presents a dramatic retelling of the book "In the Ambush of the Red Rose" by Mohsen Momeni, which is based on the life of Martyr General Ali Siyad Shirazi. The story begins with the migration of Ali's father, Ziad Khan, from Fars to Khorasan and his service in the Gendarmerie, where his diplomatic approach resolved border conflicts. Following the Allied occupation of Iran, Ziad Khan removed his military uniform in protest against the army's disrespect toward the public, eventually marrying and fathering Ali. During his childhood, Ali faced a moral test involving temptation and divine salvation, which served as a significant lesson for him. Despite his father's objections regarding the corruption in Tehran, Ali moved to the capital and later returned to witness his father's arrest and discharge from the army for protesting guard misconduct. Ultimately, Ali pursued education and teaching to support his family, setting the stage for his future military career and martyrdom in the sacred defense.
