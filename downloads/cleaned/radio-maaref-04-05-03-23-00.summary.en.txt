This radio program discussed ways to support the Imam of the Age, emphasizing the importance of raising a Mahdavi generation starting from kindergarten and schools.
The guest, Ustad Kafili, interpreted the Quranic verse "Truth prevails and falsehood perishes" to demonstrate that falsehood has always been destroyed throughout history, with the Imam's appearance being the ultimate realization of this truth.
He stressed the necessity of moving beyond mere "mourning" to a sustained "Husaini" lifestyle, where the conduct of Imam Hussein and the Mahdi is reflected in all daily actions.
Ustad Kafili warned that true expectation is not just about prayer but involves action and serving as a role model in the workplace, driving, and social interactions.
In the second part, he criticized relying on narrations from individuals who had close ties with unjust rulers, such as Zahri, citing historical accounts.
By quoting a letter of advice from Imam Zain-ul-Abidin (AS) to Zahri, he emphasized the importance of avoiding association with oppressors and being accountable for God's blessings.
Ultimately, the program's main message was that true expectants must, with full insight, be a manifestation of love and the conduct of the Infallible Imams in every moment of their lives.
