Radio Ma'aref reported on the renewed violation of the Gaza ceasefire, with Israeli forces bombing Khan Younis and Rafah. Despite urgent calls from the UN and UNICEF to reopen crossings, Israel has restricted goods entry, citing Hamas's alleged delay in returning bodies. The Mayor of Gaza warned of an immediate need for humanitarian aid and medical equipment to combat famine and disease outbreaks. In Malaysia, Friday prayer attendees held an anti-Israeli march to condemn the regime's crimes and US support. Reports also highlighted Israeli military obstruction of Palestinian farmers harvesting olives in the West Bank. Culturally, the program announced the opening of an Imam Hussein cultural center in Pakistan and an interfaith conference in Albania. Commemorations for the first anniversary of the martyrdom of Kazeroon's Friday Imam, Seyyed Sobahi, were held with a street naming ceremony. The broadcast concluded by previewing upcoming events, including the 13 Aban march and various religious and ethical programs.
