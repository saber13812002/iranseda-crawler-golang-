The radio program began by highlighting the Islamic Republic of Iran's military achievements, including the production of 30 to 40 advanced weapons systems and the significant boost in national self-confidence.
The most valued outcome of the army's development is its independence in military decision-making and the elimination of reliance on foreign countries for training pilots and technicians.
The expert then discussed the 12-day war, explaining that the US aimed to disintegrate Iran, but this plot failed due to strong national unity and the inseparable link between religious and national identity.
The second part of the show focused on the concept of "Infaq" (charity), with the guest detailing its sixth through ninth benefits based on the narrations of Imam Ali (AS).
These benefits include the settlement of debts, increased blessings in life, the growth of wealth in the eyes of God, and substantial profit for the giver.
It was emphasized that sincere charity does not diminish one's wealth but rather leads God to take responsibility for the welfare of the giver's dependents.
Finally, listeners were encouraged to give charity with the intention of helping the followers of Imam Ali and the needy, thereby attaining greater spiritual and material blessings.
