This radio program initially clarified the rulings regarding rest during the Sa'i and Tawaf of Hajj and Umrah, stating that short breaks of three to five minutes are permissible if the ritual resumes from the exact point of interruption. In the Q&A section, the ruling on missed fasts due to a thirty-year addiction was discussed, mandating the payment of fidya for each day and making up the fasts if physically possible. It was explained that wudu is recommended but not mandatory for prostrating on Quranic verses, unless a ritual bath is obligatory. The program also addressed the permissibility of marrying a deceased uncle's or aunt's wife after her waiting period, provided no other impediments exist. Other questions concerning the khums on bank interest, using self-service in restaurants, and the tasbihat in prayer were answered based on the fatwas of major clerics. The final segment emphasized the importance of supporting domestic production and avoiding economic dependence on non-Muslims, citing the examples of scholars like Ayatollah Bafghi and Marashi Najafi who refused foreign goods. This advice was reinforced by a brief exegesis of a verse from Surah Yunus, highlighting the divine laws governing societal rise and fall.
