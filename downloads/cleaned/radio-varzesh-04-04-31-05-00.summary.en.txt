The guest discussed the budgetary challenges faced during his tenure as governor, highlighting the allocation of funds for sports infrastructure in Mazandaran's rural areas. By leveraging community participation and cooperation with provincial directors, he oversaw the construction of over twenty sports halls across the region. A notable example of this approach was resolving a dispute over a sewage plant in Sari by substituting it with a sports hall, a solution that benefited both parties. These initiatives resulted in a sevenfold increase in sports facilities compared to previous periods, contributing to a rise in regional sports champions. The guest concluded by emphasizing the crucial role of the public and media in his and the province's sporting successes. The program ended with a review of historical global sports events and profiles of prominent players like Butragueño and Rommedahl.
