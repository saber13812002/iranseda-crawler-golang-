The program began with a theological discussion explaining why disbelievers will confess to monotheism on Judgment Day.
It was clarified that this confession is not a result of free will but is forced by God through the compulsion of their limbs.
In contrast, their previous claims of polytheism were made under their own free will and choice.
The host then apologized for a delay and introduced the co-hosts while marking the National Police Day.
The main segment focused on appreciating the police force, highlighting the balance between their authority and mercy in maintaining security.
Listeners shared positive anecdotes about the polite and professional behavior of police officers via SMS and phone calls.
The historical section covered Imam Khomeini's migration to Paris, the resistance of Khorramshahr during the war, and the birth of Denis Diderot.
The show concluded with religious music and special greetings to school bus drivers and the Lur people.
