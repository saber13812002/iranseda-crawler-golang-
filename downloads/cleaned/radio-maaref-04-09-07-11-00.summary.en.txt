The Supreme Leader of Iran described the Basij as a vital national asset that enhances the country's power and is essential for confronting global adversaries. He highlighted the United States' failure in the twelve-day war, noting that despite using advanced weaponry, it failed to deceive the Iranian people, which instead strengthened national unity. The Leader urged the public to maintain national cohesion, support the serving government, and strictly avoid waste in essential resources such as water, bread, and gasoline. He also emphasized the importance of spiritual connection with God and prayer for security and well-being, while responding to a letter from the granddaughter of Martyr Hajizadeh. The broadcast further announced the 48th National Quran Competition in Qom and called for blood donations during holidays due to seasonal shortages. The program concluded with a live broadcast of Friday prayers from Tehran and a segment answering various religious legal questions.
