In this radio program, Professor Mohammad Sharoudi addressed various theological and religious questions from listeners. He emphasized the importance of knowing God and maintaining humility, while stressing that abandoning prayer is a grave sin that is not forgiven without genuine repentance. Regarding the concept of sacrifice, he clarified that one's life should be dedicated to the religion and God, not to political figures, unless they are acting in defense of the faith. He also criticized popular superstitions like using Abjad numerology for marriage compatibility, advising listeners to rely on proper upbringing and prayer instead. The professor interpreted Prophet Yusuf's actions with his brothers as a necessary measure to complete a divine test by removing a greater evil. He explained that prophets, including Moses, are sent to guide and save people from oppression and ignorance, even if their communities have a history of errors. Finally, he defined the "Greatest Name" of God as a combination of spiritual rank and specific invocations, and refuted the claim that the declaration of monotheism is exclusive to the month of Rajab.
