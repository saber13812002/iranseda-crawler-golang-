In the first segment, Professor Sadeghi confirmed the presence of Imam Ali in the Cave of Hira based on reliable historical sources and analyzed a narration from the book of Kafi regarding the delay of the Mahdi's appearance. He described this narration as a complex theological debate with various interpretations by Shia scholars, rejecting claims that contradict established history. The program then examined the genealogy of Abu Talib's sons, clarifying that Ali, Ja'far, Aqil, and Talib were full brothers from Fatima bint Asad. It also identified Imam Ali's sisters, such as Ummahani, who are less prominent in historical records. In the second part, the ideological differences between Sufism and Wahhabism were explained, identifying Wahhabism as a literalist school rooted in Ibn Taymiyyah and Muhammad ibn Abd al-Wahhab. This school is characterized by takfir and opposition to practices like visiting graves and tawassul. Finally, the "Voice of Law" segment detailed a real case where a man suffering from schizophrenia was killed by a sorcerer who struck him with an iron rod. This incident highlighted the dangers of consulting fortune tellers and the fraudulent nature of such pseudo-spiritual practices, which led to the victim's immediate death.
