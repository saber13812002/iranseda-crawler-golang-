In this radio program, as the decade of Imamate and Wilayah approaches, the necessity of pledging allegiance to Imam Hussein (PBUH) and renouncing sins, public opinion, and worldly attachments was emphasized.
Key messages included prayers for Karbala pilgrims and listeners, along with promoting the culture of Qadir by mentioning the name of Amir al-Mu'minin in gatherings.
The host invited listeners to participate in the Qadir promotion campaign by sending the text "Ya Amir al-Mu'minin" to 3553 to win prizes.
The program concluded with a historical documentary about the events of June 16, featuring harsh criticism of the government's policies and foreign media.
This documentary section highlighted the oppression of the Iranian people and the urgent need to convey the truth to the world.
Finally, the program ended with congratulations on the anniversary of Imam Khomeini's martyrdom and a call to continue his path.
Participating in religious gatherings and expressing gratitude for divine blessings were presented as factors for gaining God's pleasure.
