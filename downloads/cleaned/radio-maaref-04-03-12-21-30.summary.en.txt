In this program, Seyed Mohammad Ali Javadi Amoli interprets verse 105 of Surah Al-Ma'idah to distinguish between rational (acquired) and intuitive (direct) knowledge. He argues that the Quran primarily calls humans toward intuitive knowledge in matters concerning the purification of the soul and the spiritual path of the prophets. Javadi Amoli emphasizes that the Divine Essence cannot be grasped through mental concepts, making rational knowledge merely a prerequisite for achieving direct, intuitive insight. Citing narrations from Imam Sadiq (AS), he explains that knowing God is always achieved "through God," whether via intellect, prophethood, or self-knowledge. The necessity of revelation and prophethood is also highlighted to understand specific truths that exceed the capacity of human reason. Finally, he stresses that every human possesses an innate divine nature which, through effort and mindfulness, can lead to the true recognition of God.
