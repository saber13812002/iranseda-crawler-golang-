In this radio program, the commentator explains the third characteristic of the pious from the Khutbat al-Mutaqqin, which is walking with humility. Humility is defined linguistically as lowering oneself and technically as a spiritual brokenness that prevents a person from viewing their own merits as superior to others. The speaker emphasizes that humility is an internal state, and merely performing outwardly humble actions without breaking inner arrogance is insufficient. Arrogant walking is described as a sign of the spiritual disease of "kibr," and the Prophet (PBUH) referred to such individuals as true madmen. Conversely, arrogant people will be resurrected in the form of ants on the Day of Judgment and trampled underfoot. To cure arrogance, the commentator suggests two approaches: scientifically comparing oneself to others to recognize one's own sins, and practically performing actions that directly contradict arrogance. A practical example is the story of a wealthy man who, following Imam Sadiq's (AS) order, sold dates in the market to break his arrogance.
