This program provides a detailed analysis of the Quranic verse "Indeed, Allah intends to remove from you the impurity," focusing on the concept of "explicit metaphor." Scholars such as Tabari, Shawkani, and Alusi interpret the term "Rijs" (impurity) as spiritual defilement caused by sins, contrasting it with physical purification. Allameh Tabatabaei further distinguishes between the surface and the essence, defining "Rijs" as a repulsive state resulting from the negative effects of actions on one's character rather than the actions themselves. The use of this metaphor aims to instill a stronger sense of aversion toward sin and emphasize the importance of spiritual purity. In the concluding segment, the speaker highlights the necessity of strengthening Islamic propaganda against Western media narratives. He urges religious scholars to better organize their efforts to present the true image of Islam to the world.
