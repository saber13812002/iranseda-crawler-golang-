In the radio program "Zamzame Ahkam," Hujjat al-Islam Vahidpour discussed the Islamic rulings regarding the Arbaeen pilgrimage and visiting the martyrs. He explained that if a person lacks financial means but can borrow money and repay it, performing the pilgrimage is recommended and has no religious objection. It was also emphasized that financially assisting pilgrims or sponsoring their travel carries immense spiritual reward, and even a sincere intention to participate without the means yields spiritual merit. The fatwa section addressed questions about repaying old debts, picking fruit from street trees, the khums of burial costs for women, the consumption of fidya and kaffarah for missed fasts, and the ruling on unfulfilled oaths. The program concluded by introducing the special "Backpack" show for Arbaeen pilgrims and playing religious poetry.
