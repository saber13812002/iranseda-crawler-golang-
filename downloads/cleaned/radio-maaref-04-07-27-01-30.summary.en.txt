In this radio program, Ayatollah Rza Ostadi emphasizes the necessity of preparing the youth spiritually and doctrinally before they enter adolescence. He warns that deviant groups and enemies of the Islamic system attempt to corrupt the youth through various channels, including sports and media. Consequently, institutions, the clergy, and families are obligated to foster love and trust, turning the home into a safe haven to prevent the youth from straying. In the second part, Sardar Lekzaei recounts memories from the Sacred Defense operations. He describes the partial failure of forces during the Iraqi "mobile defense" operation and the capture of several Shirazi fighters in the Faghkeh region. Sardar Lekzaei also highlights the bravery and sacrifice of the combatants during the harsh summer conditions and their interactions with enemy patrols at the front lines.
