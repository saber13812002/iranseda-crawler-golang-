The radio program began with Quranic recitation and religious supplications before transitioning to a segment on public charity.
The host expressed gratitude for monthly donations from several individuals aimed at providing clothing and livelihood for needy families.
Listeners were encouraged to support orphans and underprivileged families in the name of Hazrat Masoumeh (SA) by sending text messages to a specific number.
The broadcast continued with the recitation of Adhkar and the Ziyarat of Al-Yasin.
The program concluded by introducing the 20th specialized conference for Quran reciters and memorizers.
The main topic of this conference is the requirements for innovation in the recitation styles of Iranian Qaris.
Quranic researchers and experts are invited to submit their articles and ideas regarding this topic by mid-September.
Those interested can visit the official website of the Supreme Council of the Quran for more information.
