The radio program began with the theme of Muharram and mourning for Imam Hussein (PBUH), inviting listeners to register their names via text message.
During the discussion, the importance of avoiding superstitions in mourning rituals and remembering the suffering of Hazrat Zainab (SA) as Islam's first victim were emphasized.
The guest, Khuzestan poet Hujjat-ol-Islam Kafshiri, recited his Hussaini poems while offering condolences for the martyrdom of Ayatollah Raisi.
The recited poetry focused on themes of love, sacrifice, and resistance against oppression, paying tribute to the martyred brothers and the oppressed people of Iran.
A lengthy recitation of the "Dua-e-Ahd" (Prayer of Covenant) followed, affirming the authority of the Ahl al-Bayt (PBUH) and their ultimate victory.
The program concluded with a broadcast of an old speech highlighting the cultural and social plots of enemies aimed at destroying the youth and creating apathy.
The speaker stressed the necessity of proper youth education in universities and the awakening of Islamic nations against global powers.
The program ended with prayers for the appearance of Imam Mahdi (AJ) and the end of tyranny.
