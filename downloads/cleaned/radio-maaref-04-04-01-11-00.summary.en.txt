The news segment reported on Operation "Sincere Promise 3," involving missile strikes on occupied territories, and a US military attack on three Iranian nuclear sites. Iran's Foreign Ministry condemned the US aggression, and its UN representative requested an emergency Security Council meeting. In the interview, Ustad Mohammad Shahroudi explained that the companions of Imam Hussein did not feel pain due to their divine love and perception of absolute beauty, not a lack of nerves. Hojjat-ol-Islam Hosseini Qomi emphasized the critical role of Velayat (Guardianship) in national cohesion and warned against enemy attempts to undermine it. He also praised the public's extensive financial solidarity and support, identifying it as Iran's most powerful weapon against sanctions and crises. Finally, the Ziyarat of Imam Hussein was highlighted as a recommended act that yields a thousand obligatory deeds, noting the global impact of Muharram rituals on spreading religious teachings.
