Fatemeh Abaqasmi, the first female president of a sports federation in Iranian history, recounts the establishment of the Physical Fitness and Aerobics Federation in 2004 and the successful elections held in 2005. Despite initial support from the Physical Education Organization, she faced neglect and a lack of resources after a change in the organization's leadership, forcing her to start operations with only an appointment letter and personal funds. Relying on high motivation and her husband's support, she managed to upgrade the scientific level of the sport by attracting sponsors and hiring foreign coaches, leading national teams to world championships. After serving as president for five years and winning re-election with a full vote, she encountered unprofessional decisions and sudden structural changes during Hamid Sajjadi's tenure, which resulted in her losing the presidency again. This experience highlights the structural and occasional political challenges in Iranian sports management that hinder successful progress. Abaqasmi expresses her full readiness to manage troubled federations, emphasizing the necessity of having competent and professional individuals in managerial positions. She concludes by stressing the importance of sports for public health, particularly for women, and the need for continuous support from society and sports officials.
