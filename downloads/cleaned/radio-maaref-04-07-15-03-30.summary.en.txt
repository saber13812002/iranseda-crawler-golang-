In the "Andisheh Aftab" program, Professor Baqer Sahabi explored the mystical and philosophical thoughts of Mulla Sadra's predecessor, Mir Damad, and their influence on Imam Khomeini's works. Mir Damad established the Transcendent Theosophy school in Isfahan, introducing unique concepts such as "dehri occurrence" that received special attention from Imam Khomeini. Unlike Mulla Sadra, who remained silent on this matter, Imam Khomeini utilized Mir Damad's principles in Quranic exegesis and theological discussions, even expanding upon his classifications. Professor Sahabi emphasized that the lack of deep understanding of Mir Damad's works represents a significant intellectual loss, noting that Imam Khomeini could have provided a treasure trove of knowledge by commenting on them. In the second segment, the "Zamzam Ahkam" program addressed various jurisprudential questions from followers of different marjas. Topics included the rulings on helping a brother, eating and drinking in a parent's house, the conditions for leading prayer for someone with missed prayers, and the ruling on doubt between the second and third rakats. Answers were provided based on the fatwas of marjas such as Imam Khomeini, Ayatollah Khamenei, and Ayatollah Sistani.
