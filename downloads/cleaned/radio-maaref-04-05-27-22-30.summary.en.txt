In this radio program, fulfilling the fundamental human need for love is presented as the primary prerequisite for mental management and the cultivation of inquiry. The speaker emphasizes that while family affection is important, the ultimate and most powerful path is the heartfelt belief in the unconditional love of the Prophet and the Imam of the Time (AJ) for believers. This deep spiritual awareness frees the mind from anxiety, enhances focus and creativity, and eliminates the need to seek emotional validation through improper channels. The Quran is also introduced as a book expressing God's love for His servants, and listening to it can reinforce this sense of being loved within the individual. Citing religious narrations, the speaker demonstrates that God and the divine saints love believers even more than they love themselves, making this divine love a safe haven against life's hardships. Finally, it is recommended that by focusing on this divine love, one can maintain a calm and powerful mind where other needs are easily managed.
