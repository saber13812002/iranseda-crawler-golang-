This radio program discusses the Hadith of Kisa and the Verse of Purification, emphasizing the exclusive status of the Prophet, Ali, Fatima, Hasan, and Husayn. The speaker cites authentic narrations to demonstrate that the Prophet (PBUH) publicly declared their purity and special status by covering them with a cloak and pointing to the sky. It is noted that this event occurred repeatedly in various locations, including the homes of Umm Salma and Hazrat Zahra (SA). The discussion also critiques scholars who dismiss spiritual narrations, stressing the importance of caution in jurisprudential judgments and the fallacy of using the absence of evidence as proof of non-existence. Finally, the program highlights the power of faith in the Islamic community compared to material forces, urging the people of Iran to focus on investment for production and unity in the year 1404.
