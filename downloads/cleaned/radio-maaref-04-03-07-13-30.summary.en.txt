The radio program "Narrative of Love" featured Rahimeh Hashemi, the mother of martyr Ahmad Makian, who shared her poignant memories of her son's life and martyrdom. Despite her mother's concerns, Ahmad Makian obtained his parents' consent and joined the defense of shrines in Syria after completing training courses. The mother described her prophetic dream and the physical tremors she experienced upon receiving the tragic news, which was delivered by her son's father. Following Ahmad's martyrdom during a siege in Aleppo, his mother firmly opposed burying him in Abadan, insisting on his transfer to Section 31 of Behesht-e Mohsen cemetery. In the final segment of the program, a discussion took place regarding the urgent need for officials and families to address the issue of youth marriage and break harmful traditional barriers. This conversation not only reflected a mother's grief but also emphasized the importance of preserving religious and social values in society.
