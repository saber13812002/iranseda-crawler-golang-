In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Falah examines the primary causes of divine anger based on the Holy Quran. He identifies moral deviations as the main reason for God's wrath, listing factors such as straying from the straight path, denying monotheistic truths, argumentation, envy, and lying. Citing the Surah Al-A'raf, he describes worshipping other than God and practical lying as a deviation from monotheism that leads to wandering in both this world and the hereafter. Furthermore, referencing the Surah Ta-Ha, he highlights consuming forbidden items and misusing God's pure blessings as another cause of divine anger. He emphasizes that converting halal into haram and exceeding religious and rational limits result in a person's descent into hell. The core message of the program is to avoid moral vices and maintain purity in utilizing divine provisions to escape divine punishment.
