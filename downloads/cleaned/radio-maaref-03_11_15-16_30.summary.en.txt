This radio program addresses parenting challenges, emphasizing the importance of maintaining trust and a healthy connection between parents and children.
Parents are advised not to treat their children as opponents in a game, as humiliation and blame can drive kids away and break the bond.
For teenagers, persuasion should be achieved through dialogue, body language, and sometimes the help of third parties rather than direct orders.
For young children, repetitive verbal warnings are ineffective; instead, parents should use visual aids, positive reinforcement, and collaborative rule-setting.
The program also interprets verse 271 of Surah Al-Baqarah, highlighting that public charity encourages others while private charity preserves the dignity of the needy and forgives minor sins.
Ultimately, the focus should be on God's awareness of our actions rather than seeking recognition from people, as divine satisfaction is the primary goal.
