The program begins by recounting the resilience of Dezful's residents during the heavy bombardments of the Iran-Iraq war, highlighting their use of ancient underground shelters for survival. It then details Iran's military transformation in the missile sector, tracing the journey from purchasing missiles from Libya to reverse-engineering them and successfully launching the Shahab-1 at Baghdad. A significant portion of the discussion features the memoirs of Abdollah Rajabi, a freedom fighter who endured eight years of captivity, describing the harsh conditions in Ramadi camps and the courageous encounter between the late Shahid Aboutrabi and Iraqi commanders. Rajabi also shares the daily violence faced by prisoners and the overwhelming joy of their liberation following Iraq's invasion of Kuwait and the acceptance of UN Resolution 598. The episode concludes with a recommendation of the book "Ashgham Kon" about the life of Shahid Amir Hagh Amini, emphasizing the enduring legacy of the freedom fighters' patience and bravery.
