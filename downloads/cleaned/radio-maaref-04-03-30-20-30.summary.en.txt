In this radio program, experts condemned the military actions of the Zionist regime against Iran and Palestine by referencing international law and Islamic jurisprudence. Dr. Ghaemi emphasized that while full compliance with humanitarian law in war is difficult, even a 5% adherence is vital for saving civilian lives, and violations carry significant international legal responsibilities. He argued that Israel's claim of "self-defense" lacks legal validity because Palestine is under occupation, and preemptive defense is unjustified without proof of an imminent threat. Dr. Tashkari, relying on the Islamic chapter of Jihad, highlighted principles such as the distinction between combatants and non-combatants and the prohibition of unnecessary suffering. Islamic jurisprudence mandated the protection of groups like children, women, the elderly, and clergy in warfare long before international documents did. Both experts stressed that attacks on non-military sites, such as hospitals and health centers, violate not only Islamic principles but also the Geneva Conventions. Ultimately, these violations will pave the way for future prosecutions and the legal condemnation of the Zionist regime on the international stage.
