In this radio program, Professor Mohammadi affirmed the existence of the Barzakh world and the punishment of the grave based on Quranic verses and narrations, describing the Barzakh body as having form but no physical mass. He addressed a listener's question by explaining that complete faith consists of three pillars: verbal declaration, heart belief, and practical commitment, noting that a believer who lacks the third is termed a "fasiq believer." The discussion then covered the transfer of rewards from Fatiha and prayers to the deceased, clarifying that Barzakh sustenance is of a different nature and is provided by God through the actions of the living. Furthermore, feeling upset upon witnessing sins like extravagance or immodesty was identified as a mandatory level of enjoining good and forbidding evil, though it should not turn into harmful distress. Finally, the program emphasized the parents' duty to protect their families from hellfire and to use logical, compassionate educational methods rather than harsh confrontations to guide their children.
