This radio program began by explaining the rulings and order of the night prayer, noting that reciting specific surahs and performing Qunut is recommended for greater reward. It was clarified that using various supplications in the Qunut of the night prayer is generally permissible. The program also addressed the rules of Wudu, stating that assistance is only allowed if the individual is physically unable to perform the actions themselves; otherwise, the Wudu is invalid. Regarding the expiation for missed fasts, it was specified that payment must be made after a full year has passed and before the next Ramadan. The conditions for selecting a home base for prayer and fasting while traveling, as well as the ruling on grandparents using the meat from the Aqiqah, were also discussed. Furthermore, the reasons for Lady Fatima Masumeh's (SA) migration from Medina to Qom, including Abbasid pressure and a letter from Imam Reza (AS), were detailed. The program concluded with congratulations for the anniversary of the marriage of Imam Ali (AS) and Lady Zahra (SA) and the recitation of the Holy Quran.
