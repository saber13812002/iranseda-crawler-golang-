This text recounts the memories and events of the Iran-Iraq War, focusing on Sardar Ali Tajalli. It describes a critical moment during the siege of Susangard where Tajalli, facing severe ammunition shortages and his brother's injury, demonstrates immense bravery and sacrifice. His wife, Ansieh, expresses her admiration for his character and his willingness to seek martyrdom, referencing an interview where he spoke of being ready to buy paradise. The latter part of the text reviews Tajalli's extensive military career in various operations, including Fath al-Mubeen and Kheibar. It also mentions the martyrdom of his brother, Mehdi, during the Wolf Light operation and Ali's efforts to retrieve his body. Ultimately, Tajalli participated in the Badr operation as an anonymous fighter and was martyred in 1984. The text concludes by emphasizing the crucial role of the people in preserving the Islamic system and the importance of Friday prayers as a tool for public presence and service.
