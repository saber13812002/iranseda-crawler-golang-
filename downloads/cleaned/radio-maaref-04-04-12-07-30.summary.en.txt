This radio program expresses solidarity with the families of martyrs from the Iran-Iraq war and highlights the tragic death of a two-month-old infant named Rayan Ghasemian in Gaza. The host criticizes the occupiers and the United States for their disregard of human rights, claiming that a spy who revealed the infant's location to the enemy has been arrested. The discussion also addresses the claim by US officials that Iran is sanctioned due to fears of the Mahdi's appearance and a final war against the West. Furthermore, humanitarian aid distribution in Gaza is described as a trap designed to kill civilians by the Zionist regime. The program concludes with a call for resistance against oppression and hope for the Mahdi's arrival to avenge the oppressed.
