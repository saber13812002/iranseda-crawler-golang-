In this radio program, the physical challenges and pain associated with stretching exercises in gymnastics for children were discussed.
The guest explained that traditional leg-opening methods often caused distress, leading children to want to quit the sport.
The introduction of new methods, such as PNF techniques, has made it possible to perform these movements painlessly and comfortably.
The primary goal of these innovative approaches is to achieve full leg extension (180 degrees) without causing injury or discomfort to young athletes.
Subsequently, a broader discussion took place regarding the importance of receiving constructive feedback and correcting mistakes in the learning process.
The program concluded with the playback of various musical pieces and an introduction of Radio Varzesh as a specialized sports media outlet.
The host and guest bid farewell to the audience, wishing them happy days.
This conversation highlights the shift from rigid methods to safer and more scientific approaches in children's physical education.
