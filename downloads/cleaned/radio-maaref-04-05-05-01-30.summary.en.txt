This radio segment narrates the heroic events of the Day of Ashura and the steadfastness of Imam Hussein's (PBUH) companions. It begins with the Imam's attempt to perform the noon prayer amidst enemy fire, highlighting the martyrdom of Sa'id ibn Abdullah and Muslim ibn Awsaja, who served as human shields with absolute loyalty. The narrative also recounts the self-sacrifice of an elderly man, Urwah al-Ghafari, who insisted on fighting despite his age, and a young boy who entered the battlefield following his mother's orders after his father's martyrdom. The program then transitions to the story from the book "Nishan-e Hassan," depicting the domestic life and anxieties within the Karbala camp, particularly Nafila's concern for her children during the water crisis. Upon seeing her son Qasim's injuries and sensing the impending danger, Nafila encourages him to remain steadfast in the face of divine trials and to stay by Imam Hussein's side. This dialogue illustrates the families of the Ahl al-Bayt's readiness for sacrifice and acceptance of a bitter fate in the path of truth. Finally, Qasim, recalling the martyrdom of his father Imam Hassan (PBUH), resolves to defend his uncle with unwavering determination.
