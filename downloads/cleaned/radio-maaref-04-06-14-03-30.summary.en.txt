This radio program addressed various religious questions based on the rulings of Islamic scholars. Key topics included the prohibition of looking at women's hair via video calls and the requirement for women to cover their entire body during prayer, with specific caution regarding the face and hair. Regarding the marriage of a fatherless girl, it was clarified that she decides for herself if she has discernment, otherwise her legal guardian must give permission. Recording someone's voice or image without explicit consent is generally impermissible, except in public spaces where everyone is visible. In congregational prayer, followers are advised to recite the Takbir immediately after the imam to maximize the spiritual rewards. The broadcast concluded with Quranic recitation and morning supplications.
