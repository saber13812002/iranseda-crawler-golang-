The program begins by examining the life of Abu Dharr al-Ghifari, one of the earliest Muslims and a dedicated companion of the Prophet, who was exiled and martyred for his unwavering loyalty to Ali and his protests against Uthman's economic corruption.
The second section focuses on Qanbar, a symbol of absolute devotion to Imam Ali, who served four Imams and was later executed by Hujjaj ibn Yusuf for his Shia faith.
In response to a listener's question, it is explained that the Prophet initially faced Jerusalem during prayer to distinguish genuine obedience from tribal bias.
The change of the Qibla to the Kaaba in the second year of Hijra served as a divine test to evaluate the sincerity of the Muslim community.
This shift highlighted the Jewish attempts to undermine the Muslims and ultimately solidified the independent Islamic identity by returning to the original direction.
The lives of Abu Dharr and Qanbar stand as historical examples of steadfastness against tyranny and sincere service to the Ahl al-Bayt.
