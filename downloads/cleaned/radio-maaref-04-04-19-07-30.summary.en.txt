This radio program discussed the concept of heroism and the enduring role of martyrs in history and daily life. The host emphasized that martyrs remain spiritually alive and serve as inspiring models for advancing national and religious goals. Among the heroes, the Supreme Leader was highlighted as one of the most frequently mentioned figures in the minds of the people and in global campaigns. His presence and support, particularly during the early days of the imposed war, were described as a source of hope that restored the public's morale. The system of Velayat-e Faqih was also evaluated as a divine blessing and a guarantee of the system's survival against various challenges. The program concluded by urging the public to support these heroes by upholding the Velayat-e Faqih. Finally, the time and location of the commemoration ceremony for Martyr Izzadi (Hajj Ramezan) at the Prophet's Mosque in Tehran were announced.
