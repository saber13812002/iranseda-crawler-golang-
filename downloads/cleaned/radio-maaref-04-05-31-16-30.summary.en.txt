In this radio program, Professor Jafari refutes the hagiographic narrative of the Prophet's presence at the Battle of Badr, emphasizing his active participation and bravery on the battlefield. Citing reports from Imam Ali, he explains that while the Prophet approached the enemy during critical moments, he refrained from killing individuals directly to prevent tribal enmity and maintain social cohesion. The discussion then shifts to the linguistics of the Quran, analyzing its Semitic roots and the influence of Nabataean and Aramaic scripts on its early writing. Responding to a listener's question, the professor counters the claim that Sunni scholars ignored the event of Karbala by citing historical works by prominent Sunni figures such as al-Tabari and al-Khwarizmi. He also introduces the "Mقتل al-Hasan" software, which compiles reliable sources from both Shia and Sunni traditions. The program concludes with the rain prayer from the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya and a call for energy conservation. Finally, an announcement was made inviting the public to submit documents related to the Sacred Defense via an online system.
