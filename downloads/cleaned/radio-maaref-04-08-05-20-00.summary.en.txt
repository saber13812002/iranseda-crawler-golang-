On the seventeenth day of the Gaza ceasefire agreement, two Palestinians were killed in a drone strike on Khan Younis, while twelve Israeli soldiers were injured in a road accident. Despite Israel's acceptance of Trump's plan, Tel Aviv insisted on maintaining full security control over Gaza, and Washington intends to launch the second phase before all bodies are returned. Meanwhile, widespread protests in Germany, Norway, France, South Korea, Sweden, and Malaysia condemned US support for Israel and called for severing diplomatic ties. A hacker group claimed to have accessed confidential information regarding Israel's defense systems and arms deals. The return of Palestinian martyrs' bodies reignited the issue of organ theft by Israel, which is considered a gross violation of human rights and international conventions. In other news, a handwritten Quran manuscript was unveiled in Algeria, and academic conferences on Islamic unity were held. The Asian Civilisations Museum in Singapore was highlighted as a hub for Islamic art, and construction of a mosque in France began after a 25-year wait.
