The program begins by introducing Dr. Hassan Akbari, a traditional medicine specialist, who discusses the benefits of nettle for lowering blood sugar and improving cellular metabolism in diabetic patients. He emphasizes that unlike chemical drugs which only lower blood sugar, medicinal plants address the root cause of the disease by correcting the body's cold temperament and increasing cellular energy. In the second segment, Hujjat al-Islam Rasoul Hafez interprets verse 58 of Surah Yusuf to explain the concept of forgiveness and forbearance in the face of revenge. He references the story of Joseph and his brothers, advising listeners to respond to injustice with kindness to achieve spiritual peace and avoid harboring grudges. Finally, a message from the Supreme Leader is broadcast, stressing the necessity of unity among the army, IRGC, Basij, and the people to maintain national security and counter enemy plots. These segments collectively focus on the importance of physical and mental health, as well as social cohesion within the framework of Islamic values.
