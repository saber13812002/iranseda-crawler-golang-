This program features Ayatollah Javadi Amoli's interpretation of Surah Al-An'am, highlighting the role of seventy thousand angels in escorting students of knowledge. The commentator refutes the idea that humans can be sovereign over themselves, explaining that God's mercy stems from His benevolent nature rather than any internal compulsion. In analyzing the phrase "La ilaha illa Allah," he argues that it is not two separate propositions but rather a denial of polytheism and an affirmation of God's guardianship based on innate human inclination. The term "Fattir" is interpreted as the one who brings existence into being from the darkness of non-existence, demonstrating God's absolute power to unravel the knots of creation. Furthermore, the phrase "He provides for them and is not provided for" signifies that God meets the needs of all beings without being needy Himself. The Quran's primary purpose is to purify the human soul from polytheism and establish divine authority, rather than proving God's existence, which is considered a given. Finally, the fear of punishment and the longing for paradise are presented as the spiritual capital of believers, which, through lamentation and seeking forgiveness, keeps Satan at bay.
