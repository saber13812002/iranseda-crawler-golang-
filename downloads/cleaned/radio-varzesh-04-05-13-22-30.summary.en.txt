Ali Reza Bahadori, a veteran Iranian volleyball setter, discussed the challenges of his career in a radio interview. He expressed frustration over a financial dispute with Paykan club and a coach's personal decision to exclude him from the national team during his peak years. Bahadori believes he was unjustly sidelined for seven to eight years due to non-technical reasons, a grievance he still finds unforgivable. Despite these past conflicts, he emphasizes his commitment to professional ethics and maintaining calm relationships with clubs. The 47-year-old Paykan player intends to continue playing professionally until the age of 50, provided his physical condition allows it. He also reflected on his pride in playing for top-tier teams and his consistent presence in the Iranian league.
