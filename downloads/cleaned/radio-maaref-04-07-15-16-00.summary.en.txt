In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Mohammad Sharoudi addressed various theological and social questions from listeners. He first explained the nature of the barzakh body, describing it as an ethereal form free from physical mass and the constraints of time and space, allowing for interaction with other souls. Regarding the conflict in Palestine, he rejected the comparison to Imam Hasan's peace treaty, asserting that the ultimate goal is preserving truth and that resistance is currently a religious duty against oppression. He emphasized that remaining silent in the face of tyranny makes the silent party equally accountable as the oppressor in the afterlife. The scholar also deemed praying for death as reprehensible and identified public indecency as a continuous sin that poses a political and security threat to the Islamic system in the current context. Finally, he highlighted that keeping promises is a fundamental principle of faith, warning that breaking oaths is a grave sin with unknown consequences in the Hereafter.
