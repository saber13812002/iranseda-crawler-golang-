The radio program "Andisheha" explored the efficacy of jurisprudence in both individual and social spheres. Dr. Mohammad Javad Arasta, citing Imam Khomeini, defined jurisprudence as a genuine and complete tool for managing human life and society from cradle to grave. Two opposing viewpoints were presented: one arguing that jurisprudence must be combined with ethics to be sufficient, and another claiming it is limited to minimum requirements and incapable of optimal social management. Critics supported their stance by highlighting the lack of preventive measures in jurisprudence and the minimalistic nature of marital interactions. In response, Dr. Arasta introduced the concept of "social jurisprudence" as a complement to individual jurisprudence, identifying citizens, society, and the state as distinct legal entities. This social approach addresses the dynamic nature of social issues and aims to regulate collective life within Sharia frameworks. The program concluded that integrating individual and social jurisprudence creates the comprehensive framework necessary for effective societal management in the modern era.
