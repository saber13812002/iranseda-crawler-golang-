This radio program examined the jurisprudential rulings regarding women's sports from an Islamic perspective.
The guests affirmed the desirability of sports and physical strength for women based on Quranic verses and narrations.
A key point of discussion was adhering to Islamic hijab principles in sports environments without compromising physical activity.
It was recommended that sports officials design appropriate attire to serve as a Sharia-compliant model for other Muslim countries.
The jurisprudential rule prohibiting assistance in sin was cited as the reason for not competing against Israeli athletes.
The rule regarding the impossibility of fulfilling contract terms was analyzed in the context of injured players' wages.
Restrictions on men watching women's sports were explained with an emphasis on avoiding lust and sexual arousal.
Ultimately, it was emphasized that Islamic jurisprudence ensures both physical health and the preservation of religious values in sports.
