The radio program "Andisheha," featuring Hojjat al-Islam Borujkar, explored the rational and theological foundations of the Guardianship of the Jurist. The discussion distinguished between cosmic and legislative guardianship, emphasizing that the latter, which involves lawmaking and societal management, originally belongs to God. Theological arguments assert that God's role as the Creator implies ownership and, consequently, the right to govern and legislate for His creation. Since no human possesses the inherent right to rule over another or even themselves, this authority is exclusive to God, who delegates it to the Prophet, the Infallible Imams, and ultimately to a qualified jurist during the occultation. The primary objective of this structure is to implement divine laws to achieve human material and spiritual perfection. Finally, the program highlighted the undeniable authenticity of the Event of Ghadir, citing numerous narrations and Imam Ali's sermons as definitive proof of his designated authority.
