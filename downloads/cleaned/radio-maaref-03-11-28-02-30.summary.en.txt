The radio program first addressed the Quran's two responses to the accusation of fabrication: a challenge to produce a similar text and a warning against the severe consequences of insulting the divine. It then examined Imam Ali's (PBUH) approach to preserving the integrity of Islam, referencing Sermons 74 and 240 of Nahj al-Balagha. Despite asserting his rightful claim to succession, the Imam refrained from public protest to prevent division and maintain the unity of the Muslim community. This strategic silence and occasional retreat were interpreted not as weakness, but as a wise method to defend the core of the religion against existential threats. The speaker emphasized that defending the truth does not always require conflict or debate; sometimes, apparent cooperation with falsehood or silence is necessary for the survival of the Islamic system. This approach highlights that Imam Ali's (PBUH) primary priority was safeguarding the foundation of Islam and preventing the collapse of the Muslim society.
