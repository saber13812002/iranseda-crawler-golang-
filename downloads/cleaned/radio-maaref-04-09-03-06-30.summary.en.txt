The first section interprets verses from Surah Al-An'am, explaining that humans sincerely seek God's help during hardships but often forget to express gratitude after being saved. God is described as the savior from both visible darknesses and hidden calamities that humans are unaware of. The program then transitions to mourning the martyrdom of Hazrat Fatimah (SA), reciting ziyarat texts that emphasize her suffering and innocence. Dr. Ahmad Ghalehali subsequently outlines the characteristics of a good mother, including deep faith, effective family relationship management, overflowing affection, and the transmission of culture and values. He cites the story of Moses' mother to illustrate how strong faith enables mothers to trust God even in the most difficult circumstances, such as parting with their child. It is also noted that good mothers must balance their self-sacrifice for their children with attending to their own personal and spiritual needs. Finally, the speaker emphasizes that mothers serve as the primary bridge for cultural transmission, playing a decisive role in shaping their children's personalities and beliefs.
