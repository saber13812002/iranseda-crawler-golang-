In this Radio Ma'aref program, Hojjat al-Islam Hosseini Qomi explores "compassion" as a defining trait of prophets and the Ahl al-Bayt. Citing Quranic verses, he illustrates that prophets consistently sought guidance and mercy for their enemies, avoiding hasty retaliation. The story of Prophet Abraham, who argued with God to spare the people of Lot, exemplifies this profound compassion. Similarly, Imam Ali's decision to delay battle in Siffin and Jamal for months was aimed at providing opportunities for the conversion of his opponents. Imam Hussein also rejected the suggestion to fight at Karbala, adhering to the principle of not initiating war and prioritizing spiritual guidance. This approach demonstrates that God's saints prioritize the salvation of souls over immediate victory, even in the most dire circumstances. The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) emphasized that guiding a single person is more valuable than the rising of the sun.
