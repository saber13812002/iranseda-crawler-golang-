In this radio broadcast, following an introduction to a Quranic exegesis by Ayatollah Javadi Amoli, the program features a segment emphasizing Islamic etiquette and the specific respect owed to the Sayyids. The speaker recounts anecdotes from Hajj Tiareh and Ayatollah Nia Javad Tehrani to illustrate how revered scholars maintained kindness and patience even when faced with disrespect. The latter part of the text presents a literary narrative set in the camp of Imam Hussein (PBUH), focusing on a character named Qasem, the son of Hassan. In this story, an enemy messenger named Hafez arrives for negotiations, prompting Qasem to boldly declare his noble lineage and his preference for an honorable death over compromising with Yazid. The scene concludes with Imam Hussein (PBUH) in his tent and repeated questions from Imam Abbas regarding the reasons for their journey to Kufa, evoking the solemn atmosphere of the nights of Karbala.
