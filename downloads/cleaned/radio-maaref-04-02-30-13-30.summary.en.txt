In this radio program, Professor Seyed Ahmad Faghihi emphasized the importance of spiritual focus and facing the Qibla during prayer as a recommended etiquette. He addressed listener questions by explaining that apparent contradictions among ethical scholars stem from differences in temperament and audience needs, urging adherence to definitive Islamic principles. To overcome fear and loneliness at night, he recommended controlling the imagination through dhikr and worship. In the final segment, interpreting the Quranic verse about those killed in the path of God, he declared the victims of the presidential plane crash to be alive in service to the nation. He described the tragedy as a significant loss for the country while highlighting the virtue and faith of the deceased. The program concluded with a call for deeper connection with God, moral improvement, and a positive perspective on martyrs who served the people.
