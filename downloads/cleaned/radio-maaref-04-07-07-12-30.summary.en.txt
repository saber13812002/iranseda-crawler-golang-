Hojjat al-Islam Sharifati Tarbar discussed the harms of religious observance in the program "Bar Bal-e Sakhn," relying on the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya. He emphasized that true religiosity must be accompanied by awareness, knowledge, and understanding to prevent blind imitation and ignorance. One of the most significant pitfalls of faith is "ujb," or pride in one's worship and religious rituals, which corrupts one's deeds. Imam Sajjad (PBUH) prayed in the Du'a Makarim al-Akhlaq for God to protect his worship from being ruined by such arrogance. This pride not only destroys the fundamental philosophy of worship, which is humility before God, but also hinders spiritual growth and development. Conversely, a sin followed by sincere repentance is considered more valuable to God than worship performed with arrogance. Therefore, a believer must always consider themselves indebted to God and avoid pride and self-satisfaction in their religious practices.
