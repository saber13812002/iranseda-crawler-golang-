In the first segment, the grandfather warns against the dangers of making unfair comparisons with others, which can lead to feelings of misery and subsequent negligence. Responding to a listener's question about a four-year-old using adult vocabulary, it was explained that this behavior often stems from seeking attention or reflects the family's linguistic environment rather than causing harm. It was recommended that religious and moral education during childhood should be conducted through play and entertainment to ensure children understand concepts without feeling coerced. The second part of the program focused on the life and ethical conduct of Ayatollah Sheikh Ali Mohammad Najafi Borujerdi, who exemplified practical religious action through humility and service to the underprivileged. By establishing the Afkhat charity fund and attending to people's needs day and night, he demonstrated that religious knowledge must be accompanied by action and empathy. This approach led to a rapid acceptance of him by the city's clergy and citizens, who embraced him as a spiritual and social reference despite initial differences.
