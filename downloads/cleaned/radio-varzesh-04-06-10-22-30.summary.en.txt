The guest recalls his youth and winning the Tehran championship with the Paykan team. He discusses simultaneous offers from Esteghlal and Persepolis, as well as university offers with financial support. Ultimately, he joins the Pas team due to financial constraints and their offer of free university education and military service. He describes the strict military environment and Pas's matches in the Jam-e Takht-e Jamshid, including his participation in the national team and the Asian Games in Thailand. The guest emphasizes Pas's role as a moral school that fostered humanity and sportsmanship alongside athletic success. He expresses regret over Pas's absence from Iranian football and highlights the club's legacy in training prominent athletes and coaches. The program concludes with appreciation for the army's contribution to the country's sports development and the production team's credits.
