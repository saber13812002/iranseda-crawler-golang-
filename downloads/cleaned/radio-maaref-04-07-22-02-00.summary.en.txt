In the radio program "Hekayat-e Azadegi," Hojjatoleslam Nadem emphasized the importance of considering the consequences of one's actions, citing the hadith "When you intend, reflect." He highlighted the conscious choice of Iranian POWs to endure severe torture by the Ba'ath regime, leading to their ultimate victory and dignity. In the second segment, Hojjatoleslam Hosseini Qomi interpreted narrations from the Book of Kafi regarding asceticism and piety. He clarified that criticizing the world does not mean abandoning work, but rather avoiding material attachment. The four main benefits of piety were identified as divine honor, spiritual power, sustenance, and freedom from worldly anxieties. These spiritual blessings are fundamentally different from transient worldly honors and are rooted in reliance on God. The program concluded with the recitation of the 14th supplication from the Sahifa al-Sajjadiya and religious poetry.
