In this interview, Seyed Mansour Daryabari, the nephew of poet and sportsman Seyed Ali Akbar Daryabari, shared memories of his uncle's social and athletic activities in the Charestan neighborhood during the revolution. He recounted how Daryabari organized local football grounds and even invited prominent figures like Hassan Habibi to coach a family team. The uncle was also noted for his poetry, which often celebrated national heroes and the local community. Daryabari later discussed the decline of grassroots football in Tehran, attributing it to the commercialization of land and a lack of non-monetary motivation. He emphasized the importance of using local coaches who better understand the players' body language and cultural context. While sports like wrestling and boxing have seen significant progress, football has yet to reach its ideal global standing. The conversation concluded with a mention of successful local sports initiatives in the village of Voshtan within the Firuzkuh region.
