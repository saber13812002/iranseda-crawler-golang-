In this radio program, the Night of Power is compared to the greatness of Hazrat Zahra (SA), emphasizing that a connection with her is the key to understanding this night.
A narration from Imam Sadiq (AS) is cited, stating that knowing Fatima (SA) is equivalent to grasping the essence of the Night of Power.
A wartime anecdote is shared where a soldier, stuck in a minefield, found a safe path after seeking help from Hazrat Zahra (SA).
This story illustrates that relying on Hazrat Zahra (SA) can resolve seemingly impossible problems.
The speaker advises listeners to align their lifestyle with the teachings of Hazrat Zahra (SA) to receive the blessings of the Night of Power.
The second part of the program addressed Quranic questions, reading a verse from Surah Ar-Rum regarding divine guidance and misguidance.
The program concluded with the prayer for the reappearance of the Imam and blessings upon Muhammad and his family.
