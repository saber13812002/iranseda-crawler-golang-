The radio program began by honoring a charity that organizes pilgrimage caravans for patients and the disabled to Karbala, followed by an announcement for next week's special episode on Khuzestan.
Guest Seyyed Soleymani emphasized that reviving the Eid of Ghadir is a religious obligation and a spiritual investment that serves as the key to understanding God's straight path.
He highlighted the importance of giving gifts and creating joy on this day, sharing a personal story to illustrate how this tradition should be passed down to future generations.
The session's spiritual atmosphere was enriched by the recitation of passionate poetry by Iranian poet Seyyed Mohammad Babamiri in praise of Imam Ali and the Ahl al-Bayt.
The program featured the audio clip "My Heart Wants Karbala," which vividly depicted the emotional connection between pilgrims and the holy shrine.
In the concluding segment, the discussion addressed the fundamental differences between Islamic philosophy and pre-Islamic thought, highlighting the Quran's comprehensive role in human education.
The guest noted that the Islamic Sharia differs significantly from previous laws in its approach to propaganda and its struggle against oppressive powers.
Finally, the importance of deeply understanding the Imam's words and following the path of perfection in the contemporary era was underscored.
