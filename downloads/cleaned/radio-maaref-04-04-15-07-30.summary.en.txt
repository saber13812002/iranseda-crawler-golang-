The radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat" begins by introducing the ritual ceremonies of Yazd, such as palm carrying and kettle beating, which symbolize global devotion to Imam Hussein (PBUH). Hujjat al-Islam Mohammad Hadi Falahati analyzes the concept of "infak" (charity) in the comprehensive conduct of Imam Hussein (PBUH), describing it as the highest degree of divine generosity. He uses the metaphor of Imam Hussein (PBUH) as a flowing spring that erupts every Muharram to quench the spiritual thirst of human souls. Falahati emphasizes that entering the tent of Hussein (PBUH) requires acquiring resemblance to the Ahl al-Bayt and their companions to benefit from this divine charity. The speaker then references narrations regarding the severe thirst and sacrifices of the Imam and his companions on the day of Ashura, including their efforts to provide water to their enemies. He highlights that the intensity of the Imam's thirst and injuries reached its peak before his martyrdom, underscoring the profound grief of this event. The program concludes by inviting listeners to benefit from the monotheistic and ethical teachings of the Imam.
