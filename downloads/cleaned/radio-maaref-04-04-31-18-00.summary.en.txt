In this episode of Radio Ma'aref, Hujjat al-Islam Fala and the host discussed Islamic ethics, focusing on the concepts of "stinginess" (bukhl) and "charity" (infak). Citing Quranic verses and narrations from the Infallibles, the guest identified stinginess as the root of spiritual decline and a barrier to the path of self-purification. A key point was the definition of stinginess in the realms of "speech" and "thought," where the worst form is withholding the learning and teaching of monotheistic knowledge and the teachings of the Ahl al-Bayt. The speaker emphasized that the highest form of charity is spiritual and human-building, which leads to the existential elevation of both the individual and others. While material charity is important, intellectual and educational charity holds a superior position. Quoting narrations from the Prophet (PBUH) and Imam Sajjad (AS), he stated that seeking true knowledge is a path to paradise and earning the forgiveness of the heavens and earth. Ultimately, intellectual stinginess was described as blocking the divine miracle and hindering human evolution.
