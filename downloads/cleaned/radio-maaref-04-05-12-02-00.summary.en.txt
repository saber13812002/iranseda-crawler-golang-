In the radio program "Parsaiyan," Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the necessity of seeking intercession through the Ahl al-Bayt, citing narrations that identify them as the divine means to salvation. He explained that true success and spiritual safety are achieved only by invoking God through the Prophet's family, while turning away from them leads to destruction. The discussion included a moving anecdote about Ayatollah Milani, who would recite the elegy of Imam Hussein and sanctify his own shroud with the tears of his devoted young followers. Milani confessed that this specific devotion to Imam Hussein was the only remedy for his spiritual ailments. Similarly, Ayatollah Mostafavi attributed his religious and academic success to his consistent participation in mourning gatherings and recitations. These accounts highlight that deep love for the Ahl al-Bayt and active engagement in their mourning rituals are the primary sources of divine grace and spiritual fulfillment. The program concluded by urging listeners to maintain this sincere attachment and intercession to remain true friends of the Prophet's family.
