In this program, the funeral procession of Imam Khamenei is presented as more than mere mourning; it is a preservation of Mahdism and a response to the human conscience. The presence at this event symbolizes a global protest against the Satanic order and Western civilization that led to the martyr's death. This occasion serves as a civilizational and national covenant, revitalizing Iran's authority and expressing gratitude for its leadership. Furthermore, the procession manifests religious brotherhood and social solidarity, transcending ethnic and class boundaries. The final segment emphasizes the necessity of propaganda and awareness to reduce the Imam of the Age's isolation. This process paves the way for the appearance of the Mahdi and the victory of the truth over falsehood in human history.
