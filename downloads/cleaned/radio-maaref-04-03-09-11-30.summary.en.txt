In this radio program, Ayatollah Najm al-Din Tabasi emphasized the immense significance of the Day of Ghadir Khumm, highlighting the Prophet's unprecedented joy upon announcing the guardianship of Imam Ali. He referenced Imam Ali's historic sermon that day to underscore the necessity of reviving the culture of Ghadir among Muslims, particularly Shia communities. Drawing from the practices of Imam Reza, the scholar encouraged people to celebrate the day with grandeur by hosting feasts, giving gifts, and offering free services to their neighbors. He also cited Imam Hadi's specific prayer for Ghadir to affirm Ali's status as the "straight path" and the "strong religion of God," countering historical distortions by Umayyad and Abbasid rulers. The program concluded with a call for listeners to actively participate in Ghadir celebrations and charity campaigns by contacting the provided SMS numbers and visiting the "Ghadirim" website. Ultimately, the broadcast aimed to deepen the emotional connection of families and youth to the concept of Wilayat and the awaited Imam through these spiritual and social observances.
