In this program, Dr. Bahmani analyzed the strategic directives of the Supreme Leader in his first statement following his martyrdom. He emphasized Iran's geographical significance as the heart of the world and the Strait of Hormuz as a key power lever, criticizing past negligence in utilizing these assets. The core of the discussion was the strategy of "balance of terror," which includes a decisive military response, control of the strait, and expanding resistance fronts globally. This approach aims to increase the enemy's costs exponentially by fostering independence movements in the Global South. The speaker described this statement as a testament to the Leader's artistry in crisis management and deterrence. Subsequently, recitations and translations of verses from Surah Ghafir were broadcast. The program also announced academic and Quranic conferences centered on the martyred Leader's ideology and new media. It concluded with an invitation for listeners to follow upcoming programs and submit articles.
