In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Fala examines the vice of lying and its indicators based on Quranic verses and the narrations of the Ahl al-Bayt. He explains that liars often fabricate lies in the name of God and the Prophet to deceive others and gain material benefits or status. Citing Nahj al-Balagha, he warns of a future era where falsehood and lies will dominate, leaving the truth in seclusion. The speaker emphasizes that the lifespan of a lie is very short because falsehood is inherently transient, much like the worldly life it serves. By referencing the story of Hazrat Zahra and Imam Ali, he illustrates that when truth is oppressed, the field is taken over by the ego and Satan. Finally, he advises listeners to rely on official legal verification rather than blind trust in oaths during financial transactions to avoid being deceived.
