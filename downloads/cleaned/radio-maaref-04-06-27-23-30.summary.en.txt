This radio program began by honoring the memory of Mahmoud Farshchian, a renowned Iranian painter, highlighting his unique position in both art and religious devotion. His masterpieces, particularly the "Ashura" painting, were presented as symbols of the deep connection between art, divine concepts, and love for Imam Hussein. The guests emphasized the spiritual impact of connecting to Imam Hussein on human elevation and the global influence of Iranian-Islamic culture. A spiritual story about entering Paradise through the "Gate of Hussein" on Judgment Day was shared with the audience. In the final segment, fourteen winners of cash prizes for the Arbaeen commemoration were selected and announced from listener messages. The program concluded with prayers for Friday night visits and expressions of solidarity with the lovers of Hussein.
