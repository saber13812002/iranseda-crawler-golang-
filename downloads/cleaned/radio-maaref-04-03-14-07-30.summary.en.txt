The first segment of the program highlighted the spiritual life of Imam Khomeini, emphasizing his unwavering devotion to prayer and remembrance even in his final moments.
The show then introduced Martyr Mostafa Raddani-Pour, discussing how his deep love for the Ahl al-Bayt played a pivotal role in his jihad and the training of fighters.
In the final section, Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallah explored the virtue of humility and its necessity in the relationship between students and teachers.
Citing religious narrations, the professor described humility as the primary factor for intellectual growth, comparing it to water that nourishes the field of the mind.
He stressed the importance of avoiding arrogance and hypocrisy in educational settings, noting that the blessing of knowledge lies in respecting the teacher's status.
Ultimately, humility before truth and knowledge was presented as the key to achieving wisdom and divine guidance.
The program concluded with prayers for the martyrs and a tribute to the memory of Imam Khomeini.
