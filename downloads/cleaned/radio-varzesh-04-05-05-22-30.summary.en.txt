Ali Gharadashi, the president of the Iranian Table Tennis Federation, recounted his first foreign trip to North Korea, where he observed the high technical level of athletes despite their limited facilities. He facilitated the arrival of five North Korean players and coaches to train with the national team, a move that significantly boosted the performance of Iranian athletes. This initiative also marked the beginning of foreign player participation in domestic leagues, which had previously been nonexistent and helped enhance overall competition. After serving as the federation secretary for a decade, Gharadashi was elected president in 2014 with a high vote count and is currently in his tenth year of leadership. Although he holds a position on the World Table Tennis executive board, he has stated he does not intend to run for a fourth term, preferring to allow a new generation of capable managers to take over. Gharadashi believes that despite ongoing sanctions and financial challenges, the federation can achieve Olympic medals in the near future by relying on young talent and strategic planning.
