In this program, Professor Hassan Rahimpour emphasized the necessity of national awakening against enemy conspiracies and rumors while analyzing various models of police and security. He introduced two ineffective models: first, the impersonal and buyable police in liberal and anarchist systems where security is tied to wealth and power; and second, the executioner police in repressive regimes that enforce systematic and unjust oppression. In both models, justice and mercy are absent, and citizens' rights are measured by their wealth or power. In contrast, the Islamic model of policing is based on organized justice, service to the people, kindness to citizens, and firmness against criminals. Citing the teachings of the Prophet and Imam Ali, he stressed that security must be defined under justice and ethics, not as a tool for domination or fear. He also warned that public distrust in security and judicial institutions could lead to social collapse and mutual destruction among people. Finally, he called for a faster transition toward the Islamic model to establish true and lasting security in society.
