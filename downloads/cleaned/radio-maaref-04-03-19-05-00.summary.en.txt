The program defines death as the separation of the soul from the body rather than total annihilation.
Imam Ali (AS) emphasized that the soul's value surpasses the entire world, and Islam prescribes specific rites for the moment of death.
During the dying process, the soul becomes freer and witnesses events such as seeing angels, Satan, and the Ahl al-Bayt (AS).
Imam Reza (AS) categorized people into those who find peace through death and those whose death brings relief to society.
Renewing faith in God and the Wilayah is highlighted as the key to achieving tranquility and meeting angels at the time of death.
It was also stressed that a person will be gathered in the Hereafter with those they loved in this life.
The latter part of the broadcast addressed a jurisprudential discussion regarding inheritance in cases of simultaneous death.
Finally, listeners were invited to participate in upcoming segments by sending text messages.
