In the rulings section, pilgrims were advised to engage in dhikr and supplication for all believers and the Islamic world in Arafat and Muzdalifah.
Fiqh questions were addressed regarding prostration on iron ore, the correct method of tayammum with a single strike, and the conditions for buying and selling women's hair.
The ruling on substitute prayer for individuals with OCD and the permissibility of tayammum instead of ghusl for sinusitis patients were also discussed.
Financially, it was clarified that survivor pensions are not subject to Khums according to Imam Khomeini, whereas retirement benefits are.
The second segment emphasized the importance of moderation in worship and avoiding satanic obsessions.
Stories of Imam Khomeini and Ayatollah Golpaygani interacting with OCD patients to treat them and prevent exacerbation were shared.
Finally, historical events following the protests against Uthman and the summoning of governors to Medina were mentioned.
Malik Ashtar, as Sa'id ibn As's successor in Kufa, delivered an inflammatory sermon to prevent Sa'id's re-entry into the city.
