This radio program discusses the critical importance of teaching financial literacy to children and adolescents in both schools and families.
The guest emphasizes that parents must transparently share the process of managing household economics so that children understand financial realities.
To instill these skills, it is suggested that small financial responsibilities, such as grocery shopping or managing daily expenses, be assigned to teenagers.
By creating practical challenges, such as cooking at home instead of dining out, children can experience creativity and learn the value of saving.
In the second part, the concept of the "child as king" during the first seven years of life is interpreted, advocating for rewards over punishment.
Parents should respect the child's identity and build self-confidence by making them feel valued, while avoiding verbal destruction.
Finally, the cleric discusses the virtue of patience (Sabr) in the Quran and explains its various levels.
He identifies patience against sins and inner desires as the highest form of patience, stressing the need for self-improvement to encourage others.
