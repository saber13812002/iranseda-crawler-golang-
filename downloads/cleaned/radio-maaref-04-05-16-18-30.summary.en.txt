This radio program discussed the Islamic rulings regarding the Arbaeen pilgrimage and the visitation of Imam Hussein (PBUH).
A key topic was the priority of paying debts versus traveling, clarifying that bank loans or time-bound debts do not prevent the journey.
Payment of a debt becomes obligatory and takes precedence over the recommended visit only if the creditor demands it at the due date.
It was also explained that the Arbaeen prayer can be recited in any location with proper intention, not just within the shrine.
In the Q&A section, it was stated that pregnant or breastfeeding women who missed fasts must pay kaffarah and make up the fasts when able.
The validity of congregational prayer with additional dhikr after the Surah Al-Fatiha was confirmed as permissible.
Finally, using mosque endowments for non-religious activities like sports classes depends on the endower's permission and the trustee's approval.
The program concluded by inviting listeners to join a public campaign to host pilgrims and provided contact numbers for submitting religious questions.
