This lecture, based on verses from Surah Al-Baqarah and Al-Ahzab, emphasizes the necessity of unconditional, practical companionship with the Prophet and the Imams.
The Quran defines this steadfast companionship as the primary criterion for "righteous deeds," where hardships, hunger, and fatigue are considered acts of worship with divine rewards.
In this perspective, piety means absolute adherence to the Imam and the truthful ones, prohibiting any deviation due to fear of enemies or worldly difficulties.
Referencing the event of Karbala and Imam Hussein's (PBUH) steadfastness, the speaker illustrates that loyalty to the divine covenant, even under oppression, is the highest rank of faith.
He warns that on the long path of waiting, one must guard against Satan's traps and worldly distractions by keeping one's focus on God and the Imam.
Following the truthful ones, meaning the Infallible Imams and the Imam of the Time (AJ), is the only path to achieving certain victory and building a historical community.
Ultimately, the speaker stresses that no excuse for separating from the Imam is acceptable, and perseverance on this path is the key to salvation and divine pleasure.
