In this radio program, experts emphasized that Iran cannot remain neutral due to its sensitive geographic location in the heart of Asia and its long history of foreign incursions. They argued that Iran must be an active agent to ensure its security, drawing lessons from the instability caused by neutrality in 1941. The Islamic Republic's defensive strategy is based on creating regional allies and turning crises into opportunities, a concept designed by the Supreme Leader and Qasem Soleimani. Involvement in regional crises such as Iraq and Syria not only prevented enemy infiltration but also enhanced Iran's soft and hard power by defeating groups like ISIS and James Mattis. Furthermore, support for the Axis of Resistance in Gaza, Lebanon, and Yemen was presented as an effective deterrent against threats from the US and the Zionist regime. Finally, it was stressed that Iran has successfully consolidated its strategic position on the global stage by leveraging its military and political capabilities.
