During the 80th UN General Assembly, the Iranian president criticized the UN's silence on Israeli crimes and emphasized the need to strengthen multilateralism against US unilateralism. Concurrently, a $25 billion agreement was signed between Iranian and Russian companies to construct four third-generation nuclear power plants. In Lebanon, citizens gathered in Beirut to mark the first anniversary of Hassan Nasrallah's martyrdom, reaffirming their commitment to the path of resistance. International reports highlighted a 14% increase in settler raids on Al-Aqsa Mosque and the killing of 1,600 aid workers in Gaza over the past two years. Donald Trump's speech at the UN, which included harsh attacks on Europe, provoked strong backlash from European leaders and media. The UN Human Rights Council also condemned Israeli actions in Gaza as genocide, calling for immediate international intervention. On a cultural note, the Stockholm Community Mosque celebrated its 25th anniversary, highlighting the active social and cultural role of Muslims in Swedish society.
