This radio program discusses the significance of visiting Imam Reza (AS), highlighting its effects such as the purification of sins and the deepening of love for the Ahl al-Bayt.
The most crucial etiquette of pilgrimage is the "sense of presence" of the Imam, which requires maintaining decorum, speaking softly, and standing with one's back to the Qibla to face the Imam.
The guest cites narrations demonstrating that the prophethood of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) was foretold in the Torah and Gospel by Moses, Jesus, and David.
In the Q&A section, the difference between Khidr's (AS) killing of a boy and the Prophet's (PBUH) forgiveness of Abu Sufyan is analyzed.
It is explained that Khidr acted based on divine knowledge and cosmic wisdom, whereas the Prophet's duty was to implement Sharia and show mercy.
This distinction illustrates two levels of divine wisdom: the legislative level for the general public and the cosmic level reserved for God's special saints.
Finally, it is emphasized that pilgrimage must be performed with pure intention and a deep understanding of the Imam's spiritual status to achieve its intended spiritual effects.
