This radio program features two distinct segments focusing on literary and historical narratives. The first section introduces the book "The Forgotten" by Davoud Ghararzadeh, which presents a narrative from the perspective of Zaid Qasid, the messenger of Walid ibn Uthbah. This account vividly depicts the tense and fearful atmosphere surrounding the assassination of Imam Ali (AS). The second segment shares the memoirs of Elham Heydari, the wife of Martyr Sardar Hassan Tehrani Moghaddam. She recounts the details of their courtship and marriage ceremony, which was notably officiated by Imam Khomeini. Heydari also describes the early years of their marriage amidst the Iran-Iraq War and the devastating missile attacks by Saddam Hussein. She highlights the secrecy involved in transporting missiles and her constant anxiety regarding her husband's safety at the front lines. Finally, she reflects on the birth of their daughter Zainab on the first night of Muharram and expresses gratitude for her husband's presence at home compared to other martyrs.
