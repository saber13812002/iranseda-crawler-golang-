The radio program Zamzam Ahkam addressed various jurisprudential questions from listeners. Followers of Imam Khomeini were reminded that kindness to parents is obligatory even if they are unkind, and that the eldest son must perform the missed prayers of his deceased father. Ayatollah Sistani's ruling was cited, stating that voluntary prayers require separate intentions and cannot be combined. The conditions for a valid marriage contract were discussed, clarifying that a man is not liable for a reduced dowry if the woman agreed to it willingly. It was explained that attending Friday prayer only invalidates the Dhuhr prayer if the distance is less than one farsakh and attendance is feasible. In the Sermashe program, Professor Mohtasheyi defined wisdom as unassailable knowledge that revives the heart, identifying the Ahl al-Bayt as its primary sources. He emphasized the importance of not concealing wisdom and understanding it deeply. Finally, Ayatollah Khatami interpreted Surah Yunus, describing prudence as planning to avert punishment and faith as a belief that must be based on free will and choice.
