Following US and Israeli military strikes on Iran, the IRGC launched a wave of missile and drone attacks targeting occupied territories and US military bases in the region. The conflict resulted in the martyrdom and injury of dozens of students at a school in Minab, which officials described as the most heartbreaking recent war crime. Iranian authorities vowed to deliver harsher responses to the aggressors, while citizens across various cities held widespread rallies to support the armed forces. Concurrently, Hamas and Yemen's Supreme Political Council condemned the strikes, characterizing them as an aggression against the entire region. In the UK, reports indicated a significant rise in Islamophobic violence against Muslims and their religious sites. In the program's final segment, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interpreted verses from Surah Al-An'am, emphasizing the principle that divine punishment does not precede the establishment of proof and highlighting the role of reason in distinguishing good from evil.
