In this radio program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verses from Surah Al-Ma'ad to distinguish between the theological concept of divine punishment and forgiveness in the afterlife and their legal implications in this world. He emphasizes that a judicial system is ineffective without codified, specialized laws, which must be drafted based on Islamic principles with the consultation of experts. The necessity of a jurist possessing all qualifications (Faqih-e-Jami' al-Shartayn) to ensure laws align with divine commands, along with the need for universities to train specialists, is discussed. The program also addresses the issue of the jurist's authority in multiple countries, noting that unity of leadership is required if there is international interaction among Muslim nations. The limits of the leader's authority as defined in the constitution and the supervisory role of the Assembly of Experts in appointing or dismissing the leader are explained. Finally, the ownership of national resources and public wealth (Anfal), which belongs to the Imam, is presented as the fundamental basis for the jurist's authority. This argument demonstrates that the doctrine of the jurist's authority is rooted in Quranic and Hadith evidence rather than being merely a political or discretionary preference.
