In the "Persaman Tarbiyati" program, Dr. Heydari emphasized the importance of transforming teenagers from passive consumers into active content creators in the digital space.
Key strategies for achieving this include fostering critical thinking and encouraging children to participate in producing simple, creative content.
Responding to listeners' questions, the root causes of anger and childlessness among employed young adults were identified as financial pressure, unrealistic expectations, and environmental challenges.
Parents were advised to listen to their children's concerns in a safe and intimate setting rather than offering direct lectures or advice.
For teenagers facing inappropriate parental behavior, maintaining calm, managing their personal image, and avoiding direct confrontation were recommended.
Regarding sibling conflicts, the advice was to list and prioritize issues, then address them through logical dialogue and problem-solving.
The importance of leveraging a brother's positive traits to build a brotherly relationship was highlighted, with professional counseling suggested for severe cases.
Finally, for harmful behaviors that go beyond joking, rational parental intervention and referral to specialized counseling were deemed necessary.
