In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Tusi examines the narration of "Qirqisiya" as one of the signs of the Mahdi's appearance. Relying on geographical and historical sources, he locates this battle in northwestern Iraq along the Euphrates River, distinguishing it from the biblical Battle of Armageddon in northern Palestine. Contrary to some writers who describe this conflict as a global war involving billions, the analysis suggests that Qirqisiya is a regional yet devastating battle where the Banu Qays tribe is massacred without any subsequent blood vengeance. This narration is recorded in authentic Shia sources such as al-Kafi and Bihar al-Anwar, with Allama Majlisi confirming its good status. The key difference between this narration and the return of the Abbasids is that Qirqisiya is a specific historical and geographical event occurring before the appearance. Therefore, this battle is not a global nuclear war but an unprecedented slaughter in a specific area of Iraq that sets the stage for the Mahdi's emergence.
