The radio program begins by distinguishing between constructive sorrow and fear that drive a person toward God and worship, and destructive anxieties that hinder daily life. It cites Islamic narrations describing the world as a prison for believers and a paradise for disbelievers, urging believers to avoid worldly pleasures. The main discussion focuses on Imam Ali's (PBUH) lack of desire for the caliphate, referencing the Nahj al-Balagha. In his Shiqshiqiyya sermon, Imam Ali (PBUH) initially advised people to seek leadership elsewhere, criticizing the society for its deviation and confusion. However, the people's intense rush to pledge allegiance to him was so overwhelming that his shoes fell off and the sick were carried on others' shoulders to meet him. The program concludes by highlighting Imam Ali's (PBUH) unique status and unwavering adherence to the Prophet's (PBUH) moral example, which established the legitimacy of his authority.
