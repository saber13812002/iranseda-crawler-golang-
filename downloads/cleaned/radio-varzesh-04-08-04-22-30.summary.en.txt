Ardalan Ashtiani shared his memories of playing alongside Iran's football legends in a radio interview. He discussed his time at Persepolis under coach Begovic, noting that despite the team's star power, poor management led to disappointing results. Ashtiani also recounted his three-year stint at Persepolis and his subsequent move to Damash Dorud and the first division. He detailed the devastating ACL injury he suffered in a friendly match, which effectively ended his playing career prematurely. After multiple surgeries and a difficult return to the pitch, he transitioned into coaching. Ashtiani highlighted his coaching experiences with teams like Railway, Pas Qom, and the national youth team under Haidar Sharifi. He emphasized that recurring knee injuries still significantly impact his daily life.
