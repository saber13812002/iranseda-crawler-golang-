Ayatollah Tabasi recounts his early passion for clerical studies and the financial hardships he faced in Najaf. He describes his joy at becoming a mu'ammam as comparable to the excitement of a wedding night. The cleric emphasizes his consistent dedication to learning, noting that he never stopped studying or engaging in academic discussions, even during holidays. He also recalls the intense security pressures and fear of arrest under the Iraqi government, which eventually forced his family to migrate. In the latter part of the speech, Tabasi stresses the critical importance of unity between the clergy and the university sector. He argues that attempts to create a rift between these two groups serve the interests of foreign powers. He urges the public to remain vigilant against such plots and to continue supporting both the religious and academic communities.
