In the "Zamzam Ahkam" program, Hujjat al-Islam Shershbani, citing Imam Khomeini's fatwa, stated that socializing with families who neglect prayer is religiously permissible, though inviting them to pray is obligatory. Regarding dogs, keeping them in one's living space is deemed inappropriate due to cultural corruption and potential impurity, although having them in view for security is allowed. According to Ayatollah Sistani, if blood from a finger spreads to a mosque carpet, the carpet becomes impure and must be cleaned. Breastfeeding by a grandmother to her grandchild, if sufficient, creates a permanent prohibition of marriage between the child's parents. Artificial nail extensions invalidate wudu because they prevent water from reaching the nail bed and must be removed as soon as possible. The second segment, "Khaneh-ye Doost," discussed the life and martyrdom of Imamzadeh Soltan Ali during the Umayyad era and his resistance against tyranny. The program concluded by introducing twelve Imamzadehs in the village of Malat in Gilan and providing directions for their visitation.
