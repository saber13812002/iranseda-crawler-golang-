This radio program emphasizes the Quranic verse "Mercy to the World" to highlight the exalted status of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) in existence.
The speaker argues that God created the universe solely for the sake of the Prophet, as he is the source of mercy for all creation.
The primary purpose of human creation is the worship of God, and the Prophet serves as the divine teacher who instructs humanity in this devotion.
Without this celestial educator, the guidance of humans and the fulfillment of creation's goals would be impossible.
To illustrate the depth of this mercy, two historical narratives are shared regarding the Prophet's patience and forgiveness toward his tormentors in Mecca and a Jewish man in Medina.
These actions demonstrate that the Prophet used noble character and forgiveness to guide even his enemies toward faith and knowledge of God.
Finally, the audience is urged to emulate the Prophet's ethics to become his true followers and to pray for the relief of the Islamic world's problems.
