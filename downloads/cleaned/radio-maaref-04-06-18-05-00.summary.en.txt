The first segment emphasizes that internal justice and piety are prerequisites for the impact of one's words and actions.
Piety must become a habit that prevents a person from sinning, even in thought or decision-making.
Upon reaching this stage, Satan loses control over the believer's heart, and God governs their soul.
The second segment discusses the jurisprudential necessity of appointing a trustee to prevent the waste of endowment assets.
There are four main methods for appointing a trustee, including the endower themselves, a third party, a proxy, or a successor trustee.
If the endower fails to appoint a trustee, the religious authority must intervene to preserve the endowment.
Full authority remains with the endower until the endowment formula is recited, after which it becomes restricted.
