In this radio program, Professor Mohammadi Sharobi outlines the prerequisites for receiving divine support based on Quranic verses. He emphasizes that steadfastness in the path of Wilayat and religion is the primary condition for God's hidden assistance. A key factor is the believer's proper response to trials, which involves enduring hardships without complaint and understanding their hidden wisdom for spiritual purification. Adherence to Sharia, patience in adversity, and rejecting tyranny are identified as essential pillars for gaining divine favor. Additionally, prayer, intercession, and fasting during Muharram are recommended as spiritual tools to strengthen the soul and receive divine blessings. Finally, avoiding discord and maintaining national and religious unity are presented as the ultimate keys to achieving victory and the reappearance of Imam Mahdi (aj).
