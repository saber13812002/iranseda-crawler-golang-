In the radio program "Quranic Q&A," Professor Saleh Maleki addressed listeners' questions regarding the coherence of verses in Surah Al-Mumtahanah. He explained that the first verse establishes the general rule of not befriending the enemies of God and His Messenger, while subsequent verses, particularly those referencing Abraham and his companions, illustrate the practical application of this rule under pressure. The discussion emphasized that emotional attachment to enemies, even for the sake of family safety, is a form of misguidance, and one must prioritize the Hereafter and God's pleasure. In the second part, the Quranic distinction between "People of the Book" and "infidels" was clarified, noting that those who fight Muslims or expel them from their lands are considered enemies, whereas those who do not engage in such actions should be treated with justice and kindness. Finally, the interpretation of verse 15 from Surah Al-Hajj was presented, using the metaphor of hanging oneself to satirically depict the frustration and envy of those who deny the Prophet's victory.
