In this radio program, Dr. Mohammad Malekzadeh analyzed the reasons for the Islamic Revolution's survival and its current challenges. He summarized the system's longevity into three factors: the people's faith and insight, divine leadership based on the Guardianship of the Jurist, and the discourse of justice and independence. Malekzadeh emphasized that contrary to enemies' predictions of collapse, Iran has remained resilient and even stronger despite sanctions and wars. In the second section, he interpreted the shift toward cooperation with the East not as dependency, but as a strategic necessity to break the US dollar monopoly and counter sanctions. The expert described relations with China and Russia as based on long-term shared interests, consistent with the "neither East nor West" slogan. He also characterized the conflicts in Sudan as a proxy war by Israel and the UAE against Sudan's official government and the Resistance Front. Finally, he attributed the transformation of some former politicians into opposition figures to a lack of insight and worldly desires.
