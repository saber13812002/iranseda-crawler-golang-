Radio Maarif highlighted the vital role of youth in building cultural and artistic infrastructure, praising the significant advancements in literature and arts since the revolution. The broadcast also criticized recent remarks by the US President, reaffirming Iran's steadfastness against threats and its refusal to compromise national dignity. News was shared regarding the 8th International Congress of Quranic Thoughts, focusing on the role of women and family in reducing social harms, scheduled to be held in Qom. Extensive special programs were announced to commemorate the martyrdom of Ayatollah Raisi and his companions, including a public gathering in Tehran and international legal initiatives. On the occasion of Ferdowsi Day, the Shahnameh was celebrated as a cornerstone of Iranian cultural identity and a testament to the Persian language. The ninth day of the Tehran International Book Fair is underway, featuring high sales figures and diverse cultural events. In a separate segment, the Supreme Leader emphasized the importance of cultivating a culture of altruism and humanity among future generations through the stories of war medics. He contrasted the selfless actions of Iranian medics with the barbaric bombing of hospitals by enemies, urging Iran to globally promote these humanitarian models.
