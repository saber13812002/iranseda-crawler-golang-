Based on Sermon 149 of Nahj al-Balagha, the program emphasizes Imam Ali’s (AS) directive to refrain from blaming or criticizing others for their mistakes. The core message is that one’s own moral and material integrity requires gratitude, which is best expressed by supporting those who have stumbled rather than condemning them. The speaker shares anecdotes from prisons and religious settings to warn that anyone who criticizes others for their sins will inevitably face similar afflictions themselves. By highlighting the concept of divine concealment, the speaker urges the audience to protect the dignity of others and avoid exposing their faults. Constructive criticism must be delivered without destruction or humiliation to prevent falling into the trap of fault-finding. Ultimately, respecting privacy and avoiding the public dissemination of others' errors are presented as essential signs of true faith and piety.
