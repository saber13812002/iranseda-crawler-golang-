The program begins by emphasizing the intimate, fatherly relationship between the Imams and believers, defining love for the Ahl al-Bayt as the essence of religion.
Citing a narration from Imam Baqir, it explains that walking in the path of pilgrimage out of pure love is the highest spiritual rank, transcending the calculation of rewards and punishments.
The key message is the shift in a pilgrim's motivation from fear of hell to pure love, which breathes new life into worship acts like prayer and fasting.
It also highlights the belief in the spiritual companionship of all lovers of the Imams, from Adam to the Day of Judgment, including those who could not physically attend.
The second part of the program features a discussion with Hujjat al-Islam Vahid Bor on the jurisprudential rules during the Arbaeen pilgrimage.
He stresses the necessity of respecting others' rights and the principle of "Sabqah" regarding maintaining one's place in lines and designated areas.
According to this jurisprudential view, encroaching on others' rights or cutting in line, even if it leads to performing worship, is religiously flawed.
Therefore, etiquette and respecting public rights are essential conditions for the validity and acceptance of worship during this spiritual journey.
