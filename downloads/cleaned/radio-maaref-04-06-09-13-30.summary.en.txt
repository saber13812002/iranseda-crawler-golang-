In this discussion, West Asia expert Sadr al-Hosseini emphasized that regional conflicts are determined by social cohesion and public awakening rather than merely by missiles or fighter jets. He described the recent US and Israeli pressure to disarm Hezbollah as part of a simultaneous plot to weaken the Axis of Resistance in Lebanon, Yemen, and Syria. Hosseini argued that disarming Hezbollah would lead to the loss of Lebanon's territorial integrity and allow for Israeli military incursions into the country. Despite initial pressure following a diplomatic visit, the intensity of these demands has decreased due to the on-the-ground realities and widespread popular support for Hezbollah. He also distinguished the symbolic actions of the Lebanese army in Palestinian camps from Hezbollah's essential deterrent weapons. Regarding Syria, Hosseini identified the current ruling faction as a product of the Zionist regime and the US, warning that they are advancing separatist agendas in the south. Finally, he criticized Western civilization as discredited and lacking moral credibility due to its double standards regarding human rights and civil liberties.
