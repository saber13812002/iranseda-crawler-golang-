The broadcast began with greetings for the arrival of the month of Rabi al-Awal and honors for the martyrs of the government. The news segment reported on the President's renewal of his pledge to the revolution's ideals and his inspection of construction projects at the Imam Reza Shrine. It also highlighted the arrival of over five million pilgrims in Mashhad and the Ministry of Education's announcement of 147 new schools. The core discussion focused on the Supreme Leader's speech, which emphasized the spiritual benefits of Imam Reza's forced journey to Khorasan and the expansion of the Ahl al-Bayt school of thought. By referencing the concept of love for Imam Hussein, the Leader explained how Karbala shaped the Shia identity and their historical struggle against tyranny. In analyzing current affairs, the hostility of the United States toward Iran was interpreted not as a reaction to slogans, but as a desire for Iran's subordination. Finally, the steadfast resistance of the Iranian people against foreign plots and the enemy's attempts to create a rift between the government and the public was identified as the key factor in preserving national dignity.
