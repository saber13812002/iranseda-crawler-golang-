In this radio program, the famous marshmallow experiment in psychology was cited to emphasize the importance of mental management and avoiding focus on fears and negative news. Relying on Quranic verses and the narrations of Imam Ali (AS), the speaker advised that in critical times and war, one should rely on God's power and ultimate victory rather than focusing on the enemy's threats. He considered the martyrdom of officials and martyrs as a great capital for the revolution's credibility and strengthening the nation's morale, urging people to maintain their peace through trust in God. The role of media in creating anxiety was also mentioned, with a suggestion to receive news only from reliable and divine sources. In the final section, while congratulating the days of Muharram, the story of Abdullah ibn Hasan's martyrdom was referenced, and the program transitioned to discussing child upbringing and the impact of media on families.
