In this program, Hujjat al-Islam Mohammadi clarified that qada prayers for the deceased must be recited aloud and that performing the morning sunnah prayer before the adhan is generally impermissible. The program also stated that buying or selling loan quotas is religiously forbidden, although receiving a fee for attending the bank to sign documents is allowed. Regarding forgotten hired prayers, listeners were advised to perform them with the intention of the person who paid, even if the deceased's name is unknown. It was further explained that planting trees for Hajj is invalid if the individual lacks the financial means, and consuming alcohol is considered a major sin and impure. Living with an alcoholic is permissible only if one attempts to dissuade them from the habit without causing domestic conflict. Fiqh experts noted that while the areas for wiping during wudu must be dry, the face and hands do not need to be dried before washing. Finally, the program emphasized the spiritual peace and divine protection associated with performing prayers on time, particularly at the moment of death.
