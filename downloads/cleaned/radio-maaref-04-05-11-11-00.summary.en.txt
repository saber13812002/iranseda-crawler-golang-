The radio program began by reporting on the official opening of a border terminal and the Shahid Ali Hashemi clinic in Khuzestan, facilitated by the governor and the Ahvaz University of Medical Sciences. The main focus then shifted to the commemoration of Ayatollah Sheikh Fazlallah Nuri, a prominent cleric of the Constitutional Revolution, on the anniversary of his martyrdom. Sheikh Nuri stood courageously against colonialist influences and Westernized intellectuals, emphasizing the legitimacy of Islamic constitutionalism. British agents, having deceived some religious leaders and sown discord, managed to secure his execution. However, history has shown that his political Islamic ideology ultimately crystallized into the Islamic Revolution. The broadcast concluded with Quranic recitations and religious poetry honoring this courageous scholar.
