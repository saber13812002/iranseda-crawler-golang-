In this radio program, Professor Yousefzadeh addressed three key family-related questions. First, regarding dissatisfaction with a spouse's online presence, he advised expressing the need for more attention clearly and framing counseling as a review of the relationship rather than an individual flaw. Second, for a retiree suffering from her husband's financial control, he emphasized the importance of shared and consensual financial management, urging that a portion of the income be placed under the woman's authority. To combat a spouse's suspicion, he recommended building trust through transparency and avoiding provocative jokes. The program also announced an upcoming literary symposium honoring the poet Khaghani Shervani at the Institute of Persian Language and Literature. Finally, the show concluded with a poem by Rumi and a statement by Imam Khomeini on the necessity of deeply understanding religious teachings.
