The radio program "Zamzam Ahkam" began by discussing the Islamic rulings regarding government-subsidized currency for the Arbaeen pilgrimage. It was emphasized that receiving the subsidy with the condition of traveling and then failing to go is religiously and legally prohibited, requiring the currency to be returned to the bank. Conversely, retaining unused currency after a completed trip or using it for future trips is permissible if the initial condition was met. The program then addressed the Arbaeen ziyara, noting that while visiting Imam Hussein's shrine is highly recommended, performing it in other parts of Karbala is sufficient due to crowd safety concerns. A ruling from Ayatollah Khamenei clarified that a wife needs her husband's permission to travel only if he is present and able to grant it. The permanent mahram status of a sister to the children of her cousin's husband was confirmed, provided the marriage was valid. Micro-pigmentation was deemed not to invalidate wudu or ghusl if it does not prevent water from reaching the skin. Finally, it was stated that renting out a cybercafe for illegal activities is impermissible if the landlord is aware of the intended use, rendering the rent haram.
