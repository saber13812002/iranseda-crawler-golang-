In the radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat," Professor Mohammad Hadi Falah discussed the concept of divine anger and its causes as presented in the Holy Quran. He categorized the reasons for God's wrath into three main groups: theological deviations, moral corruptions, and behavioral misconduct. Theologically, disbelief, polytheism, and hypocrisy were identified as primary triggers for divine anger. Morally, sins such as swearing by God, envy, lying, and arguing with the Prophet and the Ahl al-Bayt were highlighted as significant factors. Behaviorally, deviating from the straight path, transgressing divine limits, terrorizing prophets and believers, and worshipping entities other than God were listed as causes of wrath. Citing the Quranic phrase "other than those who have earned Your anger," the professor emphasized that God becomes angry with those who consciously deviate from the Sharia and the straight path. He described religion as the shortest and most direct route to God, warning that deviation from it is a deliberate and erroneous choice. Finally, listeners were encouraged to reflect on Quranic verses to understand these causes and remain safe from divine punishment by adhering to the righteous path.
