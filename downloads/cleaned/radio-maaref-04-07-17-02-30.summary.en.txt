In the radio program "Persoman Enghelab," Dr. Mehdi Abutalabi addressed key political and legal challenges facing the system. Responding to a query about the lack of judicial prosecution for negligent officials, he explained that while parliamentary oversight tools like impeachment exist, defining executive decisions as crimes is complex and requires transparency from relevant institutions. The discussion then shifted to the potential Iranian withdrawal from the Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty (NPT). The expert highlighted the heavy oversight by the IAEA and the activation of the snapback mechanism, outlining both arguments for and against leaving the treaty. He further emphasized the critical importance of maintaining internal cohesion and national unity to withstand economic sanctions and military threats from adversaries. The program also featured a review of the book "Kashf-e Asrar," which provides a comprehensive analysis of Imam Khomeini's political thought. The broadcast concluded with an invitation for listeners to submit questions and a call for continued solidarity during these challenging times.
