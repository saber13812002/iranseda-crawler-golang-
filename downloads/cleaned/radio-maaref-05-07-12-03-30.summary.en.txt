In this radio program, three significant jurisprudential questions were addressed and answered. The first response emphasized that selecting a new marja must be done from among living clerics. Regarding the second question, abortion before four months of gestation is permissible if the fetus's presence causes extreme distress to the mother. However, after four months, abortion is only allowed if the mother's life is in danger, not merely due to distress. The third question concerned the validity of a permanent marriage contract, stating that the presence of two just witnesses is not mandatory for validity. Nevertheless, taking witnesses is recommended to prevent future legal issues. The program concluded by inviting listeners to submit more questions and providing contact details. Finally, a section of spiritual supplication and divine remembrance was broadcast for the audience's peace of mind.
