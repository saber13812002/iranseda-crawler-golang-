In the Parsaiyan program, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi cited narrations from the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) to identify the moral traits of those most similar to him. He emphasized that good character, gentleness with relatives and fellow believers, and controlling anger are key to emulating the Prophet's conduct. Conversely, traits such as foul language, shamelessness, envy, and arrogance distance a person from the Prophet and negate hope in their goodness. The speaker highlighted the efforts of Allameh Seyyed Mortaza Eskiri in mirroring the behavior of the Infallibles and his complete submission to their words. The deep devotion of Allameh Tabatabaei to the Ahl al-Bayt was also discussed, noting his unique practice of breaking his Ramadan fast by kissing the shrine of Fatima Masumeh (SA). Shahid Motahhari explained his admiration for Tabatabaei stemmed from his being a philosopher who was also passionately devoted to the Ahl al-Bayt. The segment concluded with a prayer for the audience to attain the opportunity of visiting holy shrines and focusing on divine light.
