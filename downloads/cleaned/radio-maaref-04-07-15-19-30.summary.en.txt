The radio program began with the recitation of a rain prayer from the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya before transitioning to a discussion on the vice of swearing and its remedies. The expert identified swearing as a major sin based on Quranic and hadith sources, highlighting its detrimental effects on both individuals and society. Proposed solutions included increasing awareness, controlling thoughts and emotions, and avoiding rationalizations for such behavior. Special emphasis was placed on maintaining a consistent religious appearance in all social spaces to avoid hypocrisy. Key strategies for overcoming this habit included remembering God, public repentance, and compensating for the rights of those wronged. The importance of prayer, anger management, and cultivating shame and modesty in moral self-building was also discussed. Finally, the program recommended abandoning the habit of foul language through consistent practice of eloquent speech and taking moral responsibility.
