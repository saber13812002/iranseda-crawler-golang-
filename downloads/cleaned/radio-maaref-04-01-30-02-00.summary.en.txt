In this program, Professor Maleki first addressed verse 22 of Surah Al-Imran, explaining that two groups of Muslims intended to retreat during the Battle of the Trench due to fear and the influence of hypocrites, but God restored their resolve. He then analyzed verses 56 and 57 of Surah Al-Isra, refuting the worship of entities other than God by clarifying that prophets and angels are themselves servants of God who serve as means of nearness (wasilah) rather than objects of worship. The speaker distinguished between worship and utilizing intermediaries, asserting that Shias worship God alone while using the Ahl al-Bayt as spiritual channels to draw closer to the Divine. The discussion also highlighted Ayatollah Borujerdi's firm resistance against the Pahlavi regime's attempt to replace the Persian script with the Latin alphabet, a move aimed at distancing society from Islamic culture. Additionally, the scholarly debates of Hujjat al-Islam Daei-El-Aslam with English clergymen to prove the truth of Islam were mentioned. The program concluded with a call to seek refuge in the Quran and adhere to divine commands to protect oneself from the deviations of the end times.
