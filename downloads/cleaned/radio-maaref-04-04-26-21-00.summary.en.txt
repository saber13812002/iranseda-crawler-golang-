The radio program "Parsaiyan" explores the conduct of religious scholars, emphasizing the virtues of contentment and detachment from material luxury. The broadcast highlights narrations from the lives of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) and Imam Ali (AS), illustrating their simplicity, ease, and lack of pretension in daily life. An anecdote about the Prophet accepting water instead of a meal during hunger serves as a practical example of this approach. The program also details the lifestyle of Ayatollah Mirza Javad Tabrizi, who insisted on using simple, old items despite receiving expensive gifts. By rejecting new clothes and pillows, he stressed that the world is transient and one's identity should not be defined by material appearances. The core message is that humans should find freedom from worldly entanglements through contentment and avoiding obsession with appearance.
