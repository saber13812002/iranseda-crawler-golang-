This radio program explores the concept of reviving the heart through sermons and contemplating death, based on Nahj al-Balagha.
Imam Ali (AS) describes human negligence and the sudden approach of death in a sermon that Ibn Adhi reportedly read over a thousand times for spiritual awakening.
Vivid descriptions of the dying process illustrate how false hopes and the eventual neglect of relatives leave a person alone in their final moments.
According to the hadith "As you live, so shall you die," the quality of one's life and actions determines the nature of their death.
The second part of the program addresses the jurisprudential issue of inheritance when two related individuals die simultaneously.
Mulla Muhammad Husayn al-Mahallati explains in Tahrir al-Wasilah that the heir must be alive at the exact moment of the deceased's death for inheritance to transfer.
If simultaneous death is proven and the order of death cannot be determined, no inheritance passes between them.
This jurisprudential discussion emphasizes the critical importance of accurately establishing the time of death to determine inheritance shares.
