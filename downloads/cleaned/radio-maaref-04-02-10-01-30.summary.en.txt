The radio program begins by highlighting the spiritual significance of the Shrine of Hazrat Masoumeh in Qom and its profound impact on major clerics.
It is explained that the shrine serves as a focal point for the Imam of the Age, causing many great scholars to remain in the shadow of its magnificence.
The show then addresses theological questions from listeners, starting with why God created humans with physical attractiveness.
The expert explains that the purpose of creation is a divine test, where women's modesty and men's avoidance of looking constitute the core of this trial.
Regarding the complaint of disbelievers about their existence in the afterlife, it is clarified that they regret their nature of disbelief, not their existence itself.
They wish they had the nature of animals or dust to escape eternal punishment, rather than being human beings who chose disbelief.
The program concludes with special prayers for health, martyrdom in the path of the Imam, and the resolution of social and personal issues.
