The program begins with a Quranic interpretation emphasizing that the remembrance of God restores spiritual sight and hearing.
The commentator explains that sins create a veil preventing humans from truly perceiving divine truths and the Prophet.
The host then encourages listeners to send blessings to the Imam, highlighting the peace and spiritual connection this practice brings.
The second part of the show celebrates National Veterinary Day while reviewing historical events that occurred on this date.
Key historical milestones mentioned include the first session of the National Consultative Assembly and the execution of Anwar Sadat.
The host engages the audience by asking about their favorite animal characters from old and new cartoons.
Listeners respond via text messages, sharing nostalgic memories of cartoons like "Watto" and "Billy."
The program concludes with music and a continued, friendly dialogue with the listeners.
