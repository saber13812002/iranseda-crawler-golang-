The radio program begins with an interpretation of verses 61 and 62 of Surah Al-An'am, emphasizing God's absolute authority over judgment and justice.
The commentator explains that any delay in judgment stems from human ignorance or the need for awareness, rather than divine inability.
The second part of the show is dedicated to International Men's Day and the anniversary of Amir Kabir's dismissal from the premiership.
It highlights Amir Kabir's services in preserving Iran's independence and his resistance against foreign interference.
The program also honors the martyrs of Operation Zolfaghar 4 and the defenders of the Sacred Defense.
Manhood is defined in this context by commitment, courage, and service to the homeland.
Listeners from across the country sent messages praising their fathers and real men.
The broadcast concludes with religious prayers and wishes for the health and success of honorable Iranian men.
