The radio program began by condemning the Zionist regime's aggression in Gaza and emphasizing the need to keep the Palestinian issue at the forefront of global news. Simultaneously, commemorative ceremonies for the Sacred Defense Day were held in martyrs' gardens nationwide, accompanied by the launch of the book "Underground Temple" regarding Operation Fath al-Mubin. Other news items included a significant cost reduction for Iranian pilgrims traveling to Karbala and the announcement of pro-Gaza rallies in three provinces. The Supreme Leader stressed the importance of Iran entering the top ten countries in artificial intelligence, while the Ministry of Science reported the country's 14th global ranking in this field. In the cultural segment, the book "Khosuf in Beirut" was unveiled on the first anniversary of Sayyid Hassan Nasrallah's martyrdom, and the "Hope for Mother" support card was announced to launch in 2026. Hujjat al-Islam Vahidi discussed generosity as a key prophetic virtue, citing stories of Imam Khomeini and Sheikh Ansari to illustrate the spiritual rewards of charity. Finally, the program warned against negotiating under threat, reaffirming Iran's resistance and strength against enemy pressures.
