The program began by emphasizing the importance of eloquence and avoiding harsh speech, using the story of Reza, who apologized to his apprentice after speaking rudely, as a model for self-control. Ayatollah Khatami outlined three key principles for improving speech: thinking before speaking, recognizing that words are actions, and acknowledging that all words are recorded for the Day of Judgment. The second part addressed jurisprudential questions, clarifying that changing the intention of a prayer from obligatory to make-up is permissible under specific conditions, while the reverse is not. It was also explained that the Friday prayer can substitute for the Dhuhr prayer, though Asr should still be prayed as a recommended precaution. Regarding marriage, while a contract with a heavy dowry is valid, religious traditions recommend a light dowry as a sign of the wife's virtue and blessing. Finally, the program provided rulings on the ritual bath for doctors handling un-washed corpses and the conditions for paying Khums on leftover money from the Arbaeen pilgrimage.
