In this radio program, the characteristics of a true worshipper were explained based on verses 24 and 25 of Surah Al-Ma'arij.
According to these verses, a genuine worshipper is one who, in addition to personal worship, pays special attention to the needy in society.
By paying zakat, khums, and charity, they do not ignore the deprived and maintain a material and spiritual connection with them.
Subsequently, the speech of Dr. Seyed Mohsen Mirbagheri was broadcast, which likely addressed these same religious and social topics.
The program concluded with the recitation of part of the Sajjadiyya prayer and a request for listeners to pray for the hosts.
Additionally, the Muhammad prayer and the Aman Zamani prayer were recited from the holy Jamkaran Mosque for health and the removal of societal calamities.
Finally, the program ended with the broadcast of poems and songs themed around the expectation of the Mahdi and hope.
