Seyyed Mohammad Mehdi Seyyed Salehi, a former striker for Esteghlal, Persepolis, and Tractor Sazi, recounts the ups and downs of his playing career in this interview. He discusses his grievances with the management of Sepahan and Esteghlal regarding a lack of respect for his captaincy, as well as severe ACL injuries sustained at the peak of his form. Seyyed Salehi emphasizes his loyalty to fans and his refusal to play for weaker teams solely for easy financial gain. He reflects on sports friendships that ended in indifference and the poor transfer decisions he made during the final years of his career. The player, who boasts accolades such as the Asian Golden Boot and league runner-up titles, is currently awaiting a suitable opportunity to manage a team after obtaining his coaching license. His greatest wish is for his son to succeed in football and to witness his son's future brilliance.
