This radio program redefines the concept of "Jihad" as persistent effort in all aspects of life, including education, politics, and community service, rather than just warfare.
The primary focus is on the factors and spiritual rewards of avoiding "Gheebah" (backbiting), which is presented as a social sin and a form of negative worship.
According to religious narrations, refraining from backbiting holds a value equal to or greater than ten thousand units of voluntary prayer, fostering practical monotheism and divine love.
A crucial point emphasized is the duty to defend a believer against backbiting in social gatherings, which is considered a social responsibility that ensures divine support in both this world and the hereafter.
Listeners participated by sending greetings to various regions of Iran, such as Gilan, Mazandaran, and Kurdistan, while sharing personal experiences related to community service and mutual aid.
The discussion concluded that avoiding backbiting and protecting others' reputations not only shield individuals from calamities but also contribute to building a healthy, virtue-oriented society.
