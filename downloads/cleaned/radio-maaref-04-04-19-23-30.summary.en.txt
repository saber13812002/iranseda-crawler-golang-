The radio program opened with a spiritual atmosphere during the Friday night of Muharram, featuring music and prayers that expressed the listeners' longing for Karbala. The guest poet, Mr. Sharafat, recited verses highlighting the fresh pain of the Husseini passion and the deep love for Imam Hussein (AS). In the interactive segment, listeners requested collective prayers for the sick and shared their personal intentions with the audience. Towards the end, Tehran residents were invited to attend the memorial ceremony for Sardar Mohammad Saeed Izadi at the Prophet Mosque in Shahid Mahallati. The show concluded with the recitation of Ziyarat Al-Yasin and the prayer for the relief of the Imam, followed by a segment featuring Imam Khomeini's speeches. In these remarks, the Imam emphasized the political and Islamic dimensions of the Karbala uprising and warned against sowing discord and satanic infiltration in society. He urged the people to remain vigilant against anti-Islamic propaganda and to maintain unity in the face of enemy plots.
