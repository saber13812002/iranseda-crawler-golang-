In the first segment, a military analyst argues that a preemptive strike is currently inadvisable as it would not prevent a future enemy attack but would instead provide them with a better justification.
Instead of initiating an attack, the focus should be on strengthening air defense and readiness to respond, thereby significantly increasing the cost of any potential enemy action.
It is emphasized that the enemy's hybrid war may also involve economic, security, and terrorist threats, requiring proactive prevention measures.
In the second segment, citing the Supreme Leader's fatwa, recording and broadcasting phone conversations without consent is deemed a violation of privacy and is religiously forbidden.
The final part of the program is dedicated to the biography of the late Hajj Alighasem Hosseini Tehrani, who repented after attending a religious lecture and spent forty days in Karbala.
After returning, he devoted his life to serving Imam Hussein (AS) and established an old hussainiyeh in Tehran that is now one of the most famous home-based shrines.
His descendants share memories of the spiritual atmosphere and the physical structure of this historic shrine, highlighting its humble origins.
This narrative illustrates how a single religious lecture can transform an individual's life from a personal driver to a devoted religious servant.
