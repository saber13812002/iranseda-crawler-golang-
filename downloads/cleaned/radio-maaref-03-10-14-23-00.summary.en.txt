In the final segment of the program, Hujjat al-Islam Erfan emphasized the necessity of the Imam as God's proof and evidence on earth, citing Imam Sadiq's narration that Imams are divine envoys guiding humanity and preserving the world.
He noted that failing to recognize this proof leads to religious misguidance, and while Imams are human, they are strengthened by divine wisdom.
The program then transitioned to "Porsman Tarikhi," featuring Hujjat al-Islam Pishvayi, who addressed historical questions in commemoration of the births of Imam Baqir and Imam Hadi.
A key question regarding the location of "Sha'ib Abi Talib" was answered by clarifying that it was located near the Masjid al-Haram in Mecca, contrary to claims that it was outside the city.
The expert explained that this valley served as a safe haven for the Banu Hashim during the three-year economic and social boycott imposed by the Quraysh in the seventh year of prophethood.
Abu Talib secured the group's safety by relocating them to this valley and frequently changing the Prophet's sleeping spot, while Ali (AS) slept in the Prophet's bed for three years as a shield.
Another question focused on the A'in family's role in promoting Shia Islam, with the answer highlighting them as prominent scholars and narrators specializing in jurisprudence, tafsir, and theology.
Notable figures from this lineage, such as Zarrarah and Hamran ibn A'in, were identified as key students of Imam Baqir who preserved both legal and theological hadiths.
