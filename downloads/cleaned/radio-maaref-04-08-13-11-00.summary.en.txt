This radio program began by offering condolences for the anniversary of the martyrdom of Hazrat Fatimah Zahra (SA), highlighting widespread mourning in Mashhad and Qom and her role as a global model for women. It also reported on the 13th of Aban rallies against global arrogance and statements from the Seminary Teachers' Association and Basij condemning negotiations with the United States. A new book titled "Ayatollah Beheshti According to US Documents" was introduced, which uses classified files to refute allegations of a secret link between the martyr and the US. In the Hajj section, it was announced that over 52,000 people have pre-registered for the 1445 Hajj, with registration open until the end of Bahman 1404. The "Zamzam Ahkam" segment addressed various jurisprudential questions, including the definition of backbiting, correct pronunciation in prayer, and rulings on Khoms and restitution of rights. Additionally, explanations were provided regarding the legality of penalties for delayed property delivery and the conditions for visiting doctors from a Sharia perspective. These segments reflect a combination of political-religious news and responses to the daily religious concerns of the audience.
