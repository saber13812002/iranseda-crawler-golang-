The night of Imam Javad's martyrdom was observed in Qom with millions of pilgrims attending mourning ceremonies. News reports highlighted the Israeli regime's killing of over 50 Palestinians in Gaza and its rejection of Qatar's ceasefire proposal. A potential agreement between the US and Al-Jolani regarding Syria's recognition of Israel and the lifting of sanctions was also reported. Iran's Minister of Roads emphasized accelerating the Shalamcheh-Basra railway project to facilitate Arbaeen pilgrimages during his visit to Baghdad. The International Conference on Islamophobia opened in Baku, while the fourth Imam Reza Congress focused on modern sciences in Mashhad. In Canada, police launched an investigation into a hate-motivated attack on a mosque. The program concluded with a discussion on how young people can emulate Imam Javad's scientific and ethical attributes.
