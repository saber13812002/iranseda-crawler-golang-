The radio program "Shifa" explored the therapeutic benefits of nettle in traditional Islamic medicine, specifically for managing diabetes and improving cellular metabolism. Dr. Hassan Akbari, a traditional medicine expert, explained that nettle contains compounds like gzelin that mimic insulin, addressing the root cause of the disease rather than just lowering blood sugar levels. In the "A Drop from the Sea" segment, Quranic scholar Rouhollah Rasoul emphasized the importance of consultation and avoiding authoritarian decision-making, citing the Quran and Nahj al-Balagha. He argued that humility in prayer enhances rationality and the willingness to accept others' opinions, a point illustrated by a short dramatic skit about a man's repeated failures due to his refusal to consult. The broadcast concluded with a speech highlighting the fundamental achievements of the revolution, such as independence and freedom, as a definitive rebuttal to the disheartening narratives spread by adversaries.
