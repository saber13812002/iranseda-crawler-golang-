In the radio program "Persman Quran," a question was addressed regarding why thinking about God's essence is discouraged despite Islam being a religion of thought. The respondent explained that while humans can contemplate God's essence, their limited mental capacity prevents them from grasping its truth, whereas God's will is understood through the order of creation. The precise order of the universe, such as Earth's rotation and the water cycle, was cited as proof that the world is not random and that reason accepts a Creator. It was also noted that the minority who deny God do so not due to a lack of evidence, but because of negligence, injustice, and internal corruption. The segment concluded by mentioning the availability of the program's audio archive in the "Iran Seda" app and offering advice for spiritual growth during Ramadan and Laylat al-Qadr. Finally, an interpretation of Surah Al-Asr was provided, emphasizing the four pillars of faith, righteous deeds, advising truth, and patience.
