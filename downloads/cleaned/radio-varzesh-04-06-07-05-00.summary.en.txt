This radio program discussed the significant growth of traditional Iranian sports like Zourkhaneh and Pahlavani wrestling, highlighting the federation's strong support for these disciplines.
A key highlight was the first-ever children's tournament held in Hamedan, which attracted 193 young athletes and their families.
The team from Isfahan emerged as the champion, followed by Tehran and Mazandaran, with the champion team also receiving an award for good sportsmanship.
To foster a sense of community and reduce intense competition, no podium was set up for the top three, and all participants were given certificates of participation.
Hosting the event at the Islamic Azad University in Hamedan was presented as a prime example of social responsibility and utilizing provincial resources for sports development.
Mohammad Reza Amiri, the new federation caretaker, stated that he has no intention of running for election and is focused solely on holding a legal general assembly.
The federation is currently preparing to host the Asian Championship in Urmia, with initial meetings already held in the presence of the Minister of Sports.
The broadcast concluded with a historical sports review, mentioning events such as Australia's first cricket victory in England and various Olympic achievements.
