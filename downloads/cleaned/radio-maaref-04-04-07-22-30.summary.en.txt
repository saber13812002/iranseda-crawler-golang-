In this radio program, the divine promise of the destruction of oppressors and the victory of the oppressed is presented as a historical and religious principle based on Quranic verses and narrations.
The primary ethical recommendation is to avoid oppressing those who have no refuge but God, even within family and social relationships.
Historical anecdotes linking oppression to illness or hardship were shared to emphasize the severity of this sin.
Imam Sajjad's presence in the mourning tent and the transmission of Imam Hussein's specific prayer for difficult times were highlighted as a legacy from Fatima Zahra (SA).
It was emphasized that people's rights, including debts and property, must be respected even after martyrdom, as the rights of others hold special importance on the Day of Judgment.
The program concluded with a prayer for the victory of the Iranian nation, the destruction of enemies, and solace for the families of martyrs.
The broadcast ended with answers to Quranic questions and condolences for religious occasions.
