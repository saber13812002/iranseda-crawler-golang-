The primary focus of this program is analyzing the duality of material and spiritual power in the global arena, interpreted through Quranic teachings. The speaker argues that imperialist powers like the United States and Israel, adopting a Pharaonic and arrogant stance, stand against the front of justice-seekers and freedom-loving nations. It is emphasized that Iran currently enjoys its highest historical popularity worldwide, a status derived from its spiritual and moral power which supersedes material strength. By comparing the current situation with the eras of Prophet Muhammad and Imam Khomeini, it is shown that the revolution leaders' predictions regarding the defeat of global arrogance and the liberation of Palestine are now coming true. The recent defeats of Israel and its allies in Gaza and Lebanon indicate the gradual weakening of these powers and the victory of Islamic resistance. Finally, by referencing personal stories from the revolution era, the deep connection between religious beliefs, self-sacrifice, and the armed struggle for Palestinian freedom and support for the oppressed people of Gaza is highlighted.
