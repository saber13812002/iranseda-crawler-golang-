The program begins with a narration of the Prophet Muhammad's (PBUH) teachings, emphasizing that salvation is achieved only through righteous action and sincere repentance before death. The Prophet's severe illness is highlighted to show that his pained moans attracted divine mercy and healing, while the angels' inquiries about his health established the Islamic tradition of visiting the sick. The segment then transitions to a dramatized story from the book "Galaxy of Nothingness," focusing on Seyyed Ali Qazi Tabatabai in Najaf. He encounters a beggar who has passively accepted his poverty, finding a false peace in his helplessness. Seyyed Ali criticizes this passivity, teaching the man that he should rely on God's will rather than resigning to his fate. Instead of offering money, Seyyed Ali proposes telling a story to awaken the beggar from his state of apathy and despair.
