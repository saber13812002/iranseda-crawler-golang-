The radio program initially emphasized the importance of good manners and caring for one's spouse, citing the teachings of the Infallibles and the conduct of Ayatollah Behjat. Subsequently, Professor Seyyed Ahmad Faghihi explained the concept of "theistic ethics," stating that awareness of God's presence is the key to correcting individual and social behavior. In the Q&A section, it was advised that even when innocent, one should clarify misunderstandings and seek forgiveness to resolve conflicts. For overcoming marriage obstacles, the program recommended respecting one's mother, praying on time, and reciting Surah Tawhid. Finally, for breaking the cycle of repeated sins, changing the external environment and conditions, along with prostration, weeping, and seeking God's forgiveness, were presented as the primary solutions.
