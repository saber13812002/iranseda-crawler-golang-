The first segment of the program detailed the rituals of Umrah al-Tamattu, including Tawaf, the Tawaf prayer, and Sa'i between Safa and Marwa. Subsequent sections addressed various jurisprudential questions, clarifying that the phrase "Allahumma anta Rabbi" is incorrect for prostration and bowing, where specific prescribed dhikr must be recited. It was confirmed that allocating one percent of one's salary to charitable causes, such as supporting a mother or the needy, is permissible. Regarding lost property, the obligation to search for the owner and, if unsuccessful, to give charity or return the item to the authorities was emphasized. Friday prayer was described as a religious and political duty, with its abandonment considered a sign of hypocrisy. The minimum required distance between two Friday prayers is one farsakh (approximately 5.5 kilometers); otherwise, the second prayer is invalid. Finally, the spiritual benefits of attending Friday prayer were highlighted, followed by an announcement for the live broadcast of the Tehran Friday prayer from Tehran University.
