In this program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verses 90 to 93 of Surah Al-Ma'idah, emphasizing the absolute prohibition of alcohol and gambling as "impure" (Rijs). He argues that the Quranic command to "not approach" sin is stronger than the command to avoid the sin itself, highlighting the severity of the prohibition. The theory of gradual revelation is rejected, as the Quran, being the word of God, does not contain contradictions; Meccan verses like Surah Al-Nahl focus on God's power and blessings rather than legal rulings. The triple repetition of "fear God, believe, and do good deeds" in the verse points to different levels of faith, from initial belief to righteous action and finally "Ihsan," which is worshipping God as if one sees Him. Divine forgiveness is discussed for those who consumed alcohol before the prohibition was revealed, provided they abstain after becoming aware of the ruling. This interpretation refutes doubts regarding gradualism and emphasizes the coherence of the Quranic message, leading to a deeper understanding of legal and spiritual obligations.
