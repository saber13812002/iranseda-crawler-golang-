The program begins with an exegesis of the verse "Ask the people of knowledge," emphasizing the etiquette of prophets in their interactions with God and humanity. Ayatollah Javadi Amoli explains that true etiquette in supplication involves attributing one's own flaws to oneself while ascribing all perfection and mercy to God. Through the stories of Job, Zechariah, and Jesus, the lecture illustrates how prophets maintained dignity and trust in God when facing adversity, presenting their requests in a reasonable and respectful manner. A central point is that God is the absolute Merciful, and no creature can possess a greater capacity for mercy than He does. The lecture further highlights that the etiquette of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) is supreme over that of all other prophets, as God never addressed him by his personal name but rather by titles like "O Prophet." Finally, the text concludes that complete faith in God, angels, books, and messengers as a unified whole is the hallmark of true believers and proper divine etiquette.
