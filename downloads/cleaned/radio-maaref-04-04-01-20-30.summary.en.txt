The program begins by highlighting the unique nature of the Islamic Revolution in Iran, which achieved stable state-building without weapons by relying on Islam. In contrast, the revolutions in France, China, and Russia were marked by widespread violence, mass executions, and a failure to establish lasting systems. The Supreme Leader emphasized that the people determined their destiny through the free referendum of 1988, choosing the Islamic Republic. The second part of the program addresses the recent threats made by the US President against Iran's Supreme Leader. Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi condemned these threats, urging Muslims to support the leadership and calling for preemptive strikes by the armed forces to deter the enemy. Finally, the program features the testimony of Ali Reza Nikpour, a former POW captured in 1988 at Shalamcheh. He described the severe physical torture, strict communication restrictions, and inhumane conditions he endured in the Ba'athist regime's detention camps.
