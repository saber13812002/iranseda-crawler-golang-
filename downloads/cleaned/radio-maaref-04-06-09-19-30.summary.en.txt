In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Fallah emphasized the prohibition of backbiting and the necessity of learning religion from pious scholars, citing Quranic verses and narrations. He referenced a hadith from the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) that strongly condemns backbiting believers who do not commit injustice, lie, or break promises. Imam Sadiq (AS) further stated that backbiting someone whose sins are unknown and unproven by two just witnesses causes one to exit God's guardianship and fall under Satan's dominion. Citing Imam Ali's (AS) advice to Imam Hassan (AS), the speaker described backbiters as individuals who pour their inner impurities into the spiritual vessels of others. Consequently, guarding one's tongue and avoiding curiosity or spreading rumors are essential conditions for maintaining divine guardianship and avoiding Satan's trap. This discussion highlights that backbiting is not merely a moral sin but a serious threat to a person's spiritual standing before God.
