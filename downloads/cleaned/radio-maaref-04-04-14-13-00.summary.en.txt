In this program, the discussion focuses on the consequences of oppression, emphasizing that no oppressor, from major rulers to ordinary individuals, achieves anything other than ruin and destruction.
The speaker highlights the fate of Yazid and historical anecdotes to demonstrate that unjust bloodshed and inequality shorten the lifespan of oppressors and are irreparable even in daily life.
An example of rectifying injustice is the story of Ayatollah Borujerdi, who, after hearing false rumors, corrected his mistake and restored the reputation of the affected individual.
Drawing on the Quran and narrations, the speaker warns that oppression can lead a person to disbelief and a bitter fate, for which they will be held accountable on the Day of Judgment.
Imam Sajjad's advice to his son to avoid harming the helpless is presented as a key moral principle.
Finally, a story about the divine zeal of Imam Abbas (AS) in saving a Muslim family in Pakistan is shared, illustrating divine support for the oppressed.
Together, these narratives convey a clear message advocating for justice and the avoidance of all forms of oppression at every level of life.
