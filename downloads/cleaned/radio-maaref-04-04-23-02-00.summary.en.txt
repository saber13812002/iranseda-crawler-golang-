The radio program begins with a narrative about Imam Hussein (PBUH) and emphasizes the spiritual rewards of fulfilling the needs of believers. Hujjat al-Islam Seyed Ahmad Faqih then discusses "theistic ethics" and the pivotal role of the Salawat prayer in divine education. Citing Ayatollah Behjat, he explains that sending Salawat automatically purifies sins without the need for specific requests for forgiveness, unlike other deeds. He argues that this purification stems from establishing a spiritual bond and friendship with the Prophet (PBUH), rather than being a mere material transaction with God. Using the analogy of approaching the sea, he outlines different levels of attention to the meaning of Salawat, urging listeners to move beyond mere words to deep heartfelt love. In the Q&A section, the challenge of maintaining "presence of heart" during prayer is addressed, with advice to practice mental control throughout the day. Finally, the issue of doubt regarding the sincerity of one's intentions is discussed, emphasizing the importance of finding a balanced path in ethical conduct.
