In this radio program, Professor Sahibi, relying on the views of Imam Khomeini, examined the life and works of Mulla Sadra. He described Sadra's works, such as the commentary on Usul al-Kafi and Asfar, as magnificent texts rooted in Quranic knowledge and the traditions of the Ahl al-Bayt, distinct from Greek philosophy. Imam Khomeini regarded Mulla Sadra as the leader of the people of Tawhid and defended him against the baseless takfir of some literalists. The Imam believed that these criticisms stemmed from ignorance of deep esoteric matters and ultimately contributed to Sadra's existential elevation. He emphasized that engaging in Transcendent Wisdom and practical mysticism requires acquiring preliminary knowledge and learning from experienced masters. It was also noted that Imam Khomeini utilized the foundations of Transcendent Wisdom and elevated them in his own writings. Finally, the program addressed listeners' religious questions regarding congregational prayer, khums, and fasting.
