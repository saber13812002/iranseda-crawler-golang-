The radio program initially emphasized the religious imperative of preserving the honor of believers and avoiding the exposure of others' mistakes, citing various narrations and the conduct of past scholars. It then transitioned to analyzing how neo-pagan and nationalist discourses spread religious doubts by exploiting national symbols to create a false dichotomy between being Iranian and being Muslim. Dr. Heshimi argued that these movements challenge believers by redefining fundamental concepts such as family, marriage, and reason, thereby introducing modern Western values into the Islamic framework. He further refuted the historical claim that Islam was imposed on Iran, noting that this narrative stems from the flawed ideologies of constitutionalists and Orientalists rather than historical facts. The discussion concluded that the primary goal of such discourse is to undermine religious identity and create ideological confusion through the promotion of modernist and secularist values.
