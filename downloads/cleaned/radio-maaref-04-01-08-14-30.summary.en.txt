The radio program began by honoring Jerusalem Day and praising the massive participation of Iranians in nationwide rallies. In the "Ayat Noor" segment, Hujjat al-Islam Hosseni interpreted verse 120 of Surah Al-Baqarah, emphasizing the Quranic warning against yielding to the enemy's demands. He stated that adversaries will never be satisfied with Muslims unless they abandon their Islamic values and identity. A key point of this exegesis is the necessity of maintaining intellectual independence and avoiding blind trust in the enemy's promises. It was also noted that only the Islamic worldview can save humanity from ideological deviations. Subsequently, a political analyst described the Iranian nation as the children of Ramadan and Ashura, citing the Supreme Leader's words. He viewed the widespread public presence as evidence that the cause of Palestine is deeply rooted in the community's religious beliefs. Finally, this resistance and support for the oppressed Palestinian people were presented as a symbol of unity and firm will against enemy threats.
