In this radio program, the Quran is described as the living and wise word of God that engages in a meaningful dialogue with its audience based on their specific needs.
A key point of discussion is the distinction between the infinite, unlimited nature of divine speech and the limited human capacity for comprehension, urging listeners not to restrict the Quran's meaning to their own understanding.
The program emphasizes the intrinsic unity between the Quran and Imam Zaman (AJ), stating that turning to the Quran is a direct path to reaching the Imam and hastening his reappearance.
The Quran addresses all of humanity through the language of innate nature, connecting with anyone whose heart is free from the corruption of evil.
In the second part, drawing from the book of Kafi, the audience is warned against developing a heart-centered attachment to the material world, which is a characteristic of the unjust.
Asceticism is clarified not as a rejection of lawful work and effort, but as a spiritual detachment from worldly dependencies.
Believers are encouraged to compete in good deeds and avoid the deceptions of the world, as the root of all trials is the love of material life.
Finally, it is advised to constantly rely on God's grace to maintain balance and prevent the world from overwhelming one's spiritual state.
