A child development expert emphasized on the radio program that abruptly and forcibly removing media from children's lives is counterproductive and potentially harmful. Instead, media usage should be managed gradually with a strict limit of less than one hour per day, while completely prohibiting screen time for children under two years old. The expert suggested organizing engaging group activities at gatherings to prevent children from feeling bored and reaching for smartphones. It was also recommended that events provide both shared spaces for children to be near their parents and separate areas for age-appropriate play. Regarding a listener's question about encouraging a teenage girl to pray, the expert advised leveraging the power of peer groups and motivating her through communal prayer. Finally, the importance of maintaining a strong emotional connection between parents and children was highlighted, as this bond significantly influences the child's religious and moral behavior.
