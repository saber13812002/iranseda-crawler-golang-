This radio program discussed effective strategies for building a close and meaningful relationship with adolescents and children.
The most crucial principle for establishing connection is the unconditional acceptance of the child, including all their strengths and weaknesses.
Parents are advised to grant their children freedom of action and foster a two-way communication style based on active listening rather than direct control.
Both verbal and non-verbal affirmations, such as meaningful eye contact, significantly boost a child's self-esteem and sense of worth.
Regarding financial issues and lying, the experts recommended addressing the root causes of needs and establishing clear household rules instead of labeling behavior.
Adolescent lying often stems from a fear of judgment, which should be managed by creating a safe space for admitting mistakes without harsh criticism.
For six-year-olds, parents should recognize their advanced cognitive and verbal abilities and involve them in decision-making by explaining situations clearly.
