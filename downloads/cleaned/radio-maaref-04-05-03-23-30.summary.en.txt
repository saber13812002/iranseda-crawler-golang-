This radio program discusses the historical and theological stance of Ahmad ibn Hanbal, known as Zahri, regarding Imam Ali (AS). The speaker cites historical texts to argue that Zahri was an opponent of the Ahl al-Bayt and prohibited praising Imam Ali during the Umayyad rule. A specific narration is highlighted where Zahri, after killing a Muslim on Umayyad orders, sought refuge with Imam Zayn al-Abidin (AS). Instead of punishing him, the Imam expressed greater concern for Zahri's neglect of prayer and sent him back to his family. The discussion further examines how Shia scholars like Sheikh Tusi and Ayatollah Khoei have analyzed and refuted Zahri's claims against the Ahl al-Bayt. The speaker concludes that Zahri ultimately distanced himself from the Ahl al-Bayt and is regarded as one of their opponents. This analysis aims to clarify historical theological disputes and emphasize the virtues of the Ahl al-Bayt.
