This radio program discusses the interpretation of a verse from Surah Al-Isra regarding the corruption of the Children of Israel and its implications during the era of the Mahdi. The speaker first analyzes the chain of narration from Tafsir Eyyashi and Al-Kafi, citing rijal works like al-Najashi and Ibn al-Qatt to establish the weakness of the narrators. Despite the weak chain, the exegesis of Allameh Majlisi and Qari Akhti is examined, defining the corruption of the Children of Israel in two stages: opposing the Torah and killing prophets, followed by the murder of Zechariah, John the Baptist, and plotting against Jesus. The esoteric interpretation of the verse points to the corruption of the Islamic Ummah through the assassination of Imam Ali and Imam Hussein, which led to the dominance of oppressors like Hujjaj and the Umayyads. The speaker draws historical parallels, comparing the current oppressive rule over the Holy Shrines and Palestine to the crimes of the Jews and Hujjaj. Finally, the promise of power returning to the Shia after the emergence of the Mahdi and the retribution against oppressors is presented as the ultimate outcome of this historical process.
