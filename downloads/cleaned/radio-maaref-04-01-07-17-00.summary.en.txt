The radio program "Culture Street" begins by exploring the concept of party culture, emphasizing the importance of understanding social etiquette and respecting the customs of different families. In the second segment, Hojjat al-Islam Ali Ghoumi highlights the necessity of creating a comfortable atmosphere for guests and avoiding intrusive behavior, citing the conduct of Imam Sadiq (AS). He also addresses the decline in family visits in the modern era due to economic challenges and geographical distances. Subsequently, the program "Treasure of Happiness" features Hojjat al-Islam Fala discussing the virtue of patience in the Holy Quran. He cites verses from Surah Al-Baqarah, Al-Hajj, and Al-Imran to explain that patience is essential when facing financial hardships, physical ailments, enemy harassment, and the denial of prophets. The scholar stresses that true patience must be accompanied by reliance on God and adherence to His divine plan, rather than being mere passive endurance. Finally, the concept of "beautiful patience" (Sabr Jameel) is explained as enduring trials without complaint or arrogance, referencing the teachings of Imam Sadiq (AS) and Prophet Jacob (AS).
