This sermon begins by emphasizing the importance of consulting religious scholars to find salvation and avoid ignorant actions.
Citing a narration from Imam Javad, the speaker introduces four ethical principles as keys to unlocking the gates of divine mercy.
The first principle is relying on patience and perseverance to remain steadfast on the path of God and the Quran amidst challenges.
The second principle involves accepting God's decree and being grateful for one's allotted share, regardless of its size.
The third principle is avoiding unlawful desires and unrealistic aspirations that distract individuals from their true purpose in life.
The fourth principle is maintaining constant awareness of God's presence, illustrated by the story of a thief who stops sinning out of fear of being watched by God.
Through historical and literary anecdotes, the speaker demonstrates that material wealth without morality and monotheism leads to corruption rather than benefit.
Ultimately, the sermon concludes that embracing these four principles and acting upon the Quran and narrations is the only path to worldly and otherworldly happiness.
