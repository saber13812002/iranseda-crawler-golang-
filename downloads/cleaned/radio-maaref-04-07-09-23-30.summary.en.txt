This radio program, dedicated to the birth of Imam Reza (PBUH), emphasized the spiritual value of secret charity and helping the needy as the best use of wealth for preserving one's honor in both this world and the hereafter.
The host cited religious narrations and personal anecdotes to encourage listeners to support a specific sick and needy mother by sending the number 8 via SMS to 3552.
Additionally, listeners' intentions for visiting holy shrines and drinking water from the Saqakhaneh in Mashhad were fulfilled on their behalf.
The latter part of the broadcast featured a religious song followed by the recitation of the Ziyarat Al-Yasin and prayers for the Ahl al-Bayt.
Concluding with a political-religious message, the program stressed the critical importance of unity among the army, the IRGC, the Basij, and the general public to counter external conspiracies.
It warned that internal division could be exploited by enemies to threaten national security, asserting that only national cohesion and reliance on domestic power ensure the country's safety.
The program ended with final prayers and hopes for maintaining peace and security in Iran.
