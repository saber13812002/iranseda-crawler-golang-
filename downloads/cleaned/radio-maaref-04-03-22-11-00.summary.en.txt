On the eve of the Eid of Ghadir Khumm, a massive Alawi flag with an area of one thousand square meters was raised in Tehran, symbolizing the people's devotion to Imam Ali (PBUH).
The President emphasized in a meeting with Ilam activists that Iran will never halt its nuclear research or reduce it to zero under external pressure.
He also strongly condemned the crimes of the Zionist regime and US support for them, stressing Iran's right to self-defense and the necessity of maintaining scientific independence.
The return operation for Iranian pilgrims from the Holy Land continues until mid-July, with average flight delays reduced to less than seven minutes.
The historical book "Al-Qarat," which narrates the era of Imam Ali's governance, reached its 22nd edition, highlighting its importance in understanding Shia history.
The 7th Iranian-Islamic Clothing Exhibition opened at Imam Khomeini Mosque, offering special discounts and featuring domestic manufacturers.
Various cultural and religious programs, including Basij Quran competitions and Ghadir celebrations, are being held nationwide to keep the memory of this great Eid alive.
