The Radio Ma'aref broadcast began with a summary of the book "In the Ambush of the Red Rose," which details the life of Martyr Ali Siyad Shirazi and the battles in Kurdistan. The narrative describes how, after securing support from President Bani-Sadr, forces were airlifted to Sanandaj but faced an emergency landing due to communication failures and roadblocks. Upon landing, the troops encountered fierce resistance from counter-revolutionaries, prompting Shirazi to direct artillery fire at enemy positions. Despite being besieged and lacking resources, the commanders worked to break the siege by establishing artillery observation posts and clearing entry routes. In a separate segment, the program offered five practical strategies to prevent theft in sports gyms. The broadcast concluded with the recitation of Ziyarat Al-Yasin and a call for the people of Kurdistan to reject ethnic division and embrace Islamic unity.
