In this radio program, Professor Rasakhi analyzed the Islamic Republic's strategies for dealing with economic and political pressures from global powers. He argued that the leadership's acceptance of negotiation principles prevented a rift between the public and the government, effectively neutralizing the enemy's tactic of blaming the system for crises. This approach not only encouraged domestic elites to think within interaction frameworks but also provided a successful model for resistance groups like Hamas in Palestine. In the second segment, Dr. Bahari addressed health topics for the spring season, emphasizing the importance of drinking adequate water at the right temperature for digestion and preventing dehydration effects. He also explained the causes and treatments of warts and skin tags, advising viewers to consult reliable medical sources rather than unverified social media recommendations. Finally, simple solutions such as exercise and deep breathing were offered to combat spring lethargy, and the edible plant "Zabar" was identified as the answer to the weekly food question.
