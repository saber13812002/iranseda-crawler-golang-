The radio program begins by emphasizing the importance of spiritual and ethical dimensions in Islam, warning against intellectual arrogance.
The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) stated that true knowledge is only beneficial if it leads to humility, weeping, and a broken heart before God.
Divine love and the deprivation of spiritual proximity are cited as primary reasons for a believer's tears, while a hard heart indicates distance from God.
The discussion then shifts to the jurisprudential issue of lifting the prohibitions of Ihram after the rituals at Mina during Umrah.
The majority of Shia scholars agree that using perfume becomes permissible after shaving or cutting hair, although wearing sewn clothes remains forbidden.
Several authentic and repeated narrations, including one from Imam Musa al-Kadhim (AS), explicitly permit the use of perfume after throwing the pebbles at the Jamarat and before shaving.
Another narration from Imam Jafar al-Sadiq (AS) reports that the Prophet (PBUH) applied musk to his head before visiting the Kaaba, highlighting the value of fragrance in the Prophetic tradition.
The final conclusion affirms the permissibility of using perfume after the completion of Mina rituals and shaving, based on the consensus of scholars and reliable narrations.
