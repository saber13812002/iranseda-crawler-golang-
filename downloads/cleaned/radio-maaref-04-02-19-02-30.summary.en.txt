In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the paramount importance of "enjoining good and forbidding evil" based on narrations from Imam Ali and Imam Baqir. He highlighted that this divine duty is the cornerstone of social justice, security, and economic prosperity. Citing the memoirs of Allameh Jafari and Ayatollah Shahidi, the speaker illustrated how religious leaders historically stood firm against injustice and rejected illicit gifts, even from kings. He argued that fulfilling this obligation requires active resistance to public sins, not merely internal disapproval. The speaker asserted that societal adherence to this principle would lead to lawful trade, reduced oppression, and overall national development. The program concluded with the recitation of spiritual supplications, asking God to guide believers toward piety and divine knowledge.
