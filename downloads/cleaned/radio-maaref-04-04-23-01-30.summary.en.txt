The speaker in the radio program emphasizes that immodesty is rooted in a lack of religious faith, referencing the 1935 events in Mashhad where people sacrificed their lives to defend hijab against security forces. He rejects the notion that the era of religion has passed, asserting that hijab and the black chador are fundamental principles of Islam and the tradition of the Prophet and his family. By highlighting that Ja'fari Shia Islam is the official state religion, he expresses pride in Shia identity and a commitment to the unchangeable principles of the constitution. The speaker also draws attention to the patience and steadfastness of Lady Zainab in Kufa, underscoring the importance of preserving the Husseini identity and defending the faith. The program then transitions to a reading from the book "Neshan-e Hassan," where Hassan and Umar discuss the threats posed by Yazid to Imam Hussein and the letters from the Shia of Kufa. These letters reveal the enthusiasm of the people of Kufa to join Imam Hussein, although Muhammad Hanafiya remains concerned about the political consequences and the risk of war with the Umayyads. The broadcast concludes with Husseini prayers and an introduction to the network's upcoming programs.
