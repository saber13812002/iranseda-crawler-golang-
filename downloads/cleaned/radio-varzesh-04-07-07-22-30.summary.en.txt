Ashoor Gholamohammadi, a veteran basketball player and coach, recounts his career highlights, including championships with the Homa and Islamic Azad University teams. He highlights the significant support from university administrators that helped integrate athletes into academic life and build strong university teams. After retiring from playing, he transitioned into coaching, serving at the Pikan club and later as the head coach of the Homa team. Gholamohammadi shares the profound impact of his first wife's passing and credits his second wife for helping him rebuild his professional life. Alongside his sports career, he has worked for years as a radio host and expert, providing analysis for basketball matches. He expresses gratitude to the mentors and coaches who guided him, emphasizing the importance of ethics and human relationships in sports. Finally, he states that his ultimate wish is to maintain strong friendships and peace in his interactions with others.
