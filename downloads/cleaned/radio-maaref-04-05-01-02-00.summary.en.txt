The program begins by emphasizing the necessity of loving the Ahl al-Bayt and its impact on spiritual purification. It then recounts the life of Ayatollah Mohammad Reza Tabasi, highlighting his deep devotion and the regular mourning gatherings he organized in Najaf. The main segment features the memories of Zahra Nasiri, the wife of martyr Javad Akbari, a defender of the holy shrines. She describes her initial resistance to marriage and her eventual acceptance of Javad due to his good character. Zahra Nasiri also discusses the hardships of her pregnancy and how their son, Mohammad Sadegh, improved their marital relationship. Finally, she narrates the shocking event of Javad's sudden departure to Syria to defend the shrine of Lady Zainab and the emotional trauma it caused her.
