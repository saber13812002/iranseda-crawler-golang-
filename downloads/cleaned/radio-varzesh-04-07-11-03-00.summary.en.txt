The guest, a former goalkeeper for Ekbatan, recounts his youth and training at Persepolis before accepting an offer to join the newly formed Ekbatan team in 1978. Despite a better financial offer from Persepolis, he chose Ekbatan due to his bond with the young teammates and advice from his elders. The team won the Tehran club championship in its first year and subsequently competed in the second division. He highlights his experience playing for the national youth, junior, and hope teams, including a match in the Great Wall of China and Beijing. He takes pride in his 14 to 15-year loyalty to Ekbatan, refusing numerous offers to switch clubs. Financial support from private individuals like Nikchou and Islami was crucial for the team's survival during the pre-revolutionary era. The guest expresses gratitude to his former teammates, emphasizing the team's strong camaraderie and distinct character.
