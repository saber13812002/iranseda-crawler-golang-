This radio program addressed various theological and religious questions from listeners. The discussion began with a fatwa error by a religious authority, emphasizing that a single mistake should not lead to their exclusion unless the deviation is intentional and conscious. The role of faith in providing psychological peace and alleviating depression was highlighted, noting that reliance on God is the primary source of security. It was clarified that cursing a believer who does not deserve it rebounds on the cursing person, whereas cursing oppressors is permissible based on Quranic precedents. A listener's interpretation of "two bows' length" as a spiritual rank where divine attributes manifest in the Prophet was validated. The possibility of new worlds being created after the Day of Judgment was also discussed based on religious narrations. Finally, the program explained the spiritual and material blessings of charity, asserting that God grants hidden prosperity to those who help the needy.
