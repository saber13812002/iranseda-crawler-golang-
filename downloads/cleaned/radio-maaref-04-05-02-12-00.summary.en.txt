This radio segment emphasizes the importance of spiritual freshness and full presence of mind during prayer, explicitly discouraging worship while feeling lethargic or distracted.
Drawing on Quranic verses and narrations, the term "sukara" is interpreted not as intoxication by alcohol, but as a state of drowsiness or mental absence that hinders the proper understanding of prayer.
Listeners are encouraged to approach their prayers with enthusiasm and a heart detached from worldly concerns to maximize their spiritual benefits.
The program also shares anecdotes about the humble character of the late Ayatollah Borujerdi, who consistently rejected grand titles and public fame.
Even in his childhood, before he could read or write, he demonstrated a deep aversion to being addressed with high-ranking religious titles.
These stories serve as a model of humility, highlighting the priority of fulfilling religious duties over seeking social status or recognition.
The broadcast concludes with the recitation of verses from Surah Al-Isra and Al-Ikhlas, along with specific supplications.
The ultimate goal is to inspire listeners to become sincere worshippers and true companions of the family of the Prophet.
