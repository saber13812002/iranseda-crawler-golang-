Ali Radei, former head coach of the national deaf futsal and seven-a-side football teams, discusses his world championship victory with the remarkable record of 21 goals scored and none conceded. He explains his unique hybrid tactical approach, which blended football and futsal by shifting formations between 4-1-1 and 1-4-1 based on ball possession. Radei expresses deep frustration over the lack of financial and administrative support from sports officials and federations following his international successes. He argues that mafia-like forces and behind-the-scenes operators in Iranian football prevent competent coaches from being hired by major clubs. Despite multiple offers from abroad, Radei prefers to stay in Iran due to his age and deep love for his homeland. He also shares his unique experience as the first Iranian coach to be named Asian Coach of the Year in squash. Referring to his father's life philosophy, Radei emphasizes that he does not dwell on the past but focuses on planning for the future. Finally, he thanks the radio program for providing a platform to share his memories and insights.
