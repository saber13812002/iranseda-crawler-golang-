The radio program "Narrative of Love" features the mother of martyr Mehdi Sabri, who shares memories of his piety, reliance on the Ahl al-Bayt, and deep love for martyrdom. She describes his moral conduct, care for others' rights, and religious habits before his journey to Syria to defend the shrines. Another segment of the program critiques political behaviors and warns against enemy plots against Iran, emphasizing solidarity with the oppressed people of Palestine. Finally, the Supreme Leader of Iran emphasizes the vital importance of universities as the incubators of the country's future managers. He highlights the foundational role of Iranian scholars in global scientific progress by referencing Islamic civilization history. The Leader notes that cultivating capable elites is the key to national power and the country's advancement.
