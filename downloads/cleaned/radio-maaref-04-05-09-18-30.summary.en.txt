The first segment of the program analyzed the 37th story from the second chapter of Saadi's Gulistan, interpreted by Dr. Irandoust. While Saadi emphasizes the necessity of seclusion for spiritual seekers, the master's proposed method of repelling visitors by lending to the poor and demanding from the rich was criticized as religiously and ethically flawed. The commentator stressed that true seclusion must maintain social responsibilities and avoid unnecessary begging, aligning with Islam's principle of moderation. Subsequently, the "Zamzam Ahkam" program addressed the rulings on Ta'ziyeh and Shabih-khani with expert Ustad Vahidpour. Key points included avoiding superstitions, the permissibility of men playing female roles in Ta'ziyeh provided it does not constitute actual imitation, and the requirement for accuracy in dialogue attributed to the Imams. The program also answered various religious queries, such as the fact that walnut stain does not invalidate wudu, the six conditions for sunlight to purify impurities, and the prohibition of intoxicating music for all. Finally, the ruling on prayer for frequent travelers and the importance of specifying one's Marja in questions were discussed.
