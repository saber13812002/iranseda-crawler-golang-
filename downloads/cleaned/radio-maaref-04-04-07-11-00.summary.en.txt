The funeral procession for over sixty martyrs, including senior IRGC commanders and media personnel, was held in Tehran with massive public participation. Gholamali Rashid, commander of Khatam al-Anbiya Headquarters, was highlighted as a key figure in the Sacred Defense and a designer of successful military operations. The Basij Media Organization announced the martyrdom of nine media activists during recent attacks. The head of Khorramabad Hawza reported that eight thousand preachers were deployed for Muharram, focusing on strengthening the front of truth and home gatherings. The land return of pilgrims has concluded, with the remaining ten thousand Hajjis being transported to Mashhad by air. Kashan also hosted a commemoration for the liturgical poet Mohtasham Kashani in honor of Muharram. The final segment discussed the divine origin of the Imams' titles and Imam Reza's prominent role in scholarly debates. The program concluded with the broadcast of Husseini poetry and mourning songs on Radio Muharram.
