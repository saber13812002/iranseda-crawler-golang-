The Ma'aref radio program began by commemorating the birth of Prophet Muhammad and Imam Jafar Sadiq, emphasizing their role in Muslim unity. Hojjat-ol-Islam Moradi highlighted the establishment of the Jafari University by Imam Sadiq as a key factor in the advancement of the Jafari school. The Supreme Leader approved sentence reductions for prisoners to mark these auspicious days. A grand "Kind Prophet" festival is scheduled for 4 PM at Vali-e-Asr Square in Tehran, featuring choirs and light shows. The President condemned the Israeli attack on Doha in a phone call with the Emir of Qatar, affirming Iran's support for the country. The International Poetry Congress on the Prophet, themed "Ahmad's Ummah," commenced at the shrine of Imam Ali ibn Musa al-Rida in Shiraz. The fourth edition of the Shahid Andarzgo Literary Prize concluded by selecting 11 works focused on revolutionary history and literature. At Imam Reza's shrine, a mass wedding ceremony for 1,850 families was held with special hospitality for pilgrims.
