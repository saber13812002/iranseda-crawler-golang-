The radio program "Narrative of Love" features the mother of martyr Arman, recounting the spiritual and disciplined life of her son. She highlights his exceptional manners, modest dress, and strict adherence to etiquette, even when he was ill or tired. Although Arman was deeply committed to religious studies and cultural activities, he pursued his engineering degree with his family's full consent, balancing his academic path with his spiritual goals. He was known for his logical arguments and thorough research, which often convinced relatives and guests of his religious views. Arman's simple lifestyle, generosity towards friends, and rejection of materialism demonstrated that martyrdom is the result of a conscious, God-centered life. The program emphasizes that martyrdom is not a sudden event but the culmination of years of striving for divine pleasure. It concludes by honoring the patience of martyrs' mothers and reflecting on Imam Khomeini's role in instilling faith and self-confidence in the Iranian revolution.
