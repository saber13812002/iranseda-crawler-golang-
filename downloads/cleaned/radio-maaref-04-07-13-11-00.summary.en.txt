In this radio broadcast, the deputy of the IRGC's coordination office reported a 42 percent decrease in theft rates and highlighted the growing role of public participation in ensuring national security. The program also covered the launch of a specialized course for training preachers in seminaries, emphasizing the need for active media engagement to address contemporary doubts. The first National Conference on Digital Islamic Sciences, focusing on artificial intelligence, was announced to be held in Qom. Additionally, the report noted the operation of 19,000 Quranic units in mosques and the donation of over 1.2 million units of blood in the first half of the year. In the final segment, religious scholars answered listeners' questions regarding prayer validity with pronunciation errors, conditions for breaking prayer, and women's travel permissions. The broadcast concluded with a preview of upcoming programs and the performance of a religious a cappella piece.
