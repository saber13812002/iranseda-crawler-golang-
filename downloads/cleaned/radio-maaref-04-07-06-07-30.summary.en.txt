The program begins by emphasizing kindness as a fundamental attribute of the Prophet of Islam, urging listeners to internalize this moral virtue in their daily lives. The second segment examines the divine honor of Martyr Sayyed Hassan Nasrallah, attributing it to his devotion, humility, and loyalty to the system of Wilayat. Subsequently, Professor Fallah cites narrations from the Ahl al-Bayt to classify cursing nature, time, and others as a major sin and a violation of Islamic ethics. He explains that insulting elements such as the wind, mountains, and night is not only meaningless but also rebounds on the speaker and hinders spiritual connection with God. The primary remedy for these transgressions is sincere repentance and a firm commitment to avoid repeating them under any circumstances. Even when facing enemies, Islam commands believers to refrain from cursing and instead focus on their own moral cultivation. Ultimately, heeding the commands of the Infallibles is the key to preserving dignity and avoiding moral vices in both society and the digital space.
