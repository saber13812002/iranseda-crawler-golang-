The program begins with a theological discussion on Quranic verses, distinguishing between divine trials and punishment while emphasizing the role of repentance in averting calamities. The focus then shifts to the birth anniversary of Hazrat Zainab Kobra and the designation of the fifth of Jumada al-Awwal as Nurses' Day in Iran. The host honors nurses as models of patience and service, particularly highlighting their dedication during crises such as the pandemic and war. Various segments include Quranic recitations, religious prayers, and expressions of gratitude to God for creating exemplary women like Hazrat Zainab. Listeners from across the country actively participated by sending congratulatory messages and introducing their loved ones who work as nurses. The broadcast also references Hazrat Zainab's historical significance and her powerful speeches during the events of Karbala. The program concludes by encouraging the audience to express their affection for nurses and to uphold moral values within the community.
