In this radio program, Dr. Shariat-Zarshenas explains the concept of "paradigm shift," emphasizing the urgent need to transition from a neoliberal model to a revolutionary paradigm in economic and cultural spheres. He argues that neoliberal currents, which have dominated the executive branch for over four decades, have caused inflation, unemployment, and class disparity by reducing the state's role and promoting capitalism. Dr. Shariat-Zarshenas contends that these groups use oppositional media stances to evade responsibility for their past failures, now using the "paradigm shift" slogan as a strategy to escape accountability. By analyzing the JCPOA and negotiations with Trump, he demonstrates that the neoliberal approach in foreign policy has led to capitulation and the imposition of enemy demands. Conversely, the revolutionary paradigm, which has been successful in the military sector, must be implemented in the economy and culture to combat social selfishness and irresponsibility. He warns that continuing neoliberal policies and delaying reforms will erode public trust and damage social cohesion. Finally, he stresses the importance of distinguishing constructive criticism from destruction and supporting the government as a revolutionary and national necessity.
