This radio program examines the principles of Islamic criminal policy regarding the protection of human physical integrity.
The primary principle is the inherent dignity of humans, which God considers sacred and treats violations of it as major sins.
Another key principle is human free will, supported by Quranic verses that affirm the choice between good and evil.
Contrasting with deterministic Western views that see criminals as compelled by circumstances, Islam emphasizes full human agency.
Accepting this freedom necessitates accepting responsibility for the consequences of one's actions, which forms the basis of punishment.
Therefore, punishment in Islam is a logical response to a conscious, free choice rather than a penalty for a compelled individual.
This approach defines criminal justice through moral and social responsibility within the framework of dynamic jurisprudence.
