The radio program "Hekayat-e Azadegi" began by broadcasting the Supreme Leader's remarks on the occasion of the Law Enforcement Day. The main segment featured Hossein Hajarati-Zadeh, a former POW from Qom, who recounted his youthful efforts to join the front lines and his participation in Operation Khorramshahr and the Majnoon Island. He described the covert night maneuver behind enemy lines and his survival in a failed operation that led to his captivity. The show also highlighted the book "Azadeh-ye Darband" and aired patriotic poetry celebrating the freedom of the POWs. In the final section, statements by General Soltanabadi, the Chief of Staff of the Armed Forces, were played. He warned the US and the Zionist regime, declaring Iran's full readiness to respond painfully to any potential aggression. The program concluded by emphasizing the unity of the people and the military, expressing hope for Iran's ultimate victory.
