On the second day of National Population Week, the radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat" explored the moral dangers of anger and rage, relying on the philosophical principle of the unity of agent and action. Citing narrations from Imam Ali (AS), the guest speaker described anger as the worst companion, arguing that it is inherently evil and, when repeated, transforms a person's very identity into rage. This vice not only distances individuals from reason and logic but also makes them the worst companions to others, thereby hindering spiritual and moral perfection. In the second segment, the "Jor'eh" program interpreted hadiths from the book of Kafi to emphasize the blessings of gentleness and forbearance in social relations and financial management. Evidence suggests that softness and moderation lead to increased sustenance and blessings, whereas extravagance and lack of consideration result in ruin, even for the wealthy. Finally, observing the Friday night and the day of remembrance for the deceased, the program concluded with prayers and blessings for the departed souls.
