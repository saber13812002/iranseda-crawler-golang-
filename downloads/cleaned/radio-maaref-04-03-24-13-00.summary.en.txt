The radio program began with praises for Imam Ali (AS) and emphasized the importance of introducing his virtues in Islam.
The core discussion analyzed the Ahl al-Bayt's method of proving Ali's superiority through wise comparisons.
It was explained that comparing Ali (AS) with non-infallible figures is usually an act of arrogance, unless necessary to clarify truth or expose hypocrites.
The conquest of Khaybar was examined as an example where Ali's bravery exposed the cowardice of the hypocrites.
The second part detailed the Battle of the Trench and Ali's one-on-one duel with Amr ibn Abd Wudd.
This duel not only saved the honor of Islam but also removed fear from the enemy camp and restored the morale of the Muslims.
The speaker illustrated Ali's power and courage at that critical moment by citing verses from the poet Abu Tammam.
The program concluded with prayers for the preservation of the Islamic system under the Imam of the Age and condolences for the late nuclear scientists.
