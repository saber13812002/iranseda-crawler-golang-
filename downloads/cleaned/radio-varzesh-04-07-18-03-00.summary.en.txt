In this discussion, Dr. Khosrow Ebrahimi Nia emphasizes the concept of "thinking like a king" to highlight the necessity of continuous learning and genuine competence for high-level management. He argues that attaining managerial positions should not rely on nepotism or external recommendations, but rather on building the capacity to solve complex problems through study and action. Ebrahimi Nia shares the story of reviving Nader Ayvazi, a paralyzed Asian champion rower, as the most memorable moment of his life, where their collaboration led to an Asian championship and national pride. He also advises against envy, encouraging individuals to look up to successful role models who achieved their status through their own efforts. In the final segment, he discusses his commitment to true cultural development and his personal support for young people until they get married. He believes that knowledge and critical thinking, rather than material wealth, are the keys to success and inner peace. This approach has allowed him to focus on nurturing the next generation and serving society alongside his sports career. Finally, he notes that Iran holds a unique position in specialized fields like uranium enrichment due to its distinguished scientists.
