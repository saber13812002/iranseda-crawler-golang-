In this radio program, Professor Pishvayi utilizes authentic Sunni narrations to affirm the necessity of loving and recognizing the authority of the Ahl al-Bayt. By analyzing reports from Sahih Bukhari and Sahih Muslim, he argues that Ali (AS) refrained from pledging allegiance for six months following the Prophet's death due to Fatima Zahra's (SA) objection. Responding to listener inquiries, the speaker clarifies that Imam Sajjad (AS) remained in Medina and was spared from the events of Harra because Yazid ordered his troops not to harm him. The discussion on the six-man council highlights that the selection of members like Abd al-Rahman ibn Awf was not based on the Prophet's satisfaction but was strategically arranged to prevent Ali (AS) from being elected. The program concludes with the recitation of Imam Sajjad's prayer for rain, seeking divine mercy and protection for the people.
