In the radio program "Persoman Quran," Hujjat al-Islam Maleki addressed listeners' questions regarding Quranic concepts. Citing verses from Surah Al-Imran, he explained the distinct status of martyrs in the Barzakh, emphasizing that they are alive and receive sustenance directly from God. Regarding the ease of understanding the Quran, he clarified that individuals benefit from the text according to their intellectual and spiritual capacity, noting that scholarly differences are mostly in details rather than core principles. He also explained Imam Ali's advice to Ibn Abbas to avoid debating the Khawarij using the Quran, attributing this to their prejudice and suggesting the use of Sunnah and consensus for inter-sect discussions. On the topic of Velayat-e Faqih, although the specific term is not in the Quran, its roots were established through verses that negate the rule of infidels and oppressors, supporting the absolute authority of a qualified jurist. Finally, he affirmed that listening to the Quran is rewarding and spiritually impactful, highlighting that one can benefit through sight, hearing, and the heart.
