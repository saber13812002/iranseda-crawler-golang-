The Abadan Radio and Television Center has played a pivotal role in introducing Khuzestan to national networks by producing over fifty programs and featuring expert guests. These efforts have significantly enhanced the region's visibility in national media, allowing people across Iran to become more familiar with Khuzestan's identity and culture. The host emphasized the Abadani people's renowned loyalty, hospitality, and revolutionary spirit, portraying the city as a symbol of patriotism and adherence to national values. In the second segment, Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallah explored the concept of "Tawazu" (humility) from a theocentric ethical perspective, defining it as a sign of inner strength and spiritual knowledge. He described humility as an attractive and soothing horizon that even arrogant individuals are subconsciously drawn to, despite their outward pride. The key takeaway is that humility is an innate trait that manifests in human behavior and stems from a natural desire for moral virtues. This program aims to bridge local identities with spiritual values by promoting Islamic ethics and showcasing regional cultural heritage.
