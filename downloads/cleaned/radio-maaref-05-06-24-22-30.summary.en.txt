In this radio program, Hajj-Agha Amir Hashem Hosseini discusses the criteria for a Quranic family, emphasizing the vital role of verbal skills and etiquette in maintaining domestic peace. Citing religious traditions, he describes the wound of the tongue as more painful than a sword cut and identifies it as the primary cause of relationship breakdowns. He warns that many conflicts stem from misunderstandings and the use of absolute statements, recommending the consistent practice of reciting prayers as a behavioral skill to manage anger during critical moments. The speaker advises focusing on and magnifying the small virtues of family members rather than dwelling on minor flaws to foster gratitude and hope. The second part of the discussion explains the Quranic engineering of speech through concepts such as "Qawl Ma'ruf" (good speech), "Qawl Layyin" (gentle speech), and "Qawl Sadeed" (firm speech). These principles involve speaking kindly, firmly stating the truth, avoiding harshness, and adapting one's tone to the listener's capacity. Ultimately, relying on the "good word" as a source of blessings and avoiding the "evil word" is presented as the key to saving families from emotional and social crises.
