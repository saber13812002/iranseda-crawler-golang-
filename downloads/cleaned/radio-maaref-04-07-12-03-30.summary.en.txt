Ayatollah Khatmi discussed the concept of Tawhid in the Kimia program, outlining its eight dimensions such as oneness, attributes, and sovereignty. He emphasized the absolute uniqueness of God in all these aspects, citing Quranic verses to refute any notion of partnership. The second segment, Zamzam Ahkam, addressed various religious questions based on the rulings of different Maraji. Key topics included the permissibility of selling a Hajj ticket by a son and the correct method for paying fasting expiation for poor prisoners. The program also clarified the Khums obligation on read books and the ruling on accepting lunch invitations at funeral gatherings. Detailed Fiqh explanations were provided regarding the conditions for performing Aqiqah for children. The responses stressed the importance of strictly adhering to Sharia guidelines in financial and worship matters. This broadcast aimed to provide religious awareness and resolve legal doubts for Radio Ma'aref listeners.
