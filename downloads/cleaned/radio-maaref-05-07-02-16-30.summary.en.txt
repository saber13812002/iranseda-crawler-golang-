In this radio program, experts discussed Iran's nuclear strategy and the Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty (NPT).
They argued that formally withdrawing from the NPT is only meaningful if Iran has actually reached the stage of testing a nuclear bomb.
From a jurisprudential perspective, while producing nuclear weapons is primarily forbidden, secondary rulings based on national interest may apply in specific circumstances.
Threatening to withdraw from the NPT without practical backing will not serve as an effective deterrent and must be accompanied by genuine readiness.
Regarding diplomacy, the panel emphasized the need to instill fear in rivals while maintaining strategic relations and strengthening public diplomacy.
Concerning Yemen, its resistance was assessed as strong enough to not require direct Iranian intervention, although Iranian technical and political support continues.
Finally, control over the Strait of Hormuz and the Strait of Bab-el-Mandeb was highlighted as a powerful lever to pressure the US and Europe in the regional balance of power.
