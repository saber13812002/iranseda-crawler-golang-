In this program, Professor Pishvayi first discusses the reasons behind Imam Ali's (AS) transfer of the government headquarters from Medina to Kufa. He attributes this move to the necessity of confronting the Battle of Jamal in Basra and the geographical proximity of Iraq to Sham, which facilitated managing Muawiyah's situation. Additionally, the bravery and support of 12,000 Kufans during the Battle of Jamal were key factors in the Imam's decision to remain in the city. The discussion then shifts to the interpretation of Surah Al-Buruj and the story of the Companions of the Trench, who were burned alive by a Jewish king in Yemen for their faith. Finally, the causes of the Umayyad dynasty's fall and the rise of the Abbasids are examined. The Abbasids exploited two major Umayyad weaknesses: the humiliation and discrimination against Mawali (Iranians) and the exploitation of the Hashemites' oppression, particularly the martyrdom of Imam Hussein (AS). Ultimately, the Umayyad rule ended with the victory of Abu Muslim's Khurasani army over Marwan ibn Muhammad's forces at the Battle of Taimkanah in 132 AH.
