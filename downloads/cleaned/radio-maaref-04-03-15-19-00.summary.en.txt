This radio program addressed various jurisprudential issues, including the rules of impurity and its transfer to objects, the conditions for travel status affecting prayer and fasting for students, and the validity of wudu with artificial lenses or minor blood. The responses were based on the fatwas of religious authorities such as Ayatollah Khamenei and Ayatollah Javadi Amoli, emphasizing the necessity of strictly adhering to Sharia conditions for valid worship. The second part of the program focused on the significance of the Day of Arafah, highlighting the importance of repentance, avoiding lies, and maintaining trustworthiness, while also discussing the virtues of Hazrat Abdol-Azim Hasani as a promoter of truth and honesty among Shias. Narrations from Imam Reza (PBUH) regarding keeping Satan away from the heart and the importance of honesty in social relationships were also shared. Finally, reports from the shrine of Sayyid Mohammad Khomayni were aired, featuring interviews with a servant and child pilgrims that emphasized the spiritual atmosphere and the pilgrims' love for service.
