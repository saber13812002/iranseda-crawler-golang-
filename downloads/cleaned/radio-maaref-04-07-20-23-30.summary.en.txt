Ayatollah Ramezani reflects on his childhood and seminary education in Qom and Mashhad, emphasizing the importance of etiquette, relentless effort, and a diverse range of academic and family activities. He recounts his experiences studying under prominent teachers and his dedication to learning various subjects, even on weekends. Ramezani believes that enduring hardships and continuously questioning teachers are key to success in the academic path. In the second part, the necessity of expanding Islamic da'wah to a global level and using foreign languages to present an accurate image of Islam is discussed. He argues that with advancements in communication, preachers must be trained for a global audience and effectively utilize media resources. The significant role of women in seminaries and their active participation in education and upbringing is also highlighted as a key achievement of the Islamic Revolution. Finally, the need to correct the image of Islam in global media and provide precise, documented perspectives to counter distortions is addressed.
