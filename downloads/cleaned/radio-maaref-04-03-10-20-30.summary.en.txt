The final drafting of the Islamic Republic's constitution concluded in October 1979 after three months of work by the Assembly of Experts, despite opposition from some groups regarding the principle of Velayat-e Faqih. The constitution was subsequently ratified by over 99% of the public in a referendum held in November 1979. In the second segment, Karimi recounts his harrowing experience as a prisoner of war in the Ramadi camp in Iraq, detailing severe hygiene issues, inadequate food, and torture. He describes a specific incident where forty prisoners were vaccinated against typhus using a single contaminated needle, an event he attributes to divine protection. The news of Imam Khomeini's passing caused profound psychological shock among the prisoners, shattering their hope of meeting the Imam after the war. Finally, Hujjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasizes the importance of patience and gratitude in the face of adversity, citing the example of Ayatollah Esfahani's resilience following the martyrdom of his son.
