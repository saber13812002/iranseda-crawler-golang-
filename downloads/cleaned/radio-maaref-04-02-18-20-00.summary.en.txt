The night of Imam Reza's birth was marked by religious ceremonies in Najaf, Karbala, and The Hague. Yemen's Ansar Allah spokesperson stated that the agreement with the US to halt attacks on ships does not include Israel, affirming continued support for Palestine. Syria's new leader, Ahmed al-Sharaa, faced strong reactions from French parties during his visit to the Elysee Palace amid allegations regarding recent killings in western Syria. In Turkey, the arrest of Istanbul's mayor and the blocking of his accounts triggered widespread protests and the resignation of a ruling party member. UK Labour MPs urged Prime Minister Starmer to recognize Palestine, while Israel intensified its bombardment of southern Lebanon. In Gaza, the martyrdom of two more journalists brought the total media casualties to 214, exacerbating the critical drug shortage in hospitals. The dire situation in Gaza, characterized by severe medication shortages and the destruction of 90% of homes, has heightened international concern.
