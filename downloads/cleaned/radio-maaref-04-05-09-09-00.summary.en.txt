In this radio program, Dr. Abutalibi emphasized the necessity of avoiding the spread of false news and slander in the media, justifying this stance from both religious and national security perspectives. He distinguished between censorship and the free flow of information, arguing that constructive criticism and exposing corruption for public oversight are not only permissible but also religious and social duties. In the second part, addressing questions about regional security threats, he referenced both the old "Newtaphorates" concept and the newer "New Middle East" plans. Dr. Abutalibi warned that the main objective of adversaries is the disintegration of regional countries, including Iran and Syria, and that countering these threats is essential for both Islamic duty and national interests. Finally, the program addressed other general inquiries, such as the religious ruling on cosmetic surgery without medical necessity and the historical heritage of the Al-Huda house in Bushehr.
