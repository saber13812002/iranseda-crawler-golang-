This radio program continues the discussion on profanity and its remedies by analyzing a narration from Imam Sadiq (AS). The story describes a foul-mouthed woman who heals her character by eating a half-eaten morsel from the Prophet (PBUH), illustrating the power of divine intercession. The host emphasizes that relying on the Quran and authentic narrations is equivalent to the physical presence of the Infallibles, allowing for moral self-correction. Citing Quranic verses, the speaker identifies the Quran as a source of healing and mercy, urging listeners to engage with it sincerely to purify their speech. A crucial point is that true transformation requires an internal desire for change; otherwise, recitation and prayer remain ineffective. The program concludes by advising against making excuses for delaying the implementation of religious teachings. The ultimate goal is to cultivate noble character by utilizing these divine resources.
