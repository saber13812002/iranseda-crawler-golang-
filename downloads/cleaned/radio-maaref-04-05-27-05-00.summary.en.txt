The radio program begins by discussing a hadith stating that humans will be resurrected on Judgment Day with animalistic or inverted faces reflecting their worldly sins.
According to this narration, ten groups of Muslims, including gossips, usurers, and the arrogant, will be gathered in forms such as pigs, donkeys, or upside down due to their specific transgressions.
These descriptions align with Quranic verses in Surah Al-Isra and At-Tahaa that mention the resurrection of disbelievers and sinners as blind, deaf, and dumb.
The second part of the program addresses the jurisprudential issue of inheritance distribution between paternal and maternal aunts.
Although they differ in lineage, both groups are considered equal in rank under Islamic law, and the primary principle is equal division among them.
Imam Khomeini ruled that if the heirs are exclusively aunts, the estate should be divided equally, rather than giving males a double share.
When both paternal and maternal aunts are present with mixed genders, the Imam recommended caution and equal distribution due to the ambiguity in prioritizing one side over the other.
This jurisprudential analysis highlights the necessity of caution and equality in inheritance cases involving these specific relatives when scholarly opinions differ.
