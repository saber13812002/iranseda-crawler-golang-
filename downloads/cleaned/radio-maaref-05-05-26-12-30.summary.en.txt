The radio program "Hekayat-e Azadegi" begins by sharing the war memories of Qolamreza Fallah, a veteran of the Iran-Iraq War. He describes the harsh conditions in the trenches and the intense firefights during the Operation Argh. A chemical attack by the enemy resulted in the martyrdom of 51 out of 53 soldiers in his trench. Only Fallah and the sentry survived, though both suffered severe poisoning and temporary blindness. After attempting to wash their eyes in a nearby river, they were captured while navigating through minefields under enemy fire. This narrative highlights the resilience and faith of the fighters during the most critical moments of the conflict. The program concludes with a song honoring the freed prisoners and an introduction to the next segment of Radio Ma'aref.
