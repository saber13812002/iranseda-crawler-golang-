In this program, Hujjat al-Islam Panahian emphasizes the concepts of "self-dignity" and "collective identity" among the followers of Imam Hussein (PBUH). He argues that the uprising of Ashura is not merely a religious issue but a global symbol of dignity and resistance against imposed humiliation, a lesson even figures like Gandhi have drawn from. Panahian believes that Arbaeen and mourning rituals provide an unparalleled setting for forming this collective identity, where mutual love, respect, and deep belonging transform individuals into proud members of this community. By comparing this feeling to the experience of combatants in the Iran-Iraq war, he demonstrates that an emotional and intellectual bond with an ideal group makes hardships bearable and sacrifices sincere. Finally, he urges mosques and religious institutions to foster this sense of identity and belonging among youth and the public by creating a loving and respectful atmosphere.
