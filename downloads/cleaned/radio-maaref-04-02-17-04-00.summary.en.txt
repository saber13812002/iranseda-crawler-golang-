This radio program began by explaining the rulings of Tawaf during Hajj, including how to identify the Black Stone corner based on the fatwas of religious authorities. The show then addressed various listener questions regarding the sale of goods at current market prices, the use of Radd al-Mazalim funds, and prayer conditions for individuals with urinary incontinence. Additional guidance was provided on spending a father's money for charitable purposes and the validity of prayers performed at different times of the day. The segment emphasized the necessity of treating obsessive-compulsive disorder rather than relying on Tayammum, while also highlighting the importance of praying on time through relevant narrations. Finally, a hadith from the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) was recited, stating that being tolerant and kind to people is as obligatory as performing God's commandments.
