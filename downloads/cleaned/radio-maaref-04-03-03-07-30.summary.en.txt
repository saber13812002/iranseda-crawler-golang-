The program begins by revisiting the memories of the liberation of Khorramshahr in 1981, highlighting the pivotal role of the people and fighters in freeing the city.
The guest, Hojjat-ol-Islam Fallah, cites Imam Ali's narrations to describe anger not as a personal trait, but as an army of Satan's soldiers.
He explains that succumbing to anger equates to internal colonization by the ego and Satan, which deprives an individual of their intellect and dignity.
Professor Fallah emphasizes that anger, like external colonization, first penetrates the human soul from within and subsequently destroys one's ability to resist.
The religious ruling on anger is then discussed, characterizing it as a major sin and a primary cause of moral corruption.
The broadcast concludes with a patriotic song honoring Iran and its martyrs, while announcing the national media's coverage of upcoming commemorative events.
