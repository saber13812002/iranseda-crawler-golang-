In this program, Professor Mohammadi explains that wisdom is a divine gift granted to those who increase their knowledge of God and obedience to Him. He distinguishes between the earthquake at the end of the world mentioned in Surah Al-Hajj and the resurrection earthquake in Surah Az-Zalzalah, clarifying that while worldly life can reflect aspects of paradise, the true afterlife is distinct. The interaction with angels after death is determined by one's deeds, with believers and followers of the Ahl al-Bayt experiencing a merciful and peaceful reception. It was also noted that while repentance erases sins, it does not nullify the rights of others or the consequential effects of actions, which must still be addressed. The Q&A section covered the different levels of the human soul, the subjective perception of time in the Barzakh, and the methods for identifying the rightful Imam. Finally, the speaker emphasized that while charitable deeds reach the deceased, physically visiting graves offers unique spiritual and educational benefits for both the living and the dead.
