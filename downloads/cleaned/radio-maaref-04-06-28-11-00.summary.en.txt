In the news segment of Radio Ma'aref, the President and the Minister of Culture highlighted the poetic legacy of Shahriar and the conversion of his home in Tabriz into a museum. The report also announced the acceptance of entries for the Imam Khomeini International Prize and upcoming conferences honoring Ayatollah Mesbah Yazdi and Ayatollah Sheikh Mohammad Yazdi, focusing on the concept of Velayat-e Faqih. The exhibition of Iranian-Islamic stationery concluded its final day, celebrating domestic production and export achievements. The first anniversary of Ayatollah Mahfuzi's passing was observed with a ceremony praising his scholarly contributions. The Parsaiyan program emphasized the importance of high ambition in achieving spiritual and material goals, recounting stories of Ayatollah Mirjowani and Ayatollah Khomeini's dedication to study despite poverty. Finally, in the Zammam Ahkam program, Hujjat al-Islam Vahid Bor explained the Islamic rulings regarding oral secretions and potential bleeding after dental procedures.
