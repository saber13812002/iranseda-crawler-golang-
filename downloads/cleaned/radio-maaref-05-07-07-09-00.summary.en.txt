In the "Persaman Quran" program, Hojjat al-Islam Maleki addresses listeners' questions regarding Quranic concepts. He first explains the Quran's strong emphasis on God having no offspring and the concept of God being "Samad," which signifies absolute self-sufficiency and the absence of any flaw or void in His essence. Referring to the story of Prophet Moses and the king's daughters, he demonstrates that the father's guardianship and the daughters' implicit consent are derived from the literal context of the verses. Next, he interprets the verse "Baqiyatullah khair lakum" in the context of trade, linking it to the preservation of Zahra's lineage and Imam Zaman (AJ) based on narrations. He also discusses the verse about people ignoring the signs in the heavens and earth, interpreting it as negligence regarding the watchful presence of Imam Zaman. Finally, he explains that the "Thamud" were the kings of Yemen, and their mention in the Quran serves to illustrate the divine tradition of destroying those who reject the truth. The overall aim of these discussions is to provide a deeper understanding of monotheism, Imamate, and historical lessons.
