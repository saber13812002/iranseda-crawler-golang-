The radio program "Zamzam Ahkam" began with a recitation of a supplication before addressing listeners' religious questions. Regarding tayammum, Hujjat al-Islam Falsadeh clarified that verbal intention is not required; simply performing the act is sufficient. On the topic of Khums, Hujjat al-Islam Zein al-Abadiny emphasized that non-cash items must be calculated based on current market prices with reasonable accuracy, rather than rough estimates. Hujjat al-Islam Sheikh Shabani explained that if the eldest brother is a follower of a marja who mandates praying for the deceased mother, he must do so, though the son's prayer also discharges the obligation. Concerning the prostration dhikr, Hujjat al-Islam Mahmoudi stated that saying "Subhan Allah" instead of the Arabic phrase is permissible according to Ayatollah Sistani, provided it is repeated three times. Hujjat al-Islam Ali Nijat noted that water does not become impure if a guinea pig dies in it, as this animal does not have flowing blood. Additionally, Hujjat al-Islam Jahavari mentioned that eating qaza (missed prayers) while in a state of janaba is makruh, unless one washes their hands first. The program concluded with ethical advice on dealing with people and an interpretation of a hadith from Imam Sadiq (AS).
