In this radio program, Professor Mohammadi Sharoudi emphasized the necessity of awareness in actions, citing Imam Ali's statement that knowledge is the root of all good. He explained that most sins and immoral behaviors stem from ignorance, and that all forms of knowledge are beneficial for humans except for forbidden ones like sorcery. Responding to listener questions, the professor discussed the wisdom behind Imam Hussein not healing a blind man, the nature of human free will versus divine will, and the distinction between the goal and the means in martyrdom. He also addressed the concept of the endless spiritual evolution of the Infallibles, clarifying that they are constantly growing and that terms like "training" carry no negative connotation. Furthermore, the discussion highlighted the importance of religious knowledge and how empirical sciences can be transformed into divine knowledge by recognizing the Creator. The professor also stressed the severe consequences of harming one's neighbor, as emphasized in Islamic narrations. Finally, he thanked the listeners for their participation and announced the rebroadcast time and the upcoming program "Sarmashh."
