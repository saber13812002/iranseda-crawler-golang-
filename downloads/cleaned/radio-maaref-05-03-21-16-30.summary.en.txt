In this radio program, the suspension of parliamentary sessions and the delegation of authority to the Supreme Leader were criticized, with a call for officials to provide transparent answers rather than relying on security excuses. The discussion also addressed recent price hikes, attributing them to the war, profiteering, and the government's subsidy plans, while urging the judiciary to impose heavy, deterrent fines on violators. The continued Israeli attacks on Lebanon and Iran's relative silence were questioned, emphasizing that a lack of explanation erodes public trust in the authorities. Finally, the spread of hijab violations in cities and the failure to enforce the hijab law were strongly criticized. The guest warned that hijab violations are sometimes organized to undermine society and called for a firm yet respectful response to offenders and the groups behind them. It was emphasized that maintaining public order and enforcing social laws remain a primary concern for the people, even during wartime.
