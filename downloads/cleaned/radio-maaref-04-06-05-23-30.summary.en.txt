The radio program began with the recitation of religious poetry and specific prayers dedicated to Imam Reza (PBUH).
A reporter provided a live update from the Bab al-Hawaaj shrine, describing the heavy crowds and long lines of pilgrims.
He highlighted the spiritual atmosphere by referencing the verse "Blessed is he" inscribed above the visitors' heads.
A segment of the show was devoted to reciting the Ziyarat of the Ahl al-Bayt (PBUH) and prayers for the release of prisoners.
A significant speech followed, emphasizing the necessity of acting for God's sake and avoiding hypocrisy in religious and social duties.
The speaker argued that true independence and overcoming fear are only possible through reliance on divine power and trust in God's support.
He cited Iran's history of resistance to demonstrate that without God's grace, standing against global powers would have been impossible.
The program concluded with final prayers and greetings to the pilgrims and the listening audience.
