In this interview, Dr. Hossein Tabasi analyzed the challenges facing student and mass sports in Iran, emphasizing the need for dynamic systems with effective feedback loops. He argued that the current focus has shifted too heavily toward elite competition and medal hunting, neglecting the essential role of recreational and play-based activities. Drawing from his experience, Tabasi highlighted that student sports competitions serve a vital function in fostering cultural integration and motivation beyond mere athletic rivalry. He also discussed his efforts to promote mass sports and public health through inter-ministerial cooperation, while criticizing the recurring mistakes caused by frequent management changes. The paradox of skiing in Iran was examined, noting that talented athletes often come from low-income areas but lack the financial means to afford expensive equipment. Additionally, the shortening of the skiing season due to climate change and the federation's lack of infrastructure ownership were identified as significant obstacles. Finally, Tabasi advised that sports should be institutionalized at all ages and combined with proper nutrition to prevent muscle loss and ensure a healthy, active old age.
