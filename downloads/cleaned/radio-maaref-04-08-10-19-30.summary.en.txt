In this radio program, the necessity of justice and piety was emphasized based on Quranic verses and narrations, highlighting that justice must manifest in individual and social behavior. Subsequently, in the "Treasure of Happiness" segment, Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallah discussed the ethical indicators of supervision and evaluation processes. He stressed the importance of evaluators remaining free from moral impurities such as personal bias, envy, and discrimination to ensure objectivity. The second indicator was the necessity of possessing adequate expertise and awareness in the subject matter to prevent superficial or rude judgments. Accountability to God and society was presented as the third indicator, requiring pure intentions and avoiding materialistic motives. Finally, a comprehensive and multi-dimensional perspective on issues, rather than a reductionist partial view, was introduced as the fourth indicator. These points were presented to instill moral virtues and improve the quality of social and scientific interactions.
