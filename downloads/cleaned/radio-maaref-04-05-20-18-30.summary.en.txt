This radio program addressed various religious and hygiene issues related to the Arbaeen pilgrimage. It was clarified that blister fluid is not ritually impure, and entering the shrine with sanitary bandages that prevent blood spread is permissible. The Arbaeen ziyara is recommended, and reciting it from a distance is still meritorious if the shrine is overcrowded. According to Ayatollah Khamenei, a non-mahram entering the room during prayer does not invalidate the prayer unless it causes harm. Khums is not due on an Umrah ticket used in the same year, but it becomes obligatory if the trip is delayed. Women must ensure their voices are not heard by non-mahram men while reciting praises to keep their worship pure. Additionally, a wife is exempt from obedience if her husband fails to provide financial support, and she may seek divorce if he persists. Finally, security advice was given, including avoiding counterfeit money and safeguarding personal belongings during the journey.
