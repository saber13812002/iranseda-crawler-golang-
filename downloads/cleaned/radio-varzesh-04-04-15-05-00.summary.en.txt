Mohammad Ibrahimi Imami, a prominent veteran wrestling referee in Iran, shared his 40-year career in international officiating during this interview. He highlighted the emotional moment of watching Hamid Sourian, his former student, win a world gold medal in Budapest in 2005, noting their enduring mutual respect and friendship. Imami expressed pride in officiating alongside legendary referees such as the late Khalili and Rahim Azimi, including an instance where three Iranian referees worked simultaneously at a world championship. He discussed his participation in major events like the 2016 Rio Olympics, the Deaflympics, and the Jakarta Asian Games, while also detailing his executive roles as head of the referees' committee and the league organization. The referee addressed the financial challenges of the profession, emphasizing that his passion for sports, rather than the modest compensation, was his primary motivation. In the final segment, he revealed his love for the arts, mentioning his acting roles in the film "Parviz Khan" and series like "Setayesh" and "Paytakht." He concluded by discussing his current activities in Pahlavani wrestling and the recent publication of a book on its history and rules.
