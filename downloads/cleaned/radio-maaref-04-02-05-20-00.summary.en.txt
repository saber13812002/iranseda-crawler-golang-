The fourth international conference of Shia preachers was held in Karbala, coinciding with the first international Safinat al-Najat mourning festival, which attracted 550 participants from 20 nationalities. The Dublin Islamic Center announced the temporary closure of its mosque, alleging that the UAE is using European institutions as security tools by illegally collecting personal data of Muslims. Simultaneously, an international campaign to defend Quds was launched in Malaysia, mobilizing 900,000 mosques to exert global pressure against the Zionist regime. In New York, protests erupted against the visit of Israel's interior minister, while the International Criminal Court reaffirmed that the legal process to arrest Netanyahu and Gallant remains unchanged. Tensions between India and Pakistan escalated sharply following a terrorist attack on tourists in Kashmir, leading both nations to implement strict measures such as expelling diplomats and suspending trade relations. Pakistan rejected India's accusations of involvement in the attack and declared its readiness for an immediate response to any external threat. These events unfolded as the Ma'aref radio network concluded its broadcast with prayers for the appearance of the Mahdi.
