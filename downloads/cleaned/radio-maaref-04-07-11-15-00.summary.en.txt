The radio program "Rahbord," featuring Dr. Mehdi Meshkibaf, examines the strategic management of Imam Khomeini in confronting US hegemony.
A central theme is the pivotal role of the United States in the Iran-Iraq War, including its intelligence, military, political, and economic support for the Ba'ath regime.
The analyst emphasizes that Imam Khomeini viewed Saddam Hussein merely as a tool of the US, identifying the global system of domination as the true enemy.
This strategic insight shifted the focus from the immediate regional threat to the primary ideological conflict between Islam and global arrogance.
By leveraging opportunities such as Hajj statements and the mobilization of the Most Oppressed, the Imam exposed US human rights violations while strengthening domestic unity.
This wise management transformed a military crisis into an opportunity to solidify national identity and expand the discourse of resistance globally.
Today, the Imam's ideals, such as the liberation of Palestine, have become a global demand, demonstrating the enduring impact of this strategic approach.
