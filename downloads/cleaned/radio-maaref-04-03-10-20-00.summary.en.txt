The main segment focused on the historical drafting of the Islamic Republic of Iran's constitution. Dr. Hassan Habibi was selected to draft the initial principles based on a proposal by Sadegh Tabatabaei, who entrusted the draft to a German journalist during the Imam's return flight. Upon arrival, the document was handed to the Imam, leading to the formation of the Assembly of Experts. The constitution was finally ratified following the elections held in September 1979.
