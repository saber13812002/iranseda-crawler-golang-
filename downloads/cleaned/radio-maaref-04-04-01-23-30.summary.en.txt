In this radio program, the guest emphasizes that having a son or a daughter each offers a unique experience, criticizing the obsessive preference for a specific gender as a parenting error. He notes that every family receives different blessings from God and should be content with what they have. The discussion then shifts to postpartum depression, which, while not highly prevalent, can occur due to hormonal changes and the stress of pregnancy and childbirth. Symptoms include sadness, hopelessness, lack of interest in caring for the child, and sleep disturbances, requiring immediate psychiatric intervention if severe thoughts of self-harm emerge. Preventing these mood disorders requires attention to physical health, proper nutrition, and a healthy lifestyle during and after pregnancy. Treatment is achievable through simple and affordable protocols guided by counselors. The program concludes by thanking the guest for providing positive strategies to improve family relationships and move toward well-being.
