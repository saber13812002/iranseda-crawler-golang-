This program features Ayatollah Javadi Amoli's interpretation of verses 106 to 108 of Surah Al-Ma'idah, focusing on a historical narrative from the Prophet's era. In this story, a dying Muslim designated two Christians as witnesses to his will, but they concealed a valuable cup from the estate. After the deceased's relatives received the assets and discovered the missing cup, they filed a complaint, leading the judge to rule in favor of the deniers based on their oath. The commentator emphasizes that a judge's ruling resolves disputes but does not alter the objective reality of ownership, allowing for a new case if the truth is later revealed. The verses command witnesses to swear by God that they seek no reward for their testimony and do not conceal the right of God. If the initial witnesses are proven to have lied, two other just witnesses must take their place to testify. These verses serve as a warning that concealing testimony and taking false oaths lead to moral corruption and divine punishment.
