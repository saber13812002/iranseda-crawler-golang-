In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Barzoui emphasized taking refuge in the ship of Imam Hussein and relying on domestic power rather than external threats. He argued that Iran will not submit to pressure from the US and Israel, urging the public to trust in divine systems and maintain unity with the leadership. The host then reported on the recent attacks on three nuclear sites in Fordow, Natanz, and Isfahan, citing the Atomic Energy Organization's statement. The organization assured the public that there was no radioactive contamination and advised citizens to rely solely on official sources to avoid rumors. The latter part of the show focused on collecting messages from citizens across the country, particularly from southern regions and the front lines. Listeners sent texts and called in to chant "Death to America," expressing their firm support for the country against enemy aggression. This wave of public support was presented as a symbol of the Iranian nation's collective will and resilience in the face of threats.
