At the beginning of the program, Hojjat-ol-Islam Vahidi emphasized the necessity of respecting all of God's creatures regardless of their social or financial status, citing the conduct of the Infallibles. He illustrated this humility by sharing stories of Ayatollah Rohani's compassionate interactions with the poor and his own security guards. In the second part, Dr. Miri defined the concept of "Cultural Iran" as extending beyond current political borders, spanning from the Caucasus to Central Asia. He rejected a Eurocentric view of history, arguing that in Iran, religion and nationality have been in interaction rather than opposition, with Shiism playing a pivotal role in shaping the national identity. Dr. Miri also stressed the need to change the discursive space following the recent war, warning that repeating past mistakes and limiting the concept of armament to military tools could lead to strategic failures. Finally, he insisted on the importance of creating a "resilient society" and comprehensively arming the economy, education, and media to counter future threats.
