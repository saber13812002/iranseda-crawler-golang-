In this radio program, Professor Shakerin critiques cosmological views such as chance, the multiverse theory, and Hawking's no-boundary model, arguing that they fail to explain the "why" of the universe's existence and its philosophical necessity. He asserts that the theistic explanation is superior to other scientific theories because it aligns with the principle of simplicity and addresses fundamental metaphysical questions. Experimental science has inherent limitations and cannot transcend physical processes to explain the existential cause of the universe. Therefore, accepting the Necessary Being as the cause of existence is the only path that resolves philosophical issues and aligns with theological foundations. The second part of the program featured responses to religious questions from followers of various clerical authorities. Scholars like Hujjat al-Islam Fallahzadeh answered inquiries regarding the Qunut prayer, spousal permission, the Imam's intention in congregational prayer, travel rulings, and Quranic prostration. These answers were based on the fatwas of authorities such as Imam Khomeini, Ayatollah Sistani, and Ayatollah Khamenei. The program concluded with advice to consult reliable sources for jurisprudential matters and well-wishes for the listeners.
