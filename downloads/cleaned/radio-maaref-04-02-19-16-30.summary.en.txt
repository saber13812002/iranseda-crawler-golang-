In this radio program, a historical analyst examines the factors behind the fall of the Umayyad dynasty and the role of the Abbasids. He emphasizes that the Abbasids exploited the perceived oppression of the Banu Hashim and the martyrdoms of Imam Hussein and Zayd ibn Ali to incite public opinion against the Umayyad rule. Furthermore, the discriminatory policies of the Umayyads towards the Mawali (Iranians) and the limited support of Imam Ali (PBUH) for this group created social grievances that the Abbasids used as a springboard. The Abbasids' activities began in a political and propagandistic phase before shifting to a military phase in 128 AH. The turning point was the defeat of Marwan II (Marwan al-Himar) in the Battle of Talakim in 132 AH, which led to the complete collapse of the Umayyad Caliphate. Following the victory of Abu Muslim al-Khurasani's army, Abu al-Abbas al-Saffah became the first Abbasid Caliph. In the second part of the program, the shared lineage of Imam Ali (PBUH) and the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) up to their twentieth ancestor, Adnan, was discussed. Finally, citing narrations from Imam Baqir (PBUH), "Rafq" (gentleness) was introduced as the lock that preserves faith and a key to establishing healthy social relationships.
