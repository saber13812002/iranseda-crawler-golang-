In the radio program "Theological Questions," Professor Mohammadi addressed listeners' inquiries regarding the concepts of death and life after death. Citing the Quran, he explained that the term "fawt" does not imply annihilation but rather "tawfi," meaning the attainment of perfection and transition to a higher stage. According to his interpretation, humans experience two deaths and three lives; the first death is the transition from the world to the barzakh, and the second is from the barzakh to the Day of Judgment. Life in the world and the barzakh is temporary, whereas life in the Hereafter is eternal and permanent. Death signifies the separation of the soul from the physical body, not the destruction of the soul, which continues to exist in the barzakh with a celestial body. In the second part, the professor addressed a question about spouses in Paradise, emphasizing that heavenly blessings are not gender-specific and individuals can choose their preferred earthly or heavenly partners. The program concluded with the recitation of a prayer for rain and the provision of tips for conserving water usage.
