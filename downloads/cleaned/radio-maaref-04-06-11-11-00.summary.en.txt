The program begins by celebrating the 9th of Rabi al-Awal, the day marking the start of Imam Mahdi's (AJ) Imamate, and announces cultural events organized by the Mahdaviyat Foundation across Iran. In the news segment, the Speaker of the Parliament condemned Europe's attempt to trigger the snapback mechanism, stating that Iran's final decision to counter this illegal action will be announced soon. Additionally, the President issued orders to accelerate the reconstruction of homes damaged during the imposed war. In the jurisprudential section, a cleric outlined the Islamic legal conditions for medical experiments on humans, emphasizing the necessity of informed consent and the absence of life-threatening risks. He noted that even with consent, serious harm resulting from negligence remains subject to legal pursuit. Responding to listener questions, the ruling of Ayatollah Khamenei was cited, stating that fasting is not obligatory for individuals with vitamin deficiencies if it causes illness. Finally, another cleric clarified that there is no religious objection to men wearing women's clothing.
