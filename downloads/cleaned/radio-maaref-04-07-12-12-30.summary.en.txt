The program began by introducing Radio Ma'aref's live streaming, archive access, and contact channels for audience interaction.
It then featured a lecture by the late Professor Fatemini focusing on the four main questions asked on the Day of Judgment.
The first question concerns how one spent their life, illustrated by a parable of a thief who stole fruit over many years without being noticed.
The next two questions address the utilization of youth and the proper spending of lawful wealth in beneficial ways.
The most critical question is about the Wilayah of Ali ibn Abi Talib, which is described as the key to entering Paradise and accountability in the Hereafter.
The lecture clarified that the grave question applies only to believers and disbelievers of value, while the indifferent remain like worms in the Barzakh.
Finally, the importance of sincere repentance and heartfelt seeking of forgiveness was emphasized, highlighting God as the Most Merciful who calls even fleeing sinners back.
