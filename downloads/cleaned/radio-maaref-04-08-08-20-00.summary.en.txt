The United Nations has called for an immediate ceasefire in Sudan, expressing deep concern over the massacre of more than two thousand civilians in El Fasher. The fall of this city to the Rapid Support Forces, coupled with foreign interference, has intensified fears regarding the disintegration and collapse of Sudan's territorial integrity. Simultaneously, the Israeli regime is facing firm resistance from the Lebanese army after extensively violating the ceasefire and invading southern Lebanon. In Iraq, the upcoming parliamentary elections, involving over 21 million voters, are set to determine the country's future political trajectory and stability. Meanwhile, an Indian court approved the complete demolition of the Sanjuli mosque on the grounds of lacking permits, sparking serious concerns within the country's Muslim community. Human Rights Watch also criticized Saudi Arabia's new Grand Mufti for making offensive remarks against Shia citizens and exacerbating religious discrimination. On the economic front, Brazil and the Philippines emphasized the development of the halal market, which is projected to exceed 100 billion dollars by 2032.
