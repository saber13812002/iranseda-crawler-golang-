The program begins by congratulating the audience on the month of Dhul-Hijjah and the anniversary of the marriage of Hazrat Zahra and Imam Ali, emphasizing the duty of believers to facilitate marriages without excessive financial demands. It is advised that spouses should be chosen rationally based on faith and character, while the marriage itself should be nurtured with love and affection. The discussion then shifts to the moral virtue of humility, which is presented not as a sign of weakness but as a marker of intellect, inner strength, and spiritual peace. Humility is described as a soldier of God and a fundamental prerequisite for acquiring other virtues, standing in direct opposition to arrogance, which is rooted in ignorance and satanic influence. The creation of humans from clay and Satan from fire symbolizes the ancient conflict between humility and arrogance, where humility is the reason for humanity's divine selection. Finally, humility is interpreted as a positive form of "self-breaking" that smooths the spiritual path by dismantling internal pride and freeing individuals from the need for external validation.
