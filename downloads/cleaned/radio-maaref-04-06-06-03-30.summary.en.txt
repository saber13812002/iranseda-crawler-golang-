This radio program began by discussing jurisprudential details regarding wudu and ghusl, specifically noting that water must reach under non-standard nails, while trimming them is recommended. After providing contact information and introducing the team, the broadcast transitioned into morning supplications that emphasized the spiritual significance of the Fajr prayer and early morning devotion. In the main segment, Hujjat al-Islam Razani-Mir discussed the principles of self-mastery, heart vigilance, and tongue control, citing a narration from Imam Ali (AS). He explained that mastering one's ego leads to a peaceful heart, which in turn ensures righteous speech, highlighting the deep interconnection of these three elements in personal development. The program concluded with the recitation of Quranic verses from Surah Ash-Shura and Surah Az-Zukhruf, which underscored the divine guidance and the illuminating nature of the Quran as a path for human life.
