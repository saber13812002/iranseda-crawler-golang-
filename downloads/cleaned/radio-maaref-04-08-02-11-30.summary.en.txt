The radio program began by discussing the spiritual life and intellectual impact of Ayatollah Nayini in Mashhad and Isfahan, noting how his new arguments transformed the local academic atmosphere. The discussion then shifted to Mahdism, citing narrations about the flourishing of knowledge and the revelation of cosmic truths during the era of the Mahdi's appearance. It emphasized that divine light will not only be preserved but will reach its peak, enhancing human spiritual capacity to understand deeper realities. Referring to a narration from Imam Sadiq (AS), the title "Amir al-Mu'minin" was declared exclusive to Imam Ali (AS), while the Mahdi (AJ) should be greeted as "Baqqit Allah" during his return. The program also highlighted the importance of lawful earnings and their blessings in both worldly and afterlife contexts, drawing parallels with the story of Prophet Shu'ayb. Finally, the broadcast included a call to support the people of Gaza and aired the live Friday sermon from Tehran University, delivered by Ayatollah Khatami, which focused on the passage of time and the proximity of death.
