In the radio program "Persoman Quran," the host explained that according to verse 61 of Surah An-Nur, Muslims should greet themselves when entering an empty house. This act, known as "Fasallamu 'ala anfusikum," symbolizes the descent of divine blessings and serves to repel devils while inviting angels. The discussion then shifted to Surah Ash-Shu'ara, identifying liars, sinners, and misleading poets as the primary targets of satanic whispers due to their engagement in falsehood and fantasy. Conversely, self-reflection and daily self-accounting are highlighted as essential traits of the Ahl al-Bayt that protect individuals from becoming part of the devil's party. The program concluded by emphasizing that truthfulness and avoiding lies are crucial for maintaining spiritual peace and social harmony.
