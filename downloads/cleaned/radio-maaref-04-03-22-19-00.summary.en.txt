This radio program provided jurisprudential answers to listener questions based on the rulings of various religious authorities. The first section addressed the khums tax on rental properties and the ruling on urine impurity when certainty is lacking. It also detailed the rules for joining congregational prayer at different stages and the proper arrangement of prayer rows. Furthermore, the legal status of buying and selling free Hajj tickets and the obligations of travelers who cannot maintain wudu were discussed. The second part of the show introduced Imamzadeh Soltan Ali, the son of Imam Baqir, located in Kashan, and recounted the history of his martyrdom. Finally, the program featured the imam of Imamzadeh Hashem's mosque, who discussed cultural and social aspects related to this holy shrine.
