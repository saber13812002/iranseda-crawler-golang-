This program critiques inaccurate narratives and emphasizes the necessity of preserving the purity of religion while examining the role of Imamate in societal governance. The speaker argues that Imamate is a rational necessity to prevent political corruption and deviation from the prophetic path, which was challenged during the era of the Rashidun Caliphs and later by court-based jurisprudence. Imam Sadiq (AS) played a pivotal role in reviving the jurisprudential school and educating society by addressing doubts and establishing proof against opponents through his debates. The discussion also highlights the martyrdom of the Ahl al-Bayt and the importance of visiting shrines and praying to maintain a connection with this school of thought. In the concluding segment, Imam Khomeini's statements are broadcast, emphasizing the power of the Iranian people's faith and its impact on the international stage. The speaker believes that the awakening of the people and their adherence to religious principles are the primary factors for flourishing and resistance against global powers.
