In this radio program, a concerned mother complained about her children's neglect of prayer and hijab upon reaching puberty, prompting the counselor to emphasize fundamental shifts in parenting approaches. The counselor contrasted past and present generations, stressing the need for parents to upgrade their knowledge and avoid violent methods like force or threats in child-rearing. He strongly emphasized creating "acceptability" for the mother in the child's eyes, positioning her as a popular and successful role model rather than a tired, complaining figure. Managing peer relationships and placing children in healthy environments were presented as key strategies to mitigate negative external influences. In the second segment, practical tips were offered for preparing an older child for a new sibling, including creative storytelling and paying special attention to the elder. The counselor warned that mental comparisons and neglecting the older child's emotional needs are the primary causes of jealousy. Finally, it was recommended that parents foster a joyful atmosphere and avoid focusing solely on the newborn to reinforce the older child's sense of security and self-worth.
