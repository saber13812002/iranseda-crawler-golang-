In this program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verse 109 of Surah Al-Ma'idah, emphasizing God's role in preserving the secrets and dignity of His servants. He explains that prophets speak only based on divine revelation in religious matters and confess to truth alone on the Day of Judgment. When God asks the prophets about their responses, they acknowledge "We have no knowledge" and "You are the Knower of the Unseen," admitting they are unaware of the inner secrets of certain individuals that God has kept hidden. This divine concealment is not due to scientific complexity but to protect the modesty of believers and prevent their embarrassment before the prophets and angels. In some cases, even the servants themselves are unaware of their past sins to shield them from shame. Thus, the verse highlights that God, as the "Coverer of Secrets," safeguards human privacy and dignity even on the Day of Resurrection.
