In this radio program, the speaker emphasizes that the primary goal of all 114 revealed books and prophets was to guide humanity toward faith by controlling sexual desires and unreasonable demands. Citing the story of Prophet Noah, he warns that abandoning moral boundaries and indulging in unbounded pleasures leads to societal corruption and destruction. The speaker compares lust to a wild donkey that can only be tamed through "contentment" and proper spiritual training; otherwise, it drives a person toward eternal punishment. Referring to Imam Reza, he defines faith as a composite reality rather than a simple internal belief, consisting of "belief in the heart," "confession by the tongue," and "action by the limbs." Using the analogy of a tree, he explains that true faith is a living entity with multiple dynamic components, not merely an empty slogan. Finally, the speaker highlights the severity of modern divine trials and stresses the necessity of a strong foundation of faith and deep belief in the Quran and the Ahl al-Bayt to navigate these challenges successfully.
