The radio program began by offering condolences for the martyrdom of Major General Mohammad Saeed Izadi and the farewell ceremony held at the shrine of Hazrat Mahsumeh.
The host drew bitter parallels between this year's Muharram and the events of Karbala, identifying Israel as the "Yazid of the time" and emphasizing the greater commitment of today's Islamic Ummah compared to the era of Imam Hussein.
A report detailed the upcoming "Husaini A'zam" ceremony in Zanjan, which is expected to attract tens of thousands of participants and three thousand volunteers on the eve of Yom al-Abbas.
In the second segment, Hujjat al-Islam Behidpour addressed religious questions, stressing the necessity of adhering to jurisprudential standards alongside the expression of mourning.
He rejected the notion that the grandeur of Muharram justifies neglecting other religious obligations, urging the media to educate the public on the proper Sharia-compliant etiquette for attending these gatherings.
The scholar specifically emphasized the strict requirements of full hijab for women and the prohibition of sex tourism within these sacred mourning venues.
He called for proactive media education to ensure that attendees are well-informed about religious do's and don'ts before entering the ceremonies.
The program concluded with an invitation for listeners to participate in the upcoming Q&A session on religious rulings and a prayer for the reappearance of Imam Zaman.
