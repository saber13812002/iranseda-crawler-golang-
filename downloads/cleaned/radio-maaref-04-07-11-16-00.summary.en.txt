The program begins by referencing the carpet-washing ceremony in Mashhad before examining the historical records regarding the death of Hazrat Masoum (PBUH). The expert notes that ancient sources lack a precise date for his passing and show no direct evidence of martyrdom, although Abbasid pressure likely contributed to his demise. The second segment defines the "Prophetic Sunnah" as a practical model for life, recommending books by Mostafa Delshad Tehrani for further study. The absence of handwritten documents from the Prophet (PBUH) and Imam Ali (AS) is explained by the practice of dictation to companions and the need to preserve prophetic dignity. The discussion also covers the first Friday prayer, led by Mus'ab ibn Umayr in Medina before the Hijra and later by the Prophet (PBUH) at Wadi Rana. Finally, the program details the "Mihna" controversy during the reign of Caliph Mamun, highlighting the theological dispute between the Mu'tazila and the Ahl al-Hadith regarding the createdness of the Quran.
