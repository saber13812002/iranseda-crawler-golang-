In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the necessity of unity between Shia and Sunni Muslims, identifying colonial policies as the primary cause of their division. He cited the teachings of Imam Ali and the story of Ayatollah Mirza Shirazi, who sent a student from Samarra to Karbala to prevent potential sectarian strife, thereby stressing the importance of avoiding inflammatory public debates. The second part of the show featured a narrative from the book "Okeanos Mashreq," following a man named Aaman who, while seeking the water of life, travels with Hajj Karim the cobbler and his daughter, Rahleh. During their journey, a physician explains that Imam Reza is the source of divine love, using the metaphor of a furnace to illustrate that proximity to the Imam increases one's capacity for spiritual blessings. Aaman questions Rahleh about the justice of God regarding non-Shia access to paradise, to which she responds by recounting Imam Reza's definitive rebuttal to his brother Zaid's claims. The program concludes with messages supporting the people of Gaza and a Quranic interpretation of verses from Surah Al-An'am by Hojjat al-Islam Javadi Amali.
