In this program, Professor Mohammadi emphasized that in Islam, piety is the sole criterion for virtue, not gender, while critically analyzing feminist and materialist schools. He argued that these ideologies harm women's identity by ignoring inherent physical and psychological differences and forcing women into male-dominated roles, thereby undermining their sacred duties as mothers and wives. According to his view, social roles should align with individual nature, and respecting women does not mean replicating male behaviors. The second part of the discussion focused on the concepts of intellect, ignorance, and free will, noting that God created intellect to allow humans to surpass angels through growth, whereas ignorance is merely the absence of knowledge. It was also highlighted that human free will, including the capacity for sin, reflects the immense responsibility of carrying God's trust. The program concluded with news updates regarding recent political reactions and casualty figures, followed by an invitation for listeners to submit their feedback.
