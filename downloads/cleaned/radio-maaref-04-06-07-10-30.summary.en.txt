This radio program examines the active role of Imam Zaman (AJ) during the period of occultation from both individual and social perspectives. The Imam resolves personal and spiritual issues, liberating individuals from both physical and internal bondage. Simultaneously, he ensures the community's guidance by dispersing groups of falsehood and uniting those who uphold the truth. These activities occur covertly, beyond ordinary human perception, clarifying that occultation signifies anonymity rather than physical absence. The guest emphasized that Imamate and Wilayat are the greatest divine blessings, without which comprehensive guidance cannot be achieved. The Imam's status as "Hujjah" serves as both a clear proof of God's existence and the removal of any excuse for deviation. Consequently, believers must deeply recognize this blessing to stay on the path of divine guidance and avoid spiritual misguidance.
