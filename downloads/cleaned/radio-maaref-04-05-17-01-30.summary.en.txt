The program begins by refuting the claim that Takfiris are anti-Shia, noting that they have inflicted more damage on Sunni scholars and mosques.
Relying on Ibn Taymiyyah's ideology, these groups label not only Shias but also established Sunni schools of thought as deviants and infidels.
By rejecting traditional Sunni authorities, they claim to represent "true monotheism" and identify Shias as their primary enemy.
The second part of the program features a dramatic retelling from the book "Let It Be" about Har ibn Yazid al-Riyahi during the Battle of Karbala.
After regretting his role in besieging Imam Hussein (PBUH), Har seeks to atone for his mistake by approaching the Imam's camp.
He fights with great bravery in the battle and ultimately achieves martyrdom.
Imam Hussein (PBUH) accepts his repentance, calls him a free and brave man, and prays for him.
This narrative illustrates that even enemies can return to the truth upon recognizing the dignity and greatness of the Ahl al-Bayt.
