In this radio program, Professor Mohammadi addressed various theological and historical questions from listeners. He explained that sinners in the afterlife will only see the bad deeds of their relatives, though God's wisdom may conceal certain sins of their children. Regarding the concept of genetics in the Quran, he noted that Islamic narrations discuss the transmission of traits from mother to child, but finding a precise equivalent for "genetics" in the Quran requires further study. He also emphasized that obeying the Marja as the deputy of the Imam of the Time is obligatory, and disobedience to him is equivalent to disobeying the Imam. On the topic of eternal paradise, the professor argued that heavenly blessings are infinite and diverse, and human fatigue stems from worldly limitations rather than the nature of pleasure itself. Finally, he answered the question of why God exists by stating that God is the Necessary Being, self-subsistent like the saltiness of salt, and does not require an external cause. The program concluded with a detailed account of the Bazi Daraz operation during the Iran-Iraq war, highlighting the strategic importance of these heights for controlling the western front.
