This radio program covered Islamic world news, focusing on religious observances and political developments. Servants of the holy shrines in Samarra and other sacred cities decorated the sites for the birth anniversary of Hazrat Zainab (SA), while mosques named after the Ahl al-Bayt in Egypt received special attention. In Najaf, infrastructure projects have begun to prepare the city for the upcoming Arbaeen pilgrimage. An academic symposium was also held in the Caucasus to discuss Transcendent Wisdom and strengthen intellectual ties between Iran and Turkey. A significant portion of the news highlighted the strong condemnation of the Zionist regime's actions in Jerusalem and the West Bank by 14 Islamic countries and two international organizations. Protesters in Malaysia marched to condemn US support for Israel and demanded the establishment of an independent Palestinian state. International bodies, including UNRWA and the WHO, emphasized the urgent need to reopen Gaza crossings for humanitarian aid and patient evacuation. The broadcast concluded with a speech by Imam Khomeini on independence and public service, reinforcing the duties of those awaiting the reappearance of Imam Mahdi (AJ).
