At the National Champion event, the Supreme Leader's forewords for three books on the Sacred Defense were unveiled. These books narrate the lives of martyrs' wives and mothers using a blend of documentary and literary styles. It was also reported that 160,000 Rezaei volunteers are serving the public across 20 specialized fields. The President offered condolences for the passing of two mothers of martyrs, honoring their sacrifice. The "Martyrs from the Perspective of the Current Generation" event featured AI systems for animating images and recognizing martyrs' faces. The Deputy of the Holy Quran Center announced a national plan to train 10 million Quran memorizers. The first national conference on family support and population youth was held in Torbat-e Heydarieh, focusing on AI. Finally, religious rulings were provided in response to listeners' questions regarding prayer, khums, and other Islamic laws.
