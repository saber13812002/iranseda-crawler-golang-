This radio program examined a case involving the deliberate destruction of a tree on private property, where the public aspect of the crime was not established due to a lack of witnesses or video evidence. Judge Mohsen Akbari emphasized the importance of environmental protection and outlined the severe legal penalties for tree cutting, including imprisonment and financial fines. He also noted that insulting others in court can lead to expulsion and detention, and that any claim requires precise documentation to be proven. Since the intent was not proven, the case was resolved through the complainant's consent and mediation by local elders. The judge stressed the Islamic emphasis on reconciliation as a way to reduce court congestion and promote social peace. The program concluded with the recitation of the Imam Mahdi's ziyarat and Imam Khomeini's words, inviting listeners to join the night of vigil.
