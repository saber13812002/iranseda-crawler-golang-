The radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat" began with the recitation of the fourteenth supplication from the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya, which seeks divine help against oppressors. Hujjat al-Islam Fala then discussed the dangerous vice of "judgment," describing it as a factor that distances individuals from God's guardianship and brings them closer to Satan. He characterized judgment as a pervasive phenomenon that extends from criticizing God and prophets to judging nature and even oneself. Citing a narrative involving Prophet Musa, the speaker highlighted that even God has been subject to incorrect judgments by people. He emphasized that Shia believers should not view themselves at an animal or ego-driven level but should instead build themselves according to a divine and rational blueprint. The primary solution to this vice is self-improvement, refraining from judging others, and avoiding situations that invite judgment from others. Finally, he advised focusing on the love of the Ahl al-Bayt and prayer to place oneself in God's care rather than dwelling on personal problems.
