This radio program discusses the jurisprudential and exegetical interpretation of the phrase "Ahle al-Bayt" in the Quran. The speaker cites scholars to argue that the term is not limited to wives due to its general linguistic scope, requiring contextual analysis. He emphasizes the importance of "siyaq" (context) as a primary tool for resolving ambiguity, referencing works by Allameh Tabatabaei and Sayyid Mahmoud Alusi. Siyaq is defined as using surrounding sentences to determine the intended meaning of ambiguous or shared terms. Examples from verses such as "Indeed, man is to his Lord ungrateful" and "And the soul and that which proportioned it" are used to illustrate this method. These examples demonstrate how context clarifies pronoun references and specific meanings within the text. Ultimately, this contextual approach is applied to clarify the precise referent of "Ahle al-Bayt" in the relevant verse.
