Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi emphasized the importance of addressing youth issues such as marriage, employment, and housing, as well as the construction of Qom's airport, during a meeting with the city's mayor. He also stressed the need to integrate civil and cultural aspects in urban management, highlighting the global significance of the Fatima Masumeh Shrine and the Grand Mosque of Qom. Concurrently, the first National Conference on Islamic Revolutionary Management was held at Qom's Islamic Sciences University, featuring 150 selected papers from over 30 academic centers. News reports also announced the development of a comprehensive national plan to train ten million Quran memorizers, accompanied by the launch of new incentive programs. A historical analysis traced the roots of the "Death to Oppression" slogan to the Iranian people's resistance against British colonialism during World War I. The Minister of Education announced the creation of a comprehensive plan to promote prayer in schools, based on statistical research regarding student participation. A commemorative ceremony for Ayatollah Dehghani highlighted his role in signing Iran's first economic resistance manifesto. Finally, the "Zamzam Ahkam" program provided religious rulings on various topics, including prayer, khums, and marital laws.
