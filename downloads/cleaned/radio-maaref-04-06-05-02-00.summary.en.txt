The program begins by emphasizing the virtue of maintaining family ties, which Islamic narrations describe as a source of blessings, increased sustenance, and protection from calamities.
A narration from Imam Kazim (AS) is cited, illustrating that severing family ties can lead to a brother's death and a reduction in one's lifespan, unless rectified through reconciliation.
Ayatollah Azmankhoyi attributes his academic and spiritual achievements to his practice of maintaining family bonds and forgiving those who wronged him.
The story of a man who recovered a lost file after seeking his sister's forgiveness highlights the power of family ties in resolving life's difficulties.
The second part of the program features the memories of Ms. Yaqubi, the mother of martyr Mehdi Sabri, a defender of the shrines.
The martyr's mother recounts the moment her son received permission for martyrdom from Imam Reza (AS) and departed for Syria with peace of mind.
She describes his preparations for the journey and his consistent reliance on the Ahlulbayt (AS) for spiritual tranquility.
The program concludes by honoring devoted women and the mothers of martyrs, offering prayers for their well-being.
