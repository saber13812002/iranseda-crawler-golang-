In this radio program, the expert emphasizes the importance of maintaining boundaries and respectful behavior in family relationships, particularly between daughters-in-law and mothers-in-law. He explains that calm conduct, rather than harsh responses, effectively reinforces limits of respect and that behavioral change requires patience and gradual steps. The second part of the discussion focuses on sleep hygiene as a critical factor for physical and mental health. The expert recommends reducing light, avoiding screens, and engaging in spiritual recitation or quiet reading an hour before bed to improve sleep quality. He also suggests using deep breathing techniques to calm the mind and body, advising professional help if sleep issues persist. The program concludes with a religious and political message stressing sincerity in work, reliance on God, and resilience against the fears of adversaries.
