The radio program began by offering condolences for the anniversary of the martyrdom of Hazrat Fatimah Zahra (SA) and honoring the 300 unidentified martyrs whose bodies were recently repatriated. The first segment featured the story of Martyr Dr. Majid Shahrvari, a university professor known for his piety, ethics, and insistence on integrating the teachings of the Ahl al-Bayt into academic life. Subsequently, Professor Mohammad Hadi Falahatpour addressed the social disease of mocking others, analyzing its roots in arrogance, materialism, negligence of God, and hypocrisy. He cited Quranic verses and narrations to explain that mockery stems from ignorance and a bad nature, often serving as a tool for the hypocritical to belittle believers. The professor warned that such behavior destroys genuine friendships and leads to both worldly and divine punishment. He emphasized that remembering death and avoiding sin are essential deterrents against this harmful habit. The program concluded with prayers for the listeners, urging them to cultivate moral excellence and avoid the pitfalls of mockery.
