This radio segment explores philosophical concepts regarding mental existence and its categorical attributes. It explains that the mental form is essentially a quality, while other categories like substance and quantity are predicated upon it accidentally. The discussion critiques the view that intellectual perception is merely the intuition of abstracts, emphasizing instead that concepts exist within the soul's intellect. Subtle distinctions are drawn between primary and common predication, highlighting the simplicity of mental forms compared to complex external entities. The program then transitions to a spiritual atmosphere by reciting the Ziyarat Al-Yasin and religious supplications. Messages are shared regarding solidarity with the people of Gaza and the importance of the Quran and Sunnah as primary sources of knowledge. The broadcast concludes with announcements for upcoming radio programs and the playing of religious music.
