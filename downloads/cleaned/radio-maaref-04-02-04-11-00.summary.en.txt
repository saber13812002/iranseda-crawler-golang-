This radio program covered several key news items, including the Hajj and Pilgrimage Organization's preparations for 825,000 pilgrims, featuring a three-to-four-day reduction in trip duration and the addition of Taif airport to the flight routes. Reports were also shared regarding services for Arbaeen pilgrims at the Shalamcheh and Chazabe borders and the support of 111,000 patrons for orphans and children of martyrs by the IRIMC. In the archaeology segment, the discovery of thousand-year-old artifacts in the parking lot of Shah Cheragh shrine in Shiraz has halted the construction of an underground tunnel intended to connect urban subway lines. In the religious discourse section, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the virtue of humility as a primary form of worship, citing the humble behaviors of prominent scholars like Ayatollah Ta'ali and Allameh Tabatabaei. Finally, Hojjat al-Islam Vahid Boru discussed the jurisprudential aspects of late payment penalties, explaining that this issue extends beyond banking relationships to include delays in debt repayment to both individuals and legal entities.
