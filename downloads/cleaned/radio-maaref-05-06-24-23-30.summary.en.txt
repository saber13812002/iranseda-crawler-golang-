In this segment of the radio program "Kashtiban," a fictional story is narrated about an injured man who is rescued by a man named Jawahir on Mount Helwan after collapsing in the city square. Jawahir, who describes himself as a prisoner of this mountain, saves the wounded man through his kindness and reveals the secret of his hidden dwelling. The injured man is astonished by the majestic mountain view and the truth about Jawahir's life, which reinterprets his dream. Following this narrative, the program continues with the recitation of Ziyarat Al-Yasin and religious supplications. Finally, a speech is broadcast that emphasizes the importance of divine motivation in education and governance. The speaker explains that the primary goal of prophets was to implement divine justice for the sake of God, not for personal fame. The segment concludes with the repetition of Radio Ma'aref frequencies and the playing of a religious song.
