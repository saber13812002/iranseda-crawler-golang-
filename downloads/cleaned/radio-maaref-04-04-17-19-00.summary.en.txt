The radio program begins by clarifying that, according to the fatwas of Imam Khomeini and the current Supreme Leader, offensive jihad is prohibited during the Major Occultation, whereas the duty of defense remains obligatory for Muslims at all times. The show then addresses various religious queries from followers, including rulings on doubt regarding the obligation of ritual baths, the permissibility of hunting with firearms, and whether the bath for major ritual impurity suffices for other obligatory baths. It also discusses the etiquette of showing respect to the Quran during religious gatherings and the permissibility of using moisturizing and sunblock creams for those observing fasting. Additionally, the conditions under which the Maghrib and Isha prayers become overdue if one sleeps until midnight are explained. The final segment identifies obstacles to the vitality of mosques, such as unrealistic public expectations, internal conflicts among staff, technical issues, and a lack of receptiveness to criticism, while offering practical solutions. The broadcast concludes with a spiritual reminder to submit to God's will in the face of life's hardships and an announcement of the Maghrib prayer time.
