This radio program first addressed the jurisprudential ruling on visiting a non-same-sex doctor, stating it is permissible only out of necessity and limited to the required extent. It was clarified that washing the face with the left hand during wudu is valid, and while extravagance in water is a major sin, it does not invalidate wudu or ghusl unless it causes water to leave the limb. Other rulings included the obligation to perform prostration on the ground, the permissibility of reciting Bismillah in Surah Tawbah, and the legality of bank interest if Islamic contracts are observed. The discussion then shifted to the distinction between ethics and jurisprudence, emphasizing that true morality transcends fear of legal punishment and is rooted in conscience and pure intention. Finally, referencing the month of Muharram, the program highlighted Imam Hussein's (PBUH) letter to his brother, noting that his uprising aimed to reform the Ummah and enjoin good rather than seek political power. Muslims were advised to prioritize self-improvement through these lessons and, if unable to visit Karbala in person, to send salutations from a distance.
