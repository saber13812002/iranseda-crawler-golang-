The radio program "Man Fatima," hosted by Fatima, explores the virtues and etiquette of the Tasbih of Hazrat Zahra (SA).
It is narrated that the Prophet (PBUH) taught this tasbih to Hazrat Zahra (SA) as a substitute for a servant due to her physical exhaustion from household chores.
Imam Ali (AS) emphasized that he never omitted this tasbih after obligatory prayers since the day he learned it from the Prophet.
Imam Sadiq (AS) specified conditions for its effectiveness, such as reciting it before moving one's feet from the prayer position and maintaining uninterrupted focus.
According to narrations, consistent recitation can resolve issues like hearing impairment and lead to prosperity in both this world and the hereafter.
Ayatollah Khomeini (RA) also considered the Tasbih of Zahra (SA) to be the most excellent form of post-prayer supplication in his book "Adab al-Salat."
The second part of the program features speeches by Hojjat al-Islam Hosseini, analyzing the spiritual manifestations of Hazrat Zahra (SA) in Quranic verses and hadiths.
The program aims to promote the Fatimi lifestyle and remind listeners of the virtues of Hazrat Zahra (SA) through the Ma'aref Radio network.
