This radio program distinguishes between the spiritual allegiance to the Infallibles and the political submission to dictators. The speaker emphasizes that true Shia identity is not defined by passively awaiting explicit commands, but by possessing the intellectual capacity to discern one's religious duties. The historical migration of Hazrat Masumeh to Imam Reza serves as a prime example of Shias acting on internal conviction to support their Imam, even in his silence. This proactive stance is further illustrated by the companions of Imam Hussein at Karbala, who sacrificed their lives based on their own understanding of the situation. The discussion also highlights the Quranic warning against taking tyrants as protectors, contrasting this with the pure monotheism required of believers. Citing Sayyid Hassan Nasrallah, the speaker notes that while the jurist preserves the dignity of the community, individual Shias must also engage in critical thinking and consultation. Ultimately, the program calls for a Shias who actively understand and fulfill their responsibilities during the occultation of the Imam.
