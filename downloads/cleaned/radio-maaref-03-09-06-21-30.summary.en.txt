The program begins by interpreting the Quranic verse "Indeed, to Allah belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth" as a comprehensive principle of monotheism. The commentator uses this concept of God's absolute ownership to refute five groups of heretics, ranging from atheists to those who associate partners in worship. The discussion then shifts to critiquing the Christian doctrine of the Trinity, which the Quran explicitly condemns as disbelief and a denial of God's absolute oneness. In contrast, the interpretation of "four" is presented as pure monotheism, affirming God's unity without any partners in essence or attributes. The text also distinguishes between ordinary creation and the unique creation of Jesus (PBUH) through the divine command "Be, and it is." Finally, the concept of "the poor of the Messenger of Allah" is explained to emphasize gratitude toward God's intermediaries and to avoid associating partners in worship. This exegesis demonstrates that true monotheism involves comprehensively recognizing God's ownership over all existence and strictly avoiding any form of polytheism in essence, creation, or lordship.
