This radio program provided various jurisprudential answers to listeners' questions. It was explained that illnesses preventing washing make tayammum permissible, while specific recitations in sitting prayer must be intended generally. It is recommended to recite the prayer "Ya Khayr al-Mus'alina" during prostration. Sediment at the bottom of a water tank does not make the water impure unless it is mixed with the liquid. Cleaning a najis sofa with direct sunlight is not permissible, as sunlight only purifies fixed structural components. Interrupting prayer to extinguish a gas fire is considered a valid necessity, requiring the prayer to be restarted from the beginning. Pregnancy alone does not permit performing missed prayers in a sitting position; they must be done normally after delivery. Finally, the importance of prayer as a key to accepted supplications and deeds was emphasized, urging listeners to pray with attention, proper wudu, and allegiance to the Ahl al-Bayt.
