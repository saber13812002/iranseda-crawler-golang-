In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the importance of detachment from the material world and practicing contentment, citing the simple lifestyle of Ayatollah Behjat as a prime example. He illustrated that avoiding extravagance and being satisfied with necessities are essential for maintaining spiritual peace and human dignity. In the second segment, Professor Faqih discussed the methods for achieving a spiritual state during prayer and gaining knowledge of God through a monotheistic perspective. He explained that observing the signs of creation, as mentioned in the Quran, reminds humans of their Creator and facilitates a deeper spiritual connection. Responding to a listener's question about diminished spiritual blessings and financial difficulties, Professor Faqih highlighted the direct correlation between piety and material prosperity. He advised that addressing these issues requires both a commitment to spiritual practices and a lifestyle change centered on contentment and the avoidance of waste. Ultimately, contentment was presented not only as a moral virtue but also as an infinite capital for achieving true well-being and tranquility in life.
