The radio program begins by explaining that, from a philosophical perspective, the existential structure of the Infallible is fundamentally the same as that of ordinary humans, relying on knowledge and will, but distinguished by a higher degree of perfection that prevents error. The host then narrates the famous story of Abu Dharr al-Ghifari, where a lion protected his sheep during prayer by divine command, illustrating the concept of "establishing prayer" as maintaining spiritual presence against Satan's whispers. This narrative demonstrates that performing prayer with full devotion can lead to miraculous outcomes and elevate the believer's spiritual rank. The speaker emphasizes that faith in the unseen has various levels, encouraging Muslims to deepen their faith to reach higher spiritual stations, similar to that of Salman al-Farisi. The discussion also touches upon the concept of the Imams' knowledge of the unseen, noting that it is will-based and may be limited in specific details by divine will. Finally, the host congratulates the audience on the start of the Week of Unity and invites them to listen to the Quranic interpretation program titled "Tabian."
