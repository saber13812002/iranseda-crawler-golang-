The radio program began with a theological discussion on the validity of the silent Imam's stance and the relationship between obeying the Prophet and obeying God.
It was explained that rejecting divine verses does not necessarily mean rejecting God's essence, as polytheists may accept God but err in their understanding of Him.
The show then highlighted World White Cane Day and the day of Imam Reza (AS), emphasizing the need for spiritual and physical purification before approaching the holy presence.
A key segment focused on showcasing the capabilities of the visually impaired and encouraging listeners to empathize by temporarily experiencing blindness.
The program concluded with congratulations to the blind community and appreciation for their efforts in various social and economic fields.
Historical events of the day, including the passing of Allameh Fays Kashani, were also mentioned, with a strong emphasis on gratitude for God's blessings.
Finally, listeners were invited to send messages sharing the successes of visually impaired individuals to express their solidarity.
