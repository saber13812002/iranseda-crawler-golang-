In the radio program "Tale of Freedom," Hassan Nouri recounts his captivity during the Fath al-Mubin operation and his escape attempts from Iraqi forces. He describes the critical moments spent motionless in a battlefield crater to avoid detection and his rescue by his comrade, Seyed Mousa, who used his fluency in Arabic to prevent them from being shot by Iraqi soldiers on multiple occasions. Despite their repeated efforts to flee, they were eventually captured after their military uniforms were identified and Nouri's lack of Arabic proficiency was exposed. The program also introduces the book "I Am Alive" by Masoumeh Abad, which details the experiences of women held captive during the war. The broadcast concludes by emphasizing the importance of national security being maintained by indigenous forces and honoring the freedom fighters who sacrificed their lives for their homeland.
