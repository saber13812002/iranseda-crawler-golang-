The program begins by interpreting Quranic verses, emphasizing the role of metaphors and stories in conveying religious concepts.
It highlights the stubbornness of disbelievers against the truth, using the story of Walid ibn Mughirah as an example of resistance.
The second part follows a narrative from the book "Oceans of the East," where the character "Amaran" is in search of the water of life.
Amaran travels with Hajj Karim the cobbler and his blind daughter, Rahleh, and seeks refuge with a physician after being rescued from bandits.
The physician reveals Amaran's deep infatuation with Rahleh by describing the charisma and spirituality of Imam Reza (AS).
Despite never seeing Rahleh's face, Amaran is profoundly affected by her words and presence, confessing his state of being smitten.
Finally, the physician identifies the Imam as the divine beloved and guides Amaran to understand that Imam Reza (AS) is the proof of God for all humanity.
This symbolic narrative illustrates that true recognition of the Imam transcends physical appearance and requires deep spiritual and heartfelt understanding.
