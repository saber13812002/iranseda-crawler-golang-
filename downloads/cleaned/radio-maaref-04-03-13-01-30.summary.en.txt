The program begins by highlighting the moral and social characteristics of Shias, such as spending in the path of Wilayah, brotherhood, visiting each other to revive religious matters, anger management, and avoiding extravagance.
A key point in this section is the emphasis on creating peace at home and refraining from verbal and physical violence in family relationships.
The speech then addresses the martyrdom of Imam Baqir (AS) and the grand seventh-day ceremony in Medina, comparing it to the humble night burial of Imam Ali (AS).
Subsequently, the narrative of the novel "Left Behind by the Son" begins, focusing on the historical uprising of people in protest against the arrest of Imam Khomeini (RA).
The story centers on Salareh, a man who, after his divorce, joins armed groups in Varamin to earn money.
Salareh encounters a wounded, hidden Turkic-speaking man in a wheat field who is fleeing from pursuers.
Upon discovering that this man is likely one of the protesters, Salareh confronts him in the dark night, leaving the story at a point of high suspense.
