The program begins by emphasizing the religious obligation of showing utmost respect and etiquette towards parents, citing various hadiths and examples.
It highlights the exemplary behavior of Islamic leaders, such as Imam Khomeini and Ayatollah Khoei, who consistently prioritized their parents' comfort and status.
The second part features the personal memories of Fatemeh Sheikhi, who recounts her deep bond with her brother, the martyr Mohammad Sheikhi.
Mohammad joined the Iran-Iraq war as a teenager and remained on the front lines despite sustaining severe injuries, including the loss of his right eye.
His mother, after praying for his recovery from chemical mine injuries, ultimately accepted his martyrdom as a divine blessing.
Fatemeh describes her brother's selfless character, noting how he chose the most dangerous front lines to spare other families from loss.
The episode concludes with the recitation of the martyr's poetry, celebrating the dedication and spiritual love of the young generation during the Sacred Defense.
