In this program, former POWs shared their memories of captivity in Iraq, including air raids, arrests, and the harsh conditions of prison.
They emphasized that while the front lines and sieges were difficult, the hardest task is performing deeds purely for the sake of God.
Ayatollah Javadi Amoli described sincerity as the soul of righteous action, stating that without it, worship is like a lifeless body.
The lives of the freed prisoners in the camps, such as sharing food with the injured and praying at night in the dark, exemplified this sincerity.
The second part of the program featured the recitation of the Dua of Nuthr from the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya for the oppressed, followed by a hadith from the Book of Kafi.
Imam Sadiq (AS) guaranteed four houses in Paradise for those who fulfill four conditions: spending without fear of poverty, spreading peace, avoiding quarrels, and being just.
The speaker highlighted the importance of stopping pointless arguments in family and social relations, emphasizing the need for justice and fairness.
The program concluded with an invitation to listen to other Radio Ma'aref shows and to answer listeners' theological questions.
