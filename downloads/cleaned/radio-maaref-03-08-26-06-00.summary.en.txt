This program analyzes Quranic verses regarding Prophet Jesus (PBUH) and his status after his departure.
Referencing Surah Al-Ma'idah, the interpreter defines "Tawafay" as death, linking it to the divine dialogue in the Hereafter.
The verbs "Mutawfikah," "Raf'ah," and "Mutahirah" in Surah An-Nisa are examined to clarify their specific theological meanings.
It is concluded that "Raf'ah" does not imply geographical relocation or purely spiritual elevation, but a state compatible with his status as a resolute prophet.
Jesus's testimony in the Hereafter regarding his community reflects God's inherent attributes of being the Knower of the Unseen and the Watcher.
Historical disputes about Jesus's birth and death stemmed from human extremism and negligence, which the Quran refutes to establish his truth.
The text notes that the Israelites were divided into believers and disbelievers during Jesus's lifetime, with God supporting his allies.
Finally, the verse in Surah As-Saff encourages Muslims to emulate the Habiriyyun as true allies of God in defending the faith.
