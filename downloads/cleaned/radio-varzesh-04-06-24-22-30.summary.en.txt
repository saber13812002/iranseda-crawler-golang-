Former Iranian national team player Mohsen Gharaji recounts his experiences during the 1989 Futsal World Cup in Hong Kong. He recalls finishing fourth in the tournament and receiving the Fair Play Trophy and the Most Technical Player award. Gharaji expresses gratitude for the warm support of the Iranian community residing in Hong Kong during that period. He also mentions a wrist injury sustained just before the team's departure, which prevented him from being in the final squad. The player discusses the qualifying matches for the 1998 World Cup, highlighting painful defeats against Japan and Australia. He critiques the tactical decisions and management issues that affected the team's performance during those years. Gharaji shares his career highlights with clubs such as Pas Tehran, Esteghlal, and Sepahan, including winning the Asian Club Championship with Pas in 1991. He reflects on the administrative and financial challenges he faced as a professional athlete and his decision to retire in 1999. Finally, he explains why he stepped away from coaching, feeling that the role did not align with his personality.
