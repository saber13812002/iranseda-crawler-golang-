In this radio program, the hosts expressed condolences regarding the martyrdom of the former president while discussing religious approaches to coping with grief. Dr. Ghalam Ali outlined three key strategies: strengthening faith in God, maintaining piety by avoiding impulsive reactions, and giving charity to alleviate sorrow. In the second segment, Professor Falah Bastani described anger as a "spark of Satan's fire" that serves as the gateway for evil to enter the human soul. He explained that during moments of rage, human reason weakens while Satan's influence intensifies, leading to hasty and destructive decisions. The scholar characterized anger as a manifestation of hell in the level of the ego, noting that it destroys personal relationships and inner security. He emphasized that controlling the ego is essential to prevent Satan from dominating one's will and causing lasting harm to both the individual and society.
