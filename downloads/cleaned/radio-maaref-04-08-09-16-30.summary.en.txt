In this radio program, Professor Hassan emphasizes the necessity of countering the enemy's cultural and media plots through "awakening" to preserve national and religious identity. Citing Quranic verses regarding the enemy's open hands and tongues, he warns that global arrogance seeks to dismantle Islamic values through both material blows (economic and military) and linguistic attacks (cultural and psychological). The discussion addresses the role of cyberspace, noting that while hardware has expanded, it can become a tool for corruption if not managed with proper content and software reinforcement. The speaker refutes four common excuses against anti-arrogance, such as denying the concepts of justice and tyranny, doubting the necessity of resistance, and claiming inability to fight, stressing the human and religious duty to stand against oppression. By referencing the spiritual victory of Karbala and the success of the Islamic Revolution, he demonstrates that resistance leads to spiritual triumph even in conditions of inequality. Finally, he advises focusing on producing healthy content and strengthening the society's spiritual and cultural fabric, rather than merely relying on tools, so that people's lives truly reflect the "color of God."
