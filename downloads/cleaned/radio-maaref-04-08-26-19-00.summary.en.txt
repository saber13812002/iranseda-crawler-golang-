This program emphasizes the social mission of Hazrat Zahra (SA) in guiding public opinion by engaging with community elites, rather than limiting her role to domestic affairs.
Citing narrations from the book of Kafi, the speaker defines true worship and Muslim identity as being deeply concerned with the welfare and distress of other Muslims.
The discussion highlights women's social intelligence and soft power, arguing that they play a pivotal role in managing public opinion and maintaining family cohesion.
It warns that enemies of Islam focus on undermining the pillar of women and the family to destabilize society from within.
The program urges women to recognize deceptions and utilize their potential for social correction and advising the community.
This approach presents a model of courage and social responsibility that transcends traditional domestic roles.
Ultimately, the goal is to encourage the audience to become active managers of public opinion by following the path of the Infallibles.
