This radio program examines the pivotal role of Imam Sajjad (AS) in preserving the legacy of the Ashura uprising and transmitting its message to future generations.
The speaker highlights Imam Sajjad's spiritual status as the son of Fatima and the leader of the pious, referencing a poem by Farazdaq.
The presence of the Ahl al-Bayt, particularly Lady Zainab and Imam Sajjad, was crucial in maintaining the identity of Karbala and exposing the tyranny of the Umayyads.
Through his intense weeping, fasting, and sermons, Imam Sajjad immortalized the martyrdom and greatness of his brother, Imam Hussein (AS).
Having witnessed the tragic scenes of Karbala firsthand, including the severed hands of his uncle Abbas (AS), he constantly reminded the public of the family's sacrifices.
The second part of the broadcast narrates the story from the book "Nishan-e Hassan" regarding the decision of Imam Hassan's (AS) children to join Imam Hussein's (AS) caravan.
This section emphasizes the absolute loyalty of the subsequent generations to the authority of the Ahl al-Bayt and their readiness to stand for justice despite the dangers.
