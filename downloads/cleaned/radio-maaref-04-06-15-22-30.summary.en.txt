This segment of the program first explains the jurisprudential rulings regarding revocable divorce and the waiting period. Based on the first verse of Surah At-Talaq, the wife must remain in the home during this time and refrain from leaving, even with her husband's permission, to allow for the possibility of his regret and return. If the husband revokes the divorce, it is annulled without the need for a new marriage contract, but after the waiting period ends, the couple becomes non-mahram to each other. The discussion then shifts to the topic of returning rights to people during the month of Rabi al-Awwal. According to the permission granted by religious authorities, believers can pay up to 1,500,000 Tomans to the poor to compensate for past wrongs, provided the cash is given directly to the poor and not to dependents such as children or spouses. It was also emphasized that debts owed to the government must be paid separately to the relevant accounts, and that "rights of people" include issues like under-selling, property damage, and backbiting. Finally, the program concluded with the recitation of verses from Surah Al-Anfal and a prayer for the birth of Imam Sadiq (AS).
