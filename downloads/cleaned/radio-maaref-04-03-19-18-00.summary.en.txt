In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Bagherian emphasizes, based on Quranic verses, that "arrogance" and "greatness" are exclusively attributes of God. Using the analogy of sunlight, which is colorless but takes on the color of objects it hits, he explains that any human claim to greatness or independence stems from a deviation from divine grace and is rooted in Iblis's arrogance. The Prophet and the Ahl al-Bayt have warned against wearing the "robe of divine greatness," which fits only God. The discussion then shifts to the spiritual effects of worldly actions, specifically the concept of "being beloved by God," which offers a joy surpassing popularity among people. To achieve this divine love, reciting the Quran with a moderate voice at home and sharing God's word with family is recommended as a spiritual and educational practice. This act not only prevents Satan's whispers and hypocrisy but also leads to God's pleasure and inner peace for the individual.
