In this program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli begins his exegesis of the first verses of Surah Al-An'am by examining its historical and narrational context. Relying on internal and external evidence, he argues that the surah was revealed in a single, coherent session. Given the primary focus on monotheism and the refutation of polytheism, he concludes that the surah was revealed in Mecca. A significant point derived from narrations is that seventy thousand angels accompanied the revelation of this surah due to the frequent mention of God's names. The speaker emphasizes that merely citing narrations is insufficient for establishing exegetical facts; one must rigorously verify the authenticity and implication of the sources. Interpreting the first verse, he highlights the unity of creation and the reality of the afterlife, noting that God is aware of both the manifest and the hidden actions of His servants. Finally, he explores the deeper meaning of "heavens" in the Quran, suggesting it refers to manifestations of divine power beyond mere physical celestial bodies.
