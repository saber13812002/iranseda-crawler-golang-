Mohsen Ashouri, a karate veteran, discussed the national team's historic 2003 world championship victory in a radio interview, noting it was achieved despite restrictions on women's participation. He criticized political interference and the appointment of non-specialist managers in sports federations, emphasizing the need for decentralized authority and horizontal management in the National Teams Organization. Ashouri argued that technical committees should consist of the most up-to-date experts but often serve merely as tools to legitimize managers' decisions. He recounted memories from the Hashemi Rafsanjani era, highlighting the strict physical fitness tests required for team travel. On a personal note, Ashouri shared his early marriage at age 19 and his children's continued involvement in karate. He also expressed his passion for football and his support for the Esteghlal club, recalling several of its legendary players. Finally, Ashouri emphasized karate's role in instilling respect and discipline, expressing his wish for Iranian karate to gain global recognition and bring honor to the country.
