This radio program emphasizes the value of human beings and the necessity of caring for the hearts of those around us as part of the path to God.
According to narrations, when believers embrace and reconcile, Satan screams in anger, whereas their quarrels and separation cause him to laugh, as this distance them from God.
The behavior of Imam Hussein (PBUH) was cited as a model, where he took the initiative to reconcile with his half-brother, Muhammad ibn Hanafiyya, despite being the elder.
The mercy and forgiveness of believers were highlighted through the story of Imam Baqir (PBUH), who freed his servant after the death of his child.
In the second part, using a factory analogy, it was explained that accepting Islam means submitting to divine laws, making the claim of "no compulsion" invalid after one has already embraced faith.
Finally, the program stressed a crucial Quranic point: befriending disbelievers and fearing their threats are primary causes of God's anger.
This behavior is considered a deviation from monotheism and a form of worshipping idols, which harms the believing community.
The program concluded with congratulations for the Ten Days of Karameh and prayers for all families.
