In this radio program, Dr. Mehdi Meshkibaf refutes the claim of Iranian involvement in the Khobar Towers bombing by citing US officials' admissions that it was an al-Qaeda operation. He emphasizes the American strategy of "soft subversion" and cultural aggression in the 1970s and 1980s, which aimed to change Iran's power structure from within by targeting the middle class. Meshkibaf describes negotiations with the US not as a solution to the conflict, but as a tool to demonstrate the system's hegemonic nature and political deception. He stresses the need for unity, faith, and reliance on reason and science to build immunity against future threats, noting the failure of the enemy's cultural and military attacks. Finally, the program addresses the severe drought and reduced rainfall, urging citizens to manage water consumption as a religious and social necessity.
