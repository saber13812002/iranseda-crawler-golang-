The primary focus of this radio segment is the Quranic concept of "Sunnat al-In'ikas" (the law of reflection), which asserts that any injustice committed against the vulnerable will ultimately return to the oppressor. The speaker argues that the global community, fully aware of Israel's historical aggression in Syria, Lebanon, and Gaza, is now showing unprecedented support for Iran in response to recent attacks. This widespread international solidarity, including expressions of joy from some Europeans regarding Iran's defense, is presented as evidence of the moral and strategic defeat of the adversary. Drawing a parallel to the story of Moses and Pharaoh, the speaker suggests that the enemy's attempts to stage a global humiliation for Iran have inadvertently set the stage for their own downfall. The power of the West and the "falsehood" (batil) is described as insignificant and negligible when compared to the infinite power of God and the "truth" (haqq). Furthermore, referencing the Battle of Uhud, the text illustrates that even when defeat seems apparent, a will connected to God can lead to the enemy's chaotic retreat. The core message is that the next generation must understand that truth is eternal in history, while falsehood, despite its temporary strength, is destined for ultimate destruction.
