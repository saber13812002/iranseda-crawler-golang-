In the radio program "Persoone Enghelab," Dr. Mehdi Aboutalibi analyzed the reasons behind the hostility of the West and Israel toward Iran, arguing that it stems from their opposition to the Islamic Republic's political independence and religious identity. He emphasized that after failing to overthrow the regime through war, sanctions, and internal unrest, the enemy's remaining hope lies in causing public exhaustion and internal collapse. The expert described Iran's relations with China and Russia as being based on shared national interests and countering US hegemony, rather than emotional friendship. The program also featured a book review of "Re-reading the System of Hegemony," which examines the structure of global dominance from the perspective of the Islamic Revolution. Responding to questions about "Strong Iran," Aboutalibi viewed the recent twelve-day conflict as an opportunity to consolidate defensive power and enhance Iran's international credibility. He asserted that Iran's prestige has not been broken; instead, the country has identified its weaknesses and strengthened its security infrastructure. The overall conclusion is that Iran continues to stand firm against external pressures by relying on its social capital and military strength.
