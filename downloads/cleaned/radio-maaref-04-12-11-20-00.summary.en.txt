The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps announced the start of the twelfth wave of Operation "Sincere Promise 4," targeting military and security positions in Tel Aviv, Haifa, and East Jerusalem with ballistic missiles and drones. During these engagements, Iran destroyed three US fighter jets and struck US military bases in Qatar, Kuwait, and Bahrain with Kheibar and strategic missiles, reportedly overwhelming Patriot defense systems. Simultaneously, Hezbollah in Lebanon launched missiles at northern occupied territories to avenge the martyrdom of Iran's Supreme Leader, while Iraqis held widespread protests in Baghdad condemning US and Israeli aggression. The Provisional Council of Article 110 of the Constitution, including President Pezeshkian, convened to exercise legal authorities until a new leader is appointed. Enemy attacks damaged seven medical centers, including Gandhi Hospital and Mina School, resulting in the deaths of 159 students and significant damage to critical infrastructure. Despite these strikes, people in Iran and the region emphasized national unity and continued resistance through gatherings in streets and mosques. International media and artists also reacted strongly to the leader's martyrdom and the escalating regional conflicts.
