In the radio program "Persaman Etteqadi," Professor Mohammadi emphasized the necessity of linking the Quran and Wilayat to achieve true salvation. He explained that the Quran serves as a divine roadmap, while the Ahl al-Bayt act as its interpreters and translators. Separating these two elements renders both invalid and distances humans from their ultimate destination. In response to another question, the distinction between two perspectives on the world, labeled "Bilaghah" and "Mal'unah," was clarified. The world is highly valuable as a farm and school for acquiring righteous deeds and attaining divine proximity. However, the world defined by attachment to material wealth and status, which lacks true reality, is the source of sin and error. Therefore, individuals should utilize worldly credentials to build their capital for the afterlife.
