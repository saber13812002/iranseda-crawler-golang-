This radio program provided jurisprudential answers to several questions from followers of different Maraji. Regarding prayer, Hujjat al-Islam Alian Najat confirmed that reciting short surahs and brief supplications after the Fatiha is permissible. In response to a question from a follower of Ayatollah Sistani about Zakat al-Mal, Hujjat al-Islam Mahdudi advised paying only the amount one is certain of owing, while exercising caution in cases of doubt. Hujjat al-Islam Jawaheri also clarified the distinction between "haram" and "not permissible," noting that the latter is often used for actions that are not definitively forbidden but are prohibited out of precaution. Finally, Hujjat al-Islam Zayn al-Abidin emphasized the Quranic ruling that a son's inheritance share is twice that of a daughter, refuting the idea of equal distribution.
