In this radio program, Dr. Mohammad Malekzadeh analyzed the true nature of US negotiations with Iran, arguing that they serve as a tool to contain independent powers and reduce Washington's military costs rather than to achieve peace. He emphasized that US calls for negotiation are a tactic to create divisions within the resistance front and attract moderate domestic forces, allowing the US to achieve its goals without direct military engagement. The expert warned that some of these forces continue to advocate for negotiations despite being aware of America's true intentions, indicating either deep deception or bad faith. The second part of the discussion focused on the impact of recent Iraqi elections on the Axis of Resistance, noting that the prevailing discourse in Baghdad centers on national dignity, anti-corruption, and avoiding dependence on foreign powers. Malekzadeh believes that the Islamic Republic of Iran can leverage this nationalist discourse to strengthen alignment with Iraqi currents and prevent their gradual shift toward secularism and distance from the resistance. He also stressed the principle of Iran's non-interference in neighbors' internal affairs, advocating for the region to be managed by its own countries. Finally, the program concluded by referencing the sensitive situation of Hezbollah in Lebanon and the necessity of maintaining the cohesion of the Axis of Resistance against external pressures.
