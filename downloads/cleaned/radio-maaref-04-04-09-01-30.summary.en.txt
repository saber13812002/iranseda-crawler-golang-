The broadcast begins with a Quranic exegesis highlighting the presence of cunning hypocrites around the Prophet in Medina who secretly sought to deviate the Muslim community.
The speaker explains that despite their outward appearance of faith, these individuals were deeply opposed to Islam's path and operated with extreme subtlety.
Drawing on Shia narrations, the speaker outlines two stages of worldly punishment for this group, which are expected to occur during the reappearance of Imam Mahdi.
The program then transitions to reciting the Ziyarat Nahiyah, vividly depicting the final moments and martyrdom of Imam Hussein in Karbala.
The second segment features a narrative from the book "Forgotten," told from the perspective of Ali ibn Ta'an, a soldier in Yazid's army.
This soldier expresses profound remorse for his army's cruelty and their failure to assist Imam Hussein when he was dying of thirst in the desert.
He confesses to having blood on his hands from a man who had previously saved his life, illustrating the depth of his guilt and repentance.
The program concludes by inviting listeners to stay tuned to Radio Ma'aref for the continuation of the narrative from the book "Forgotten."
