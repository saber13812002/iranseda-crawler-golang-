In this program, Professor Javad Mohaghegh narrates his memories of his seminary studies at the Haghani School in Qom. He describes the school's revolutionary atmosphere and the rigorous academic discipline under the management of Martyr Qodsavi. Mohaghegh highlights the influence of prominent teachers like Mousavir and Jannati in shaping the political and ethical thoughts of the students. He also recalls the students' revolutionary missions to distribute Imam Khomeini's declarations and their confrontation with SAVAK repression. In another segment, a witness reports on the Pahlavi regime's violence in Qom and the massacre of seminary students at the Fizeh. These testimonies describe the physical destruction and heavy bloodshed following the attacks on the seminaries. Finally, the program concludes with sharp criticisms of the United States and the United Nations as tools of hegemony over Muslim countries.
