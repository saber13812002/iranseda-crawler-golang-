Former national basketball player Farzam Khalki recounts his journey from being selected for the senior national team camp to winning national and Asian titles with Kerman and Urmia teams. He shares memories of participating in the Asian Games in Manila and Beijing, as well as his military service where he won a championship with the Army team. After completing his service, Khalki pursued a degree in electrical engineering at Amirkabir University of Technology while working at IBM Iran. He later rose to managerial positions at the Central Bank and the Sadad data processing company, where he played a key role in launching the "Bale" messaging app. Khalki also served as the head of the national handball teams, leading the squad to a runner-up finish in the Olympic qualifiers. He concludes by defining success not as reaching a goal, but as the continuous effort and having multiple objectives to avoid the feeling of failure.
