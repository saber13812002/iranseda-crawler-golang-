The program began with Quranic recitation and prayers before discussing the prohibition of backbiting based on Quranic verses.
The guest cited verse 19 of Surah An-Nur, stating that spreading scandal and defaming believers leads to severe punishment in both this world and the hereafter.
Relying on a hadith from Imam Musa al-Kadim (AS) addressed to Muhammad ibn Fuzayl, he emphasized that if our eyes and ears report falsehood, we must deny them rather than slander our Muslim brothers.
The concept of "Bab al-Hawaajj" (Gate of Needs) in this hadith refers to one who fulfills our hidden needs without our awareness.
Additionally, referencing verses 10 and 11 of Surah Al-Qalam, listening to backbiters, especially those who swear oaths to validate lies, was condemned.
The discussion concluded that preserving others' honor and avoiding character assassination are essential religious and ethical obligations.
The second part of the discussion on backbiting from the perspective of narrations was postponed to the next episode.
