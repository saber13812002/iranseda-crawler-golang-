In this radio program, Professor Mohammad Hadi Fala examines the reasons for divine anger based on Quranic verses.
The primary causes of God's wrath are identified as transgressing divine limits, disobeying God's commands, and the assassination of prophets and believers.
It is explained that the assassination of believers includes not only physical killing with weapons but also spiritual assassination through speech, backbiting, slander, and tarnishing reputations.
According to the Quran, anyone who intentionally kills a believer faces eternal punishment in Hell, God's anger, and exclusion from His mercy.
A key point is that verbal wounds and slander can spiritually kill a believer far more severely than a sword, constituting grave sins.
The program highlights the Ziyarat of Imam Abbas (AS), emphasizing that character assassination and verbal abuse are significant factors that provoke divine anger.
Finally, the program concludes with prayers for the reappearance of the Imam and blessings upon the Ahl al-Bayt, warning listeners to avoid such harmful actions.
