The radio program began by discussing the strategic defeat of the United States in its psychological and military war against Iran following the revolution. It emphasized that the Iranian people's resistance shattered the global myth of America's absolute power and exposed the fear of Western officials regarding the public trial of Mohammad Reza Pahlavi. The second part of the broadcast featured religious rulings provided by representatives of various clerical authorities in response to listener questions. Topics included the permissibility of using housing loans to buy a second home, the conditions for leading congregational prayer while seated, and the obligation of the prayer of thunder and lightning. Additionally, rulings were given on the khums obligation for working women whose income is spent on medical expenses and the purification of items contaminated with impurities. The program concluded by providing contact details for submitting further religious inquiries and reminding listeners to specify their marja when asking questions.
