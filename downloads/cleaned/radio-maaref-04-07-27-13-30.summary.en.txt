The radio program "Enduring Memories" introduced two commanders of the Sacred Defense, Sardar Lakzai and Sardar Tehourian. Sardar Lakzai recounted the Iraqi "mobile defense" operation in the Fakkah region and the 17th Ali ibn Abi Talab Division's successful counter-offensive. He shared a moving story of a Shirazi soldier who survived three nights in a trench by seeking refuge in Imam Hussein (AS). Sardar Tehourian described his severe injury in Operation Karbala 5 and the miraculous expulsion of a shrapnel from his throat during a 1986 enemy attack. The program also highlighted the country's scientific, industrial, and security advancements, as well as the dynamic activities of youth in various fields. It concluded by emphasizing divine miracles and the resilient spirit of the war veterans.
