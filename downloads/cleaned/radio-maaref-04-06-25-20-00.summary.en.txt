Radio news reported on Israeli airstrikes targeting the port of Hodeidah in Yemen and the killing of a Palestinian footballer in Gaza. The intensity of the attacks is such that explosions are audible in Tel Aviv, prompting the European Union to threaten further sanctions. In the West Bank, schools have reopened despite widespread destruction and the detention of teachers and students. Hezbollah in Lebanon announced extensive plans to commemorate the first anniversary of the martyrdom of its former leaders. At the Baghdad Book Fair, a book featuring the Iranian leader's views on Palestine was unveiled, highlighting the significant presence of Iranian publishers. The report also covered new threats against mosques in the Netherlands and introduced the Moscow Cathedral Mosque as a major European cultural center. Finally, the 43rd anniversary of the Sabra and Shatila massacre was marked in Lebanon, with a strong emphasis on the continuation of resistance.
