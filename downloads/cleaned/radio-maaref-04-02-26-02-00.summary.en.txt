In this program, Professor Jafari first addresses the historical ambiguities regarding the fate of Talib, the elder brother of Imam Ali (PBUH), noting the lack of definitive records about his conversion to Islam or martyrdom. He then discusses the late birth of Imam Javad (PBUH) at his father's age of 47, which fueled doubts among opponents, before the Imamate was transferred to him following the martyrdom of Imam Reza (PBUH) in 203 AH. Imam Javad's (PBUH) tenure was marked by political pressure from Caliphs Ma'mun and Mutawakkil, culminating in his martyrdom in Baghdad in 220 AH. The discussion then shifts to the birth of Imam Zaman (AJ) in 255 AH in Samarra and the beginning of the Minor Occultation, mediated by four specific deputies. The professor clarifies that meeting Imam Zaman (AJ) is solely at his divine will, invalidating any claims of personal agency or scheduled meetings by others. In the third section, the lineage of Lady Najmah, the mother of Imam Zaman (AJ), is traced to the Eastern Roman Emperor, highlighting her upbringing by Lady Hakeema. Finally, the program concludes with the story of Prophet Zechariah, his guardianship of Maryam, and his martyrdom alongside his son, Prophet Yahya (PBUH), in response to a listener's inquiry.
