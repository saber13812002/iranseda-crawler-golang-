The radio program Parsaiyan began with the recitation of the Dua Kumayl, followed by a discussion on the conduct of religious scholars. Hujjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the importance of practicing what one preaches, citing warnings from Imam Ali and Imam Sadiq about the severe punishment for those who speak truth but do not act upon it. Two anecdotes were shared to illustrate this principle, highlighting the extreme caution of Ayatollah Golpayegani and Ayatollah Jafari Tehrani in their daily lives. Golpayegani immediately vacated his rented home upon suspecting the landlord might need it, prioritizing the other's rights over his own comfort. Similarly, Jafari Tehrani asked a guest to dispose of his mineral water bottles to prevent a garbage collector from questioning his faith due to his illness. These stories demonstrate how scholars protect the beliefs of others through their meticulous actions. The program concluded with a call for small individual actions to benefit the nation and introduced a story from the book "Jamandeh ast Pesar Khoda," which will continue in the next episode.
