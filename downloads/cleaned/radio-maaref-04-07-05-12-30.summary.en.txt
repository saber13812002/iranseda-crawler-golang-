In this radio program, Hujjat al-Islam Fallahzadeh discusses the unique status of Imamzadeh Abdol-Azim on the occasion of his birth anniversary.
The central argument highlights the historical precedent of "fiqhi marja'iyya" during the presence of the Imams, who directed followers to consult religious scholars for legal rulings.
Citing narrations, the speaker notes that visiting Imamzadeh Abdol-Azim carries rewards equivalent to visiting Imam Hussein for those unable to travel to Karbala.
A key ethical lesson is the necessity of presenting one's beliefs and practices to religious experts to ensure their correctness, mirroring Imam Abdol-Azim's own consultation with Imam Hadi.
Addressing the current crisis in Gaza, the speaker defines jihad in the era of occultation as encompassing spiritual, financial, verbal, and emotional efforts.
It is emphasized that even without material capability, verbal support and sincere prayers for the oppressed in Palestine are essential religious duties.
Finally, the widespread presence of Imamzadehs across Iran is interpreted as a strategy to preserve the faith against tyrannical rulers and promote the Shia school of thought.
