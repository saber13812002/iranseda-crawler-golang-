The 22nd International Conference of Husaini Poets and Servants is being held in Mashhad with the participation of domestic and international guests. Concurrently, the "Us and the West" conference is examining the Supreme Leader's views on Western civilization and colonialism through specialized sessions. Other news includes the construction of over 2,400 schools by donors and the expansion of health cooperation between Iran and Saudi Arabia via the Red Crescent. The 11th commemoration of Allameh Tabatabaei has begun with extensive programs in Iran and Islamic countries, as UNESCO has recognized him as a global figure in philosophy. The custodian of the Astan Quds Razavi has called for preventing inappropriate construction in Mashhad to preserve the city's cultural identity. The second international "Maroof" festival, focusing on enjoining good and forbidding evil, has also commenced. In the religious Q&A segment, scholars clarified rulings on khums, revocable divorce, wudu etiquette, and loan transactions according to various maraji'.
