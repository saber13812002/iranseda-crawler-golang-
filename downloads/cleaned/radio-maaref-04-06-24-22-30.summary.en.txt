In this radio program, the concept of "perspective" is presented as the primary key to freeing individuals from worldly grief and depression. The speaker argues that accepting the world as a place of divine trial allows people to endure hardships with inner peace, viewing them as part of God's plan. Changing this perspective is a gradual process that requires deep and continuous study of the Quran, which guides human vision toward divine realities by describing the true nature of phenomena. The importance of associating with religious scholars and mystics, particularly in places like Takht-e Foulad in Isfahan, is highlighted as a crucial factor in correcting one's outlook. The discussion also emphasizes the need to guard one's perspective against distortions from Satan and media, as these views directly influence life decisions. Referring to the natural evolution of human perception over a lifetime, the speaker stresses the necessity of aligning one's view with the Quranic perspective to achieve lasting tranquility. The program concludes with the recitation of verses from Surah Tawbah, a prayer for the Mahdi, a call to support the people of Gaza, and an introduction to the educational segment of Radio Mard.
