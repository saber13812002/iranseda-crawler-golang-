In this radio program, Ayatollah Sefi Mazandarani interprets the concept of "deceiving God" based on authentic narrations. He explains that this deception includes not only overt sins but also hidden heart sins and hypocrisy in worship, of which God is fully aware. Citing a narration from Sheikh Tusi, the speaker notes that hypocrites, upon witnessing the miracles of Imam Ali (AS), deepened their enmity and disbelief rather than accepting faith. The commentator emphasizes that the increase in their disbelief was a direct result of their own stubbornness and misuse of divine verses, not a direct creation of disbelief by God. The purpose of the verses was correction and guidance, and the resulting harm stemmed from the sinners' own free choices. Finally, the distinction between divine success for believers and the punishment of disbelievers, which arises from their own obstinacy, is clarified.
