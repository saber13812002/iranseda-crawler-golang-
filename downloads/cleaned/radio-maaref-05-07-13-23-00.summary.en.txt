The program begins by honoring two martyred police officers in Sistan and Baluchestan, urging media to better highlight the efforts of security forces. The main discussion focuses on mental management and resisting the enemy's psychological operations. Haghaj Panahian outlines three key pillars for managing one's mind: refusing to let others control it, self-discipline, and entrusting it to God. Citing Quranic verses, he explains the constant intellectual aggression of the arrogant against the oppressed. He emphasizes that individuals engaged in jihad and resistance develop high insight and are immune to deception. His recent trip to Lebanon demonstrated that people involved in resistance possess exceptional awareness of conspiracies. The discussion concludes with advice to avoid excessive propaganda and instead rely on deep insight to withstand cultural attacks.
