Veteran footballer Reza Kangarani shared his career journey on the radio program "Mahvateh-ye Gotohoo," starting from his childhood in Tehran's Hashem Abad neighborhood. Discovered at age thirteen, he joined the Pura club where his standout performance against Persepolis caught the attention of prominent coaches. Kangarani recounted his experiences with the Armed Forces and Olympic national teams, expressing regret over their elimination in the 1992 Barcelona Olympic qualifiers due to administrative issues and military service. He expressed regret for staying with the Fath club instead of joining Persepolis, citing financial discrepancies and a lack of professional guidance during that decision. He also mentioned turning down a transfer offer to Germany due to his father's opposition and his young family's situation. Kangarani described his time at Fajr Sepasi Shiraz and Baragh Tehran, highlighting the strong teams and intense competition of that era. Finally, he criticized the political and technical disruptions at Baragh Tehran that hindered their promotion and noted his subsequent move to the Keshavarz club in 1999.
