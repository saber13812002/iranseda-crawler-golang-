The "Persman" program on Radio Ma'aref, hosted by Professor Mohammadi Sharoudi, addressed various theological and religious inquiries from listeners. Regarding the question of cursing officials, the professor emphasized that judgment without full knowledge of all causes is erroneous, and cursing is only permissible for clear enemies of truth. In comparing the merit of sending blessings (Salawat) versus cursing, it was noted that while Salawat holds a higher rank, removing hatred through cursing is a natural prerequisite to cultivating love. The program clarified that enduring illness is compensated by divine "reward" (Owz) rather than the "merit" (Sawab) associated with worship, which will be repaid in the Hereafter. It was explained that reading the Persian translation of the Quran does not carry the specific merit of reciting the Quran itself, though it earns the merit of study; listeners were advised to learn Arabic recitation by listening to audio recitations. Historically, the Khawarij were identified as a group who left the faith by rebelling against the infallible Imam, thereby becoming apostates. Finally, the ruling on unfulfilled vows was discussed, confirming that if the vowed action is performed before the wish is granted, no expiation is required, and the merit of the action is still recorded for the individual.
