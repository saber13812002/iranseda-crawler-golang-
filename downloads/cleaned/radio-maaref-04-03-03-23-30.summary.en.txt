In this radio program, the commentator emphasizes two fundamental principles derived from the Quran: sincerity in action and the revival of the Prophet's traditions as prerequisites for divine guidance. Citing a verse from Surah Al-Imran, the speaker explains that Prophet Jesus (PBUH) called his companions to God after observing the practical disbelief and behavioral deviation of the Children of Israel, despite their outward faith. This practical disbelief was characterized by a lifestyle that contradicted Islamic directives. The commentator interprets the verse "Lnahdinahum صراطا مستقیما" to mean that God guides only those who engage in the struggle to clarify the truth and maintain absolute sincerity in their deeds. Furthermore, referencing Surah As-Saff, Muslims are urged to emulate the disciples of Jesus by becoming supporters of God's religion and striving for its victory. The core message is that the prerequisites for guidance lie within human choice, and God determines one's fate based on wisdom and the individual's own decisions.
