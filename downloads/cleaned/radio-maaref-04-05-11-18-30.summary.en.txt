This radio program discussed the Islamic legal rulings regarding the Arbaeen pilgrimage to Karbala.
Participating in this event is considered a highly recommended act of worship, with the reward increasing with each step taken.
Performing symbolic walks in other cities with the intention of spiritual closeness is also deemed meritorious.
The segment addressed specific legal questions, including the deadline for making up missed evening prayers and the rules for couples with different religious authorities.
Further rulings clarified the mahram status of maternal and paternal uncles and the prohibition of consuming eggs with blood spots.
Additional guidance was provided on setting aside Khums for future essential needs and purchasing missed prayers from a deceased person's estate.
The broadcast concluded with a live report from Qom, highlighting the preparations of pilgrims for their spiritual journey to Karbala.
