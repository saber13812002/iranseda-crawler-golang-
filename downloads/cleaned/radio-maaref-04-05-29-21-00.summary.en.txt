This radio program first highlights the vital role of pilgrimage in the lives of prominent Shia scholars such as Allameh Amini and Ayatollah Vazma Bahjaad. These scholars strictly adhered to pilgrimage etiquette, such as sitting at the feet of Imam Ali (PBUH) and walking barefoot on Ashura, to draw spiritual blessings from the shrines. Ayatollah Vazma Bahjaad even declined personal travel invitations to avoid missing the opportunity to visit Imam Reza (PBUH) in Mashhad. The second part of the program features a narrative from the book "Galaxy of Nothingness," which chronicles the life of Ayatollah Seyed Ali Ghazi Tabatabaei from his birth in Tabriz to his migration to Najaf. This story depicts the arduous and passionate journey of Ayatollah Tabatabaei and his wife, Rakhshandeh Sadat, to Karbala and their decision to remain in the land of the Ahl al-Bayt. Finally, the program discusses the Supreme Leader's ruling that a student's temporary residence does not constitute their "homeland" for prayer purposes unless they intend to stay for at least ten days. Additionally, Quranic researchers were invited to submit papers for a specialized reciters' conference, and a call was made for the public to submit documents related to the Sacred Defense.
