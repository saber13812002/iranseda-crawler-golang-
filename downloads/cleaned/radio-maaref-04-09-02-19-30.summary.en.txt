The radio program "Ganj-e Sa'adat" began with a recitation of a rain prayer from the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya before transitioning to a discussion on the causes of mockery. Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallah identified ignorance of religious teachings and the Quran as the primary reason people engage in mocking others. He explained that the illusion of possessing material, scientific, or positional advantages leads individuals to view others with contempt. Examples were provided, such as mocking elderly people for their lack of technological knowledge or workers for their appearance, to illustrate this improper behavior. Citing Quranic verses and narrations from the Ahl al-Bayt, the speaker described mockery as a sign of ignorance and a lack of deep reflection. It was emphasized that possessing knowledge or wealth does not grant anyone the right to demean others, and mutual respect is essential. Finally, listeners were urged to avoid mockery and foster a more respectful environment by applying these ethical insights.
