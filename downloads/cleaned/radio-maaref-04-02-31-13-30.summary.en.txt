The radio program "Narrative of Love" featured the memories of Mrs. Rahim Makian, the mother of martyr Ahmad Makian. This young Khuzestan cleric and defender of the shrine was martyred in Syria in 2016. The mother described her family's religious upbringing, simple lifestyle, and her father's deep devotion to the Ahl al-Bayt and the Quran. She highlighted Ahmad's calm demeanor, contentment, and strong emotional bond with her from early childhood. Ahmad participated in Quran classes from a young age and excelled in both religious and general studies due to his high intelligence. Listener messages expressed the profound impact of the story and solidarity with the families of martyrs. The program concluded by inviting listeners to tune in for future segments and other broadcasts on Radio Ma'aref.
