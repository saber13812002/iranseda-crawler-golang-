Ali Hamoudi, a former national team player and star of major Iranian clubs, discussed the financial and legal challenges he faced during his professional career. He recounted the experience of going unpaid by Esteghlal Ahvaz and Sepahan, noting how Dr. Omidvar Razzai intervened to help him recover his dues through club checks. Hamoudi also addressed the controversial issue of his military service, clarifying that he obtained a medical exemption due to a congenital condition rather than through any fraudulent means. He shared his disillusionment with the Iranian football system after witnessing corruption and match-fixing in the lower leagues, which ultimately led to his retirement. Despite having a contract worth 1.5 billion tomans at his peak, Hamoudi explained how financial pressures and mental fatigue forced him to stop playing at the age of 38. He is currently pursuing coaching certifications and hopes to build a successful new career in football management.
