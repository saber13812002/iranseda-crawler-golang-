This tafsir of Surah Al-Ma'idah examines the public nature of questioning on the Day of Judgment. The prophets' response of "we have no knowledge" serves to rebuke their nations and intensify the fear of accountability, rather than indicating absolute ignorance. This denial of knowledge contrasts with other Quranic verses that affirm the prophets' access to hidden knowledge through divine revelation. Consequently, the referenced ignorance pertains to innate, eternal knowledge exclusive to God, not acquired knowledge from revelation. The text refutes the interpretation that attributes innate hidden knowledge to the Imams, clarifying that Shia belief reserves such knowledge solely for the Divine. Finally, the daily reporting of deeds to the Infallibles is presented as a theological reality grounded in Quranic evidence.
