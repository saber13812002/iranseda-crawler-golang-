Seyyed Mohammad Mehdi Seyyed Salehi recounts his turbulent yet successful career in Iranian football, highlighting his multiple returns to top clubs. He reflects on his peak performance at Esteghlal, where he won the Asian Golden Boot in 2007 without a national team cap, and his captaincy at Sepahan, which led to several league and Asian titles. The player credits key figures like Engineer Saipanam and a manager at Pیکان for helping him regain his form and reputation. He discusses significant conflicts with foreign coaches such as Kanchel at Sepahan and Tony Olivi at Tractor, which caused repeated departures from these teams. Despite injuries and a lack of trust from some coaches, he proved his worth by scoring 16 goals in 16 weeks for Tractor, demonstrating his enduring ability to compete at the highest level. Seyyed Salehi emphasizes the importance of transparency and self-respect in his career decisions, notably rejecting a one-billion-toman offer from a lower division team to maintain his integrity and respect for fans. Ultimately, he turned down offers from Persepolis and Esteghlal to sign with Persepolis, aiming to continue his successful journey in the Persian Gulf Pro League.
