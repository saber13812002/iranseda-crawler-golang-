In this program, Professor Sahabi analyzes the thoughts of Imam Khomeini regarding Farabi and Ibn Sina. The Imam views the Virtuous City not merely as a political structure but as a spiritual process of purifying the human soul, where Sharia serves as a tool for moral reform. The second part discusses Ibn Sina's turbulent life and the significant impact of his long residence in Isfahan on the establishment of its philosophical school. Imam Khomeini praises Ibn Sina as a "wonder of the age," particularly highlighting his work, the Shifa, and its theological sections. The discussion emphasizes Ibn Sina's religious inclinations and the closeness of his later philosophy to Shia Islam during the Buyid era, especially in works like the Ma'rajnama. Prominent students such as Bahmanyar and Abu Abdullah Mas'udi are also noted for their role in transmitting the Sheikh al-Ra'is's intellectual legacy. The program concludes by introducing Ibn Sina's key works and affirming his pivotal role in the history of Islamic wisdom.
