This radio program examines the profound impact of friends on human character, citing Islamic narrations that emphasize the importance of evaluating a person's associates to understand their true nature. This principle is highlighted as crucial for making informed decisions, particularly in selecting a life partner or engaging in long-term collaborations. The discussion also addresses how innovators and instigators of fitna spread religious deviations by attracting followers, thereby causing significant harm to the community. In the second part, the fiqh concept of "Shuhrah Fiqhiyya" (popular scholarly opinion) is explained within the context of Usul al-Fiqh. This concept is considered valid when a view is widely accepted among jurists without direct textual evidence, yet is validated through the rule of kindness and the implied approval of the Infallible Imam. Consequently, Shuhrah Fiqhiyya serves as an independent basis for deriving legal rulings in Islamic jurisprudence.
