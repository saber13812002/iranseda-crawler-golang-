In this program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli explained that unnecessary and provocative interactions between men and women are forbidden, emphasizing that conversations should be limited to what is strictly necessary. He also ruled that while working on Ashura and Tasu'a is generally disliked, it is permissible if there is a necessity or to facilitate the needs of other believers. The second part of the show focused on the importance of prayer, citing a narration from Imam Sadiq (AS) that highlights how prayer can save even sinners from punishment. Subsequently, Masoud Sarvi reported on the spiritual atmosphere and beautiful architecture of the Qasem and Ibrahimeh Imams in Astara. The program concluded with the recitation of verses from Surah Al-Hijr by reciter Mehdi Adili and the announcement of contact numbers for submitting religious questions.
