In this radio program, Ashoshan Darvishi, the new head of the Zourkhaneh and Pahlavani Wrestling Federation in Kermanshah, discussed the challenges of reviving this traditional sport based on his previous management experience in wrestling. Emphasizing the region's historical pride and the sport's 700-year heritage, he highlighted the lack of scientific training, specialized coaches, and referees as major issues. His plan includes establishing a specialized academy, upgrading the qualifications of coaches and referees, and providing standard equipment for various age groups, including children. He also aims to restore old Zourkhanehs and expand activities to other cities in the province to return the sport to its rightful place. In the second segment, Colonel Seyed Ali Mowjher, head of the Sports Basij, emphasized the organization's role in promoting mass sports and reviving local traditions. He announced talent identification programs in counties and the utilization of 4,512 sports facilities to enhance public health and social vitality. Mowjher also stressed the importance of unity and coordination among sports organizations and federations to address cultural-sports gaps and unlock Iran's hidden sports potential.
