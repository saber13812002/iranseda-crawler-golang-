In this radio program, legal and jurisprudential experts analyzed the recent aggression by the Zionist regime and the US government against Iran from both religious and international perspectives. From a jurisprudential standpoint, insulting and threatening to assassinate Iran's Supreme Leader is considered a violation of Islamic principles and constitutes an insult to religious authority, fostering hatred among nations. Internationally, such threats against heads of state and official figures are a blatant violation of national sovereignty and international conventions protecting such individuals. Furthermore, the US military attack on three Iranian nuclear facilities was deemed contrary to the laws of armed conflict, humanitarian law, and the Nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty (NPT). Experts emphasized that targeting nuclear installations is strictly prohibited even during wartime due to the risk of widespread contamination. These US actions are not only legally baseless but also contradict the spirit of international treaties aimed at ensuring security and preventing war. Ultimately, these attacks were characterized as unilateral and self-serving, lacking any legal or jurisprudential justification in global or Islamic systems.
