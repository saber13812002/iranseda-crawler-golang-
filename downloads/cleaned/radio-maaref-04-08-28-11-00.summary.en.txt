During the martyrdom of Hazrat Fatimah (SA) and Book Week, the Islamic Revolution Cultural Research Institute published the Supreme Leader's annotations on three books detailing the lives of martyrs' mothers and wives. Simultaneously, the call for the first International Conference on Imam Khomeini's School of Ijtihad was released, focusing on legal discourses and scheduled for next year in Mashhad. The Islamic Culture and Thought Institute also held a national conference on the philosophy of logic (Manlogh-e Fahmidin) to advance scientific discourse. In sports, the Palestinian national team played a symbolic match against the Catalonia team in Barcelona to support the people of Gaza, with ticket proceeds donated to humanitarian projects. In education, Tehran's District 15 successfully implemented the "Monhaj" plan, enabling approximately 700 fourth-grade students to memorize parts of the Quran. The "Zamzame Ahkam" program addressed various religious inquiries from listeners, covering topics such as Khoms, Wudu, and prayer.
