This radio program recounts memories of the life and missionary activities of Ayatollah Fazel Golpayegani. He strongly opposed the use of photography for religious promotion, explicitly asking people to avoid taking his picture. During his first missionary trip to a small, underdeveloped village, he worked to rebuild a dilapidated mosque despite harsh weather and a lack of basic facilities. By establishing a transparent system for recording assets and calculating Khums, he gained the villagers' trust and organized their financial and religious affairs. The Ayatollah emphasized the necessity of combining theoretical seminary knowledge with practical skills in religious duties such as washing the dead. The program also discussed a narration from the book of Kafi regarding the frequent visits of the angel Gabriel to Lady Fatima (SA) after the Prophet's (PBUH) passing. This narration highlights the high spiritual status of Lady Zahra (SA) and the significance of her direct connection to revelation during that critical period.
