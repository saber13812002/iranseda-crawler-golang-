The radio program reported the passing of renowned Iranian painter Mahmoud Farshchian at the age of 96, noting that his funeral will be held in Isfahan. It also announced that the Islamic Revolution Document Center will soon publish a comprehensive encyclopedia on the twelve-day war. A significant portion of the broadcast addressed the religious rulings regarding government-issued currency for pilgrims traveling to the holy shrines in Iraq. The discussion emphasized that unused funds must be returned to the bank if the travel condition is not met, while leftover amounts after a valid trip are permissible to keep. The program further explored the theological concept of the Prophet Muhammad's superior existential rank among all prophets, distinguishing it from chronological order. Finally, it highlighted the charitable traditions of prominent clerics like Akhund Khorasani and Ayatollah Marashi Najafi, who secretly supported students and the poor.
