The radio program "Andisheha," featuring Dr. Ali Reza Bahrami, explored the theological and philosophical challenge of justifying evil in the world. The discussion categorized evil into natural and moral types, emphasizing that natural disasters are integral to the cosmic balance and divine wisdom. Dr. Bahrami refuted atheist arguments that evil negates God's omnipotence, instead highlighting the role of trials in human spiritual evolution. He distinguished between "ta'dis" (realistic interpretation of evil) and "dafaa" (theological defense), urging that evil be understood within the context of human free will and divine decree. The speaker clarified that moral evil stems from human sin and should not be attributed to God's will. Finally, the program stressed the critical importance of Islamic unity and the role of intellectuals in fostering solidarity to counter the divisive tactics of enemies.
