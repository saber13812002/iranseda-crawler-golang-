In this radio program, two jurists discussed the jurisprudential and legal aspects of a father killing his child and the associated penalties in Islam.
The guests emphasized that narrations stating "you are property" do not imply absolute ownership by the father and cannot be used to derive a legal ruling permitting murder.
Islam fought against the pre-Islamic view that considered humans as property, and it classifies the intentional killing of a child as a major sin and a serious crime.
Although a narration from Imam Hassan (AS) states that a father is not subject to retribution (Qisas) for killing his child, this does not mean he is innocent, as he faces severe deterrent punishments, expiation, and blood money.
The acceptance of the father's exemption from Qisas is based solely on obedience to the narration of the Infallible, and without this specific evidence, the general Quranic principle of retribution would apply.
This ruling absolutely does not justify or permit the killing of a child, and an intentional perpetrator is considered deserving of Hellfire.
The prohibition of killing and its classification as a crime also extends to non-Muslims who are not in a state of war, as the sanctity of life is universally protected in Islam.
The program concluded with the recitation and translation of verses from Surah Al-Baqarah regarding the importance of documenting debts and providing testimony.
