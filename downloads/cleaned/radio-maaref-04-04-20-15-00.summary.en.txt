In this radio program, a military expert highlighted Iran's success in the 12-day war, noting that it neutralized two nuclear powers without external assistance. He emphasized that natural defenses like the Fordow facility and the development of hypersonic missiles have created a decisive deterrent against adversaries. The expert also pointed to the pivotal role of the Axis of Resistance and the localization of advanced technologies in groups such as those in Yemen and Palestine. He argued that the Zionist regime made a miscalculation regarding the resistance's capabilities, leading it into a corridor of consecutive defeats. In the second segment, a cleric stressed the importance of holding home gatherings to revive the cause of the Ahl al-Bayt and foster moral education. He noted that these gatherings should be held with balance in both times of joy and sorrow to provide both solace and instruction. Finally, the discussion concluded by emphasizing the necessity of maintaining a connection with the Quran and following the path of the Infallibles for spiritual and social peace.
