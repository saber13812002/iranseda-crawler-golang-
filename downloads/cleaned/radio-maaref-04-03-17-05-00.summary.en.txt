Remembering God in the heart serves as a better guardian against sin than merely holding a prayer bead. Piety, defined as abstaining from sin, guarantees the rectification of one's affairs and honor in both this world and the hereafter. Divine promises are the best of all, whereas worldly status and wealth are transient and perishable. Prophet Joseph (AS) exemplifies how piety leads to high spiritual and worldly ranks. Muslims should follow the guidance and Sunnah of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) rather than imitating the West. The Prophet's Sunnah includes respectful behavior, caring for the weak, and staying awake at night for worship. Night vigil and recitation of the Quran elevate a person to the "Maha Mubarak" rank and prevent spiritual darkness. Ultimately, adhering to the Prophet's moral virtues is the key to human perfection and happiness.
