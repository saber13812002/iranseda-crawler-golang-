This program offers a symbolic interpretation of the story of Prophet Abraham's sacrifice, emphasizing the two main trials of relinquishing worldly attachments and absolute obedience to divine command. In this narrative, Prophet Ismail and Hajar are introduced as the two main pillars of success who, through their full cooperation, overcame Satan's obstacles, symbolized by the stoning ritual in Hajj. The story is metaphorically linked to the status of Imam Hussein (AS), whose martyrdom is interpreted as the ultimate sacrifice and the pinnacle of monotheism before God. The speaker connects this emotional and spiritual bond with the pilgrims' journey to Karbala, linking it to the Hajj rituals and the concept of "Hajj in Karbala." Finally, the prayer "Anqada al-Mudda qabla al-Ta'ahud" is recommended as a solution to problems and a responder to spiritual needs. The text concludes with the recitation of Husaini ziyarat and religious hymns in memory of Imam Hussein (AS) and Imam Mahdi (AJ).
