The guest, reflecting on his long tenure as a national team captain, attributes his success to a deep passion for table tennis and unwavering focus. He criticizes the limited mindset of previous generations regarding competition with Asia, urging the new generation to aim for higher global standards. As a coach for junior teams, he emphasizes the importance of teamwork and professional support to build player confidence. Historic achievements, such as medals in Asian and World Championships, have significantly raised Iran's profile on the international stage. The participation of young players in top European and American leagues further demonstrates this progress. While highlighting promising future talents, he stresses the need for more professional planning and increased financial support from the federation and the government. Finally, he calls on sports administrators to facilitate the establishment of academic and training centers by providing facilities based on meritocracy.
