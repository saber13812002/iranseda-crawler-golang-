In this radio program, the concept of "mercy for all worlds" is emphasized, presenting human unity as a primary manifestation of the Prophet's mission. The speaker argues that true unity is only achievable when based on monotheism and spiritual growth, rather than material interests or selfishness. The Quran instructs believers to first highlight commonalities with those of different beliefs before addressing differences through admonition, wisdom, and the best form of debate. Insulting the sacred values of others does not lead to guidance but rather results in blasphemy against God and increased hostility. The Islamic Revolution, by establishing the first religious government in the modern era, disrupted the global balance of power and inspired worldwide changes. The most significant threat to this movement is sectarian and ethnic warfare among Muslims, which is considered the only factor capable of defeating them. Therefore, preventing internal conflicts and maintaining unity are the top priorities for the survival and expansion of this movement.
