The radio program began with an exegesis of a Quranic verse emphasizing repentance from three major sins: shirk, murder, and fornication.
It highlighted that sincere repentance, faith, and righteous deeds can transform these grave sins into good deeds, a concept supported by a hadith linking this transformation to four virtues: honesty, modesty, good character, and gratitude.
The second segment addressed various jurisprudential questions, starting with the permissibility of a male dentist examining a female patient.
It was ruled that looking at internal parts like the mouth without sinful intent and physical contact using gloves or tools are permissible.
Additionally, internal blood accumulating in the mouth during treatment is considered pure, though consuming it is forbidden unless there is a necessity.
Another ruling confirmed that a person unable to prostrate due to knee pain may pray while sitting, with the recitation of "bi-hawli Allah" being recommended.
Further answers covered topics such as the validity of waqf, the method of wiping the head during wudu, and the prohibition of eating eggs from animals whose meat is haram.
The broadcast concluded with morning prayers and supplications marking the anniversary of Lady Fatima Masumeh's (SA) arrival in Qom.
