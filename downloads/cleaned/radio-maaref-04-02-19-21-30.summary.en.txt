This lecture on Mahdism examines the policy of Imam Mahdi (AJ) regarding Jews and People of the Book during the era of reappearance. Contrary to some narrations suggesting the acceptance of Jizya, stronger evidence indicates that the Imam will not accept it, as this would imply validating their deviant beliefs. The texts suggest that during the reappearance, adherence to deviant religions and symbols like the cross or idolatry will be completely abolished. A narration from Imam Ali (AS) highlighting his seventy virtues was analyzed, emphasizing the certainty of the Imam's appearance. Another narration indicates that the Imam will "migrate" to Jerusalem rather than launch a military campaign, implying the area will have been previously cleared of Jewish influence. A detailed sermon by Imam Ali (AS) was cited, predicting the final battle against the Dajjal and the execution of his followers in Jerusalem. After the departure of Jesus (AS), Imam Mahdi (AJ) will remain as the sole leader, establishing absolute justice. Ultimately, he will eliminate Jews, Christians, and innovators, establishing a pure and just global order.
