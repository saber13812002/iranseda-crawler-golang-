In this program, Professor Pishvayi highlights the role of England in supporting extremist groups and compares the Wahhabi destruction of holy graves to the historical Kharijites. He argues that modern Wahhabis have narrowed the circle of faith while expanding the circle of disbelief by declaring other sects as infidels. The historical and legal reasons behind Aisha's opposition to Imam Hassan's burial in the Prophet's shrine and Marwan's order to shoot the funeral procession were explained. The permissibility of combining Dhuhr and Asr, as well as Maghrib and Isha prayers in Shia jurisprudence was discussed, citing the Prophet's own practices. The importance of praying for the Mahdi's reappearance and fulfilling religious duties such as enjoining good and forbidding evil was emphasized. The incorrect justification of abandoning religious duties to hasten the appearance was refuted as a political plot.
