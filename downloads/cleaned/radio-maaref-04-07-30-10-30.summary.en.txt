In the "Ma'arif" radio program, Dr. Pourhosseini emphasized the consumption of mallow and basil seeds during autumn based on Islamic traditional medicine. She explained that these foods, with their mild warm and moist nature, are effective against the cold and dry temperament of autumn and help prevent uric acid-related diseases. The correct method of consumption involves brewing mallow with warm or hot water and adding rosewater, rather than using cold water, to preserve its therapeutic effects. In the second segment, Hojjat al-Islam Rasoul interpreted verse 180 of Surah Al-Imran, stressing the importance of proper financial management and avoiding stinginess or excessive hoarding. He noted that God desires His servants to create comfort according to their financial capacity and to view wealth as a means of life rather than an end. Finally, a speech by Imam Khomeini was aired, praising the youth for their steadfastness against oppression and for preserving Islamic identity. The program aimed to promote a religious lifestyle and raise awareness among listeners.
