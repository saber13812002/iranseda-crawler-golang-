The radio program "Qom Est" on the Ma'aref network began by introducing upcoming segments and honoring the day of the poet Saadi Shirazi. The literary segment highlighted Saadi's distinguished status in world literature and the enduring impact of his works on Persian culture. The program then transitioned to "Zamzam Ahkam," featuring a discussion with Professor Vahidpour on religious rulings. The main topic was the jurisprudential status of banking investments and the interest earned from them. Professor Vahidpour compared banks to legal entities and outlined three scenarios regarding the permissibility of the profit. The first scenario assumes certainty that the bank's activities are halal, making the profit permissible. The second assumes certainty that the activities are haram, rendering the profit impermissible. The third and most common scenario involves uncertainty about the nature of the bank's operations, which requires further examination of religious laws.
