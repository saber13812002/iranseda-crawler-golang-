The radio program focused on preparations for the Arbaeen pilgrimage, highlighting extensive infrastructure and service upgrades at the Shalamcheh and Chazabe borders. Officials reported the installation of thousands of square meters of shade and the readiness of the Qom People's Arbaeen Headquarters to host fifteen thousand pilgrims. Truck traffic on pilgrim routes was banned during peak days to ensure order and safety. The broadcast also covered cultural activities, including a women's poetry gathering that compared the events of Karbala with the resistance of the Palestinian people. Additionally, the secretary of the National Youth Population Headquarters announced the launch of a call for nominations for the National Youth Population Award. The program concluded with a discussion on the religious rulings regarding Ta'ziyeh performances and the necessary conditions for their proper execution.
