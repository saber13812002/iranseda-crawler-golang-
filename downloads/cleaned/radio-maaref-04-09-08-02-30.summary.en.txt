In the radio program "Porsaman," Professor Maleki addresses various Quranic questions from listeners. He first explains that reading the Quran in Arabic is essential as the official language of the religion to maintain global Muslim unity and ensure a deeper understanding, although studying Persian translations and tafsirs is also highly recommended for grasping the meanings. In the second segment, the apparent differences in the narrative of Prophet Moses in Surah An-Naml and Surah Qasas are examined. Professor Maleki emphasizes that these differences are not contradictions but rather reflect different narrative angles used by the Quran to convey complementary points and align with the context of revelation. Responding to the third question, he interprets a verse from Surah At-Tawbah stating that some spouses and children may be enemies to believers. By referencing the historical context of the Hijrah, he clarifies that this verse does not imply the destruction of family bonds but serves as a warning to be vigilant against spiritual and worldly obstacles that hinder moral elevation. Finally, he reminds listeners that wealth and children are divine tests, and the foundation of the family should be preserved through forgiveness and guidance towards the truth.
