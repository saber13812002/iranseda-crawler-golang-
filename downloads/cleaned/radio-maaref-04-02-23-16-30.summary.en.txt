In the radio program "Persaman Eteghadi," listeners' questions regarding the concept of "Ajdeh" and harsh terms in religious narrations were addressed.
The expert explained that Ajdeh in Arabic texts refers to very large snakes, and the narration about Bab al-Safwan refers to a Jinn appearing in that form.
He emphasized that Jinn have the ability to change their shape and appear in various forms, and this narration is valid in terms of authenticity and implication.
The second part criticized literal translations of religious texts, stressing the need to understand metaphors within their specific historical and linguistic context.
The key point is that phrases like "wail to your state" in narrations are not meant to frighten the audience but serve as literary and educational idioms for behavioral correction.
It was emphasized that the literal meaning of words must be examined in their specific time and conditions to prevent misinterpretation and fear.
Finally, the importance of paying attention to the youth period and the necessity of continuous personal and spiritual growth were highlighted in the "Kiyaban Farhang" segment.
