This radio program addressed contemporary parenting challenges, specifically focusing on sleep disturbances in children caused by digital media misuse and unhealthy snacks.
Experts emphasized that lack of motivation for worship and poor sleep quality stem from inappropriate lifestyles and the absence of a peaceful, inspiring environment.
In response to a query about a five-year-old's misbehavior, it was advised that this issue arises from a lack of social interaction and should be corrected through increased family engagement and educational storytelling.
The discussion also highlighted the importance of parents serving as practical role models and maintaining structured routines to prevent risky behaviors in adolescents.
The second part of the program focused on interpreting the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya, stressing the need for a striving perspective on life and avoiding excessive materialism to achieve moral and spiritual perfection.
Hojjat al-Islam Ghalam Ali explained that the world should be viewed as a means to gain divine pleasure rather than an ultimate end in itself.
Finally, the program concluded with safety reminders for Nowruz travel and an invitation for listeners to submit their questions for future episodes.
