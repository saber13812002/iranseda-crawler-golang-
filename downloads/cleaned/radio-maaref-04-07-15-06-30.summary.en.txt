The program began with a jurisprudential discussion on the obligation of returning greetings during Friday prayers, emphasizing the necessity of the listener's awareness. A Quranic interpretation followed, clarifying that the pronoun in the verse refers to the Quran itself rather than Abu Talib, based on the consensus of his faith. The second segment focused on National Rural and Tribal Day, inviting listeners to share local specialties and handicrafts from various Iranian regions. Significant historical events were reviewed, including Mukhtar al-Thaqafi's uprising, the establishment of Iran's flag colors, and the birth of Niels Bohr. The broadcast also addressed the Al-Aqsa Storm and the Day of Palestinian Hamasa. Finally, listener messages were read, highlighting regional products such as saffron, pomegranates, and olives from different cities.
