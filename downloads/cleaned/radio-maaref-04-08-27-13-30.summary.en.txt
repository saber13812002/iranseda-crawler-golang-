The radio program "Tales of Freedom" featured Mehdi Golefshani, an Iranian POW held by Iraq from 1984 to 1990. Golefshani described the enemy's attempts to brainwash prisoners and their resistance against cultural tools like television and vulgar music in the camps. He recounted a one-week strike by the prisoners due to continuous music broadcasts, which eventually led to an agreement with the Iraqi commander to silence the speakers during prayer times. Relying on prayer and the Quran, the prisoners maintained their faith and identity despite torture and harsh prison conditions. Golefshani also detailed the extreme difficulty of fasting during the Iraqi summer heat, amidst severe food and water shortages. He emphasized that adherence to worship was the primary factor in the prisoners' survival and dignity. The program concluded by praising the achievements of Iranian youth in international sports and science, highlighting their role in countering the enemy's soft war.
