The program interprets Quranic verses to define "raising of the heart" as a continuous spiritual elevation through constant remembrance of God.
A spiritual veil arises when an individual neglects divine remembrance and becomes entangled with material worldly concerns.
According to a narration from Imam Sadiq, three key principles for human perfection are equality, justice in rights, and perpetual remembrance of God.
The broadcast emphasizes the necessity of simplicity and detachment from material accounting, citing the example of Sheikh Mufid's ascetic lifestyle.
In the second segment, the concept of humility is explored through Hadiths from the book of Kafi, defining it as sitting in positions lower than one's status.
A practical example of humility is provided through Imam Sadiq's approval of a man personally carrying household groceries, a task the Imam himself wished to perform.
Ultimately, humility is characterized by natural, egalitarian behavior in public spaces, avoiding any form of self-importance or social distance.
