The radio program "Narrative of Love" on Radio Ma'aref features an interview with Nafiseh Kermani, the wife of Martyr Colonel Ali Agazadeh. Nafiseh recounts their simple marriage during their university years, highlighting their deep spiritual compatibility and her husband's excellent character. She shares memories of their traditional wedding ceremony and their shared pilgrimage trips, particularly to the holy shrines and the "Light of the Path" tours. She explains how her husband's profound spiritual connection with the shrines brought peace to her and their family. The program aims to keep the memory of the martyrs of the Quds Force and the Sacred Defense alive. At the end, the contact numbers for Radio Ma'aref and the IRIB were announced. Other morning and evening programs on Radio Ma'aref were also introduced.
