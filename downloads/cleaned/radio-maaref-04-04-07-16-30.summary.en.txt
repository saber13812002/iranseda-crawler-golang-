This segment of the program begins by discussing the role of guardian angels in protecting humans and recording their deeds.
The primary advice given is to avoid fearing jinn and instead focus on real-life dangers, such as reckless driving.
It is explained that the probability of physical interference by jinn in daily life is extremely low, estimated at only one percent.
For peace of mind and protection from whispers, reciting Surah Al-Falaq and Al-Nas, invoking the intercession of the Sayyids, and reading the Quran are recommended.
An Islamic and Quranic lifestyle is presented as the strongest shield against any negative influences.
The discussion then shifts to interpreting verse 37 of Surah Al-Ahzab, which addresses the Prophet's marriage to Zaynab bint Jahsh after her divorce from Zayd.
The main objective of this marriage was to break the pre-Islamic taboo against marrying a freed slave's ex-wife and to remove hardship for believers.
This event demonstrates the divine will to change incorrect social customs and ensure ease for Muslims.
