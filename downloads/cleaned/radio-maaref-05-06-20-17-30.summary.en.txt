In this radio program, Professor Mohammad Hadi Falahati explores the pathologies of human reason based on the Quran and Islamic traditions. He argues that while reason is a divine gift, it is susceptible to specific flaws and errors that should neither be ignored nor used to dismiss its value entirely. A primary cognitive error identified is substituting mere conjecture for certainty, leading to hasty decisions based on guesswork rather than solid proof. The Quran criticizes the majority of people for engaging in baseless speculation and blindly following ancestral customs without independent thought. Self-conceit and arrogance are also highlighted as major obstacles that extinguish the light of reason and prevent individuals from discovering the truth. Consequently, Quranic rationality requires purifying the heart from sins, cultivating conscience, and avoiding blind adherence to societal norms. Ultimately, achieving worldly and spiritual salvation depends on utilizing reason as a divine argument while rejecting blind imitation of others.
