This radio program features the speeches of Martyr Moharrab, Ayatollah Sadoughi, focusing on the status and steadfastness of the Shia community. The speaker highlights the historical resilience of Shias, from the events of Karbala to the modern era, emphasizing their unique patience and sacrifice. The Islamic Revolution is presented as the culmination of this endurance under the leadership of Imam Khomeini against global powers. Military victories during the Iran-Iraq War are cited as evidence of strength derived from divine guidance and the concept of Wilayah. A central theme is the interpretation of a hadith by Imam Sadiq, which states that Shias possess greater patience than the Ahl al-Bayt. This is explained by the fact that the Ahl al-Bayt endure hardships with full knowledge of their spiritual rewards, whereas Shias endure them in uncertainty. The program concludes with a prayer for continued service to Islam and the recitation of poetry.
