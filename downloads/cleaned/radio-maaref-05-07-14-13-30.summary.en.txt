The program began with recitations of devotional poetry and prayers before transitioning to a legal segment featuring Judge Razaqi. The case involved a dispute over five million tomans between two drivers after a car accident, where one claimed the money was for depreciation while the other argued it was a verbal agreement to be returned after insurance payout. The judge ruled the verbal agreement valid under Article 10 of the Civil Code and Quranic verses on keeping promises, ordering the defendant to return the funds. He clarified that while the plaintiff has the right to claim depreciation, it must be pursued through a separate lawsuit rather than withholding the agreed-upon money. The religious segment followed, interpreting Quranic verses that prohibit consuming wealth without legitimate cause and emphasizing the importance of respecting others' rights. The broadcast concluded with a political analysis highlighting the strategic defeat of the Zionist regime against regional resistance and international support.
