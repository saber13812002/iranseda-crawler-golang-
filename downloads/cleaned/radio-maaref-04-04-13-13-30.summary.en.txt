The radio program begins by inviting listeners to submit audio works for the "Novagolan Hosseini" campaign. It then narrates the events of the Battle of Karbala, where Imam Hussein's brothers are martyred. After witnessing their deaths, Abbas (AS) requests permission from the Imam to secure water for the camp. During his journey, he engages in combat with the enemy forces, and his right hand is severed by Zayd al-Riqat. Despite his severe injuries, Abbas (AS) continues to defend the Imam using his left hand. Ultimately, Hakim al-Tufayl cuts off his left hand, leading to Abbas's martyrdom. Imam Hussein (AS) reacts with profound grief upon seeing his brother's death. This narrative is part of a martyrdom series broadcast on Radio Moharram.
