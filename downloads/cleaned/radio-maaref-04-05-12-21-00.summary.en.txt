In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the necessity of seeking intercession through the Ahl al-Bayt to achieve salvation, citing narrations from Imam Baqir. The segment included an anecdote about Ayatollah Mirani, who would rub his own shroud with the tears of young men weeping for Imam Hussein to seek God's witness to their love. It also referenced Ayatollah Mousavi's views on the spiritual and worldly success derived from reciting elegies and seeking intercession. The main narrative, drawn from the book "Let Your Wound Bleed," depicted Har ibn Yazid Ri'ahi informing Imam Hussein of the Kufans' betrayal and their pledge of allegiance to Yazid. Upon hearing this, the Imam decided not to proceed to Kufa but continued his journey in the desert, intending to head toward Mecca. The episode concluded with Har drawing his sword upon seeing Tarmah and Ibn Ziyad's army, declaring his refusal to fight against the Imam's forces.
