This radio program began by addressing fiqh questions answered by Ayatollah Soubhani regarding prayer with a hearing aid, the khums on subsidy cards, and religious gifts. The broadcast then highlighted the importance of respecting believers by sharing anecdotes about the late Ayatollah Rohani's humble and compassionate treatment of workers and those in need. Subsequently, the discussion shifted to the philosophy and effects of attending Friday prayers, emphasizing unity, social awareness, and moral education. The host noted the spiritual sweetness and sense of community experienced by worshippers during this major gathering. An expert, Hojjat al-Islam Shams, was invited to elaborate on the deeper philosophy of Friday prayers. He described the prayer as a significant collective worship and a socio-political assembly. The program concluded by encouraging listeners to use the Iran Sound app and tuned into the live broadcast of Friday prayers in Tehran and Qom.
