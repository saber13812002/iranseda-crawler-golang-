The news segment of Radio Ma'aref reported on the IRGC's missile strikes in Operation Solemn Promise 3 towards occupied territories. The National Security Council announced a final deadline for identifying deceived individuals, warning of strict action against those collaborating with the enemy. The Deputy Minister of Agriculture threatened legal action against hoarding and price gouging of essential goods. The Hajj and Pilgrimage Organization stated that the return of 35,000 pilgrims would be completed by the fifth of Muharram. The Foreign Minister arrived in Istanbul to attend an Organization of Islamic Cooperation meeting. In the Q&A section, a mosque imam clarified that giving charity to needy relatives counts as both charity and family support. The broadcast concluded with Husseini anthems and Muharram slogans honoring Imam Hussein.
