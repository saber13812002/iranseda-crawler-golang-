The radio program "Enduring Memories" began by revisiting the sacred defense era, featuring the recollections of Sardar Mohammad Bagher Nikkhah Bahrami. He detailed his experiences in the fronts of Susangard and Khorramshahr, highlighting the establishment of front-line propaganda units and the creation of prayer trenches to maintain the spiritual morale of the fighters. The program also recounted the tragic story of Ali Reza Allahyari, a Pasdaran who was executed by Iraqi forces after his identity was revealed during captivity. Sardar Bahrami described the critical role of clergymen in providing comfort to soldiers during intense combat situations. Additionally, the narrative included the memories of martyr and veteran Seyyed Mojtaba Mirzaei regarding the Karkheh Koor operation and the bravery of young fighters. The broadcast also referenced the legal complaint filed by martyr Seyyed Hossein Olum-al-Hoda against his pre-revolutionary torturers. Finally, the program emphasized that great Iranian poets, from Ferdowsi to Hafez, consistently drew their inspiration and wisdom from Islamic teachings and the Quran.
