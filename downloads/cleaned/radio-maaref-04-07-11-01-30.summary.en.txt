The program begins with a sharp critique of the United States' inconsistent and unreliable policies toward Iran, arguing that Washington's frequent shifts from threats to negotiation offers reveal a strategic weakness and lack of credibility. The speaker highlights the perceived military and intelligence failures of the US against Iran, suggesting that global fear of potential American and Israeli attacks has diminished due to Iran's demonstrated resistance. The discussion then transitions to the strategic necessity of nuclear energy for Iran, emphasizing its vital benefits in medicine, agriculture, industry, and national energy security beyond mere military applications. It is argued that mastering nuclear technology is essential for Iran to achieve economic independence and prevent global powers from monopolizing fossil fuel resources. In the final segment, the program honors the day of the passing of Lady Fatima Masumeh (SA) by recounting the biography of Lady Fatima Khadimah, the mother of Imam Reza. This section narrates stories of her noble character, courage, and deep familial bond with the Ahl al-Bayt, portraying her high moral and spiritual status.
