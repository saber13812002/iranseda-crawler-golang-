This radio program begins by celebrating Girls' Day and praising the status of Hazrat Fatima Masumeh (SA) alongside Imam Reza (AS). The main discussion then shifts to the concept of divine anger, analyzing it through the Quran and the narrations of the Ahl al-Bayt (AS). Professor Mohammad Hadi Falah identifies three primary causes for God's wrath: deviation from the straight path, denial of monotheistic truths, and moral deviations. The first moral deviation is having a bad opinion of God, which is directly linked to a believer's perception of the Divine as stated in the Hadith Qudsi. The second cause is engaging in argumentation with the Prophet and the Ahl al-Bayt (AS) after having already accepted their truth through sound reason. Finally, envy towards Muhammad and his family is described as the root of disbelief and self-destruction, leading to severe and repeated divine punishment.
