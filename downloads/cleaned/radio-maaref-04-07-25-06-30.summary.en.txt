The program explores the degrees of doubt and its relationship with monotheism and faith, arguing that absolute doubt is of Satanic origin rather than rational or divine. True monotheism encompasses belief, knowledge, ethics, and action, where any weakness in these areas constitutes a hidden form of shirk. The discussion then shifts to a narrated interpretation of a Quranic verse regarding those whose hearts and ears are sealed by God. The Prophet (PBUH) identifies the incident where Imam Ali (AS) and Thabit ibn Qays were thrown into a well by hypocrites as the occasion for this revelation. A miraculous event occurred where Ali (AS), despite being heavier, reached the well's edge faster due to divine wisdom and saved his companion. The hypocrites witnessed this miracle and the safety of Ali (AS) from heavy stones but remained steadfast in their disbelief. The narration emphasizes that spiritual ranks are achieved through lifelong effort and piety, not merely by human will.
