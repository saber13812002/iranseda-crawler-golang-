In this radio program, the guest shared his international experiences performing Zourkhaneh music in England and Italy, highlighting the art's role in promoting Iranian culture. By referencing his championship in global festivals, he demonstrated how blending Iranian musical modes with classical poetry creates an engaging atmosphere for Western audiences. Through live performances of Rumi and Shahnameh pieces, he proved that Zourkhaneh rhythms can resonate with the younger generation and attract them to the tradition. He also emphasized the importance of preserving the authenticity of Zourkhaneh, warning against reducing it to mere ceremonial recitations and advocating for dynamic, movement-based performances. Towards the end, the guest addressed the issue of ranking masters, calling for a review of the evaluation process and increased media promotion for the art. Using a cooking analogy, he cautioned that every element must be placed correctly to maintain the overall quality of the performance. The discussion concluded with an invitation for the audience to deepen their understanding of Zourkhaneh and support experienced masters.
