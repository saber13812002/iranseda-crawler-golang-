In this radio program, the speaker emphasizes that Imam Sajjad's (PBUH) education and preaching remained effective despite political siege and suffocation, highlighting his role in training future generations and sending missionaries worldwide. He presents the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya as a model for expressing religious teachings through supplication and virtual speech, which brings peace even in crises like the pandemic or war. Referring to historical and contemporary injustices, the speaker recommends reciting specific prayers from the Sahifa to alleviate oppression, ward off danger, and soothe grief. The importance of utilizing divine blessings such as the Quran and narrations to find life solutions is also stressed. The program concludes with the recitation of verses from Surah Ash-Shura and the playing of Hussaini elegies, transitioning to a theological Q&A session with Professor Mohammadi.
