This text marks the conclusion of a special radio program for "Quds Day" during the month of Ramadan, beginning with praise for the Supreme Leader. The host commends Iran's successful navigation of four decades of crises under his leadership and thanks the public, particularly children and families, for their enthusiastic participation in the Quds Day march. After offering prayers and expressing hope for the appearance of the Savior and the eradication of oppression, the program transitions to a recitation of the Holy Quran. The recitation includes verses from Surah Ash-Shu'ara, Surah Ta-Ha, and Surah Sad, covering stories of prophets and the attributes of the inhabitants of Paradise. The final section of the recitation features verses from Surah Sad that refute the claims of the disbelievers regarding the Prophet and describe divine punishment. The audio transcription ends with a repetitive and disordered recitation of specific verses.
