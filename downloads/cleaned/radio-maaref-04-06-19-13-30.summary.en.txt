This radio program began by recounting the life and martyrdom of Dr. Ali Mohammadi, a nuclear scientist, through the words of his wife, Ms. Karami. She described his early childhood, his intense passion for mathematics and physics, and his academic journey at Shiraz University. The narrative also covered his revolutionary activities during his student years and the traditional nature of his marriage. The second part of the broadcast featured the Supreme Leader's remarks on the occasion of the Prophet's birth and Unity Week. The Leader emphasized the importance of adhering to the Prophet's humanistic teachings, such as knowledge, wisdom, ethics, and mercy. He stated that the primary priority for the Islamic world today is achieving unity among Muslims. The Leader called upon officials, scholars, and intellectuals to work towards the cohesion of the Islamic Ummah and to avoid division.
