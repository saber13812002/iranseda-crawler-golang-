The radio program "Enduring Memories" aired to honor the Persian language and the veterans of the Sacred Defense. Sardar Mohammad Bagher Nikkhah Bahrami recounted the initial Iraqi invasion, including the bombing of Tehran airport and his deployment to Ahvaz. He described the early battles at Susangard, noting how Iraqi tanks got stuck in the mud and were targeted by tank hunter units. Colonel Qasem Karimi shared his first mission in Kermanshah, detailing the unit's movement toward the Zayandeh Rud bridge. He explained that a mistaken report by an Iraqi pilot prevented a direct confrontation, allowing Iranian forces to secure the area without casualties. The program concluded by emphasizing the need for a new scientific renaissance to maintain Iran's global standing. It also highlighted the successful launch of the Nahid satellite and the critical role of science in establishing national authority.
