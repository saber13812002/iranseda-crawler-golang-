The program began by emphasizing the importance of charity and avoiding the regret of unfulfilled good deeds after death.
The main segment focused on the concept of arrogance and its causes, based on Quranic verses.
Professor Fallah explained that God explicitly questions humans in the Surah Al-Infitar, defining arrogance as an error in the path of monotheistic upbringing.
The primary cause of arrogance is the deception of the ego, which beautifies sins and mistakes, making them appear easy and acceptable.
This self-deception can also be influenced by the whispers of jinn and human devils, as well as worldly attractions.
Quranic interpretation suggests that humans fall into arrogance by forgetting God's lordship and generosity.
Recognizing these factors and guarding against them is essential for spiritual health and moral progress.
Finally, listeners were advised to reflect on the causes of arrogance and protect their souls from internal and external deceptions.
