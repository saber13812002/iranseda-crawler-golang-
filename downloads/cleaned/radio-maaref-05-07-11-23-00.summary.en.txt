In this Radio Ma'aref program, the first characteristic of the pious mentioned in Imam Ali's Khutbat al-Mutaqqin, "correct speech free from error," was analyzed.
The core message is that human worth lies in moral virtues rather than wealth or power, and the pious consistently embody these virtues in all aspects of life.
Speech is described as a divine gift with significant risks because it is always accessible, cost-free, and carries less social stigma than other sins, making it a frequent tool for wrongdoing.
Correct speech involves speaking at the right time and remaining silent otherwise, a skill essential for interacting appropriately with various groups of people.
One must address children with affectionate terms, treat elderly parents with utmost respect to avoid their negative prayers, and speak gently to sinners while being candid with hypocrites.
When praying, one should speak calmly without raising their voice, as God is near, and when dealing with non-believers, one must maintain decorum to prevent mutual disrespect of sacred values.
Ultimately, mastering the ethics of speech and controlling one's tongue is the key to resolving life's problems and achieving the status of the pious.
