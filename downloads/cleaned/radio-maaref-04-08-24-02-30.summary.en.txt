In the radio program "Porsaman Quran," featuring expert Haghghasem Maleki, several listener questions were addressed. The first discussion clarified that the "soul" is a creation of God rather than part of His essence, with the phrase "breathed into them My spirit" signifying human dignity. Regarding internet filtering and the verse "they follow the best," the expert explained that this does not imply listening to all speech, but rather selecting the best from permissible options while avoiding falsehoods and doubts. The title "Spirit of God" for Prophet Jesus (PBUH) was interpreted as a result of his unique creation without a father, directly by God's will. On the topic of guidance and misguidance, it was emphasized that God does not force anyone into misguidance; instead, He guides those who accept truth, while misguidance is the outcome of human free will. The repetition of "Which of your Lord's favors would you deny?" in Surah Ar-Rahman was described as an educational method to reinforce blessings and remind humans of their duties. The program concluded with a Quranic prayer for family peace and the guidance of the pious.
