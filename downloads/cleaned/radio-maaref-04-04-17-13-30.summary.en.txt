In the radio program "Mashgh-e Rah," Hojjat-ol-Islam Faghihi emphasized the educational and monotheistic role of sending blessings (Salawat) in prayers, citing the Supreme Leader's statements. He explained that Salawat serves as a divine intermediary that ensures prayers are answered and helps individuals recognize their dependence on God's saints. The guest also discussed the consequences of missing the morning prayer, comparing it to spiritual starvation that leads to moral weakness and sin over time. He highlighted the importance of understanding the true value of the morning prayer, likening it to a Hajj journey or a meeting with high officials to motivate waking up early. The program then shifted to the era of Imam Sajjad (AS), who, after the tragedy of Karbala, used wise policies to reunite the scattered Shia community. Through teaching authentic Islamic knowledge, Imam Sajjad guided the community through a crisis and expanded the influence of Shia Islam across various regions. Finally, the discussion touched upon the transfer of Imamate to Imam Baqir (AS), noting that the former handed over a chest containing spiritual secrets rather than material wealth.
