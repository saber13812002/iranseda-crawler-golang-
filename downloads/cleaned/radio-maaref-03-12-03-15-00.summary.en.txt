This radio program analyzed the personal and managerial characteristics of Ayatollah Khamenei as the leader of the Islamic Republic. The host and expert emphasized the necessity of three conditions for a leader: ijtihad, management, and political piety (justice), noting that such strict criteria are unique to Iran's system. A key point of discussion was Ayatollah Khamenei's explicit and firm rejection of his own candidacy during the Assembly of Experts meeting, highlighting his lack of any lobbying for power. After the Assembly insisted, his acceptance of the duty demonstrated his deep commitment to continuing Imam Khomeini's path and serving the people. His four decades of managerial experience during the war and sanctions were presented as a significant asset for the system's stability. Furthermore, his ability to explain public opinion and build public trust was cited as a crucial leadership trait. Finally, the repeated predictions by enemies regarding the system's collapse were challenged by the Islamic Republic's increasing strength over the years.
