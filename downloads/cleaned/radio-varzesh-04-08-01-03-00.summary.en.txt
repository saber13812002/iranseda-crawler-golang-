The guest criticized the personal and non-technical conflicts in Iranian football, highlighting management issues such as the delayed arrival of players for national team matches. He described the golden era of the national team during the 1996 Asian Cup, where a smart mix of young and experienced players under domestic coaching led to a bronze medal and continental championship. These successes paved the way for player migrations to Europe and qualification for the 1998 World Cup. Conversely, he attributed later failures, such as in 2002, to a lack of attention to nutrition and rest principles, as well as non-technical interference on match days. The guest emphasized that excuses regarding hotel conditions or food in host countries are invalid justifications for defeats. He concluded that the focus should remain on internal technical and managerial improvements rather than external factors.
