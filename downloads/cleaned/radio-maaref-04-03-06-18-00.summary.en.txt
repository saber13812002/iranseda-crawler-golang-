This radio program primarily discusses the moral virtue of humility and modesty. Humility is defined as an internal and spiritual quality that, while manifested in outward behavior, is rooted in the heart. Imam Sadiq (AS) categorized humility as part of the "lesser army" of virtues, in contrast to arrogance. This virtue exists in various degrees and is possessed by prophets and saints alike. According to the Quran, those who hold high positions should not consider themselves superior to others, as exemplified by Prophet Noah's (AS) approach to his people. The Quran uses the earth as a symbol of humility, encouraging believers to be modest with one another while remaining firm against disbelievers. The second part of the program highlights home-based Quranic sessions in Bafq, Yazd, which involve students and school staff. These sessions focus on teaching recitation and tajweed while fostering friendly relationships and identifying Quranic talents. With a simple and friendly approach, these gatherings continue even during holidays, effectively engaging youth and guiding them away from virtual spaces toward religious values.
