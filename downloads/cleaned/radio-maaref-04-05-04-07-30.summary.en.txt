The radio program began by highlighting the positive traits of people from various Iranian cities, such as kindness, hospitality, and devotion to the Imam. The discussion then shifted to the negative impact of social media on teenagers, particularly regarding body image dissatisfaction and diminished self-confidence. Experts advised parents to enhance their children's media literacy and guide them toward spiritual beauty to mitigate these digital harms. The main segment featured a lecture by Hujjat al-Islam Fala on the concept of "stinginess," clarifying that it extends beyond money to include withholding effort, service, and even greetings. The speaker outlined four dangerous consequences of stinginess: personal unrest, the loss of wealth, a fall from dignity into degradation, and ultimately, spending in the path of sin. It was emphasized that stinginess is a form of disbelief and disobedience to God, which alienates an individual from divine grace. The program concluded by reinforcing that true dignity is found in generosity and obedience, not in hoarding.
