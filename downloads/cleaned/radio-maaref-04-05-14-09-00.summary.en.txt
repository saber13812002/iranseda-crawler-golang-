The first segment of the program discussed the hierarchy of martyrs and scholars, emphasizing that scholars are the cause of martyrdom and thus hold a primary position, while final judgment on merit belongs solely to God.
Subsequently, the distinction between major and minor sins was explored, noting that factors such as trivializing a sin, persisting in it, or committing it openly can elevate a minor sin to a major one.
Listeners were also advised to conserve water and electricity due to energy crises and to take health precautions during pilgrimage trips in hot weather.
The final part of the broadcast focused on the Israeli attack on Astaneh Ashrafiyeh, which resulted in the martyrdom of the family of the late scientist Dr. Mohammad Reza Sadeghi.
The martyr's brother, the sole survivor of the family, spoke about the targeted assassination of Iranian nuclear scientists by Mossad with US complicity.
He highlighted the role of these assassinations in delaying Iran's nuclear program and recounted the tragic loss of his brother and other relatives in the missile strike.
Finally, references were made to Western documentaries detailing intelligence efforts to prevent Iran from acquiring nuclear technology through the assassination of its experts.
