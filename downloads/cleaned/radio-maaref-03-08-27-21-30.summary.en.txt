The text provides a tafsir analysis of a Quranic verse promising that the People of the Book will believe in Jesus before their death. The speaker argues that if this belief were meant to be genuine and spiritually beneficial, it would contradict historical facts and verse 55 of Surah Al-Imran, which states that the People of the Book divided into two groups after Jesus's departure. Consequently, this belief is considered superficial and devoid of true spiritual value. The primary reason for this is the profound historical disagreements among various groups regarding Jesus's identity, prophethood, and even his existence. Due to these fundamental disputes, it is highly unlikely that all People of the Book held a uniform and complete belief in Jesus's truthfulness. Ultimately, the speaker concludes that the verse does not imply a universal and sincere belief in Jesus by all People of the Book, but rather refers to a limited or apparent acceptance.
