The radio program "Narrative of Love" began with an interview with the mother of martyr Javad Allahkaram.
The martyr, born in Tehran in 1981, served as a military advisor in Syria.
He was wounded three times and was martyred in 2016 during an attack on the city of Khan Touman.
His mother spoke about his humble character, devotion to prayer, and sense of responsibility from childhood.
She expressed her peace of mind derived from the Quran, viewing his martyrdom as a conscious choice for God's pleasure.
The 73-year-old mother emphasized that mothers must raise their children with energy and without fatigue to serve society.
The program concluded by inviting listeners to send their messages via the provided communication channels.
