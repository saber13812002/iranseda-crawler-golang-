In the radio program "Zamzam Ahkam," religious rulings were provided in response to listeners' questions.
According to the religious authority, bonuses are not subject to Khums, but any investment profit exceeding annual expenses is taxable.
The expiation for intentionally breaking a Ramadan fast involves feeding sixty poor people, fasting for sixty days, or paying a specific penalty.
The second segment featured the Supreme Leader's statements on the necessity of strengthening Iran's national and scientific power.
It was emphasized that the country's dignity relies on standing firm against sanctions and the nation's steel-like will.
The program concluded with religious supplications and an invitation for listeners to submit more legal questions.
Additionally, the broadcast time for the morning show "Sobh-e Niaz" and the radio's contact channels were announced.
