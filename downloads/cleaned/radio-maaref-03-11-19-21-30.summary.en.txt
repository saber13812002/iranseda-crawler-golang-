In this lecture on Mahdism, the policy of Imam al-Zaman (AJ) towards Jews and other non-Muslim groups during the era of reappearance was examined. Citing verse 83 of Surah Al-Imran and narrations from Imams Kazim and Sadiq (AS), it was emphasized that all inhabitants of the earth must accept Islam, either voluntarily or by force. A narration from Imam Kazim (AS) explicitly states that Islam will be offered to Jews, Christians, and other groups, and acceptance requires the full implementation of Islamic laws. Conversely, those who refuse to accept Islam will lose the right to life and be eradicated from the East and West until only monotheism remains. The instructor analyzed the chains of transmission, noting that while some narrations are marasil, they are not rejected due to their strong textual content and multiple corroborating evidence. A narration from Amir al-Mu'minin (AS) was also referenced, mentioning celestial signs and boundaries in Ramadan prior to the reappearance. The final conclusion is that the global government of Imam al-Zaman (AJ) is based on completing the argument and compelling the acceptance of monotheism and prophethood, leaving no third option for opponents.
