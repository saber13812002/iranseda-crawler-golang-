On the eve of the Arbaeen commemoration, the speaker emphasizes the significance of the visit and its conceptual analysis. Citing historical sources, he identifies Arbaeen as the day the head of Imam Hussein (PBUH) was buried, marking the end of the Ahl al-Bayt's siege. The concept of "Bazl," meaning generosity and sacrifice, is presented as a key characteristic of true Shia and believers. Imam Hussein (PBUH) sacrificed his life to save society from ignorance and corruption, serving as a model for investing in the faith. Every individual should contribute to preserving religion according to their specific capabilities, whether through wealth, writing, art, reputation, or even marriage and modesty. These sacrifices not only prevented the fragmentation that followed Karbala but also kept social awakenings alive throughout history. Finally, the speaker urges everyone to be defenders of the faith, moving from mere expectation to active sacrifice and action.
