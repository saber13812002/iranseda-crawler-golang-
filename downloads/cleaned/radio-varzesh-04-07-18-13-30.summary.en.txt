In the Asian Water Polo Championship semifinal, the Iranian national team faced Japan's second squad, which features a very young player pool. Iran initially took the lead with goals from Ali Reza Mehri and Mehdi Yazdan Khah, but Japan shifted the momentum with rapid, targeted attacks on the left side of the goal. Despite Amin Qavidl's efforts to close the gap, one of his goals was disallowed by the referees due to a possession time violation. Japan solidified its lead with multiple goals from their young captain, Anishi Ora, and other players. Iran trailed 8-5 at halftime and remained three goals behind (11-8) during the second half. Although Iran has a history of comebacks in difficult matches, they are currently struggling with defensive issues and frequent turnovers.
