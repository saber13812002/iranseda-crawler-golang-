This radio program, broadcast on the 12th of Muharram, the martyrdom anniversary of Imam Sajjad (AS), began with special condolences and a review of Ashura events.
The main segment featured an interview with Dr. Mohammad Saber Jafari, who analyzed Lady Zainab’s (SA) sermon in the Kufa market.
Citing the Supreme Leader’s insights, the expert described this sermon as a profound analysis of the Islamic society's condition and a sharp critique of Kufans' deceit.
The behavior of the Kufans was metaphorically compared to "thunder without rain" and a "cow that kicks after giving milk," symbolizing broken promises and betrayal.
A key point in the discussion was the distinction between valuable repentance and "reactionism," or the incorrect return to ignorance, which Lady Zainab (SA) condemned.
The program also highlighted the role of "Jihad of Clarification" in the Ashura uprising and the efforts of Lady Zainab (SA) and Imam Sajjad (AS) to enlighten the public.
Finally, listeners were invited to share their feedback on these analyses by sending messages to the specified SMS and phone numbers.
