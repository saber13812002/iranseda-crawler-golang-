The radio program began by explaining Ayatollah Makarem Shirazi's ruling on the execution of the hadd for alcohol consumption, which involves eighty lashes administered by the city ruler. It then cited Ayatollah Sistani's fatwa stating that sending salam to believers is recommended but not obligatory. The broadcast also covered Ayatollah Sobhani's permission to recite specific supplications during ruku and Ayatollah Shabih Zanjani's ruling that passing gas during wudu does not invalidate it. Additionally, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli's view that women with long hair do not need to touch their scalp directly during masah was presented. A significant portion of the show featured a lecture by Hojjat al-Islam Taheri on the paramount importance of prayer as the first obligation and the primary question in the Hereafter. The program concluded with a report from Golestan Radio, where the host interviewed two pilgrims at Imamzadeh Melat regarding their spiritual experiences and answered prayers.
