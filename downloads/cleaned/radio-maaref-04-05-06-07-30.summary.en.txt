The program begins by citing a Foreign Policy report indicating that US oil sanctions are no longer effective. It then highlights the sacrifice of a rescuer who died in the Caspian Sea while saving a family of six. The discussion compares Iran's 2011 capture of an RQ-170 drone with the US recently unveiling the "Lucas" drone, which was reportedly based on a sampled Shahed 136. The host emphasizes that these events reflect the pride Iranians take in their identity and resilience. The twelve-day war with the Zionist regime is presented as a moment that revealed the deep solidarity and self-sacrifice of the Iranian people. While the opposing regime tried to trap its citizens at the borders, Iranians risked their lives to return to their homeland via land routes. This behavior is rooted in a profound love for Iran and a willingness to make sacrifices against enemies. The show concludes with prayers for the Imam's reappearance and expressions of pride in being Iranian and connected to the leadership.
