The program begins by criticizing rulers who deceive the public with a polished exterior, leading them toward corruption and a revival of pre-Islamic values. It then transitions to a suggestion to recite a specific prayer for Imam Mahdi, honoring the memory of martyr Sayyid Hassan Nasrallah. The main discussion focuses on interpreting the phrase "Correct me by Your grace and heal me by Your power" from the Makarim al-Abrar prayer. The speaker outlines ten areas of correction, including beliefs, behavior, economics, family, self, society, worldly affairs, the afterlife, relationships, and religion, emphasizing the need to acknowledge faults and use reason and the Quran as standards. The second part addresses the concept of healing, examining spiritual and moral diseases such as envy, hypocrisy, and arrogance mentioned in Islamic texts. A book titled "Moral Diseases and the Quran's Healing" is introduced, offering Quranic solutions for forty-four moral ailments. Finally, the role of Imam Mahdi in universal correction is highlighted, reminding believers that awaiting justice requires them to actively pursue reform and avoid corruption themselves.
