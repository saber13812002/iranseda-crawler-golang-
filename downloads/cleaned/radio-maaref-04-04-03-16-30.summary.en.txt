In this radio program, Professor Mohammadi used the analogies of prayer and fasting to explain that faith is an interconnected whole where partial acceptance is meaningless. He emphasized that denying any essential pillar of religion, such as jihad, invalidates the entire faith, making the concept of percentage-based religiosity impossible. In the second segment, the host addressed a listener's question regarding the consciousness of animals and inanimate objects, affirming the existence of cosmic praise in all creation. The professor cited the story of Prophet Solomon and scientific experiments to demonstrate that even inanimate objects possess a form of awareness and respond to divine speech. He also highlighted the ability of certain holy figures to understand animal languages and influence nature through spiritual power. The program concluded with a news segment featuring the Supreme Leader's statement praising the armed forces' resistance and the defeat of the enemy's strategic plans.
