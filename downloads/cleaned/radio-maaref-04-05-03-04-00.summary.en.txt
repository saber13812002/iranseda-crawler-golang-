The radio program begins by praising the greatness of the Quran and its role in guiding humanity.
The central theme is the interpretation of the verse "whom Allah wills to guide, He expands his chest" and the concept of "Sharh-e-Sadr."
This expansion is defined as opening the soul and heart to accept Islamic truths and moving upon divine light.
Achieving this state requires abandoning sins, including disliked acts, performing recommended deeds, and maintaining constant self-awareness.
The second part of the program addresses social etiquette and travel, emphasizing the religious duty to inform friends and family before traveling.
The speaker criticizes the significant decline in family and social relationships in society and stresses faith in divine promises regarding hospitality.
The program concludes with prayers for the Imam of the Age and the recitation of a religious song.
