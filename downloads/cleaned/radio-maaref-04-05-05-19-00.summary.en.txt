The radio program "House of the Beloved" began by discussing the significance of prayer in Islamic traditions and its spiritual connection with God.
Hojjat al-Islam Seyed Reza Taheri explained that prayer is a path to the forgiveness of sins and a means to avoid spiritual misery.
Citing the words of Imam Ali, he described the five daily prayers as expiation for minor sins, provided one avoids major sins.
It was emphasized that prayer should not be used as an excuse to commit sins, but rather serve as a deterrent and a purifier.
In another segment, prayer was presented not merely as a dry obligation, but as a divine gift and a path to spiritual ascent and love for God.
The program concluded with a tribute to the martyr Seyed Mostafa Sadati Armaghi and his family, who were killed in a recent attack.
This memorial highlighted the martyrdom of the nuclear physicist and his children during the recent conflict.
