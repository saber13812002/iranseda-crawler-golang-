This radio program discussed the virtues and high status of Imam Abbas (AS) as the standard-bearer and water-carrier of Karbala.
The concept of "Nasr" (support) in Islam was interpreted in three ways: supporting the religion, God's creation, and God's proof.
Multiple confirmations from the Infallible Imams, including Imam Hussein (AS) and Imam Sadiq (AS), emphasized his special position.
The importance of the flag in Islamic battles as a symbol of victory and courage was highlighted.
Serving the thirsty was introduced as a major virtue of Imam Abbas and the first reward in the Hereafter.
The martyrdom of Holy Defense veterans and Qasem Soleimani was compared to Imam Abbas's sacrifices for his brother.
The program concluded with Quran recitation and a specific prayer for Imam Abbas.
The second part of the broadcast introduced the "Family Questions" program and couples' counseling services.
