In this program, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interprets verses 94 and 95 of Surah Al-Ma'idah, explaining the concept of "ibtila" as a divine test designed to reveal human potential and inner character. He confirms the prohibition of hunting wild animals while in the state of Ihram, clarifying that this ruling applies specifically to the state of Ihram rather than merely being within the sacred precincts. If an animal is hunted intentionally, a compensation equivalent to the killed animal must be determined by two fair Muslim experts. This compensation must be utilized in Mecca through feeding the poor or fasting, although the specific sequence of these options requires reference to hadiths. Repeating the intentional offense after paying compensation results in both the repetition of the penalty and divine retribution. God has forgiven sins committed before this ruling was revealed, but continuing to sin after gaining knowledge leads to severe consequences.
