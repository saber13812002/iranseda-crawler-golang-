The radio program "Barbal Sakhan" began by honoring the memory of Ayatollah Raisi before discussing the philosophy of visiting Imam Reza (AS). Speaker Hujjat al-Islam Sharifati Tabar described this visit as an opportunity to learn from the Imam's conduct and apply his teachings in daily life. Citing a hadith from the Eighth Imam, he outlined five key characteristics of God's best servants. The first trait is feeling joy when performing good deeds for others, recognizing it as a divine blessing. The second is seeking immediate forgiveness after committing a sin or wronging someone to prevent conflicts from escalating. The third involves expressing gratitude to God and the intermediaries of His grace when receiving kindness. The fourth characteristic is maintaining patience and steadfastness in the face of life's hardships and calamities. Finally, the fifth trait is controlling anger and showing forgiveness when feeling upset or provoked. The speaker emphasized that practicing these virtues is essential for resolving social issues and building a healthy, cohesive community.
