At the beginning of the program, Mekarim Shirazi discusses the principle of Istihsab in Usul al-Fiqh, using examples of logical necessity and concomitants.
He analyzes the relationship between assuming the thirtieth day of Ramadan and the Id al-Fitr to demonstrate how logical rulings are derived.
The discussion then shifts to the spiritual topic of the Day of Judgment and the status of Fatimah Zahra (SA).
It is narrated that in the gathering, all creation lowers their heads in respect of her greatness, and two white garments are placed upon her.
Fatimah (SA) recites verses from Surah Fatir, expressing gratitude to God for being freed from worldly sorrows and attaining eternity.
The text highlights a divine dialogue where God invites her to make a request.
In response, Fatimah (SA) declares that God is her only desire and asks for the salvation of her lovers and the lovers of the Imams from hellfire.
God swears by His majesty that He had already vowed, two thousand years before creation, to protect the lovers of Fatimah (SA) from punishment.
