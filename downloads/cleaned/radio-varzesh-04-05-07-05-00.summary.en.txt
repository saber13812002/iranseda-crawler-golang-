The guest recalled the high level of talent and readiness of Iran's youth national team in the past, noting their victories against European and Asian rivals. He emphasized that while modern training and nutrition now meet global standards, the primary gap between Iranian and European football lies in cultural discipline and social order. The guest shared anecdotes about learning tactical skills from foreign coaches like Alain Wittel and playing alongside legends such as Ali Parvin and Ashiani. He also discussed his transition from professional football to dentistry, highlighting how football influenced his academic and social path. Finally, the guest expressed gratitude to the media for keeping the memories of football pioneers alive and mentioned his ongoing social and sports activities with football elders.
