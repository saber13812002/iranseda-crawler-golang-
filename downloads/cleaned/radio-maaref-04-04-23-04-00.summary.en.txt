This radio program discusses the difference between the complete certainty of God's saints and the natural anxiety experienced by ordinary believers until the moment of death.
Based on Quranic interpretations, continuous sinning and mockery of religion lead to a bad ending and the denial of divine truths.
Achieving a good ending requires piety, appreciating God's blessings, and avoiding arrogance after committing mistakes.
Respecting individuals who invoke the name of the Ahl al-Bayt, even if they are insincere, is cited as a factor in attaining a good ending.
A hadith attributed to Jesus (PBUH) emphasizes that the value of a person's actions depends on the "last stone" of the building, meaning their state at the end of life rather than just the beginning.
The second part interprets the miracles of Jesus (PBUH) with a focus on the phrase "by the permission of God" to demonstrate that all such powers originate from the Divine.
The distinction between divine miracles and personal knowledge of the unseen is clarified, noting that miracles strictly require God's permission, while knowing hidden matters may be achieved through other means.
