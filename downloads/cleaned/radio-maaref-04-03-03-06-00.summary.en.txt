In this radio program broadcast on the occasion of Khorramshahr's liberation, Ayatollah Javadi Amoli interpreted verse 105 of Surah Al-Ma'idah. He explained that inner blindness stems from forgetting God's verses and spiritual negligence, which manifests as physical blindness on the Day of Judgment. The interpretation highlights three levels of "remembrance" in the Quran: remembering blessings for gratitude, remembering God for divine attention, and absolute remembrance for reaching the station of contentment. Imam Ali (AS) also warned in Nahj al-Balagha that focusing on external matters rather than self-purification leads to confusion and spiritual wandering. Therefore, the path to salvation from blindness and misguidance is to concentrate on self-discipline and returning to the remembrance of the Creator. This process ensures that God also remembers the servant, thereby illuminating their existence. Ultimately, only those who have attained the station of contentment and a tranquil soul are worthy of the invitation to "My servants" and "My garden."
