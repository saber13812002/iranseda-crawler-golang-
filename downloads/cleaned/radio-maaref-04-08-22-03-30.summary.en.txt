The program challenges the claim that Talha ibn Ubaydillah repented and pledged allegiance to Imam Ali after the Battle of Jamal.
Professor Jafari cites authentic narrations where the Imam labeled Talha a covenant-breaker and troublemaker after his death.
The absence of any mention of Talha's repentance in the Imam's official letters further refutes this claim.
Additionally, Talha's previous disobedience to the Prophet during the revelation of the Hijab verse contradicts his supposed martyrdom.
The second part of the discussion analyzes the troop numbers, estimating Imam Ali's force at approximately 12,000 soldiers.
The casualties on the side of the Jamal army are estimated at around 20,000 based on historical counting methods and reports.
In contrast, the casualties for Imam Ali's army were significantly lower, ranging from 400 to 500.
This disparity in casualty figures aligns with the defensive nature of Imam Ali's army and their ultimate victory.
