In the radio program "Mashq-e Rah," Hojjat al-Islam Faghihi discussed the role of supplication in cultivating monotheistic ethics and spiritual awareness. He emphasized that listing specific needs in prayer helps individuals overcome self-sufficiency and recognize their absolute dependence on God. The guest also provided remedies for ostentation and excessive concern with public opinion, recommending focused prayer and remembering that God is greater than all creation. In the Q&A section, he advised against prolonged feuds with friends, highlighting forgiveness as a sign of spiritual strength rather than weakness. The discussion then shifted to the Sacred Defense, noting how it rapidly transformed young individuals into prominent military commanders. This swift development of hidden talents was described as a miracle of the revolution, demonstrating the power of growth under extreme conditions.
