In the radio program "Ma'aref," expert Mehdi Yousefian addressed Mahdavi questions, explaining that judicial rulings during the Imam of the Time's (AJ) governance will primarily follow Islamic Sharia, though special methods similar to those of Prophet David and Solomon may be used in unique cases. He emphasized that the prevalence of justice in that era will drastically reduce the need for formal court proceedings. Yousefian further outlined the path to connecting with the Imam, highlighting strict adherence to religious duties, fulfilling social and family responsibilities, maintaining ethical business practices, and keeping the Imam's remembrance alive, while noting that any meeting with the Imam is solely at his discretion. The program then transitioned to a geopolitical analysis of Syria featuring Dr. Mehdi Moshibaf. Moshibaf, referencing the Supreme Leader's speeches, focused on explaining the causes behind Bashar al-Assad's collapse and mapping the region's future. He argued that the swift fall of the Assad regime is the result of a long-term process following the dissolution of the Ottoman Empire, rather than just recent events. The aim of this discussion is to clarify public misconceptions regarding the region's trajectory and Iran's position.
