Ayatollah Ramezani Gilani discusses his modest lifestyle in contrast to the luxury and class divide observed among some officials. He recalls his childhood passion for reading and answering religious questions, which laid the foundation for his later religious activities and public speaking. The cleric criticizes the punitive and violent disciplinary methods of the pre-revolutionary era, advocating for their replacement with counseling, compassion, and guidance in modern education. He also highlights the profound spiritual impact of following the Imam's line in shaping his character and beliefs. The discussion emphasizes the resilience and unity of the Iranian people against plots and attempts to sow discord. These insights suggest that the integration of education, religious upbringing, and social awareness is key to societal resistance and progress.
