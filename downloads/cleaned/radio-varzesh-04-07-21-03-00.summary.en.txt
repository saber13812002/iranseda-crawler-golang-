In this discussion, Ali Akbar Kashian emphasized the need for a fundamental reform of ethics and corruption in Iranian sports by elevating the status and comprehensive training of coaches. He argued that coaches must be proficient not only in technical skills but also in psychology, ethics, and crisis management, citing the specialized courses he has organized in Mashhad. Kashian also introduced the initiative to establish "Mosque Clubs" as a unique capacity for promoting mass sports and nurturing the younger generation. Supported by the Ministry of Culture and welcomed by the Ministry of Sports, this plan aims to utilize the extensive network of mosques to bring sports to local neighborhoods and deprived areas. The primary goal of this initiative is to attract youth, reduce social harms, and ensure equitable access to sports facilities. By referencing the successful experience of churches in the United States, Kashian highlighted the high potential of this project to uncover hidden talents and increase mass sports participation on a million-scale.
