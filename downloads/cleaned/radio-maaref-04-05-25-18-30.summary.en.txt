In the religious rulings segment, disregarding the command of congregational prayer is forbidden, though absence due to a valid excuse or laziness is not a sin, despite losing the spiritual merit. The practice of hurrah in mourning ceremonies is permissible unless it leads to disrespect or diminishes the dignity of the mourning in the eyes of others. The educational section highlights that prayer serves to purify believers from shirk and eradicate the vice of arrogance. Imam Ali (AS) emphasized that prayer instills humility before God, which prevents arrogance towards other creatures in daily life. Furthermore, prayer acts as a factory for human development, protecting individuals from committing major sins and indecencies. The program also reports on the significant participation of youth in the Muharram mourning ceremonies at the Imamzadeh Ali Akbar shrine in Tehran.
