In this program, Professor Pishbahi discussed the specific deputies of the Imam of the Time (AJ), emphasizing that their selection is a direct appointment by the Imam rather than a council-based process. He recommended two books for further study: "Nawab Arba'a" by Abbas Rasakhi Najafi and "A Study on the Lives of the Imam's Specific Deputies" by Ali Ghararzadeh. The primary characteristic of these deputies was described as steadfastness and resistance to pressure in order to preserve the Imam's secrets. The second part of the discussion addressed historical accusations against Khwaja Nasir al-Din Tusi, where the professor refuted the claim that he encouraged Hulagu's attack on Baghdad. Citing reliable historical sources, he explained that Tusi actually mitigated the Mongols' behavior to prevent widespread slaughter. The origin of these false accusations was attributed to Ibn Taymiyyah and his students, who aimed to discredit Shia scholars. The book "The Caliph, the Abbasids, and the Mongols" was also introduced to highlight the incompetence of the Abbasid Caliph in the fall of Baghdad. The program concluded with the recitation of Imam Sajjad's prayer for rain and an announcement regarding the submission of articles to the Supreme Council of the Quran.
