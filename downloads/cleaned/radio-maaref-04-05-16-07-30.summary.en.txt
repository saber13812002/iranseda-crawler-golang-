The program begins by highlighting the courage and sacrifice of the Iranian people in the face of enemy threats, contrasting their resilience with the historical stubbornness of the Children of Israel.
Key traits observed during recent events include self-sacrifice, maintaining hope, and relying on God while strategically planning a response to aggression.
The second part of the broadcast analyzes the role of speech in revealing human character, based on the narrations of Imam Ali (AS).
Language is described as a dual tool that can either protect life through virtue or destroy it through vice and moral terrorism.
A clear distinction is drawn between the "language of the heart," which brings peace and love, and the "language of the ego," which causes chaos and despair.
According to the narration "Al-Lisan Mizan," speech serves as a scale that measures a person's intellect, knowledge, and moral standing.
Ultimately, it is emphasized that individuals are hidden beneath their tongues, making their speech the primary indicator of their true identity and beliefs.
