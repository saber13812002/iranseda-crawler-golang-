At the beginning of his address, the Supreme Leader congratulated the nation on the arrival of the new academic year, emphasizing the importance of investing in the exceptional talents of Iranian youth. He honored the memory of Martyr Hassan Nasrallah and recent martyrs, stressing the enduring legacy of resistance in the region. The Leader highlighted national unity as a resilient strategic factor that frustrated the enemy's plans during the recent twelve-day conflict. He described uranium enrichment as a vital scientific achievement with extensive applications in medicine, agriculture, and energy, rather than merely a political issue. Rejecting Western claims that national unity is fading, he affirmed that Iran, as one of the few nations with enrichment capabilities, will never submit to external pressure. Finally, the Leader assessed negotiations with the current US administration as futile and potentially harmful to national interests. He argued that the American side seeks to impose conditions for the complete dismantling of Iran's nuclear and missile programs, rather than engaging in a balanced dialogue.
