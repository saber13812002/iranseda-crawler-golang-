In this radio program, Professor Mohammadi emphasized the importance of remaining steadfast against the mockery of religious enemies, citing Quranic verses and narrations. He described faith as an unparalleled jewel that should not be abandoned due to the ignorance or envy of others. The speaker outlined the paths of recognizing truth through reason and spiritual insight, encouraging listeners to open their hearts to divine realities through proper worship. In the Q&A section, the virtues of reciting "La ilaha illa Allah" during prostration were discussed, along with the need for caution against potential satanic influences in high-volume recitations. The necessity of dissociating from oppressors before declaring enmity and the availability of repentance until the moment of death were also highlighted. The program further addressed the etiquette of hospitality and visiting based on the Quran and the Prophet's tradition, advising against asking guests intrusive questions. Finally, the immense value of maintaining family ties was presented as a key factor in extending life and bringing blessings.
