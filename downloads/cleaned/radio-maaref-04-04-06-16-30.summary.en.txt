In this radio program, Dr. Mohammad Saber Jafari analyzes Imam Hussein's (PBUH) departure from Mecca and its underlying causes. He explains that upon learning of Yazid's assassination plot, the Imam decided to leave to preserve the sanctity of Mecca and prevent bloodshed within the holy precincts. During his four-month stay in Mecca, the Imam utilized the Hajj and Umrah seasons to expand his social base through public enlightenment and clarification of truths. Simultaneously, extensive communication was established with the people of Kufa, resulting in thousands of letters and pledges of allegiance. The Imam sent Muslim ibn Aqil to Kufa to prepare the ground for the uprising, but the martyrdom of Muslim and Hani altered the course of the journey toward Karbala. A key point emphasized is that pledging allegiance to Yazid was impossible for the Imam, even under the pretext of taqiyya, as he represented absolute truth while Yazid represented absolute falsehood. Finally, the concept of "Jihad of Clarification" is introduced as the central axis of the Imam's activities, extending the Prophet's and Imam Ali's (PBUH) command to combat deviations through both speech and action.
