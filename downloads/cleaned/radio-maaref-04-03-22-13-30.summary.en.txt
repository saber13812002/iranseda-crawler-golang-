The radio program "Narrative of Love" concluded its first section with an interview featuring Fatemeh Rezaei, the wife of the martyr pilot Bahman Masaei. Fatemeh recounted the final moments of her husband's mission and her deep anxiety regarding his safe return home. The news of the plane crash at the Saravan border and Bahman's martyrdom remains the most painful and enduring memory of her life. Due to her fear of delayed or false news, she had always requested that Bahman tell her the truth immediately if anything happened to him. After confirming his martyrdom through his friends, Fatemeh traveled from Zahedan to Tehran to see his body, despite being a breastfeeding mother and suffering from physical injuries. Although her face was severely damaged, she insisted on seeing him one last time despite the concerns of those around her. The program highlights the profound love and patience of martyrs' wives as they face the grief of losing their beloved partners.
