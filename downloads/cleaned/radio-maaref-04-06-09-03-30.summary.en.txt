In the "Zamzam Ahkam" program, jurisprudential answers were provided to listeners' questions. According to Ayatollah Sistani's fatwa, taking medication at night is permissible for those fasting, and no make-up or expiation is required if one cannot fast. Regarding ritual washing, washing the head and neck at the beginning is sufficient, with no need to repeat it for other body parts. Based on Ayatollah Javadi Amoli's view, blood impurity up to the size of a finger joint does not invalidate prayer, but exceeding this amount does. Concerning Khums, money lent before the Khums year and received after it is subject to Khums, unless it is from exempt income. The second part of the program began with morning prayers and Quran recitation. Hojjat-ol-Islam Razani emphasized the importance of guarding the heart, soul, and intellect against sins, citing a hadith from Imam Sadiq (AS). He described the world as a marketplace for acquiring truths and stressed the necessity of spiritual awakening and avoiding heedlessness in worldly life.
