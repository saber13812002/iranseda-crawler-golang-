This radio program, which began by celebrating the birth of Imam Musa al-Kadhim, analyzed the regional security developments following Iran's missile strikes on Israel. The Iraqi Prime Minister condemned Israel's use of Iraqi airspace and stated that a formal complaint has been filed with the UN Security Council. Hezbollah in Iraq warned that American bases in the region would be targeted if the United States intervenes militarily. Pakistan's Defense Minister expressed unequivocal support for Iran and called for a united resistance front against Israel. In Venezuela, the president compared Netanyahu to Hitler, while military officials paid tribute to the Iranian martyrs. The Afghan Council of Scholars described the conflict as a war between Islam and disbelief, with the local population expressing joy over the success of Iran's missile attacks. American media reported extensive destruction in Israel and Tel Aviv's request for military aid from Washington, while President Trump remained silent when questioned about direct US involvement.
