In this radio program, Professor Mahmoudi emphasized the hadith "Wait for the relief, do not despair of God's mercy," identifying hope as the driving force behind a believer's efforts and social activity. He described despair as a grave sin that leads to stagnation, arguing that Shia Muslims must maintain a higher level of hope than other sects to remain motivated and active. The speaker highlighted the central role of love in the concept of waiting, stating that it beautifies life and guides actions, requiring true waiters to align their behavior with the moral standards of the Imam. Subsequently, Professor Kafil referenced Quranic verses to predict a bright future where truth prevails, urging Muslims to engage in struggle, movement, and unity during the period of occultation. He noted that despite the apparent power of falsehood, it will ultimately be destroyed, and divine light will illuminate the earth. The discussion concluded with a narration from Imam Sadiq (AS) about an elderly waiter, illustrating that the act of waiting itself elevates a person's spiritual status, even if they do not live to see the reappearance. Ultimately, the program concluded that true waiters must cultivate love, perform obligatory duties, and adopt Mahdavi behaviors to become genuine supporters of the Imam and prepare the ground for his return.
