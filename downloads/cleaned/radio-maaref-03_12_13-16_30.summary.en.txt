This radio program discusses strategies for managing anger and creating a peaceful family environment with teenagers. It recommends using empathy, friendly dialogue, and stop signals to control tension instead of criticism or judgment. Parents are advised to act as guides who illuminate the path rather than interfering directly in their teenagers' decisions. The importance of respecting teenagers' privacy and allowing them time alone to establish their identity is also emphasized. Parents are cautioned against intrusive curiosity that disrupts this necessary solitude. In the final section, religious teachings are used to highlight the need for a long-term, spiritual perspective in economic and educational activities. This mindset helps parents face daily challenges and cultural or economic battles with peace and success.
