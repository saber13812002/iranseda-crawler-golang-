In this radio program, religious rulings were provided in response to listeners' questions.
According to Imam Khomeini's fatwa, women selling dried bread at home with their husband's permission do not owe Khums if they do not earn a profit.
Wearing socks during prayer is only mandatory in the presence of non-mahram individuals.
While praying at the first time is highly recommended, those who are excused can still receive its reward with sincere intention.
For cleaning heavy carpets, it is advised to cut out the soiled section and wash it with water.
Individuals who have relocated from their hometown to another city must pray in a shortened form when visiting their original home.
A gap of more than one meter between rows in congregational prayer invalidates the prayer by breaking the connection.
The ethical segment emphasized avoiding backbiting and damaging others' reputations, warning of severe consequences for such sins.
