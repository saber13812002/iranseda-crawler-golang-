The program began with gratitude to the listeners and a tribute to the martyrs Sayyid Hassan Nasrallah and Sayyid Hashem Safi al-Din.
A digital space expert advised adopting a balanced and active approach to utilizing the internet while guarding against its risks.
In the religious segment, Professor Mohammad Hadi Fallah emphasized the absolute prohibition of cursing and abuse in the Quran and Hadith.
Citing a verse from Surah Al-An'am, he explained that cursing even the idols of polytheists is forbidden and stems from ignorance and shirk.
Imam Sadiq (AS) stated that a believer cursing idols invites polytheists to curse God, thereby committing a hidden form of shirk.
The Prophet (PBUH) and Imam Ali (AS) also strictly forbade all forms of verbal abuse, even towards enemies, in various narrations.
The conclusion is that cursing contradicts Islamic ethics and should be replaced with fair and constructive criticism.
The broadcast concluded with a prayer for the relief of the Imam and an introduction to upcoming programs on the Ma'aref network.
