This radio program, featuring various guests, describes the Arbaeen pilgrimage as the world's largest media platform for showcasing Islam's message of love and peace. Guests highlighted the presence of pilgrims from across the globe, including an English tourist, arguing that this event neutralizes the violent image of Islam often portrayed by Western media. The discussion included memories of martyrs from the 12-day war and the significant influence of Qasem Soleimani on Arbaeen culture. The guests emphasized the ethical qualities of pilgrims, such as self-sacrifice in extreme heat, viewing these traits as a manifestation of the Quran and a preparation for the Imam's reappearance. Interviews with pilgrims in the studio, including a student and an elderly woman, demonstrated the broad demographic of those devoted to Imam Hussein. Finally, listeners were encouraged to support pilgrims and veterans through messages and financial donations. The program concluded with prayers for the Imam's reappearance and an announcement for the next day's broadcast.
