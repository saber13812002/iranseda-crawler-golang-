In this segment of the program, managing the mind through complete reliance on God is presented as the primary key to peace and success. The speaker emphasizes that individuals must cleanse their minds of negative worries and worldly dependencies by relying on divine power. It is believed that God never leaves His servants alone and will always provide something better if a specific request is not granted. Various narrations from the Prophet and Imams indicate that sincere reliance prevents Satan from dominating believers and even makes societal leaders dependent on the devoted. Finally, listeners are encouraged to maintain a continuous connection with God and practice gratitude during trials to keep their minds on a positive path.
