In this program, Dr. Jabbari analyzes the wills of Imam Ali (AS) in Nahj al-Balagha to clarify the approach of the Infallibles towards their assassins. He refutes incorrect claims about the lack of retribution, emphasizing that Imam Ali (AS) prohibited pre-Islamic tribal revenge but explicitly ordered the punishment of the killer. The difference in treatment between Ibn Muljam and the hidden assassins of the Prophet (PBUH) and Imam Hasan (AS) stems from the public nature of the former's crime versus the concealed nature of the latter. Imam Hasan (AS), acting as the rightful heir and following his father's will, executed the killer due to the latter's lack of remorse. This approach demonstrates adherence to Islamic justice and distinguishes it from the indiscriminate revenge of the pre-Islamic era. The historical reports regarding Ibn Muljam's fate are consistent with the wills, confirming that mutilation did not occur.
