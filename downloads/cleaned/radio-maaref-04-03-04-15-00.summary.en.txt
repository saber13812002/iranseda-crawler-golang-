This radio program discussed the necessity of government intervention to mobilize idle micro and macro capital and prevent economic stagnation. Experts emphasized the critical role of public media, such as national television, in providing transparent information about investment opportunities and market prices to prevent exploitation by opportunists. Standardized model contracts between entrepreneurs and investors were deemed essential to resolve legal ambiguities and disputes over equity distribution. A major obstacle identified was the low productivity and lack of skill-based training in the education system, which leads to wasted resources and graduate unemployment. It was suggested that the government should adapt international experiences and support incubation centers to effectively bridge the gap between idea generators and investors. Mobilizing idle state and university assets was also proposed as a strategy to boost revenue and employment. Finally, it was stressed that creating a trustworthy environment and ensuring fair oversight of economic contracts is a religious and legal duty for the Islamic government to enhance public welfare.
