The radio program began by reporting on the meeting between Hajj officials and Iran's Supreme Leader, emphasizing the implementation of Quranic programs for the 1446 Hajj season. It then covered the commemoration of Imam Ahmad ibn Musa in Shiraz and the special events for the "Decade of Karimat" marking the martyrdom of President Raisi. The "Under the Shadow of the Sun" caravan was introduced as it traveled to Qom to promote the Imam Reza shrine and serve underprivileged communities. The broadcast also highlighted the extensive activities of 1,800 Basij construction groups across 24 provinces, focusing on poverty alleviation and religious propagation. A segment featured a story about the ascetic lifestyle of Ayatollah Hujjat, stressing the importance of not being attached to worldly possessions. Finally, an expert detailed the 24 prohibitions for pilgrims in Ihram, including restrictions on marriage, using perfume, and removing hair or nails. These rules were explained as either common to all pilgrims or specific to men and women.
