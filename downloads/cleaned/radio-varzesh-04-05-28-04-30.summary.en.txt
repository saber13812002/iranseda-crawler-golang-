Naser Shahsavar, former CEO of Pیکan FC, discusses his early passion for wrestling and football in West Tehran during this interview. He explains how his father, a prominent cleric and political activist, strongly supported sports, paving the way for his professional career. Shahsavar recounts his experiences during the Iran-Iraq War, including his presence on the front lines and close collaboration with Martyr Hassan Tehrani Moghadam. He highlights Tehrani Moghadam's pivotal role in managing the Saba Battery club and its successes in football and basketball, such as winning the Asian championship. Shahsavar emphasizes that Tehrani Moghadam had a deep, professional vision, strengthening teams with suitable and even foreign players. This partnership led to numerous regional accolades for the Saba Battery club. Finally, Shahsavar refers to the lasting impact of Tehrani Moghadam on Iran's sports and management culture.
