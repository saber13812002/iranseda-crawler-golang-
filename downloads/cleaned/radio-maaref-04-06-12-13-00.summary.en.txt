This lecture focused on the status of women and men in Islam, highlighting the life of Lady Khadija Kobra.
The speaker emphasized the complete equality of genders before God, citing Quranic verses that identify piety as the sole criterion for human worth.
He defined "Hayaat Tayyiba" as achieving a spirit of contentment and satisfaction with God's provisions, criticizing excessive future-oriented efforts that neglect present religious duties.
The two major deeds of Lady Khadija, her financial support of the Prophet before marriage and her acceptance of him as a husband, were presented as prime examples of righteous action.
By outlining Lady Khadija's criteria for choosing a spouse, such as lineage, honor, trustworthiness, and good character, the speaker provided a model for successful marriages.
The session concluded with prayers for the removal of obstacles to youth marriage and the hastening of the Imam's reappearance.
