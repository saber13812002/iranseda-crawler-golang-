In the "Rahbord" program, Professor Mehdi Rasakhi analyzed the Supreme Leader's management strategies, emphasizing the Islamic Republic's resilience against enemy plots. He asserted that no external factor can defeat Iran, attributing the successful management of social and military crises to "people-centered crisis management" and high societal resilience. Rasakhi rejected Westernization, citing the failure of negotiations with the West, and identified "civilization-building" and preparation for the Mahdi's reappearance as the country's ultimate goal. He viewed recent conflicts as a precursor to the formation of a new Islamic civilization, urging the preservation of unity between the people and the leadership. In the second segment, Dr. Zahra Sarbaz Hosseini, on the "Salamati" program, highlighted the importance of adequate, quality sleep for regulating metabolism and preventing obesity. She also advised against consuming powdered senja due to contamination risks, recommending instead that it be consumed fresh and occasionally.
