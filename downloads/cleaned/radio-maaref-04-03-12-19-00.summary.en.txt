In the Zamzam Ahkam program, various jurisprudential questions from listeners were addressed. Ayatollah Shabani emphasized that after the passing of Imam Khomeini, followers must select a living marja and strictly follow his rulings. Regarding passing through ownerless land, it is permissible if the owner does not object and no harm is caused, implying tacit consent. For unauthorized use of hospital medication, returning the item to the institution at current market value is mandatory, and merely paying compensation is insufficient unless official permission was granted. Ayatollah Shabiri Zanjani stated that reciting either the second or third salam is sufficient to conclude the prayer. Concerning missed prayers, Ayatollah Khamenei ruled that if a person hopes for recovery, they must perform the missed prayers in a standing position after healing. The second part of the program introduced the Amir Sadeq shrine in Lahijan and its charitable services. Finally, a lecture discussed the reasons for Imam Abdul-Azim Hasani's migration to Iran and his virtues.
