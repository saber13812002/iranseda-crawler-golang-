This radio program was broadcast on the occasion of Tashu'a, commemorating the martyrdom of Imam Abbas (AS), beginning with recitations of poetry and the Ziyarat. Reports from across Iran indicated that streets were filled with mourners performing self-flagellation and reciting elegies to honor the loyalty and bravery of the standard-bearer of Karbala. In the news segment, the Iranian President described cooperation with Islamic nations as the best solution to lifting sanctions, while the Pakistani Prime Minister condemned the Zionist regime's aggression. The Chief of Staff of the Armed Forces warned that a punitive plan would be executed if the enemy repeated its mistakes, despite previous ceasefire efforts. The book "Khanabat" was also highlighted, featuring a 1976 dialogue between the Supreme Leader, Martyr Motahhari, and Dr. Shariati analyzing the revolution of Imam Hussein (AS). The program concluded by inviting listeners to send messages and recite the Ziyarat for the relief of the Imam of the Time (AJ) and the recovery of the wounded.
