The program "Parsaiyan," featuring Islam Vahidi, explored the ethics of religious scholars, emphasizing the necessity of simplicity in their lifestyle.
Vahidi argued that displaying wealth undermines empathy for the poor and that religious leaders should avoid ostentation to maintain social solidarity.
The second segment, titled "Narrative of Love," featured Zainab Sadat Hosseini, the wife of martyr and defender of the shrine, Sayed Ahmad Hosseini.
Zainab recounted the difficult moment of receiving the news of her husband's martyrdom and her effort to remain composed for the sake of her family.
She shared her late husband's final notes, written before his operation, which provided financial instructions and spiritual reassurance to his family.
These notes revealed his deep concern for his family's future and his spiritual state regarding the events of Ashura.
Zainab also described her visit to the shrine of Lady Zainab (SA), where she sought solace through a heartfelt spiritual connection.
The program aimed to honor the memories of martyrs' wives and mothers by highlighting their hidden sufferings and resilience.
