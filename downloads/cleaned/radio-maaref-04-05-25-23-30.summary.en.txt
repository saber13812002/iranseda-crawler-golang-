In this segment of the program, the Quranic concept of "Makar" (stratagem) is divided into two types: good, which means problem-solving, and evil, which refers to the enemy's deception to mislead people.
The guest explains, citing Quranic verses, that the evil stratagems of enemies like the Yazidis and Pharaohs ultimately backfire, leading to their own destruction.
This is a fixed divine law where any conspiracy or oppression against the truth and the oppressed eventually benefits the oppressed and harms the oppressor.
History demonstrates that although enemies may temporarily cause confusion or harm to believers, in the end, truth prevails and falsehood is annihilated.
Referring to the martyrdom of Imam Hussein (PBUH) and his companions, the guest emphasizes that martyrdom in the path of God is "beautiful" and the primary motivation of fighters is serving Islam and God.
He notes that the bitterness resulting from martyrdom and suffering stems from incomplete knowledge and a lack of perfection, which can be transformed into joy through proper understanding of the goal.
Finally, listeners are encouraged to clarify their motivation for serving the religion by studying the Quran and Hadith, accepting martyrdom as a great victory.
