This radio program discussed Islamic rulings regarding prayer for travelers and the takbirs in worship. Hujjat al-Islam Mahdavi explained that a traveler intending to stay for ten days must perform full prayers, though they may return home early if a work necessity arises. It was clarified that only the takbirat al-ihram is obligatory, while other takbirs are recommended. Additionally, a traveler with the intention of staying is permitted to travel within a 22-kilometer radius of their residence. Hujjat al-Islam Jawaheri noted that merely listening to the Quran does not grant the same reward as reciting it. The broadcast concluded by providing contact details for submitting questions and announcing the time of Maghrib prayer in Qom.
