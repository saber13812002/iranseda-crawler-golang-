In this radio program, analysts emphasized that the recent military strikes on university professors reveal the deep fear of the Zionist regime and its allies regarding Iran's soft and scientific power. They argue that the 70-year-old global order, based on Western interests, is collapsing, while Iran presents a new model of power-building by relying on indigenous knowledge and social cohesion. The Supreme Leader has called for a national consensus on the country's current coordinates, urging the nation to move more rapidly toward higher horizons. The analysts contend that the silence and support of major powers for recent atrocities have exposed the complete illegitimacy of international bodies like the UN Security Council. Consequently, Iran stands at a historic turning point and must act as a primary agent in shaping the new regional and global order rather than remaining passive. It was also stressed that Iran's diplomacy should adopt a "point-target" approach, similar to its military operations, to firmly establish the country's authority.
