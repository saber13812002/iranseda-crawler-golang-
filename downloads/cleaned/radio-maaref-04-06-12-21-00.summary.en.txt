The program began with a call for financial assistance for the people of Gaza, followed by a discussion on the ethics of humor in Islam. Citing narrations from the Prophet and the Ahl al-Bayt, healthy humor and adherence to religious boundaries were presented as positive traits for believers. Examples of the gentle and humorous behavior of Ayatollah Behjat and Imam Reza were shared to illustrate the positive impact of this approach on human relationships. The main segment featured a narrative from the book "Ujyanus Mashreq," where Hajj Karim the cobbler and his daughter Rahel discuss the corruption of the world and the necessity of following the Imamate during their journey to Khorasan. In this story, the Imamate was introduced as the spirit of Islam and a divine truth established before the creation of humanity, with denying it equated to denying prophethood. Finally, a legal and religious consultation addressed the inability to reclaim a gift that has already been consumed by the recipient. The program concluded with a call to submit documents related to the Sacred Defense to the relevant foundation and an introduction to the next show, "Barkarane Noor."
