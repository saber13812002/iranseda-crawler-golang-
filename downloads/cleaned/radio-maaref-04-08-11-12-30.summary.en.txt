Hojjat al-Islam Hamed Kashani emphasizes the Shias' lack of awareness regarding the true stature of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) and the need for a deeper understanding of his character. Citing Quranic verses and narrations, he explains the infinite greatness of the Prophet compared to human limitations, highlighting divine attributes like "compassionate and merciful" and "eager to guide believers." A key point is the interpretation of the verse "A Messenger has come to you from among yourselves," which signifies the Prophet's apparent similarity to people to foster intimacy, rather than equality in spiritual rank. Kashani points to the Prophet's humility and his persistent concern for guiding people, even at the end of his life, urging Shias to move swiftly toward perfection in response to this divine kindness. He also references the virtues of Imam Ali (AS) and his close relationship with the Prophet, noting that praising Ali is often framed through his connection to the Prophet. Finally, the speaker concludes by calling for greater attention to the Prophet's character and inviting listeners to share their media needs.
