The radio program began by inviting listeners to participate in the national "Ya Zahra" campaign by sending condolence messages for the martyrdom of Lady Fatimah (SA). The main segment, titled "Shifa," explored Islamic traditional medicine recommendations, emphasizing the necessity of avoiding the simultaneous consumption of garlic and onion due to their intense heat and dryness. Excessive onion consumption, particularly in hot and dry climates, was identified as a cause of skin spots, dizziness, and digestive issues, while combining cold foods like ash with cold fruits such as pomegranate and prune was strongly discouraged. In the "A Drop from the Sea" segment, Hujjat al-Islam Rasoul, referencing verse 22 of Surah An-Nur, discussed the virtue of forgiving others to achieve mental peace and divine forgiveness. He described forgiving others as a transaction with God to have one's own mistakes forgiven, citing Nahj al-Balagha. Finally, the program highlighted the unique virtue of Lady Zahra (SA), noting that the Angel Gabriel frequently visited her during her martyrdom to convey important matters, a distinction not granted to any other non-prophet.
