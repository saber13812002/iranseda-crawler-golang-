Ziyarat is described as a spiritual migration from the self toward the Imam and ultimately to God, where true monotheism is derived from the Imam of the Time. The Imam is likened to the sun and a rain cloud that revives dead hearts with light and mercy while witnessing the inner states of believers. To reach this station, one must migrate from worldly attachments with pure intention and renew their covenant with the Imam. Each step in this journey removes a veil and brings the seeker closer to divine proximity, even if performed remotely by greeting the Imam from a rooftop. This process leads to continuous repentance for previous steps and deeper spiritual understanding. The narrative concludes with the story of Sayyid Murtadha, illustrating that the greatest miracle is refraining from using supernatural powers for livelihood and instead earning a living through manual labor.
