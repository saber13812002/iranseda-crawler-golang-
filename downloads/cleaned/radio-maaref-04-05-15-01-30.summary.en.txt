This program examines the pivotal role of Hazrat Zainab (SA) in protecting Imam Sajjad (AS) and managing the crisis following the events of Karbala. With courage and divine insight, Zainab (SA) defended the life of the Proof of God during critical moments, including the passage through the battlefield and the presence of Ibn Ziyad. Upon returning to Kufa, the Ahl al-Bayt faced an atmosphere of public shame and condemnation, where Zainab (SA) and Imam Sajjad (AS) exposed the Kufans' betrayal through harsh sermons. Although the people of Kufa initially declared their loyalty, Imam Sajjad (AS) realistically instructed them to remain neutral and avoid further involvement. During Ibn Ziyad's celebration, the Ahl al-Bayt were humiliated by the display of Imam Hussein's (AS) head, but Zainab (SA) again saved Imam Sajjad (AS) by preventing any physical harm. This narrative highlights the heavy responsibility of the Ahl al-Bayt in transforming their martyrdom into a lasting movement while preserving their dignity against their enemies.
