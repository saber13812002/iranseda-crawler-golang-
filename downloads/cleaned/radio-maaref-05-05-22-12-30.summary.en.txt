In this radio program, the mother of martyr Javad Allahkaram, a defender of the shrines, spoke about her love and patience regarding her son's martyrdom. Citing Quranic verses, she viewed his martyrdom as a conscious choice and a divine blessing, refusing to express any grief or protest against fate. She recounted her son's compassionate actions in Syria, such as preventing enemies from destroying water supplies and helping those in need. Despite her hidden longing, she emphasized the importance of resilience and avoiding public crying to prevent the enemy from taking joy. She believed that raising a child on the path of martyrdom is a great honor and that one should be grateful to God. The program continued with a live report on the atmosphere of pilgrims at Imam Reza's shrine during his martyrdom period. The broadcast concluded with prayers for the listeners and a sermon on the true believer from the perspective of Imam Reza.
