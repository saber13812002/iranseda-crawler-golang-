The program begins with a story about neighbors arguing over streetlights, illustrating how aggressive language can turn friendship into resentment.
Ayatollah Khatami then emphasizes the sanctity of secrets, explaining that all confidences are trusts unless keeping them hidden harms the Islamic system.
He cites the story of the "Naghab" coup and the rebels' practical repentance to demonstrate that disclosing certain secrets is mandatory for national security.
The second part of the broadcast features the "Zamzam Ahkam" segment, providing jurisprudential answers on prayer in rented land and the correct method of wudu.
Additional rulings address finding the Qibla in the desert, the prohibition of illegal movie downloads, and the proper disposal of papers bearing God's name.
The program also covers the precautionary ruling on shaving beards with machines and the specific surahs required in the Fatiha for the deceased.
Finally, liability for water damage in a building is determined based on the negligence of either the tenant or the landlord.
The episode concludes by inviting listeners to submit religious questions and promoting the official Radio Ma'aref channel on the Eitaa messaging app.
