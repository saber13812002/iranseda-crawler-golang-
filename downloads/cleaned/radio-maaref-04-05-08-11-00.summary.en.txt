The radio program began by reviewing infrastructure preparations for the Arbaeen pilgrimage, including the creation of 650 hectares of parking and the provision of free Wi-Fi at border terminals.
Other key travel news included a 20 percent increase in train capacity and the sale of Arbaeen flight tickets at approved rates.
In the cultural section, the first AI-based hadith research assistant, built on 400,000 Shia hadiths, was unveiled, while scholars refuted the superstition of the inauspiciousness of the month of Safar.
Mourning ceremonies for the martyrdom of Hazrat Raghia (SA) were held nationwide, emphasizing patience and loyalty with significant public participation.
Officials from the Astan Quds Razavi announced the establishment of Arbaeen service camps at various borders in cooperation with provincial volunteers.
In the religious rulings segment, Professor Vahidpour stressed the importance of decorum and selecting dignified individuals for roles in Ta'ziyeh performances.
He warned that using disreputable people for sacred roles yields counterproductive results and disrespects the martyrs.
The program concluded with prayers for the relief of the Imam and expressions of solidarity with the families of recent martyrs.
