The radio program began by reporting on a large gathering of workers with the Supreme Leader and the dispatch of an official to oversee the 1404 Hajj pilgrims.
Reports were shared regarding Iranian pilgrims in Medina coinciding with Imam Reza's birthday, as well as the centennial conference of the Qom Seminary.
Cultural segments highlighted the honoring of Imam Reza's cultural servants, public celebrations, and book sales statistics from the Tehran International Book Fair.
Brief news items covered endowments in Gilan, conflict resolution in Khuzestan, and registration for non-profit schools.
The main segment focused on the jurisprudential rulings of Umrah al-Tamattu, starting with a story about Ayatollah Bafrui's bravery in enjoining good.
A jurisprudential expert then detailed the fourth step of Umrah, which is the Sa'i between the hills of Safa and Marwa.
He emphasized the importance of proper intention, the seven-round path between the hills, and the recommendation to perform Haroula in a specific section.
These detailed explanations were provided to guide pilgrims in correctly performing the rituals and gaining spiritual rewards during their Hajj journey.
