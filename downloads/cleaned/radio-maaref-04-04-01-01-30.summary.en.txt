This radio program explores the dual dimensions of spiritual and political leadership based on the teachings of Imam Ali (PBUH). It highlights the profound spiritual status of the Ahl al-Bayt in their intimate knowledge of God, citing relevant Quranic verses and narrations. The discussion then shifts to the political attributes of a just ruler, emphasizing the necessity of rationality, piety, and the strict enforcement of divine laws without compromise against falsehood. The reciprocal rights of the Imam and the people are detailed, noting that the leader is responsible for advising, educating, and nurturing the society, while the people must pledge allegiance and obey. Illustrative stories of Imam Ali's practical service to the needy are shared to demonstrate the accountability of religious governance. The program concludes by stressing the importance of physical and spiritual strength for believers, ending with prayers for the success of the Islamic revolution and the early return of the Imam of the Age.
