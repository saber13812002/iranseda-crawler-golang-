In this radio program, Dr. Mohammad Ali Lili analyzed the goals of the Islamic Revolution, noting that while significant progress has been made in science and independence, cultural aggression and moral decay remain major challenges. He emphasized the need for collaboration between the government, intellectuals, and the public to combat these issues. The absence of Iran from the Sharm El-Sheikh summit was interpreted as a wise diplomatic decision to preserve national dignity, as participating alongside the US and Israel—who had previously attacked Iran—would have contradicted the Islamic Republic's principles. Furthermore, the Al-Aqsa Flood operation was analyzed as a turning point that shattered the myth of Israel's invincibility and thwarted the Abraham Accords. This operation demonstrated that resistance, driven by faith and limited resources, can shift the regional balance of power and internationalize the Palestinian cause.
