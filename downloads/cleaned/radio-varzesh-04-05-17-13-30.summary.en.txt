Seyyed Shahram Heravi, the head coach of the national karate team, discussed the sport's historic achievements in a radio interview. He highlighted the victory of Sajjad Ganjzadeh, who secured Iran's first-ever Olympic bronze medal in karate at the Paris Games. Heravi recounted the controversy surrounding Bahman Eskri, noting that although his doping case was cleared, a one-year ban cost Iran an Olympic quota. He also described the challenging conditions of the Tokyo Olympics, including the impact of the pandemic and Ganjzadeh's hand injury during training. The coach criticized the lack of financial support and low salaries for coaches, emphasizing the importance of faith and ethics in sports. Finally, Heravi expressed optimism about the next generation of karatekas, hoping they will bring more success to the sport in future Olympic cycles.
