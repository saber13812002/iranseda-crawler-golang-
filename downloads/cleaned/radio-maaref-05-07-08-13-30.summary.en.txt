The program began with an expression of love for Imam Reza (PBUH) and the performance of a devotional song before transitioning to a legal segment. In the first case, a building manager was held liable for fire damage due to failing to insure the property as required by law. The judge emphasized that building managers must insure the structure and provide official minutes of their election to the court. The second case involved a dispute over parking space rental between neighbors, which was resolved through emotional mediation and a mutual settlement. In the final segment, a cleric stressed the importance of preserving the purity of the Persian language in modern lyrics and literature. He criticized the low quality of contemporary songs while praising the enduring eloquence of classical poets like Saadi, Rumi, and Saib.
