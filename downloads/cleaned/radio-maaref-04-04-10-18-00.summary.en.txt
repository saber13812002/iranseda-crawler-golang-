In the "Ganj-e Sa'adat" program, Hujjat al-Islam Fala discussed the virtue of "infak" (charity/spending) and defined human perfection as dedicating oneself to the path of obedience to God and the Imam. Citing a narration from Imam Hussein (PBUH) on the night of Ashura, he equated knowing God with recognizing the Imam of the Time and submitting to his existential channel. The scholar emphasized that the only path to perfection is adhering to Sharia, performing obligatory acts, and avoiding prohibitions, thereby becoming a "sacrifice for God" in the shadow of the Infallibles. Using the story of Sayyid ibn Abdullah in Karbala as a practical model, he urged listeners to protect the truth of prayer and the Imam's efforts against the "arrows" of negligence and social media. In the second segment, Dr. Najafi compared the recent war with historical conflicts, highlighting the role of the Supreme Leader and social cohesion as key factors in victory. He also criticized the condescending tone of Western officials, referencing France's historical betrayals in the treaties of Gulistan and Turkmenchay. Dr. Najafi argued that the core issue of the war extends beyond nuclear matters, representing a struggle against neo-colonialism and the preservation of Islamic identity.
