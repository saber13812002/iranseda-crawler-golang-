In this radio program, the importance of empathy and the optimal management of water and electricity consumption to serve fellow citizens was emphasized. The painful narrative of Zahra Nasiri, the wife of martyr Javad Akbari, a defender of the shrines, was shared. Zahra described the moments of her husband's martyrdom, her initial inability to accept the news, and the hardships that followed, including her absence from the funeral procession due to intense grief. She also recounted her painful interactions with her children, who were searching for their father, and her spiritual dreams of Javad advising her to be patient. The final segment featured the Supreme Leader's remarks on the critical role of IRIB in cultivating moral virtues such as frugality, trustworthiness, and the rejection of consumerism. The Leader stressed the need to elevate deep religious knowledge and avoid reducing faith to superficial emotions in media programming. These discussions highlight that the media must simultaneously convey human stories while fulfilling its moral and religious responsibility in educating society.
