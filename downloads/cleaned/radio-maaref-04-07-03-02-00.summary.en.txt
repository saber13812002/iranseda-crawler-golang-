The program begins by highlighting the virtue of generosity as a core Islamic ethic, citing the examples of Imam Khomeini, Sheikh Ansari, and Ayatollah Sharabiani.
These figures demonstrated selflessness by distributing their personal belongings and gifts to those in need rather than keeping them for themselves.
The discussion then shifts to the cultural reforms of Imam Ali (AS) during his caliphate, focusing on his efforts to revive the Prophet's traditions.
Imam Ali (AS) actively opposed innovations introduced during the previous caliphates, such as the Tarawih prayer and the omission of Takbir during prostration.
A significant portion of the talk addresses the historical policy of prohibiting the writing of Hadith, which began under Abu Bakr and was continued by subsequent caliphs.
Imam Ali (AS) countered this by encouraging the community to document knowledge and establishing proper methods for transmitting and reading Hadith.
The segment concludes by referencing the "Jami'at Ali," a collection of Hadith dictated by the Prophet (PBUH) and written down by Imam Ali (AS), which played a crucial role in preserving Islamic tradition.
