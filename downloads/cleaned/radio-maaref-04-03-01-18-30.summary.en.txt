The radio program "Zamzam Ahkam" featured a discussion on the rites of Umrah Hajj with Professor Tabatabai.
The first section detailed the rituals for the 11th and 12th of Dhu al-Hijjah, including the stoning of the Jamarat and staying in Mina.
The professor advised pilgrims to use less crowded paths, such as the rear or left side of the Jamarat, to avoid congestion.
It was also explained that incapacitated pilgrims can appoint proxies to perform the stoning on their behalf.
A crucial point emphasized was the requirement to remain in Mina until the Dhuhr prayer on the 12th of Dhu al-Hijjah before departing.
The second section addressed various religious questions submitted by listeners to different Maraji.
Topics included the permissibility of touching a non-mahram boy, the status of the Dhuhr prayer after Jumu'ah, and rules regarding purity.
Finally, the program discussed etiquette regarding consuming food at a relative's home and the concept of implied consent.
