The first segment of the program emphasized, citing narrations from Imams Baqir and Sadiq, that the criterion for complete faith is loving and hating for the sake of God.
A person's emotional and intellectual connection with righteous servants indicates their goodness, whereas an inclination toward sinners signifies weak faith.
Subsequently, Professor Vahidpour explained medical rulings, stating that teeth are considered lifeless parts and do not require burial after extraction.
In contrast, gum tissue is considered living and becomes impure if detached, requiring strict adherence to religious precautions.
Fatawa addressed listener questions regarding prayer in one's native land, wudu with eyebrow oil, and a son-in-law's permission to bury his mother-in-law.
The correct method for performing ghusl with a plastered leg, using plastic to prevent water penetration, was also detailed.
The program concluded with an invitation to remember God for inner peace and the broadcast frequencies of Radio Ma'aref.
