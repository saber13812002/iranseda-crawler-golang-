In this radio program, listeners were asked to send their answers to a tafsir question regarding verse 7 of Surah Ibrahim to the Army's Ideological and Political Organization. The question focused on identifying a major divine blessing mentioned by the Supreme Leader, with options including the Islamic Revolution, security, and sustenance. The winners of the "Ayat Noor" contest will be announced tomorrow after the 8 AM news via the Radio Ma'aref Eitaa channel. The majority of the broadcast featured a recitation of verses from Surah Yusuf, narrating the brothers' return to Egypt and their encounter with Joseph. These verses detail the process of discovering the stolen cup and Joseph's secret plan to keep his brother by invoking their father Jacob's grief. Jacob expressed his deep sorrow over the separation from his sons while advising them not to lose hope in God's mercy. Ultimately, Joseph revealed his identity, forgave his brothers, and instructed them to place his shirt on their father's face to restore his sight. This Quranic narrative emphasizes divine kindness and the ultimate reward of patience and piety.
