In this program, the concept of "success" is defined as discovering one's personal duty and avoiding unhealthy comparisons with others. Citing Quranic verses and narrations, the speaker emphasizes that true honor, power, and sustenance are blessings of piety and obedience to divine commands, rather than worldly wealth or status. The discussion also highlights the necessity of justice in distributing rewards and reducing class gaps, while criticizing envy and materialistic expectations as obstacles to spiritual growth. In the second part, a narration from Imam Sadiq (AS) is interpreted to describe four key traits of a pious person: divine honor, spiritual strength, inner contentment, and rising above worldly attachments. These points illustrate that true success lies in understanding one's duty and relying on God.
