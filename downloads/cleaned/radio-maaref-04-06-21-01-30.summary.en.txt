In this radio program, the Prophet of Islam and Imam Sadiq (AS) discuss the moral corruption stemming from the love of the world, greed, and arrogance. According to this perspective, individuals who prioritize material values over spiritual ones become internally corrupt and transform into predatory, dangerous beings. Imam Sadiq (AS) identifies the root of this apparent corruption as internal decay, driven by psychological factors such as greed, arrogance, and the illusion of a long life. The Prophet (PBUH) also warns that separating ethics from economics and livelihood from the afterlife fragments human nature and leads to social decay. This corruption manifests at various levels of society, including the market, politics, and family, through bribery, betrayal, and inequality. Following this, a dramatic retelling of the book "The Eastern Ocean" was broadcast, narrating the story of a man named Emaran. Emaran, while searching for the water of life, meets Haj Karim the cobbler and encounters two bandits named Marwan and Ashes during his journey. The episode concludes with a physical confrontation among the groups, where Haj Karim bravely defends his daughter.
