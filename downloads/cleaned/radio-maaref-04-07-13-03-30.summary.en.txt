The radio program began by discussing the principles of faith, emphasizing the necessity of using rational proofs rather than unsubstantiated claims in theological matters.
It was stressed that attributing statements to God or the Prophet without evidence constitutes "speaking without knowledge," which invalidates fasting during Ramadan.
A political analysis of nuclear negotiations with the United States was presented, arguing that such talks primarily benefit the American side and are detrimental to Iran.
Several religious rulings were addressed, including the validity of prayers performed by individuals who mispronounce specific letters and the conditions for making them up.
The permissibility of breaking a prayer to save life or property, as well as repeating a prayer in congregation after praying alone, was also examined.
Regarding women's travel, it was clarified that a husband's permission is not required for performing obligatory duties, buying essential goods, or seeking medical treatment.
On the topic of Khums, it was explained that paying it immediately upon earning income generally eliminates the need for a separate annual Khums year.
Finally, mothers were advised to read prayers aloud for their children's education only during non-Zuhr and Asr prayers or as a practice session.
