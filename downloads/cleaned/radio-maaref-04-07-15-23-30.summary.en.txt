The guest highlighted the importance of government officials accompanying the private sector to resolve issues and boost morale, citing over 500 visits to livelihood institutions during the pandemic. He emphasized the need to implement the Supreme Leader's mandates for a resistance economy, including high-quality domestic production, promoting local consumption, and curbing waste. The speaker stressed that the economy must be transferred from state control to the people, with banks providing low-interest loans to support national investment. He also called for reducing the country's economic dependence on oil, a legacy of the previous regime that proved vulnerable under sanctions. Regarding the clergy, the guest noted that while they have a significant role, they must improve their engagement with the public and resolve internal mosque conflicts to attract youth. He suggested adopting successful models from Tehran to establish councils that address mosque-related disputes and foster community cohesion. Finally, the guest argued that spiritual well-being is superior to material wealth, asserting that true happiness stems from Islamic ethics and spiritual blessings rather than physical assets.
