The radio program "Enduring Memories" features veterans Sardar Zolqadr, Sardar Atai, and Hassan Javanmardi recounting their experiences during the Iran-Iraq War. Sardar Zolqadr describes the intense resistance against enemy tanks during Operation Karbala 8 and shares the story of a comrade who had pre-written his martyrdom date in his notebook. Sardar Atai narrates the sacrifice of a young soldier from Saravan who had a prophetic dream of meeting the Imam before his death in Operation Badr. Hassan Javanmardi recounts the bravery of the fighters during the siege in Operation Khorramshahr, where wounded soldiers refused to retreat despite the overwhelming enemy force. These testimonies highlight the deep faith and readiness of the warriors to achieve martyrdom in the path of justice. The program concludes by discussing the political and spiritual attractions of the Islamic Republic, which draw the attention of nations and youth worldwide.
