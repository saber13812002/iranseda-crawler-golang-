The radio program began by addressing several jurisprudential questions, clarifying that using two markers for the forehead and nose during prostration is permissible and even recommended as a precaution. It was advised that individuals who have received bribes should contact their marja's office to determine their specific religious obligation. Regarding travelers, it was stated that prayers must be performed in full until the distance threshold for shortening is reached, and any barrier like nail polish found after wudu must be removed to repeat or make up the prayer. The program also recommended performing the daily optional prayers with the intention of qada and nearness to God. The second part of the broadcast focused on the moral concept of asceticism, emphasizing the need to detach the heart from material worldly pleasures. By citing the lives of the Prophet and the Imams, the speaker explained that true asceticism does not mean isolation but rather maintaining a spiritual focus while fulfilling worldly duties and earning a halal livelihood. Accumulating wealth for the sake of Islam's dignity and eliminating polytheism is compatible with asceticism if the intention is pure.
