This radio program discussed the month of Rabi al-Awal and the religious obligation of "Red Muzalim," which involves compensating for past wrongs. It was explained that if the victim or their heirs cannot be found, the debt must be paid to the poor with the permission of a religious authority. To facilitate this, general permission was obtained from major clerics to donate up to 1.5 million Tomans to the poor as a substitute for these debts. The payment must be made in cash directly to the poor and cannot be given to mosques or schools. The speaker emphasized that anyone with a certain knowledge of their debt must settle it immediately, rather than waiting for the end of the month. The program concluded with a call to support the people of Gaza and the recitation of verses from Surah Hadid.
