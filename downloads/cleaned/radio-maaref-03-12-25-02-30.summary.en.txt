This radio program begins by analyzing Quranic verses that emphasize the Prophet's (PBUH) refusal to compromise with polytheists and his steadfastness against political and social pressures. The discussion then shifts to the spiritual effects of divine love, highlighting that freedom from regret and remorse regarding the past is a key outcome of this love. Dr. Sajedi explains that a person who loves God does not feel regret because their heart is attached to the truth and they fulfill their religious duties with genuine affection. In contrast, sinners often suffer from guilt and anxiety due to their lack of divine love and deviation from the right path. To cultivate this love and gratitude, the program recommends changing one's perspective from pessimism to optimism and consistently recalling God's blessings during daily supplications. Reciting and reflecting on Quranic verses about divine favors is also suggested as an effective method to nurture gratitude and deepen one's love for God. Finally, the program cites Imam Khomeini's unwavering defense of Islam and his lack of regret to illustrate that hearts aligned with God act based on duty rather than outcome, which is the hallmark of true divine love.
