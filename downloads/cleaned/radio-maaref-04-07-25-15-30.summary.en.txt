In the radio program "Rahbord," expert Mehdi Moshibaf analyzed Imam Khomeini's management strategies during the imposed war. He argued that Europe's silence and the US's covert support for Iraq revealed America's essential and permanent enmity. Moshibaf also refuted reductionist views of prophets, asserting that Prophet Jesus (PBUH) would have fought against disbelievers like Moses and Noah (PBUH) had he lived longer. Diplomatically, the US used its veto power in the UN Security Council to avoid condemning Iraq's aggression and instead labeled Iran as a state sponsor of terrorism. This international inaction and the attempt to isolate Iran led the Imam to question the legitimacy of the international system based on superpower dictates. Consequently, the Imam's approach divided the world into two camps: supporters of the oppressors and the oppressed. This strategic stance became the foundation of Iran's foreign policy in the subsequent decades.
