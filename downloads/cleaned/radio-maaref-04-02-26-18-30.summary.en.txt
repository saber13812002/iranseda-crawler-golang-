This radio program recounts the story of Amir ibn Jumuh's martyrdom during the Battle of Uhud. His wife, Hind, attempted to transport his body to Medina, but the camel refused to move in that direction. Instead, the animal eagerly ran back toward the battlefield of Uhud. Confused, Hind consulted the Prophet Muhammad, who explained that Amir had prayed not to return to his home. The Prophet revealed that Amir was already with the martyrs in the afterlife and that God did not wish for his body to return to Medina. The broadcast then transitioned into a poetic segment praising the mosque and prayer as spiritual sanctuaries. It concluded with the sunset time in Mecca and spiritual advice for finding inner peace.
