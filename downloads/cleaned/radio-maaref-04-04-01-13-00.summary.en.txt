In this radio program, Hojjat al-Islam Hassan Ramezani examines the scientific, spiritual, and political dimensions of Imamate based on Nahj al-Balagha. He emphasizes that the Infallible Imams serve as the primary scientific and spiritual references, possessing divine secrets, a status even acknowledged by Sunni scholars like Ibn Abbas. Politically, Imamate is defined as governance based on divine laws, requiring justice, wisdom, and an uncompromising stance against falsehood. The Imam Ali (PBUH) outlines four rights of the people over the Imam, including advice, fair distribution of resources, education, and moral upbringing. Conversely, he lists four rights of the Imam over the people, such as loyalty to the covenant, support in battle, sincerity in private and public, and obedience to commands. This discourse highlights that the relationship between the Imam and the people is founded on mutual rights and shared responsibilities. The ultimate goal of this structure is to establish an aware, just, and spiritually oriented society.
