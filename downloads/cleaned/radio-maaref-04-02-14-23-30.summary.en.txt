In this radio program, the speaker emphasizes the loyalty of Imam Abbas (AS) and his brother Musa ibn Ja'far (AS) to highlight the spiritual status of Imam Reza (AS) and his brother Ahmad ibn Musa (AS). He refutes fabricated narrations and stresses that Imamate is a divine appointment, discouraging unwarranted comparisons between historical figures. By referencing the five pillars of Islam, the speaker identifies Wilayat (loyalty to the Imam) as the fifth pillar, which admits no exemption, unlike other religious duties. He urges the community to preserve the nation's image by strictly observing hijab, honesty, and trustworthiness in daily life. The discussion also touches on the historical and spiritual parallels between Imam Reza (AS) and Musa ibn Ja'far (AS). Finally, the speaker concludes by encouraging listeners to recite ziyarat texts and follow the Imams' conduct to establish justice, ending with prayers and blessings.
