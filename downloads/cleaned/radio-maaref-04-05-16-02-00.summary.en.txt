In the first segment of the program, Hojjat al-Islam Vahidi emphasized the critical importance of utilizing life's opportunities and avoiding the waste of time, citing various narratives from prominent scholars. He shared anecdotes from Ayatollah Dinparvar, Ayatollah Golpayegani, and Imam Khomeini, illustrating how these great figures dedicated even brief moments, such as walks or travels, to dhikr and remembrance of God. Additionally, Ayatollah Kohsani gently advised students to prioritize their studies and personal growth over spending time on travel to attend his classes. This section concluded by highlighting that even in difficult conditions, such as heavy rain, these scholars remained steadfast in their commitment to teaching and activity. In the second segment, the program "Roayat-e Eshq" featured Raghieh Hosseini, the mother of the martyr and defender of the shrines, Sayyid Mohammad Hosseini. The mother spoke about her childhood in a humble neighborhood, her aspirations to join the front lines during the Iran-Iraq War, and the profound influence of Lady Zainab (SA) on her life. She recounted the final moments before her son's deployment to Syria and his instructions to maintain dignity and composure during his absence. The conversation concluded by emphasizing the mother's deep love for her martyr son and honoring the sacrifices made by the defenders of the shrines.
