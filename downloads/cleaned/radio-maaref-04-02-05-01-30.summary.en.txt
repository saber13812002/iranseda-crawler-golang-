The text begins by outlining Imam Khomeini's views on the incompatibility of revolutionary struggle with a pursuit of luxury and comfort. He emphasizes that the war between right and wrong is a global ideological battle without geographical borders, necessitating support for the oppressed worldwide. The Imam warns that enemies aim to undermine the revolution from within by infiltrating universities and altering its content. The second part of the text features a radio program where Dr. Jafari answers listener questions. He explains that, according to the Quran and narrations, the Magi had a prophet and a holy book, and were treated as People of the Book in early Islam. He refutes the narrative of Anushirvan's justice based on weak historical and hadith evidence, citing the rigid Sasanian class system as a counter-argument. Finally, he introduces Abazar and Qanbar as two of the four pillars of Shia Islam and loyal companions of Imam Ali.
