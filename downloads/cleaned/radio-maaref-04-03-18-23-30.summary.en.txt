In this radio program, the family counselor emphasizes the concept of a "transition period" in early marriage, comparing it to adolescence where individuals are still adjusting to their new roles.
Citing religious teachings, the expert stresses the importance of patience and allowing couples time to gradually get to know each other, warning against rushing to achieve full harmony in the initial months.
The counselor also addresses other family challenges, such as a spouse's exhaustion from responsibilities or a lack of attention, suggesting solutions like active listening, expressing love in the partner's specific love language, and creating opportunities for rest and shared activities.
Towards the end, the discussion expands to theological dimensions and the defense of the Wilayah, highlighting the need to emulate Imam Ali's (PBUH) behavior during difficult historical periods to maintain Islamic cohesion.
The program concludes with the aim of providing practical solutions to resolve marital challenges and strengthen the emotional bond between spouses.
