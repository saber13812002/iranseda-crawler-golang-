In the second segment of the radio program "Kimia," Ayatollah Saeed Ahmad Khatami discussed the "Risalah al-Huquq" of Imam Sajjad (PBUH). Relying on the writings of Ayatollah Borujerdi, he emphasized the authenticity and strong chain of narration for this text. He introduced the narrators, highlighting Thabit ibn Dinar, who met four Imams and was the father of three martyrs. Khatami argued that the profound nature of the text serves as evidence of Imam Sajjad's infallibility. The third segment, "Zamzam Ahkam," addressed various religious questions from listeners. Responses included the ruling that birds are pure and that cooked raisins are permissible to eat. The program also clarified the conditions for receiving charity through agency and the validity of rent payments. Finally, it affirmed the impurity of dogs and the permissibility of reciting both "Malik" and "Malik" in the prayer.
