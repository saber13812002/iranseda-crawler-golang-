The discussion emphasizes the critical need for systematic feedback and dynamic assessment in sports organizations to prevent stagnation. Dr. Tavousi argues that the education system should shift its focus from solely pursuing medals to promoting mass sports and nurturing national talent. He recalls how student competitions served not just as athletic events but as vital platforms for cultural integration and intrinsic motivation. His career trajectory reflects a transition from professional sports to a strong commitment to mass sports and public health initiatives. The speaker highlights structural challenges in the Iranian Ski Federation, such as reliance on private facilities and the shortening of ski seasons due to climate change. He also stresses the importance of maintaining scientific continuity in sports management rather than making arbitrary administrative changes. Finally, he advises the public to institutionalize physical activity in their daily lives. Combining regular exercise with proper nutrition is essential to counteract age-related muscle loss and ensure a healthy, active old age.
