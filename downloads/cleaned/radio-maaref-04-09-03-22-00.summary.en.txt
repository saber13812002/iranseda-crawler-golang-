The program began with brief news updates, including the funeral of 300 unidentified war martyrs, the farewell ceremony for martyr Haidar Mazlum in Lebanon, and increased public participation in the orphans' care plan. The main segment featured the memoirs of former POW Esadollah Nekhai, a Basij member who was captured during a fierce night battle. He described the severe lack of water and equipment, as well as the intense tank fire that led to his injury and subsequent capture. Nekhai recounted the terrifying conditions in Iraqi prisons, such as the "Tunnel of Terror," and the severe physical torture inflicted by soldiers. He also highlighted the prisoners' resistance against punishment and their organization of protest events within the camps. Following this, Abdolkarim Azandrani emphasized the importance of relying on God and continuing to pray while in captivity. He shared a memory of an Iraqi guard who, years later, attributed the freedom and salvation of the prisoners to their unwavering faith and trust in God.
