This radio program emphasizes the necessity of simultaneously caring for both physical and spiritual health by preventing harmful influences from entering the mind and body. Citing narrations from the Ahl al-Bayt, the speaker identifies gossip and backbiting as the worst moral vices that destroy a person's spiritual well-being. The Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) and Imam Ali (AS) described avoiding slander as a vital duty for Shias and those under the guardianship of the Commander of the Faithful. Even if gossip is presented as truthful, it remains strictly prohibited and must be rejected as invalid. Additionally, the Prophet's preventive health recommendations, such as covering food containers, avoiding overeating, and maintaining hygiene, were highlighted as essential for physical well-being. Ultimately, moral cultivation and purifying the heart from vices are presented as prerequisites for accepting virtues and achieving spiritual elevation under divine guidance.
