The program begins by highlighting the spiritual value of smiling and kindness in elevating a person's status before God. In the traditional medicine segment, Dr. Pour Hosseini warns against food incompatibilities, specifically advising against consuming rice with vinegar or pickles unless the rice is cooked with meat and oil. He explains that preparing rice as a mixed dish, such as dampakhtak, mitigates the negative effects of vinegar on digestion. The Quranic section, led by Hojjat al-Islam Rasoul, discusses the power of anger as a divine gift that must be controlled to maintain religious and social order. Citing Surah Ash-Shura, he emphasizes that avoiding major sins and indecencies is a prerequisite for managing anger and practicing forgiveness. Finally, the broadcast addresses the context of the Iran-Iraq war, stressing the importance of Muslim unity and adherence to Islamic principles to resist foreign plots and preserve national identity.
