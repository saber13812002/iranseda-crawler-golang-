The radio program "Rain of Kindness" narrated the story of a seven-member family living in a deprived 48-square-meter house after the father's death. The young mother, with her two orphaned children, Alireza and Raghda, returned to her parents' home, facing severe lack of amenities and the grandfather's serious illnesses. The program guests emphasized the blessing of charitable acts and the significant impact of small donations in solving this family's problems. They urged listeners to send the text "Ya Ali Asghar" to the provided numbers to help secure the family's livelihood and improve their living conditions. The main goal of the program is to create hope and assist this family in achieving peace and housing security.
