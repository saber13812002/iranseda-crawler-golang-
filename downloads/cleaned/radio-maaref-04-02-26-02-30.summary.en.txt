In this radio broadcast, the schedule for the "Asr Jibani" contest on Radio Ma'aref and related programs on the voice of virtue and nature was announced. The speaker then emphasized the concept of "Yaqeen al-Hajar," referring to the steadfastness of faith in the face of natural and social events. It was recommended to recite the seventh supplication from the Sahifa Sajjadiya with attention to its meaning to alleviate anxiety and find peace. The supplication includes phrases asking God for protection against punishments and hardships. Subsequently, relying on Quranic verses, the necessity of maintaining hope and preventing despair in society was highlighted. The speaker repeatedly stressed the importance of keeping the hope we have in God alive. This frequent repetition aimed to reinforce a positive mindset among the audience. Ultimately, the main message of the program focused on strengthening faith, reciting prayers, and maintaining hope in difficult conditions.
