The radio program began by commemorating the martyrdom of Lady Fatima Zahra (SA) before transitioning to the biography and memories of Ayatollah Najm al-Din Tusi. The segment highlighted his father, a devout and humble merchant who, despite financial hardships, dedicated his life to religious observance and raising righteous children. The Tusi family's legacy is marked by their children's significant contributions to religious scholarship, literature, and the Holy Defense. The broadcast also addressed the upcoming 13 Aban rally, emphasizing the Iranian nation's resistance against global hegemony and its commitment to freedom. The spiritual atmosphere was further enhanced by the recitation of religious litanies and the playing of patriotic anthems. Finally, the host outlined the upcoming schedule for Radio Ma'aref, featuring theological lectures and nightly news updates.
