This Radio Ma'aref broadcast featured a dramatic reading of the book "Sabz Abi," performed by Maakan Rezaei Pour and Amir Abbas Tofaghi. The narrative follows Sayed Naser Din, who travels from Xinjiang to Shanghai to restore an ancient mosque. After his tools and vital blueprints are stolen, he encounters a woman named Shian who initially claims to be poor. Shian is revealed to be a Buddhist artist who stole the plans to save her family's temple from destruction. She confesses her actions to Sayed Naser and voluntarily surrenders to prison to face the consequences. The program concluded with a religious ruling from the Supreme Leader regarding Hajj eligibility and mahr. Additionally, the new schedule for Radio Ma'aref's programs in 2025 was announced.
