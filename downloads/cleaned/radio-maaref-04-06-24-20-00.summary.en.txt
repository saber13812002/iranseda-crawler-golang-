Recent meetings between Iranian and Turkish leaders emphasized the necessity of regional unity to counter threats from the Zionist regime. While Israeli military attacks on Gaza and other cities continue, resulting in dozens of Palestinian casualties, an international civilian fleet has departed from Tunisia and Libya to break the Gaza blockade. Analysts warn that rising tensions between Turkey and Israel could make the former the next target of attacks. Meanwhile, a conference in Pakistan focused on Islamic unity aimed to prevent division in the face of upcoming challenges. Other segments highlighted the increase in Islamophobia in Scotland and the destruction of Islamic heritage in Greece, both of which drew negative reactions from experts. The program concluded with the commemoration of the night of Fatima Masumeh's (SA) arrival in Qom, extending congratulations to the listeners for this religious occasion.
