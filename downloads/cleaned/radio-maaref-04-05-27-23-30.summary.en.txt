In this radio program, child development experts emphasized the importance of parental calmness, warning that emotional reactions can exacerbate children's screaming and tantrums.
To reduce stress in children, engaging in soothing activities such as playing with soil, clay, and finger painting was recommended for their daily routine.
The second section addressed the impact of parental role models on children's worship, noting that a mother's prayer alone is insufficient without being a beloved and well-behaved figure.
It was stressed that children emulate parents who are successful and respected in various life aspects, including arts and ethics, rather than just religious observance.
Subsequently, practical tips for water conservation in the kitchen were provided, including using low-flow faucets and proper dishwashing techniques.
Finally, the speakers highlighted the necessity of national scientific and industrial independence, urging the cultivation of domestic expertise to avoid reliance on foreign powers.
They concluded that serving the nation and God through self-sufficiency and independence is a significant religious and national duty.
