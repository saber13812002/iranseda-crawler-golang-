In the jurisprudence segment, Dr. Javadi Amoli clarified that the intention for making up missed prayers for the deceased must be held only in the heart, as verbalizing it is unnecessary and may lead to confusion. The program then shifted to the topic of the Arbaeen pilgrimage, emphasizing the need to articulate the Husseini front against the Yazidi forces, particularly in the post-war context. To globalize the message of Imam Hussein (PBUH), it was suggested to build friendships with non-Iranian pilgrims through simple gestures like giving gifts. Safety driving tips were also provided for travelers on the roads leading to the holy sites. Finally, the broadcast highlighted the spiritual light of the Quran and prayer, urging listeners to illuminate their homes through Quranic recitation and performing prayers.
