The "Ma'aref" radio program begins by honoring the memory of Martyrs Rajai and Bahonar, emphasizing their symbolic status in the revolution.
Their defining characteristic was divine struggle and a sincere pursuit of God's pleasure in both personal and political life.
Unlike common expectations, their administrative record is not the primary model for emulation due to the brevity of their tenure.
The core argument highlights the complete alignment of their actions with religious principles and a commitment to serving the people.
The main objective for officials and managers should be gaining divine approval and working honestly for the public.
This approach must serve as the guiding motto and ultimate goal for all governmental and personal activities.
Even in private tasks without supervision, the primary motivation must remain pure and based on God's satisfaction.
