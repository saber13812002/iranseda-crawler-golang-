The program begins with a philosophical discussion on the nature of mental and external existence, arguing that objects exist in the mind by changing their essence, such as substance becoming a quality, rather than retaining their exact external reality.
The concept of "revolution" in this context refers to a change in attributes or form, which requires a shared matter or ambiguous element, whereas a complete revolution in essence without shared matter is impossible.
A key point is that the necessity of mental existence only requires a vessel for scientific forms to be realized, not that objects remain in the mind exactly as they are in the external world.
Following the philosophical segment, the program continues with the recitation of Ziyarat Ashura and religious supplications to create a spiritual atmosphere.
The radio schedule for the upcoming days is then announced, featuring memories of Holy War veterans, hadiths, and Quranic recitations.
The final part includes the recitation of romantic poetry and a speech addressed to the youth and warriors.
This speech emphasizes spiritual victory and the conquest of the ego, praising the courage and sacrifice of the youth in maintaining national security and the front lines.
Finally, prayers are offered for the health of the warriors and the honor of Islam and Muslims, concluding the program with music.
