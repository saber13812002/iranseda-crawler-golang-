This radio broadcast begins with a tribute to the late Professor Mehdi Pishbahi and his historical Q&A program. In the subsequent segment, Hujjat al-Islam Masoudi interprets a hadith from the book of Kafi, emphasizing the importance of humility and rejecting worldly power. He cites the examples of Prophets David and Solomon to illustrate that spiritual rank is preserved by avoiding the temptation of absolute kingship. The core message encourages listeners to maintain a humble character in all aspects of their lives. The program then shifts to the national slogan for 1404, which focuses on production growth through public participation. The Supreme Leader has assigned the duty of investing in production to the government, industrialists, and ordinary citizens. The goal is to pool both large and small capital to revitalize domestic manufacturing and resolve livelihood issues. This economic strategy is presented as the key to restoring Iran's economy and enhancing the global competitiveness of Iranian products.
