In this radio segment, Professor Mohammadi addresses a listener's question regarding the lack of spiritual presence during religious visits. He explains that this issue stems from a lack of deep knowledge of the Infallibles and suggests that studying their virtues and observing visitation etiquette, such as reciting specific prayers, can enhance spiritual connection. The professor distinguishes between the core actions and the rituals of visitation, emphasizing that proper adherence to these rituals increases the spiritual energy of the act. He also draws a comparison between the imprisonment of religious scholars during the Islamic Revolution and the current detention of criminals, criticizing the West for its double standards in condemning executions. The program concludes with the airing of a religious song and a final prayer for the listeners.
