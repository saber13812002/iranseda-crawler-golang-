In this radio program, the mother of martyr Seyed Mohammad Hosseini from Afghanistan narrates the emotional and painful experience of sending her son to Syria to defend the shrine of Lady Zainab. She describes their deep emotional bond and his supportive nature at home, which was accompanied by religious prayers during their final moments together. The mother shares her intense fears and a prophetic dream about her husband being shot in the heart, which foreshadowed her son's martyrdom. After Seyed Mohammad's death, his friends recount his spiritual presence at Lady Zainab's shrine and his prayers for his mother. This narrative highlights the sacrifice of martyrs' mothers and the role of faith in enduring great grief. The program concludes by emphasizing the need to explain the Husseini front during Arbaeen and to support the oppressed people of Gaza. Listeners are also encouraged to send SMS messages to a specified number to provide financial aid to the people of Gaza.
