This program addressed various theological and educational inquiries from listeners. Regarding child-rearing, parents were advised to seek expert help to resolve their children's worldview doubts and then leave the outcome to God after fulfilling their duties. Subtle distinctions between concepts like soul and spirit, as well as the nature of the barzakh eye, were explained. In the discussion on monotheism, it was emphasized that while God is ontologically and spatially distinct from creation, He is constantly present through sustaining presence. It was clarified that prophets and Imams were physiologically human, and their infallibility stemmed from divine grace rather than a different species. In the ethics section, factors like genetics and environment were noted to influence personality, yet human free will always remains intact. Finally, kissing the shrine walls with the intention of receiving spiritual energy was deemed not to be shirk, as its effect is attributed to God's will and the connection to the divine representative.
