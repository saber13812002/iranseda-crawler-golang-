The head of the Hajj and Pilgrimage Organization announced that the first groups of Iranian pilgrims will depart for Medina on May 6, with an average stay of 33 days. The cost of the trip ranges from 230 to 288 million Tomans, and each pilgrim is allocated 15 euros in pilgrimage currency, accessible via the "Bale" application. Concurrently, the fourth global conference on Imam Reza (AS) will focus on human rights, featuring speakers from 21 countries. The city of Oush in Fars Province was selected as the ninth Capital of the Book of Iran, while the Ministry of Defense is hosting a nationwide conference for Quran teachers. In the religious segment, Ayatollah Vahidi emphasized the asceticism of scholars by recounting a story about Ayatollah Hujjat's detachment from worldly wealth. The program concluded with a detailed explanation of the rituals and rules for Hajj and Umrah by a jurisprudence expert.
