In a discussion with Professor Mehdi Rasakhi, the managerial strategies of the Supreme Leader in the Arbaeen of Dignity Martyrs statement were analyzed. The central theme was the vital role of the people in fostering national unity and thwarting the enemy's chaos projects, which strengthened social cohesion despite economic challenges. Rasakhi emphasized that this victory resulted from the synergy of the people, leadership, and elites, allowing Iran to maintain its regional authority without relying on foreign powers. He also stressed the necessity of continuing scientific and military advancements in fields like missiles, drones, and artificial intelligence to preserve Iran's global standing. The enemy's psychological warfare aimed at demoralizing the public failed, and this internal resilience significantly boosted Iran's credibility among nations, including in Mecca and Arab countries. Finally, maintaining this authority and national unity was presented as a critical and sensitive duty for officials and media to prevent the loss of this political and spiritual capital.
