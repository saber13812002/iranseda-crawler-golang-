The radio program "Parsaiyan" began with the recitation of the Dua al-Tawassul from the Sahifa al-Sajjadiyya, emphasizing its profound divine teachings.
The main segment explored the conduct of scholars, focusing on the good humor and cheerfulness of Imam Khomeini.
Several anecdotes were shared to illustrate the Imam's kind and humorous interactions with doctors and his close associates.
Subsequently, a short story titled "Moonlit Nights in the Cell" from the life of Ayatollah Najm-Abadi was broadcast.
In the story, a young seminary student meets a middle-aged, poor man who joins him as a cellmate and servant.
Initially, the student treats the man with reservation and distrust, maintaining a strict distance.
However, during a difficult study session, the man explains complex lessons with clarity and eloquence.
This revelation leads the student to realize the man's immense scholarly and spiritual depth, causing him to regret his earlier reserved behavior.
