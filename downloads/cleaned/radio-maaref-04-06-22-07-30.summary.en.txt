In this radio program, the importance of self-accountability and benevolence towards others in gaining divine pleasure is explained based on narrations from the Prophet Muhammad and Imam Sadiq (AS). The Prophet stated that God supports believers as long as they help one another, and making a believer happy is equivalent to making God happy. Therefore, a believer should act wisely by performing deeds that yield the highest spiritual return, such as resolving the problems of Muslims and aiding the oppressed. Even small acts like smiling, praying for others, and listening to their troubles are highly valued by God and can substitute for larger deeds. The host illustrated the significant social impact of seemingly minor actions by sharing the story of an elderly man who collected nails from the streets to prevent car punctures. Finally, emphasizing the power of motivation and hope for a bright future, the audience was encouraged to build society and alleviate the suffering of others through good deeds.
