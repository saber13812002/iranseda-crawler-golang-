The radio program "Andisheha," featuring Dr. Azodin Razanjad, examined the organizational and political ties between the Baha'i Faith and the Zionist regime. The guest argued that Baha'ism has evolved from a religious movement into a political organization dependent on Western and Jewish intelligence networks. The faith's decision-making center, the House of Justice, is located in Haifa, Israel, and is largely composed of members from Western intelligence services. Baha'is provide direct economic support to the Israeli regime through financial penalties, alms, and religious ceremonies. Furthermore, Baha'i leaders regard Israel as their promised land and lend religious legitimacy to it, thereby promoting Zionist political goals. The program concluded by emphasizing the need to strengthen students' national self-confidence and prevent the political exploitation of universities by external movements.
