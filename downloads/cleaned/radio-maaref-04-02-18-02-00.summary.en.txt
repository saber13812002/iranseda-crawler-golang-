The radio program began by highlighting how social media shapes distorted preferences and the need for organized, specialized efforts to promote good and prevent evil.
A major focus was the introduction of the book "Rub Payan," which compiles 568 pages of the Supreme Leader's statements on socialism and enemy recognition.
The discussion emphasized the book's distinction between enemy identification and strategic response, urging Muslims to develop political and cultural insight against conspiracies.
In response to listener questions about Iran's military policy, it was clarified that supporting the oppressed should involve training and empowerment rather than fighting wars on their behalf.
It was stressed that occupied nations must stand on their own feet, with Iran providing support and advice to avoid creating long-term dependency.
Historically, the occupation of Palestine following the collapse of the Ottoman Empire and Britain's role in establishing Israel were explained as acts of military aggression.
Finally, the program called for unity among the Islamic world to confront Zionist oppression and urged Muslims to awaken their conscience regarding the rights of the oppressed.
