The program began by contrasting rural and urban life, highlighting the value of authenticity and spiritual peace found in nature. The literary segment featured political poetry and a discussion about a neighbor's university entrance exam rank. The main focus was a continuation of the discussion on the consequences of arrogance and attachment to the world, led by Professor Fallah. He identified poverty, regret, humiliation, sorrow, and destruction as the worldly results of arrogance. The speaker also emphasized that the arrogant will be deceived in the afterlife and face severe regret. The final message urged listeners to avoid the deceptions of the ego and the material world, relying instead on spirituality for salvation on the Day of Judgment.
