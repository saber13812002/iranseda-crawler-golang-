The program begins with the recitation of the Fatihat prayer and mentions of the Ahl al-Bayt, transitioning into a discussion on the concept of charity within the framework of Wilayat. The speaker emphasizes that accepting the guardianship of Imam Ali and dedicating one's heart to him is essential for achieving true monotheistic upbringing and generosity. Citing Imam Ali's advice to Imam Hassan, the text presents charity in the path of God and avoiding hoarding for others as a way to lighten the burden of the afterlife. It warns that stinginess towards the Prophet and his family disqualifies a person from the spiritual benefits of Wilayat and makes them unworthy of paradise. Shias are advised to help those in need, viewing this act as an opportunity to transfer the weight of life's burdens to others and earn rewards on the Day of Judgment. The text concludes that adhering to these teachings results in raising children who, like Imam Hassan, dedicate their entire lives to God and the cause of Imam Hussein's martyrdom.
