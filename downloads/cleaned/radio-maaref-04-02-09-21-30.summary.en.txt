In this program, Dr. Talah Javadi interprets verses 90 to 93 of Surah Al-Ma'idah, emphasizing the absolute prohibition of alcohol and gambling. He explains that "khamr" literally means covering, referring to the substance that conceals reason under the veil of lust and anger. By referencing Surah Al-A'raf and Al-Baqarah, the speaker argues that alcohol is a "great sin" and a tool of Satan designed to foster enmity and sever the bond with God. The prohibition is reinforced through eight distinct Quranic indicators, including its description as an abomination and a work of Satan. The ruling applies to all intoxicating substances, not just grape wine, as any agent that impairs reason is forbidden. The speaker notes that alcohol destroys intellect and prevents the fulfillment of religious duties such as prayer. Finally, he stresses the importance of maintaining both social and divine relationships by completely abstaining from intoxicants to avoid societal corruption.
