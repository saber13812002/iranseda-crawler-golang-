The program began by offering condolences for the martyrdom anniversary of Imam Reza (PBUH) and emphasized maintaining the spiritual atmosphere of Muharram through daily prayers and attending religious gatherings.
The main discussion focused on the causes of lying, including ignorance, spiritual inferiority, and a lack of manliness.
Listening to lies and rumors was identified as a critical gateway to hell that weakens reason and strengthens ignorance.
Imam Ali (PBUH) warned in Nahj al-Balagha that repeating and spreading falsehood turns a person into a liar and removes goodness from their speech.
In the modern era, social media serves as a fertile ground for the rapid spread of rumors, necessitating caution before accepting news.
It is advised to avoid quoting unverified sources and to sever ties with dishonest individuals in all aspects of life.
Finally, the importance of choosing sincere companions was highlighted, urging listeners to avoid lying to align themselves with the path of divine guidance.
