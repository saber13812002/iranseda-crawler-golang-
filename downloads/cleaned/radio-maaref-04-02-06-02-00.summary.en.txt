In this radio program, Professor Maleki analyzes the story of the believer from Pharaoh's family in Surah Qaf, highlighting his courageous defense of Prophet Musa. The speaker identifies Pharaoh as a symbol of arrogance and extravagance, explaining that God seals the hearts of the arrogant to prevent them from accepting the truth. The concept of "Yawm al-Ahzab" is discussed as a warning against the fate of previous nations like Noah and Aad who rejected their prophets. The second part addresses the question of how the Ahl al-Bayt memorized the Quran so quickly, distinguishing their "direct knowledge" from ordinary acquired knowledge. A story about Shahid Sadouqi, who completed a Quran recitation every three days, is shared to demonstrate the feasibility of this practice alongside daily activities. The lecture concludes by advising listeners to transcend the literal words of the Quran to grasp its deeper meanings through consistent practice.
