In this radio program, the speaker emphasizes the spiritual and social value of the worker, identifying them as the main pillar of societal stability and a superior human asset compared to financial or scientific capital. He highlights two fundamental virtues of workers: earning a halal livelihood through personal effort and meeting the needs of others, which are considered moral and religious merits. The speaker warns that the enemies of the Islamic Republic have attempted to paralyze the working class since the revolution, using tactics ranging from Marxist infiltration to foreign espionage. He stresses the necessity of ensuring job security for both workers and entrepreneurs, claiming that the previous government successfully reopened approximately 8,000 closed factories through supervisory and judicial actions. Improving workers' technical and professional skills through specialized institutes and in-house corporate training is presented as a key strategy for enhancing production quality and social welfare. Furthermore, physical safety in mines and factories is identified as a critical priority to prevent tragic accidents. Finally, supporting domestic production and consuming local goods is recommended as a moral and economic duty to support Iranian workers and investors.
