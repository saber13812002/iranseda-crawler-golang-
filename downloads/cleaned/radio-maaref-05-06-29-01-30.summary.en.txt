This radio program interprets the occultation of Imam Mahdi (AJ) as a divine mission and a rigorous test designed to purify the faith of true believers.
The speaker emphasizes that the prolonged nature of this period, similar to the story of Prophet Noah, serves as an opportunity for spiritual growth and distinguishing genuine believers from hypocrites.
To maintain certainty and prevent the Imam from being forgotten in daily life, consistent recitation of supplications and building one's life around prayer and guardianship are deemed essential.
Listeners are warned against the "traces of Satan" that cause spiritual states to fade after prayer or visitation, urging them to remain vigilant of their hearts.
In the final section, popular uprisings, particularly in Iran, are described as a precursor to the appearance and ultimate victory, citing ancient narrations.
The speaker advises that as the time of reappearance approaches, trials will become more severe, requiring patience and steadfastness to prepare for this great era.
The program concludes with prayers for the hastening of the Imam's appearance and hope for ultimate victory and the arrival of the age of light.
