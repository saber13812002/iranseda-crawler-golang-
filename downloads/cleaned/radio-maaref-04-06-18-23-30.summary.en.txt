In this radio program, the distinction between the reward of reciting the Arabic Quran and reading its translation was discussed; while translation offers rewards for reflection, the specific virtues of recitation are exclusive to the original Arabic text. The program also addressed the nature of the promised Paradise, explaining that humans must complete their earthly trial and acquire divine knowledge before entering the Garden of Eden. Furthermore, the concept of sharing the rewards of good deeds with others was clarified, confirming that a general intention to share all future good deeds is sufficient without the need for repetition in each act. It was also specified that while obligatory acts like prayer cannot be performed on behalf of others, the rewards earned from these acts can be dedicated to them. Finally, the importance of faith and divine knowledge as the foundation of human salvation was emphasized, noting that prophets were sent to guide people toward God and away from hell.
