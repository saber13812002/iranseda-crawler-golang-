In this program, Professor Mohammadi emphasizes divine justice, assuring the oppressed that God will avenge them against their oppressors. He refutes the claim of being deprived of spiritual blessings, defining faith in God as the greatest spiritual grace. Addressing human violence, he clarifies that evil is not innate but stems from poor choices and Satan's interference, which can be repelled by invoking God's name. The reason for Imam Ali's non-violent response to the attack on Hazrat Zahra's house was to prevent civil war and protect Islam from collapse. True courage in such contexts is defined as restraining oneself from drawing a sword to preserve the religion from destruction. The broadcast concludes with a prayer for rain and a recommendation to conserve water due to resource scarcity. Finally, the 1404 slogan of "investment for production with public participation" is presented as the key to resolving economic challenges.
