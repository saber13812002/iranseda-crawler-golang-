This radio program explains love as the mortar of unity that connects hearts and facilitates social cohesion. True leadership belongs to those who dissolve their ego in God's will, using altruism to attract others, as symbolized by Talut. Divine obedience is presented as the core of this leadership, where following a divinely appointed commander is equivalent to obeying God. Effective command requires a combination of theoretical and practical wisdom, specifically the ability to recognize and implement truth while refuting falsehood. Worldly sovereignty is described as a manifestation of God's absolute ownership, preparing believers to understand divine kingship on the Day of Judgment. A hadith from Imam Sajjad (AS) highlights that those who love each other purely for God's sake will enter Paradise without undergoing the trial of judgment. This underscores that pure divine love is the essential fuel for spiritual progress and the key to salvation in the afterlife.
