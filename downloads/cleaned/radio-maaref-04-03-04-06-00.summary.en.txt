This program analyzes the concept of "O'likum anfusakum" from verse 105 of Surah Al-Ma'idah, based on the exegesis of Ayatollah Javadi Amoli. The interpreter emphasizes that the Day of Judgment is a venue for the manifestation of inner human realities rather than the creation of new events. In this spiritual framework, a person is simultaneously the traveler, the path, and the destination, selling their soul either to God or to Satan. If the soul is sold to God, the individual becomes a spirit and a garden, with their inner paradise manifesting; if sold to Satan, they become the fuel for hellfire. The discussion also details the presence of deeds on Judgment Day, where the human soul acts as the scribe, the book, and the written content. While angels and other witnesses play complementary roles, the inner ledger of the soul aligns with angelic records because it cannot contain lies. Ultimately, the Day of Judgment is described as the unfolding of this inner ledger, revealing all deeds with the exact quality in which they were performed.
