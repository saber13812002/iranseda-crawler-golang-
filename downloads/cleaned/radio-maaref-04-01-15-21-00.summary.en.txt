In this broadcast from Radio Ma'aref, researcher Dr. Nasabeh discusses the history of the Persian Gulf's occupation by colonial powers and the role of prominent figures in southern Iran. The main topic is the liberation of Kharak Island from Dutch forces by Mir Mehna, the young governor of Bandar Rig. By destroying the English trading house and rejecting their claims of superiority, Mir Mehna demonstrated that Iran would not allow unrestricted foreign entry. He employed a military ruse to capture two Dutch warships by disguising his vessels as supply transports. Although English warships intervened to support the Dutch, Mir Mehna's swift action prevented the enemy from regrouping. This narrative highlights how Western colonial powers united whenever their shared interests were threatened, offering a historical lesson for Iran. The program also emphasizes the importance of understanding history to better comprehend the country's current situation and future path.
